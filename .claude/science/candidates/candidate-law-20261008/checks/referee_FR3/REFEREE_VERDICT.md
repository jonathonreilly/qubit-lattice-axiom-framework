1. **Continuous-time swap gas — FAIL confirmed, mode count corrected.** The symmetric exclusion generator is self-adjoint and the total occupancy Fourier mode is an exact diffusive eigenfunction ([fr3_swap_exact.py:26–48](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/referee_FR3/fr3_swap_exact.py:26)). However, “one density mode” is true only in the unlabeled occupancy sector. Permanent inert contents introduce additional conserved composition fields. The script’s own exact check gives residual \(8.25\times10^{-2}\) for a content-density candidate, so it is not the same exact free-walker eigenmode ([fr3_swap_exact.py:91–98](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/referee_FR3/fr3_swap_exact.py:91)). Those content fields—including tensor-valued labels—are nevertheless diffusive hydrodynamically, not propagating TT waves.

Thus there is no evidence for a hydrodynamic TT wave, but “monotone for every observable” is false as a theorem for this tick.

3. **Wind gas conservation and hydrodynamic modes — confirmed within mean field.** Direct recomputation gives a four-dimensional \(k=0\) nullspace: \(N\) plus three momentum components. Consequently the long-wavelength sector contains one sound pair and two shear-diffusion modes; the stated

follows from the product-state linearization ([fr3_wind_meanfield.py:32–75](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/referee_FR3/fr3_wind_meanfield.py:32)). 5. **Collisionless wind gas — still FAIL.** At \(\gamma=0\), all six species populations are conserved and produce collisionless kinetic branches. Their speeds are anisotropic. A nonzero TT projection of a mode’s diagonal kinetic stress is not equivalent to a helicity-\(\pm2\) eigenmode. Along a lattice axis, the propagating pair has zero TT stress; the modes with TT fraction \(1/2\) are nonpropagating. Generically the projected fractions reported by the script belong to mixed kinetic modes with unequal speeds ([fr3_tt_content.py:15–31](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/referee_FR3/fr3_tt_content.py:15)). They are not an isotropic two-polarization graviton sector.

6. **Detailed balance claim — valid only in its precise continuous-time form.** For a continuous-time generator satisfying detailed balance, self-adjointness in \(L^2(\pi)\) gives real nonpositive eigenvalues and a stationary same-observable autocorrelation
\[
C_A(t)=\sum_n |\langle A,\phi_n\rangle_\pi|^2e^{\lambda_n t},
\]
which is completely monotone. This does not say every cross-correlation or arbitrary nonequilibrium expectation must be monotone. A reversible discrete-time transition matrix has real eigenvalues, but negative eigenvalues can produce period-two alternation. The theorem applies to the continuous-time swap model and the continuous-time interpretation of the vacancy Metropolis model; it does not apply to the nonreversible A27 tick or wind streaming dynamics.

7. **Dense vacancy model — FAIL confirmed, “crystal” claim narrowed.** Its proposal is symmetric and the acceptance ratio is exactly the change of the stated pair weight, so detailed balance holds ([fr3_lg.c:77–81](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/referee_FR3/fr3_lg.c:77)). It conserves the two content populations separately—equivalently \(N\) and magnetization—but not pair energy, and supplies overdamped diffusive fields. It has no record displacement, velocity, or conjugate momentum variable, so this implementation has no phonons. Fixed \(\mathbb Z^3\) itself does not prohibit phonons: a fixed reference lattice with dynamical displacement and momentum fields supports them. Ordinary transverse phonons are helicity \(\pm1\), however, and would still not satisfy FR3.

8. **Speed claim — algebra correct, phenomenological inference conditional.** The differential relation \(d c_s/c_s=-d\rho/[2(1-\rho)]\) follows from the mean-field formula ([fr3_sound_vs_density.py:4–7](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/referee_FR3/fr3_sound_vs_density.py:4)). Matching another light sector would require an unexplained rate normalization. If one additionally demands pointwise \(10^{-15}\) stability with all other parameters fixed, then \(|\delta\rho|\lesssim2(1-\rho)10^{-15}\). But GW170817 constrains an integrated propagation delay, approximately \(-3\times10^{-15}<(v_g-c)/c<7\times10^{-16}\), not directly pointwise density uniformity ([LIGO/Virgo/Fermi/INTEGRAL analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.05834)).

**Strongest correct statement:** For the specified symmetric swap gas, the six-direction wind gas, and the overdamped vacancy-Metropolis lattice gas, record motion does not produce two massless transverse-traceless helicity modes at a protected light speed: the swap and vacancy fields are diffusive, while the wind gas has scalar sound, vector shear, and only one axis-TT-like stress component, which is gapped when scattering is present and lacks the cross polarization. This is a model-specific exclusion, not a no-go theorem for the entire moving-record framework: record identities could encode displacements and momenta, and records could carry a symmetric tensor plus a conjugate/current variable with local wave dynamics, although such an extension would still need TT constraints, two polarizations, isotropy, and non-tuned equality with the light-sector speed.
