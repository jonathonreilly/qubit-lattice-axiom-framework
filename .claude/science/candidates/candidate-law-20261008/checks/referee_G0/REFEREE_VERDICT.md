## VERDICT: CONFIRMED WITH CORRECTIONS

The FAIL is confirmed only for the finite, single-monomial product classes actually enumerated, with the holonomy required to equal the scalar \(-1\) on the entire occupation-specified 13-qubit domain. It is not a general no-go for arbitrary local one-qubit-per-site Hamiltonians.

1. **Class A — confirmed.** Independent enumeration reproduced exactly 32 of 2048 relaxed-covariant patterns and \(+1\) on all 20 junctions for every pattern. I also checked that all 32 admit frames satisfying the projective group law, not merely the relaxed stabilizer test. See [referee_independent.py:95](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/referee_G0/referee_independent.py:95).

2. **Classes B/C — corrected.** The exhaustive headline is valid in the narrower form: no enumerated candidate has a state-independent scalar \(-1\) holonomy; every nonzero scalar result is \(+1\). The rerun reproduced:

   - B: 2560 candidates, no scalar \(-1\).
   - C1: 8192 candidates; 2560 scalar \(+1\), 1536 nonscalar, 4096 classified “zero.”
   - C2: 20,480 candidates; 2560 scalar \(+1\), 1536 nonscalar, 16,384 classified “zero.”

   But “no candidate gives \(-1\) on any junction” and “non-vanishing holonomies are always \(+1\)” are false. The covariant C candidate `XS0|S0,S1,S1,S1,S1` takes both \(+1\) and \(-1\) on every T-junction as the link background varies; the independent reproduction is at [referee_independent.py:111](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/referee_G0/referee_independent.py:111). It fails PASS because it is nonscalar, not because it never produces \(-1\).

3. **Controls — confirmed, with wording narrowed.** Every tournament makes each pair of distinct hops anticommute once, so each three-leg sign contains three minus signs and equals \(-1\). The subgroup enumeration covers all 30 subgroups of \(O\): invariant tournaments exist only for the identity and four \(C_3\) subgroups. A total order induces a transitive tournament; the enumeration used all tournaments, which is a stronger obstruction than testing total orders alone.

4. **Helper face qubits — corrected.** Under the relaxed stabilizer filter, the nontrivial face-orbit pattern flips all eight corner junctions while leaving all twelve T-junctions \(+1\). But those corner-flipping patterns fail the exact order-two frame group law: [c9b_face_detail.py:9](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/referee_G0/c9b_face_detail.py:9). Thus the positive corner statement is only about the relaxed superset. The negative T-junction result remains valid for the searched helper Pauli orbits.

5. **General pair-sign argument — corrected.** It is a valid proof for sign-type product factors—those for which every local factor pair commutes or anticommutes—and it can be extended to arbitrary finite range after repairing the geometric argument. One half-turn pairs off-axis sites; a second proper turn pairs the two non-origin sites on the fixed axis; the origin contributes zero because all proper occupation-transfer factors there are proportional to \(\sigma^-\). Hence the opposite-direction commutation parity is even and every T sign is \(+1\).

   It is not a proof for arbitrary product dressings. [c8_signformula.py:1](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/referee_G0/c8_signformula.py:1) explicitly assumes sign-type relations; continuous/non-Clifford factors need not commute or anticommute. The nonscalar C example demonstrates the failure of that reduction even for a fully covariant product table.

6. **Scope — mostly confirmed, but BKSF must be stated narrowly.** Sums of several terms, continuous non-Clifford dressings, nonproduct composites such as CZ, constrained code/flux sectors, corner records, nonlocal strings, and extra qubits/modes remain untested. Original BKSF indeed requires an ordering of incident edges, but the calculation rules out only keeping that order/tournament invariant under the restricted direction-permutation plus onsite-\(Z_4\)-phase action—not every symmetry implementation of BKSF. The BKSF construction’s ordering dependence is explicit in the [superfast-encoding paper](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.1.033033). The Verstraete–Cirac statement is accurate: its locality mechanism introduces auxiliary Majorana degrees of freedom, as stated in the [original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0508353).
