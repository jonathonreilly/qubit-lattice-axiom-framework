  E'-conserving: branch (b) cannot coexist with gauge coupling of the identically charged pi copy.

## Test K3-T1: signed linear response of U_g's arc vacuum to a uniform Peierls field

Object: F3's U_g (copied definition from F3 scripts/r1f2_vertex.py: U = a - i g sin(k).sigma,

- A_abs(w) = (2pi)^-3 Int d^3k sum_j |M_j|^2 delta(2 eps_+(k) - w)          (light sector, absorbs w)
- A_em(w)  = (2pi)^-3 Int d^3k sum_j |M_j|^2 delta(2 eps_+(k) - (2pi - w))   (pi sector, emits w)
Computed by radial root-finding around every node (8 corners), 2000+ directions; plus a
coarse full-BZ histogram cross-check that no weight at |2eps_+| < w_max or > 2pi - w_max comes
from outside the corner shells.

Frequencies: w = 0.2, 0.1, 0.05, 0.02, 0.01 (lattice units, 1 tick = 1).

  net absorption A_abs - A_em is < 5% of A_abs; and the pi-sector term enters only as emission at

- **F3-A-READING-SURVIVES:** the pi sector carries absorption weight at small w > 0 comparable to

  is only modified, not reversed).
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** ratio within 5-20% of 1, or root-finding fails on > 1% of directions.
