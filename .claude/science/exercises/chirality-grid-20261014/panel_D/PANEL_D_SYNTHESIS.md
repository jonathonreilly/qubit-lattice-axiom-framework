## Corrections to the dossier and packet, from the panel

1. The corner package X2 needs **32** complex fermion modes per site, not 16 (skeptic, qinfo, lattice).
2. **Set C also carries a price.** The kinetic-isotropy primitive needs re-reading on Z³ × Z_τ, plus the tuned matter speeds: 4 per charge class per order (skeptic).
3. **"Π-W for matter" does not repair K2c's failure.** K2c's weight is real and positive (gravity: phase +1 to 1e-16). The failure belongs to the overlap determinant, not the gauge weight: a strictly positive-type triangle weight also fails on the torus (lattice: Lt = 12, relative −6.2e-6, error estimate 7.7e-11; same family, unrefereed). "Π-W" for matter therefore means abandoning OS positivity, which conflicts with AX:60-61 and WALL:44. Rename it Π-W′ so it is not priced as the milder Π-W.
4. **"5D without Pauli–Villars" is RP only for vector-like content.** Chirality then needs far-wall gapping, i.e. frontier question (I).
5. **Z₄ fermion number** needs a registered ΔF = 4 (proton decay) row. It is safe by about 1e10 at Planck-scale dimension 6 and would fail at about 1e16 GeV (skeptic, qinfo, strategy). The strategy lens adds that Z₄^F = Z₄^X implies Dirac neutrinos and no 0νββ, a prediction (being checked in OBS-1).
6. **Missing from the price list:** the gauge-fixing approach (Rome; Bock–Golterman–Shamir), and D-theory (Brower–Chandrasekharan–Wiese) as a candidate single "ladder per site" carrying the links, mass protection and the slab (lattice).
7. **Two landed notes read the axiom as one fermion mode per site.** They need re-scoping if a register is adopted (qinfo).

## Owner question raised during the panel (Jon, 21:10Z)

"Why does the site need to carry the colour — can't it be combinations of sites?" Answer given: the "≥ 16 per site" bound assumes the colour gauge acts on each single site. Spreading colour over sites is possible in three ways:
- **(i) blocks:** a translation price;
- **(ii) staggered-style twisted translations:** open for continuous SU(3); the lattice lens says staggering works only for finite groups;
- **(iii) emergent colour:** unbuilt in 3+1D.
