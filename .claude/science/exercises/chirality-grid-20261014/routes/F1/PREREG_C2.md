# F1 check C2 — pre-registration (written 2026-10-14 13:43Z, before running)

**Purpose.** Locate, on the SPIN10 note's own slab (`SPIN10_…_2026-09-14.md:168-186`), where the
Golterman–Shamir (GS, arXiv:2505.20436 §III) "bound-state" picture is forced to put the extra light
states once the mirror B_L is paired with a local composite χ of opposite chirality. This turns GS's
conjecture into concrete falsifier signatures for the decisive many-body test (it is not itself that test).

**Model class (supplied, quadratic "reduced" models in GS's sense).** Slab h_L(k), L = 2 and 3, Wilson
b(k) = Σ(1 − cos k_i), plus a composite χ on the mirror layer coupled on-site to B_L by Δ ∈ {0.3, 1, 3}:
- (a) χ is a bare lattice Weyl, h_χ = +σ·s(k) (no partner);
- (b) χ has its own Wilson partner χ′: [[σ·s, r b],[r b, −σ·s]] on (χ, χ′), r = 1.

**Measured.** All zero-energy points on a 16³ grid plus exact corners and k = 0; the chirality of each
(Chern number of the occupied bands on a small sphere); where the zero modes live (layer/field weights).

**Fixed readings.**
- **GS-a CONFIRMED** if in class (a) the k = 0 mirror is gapped and new zero modes appear at the 7 nonzero
  corners, dominated by χ / far-layer weight, with net chirality equal to the mirror's (spectrum
  vector-like overall).
- **GS-b CONFIRMED** if in class (b) the corners are gapped and a zero mode with the mirror's chirality
  appears at k = 0, dominated by χ′ (a massless composite with mirror quantum numbers: the
  Eichten–Preskill failure).
- **ANOMALY** if either class shows a net chiral light spectrum (that would contradict Nielsen–Ninomiya and
  mean a bug).

**Scope.** Free reduced models only; they obey NN by construction, so the check is a locator, not
evidence
for or against GS. The many-body question is untouched.
