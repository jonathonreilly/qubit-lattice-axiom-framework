## (a) Tried and failed, with reasons

1. **Wilson term on the Cl(3) Pauli walk.** It removes 7 corners but breaks chiral anticommutation (DW-edge :46-49; Wilson-breaks historic intake [SYN] :19). The scalar hop alone does not gap the corners (block 77 :33; block 82 :27, where covariance forces a scalar symbol at a corner).
2. **Clock/rate fields to gap the species.** Every positive rate field keeps exactly 16 zero modes (block 119 :33, :89).
3. **Staggered mass mε.** It gaps by pairing opposite-sense corners (k ↔ k+π(1,1,1)), so it is vector-like: a mass, not chirality (block 77 :44-52; record-native mass :5 "two Dirac flavors"). It is also the only conservation-keeping site term (block 139 :42-43).
4. **Domain walls in a supplied record-time coordinate.** One Weyl per wall, always an opposite partner on the anti-wall or the interval end. Counts are balanced (record-time note :96, :100). The inflow is an avoided crossing (:90). The exact-cone and universal claims are withdrawn (:130).
5. **Vortex in a 2D record time / strings.** The 2D vortex has a balanced index (vortex :172-177). Strings carry 2n co-moving modes, always paired with −2n. Their handedness is the Jackiw–Rossi −Γ_3, not γ5 (taste-singlet :184-186, :217). The NN taste-triplet second mass cancels to net 0 (:195). A flux tube alone gives nothing (:207).
6. **Mirror removal by symmetric (Spin(10)) atomic gaps on a Wilson slab.** Freezing the full layer keeps the partner. Freezing only the mirror slots restores 8 Weyl doublers, 4+ and 4− (SPIN10 :200-221). A quadratic auxiliary also keeps them (§7 :300+). Reason given: the projection endpoint is a strong-coupling limit, which matches the Golterman–Shamir warning (:373-374).
7. **Edge spinor → SM 15.** The dimensions mismatch (2 vs 8; 16 vs 15) and the 6+2 subset is anomalous (SM map :156-205).
8. **Factorized chiral-walk coin in 3+1D.** This is a gravity-transport failure, not a chirality one. Coupling the coin helped (coupled-coin :43-51).
