## Exercise Result

The analogy is useful only after it is translated into finite framework terms:

- blend = coexistence of two candidate hands/sectors;
- flip = symmetric transfer or involution between them;
- filter = asymmetric section, sink, source character, chiral anticommutation,
  boundary condition, or readout rule;
- dynamic kinetic resolution = flip plus filter.

The finite runner shows that blend and flip do not select a hand.  Dynamic
resolution selects only because the asymmetric filter is already present.  This
makes the frontier target sharper: derive the filter/section natively.
