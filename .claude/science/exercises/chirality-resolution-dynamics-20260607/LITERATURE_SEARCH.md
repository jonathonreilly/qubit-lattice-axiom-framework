# Literature Search

The following sources were used as analogy or proof-template pointers only.  No
paper result is imported as a framework derivation.

## Enzyme / Resolution Analogy

- Markus Schober and Kurt Faber, "Inverting hydrolases and their use in
  enantioconvergent biotransformations", Trends in Biotechnology 31(8), 2013.
  PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3725421/

  Use here: vocabulary for kinetic resolution versus enantioconvergent/dynamic
  processes.  The key analogy is that exceeding a racemic-yield wall requires a
  stereoselective operation, not just a blend.

- Jeremy A. Bearne and coauthors, "Racemases and epimerases operating through a
  1,1-proton transfer mechanism: reactivity, mechanism and inhibition", 2020.
  PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8142540/

  Use here: reversible stereochemical flip mechanisms are real in chemistry, but
  reversibility alone does not provide a selection direction.

## Lattice / Index Pointers

- Simon Catterall, "Chiral Lattice Fermions From Staggered Fields",
  arXiv:2010.02290.  https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.02290

  Use here: pointer that staggered-field chiral mechanisms can be studied with
  explicit filters/interactions.  Not used as a framework premise.

- Fukaya et al., "The index of lattice Dirac operators and K-theory",
  arXiv:2407.17708.  https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.17708

  Use here: pointer that eta/spectral-flow index machinery is an active
  mathematical route for lattice Dirac operators.  Not used as a framework
  premise.
