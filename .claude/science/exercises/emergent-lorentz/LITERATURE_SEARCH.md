## Verdict
**No literature mechanism FORCES `c_t=c_s` in a FREE + gauge theory on a regular spatial lattice
with no custodial symmetry.** The marginal dim-2 operator (i) is marginal at the Gaussian fixed
point → runs at most logarithmically (Chadha–Nielsen); (ii) is regenerated at O(1) by radiative
corrections with the lattice/Planck cutoff (**Collins et al** — the naturalness theorem); (iii) has
no custodial protection (SUSY is the known protector — absent). The two attractor routes
(interacting fixed point; custodial symmetry) both require something the FREE axioms lack.

→ This is precisely why the BUILT result is scoped **free/tree-level + structural** (the marginal
gate is dissolved by route-selection on the native continuous-time surface, where the dim-2 invariant
space is 1-dimensional). The interacting radiative-naturalness question (Collins et al) is the named
honest residual #2 and the right next target; the candidate forcing-route is the interacting IR fixed
point (Bednik–Pujolàs–Sibiryakov / GNY) — to be translated into a framework β-function runner, not imported.
