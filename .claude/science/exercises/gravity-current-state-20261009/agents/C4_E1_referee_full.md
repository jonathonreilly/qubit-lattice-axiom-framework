# VERDICT: CONFIRMED WITH CORRECTIONS

The worker's bounded FAIL survives adversarial checking under the FIXED reading in `TESTS.md:1388-1391`: strong matter dynamics can make matter-sector relative speeds irrelevant under stated fixed-point hypotheses, but in the examined spectator-gravity class it neither makes the local TT vacuum response an irrelevant matter operator nor produces IR attraction of the graviton speed. Several statements need narrower scope, and the claimed extra graviton-speed “tuning” is a generic cost rather than a refereed count.

## 1. Redundancy, marginality, and comparison with BPS

**Confirmed, with hypotheses made explicit.** A common `T_00` deformation is the tangent to a constant-background-metric family and is redundant after completing the deformation beyond first order; this is stated correctly at `E1_report.md:71-79` and qualified correctly at `E1_report.md:247-248`. At a product fixed point, separately conserved stress tensors give physical but exactly marginal relative speeds (`E1_report.md:81-86`). If an interaction instead drives the theory to a unitary 4D CFT with a unique conserved stress tensor, and the relative-speed perturbation has nonzero overlap with the orthogonal spin-2 primary, that primary is nonconserved and therefore has strictly `Delta > 4`; its coupling is irrelevant (`E1_report.md:87-90`). The strict inequality follows from saturation of the spin-2 unitarity bound only for a conserved current. This is the same structure described by [Bednik–Pujolàs–Sibiryakov (BPS)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.0011): relevant inter-sector coupling can lead to a unique stress tensor and Lorentz attraction, whereas irrelevant coupling leaves a Lorentz-violating fixed line. It proves the sign of the exponent, not that it is large, and it does not by itself remove separately allowed spin-1 deformations.

## 2. Operators under proper cubic `O` plus time

**The representation count is confirmed; the discrete-symmetry conclusion is overstated.** An independent projector calculation finds cubic singlets at spins `l = 0,4,6,8,9,10,12,...`, none at `l = 1,2,3`, and the first nonscalar at `l = 4` (`referee_checks.py:18-50`, `referee_checks.out:1-3`). Thus the common spin-2 speed is redundant, a nonconserved relative spin-2 deformation is irrelevant under the item-1 hypotheses, and spin `>= 3` and cubic `l = 4` anisotropies are irrelevant by the 4D unitarity floors (`E1_report.md:103-121`). Corrections: the dangerous vector range is `3 <= Delta <= 4`—with the nonconserved part `3 < Delta <= 4`—not merely the open interval `3 < Delta < 4`; a conserved `Delta = 3` current gives a chemical potential that is not generically removable, and the `Delta = 4` endpoint can be marginal. Also, “every `u·V` term is CPT-odd” (`E1_report.md:113`) and “needs an antiunitary symmetry including inversion” (`E1_report.md:124`) are too broad. The minimal-SME examples `a_0`, `b_0`, and `k_AF` are CPT-odd, but an abstract vector primary needs its own discrete quantum numbers, and `k_AF` is not cleanly a gauge-invariant local CFT primary. A CPT-like antiunitary operation containing inversion is one sufficient closure, not a necessary one: separate charge conjugation can forbid `a_0`, while parity/axis reversal can forbid `b_0` and `k_AF`. Proper `O` alone supplies neither (`MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md:35-41`; compare `C4_F2_referee.md:39-42`).

## 3. One-loop relaxation and drag

**The Anber–Donoghue transcriptions and linear checks are confirmed.** Direct linearization of the published beta functions gives Yukawa coefficients `(1/4,-1/24) g^2/pi^2` and QED coefficients `(1/6,-1/3) e^2/pi^2`; the weighted sums `1 beta_b + 6 beta_f` and `12 beta_gamma + 6 beta_f` vanish, while the relative rates are `7g^2/(24pi^2)` and `e^2/(2pi^2)` (`referee_checks.py:53-62`, `referee_checks.out:4-7`; [Anber–Donoghue](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0789)). This validates `E1_report.md:98-101` in those two weakly coupled examples. The general formula at `E1_report.md:92-97` is nevertheless a scaling/multiplet-recombination argument: its coefficient also depends on the interaction and OPE normalization, and a `C_T`-weighted terminal mean is not established as a global invariant through an arbitrary strong flow with changing degrees of freedom. What is exact is the zero mode along the total conserved stress tensor. Elementary gauge drag being logarithmic is supported; “the graviton is frozen” (`E1_report.md:128-136`) should mean that an irrelevant gravitational coupling supplies no IR attraction, not that gravity receives literally no finite threshold or loop renormalization.

## 4. Chadha–Nielsen/DGLAP and the quoted SM factors

**The matrix identification and arithmetic are confirmed, but the labels are too inclusive.** Isotropic quark/gluon or electron/photon speed coefficients multiply the same spin-2 operators as the `n = 2` twist-2/DGLAP system, up to transpose and RG-normalization conventions; the zero eigenvector is the conserved total stress tensor (`E1_report.md:138-147`). Independent moments give `16/9` and `n_f/3`, hence the reported `n_f = 6` singlet exponent `1.07937`, Planck-to-TeV singlet factor `0.19045`, and instantaneous `gamma = 0.10823` at `alpha_s = 0.09` (`referee_checks.py:64-99`, `referee_checks.out:8-12`). The `0.19` is only the quark–gluon singlet eigenmode with fixed `n_f = 6`; a flavor-nonsinglet mode gives about `0.458`, so it is not a universal QCD suppression factor. The `0.779` value is also correct for an electron-only QED toy run from `M_Pl` to `m_e`, but not for the full thresholded electroweak Standard Model. Thus `E1_report.md:149-156` correctly establishes that measured weak couplings are far too slow, while “SM drag factors” should be read as illustrative one-loop mode factors rather than a full-SM evolution.

## 5. TT stiffness and graviton speed

**The bounded failure is correct; the shell factor is conditional rather than a theorem.** The local TT coefficient in the background generating functional is a contact/local gravitational coupling, not a nontrivial primary deformation of the matter CFT, so matter-primary anomalous dimensions do not automatically make it irrelevant (`E1_report.md:160-164`). Once it is matched to an independent dynamical metric, the corresponding mass-squared is a relevant gravity-sector coupling. That mass interpretation requires a stationary background/tadpole cancellation and kinetic normalization; for an emergent graviton the computed stiffness is only a naturalness diagnostic (`PRICE_NOTE.md:20-24`). The integral ratio `4/(4+gamma)` is algebraically correct if, and only if, `eta(E)=(E/Lambda)^gamma`, each shell contributes linearly as `E^3 eta(E)dE`, and there is no symmetry, sign cancellation, or sum rule (`referee_checks.py:113-116`, `referee_checks.out:19-23`). It is therefore not a universal suppression bound; the worker acknowledges this at `E1_report.md:247-250`. The displayed `(178 eV)^4` uses a naive reduced-Planck conversion, whereas the packet's refereed P0 normalization is `2.39e8 eV^4 = (124 eV)^4` and a residual fraction `5.7e-99` (`referee_checks.out:24-25`, `PRICE_NOTE.md:22-24`); this factor-of-four normalization difference does not affect the verdict. For graviton speed, the correct result is again absence of IR attraction through an irrelevant spectator coupling (`E1_report.md:181-185`), not exact nonrenormalization. Absent a gravitational Ward identity, sum rule, or UV matching, the relative graviton speed remains an independent coefficient constrained at roughly `10^-15`; calling it definitively “one more tuning” overstates P0, whose total speed-sector tuning count remains unresolved (`PRICE_NOTE.md:24`, `C4_F2_referee.md:37-42`).

## 6. Required exponents and literature comparison

**The freeze-out requirements are confirmed, but one claimed pass is arithmetically false.** For order-one UV Lorentz violation and freeze-out at `1/10/100 TeV`, direct recomputation gives the report's `gamma` ranges `1.15-1.59`; the above-exit, positive-sign proton-Cherenkov comparator instead requires `2.76` (`referee_checks.py:102-110`, `referee_checks.out:13-18`; `E1_report.md:207-218`). `gamma = 2` gives about `7e-31` at a `10 TeV` exit and passes the frozen low-energy matter bounds, but at `10^20 eV` it gives about `6.7e-17`, not the required `4.4e-23`. Thus the claim that BPW `kappa = 2` passes “including the 2.76 case marginally” (`E1_report.md:218`) is wrong by roughly six orders of magnitude (`referee_checks.out:18`). It passes that UHE row only if the constrained sign is absent or that elementary above-exit extrapolation is inapplicable. [Baggioli–Pujolàs–Wu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11552) do exhibit bottom-up holographic examples with generic LV exponent `kappa` of order `1-2`, generated with an order-`10-20` bulk quartic; this is evidence of possible exponent size, not a nearest-neighbour qubit realization or a constructed SM flow. The comparison to BPS at `E1_report.md:65-67,215-218` mixes conventions: BPS's roughly `0.7` is a special observable scaling as `2 alpha` because its leading correction is quadratic, while the underlying generic least-irrelevant exponent discussed there is about `alpha <= 0.35`. None of these papers supplies R1-R5 or the nearest-neighbour Hamiltonian whose absence is correctly admitted at `E1_report.md:222-230`.

## Strongest correct statement

For a unitary 4D matter flow to a Lorentz-invariant fixed point with a unique stress tensor, no allowed vector deformation in `3 <= Delta <= 4`, and an elementary or induced spectator graviton coupled only through irrelevant gravity, the common matter speed is redundant and interacting matter-sector relative speeds are irrelevant, but matter RG neither turns the local TT vacuum-response coefficient into an irrelevant matter deformation nor attracts the graviton speed to the matter cone; absent a separate gravitational Ward identity, exact sum rule, or UV matching condition, the P0 TT naturalness problem remains and graviton–matter speed matching remains an independent unresolved cost, while measured-SM logarithmic running is far too slow. A holographic `kappa` near 2 demonstrates that the frozen low-energy matter bounds can be met conditionally, but it misses the report's sign-dependent above-exit proton comparator, and no nearest-neighbour qubit law yielding that stage and the Standard Model has been exhibited. Therefore E1 is **FAIL (bounded)** for the examined spectator-gravity class, not a no-go against all strong-dynamics or emergent-gravity constructions.
