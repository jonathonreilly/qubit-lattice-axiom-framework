Full referee report: [E2_referee.md](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C4/ref_E2/E2_referee.md:1)

1. **One-loop coefficients:** Confirmed, including the QED singlet rate and gauge-parameter independence at one-loop logarithmic order. They agree with [Chadha–Nielsen](https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(83)90081-0) and [Anber–Donoghue](https://arxiv.org/html/1102.0789v2). Correction: the Roy–Juričić–Herbut comparison uses a formal field-content mapping, not “two-component counting.” [Report:7](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C4/ref_E2/E2_referee.md:7)

2. **Integrated suppressions:** QED 0.826858 and QCD 0.190270 are confirmed. The SM value 0.911 is reproducible but is a largest singular value in a chosen unweighted norm, not a basis-independent RG eigenmode. [Report:14](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C4/ref_E2/E2_referee.md:14)

3. **Power laws:** GNY gives 0.4667 and 0.6190; the large-N singlet `4−d`, QED3 `64/(5π²N₂c)`, and GN `16/(15π²N)` calculations reproduce. The QED3 convention agrees with [Vafek–Tešanović–Franz](https://arxiv.org/html/cond-mat/0203047). The BPS `z≤2` statement applies only to their listed subclasses, not every 4D CFT. [Report:19](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C4/ref_E2/E2_referee.md:19)

4. **Photon cone:** The linearized identity is correct. The `S≈10⁻²` optimum is correct only for the scanned elementary unit-charge species with `α_UV≤1`; it is not a universal bound for arbitrary charged strong sectors. [Report:26](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C4/ref_E2/E2_referee.md:26)

5. **Bounds:** The required exponents 1.27564, 1.37028, and 2.50898 and the 8–13 available decades are correct. Neutrino and UHECR results remain conditional on E2’s memory-comparator bounds; LHAASO alone suffices for the verdict. [Report:36](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C4/ref_E2/E2_referee.md:36)

6. **Graviton:** A nonzero canonical mass is relevant and its dimensionless importance grows by 1.4884×10³² to TeV energies. But this does not establish that every realization generates such a mass. The stiffness fraction and graviton-speed pull are model-dependent cutoff estimates; “the graviton is frozen” is not proved. [Report:42](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C4/ref_E2/E2_referee.md:42)

7. **Verdict:** The FIXED FAIL clause is met without the disputed graviton claims: the examined continuous-time QED realization’s bound-required electron–photon split runs much too slowly. The conclusion does not exclude composite, hypercubic-protected, or emergent-gravity constructions. [Report:48](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C4/ref_E2/E2_referee.md:48)

**Strongest correct statement:** For F2’s examined continuous-time lattice QED realization, the verified one-loop flow suppresses the cutoff split 8.55×10⁻³ only to 7.34×10⁻³ at 1.4 PeV, while photon survival requires at most 2.66×10⁻¹⁹; the result therefore misses by 2.76×10¹⁶ and satisfies the FIXED “needed running is too slow” FAIL clause. The coefficient and exponent arithmetic is otherwise sound in its stated conventions, but the SM 0.911 number is norm-specific, the `S∼10⁻²` result is an elementary-species scan rather than a universal photon bound, and the graviton stiffness/speed claims are conditional estimates. Thus the strongest defensible verdict is **FAIL (bounded)**, not a universal no-go.
