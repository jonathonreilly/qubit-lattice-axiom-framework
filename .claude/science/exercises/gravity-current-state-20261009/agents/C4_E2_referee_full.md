## 4. Photon-cone identity and the suppression scan

- **Identity confirmed, with its stated perturbative scope.** Expanding `c_γ = √(b/a)` for `a = (e_UV²c_γ0)⁻¹ + X/c_m` and `b = c_γ0/e_UV² + Xc_m` gives
  
  `c_γ−c_m = (c_γ0−c_m)(e_IR²/e_UV²) + O((c_γ0−c_m)²)`,
  
  exactly as shown independently in `referee_checks.out:17` and `referee_checks.out:19`. "All orders in matter self-coupling" is defensible only when the matter sector itself has one Lorentz cone, so its conserved-current two-point function has the covariant tensor form; the statement remains first order in the cone split and leading order in gauging that current.
- **Elementary-species scan confirmed.** With α(TeV) = 1/128 and α_UV ≤ 1, the optima are 1.0539×10⁻² for 16 Dirac species and 1.1960×10⁻² for 64 complex scalars (`referee_checks.out:20`, `referee_checks.out:21`). Allowing scalar α_UV ≤ 4π gives 1.2047×10⁻³ (`referee_checks.out:22`).
- **Material correction:** `S ≃ 10⁻²` is not a universal lower bound on an elementary photon's *total relative-cone* suppression. It is the result of this one-loop scan over identical elementary unit-charge Dirac/scalar species with α_UV ≤ 1. The photon-side factor is fixed by α_IR/α_UV, but the matter-side exponent also suppresses the relative split (`E2_report.md:121`, `E2_report.md:123`). For a general charged strong CFT the worker gives only the suggestion `p_m ∼ O(1)/C_T` (`E2_report.md:125`); without a proved bound on that ratio, the scan cannot exclude every strongly coupled/composite route. Consequently the broad wording at `E2_report.md:23` must be narrowed to the stated elementary/one-loop class.
