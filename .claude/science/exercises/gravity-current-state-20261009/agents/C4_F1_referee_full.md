The quoted natural-box fraction is a reasonable order-of-magnitude statement but is prior- and box-dependent; the stated box gives $2.93\times10^{-8}$ before the extra $10^{-15}$ beta factor (F1_referee_checks.py:27, F1_referee_checks.out:2). The $\alpha\lesssim3.2\times10^{-9}$ number is only the worker's explicitly conditional substitution of the weak-field PPN expression into a strong-field hatted bound: the pulsar observable contains neutron-star sensitivities and is not presently identical to weak $\alpha_2$. On the cancellation branch, the weak $\alpha_1$ constraint gives $\alpha\lesssim2.5\times10^{-5}$; $2.5\times10^{-6}$ again uses the conditional hatted surrogate (F1_report.md:123, F1_referee_checks.out:6). This distinction is consistent with [Shao's theory-independent strong-field bound](https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.2552) and with [Barausse's khronometric sensitivity analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.05958). The GSS strong-coupling expression and $M_{\rm SC}\simeq1.37\times10^{12}$ GeV at $\alpha=10^{-9},\gamma=10^{-2}$, using the reduced Planck mass, are correct (F1_report.md:143, F1_referee_checks.py:36, F1_referee_checks.out:8). But “the Hořava cure is unavailable” is established only for the displayed bare lattice identification $M_*=M_{\rm grid}$: [GSS](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1711.08845) makes the Crab lower bound conditional on a universal Lorentz-violation scale and discusses suppressed percolation. A distinct lower gravitational higher-derivative scale is not excluded by this calculation (F1_report.md:200).

### 3. C3 rotation sector, lapse transport, and residuals

The rotation test is genuinely a subcase of the momentum-constraint bracket: one smearing may be a constant translation and the other an infinitesimal rotation. The supplied residuals rerun exactly (scripts/f1_fdiff_c3_residual.out:1), and the proposed Hessian weight has zero Brillouin-zone average, not one, because the integral of a periodic second derivative vanishes (F1_referee_checks.py:78, F1_referee_checks.out:17). For a nonprojectable lapse treated as a spatial scalar, the extended momentum generator must transport $N$; in the same fixed-generator, single-band ansatz this produces the same affine-lapse/Hessian obstruction. What is not proved is the worker's extension to all “regular, local, finite-band matter”: the C3 referee expressly leaves multiband/interband, crossing/Berry, and auxiliary-constraint routes open (RC_C3_referee.md:19), while the L2 referee likewise says matrix-valued tastes and dressing are outside its proof (RN_L2_referee.md:5, RN_L2_referee.md:22). The full extended lapse canonical algebra was also not calculated independently in F1. Thus the residual establishes a scoped single-band obstruction, not the class-wide nonrealizability used in the headline verdict (F1_report.md:202).

The worker's one-coefficient-at-a-time static calculations also reproduce. The inference “at least five P0-level tunings,” however, is false for its own complete static scalar block. The natural-size correlated family

\[
\mu_{A_1}=\mu,\qquad m_4=\frac43\mu,\qquad \mu_n=\frac{16}{3}\mu

has zero scalar mass determinant and exactly the desired $n_k\propto1/\widehat k^2$ pole with $\gamma_{\rm PPN}=1$, without separately suppressing those three coefficients (F1_referee_checks.py:54, F1_referee_checks.out:12). In the same naturalness convention, Case B therefore has two P0-level TT mass suppressions plus one scalar determinant cancellation, and a separate Cassini-level ratio condition—not five demonstrated P0-level suppressions. The shift/gravitomagnetic sector and the dynamical stability of this correlated family remain uncomputed (F1_report.md:240, F1_report.md:286). P0 counts two independent TT shear counterterms and explicitly leaves the speed sector open (PRICE_NOTE.md:71); a fair comparison counts independent cancellation conditions at the same precision, not the number of symmetry-allowed coefficients. A generic Case-B EFT may consequently still cost more than P0, but the claimed lower bound of five is not established.

### 5. Case A tuning table

The full nonlinear FDiff invariant $\int dt\,d^3x\,N\sqrt h\,R^{(3)}$ is linear in $N$ and expands with the relation $g_n=2g$ in the worker's conventions; an independent lapse mass is absent. Those conclusions follow from the nonlinear FDiff/time-density completion, not merely from linearized uniform time reparametrization. Moreover, $\int N\sqrt h\,\Lambda$ is invariant and generates lapse–trace mixing together with tadpoles and metric masses; $m_4=0$ follows only after the flat-background/cosmological-constant tuning, so “forbidden by time reparametrization” is too strong (F1_report.md:178). The proposed second $10^{-15}$ cubic-anisotropy tuning is also not established: GW170817 samples one propagation direction, and Cherenkov constraints are one-sided; the worker itself labels the extrapolation “suggested” (F1_report.md:188, F1_report.md:282). The Case-A table is therefore a conditional bookkeeping exercise, not a proved pair of $10^{-15}$ tunings.
