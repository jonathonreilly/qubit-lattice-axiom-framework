### Strongest correct statement

For the specific non-compact naive/staggered QED realization on \(Z^3\times\mathbb R\), the one-loop calculation robustly gives \(\delta c_f=-16.06\), \(\delta c_\gamma=-0.671\) per Dirac taste, and \(\Delta=-14.72\) for one two-taste staggered field, corresponding at \(\alpha=1/137\) to \(c_e-c_\gamma=-8.55\times10^{-3}\); this would permit photon decay above 7.8 MeV and exceeds the 1.4 PeV survival bound by \(3.2\times10^{16}\), so that realization requires one QED relative-speed counterterm tuned to roughly \(3\times10^{-17}\) of its natural loop size. At \(\xi=1\), genuine hypercubic symmetry removes all such rank-2 dimension-4 splittings, while the tested \(\xi=2\) action gives \(\Delta=-10.26\). This establishes a severe, regulator-specific matter naturalness problem for the examined continuous-time and anisotropic-lattice models, but not a bounded FAIL for preferred-foliation matter theories in general.
