**Strongest correct statement:** Under the stated first-moment boost/current construction, a nontrivial positive-energy finite-\(N\) Bloch theory cannot satisfy exact global Poincaré closure: local closure forces the common equation \(\operatorname{Hess}(E_\alpha^2/2)=\beta I\), while the periodic trace sum and, for continuous \(P(k)\), determinant winding prevent finitely many patches from closing on the torus. With integrated positive-energy Poincaré symmetry or suitable discrete band-gluing assumptions, every nontrivial component unfolds into a complete reciprocal-lattice tower; hence every finite-band truncation is inexact and exact closure requires an infinite-dimensional, unbounded one-particle fibre. This does not prove an infinite microscopic on-site Hilbert space or unbounded microscopic rule for interacting/composite qubit matter.
