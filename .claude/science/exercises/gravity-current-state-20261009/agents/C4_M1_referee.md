For uniformly finite-range, termwise-number-conserving empty-vacuum models, every fixed finite-\(N\) sector is additionally excluded by the bounded-sector theorem. This does not exclude non-first-moment generators, non-number-conserving embedded sectors in \(d\ge2\), irrational centres, massless sectors, or unbounded continuum finite-\(N\) sectors.
