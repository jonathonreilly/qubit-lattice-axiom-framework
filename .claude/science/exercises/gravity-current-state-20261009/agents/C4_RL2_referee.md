1. **One-dimensional reduction:** measure, Palm closure, and fixed-\(q=p\Lambda\) Volterra equation are correct. Finite-\(p\) results depend on \(p/\sqrt\rho\); only the limiting cone ratio is density-independent. Reruns reproduce the mean values. The intercept is about 1% using resolved data and 0.2% only conditionally on common-mode cancellation in the coarse tiny-\(p\) runs. The Penrose transfer is a sound leading-needle argument, consistent with known leading-singularity results, but lacks a uniform limit theorem for this nonlocal inverse and has no finite-\(\ell\) correction calculation. [Report lines 22–111](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C4/ref_RL2/RL2_referee.md:22), [Harte–Drivas](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.0540).

2. **Van Vleck identity:** exact, but it is an algebraic consequence of the definition of \(g\), not an independent multiplicative chain rule for \(\Delta\). The last-hop factor tends locally to one; accumulation through all renewals remains argued rather than proved. [Report lines 113–140](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C4/ref_RL2/RL2_referee.md:113).

3. **Proper-time variance:** \(\tau^2=O(Ds)\) is correct for each source–observer pair, with a source-shape-dependent coefficient. But the stated Mecke bound does not close for the sprinkled-source field: its off-diagonal term contains joint two-point Palm inverses, which cannot be replaced by the one-point \(\mathbb EH^2\) without another theorem. The timelike Gaussian Monte Carlo is also not automatically Lorentz-equivalent to the entire extended astrophysical source. [Report lines 142–193](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C4/ref_RL2/RL2_referee.md:142).

4. **Rates:** independently confirmed \(k=2.84463,3.02946,3.33396\), the node constants, and all fitted MC slopes. The observed MC/ladder range is 0.68–0.98. The \(\sqrt\epsilon\) exponent is structural for each isolated ladder, not proved for the full variance because node and interior amplitudes may cancel before squaring. 6. **Fixed verdict:** PASS is not established because bounded physical noise is unproved; FAIL is barred because unbounded noise still requires a 63-decade extrapolation and the mean shows no \(O(1)\) mismatch. **INCONCLUSIVE, leaning FAIL is correct.** The mean remains a genuine bounded positive result, scoped to exact plane waves and strongly supported leading Penrose/needle order—not a completed finite-density conformal-lens theorem. [Report lines 253–270](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C4/ref_RL2/RL2_referee.md:253).

**Strongest correct statement:** For all three stable six-layer scalar operators, the exact plane-wave Volterra reduction and Van Vleck cancellation, independently checked normalizations, and rerun numerics strongly support GR-like \(\sqrt\Delta\) focusing at leading needle order; meanwhile the accessible variance data consistently favor exponential growth with ladder-like rates, but neither the physical small-\(\epsilon\) exponent nor the extended-source proper-time bound has been proved. Therefore RL2 is **INCONCLUSIVE, leaning FAIL**, while its scoped mean result should remain recorded as positive.
