# VERDICT: NOT CONFIRMED

V2's central conclusion is not confirmed.  The finite-volume operator
`U_h H U_h^dagger` is of course exactly isospectral, but the genuinely local,
translation-invariant thermodynamic coupling obtained by copying its bulk
kernels is not the same object in trace per unit volume.  Its free-scalar
vacuum energy has the nonzero Jacobian response found by V3.  Rule R therefore
does not establish `mu = 0` for the stated local coupling without an additional
vacuum-energy subtraction R0.

## 1. Decisive V2/V3 conflict

There are two limits which V2 conflates.

1. On every finite open box, if the full boundary-sensitive first moment is
   retained, `H_full,L(h) = U_L(h) H_L U_L(h)^dagger` has exactly the same
   spectrum as `H_L`.  Thus `E_0,L(h)/V_L` is independent of `h` before taking
   the limit.
2. The local coupling used in the infinite system is obtained by retaining the
   bulk commutator kernels and periodizing them.  Because the generator contains
   `x_j`, the discarded boundary terms have coefficients of order `L`; surface
   support therefore does not make their total contribution subextensive.  The
   periodized bulk operator has a changed density of states and is not the
   trace-per-volume limit of the full finite-box conjugation.

For the lattice scalar

`omega(k) = sqrt(m^2 + 4 sum_i sin^2(k_i/2))`,

the local E-channel flow in the RN-L1 normalization is generated by

`v(k) = (sin(k_x), -sin(k_y), 0)/2`.

If `Phi_epsilon` is this flow, the local bulk symbol is
`omega(Phi_epsilon^{-1}(q))`, and hence

`e_vac(epsilon) = integral_BZ dq omega(Phi_epsilon^{-1}(q))/2
                = integral_BZ dk J_epsilon(k) omega(k)/2`.

The flow Jacobian obeys
`J''_0 = (div v)^2 + v dot grad(div v)`, so

`J''_0 = [(cos k_x-cos k_y)^2-sin^2 k_x-sin^2 k_y]/4`.

Independent Brillouin-zone quadrature gives

`mu_E(m=1) = +1.9703886418785e-3`,

stable from `L=32` through `L=128`; direct finite differences of the exact
flow converge to the same value.  The T2 vector field is divergence-free, so
`J=1` and `mu_T2=0`.  The independent implementation is
[referee_v2_checks.py:13](referee_v2_checks.py#L13) and its two-route numerical
result is [referee_v2_checks.out:1](referee_v2_checks.out#L1).  This reproduces
V3's independently supplied value and mechanism
([V3_report_unrefereed.md:69](V3_report_unrefereed.md#L69),
[V3_report_unrefereed.md:80](V3_report_unrefereed.md#L80)).

Accordingly, V2's use of exact isospectrality for the local thermodynamic
Hamiltonian is invalid ([V2_report.md:5](V2_report.md#L5),
[V2_report.md:39](V2_report.md#L39)).  The condition
`D Omega_h = D^dagger Omega_h = 0` is sufficient only after `D` has been allowed
to include the missing h-dependent c-number.  That extension is R0: strained
vacuum normal ordering.  It cancels the answer by construction and would do so
for any coupling.  Moreover, the free momentum annihilates the free vacuum, yet
the local E response above is nonzero, so `P_lambda Omega=0` is not sufficient
for the claimed thermodynamic-local conclusion; the asserted iff statement
([V2_report.md:48](V2_report.md#L48)) lacks the necessary boundary/domain
hypothesis.

## 3. The `O(lambda^2)` and `O(lambda^3)` claims

The narrow interaction identity survives.  For a translation-invariant
anti-Hermitian `S`, set `X=[S,H_2]`.  Then
`X_n0=-(E_n-E_0)S_n0`; second-order perturbation theory contributes
`-sum_n (E_n-E_0)|S_n0|^2`, while
`<0|[S,[S,H_2]]|0>/2=+sum_n (E_n-E_0)|S_n0|^2`.
The cancellation is exact, including sign and factor, and does not involve the
first-moment boundary.  This confirms only the Kato-dressing interaction piece
already identified in V1 ([C4_V1_report.md:128](C4_V1_report.md#L128)).  It does
not make the total local `mu` vanish: the `O(lambda^0)` scalar term above is
already nonzero, and transport of the interacting one-body self-energy can add
another Jacobian term at `O(lambda^2)`.

For the restricted class
`Delta=lambda(epsilon X_1+epsilon^2 X_2+...)` with number-conserving `X_i`,
V2's order counting is consistent: selection rules remove the term linear in
the first strain derivative, and the first cloud contribution to the Hessian is

`mu^(3) = -2 lambda^3 <T_4|X_2|T_4>`.

But no actual non-R `X_2` was constructed.  The computed
`<T_4|V_22|T_4>=8.57e-8` is only a proxy; V2 explicitly leaves an unknown
order-one weight and labels `about 1e-7 lambda^3` SUGGESTED
([V2_report.md:101](V2_report.md#L101),
 [V2_report.md:235](V2_report.md#L235)), and lists the specific Hessian as not
done ([V2_report.md:243](V2_report.md#L243)).  The formula is therefore a
conditional scale estimate, not a checked coefficient or a general theorem for
all non-R splits.  It is also subleading to the free Jacobian anomaly unless R0
has first been imposed.  V2's finite-order quasi-local induction does not close
the nonperturbative exact-W claim; that remains explicitly open
([V2_report.md:129](V2_report.md#L129)).

## 4. Scattering defect, composite scaling, and the Eotvos observable

The normal-process identity is exact:

`sin(Q/2+kappa)+sin(Q/2-kappa)
 -sin(Q/2+kappa')-sin(Q/2-kappa')
 =2 sin(Q/2)(cos kappa-cos kappa')`.

An independent random check gives a maximum error `3.9e-16`
([referee_v2_checks.out:11](referee_v2_checks.out#L11)).  Rerunning the supplied
shell code also reproduces the low-momentum power laws and its reported table
([v2_defect.out:1](v2_defect.out#L1)).  The robust conclusion is a generic
relative defect of order `(ka)^2` for `f=sin k`, and order `(ka)^4` for the
improved symbol.  The coefficient `0.037` is not universal: it is the median of
V2's particular ball/shell sampling and normalization; the 90th percentile is
roughly three times larger, and the ratio is singular when the incoming total
momentum is small.

For V2's selected isolated two-body band, the kinematic identity
`f_B=2 sin(Q/2)<cos kappa>` and the limit
`eta_mom=1-<cos kappa_x>` are correct
([V2_report.md:162](V2_report.md#L162)).  Smooth wavefunctions give
`eta_mom=O((a/r)^2)`, but `0.39` is a coefficient for the specific Gaussian-well
families and rms convention, not composite universality.  A 3D zero-range tail
does give the nonanalytic `O(p_B a)` scaling, but the quoted coefficients
`2.5--7.6` are regulator/mass dependent and, as V2 says, not converged at the
smallest binding ([V2_report.md:174](V2_report.md#L174)).  The `1e-40` to
`1e-20` estimates are therefore conditional model estimates at `a=l_P`, not a
general bound.

Most importantly, `eta_mom=1-f_B/Q` is not the Eotvos ratio.  MICROSCOPE
constrains the composition-dependent difference of free-fall accelerations,
equivalently passive gravitational versus inertial mass, not a uniform-TT-
strain momentum-flux ratio.  V2 acknowledges that it did not test the
Newtonian lapse coupling ([V2_report.md:193](V2_report.md#L193)).  A uniform
lapse merely rescales time/energy and produces no force; the needed test is a
spatially varying lapse and the composite center-of-mass acceleration,
including binding energy.  The MICROSCOPE comparison is consequently not an
observable-level bound on V2's `eta_mom`.  The experiment's actual observable
is stated in the primary final-results paper,
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.129.121102.

Thus the scattering identity and parametric model scalings survive, but the
claim of demonstrated Eotvos safety does not.  “No feedback into mu under R”
is defensible only for the restricted interaction anomaly after R/R0; it is
false for the total genuinely local coupling because `mu_E^free` is already
nonzero.
