1. **Theorem 1.** Part (a) is correct after requiring finitely many species, \(c_s>0\), uniformly compact rescaled spectra, and a finite signed measure. Under those hypotheses, the large-\(t\) moments determine the entire aggregate spectral measure. It does not cover massless \(c_s=0\) species, and measure equality is weaker than one-to-one band pairing ([O3_report.md](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_O3/O3_report.md:45)). Part (b) is false under H1–H3: \(\mu_\epsilon=\delta_{\epsilon^2}-\delta_0\) has \(\partial_\epsilon^2E(0;t)=0\) for every \(t\), but \(\partial_\epsilon^2\mu_\epsilon|_0=-2\delta'_0\ne0\). “No tuning on an interval” is a sufficient robustness hypothesis, not an observation-derived equivalence ([O3_referee.md](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_O3/O3_referee.md:17)).

2. **Precision and additivity.** The arithmetic reproduces: \(1.48\times10^{-84}\,\mathrm{GeV}^2\), square-root scale \(1.22\times10^{-33}\,\mathrm{eV}\), and the inherited \(E_g\) tolerance \(1.6\times10^{-63}\,\mathrm{GeV}^2\), square-root scale \(4\times10^{-23}\,\mathrm{eV}\) ([o3_pairing_precision.out](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_O3/o3_pairing_precision.out:1)). These are bounds on weighted mass-squared mismatches; their square roots are not generally physical mass differences. Free decoupled-sector energies are additive, so an internally paired hidden sector contributes zero, but additivity alone does not forbid a protected cross-sector cancellation ([O3_report.md](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_O3/O3_report.md:69)).

3. **Theorem 2.** Confirmed at finite volume. For a fixed finite graded \(V\), odd nilpotent \(Q(\epsilon)\), and \(H=\{Q,Q^\dagger\}\), nonzero \(\operatorname{sdim}V\) guarantees a zero mode and \(E_0(\epsilon)=0\) for every \(\epsilon\). It is sufficient, not necessary. At infinite volume the trace/index and existence of a normalizable vacuum require separate control, although a sequence with exactly zero finite-volume ground energies has zero limiting energy density ([O3_report.md](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_O3/O3_report.md:80)).

4. **Qubit claims.** The finite-dimensional CCR obstruction and the \(2\times2=4\) independent-mode dimension count are correct in their stated on-site scope. The XX rerun exactly reproduces the \(2.93\times10^{-14}\) discrepancy and \(E_0=-6.472136\), confirming that JW supplies one spectrum, not a cancelling copy ([o3_qubit_duality_index.out](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_O3/o3_qubit_duality_index.out:1)). The proof excludes an exact on-site CCR oscillator, not every collective or emergent bosonic band of a qubit lattice ([O3_report.md](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_O3/O3_report.md:108)).

5. **FSdB.** The zero energies, positive-energy doublets, cusp under the defined direct-\(H\) perturbation, period-3 partners, and transfer-matrix indices reproduce. The 1D results are consistent with \(z=1\), also supported by the known M1 solution. The claimed 3D index growth is established only for the computed \(3\times3\times L_z\) tube, not a cubic thermodynamic limit ([o3_qubit_duality_index.out](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_O3/o3_qubit_duality_index.out:14)). Strain through \(Q\) is a legitimate local supersymmetry-preserving anisotropic deformation, but no bridge identifies it with gravitational \(E_g\); the report acknowledges this omission ([O3_report.md](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_O3/O3_report.md:159)). Exact many-body doublets do not by themselves imply observable elementary superpartners. The table’s observational “excluded” label is therefore too strong. Likewise, the frustration-free \(z\ge2\) result is class-bounded—principally a 1D theorem—not a universal exclusion ([Gosset–Mozgunov](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00088)).

6. **Labels and fixed verdict.** Part 1 should be **DERIVED (bounded/conditional), with corrections**: Theorem 1(a) survives in its narrowed positive-mass free class, while part (b), the massless extension, and the observable-superpartner bridge do not. Under the literal fixed O3 readings, the qubit-pairing result is **INCONCLUSIVE**, with a valid bounded-FAIL subresult for independent on-site CCR+CAR modes. The proof does not exhaust every explicit qubit rule permitted by the registered question ([TESTS.md](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_O3/TESTS.md:2356)).

**Strongest correct statement:** For finitely many free lattice species with positive common mass-scaling coefficients and compact rescaled spectra, exact vanishing on an open mass interval forces equality of the aggregate signed density-of-states measures; the precision arithmetic and free-sector additivity are correct. A finite graded system with nilpotent \(Q\), \(H=\{Q,Q^\dagger\}\), and nonzero superdimension has exactly zero ground energy and paired positive-energy levels under every \(Q\)-preserving deformation. A two-state site cannot contain independent exact CCR and CAR modes, and exact dualities do not duplicate the spectrum. FS M1 supplies a genuine interacting many-body escape from “interactions always break pairing,” but only after adding a Hamiltonian, grading, and coupling-through-\(Q\) rule; it establishes neither the physical \(E_g\) coupling nor visible particle partners. Thus the on-site/free subclass is bounded FAIL, while the full qubit-pairing test is INCONCLUSIVE.
