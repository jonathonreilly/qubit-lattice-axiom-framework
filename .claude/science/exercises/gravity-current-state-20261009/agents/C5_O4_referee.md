4. **Field-strength and conserved-quantity claims — model-specific core confirmed, general wording rejected.** For independent Gaussian records at density \(\ell^{-4}\), with variance \(\sigma_E^2=\kappa'\hbar c/(\epsilon_0\ell^4)\), the powers of \(\ell\) cancel. The actual coefficient is
   \[
   -\ln V=\frac{T}{8\kappa'\hbar}\epsilon_0\!\int\Delta E^2d^3x
          =\frac{U_{\Delta E}T}{4\kappa'\hbar},
   \]
   not an exact universal “energy over \(\hbar\)” law ([O4_report.md:180](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_O4/O4_report.md:180>)). It assumes independent cells, a particular Gaussian readout and vacuum-resolution scaling; correlated field vacuum fluctuations or other smearings need not obey it. Electromagnetic \(F_{\mu\nu}\) is gauge-invariant, but the report’s gravitational analogue \(\nabla\Phi\) is not a local gauge-invariant gravitational observable; curvature is. Identifying its near-zone result with Diósi–Penrose is therefore a model analogy, not a derivation. The underground result excludes the specified parameter-free DP collapse model, not every field-record ontology ([Donadi et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.13490)).

   For conserved quantities, an ideal lock of \(Q\) injects no energy only when \([H,Q]=0\). A *local density* \(q(x)\) usually does not commute with \(H\); only its spatial integral is conserved. Thus the move from “conserved observable” to “local conserved density” is invalid ([O4_report.md:169](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_O4/O4_report.md:169>)). The one-hour ion observation does constrain a fully distinguishing Markovian qubit lock to roughly \(10^{-4}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}\); \(3\times10^{-4}\) is a conservative \(1/3600\) estimate, while the published fitted \(T_2\approx5500\) s gives \(1.8\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}\) ([Wang et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-20330-w)).

6. **Bell / Admissibility — confirmed with a scope correction.** Under measurement independence, single outcomes, no retrocausality and Bell-local conditional independence,

**Strongest correct statement:** Assuming ordinary quantum evolution and a permanent, perfectly distinguishable classical record variable, every record channel that acquires branch information satisfies \(V\leq BC(p_A,p_B)\), with the stated Poisson-process affinity formula exactly; observed interference therefore bounds any such additional record channel. Under the much narrower H3 model—an externally identified fixed-cell Poisson process of intensity \(\ell_P^{-4}\sqrt{-g}\) in branchwise weak Newtonian metrics—the leading point-mass calculation gives \(-\ln BC=2\pi m^2c^3dT/\hbar^2\), with algebraic cancellation of \(G\), and is catastrophically large for the illustrative matter-wave parameters. Gaussian localization at \(0.1\,\mu\mathrm m\) is also bounded to about \(5.3\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}\) per nucleon by the deliberately generous Earth-heat allowance, while Bell data exclude an independently factorized local joint outcome law under H5. None of these results proves that all matter-responsive or propagation-affecting microgeometry must be unrecorded, that Y1–Y4 exhaust permissible records, or that “records copy, never measure” is necessary.
