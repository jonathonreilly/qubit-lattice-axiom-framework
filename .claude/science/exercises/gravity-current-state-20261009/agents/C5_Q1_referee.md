Exact Poisson density covariance is incompatible with a local vacuum Wightman correlator, but this does not exclude Poisson measurement outcomes or prove that a positive-energy vacuum is the only noise-free mechanism. P-Q1 is a sufficient candidate price, not a necessity theorem.
