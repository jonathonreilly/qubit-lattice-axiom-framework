## 4. Lemma J and the supercharge-algebra claim

**Lemma J is correct only with an onsite-charge hypothesis.**  Independent real commutant calculations give dimension one both for the six massless generators and for all seven massive generators (`R10-5_referee_checks.py:78-97`, `R10-5_referee_checks.py:202-207`, `R10-5_referee_checks.out:3-4`).  Hence no momentum-independent onsite real \(J\) commuting with the Clifford-flat \(M\) can satisfy \(J^2=-1\), and doubling the real module is sufficient for an onsite internal \(U(1)\).  But class \(\mathcal S\) does not explicitly require \(J\) to be onsite or momentum independent; the inference from \([J,M]=0\) to commuting with every Clifford generator uses exactly that extra restriction (`R10-5_report.md:158-161`).  The strongest proved version is therefore: **an onsite internal charge action commuting with \(Q\) requires two copies**.  Lemma J is not needed for Lemma PD or for the fixed FAIL.

The statement about \(Q^2=H\) also needs convention and scope.  For a nilpotent complex charge in the ordinary 4D \(N=1\) super-Poincare algebra, \(\{Q,Q^\dagger\}\) is a null combination \(H+\hat n\cdot P\), so it cannot be pure \(H\); \(N=2\) is the minimal ordinary 4D algebra in which two opposite null contributions can sum to \(H\) when central charge vanishes.  A topological twist is one realization, not logically required just for that algebraic combination.  In SUSY quantum mechanics, meanwhile, the Hermitian charge \(Q+Q^\dagger\) already squares to a multiple of \(H\).  The narrower claim that this exact \(Q\) pairs electron/selectron and photon/photino but does not by itself equate the electron and photon speeds is correct (`R10-5_report.md:181-188`).  Groot Nibbelink–Pospelov's dimension-five result assumes the larger unmodified supercharge-and-translation algebra, so it supports the report's distinction between a lone lattice QM charge and full super-Poincare protection ([hep-ph/0404271](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0404271)).
