**Strongest correct statement.** Within free number-conserving separable S/G matter and R12-1’s local-germ ansatz, the current-aligned momentum removes the SS Killing defects and leaves an off-box JS/JJ defect whose minimal solution has a \(1/r^2\) kernel and a Bianchi-fixed non-TT \(1/r^4\) linear spatial-curvature tail; ordinary linearized `Kill` freedom or TT additions cannot cancel that projection, and a chosen smooth placement makes the tail vanish on the box-mode algebra. This does not establish physical nonlocality: the curvature itself transforms under the completed momentum constraint, no globally closed degree-three constraint system or invariant physical domain exists, and no bounded gauge-invariant instantaneous responder has been constructed. Lemma A and the exhaustive \((\alpha)/(\beta)\) fork do not close. W7 therefore remains **OPEN**; P-pf1 shares its zone-mean obstruction but relocates the cost to a discontinuous \(1/n\) matter generator with a face delta and no-umklapp restriction.
