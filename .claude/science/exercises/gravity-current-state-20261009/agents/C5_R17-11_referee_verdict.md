   \[
   N(\lambda+P)=g_E(t),\qquad \dot\lambda=-g_E\theta_n .
   \]
   Thus \(W\) does not enter \(\dot\lambda\). Under (S), \(g_E=0\) wherever the slice expands somewhere, and
   \[
   p_E=-\lambda-\kappa W,\qquad
   E+\kappa T_v=-(\lambda+\kappa V)g .
   \]
