3. **Lemma M — confirmed in its stated single-amplitude scope; consequences narrowed.** For a scalar pair amplitude transforming as `T_xy`, `C2y` gives `ρ(−Kx,Ky,−Kz) = −ρ(Kx,Ky,Kz)`. At `Kx=π, Kz=0`, torus periodicity makes that point fixed, hence `ρ=0`. A doubler-free M weight instead requires `|ρ|² = g(π)g(Ky) > 0`, so no continuous periodic covariant amplitude realizes it (`R17-4_report.md:114-126`; the required modulus is explicitly nonzero in `R17-4_referee_checks.out:22`). Absolute summability would imply a continuous Fourier symbol, so the kernel cannot be absolutely summable. The proper-rotation premise is supplied at `MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md:35-41`, but the tensor-amplitude identification remains part of the downstream stress prescription, not axiom content (`MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md:114-130,178-190`). Two corrections follow. First, the theorem excludes a **single-band, single-amplitude, C2y-covariant** realization; R9-9 specified only `|V|²` and assumed the pair-stress completion (`C5_R9-9_report.md:44-47`), so those hypotheses must remain explicit. Second, non-absolute-summability is rigorous, but “therefore `1/r`” additionally assumes the natural piecewise-smooth jump branch; arbitrary nonlocal realizations need not share that exact asymptotic. The M integrals remain well-defined prescription-level numbers, but they are not witnesses from a local covariant scalar stress. R16-3's primary `REFUTED` is unchanged: its independently confirmed U Z1 witness already has positive T2g, and U also has common axis zeros (`C5_R16-3_referee_verdict.md:3-17`; `C5_R16-3_report.md:203-210,253-264`).
