**Strongest correct statement.** For finite-\(N\) free quadratic matter with continuous quasi-local kernels, symmetric stress and projectable lapse, W1 locks a canonical local-in-time leading shift generator to the forward momentum density; exact finite mixing then kills a single-band periodic momentum symbol, while the multiband Riesz-partner argument gives \(k\)-independent nonzero spectra for one small mixing eigenvalue, and for finitely many small eigenvalues under analyticity. Regular linear field-dependent corrections cannot alter the equal-energy \(O(q)\) tangential identity; conditional on H-RB, an isolated analytic band requires rank \(C=3\), whereas analytic continuation plus tame Reeb topology gives rank \(C\le1\).
