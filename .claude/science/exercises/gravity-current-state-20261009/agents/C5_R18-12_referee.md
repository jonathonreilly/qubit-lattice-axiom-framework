# VERDICT: CONFIRMED WITH CORRECTIONS

The fixed reading **BOUNDED** is met, but only by the dark-matter bound on a surviving conserved yield, not by the quoted reheating-temperature windows. The free-spectrum corner results contain a correct core; the universal interaction-stability dichotomy, the universal no-removal rate, the physical freeze-in brackets, and the claim that translation invariance makes standard expansion undefined are not established.

## Per-item findings

1. **Theorems C and C′ — free core confirmed; universal/interacting form not confirmed.** Translation invariance makes total crystal momentum modulo $2\pi$ a sector label. Therefore, *if* the infimum in a fixed charge-and-momentum sector is attained by an eigenstate, that sector bottom has no lower-energy decay within the sector. The report instead defines an infimum and immediately calls it an absolutely stable ground-state particle; in infinite volume the infimum can be an unattained multiparticle threshold, and degeneracy allows equal-energy mixing (`R18-12_report.md:122-128`). Translation symmetry alone also fixes neither the value of the sector bottom nor whether it is a one-particle excitation, so the “every grid law” light-copy/Planckian-particle dichotomy is too broad (`R18-12_report.md:35-40,126`).

   For the **free additive G + R85/std spectrum**, the lower bound is correct. Concavity gives $h(k)/k\ge h(\pi)/\pi=0.7228169>2/\pi$; $d(q)\ge2\sin(q/2)\ge(2/\pi)q$; and the torus triangle inequality gives
   
   \[
   \sum_i E_i\ge {2\over\pi}\sum_i|k_i|_T\ge {2\over\pi}\left|\sum_i k_i\right|_T.
   \]
   
   Thus the free corner photons saturate the sector floor at $2,2\sqrt2,2\sqrt3$ per $a^{-1}$. Their zero group velocity and $Q_D/E=-2$ on each active corner axis are also correct (`R18-12_report.md:130-138`; `scripts/r1812_abundance.out:1-12`; `referee_r1812_independent.out:1-8`). The free D1 corner energies $m+nW_\pi$, $W_\pi=0.0953$, charge, and $\Delta=0$ are correct; the one-axis argument extends to all three corners by separating mirror, transition-slab, and box cases (`R18-12_report.md:139-145`; `scripts/r1812_kinematics.out:91-102`). None of these free additive bounds survives a general interaction automatically: interaction/binding energy is absent from the triangle proof, the corner photon is not separated from states containing arbitrarily soft photons, and no interacting spectral inequality or pole theorem is supplied. “Perturbatively stable” and the identification of the interacting ground state with the free doubler/photon must therefore be deleted or made an explicit extra premise (`R18-12_report.md:76,130`; `referee_r1812_independent.out:8`).

2. **Decay certificates and Lemma L — open kinematics confirmed on the sampled/grid scope; lifetime and strict no-emission wording do not follow.** All three supplied runners reproduce their scientific rows; the kinematics and envelope outputs differ across reruns only in printed elapsed seconds. A negative finite-grid margin is a valid witness that a channel is kinematically open, and the reported sampled fractions reproduce (`scripts/r1812_kinematics.py:34-41,51-63`; `scripts/r1812_kinematics.out:3-28`). Conversely, a nonnegative grid margin is not a closure certificate, as the script itself says, and the min-plus envelope is finite-grid and iterated only through the displayed finite neutral sets (`scripts/r1812_kinematics.py:3-5`; `scripts/r1812_envelope.py:43-50`; `R18-12_report.md:165-170`). The claimed $\tau\sim a/\alpha\sim10^{-42}\,\mathrm{s}$ is only dimensional speculation: no matrix element or phase-space integral was computed (`R18-12_report.md:88,172`; `scripts/r1812_abundance.out:62`). It cannot by itself remove BBN/CMB injection bounds.

   Lemma L correctly proves that emission has no **negative energy margin** when $\varepsilon$ is $L$-Lipschitz and $\omega(q)\ge L|q|_T$ (`R18-12_report.md:147-150`). It does not prove “no emission” because every inequality may saturate. For example, on the massless S mirror branch with the max photon, $K=2.0\to k'=2.2+q=-0.2$ has $E(K)=E(k')+|q|=\pi-2$ exactly (`referee_r1812_independent.out:10-13`). Strict exclusion needs $\omega>L|q|_T$ for nonzero $q$, or a strict matter Lipschitz bound plus treatment of equality, phase space, and the vertex.

3. **Γ/H, freeze-in, and brackets — arithmetic reproduced, physical bounds not established.** The printed (4.0\times10^{-4}), (9.1\times10^{-7}), and (5.8\times10^{-3}) follow exactly from the assumed (n_{\rm partner}=0.1T^3), (\sigma_{\rm gauge}=\alpha^2/M^2), (\sigma_{\rm grav}=a^2), and radiation-era (H) (`scripts/r1812_abundance.py:55-56,105-112`; `scripts/r1812_abundance.out:50-56`). But (0.1T^3) is not demonstrated to upper-bound all eligible partners. At (T=1/a), using all relativistic SM number degrees gives (n/T^3=11.63) and the same ansatz gives (\Gamma_{\rm grav}/H=0.678), while (Y=0.1) gives (0.273); which density can transfer the required corner momentum is precisely the missing collision calculation (`referee_r1812_independent.out:29-33`). Channel-dependent matrix elements, multiplicities, screening, and the validity of semiclassical radiation-era (H) at (T\sim M_{\rm Pl}) are also absent. Hence the rate rows are benchmarks, not the claimed universal ceiling, and “whatever is made stays” is too strong (`R18-12_report.md:176-179`).

   The independent closed-form evaluation of the worker's two ansatz integrals reproduces the stated D1 (4.16–6.27\times10^{16}) GeV and corner-photon values (`referee_r1812_independent.out:21-24`; `scripts/r1812_abundance.py:57-67`). But the (\kappa\in[10^{-6},10^2]) scan is an assumed eight-decade range, not a derived error bracket, and the corner-photon (\alpha^3T^4x^{3/2}e^{-x}) source is postulated rather than obtained from Peierls matrix elements (`scripts/r1812_abundance.py:7-13,61-63`). Moreover, a bath made only of linear box quanta with total momentum ((\pi,0,0)) has threshold at least (\pi/a) by the ordinary triangle inequality; (2/a) is the all-sector spectral floor, not a box-bath production threshold (`R18-12_report.md:184-185`; `referee_r1812_independent.out:25-27`). The (T_{\rm RH}\ge1/a) overclosure factors are arithmetically correct only after assuming (Y_0\in[10^{-4},10^{-1}]); that population is not derived (`R18-12_report.md:189`; `referee_r1812_independent.out:18-19`). Finally, the BK18 tensor bound is not a model-independent reheating cap, as the earlier referee already records (`C5_R9-1_referee_verdict.md:11-12`).

4. **Lemma X — conserved crystal momentum is correct; “standard expansion is undefined” does not follow.** For a fixed translation representation and ([T_a,H(t)]=0), the translation eigenvalue/comoving crystal momentum is conserved (`R18-12_report.md:213-216`). Standard FRW redshift does not require a self-map (k\mapsto k/R) of the Brillouin torus: comoving (k) can remain fixed while physical momentum and energy redshift through the time-dependent metric/scale factor. What is genuinely unspecified is whether the fundamental lattice spacing and the edge cutoff are physical or comoving, and how the fixed-(a) microscopic Hamiltonian is embedded in FRW. The axioms provide no time metric or persistence dynamics (`MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md:114-130,173-190`). Therefore Lemma X identifies a missing cosmological bridge; it does not defeat “standard expansion” or prove that P-r1812c is its unique realization (`R18-12_report.md:215-219,231-233`).
