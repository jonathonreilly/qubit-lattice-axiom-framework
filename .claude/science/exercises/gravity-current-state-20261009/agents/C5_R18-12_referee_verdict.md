1. **Theorems C and C′.** Translation invariance does preserve crystal-momentum sectors, and an attained sector-bottom eigenstate cannot decay to lower energy. But an infimum need not be an eigenparticle, equal-energy mixing remains possible, and translation symmetry alone does not establish the universal light-copy/stable-Planckian-particle dichotomy ([R18-12_report.md:122](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R18-12/R18-12_report.md:122>)). The free additive G-spectrum inequality
   \[
   \sum_iE_i\ge(2/\pi)\sum_i|k_i|_T\ge(2/\pi)|K|_T
   \]
   is correct, as are the free corner-photon and D1 values ([independent check:1](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R18-12/referee_r1812_independent.out:1>)). It does not survive arbitrary interactions without an interacting spectral/binding-energy bound, so “perturbatively stable” is unproved ([R18-12_report.md:130](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R18-12/R18-12_report.md:130>)).

2. **Decay certificates and Lemma L.** Negative grid margins validly certify open channels; nonnegative margins do not certify closure, and the quoted \(10^{-42}\) s lifetime lacks a matrix element and phase-space calculation ([R18-12_report.md:152](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R18-12/R18-12_report.md:152>), [R18-12_report.md:170](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R18-12/R18-12_report.md:170>)). Lemma L proves only a nonnegative energy margin. Equality occurs, for example, at \(K=2.0,\ k'=2.2,\ q=-0.2\) on the massless S mirror branch ([independent check:10](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R18-12/referee_r1812_independent.out:10>)); strict no-emission needs additional phase-space, vertex, or strict-inequality analysis.

3. **Rates, freeze-in, and brackets.** The worker’s arithmetic reproduces, but \(\Gamma/H\le6\times10^{-3}\) is not a universal ceiling: it is the selected \(n=0.1T^3\) normalization. Using all relativistic SM number degrees in the same ansatz gives \(0.678\) at \(T=1/a\) ([independent check:29](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R18-12/referee_r1812_independent.out:29>)). Eligible partners and actual matrix elements remain uncomputed. The freeze-in integrals reproduce, but \(\kappa\in[10^{-6},10^2]\) is assumed, not derived. A linear box bath has threshold \(\pi/a\), not \(2/a\), so its applicable corner-photon range is \(6.6\)–\(9.8\times10^{17}\) GeV, not \(4.2\)–\(9.8\times10^{17}\) GeV ([independent check:21](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R18-12/referee_r1812_independent.out:21>)). The \(10^{23}\)–\(10^{27}\) overclosure factors are correct only conditional on the assumed \(Y_0\) range.

4. **Lemma X.** Conserved crystal momentum under a translation-invariant \(H(t)\) is correct ([R18-12_report.md:213](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R18-12/R18-12_report.md:213>)). It does not defeat standard expansion: FRW may conserve comoving crystal momentum while physical momentum and energy redshift through the scale factor. What is genuinely undefined is whether the lattice spacing/cutoff is physical or comoving and how the fixed-\(a\) Hamiltonian couples to FRW; the axioms leave that bridge open ([MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md:173](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R18-12/MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md:173>)).

5. **Fixed reading.** The density conversion gives
   \(Y\le3.76\times10^{-28}\) for the lightest D1 doubler and
   \(Y\le1.79\times10^{-29}\) for a \(2/a\) corner photon ([independent check:15](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R18-12/referee_r1812_independent.out:15>)). These are checked limits on a surviving present yield—and on a post-number-changing initial yield if comoving number and entropy are conserved. That suffices for **BOUNDED** in its narrow conditional sense ([TESTS.md:7015](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R18-12/TESTS.md:7015>)); it does not validate the physical \(T_{\rm RH}\) ceilings or an unconditional primordial-population bound. **SELF-CLEANING is not met.** P-W7edge0 remains an initial-state/history condition, not a theorem of the dynamics.

**Strongest correct statement.** For the specified free S/G/D1 bands, crystal momentum is conserved modulo \(2\pi\); the additive G + R85/std spectrum obeys \(E_{\rm tot}\ge(2/\pi)|K_{\rm tot}|_T\), giving free-sector corner photons of energies \(2,2\sqrt2,2\sqrt3\) per \(a^{-1}\), zero corner group velocity, and \(Q_D/E=-2\), while the free D1 corner modes have energies \(m+nW_\pi\), retain charge, and have \(\Delta=0\). The finite-grid calculations certify many open one-photon channels but neither decay widths nor interacting stability. Any surviving conserved relic component is bounded by dark-matter density at \(Y\le3.76\times10^{-28}\) for the lightest doubler or \(Y\le1.79\times10^{-29}\) for a \(2/a\) corner photon; this conditional yield ceiling supports **BOUNDED**, while the universal corner dichotomy, universal \(\Gamma/H\) ceiling, quoted reheating brackets, strict Lemma-L no-emission claim, and assertion that translation invariance invalidates standard expansion are not confirmed.
