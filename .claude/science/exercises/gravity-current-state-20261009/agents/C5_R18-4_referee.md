## 1. Lemma S0 — confirmed

At a point where the limit directions of \(\hat d\) include two non-collinear
lines, continuity of the analytic coefficient \(b_j\), together with
\(b_j(k)\parallel d(k)\) off the crossing set, forces \(b_j(x)\) to be parallel

rank-three assumption on the limit-direction set is required.  This proves
continuity of the two momentum eigenvalues, but not continuity of the
eigenprojectors.  `R18-4_report.md:111-122`.

## 2. Theorem WX — confirmed after three corrections

### Cover

The orientation character of the real line field on the complement of the
conical set defines a double cover.  Once the meridian character is trivial,
it factors through \(\pi_1(T^3)\), and the resulting unbranched cover is either
two copies of \(T^3\) or a connected index-two torus cover.  The argument must

### Analytic continuation and conical points

On a connected component of the analytic region, the nonzero sets of the
non-identically-zero analytic momentum and velocity are open and dense.  Thus
the tangential identity makes \(B_{abj}\) vanish on a nonempty open set and
analytic continuation gives \(B\equiv0\).  Hence
\(d(\pi_j-C_j\cdot k)\wedge dE=0\) on that region.  At the codimension-two or
codimension-three conical set, no analytic continuation of an eigenline is
available or needed: S0 makes \(\pi_j\) continuous, and the subanalytic/Hardt
decomposition has finitely many adjacent contour pieces.  Continuity glues
their constants across each connected contour; a contour contained in a
conical line is obtained as the limit of the adjacent tubular contours.  This
is the precise continuity replacement.  `R18-4_report.md:140-165`.

### Reeb step

The report's cited Reeb facts are not stated accurately enough for this use.
Kaluba–Marzantowicz–Silva treat \(C^1\) functions under isolated-critical-point
hypotheses, so that paper is not by itself a theorem for the Lipschitz sorted
band.  Gelbukh supplies the cycle-rank bound for a continuous function with a
finite Reeb graph, but not the report's exact phrase "regular level cycles
generate the kernel."  The correct replacement is the levelset-tame
horizontal-homology theorem: for a levelset-tame function, the kernel of
\(H_1(M)\to H_1(R_E)\) is the subgroup generated by cycles carried by level
sets, and the quotient is \(H_1(R_E)\).  The branch energy here is continuous
and globally subanalytic on a compact finite torus cover, hence levelset-tame
by analytic stratification and Hardt triviality.  Gelbukh's bound gives
\(b_1(R_E)\leq\operatorname{corank}\pi_1(T^3)=1\); therefore the horizontal
subgroup in \(H_1(T^3)\cong\mathbb Z^3\) has rank at least two.  The contour
constancy above makes \(C\) annihilate that subgroup, so \(\operatorname{rank}C
\leq1\).  This supplies the needed replacement for `R18-4_report.md:152-159`.

## 4. Lemmas X1/X2 and recalled analytic-topology facts

X1 is correct.  Analytic division by a local defining function of a smooth
two-dimensional stratum, iterated to the finite common order, gives
\(d=g^m e\) with \(e\) generically nonzero on the stratum.  The exceptional
set is a proper analytic subset of dimension at most one, and a compatible
analytic stratification handles the lower-dimensional strata.
`R18-4_report.md:227-232`.

The two-variable convergent real power-series ring is a UFD, analytic plane
curve germs have finitely many real half-branches, and every one-dimensional
real analytic branch contributes two half-branches.  The report nevertheless
makes one false intermediate statement: not **every** irreducible exponent in
\(\Delta=|d|^2\) is even.  For example \(x^2+y^2\) is nonnegative, irreducible
over the real analytic germ ring, and occurs with exponent one, but its real
zero germ is isolated and contributes no crossing of the meridian.  The
correct statement is that every irreducible factor having a real
one-dimensional zero branch occurs to even order; at a regular real point it
changes sign, while a sum of squares cannot.  Division then gives the same
integer \(m_i=a_i/2\) on every half-branch of that irreducible curve.  Since
the number of its half-branches is even, its total orientation-flip
contribution is even.  Isolated sign-definite factors contribute zero.  This
repairs X2.  `R18-4_report.md:234-240`; `r184_referee_checks.py:52-57`;
`r184_referee_checks.out:5`.

The meridian-normal-generation statement is applicable after saying its
needed hypotheses: use a closed, locally contractible subanalytic set with a
compatible Whitney stratification; generic meridians of its codimension-two
strata normally generate the kernel of the complement map, while
codimension-three point strata add no local \(\pi_1\).  Analytic
stratification supplies this setting here.  The 45 monodromy runs are useful
controls only; they do not prove the parity theorem.  `R18-4_report.md:242-245`;
`r184_cross.out:41-50`.
