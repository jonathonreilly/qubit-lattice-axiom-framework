## Per-item findings

1. **Combined equations and Bianchi reduction — confirmed conditionally, with a domain correction.**  From
   \[
   T_v=-Vg+Wh,\qquad Q_\nu=\nabla^\mu(Wh_{\mu\nu}),\qquad
   E=\lambda nn+p_Eh,
   \]
   and \(\nabla(T_m-Vg)=0\), Bianchi gives
   \[
   \nabla(E+\kappa Wh)=0,\quad N(\lambda+p_E+\kappa W)=g(t),\quad
   \dot\lambda=-g\theta_n.
   \]
   This is the already-refereed R17-11 identity (`C5_R17-11_referee_verdict.md:5-20`) and is independently reproduced in `R18-6_referee_checks.out:1-3`.  With (S), \(\dot\lambda=0\); if a **connected component** of a slice contains a point with \(\theta_n\ne0\), then its slice-global \(g\) is zero throughout that component.  A wholly static component is not covered, exactly as the prior verdict warns (`C5_R17-11_referee_verdict.md:20`).  Then \(p_E=-\lambda-\kappa W\) and
   \[
   G=\kappa(T_m+T_v)+E=\kappa\!\left[T_m-(V-e_0)g\right].
   \]
   Hence the reduced classical equations are exactly GR; the static isotropic-fluid equations are TOV with no \(W\), the exterior under P93 has \(\beta=\gamma=1\), \(\widetilde\Lambda=0\), and the R17-11 Lemma-S channel is absent.  The worker's checks support precisely these statements (`scripts/r186_cbox_bridge.out:1-15,23-25`; `R18-6_report.md:75-82,120-128`).  This is an on-shell result inside H-imp/H-m/class \(\mathcal D\), not a lattice derivation or completed action (`C5_R17-11_referee_verdict.md:22-29,41-45`).

2. **J1 — the two readings differ materially.**

   - **Literal registered N:** N sources the metric by the unabsorbed total material/vacuum stress plus gravitational self-stress.  Since \(\nabla(T_m+T_v)=Q\), the contracted Bianchi identity requires \(Q=0\).  Its energy and spatial projections are \(W\theta_n\) and \(D_iW+Wa_i\); on a connected expanding slice, \(Q=0\) forces \(NW\) to be slice-constant and zero, hence \(W=0\).  For a static well it only forces \(We^\Phi=\text{const}\), not pairing by itself; constant proper-unit \(W\) then fails unless \(W=0\).  These checks are explicit at `scripts/r186_cbox_bridge.out:17-21` and independent at `R18-6_referee_checks.out:4-7`.  Therefore literal N and nontrivial (d-i) are **JOINT-CONFLICT** in the stated expanding, nonzero-\(W\) arena.
   - **Modified N:** if “total” is newly defined to include the E/creation receiver stress, or N is explicitly replaced by \(N_{\rm red}\) sourced by \(T_m-(V-e_0)g\), the opposite E stress cancels \(Wh\) and the bridge is jointly consistent.  But R16-14 calls N a new, uncompleted nonlinear source/action bridge and does not establish additional stress identifications (`C5_R16-14_referee_verdict.md:15-21`); the axioms also leave source/action identification open (`MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md:173-190`).  Accordingly J1 is a substantive completion/price, not a wording-only correction.

   The conflict is deliberately narrow.  It is escaped by \(W=0\), a wholly static special configuration with \(NW\) constant, including the E/creation receiver in N's total stress, adding another conserved receiver, or abandoning the Einstein/LL conservation identity.  The first two remove the generic expanding nonzero-enthalpy case; the latter three change the bridge.  No broader no-go is claimed.

3. **J2(ii) — isotropy needs invariance, and the virial argument is only quadratic-TT.**  A one-point stress expectation in a state invariant under proper cubic rotations has zero vector part and spatial tensor proportional to \(\delta_{ij}\). The group-average check demonstrates the distinction (`R18-6_referee_checks.out:8-11`); the worker's sampled dispersions only check invariant free-vacuum examples (`scripts/r186_vacuum_parts.py:20-44`). Therefore the identification of \(\langle t_{LL}\rangle_0\)'s energy with the complete quantum-gravity ground-state energy is justified only for gauge-fixed, free TT modes with matched normalization/regulator.  It is not proved for constraints, gauge/ghost sectors, interactions, or the gauge-dependent full LL pseudotensor.  The classical-GR branch needs none of this; the claimed quantum J2 closure remains OPEN.
