## Strongest correct statement

For the specified free periodic lattice Hamiltonians, the completed-law thermodynamic ground-energy density \(e_0=\lim E_0/V\) exists, with \(L^{-4}\) gapless and exponential gapped finite-size corrections, and sudden addition of a site as an uncorrelated local-ground-state factor while leaving the old vacuum unchanged costs \(0.0405\)–\(0.0596\) per scalar site and \(0.677\)–\(0.976\) per tested Wilson–Dirac site. If that birth is subsequently evolved unitarily while energy is measured against the completed Hamiltonian and the extraction power is bounded by \(\Gamma\), then \(A_{\rm fix}\ge\Delta E_{\rm bp}^2/(2\Gamma)\), giving the quoted enormous standing density under homogeneous dilute additive creation. For a genuinely growing law, however, the axioms do not select the Hilbert-space embedding, instantaneous Hamiltonian, energy comparator, insertion map, interaction norms, or event additivity: one may use a fixed completed-law \(e_0\) and obtain transient gravitating excess, or an instantaneous \(e_0(t)=\min\operatorname{spec}H(t)/V(t)\), which is well defined once \(H(t)\) is supplied but need not be constant. Consequently R19-10 establishes a strong conditional creation-energy obstruction and a missing condition in the C-box-E realization, not that every local growing lattice violates P93 or satisfies the fixed INJECTS clause.
