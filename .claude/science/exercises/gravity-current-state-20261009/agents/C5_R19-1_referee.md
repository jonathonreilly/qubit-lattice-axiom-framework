6. **Theorem 6 -- core no-light-like result confirmed after making boundedness explicit.** Under the stated spectral split, `Z`, `Z omega`, and `Z omega^2` are differences of even analytic moments (`R19-1_report.md:233-244`). On a punctured neighborhood their ratios are meromorphic functions of `t^2`; if the isolated branch is locally bounded (equivalently for the intended low branch, `0 <= omega(t) < Delta`), the singularity is removable, so a branch tending to zero begins at even order and cannot be `c|t|`. The analytic-remainder assumption also deliberately excludes continuum mixing, exactly as the report acknowledges (`R19-1_report.md:245-253`). The theorem therefore does not close the full RK class.
