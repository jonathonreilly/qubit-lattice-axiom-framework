# VERDICT: NOT CONFIRMED

The report establishes several useful bounded results, but its quantum-constraint proof and covariant-noise no-go do not close, one GW estimate is dimensionally wrong, and the fixed `FAIL` quantifier is not met. The registered verdict is therefore **INCONCLUSIVE**, not “FAIL (bounded).”

1. **Lemmas B and B′ — core confirmed, proof/scope corrections required.** For a connected noncompact hypersurface, an order-zero source \(J=\delta(\sigma)\alpha\) that decays spatially can be closed only if it vanishes: the \(\delta'\) term removes tangential components and closedness makes the remaining normal coefficient constant, which decay then kills. However, the report’s claim that the \(\delta'\) and \(\delta\) terms vanish independently is too quick because \(f(\sigma)\delta' = f(0)\delta'-f'(0)\delta\); one must first choose a clean normal/tangential representative. Decay is essential: \(c\,\delta(\sigma)d\sigma=d[cH(\sigma)]\) is a nonzero closed counterexample without it ([R2-3_report.md:104](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R2-3/R2-3_report.md:104>)). Independent exact modular elimination confirms the lattice nullities \(0,9,18\), so the numerical check is genuine, though only a finite toy certificate ([r23_referee_checks.out:1](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R2-3/r23_referee_checks.out:1>)). Lemma B′’s general formula omits a time-independent integration term; its application remains correct only under the additional assumption \(J_i=0\), giving \(\nabla J_0=0\) and hence uniform density for the stated local heating model ([R2-3_report.md:115](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R2-3/R2-3_report.md:115>)). This agrees with JPS’s explicit integrability requirement and their restriction to homogeneous cosmology; they themselves note that generic local violations need not satisfy it ([Josset–Perez–Sudarsky](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.04183)).

2. **Semiclassical case — confirmed only under H-grav.** If the metric is required to solve the distributional Einstein or trace-free Einstein equation globally and the only source is the collapsed conditional matter stress, a pointer-localizing jump produces a nonclosed, spatially decaying surface source, so no solution exists ([R2-3_report.md:121](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R2-3/R2-3_report.md:121>)). It is not a theorem about every semiclassical collapse construction: metric resets, an additional collapse-field stress, or another compensating source change the distributional problem. E1 explicitly makes Einstein’s equation hold only between flashes ([R2-3_report.md:243](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R2-3/R2-3_report.md:243>)).

3. **Lemma H is narrow; Lemma Q is not proved.** Lemma H is correct for normalized real position multipliers with the nonrelativistic Hamiltonian \(p^2/2m+V(x)\): the gain is the positive state-independent c-number \(\lambda\hbar^2\int|\nabla f|^2/(2m)\) ([R2-3_report.md:126](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R2-3/R2-3_report.md:126>)). That proof does not cover the registered relativistic scalar/Dirac QFT setting; the c-number result depends on quadratic dispersion. For Q, step 2 is valid if one has already established the operator identity \([P^\mu,K]=0\): an exactly translation-invariant quasilocal element of an irreducible net is scalar. Step 1 establishes at most \([C_\nu,K]\Psi=0\) on the physical subspace; it does not justify promoting this to a full quasilocal-algebra identity, and the claim that the asymptotic charge commutes with \(K\) is precisely what fails for gravitationally dressed physical operators ([R2-3_report.md:130](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R2-3/R2-3_report.md:130>)). The established dressing theorem supports the narrower statement that compact operators carrying Poincaré charge require nonlocal gravitational dressing, not that every constraint-preserving operation is scalar ([Donnelly–Giddings](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.01025)). Consequently, “every \(\Pi_{\rm pos}\) flash breaks the quantum constraints for every state” is not demonstrated.

6. **Lemma V — not confirmed.** The finite-measure statement is correct: a finite positive Lorentz-invariant spectral measure is supported at \(k=0\), so an ordinary stationary covariant random field with finite point variance is constant. But relativistic noises can be distribution-valued and have locally finite yet infinite-total spectral measures. Infinite vacuum excitation follows only when the noise spectrum overlaps nonzero timelike vacuum spectral weight; spacelike/tachyonic spectra evade that step. Pearle explicitly constructed a relativistic tachyonic-noise collapse model with no vacuum excitation, at the cost of nonstandard/nonlocal evolution, and later gave another relativistic scalar-field collapse model claiming no vacuum energy production ([Pearle 1999](https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.59.80), [Pearle 2014](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.5074)). Therefore [R2-3_report.md:231](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R2-3/R2-3_report.md:231>) proves only the finite-point-variance/overlapping-spectrum obstruction, not the claimed exclusion of covariant collective laws.

7. **Verdict — INCONCLUSIVE under the FIXED readings.** `FAIL` requires a proof covering *every* covariant flash law dense enough for definiteness ([TESTS.md:2555](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R2-3/TESTS.md:2555>)). The report instead proves bounded results for surface-supported semiclassical jumps and nonrelativistic independent position multipliers, while Q is incomplete and V leaves concrete colored/tachyonic and mediated collective routes. E1 violates the global-Einstein reading of (b), E2 abandons the supplied graviton, and E3 is not a collapse law; but E4/nonlocal gravitational dressing and covariant collective or colored constructions remain genuine unclosed routes. “FAIL (bounded)” is a useful intermediate result, not one of the registered final readings.

**Strongest correct statement:** For a spatially decaying matter-stress jump confined to one noncompact collapse hypersurface, the resulting nonconservation current is not integrable unless it vanishes, so neither ordinary semiclassical Einstein gravity nor its unimodular trace-free form admits that jump as a single global distributional solution without an additional compensating source or metric-reset rule. Independent nonrelativistic position flashes obey the exact heating and decoherence–heating formulas given above; at the registered pointer parameters, the Earth heat allowance limits an isolated pointer to about \(20\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}\), although environmental amplification can exceed \(10^3\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}\). Their directly estimated gravitational signals remain negligibly small after correcting the GW spectrum. These results establish bounded failures for bare, independently flashing position-collapse laws, but they do not exclude nonlocal gravitationally dressed operations, relativistic collective/colored collapse laws, or other compensating constructions; accordingly, R2-3 is **INCONCLUSIVE** under its fixed universal quantifier.
