This does not establish a universal incompatibility between set B and Record, because non-RP tagged laws, probabilistic permanence, noncommuting quantum slices, non-nearest-neighbour laws, and OS-reconstructed real-time records remain outside the theorem, and no supplied construction meets all three FIXED conditions—especially literal criterion (iii).
