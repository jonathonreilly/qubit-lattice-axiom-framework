1. **Lemma M:** The scalar torus action is ergodic and every invariant \(L^2\) scalar is constant. 2. **Proposition P:** \(s_c=2\) is correct modulo standard affine-symmetric-space orbit counting; all supplied counts reran byte-for-byte. The script counts norm shells, not individual orbits. More importantly, the convergent Poincaré series defines a finite orbit weight—not a convergent hopping operator. The rejection of “power-law quasi-locality” is correct because the resulting kernel is constant on infinite orbits. The claim that exponent \(2\) itself confirms finite covolume is false. `R3-1_report.md:95-115`. The invoked counting framework is consistent with [Eskin–McMullen](https://dash.harvard.edu/bitstreams/7312037c-7d25-6bd4-e053-0100007fdf3b/download).

3. **Proposition R:** A nonzero past-timelike, polynomially bounded orbit kernel converges absolutely precisely in the forward tube. Nonzero spacelike or time-symmetric kernels have no ordinary pointwise/unconditional Fourier series anywhere, although distributional or regularized formulations remain possible. The explicit law is therefore established only on exponentially past-decaying fields/in the tube, not as generic real-time dynamics. Retardation supplies an arrow but no invariant rest-frame vector; O1 Lemma E does not apply to this noncompact infinite group. `R3-1_report.md:117-136`.

4. **Invariant counts:** Confirmed: degrees 1–6 give \(0,1,0,1,0,1\), and there are two Lorentz-invariant symmetric-tensor mass structures. Only one special relative combination is Fierz–Pauli. The universal “no Taylor expansion at zero” statement is too broad: global \(L^2\) regularity and analyticity on a full light-cone neighborhood are excluded, but every possible local/asymptotic expansion after signed shell cancellations is not. `R3-1_report.md:142-166`.

5. **Per-realization Lorentz violation:** The requested scaling run reproduced exactly. Direction dependence at imaginary momentum is genuine continuous-Lorentz breaking of the fixed realization’s tube symbol, not truncation error. But the fitted exponent is unstable: reasonable fit windows give relative slopes from \(0.607\) to \(2.281\), and one low-\(\kappa\) window even gives a positive absolute slope. Thus neither \(\kappa^{1.3}\) nor a nonshrinking absolute IR limit is established. Imaginary-momentum anisotropy is an analytic diagnostic, not yet a physical dispersion or observable LV coefficient. `referee_fit_sensitivity.out:1-12`; `R3-1_report.md:174-198`.

6. **Resonances and momentum:** All resonance counts reran exactly, and Bragg-cone accumulation is kinematically correct. Their residues and physical strength remain kernel-dependent. Quasi-momentum is conserved modulo \(2\pi L_7^*\), but exact momentum for every in-band process is false: multiparticle Umklapp can use arbitrarily low individual momenta whose total transfer is reciprocal. The gap protects one-to-one processes and processes whose total transfer remains inside the gap, not arbitrary perturbative order. The regular-band boost-wall theorems consequently do not apply as quoted, but are not refuted. `R3-1_report.md:200-238`.

7. **Reading:** **INCONCLUSIVE remains correct.** PASS fails because no convergent real-momentum dynamics or Lorentz-covariant low-energy S-matrix is constructed. FAIL fails because there is no dimension-\(\le4\) polynomial LV operator and no exclusion of every generalized/distributional law. `TESTS.md:2660-2670`.

**Strongest correct statement.** For \(L_7\), every nonzero displacement lies on an infinite \(\Gamma_L\)-orbit, forcing invariant scalar \(L^2\) torus symbols to be constant and nonzero invariant hopping kernels to be non-summable and non-decaying along their orbits. Fixed-orbit Poincaré coefficients converge exactly for \(s>2\), but do not produce a convergent hopping law. Nonzero retarded timelike kernels possess ordinary holomorphic symbols only in the forward tube; time-symmetric kernels require distributional treatment. Polynomial coefficients, when they exist, are proper-Lorentz invariant with the reported counts. The numerical shell sums genuinely exhibit realization-specific automorphic direction dependence and Bragg-cone accumulation, but establish neither a robust IR scaling exponent nor an observable real-momentum Lorentz violation. Momentum conservation is only modulo the reciprocal lattice, with exact conservation restricted by the total-transfer gap. The campaign reading is therefore **INCONCLUSIVE**.
