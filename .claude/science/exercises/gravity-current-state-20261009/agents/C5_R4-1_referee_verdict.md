Class number one follows from the square-free-determinant theorem for indefinite forms, not merely the numerics ([Eskin–Rudnick–Sarnak](https://www.math.tau.ac.il/~rudnick/papers/siegelmass.pdf)).

2. **Mean-\(\Box\) weights — confirmed.** The moment cancellations, normalization and explicit four-shell weights are correct; independently \(f=(12.1853,-24.3706,73.1117,-16.2470)\), \(c=-1.75562\) ([report:20](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R4-1/R4-1_referee.md:20>)).

3. **Numerics — reproduced, but sampling corrected.** The 48-frame raw NPZ rerun is bit-for-bit identical. Positive relative and absolute fitted growth is real in the sampled balls. However, \(\eta\le0.6\)–1 samples a local hyperbolic ball, not demonstrated Haar measure on \(\Gamma_L\backslash H^3\); it cannot measure the claimed ensemble rms. Also, the scripts implement 18 main kernels and six flat families—not 23. Dividing by \(\Box\phi\) is fair for the effective-law test: the fixed-peak absolute deviation itself has a positive, approximately linear fitted growth, so this is not solely a normalization artefact ([report:36](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R4-1/R4-1_referee.md:36>)).

4. **Spectral theorem — confirmed with corrections.** Palm/Haar Parseval is sound. For \(r>0\), the transform needs an additive remainder; \(r=0\) grows as \(\ell\log\ell\), not by the rotating formula; exceptional modes grow as \(\ell^{1+t}\) only when their leading coefficient is nonzero. PASS still forces all tempered \(A_j(f)=0\). The wavefront contradiction survives on the orbifold because finite torsion stabilizers add only finitely many lifted rays. The theorem requires a nonzero off-site orbit and proves a cubic-normalized limsup, not pointwise \(\Theta(\ell^3)\) for every realization ([report:56](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R4-1/R4-1_referee.md:56>)).

5. **Relation to R3 — extension, not contradiction.** R4-1 closes the finite-support, moment-cancelled ensemble-rms question left open by R3-1. R3-3 measured a different positive one-hop response relative to its own mean; its \(D^{-1}\) result is compatible ([report:128](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R4-1/R4-1_referee.md:128>)).

6. **Reading — FAIL (bounded), with notation corrected.** Growth is not literally \(O(1)\). The FIXED clause must mean “order unity or worse / does not vanish.” Under that intended reading, FAIL is correct for fixed finite-orbit linear retarded scalar kernels in Palm/Haar ensemble rms ([report:136](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R4-1/R4-1_referee.md:136>)).

**Strongest correct statement.** For the cocompact \(L_7\) LRL ensemble, four-shell retarded weights exist whose Haar mean is \(-k^2+O(a^2k^4\log ak)\); but every fixed linear retarded scalar kernel supported on finitely many \(\Gamma_L\)-orbits with at least one nonzero off-site weight obeys, on fixed-peak isotropic Gaussians at a Palm point, \(\limsup_{\ell\to\infty}\|B\phi_\ell-\mathbb EB\phi_\ell\|_2/\ell>0\), and hence \(\limsup R_\ell(a/\ell)^3>0\). This is an ensemble-rms finite-orbit obstruction, not a pointwise theorem for every realization or a result about infinite-support, nonlinear, curved, or internal-index operators.
