3. Route 2 — exponent confirmed; probabilistic and R2-4 claims corrected.

   The formal ratio \(e^{3.3087\times10^{122}}\) is correct ([r53_numbers.out:18](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R5-3/scripts/r53_numbers.out:18>)). However, \(e^{3\pi/G\Lambda}\) is non-normalizable at \(0^+\); it is a formal weight, not a probability predicting exact zero. Coleman’s construction adds another exponential, and Polchinski’s four-dimensional saddle phase reverses the naive sign ([Polchinski 1989](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0370269389903870)).

   R2-4 applies if the global-\(\Lambda\) variable is part of a full positive joint measure satisfying the local-source and GR-response assumptions W1–W3 ([C5_R2-4_report.md:103](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R5-3/C5_R2-4_report.md:103>)). It does not exclude a marginal prior on a global constant by itself.

   Finally, \(\Lambda_*=\sqrt{24\pi^2/V}\) is algebraically correct, but it is a minimum of the real-axis exponent and the displayed integral diverges at both endpoints. It is not a controlled saddle without a specified contour and measure ([referee:47](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R5-3/C5_R5-3_referee.md:47>)).

4. Route 4 — core Gibbs–Duhem objection confirmed.

   For conserved \(N\), free \(V\), and zero external pressure, \(\epsilon-\mu n=0\) follows. For forming, nonconserved records at fixed volume, an accessible interior minimum would give \(\epsilon'(n_*)=0\), but \(\epsilon(n_*)\) remains arbitrary under an additive shift ([R5-3_report.md:214](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R5-3/R5-3_report.md:214>)). Monotone formation need not actually reach that minimum; it may saturate or stop kinetically. Moreover, coupling gravity to \(\epsilon-\mu n\) requires an extra q/four-form source rule. KSV likewise find that their reversible Minkowski asymptote still needs specially selected initial data ([KSV 1601.04676](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.04676); [referee:63](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R5-3/C5_R5-3_referee.md:63>)).

5. DESI/curvature — numbers right; interpretation needs care.

   DESI DR2 reports \(10^3\Omega_K=2.3\pm1.1\) for DESI+CMB in \(\Lambda\)CDM+\(\Omega_K\), with positive sign open and no significant nonflat preference ([DESI DR2 Results II](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.14738)). A closed \(\Omega_K=-0.003\) is 4.8σ from that central value, so “about 5σ” is arithmetically correct within that likelihood. But \(|\Omega_K|\le0.002\) is a chosen benchmark, not the quoted DESI interval. This distinction does not rescue the order-unity \(0.9166\) prediction. The \(w_0>-1,w_a<0\) comparison is qualitative and may involve phantom crossing ([referee:72](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R5-3/C5_R5-3_referee.md:72>)).

6. Reading — confirmed with the above scope corrections.

   Routes 1–4 remain OPEN; neither bounded exclusion establishes a general no-go, and no route meets the fixed VIABLE criterion ([referee:81](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R5-3/C5_R5-3_referee.md:81>)).

Strongest correct statement: Within a Levi-Civita metric theory with conserved true matter stress, a prescribed c-number vacuum subtraction cannot protect the geometry across phase transitions: the electroweak shift alone requires cancellation at roughly one part in \(4.7\times10^{54}\) unless an additional global, dynamical, symmetry, or modified-gravity equation is supplied. The original global KP \(\langle R\rangle=0\) rule is incompatible with a homogeneous ΛCDM-like past and observed near-flatness absent extraordinary future loitering, but this does not extend to local or Gauss–Bonnet sequesters. Positive full W1–W3 Euclidean measures cannot reproduce GR; the Baum–Hawking factor is divergent; and forming-record thermodynamics leaves an unfixed additive constant. Therefore the campaign contains no untuned, observationally viable derivation of \(\langle\Lambda\rangle=0\), but it does not prove that no augmented rule set can achieve one.
