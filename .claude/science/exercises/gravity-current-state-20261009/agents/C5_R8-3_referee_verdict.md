# VERDICT: NOT CONFIRMED

The worker establishes a valuable conditional no-go, but not the advertised exclusion of all covariant smeared Gaussian CQ gravity. The load-bearing additivity assumption is not established for general non-Markovian kernels, tensor-channel separation weakens the stated trilemma, and the OWD corollary is too broad.

1. **Lemma 1 — essentially confirmed.** A positive, locally finite Lorentz-invariant measure on the future momentum cone is either zero or has infinite total mass. When the contracted golden-rule integrand is a nonnegative Lorentz scalar, “infinite transition rate per spacetime four-volume” is the correct diagnosis. Pearle supports precisely this timelike-noise problem; [Bedingham](https://arxiv.org/html/1103.3974v1) and [Tumulka](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0406094) evade it by changing the dynamics, not by disproving the measure lemma. See [R8-3_referee.md:26](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R8-3/R8-3_referee.md:26>).

2. **Lemma 2 — algebra confirmed, scope not confirmed.** AM–GM gives the stated floor under H3′. But H3′ is an additional pointwise additivity assumption, not a consequence of CP for arbitrary covariant non-Markovian histories. [Layton et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.02557) explicitly note that general non-Markovian kernels need not satisfy the instantaneous trade-off. See [R8-3_referee.md:64](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R8-3/R8-3_referee.md:64>).

3. **Lemma 3 — generically confirmed with qualification.** A light-cone jump produces the claimed logarithm under the standard retarded prescription. An independent finite-bandwidth pulse gives a nonzero coefficient, so the effect is not confined to monochromatic sources. Light-cone finite-part counterterms remain outside the proof and must be excluded explicitly. See [R8-3_referee.md:98](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R8-3/R8-3_referee.md:98>).

4. **Theorem 3 — confirmed only for the common-scalar/additive subclass.** Newton alone does not force a nonzero spin-2 form factor: the scalar channel can reproduce the static Newtonian amplitude while the spin-2 channel vanishes. That escape fails lensing, photon universality, and correct TT radiation, so the theorem can be repaired by requiring a nonzero GR-normalized spin-2 coupling at the light cone. See [R8-3_referee.md:125](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R8-3/R8-3_referee.md:125>).

5. **Corollaries — mixed.** The timelike growth of \(e^{\ell^2\Box}\) is a genuine fatal problem in the proposed Hermitian scalar construction. The blanket causal-smearing conclusion still depends on H3′. “OWD as written” is incorrect: OWD’s simplest displayed covariant kernel is trace-only and therefore does not couple to flat-space free Maxwell stress. An ultra-local positive spin-2 OWD kernel would inherit the white-noise problem, but that does not exclude the [OWD framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.07283) or its non-Markovian kernels. See [R8-3_referee.md:163](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/C5/ref_R8-3/R8-3_referee.md:163>).

6. **Preferred-frame route — OPEN, estimates preliminary.** The \(\ell^{-7}\) scaling is dimensionally plausible, but the coefficient and cosmological photon bound are not derived. More seriously, the proposed null-regulated \(D_0\) is not the pointwise least-decoherence kernel for an unchanged \(1/s\) propagator; its excess over the floor diverges near the null cone. The \(10^{-8}\)–\(5\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm m\) interval is therefore a search target, not a demonstrated allowed window.
