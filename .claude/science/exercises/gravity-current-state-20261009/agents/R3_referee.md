# VERDICT: CONFIRMED WITH CORRECTIONS

The overall **INCONCLUSIVE, leaning FAIL** verdict is supportable for R3, but the proposed bounded **Class-A FAIL is not**. It remains INCONCLUSIVE because the variance calculation has not been connected to the TT propagator or damping.

1. **Relabelling and locality — mostly confirmed.** At fixed \(n\), relabelling acts by \(S_n\), the stabilizer of a representative is \(\mathrm{Aut}(C)\), and the unlabelled configuration space is discrete; there are no infinitesimal local generators or Noether-II identity ([R3_report.md:27–41](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/laneR/ref_R3/R3_report.md:27>)). Two corrections: finiteness/discreteness alone does not exclude a local gauge symmetry—lattice discrete gauge theories exist—and relabelling still constrains observables to be isomorphism-invariant. A labelled relation matrix can change purely by permutation; after quotienting this is the same point, while a genuine change of isomorphism class is not gauge. The point-move experiment supports \(\rho\delta T^3\): the endpoint exponent is about \(3.21\), and flipped partners remain at \(O(T)\) distance ([nd_pointmove.py:7–23](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/laneR/ref_R3/scripts/nd_pointmove.py:7>)). “No order perturbation is pure gauge” is therefore correct only on the unlabelled quotient and modulo accidental isomorphisms.

2. **Nonlocal TT mass — confirmed with qualifications.** With \(\sqrt{-g}\), a covariant tensor \(\Box^{-2}\), and covariantly chosen Green function/boundary conditions, \(C\Box^{-2}C\) is Diff-invariant. Around flat space and for \(k\neq0\), \(C^{(1)}C^{(1)}\sim k^4P^{(2)}\), so division by \(k^4\) gives a kernel proportional to \(P^{(2)}\); its normalization needs the appropriate \(M_{\rm Pl}^2m^2\) when \(h_{\mu\nu}\) is dimensionless ([R3_report.md:50–54](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/laneR/ref_R3/R3_report.md:50>)). Thus Diff alone does not ensure actual gaplessness of a nonlocal \(\Gamma\). But the report has not shown that the sum *generates* this term, and fixed criterion (ii) quantifies only over **local** terms ([TESTS.md:701–706](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/laneR/ref_R3/TESTS.md:701>)); this counterexample therefore cannot itself make criterion (ii) fail.

3. **Bilayers — algebra confirmed, scope corrected.** On a height-\(\le2\) order every relation is a link, so \(N_0=R\) and \(N_{k>0}=0\). For a fixed labelled/coloured balanced split, \(Z=e^{i\phi_nn}(1+e^{i\phi_l})^{n^2/4}\), and \(|1+e^{i\phi_l}|<1\) exactly on \((2\pi/3,4\pi/3)\) ([R3_report.md:58–76](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/laneR/ref_R3/R3_report.md:58>)). That exact factorization is not the exact unlabelled \(S_n\)-quotiented order sum; its “iff” must be scoped to the fixed-split ensemble. The resulting \(1.1325\,l_p\) is the elementary \(|1+e^{i\phi}|<1\) edge. Loomis–Carlip’s sufficient saddle/bounding condition gives about \(1.1361\,l_p\), and Mathur–Singh–Surya extend the same range to the KR link action; the difference is not merely a demonstrated convention change. [Loomis–Carlip](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.00064), [Mathur–Singh–Surya](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.07623). Later work also extends the full BDG suppression to typical \(K\)-layered sets, which the report omits. [Carlip–Carlip–Surya](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18238). For Class B, “never suppressed” is correct in the intended asymptotic scope: for fixed finite real coefficients and an unrestricted fixed-\(n\) sum, the positive balanced-bilayer contribution is \(e^{-an}(1+e^{-b})^{n^2/4}\); quotienting costs at most \(e^{O(n\log n)}\), leaving positive \(e^{c(b)n^2}\) growth. It is not a theorem for \(n\)-dependent coefficients, restricted sample spaces, or nonlinear actions.
