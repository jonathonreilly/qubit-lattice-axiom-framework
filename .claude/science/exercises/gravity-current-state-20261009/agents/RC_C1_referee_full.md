# VERDICT: CONFIRMED WITH CORRECTIONS

The fixed **C1 FAIL** is supported, but only in its expressly bounded sense: the scalar reduction is valid under a sharpened regular-collision reading of H1--H5, and B1 rigorously excludes an exact SLAC eigenvalue of every finite-component periodic real-analytic one-body matrix.  The report must not abbreviate that as “no finite network,” and it does not close interacting bands dressed by gapless continua.  The two-band touching construction is a genuine escape from the *single smooth band's approximation floor*, not from B1 or an exact finite-range no-go.

## Findings

1. **Lemma C -- finite-range theorem confirmed; total-level and loophole wording need qualification.**  In the nonidentity Pauli-string sector, finite nonempty supports have trivial translation stabilizer, so the local algebra is a direct sum of free modules over the Laurent ring.  The elements `u_j=1-t_j^{-1}` are a regular sequence; tensoring their Koszul resolution with that free module makes `H_1=0`.  The identity sector has zero `u_j` action and contributes exactly the constant vectors.  Thus the finite-range “curl plus c-number” result is correct and answers RN-L3's stated cohomology caveat (`C1_report.md:55-67`; `RN_L3_referee.md:9-10`).  The box calculations are illustrations, not proof, but reproduce (`c1_checks.out:1-3`).  C-tot's axial-limit argument also correctly gives `W_j(q=0)=0` for every regular kernel sector for which one may vary total transfer in the `e_j` direction; it should explicitly assume such a momentum-kernel/Fock decomposition and interior continuity.  Its survivor is a multiple of the identity (volume on a finite torus), not particle number unless `N` at `C1_report.md:81` means number of sites.  The projector `Pi=I-u^*u/|u|^2` is exactly transverse and has a direction-dependent zero-transfer limit; its generic kernel has the observed `R^{-d}` tail and logarithmically divergent `l1` norm in `d=2` (`C1_report.md:83-87`; `c1_harmonic.out:1-3`).  But assigning a value at the single `q=0` point does not by itself define an extensive infinite-volume charge: for a non-`l1` kernel the total is prescription-dependent.  It is a valid formal counterexample to dropping transfer regularity, not yet an admissible quasi-local improvement carrying an arbitrary total.

2. **Lemma R -- the C1 level-set proof is essentially valid and genuinely differs from Spohn.**  At a regular pair, `grad Omega=grad E(a)-grad E(b) != 0`; constancy of `g` on the C1 level hypersurface implies `grad g` is parallel to `grad Omega` without differentiating `F` twice.  Noncollinear gradient image then makes the pair multiplier global, and connectedness integrates the result to `F=lambda E+b.k+c` (`C1_report.md:99-112`).  The short “two triangles” sentence should be expanded, but the geometry is sound.  The application in Theorem A also needs a missing measure-theory sentence: H4 gives nonzero amplitude only almost everywhere on the full collision manifold, whereas Lemma R is stated for every collision.  A coarea/Fubini argument on regular shells, followed by continuity and density, supplies the bridge if the collision measure has full support; that condition should be made explicit.  [Spohn's proposition](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0605069) instead treats periodic integrable invariants on the full torus collision relation, assumes C2 dispersion plus generic Hessian nondegeneracy, and obtains only `aE+c` because periodicity kills `b.k` (`RN_L2_referee_full.md:86-112`).  Here the law is deliberately restricted to normal collisions on a lifted open zone, so ordinary lifted momentum is conserved and `b.k` remains; stronger pointwise/C1 hypotheses replace Spohn's weak-derivative machinery.  The supplied seeded run exactly reproduces SLAC defect `0` and KG maximum `2.444` (`c1_checks.out:5-13`).  The KG maximum is sampler-dependent, not a universal constant: an independent 900-collision run gives `2.412252`, while SLAC remains at roundoff `4.44e-16` (`referee_c1_checks.out:4`).

3. **Theorem A -- scalar core confirmed with three scope corrections; common speed is proved only along the stated scattering graph.**  Componentwise Lemma R gives `E grad E=aE+Bk+c`; symmetry of the Hessian forces `a=0` unless the gradient image lies in an affine line, and proper cubic covariance excludes such a nonconstant line image and reduces `B` to a scalar and `c` to zero (`C1_report.md:118-132`).  Strictly the conclusion is `E^2=m_0^2+beta|k|^2` with nonzero real `beta`; writing `beta=c^2>0` needs the additional physical hypothesis that the branch has positive curvature/minimum at the origin.  Extension across the exceptional set `S` also needs continuity of the band on all of `U`, not merely the displayed regularity on `U\S`.  Once `beta != 0`, conservation makes every nonzero-umklapp amplitude vanish because its vector-charge defect is `2pi beta m`.  For a smooth, persistently degenerate taste block, Lemma D makes the current `E grad E` times the identity, so the scalar argument applies; an isolated crossing or a non-smooth projector is not such a block.  If A and B each self-scatter, their forms are `f_A=beta_A k`, `f_B=beta_B k`; any nonzero-transfer AB collision gives `(beta_A-beta_B)(k_1-k_3)=0`, hence `beta_A=beta_B`.  Thus the common coefficient claim at `C1_report.md:139` is correct but should have included this one-line proof.  It propagates only through connected nondegenerate mutual-scattering edges.  The stronger sentence that every scattering mediator is SLAC with one common speed overreaches the report's own admission that interspecies-only scattering is merely argued (`C1_report.md:136-147`), and it does not cover gapless sectors lacking sharp asymptotic bands.

4. **B1 -- confirmed as rigorous for every `d>=1`.**  `chi(s,k)chi(-s,k)` is invariant under `s -> -s`, hence is a polynomial in `s^2` with real-analytic periodic coefficient functions.  Substituting `s^2=m^2+c^2|k|^2` therefore gives a single-valued real-analytic function on all of `R^d`, including `m=0`.  Vanishing on an open set forces global vanishing on connected `R^d`; at each lifted `k`, at least one of `+G(k)` or `-G(k)` is then an eigenvalue.  A periodic continuous finite matrix has uniformly bounded operator norm and hence bounded spectral radius, contradicting unbounded `G`.  No analytic labeling of individual eigenvalues is used, so crossings, non-Hermitian matrices, and `d=1` cause no problem (`C1_report.md:152-166`).  “No finite network” at `C1_report.md:17` must be replaced by “no finite-component periodic real-analytic quadratic/one-body network (or finite analytic linearization).”  Finite local interacting systems with continuum self-energies are not matrices of this class merely because the number of microscopic fields per cell is finite.

5. **B2 -- kink lemma confirmed; threshold corollary needs its analyticity premise stated rather than derived from positivity alone.**  If `F` is jointly analytic and the root is simple, the analytic implicit-function theorem gives an analytic periodic branch through the identified face, incompatible with the opposite one-sided derivatives of the SLAC cusp (`C1_report.md:168-172`).  For scalar `F=lambda-h-Sigma` and `Sigma(lambda,k)=integral rho(k,omega)/(lambda-omega) d omega`, the sign convention in the report gives `partial_lambda Sigma=-integral rho/(lambda-omega)^2`, hence `partial_lambda F>=1`; positive spectral weight therefore prevents a multiple root below threshold.  If, in addition, `Sigma(k,lambda)` is jointly analytic in `k` below a continuous threshold and the band remains strictly below it on the connected open cube, identity continuation propagates SLAC from an open patch to the cube.  A strictly positive gap at any face point then activates the kink contradiction, so exact SLAC requires threshold contact at every face point (`C1_report.md:174-180`).  A Herglotz representation by itself controls the energy variable; it does not guarantee joint momentum analyticity.  Gapless mediators therefore remain genuinely open, and the power counting and Fermi-sea comments remain suggestions/arguments, not parts of B2 (`C1_report.md:180-184`).

6. **Q1 -- the whole-zone results, window exponents, and two-band mechanism check, with numerical-scope qualifications.**  The periodic target `k^2` has a derivative jump at the face, so best degree-`n` trigonometric approximation is `Theta(1/n)`; the measured asymptotic constant `1.7603` is consistent with `2pi` times the Bernstein constant for `|x|` (`C1_report.md:190-193`; `c1_approx.out:1-10`).  For any periodic `C1` function `q=E^2`, the two endpoint residuals are `q'(pi)/2-pi c^2` and `q'(-pi)/2+pi c^2`, with equal derivatives; their difference is `2pi c^2`, so the supremum is at least `pi c^2`.  Equality for every `n` holds for the even cosine approximants used here, not for every arbitrary smooth band; the universal statement is the lower bound.  The independent endpoint check confirms the bound (`referee_c1_checks.out:1`).  On a window, mapping `x=-1` into the Chebyshev coordinate for `[cos kappa,1]` gives `-(3+cos kappa)/(1-cos kappa)`, hence the stated exponents `rho=3.229782,1.762747,0.806399`; the square-root singularity supplies the `n^{-3/2}` prefactor, and differentiation preserves the exponential rate (`C1_report.md:194-195`; `referee_c1_checks.out:2`).  The reliable bounded-LP rows support the same rates until their `1e-8`--`1e-10` floor (`c1_approx2.out:51-71`).  Apparent off-window blow-ups in later double-precision Chebyshev rows are roundoff amplified by extrapolation, not physical growth: a 90-digit recomputation gives face values below `pi^2`, rising smoothly to `8.99`, `9.10`, and `9.30` at `n=60` for the three windows (`referee_c1_checks.out:3`).  Thus “constraint not binding” is numerically supported over the resolved range, but the scripts do not constitute an all-`n` constrained-minimax theorem.  Finally, the two-band construction is mathematically real: positivity of the truncated `alpha^2` permits Fejer--Riesz factorization, and an exact face degeneracy supplies the cusp while smooth `D0` and `alpha^2` have super-algebraically convergent Fourier truncations.  The supplied numbers reproduce at a different FFT size (`c1_twoband.out:1-10`; `referee_c1_checks.out:5`).  It escapes the one-smooth-band `1/n` and `pi c^2` floors, but not B1: every finite `n` is only approximate.  Nor is the partner-band obstruction proved inevitable.  The partner has the opposite cusp and its projector is singular at the touching, so generic analytic interactions couple it and violate the scalar isolated-band hypotheses; an exact symmetry/selection-rule decoupling has not been excluded (`C1_report.md:198-207`).

7. **Fixed verdict -- bounded FAIL, not INCONCLUSIVE.**  The fixed rule asks whether the reduction holds and exact realization is impossible for the explicitly stated finite-component quadratic class (`TESTS.md:1107-1110,1129-1134`).  After the regular-shell and band-continuity qualifications above, the scalar reduction holds, and B1 completely closes that quadratic analytic class.  The two-band sequence never gives an exact finite network, while gapless-mediator dressed bands are an additional interacting class that the report openly leaves unresolved.  A no-go scope stress test therefore leaves the following honest boundary: finite analytic matrices are closed; analytic simple dressed roots are closed at a gapped face; band touchings evade the single-band approximation floor but not exact B1; gapless continua, transfer-singular `R^{-d}` improvements, open-set scattering zeros, interspecies-only matrix scattering, and `d=1` reduction remain unclosed.  Those routes forbid the report's broad “no finite network” shorthand but do not change the registered finite-component-quadratic FAIL criterion.

## Strongest correct statement

Under transfer-regular improvement kernels, additive exact asymptotic conservation, a regular full-support normal-collision relation in `d>=2`, and a continuous isolated scalar band with noncollinear gradient image, closure forces `E^2=m_0^2+beta|k|^2` on the lifted zone and forces nonzero-umklapp amplitudes to vanish; cubic symmetry alone fixes scalar `beta` but not its positive sign.  Smooth persistent taste degeneracies inherit the same scalar symbol, and self-scattering species connected by nondegenerate mutual scattering share `beta`, while crossings, interspecies-only scattering, and gapless non-particle sectors are not covered.  No finite-component periodic real-analytic one-body matrix in any `d>=1` can possess that exact SLAC eigenvalue, and a simple jointly analytic dressed root cannot carry its face cusp; with a positive-spectral-weight scalar self-energy this excludes any face point where the continuum remains strictly gapped, but not a gapless continuum.  For one smooth band the best whole-zone `E^2` error is `Theta(1/n)` (numerically `1.7603/n`) and the closure residual is at least `pi c^2`; window errors have the stated Bernstein--Walsh exponents, while the fixed global-boundedness constraint is numerically nonbinding only over the resolved range.  A face-touching two-band construction genuinely improves whole-zone error and residual super-algebraically at finite range, but is never exact at finite range and leaves, rather than proves, the partner-band interaction obstruction.  Therefore the unchanged fixed reading is **C1 FAIL, bounded to finite-component analytic quadratic networks (and the stated analytic simple-root dressed subclass)**, with gapless-mediator dressed bands and singular improvements remaining open.
