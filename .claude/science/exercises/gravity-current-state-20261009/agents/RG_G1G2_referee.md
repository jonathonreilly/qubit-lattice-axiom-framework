   \[
   \langle f|[S,P]|i\rangle=(P_i-P_f)S_{fi}=D_s\,T_{fi}=0
   \]
   componentwise. There is no probability or occupation weighting. \(D=+2\pi\) and \(D=-2\pi\) belong to distinct final-state matrix elements and cannot cancel; only diagrams contributing to the same total \(T_{fi}\) may cancel. ## Strongest correct statement

For nearest-neighbour TB matter in \(d\ge2\), and for any integrable lift of crystal momentum, an exact conserved charge that acts additively and diagonally on asymptotic particles forces the total \(2\leftrightarrow2\) amplitude to vanish almost everywhere on a positive-measure umklapp set—an open set for ordinary piecewise-smooth fundamental domains—and any globally meromorphic amplitude on connected \(M_{\rm reg}\) must therefore vanish identically; this rigorously covers finite-depth tree exchange, finite-component quadratic-network resolvents, and nonzero rank-one contact scattering, with the KG version conditional on unproved connectivity. The same conclusion extends only with additional hypotheses to coupling-analytic Born families and factorized positive-spectral-weight exchange. G2’s binomial product construction has exact nonzero finite-depth suppression \(16^{-r}\), static Lindhard exchange does not improve the exponent, and \(10^{-100}\) amplitude suppression costs about \(83\) binomial steps or conservatively \(5\times10^2\)–\(9\times10^2\) fixed-window steps while suppressing lattice-scale normal scattering too. Fixed-strong-coupling dressed vertices, general interfering spectral exchange, and nonperturbative non-contact finite-range interactions are not closed; hence the strongest FIXED reading is **INCONCLUSIVE**, not bounded FAIL.
