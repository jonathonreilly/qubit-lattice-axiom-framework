# VERDICT: CONFIRMED WITH CORRECTIONS

## 1. Sourcing theorem

**Confirmed only with a regularity/scope correction.** For the fixed 09-14 gravity stencils and the displayed linear constraints, preservation of the scalar constraint requires the divergence of the shift source to equal the energy-current divergence, while preservation of the vector constraint requires a symmetric-stress continuity equation for that same source (`G3_report.md:22-26`; the stencil complex is fixed at `LOCAL_FINITE_CLOCK_TENSOR_CONSTRAINTS_CUBIC_DISPERSION_AND_LINEAR_GRAVITY_BOUNDARIES_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-09-14.md:95-102,145-149`). Thus (P_{\rm shift}) is fixed only modulo the kernel of `div`; exact equality to a chosen energy-current density is stronger than closure itself. For a translation-covariant source whose transfer-momentum form factor is continuous at (q=0), the one-particle continuity equation gives (p_j(k,k)=E(k)\partial_jE(k)), since the total-energy condition gives (t(k,k)=E(k)). The report states continuity only for (t) (`G3_report.md:28-31`), but continuity/controlled boundary behavior of (P), or equivalently a well-defined dipole integration by parts, is also needed. Arbitrarily nonlocal sources can evade it through a discontinuous zero-mode/harmonic term; regular divergence-free additions have zero one-particle diagonal but can still alter higher-body densities, and a separately conserved network sector can carry part of the source. Modified gravity stencils or matter-dependent constraint completions are outside this theorem. Subject to the regular, matter-only, scalar-band hypotheses, nearest-neighbor KG gives (E\nabla E=\sin k), not the sawtooth (`g3_sourcing.out:1-8`), and requiring (E\nabla E=c^2k) integrates to (E^2=m^2+c^2|k|^2) on the open zone: the SLAC conclusion is correct in that scope (`G3_report.md:33-36`). It is not a theorem for every nonlocal density, every matrix/taste band, or a combined matter-network source.

## 2. Separable stress and tails

**Confirmed, with the tail exponent qualified.** For an even separable real-scalar kernel (W=m^2+\sum_iw(k_i)) and the stated symmetrized (T_{00}), the displayed (P_j) and symmetric (T_{ij}) obey both Fourier identities algebraically (`G3_report.md:38-47`); an independent random-momentum check gives absolute residuals below (8.0\times10^{-15}) (`referee_g3_checks.py:27-49`; `referee_g3_checks.out:1-3`). The exceptional `qhat=0` values require the derivative limits used by the worker, and the SLAC face remains a measure-zero/distributional boundary. The (P) maximum exponent (-2.01), the effective (T) fit (-1.73) over (R=16\ldots128), and the nearly constant (1.38) loss of the finite-box (P)-tail sum per doubling reproduce (`referee_g3_checks.out:5-14`), supporting logarithmic failure of absolute summability for (P). But (-1.7) is not a demonstrated asymptotic pure power: extending the fit to (R=64\ldots256) moves it to (-1.89), consistent with a log-corrected (R^{-2}) tail (`referee_g3_checks.out:12-13`). The underlying sawtooth derivative density itself has the advertised (1/r) kernel (`g3_sourcing.out:35`). These identities establish first-class closure only at the stated linear order (`G3_report.md:58-60`).

## 3. Sawtooth strain flow and the fixed-zone (\mu_E)

**The no-unitary conclusion is confirmed; the physical-floor interpretation is not forced.** On each half-zone the symmetric dilation generator associated with (dk/ds=k) is unitarily equivalent to a one-sided translation on a half-line. Its deficiency indices are unequal (the ordering `(1,0)` versus `(0,1)` is convention-dependent), so it has no self-adjoint extension on the same one-particle Hilbert space; reaching the face in time (\ln(\pi/k_0)) is therefore a genuine obstruction for the exact sawtooth E-channel generator (`G3_report.md:75-80`; `g3_face.out:1-6`). Independent differentiation of the fixed-cube integral reproduces (\mu_E=1.726759,1.711506,1.668169) at (m=0,0.5,1) (`referee_g3_checks.py:88-99`; `referee_g3_checks.out:16-19`). Those numbers belong to the particular nonunitary fixed-Brillouin-zone completion, however; they are not a Ward-protected observable or an unavoidable physical floor. A boundary sector, enlarged Hilbert space, counterterm, smooth approximation, or another nonlinear gravity/matter completion changes the question. The T2 sawtooth vector field is divergence-free with matching normal components at the faces, so its measure-preserving flow is not affected by the E-channel deficiency (`G3_report.md:82-86`).

## 4. (O(\lambda^2)), filtering, floor, and cross term

**The structural (\epsilon_U^2) law is confirmed, but the sawtooth table and quoted floor are not thermodynamic-limit results.** In design A, each vacuum (0\to4\) tuple lies in exactly one of the normal/umklapp supports, so the retained basketball graph is exactly (\mu_N+\epsilon_U^2\mu_U) with no term linear in (\epsilon_U) (`g3_epsU_mu.py:1-9,49-75`; `G3_report.md:90-104`). The sin-flow controls reproduce. The sawtooth rows, however, use only (L=11,15,19) (`g3_epsU_mu.out:8-10`; `g3_epsU_slac.out:1-3`), too coarse to resolve the (O(|h|)) face-crossing layer. A common-sample discrete-grid check reproduces the worker at (L=15,19), then changes strongly: for source-consistent SLAC it moves from ((\mu_N,\mu_U)=(2.41,4.24)\times10^{-6}) at (L=15) to ((0.926,-0.435)\times10^{-6}) at (L=127) (`referee_g3_gridcheck.py:34-46`; `referee_g3_gridcheck.out:2,4,6,8,10`). A continuum-first scrambled-Sobol calculation gives stable estimates near (\mu_N=+0.80\times10^{-6}), (\mu_U=-0.70\times10^{-6}) for SLAC (`referee_g3_checks.py:125-152`; `referee_g3_checks.out:21-28`). Therefore the positive 4.3–4.7e-6 umklapp coefficient and the claimed 1.5–1.7-natural floor (`G3_report.md:95-106`) are not confirmed; this design still has an (\epsilon_U)-independent floor, but the continuum estimate is about 0.5 of the quoted 1.67e-6 benchmark, and the umklapp Hessian has the opposite sign. The calculation is only the tadpole-subtracted I4 contribution, not the full bare onsite-φ⁴ Hessian (`RN_L2_referee_full.md:216-243`). In design B, the support-overlap measure (I_X\simeq5.8\times10^{-5}|h|) is reproduced in scale and yields a term linear in (\epsilon_U), but it is a nonanalytic `|h|` cusp, not a finite (\mu=E_0''(0)) (`g3_cross_mc.out:1-6`; `G3_report.md:108-111`). Finally, the RN-L2 dressing loophole is load-bearing: all these vacuum pieces are off shell and an analytic (O(\lambda)) dressing can in principle cancel them without repairing the on-shell collision defect (`RN_L2_referee_full.md:263-269`). Thus neither the floor nor the cusp is forced by conservation failure.

## 6. Fixed reading

**G3 by itself gives neither PASS nor FAIL.** PASS requires an explicit network law meeting all three conjuncts, which G3 does not supply, while the fixed FAIL is the G1 class no-go (`TESTS.md:1018-1025`). G3 does establish a new necessary condition only for the regular matter-only sourcing ansatz: with the 09-14 VS stencils, a scalar one-particle sawtooth shift charge requires SLAC dispersion, so ordinary nearest-neighbor KG cannot be that regular source. It does **not** make lane-RG PASS impossible for all nearest-neighbor matter, because a singular/harmonic divergence-free addition, a separately conserved network current, matrix/taste structure, or a modified gravity/constraint completion is not excluded.

**Strongest correct statement.** With the fixed 09-14 VS constraints and regular transfer-momentum kernels, scalar-constraint preservation fixes the shift source's one-particle symbol to (E\nabla E); hence a matter-only sawtooth source requires an exactly SLAC one-particle dispersion, and the supplied even-separable SLAC stress realizes the linear continuity identities but is power-law nonlocal and not absolutely summable. The exact sawtooth E-channel dilation has no self-adjoint generator on the fixed Brillouin-zone Hilbert space, while the reported (+1.7) fixed-zone Hessian is completion-dependent. At (O(\lambda^2)) the disjoint-support design has an exact (\mu_N+\epsilon_U^2\mu_U) form, and the alternative design has a verified (\epsilon_U|h|) cusp, but the worker's small-(L) sawtooth coefficients and (1.5)–(1.7)-natural floor are not continuum-converged and no vacuum term is forced in view of the RN-L2 off-shell dressing loophole. The conditional quadratic readout is (n_*\sim113/c), but no universal finite or infinite (n_*) follows. Therefore G3 neither passes nor fails lane RG and does not rule out every PASS with nearest-neighbor matter; it rules out the regular matter-only nearest-neighbor scalar sourcing route and makes exact SLAC dispersion (or an explicitly demonstrated alternative carrier/completion) an additional requirement for that route.
