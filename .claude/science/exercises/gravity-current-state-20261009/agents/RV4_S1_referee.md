**Strongest correct statement:** For gapped, single-band free quadratic matter with the RN-L1 energy-current momentum \(p=\nabla(\omega^2/2)\), the translation-covariant bulk-flow coupling has the stated Jacobian response; no periodic momentum field makes every shear flow divergence-free, axis hopping protects only \(T_{2g}\), body-diagonal hopping protects only \(E_g\), canonical SLAC leaves \(\mu_E(1)=1.4832833\times10^{-2}\), and the examined density prescriptions reduce to an exact-stress violation, a tuned coefficient, or R₀. A finite-range nonseparable symbol can zero both channels at one chosen mass but the zero drifts with mass, while mass-interval cancellation is excluded for single-minimum analytic separable symbols and for Morse symbols whose relevant van Hove singularities cannot cancel at coincident critical levels. Compact Ward covariance would enforce \(\mu_E=\mu_{T2}\), but it is incompatible with the field-independent \(e^{-iP[\xi]}\) bulk response. Thus the fixed result is **FAIL within these classes**, not a universal no-go; field-dependent generators, degenerate or coincident-level nonseparable bands, inequivalent multi-minimum separable bands, multiband/Berry structures, and interactions remain open.
