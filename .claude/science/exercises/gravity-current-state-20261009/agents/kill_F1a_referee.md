2. **The claimed Last–Penrose “exact” no-go is incorrect.**  
   The actual Fock-space identity is
   \[
   \mathrm{Var}\,F=\sum_{n\ge1}\frac1{n!}\|T_nF\|^2_{L^2(\lambda^n)} ,
   \]
   as stated in [Last–Penrose, Theorem 18.6](https://stoch.math.kit.edu/img/Last/lastpenrose2017.pdf). It says a *fixed finite-window functional* has zero variance iff all its chaos kernels vanish. It does **not** imply \(\mathrm{Var}\,F_V\propto |V|\), because the kernels \(T_nF_V\) themselves depend on the window. \(S(0)=0\) requires only \(\mathrm{Var}\,F_V=o(|V|)\), not zero variance. A frozen Poisson-derived coboundary provides a direct counterexample: if a local random field is a Laplacian, or a conserved tensor stress is a double curl of a short-range Poisson shot-noise potential, then \(S(q)\propto q^4\) although its chaos kernels are nonzero. Force balance alone also does not always imply \(q^4\), and mobility is not mathematically necessary to impose such an identity. Thus the numerical \(S(0)>0\) result for these models is strong; the purported theorem for all frozen Poisson-type laws is not. Mobile sites are sufficient for realization-wise affine invariance ([R3_price.md:38](</private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/referee_KF1a/R3_price.md:38>)), but necessity is unproved.

## Strongest correct statement

An SL(3)-invariant point-process law guarantees only that the thermodynamic **ensemble-averaged** vacuum energy is shear-independent; it supplies no realization-wise Ward identity. In the massive scalar graph and 2D Delaunay examples actually tested, a frozen realization has a random TT tadpole and/or tangent stiffness with nonzero \(S(0)\); with standard gravitational normalization that disorder is IR-relevant and destroys perturbation theory about flat space at tens of lattice spacings, while the associated cutoff and propagation bounds are phenomenologically incompatible. Therefore F1a, relying only on symmetry of the frozen ensemble, is not a viable separate solution. What is not established is the stronger universal proposition that every frozen Poisson-derived theory must have \(S(0)>0\), or that mobile sites are the only escape: an additional exact realization-wise cancellation, coboundary/conservation identity, or hyperuniform stress construction could evade the argument, but it would be a new protecting mechanism beyond Lemma L/A.
