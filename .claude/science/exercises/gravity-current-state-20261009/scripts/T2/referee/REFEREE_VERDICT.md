4. **Major RP correction: C/C′ do not enter the positive-transfer/Lemma-N class at any finite \(s^2\).** A, B and D have sound analytic Nevanlinna/positive-mixture arguments; their finite Hankel tests are consistent supporting checks. C/C′ have exact link-RP, but finite \(T=10\) Hankel tests are insufficient ([T2e_rp_checks.py:29–50](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/referee_T2/T2e_rp_checks.py:29)). At generic spatial momentum their box-blocked correlations satisfy
\[
G_n=\sum_\ell w_\ell\frac{\cosh E_\ell-1}{E_\ell^3}e^{-nE_\ell},\qquad n\ge1.
\]
An unshifted positive Hausdorff measure would require total mass \(\sum_\ell w_\ell(\cosh E_\ell-1)/E_\ell^3\), which diverges because sinc alias weights decay polynomially while \(\cosh E_\ell\) grows exponentially. Finite \(s^2\) changes only \(G_0\) and cannot repair this. The independent cutoff control is [referee_independent_checks.py:34–54](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/referee_T2/referee_independent_checks.py:34). Thus the reported passes at \(s^2\ge0.5\) are finite-order false assurances ([T2e_extra.out:5–7](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/referee_T2/T2e_extra.out:5)). Link-RP alone can support a two-step/reflected construction, but does not certify the required positive one-step transfer matrix for the lattice slice field; the standard positive-transfer framework is due to [Lüscher](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01614090) and [Osterwalder–Seiler](https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-4916(78)90039-8).

5. **Lemma R is only conditionally correct.** Momentum non-additivity does obstruct extending the relabelling as an exact symmetry of a generic local interaction: standard vertices conserve \(\sum k_i\), while relabelling produces conservation of \(\sum\Phi^{-1}(k_i)\). Preserving both forces an additive torus automorphism, hence an element of \(GL(3,\mathbb Z)\), and a continuous deformation from the identity is trivial. But the hypotheses stated in [R4_misframing.md:37–40](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/referee_T2/R4_misframing.md:37) do not imply that every symmetry normalizes the designated translation subgroup. Lemma R therefore excludes ordinary local, additively momentum-conserving interacting extensions—not every conceivable interacting law.
