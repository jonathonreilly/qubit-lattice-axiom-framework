# Routes to attack (kill round)

Each route below was proposed by one of four independent agents. Their full texts are in the same folder: agent1_condensed_matter.md, agent2_quantum_gravity.md, agent3_axioms_price.md, agent4_misframing.md.

- F1 (price lemma). Finite slots plus an exact additive local rule force every sector-preserving local Hamiltonian into the "character class". This holds in every eigenstate, by Haar averaging over the compact rule group (agent 1, route 1) or by Lemma P, compact variables making weak invariance strict (agent 3, route 1). So the TT f-sum is O(q^4) and the graviton is soft or needs an incompressible TT channel. The only exits: non-compact slots, or the rule violated at O(1) in the vacuum. Agent 3 adds that the CCR no-go is not load-bearing, since collective fluctuation operators obey the CCR in the limit.
- F2 (strong coupling / incompressible). Gu–Wen's N-type regime (agent 1, route 2), or a gapless finite-slot state with power-law E–E tails and chi_TT ~ q^2 (agent 3, route 2).
- F3 (non-ground state). The wall rests on a ground-state premise. Einstein's spin-2 f-sum needs a negative compression weight v0 = -v2/3 (the conformal-factor problem). A population-inverted realized state, with record formation downhill, could supply it (agent 2, route 1).
- F4 (entanglement or thermodynamic gravity). There is no lattice graviton; Einstein's equation comes as an equation of state (Jacobson 2016; FGHMV 2014). Tests: modular-Hamiltonian locality for the walker sea (agent 1, route 3); I(ball : outside) scaling on frozen-box ensembles (agent 2, route 2). Also Lemma MI: Admissibility read as a Markov random field gives I(A : A^c) <= |dA| ln 2 (agent 3, route 3).
- F5 (composite / induced graviton). The walker sea induces the metric's action (Sakharov). Test: the O(q^2) helicity +-1 block of the induced action on sym(qhat (x) e), after tuning probe 6's two O(1) cubic numbers (agent 4, route 2).
- F6 (observational purity). Radiating conserved quadrupole sources couple to helicity +-1 tensors with direction-averaged weight equal to TT's, and static sources couple to them not at all. So gapless partners with O(1) residue are excluded by binary-pulsar bounds whatever their speed. Partners slower than c are also excluded by gravitational Cherenkov (agent 4, route 1; agent 1 citing Moore–Nelson).
- F7 (statics first). In the broken branch the static minimiser gives PPN gamma = 1. The perihelion needs the lattice lapse's second-order static response to give beta_eff; with a GR lapse it gives 0.834 x GR (agent 4, route 3).
- F8 (A1 prediction). Hojman–Kuchar–Teitelboim plus Bahr–Dittrich predict that test A1 (second-order lapse-algebra closure) fails at bounded range, with pseudo-constraints fixing the lapse; closure needs a non-local perfect action (agent 2, route 3).
