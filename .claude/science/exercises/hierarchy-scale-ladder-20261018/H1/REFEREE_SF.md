Main's B3b-branch is the one-component staggered (P-FLUX) class (`HIERARCHY_B3_STAGGERED_SUPPLIER_CASCADE:53,93-100`). There, translations act as gauge-dressed shifts. The shift-invariant on-site plus nearest-neighbour space is 9-dimensional. In six random members, every singular-value multiplicity is divisible by 4 and the zero count is never 1.
