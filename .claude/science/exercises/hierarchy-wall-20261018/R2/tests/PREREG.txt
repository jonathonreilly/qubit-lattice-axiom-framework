R2 pre-registration (written before any run), 2026-10-18.

Declared model (not axiom content): hard-support reading of Admissibility.
A rule is a set A of allowed 7-site star configurations (centre bit + six
face bits), closed under the 24 proper cubic rotations acting on positions,
with Pauli labels acted on trivially or by the sign twist (bit flip for
rotations whose axis permutation is odd). Quantum fluctuations: a local
perturbation of strength eps; the tunnelling order between two valid
configurations differing on a finite set S is |S| for one-site terms and at
least ceil(|S|/2) for two-site terms.

T1 (main). For every rule and every valid base configuration of period 2 in
each axis, and layered bases of period 3 and 4, compute the largest finite
minimal flip M = max_A min{|S| : S finite, nonempty, base xor S valid},
restricted to flips inside a 6^3 box (stability checked at 7^3 for a
subset). Exact SAT/CEGAR.
  Route 1 survives as an O(1)-spurion mechanism only if M >= 15
  (eps ~ 0.1 then gives eps^M <= 1e-15). If M <= 8 it is killed in this class.
T2. For the extremal rule found, compute the exact order-M tunnelling
coefficient C_M (sum over orderings of products of inverse intermediate
penalties, penalty J per violated star) and the second-order diagonal
detuning between the two configurations. If the detuning is O(eps^2/J) and
nonzero, the order-M process sets a bandwidth, not a gap.
T3. For the soldered branch (Chamon rule, the unique soldered Pauli rule on
main), compute the minimum weight of a nontrivial logical operator d(L) on
L^3 tori, L = 4 exactly (search to weight 6), L = 6 bounds. This is the
order at which a one-site perturbation first splits the code space.
Memory cap 0.4 GB via capped.sh, one process at a time.
