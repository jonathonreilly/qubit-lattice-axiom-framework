# The hierarchy wall (exercise, 2026-10-18)

## Plain version

Nature has numbers that are tiny compared with the grid's own scale.
- The weak-force scale is about 10⁻¹⁷ of the Planck scale.
- The electron's mass is about 10⁻⁶ of the top quark's.

We want the grid's rules to produce these numbers without anyone putting them in by hand.

So far, every way of climbing from the grid to everyday scales has failed:
- **Carrying data up ("transport").** The scale ladder can carry structural facts upward, but it cannot pick the size of a coupling that matters at long distances.
- **Running couplings.** A coupling that runs slowly would generate the gap only if its strength sits in a narrow window, about 0.17–0.36. The grid's only value of that coupling (β = 6) lies outside the window.
- **Symmetry breaking at the grid.** A single breaking of the grid's symmetry gives at most two mass levels, and it treats every particle type the same way.

Getting past the wall means one of two things:
- a mechanism built only from the axioms' own data that produces ratios of 10⁻¹⁵ or smaller;
- a proof that some named extra number is unavoidable.

- v/M_Pl ≈ 2e-17.

**What the evidence does not cover:**

- integer extents, such as clockwork chains and domain walls;
- two amplitudes, and dilute breaking;
- record or Born-weight structure;
- the record count or cosmological history;
- non-Euclidean time.
