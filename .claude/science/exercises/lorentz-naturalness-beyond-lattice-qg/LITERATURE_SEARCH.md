## Synthesis (honest)
The literature PARTIALLY supports the reframe but does NOT rescue it. The reframe's first half is correct:
for a FIXED fundamental theory δv is COMPUTED not tuned ('t Hooft/Wetterich), and graphene (Giuliani et al.)
is the rigorous existence proof that a fixed lattice yields emergent Lorentz with computed velocities. BUT
the decisive axis is the convergence RATE (Bednik et al./Anber–Donoghue): a weakly/AF-coupled fixed theory
flows only LOGARITHMICALLY, so Collins' problem PERSISTS even when δv is computed — being "fixed and finite"
is necessary, not sufficient. Graphene succeeds because it is single-species (no species-difference
observable); the framework has multiple gauge reps flowing at different rates and no custodial symmetry.
So: the framework is better than a generic EFT-with-cutoff (δv is genuinely computed → "naturalness" →
"prediction"), but it inherits the residual species-δv graphene's single-species structure avoids. The
honest status: a COMPUTED-OR-UNCOMPUTED prediction (not an unnatural tuning); the actual number is the open
task; the field-wide α_s/4π is a prior, not the framework's posterior.
