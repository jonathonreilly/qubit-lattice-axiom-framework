## Elon reduction (Exercise Two)
- Exact requirement: a NUMBER (the species δv), compared to a bound — NOT a naturalness argument.
- DELETE the EFT scaffolding (sliding cutoff, free coupling, operator tower): the framework has a fixed
  finite description. What remains is "compute δv."
- Smallest object: two species (differing only in gauge Casimir) on the shared sin(p_i a) kernel; one-loop

- Classification: this is a COMPUTATION problem, not a selector/symmetry problem.

## Decisive verdict on the stakes (v): (c) UNCOMPUTED
- NOT (a) passing: the tree-level Planck-suppression covers only the dim-6 irrelevant operator; the
  marginal (dim-4) piece is a different operator, genuinely radiatively generated, NOT Planck-suppressed.
- NOT (b) falsified: δv~α_s/4π~6e-3 is the Collins literature ESTIMATE substituted in, NOT the framework's

  could move the actual number and none is quantified.
- THEREFORE (c) UNCOMPUTED. The honest label is "uncomputed, not falsified" — and a fixed theory does not

  differential marginal δv on the native surface at β=6), not a custodial-symmetry hunt. Finding a
  symmetry is the FALLBACK if the computation lands above bounds — not the primary task.

the shared kernel does not cancel the species difference (it's O(1)·α_s/4π via the Casimir difference).

below bounds) or a falsification (if it lands at ~10⁻³). That is the honest, decisive state.
