**The wall.** We want three generations whose masses span about 10⁶, with the observed hierarchical mixing, from the grid's own structure (see WALL.md).

**Provenance.** Five simulated physicists (Opus 5.5, xhigh) with distinct routes; a kill round; one decisive test (T-2AMP). All are the same model family and none is refereed by another vendor.

**What the kill round found.** No negative is wrong. Every symmetry ceiling shares one premise: *the realized vacuum is symmetric*. A spontaneous Eg order with a generic D2h direction arises from the general Landau energy in about 9% of coefficient space, with no supplied direction. It gives 3 levels and a nontrivial CKM, but ratios set by Landau ratios, which are free.

**What remains open.**
- **The Landau test.** Compute GEN-2's rod-model Eg Landau coefficients (b₃, c₆). If no admissible coupling gives a D2h minimum, all the negatives close without the symmetric-vacuum premise.
- **Koide's r and δ.** These are main's open gates.

**The wall now (reading).** For every placement tried, an isotropy-preserving lattice symmetry structure yields at most two levels, or a permutation CKM. Escaping needs either a spontaneous anisotropic order, which brings Lorentz-violation costs and free ratios, or supplied numbers: ε, a second amplitude, per-sector labels, or Koide's r and δ. **No route derived the mass hierarchy.**
