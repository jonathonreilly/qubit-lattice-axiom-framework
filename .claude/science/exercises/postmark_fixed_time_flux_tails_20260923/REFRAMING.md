1. **Algebraic correction:** what is the first finite-spin correction on a fixed physical flux state? This is answered by the exact legal-path coefficient D.
2. **Dynamics:** does this core expansion control a normalizable vector under fixed laboratory time when the leading generator grows like C? This remains unanswered. The candidate correction is unbounded, and an interior macroscopic-flux edge sequence has a nonzero coefficient limit; statewise propagation still needs proof.
3. **Framework inference:** does the conditional model imply a TOE claim or force an axiom revision? No. The minimal framework does not derive this Hamiltonian, preparation, or observable map.

The computed coefficient witness is sharper than “the interactions weaken as the system grows.” The local hopping coefficient has a correct first derivative at fixed flux, while the complete generator has a specified nonzero edge limit on an interior sequence with n/S=1. Floating-point evolution suggests roughly one third of the exact-spin norm lies at n>=S, but does not certify a limit. The decisive route is a spectral or semiclassical propagation result for G_S=M_S^2-C M_S and its bounded observables. A small-time rescaling is a different target and is explicitly excluded from the frozen fixed-time comparison.
