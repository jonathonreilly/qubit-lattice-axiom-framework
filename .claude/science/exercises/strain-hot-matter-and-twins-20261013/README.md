## Plain summary

1. **Hot matter on the root's own strain law (F1–F6).**
   - **Fermions.** Under the registered strain law, the Wilson-type fermions' additive mass responds to strain. Read through the canonical mapping, this gives graviton masses inside Earth-density matter far above what GW170817's Earth crossing allows (F1, F2).
   - **But the registered texts do not fix the interacting law.** A spectrum-preserving unitary dressing (F3) and the choice of gauge-path family (F4) both move the response, and a dressing can tune it to zero. So F1 is recorded **OPEN** (refereed).
   - **The rule tested in F5** (re-set the fermion critical mass on every strained law) is clean and covers the fermion channel, but it leaves the Higgs channel and a vacuum knock-on. **RULE-PARTIAL** (refereed).
   - **The Higgs mass² responds to strain at grid strength** under the same law (F6, **SCALAR-HAZARD**, refereed). Under the nanohertz gravitational-wave background it would move the electroweak vacuum by about 10³ of itself.
2. **Panel F (five lenses).**
   - All five rated the strain-covariant critical-mass rule "sound with conditions".
   - Their joint proposal is one primitive: "every zero-strain renormalization condition holds on every uniformly strained law".
3. **Staggered fermions (NF-1).**
   - Discrete symmetries (Θ, P_stag) force the strain-dependent additive mass to vanish exactly.
   - The price is two identical tastes per field. Recorded **OPEN**, on the literal U(1)_ε wording.
4. **Emergent coarseness (E1).**
   - A correlation-length cutoff stretches with the field.
   - Tuned gaps still move with a fine-scale coefficient; symmetry-protected gaps stay zero. **MECHANISM-SPLIT**.
   - A composite (transmuted) Higgs scale responds only multiplicatively. The clock margin depends on the model: about 6×10⁷ in the toy, and 5.6×10¹¹ only under an extra assumption.
5. **Twins (TW-1/2/3).**
   - **TW-1, twin = antiparticle:** works only for real representations, so only ν_R (HALVE-PARTIAL).
   - **TW-2, twins as generations:** safe at second order (SPLIT-SAFE). But the direction-fixed splitting opens a branch-dependent first-order clock hazard, ×4.7–17.8 for the minimal symbol.
   - **TW-3, symmetric gapping:** Θ protects the light sector. On-site gapping cannot select twins (OPEN).
