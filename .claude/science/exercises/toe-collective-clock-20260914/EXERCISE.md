## Exact wall

For one fixed sufficiently large finite N, find an open coupling region of a
specified local three-spatial-dimensional Z_N Hamiltonian, an infinite-volume
state, and a physically matched gauge-invariant observable whose low-energy
spectral measure has the required photon behavior. A gaplessness theorem is a
useful weaker milestone; a two-sided Euclidean covariance estimate is another.
Neither automatically supplies a simple one-particle pole, native readout,
charged matter, a common cone, gravity or a full TOE. Distinguish these targets.
The order of limits must not let N grow with volume, observation time or probe
wavelength after claiming fixed payload. Boundary sectors and zero modes must
be explicit. N=2, gapped strong-electric regimes, frozen ice states and finite
boxes are mandatory counterchecks. Exclude a claim based only on positive local
curvature, finite-order perturbation, small raw defect density or perimeter laws
without their gaplessness bridge.

The present attempt to import isotropic Euclidean dilute-current estimates into
the fixed-N continuous-time limit fails: short neutral temporal currents are not
dilute in the proposed Gaussian reference. Single-link exact blocking resums
these currents but neglecting magnetic coupling has a nonzero harmonic-limit
error. This is a failed route step, not an axiom incompatibility or an exclusion
of collective resummations. The existing Gaussian correction is constructive
but has no compact-defect/source matching theorem.

Allowed inputs are the approved foundation plus explicitly named conditional
model hypotheses. The four axioms and three primitives are assumptions in this
exercise. Their availability does not make a chosen gauge action or state a
consequence. No empirical fit is being used as a proof premise. A decisive
phase advance must pay a uniform estimate on the actual source-dependent
model; a decisive axiom wall would require impossibility across the allowed
foundation, not across this one Hamiltonian ansatz.

## Reduction

1. The nearest-neighbor continuous-time clock Hamiltonian is a chosen test
   sector, not a foundation requirement. A fixed positive Euclidean transfer
   followed by reconstruction is a different valid conditional attack if its
   locality and observable map are stated. It cannot inherit a native dynamics
   claim merely by avoiding the singular limit.
2. Separate three joins: fixed-payload carrier; thermodynamic gapless phase;
   physical record/readout. The first is already constructible. Do not solve it
   repeatedly while leaving the second unchanged.
3. The smallest object falsifying naive block factorization is one reduced
   plaquette clock. The smallest object exposing failed raw-current dilution is
   a single closed temporal plaquette in the full anisotropic Gaussian field.
4. Replace a numerical search for an apparent small eigenvalue by an analytic
   criterion on a source-dependent partition function or a variational sequence
   in the actual infinite-volume observable algebra.
5. A state selector and a phase existence theorem are independent obligations.
   Finding a phase does not select it; lack of selection does not refute its
   existence. Do not relabel either as an axiom defect.
