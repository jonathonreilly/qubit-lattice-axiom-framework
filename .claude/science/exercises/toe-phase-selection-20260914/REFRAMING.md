| Reframe | What moves | Simpler | Harder | Route opened | First decisive test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Invariant sector -> selected sector | The state being studied | Removes a hidden assumption | Must compare mobile charged components | Sector energy ordering | Find all blocks of a complete finite physical space |
| One hard constraint -> two separate penalty limits | Which transitions are removed | Local branch classification | Need to control finite penalties later | Exact projected generator | Zero-divergence wraps around one link |
| Global norm closeness -> local defect control | Notion of approximation | Constants can be independent of volume | Does not automatically control infrared physics | Local variational bounds | Bound energy of one charge-creating move |
| Positive amplitudes -> specific symmetry action | Content of the PF statement | Original gauge invariance is transparent | Integer charges remain diagonal labels | Correct symmetry/sector proof | Compare gauge permutations with divergence projectors |
| Finite carrier -> emergent conservation law | Where U1 appears | Exact hard-limit commutator can be checked | Finite-penalty stability remains | Effective dynamics | Retain every projected harmonic |
| Gaplessness -> photon spectral data | Strength of target | Avoids an inflated closure claim | Pole and linear dispersion need further proof | Infrared/spectral analysis | z=2 or critical counterexample to the implication |
| Law-level roles -> Record-carried roles | Source of orientation and periodic pattern | Translation covariance need not prohibit a realized pattern | Formation and selection are still open | Native compiler | Check law covariance and state transformation separately |

The first-principles simplification is to stop asking a representation map to
answer a state-selection question. A useful correction to that map can still
be a scientific milestone, provided the phase and native derivation remain
explicitly open.
