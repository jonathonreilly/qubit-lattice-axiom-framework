# Actual cube-birth energy spread and effective finite-spin balance

This review proposal keeps the supplied local compensation and original
formation instrument. At an actual resolved birth on the cube with integer
face flux n, the effective Hamiltonian `h=K D+delta H4` has output variance
`K² n⁴+392 delta²`. The all-mark initial energy derivative is
`kappa(3216 delta-96 K n²)` for both resolved and coherent instruments. The
conditional states of those instruments differ; the displayed variance belongs
to the specified resolved mark only. Exact finite-spin path weights show that
the order-n⁴ electric spread remains for `n=floor(xS)`, `0<x<1`, within the
finite-spin effective model.

The physical link field has not been erased at a birth. Occupancy changes which
edges contribute to the supplied active-edge electric operator D. The resulting
energy accounting therefore concerns both records and fields. It cannot be
replaced by a field-only postbirth assumption.
