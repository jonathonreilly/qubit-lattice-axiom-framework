# PRE development and failure record

The first mathematical route considered was a finite programmed spin clock.
Two tempting simplifications fail: equal-duration gates retain the clock's
nonlinear sin² physical-time map, and a program containing the original free
system evolution cannot simply have the same free Hamiltonian added again.
Both failures have explicit persistent-error controls; neither is hidden by
an endpoint-only transfer result. The inverse clock grid and the counterphase
in the final construction were specified before the new controls were run.

The three independently written controls ran successfully on their first
executions. No campaign model or autonomous author builder was imported. Exact
finite-space identities and direct single-Hamiltonian propagation are distinct
from the large-N ideal-battery clock/channel example. The latter does not
silently replace the actual finite battery in the theorem.

A further limitation was retained during proof review: every fixed finite
construction has initial reduced derivative A, not A+D, because its initial
mean clock interaction vanishes. Uniform one-time channel convergence does
not transfer derivative or microscopic instantaneous-power convergence.
The explicit 2T recurrence also excludes an all-time irreversible reading.

The supplied battery/collision packet remains a provisional mathematical
premise of this PRE. This task independently reconstructs the clock extension,
not the earlier packet's complete physics. Author comparison is intentionally
withheld until the PRE seal exists. Source snapshots are addressed by relative
aliases and exact hashes for portability.
