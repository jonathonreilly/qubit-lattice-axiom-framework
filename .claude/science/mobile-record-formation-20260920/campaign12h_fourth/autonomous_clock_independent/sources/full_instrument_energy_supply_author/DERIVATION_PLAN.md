# Personal plan: a finite supply for marked collision approximations

2026-09-24, before controls. This is a constructive route beyond the restricted
fixed-input fuel transfer. It is not an autonomous reservoir proposal.

1. Keep the complete physical sixteen-state star, lambda=0, original resolved
   or coherent j, H and GKLS rates. For the explicit commensurate joint sequence
   delta=K, epsilon² S(S+1)=1, derive the entire energy grid from the exact
   matrices: energies 0, omega C, omega(C+3), omega=delta C, C=S(S+1).
2. For a bounded finite-system Hamiltonian with integer energy labels 0..B,
   build a genuinely finite positive-energy battery and lift an arbitrary
   unitary on system plus zero-energy flags into each complete total-energy
   block. Use identity only on incomplete boundary blocks. Do not use a cyclic
   wraparound shift, a negative-energy reservoir, or a state-dependent unitary.
3. Choose a sine-profile battery state in a buffered interval. Prove a
   reference-stable channel error from the exact nearest-shift overlap. Track
   battery mean energy and its exact balance against system energy. Check
   whether the error can be controlled for a sequence with the SAME battery
   without assuming that the battery is reset or uncorrelated after each step.
4. Build the marked collision instrument with K0=sqrt(I-tau Gamma),
   Kj=sqrt(tau) Lj followed by the actual Hamiltonian evolution. Prove a
   reduced-channel finite-time approximation to the original GKLS generator.
   Keep discrete flag accuracy separate from continuous event-time claims.
5. Derive the order of limits and an energy-moment accuracy requirement; do not
   substitute a trace-norm claim for microscopic energy control. State resource,
   scheduling, locality, coupling-strength, flag-entropy and preparation costs.

The energy-translation lift is established machinery, e.g. Aberg arXiv:1304.1060
equation(1); its time-control caveat is explicit there. The finite battery,
buffer and error bound used here will be proved directly. No catalytic reuse
or intrinsic physical selection is claimed. Root authors the construction;
selective independent reconstruction must precede any confirmation language.

Personal extension before controls: a separate finite ladder for each distinct
positive system energy may remove the commensurability restriction. Label the
system energy blocks by vectors 0,e_1,...,e_r and conserve each label plus its
ladder coordinate. An initial product of sine profiles supported on 1..L lies
only in complete total-label blocks of a 0..L+1 battery cube. The shift for one
energy block changes only one coordinate, so a reference-stable bound may be
independent of r, although battery dimension is (L+2)^r and mean energy counts
all ladders. This must be proved and tested, not inferred from a cyclic shift.
If valid, it covers the electric family without selecting lambda or imposing
rational energy gaps. The earlier one-ladder commensurate route is optional.
