decompositions or weaker observational goals remain open alternatives.

are separate explicit premises, not consequences of the formation law.

remain supplied resources; no preparation-selection closure is claimed.

Hamiltonian norm, with an explicit precision sequence. Derivatives are excluded.
N5: The derivative mismatch is for these finite constructions and preparation;
it is not a universal autonomous no-go theorem.
N6: Reduced one-time dynamics is achieved conditionally; exact marked processes,
local autonomous baths and preparation mechanisms remain separate questions.
N7: A strongly engineered simulator does not identify nature's physical law.
N8: The two clock constructions and the one-birth star have distinct limits.
