# Scope challenges for the local-volume result

The positive result is a spatial limit of the supplied compensated target.
The negative boundaries concern the full bounded rotor algebra and a chosen
global representation. They do not prohibit continuous local physics.

1. **Alternative routes.** A smaller invariant regular algebra may have norm
   time continuity; it is not constructed here. Locally normal expectations
   already have time continuity. A different global representation can describe
   an evolved state that leaves a selected folium. These are alternatives,
   not eliminated routes to a physical theory.
2. **Independent premises.** Root time sampling and independent electric capping
   are distinct passages to the same bounded-locality machinery. The earlier
   compensated target and local-pair identity are shared checked premises,
   not independently derived again by these topology controls.
3. **Hidden assumptions.** Strongly commuting electric terms, bounded local
   remaining interactions, finite range and degree, normal local B(H) maps,
   receding compatible boundaries and locally normal initial states are explicit.
   Bounded-spin volume estimates alone would not establish this rotor result.
4. **Residual matching.** Exact geometry/rate controls test constants and scope.
   No numerical infinite-volume extrapolation, optimal signal speed, uniform
   microscopic error or field-moment estimate is claimed. Only two of the new
   overlap counts were separately reconstructed.
5. **Rhetoric.** Norm convergence in space is not norm continuity in time.
   The corrected representation sentence must accompany the original note.
   Norm-time failure is a precise topology distinction, not a physical failure
   of local normal-state evolution or evidence against record formation.
6. **Partial closure.** A UCP local evolution, preserved Gauss constraints and
   finite local formation intensities are obtained. Global density matrices,
   unbounded-observable moments, stationary phases and microscopic-limit
   exchange remain separate tasks.
7. **Steelman.** One might try a familiar bounded Lindblad locality theorem
   directly. The uncapped coefficients fail its norm-continuity premise;
   both proofs explicitly repair that step. A faithful locally normal
   representation leaves the Wilson-loop norm counterexample intact.
8. **History.** The earlier spin-half local theorem used a prepared code and
   a formation rate tending to zero. It is read for scope comparison only.
   The original-law field-escape results and the new compensation construction
   retain their different premises. Local normality is not quietly promoted
   to a global trace-class state or finite global clock.

This scoped scrutiny uses the already-read no-go discipline but assigns no
formal no-go/retained verdict. Original evidence, ambiguity and correction
remain preserved with the independent acknowledgment.
