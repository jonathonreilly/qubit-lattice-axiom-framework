# Energy selection changes whether actual formation survives

This conditional result keeps the original target Hamiltonian and explicitly
changes its first formation instrument. It compares supplied quantum models;
it does not derive a reservoir or native law. On the eight-site ring, an
expanding energy window selects the compact-field sector and allows two actual
formations with a full density limit. On the cube, a window narrow compared
with the fast energy scale suppresses the first formation at fixed coupling.
Geometry matters through the fast operator's point spectrum.

## Statement and mechanism

Set `eta=K S(S+1)` with fixed K>0 and fixed delta,kappa. On the physical N6
spin boxes embedded in a common rotor space, `H6,S/eta -> H2` strongly with
a uniform operator bound. If a fixed input has atomless H2 spectral measure,
its weight in every moving interval of width `o(eta)` vanishes, uniformly
over the center and over norm-compact input families. This is not uniform
over all unit vectors or unrelated spin-dependent states. No gap, absolute-
continuity density or numerical rate is required.

For the ring let F be the `H2=-4` flat projection. The prepared Hamiltonian
theorem extended to zero loss and both time signs gives

    g(H6,S+4 eta) -> g(h_F) F,  g in C0(R).

Here h_F is the checked selfadjoint electric/magnetic flat Hamiltonian. For
`R_S -> infinity`, `R_S/eta -> 0`, the sharp projector
`Pi_S=1_{|H6,S+4 eta|<=R_S}` tends strongly to F. Fixed sharp endpoints require
spectral continuity points; a fixed smooth filter generally gives a different
acceptance and a nonscalar loss.

The new first jump is `sqrt(kappa) Pi_S B_mu,S`, with its own Lindblad loss.
The Hamiltonian and second jump remain the original target ones. This changes
the generator; it does not describe normalized conditioned microscopic paths
or introduce a rejected-event detector. Exact limiting losses are `8 kappa I`
for the first stage and `4 kappa I` on F for the second. Starting in N4 with
any fixed normalizable field density and physical trace-norm approximants,
the full changed-target density converges uniformly on compact times. Its
limiting number probabilities are

    p4(t)=exp(-8 kappa t),
    p6(t)=2[exp(-4 kappa t)-exp(-8 kappa t)],
    p8(t)=[1-exp(-4 kappa t)]^2.

The cube's exact physical H2 has no point spectrum. Every moving subfast window
therefore makes the selected first jumps vanish strongly on the actual compact
source families. With their own loss, the full density stays asymptotically
in N4 under its checked electric/magnetic Hamiltonian, with counts `(1,0,0)`.
This does not assume an unprepared N6 state limit or solve the original
instrument's global second-count law.
