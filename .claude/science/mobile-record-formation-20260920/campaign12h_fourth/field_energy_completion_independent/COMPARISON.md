## Required terminal-sector qualification

The frozen note states at lines 142-144 that on a cyclic graph the lambda=0
fully occupied sector has infinitely many zero-energy circulation states.
Read this as follows:

    On a finite cyclic graph, whenever a Gauss-compatible fully occupied
    sector exists, adding arbitrary integer circulations gives infinitely
    many terminal rotor states with h_0=0.

Without the existence clause, a six-cycle is a counterexample. It has three
A vertices and three B vertices. Summing Gauss requires sum_x q_x=3. Six
occupied hard-core sites have q_x in {+1,-1}, whose sum is even; it cannot
equal 3. The new exact control independently enumerates all 64 full charge
words and finds zero compatible words. On disconnected graphs the condition
must hold componentwise. More generally, in this rotor model, full occupancy
requires an even number of B vertices in each connected component: the
number of minus charges is |B_component|/2. Conversely, that parity permits
a charge word of the required total, and integer Gauss fields can be solved
on a spanning tree; cycles then add arbitrary integer circulations. Existence
does not imply reachability under a particular formation history.

The root accepted this narrow correction during comparison and will prepare
a separate source-side scope correction before publication use. This file

## Independent derivation of the new energy formulas

Use u_n for the first resolved output with old hop 0->2 and v_n for the
output with old hop 0->4; these are the six-record words before the second
mark, not the terminal words a_n,b_n. Each has coefficient one. The own
primitive-path builder gives

    D(u_n)=0,                   E2(u_n)=4n^2+4n+2,
    D(v_n)=2n^2,                E2(v_n)=4n^2+2n+2.

Hence the two diagonal values are

    d_u=lambda(4n^2+4n+2),
    d_v=2(1-lambda)n^2+lambda(4n^2+2n+2).

The normalized first output is psi_n^(1)=(u_n+v_n)/sqrt(2), so

    mean(D_lambda)=(d_u+d_v)/2
                  =(1+3lambda)n^2+3lambda n+2lambda,
    Var(D_lambda)=(d_u-d_v)^2/4
                 =[(1-lambda)n^2-lambda n]^2.             (C1)

These identities are algebraic for every integer n and every supplied fixed
lambda in [0,1]. Their scope is the immediate marked output of Omega_n.
Propagation before a later first event generally changes the input density.
The output is not a two-point spectral distribution of the full h_lambda.

The own frozen canonical H4 implementation gives 47 nonzero words in
H4(u_0+v_0). It agrees word-for-word with the author certificate of SHA256
e2266dbcb0de8faddfcd265a6e7c3c995bb66cc9d9ce17f84826f360af676b39.

## Initialization and conditioning qualifications

The general bounded-compensation theorem and the common target both use
deterministic P initialization. The author's line 104 must retain that scope:
the initial spin densities are P-supported and converge in trace norm to a
rotor P density. Arbitrary physical W>0 densities are not silently included.
The added diagonal is bounded on finite spin boxes. Replacing those boxes by
uncut rotors at fixed finite C would produce an unbounded microscopic addition
outside the quoted bounded-compensation theorem.

The terminal Wilson formula is exact for the ideal normalized composition
B2 B1 Omega_n. The positive-probability experiment uses two ordered marks in
a finite window (0,t), propagates with the complete no-event generators before
and between the marks, and integrates the resulting output density. This
integral divided by its probability tends to the ideal composition as t goes
to zero; it is not generally equal at a fixed positive t. PRE_REPORT.md gives
the full integral and proves probability kappa^2 t^2+o(t^2).

For microscopic accessibility, first let spin tend to infinity at a fixed
positive window and fixed later terminal waiting time. The limiting event
has positive probability, so the finite-register density convergence also
permits normalization of that conditional output. Only afterward may the
window shrink. The O(epsilon) absolute density bound is not uniform control
of conditional states if the event probability is of order epsilon or smaller.
No convergence theorem for arbitrary exact-jump-time conditioned histories,
total-variation path measures, or a simultaneous vanishing-window limit is
added. The original resolved instrument is preserved throughout.
