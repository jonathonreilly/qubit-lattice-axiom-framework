# Scope stress test, not a universal no-go packet

The main deliverables are positive conditional constructions and inequalities.
The stationary-reservoir restriction keeps its conservation and compatible
readout hypotheses attached. It does not exclude coherent supplies or changed
readout resources. The independent PRE's complete N1-N8 checklist is retained.

N1: Coherent fuel, supplied drives, growing reservoirs, incompatible readout
and altered energy decompositions remain alternatives outside particular bounds.
N2: Energy positivity, exact conservation, moment reproduction, stationarity
and readout compatibility are separate hypotheses, not interchangeable walls.
N3: Coherence, pure flags, memory, spectral coupling and external scheduling
are named resources; zero flag energy does not make purity free.
N4: Density errors are multiplied by microscopic observable norms before
energy conclusions; no vanishing density error becomes an energy theorem.
N5: No universal reservoir impossibility, physical selection or TOE rhetoric.
N6: Fixed-input output, finite discrete instrument and continuous reduced
channel are distinct partial closures with separate proof obligations.
N7: The strongest objection is that designed resources encode the desired
law instead of deriving a natural local reservoir. That limitation is accepted.
N8: The original formation instrument and source identities stay fixed, and
stronger independent results are not retroactively assigned to frozen notes.

No five-route no-go or formal retained/landing PASS is manufactured here.
