# Scope scrutiny of capacity and terminal counts

This is an N1–N8 style stress test of the precise birth-only target result.
It does not claim a formal no-go packet PASS or a retained TOE obstruction.
The five-route submission rule for a formal no-go is not being represented
as met by relabeling variants of the same number invariant.

1. **Alternative routes (N1).** Record transport and Hamiltonian pair motion
   preserve total N, so they cannot refill global capacity; the explicit 6x6
   control shows they can reopen a *local* birth opportunity. A changed jump
   instrument still has +2 number grade in this proof only if that grade is
   checked. Reverse reactions, enlarging sites, boundary escape and a fuel
   reservoir are different models and remain open, not ruled out. Infinite
   spatial extent is addressed only by the translation-invariant mean balance.
2. **Wall relations (N2).** Source selection of compensation and microscopic
   volume exchange are distinct obligations. The latter depends on both a
   microscopic all-volume estimate and target construction; neither follows
   from finite capacity. Autonomous energy accounting is another obligation
   with unknown implications for either. These are named open dependencies,
   not asserted independent impossibility walls.
3. **Hidden premises (N3).** A always occupied, B hard capacity one, births
   add exactly two and never destroy records, local finite-range bounded
   non-electric terms, selfadjoint electric diagonal, and the precise two
   instrument conventions are explicit. The infinite claim adds a locally
   normal translation-invariant state and the checked target volume limit.
4. **Residual matching (N4).** The 6x6 coefficient checks reactivation from
   one instantly dark basis state. The seven-site matrix checks eventual
   *count* probabilities; it is no witness for an infinite lattice.
   Periodic dark states witness non-full stationary occupancy, not
   accessibility from the empty-B infinite preparation. No old audit wall
   or unrelated no-go is cited as a substitute.
5. **Rhetoric (N5).** State the finite graph event cap pathwise, the infinite
   bound for mean event intensity per vertex, and the exact seven-site count
   distribution separately. Pointwise infinite extinction, samplewise
   spatial density, global infinite count and full density convergence are
   not established. The finite path is not a no-formation theorem.
6. **Partial closure (N6).** Repeated formation after motion is exhibited;
   stationary vacancies and finite capacity coexist. The local electric
   moment candidate is separate and has not yet had an independent check.
   No new axiom is adopted and no convention change is called a physical
   energy source.
7. **Steelman (N7).** A viable TOE could make sites, unload records, or allow
   inhomogeneous escape, maintaining creation even though the present fixed
   birth-only graph saturates in mean. The current algebra does not exclude
   any of those mechanisms. A measured continuum connection remains a
   separate terminal obligation.
8. **History (N8).** Earlier original-law ring/cube work had unprepared
   electric escape and no global count limit. Local compensation changes the
   Hamiltonian, while energy selection changes the first instrument. The
   present capacity identity is read only in the compensated target and
   does not retroactively settle either earlier route.

The exact balance theorem rests on its algebra, not on the number of routes
listed here. This scrutiny is a scope record for a review PR, not a formal
negative-claim disposition or audit certificate.
