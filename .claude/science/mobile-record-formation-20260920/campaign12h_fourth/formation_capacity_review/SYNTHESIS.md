quantum rotor target. Each actual formation increases total record number

They exclude a permanently positive **time-averaged** homogeneous birth

infinite-volume rate decay or a universal saturation pattern.

initial birth rate is zero but magnetic motion produces a first-event

physical path reaches a stationary state with vacancies on one third of
first births. The new terminal-count argument closes the remaining count
probability on that path: exactly one formation with probability 1/3 and
exactly two with probability 2/3, for either actual instrument convention
and every allowed K, delta and positive kappa. It does not determine a
common waiting-time or final density matrix.
