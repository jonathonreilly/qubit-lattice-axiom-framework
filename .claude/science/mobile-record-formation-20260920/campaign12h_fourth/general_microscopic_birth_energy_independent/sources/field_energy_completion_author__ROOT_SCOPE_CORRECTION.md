The independent PRE and comparison establish the following precise reading.

1. The common density theorem uses **P-supported initial densities**. The
   finite-spin densities are P-supported and converge in trace norm to a rotor
   P density. The phrase “physical initial densities” in the frozen note does
   not add arbitrary W>0 initialization.

2. The cyclic terminal degeneracy statement presupposes **an existing
   Gauss-compatible fully occupied sector**. On a finite connected component,
   summing Gauss requires total charge |A|. Full occupation then requires
   `number of minus charges=|B|/2`, so |B| must be even. The six-cycle has
   |B|=3 and is a counterexample to unconditional terminal-sector existence.
   Conversely, in the rotor model that parity allows a charge assignment with
   the required sum, and a spanning tree solves its integer Gauss field. When
   such a sector exists and the graph has a cycle, arbitrary integer cycle
   circulations provide the claimed infinite terminal field family. On a
   disconnected graph the condition applies componentwise. Existence alone
   does not prove reachability. The two-mark cube construction supplies its
   own actual reachability and is unaffected by this correction.

3. The Wilson phase is exact for the ideal normalized two-jump composition.
   At a fixed positive event window, the conditional density includes the full
   propagation and integration over both event times; it need not equal that
   ideal pure state. The normalized finite-window state tends to the ideal
   composition as the window shrinks. To transfer the experiment from the
   microscopic family, first take S to infinity at a fixed positive window,
   then shrink the window. No arbitrary simultaneous t(S)->0 limit, or
   exact-jump-time path-measure theorem, is asserted.
