## Decisive premises and countercontrol

The electric diagonal strongly commutes with a one-link weight. Hard-core
occupancy forces the net magnetic pair shift of that link to at most one;
the old hop and marked birth use distinct edges, so each jump also shifts
at most one. Pairs anchored at the chosen link's A endpoint must be counted
through **all** its B neighbors, up to `z(z-1)`. The independent PRE's exact
six-vertex Gauss-compatible witness proves that replacing this by only
`z-1` anchors adjacent to the chosen B endpoint would miss a nonzero
matrix element. The root coefficient uses the safe count. The initial
shift-two concern was screened on complete path8, ring8, cube8 matter
sectors and a distinct path7 physical matrix, but the all-graph proof is
the hard-core path argument, not those finite scans.

For each bounded clipped weight the weighted Lindblad identity is exact;
the analytic generator inequality uses Fourier shift components and actual
coherent/resolved channel norms, then a bounded weak equation and
Gronwall. The one-sided cutoff needs an epsilon regularizer because it
approaches zero at one electric end; the direct absolute cutoff is already
boundedly invertible. Monotone convergence gives unbounded moments.
Finite-volume restrictions of an infinite Gauss state need not satisfy an
artificial boundary Gauss constraint, so the operator estimate is made on
the full effective local tensor before the checked observable-norm volume
passage. No global infinite density matrix is invoked.

The root controls returned zero: 3510 resolved columns over path8/ring8/
cube8, 130 nonzero ring circulation translations, an exact generic
weighted-dissipator identity, and 36 physical weighted-generator
eigenvalue checks on the distinct path7 PRE matrix. The independent PRE
has its own exact tree witness, path-matrix scan and finite weighted form.
Its external original stdout/stderr were copied byte-identically into the
publication unit after PRE sealing; their hashes still match PRE_SEAL.
Its numerical matrix check is corroboration, not proof. The result's
coefficient is conservative; a possible smaller coefficient from combining
the two methods is left as an optional observation in the comparison,
not adopted as a theorem here.

The bound keeps the field locally controlled at any fixed time in this
supplied target, including while actual additional pairs may form. It does
not choose the compensation or formation instrument, remove original-law
post-birth field escape, give a volume-uniform microscopic-to-target error,
prove uniform long-time activity decay, derive a reservoir, or identify a
photon/particle/continuum phase. No source landing or audit application
is performed by this review unit.
