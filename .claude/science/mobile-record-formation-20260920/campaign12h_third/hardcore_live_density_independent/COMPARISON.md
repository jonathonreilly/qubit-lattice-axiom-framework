## 1. Fourth-order ring coefficient and its premises

\[
H_2=-PTDTP,\qquad K_2=PTD^2TP,\qquad
H_4=-PTDTDT DTP-\tfrac12\{K_2,H_2\}.
\]

initial plus-record/ice sector, (M=dV/2) first hops are allowed. The folded

\[
H_{\rm eff}=
[-Mt^2/\Delta+M(2d-1)t^4/\Delta^3]I
-2t^4/\Delta^3\sum_pX_p+O_V(t^6/\Delta^5).

The author's six column records obey the exact disjoint-pair, return-path,

## 2. Live formation and the fixed-volume comparison

For one coherent sign, or for the two charge-resolved jumps taken together,
the local total loss is (\beta P_{\rm vac,e}). Applying both coherent signs
at their original strengths would double this loss; the sources and runners
treat these as alternative refinements, not as that doubled model. Equality
of loss does not make their full CP maps equal. The PRE physical-rectangle
countercontrol remains relevant and is not contradicted by the sources.

Every first hop makes precisely (2d-1) vacant nearest-neighbor edges. On the
first-excursion subspace, loss is (\beta(2d-1)I), so second-order elimination
gives the common complex shift

\[
-\frac{Mt^2}{\Delta-i\beta(2d-1)/2}.
\]

Its twice-negative-imaginary part is the stated leading probability-loss rate.
The source expressly does not identify finite-time survival with an exact
exponential or use this leading shift as the later-event law.

For the local square, an independent characteristic-polynomial expansion gives,
with (a=\Delta-i\beta/2),

\[
E_{\rm odd}=-2t^2/a+4t^4/a^3+O(t^6),\quad
E_{\rm even}=-2t^2/a+[4/a^3-4/(\Delta a^2)]t^4+O(t^6).
\]

These agree with the author. Numerical exponentials evaluate the complete
finite generator; they are not roundoff-free symbolic survival values.

The source's direct bound is valid independently of that perturbative rate.
Writing (v=|t|\|T\|<\Delta/2), the Sylvester equation between (QH_0Q)
and the low spectral cluster gives
\(\|P'-P\|\le v/(\Delta-v)\). Consequently bare-low-state closed leakage
is at most (q_*:=\min\{1,2v/(\Delta-v)\}) in norm at every time.
Since (\Gamma P=0) and (g=\|\Gamma\|), the contractive no-event
Duhamel equation gives

\[
\|Q\psi_c(s)\|\le q_*(1+gs/2),\quad
\mathbb P(T_{\rm first}\le T)
\le gq_*^2T(1+gT/2)^2.
\]

The trivial bound one can be imposed. The full dissipator on a closed pure
state has trace norm at most (g(q_*+q_*^2)); Lindblad contraction then gives
the stated global trace-norm comparison (gT(q_*+q_*^2)). Purification or
convexity supplies the mixed/reference-state extension. The argument permits
the stated number-preserving Hamiltonian in later number sectors.

At fixed (V,T,\beta,J=2t^4/\Delta^3>0), this supports the ring limit with
continuing finite-parameter formation enabled. Its probability on that fixed
window tends to zero. It supplies no volume-uniform field approximation and

## 3. The original density bound's sharper cancellation

The author uses an additional cancellation not needed for my looser PRE bound.
Let (K=H_A+N_B), (H_A) the number of A vacancies, (N_0=V/2), and

\[
H'=H-\Delta(N-N_0)/2=\Delta K/2+tT.
\]

Each hop A-to-B raises (K) by two, while a pair birth leaves (K) unchanged.
The initial fixed-number state and the covariant birth dissipator keep the
density block diagonal in (N); hence (H) and (H') generate the same
actual density evolution. This is an algebraic rotating-frame convenience,
not deletion of an apparatus energy cost.

For an oriented hop (b_e), the exact birth adjoint is
\(\mathcal D^*b_e=-\beta c_eb_e/2\), where (c_e) sums third-site vacancy
projectors on the other edges incident to its endpoints, and
\(0\le c_e\le4d-2\). The same-edge contribution vanishes. Hard-core
orthogonality makes this identity hold for each of the three refinements.
With (k=\langle K\rangle\) and (C_d=d(4d-2)), Cauchy-Schwarz gives

\[
|\langle T\rangle|\le2d\sqrt{Vk},\qquad
|\langle\mathcal D^*T\rangle|\le C_d\beta\sqrt{Vk}.
\]

The exact identities and the (H') subtraction were independently rechecked
in my complete 24-state physical rectangle with both birth branches present.
This is a check of local algebra, not a replacement for periodic geometry.

Put \(\epsilon=|t|/\Delta\), \(x=\sqrt{k/V}\). Integrating the energy
equation and taking the maximum of (x) over the interval yields

\[
\frac{\langle K(s)\rangle}{V}
\le\epsilon^2[4d+2C_d\beta s]^2.
\]

The division by the maximum is only needed if it is positive; the zero case is
immediate. On the reachable number support, (H_A\le K/2), so the total birth
rate satisfies (\langle\Gamma\rangle\le d\beta k). Its integral gives

\[
\frac{\mathbb E B(T)}V\le d\beta\epsilon^2
[16d^2T+8dC_d\beta T^2+\tfrac43 C_d^2\beta^2T^3].
\]

The coefficients were also checked symbolically. These are uniform finite-time
spatial-mean bounds and valid sharper replacements for my PRE constants. The
source's stated longer-window sufficient condition is conservative, not an
optimization claim. No high-probability or field-local conclusion follows solely
from either mean-density estimate.

## 4. Homogeneous field-star variant

Let (R=\frac12\sum_x(\operatorname{div}E_x)^2\), (M=N_-), and (M_A)
the number of negative records on A. In the selected Gauss sector,

\[
R=K/2+2M_A=H_A+M+2M_A,\qquad R\ge M,\ K/2.
\]

In the no-minus initial-number sector (R=N_B=H_A) exactly, explaining
the unchanged closed ring calculation. After a birth the extensions differ.
An A-plus/B-minus birth changes (R) by zero; an A-minus/B-plus birth changes
it by two. Thus the new exact local adjoint is
\(\mathcal D^*R=2\beta\sum_eV_{e,A-}^\dagger V_{e,A-}\).

At a vacant A site Gauss gives divergence (-1). Of its (2d) outward fields,
exactly (d-1) are positive and can supply the energy-raising A-minus branch.
This yields

\[
0\le\mathcal D^*R\le2\beta(d-1)H_A\le2\beta(d-1)R.
\]

This use of the spin-half Gauss constraint is important: a generic bound on
the number of incident edges alone would not produce the author's coefficient.
The remaining estimates are
\(|\langle T\rangle|\le2d\sqrt{2V\langle R\rangle}\) and
\(|\langle\mathcal D^*T\rangle|\le C_d\beta\sqrt{2V\langle R\rangle}\).
Energy comparison and one Young inequality give, for (u=\langle R\rangle/V),

\[
u(\tau)\le8d^2\epsilon^2+
4(d-1)\beta\int_0^\tau u+

The comparison solution (u\le\epsilon^2z_d^2) has
\(z_d'=2(d-1)\beta z_d+\sqrt2 C_d\beta\),
\(z_d(0)=2\sqrt2d\). For (d>1),

\[
z_d=\sqrt2\{[2d+C_d/(2(d-1))]e^{2(d-1)\beta\tau}
-C_d/(2(d-1))\};
\]

for (d=1), (z_1=2\sqrt2(1+\beta\tau)). Both the ODE and limit were
checked symbolically. At \(\epsilon=0\) the initial subspace is frozen, so no
division or non-Lipschitz comparison issue is needed. Since (M) counts formed
pairs, the bound controls their mean as stated.
