## 2. Quotient and time normalization

The notational translation to the blind report is

\[
 h=\sqrt{K/J}=g^2,\qquad \omega_0=\sqrt{KJ}=c/(2a).
\]

It reconciles the author's vector error `O(g^2)` with the independent `O(h)`
bound, without changing physical time. Fixing only a ratio of couplings would
not suffice: the blind single-square fixed-`K` phase countercontrol remains
valid, but does not contradict the author's explicit normalization.

The gauge-gradient space and the three constant link directions are exactly
`ker C`. The orthogonal projection onto their complement has rational entries,
so `2 pi P_S Z^(3V)` is discrete. It spans the coexact space and gives a compact
quotient. The quotient functions are precisely invariant under gauge and
constant-link translations. In the electric basis these conditions are
`BE=0` and zero total electric component in each direction. Discrete divergence
makes the latter equivalent to zero flux through each transverse plane.

Thus the author's removal of the three harmonic directions is a specified
electric sector, not a consequence of Gauss alone. Its dimension
`2(V-1)` and positive curl square agree with the independent incidence and
Fourier argument. A global classification of all compact minima is unnecessary
for the local cutoff packet theorem. The author correctly refrains from using
one as an unproved eigenstate-localization claim.

\[

\[
