## 3. Combined scientific scope

The frontmatter, introduction, part statements and closing qualifications
preserve the following distinctions already examined in the earlier work.

- The thirteen-state axis-loop alphabet, centered divergence, atomic
  collective moves, rates and fugacities are supplied choices. They are not
  consequences of the minimal axioms or a claim to satisfy every axiom.
  The introductory fifteen-state comparison is explicitly distinguished
  from this thirteen-state model.
- Atomic four-record translations and permutations preserve labels and
  capacity; loop births preserve the exact Gauss constraints. These facts
  alone give neither the nearest-neighbor forming-label property nor a
  collective-wave or quantum theorem.
- The reversible conservative ensemble is the Gauss-conditioned product.
  Winding/count sectors and possible reducibility are retained. Its
  stationarity does not establish that irreversible formation selects it.
- Leading fugacity coefficients are finite-volume coefficients. The
  separate polymer argument supplies the controlled uniform dilute domain;
  uniformity is not inferred from those coefficients or finite test counts.
  Branched balanced components and midpoint capacity remain in that proof.
- The general-observable covariance bound includes the corrected
  observable-amplitude factors. The vacant-box passage imposes Gauss
  constraints at every incident charge vertex, including exterior boundary
  vertices. It does not claim arbitrary-boundary uniqueness.
- The analytic quadratic infrared covariance belongs to that controlled
  equilibrium regime. It is not a selected extended-loop/Coulomb phase,
  a high-fugacity theorem, a mixing theorem, or a dynamical Maxwell limit.
- The explicit accessible-background locality counterexample is part of
  the combined claim, including its conditional probability comparison.
  A common change of the formation clock cannot repair differing normalized
  forming-label odds. The finite-footprint boundary observation is not
  expanded into a universal impossibility theorem for all generators.
- `bounded-support`, `conditional-support`, the explicit absence of an
  independent audit verdict, and the requirement of an independent audit
  before effective retained status are all present. No retention verdict is
  supplied by this assembly review.

Some original research-next-step prose is preserved as working-note context;
it does not supply additional theorems. In particular the Part A discussion
does not establish a limit theorem for its new collective dynamics. The
publication's current review status is now stated consistently at the Part B
opening and the corrected Part B closing review sentence.
