## Reconstructed load-bearing arguments

**Domain and birth kernel.** A pair carries a unique antipodal content class.
At a finite or countable set of existing classes, an absolutely continuous
spherical mark avoids every old class almost surely. Countably many such
births preserve the domain without a dynamic nonlocal rejection rule. This
does not prove an infinite-volume process exists; the note claims only the
conditional countable-history fact and uses a finite-volume process.

The spherical mean of n is zero, so each vacant-edge rate integrates to beta.
The density is at least `(1-|epsilon|)/(4 pi)>0`. Endpoint reversal gives
`f_{-d}(-n)=f_d(n)` and proper rotation covariance follows from preservation
of `n.d`. At a currently vacant x the instantaneous hazard is `beta r_x` and
the conditional density is the equally weighted mixture over its r_x vacant
neighbors, exactly Eq. (2). Its mean is
`epsilon sum_{d in D_x}d/(3 r_x)`. The r_x=0 convention is unused. This is
an event kernel conditional on the current pre-event state, not an eventual
first-event law conditional only on an earlier state.

**Moves and projection.** The slide `(a,b,c)` takes the old record at a to b
and the old record at b to c. It preserves both contents and the antipodal
pair, and `(c,b,a)` restores the exact marked state at the same rate. The
acceptance is a three-site test on the stipulated unique-partner domain.
Both one-corner square rotations preserve every record and partnership;
they have distinct marked targets but the same geometric target. Their
projected rate is `2 nu`. Inverse-channel pairing proves symmetry of the
birth-free geometric slide/flip generator and reversibility of uniform
matchings within each communicating component. It does not identify the
law selected by irreversible formation.

**General filling proof.** For any finite simple graph with perfect matching
P and nonfull matching M, an M-unmatched vertex starts an M/P alternating
path in their symmetric difference. That path cannot enter a cycle, and
its other endpoint is also unmatched in M because P has no unmatched
vertices. Write its vertices `v_0,...,v_{2 ell+1}`. The legal slides
`(v_{2j+2},v_{2j+1},v_{2j})` move the vacancy successively along the path;
the final two vacant vertices are adjacent and admit a birth. The sequence
has `ell+1<=|V|/2` events, preserves old keys, and increases the pair count.
The proof covers nonbipartite and disconnected graphs and reads no global
reference matching in the actual rates.

The matching projection is a finite-state Markov chain: birth, slide and
flip rates depend only on the geometry after integrating marks. Pair count
is monotone. Each communicating class has fixed count, and the path above
rules out every nonfull closed class. Consequently the full set is reached
almost surely, with finite mean hitting time at every fixed graph and
positive beta,kappa. A finite-chain uniformization gives a positive minimum
success probability over finitely many transient states, hence a geometric
tail in sufficiently long step blocks. Individual full states need not be
absorbing when nu>0; the full set is. No uniform-in-size rate follows.
