# No-Go Ledger

## NG-1: Epsilon Shortcut

Route:

```text
{epsilon, D_staggered}=0 -> epsilon is spacetime gamma5
```

Result: pruned.

Wall: `epsilon` is a site-parity grading on the lattice factor. It
anticommutes with odd hopping, but commutes with the one-site spatial
`Cl(3)` generators.

## NG-2: Factored Epsilon Tensor Onsite Matrix

Route:

```text
Gamma_chi = epsilon tensor B,  B in M_2(C)
```

Result: pruned.

Wall: anticommutation with all three spatial generators forces
`{B,sigma_i}=0` for all `i`, hence `B=0`.
