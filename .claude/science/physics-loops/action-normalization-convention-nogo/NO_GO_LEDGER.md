# No-Go Ledger

- Self-consistency convergence does not select `c`.
- Rescaling `(c, G) -> (c/a, a*G)` leaves `c*G` fixed.
- PPN gamma readout is independent of positive `c` under `Phi = c*f/2`.
- Massive-probe deflection is not a null-ray c-fixing channel.
- `c=1` and `c=2` are convention representatives.
