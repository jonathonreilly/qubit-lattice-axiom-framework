## Result, in one paragraph
Write each record as `(ρ cos φ, ρ sin φ, ζ)`. The correlation of the first two components is `Re⟨ρ_0ρ_x e^{i(φ_0 − φ_x)}⟩`; shifting every angle by `i a_y` leaves each period integral unchanged (the integrand is entire and periodic), multiplies the observable by `e^{a_x − a_0}` and each bond weight's modulus by at most `e^{β(cosh(a_y − a_z) − 1)}` (`cos(φ + ia) = cos φ cosh a − i sin φ sinh a` and `cos φ cosh a ≤ cos φ + cosh a − 1`), so `|⟨s_0^1s_x^1 + s_0^2s_x^2⟩| ≤ exp(a_x − a_0 + βΣ_b(cosh(a_y − a_z) − 1))` for any real `a`, and `|⟨s_0·s_x⟩| ≤ (3/2)` times the same by summing the three component pairs (P1). With `a_y = γ log((1+R)/(1+d(y)))` inside the disc of radius `R = d(x)` and zero outside, on any window containing the disc or on the torus: `|a_y − a_z| ≤ γ/(1 + min(d(y), d(z)))` on every bond, and the bond sum is at most `2γ²cosh γ(1 + 8H_R)` by sup-norm shells of at most `8j` sites (P2). Hence `|⟨s_0·s_x⟩| ≤ (3/2)e^{18βγ²cosh γ}(1+R)^{−(γ − 16βγ²cosh γ)}`, and with `γ = min(1, 5/(256β))` and `cosh 1 ≤ 8/5` (from `8/3 ≤ e ≤ 11/4`): `|⟨s_0·s_x⟩| ≤ (3/2)e^{9/16}(1 + d(x))^{−κ(β)}`, `κ(β) = 5/(512β)` for `β ≥ 5/256`, `κ(β) = 1 − 128β/5 ≥ 1/2` below, uniformly in the window, the exterior records and the side (P3). On the torus `M_N² ≤ 6e^{9/16}(1+L)^{−min(κ,1)} + 1/(4L²)` (a power of the side where block 20 had `(log L)^{−1/2}`); every infinite-volume static law on the plane, should one exist, has the same decay; and since the exponent depends on `β`, no Green-function-type kernel exists on planes at any coupling (P4).
