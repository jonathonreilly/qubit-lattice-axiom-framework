## Result, in one paragraph
In level time the three-dimensional formation law is a three-predecessor majority automaton with one-sided noise `ε(p,q,r) = max(d_1, d_2, d_3)`, strictly decreasing in `p`, dominating the coarse-graining "not `a`" (T0, re-proved). A one at `x` that is not itself a noise site has a winning pair of one-predecessors; following excuses along the three charges `M_k(z) = z_k − τ(z)/3` (each rising by `1/3` along a suitable predecessor; their sum is zero) and joining pieces at one level by forks between siblings, the refinement of spanned clusters — the cause graph of a cluster is connected (T1); the excuse identity `Σ_k M_k(Excuse_k(v_k)) = Span + 1` and fork pairs have size one (T2); the spanning lemma redistributes a span over a minimal cluster–fork tree without changing its total (T3) — produces a tree of arrows and forks through one-sites rooted at `x` whose span rises by at least one per refinement and equals the fork count at the end, so that with `n` noise nodes it has exactly `n − 1` forks and at most `3(n − 1)` arrows, every node has at most one arrow to a predecessor and the noise nodes are exactly the nodes with none (T4). Such trees lift injectively to admissible subtrees of the typed regular tree of the twelve edge types, whose weight sum with `t` per arrow and `s` per fork obeys a three-term recursion bounded by an exact rational super-solution: at `(t, s) = (91/1000, 1000/107653)` it is below `391/100` (T5), so `P(η_x = 1) ≤ (391/100)ε ≤ 3/10⁵` uniformly in `x` for every `ε ≤ ε_0 = 7/10⁶` (T6). Hence, whenever `ε(p, q, r) ≤ ε_0` — at `(p, 1, 2)` for `p ≥ 285718` — the formation law started from a constant plane keeps every later site within `3/10⁵` of that value, and its level automaton has at least six distinct translation-invariant invariant laws: an ordered phase (T7). The construction is executed on every noise configuration of the depth-2 backward cone, on every depth-3 configuration with at most four noise sites, on random cones of depth `3`–`10` and on line and triangle seeds: the tree property, the marks, the spanning identity at every refinement and the edge bound hold in every case (largest ratio `2.875`); the `66103` subtrees with at most four edges are counted against the recursion's coefficients.
