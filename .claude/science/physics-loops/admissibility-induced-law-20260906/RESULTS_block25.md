## Result, in one paragraph
In level time the three-dimensional formation law is a three-predecessor majority automaton with one-sided noise `ε(p,q,r) = max(d_1, d_2, d_3)`, strictly decreasing in `p`, dominating the coarse-graining "not `a`" (T0, re-proved). A one at `x` that is not itself a noise site has a winning pair of one-predecessors; following excuses along the three charges `M_k(z) = z_k − τ(z)/3` (each rising by `1/3` along a suitable predecessor; their sum is zero) and joining pieces at one level by forks between siblings, the refinement of spanned clusters — the cause graph of a cluster is connected (T1); the excuse identity `Σ_k M_k(Excuse_k(v_k)) = Span + 1` and fork pairs have size one (T2); the spanning lemma redistributes a span over a minimal cluster–fork tree without changing its total (T3) — produces a tree of arrows and forks through one-sites rooted at `x` whose span rises by at least one per refinement and equals the fork count at the end, so that with `n` noise nodes it has at most `4(n − 1)` edges (T4). Marked subtrees with `k` edges of the degree-12 graph number at most `2·96^k` (T5), so `P(η_x = 1) ≤ (192/95)ε/(1 − 96⁴ε)` uniformly in `x`, at most `1/42024960` for `ε ≤ ε_0 = 1/(2·96⁴)` (T6). Hence, whenever `ε(p, q, r) ≤ ε_0` — at `(p, 1, 2)` for `p ≥ 339738628` — the formation law started from a constant plane keeps every later site within `1/42024960` of that value, and its level automaton has at least six distinct translation-invariant invariant laws: an ordered phase (T7). The construction is executed on every noise configuration of the depth-2 backward cone, on every depth-3 configuration with at most four noise sites, on random cones of depth `3`–`10` and on line and triangle seeds: the tree property, the marks, the spanning identity at every refinement and the edge bound hold in every case (largest ratio `2.875`).
