| bilinear existence | bilinears span `M_4(C)` and host many Clifford bases | existence does not select a coframe |
| CAR-to-channel inference | two-mode CAR is invariant under orbital rotations and many single-particle symbols | CAR does not label normal/tangent modes |
| coefficient matching | selector fraction `p` is continuous and `c(p)=(2+2p)/12` | `p=1/2` needs a physical selector |
| uniqueness language | only four patterns and one affine NN ansatz were enumerated | no global uniqueness claim is available |

Do not retry these routes without a genuinely changed premise surface.
