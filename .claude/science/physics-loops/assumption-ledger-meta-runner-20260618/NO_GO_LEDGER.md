# No-Go Ledger

- The assumption ledger cannot be treated as a bounded theorem while it lacks
  one-hop retained-grade authority edges for every ingredient row.
- The physical `R_conn` / `K_EW` selector cannot be treated as derived by this
  ledger. The ledger remains a pointer to the exact `F_adj = 8/9` algebra and
  the conditional selector boundary.
- Runner registration does not certify any ingredient row.
