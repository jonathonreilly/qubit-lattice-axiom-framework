# Assumptions And Imports

- The Diamond sensor prediction note is high-priority and currently
  `unaudited`.
- The note's physical scope remains a bounded discriminator design, not a
  closed lab prediction.
- The ideal lock-in detector theorem remains a cited dependency and still
  owns the detector-map bridge.
- The prediction probe may assert only the toy-law properties it prints:
  zero delay gives zero phase, positive delay gives positive phase, and phase
  is monotone in frequency/delay for the toy delay law.

No new lab calibration, NV coupling map, source-field derivation, observed
value, fitted selector, or axiom is introduced.
