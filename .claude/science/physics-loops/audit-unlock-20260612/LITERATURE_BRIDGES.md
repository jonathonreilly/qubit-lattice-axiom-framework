# Literature Bridges

The dimension wrapper keeps literature as parallel context:

- J. Bertrand, 1873, for the broader all-bounded-orbits-closed theorem. The
  finite stable-circular-orbit edge is runner-checked natively and does not
  import the full theorem.
- H. Goldstein, `Classical Mechanics`, for textbook Bertrand context.
- F. R. Tangherlini, P. Ehrenfest, and Bures-Siegl for broader dimensional
  Coulomb and hydrogenic discussion.

No literature theorem is load-bearing in the 2026-06-12 Coulomb repair. The
load-bearing Coulomb companion is only the Green-kernel scaling lemma:

```text
Q_d[psi_lambda] = lambda^2 T - lambda^(d-2) U.
```
