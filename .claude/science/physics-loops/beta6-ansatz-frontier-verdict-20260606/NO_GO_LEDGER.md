# No-Go Ledger

- Single-ratio tadpole/geometric continuation is falsified by the current
  exact coefficient frontier. The leave-one-out checks miss every target
  `d_7..d_11`, and the `d_9` sign is wrong.
- Bare single-plaquette tadpole self-consistency still does not independently
  reach the beta=6 comparator.
- d-log-Padé is not a retained closure route at this depth: `d_9` fails, `d_10`
  is a one-order support hit, and `d_11` fails badly.
- Seven-coefficient Padé continuation has a broad spread; it is diagnostic,
  not a value theorem or bound.
