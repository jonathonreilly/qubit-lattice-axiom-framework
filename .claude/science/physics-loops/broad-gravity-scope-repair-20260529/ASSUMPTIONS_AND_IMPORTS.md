# Assumptions And Imports

- In-scope: supplied action `S=kF(path,phi)` and phase rate
  `omega=k(1-phi)`.
- In-scope: algebraic cancellation of nonzero `k` from stationary-path and
  phase-rate ratio equations.
- Out of scope: deriving the weak-field action from retained primitives.
- Out of scope: source readout, closure identity, continuum bridge, and
  null-geodesic bridge.
- No new axiom or gravitational observation is load-bearing.
