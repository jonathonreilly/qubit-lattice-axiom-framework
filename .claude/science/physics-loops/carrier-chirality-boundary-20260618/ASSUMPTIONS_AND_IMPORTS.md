# Assumptions And Imports

- No new axiom is introduced.
- The finite `SO(3)`/`SU(2)` cover-blindness calculation is computed directly
  in the runner.
- The scalar-kernel and displayed staggered `{eps,D}=0` checks are computed
  directly in the runner.
- Retained-bounded staggered/Kawamoto-Smit and Grassmann surfaces are treated as
  route context, not as a proof of physical matter-state-law closure.
- `CHIRALITY_GATE_IS_TWO_INDEPENDENT_GATES_DIRAC_VS_GENERATION_SCOPING_NOTE_2026-06-08`
  is used to prevent the old "same gate as generation/r=1/2" overclaim.
