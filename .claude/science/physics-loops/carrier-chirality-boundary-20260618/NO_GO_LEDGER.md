  `SU(2)` cover, so it does not force the per-site state law.
- The `Cl(3,0)->Cl(3,1)` extension acts on the abstract algebra, not on the
  per-site `C^2` state module.
- A spin-blind scalar kernel remains compatible with the trivial scalar lift.
- The old consolidation into generation/r=1/2 chirality is not valid under the
  retained two-gate scoping.
