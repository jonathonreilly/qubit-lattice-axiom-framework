# Assumptions And Imports

## Native Surface

- `Lattice`: finite incidence/combinatorial substrate.
- `Quantum`: finite operator and state language.
- `Record`: post-realization record append/tally, not a source of carrier
  chirality.

## Not Supplied By The Native Surface

- dynamics or transition rates;
- probability weights;
- chirality or CAR/statistics;
- source/action;
- canonical orientation section;
- readout weights or a physical species bridge.

## Imports Exposed By This Scout

- Chiral anticommutation `{D,gamma5}=0` is the filter that forces staggered
  `epsilon`.
- A canonical orientation section is the filter that would turn a hosted torsor
  into an active signed source.
- A kinetic/source operator is required before dynamic resolution is physics
  rather than finite bookkeeping.
- A holomorphic or signed readout must be selected by a theorem before it can
  carry Koide/generation weight.

## Literature Boundary

The enzyme and lattice papers cited in the exercise packet are analogy and
proof-template pointers only.  They are not load-bearing premises.
