# Literature Bridges

These are pointers for route design, not premises.

- Enantioconvergent biotransformation literature gives a clean vocabulary:
  kinetic resolution has a yield wall unless an asymmetric/inverting operation
  is available.
- Racemase/epimerase literature shows reversible flip mechanisms are physically
  real, but reversibility alone is not selection.
- Staggered lattice-fermion literature motivates explicit filters/interactions
  rather than hidden chirality assumptions.
- Lattice Dirac index/K-theory literature motivates eta/spectral-flow as a
  possible route for a native boundary filter.

Use rule: cite in parallel after the framework proof is done; do not import the
paper result as the proof.
