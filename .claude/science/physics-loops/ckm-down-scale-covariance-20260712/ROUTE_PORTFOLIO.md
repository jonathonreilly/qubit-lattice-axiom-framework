## Stuck fan-out synthesis

Five orthogonal frames were attacked: Casimir/group theory, projector
determinant, NNI/orientation, perturbative RG, and scale convention/RGI mass.
They agree on one constructive core and one exact obstruction. The determinant
explains how a `5/6` power can arise on a six-state rank split. It does not type
the operator or the CKM readout. RG covariance proves that scale transport
cannot improve a relative discrepancy when applied to both sides. The best
remaining positive attack is therefore not another scale calculation; it is
the pair of typed maps (4.1)-(4.2) named in the source note.
