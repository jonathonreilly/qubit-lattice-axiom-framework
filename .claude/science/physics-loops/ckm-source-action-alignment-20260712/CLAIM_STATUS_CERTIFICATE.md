## N7 — Hostile steelman

A hostile reviewer should first accuse the positive-volume construction of
circularity, not praise it as a derivation. Once `t` is placed with unit power
on a rank-three block and `R^(-1)` with unit power on a rank-five block,
`Gamma_vol=(3 log t-5 log R)^2/18` is exactly the target residual squared in
logarithmic coordinates. General powers `alpha,beta` produce any exponent
`5 beta/(3 alpha)`. The construction therefore has zero predictive selector
content until `W2-W5` are independently derived. Its durable value is narrower:
it provides a typed target-equivalent representation and an explicit physical
research program. This steelman defeats an overbroad closure claim, so the
artifact is deliberately bounded and the negative result is restricted to the
tested route classes.
