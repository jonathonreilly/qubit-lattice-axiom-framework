- The 3-dimensional symmetric-base SU(3) carrier supplied by
  `CL3_COLOR_AUTOMORPHISM_THEOREM.md`.
- The embedded Gell-Mann generators on that carrier.
- Finite-dimensional linear algebra on `End(C^3)`.

## Explicit Non-Inputs

- No physical SM-color identification.
- No quark/antiquark carrier bridge.
- No meson existence, confinement, or asymptotic-state theorem.
- No new axioms.
- No observed values or fitted parameters.
