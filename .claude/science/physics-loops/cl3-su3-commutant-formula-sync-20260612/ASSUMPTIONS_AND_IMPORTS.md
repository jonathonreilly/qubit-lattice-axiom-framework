# Assumptions And Imports

- No new axiom is introduced.
- No audit ledger, queue, verdict, or effective-status file is edited.
- The standard Gell-Mann generator convention is `T^a = lambda^a / 2`.
- Single-block normalization is `Tr_3[T^a T^b] = (1/2) delta_ab`.
- Full embedded normalization is `Tr_8[T^a_8D T^b_8D] = delta_ab` because
  `Tr[I_2] = 2` on the fiber.
- Structure constants are computed as
  `f_abc = (2/i) Tr([T^a,T^b]T^c) = (1/(4i)) Tr([lambda^a,lambda^b]lambda^c)`.
