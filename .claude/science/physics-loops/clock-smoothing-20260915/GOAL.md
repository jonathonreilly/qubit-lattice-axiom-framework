into a continuous magnetic flux carrier with an explicit positive electric
correction, without changing magnetic charges or subsequential macroscopic
flux limits. Prove a uniform real C3 local extension in a stated sufficient
range. Success is the normalized probability identity, a constructive
extension proof and an L2/H^-s coupling, with finite falsifiers of the
load-bearing calculations.

three-state phase, or an axiom update. The explicit smallness range needs
large but fixed N at large beta. It is not an N-to-infinity limit.
