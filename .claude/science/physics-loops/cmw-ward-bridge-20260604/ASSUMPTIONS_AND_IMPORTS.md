# Assumptions And Imports

## Framework-Native Inputs

- Finite periodic `Z^d` Fourier boxes.
- Lattice dispersion `E_k = 2 sum_mu(1 - cos k_mu)`.
- Finite Gibbs-state Bogoliubov inequality.
- Explicit W1-W4 Ward-normalized order/charge hypotheses with constants
  independent of volume.

## Parallel Literature

Mermin-Wagner, Hohenberg, Coleman, and Bogoliubov are cited only as historical
context in the existing bridge note. The new proof does not import their
theorems as authority.

## Open Boundary

This branch does not prove W1-W4 for every possible Hamiltonian with an
abstract continuous symmetry. Downstream broad claims need a separate
operator-construction theorem.
