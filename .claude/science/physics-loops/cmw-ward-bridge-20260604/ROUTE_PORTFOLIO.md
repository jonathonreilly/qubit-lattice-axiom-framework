# Route Portfolio

## Route A: Explicit W1-W4 Bridge

Prove the finite-volume implication from W1-W4 plus divergent `I_d` to
`m_L -> 0`. Implemented here.

## Route B: Fully Generic CMW Theorem

Prove W1-W4 from arbitrary continuous symmetry and local Hamiltonian
hypotheses. This is broader and remains open.

## Route C: Pure Narrowing

Demote the row to IR-sum support only. Rejected for this block because Route A
adds real bridge science without pretending to solve Route B.
