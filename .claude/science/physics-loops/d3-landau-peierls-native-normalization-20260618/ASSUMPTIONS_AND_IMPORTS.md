# Assumptions And Imports

- No new axioms are introduced.
- The Peierls link convention is the existing finite-torus convention in
  `scripts/frontier_d3_orbital_response_decomposition_2026_06_13.py`.
- Mathematical inputs are ordinary finite-dimensional algebra, the midpoint
  Euler-Maclaurin coefficient, and the single-band smooth-patch expansion.
- The Landau-Peierls name is kept as a literature label only; the source claim
  is the repo-native derivation of the normalization in this convention.
- Audit status remains external to this branch.
