## Stuck fan-out synthesis

Three independent read-only attacks were run in parallel.

1. The constructive GR attack found the throat identity
   `Lambda=lambda_1(S^3_L)` under already-supplied pure-vacuum Einstein data,
   but no sourcing or frozen-radius theorem.
2. The semantic attack constructed expansions of an identical foundation and
   intrinsic spectrum with different cosmological coefficients and histories.
3. The numerical attack found that the old graph correction worsens under
   refinement and supplied a rotational `S^3` stencil with coefficient
   `-1/12`, disproving the claimed universal `-1/4`.

All three agree that the missing object is a physical variational coupling and
radius selector, not more cosmological algebra.  The selected no-go route makes
the largest defensible claim-state movement without adding a new axiom.
