# Assumptions And Imports

## Explicit Inputs

- The current `frontier_dimension_selection.py` runner geometry and constants.
- The runner's analytic potential family `f_1=r`, `f_2=log(r)`,
  `f_d=r^{-(d-2)}` for `d>=3`.

## Removed Load-Bearing Import

- WKB/eikonal/Fermat sign reasoning is not used as the sign proof.

## Remaining Imports

- The all-d potential family is still an admitted runner specification and
  broader authority issue.
- Upper-bound Bertrand/Coulomb packets remain conditional.
