Scores are `claim movement / trace / import retirement / review leverage /
artifactability / hard-residual pressure`, each from 0 to 3.

| Route | Score | Result |

## Fan-out synthesis

All routes agree that the finite matrix identities are exact and reusable.
They disagree only on how much can be called canonical: Hilbert--Schmidt or
isospectral normalization gives a mathematically convenient representative,
but `c Tr` is equally invariant until a physical cross-block normalization is
supplied.  The selected countermodel route tests the broader missing theorem
directly and leaves every future bridge route open.
