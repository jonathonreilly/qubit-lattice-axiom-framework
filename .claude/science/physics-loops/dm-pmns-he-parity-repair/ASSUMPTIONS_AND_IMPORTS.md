# Assumptions And Imports

## Binding assumptions

- Supplied fixed-chart matrix `Y(delta)` with real `x_i,y_i`.
- Matrix algebra over `H_e(delta)=Y(delta)Y(delta)^dagger`.

## Explicitly not imported

- No reduced `N_e` surface authority.
- No seed averages, favored-column closure functional, eta normalization, or relative-action selector.
- No KKT branch classification or stationary-minimality theorem.
- No abundance readout, PMNS-column value law, observations, or fitted selectors.
- No new axiom.
