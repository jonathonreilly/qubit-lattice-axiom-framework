## Fan-out synthesis

### Record-additivity characterization

Record additivity is over finite disjoint record collections. It does not
supply a probability simplex, coarse-graining consistency, data processing,
or a source-to-record map. Characterization theorems for relative entropy
would require precisely those extra premises. This route cannot be positive on
the current base.

### Log-det effective action

The Legendre identity is valid mathematics once a positive matrix source and
log-det generator are supplied. The current axiom memo explicitly leaves
log-det readout and source/action structure outside the axioms. Even granting
the identity does not derive the physical instruction to minimize it or the
observational closure equality.

### Cubic-symmetry weighting

The `x` and `y` blocks are downstream PMNS source modalities, not primitive
spatial axes. No supplied cubic action exchanges them. The new runner makes
this obstruction falsifiable by constructing positive same-surface KL sources
whose minimizer flips under positive modality reweighting.

### Transport/normalization closure

The equality uses the observed target. A target-free premise set cannot choose
between two otherwise identical completions with different comparator values.
The new runner constructs this pair exactly.

### Conservative-completion theorem — selected

This route directly addresses entailment: keep one base model fixed and vary
only downstream selector interpretations. Distinct total, state-neutral,
single-valued laws over the same base prove the adopted law is not a consequence
of the base. It is selected because it closes the auditor's exact blocker
negatively without relying on unaudited sister claims.

### Convention definition

This preserves the existing conditional optimizer but cannot answer the
first-principles question. It remains a valid partial-closure path, not a
theorem route.
