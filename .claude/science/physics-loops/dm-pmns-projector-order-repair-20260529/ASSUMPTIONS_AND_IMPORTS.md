# Assumptions And Imports

## Minimal Surface

- Finite `3 x 3` positive-definite Hermitian matrices `H_nu` and `H_e`.
- Ordinary finite-dimensional spectral theorem for Hermitian matrices.
- For the intrinsic ordered-packet reading only: both spectra are simple and
  eigenvectors are labeled by ascending eigenvalue order.

## Not Used

- No DM transport helper.
- No physical `N1` column selection.
- No `eta/eta_obs` diagnostic.
- No PMNS value law or carrier derivation.
- No new repo-wide axiom.

## Remaining Boundary

Without the ascending-eigenvalue label convention, the pair determines only a
row/column permutation class of packets. Degenerate spectra remain explicitly
outside the intrinsic claim.
