# No-Go Ledger

## Nonzero Dynamics

The zero Hamiltonian is in the allowed class. Nonzero dynamics requires an
additional premise or selector.

## Couplings

Real linear combinations of allowed Hermitian terms remain allowed. Coupling
values are not selected by class membership.

## Truncation

Higher-range gauge-invariant-local terms are still class members. Lowest-order
truncation is a separate principle.

## Action Shape

Wilson, heat-kernel, Manton, or other finite-beta shapes require a selector
inside the allowed class.

## Dial Selection

The form-class predicate has no generation/Koide dial term.
