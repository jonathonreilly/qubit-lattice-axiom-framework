# Assumptions And Imports

## Current-Surface Inputs

- Finite eta is read as finite spectral asymmetry over a finite-dimensional
  self-adjoint operator.
- Spectral flow is read as net oriented zero crossing count for a path of
  finite self-adjoint operators.
- Gapped eta is locally constant.
- Closed paired chiral/bulk spectra have `+/-` pairing and therefore zero eta.
- The signed-gravity active source must be orientation-odd to realize
  `[+1,-1]`.

## Open Imports Exposed

- A path orientation is needed before spectral flow has a sign.
- A mass-sign or boundary-section convention is needed before eta sign selects
  a branch.
- A source-action cross term is needed before an eta label becomes an active
  variational source.

## Imports Not Added

- No continuum APS fixed-point theorem is imported.
- No boundary geometry, Wilson/domain-wall mass, gauge background, or new
  chirality axiom is admitted.
- No audit status is imported or modified.
