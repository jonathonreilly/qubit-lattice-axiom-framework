# Assumptions And Imports

## Context Dependency

- `self_consistency_forces_poisson_note` supplies retained-bounded context for
  why Poisson-type operators are useful on the tested surface.

## Stipulated Inputs

- A finite weighted graph Laplacian `L`.
- A supplied scalar source density `rho`.
- A supplied coupling `G_c`.
- A supplied screening parameter `mu^2`.
- The displayed quadratic action
  `S = 1/2 Phi^T(L+mu^2 I)Phi - G_c rho^T Phi`.

## Firewalls

- The action is not derived from axioms.
- The mass term and source coupling are not selected by this row.
- No uniqueness among all field operators is claimed.
