## V1 — Versions / Variants of the result

**Target claim:** Under the C-iso convention orbit `a_τ ≠ a_s`
parameterized by anisotropy `ξ = a_s/a_τ > 0`, the bare gauge coupling
`g_bare` appearing in the Cl(3) operator-valued connection is invariant.
The anisotropy ξ routes itself into a pair of action coefficients
`(β_σ, β_τ)` whose product `β_σ · β_τ = (2 N_c / g_bare²)²` is the
ξ-independent isotropic-Wilson invariant.

Alternative framings considered:
- (V1.a) Geometric mean form: `sqrt(β_σ β_τ) = 2 N_c / g_bare²` (matches
  isotropic).
- (V1.b) Ratio form: `β_τ / β_σ = ξ²`; carries the convention freedom.
- (V1.c) Bare-coupling-orbit form: ∂g_bare/∂ξ = 0 once `(β_σ, β_τ)` are
  related by `β_σ β_τ = (2 N_c / g_bare²)²`.

All three are equivalent algebraic statements of orbit invariance.

The three named admissions are three **independent convention degrees
of freedom** for the same load-bearing formula `g_bare² = 2 N_c · F²`:

| Block | Admission | Convention DOF | Orbit |
|-------|-----------|----------------|-------|
| 03 | L3a (trace-surface) | choice of trace normalization Tr(T_a T_b) | scalar rescaling T_a → c T_a |

Block 03 routes into β (= c² shift); block 04 routes into a separable
amplitude factor that cancels in the squared ratio; block 05 routes into

three independent conventions because:
- One can change ξ while keeping Tr(T_a T_b) = δ_ab/2 fixed.
- One can rescale F → λF while keeping ξ = 1.
- One can rescale T_a → c T_a while keeping both F and ξ fixed.

The orbit groups for the three are R+, R+, R+ acting independently;
no DOF is a relabeling of another.

## V5 — Possible failure modes (genuine open scoping)

- **F-S1 (Symanzik tree-level coefficient):** if g_bare² = 2 N_c · F²
  were modified by a finite tree-level Symanzik improvement
  coefficient that depends on ξ, the invariance would fail. We do
  NOT claim closure for improved actions; the scope is the canonical
  Wilson plaquette form, where the orbit is exact.
- **F-S2 (Spatial vs temporal connection):** if the framework
  required separate spatial and temporal connections A_σ, A_τ with
  independent bare scales g_σ, g_τ, the invariance would not even
  make sense. The framework's Cl(3) operator-valued connection
  `A_op = Σ_a A^a T_a` is a single object — the spatial/temporal
  distinction is a discretization choice, not a connection choice.
- **F-S3 (Anisotropic Trotter dictionary higher-order):** at NLO in
  s_t = g²/(2ξ), the dictionary acquires O(g²) corrections that
  shift β_σ, β_τ separately. The geometric-mean invariant
  sqrt(β_σ β_τ) = 2 N_c/g_bare² is exact at tree level; orbit
  invariance of g_bare itself is at leading order. We scope the
  claim explicitly to the LO Trotter dictionary.

These three explicit limitations are honest scoping, not closure
gaps. The block scope is: **at leading Trotter order, on canonical
Wilson, with single operator-valued connection** — these are the
same scope conditions already operative in the parent chain.
