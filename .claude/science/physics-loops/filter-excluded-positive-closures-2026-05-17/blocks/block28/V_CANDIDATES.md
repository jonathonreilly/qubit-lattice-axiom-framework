**Audit verdict (paraphrased):** L2 spatial clustering on canonical Cl(3)⊗Z^3

## Candidates (distinct from block 25 = OS reflection positivity Case-A)

### V1 — Spatial slab transfer-matrix existence (constructive lemma)
Construct, for each lattice direction `x ∈ {1,2,3}` of Z^3 and a finite block
Λ ⊂ Z^3, a positive Hermitian "slab transfer operator" `T_x` on the slab
Hilbert space `H_slab(x)` perpendicular to `x`, generated by partitioning the
Cl(3)-on-Z^3 Hamiltonian into intra-slab + inter-slab pieces. Stop at
existence of `T_x` and its spectral decomposition; no gap claim.

### V2 — Operator no-go: LR + Kubo are not sufficient (negative theorem)
Exhibit a finite-dim Hermitian H̃ on Cl(3) at one site such that:
(i) the LR bound (vacuously) holds (commutators outside the trivial light
cone are exactly zero), (ii) the Kubo identity holds, but (iii) static
β-thermal connected correlators do NOT decay because the spectrum is
degenerate (gapless). This rules out the bypass route "L1 alone implies L2".

### V3 — Spatial-direction conditional bridge mirror (closed-form spectral identity) [SELECTED]
Mirror the 2026-05-09 temporal bridge theorem (B) for the spatial direction:
conditional on (i) existence of a slab transfer matrix `T_x` along lattice
direction `x` with the OS support setup, and (ii) a spatial transfer-matrix
gap `Δ_x := -log(λ_1(T_x) / λ_max(T_x)) > 0`, prove the finite-block spatial
connected-correlator decay
    | ⟨A_p B_q⟩_0 - ⟨A_p⟩_0 ⟨B_q⟩_0 | ≤ ‖A‖‖B‖ · exp(-|p_x - q_x| · Δ_x)
and the finite-temperature analogue. Closed-form via spectral decomposition
+ Cauchy-Schwarz, exactly mirroring B.6-B.7 of the temporal bridge note.
Gap input remains explicitly open.

### V4 — Strong-coupling Δ_T derivation (full closure attempt)
Derive Δ_T > 0 perturbatively at infinite g_bare on the canonical Hamiltonian
via convergent character expansion. Too large for one block — defer.

### V5 — Spatial↔temporal gap equivalence (reduction theorem)
Show that on Z^4 (Wick-rotated A_min) the spatial and temporal transfer
matrix gaps are equal up to lattice spacing ratios under canonical
boundary conditions. Reduces the spatial gap question to the temporal gap
question. Requires Z^4 (not Z^3) — outside this row's scope.

## Selection: V3

Rationale: (a) V3 is the cheapest narrow positive theorem that directly
addresses repair-target (b) — a retained spatial cluster-decomposition
theorem with explicit constants (conditional on the spatial gap input,
parallel to how the temporal bridge is conditional on Δ_T). (b) V3
strictly extends the bridge note's coverage: temporal direction was
already conditionally closed; V3 adds spatial direction at the same
authority level. (c) V3 is closed-form spectral theory (same toolkit
as B.6-B.7), no new admissions. (d) V3 is distinct from block 25's RP
Case-A det(M_KS+mI)>0 subtheorem (different OS axiom; different proof
shape). (e) The gap input `Δ_x > 0` remains a named open input,
exactly as `Δ_T > 0` remains open for the temporal bridge — no hidden
admissions added.

V1 is a strictly weaker sub-step of V3 (V3 needs T_x existence as a
hypothesis but does not construct it). V3's hypothesis-set is
explicitly tagged. The full chain "T_x exists ∧ Δ_x > 0 ⇒ spatial L2"
becomes a closed-form conditional theorem.

V2 is also worth landing as a small no-go corollary; included as an
exhibit (E4-mirror) in the runner if time permits.
