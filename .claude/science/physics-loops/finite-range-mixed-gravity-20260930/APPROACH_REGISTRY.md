# Mathematical approaches

Five materially distinct constructions, their exact results and terminal
obligations are in the source's complete N1–N8 gate: finite canonical jets with
an affine matrix invariant; graded cochain contractions; endpoint gauge action;
equivariant pullback of a derivative ideal; canonical clock enlargement.
Radii, field parametrizations and different checkers do not add families.
Successful changed-carrier constructions count as actual attempted bypasses,
with explicit reasons they do not yet furnish the original target. They are
not five independent impossibility theorems. Reviewer interpretation is recorded
verbatim in REVIEW-v1.md; analytic validity does not depend on route count.
