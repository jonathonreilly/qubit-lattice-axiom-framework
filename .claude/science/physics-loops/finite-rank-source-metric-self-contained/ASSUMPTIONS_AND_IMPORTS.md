# Assumptions And Imports

No new physics axiom is introduced.

Load-bearing construction is local to
`scripts/frontier_finite_rank_source_to_metric_theorem.py`:

- fixed `15^3` Dirichlet lattice;
- seven-site finite-rank support and finite support matrix;
- Woodbury/Dyson compressed source solve;
- Schur Dirichlet-to-Neumann exterior shell matrix;
- radial harmonic projection and finite-difference residual diagnostic.

External Python dependencies are numerical libraries already used in the repo:
NumPy and SciPy.
