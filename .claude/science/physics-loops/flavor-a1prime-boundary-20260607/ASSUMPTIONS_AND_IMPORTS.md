# Assumptions And Imports

## Supplied Surface

- The real group-element basis `{e,g,g^2}` for `R[Z3]`.
- The normalized trace pairing `Tr(A^T B)/3`.
- The supplied support-count comparison between two real slots and one
  complex slot.
- Quoted mass, splitting, and angle comparator inputs.

## Proved In Packet

- The real group-element basis has Gram matrix `I`.
- `J-I=g+g^2` has coefficient vector `(0,1,1)` in that basis.
- The finite `C^3=I` carrier model contains the cube-root phase orbit checked
  by the runner, not a continuous U(1) phase.
- The signed Hermitian `Q` expression is independent of the Brannen phase.
- The quoted comparator values produce the displayed stress-test numbers.

## Remaining Imports
