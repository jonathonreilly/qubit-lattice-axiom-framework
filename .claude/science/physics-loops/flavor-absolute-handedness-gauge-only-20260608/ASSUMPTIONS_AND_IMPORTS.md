# Assumptions And Imports

## Load-Bearing

- Staggered axis ordering leaves full `S_3` unbroken on the relevant triplet surface.
- `sign(Delta)` transforms by the `S_3` sign representation.
- `+delta` and `-delta` are equal sorted mass multisets under an axis relabeling.

## Context Only

- `L_3(1,2)=2/9` is kept as an operator-side identity and invariant magnitude context.
- The number of generations remains separate context.
- The PDG comparison remains a non-load-bearing comparator.

## Not Imported

- No bridge is imported that turns `2/9` into a physical charged-lepton single-summand readout.
- No bridge is imported that turns inter-sector relative orientation into a CKM/PMNS CP or mixing observable.
- No new axiom, fitted selector, or observed target value is used.
