# Assumptions And Imports

## Native finite content used

- The cyclic triplet has eigenvalues `{1, omega, omega^2}`.
- The bare characters are `1`, `omega+omega^2=-1`, and `1+omega+omega^2=0`.
- The doublet determinant-denominator terms are
  `1/((omega^k-1)(omega^(2k)-1))=1/3` for `k=1,2`.
- `C_3`-equivariant Hermitian operators have circulant form
  `H=aI+bC+conj(b)C^2`.
- `Q(r)=1/3+(2/3)r` is used only as candidate-section arithmetic.

## Not imported as closure

- physical charged-lepton carrier / species-to-flavor identification;
- physical basepoint / zero-section selection;
- continuum APS eta authority;
- audit ledger status;
- a theorem that there are exactly two independent irreducible inputs.
