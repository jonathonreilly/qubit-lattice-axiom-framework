# No-Go Ledger

## Bare-character shortcut

Claim tested: `2/9` is a bare `C_3` finite-representation character.

Result: false. The available bare characters are `1`, `-1`, and `0`.

Consequence: any route retaining the value `2/9` must retain the doublet
determinant-denominator/resolvent content. It cannot discard that apparatus
and still keep the value.

## Equivariance-only basepoint selection

Claim tested: `C_3` equivariance selects `r=1/2`.

Result: false. `H=aI+bC+conj(b)C^2` commutes with `C` for freely varying `r`.
