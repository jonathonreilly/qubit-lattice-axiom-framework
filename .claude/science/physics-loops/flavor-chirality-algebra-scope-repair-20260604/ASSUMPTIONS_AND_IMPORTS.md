# Assumptions And Imports

Closed inside this packet:

- Two-site native qubit ladders commute across sites.
- Two-site Jordan-Wigner dressed ladders anticommute.
- Both generator families span the same ungraded `M_4(C)`.
- The second Jordan-Wigner ladder is not local to the second native qubit
  factor as an `I tensor B` operator.
- The supplied first-order Pauli symbol squares to the scalar
  `sum sin(k_mu)^2` symbol.
- The supplied nearest-neighbor `Z^3` hop anticommutes with bipartite parity.
- The graph Laplacian is A2-local and symmetric but not chiral.
