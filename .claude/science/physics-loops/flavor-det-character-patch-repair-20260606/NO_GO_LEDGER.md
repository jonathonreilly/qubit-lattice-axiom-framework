# No-Go Ledger

- Record additivity alone does not select determinant because trace is additive
  over direct sums.
- Finite site dimension two does not by itself choose fermion over hard-core
  boson; the cross-site statistics frame remains a separate gate.
- A general coupled KS/Dirac determinant does not factorize over arbitrary
  disjoint subsets when off-block couplings are present.
