# Assumptions And Imports

## Used Directly

- The explicit finite `C3` shift matrix in the runner.
- Standard finite matrix algebra for anti-Hermitian structure, projectors,
  involutions, commutators, and two-dimensional rotation determinants.

## Deliberately Not Used As Load-Bearing

- Any `det_C` or `det_R` mapping to `r` or `Q`.
- Any default physical readout convention.
- Any first-order action or Berezin bridge for the generation coefficient.

## Axiom Count

No new axiom is introduced. The repair narrows the source claim to the finite
algebraic no-go.
