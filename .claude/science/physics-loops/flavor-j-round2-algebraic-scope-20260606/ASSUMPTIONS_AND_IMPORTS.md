# Assumptions And Imports

## Used Directly

- The explicit finite `C3` shift matrix in the runner.
- Standard finite matrix algebra for Hermitian circulants, bilinear invariance,
  determinant scaling, and Gaussian/Pfaffian normalization checks.

## Deliberately Not Used As Load-Bearing

- P1 carrier premise.
- Round-1 `U(1)_b` measure-neutrality.
- Any `det_C` or `det_R` mapping to `r` or `Q`.
- Any Dirac-vs-Majorana physical assignment.

## Axiom Count

No new axiom is introduced. The repair narrows the claim boundary to what the
finite packet verifies.
