# No-Go Ledger

## NG-1: Record alone does not derive the instrument

Record applies after a readout context supplies the finite central-sector
decomposition and fixed `K`/CPT conjugation. This branch found no native step
that makes Record produce that context.

## NG-2: The K-odd phase is not the K-even measure selector

The runner verifies:

```text
Tr(I J) = 0
Tr(S J) = 0
```

So the K-odd phase channel is orthogonal to the K-even two-letter record
channel.

## NG-3: Weight maps do not collapse

Dimension/tracial weights give `r=1`, while block-count weights give `r=1/2`.
The finite algebra does not choose between those maps.
