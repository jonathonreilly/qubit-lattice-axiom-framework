# No-Go Ledger

## C3 Alone Selects Trace

Result: no-go within this finite packet.

Reason:

- `rho_(1:1)=1/2 P_s + 1/4 P_d` is PSD, trace one, and commutes with `C3`.
- The normalized trace has block masses `1:2`, but C3 covariance also admits
  the `1:1` state.
- Full `U(3)` conjugation distinguishes the two states and is therefore a
  stronger selector than C3.

Scope:

This no-go is conditional on the stipulated symmetry surface "C3 only". It does
not decide whether the full framework includes a stronger generation symmetry.
