# Assumptions And Imports

Allowed local premises:

- finite real `3x3` cyclic-shift representation of `C3`;
- central idempotents `e0=(I+C+C^2)/3` and `e1=I-e0`;
- finite-dimensional positivity, trace, commutator, and eigenvalue checks.

Retired/removed as load-bearing premises:

- the invalid claim that `e0 A e0 + e1 A e1` is center-valued for arbitrary
  `A`;
- the claim that this restricted packet derives the trace reference state;
- the claim that this restricted packet derives the Frobenius beta-family;
- the claim that this restricted packet derives the physical `Q(r)` readout.

Still open:

- a retained state-selection/readout/dynamics theorem selecting the equal
  central-block state as physical.
