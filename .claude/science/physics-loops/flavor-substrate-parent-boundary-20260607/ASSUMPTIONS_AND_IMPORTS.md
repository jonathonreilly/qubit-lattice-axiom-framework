# Assumptions And Imports

## Supplied Surface

- `M_2(C) tensor generation-C_3`.
- Coin-blind grading `I_2 tensor Gamma_chi`.
- `Z_2` spin-factor reading for the two-dimensional coin charge.

## Proved In Packet

- The parent ansatz splits into even value and odd chiral slots.
- The value side commutes with `Gamma_chi` and `C`.
- The chiral side anticommutes with `Gamma_chi`.
- `diag(1,omega)` has order 3 and determinant `omega`.
- `diag(1,omega)` is not one of the supplied `Z_2` spin-factor charges.

## Remaining Imports

- Native status of `diag(1,omega)` inside full complex `M_2(C)`.
- General classification of all possible parent operators.
