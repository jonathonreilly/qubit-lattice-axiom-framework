# Assumptions And Imports

No new axiom is introduced.

The packet assumes the restricted two-level real `C_3` circulant chamber

```text
H = a I + b(C + C^2),  a > 0,
lambda_s = a + 2b, lambda_d = a - b, r = b^2/a^2.
```

The formulas are finite algebra/source-packet checks. The following are not
derived in this branch:

- physical charged-lepton readout class;
- singular-value/Yukawa identification;
- block-count/reference-state selector;
- Fourier modulus selector `r = 1/2`;
- phase/sign chamber selection.
