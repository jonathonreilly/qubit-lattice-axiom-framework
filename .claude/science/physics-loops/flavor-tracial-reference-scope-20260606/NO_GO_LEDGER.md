# No-Go Ledger

## Tracial/Product/Modular Route Selects Q=2/3

Result: no-go on the assumed finite carrier/readout surface.

Reason:

- `rho=I3/3` gives central-block weights `1/3:2/3 = 1:2`.
- The equal-block state is positive, C3-invariant, and trace one, but it is
  non-tracial.
- The trace has trivial Tomita modular flow.
- Product trace leaves the same generation-block ratio.
- Tested positivity matrices admit both candidate weights.

Scope:

This no-go does not derive the carrier/readout and does not rule out a
non-tracial, chiral, finite-gap, or admitted block-measure selector.
