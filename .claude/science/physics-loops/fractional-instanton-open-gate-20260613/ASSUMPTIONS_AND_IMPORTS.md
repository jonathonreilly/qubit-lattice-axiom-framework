# Assumptions And Imports

Allowed imports:

- External twisted-`T^4` SU(N) fractional-instanton charge/action context.
- External dilute-gas schematic form as published gauge-theory context.

Forbidden hidden imports:

- Framework substrate or observable identification.
- Framework-native proof of the twisted-`T^4` sector.
- A determinant, measure, phase-space, or running-coupling prescription.
- Dilute-gas convergence or condensate closure.
- Any positive hierarchy bridge, including `alpha_LM^16`, `v/M_Pl`, or
  `alpha^N` closure.

The repair adds a downstream firewall and runner checks for these boundaries.
