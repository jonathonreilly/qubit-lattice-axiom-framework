# Route Portfolio

## Route A: Orthonormal eigenspinor bridge

Status: used.

The runner diagonalizes the Hermitian Dirac Hamiltonian and verifies that the
positive- and negative-energy columns are orthonormal. It then checks
`U_+ U_+^dag + U_- U_-^dag = I_4` as a finite projector resolution.

## Route B: Covariant spinor normalization with measure factor

Status: not used.

This would require rewriting the field expansion with covariant `2E` spinors
and the compensating `1/(2E)` weight. The branch explicitly does not claim that
route.
