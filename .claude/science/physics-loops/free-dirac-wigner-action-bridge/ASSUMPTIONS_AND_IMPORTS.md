# Assumptions And Imports

No new axiom, observed value, fitted selector, or retained-status claim is
introduced.

The bridge works at bounded free-continuum scope on the supplied one-particle
mass-shell carrier:

```text
H_1 = L^2(H_m^+, d^3p/(2E); C^2)
```

It cites the existing companion free-Dirac representation packet in parallel
for finite checks of the Poincare algebra, mass-shell preservation, invariant
measure, and SU(2) Wigner carrier. The new bridge supplies the missing direct
functional-analytic step: unitarity, semidirect product law, strong continuity
on the dense smooth carrier, density extension, and Stone consequence.

It does not derive Lorentz symmetry from the baseline lattice framework, spin
statistics, interacting fields, or any physical Standard Model closure.
