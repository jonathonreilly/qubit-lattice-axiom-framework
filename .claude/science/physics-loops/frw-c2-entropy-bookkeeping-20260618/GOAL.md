# Goal

Narrow the FRW C2 residual by proving the finite source-free entropy
bookkeeping:

- total comoving entropy is conserved exactly under finite updates with zero
  total source;
- nonzero source terms change total comoving entropy;
- `g_*S T^3 a^3 = const` is the exact step invariant;
- conserved charge over conserved entropy remains invariant.

The goal is not to prove the real cosmological era is source-free, not to
derive the Standard Model `g_*S(T)` table, and not to audit or retag the row.
