# No-Go Ledger

- The repair does not prove a unique global logarithm branch. The helper
  runner intentionally checks a center-sector link and a discrete co-root
  branch shift.
- The repair does not derive a continuum gauge field. It derives only the
  finite-link logarithm needed to express a link holonomy in the fixed
  generator basis.
- The repair does not derive Wilson action normalization, beta=6, or running
  coupling physics.
