# No-Go Ledger

## Not Attempted: Native SU(2) Tadpole Closure

The literature interval `u_0(SU(2)) in [0.96, 0.98]` is the remaining
load-bearing import. This branch does not attempt to derive it from the
framework because that requires a nonperturbative SU(2) lattice/tadpole
calculation or an accepted bounded admission policy.

## Not Claimed: Physical EW VEV Derivation

The scale bridge uses the hierarchy candidate map only to compute a
dimensionless scale log. It does not promote the hierarchy candidate readout
into a physical electroweak VEV theorem.

## Not Claimed: Full Continuum QFT From Cl(3)

The inverse-alpha bridge derives the integration step once the one-loop beta
coefficient convention is supplied. It does not derive continuum
renormalization theory from first principles.
