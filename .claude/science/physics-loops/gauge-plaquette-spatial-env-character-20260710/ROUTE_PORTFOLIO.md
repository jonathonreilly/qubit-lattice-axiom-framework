## Stuck fan-out synthesis

1. **Measure frame:** integrate Fourier modes before conditioning. This yields
   the exact deletion identity and avoids separate simulations for each `W`.
2. **Operator frame:** define the stripped residual and central kernel as two
   actions of the same unmarked-DOF integral, then use Schur orthogonality.
3. **Numerical frame:** sample the full positive Wilson measure and reweight by
   the inverse marked factor; use independent chains and beta-zero controls.
4. **Tensor frame:** keep original independent links but block by the marked
   stabilizer; do not tie orbit variables.
5. **Recoupling frame:** contract intertwiners rather than dense link-label
   factors; prior cost notes leave this route open.

The first three routes compose into one reviewable science block. The last two
remain exact fallback routes if statistical data cannot support a coefficient
certificate.
