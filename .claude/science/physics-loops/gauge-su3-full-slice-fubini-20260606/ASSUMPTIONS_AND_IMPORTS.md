# Assumptions And Imports

Retired or reduced:

- SU(2) toy as the load-bearing reason product factorization should hold.

Still open:

- The actual physical Wilson slab must supply a rim/far plaquette-support
  partition.
- The mixed-kernel compression bridge remains separate.
- No explicit `B_6(W)`, `eta_6(W)`, `K_6^env`, or `rho_(p,q)(6)` evaluation is
  derived here.

Mathematical inputs used:

- `SU(3)` is compact.
- Finite products of Haar probability measure factor as product measure.
- The Wilson plaquette action is continuous and bounded on finite products of
  compact group variables.
- Fubini/Tonelli applies to the positive bounded integrand.
