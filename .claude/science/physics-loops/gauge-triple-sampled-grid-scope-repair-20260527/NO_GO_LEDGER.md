# No-Go Ledger

## Empirical Lipschitz Continuous Box

Status: demoted from binding claim.

Finite sampled gradients with a safety factor do not certify a global
Lipschitz constant. That route remains useful scouting evidence, but it cannot
carry the formal continuous-box no-go.

## Sampled Grid

Status: preserved.

The existing dense runner exhaustively checks the finite sampled grid and
reports a strictly positive minimum gap.
