retirement, review closure, artifactability, creative leverage, hard-residual
pressure, minus overclaim risk.

## Stuck fan-out synthesis checkpoint

The frames are orthogonal: measure-theoretic factorization, exact finite
contraction, transfer/Perron construction, identifiability, and convergent word
expansion. The first two are selected because they directly distinguish a
derived environment from a relabeled coefficient packet. The geometry theorem
remains live because the parent note itself does not fix the finite spatial
complex whose environment coefficients it asks to compute.
