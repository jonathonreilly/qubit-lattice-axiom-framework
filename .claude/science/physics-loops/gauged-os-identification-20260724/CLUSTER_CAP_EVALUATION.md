## Evaluation

It is not a walk/Lieb-Robinson block: it contains no locality,
envelope, or LR content at all. It sits on the RP/Gram surface, and
its reopening basis is not self-generated — it comes from a
nine-surface reconnaissance in which two INDEPENDENT scouts, given
different briefs (continuum/OS reconstruction; gauged transfer
identification), converged on the same landed object with two
orthogonal, decidable extensions. That convergence is the basis.

The content is also not incremental: one part CORRECTS a landed
theorem's generalization clause and adds a necessary hypothesis that
the fixed-background lane never stated; the other part establishes a
positive result (PSD on the full algebra) together with a
representation-fatal obstruction that bounds what any OS
reconstruction on this carrier can deliver. Both are the kind of
boundary statement that prevents future work from shipping a false
sentence — the same category as the alias lemma that the sibling PR
landed after review.

Against: it could be split into two notes (gauged; full-field).
Answer: they share one subject — the extension boundaries of a single
landed theorem — and splitting would duplicate the entire hypothesis
and convention preamble. It is shipped as one note with two clearly
gated parts, mirroring the sibling's S1/S1b/S2/S3 structure.

Residual after this note (named, NOT commitments): the multi-mode
Gaussian kernel input; the fixed-background operator identification
itself (bounded here, not completed); the U-integrated measure;
interacting transfers; the continuum limit; and what the correct
