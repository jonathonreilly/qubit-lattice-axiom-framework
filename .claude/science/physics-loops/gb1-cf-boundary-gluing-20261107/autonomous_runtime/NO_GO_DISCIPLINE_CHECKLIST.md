# Scope discipline for explicit examples and unclosed routes

This is a positive model-existence/conditional-construction packet with narrow
counterexamples. It does not assert a general no-go, impossibility of physics,
or the necessity of changing an axiom. No negative-packet PASS is claimed.

## N1 — Alternative routes

Actual routes examined: (1) permanent phase ticks with fixed jump channels,
proved for a budget-dependent finite neighborhood; (2) a reversible/unrecorded
clock, tested against the explicit same-record/different-phase witness;
(3) a spatial conveyor for strictly short-range actuation, considered but not
proved; (4) the existing local probability-program route, read as prior art,
which reproduces cylinders without establishing this native gauge-register
implementation. The last two remain live alternatives, not ruled-out families.
There is no five-family exclusion certificate or retained-authority route
exhaustion. The schema's five-route requirement is therefore unmet. The claim
is narrowed to the displayed positive constructions and individual witnesses;
their mathematical validity does not depend on inventing a fifth route.

## N2 — Dependency relationships

No number of independent physical walls is asserted. A specified seed is
needed for the particular full-R reconstruction, and a chosen jump law supplies
both sequencing and formation in that model. These are not counted as two
independent mechanisms after the jump construction. A uniform short-range
realization of those jumps is a stronger implementation obligation, while
selecting their physical form is a different question. No equivalence or
non-implication theorem between those latter questions is asserted.

## N7 — Steelman

An opponent can correctly say that a physical framework need not uniquely
select every coupling or boundary condition to be scientifically productive.
The existence of two admissible laws does not establish that this framework
cannot constrain a useful family, support an empirically selected law, or
derive an important bridge. A local conveyor with actual record-triggered
channels could also improve the present implementation. These routes remain
open. They defeat any broad claim of futility, but do not identify a=1/4 with
a=3/4 for the fixed algebraic I readout or repair the specific omitted-data
examples without changing their stated conditions.
