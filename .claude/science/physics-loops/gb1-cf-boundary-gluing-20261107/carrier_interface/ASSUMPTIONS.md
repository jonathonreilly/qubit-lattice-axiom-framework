Pure codeword inputs and deterministic classifiers already establish the
capacity and optimal finite testing results. Arbitrary program mixtures are
used here as a sufficient construction of the full classical effect cube
and menu-refinement extension, not asserted to be the only mechanism.
Pair refinements use fixed M>=3; simultaneous copying also requires M>=K.
Timing readout is an additional interface choice and is not supplied here.
The code, alphabet and law parameters vary with K,M; the spatial layout and
radius stay fixed. The result does not claim one fixed device implements
every K simultaneously or that every lattice realization is classical.

The operational effects summarize selected output questions. Fine and program
records remain available; equality of a coarse response does not identify
complete histories or all future uses. No new physical axiom, primitive,
empirical prediction, universal no-go or minimal amendment is asserted.
