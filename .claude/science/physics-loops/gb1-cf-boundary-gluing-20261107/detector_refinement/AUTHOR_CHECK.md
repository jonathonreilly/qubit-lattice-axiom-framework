The checked mathematical chain is:

1. The typed header selects a proper spatial frame. All enabled sites on
   the isolated-prefix domain belong to its finite target set. Every such
   target sees the entire patch within radius 16; no unexamined outside
   event can occur. This is a proof for the declared domain, not a general
   finite simulator of arbitrary lattice configurations.
2. Exact predecessor recognition admits one race birth, then the fine
   writer, then the coarse writer. Center-excluded deletion tests give
   positive support to every permanent record, including recorded zero.
   Almost-sure termination and the mean time 1/N+2 use the supplied clocks.
3. The competing-exponential integral gives uniform winning slots. The
   independent oracle also uses slot symmetry and a global event graph,
   rather than importing the primary's local parser. All probabilities
   follow from the chosen activation/rate architecture.
4. Linear channel counts realize all 25 diagonal quarter-grid effects and
   all 225 ordered summable pairs. Every pair fits a three-outcome program.
   General refinements remain limited to at most four fine outcomes.
5. Squared channel counts preserve normalization and actual event grouping,
   but give 1/2 versus 1/3 across two programs with the same nominal matrix
   label. They refute that label identification, not ordinary additivity.
6. The actual four-preparation responses fail the proposed Pauli dictionary.
   A separate identity program perfectly distinguishes four selectors, so
   a density/single-POVM representation of that entire interface has d>=4.
   The detector reads a classical preparation label: this does not exclude
   a qubit with side information or a smaller, restricted quantum interface.
