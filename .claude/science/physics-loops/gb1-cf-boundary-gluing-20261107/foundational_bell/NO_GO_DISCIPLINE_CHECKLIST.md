## N7: strongest opposing case

The S=4 construction contains a controller with remote-setting dependence. A
physical theory could correctly restrict its Bell experiments to causally
separated events and exclude this mechanism. That would not rescue a claim
that the current words alone select quantum statistics: the physical causal
restriction and the joint probability mechanism must be exhibited. Conversely,
Bell factorization is an extra classical mechanism, so its failure cannot be
called a refutation of every record-only framework. Both qualifications are
part of the result, not proposed universal exclusions.
