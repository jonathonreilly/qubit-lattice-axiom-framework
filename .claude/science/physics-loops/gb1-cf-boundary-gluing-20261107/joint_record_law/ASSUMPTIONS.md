# Added assumptions: joint record law

None of the following is adopted as a framework axiom. They define conditional
models and competing ways to interpret their physical realization. The current
registry and all three approved primitive source notes were checked at unchanged
hashes. Units, kinetic-form isotropy and pointwise realized-state evaluation
do not supply any of these probability, composition or preparation bridges.

| ID | Added condition | What it supplies; what fails without it |
|---|---|---|
| O1 | Two identifiable wings, a common trial, local setting records and fixed binary readout | Defines the statistic; no experiment is named by the axioms. Output values are +/-I, never absence. |
| O2 | Positive occurrence of all setting pairs, choices independent of the preparation and relevant non-setting inputs, and no setting/outcome-dependent rejection | Gives one comparable experiment. The mathematical conditional kernel itself does not generate the choices. |
| O3 | Repeated fresh preparations, physical timing/calibration and justified statistical inference for a laboratory test | The theorem concerns exact probabilities for one trial; finite data are not exact probabilities. No erasure of old records is allowed. |
| Q1 | A composite M4 *-algebra with its positive cone, commuting local M2 embeddings, and the physical meaning of local effects and their tensor products | Supplies the operational composite; one-site M2 does not prove this. |
| Q2 | One normalized, nonnegative, context-independent probability function on ALL composite effects, additive on effect sums <=I | Forces trace-form probabilities by the checked finite Busch argument. This includes entangled effects and physical coarse-graining; it is substantially stronger than a normalized table on each measurement menu or no-signalling. |
| Q3 | A preparation with zero probability of the total-spin-one projector | Positivity then selects the singlet. This resource is not selected by geometry or covariance alone. I/4 is an explicit invariant alternative; Werner mixtures exhibit varying Bell strength. |
| Q4 | Each local axis n is implemented by the projectors (I+/-n.sigma)/2, with one common preparation for every choice | Turns the operator calculation into the stated record law. Fixed ideal directions select the maximum; calibration errors are not derived away. |
| Q5 | For a local quantum realization: tensor-local instruments/isometries, initialized local pointers, and irreversible content registration | Gives a no-message quantum circuit. Outcome effects alone do not determine an instrument or pointer permanence. This is an alternative implementation theorem, not proof of the atomic native record law's physical dynamics. |
| Q6 | For explicit preparation: product-state initialization, H/CNOT/Z gates in the common past, distribution before settings, and coherence preservation | Constructs the singlet in the supplied quantum circuit model. Native physical actuation and source-tag calibration remain open. This circuit is sufficient, not required by Q3. |
| R1 | Ten prepared matrix records, eight detector-arm records and a source-tag buddy pair; all other sites blank | Supplies a literal finite apparatus and meaning of its preparation tag. Its genesis is not supplied. Exact real-axis entries are ideal data; twelve occupied sites is not a finite-precision claim. |
| R2 | The explicitly specified total local F and the domain of all translated/rotated prepared patches and their positive terminal branches | Supplies local probabilities and legal-state domain. F is fixed and nonconstant; arbitrary external record mosaics are not declared legal apparatus states. |
| R3 | One indivisible two-site formation event with probabilities from Q1-Q4, followed by absorption | Gives the record-only atomic kernel. The two targets form together; no state with only one output is admitted. The distant joint coupling and occurrence choice are extra formation assumptions, even though each marginal is local. |
| R4 | A state-independent dictionary for +/-I output and metadata, and an intrinsic geometric parser | Preserves content-only readout, one record per site and spatial covariance. Physical role recognition/calibration remains supplied. |
| S1 | For the sequential test only: probabilities conditional on the full current record configuration must equal F of the unchanged local neighborhood; no remote setting/outcome has entered it; B forms next on both A branches without intervening updates or filtering | The displayed two-state conditional collision rules out that sequential extension. This hypothesis must not be silently replaced by conditioning only on a detector's accessible history. |

Dependencies: Q1-Q2 imply the trace representation; Q3 chooses the state;
Q4 chooses the measurement-to-record bridge. The upper quantum bound uses
Q1-Q2 and binary local effects with a common preparation, not Q3 or the
specific maximizing axes. Q3-Q4 give the displayed saturation. Q5-Q6 give a
standard quantum implementation conditional on physical quantum resources.
R1-R4 give a separate atomic lattice realization, with no new state variable.
S1 identifies the precise issue with identifying these two realizations.
These are alternatives and implications, not a count of independent walls.

The density matrix is determined by the declared preparation in the tested
family. A general record-to-density map for arbitrary physical preparations
is not proved. Reading a source tag does not certify its claimed laboratory
meaning. A mixture or hidden prestate under the same records would require
its own sufficiency argument.

The separate eighteen-record construction in INDEPENDENT.md includes locally
formed fair setting ports and an occupancy-based scheduler; it supplies a
mathematical instance of O2's independent choices under its own domain and F.
Its initial apparatus and physical scheduling remain conditions. That hand
proof is not the twelve-record diagnostic and was not numerically replayed.

No Bell-score coefficient, observed value or four-row probability table is
used to define the operator law. Nonetheless the quantum probability structure
itself is a substantive added assumption. The polynomial alternative in RESULT
preserves R1-R2, the atomic update pattern and no-signalling but fails Q2 with
the stated effects. It shows why the new assumption is doing physical work.

The conditional possibilities left open are an independently justified atomic
formation mechanism, a local quantum bridge with a careful meaning for
Admissibility, or a different law/state formulation. None is established by a
terminology change, and none is adopted here on the owner's behalf.
