### 1. The twelve-record construction is coherent on its declared domain

buddy `c+2e1` and, after the jump, the center's scalar outcome. The setting
records at `c+e2` and `c+2e2` behave correspondingly. Trigger and setting
arms are not nearest neighbors of one another. Every such old record stays
in the support of the empirical fallback, while each new outcome remains
supported by the unchanged detector neighborhood. The source pair has only
its same-content buddy. All inter-patch distances exclude an extra neighbor
for every integer `L>=6`. Thus neither the marginal calculation nor the
permanence check omits a neighboring record.

The geometric parser also survives the obvious false-hole test. The blank
site `c+e1+e2` has a trigger and a setting among its immediate neighbors, so
the local `F` can be fair there too; that does not select it for formation.
It lacks the two-step trigger and setting rays and therefore fails the
global apparatus parser. A reversed trigger ray from `c+3e1` has no
orthogonal matching setting ray. The only same-tag unit-separated pairs
are the two supplied arms in each patch; the separation excludes a mixed
pair from different patches. The source's distinct scalar tag cannot be
mistaken for a trigger or setting. This proves uniqueness on the supplied
domain; it does not claim a unique parser on arbitrary mosaics.

The total setting predicate does not demand Hermiticity: it tests trace zero

### 2. Positive-effect representation and singlet selection have the stated hypotheses

The effect proof supplies the needed distinctions: a single nonnegative
normalized function on all composite effects, context independence, and
operational coarse-graining by operator addition. Partial additivity yields
monotonicity and rational homogeneity; rational squeezing gives real
homogeneity. The scaled positive-cone extension is independent of scale,
and `A+B'=A'+B` makes the Hermitian extension independent of decomposition.
Finite trace duality and positivity on rank-one projectors then give a
unique positive trace-one matrix. No continuity premise or dimension-three
projective Gleason theorem is silently needed.

The normalized matrix-content measure counterexample is valid: the two
labeled effects `I/2,I/2` form a POVM even though the singleton content mass
at either is zero under `delta_I`. Ordinary measure normalization therefore
does not imply the extra effect calculus. Requiring all composite effects,
including entangled ones, also prevents importing an unjustified composite
conclusion from separate one-site effect functionals.

For preparation, expanding `sum_i J_i^2` gives
`(3I+sum_i sigma_i tensor sigma_i)/2`; therefore the stated triplet projector
is correct. Its complementary rank-one vector is the displayed singlet.
`Tr(rho Pi_triplet)=||Pi_triplet sqrt(rho)||_HS^2=0` forces support in that
one-dimensional complement, and normalization selects the singlet. The
maximally mixed state is a genuine rotationally invariant counterexample
to selection by covariance alone. The Werner mixtures preserve positivity
on the stated `0<=eta<=1` domain and scale every correlator by `eta`.

The preparation circuit is also correct in the stated order:

### 3. The local quantum circuit is a separate implementation theorem

For the stated isometry, orthogonal pointer labels and
`sum_a P_a(n)^2=I` give `V_n^dagger V_n=I`. The two-dimensional input
subspace of a system with an initialized two-state pointer can be extended
to an orthonormal basis, so a local unitary extension exists. The two wing
isometries depend only on their own settings and act on disjoint factors.
Their joint pointer probabilities equal the supplied product-effect Born
law. The projective branch maps commute, and summing the other wing's
trace-preserving instrument gives the claimed operational no-signalling
identity, also with passive references.

Neither an isometry nor its outcome effects alone give permanent classical
records. Q5 explicitly supplies irreversible registration, and Q6 supplies
the preparation and distribution before settings. No record is overwritten;
fresh trials require fresh sites and resources. The quantum circuit's
unrecorded carrier and pointer degrees of freedom are not included in the
twelve-record construction's resource count or proved to be supplied by

The universal quantum CHSH argument is valid: the squared-operator identity
uses dichotomic observables and commuting wings; its norm estimate then
holds in every positive normalized state. The block dilation of a Hermitian
contraction squares to identity because `A` commutes with
`sqrt(I-A^2)`. Separate local ancillas preserve cross-wing commutation and
the original correlators, so the stated binary-POVM extension is justified.

### 4. The polynomial alternative is a genuine matching-domain counterexample

For `h(t)=(3t-t^3)/2`, the derivative is nonnegative on `[-1,1]` and strictly
positive in the interior; endpoint values are `+/-1`. Thus `|h(t)|<1` in
the interior and the only zero joint probabilities occur at the same
endpoint/outcome combinations as for the singlet rule. The claim of the
same positive terminal support is correct, not merely a same-marginal
claim. Both marginals remain fair, and the dot-product dependence preserves
common rotations without selecting a four-row table.

At `t=1/sqrt(2)`, `h(t)=5/(4sqrt(2))`, and oddness gives the opposite
value at `-t`. The four CHSH terms therefore have magnitude
`4h(1/sqrt(2))=5/sqrt(2)>2sqrt(2)`. With the stated product-projector
identifications, a positive normalized context-independent effect extension
would contradict the proved operator bound. This correctly rules out that
extension and the specified local quantum implementation, not the atomic
record law or every imaginable nonclassical model.
