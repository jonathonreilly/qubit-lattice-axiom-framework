# Premises and physical decisions

Current axiom authority is unchanged main bf92a1d7. The local instrument below
is a conditional quantum model. AXIOM_PROPOSAL.md is an explicit proposed
foundation revision, not an already approved premise of these results.

| ID | Supplied condition | Exact role |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Tensor composition of finite native qubits, compatible positive normalized quantum states and trace/effect probabilities | Defines joint quantum predictions. One-site M2 alone does not supply it. |
| C2 | Linear completely positive instruments for fixed classical controls; trace-preserving nonselective sum | Gives physical operations compatible with entangled references, normalization and operational no-signalling outside the support. Positivity on one isolated qubit is weaker. |
| C3 | A chosen singlet preparation before independent settings, calibrated P_a(n), and preserved coherence | Selects the saturating example. Other input quantum states remain legal; the general cap does not require a singlet. |
| C4 | Local pointer code 00/01/10 on two native qubits, fixed record-content dictionary, one readout independent of the stored value, and registration | Identifies empty and +/- outcomes. Three native qubits per detector count system/pointers only, not all apparatus or source resources. |
| C5 | The explicit absorbing local Kraus maps and one realized outcome per formation | Makes permanent local records. Irreversibility and actual outcome selection are stochastic instrument premises, not derived from unitary premeasurement or decoherence. |
| C6 | Fixed q per activation, or local constant positive rate kappa with q(t)=exp(-kappa t) | Supplies occurrence. Constant total hazard excludes hidden outcome-dependent timing selection; the time parameter and physical rate are not derived. |
| C7 | Local instrument controls have no post-choice setting/outcome information from the other detector | Establishes the intended no-message implementation. A relayed outcome may encode a setting with shared data and therefore violate this condition. Final marginal no-signalling is not a sufficient control-flow check. |
| C8 | One trial with both outcomes eventually registered, or an outcome/setting-independent finite-deadline selection | Defines the compared CHSH statistic. Repeated trials require fresh systems, pointer sites and an explicit statistical protocol. |
| H1 | Known preparation and calibrated instrument history recoverable from permanent records, including relevant noncommuting order | Makes rho(R) a single predictive function on that prepared domain. No extra independent density-state coordinate is necessary there. Hidden instrument differences and unknown preparations invalidate it. |
| H2 | If H1 is not imposed, retain predictive quantum information beyond R | Represents same-record physically distinct preparations. One-site density matrices alone do not encode entanglement or all joint probabilities. |
| L1 | For the excluded sequential interpretation: full-current-record conditional odds are a function only of an unchanged remote neighborhood; both branches continue without filtering | Gives the explicit 0-versus-1 conditional collision. This is stronger than locally accessible conditioning or no-signalling. |
| L2 | For the classical Bell bound: complete common-past variable independent of settings; local response kernels with screened-off/independent local noise; no post-choice causal path or biased trial selection | Gives CHSH<=2 despite arbitrarily many pre-choice neighborhood relays. This is a classical causal assumption, not a consequence of no-signalling alone. |
| E1 | Conditional preparations obey the same positive linear local effect law across all measurement choices, with the declared Pauli interpretation | Excludes the polynomial counterexample and yields the displayed direct two-wing projector bound. This smaller condition alone is not a general quantum dynamics or composition axiom. |
| M1 | For the measurable-outcome extension only: finite-dimensional carrier, standard Borel outcome space, countably additive CP instrument with trace-preserving total, measurable content map | Gives an instrument-density conditional state almost everywhere by the finite Choi/Radon-Nikodym argument. It does not provide continuum-capacity physical memory. The fixed law must choose measurable versions and its admitted null-history domain. |

No density matrix is inferred merely from a source tag's spelling. H1 requires
a physically justified calibration/preparation relation and records sufficient
for the particular protocol. In the explicit one-shot model no-event evolution
on a legal empty sector is scalar, so it does not introduce an unrecorded phase
or waiting-age variable. More general Hamiltonians, rates or no-event filters
must be checked again; no arbitrary autonomous-process sufficiency is claimed.

The proposed general-state revision chooses H2 while admitting H1 as a
restriction. It changes the current statement that a state is only a record
configuration. Changing only that statement would not resolve L1 or select
quantum probabilities; composition, the probability law and conditioning
semantics also matter. Record's permanence and content-only readout need not
be weakened for the explicit model.
