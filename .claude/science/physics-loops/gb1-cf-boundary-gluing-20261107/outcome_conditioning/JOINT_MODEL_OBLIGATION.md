# Proof required before any axiom update

The owner requires proof that the change works before changing the axiom.
The governing sources are unchanged. The finite result establishes a local
quantum instrument with supplied preparation, controls, pointer dictionary,
readout and occurrence law. It is not yet a joint model of every proposed
axiom clause on one completely specified physical register.

## Exact next target

Exhibit one finite two-detector experiment on native Z3 sites, with two local
setting choices per detector, that satisfies all proposed clauses jointly and
attains the quantum CHSH value under the stated independent-setting protocol.
A calibrated quantum law and preparation may be explicitly selected as model
inputs: consistency does not require deriving a fundamental Hamiltonian or
creating a universe from no initial data. But every physical control, pointer,
marker, activation and readout resource used in the model must be allocated,
and the claimed state/control dependence must be verified, not encoded as
unread free metadata. The chosen laws must satisfy the stated no-privilege and
spatial covariance requirements with their context transformations explicit.

## Stop conditions and alternatives

A construction fails this target if an outcome/control is provided by a free
classical label with no compatible pointer, if a readable zero is used as the
blank state, if the law consults inaccessible remote data, if a prior record
can change, or if the joint conditional predictor loses instrument/order or
control correlations. A finite covariance check alone does not supply physical
calibration or prove a law on all reachable histories.

If a clause blocks the completion, retain the witness and revise the proposal
as a proposal. A prepared record-only state description remains an alternative
where a proven reconstruction works. The present evidence does not show that
all record ontologies fail, that this amendment is minimal or unique, or that
any quantum model is empirically the correct fundamental theory.

Research checks may support a later axiom decision only at their actual scope.
The source-consistency companion and vocabulary checks are not proof of this
joint-model target. No canonical axiom update, axiom PR, audit invocation or
retained-status change follows from the current milestone.
