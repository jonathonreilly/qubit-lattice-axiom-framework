# Scope stress test for the conditional boundaries

This is a positive conditional construction with finite counterexamples, not a
framework-wide no-go submission. negative_packet_pass is false. No five-route
exhaustion or universal need for one ontology is claimed. Current no-go and
conformance source identities are in source_hashes.json.

## N1: alternative routes

ATTEMPTED: a distant atomic pair law (prior packet), local absorbing quantum
instruments (this packet), a prepared record-only quantum reconstruction,
a general joint-state extension, and local conditional-effect consistency.
The local construction succeeds on its stated quantum premises. Prepared
record-only sufficiency also succeeds when its history/calibration hypotheses
hold. The smaller effect condition bounds the Bell experiment but does not
supply general dynamics. ATTEMPTED: classical local response screening with
arbitrary pre-choice relays; its standard Bell bound follows only with complete
factorization and independent settings. OPEN: alternative microscopic laws,
other state representations, causal structures and empirically tested models.

## N2: wall independence

No wall count is asserted. Full-history neighborhood screening and no-signalling
are different assumptions. Quantum positivity/composition/instruments are a
linked supplied structure, not three independent failures of the old framework.
Pointer capacity refers to a fixed decoder and allocated Hilbert dimension.

## N3: hidden premises

## N4: matched residuals

The 0/1 conditional collision applies while Bob is unformed and the full remote
outcome is conditioned upon. It does not apply to Bob's accessible-history
mixture or exclude quantum-local operations. The order witness fixes the future
test; the capacity witness fixes the decoder; the transpose witness includes
an entangled reference. Each counterexample is compared on its own domain.

## N5: resolution and coverage

Seven axes, 49 setting pairs and all local matrix units at the specified q are
exact diagnostics. All-axis/reference/continuous-time results rest on proofs.
No apparatus, infinite lattice process, reservoir or repeated-trial model is
numerically simulated. The six dynamic qubits do not count the entire apparatus.

## N6: partial closure

Independent local events and positive one-wing intermediate histories close
the earlier distant-atomic-event modeling gap under quantum assumptions.
The state can still be reconstructed from sufficient prepared records. The broader quantum foundation remains candidate text only. The owner
requires the complete joint-model proof before any axiom change; no logical
inevitability is deduced by ruling out every record ontology.

## N7: strongest challenge

The old neighborhood clause may have meant locally available conditioning;
under that interpretation the displayed contradiction does not apply. The
proposal states this distinction explicitly while adopting the quantum rule
that the old record constraints did not select. Its stochastic actualization
clause supplies a one-outcome law; it does not solve the unitary measurement
problem. Imperfect physical memory and finite apparatus resources remain
realization questions for the ideal permanent-record premise.

## N8: earlier echoes

The previous law already separated no-signalling marginals from causal
factorization and exhibited a superquantum polynomial control. It is preserved.
The new proof excludes that control through consistent quantum probabilities,
not through permanence alone. Prepared-history sufficiency was already known
on a supplied domain; the present result narrows its generalization rather than
rebranding it as a universal state theorem. Other frameworks remain live.
