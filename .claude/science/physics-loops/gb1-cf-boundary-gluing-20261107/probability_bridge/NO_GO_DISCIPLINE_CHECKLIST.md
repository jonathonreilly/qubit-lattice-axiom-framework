## N7 — Strongest challenge

The countermodels use special legal sectors, while the physically relevant
prepared sector might be singled out by additional derived structure. A
critic should demand an explicit native detector/refinement construction,
not accept this note as evidence that a quantum sector is impossible. The
terminal test is a proved event-to-effect map and probability factorization
across its different realizations, including the refinement in section 5.
Such a construction could close the physical bridge without changing the
axioms, though selecting its dynamics would still require justification.
This does not invalidate the exhibited law nonuniqueness; it limits what
that nonuniqueness says about a future physical sector.
