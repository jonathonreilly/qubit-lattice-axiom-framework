# Mathematical approach registry

| Family | Mechanism | Terminal obligation | Strength relative to full GB1-CF |
|---|---|---|---|
| Link-matrix construction | Sparse exact operator action and endpoint covariance | Explicit finite-patch operators and physical invariance | Strictly weaker |
| Haar/fusion reduction | Local singlet constraints and link integrations | Complete physical sector and independently derived coefficients | Strictly weaker |
| Patch joint update | Functional calculus of a finite symmetric Hamiltonian | Exact square-exchange covariance at finite angles | Strictly weaker |
| Native extension | Consistent local code and compatible overlapping update schedule | Homogeneous fixed-register cubic law satisfying the current axioms | Still open; not treated as a routine corollary |

No global route is classified as impossible by this probe. The fixed pair is
an easier model of the target's shared-link issue, not a renamed full proof.
