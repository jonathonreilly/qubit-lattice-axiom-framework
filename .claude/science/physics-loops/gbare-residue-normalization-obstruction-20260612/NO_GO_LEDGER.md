# No-Go Ledger

## D17 uniqueness plus canonical agreement does not pin g_bare

Claim pruned: D17 scalar-singlet uniqueness and canonical-surface agreement are enough to derive off-surface `g_bare=1`.

Reason: those facts do not fix the scalar residue-normalization multiplier `R(g_bare)`. The family `R(g_bare)=g_bare^2` has `R(1)=1`, keeps the same scalar-singlet direction, and makes the same-direction representation match Rep A for every positive `g_bare`.

Scope: this does not rule out a future retained proof of `R(g_bare)=1`; it only rules out deriving `g_bare=1` from the current packet without that bridge.
