# No-Go Ledger

- Generation/species-label bijection cannot determine `N_F`; permutation
  conjugacy preserves `Tr(T_a T_b)`.
- Physical-color naming is not needed for this trace normalization and cannot
  by itself derive the finite scale.
- Tracing the same gauge generators on the full matter tensor `V_3 x C^2`
  doubles the trace, so the target surface must stay the graph-first gauge
  trace on `V_3` unless a later quotient theorem is supplied.
