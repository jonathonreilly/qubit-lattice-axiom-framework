## Selected Route: Root SU(2) Scale Transport

matrix-unit generators:

```text
J_x = (E_ij + E_ji)/2
J_y = (-i E_ij + i E_ji)/2
J_z = (E_ii - E_jj)/2
```

This route proves the active two-plane is exactly Pauli/2, the `V_3` trace
Gram is `(1/2) delta_ab`, and positive rescaling breaks the unit bracket
unless the scale is unchanged.
