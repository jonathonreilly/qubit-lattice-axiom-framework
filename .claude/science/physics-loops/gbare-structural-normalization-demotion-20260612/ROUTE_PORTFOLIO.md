# Route Portfolio

## Route A: Positive `g = 1` theorem

Attempted conceptually and rejected for this PR. Fixed generator normalization
does not fix a separate Wilson action coefficient. A positive theorem would
need a new retained derivation of the Wilson action coefficient or physical
connection normalization.

## Route B: Honest demotion/source-boundary repair

Chosen. Keep the exact structural support and expose the residual multiplier
family `rho = g^2`, with `beta = 2 N_c / rho`. This directly repairs the
renaming overclaim while preserving usable science.

## Route C: Action-family robustness

Left open. A future theorem could show `rho = 1` across Wilson/Symanzik/native
action families, but this PR does not claim that.
