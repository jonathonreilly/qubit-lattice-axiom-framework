# No-Go Ledger

## No-go: Record alone derives gamma=0

Record supplies finite readout over supplied sectors. It does not supply a
prior, a Born rule, or an entropy arrow.

## No-go: objectivity derives equal record-letter weights

Existing objectivity no-go says objective records fix the alphabet, not the
weights.

## No-go: tracial/Born route gives Q=2/3

The tracial state reads the rank-1/rank-2 blocks by dimension, giving
`gamma=1`, `s=1`, `Q=1`.

## No-go: dimension/Born endpoint is inconsistent

The theorem classifies `gamma=1` as a valid stable supplied arrow. It is not
refuted; it is the comparator endpoint.

## No-go: stability implies physical selection

Both `gamma=0` and `gamma=1` are stable for their supplied priors. The
physical prior remains a separate gate.
