# No-Go Ledger

## No-go: three complex character letters are native Record letters

`P+` and `P-` are exchanged by K/CPT. Record names the K/CPT orbit, so the
faithful pair is one doublet record sector unless a K-breaking orientation is
imported.

## No-go: faithful doublet has a proper K-stable central sub-idempotent

Exact enumeration of real central idempotents gives only `0,I,P0,P1`.
Therefore `P1` has no nonzero proper K-stable central sub-idempotent.

## No-go: K-real C3-invariant readout can split the doublet

Every K-real C3-invariant Hermitian observable has form
`alpha I + beta(C+C^2)` and assigns the same eigenvalue to both faithful
characters.

## No-go: partition selector determines weights or dynamics

The theorem selects the native central partition only. Block-count versus
dimension weighting, Born probability, and the physical arrow remain separate
gates.
