# No-Go Ledger

| Route | Why it fails | Reusable lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Dim=2 forces Grassmann/CAR | Hard-core boson also has per-site dimension 2. | Preserve the statistics-agnostic no-go. |
| Ungraded algebra forces CAR | Hard-core and JW/CAR generate the same matrix algebra. | The selector must be functional/graded, not ungraded. |
| Parity F alone supplies GL(F) | Both frames are F-odd and bilinears are F-even. | Parity is a carrier, not a selector. |
| Identification bridge fully closes the matter surface | The matter-functional clause I-4 is not discharged. | Keep I-4 explicit. |
