# Assumptions And Imports

## Runner Surface

- Finite `20 x 20 x 20` ordered cubic lattice.
- Poisson field from a point source.
- Path action variants `S=L(1-f)` and imposed `S=L(1-f-f^2/2)`.
- Hand-implemented retarded source sampling by earlier layer index.

## Removed From The Binding Claim

- Physical gravitational waves.
- Physical post-Newtonian observable.
- Derived retarded-potential law.
- Derived `c_lattice` or physical time normalization.
- Derived `f^2` action coefficient.

## Still Open

A physical bridge would need to construct the dynamical carrier, readout map,
retarded law, and action coefficient from retained primitives.
