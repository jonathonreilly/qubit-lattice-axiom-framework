```text
H_s = H_0 + s I
lambda_axis(k_s) + s = E
n(s) = k_s/k0 = 1 - c_E s + O(s^2)
c_E = 1/(k0 lambda_axis'(k0))
phi_action = c_E s
```

This directly supplies the sign and normalization required by the existing
fixed-energy eikonal bridge.
