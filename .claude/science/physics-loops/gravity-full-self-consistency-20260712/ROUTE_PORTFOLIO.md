# Route Portfolio

| Route | Attack frame | Upgrade potential | Hard-residual pressure | Result |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Direct inverse theorem | derive the Green-map equality rather than consume it | 1 | 2 | inversion begins only after equality of full inverse graphs is supplied |
| Weak-field/Kubo response | derive the field kernel from first-order response | 2 | 3 | response is `L^{-1}` after `L` and source coupling are supplied; identifying it with `H^{-1}` remains the bridge |
| Variational action | derive field equation from stationarity | 2 | 3 | action variation returns the chosen quadratic kernel; action selection remains load-bearing |
| Ward/symmetry | use translations, cubic rotations, self-adjointness, and `Cl(3)` covariance | 2 | 3 | fixed-`H` fields `cH` and `H(I+H)` retain every named symmetry |
| Fixed point/backreaction | obtain the kernel from a self-consistent matter-field loop | 2 | 3 | the update law already installs the kernel; convergence cannot derive it |
| Record/readout | use fixed additive records to select the source/field response | 1 | 2 | Record supplies neither action/source identification nor a field-operator selector |
| Static-sector restriction | identify field and propagator kernels after setting frequency to zero | 2 | 3 | restriction does not equate independently supplied kernels |
| Exact countermodel | hold `H,G_0` fixed and vary only `L` with the same operator domain/range | 3 | 3 | directly closes the named positive route negatively |

## Fan-out synthesis

All positive frames agree on the same residual: a field kernel or action must
be selected before response, variation, or fixed-point algebra begins. The
countermodel route is decisive because it fixes the audited massless
propagator and constructs translation/cubic/Clifford-compatible field
operators with different inverse maps.
