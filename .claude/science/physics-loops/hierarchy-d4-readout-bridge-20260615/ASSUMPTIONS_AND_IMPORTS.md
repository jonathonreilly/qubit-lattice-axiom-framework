# Assumptions And Imports

## Native/source inputs

- The endpoint coefficient formulas already recorded in
  `HIERARCHY_EFFECTIVE_POTENTIAL_ENDPOINT_NOTE.md`:
  `A_2 = 1/(8 u_0^2)`, `A_4 = 1/(7 u_0^2)`, and
  `A_inf = 1/(4 sqrt(3) u_0^2)`.
- Four-dimensional effective-potential density has mass dimension four.
- The readout scale `v` has mass dimension one.

## Bounded bridge premise

- A common dimension-four density scale is compared across endpoints:
  `rho_* = A(L_t) v(L_t)^4`.

## Explicitly not used

- Observed electroweak VEV.
- Planck scale.
- `alpha_LM` or other coupling values.
- Fitted selector.
- New axiom.
- Audit verdict or ledger update.
