# Assumptions And Imports

## Binding assumptions

- Formal scalar radial coordinate `r >= 0`.
- Quartic potential `V(r) = (1/2)m2 r^2 + (1/4)lambda r^4`.
- Stability premise `lambda > 0`.

## Explicitly not imported

- No observed Higgs mass or electroweak scale.
- No PDG values, threshold corrections, fitted selectors, or SM running inputs.
- No Cl(3)/Z^3 scalar-order-parameter derivation.
- No Coleman-Weinberg substrate derivation.
- No new axiom.
