# No-Go Ledger

## A2 direct shortcut

Status: no-go over supplied dimensionless inputs.

`g_bare = 1`, `beta = 6`, plaquette/u0, APBC, and `c_cell = 1/4` are
dimensionless. They do not select a dimensional `kappa` because common
rescaling of `S_dim` and `kappa` leaves the phase unchanged.

## Finite-rank canonical commutator

Status: no-go on the rank-four block.

Finite matrices satisfy `Tr([X,P]) = 0`, while a nonzero canonical action
commutator would have nonzero trace on the rank-four block.
