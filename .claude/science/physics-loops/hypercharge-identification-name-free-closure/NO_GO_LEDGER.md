# No-Go Memory

- An absolute numerical U(1) generator normalization cannot be selected by
  the commutant line alone: common reciprocal rescaling
  `(Y,Y_H,g_Y) -> (cY,cY_H,g_Y/c)` preserves both the covariant-derivative
  products and `Q=T_3+Y/(2Y_H)`.
- Therefore an honest physical readout must distinguish the invariant ratio
  `alpha/Y_H` from the conventional coordinate choice `Y_H=+1`.
- The commutant decomposition does not select a unique physical SM species or
  the lower-singlet relative charge; both remain explicit interpretation or
  readout inputs.
- This boundary does not obstruct the scale-free ratio or the bounded
  conventional SM table.
