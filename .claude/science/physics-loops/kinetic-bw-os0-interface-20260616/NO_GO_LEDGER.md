# No-Go Ledger

- No automatic B-W route: `|d omega/dk|=1` for the strict unitary tick does not
  determine the positive Euclidean transfer exponent. The family
  `E_E(k)=r |omega(k)|` preserves the same real-time theorem while varying the
  inferred OS0 slope.
- Positive transfer and unitary tick objects are distinct: `exp(-r k)` is a
  positive contraction while `exp(i k)` is a unitary phase for `k != 0`.
