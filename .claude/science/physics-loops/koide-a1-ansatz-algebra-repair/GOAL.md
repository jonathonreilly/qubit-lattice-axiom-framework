# Goal

Repair `koide_a1_loop_final_status_2026-04-22` without adding axioms or
claiming physical charged-lepton closure. The useful science preserved here is
the exact algebra of the declared Koide A1 quartic ansatz:

```text
V(Phi) = [2 tr(Phi)^2 - 3 tr(Phi^2)]^2
       = 81(a^2 - 2r^2)^2.
```

The block aims to make that finite algebra auditable and to expose the real
remaining blocker: deriving the ansatz and the physical lepton identification
from the framework rather than assuming them.
