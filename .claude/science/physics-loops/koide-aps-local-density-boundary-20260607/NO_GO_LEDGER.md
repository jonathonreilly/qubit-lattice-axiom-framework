# No-Go Ledger

## Finite Cone Caps Do Not Prove Global PL S3

The ledger statuses for `s3_cap_uniqueness_note`,
`pl_topology_infrastructure_textbook_import_note_2026-05-17`, and
`s3_general_r_derivation_note` are bounded finite certificates. Their audited
scopes explicitly exclude arbitrary PL cap uniqueness, all-R PL S3
identification, and physical closure.

## Diagnostic ABSS Checks Do Not Prove Ambient Applicability

The runner's smoothability, spin, Morse-Bott, and spin-lift checks are useful
inside a supplied `PL S^3 x R` route, but they do not construct or audit that
route from framework primitives.
