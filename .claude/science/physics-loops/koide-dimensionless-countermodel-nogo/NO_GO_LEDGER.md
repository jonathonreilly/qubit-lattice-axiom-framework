# No-Go Ledger

## Exact Countermodels

- `Q(0,1/4) = 8/9` and `Q(0,-1/4) = 8/15`, so the finite two-channel
  algebra does not force `Q = 2/3` unless a physical law sets `z = 0`.
- `spectator = 1` gives `delta = 0`.
- `spectator = 1/2` gives `delta = 1/9`.
- `endpoint_shift = 1/9` gives `delta = 1/3`.

Therefore finite endpoint algebra does not force `delta = 2/9` unless a
physical law selects line-local support and the basepoint.
