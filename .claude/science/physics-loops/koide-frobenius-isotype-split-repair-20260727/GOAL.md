# Goal

Determine whether positive-definiteness, unitary Ad-invariance, and
scalar/traceless orthogonality force the Frobenius point `beta = 0` in
`B_{alpha,beta}` on `Herm(3)`.

The completion witness is a globally valid `beta != 0` counterexample and an
explicit calculation showing that the AM-GM output still depends on the free
relative weight. A physical charged-lepton derivation or a claim about all
stronger future normalization principles does not count as part of this goal.
