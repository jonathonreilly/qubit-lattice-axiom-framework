# Assumptions And Imports

## Native Inputs

- Hermitian circulant parametrization
  `H = a I + b C + bbar C^2`.
- Frobenius inner product on matrix algebra.
- Isotypic projectors
  `pi_+(H) = (tr H / 3) I` and `pi_perp(H) = H - pi_+(H)`.

## Stipulated For The Bounded Theorem

- The block-total scalar law `S_block = log E_+ + log E_perp` is treated as the
  candidate branch being analyzed, not as a derived canonical physical law.

## Explicitly Not Imported As Closure

- Canonical scalar-lane `SO(2)` quotient.
- Physical uniqueness of block-total weighting over determinant/rank weighting.
- PDG charged-lepton masses as proof input.
