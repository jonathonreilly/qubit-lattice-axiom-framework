# Assumptions And Imports

Allowed framework inputs:

- one-site Pauli/Cl(3,0) representation;
- finite `Z^3` nearest-neighbor staggered kinetic operator with normalized
  `1/2` hopping;
- finite matrix algebra over the sampled periodic/open boxes;
- the source-level Kawamoto-Smit phase identity as a bounded bridge surface,
  with no status promotion by this branch.

Forbidden hidden inputs:

- no fitted physical constants;
- no observed mass/charge targets;
- no new axiom;
- no audit verdict or ledger retagging;
- no assertion that the physical matter-state spinor law follows unless a
  separate theorem proves it.

New exposure:

- the row remains blocked, if at all, by the physical
  KS-to-matter-state-law bridge rather than by stale cache/normalization or
  hard-coded runner disposition checks.
