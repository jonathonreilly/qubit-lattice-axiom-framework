# No-Go Ledger

## Local quotient by definition

Status: rejected as a retained-positive route.

Reason: choosing the two-slot carrier locally proves the reduced algebra but
does not prove that the physical charged-lepton scalar lane lives on that
carrier.

## Unreduced determinant alone

Status: exact obstruction.

Reason: `det(alpha P_+ + beta P_perp) = alpha beta^2` gives weights `(1,2)`
and therefore lands at `kappa = 1`, not the MRU leaf.

## Equality by numerical target

Status: rejected.

Reason: using the desired MRU ratio as a selector would be a fit, not a
framework-native derivation.
