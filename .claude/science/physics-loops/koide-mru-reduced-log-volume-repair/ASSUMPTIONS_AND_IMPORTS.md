# Assumptions And Imports

No new axiom is introduced.

The only assumptions are formal reduced-carrier definitions:

- `rho_plus > 0`;
- `rho_perp > 0`;
- `rho_plus^2 + rho_perp^2 = E_tot > 0`;
- `kappa = 2 rho_plus^2 / rho_perp^2`.

No physical SO(2)-quotient or charged-lepton scalar-lane readout is imported.
