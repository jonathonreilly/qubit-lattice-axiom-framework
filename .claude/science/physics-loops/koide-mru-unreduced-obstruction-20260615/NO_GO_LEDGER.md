# No-Go Ledger

## Unreduced determinant carrier

On the unreduced `3 x 3` isotypic-scalar carrier,
`det(alpha P_+ + beta P_perp) = alpha beta^2`. The determinant/log-volume
weights are therefore `(1, 2)`, so the stationary leaf gives `kappa = 1`.
This route cannot force MRU.

## Spectrum-native scalar route to SO(2)

Prior repo context records that generic scalar observables on `Herm_circ(3)`
such as `tr(H^3)` and `det(H)` carry `cos(3 arg b)` dependence. Therefore
"scalar observable" alone does not derive the `SO(2)` quotient.
