# Assumptions And Imports

Load-bearing:

- finite real `b`-plane Kähler algebra;
- finite circulant matrix algebra;
- finite Kähler-Dirac form-degree model encoded in the runner.

Not load-bearing:

- identifying the circulant coefficient `b` with a physical first-order
  Kähler-Dirac B-field amplitude;
- selecting `r=1/2`;
- deriving the charged-lepton Koide value.

No new axiom is introduced.
