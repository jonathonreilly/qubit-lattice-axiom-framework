# Assumptions And Imports

- The faithful spin-1/2 one-mode input is supplied for the soft-Bose/CAR finite
  discriminator; this branch does not derive it.
- The scalar/RP check is a finite toy witness over the runner's sampled kernel,
  not a retained continuum scalar microcausality theorem.
- The nearest-neighbour frame comparison is a finite spectrum comparison, not a
  statement that all local dynamics agree.
- No new axiom is introduced.
