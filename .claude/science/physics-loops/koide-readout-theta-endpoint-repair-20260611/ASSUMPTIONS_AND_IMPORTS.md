# Assumptions And Imports

## Closed Inputs

- Unit residues correspond to the physical normal/Hermitian spectral readout.
- The runner samples the weighted readout functional directly on the
  `Z=(1,t,t)` family.

## Not Imported

- No dynamics principle selecting `r=1/2` is introduced.
- No new residue theorem or reference-state admission is introduced.
- No new axiom is introduced.
- No audit verdict or ledger status is modified.

## Repair Point

The doublet-symmetric residue family has two theta-independent endpoints:
`t=0` is single-pole collapse with `Q=1`, while `t=1` is the unit-residue
democratic branch with `Q=(1+2r)/3` and `r` still free.
