# Assumptions And Imports

Allowed inputs:

- Route-diagnostic spectra used by the runner.
- PDG charged-lepton square-root comparator as a non-load-bearing sanity check.

Forbidden hidden imports:

- Observed masses as derivation inputs.
- Adoption of any signed or unsigned readout rule.
- A no-go theorem against all signed readout routes.
- Closure of `r = 1/2`, Koide `Q = 2/3`, or holomorphic polarization.
