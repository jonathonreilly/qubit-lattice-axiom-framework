# Assumptions And Imports

- In-scope inputs are formal algebraic variables and substitutions only:
  `z=0`, `selected=1`, `spectator=0`, and `c=0`.
- The real nontrivial `Z_3` doublet and its `2 pi/3` rotation are used as a
  finite representation calculation.
- The physical charged-lepton zero-source readout is out of scope.
- The physical Brannen endpoint as the whole real nontrivial `Z_3` primitive is
  out of scope.
- The physical determinant-line based endpoint readout is out of scope.
- No observed Koide value, fitted mass input, or new axiom is used.
