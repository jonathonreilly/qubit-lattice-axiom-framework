# No-Go Ledger

- No attempt is made to infer audit status from source edits.
- No attempt is made to turn finite normalization into a Record-derived
  physical measure.
- No attempt is made to treat comparator spin-statistics literature as a
  framework derivation.
- No attempt is made to use `eta_obs` as a proof input.
- No attempt is made to derive Koide equal-block weighting from Record.
