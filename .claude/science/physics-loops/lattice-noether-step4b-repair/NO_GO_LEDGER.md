# No-Go Ledger

- Literal `(5) -> (3)` substitution route:
  A finite two-site shift is not a local infinitesimal Lie generator. Literal
  substitution into the bilateral current gives an extended-stencil expression,
  not density (3).

- E6-as-symbolic-proof route:
  E6 checks a free-carrier propagator expectation on a finite block. It does
  not prove an arbitrary-field on-shell identity.

- Ledger-retag route:
  Out of scope. This PR only queues the source row for independent audit.
