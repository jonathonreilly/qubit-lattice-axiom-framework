# No-Go Ledger

- The retained Noether row does not derive the canonical density (old
  formula `P_x`) as an arbitrary-field Ward current.
- The two-step shift character cannot distinguish one-step momenta that
  differ by `pi`; full one-site Brillouin momentum is therefore outside
  this repair.
- A generic `T_2`-invariant finite operator need not commute with `T_1`.
  The runner deliberately checks this boundary.
