# Assumptions And Imports

- Uses the existing Brannen/circulant sqrt-mass form as the row's declared
  surface.
- Uses exact `sympy` algebra for elementary symmetric functions and phase
  stationarity.
- No PDG mass values, fitted selectors, literature constants, or new axioms are
  introduced.
- The value `delta=2/9` is used only as the target under test, not as a derived
  framework output.
- General real `C3` scalar actions may depend on both `Re z^3` and `Im z^3`;
  excluding `Im z^3` requires an explicit conjugation-even/spectral restriction.
