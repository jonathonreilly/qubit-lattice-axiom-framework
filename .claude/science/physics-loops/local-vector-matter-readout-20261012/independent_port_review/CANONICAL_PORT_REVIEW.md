## Scientific argument and combined premises

The field leaf is the explicit constant-coefficient action with G>0,
alpha>0, 0<D<=N and fixed unit e. It has positive kinetic and gradient
coefficients and a nonnegative divergence coefficient; the latter vanishes
when D=N. The linked field proof supplies its local flux, two speeds, complete
retarded tensor and static scalar Green function. No fitted nonlinear response,
framework primitive, measured speed or accepted physical-gravity identity is
substituted for these assumptions.

The matter leaf adds positive inertial masses, ordinary nonrelativistic
kinetic energy, conserved mass density and reciprocal coupling -rho e.u.
The energy exchange signs are consistent: field work is -rho phi_t, matter
power is -J.grad(phi), and continuity produces the opposite sum in the
interaction-energy derivative. Static elimination gives half-counted negative
interaction energy. Positive field energy does not prove a lower bound for
the full interacting Hamiltonian. Smooth profiles regularize source fields;
they do not complete internal stresses, self-force, inertial-mass matching
or compact-body structure.

The static inverse gives the stated even anisotropic pair potential. Its
equatorial restriction yields G_eq=G(N+D)/2 and the circular orbit relation.
The z expansion yields nu_z^2/Omega^2=(3D-N)/(N+D), with the stated restoring,
marginal and unstable regimes. Fixed e removes full rotational invariance
but does not spoil translation symmetry or reciprocal pair forces. The
q=5 controls are explicitly prescribed and vertically unstable; they are
not self-consistent stable binaries.

For fixed nonzero frequency, the retarded tensor's intervening time integral
has an antiderivative proportional to exp(i omega tau)
[tau/(i omega)+1/omega^2]. Its r-dependent endpoints therefore give O(r^-2)
after the r^-3 prefactor. The DC limit instead contributes at O(r^-1).
The note correctly does not interchange these limits. Conserved mass and
the leading conservative centre-of-mass assumption remove the radiative
monopole/dipole derivatives at the stated order; they are not all-orders
claims about matter momentum in the presence of field momentum.

The density Taylor factor 1/2, propagator c_s^-2 and extra two time-shift
derivatives produce u_s proportional to -G I''/(2 alpha r c_s^4).
The outgoing mode flux then gives the positive power coefficient
G/(16 pi alpha c_s^7). Transverse and longitudinal projectors are orthogonal
despite different retarded times. Units agree: the far-zone field is a
potential and the integrated flux is energy per unit time.
