# Bounded theorem and controls

The supplied quadratic field and positive masses define a conditional model.
Energy exchange, pair potential, vertical restoring frequency, leading
multipole radiation, exact linear endpoint displacement and network rank
are proved in the canonical note. The runner checks six conservative/radiative
and five readout families. Full-field nonlinear completions, arbitrary-speed
radiation, relativistic clocks and optical transfer are untested routes.
No universal no-go or exhaustive route coverage is asserted.

Independent methods include Bessel/adaptive quadrature, weak self-consistent
reviewer fixture, matrix exponentials and exact Gram/minor algebra. Synthetic
q=5 sources and false-formula controls retain their explicit limitations.
