# Assumptions And Imports

## Minimal premise set

- Continuous time kinetic term `p0^2`.
- Spatial `Z^3` central-difference kinetic term.
- `O_h` signed-permutation symmetry of the spatial lattice axes.

## Avoided imports

- No literature theorem is load-bearing.
- No observed Lorentz-violation bound is used.
- No fitted coefficient, beta value, or Standard-Model numerical input is used.
- No new axiom, primitive, or admission is introduced.

## Remaining open inputs

- Physical interacting one-loop velocity RG.
- Physical coefficient multiplying the spatial-only channel.
- Fixed-point anomalous dimension and sufficiency against LV bounds.
