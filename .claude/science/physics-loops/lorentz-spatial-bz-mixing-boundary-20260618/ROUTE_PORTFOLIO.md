## Selected route

Expand the spatial central-difference dispersion and project the leading
quartic artifact back to the quadratic kinetic channel. Because the artifact is
independent of `p0`, the time coefficient vanishes. Because the spatial Hessian
averages to one scalar over the signed-permutation `O_h` orbit, the spatial
channel is one scalar.
