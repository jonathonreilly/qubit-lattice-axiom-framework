# Assumptions And Imports

## Structural Inputs

- Finite-dimensional qubit-lattice carrier.
- Orthogonal projective family with displayed physical labels satisfying
  `P_r != 0`.
- Canonical Naimark/Lueders frame used by the source row.

## Imports

No observed values, fitted selectors, numerical comparators, unit conventions,
or new axioms.

## Boundary

Formal zero-effect labels are bookkeeping-only. The runner includes an explicit
zero-projector edge case so the necessity clause no longer overstates that any
label mixing whatsoever must break `K_P=P`.
