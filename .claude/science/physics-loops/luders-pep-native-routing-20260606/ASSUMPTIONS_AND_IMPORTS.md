# Assumptions And Imports

No new axioms are introduced.

Used inputs:

- Finite qubit-lattice operator algebra.
- Parent consistency requirements (U1)-(U4).
- Native finite operator-algebra bridge for `M_{P,E}=PEP` and trace/effect
  pairing.
- Positive-probability conditioning domain.

Imports retired or reduced:

- The parent no longer leaves PEP composition and trace/effect pairing as bare
  admitted standard-math steps.

Imports remaining:

- The (U1)-(U4) framing remains an input.
- Full sequential-product uniqueness theory on arbitrary effect algebras remains
  a comparator, not a derivation input.
- Broader Born-chain dependencies remain separate.
