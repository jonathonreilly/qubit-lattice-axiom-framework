# No-Go Ledger

- Boundary equations `M_{P,I}=P` and `M_{I,E}=E` alone do not select `PEP`.
  The Jordan candidate `(PE+EP)/2` satisfies those boundaries but can be
  indefinite and fails the two-step probability identity.
- Record axiom alone cannot repair this row. Its scope explicitly excludes
  probability, normalization, measurement/decoherence dynamics, and readout
  context selection.
