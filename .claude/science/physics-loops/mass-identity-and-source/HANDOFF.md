## What the campaign showed (plain language)

- The positive: we asked what a gravitational pull is ALLOWED to grab
  onto in this framework, and computed the complete answer instead of
  assuming one. At the sizes and complexity bounds we could check
  exhaustively, there are exactly three conserved things: each
  particle type's count, and the total energy. Nothing else survives
  the dynamics. Coupling to particle counts is already ruled out (it
  makes bound objects fall wrong); what remains is energy, times one
  universal constant — the same single free number every theory of
  gravity keeps (Newton's G). The controls behaved perfectly: with
  interactions off the conserved list balloons; with species mutually
  decoupled their energies stay separate. The interaction the Record
  axiom forces is exactly what fuses the energy books into one total —
  the mediator is what makes a species-blind source possible.
- The negative: the clean "E = mc^2 as an identity" test — comparing
  the first and second bound states' inertia-to-energy ratios — cannot
  be run at the computer sizes reachable here. Three careful attempts
  showed why: at weak coupling the second state blurs into the
  two-particle continuum; at strong coupling it turns out to live at a
  different corner of the lattice's momentum space (a real, measured
  discovery about the staggered construction — stable across volumes)
  and its band is too narrow to fit reliably. One grid point sits
  between the two failure modes; a trend needs at least two. The
  honest instrument reported its own limits; the identity itself is
  neither confirmed nor refuted by this.
