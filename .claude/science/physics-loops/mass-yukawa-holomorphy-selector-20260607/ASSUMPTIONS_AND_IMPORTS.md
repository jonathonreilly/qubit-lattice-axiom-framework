# Assumptions And Imports

## Current-Surface Inputs

- The faithful doublet has a commuting complex structure `J`.
- The real and complex determinant functors satisfy
  `det_R = det_C * conjugate(det_C)` on a complex line.
- The Koide dial maps doublet count `(1,2)` to `r=1` and `(1,1)` to `r=1/2`.

## Open Imports Exposed

- A physical action-family theorem is needed to choose the Pfaffian or
  holomorphic determinant.
- A staggered-Dirac mass/Yukawa realization is needed if that theorem is to be
  native to the framework.
- Rooted spin-generation entanglement remains a separate possible way to
  change the field module.

## Imports Not Added

- No observed lepton masses are used.
- No continuum determinant theorem is load-bearing.
- No new holomorphic-action axiom is admitted.
- No audit status is modified.
