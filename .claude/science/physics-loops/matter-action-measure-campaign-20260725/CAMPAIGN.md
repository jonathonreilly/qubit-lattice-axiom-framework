## The honest difficulty, stated up front

physical-observable identification" OUTSIDE axiom content, and the
axiom memo states plainly that Admissibility is NOT a dynamics axiom
and that the axioms do not choose a Hamiltonian or transfer operator.
This session has needled that sentence into a dozen notes.

So the campaign must confront, FIRST, whether the obligation is even
well-posed:
- If the axioms supply no dynamics BY DESIGN, then "derive the matter
  action" cannot mean "from the four axioms alone".
- It might mean: derive it from axioms PLUS approved primitives; or
  derive it up to a finite menu and then show the menu has one
  admissible element; or show a selection principle (reflection
  positivity, microcausality, record-compatibility) cuts the menu to
  a point.
- Or the honest answer is a sharp NO-GO: the action is irreducibly
  supplied, and every matter-sector value in the framework inherits
  that supply. That would be a major, publishable, structural result
  about the framework's reach.

Any of those is a win. Adopting an action and calling it derived is
the failure mode.
