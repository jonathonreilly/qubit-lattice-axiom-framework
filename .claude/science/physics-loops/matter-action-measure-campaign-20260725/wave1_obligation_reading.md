## HEADLINE

**The first conjunct is not a registered target and has no registered success
condition. It is a subordinate, instrumental clause inside the closure
criterion of a *grain*-counting obligation, and both of its nouns — "the
physical matter action" and "its measure" — are undefined everywhere in the
corpus.** The repo's own most recent surface on this criterion calls them, in
those words, the criterion's **"unknowns"**
(`docs/KCPT_COUPLING_TRIPLE_BEREZIN_COUNT_BINARY_MEASURE_COLLAPSE_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-07-17.md:296-299`).

Consequences for the campaign, stated up front:

1. There is **no repo definition** of "the physical matter action" to derive
   or to prove supplied. All 16 non-ledger occurrences of the exact phrase
   are the obligation text, verbatim quotes of it, or disclaimers of the form
   "this note does not derive the physical matter action". Zero construct it.
2. The machine-registered target (`derivation_obligations.json:13`) and the
   machine-registered self-liquidation condition (`:15`) **both omit the
   action-and-measure clause entirely**. They name only the grain. Nothing in
   `audit_lint.py` reconciles the JSON `target` with the note's
   `## Closure criterion`; the lint checks only `canonical_ids == nodes` and
   non-overlap with the axiom/primitive foundation
   (`docs/audit/scripts/audit_lint.py:693-706`).
3. Therefore: **deriving "the physical matter action" is, as registered,
   neither necessary nor sufficient to liquidate this obligation.** It is one
   stated route to the thing that *is* registered — the count-once/count-twice
   grain. A campaign that derives an action and does not settle the horn
   discharges nothing registered; a campaign that settles the horn by any
   other retained route liquidates the obligation without ever deriving an
   action.
4. The campaign brief's framing that this is "the gate behind which every
   matter-sector value in this framework sits" is an **extrapolation, not the
   obligation's text**. The obligation is scoped, in both the note and the
   registry, to *the physical charged-lepton matter action*
   (`docs/AC_ORBIT_OCCUPANCY_STATISTICAL_GRAIN_DERIVATION_OBLIGATION.md:11-13`;
   `derivation_obligations.json:13`). The framework-wide "source/action"
   gate is a **different, unregistered** object (see §c).

This does not kill the campaign. It relocates its first deliverable: before
anything can be derived or proven supplied, Wave 2 must **declare** what
object is meant, and that declaration is precisely where an unnoticed supply
can hide.

   `ACPHILAMBDA_OCCUPANCY_DETERMINANT_POWER_SPLIT_EXACT_SUPPORT_NOTE_2026-07-04.md:98-100`.

cannot be read as "a state".

5. **Live ledger row** (`docs/audit/data/ledger/ac/ac_orbit_occupancy_statistical_grain_derivation_obligation.json`),

     for closure but supplies neither the physical matter action and measure

**Therefore, stated exactly:**

- **Necessary and sufficient, as registered:** a retained, independently
  audited theorem deriving the count-once-vs-count-twice grain for the
  charged-lepton surface, without inserting the desired charged-lepton value
  or readout dictionary.
- **The first conjunct's status:** a *stated route* to that, with no
  independent pass/fail test, no definition of its objects, and no registry
  presence. **Deriving "the physical matter action and its measure" is neither
  necessary nor sufficient on the registered surface.**
- **What the campaign would have to do to make the first conjunct testable at
  all:** declare the two objects (carrier, field content, functional type,
  measure type) explicitly as a declared reading, and then either (i) derive
  that declared object from axioms + approved primitives, or (ii) prove no
  such derivation exists — in which case the residual supply is *the
  declaration itself*, and the honest no-go must be stated **relative to the
  declared class**, exactly as
  `ACPHILAMBDA_MEASURE_BINARY_AXIOM_UPDATE_NO_GO_NOTE_2026-07-04.md:N5` insists
  ("It is not a universal no-go against future matter-action or
  readout-partition theorems").

That is itself the important finding requested: **the obligation does not
define a success condition for its first conjunct.**
