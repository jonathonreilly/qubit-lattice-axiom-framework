# Local milestone content evaluation

OPEN — one coherent metric/clock construction warrants a separate review unit.

The evaluator read the current canonical proof and successful complete output,
and compared their role with the already opened positive-response, quantum
visibility and vector/matter milestones. Applying the conservative interpretation
that the broad gravity work belongs to one cluster, this is the required
additional-PR judgment. It concerns delivery value only, not an audit outcome.

The new load-bearing content is a different supplied metric/scalar action with
an explicit conserved source, optical metric, outgoing tensor/scalar radiation,
and canonical scalar covariance. Its single invariant connects weak force and
bending to both circular radiation and a specified two-center clock channel.
The earlier vector field and mechanical readout do not provide these equations
or their physical identifications. The exact finite-time difference-source
construction and common normalization form a useful testable chain within
clearly stated hypotheses. The known action itself is not claimed as novelty.

The artifact type remains a conditional bounded theorem, so a new type label
alone does not justify opening. Independent reviewability does: the complete
two proofs and all numeric result classes are present, with no unmerged sibling
premise or hidden historical execution. They belong together, which is why the
field and clock are grouped instead of opening a PR for each. Earlier incomplete
metric alternatives remain campaign work. The marginal review cost buys a new
source/observable relationship, not extra instances or a larger check count.

This decision creates no momentum toward calling the model true. Real sources,
state preparation, cosmology and likelihoods remain unresolved. New finite-body
and matter-charge work will be considered as a later coherent synthesis, with
its own value judgment, rather than appended as unreviewed scope here.
