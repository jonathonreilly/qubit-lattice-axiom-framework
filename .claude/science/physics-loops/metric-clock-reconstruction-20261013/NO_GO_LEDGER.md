# Negative-result scope

No route-exhaustion or impossibility theorem is proposed by this milestone.
Positive kinetic algebra, exact common-source cancellation at coincident
centers and explicitly false-formula controls are constructive calculations
under stated premises. They do not exclude screened/nonuniversal theories or
other quantum states. The scope omissions are unresolved work, not a claim
that no route exists. The focused port reviewer independently confirmed this
reading; a five-route search packet is not substantively triggered.
