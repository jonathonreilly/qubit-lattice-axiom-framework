# Observations-first tensor plus positive scalar reconstruction

Status: conditional-support. Lead author: this chat. This is a new campaign
candidate block, not a claim of a new theory in the literature.

The completed scalar metric family can reproduce leading bending but lacks
transverse trace-free radiation; its circular power calibration selects a
markedly subluminal scalar speed. Published GW170814 polarization comparisons
and GW170817 electromagnetic timing are exposed development context. They
motivate reconstructing a tensor sector and a common physical light cone.
Their assumptions do not by themselves prove an exclusive field inventory.

## Supplied reconstruction and proof targets

Supply an Einstein metric g*, a finite set of dimensionless scalars phi^A
with constant positive-definite field metric K_AB, positive G*, and universal
matter metric gtilde=exp(2 alpha_A phi^A) g*. Alpha is a constant covector.
The gravity action is c^3/(16 pi G*) times integral sqrt(-g*) of
[R*-2 K_AB g*^(mu nu) partial_mu phi^A partial_nu phi^B]. Matter's action is
supplied as S_m[gtilde,Psi], and no microscopic matter theory is derived.
Use asymptotic A=1, phi=0 and Minkowski metric; no scalar potential or screening.
This familiar tensor-multiscalar family is a constructive candidate, not forced
uniquely by observations or by the repository axioms.

Personally derive the field equations and mixed stress identity, static
Newtonian/lensing coefficient map, two tensor plus scalar wave readouts, and
leading circular weak-body radiation with source and detector coupling from
the same action. Retain compact-body sensitivities as an open obligation.
The invariant a=alpha^T K^-1 alpha must be nonnegative. Test whether the
observed near-unit bending ratio leaves room for a dominant universal scalar
force in this restricted unscreened family, and whether a circular radiation
identity follows from the same parameter rather than an independent speed fit.

## Frozen checks

Use five explicit field-space examples: (K,alpha)=(1,0),(1,1/10),(1,1),
(diag(2,3),(1/5,-1/7)), and ([[2,1],[1,3]],(1/4,1/3)).
For each, check basis-invariant a using two nontrivial invertible field
redefinitions, static response, exact algebraic inversion and leading fluxes.
Freeze inclination cosines -1,-.6,0,.6,1 and orbital phases 0,.37,1.2,2.5.
Compute far-zone tensor projection and scalar strain from Cartesian source
moments, and compare with separately derived angular pattern formulas.
Integrate tensor and scalar flux on a Gauss sphere rather than inserting the
closed integrated power. Check source conservation separately from TT-only
projection. Numerical orbit controls are supplied weak, circular point-body
systems, not observed binary mass estimates.

For observation calibration use the already-exposed VLBA gamma=.9998 with
reported uncertainty .0003. The illustrative interval [.9992,1.0004] is a
chosen plus/minus-two-reported-uncertainty working rectangle, not a new
confidence interval or likelihood. Intersect it with the model domain only
after the analytic map. No adjustment of that rectangle after results.
GW polarization and timing context are not labelled independent holdouts.
No actual compact-binary waveform likelihood or new empirical success is claimed.

Preserve source URLs, reading limits, every failure, executed source hashes,
proof assumptions and a focused independent check before a milestone claim.
