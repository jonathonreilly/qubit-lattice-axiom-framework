# Approach families
Direct real-space integration derives the envelope. Fourier charge projection and isolated-device basis diagonalization supply the primary finite calculation. Independent raw-phase quadrature and full charge-photon diagonalization test the load-bearing spectrum along a different implementation path. Raw-only Ramsey reanalysis supplies calibration alternatives; line-shape extraction supplies measured-center alternatives. A dissipative driven-observation derivation is uncompleted. None of these numerical constructions claims to derive canonical quantum mechanics from framework primitives.
