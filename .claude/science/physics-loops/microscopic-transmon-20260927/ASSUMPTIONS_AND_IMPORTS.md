# Premises and imports
Canonical charge/photon Hilbert spaces, Hamiltonian evolution, local short-channel potential, charge coupling, rectangular uniform current/field, negligible screening, equal critical current density, shared transparency and magnetic thickness are supplied assumptions. AFM areas and source field nodes are observations under those interpretations. The cavity constants and EC/Jsum/tau snapshots are calibrated quantities. Endpoint parity center/dispersion is an observation convention, not a derived detector. All physical identification and total error budgeting remain open. No framework primitive is proposed or changed. Exact source/data byte identities are carried in the data provenance and historical review evidence.
