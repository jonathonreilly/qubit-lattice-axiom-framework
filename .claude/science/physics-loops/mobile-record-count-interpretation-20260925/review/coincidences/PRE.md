## 4. A sufficient positive-window limit with all channels

Let `Psi_n,g=J_12 phi_n,g`, `U(t)=exp(-iht)` and `T(t)=exp(t L)`. On every branch, all first and second electric derivatives of the smooth initial packet have bounds `O(g^-1)` and `O(g^-2)`. Integer strings add fixed derivatives. The original `B_i` is a finite smooth matter-matrix multiplier in link angles; the same Sobolev bounds therefore hold for `B_i Psi_n,g`. The actual D in every relevant charge block is a fixed quadratic polynomial in these momenta. Consequently

    Psi_n,g, B_i Psi_n,g in D(D)=D(h),
    ||h Psi_n,g||+||h B_i Psi_n,g||<=C/(tau g^2),
    ||B_i Psi_n,g||<=Cg,
    ||B_2B_1 Psi_n,g||+||B_1B_2 Psi_n,g||<=Cg^2.            (8)

The last two bounds follow directly from (2) and initial Gaussian moments. No generic invariance of the possibly degenerate D-domain under arbitrary charge-changing operators is assumed: (8) is proved on these explicitly smooth vectors using **all** electric derivatives. No H4 eigenstate or vacuum magnetic evolution is assumed. Bounded H4, multiplied by `delta=O(g^-2/tau)`, is enough for the displayed estimate.

For `s,u>=0`, `s+u<=b`, unitarity, bounded jumps and the domain inequality `||(U(t)-I)eta||<=t||h eta||` give

    ||B_2 U(u)B_1 U(s)Psi-B_2B_1 Psi||<=C b/(tau g^2).

The expansion around the initially `O(g^2)` joint output yields

    | ||B_2 U(u)B_1 U(s)Psi||^2-||B_2B_1 Psi||^2 |
            <=C[b/tau+b^2/(tau^2 g^4)].                    (9)

The analogous bound holds with labels reversed. It does not require a domain estimate on `B_1 U(s)Psi`, because the difference was split around the fixed vector `B_1 Psi`.

The full dissipator is bounded on trace class by `2 kappa G`, so bounded Duhamel and contractivity give

    ||T(t)-U_t||_(1->1)<=2 kappa G t,
    U_t rho=U(t)rho U(t)*.                                 (10)

This estimates the effect of **all** background channels; it does not remove them. For positive-window counts `N_i=N_(j_i)([0,b])`, the exact distinct-label factorial formula is

    E_n[N_1 N_2]=kappa^2 int_(0<s<t<b)
       Tr[B_2*B_2 T(t-s)(B_1 T(s)rho_n B_1*)] ds dt
       + the term with 1 and 2 exchanged.                  (11)

It follows from the original labeled marked-map expansion, or its finite count-register realization. Intermediate T's include arbitrary other events. The two ordered areas add to `b^2`, and (7), (9), (10) imply

    E_n[N_1N_2]/(kappa^2 b^2)
       =g^4 Q_n+O(g^6)
        +O[b/tau+b^2/(tau^2g^4)+kappa C_G b].              (12)

To pass from this moment to the requested event let

    p_(n,12)=P_n(N_1>=1 and N_2>=1).

The total intensity is at most `Lambda=kappa G`. Three ordered marked maps give `E[(N_tot)_3]<=(Lambda b)^3`, independent of any Poisson assumption. On total count at most two, `N_1N_2` equals the requested indicator. On higher counts,

    0<=N_1N_2-1_(N_1>=1,N_2>=1)<=(N_tot)_3/2.

Thus the difference between (11) and `p_(n,12)` is `O((kappa G b)^3)`. At fixed graph, tau and kappa, one sufficient regime is

    b=b(g)>0,  b/(tau g^4)->0.                             (13)

Then the **actual full-process** limits are

The single-event limit follows from the parent's one-mark proof applied to the same four-branch smooth preparation; (13) is stronger than its sufficient `b=o(tau g^3)` condition. One can also repeat its small-output proof directly. The joint normalized error furnished above is

## 6. Raw and vacuum-subtracted normalizations

Define the raw normalized fresh-window coincidence

    C_n(g,b)=p_(n,12)/(p_(n,1)p_(n,2)).

From (14),

    C_0 -> 1+2c12^2/(v_1v_2),
    C_1 -> [v_1v_2/4+c12^2/2+(v_2 A+v_1 B)/2+2c12 R]
                  /[(v_1/2+A)(v_2/2+B)].                  (16)

Vacuum correlations need not factor even though the physical detector supports do. Both raw probabilities remain nonnegative. If `A=0` or `B=0`, the corresponding leading signal excess vanishes; the following normalized subtraction then has no justified finite denominator. Dark packets must not be assigned a zero value of that undefined ratio.

For `AB>0`, suppose one uses **independently calibrated vacuum singles** as if they were independent additive backgrounds. The precise corresponding algebraic statistic is

    G_ind = [p_(1,12)-p_(1,1)p_(0,2)-p_(0,1)p_(1,2)
                         +p_(0,1)p_(0,2)]
             /[(p_(1,1)-p_(0,1))(p_(1,2)-p_(0,2))].         (17)

It has the full-process ordered limit

    G_ind -> (c12^2/2+2c12 R)/(AB).                         (18)

Independent repetitions for calibration do not make the two detector backgrounds physically independent. Equation (17) is a defined estimator; interpreting it as a signal `g^(2)` requires an additional background model. That interpretation is not implied by the instrument.

A different prescription first measures the actual joint vacuum background and subtracts mixed single-background terms:

    G_jointvac = [p_(1,12)-p_(0,12)
                   -p_(0,1)(p_(1,2)-p_(0,2))
                   -p_(0,2)(p_(1,1)-p_(0,1))]/[Delta p_1 Delta p_2]
               -> 2c12 R/(AB).                            (19)

It differs from (18) by the intrinsic connected vacuum coefficient `c12^2/(2AB)`. Merely subtracting `p_(0,12)` would give yet another value, retaining the terms `(v_2 A+v_1 B)/(2AB)`. These prescriptions must not be conflated.

For a useful exact reference check, put `e_i=exp(-g^2 v_i/2)`, `z=g^2 c12`, and `a_i=1-e_i`. Equation (4) yields

    S_0i=a_i, S_11=a_1+g^2e_1 A, S_12=a_2+g^2e_2 B,
    J_0=a_1a_2+e_1e_2(cosh z-1),
    J_1=J_0+g^2[e_1 A+e_2 B-e_1e_2(A+B)cosh z
                                      +2e_1e_2 R sinh z]. (20)

Here S and J are reference expectations of E_i and E_1E_2, not positive-window probabilities without their rate/time factors. The independently subtracted reference ratio is exactly

    [(1-g^2(A+B))(cosh z-1)+2g^2 R sinh z]/(g^4 AB).        (21)

For `c12=0`, (21) is zero for every reference g and (18) vanishes. Conditional on that extra orthogonality and `AB>0`, the raw one-excitation value lies between `3/4` and one: with `x=A/v_1`, `y=B/v_2`, Bessel gives `x+y<=1`, and maximizing `xy` at fixed sum proves the bound. Thus a nonzero raw coincidence does not by itself contradict a zero independently subtracted statistic. No particular spatially disjoint plaquette pair with exact zero covariance is being assumed here.

## 8. Relation to an optical coincidence experiment

The allowed primary [Brouri et al., quant-ph/0007032v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0007032v1) normalizes raw delay-bin coincidences as `C_N=c/(N_1N_2 w T)` and then uses an independently measured signal fraction `rho=S/(S+B)` and a background-independence assumption. Its corrected signal statistic is `[C_N-(1-rho^2)]/rho^2`. For the quoted `rho=0.34`, a corrected zero corresponds to raw `C_N=0.8844`. These are previously checked primary method facts; no new data point or fit is inferred here.

At the level of normalized mean algebra, (17) has that form with unequal-arm signal fractions `(p_(1,i)-p_(0,i))/p_(1,i)`. But the experiment's stray-light background is not this prepared quantum vacuum, and its stationary source stream, beam splitter, two APDs and delay-bin functional are not the fresh simultaneous-window trial defined here. The field/matter isometry is supplied; it has not been generated from that experimental source or implemented by those detectors. There is also no physical clock, optical-band, long-propagation or finite laboratory-error control supplied by (13).

Ideal absorption coincidence has the normally ordered annihilation effect and vanishes on a one-photon state. The original effects (2) retain quadrature and vacuum terms. Agreement with zero after a particular subtraction, or a negative value of that subtraction in another legal preparation, does not identify the two instruments. A comparison requires a derived preparation and geometry, observed count functional, background calibration and independence justification (or a measured correlated-background model), timing response and controlled errors at the experimental scales. The present result neither confirms nor excludes the framework against that unmatched experiment, nor excludes other preparations, collective responses or longer histories.
