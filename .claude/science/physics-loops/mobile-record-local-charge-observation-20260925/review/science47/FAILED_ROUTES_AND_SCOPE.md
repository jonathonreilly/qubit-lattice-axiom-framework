# Routes and limits preserved before the PRE seal

1. A Taylor expansion of the full generator on an arbitrary field density is
   not licensed: an arbitrary normal density need not have electric moments
   or belong to the Hamiltonian commutator domain. The proof uses a mild
   interaction-picture expansion instead.
2. Uniform norm continuity of electric-conjugated rotor shifts is false.
   A plaquette circulation of magnitude M has a one-unit circulation energy
   difference K(8M+4). At t=pi/[K(8M+4)] the conjugated shift differs from its
   original action by norm 2 on that input, although these times tend to zero.
   This defeats that proof route, not the local covariance result.
3. A global bounded-generator norm or a no-first-event approximation produces
   constants growing with total volume. Neither is used to claim a local
   volume-independent error. Connected local interaction clusters retain all
   remote and later events, including their loss terms.
4. Keeping only one resolved sign changes the coefficient. The deliberate
   plus-only mutation is rejected by 928 and 3456 covariance entries on L4/L6.
   Dividing the coherent sum by sqrt(2) would likewise change the supplied
   instrument; that normalization is not introduced.
5. Dropping the electric empty-B gate changes 34 of the 40 exact physical-tree
   diagonal entries. Dropping loss produces trace 1.064256...; dropping
   recycling gives trace 0.938004... and zero second-birth probability in the
   controls. These are deliberate wrong-model discriminators, not failed
   executions of the correct model.
6. The finite-tree control has no harmonic field degree of freedom. Its full
   physical Gauss space is finite, not a truncated rotor. It checks the full
   generator, signs, gates and later births; it does not numerically establish
   the arbitrary-normal-field theorem. That conclusion uses the analytic
   bounded local expansion. The torus Gram controls use two finite fields and
   corroborate, rather than exhaust, the arbitrary-field argument.
7. A uniform bound for each fixed local covariance cannot be summed over an
   increasing set of pairs to assert a volume-uniform Fourier-space remainder.
   An infinite-volume dynamics or microscopic joint time/volume limit is not
   constructed here. No third-order remainder or electric-energy derivative
   is claimed without additional analysis.
