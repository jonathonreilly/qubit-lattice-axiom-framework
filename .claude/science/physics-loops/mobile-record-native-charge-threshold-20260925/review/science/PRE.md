## 1. Flat operator and independent path construction

Take B to be the even sublattice of \(\mathbb Z^3\) for the local calculation; this is a coordinate translation of the physical bipartition. Let \(X\subset B\) be occupied sites, and let
\[
k_{ac}(D)=\#\{(u,v):u\in N(a),\ v\in N(c),
u\ne v,\ \{u,v\}=D\}
\]
for an unordered overlapping A pair \(\{a,c\}\). The flat outward map adds \(D\) only when \(D\cap X=\varnothing\). Its return can remove any \(D'\subset X\cup D\) with the same kernel. Thus its exact occupation Gram column is
\[
Q_{Y X}
=2\sum_{\{a,c\}}\sum_{\substack{D\cap X=\varnothing\\
 D'\subset X\cup D\\Y=(X\cup D)\setminus D'}}
k_{ac}(D)k_{ac}(D').                                  \tag{1}
\]
Old B records can be returned. They are not artificially frozen. The code uses this formula and independently checks the near columns by four **ordered** outward/return moves, without using an unordered kernel for that second calculation.

If \(X\) does not meet the two-star union, this pair contributes only its empty-sector diagonal \(2\sum_D k_{ac}(D)^2\). Consequently the increment \(R\) can be defined directly by subtracting that diagonal only for pairs whose union meets \(X\). This avoids subtracting two infinite extensive operators. At most 186 pairs are active for two records; columns have uniformly bounded finite support. \(R\) is bounded self-adjoint, has nonnegative off-diagonal entries and is the local limit of \(Q_{2,L}-642nI\). This is a flat occupation operator; no infinite-volume full Gauss state or electric Hamiltonian is asserted.

## 4. Charge of the uniform-minus comparison

Physical first-postbirth P space has all \(n\) A sites occupied, two occupied B sites \(X=\{b,c\}\), and one minus label. Set \(M=n+2\). The parent's color comparison at flat field is
\[
u_X=M^{-1/2}\sum_{z\in A\cup X}|X;\text{minus at }z\rangle.    \tag{10}
\]
For any physical dressing with these equal conditional color probabilities, bounded diagonal charge expectations do not depend on the field phases or electric-flow choice. Each color assignment admits an integer Gauss flow because total \(q-\mathbf1_A\) is zero. The explicit control constructs and verifies such flows for all 110 colors at \(L=6\).

Conditional on \(X\),
\[
\mathbb E q_z=\frac nM\quad(z\in A\cup X),\qquad
\mathbb E(q_a-1)=-\frac2M\quad(a\in A).                      \tag{11}
\]
Vacant B sites have charge zero. The total Gauss charge is zero, while the total raw matter charge is \(n\), balanced by the background. If \(Q_B=\sum_{b\in B}q_b\) and \(Q_A^{\rm def}=\sum_{a\in A}(q_a-1)\), then
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
Q_B&2&0\\ \hline
\Pr&n/M&2/M
\end{array},\qquad Q_A^{\rm def}=-Q_B,
\]
\[
\mathbb E Q_B=\frac{2n}{M},\qquad
\operatorname{Var}Q_B=\frac{8n}{M^2}.                       \tag{12}
\]
These aggregate laws also hold for a superposition or mixture over two-site occupations with uniform-minus conditional colors. In particular the two B records are not an opposite-charge pair in that comparison: both have positive mean tending to \(+1\), while the compensating mean A defect is spread across the background. “Charge” here means the supplied operator, with no measured charge unit attached.
