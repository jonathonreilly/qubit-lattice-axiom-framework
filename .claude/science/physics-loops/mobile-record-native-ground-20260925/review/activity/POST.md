## 2. The primitive inclusion is entrywise, and it is justified

Keep exactly the original operators and all electric shifts. Define
\[
 M_{ab}=PF_a^*\mathbf1_{\{q_b=0\}}F_aP.
\]
On the intermediate space, \(a\) is empty, both birth signs have unit
rotor amplitude, and their final \(a\)-charges are orthogonal. Thus
\[
 \Gamma=2\kappa\sum_{a,b\sim a}M_{ab}                       \tag{P1}
\]
for both the resolved channels and the specified unnormalized coherent
sum on each edge. This agrees with the blind PRE star identity:
\(\sum_bM_{ab}=(v_a-1)M_a\). Coherences between different source
charges are retained. Equality of losses does not identify gain maps.

Let \(f_{cb}\) be the individual outward hop from \(c\) to \(b\).
For \(c\ne a\) adjacent to \(b\), \(c\) remains occupied after \(F_aP\).
The exact retracing operator on that intermediate space is
\[
 f_{cb}^*f_{cb}=n_c(1-n_b)=1-n_b.
\]
The electric shift out and back cancels with its own charge, even if
the input is a superposition of colors and electric words. Consequently
\[
 PF_a^*f_{cb}^*f_{cb}F_aP=M_{ab}.
\]
These are a specified subset of the paths in
\(Q_{ac}=PF_a^*F_c^*F_cF_aP\). In the original full physical
charge/electric basis every legal path has a nonnegative integer
coefficient. All remaining paths therefore make nonnegative **entries**.
This proves the author's elementwise inclusion. The argument also
preserves returns of old \(B\) records by \(F_a^*\).

Every edge \((a,b)\) has five choices of \(c\ne a\) at \(b\).
Every retained pair has at most two common neighbours for \(L\ge6\).
Counting the two orientations of a pair gives
\[
 5\sum_{a,b}M_{ab}
 \le_{\rm entry}\sum_{\{a,c\}}2r_{ac}Q_{ac}
 \le_{\rm entry}4Q,
\]
and hence
\[
                  \Gamma\le_{\rm entry}\frac{8\kappa}{5}Q.       \tag{P2}
\]
There is no Loewner inference here. In particular, \(F_c^*F_c\) also
contains interference terms: deleting some paths is not generally a
positive-operator subtraction. As a purely algebraic warning, the
positive matrices \(I_2\) and the all-ones \(2\) by \(2\) matrix satisfy
entrywise domination, while their difference has a negative eigenvalue.
That example only explains the logical distinction; it is not a claimed
physical counterexample to a stronger operator inequality in this model.
The author's final proof uses (P2) in the valid manner below.

## 3. Phase cost, electric form domain, and the sector bound

\[

\]
are nonnegative. Symmetry of the real matrices gives
\[
 \Delta Q=\sum_{x,y}Q_{yx}d_{xy}\ge0,\qquad
 0\le\Delta\Gamma\le\frac{8\kappa}{5}\Delta Q,

\]
