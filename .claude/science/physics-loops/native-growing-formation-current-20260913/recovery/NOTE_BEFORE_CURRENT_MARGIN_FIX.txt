## Theorem and proof obligations

For every finite observation horizon T and a finite observation box, the
specified finite native construction has the following properties. Every
supported event history follows the same explicit content kernel F on its
prepared digital nearest-neighbor domain, leaves every older Record fixed,
and preserves the protected matter state functional under its event-free
Hamiltonian evolution. There is a finite initial even-parity state with
nonzero cell-averaged current in all three directions throughout the horizon.
The single-seed version has the formation probability bounds in section 10
and a carrier window of order (R+(gamma+t)T+log(1/epsilon))³, with collision
storage accounted separately. Here R encloses both the seed and observation
box; rates and hopping use the supplied time convention.

The Record-history distribution is the one explicitly constructed from the
program conditions and independent clocks. A readable matter-current
interface is an additional task; the current observable here belongs to the
supplied CAR representation. This theorem supplies neither an interacting
phase nor an empirical prediction or completed TOE.

## 2. A biased gauge pulse and its complete conditional instrument

Let Z=Z_e, S=S_e. The cycle algebra gives S*=S, S²=Z²=I,
SZ=-ZS. Thus Y=iZS is a Hermitian involution. For real theta put

    U(theta)=exp(-i theta Y/2)=c I+s ZS,
    c=cos(theta/2), s=sin(theta/2), Q_z=(I+zZ)/2.

This is a physical bounded-support unitary: its generator is the cycle
Pauli operator times the local Z. No global code projector is used as a
control. For the plaquette witnesses below, its support lies in the four
vertex stars, with at most twenty native factors and physical radius four
from the target. Up to four nearest-neighbor program controls make at most
twenty-four factors. A spatial synthesis into two-site gates is not supplied.
On the incoming code,

    K_z P_h := Q_z U P_h = (c+z s) Q_z P_h.                 (11.1)

Because P_h Z P_h=0,

    P_h K_z* K_z P_h = p_z P_h,
    p_z=(1+z sin(theta))/2.                               (11.2)

For |theta|<=pi/2 the branch is sqrt(p_z) times the original fair
isometry sqrt(2)Q_z P_h. If p_z=0 that branch is absent. For every state
on P_h, including states entangled with an arbitrary reference, the
normalized surviving matter state therefore equals its pre-event state.
This is equality of the corresponding state functional/dictionary, not
equality of the orthogonal physical Record branches.

Every O in A_B commutes with S and with Z: its generators have X support
only on protected edges. Consequently [O,U]=[O,Q_z]=0. Combining this
commutation with the scalar effect (11.2) gives the stronger operator identity

    P_h K_z* O K_z P_h = p_z P_h O P_h.                  (11.3)

The entire protected energy distribution and its correlations with a
reference survive each branch. Also [U,H_B]=[Q_z,H_B]=0 as full-carrier
operator identities. A nonzero hopping coefficient on the candidate
generally violates these identities; zero candidate hopping is essential.

For distinct unused e,f, Z_e commutes with S_f, because f's cycle contains
only f and protected edges. All S_e commute. Therefore all Y_e commute,
all cross-edge K_e,z commute, and their coefficients may be any functions
of already fixed commuting program Records. The surviving S_f and all old
Z Records commute with the next instrument. Induction proves (11.1)–(11.3)
for every supported history. Arbitrary interleaved H_B dwell adds only the
same matter evolution that would occur without the events. There is no
error accumulating with the number of formations.

Equation (11.2) gives the same conditional Record probabilities for every
protected matter input with the same program history. The present construction
therefore specifies no matter-dependent Record readout.

## 3. Literal nearest neighbors and a varying content law

Protect all other edges. Each candidate has a plaquette detour through
the adjacent even-y row. No detour uses another candidate. The protected
graph is connected: all y,z edges remain and every even-y row retains x
edges. R has density 1/2 per virtual vertex, or 1/6 of virtual edges and
1/16 of physical sites. In a finite open box include only candidates whose
selected detour is present; protect the other boundary edges.

For the first, static-program construction, all physical vertex sites carry
supplied permanent digital program Records Z=z_v. The two endpoints of a
native edge are its literal nearest neighbors; none of its four transverse
neighbors carries a Record. Old native edge Records are never nearest
neighbors of another native edge center. Fuel below remains unrecorded.
Choose

    theta = pi(z_L+z_R)/12,
    p_+ = 1/2+(z_L+z_R)/8 in {1/4,1/2,3/4}.             (11.4)

The controlled unitary is exp[-i pi(Z_L+Z_R)Y_e/24]. It preserves its
digital program registers. Only the angle depends on the nearest-neighbor
conditions; the physical pulse support includes the bounded cycle stars.

A single content kernel on a specified nonempty finite family eta of
rank-one neighbor Records is

    F(eta) = (1/|eta|) sum_(rho in eta)
             [(3/4) delta_rho + (1/4) delta_(I-rho)].    (11.5)

Here the site matrix algebras have the supplied common identification.
F is invariant under neighbor permutations and equivariant under simultaneous
unitary conjugation. On the prepared commuting two-symbol domain (11.4)
is exactly F, including all conditional histories and arbitrary matter
states in the code. It varies between actually realized program conditions.
There is no claim of universal quantum programming for nonorthogonal
unknown states, or a physical implementation of (11.5) outside this digital

The periodic role pattern and its common basis are state/control conditions.
Translations and rotations of the prepared construction obey the same F;

The program Records are initial data here; their own formation is not yet

## 4. A supplied absorbing event process and finite collision dilation

Put one distinct fuel qubit at the spectator site x_e+e_z for each candidate
center x_e, initially |1>. These sites have two odd coordinates and are
disjoint from native qubits and endpoint programs. For a supplied rate
gamma>0 define trajectory jump operators

    J_z = sqrt(gamma) K_z tensor |0><1|_fuel.

Since sum_z K_z* K_z=I on the full carrier, sum_z J_z*J_z=gamma P_1.
The first jump has an exponential waiting time, gives law (11.2), empties
the fuel, and makes the edge a permanent Z Record in this forward process.
All other operations preserve this Z. Ready-site clocks are independent;
the content law is conditioned on the actual local program Records.
The GKSL generator, jump unraveling, clock, and the identification of a
jump with formation are conditions, not deductions from the axioms.

Its exact duration-h channel has Kraus operators

    A_0 = I tensor(P_0+exp(-gamma h/2)P_1),
    A_z = sqrt(1-exp(-gamma h)) K_z tensor |0><1|.

For a three-state environment with labels idle,+,- initially idle, define

    B = sum_z K_z tensor |0><1| tensor |z><idle|,
    V=B+B*, beta=arccos(exp(-gamma h/2)).

B*B=I tensor P_1 tensor |idle><idle|, B²=0, BB* is a projector
orthogonal to B*B. Thus V³=V, and exp(-i beta V) supplies the displayed
Kraus operators up to the harmless common jump phase -i. Fuel-zero with
an idle environment is dark. Each time bin requires a fresh environment
which is never recoupled. Two qubits embed its three labels; for n bins
and m candidates the storage cost is at most 2nm environment qubits plus
m fuel qubits, in addition to the physical native and program carriers.
Environment placement and routing are additional implementation tasks.
With zero assigned fuel/environment energy the collision commutes exactly
with H_B. This conditional energy statement does not price controller
preparation, timing, or spatial synthesis.

At supplied elapsed time tau, the ideal process has candidate formed
fraction 1-exp(-gamma tau), hence bulk formed density
(1-exp(-gamma tau))/16 per physical site. A fixed finite
region saturates. The late-time formation rate there tends to zero.
Fresh environments are essential for intermediate permanent histories:
one finite reversible environment reused indefinitely can reverse a jump.
An exact channel at a single final time alone is not an autonomous
permanent-formation theorem.

## 5. A nonzero three-direction current in the protected graph

Now take all protected hopping coefficients equal to t>0. Write a virtual
site as (x,2m+s,z), s=0,1. Both sublattices have z hopping, only s=0 has
x hopping, and each s=0 site has y neighbors at offsets +1 and -1.
For a Bloch vector exp[i(k_x x+2k_y m+k_z z)] in the cell convention, the
off-diagonal matrix element is t(1+exp(-2ik_y)). Moving the internal s=1
phase into the basis makes it 2t cos(k_y). Thus the physical-position
Bloch symbol and its two eigenvalues are

    h(k)=2t cos(k_z) I + [[2t cos(k_x),2t cos(k_y)],
                          [2t cos(k_y),0]],
    E_±=2t cos(k_z)+t cos(k_x)±t D,
    D=sqrt(cos²(k_x)+4cos²(k_y)).                         (11.6)

Use k_x,k_z in [-pi,pi) and k_y in [-pi/2,pi/2). The upper-band patch

    W=[pi/3,2pi/3] × [pi/6,pi/3] × [pi/3,2pi/3]

is away from degeneracies. On W, 1<=D<2, |cos(k_x)|<=1/2,
sin(k_x),sin(k_z)>=sqrt(3)/2, and cos(k_y),sin(k_y)>=1/2. Differentiating
(11.6) gives the uniform velocity bounds

    v_x=-t sin(k_x)(1+cos(k_x)/D) <= -sqrt(3)t/4,
    v_y=-4t cos(k_y)sin(k_y)/D    <= -t/2,
    v_z=-2t sin(k_z)              <= -sqrt(3)t.            (11.7)

These are group velocities in supplied virtual-lattice/time units, not
an emergent relativistic cone or a clock match. The patch volume is
pi³/54, while the two-site Brillouin zone has volume 4pi³. There is one
upper-band mode per cell, hence W contains 1/432 modes per virtual vertex.

Define the infinite number-conserving quasifree CAR state by

    C = I/4 + (1/2) 1_W(k) P_+(k).                       (11.8)

Its covariance lies between I/4 and 3I/4 and commutes with h. The uniform
I/4 part has zero bond current. For the oriented protected bond v→v+e_a,
use j_v,a=i t(c_v* c_(v+e_a)-c_(v+e_a)* c_v), with zero on a missing bond.
Its spatial average is the one-particle velocity i[h,X_a]. The Bloch
Hellmann–Feynman identity then gives current per virtual vertex

    J_a = (1/(16pi³)) integral_W v_a(k) d³k.             (11.9)

The factor is (two vertices per cell)^(-1), covariance weight 1/2, and
Brillouin-zone measure (4pi³)^(-1). Equations (11.7)–(11.9) imply

    J_x <= -sqrt(3)t/3456,
    J_y <= -t/1728,
    J_z <= -sqrt(3)t/864.                               (11.10)

Cell averaging means averaging the oriented bonds rooted at the two y
sublattices. The off-diagonal phase change above does not remove the
physical internal-position term from the current: (11.9) follows either
in the physical-position basis or from i[h,X] before the phase change.
This is a supplied CAR current observable. The blind Record signs alone
do not measure it.

## 6. Finite native preparation and an arbitrarily long current window

No restriction of an infinite physical cycle-code state is assumed. Let
Omega be a finite open virtual box with its y interval starting at an even
integer. Every odd-y x candidate has its detour in the adjacent lower
even-y row. Then the protected finite graph is connected and its hopping
matrix h_Omega is the compression of the infinite protected h. Let
C_Omega be the compression of (11.8). Its spectrum nu_j stays in [1/4,3/4].
Construct the finite quasifree density with these occupations and condition
on even total fermion parity. Its preparation succeeds with probability

    (1+product_j(1-2nu_j))/2 >= (1-2^(-M))/2,

where M=|Omega|. In the occupation eigenbasis the conditioned covariance
remains diagonal. Direct summation over the other independent occupations
gives

    nu_j^(even)-nu_j =
      -2nu_j(1-nu_j) product_(l!=j)(1-2nu_l)
       / [1+product_l(1-2nu_l)].

Consequently

    ||C_Omega^(even)-C_Omega|| <= delta_M,
    delta_M=2^(-M)/(1-2^(-M)).                           (11.11)

Parity conditioning need not preserve Gaussianity. Only the covariance
bound is used. Map this even finite density into the original connected
native cycle code by the imported faithful finite dictionary. This gives
an actual finite physical initial state and avoids a boundary/parity
compatibility shortcut. Its protected two-point function evolves by h_Omega
even though its higher correlations need not be Gaussian.

For each observed bond endpoint at graph distance at least d from the
outside of Omega, the vectors h_Omega^n delta_v and h^n delta_v agree for
n<d, with the finite vector extended by zero. Both Hamiltonian norms are
at most 6t. For 0<=s<=T define a=6tT and R_d(a)=sum_(n>=d) a^n/n!.
The exponential series gives

    ||exp(i s h_Omega)delta_v-exp(i s h)delta_v|| <= 2R_d(a).

The norm of C is at most one. Applying this bound to the two vectors in
each covariance entry and adding (11.11) yields the uniform entry error

    |C_finite(s)_(v,w)-C_infinite_(v,w)| <= 4R_d(a)+delta_M.
                                                               (11.12)

This comparison includes arbitrary times up to T, not just a selected
momentum eigenstate in a periodic finite box. An oriented bond-current
error is at most 2t times the right side of (11.12). The same bound holds
for the two-site cell average in each direction.

For d>=2a, ratios of successive tail terms are at most 1/2 and d!>=(d/e)^d,
so R_d(a)<=2(ea/d)^d. For 0<epsilon<=1, it suffices to choose

    d >= ceil(max(12 e tT, log_2(16/epsilon),1)),
    M >= ceil(log_2(4/epsilon))                         (11.13)

to make (11.12) at most epsilon. In particular epsilon<=1/6912 preserves
at least half the weakest nonzero current bound in (11.10), throughout
the observation region and 0<=s<=T. A finite physical carrier enclosing
a virtual observation box of side R therefore has size
O((R+tT+log(1/epsilon))³); the constants include the midpoint placement,
native edges, static programs and fuel. Collision storage is additional
and was priced separately in section 4.

Every supported formation history preserves all protected matter
observables by (11.3). Thus the current/window bound holds on every such
history, with no event-count error. Increasing R permits an unbounded
number of distinct formations during the same protected evolution. This
is a finite growing-region construction with explicit preparation and
resources; it does not assert a completed infinite physical code model.


## 7. Disjoint physical roles and one conditional content law

Keep native qubits at one-odd-coordinate edge centers, and the same candidate
x edges with odd virtual y. Define the program graph P to consist of physical
sites having at least two odd coordinates, with literal nearest-neighbor
edges. Its three-odd sites are the vertices of a cubic graph of spacing two;
its two-odd sites subdivide each edge once. P is connected, with degree six
at a coarse vertex and degree two at an edge interior. It is disjoint from
all native edge qubits and all all-even physical vertex sites.

Place an optional native fuel qubit at the all-even tail 2v of each candidate
edge (v,v+e_x), initially unrecorded in |1>. Candidate tails are distinct.
This replaces the static version's transverse fuel location; it does not
add a second qubit at a program site. The four transverse nearest neighbors
of a candidate native qubit belong to P. Its two endpoint neighbors are
unrecorded fuel/spectator sites.

Initially one three-odd program site o is a supplied digital Record. Every
other program qubit is prepared in |+x>. Program sites are not rotated or
otherwise used as controls until they themselves are already Records, except
for their own single formation pulse. No static endpoint program Records
are present. Existing Records serve only as digital Z controls.

The content law at every new program or native event is exactly

    F(eta)=(1/n) sum_(rho in eta)
            [(3/4)delta_rho+(1/4)delta_(I-rho)], n=|eta|>0.       (C.1)

Eta lists all actual nearest-neighbor Record contents. In the common
digital context let their signs sum to b. Then p_+=1/2+b/(4n). The common
law is covariant under neighbor permutations and simultaneous conjugation,
and varies with the supplied conditions. All digital event probabilities
lie in [1/4,3/4]. From the same fixed seed, finite birth orders and all-plus
or all-minus signs at a candidate's four neighbors therefore have positive
probability. The different candidate probabilities 3/4 and 1/4 occur within
one prepared history family, not only after changing the preparation.
The role pattern and initial
Pauli/preparation context are additional conditions, as before.

An unrecorded program site becomes enabled when at least one of its program
neighbors has formed. It uses (C.1), including any already formed native
neighbors in eta. In fact a native candidate adjacent to an unrecorded
program target cannot yet have formed, because its four-neighbor enabling
condition includes that target. Thus those native contributions are absent
at the program site's actual first event. Its ready |+x> factor is rotated by

    U_program=exp(+i theta sigma_y/2), theta=arcsin(b/(2n)),

then measured in Z. The sign of this rotation matters: its final Z mean is
sin(theta)=b/(2n). All its controls are fixed Records. A native candidate
becomes enabled after its four program neighbors have formed. It then uses
the cycle pulse of the preceding construction with theta=arcsin(b/8). Its n is
exactly four, so its probabilities are again (C.1).

This is a forward process whose supplied occurrence rule uses the Record
configuration to select enabled unrecorded targets. It does not define a
readout value for an absent Record. It also does not implement the presence
guard as a reversible quantum operation on an otherwise unspecified ready
space. Program occurrence, initial readiness, and the single seed remain
conditions. The optional native fuel realizes a native absorbing channel;
no extra per-program fuel factor has been silently allocated.

## 8. Full finite-history closure

Take an initial product of the program ready/seed factors, native fuel,
and any native matter code state (possibly with a matter reference).
The program instruments commute with the protected matter algebra: their
targets are disjoint, and every native control is a candidate Z which
commutes with that algebra. Their scalar effect on the still untouched
|+x> target gives (C.1), independently of the matter state.

Every native event is the scalar-effect isometry in the preceding construction.
It leaves all program Records unchanged. Any unused candidate's cycle
check survives all earlier events because its witness contains no other
candidate edge. Program events preserve those checks as well, including
when a native Z is used as a control: that control is already a fixed
Record and its cycle check was removed earlier. Therefore induction over
every supported finite history proves:

* every new content law equals F of the actual nearest-neighbor Records;
* every old Record is unchanged;
* every still-unrecorded program target is ready for its stated pulse;
* the protected matter state functional equals its event-free H_B dwell.

Shared controls need not make all abstract program event operators commute.
Events use their actual causal histories; the induction does not reorder
events whose condition sets change. This distinction avoids extending the
static commuting-instrument statement beyond its hypotheses.

All Record histories, including occurrence times below, have the explicit
program-dependent law just derived for every initial protected matter state
on the same code and program preparation.

## 9. A supplied nonexplosive clock process

Each program site receives an independent Exp(gamma) waiting time when it
first becomes enabled; each candidate native site likewise receives one
when all four program neighbors have formed. Enabling is permanent. Content
draws use independent uniform random variables with the probabilities (C.1).
The waiting times do not depend on those draws or on matter.

If N_P program sites have formed, at most 6N_P unformed program sites are
enabled. At most 6N_P native candidates could yet have been activated, since
activation requires adjacency to a formed program site. Thus the total
event rate is at most 12 gamma N_P, and hence at most 12 gamma N_total.
Comparison with the linear pure-birth process proves nonexplosion. More
explicitly, its successive mean holding times are (12 gamma n)^(-1);
the centered independent holding-time series has summable variances while
the sum of the means diverges. The comparison process has no finite-time
accumulation, so neither does this one.

Assign each program site v≠o an independent Exp(gamma) variable E_v at the
start; unused clocks may be sampled in advance. Its formation time satisfies

    T_P(o)=0,
    T_P(v)=E_v+min_(u adjacent to v in P) T_P(u).                (C.2)

It is the minimum sum of site waiting times along a path from o to v.
Positive weights allow minimizing paths to be simple. Native events do not
alter program enabling, so their content dependence does not change (C.2).
Every fixed program vertex is reached almost surely by bounding (C.2) with
the finite waiting-time sum along one fixed path. Countability makes this
true simultaneously for all sites. Every candidate is subsequently enabled
and forms almost surely. The infinite program process thus produces
unboundedly many Records without explosion.

This infinite statement concerns the supplied classical Record/time law and
its local instruments. The preceding construction supplies finite physical native
states for arbitrarily large regions, not an unproved global infinite
native cycle-code state. The finite-window conclusion below uses only
finite carriers and finite portions of this process.

## 10. Explicit growing front bounds without simulated growth

Distance in P from a three-odd o equals the physical Manhattan distance:
one may change coordinates that end odd by even steps through subdivided
coarse edges, then, if necessary, make the final step to the one even
coordinate. Thus B_P(n) lies in a physical cube with at most (2n+1)^3 sites.
For each v in B_P(n), fix one path of length at most n. The exponential
moment at gamma/2 gives

    Pr[T_P(v)>s] <= 2^n exp(-gamma s/2).

A union bound, requiring no independence between these path sums, yields

    Pr[B_P(n) not all formed by s]
      <= (2n+1)^3 exp(n log(2)-gamma s/2).                     (C.3)

Set n=floor(gamma T/(8 log(2))). All candidates at physical Manhattan
distance at most n-1 from o have their four program neighbors in B_P(n).
Applying (C.3) at s=T/2 and the remaining native exponential waits gives

    Pr[one such candidate is unformed at T]
      <= (2n+1)^3 [exp(-gamma T/8)+exp(-gamma T/2)].            (C.4)

For small T the right side may exceed one; (C.4) is then a valid but
uninformative bound. It is not a probability estimate fitted from growth
simulations. For n>=7, let m=floor((n-1)/3). The contained cube of coordinate
radius m has at least m² floor(m/2) candidates: odd x and even z each occur
at least m times, and physical y congruent to 2 modulo 4 occurs at least
floor(m/2) times. Thus (C.4) gives a positive constant times T³ native
formations with probability tending to one. No constant continuing rate
inside an already saturated fixed region follows.

For an upper front, reaching program distance at least n before time T
requires a simple ancestry path whose first n waiting times sum to at
most T. There are at most 6^n such path prefixes. When n>gamma T, the
optimized exponential lower-tail bound for a sum of n independent waits is

    Pr[sum_(j=1)^n E_j<=T] <= (e gamma T/n)^n.

Therefore

    Pr[program distance >= n reached by T]
       <= (6 e gamma T/n)^n <= 2^(-n),
       if n>=12 e gamma T.                                  (C.5)

A formed native candidate is adjacent to a formed program site, so its
physical radius exceeds the program radius by at most one. The constants
are deliberately loose analytical bounds, not a claimed limiting shape
or a relativistic propagation speed.

## 11. Joining the front to finite native transport

For any prescribed finite T and observation region, choose the finite
native box and even-parity preparation by equations (11.11)–
(11.13). Include the candidate ball used in (C.4), every corresponding
protected witness cycle, its program paths, and the current observation
region. Program paths chosen for (C.3) stay inside that ball. A finite
