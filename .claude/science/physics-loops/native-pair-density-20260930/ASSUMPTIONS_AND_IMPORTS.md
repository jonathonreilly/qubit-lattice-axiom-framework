# Inputs and counterfactuals

Supplied: tensor-product Hilbert interpretation of the physical-site M2 algebra,
chosen |0>,|1> basis, Hamiltonian (3), positive mu,tau, expectation rule and
chemical-potential perturbation. The finite torus has L>=5; all particle sectors
are kept. Standard finite-dimensional linear algebra and explicit cosine modes
are proved or evaluated directly. No empirical numbers enter. The three approved
primitives retain their exact grants; none selects these dynamical inputs.

Changing tau to zero removes the gradient estimate; changing the stabilizer or
pair channels changes the positivity proof. Changing dimension changes the
point-resistance argument. These are theorem hypotheses, not universal walls.
The chosen variational state is a witness, not a selected physical state.
