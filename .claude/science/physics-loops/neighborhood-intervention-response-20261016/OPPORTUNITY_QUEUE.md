# Opportunities

1. Derive the same-action scalar/metric/material response to a physically identified source; keep source, readout and preparation explicit. This directly attacks the physical bridge left open by the finite model.
2. Characterize a realizable local intervention and reserve test settings. This can discriminate endpoint-equivalent candidate mechanisms without fitting each environment separately.
3. Separate history/preparation effects from current-trial path influences in interference tests. This needs observations that vary intermediate relations while retaining an independently specified readout.

Further toy networks without a discriminator rank below these tasks. None has an invented success probability or an assumed selector counted as a derivation.
