# Four-layer original readout at transverse momentum

Focused independent derivation using `/tmp/neutral_four_layer_root.md` and the previously checked periodic winding/source coefficient. Longitudinal length is four with periodic z; Q is transverse and Qz=0. Physical C_Q=V^(-1/2) sum_(r,z) exp(-i Q.r) Z_(r,z)Z_(r,z+2). The transverse graph may be open or periodic, but the wrapping statements below require allowed periodic momenta.

## 1. Resolve the uniform derivative into physical columns

Introduce separate real derivative parameters h_r multiplying each column's distance-two readout. They are only a device to calculate the original h=0 observable. Fourier combination is taken afterward, so no Hermiticity assumption about a complex single-Q perturbation is needed.

For a transverse plaquette, the leading Hamiltonian coefficient is -4J lambda^4/128. Each vertex contributes equally to its source derivative: the selected four-edge loop is invariant under vertex permutations generated by its square symmetries at equal transverse couplings. Its uniform derivative has relative factor 3/J, hence the derivative per vertex is 3/(4J). Thus the column-local source contribution is

    -(3 lambda^4/128) sum_(vertices r of p) h_r P_p

at the level of the linear h coefficient.

For an adjacent column pair, the leading winding coefficient is -5J lambda^4/128. Its two endpoints contribute equally. More explicitly the intermediate gap is

    8J c(S)-2d2(S)(h_i+h_j),

so the source derivative per endpoint is -gamma_4 A_4/(4J), with A_4=36/5. This equals -9 lambda^4/128. Therefore its column-local derivative is

    -(9 lambda^4/128)(h_i+h_j) E_ij.

These local decompositions reproduce -12P and -18E at uniform h. Repeated-edge scalar terms may also have a source derivative; they contribute only an identity coefficient, which should remain separated or centered. They do not alter the displayed nonconstant terms.

The effective-source connection does not affect this leading order in the consistent real canonical gauge. Its proper-local N=0 block is diagonal and real antisymmetric below winding order; the first possible nontrivial connection is order four and its commutator with the first nonconstant effective Hamiltonian is order eight. Fourier superposition of the real local derivatives preserves that conclusion.

## 2. Fourier form factors

Set h_r=exp(-iQ.r)/sqrt(V) in the local derivative coefficients. For a unit plaquette with consistently lifted center c_p,

    sum_(r in vertices(p)) exp(-iQ.r)
      =4 cos(Qx/2) cos(Qy/2) exp(-iQ.c_p).

For a unit edge in direction a with lifted midpoint c_e,

    exp(-iQ.r_i)+exp(-iQ.r_j)
      =2 cos(Qa/2) exp(-iQ.c_e).

Hence the proposed formula is correct:

    C_eff,Q = scalar_Q I + lambda^4/(128 sqrt(V)) O_Q + higher orders,

    O_Q = -12 cos(Qx/2)cos(Qy/2)
                   sum_p exp(-iQ.c_p) P_p
          -18 sum_(a,edges e parallel a) cos(Qa/2)
                   exp(-iQ.c_e) E_e.

At Q=0 this becomes -12P-18E=3K-3E for K=-4P-5E. The normalization retains V=4 times the number of transverse columns. The coefficients are for the original source, not an adjustable coupling. O_Q†=O_-Q follows directly from the real local Pauli terms.

## 3. Wrapped centers

On a periodic transverse length R_a, allowed Qa=2pi n_a/R_a ensures exp(-iQa(r_a+R_a))=exp(-iQa r_a). A wrapped edge joining R_a-1 and 0 must first be lifted as R_a-1 and R_a, with center R_a-1/2 (equivalently -1/2 modulo R_a). A wrapped plaquette must use a consistent unit-square lift in each direction. Then the same form-factor identities hold exactly.

The ordinary arithmetic average of endpoint representatives R_a-1 and 0 is generally wrong: it describes a long chord rather than the native unit edge. Changing a valid lifted center by an integer period leaves its Fourier phase unchanged. Shifting Qa by 2pi changes both the half-integer-center phase and the appropriate half-angle cosine sign, leaving the full physical vertex sum unchanged. Thus the individual center phase or cosine should not be interpreted separately as a periodic observable.

On an open transverse graph the identity also holds for ordinary geometric centers and arbitrary real Q, without any momentum-quantization condition. Open boundaries can give a nonzero scalar Fourier coefficient, which centering removes if required.

## Scope and useful immediate consequence

This verifies the leading native four-layer source geometry. It does not establish a response spectrum at finite Q or an actual microscopic pole. For an even periodic transverse grid, Q=(pi,pi) makes both the plaquette and edge contributions vanish at this order; that is leading-order readout darkness, not an all-orders selection rule. For general Q, the edge orientations and plaquettes carry different form factors, so the uniform identity involving K cannot simply be reused as an eigenstate source formula at nonzero momentum.
