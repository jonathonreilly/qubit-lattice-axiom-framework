# Exact protected plane factor and neutral-state equivalence

The representation dimensions and proposed neutral-state conclusion are correct. The statement can be made for arbitrary initial density matrices, not only stationary ones, provided the entire observable/dynamics protocol remains plane-neutral.

## General finite-Pauli representation

Let W be a dimension-d binary Pauli-vector subspace on N physical qubits. Let the restricted symplectic form have rank 2r, and set s=d−2r. Its radical is W intersection W-perp, of dimension s. Symplectic linear algebra gives a Clifford coordinate system with r anticommuting X/Z pairs generating r full matrix qubits, and s independent commuting Z generators on distinct additional qubits. The remaining m=N−r−s=N−d+r qubits are multiplicities. In particular m>=0.

Writing z for the 2^s joint central characters, the Hilbert space decomposes as

H_phys = direct_sum_z [ C^(2^r) tensor C^(2^m) ].

The associative *-algebra generated by W, and its full operator commutant, are respectively

A_W = direct_sum_z [ M_(2^r) tensor I_(2^m) ],
A_W' = direct_sum_z [ I_(2^r) tensor M_(2^m) ].

The center gives independent block projectors, so both displayed direct sums permit arbitrary coefficients in each central sector. Dimension checks are 2^s 2^r 2^m=2^N, dim A_W=2^(s+2r)=2^d, and dim A_W'=2^(s+2m)=2^(2N−d). The Pauli basis of the commutant is W-perp.

This is a unitary representation decomposition, not a reduction in physical qubit count or a derivation of new microscopic tensor factors tied to spatial sites. The coordinate choice within each factor need not be unique.

## Open 3x3x3 plane algebra

Here N=27 and the plane-vector dimension is d=8. Each X plane anticommutes with every Y and Z plane because the intersection line has odd length; planes of one family commute. Restricted symplectic rank is two, so r=1 and s=6. Consequently m=20 and

H_phys = direct_sum_(64 central sectors) [ C² tensor C^(2^20) ].

The plane algebra is direct_sum_64(M2 tensor I), and its commutant is direct_sum_64(I2 tensor M_(2^20)). Independent binary elimination of the nine original plane masks gives d=8 and restricted symplectic rank two, verifying the dimension counts directly.

By the previously checked bond-span theorem, the associative algebra of native bond operators is exactly this plane commutant. This means products and linear combinations of bond operators generate it. It does not mean the algebra generated by the single Hamiltonian H alone is the entire commutant.

## Exact state-import elimination for neutral protocols

Every native H and plane-neutral observable has block form H_z=I2 tensor h_z and O_z=I2 tensor o_z. Time evolution, products of time-evolved neutral observables, commutators, and linear responses retain this form. Define the unnormalized multiplicity density matrix

rho_M,z = Tr_protected( Pi_z rho Pi_z ).

Then every neutral multitime expectation is determined only by these rho_M,z:

Tr rho O1(t1)...Ok(tk) = sum_z Tr rho_M,z o1,z(t1)...ok,z(tk).

Off-diagonal central-sector coherences and the protected-factor state are invisible to this algebra. Any two states with the same family rho_M,z have exactly the same neutral multitime correlations under native dynamics. The protected factor may be entangled with the multiplicity factor; only the reduced density matrices matter. It would be incorrect to change the joint state in a way that changes rho_M,z and claim equivalence merely because the operation was described verbally as changing the protected state.

In particular, the full plane-group twirl produces

rho_twirl = direct_sum_z [ (I2/2) tensor rho_M,z ],

without changing any neutral multitime expectation. If rho was stationary, rho_twirl remains stationary because every plane symmetry commutes with H. Thus the earlier requirement of a plane-invariant state can be imposed by twirling without extra assumptions FOR THIS NEUTRAL OBSERVABLE ALGEBRA. Charged observables can distinguish the original state from the twirled state; this does not prove that the physical preparation itself was symmetric. For example, the selected pure stationary benchmark can be replaced by this symmetric mixed representative when discussing exclusively neutral response, but not when inferring charged one-site correlations.

## What degeneracy does not supply

Within a central sector, the protected twofold energy degeneracy is exact because H is identity on the protected factor. A neutral observable is also identity there: its matrix element between orthogonal protected states at fixed multiplicity state vanishes. Thus transitions that change ONLY the protected factor cannot carry neutral spectral weight. Varying or mixing that factor alone cannot create a neutral zero-frequency mediator.

This does not exclude static/elastic neutral weight already present in the multiplicity state, degeneracies of h_z, degeneracies across central sectors, or ordinary conserved quantities and thermal fluctuations. Neutral operators cannot transition between different central sectors, but sector populations can affect measured neutral statistics. The central weights p_z=Tr rho_M,z and the multiplicity states rho_M,z/p_z remain preparation/state imports unless independently selected. Nor does the representation theorem imply a bulk scalar spectrum, gap, physical preparation, or cosmological coupling.
