# Focused plane-character selection check

Disposition: PASS for even periodic side lengths, the previously established isolated lowest band, and observables commuting with every stated plane generator. No lower bound on splittings inside that band is needed. This checks the first half of the draft, not its Gaussian-filter/Lieb–Robinson calculation.

## Phases and representation

Write X_x=product over the yz plane of sigma^x and Y_y=product over the xz plane of sigma^y. Distinct X generators and distinct Y generators commute. Each X_x,Y_y pair intersects on Lz sites, so their commutation sign is (-1)^Lz=+1. Every generator squares to one.

In a computational basis configuration, sigma^y|s>=i s|-s>, s=+/-1. On an aligned column pattern, therefore, Y_y contributes the phase

 i^(Lx Lz) product_x s_xy^Lz.

For even Lx and Lz this is exactly +1. X_x has no phase. Thus on the wall-free plaquette-positive configurations the action is genuinely a permutation representation, with no unaccounted projective sign. The root's even cube is a special case. Merely even Lz with odd transverse size would require retaining an extra constant Y-plane phase; the all-even hypothesis avoids that issue.

Every plaquette-positive pattern on the periodic transverse grid is s_xy=a_x b_y. This follows by fixing one row and one column and using the elementary plaquette identities to propagate; periodic closure introduces no additional holonomy for a field of site signs. The parametrization has exactly the redundancy (a,b)->(-a,-b). Hence there are 2^(Lx+Ly-1) patterns, and the plane flips act transitively with stabilizer generated by flipping all X planes and all Y planes together.

On the full physical Hilbert space the product of these generators is

 (product_x X_x)(product_y Y_y)=i^V Z_all=Z_all,

since V=Lx Ly Lz is divisible by four. This is not an identity operator on the entire Hilbert space. On the aligned-column band Z_all=product_xy s_xy^Lz=+1, so it is the identity there. Consequently this band carries the regular representation of the quotient Z2^(Lx+Ly-1). In full-generator character labels, the allowed characters obey the single constraint product_x chi(X_x) product_y chi(Y_y)=+1. Each allowed character occurs once, and the others occur zero times.

## Inheritance by the exact lowest band

The fixed-order normal form, its symmetry-preserving dressing, and the exact transformed Hamiltonian all commute with the same finite plane group. Along the interpolation from B_m to B_m+R_m, the established positive gap to the complement remains open. The lowest-band spectral projection thus varies continuously and commutes with every group projector. The ranks in each character sector are integer-valued continuous functions, hence constant. This argument is independent of the exponentially large total dimension and of possible level crossings or splittings inside the lowest band.

The exact band therefore has exactly one state per allowed character. Equivalently, each character line is automatically an eigenspace of the exact Hamiltonian restricted to that band. It is not necessary to identify its energy or prove that all the character energies coincide.

## Neutral observables and arbitrary ground density matrices

For A commuting with every plane generator, character conservation gives <chi|A|chi'>=0 whenever chi differs from chi'. Since each allowed character line in the band is one-dimensional, the band compression of A is diagonal in the same basis as the Hamiltonian. The same is true of B. Hence all band-to-band contributions to a retarded commutator vanish identically, including contributions at arbitrarily small nonzero band splittings. In fact no distinct band states are connected at all by a neutral observable.

An actual ground state may be a superposition of exactly degenerate lowest characters, and a ground density matrix may have coherences between them. The complete operator [A,tau_t(B)] is itself plane invariant, so all its matrix elements between different characters vanish. Its expectation therefore depends only on diagonal character populations. Each ground-character contribution has zero internal-band retarded term and a gap to all remaining intermediate states bounded below by the isolated-band gap (a conservative Delta0 Jz/2 is valid for small lambda). The same bound extends to every density matrix supported on the actual ground space.

Unknown diagonal values of A can produce elastic connected covariance in a mixture or superposition, but not a retarded internal-band susceptibility. This distinction is essential: the result is a neutral transition gap, not a statement that every connected covariance has a spectral gap or that the microscopic ground state is unique.

The local longitudinal distance-two C_j, individual native energy bonds, and commutators of neutral operators are indeed neutral under the specified planes. The conclusion excludes neither charged observables nor other parameter regimes, and does not assert a thermodynamic limit.
