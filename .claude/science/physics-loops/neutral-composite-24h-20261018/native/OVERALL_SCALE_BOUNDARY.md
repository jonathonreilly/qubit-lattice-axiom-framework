# Overall scale cannot independently create a long-range scalar

Direct root derivation,2026-10-18. Conditional on the existing single-scale native candidate H(J)=J H1, J>0, fixed lattice geometry and fixed dimensionless preparation (eigenstate or Gibbs parameter beta J). No new interaction or observational fit.

Write O_A(J)=J^a O_A(1), O_B(J)=J^b O_B(1). C has a=0; local e has a=1. Eigenvectors and dimensionless correlation lengths are independent of J; eigenfrequencies scale by J when hbar=1. The retarded response to the conventionally defined probe is

chi_AB(t;J)=J^(a+b) chi_AB(Jt;1),
chi_AB(omega;J)=J^(a+b-1) chi_AB(omega/J;1).

Thus static C/e mixed response is J-independent, CC response scales as1/J and ee response asJ, whenever these static limits exist and for the same dimensionless state. This is an exact scaling statement, not proof that the limits or a pole exist.

If a dimensionless low-energy pole actually exists, its frequency and velocity in lattice-coordinate units both scale with J. For a gapped dispersion omega²=J²[Delta1²+v1²q²+...], the static range v/Delta=v1/Delta1 is unchanged in lattice sites. Taking J small slows all these dynamics together; it does not turn a finite dimensionless-range channel into a massless long-range force. Equivalently chi_CC~J Z1/[J² Omega1(q)²-(omega+i0)²] for a pole represented with the stated response normalization, so its residue cannot be assumed fixed while adjusting J.

With an independently supplied physical spacing a_lat and hbar restored, speed is a_lat J v1/hbar and frequency J Delta1/hbar. Calibrating J or a_lat to a measured clock/ruler does not derive either scale or the physical identification of this mode. An exactly gapless H1 would of course remain gapless for every J>0; that possibility must be established from H1 and its state, not by rescaling. At fixed physical temperature, changing J changes beta J and may change the state/phase; that is a separate state-selection route, not this fixed-state rescaling argument.

This directly limits a proposed shortcut from the finite benchmark to gravity: the numerical gap near1.739J cannot be made a macroscopic mediator just by choosing a tiny J while silently holding its signal speed fixed. No bulk gap is inferred from that finite number. A demonstrated dimensionless long-range channel, physical scale bridge and common matter coupling remain required.

For this positive numerator use the +i retarded-commutator convention. At complex frequency z the scaling compares z/J; a numerical regulator eta must scale with J.
