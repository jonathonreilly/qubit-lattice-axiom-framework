# Protected plane factor and neutral observability

Direct root derivation. Conditional on the full open compass bond algebra; no new interaction. The plane Pauli vector space W has d=Lx+Ly+Lz-1. Let its restricted symplectic rank be2r and its radical dimension z=d-2r. A symplectic basis consists of zcentral generators and ranticommuting pairs. Clifford coordinates then decompose the N-qubit space into2^z central sectors, each with protected factor dimension2^r and multiplicity dimension2^(N-z-r). Plane algebra acts as M_(2^r) tensor I; its commutant, the neutral bond algebra, acts as I tensor M_(2^(N-z-r)).

For3³, d8,rank2,r1,z6. There are64central sectors, with protected dimension2 and neutral multiplicity dimension2^20. This matches the exact seven commuting-label reduction. Every native H and every plane-neutral observable act trivially on the protected factor. Any multitime product of such observables depends only on the reduced density matrix on the neutral multiplicity factor in each central sector; protected coherences, entanglement and mixing with that factor do not create an extra neutral response once those reduced states are fixed. Central-sector weights and neutral states remain physical preparation imports.

The protected factor guarantees spectral degeneracy but cannot supply a neutral mediator through transitions in that factor: neutral operators have zero offdiagonal matrix elements between its orthogonal basis states and identical diagonal matrix elements at fixed multiplicity state. Plane averaging changes its state to maximally mixed while preserving all neutral multitime correlators. This eliminates the need to choose a protected plane-qubit orientation for this neutral-observable sector, not the need to derive the physical state overall. Accidental/multiplicity degeneracies, critical limits and other operators are not excluded. The conclusion is not that every zero-energy mode in the model is invisible.
