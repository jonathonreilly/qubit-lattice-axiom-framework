# A native four-feature process and the limit of the XY charge

Status: conditional-support for the supplied finite four-rung Hamiltonian and its isolated low band. The exact algebraic certificate below strengthens the earlier numerical charge test. It is not a bulk phase theorem or an exclusion of every possible conserved charge.

Write H/J_perp=H0+lambda V, lambda=J_z/J_perp, for the exact positive-parity rung representation. The sixteen-dimensional low space P is the tensor product of four low rung doublets. Define Q0=sum_i tau3_i using the previously derived local operators. No term is added to H. At sufficiently small lambda the finite low spectral band is isolated and has a canonical polar effective Hamiltonian

K=E0 I+lambda² K2+lambda⁴ K4+lambda⁶ K6+... .

Conjugation by the product of G on alternating rungs sends V to -V and is identity on P, so the canonical K is even. The all-up and all-down Q0 eigenvectors, u and d, have Q0 eigenvalues +4 and -4. Both are eigenvectors of K2 with eigenvalue -3/sqrt(2), and of K4 with eigenvalue -13/(16sqrt(2)). These follow from the displayed linked second/fourth-order interactions, and the exact recursion independently verifies their lower Bloch coefficients.

## Exact sixth-order coefficient

The wave operator in intermediate normalization P Omega=P obeys

Omega0=P; b_n=P V Omega_(n-1); Omega_n=R[V Omega_(n-1)-sum_(j=1)^(n-1) Omega_(n-j)b_j],

where R=Q(E0-H0)^(-1)Q and b is the Bloch effective Hamiltonian relative to E0. Root evaluates this recursion with exact arithmetic in Q(sqrt(2),i), in an explicitly checked orthogonal (not unit-normalized) eigenbasis of each eight-state rung. The diagonal Gram matrix is (8,4,2,2,2,2,8,4). The local up/down vectors have norm squared 8, so the four-rung bra/ket matrix element is divided by 8^4. At each order below six, the projected down-column is checked exactly proportional to the original down vector before using its scalar lower coefficient in the next recursion. Real microscopic matrices relate the up column by complex conjugation.

The result in this fixed phase convention is

<u|b6|d> = -63 sqrt(2)/256 = -63/(128sqrt(2)).

This equals the canonical K6 matrix element. To see why, let M=Omega†Omega and S=M^(1/2). Then K-E0=S b S^(-1). The first intermediate states all have denominator -4sqrt(2), so M2=-K2/(4sqrt(2)); hence [S2,K2]=0 and b4=K4. At order six the difference K6-b6 is [S2,b4]+[S4,b2]. Its u,d matrix element vanishes because u,d share both lower eigenvalues. Odd coefficients vanish. This avoids treating a rational-looking floating result as a proof.

The separate full sixteen-column floating recursion checks Hermiticity, reproduces K2/K4, and gives ||[K6,Q0]||_F=63/16 to numerical precision. The exact certificate establishes the specified off-diagonal element; it does not certify every entry of K6. In physical units this amplitude has magnitude [63/(128sqrt(2))] J_z^6/J_perp^5. It is a simultaneous transition of four collective features generated by the existing bonds.

## Finite analytic-charge obstruction

Suppose an analytic charge Q(lambda)=Q0+lambda Q1+lambda² Q2+... commuted with K. Its order-six equation would be

[K6,Q0]+[K4,Q2]+[K2,Q4]=0.

The last two commutators have zero u,d matrix element for arbitrary Q2,Q4, because the respective lower eigenvalues coincide. The first is -8<u|K6|d>, which the exact certificate shows is nonzero. Thus no such analytic deformation of this specified Q0 exists in this finite four-rung low band. The result allows other charges, nonanalytic limits, and other symmetry definitions. It does not settle emergent infrared symmetry, relevance of this process at long distance, isotropic coupling, or three-dimensional behavior.

This is useful positively as an explicitly derived interaction beyond the XY approximation. A free conserved-number description would omit a real microscopic process. Whether the interacting collective theory supports a useful scalar field remains open.
