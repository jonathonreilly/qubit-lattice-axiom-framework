# Prior-art context

Primary sources inspected October18 2026:
- Dorier, Becca and Mila, *The Quantum Compass Model on the Square Lattice*, https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0501708 . The abstract describes high-order perturbation theory and exponentially collapsing low-energy manifolds in the anisotropic two-dimensional model.
- Trousselet, Oles and Horsch, *Magnetic properties of nanoscale compass-Heisenberg planar clusters*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.0102v2 . The abstract discusses column-flip excitations and an effective XYZ model in a model with Heisenberg perturbations.

These are prior-art context, not imported coefficients or evidence for the present three-dimensional continuum. We do not add the Heisenberg interaction, import its effective model, or claim novelty for column tunneling. The native fixed-L coefficient and growing-box readout bounds are derived in the linked root proof.
