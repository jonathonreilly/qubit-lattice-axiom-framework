# Assumptions And Imports

- Finite runner block Dirac matrix `D`.
- Explicit finite source `J`.
- Invertibility of `D` and `D+J` on the checked source neighborhood.
- Standard finite determinant, trace, and matrix derivative identities.

- P2 scalar-generator selection.
- Tier-A admitted-input propagation.
- Electroweak `v` comparator.
- Canonical hierarchy baseline as a theorem input.
