# Assumptions And Imports

## Load-Bearing Admissions

- P1: scalar observables are additive over independent subsystems.
- P2: the scalar bosonic generator is a continuous function of `|Z|` alone.

## Checked Candidate Properties

- Source evenness of the selected `log|det|` candidate on the runner block.
- Finite-block regularity near zero source for the selected candidate.
- Zero-source baseline convention and additive-shift invariance for local
  source-derivative observables.

## Out Of Scope

- Derivation of P1 or P2 from retained primitives.
- Promotion of `CPT_EXACT_NOTE` or any upstream row.
- Numerical `v` readout as a load-bearing input.
- Any audit retag. The pipeline only queues this source for independent audit.
