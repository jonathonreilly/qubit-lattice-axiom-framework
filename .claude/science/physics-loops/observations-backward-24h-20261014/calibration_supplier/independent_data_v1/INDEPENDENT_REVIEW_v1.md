# Independent calibration-supplier computation before author exposure

I independently decoded the first 600 complete records of each pinned X and Ka RSR product, preserving their 71 header fields and all 1,200,000 complex samples in the prescribed calculations. Entire raw files and labels were SHA256-checked; only these first 600 payloads per band were decoded anew. The fixed split is samples 0:500 for frequency selection and 500:1000 for the subsequent diagnostic. These raw products were already exposed in earlier work. This is implementation independence, not an independent empirical test.

The stdlib decoder uses signed big-endian Q,I words and returns corrected I+iQ=(2I+1)+i(2Q+1). All 1200 newly computed corrected-I/Q hashes also match the earlier, separately implemented M9 integer-word decoder hashes. Raw-record, raw-payload, full corrected-I/Q and both half-block corrected-I/Q hashes are retained per record. All first-600 time stamps, sequence values, sample rates and lengths match the declared window. There are no recorded hardware errors, override flags, extreme components or time/grid anomalies in this selection. No row was clipped, removed or reclassified by a fit.

The independent optimizer uses a 16000-bin FFT and its own golden-section direct-correlation refinement over the fixed one-bin radius. Its training input contains only the first half. It is a local numerical procedure, not a global-frequency theorem. Four known-tone controls, a signed-I/Q control, the DFT-rank and Parseval controls and two independent beta-threshold controls passed: nine controls in total. New execution exited 0 with empty stderr in 8.496001459192485 seconds wall time (8.249369082972407 seconds internal), against 180 seconds. No author calibration-supplier code or result was read before these artifacts were frozen.

On validation, the complex amplitude is projected out at the training-derived frequency. The ledgers retain covariance at the seven prescribed lags, both raw and normalized pseudo-covariance, derotated alternatives, and low/high non-DC DFT energies. Median normalized lag-one magnitude is 0.23212111381190248 for X and 0.8361340694441263 for Ka; median rank-normalized high/low energy ratio is 0.6116630657571722 for X and 0.08676333441418502 for Ka. These describe the exposed residuals; projection changes their dependence and these are not calibrated correlation p-values or physical noise covariance estimates.

The independently derived thresholds are carrier 0.10342141434228364, high/low 1.1410430727198448 and low/high 1.127315389589019 at alpha=0.01. At alpha=0.01/1200 they are 0.15870819230730582, 1.2373809690894606 and 1.2223626032099921. The finite-domain proof, ranks, quantization premise and allocations are set out in the pre-exposure PRECHECK_v1.md. Carrier confidence at the true candidate uses the extra rank-one direction of the rank-two augmentation and residual complex rank 498; color ranks are 248 and 251. The supplied conditional spherical-direction law eliminates a radial scale from the ratios, but does not derive that direction law from observations.

The real-arithmetic sufficient enclosure uses the declared k=0.999m and Dirichlet arc radius. At alpha=0.01, 1192 records obtain an outside-arc enclosure; the finite-cell color calculation numerically excludes every candidate frequency for 1081 records (X 489, Ka 592). Eight Ka records obtain no enclosure, and 111 additional X records are not excluded by these sufficient bounds. At alpha=0.01/1200, 1179 records obtain an enclosure and 746 obtain numerical whole-domain exclusion (X 168, Ka 578). Twenty-one Ka records obtain no enclosure; another 432 X records and one Ka record remain unexcluded. Every non-enclosure and non-exclusion is listed with its exact record identity. A failure to exclude is not model confirmation.

The quantization margin is a supplied ideal-cell Euclidean outer radius, not physical receiver noise. The Lipschitz cell margin establishes the stated implication in exact arithmetic. Floating FFTs, beta quantiles, optimizer, norms and extrema have no outward-rounding error budget here, so these output exclusions are explicitly numerical evidence, not interval certificates. Family-level bookkeeping needs no record independence, but it still depends on each record's conditional error-direction premise. An observed numerical rejection challenges that combined tone/direction/quantizer model; it does not identify which supplier fails or derive a scalar signal, calibrated receiver covariance or a physical noise upper bound.
