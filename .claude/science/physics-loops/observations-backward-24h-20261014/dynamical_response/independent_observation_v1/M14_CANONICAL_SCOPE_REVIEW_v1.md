## Covariance proof correspondence

With C_AB=Re E[AB*] and a common differentiable ensemble, the ordinary product rule gives `(C_SW)'=C_S'W+C_SW'`. In the smaller W-weighted collection, the note correctly keeps the separate time-leg quantity eta in `M=P'+P(2gW−eta)`. A common deterministic transfer is sufficient to replace eta by gW, but arbitrary free states do not supply it. A finite-ensemble counterexample to that replacement is not a universal observational obstruction.

The new larger collection removes that separate derivative by a different equation, rather than by assuming it. From the already checked momentum and Euler equations,

    V'=hV−(S+T)/2,
    S'=−(1+x/2)S−yT/2−JR V,

one obtains `S'−yV'=aS+cV`, with `a=(y−x−2)/2` and `c=−hy−JR`. Multiplication by `(S−yV)*` and real expectation produce the stated symmetric total derivatives. The proof holds y as an epoch coefficient; it does not differentiate the time-dependent random variable S−y(N)V and omit a y' term. The amended parentheses in Eqs17–18 remove a notation ambiguity without changing this argument.

The displayed five-column row and lift `(x,y,y²,JR−xy/2,JRy)` agree with the independently frozen derivation after its explicitly recorded invertible coordinate transformation. Both nonlinear consistency tests are essential. Rank alone identifies a lifted vector, not physical coefficients. Recovery additionally needs J>0, R>0, nonzero y and x/y>0. Five rows are sufficient, not claimed minimal. Extra rows are consistency tests; exact monomial equalities are not statistical rejection rules for noisy observations.

Rank is attainable within the stated free-state initial-value domain. The map from `(C_SS,C_SV,C_VV,C_ST,C_VT)` to the row has determinant1/8; positive-definite (S,V,T) covariances have an open domain. The independent finite bounded ensemble construction supplies an alternative nonzero-determinant witness. With b nonzero, phi,v,X can represent finite S,V,T, Z remains free and positive dust permits the constraint to fix n. A sufficiently small common amplitude preserves rank and local linear validity. This is neither observed rank nor a uniform conditioning guarantee, a selected probability law, arbitrary-amplitude validity or a complete cosmic-history construction. Cross-band correlations do not invalidate the exact row equations, but belong in a real likelihood's joint error model.

The five-covariance result therefore supplies a meaningful ideal input reduction: no direct slip covariance or coherent-mode phase and no separately measured derivative time leg are required. It still needs the calibrated same-epoch auto/cross spectra, their total derivatives, common background quantities and nondegenerate row structure. Neither the inspected sources nor this algebra supply that measurement operator.
