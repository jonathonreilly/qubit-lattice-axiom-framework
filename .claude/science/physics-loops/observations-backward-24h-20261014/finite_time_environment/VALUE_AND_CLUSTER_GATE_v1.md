## Cluster decision

OPEN for one coherent unit comprising the temporal norm, moving functional and
force/energy bridge. These are interacting steps toward one observation bound,
so they are not separated into multiple PRs. The result is mathematically
self-contained. This is a repository contribution, not a claim of new Fourier
analysis, new gravity or an exhaustive literature novelty search.

The previously checked source-age and localized-data working calculations remain
preserved in the campaign workspace. They are not proof premises of this
publication, which supplies an alternative temporal observable. They may be used
later when composing forcing/history error budgets. Publishing more examples of
the same norm would not warrant another PR; the next substantial target is an
observationally justified residual or energy budget with actual clock/path
calibration.

## Negative-claim boundary

The canonical claim is a positive conditional operator inequality. Rectangular
window, omitted derivative and dropped geometric-term controls are specific
finite examples in the declared model. No universal negative theorem, primitive
exhaustion or assertion that further derivation is impossible is proposed.
Historical working exploration is retained locally, not promoted as a general
negative result. The N1-N8 route-exhaustion gate is not claimed passed. Its
strict applicability must be reassessed if a later unit proposes such a claim.
