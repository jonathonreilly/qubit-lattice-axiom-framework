    delta'=W+3Phi',
    W'+(2+h)W=-K^2 Psi/H^2.

Retain the explicitly conditional Weyl/source relation and coupled scalar response

    L=B delta/F,
    -K^2 Psi=rho delta(1+q)/(2F).

Then with P=L/W and P3=W'/W,

    q_obs=[2a^3 E^2/(3P)](P3+2+h)-1.

No continuity approximation is used in this quotient. It still needs the supplied linear dust Euler model, physical clock/H, nonzero W and L, a nonzero Fourier wavenumber, and the light-scalar response for q_obs=q_geom. It does not make that gravitational response exact or relativistically complete.

Define the density primitive

    J_d(N)=integral from N0 to N of W(s)ds + 3[Phi(N)-Phi(N0)].

Then delta=J_d+d exactly under linear continuity. The supplied candidate closure becomes

    (b-q)(J_d+d)=b(1-3q)vL,
    [-q J_d,1,-q,-(1-3q)L] . (u,d,w,v)=-J_d,
    u=1/b, w=u d.

Every algebraic rank/compatibility/domain statement in the original proof holds with I replaced by J_d. The differential form must be changed, rather than blindly retaining P=L/W: it is

    (r_b L)'=(W+3Phi')/v,
    [L/(W+3Phi')][r_b L'/L+r_b,q q']=1/v,

where division by W+3Phi' is permitted only when nonzero. The integrated form avoids this extra division and need not differentiate Phi. W!=0 does not imply J_d'=delta'!=0. The Möbius necessity for two distinct shapes still holds as a pointwise identity in J_d; treating q as a single-valued varying function of J_d requires the corresponding local invertibility/nonconstant-J_d qualification. Constant-density portions need separate treatment.

Phi must be supplied in the same mode normalization as W and L. Under their stated physical definitions it can also be reconstructed using the velocity Euler equation and the measured Weyl sum:

    Phi= -3H0^2 L/(a^3 K^2) + H^2[W'+(2+h)W]/K^2.

This is a conditional observation map, requiring the same mode, clock, Fourier identification, relative calibration and linear dust dynamics. It must not be replaced by an independently normalized potential. A common unknown constant factor multiplying W,L,Phi,J_d and d cancels in the inverse. A time-dependent gain or unrelated channel gains generally do not. If Phi itself is not supplied or reconstructed, adding its unknown contribution is not an executed reconstruction.

This corrects one kinematic omission. The leading Weyl/source relation can still omit gravitational time derivatives, density-gauge corrections, curvature, effective scalar masses/mixing and other sources. The note therefore claims exact algebra and exact linear dust kinematics conditional on the stated gravitational response, not a full relativistic modified-gravity observation theorem.
