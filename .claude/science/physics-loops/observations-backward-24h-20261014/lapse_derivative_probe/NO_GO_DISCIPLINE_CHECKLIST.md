# Scope discipline for the first-lapse-derivative extension

Claim type: bounded_theorem. Actual current surface status: conditional-support.
This is a conditional comparison and an exact separation of two sufficient
criteria. It is not a no-go for scalar environments or an empirical exclusion.

N1. Five distinct mathematical/measurement routes were considered: original
four-curvature positive Green comparison (M17, supplies a valid alternative);
lapse rescaling (attempted here, removes the direct lapse Laplacian); potential
maximum comparison (M17, retains a numerical Hv supplier); signed weak curvature
functionals (independent measurement derivation, not interchangeable with C_+);
complete coupled matter/metric realization (not executed; remains open).
These are route distinctions, not a claim that five physical escape routes fail.

N2. The retained conditions are conditional action, static complete fields,
regular bounded physical geometry, complete scalar boundary and calibrated
geometric error envelopes. Static geometry does not imply static fields.
Derivative envelopes imply some regularity, but qualitative C3 regularity alone
does not supply numerical errors. Boundary control and interior metric coverage
are distinct. Clock normalization cancels and is not a separate condition.

N3. The proof explicitly names its action, parameter, staticity, geometry,
regularity, boundary, source-sign and measurement assumptions. The exponent is
fixed by cancellation in an algebraic change of variables; it is not a state
selector. No physical matter realization, fitted observation or primitive is
hidden in the oscillatory synthetic metric.

N4. Only M17's exact positive-Ricci comparison criterion is tested by the
oscillatory family. Its eta<1 hypothesis fails; its conclusion is not disproved.
The smooth family does not witness a complete candidate or universal measurement
impossibility. Nearest landed cosmological curvature and clock results have
different temporal targets and are search context, not reused counterexamples.

N5. Primary stdout distinguishes per-element rescaling/variation, per-site
synthetic profiles, unexecuted dynamical modes, finite-domain comparison and
unexecuted lattice/TOE closure. Numerical grids do not prove the continuum
theorem; the written positivity proof and analytic rational bounds do.

N6. Positive routes remain: use either valid geometric criterion, supply a
calibrated whole-domain envelope, derive a matching weak certificate, or solve
the full field/matter environment. No local failed numerical check terminates
the campaign; original failures and their resource/integration repairs remain.

N7. Counterfactuals are explicit: vanishing/nonpositive lapse, missing boundaries,
time-dependent fields, or eta_t>=1 leave this proof's domain. Neither a spatial
metric value fit nor an arbitrary interpolation supplies derivative errors.
Those limits do not rule out other comparison mechanisms.

N8. No theory-wide no-go, source selection, empirical acceptance, formal audit,
retained-grade status or complete TOE is claimed. The extension reduces one
direct derivative supplier, while spatial curvature and central/coordinate
calibration remain conditional. This is intended for the existing coherent M17 PR.
