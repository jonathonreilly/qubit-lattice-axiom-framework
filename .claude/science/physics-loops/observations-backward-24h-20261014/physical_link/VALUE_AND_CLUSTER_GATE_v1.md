## Cluster-cap decision

OPEN — the proposed third unit supplies a distinct observation construction.

The preceding two units address the ideal scalar inverse and the conversion of
an exposed archived count product. Their proofs do not supply the response of
multiple chirped coherent channels to arbitrary initial receiver phases. The
new unit adds that load-bearing operator, an exact construction that eliminates
the initial phases from selected contrasts, and the joint operator/count error
bound. Those results resolve a concrete missing measurement assumption without
assigning a preferred initial state. The cold-plasma algebra and projection
tools are known; their role is stated, and no new fundamental law is claimed.

The claim type remains bounded_theorem, so type diversity alone does not
justify opening it. Its different mathematical object and physical bridge do.
The source and computation are self-contained, read no ancestor result, and can
be reviewed independently. The three helper modules form one coherent unit
rather than three further PRs. Separate implementations exposed real errors
in normalization, joint bounds and mean assumptions, which were corrected and
preserved as witnesses. A single focused unit now has more review value than
leaving that corrected construction dispersed across working notes.

The scope is limited. No complete three-link observable has been extracted from
the development archive; metadata and derived atmospheric calibration products
are not substituted for such an observation. The scalar action, history,
scales and environmental solution remain open. Further PRs consisting only of
new projection examples should be backlogged; the next substantive priority is
the finite-time environmental/history approximation and its observed scales.
