# Scope and alternatives for projected covariance

The target is a constructive conditional necessary constraint. No general no-go,
minimal-data theorem or retained action claim is proposed. These N1–N8 entries
bound its local failures and identify what could change them.

1. Object: ideal full-sky scalar shear/velocity covariances on 0<r<2chi. Sources:
   the canonical note, fixed synthetic protocol, independent geometry, weak and
   primary-source reports; exact source identities accompany the final cache.
2. Premises: calibrated flat expanding geometry, Born approximation, scalar
   flow, smooth real homogeneous/isotropic signal correlations, supplied field
   law, separated noise, no arbitrary mask. No initial covariance is selected
   for the implication; synthetic ensembles are chosen solely as controls.
3. Routes tried: direct velocity-potential integration (needs domain information),
   ordered differential elimination (works conditionally), cancellation of the
   measured angular nulls (works only for stated span), lifted coefficient
   inversion (rank witness but severe conditioning), weak integration (works
   with exact boundary qualification; finite-window supplier remains open).
4. Failure object: E has a nonzero smooth kernel on positive r; no global PSD
   physical covariance is claimed for that kernel. Rank/conditioning examples
   concern the saved synthetic matrices only. The weak moving-boundary
   counterexample concerns an insufficient weight-jet condition, not physics.
5. Resolution: per-element operators and coefficients; per-site no environment
   solved; per-mode five homogeneous spectral shells; per-block two backgrounds
   and a separate exact local rank example; lattice-wide no fundamental closure.
   The primary prints all five scopes. No route is ruled out at another scope.
6. Live alternatives: more angular information or calibrated masks, different
   velocity geometry, unequal-time correlations, finite basis restrictions,
   regularized estimators with measured error budgets, and other dynamical
   classes. None is dismissed or silently adopted as a selector.
7. Prior comparison: M14 supplied a coefficient lift from unprojected scalar
   covariance histories. This block derives the physical radial projection,
   changing-distance compatibility operator, angular-null cancellation and
   adjoint error functional. Earlier generic covariance feasibility does not
   supply these observation operators. The prior-search and repo-context records
   delimit the search; no worldwide priority claim is made.
8. Primitive/claim boundary: registered unit, isotropy and realized-state roles
   do not supply actual geometry, signal ensemble, measurement calibration,
   action, potential, initial history, scale or environment. Those remain open.
   No formal audit verdict or authority surface is changed.
