boundary of BLOCK03_MATERIAL_CANDIDATE_v1.md; it does not adopt a closed spatial

Keep the declared static dust stress and geometry, but ask what would happen
if that geometry and stress were independently justified through an equator of
a complete round spatial three-sphere. Proper radial distance chi gives

    r=sin(sqrt(k)*chi)/sqrt(k),
    ds^2=-dt^2+dchi^2+r(chi)^2 dOmega^2,
    0<=chi<=pi/sqrt(k).

The zero of B at the equator is an areal-coordinate turning point, not a lapse
horizon. A scalar smooth in chi must remain finite there. The angular-minus-time
field equation, written before dividing by dr/dchi, is

    2kF-rho0=sqrt(k)*cot(sqrt(k)*chi) dF/dchi.        (1)

Away from the equator it integrates to

    F=rho0/(2k)+A/cos(sqrt(k)*chi)^2.                (2)

requires A=0. The same argument applies on the southern hemisphere. Thus in
this enlarged, stipulated static spherical domain, a finite scalar is constant,
F0=rho0/(2k). One can also evaluate (1) at the equator for a smooth scalar:
the derivative coefficient vanishes and directly forces the same value.

    -3kF0=-rho0-V(F0),   -kF0=-V(F0),

undetermined, apart from any separately imposed positive-kinetic condition;

be an existence example, not a derivation of the physical potential.
