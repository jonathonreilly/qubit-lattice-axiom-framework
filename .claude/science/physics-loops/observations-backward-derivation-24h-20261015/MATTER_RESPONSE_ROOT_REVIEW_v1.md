## What was established

Within the supplied canonical scalar, universal conformal dust and flat FLRW
class, the direct matter-clock system reconstructs A, V and the Einstein clock
from compatible histories without supplying an entire A(tau) function. It
requires registered h, signed canonical b and fixed-bare-density kappa, all
still ideal inputs. The narrower C!=0 reduction has a degree-six filter. A
nonzero polynomial at an interior time bounds scalar-velocity histories there
by six; continuous normalization freedom and singular histories remain.

The independent positive-density C=N=0 example shows that the excluded set
can be physically admissible within that conditional class. It is preserved
with attribution and personally rechecked. The v2 note narrows the counting
language and adds this example; original v1 source/output bytes remain intact.

The scalar, metric and dust response equations retain source variations,
acceleration and the metric constraints. Eliminating the bare density produces
mu-alpha*Fforce and additional source/derivative terms. It therefore does not
justify identifying a fitted response denominator with fixed-bare-density mu.
The full matter-time equation has damping 3(H_J-bx), with the conformal
acceleration included once. A post-exposure root derivation verifies the
Hamiltonian propagation and spatial trace. No minimum or initial mode was
selected to make the correspondence work.
