Prepared after the checked neighborhood exploration report, as a prospective

## A concrete prior-art comparison

The inspected main text of
[Chen et al., arXiv:1612.00490v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1612.00490v3)
reports an experimentally realized 1:3 internal resonance and a ringdown
interval sustained by energy exchange between mechanical modes. It also gives
a simplified damped Duffing-plus-linear-mode model and notes a measurement
bandwidth limitation on small amplitude oscillations. Those facts make it
relevant to hidden environmental dynamics, but they do not validate our
particular coupling or conservative equations.

Our supplied w*q^2 coupling drives w at twice the imposed q frequency.
The paper's observed 1:3 resonance therefore cannot simply be labeled the
same coupling. Its simplified model is an approximation with damping and
feedback, not a derivation of our action. The actual symmetry, modes, actuation
and observable map must decide which terms are admissible. This comparison is
exposed development context. No quantitative fit to that paper was performed.

Read scope: arXiv version3 main-text HTML, including model equation1 and
experimental/ringdown discussion; not the supplemental derivations or raw
data. A publisher-page text search returned an internal retrieval error.
That failure says nothing about whether a reusable dataset exists.

## Required observations and calibrations

Before testing the adaptive candidate against a real record, obtain or derive
the following for a specified apparatus:

- Time stamps and a known time-response/bandwidth calibration.
- A physically defined coordinate and imposed history, with uncertainty in
  its first two time derivatives; an arbitrary voltage label is not yet q.
- The conjugate source force or an independently justified conversion, with
  its uncertainty and common versus setting-dependent instrument effects.
- The environmental degrees of freedom, permitted coupling symmetries and
  estimated dissipative effects. The conservative candidate cannot silently
  absorb dissipation into a separately fitted response for every trajectory.
- A documented trial and window-selection rule, including exclusions,
  saturation, missing records and actuator transients.

The division-free block18 test avoids selecting the initial hidden state and
avoids a coordinate denominator. It does not remove these measurement
requirements. The multimode frequency test additionally needs a justified
harmonic extraction, transient treatment and a pole-free range.

## Separation before a real comparison

Calibration establishes physical units and response/error bounds independently
of the candidate's fitted coefficients wherever possible. Exposed development
histories select and challenge the candidate terms and estimate one shared
coefficient set. Freeze that set or its stated uncertainty region before
opening reserved histories.

Reserve both motion histories and weak test-function shapes. Block19 gives
an explicit force perturbation missed by a finite selected test span, so
additional functions are part of model checking, not decorative repetitions.
A physically justified bandwidth or model-error bound is needed before a
finite family could supply a broader certificate. Record failures without
moving them into the calibration set after seeing their residuals.

Use the stated deterministic residual bounds when their hypotheses actually
hold. A small residual is consistency with a specified observation contract,
not a unique action determination. Test alternative mechanisms and include
actuator/environment changes that might discriminate endpoint-equivalent
models. If a common candidate fails, preserve that failure before changing
its action, terms or measurement identification.

## Connection back to the scalar campaign

The transferable result is a method for comparing environments under one
action while eliminating nuisance preparation from a necessary test. Applying
it to the scalar theory requires deriving the actual observable/field/source
bridge in that setting. Mechanical oscillator variables cannot be identified
with scalar gravity, quantum state amplitudes or a Born record process merely
because their equations contain similar symbols.
