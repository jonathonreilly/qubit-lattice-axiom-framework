# Recursive mechanisms: a research direction, not a new physical premise

Status: author research intake and elementary linear-response probe. No retained
claim, independent check, empirical validation or new theory selection is made.
This supplements the fixed-action response route; it does not restart the clock.

## Public source and its scope

Nick Cammarata's [Neural Networks Are Already Clean](https://already-clean.goodfire.com/)
is explicitly a working hypothesis. Its proposed alternative to arbitrary
packing is recursive generalization: related mechanisms can compose and branch.
The relevant transfer is to study causal reuse and make predictions across
contexts. The essay's usage concerns learned representations, not quantum
superposition. Its fractal language does not establish a spacetime fractal,
exact scale invariance or any particular dimension.

The essay acknowledges the valid experiments in
[Toy Models of Superposition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.10652). Its disagreement
concerns how well those tasks capture reuse in larger networks. Therefore this
intake neither dismisses representational superposition in all models nor treats
the essay's broad interpretation as settled empirical evidence.

Relevant prior physics connections include
[Mehta and Schwab](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.3831), on a particular variational
renormalization/RBM mapping, and
[Lin, Tegmark and Rolnick](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.08225), on locality,
symmetry and hierarchical composition. These are reasons to formulate precise
questions, not a derivation of our scalar action or a claim that all neural
networks implement renormalization. Only their stated abstract-level scope was
used here; no theorem from either paper enters the calculation below.

## Translation into the current campaign

1. Shared causal response: derive the coupled scalar/metric/material response
   with one fixed action. Use the same disturbance to predict several identified
   readouts. Fit permitted nuisance parameters on development inputs, freeze
   them, and test withheld channels or environments. Separate environment fits
   and an attractive latent-variable diagram do not establish causal reuse.
2. Scale consistency: compare dimensionless observables at several resolutions.
   Derive any coarse-graining operation and coupling flow instead of declaring
   exact self-similarity. Test withheld scales and alternative non-fractal models.
   Inverting coarse-graining need not recover a unique microscopic action.
3. Representation checks: compare physical response functions after consistent
   changes of field variables or internal coordinates. Sparse or interpretable
   coordinates alone do not identify physical degrees of freedom. The original
   matter coupling and measurement map must transform too.

These are author proposals prompted by the essay, not results asserted by it.
No action, potential, initial history, absolute scale or measurement bridge is
retired by the analogy. In particular, the universe is not assumed to optimize
a neural-network training loss.

## A small observation-facing consequence of a shared mode

Use a supplied, finite-dimensional linearized dynamical operator L(s), analytic
near a complex frequency s0. The physical perturbation/readout contract is

    L(s) q = B(s) f,       y = C(s) q + D(s) f.

Suppose its inverse has an isolated simple pole there with one-dimensional
right and left nullspaces, generated by v and w respectively, and
w^T L'(s0) v != 0. B, C and direct response D must be analytic there.
The residue is

    R = Res[y/f] = (C(s0) v) (w^T B(s0)) / (w^T L'(s0) v).

To see this, write L=L0+(s-s0)L1+... and L^-1=P/(s-s0)+Q+....
The two inverse identities imply L0 P=P L0=0, hence P=alpha v w^T.
Left-multiplying L0 Q+L1 P=I by w^T gives
alpha (w^T L1 v) w^T=w^T, fixing alpha as displayed. Multiplying by
analytic B and C and adding analytic D gives the stated observed residue.
This is standard local linear algebra re-derived here, not a new physics law.

Consequently R has rank at most one, and all its 2 by 2 minors vanish:

    R_ab R_cd - R_ad R_cb = 0.

For two independently calibrated forcing channels and two readouts this is
R11 R22=R12 R21. It is a conditional shared-mode consistency relation, not a
generic identity for a complete measured response at an arbitrary frequency.

The probe uses dimensionless exact rational matrices and two distinct poles.
It checks a non-normal internal realization, its residues, consistent internal
coordinate changes, and both invertible and rank-losing readout changes. It
also checks the important failure cases: the full response and the sum of two
unresolved modes can have rank two, and degenerate modes at a common pole can
have a rank-two residue. A singular readout can conceal that rank.

This does NOT count fundamental fields. A single field can have many spatial
modes; several fields can contribute to one isolated mode. Continuum spectra,
gauge constraints, cuts, boundary-dependent modes, direct response, source
correlation, calibration error and unresolved poles need separate treatment.
The observed relation is necessary under the stated premises, not sufficient
evidence for the revised scalar theory. No physical scalar/metric/dust operator,
pole or apparatus has yet been derived by this probe.
