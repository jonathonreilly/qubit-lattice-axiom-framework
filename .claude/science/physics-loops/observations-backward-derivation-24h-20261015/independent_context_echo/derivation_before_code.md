# Independent proof reconstruction before runner inspection

Pauli multiplication gives BA=(a.b)I+i(b cross a).sigma, so U0=[(1+a.b)I-i(a cross b).sigma]/sqrt(2(1+a.b)). This unitary has determinant one and transports A to B. If P_j carries the anchor Z to A_j, then exp(-i t A_j/2) P_j = P_j exp(-i t Z/2). Induction therefore factors the full product into P_final exp(-i kappa sum(n.(a_j cross a_{j+1})) Z/2). Constant laboratory-frame n is needed for the unweighted W formula. On the positive anchor eigenstate the extra phase has the negative sign stated.

Let h=pi/N, c=cos beta and a0=(sin beta,0,c). The rotating spin frame is R=exp(-i h sigma_z), with R^N=-I. Writing successive pair rotations as conjugates of one edge, the loop is minus the Nth power of R^-1 U0(a1,a0). This matrix fixes a0 and its positive-eigenstate eigenvalue has argument atan(c tan h). The -I produces phase -pi+N atan(c tan h) on the branch approaching zero at beta=0. For N>=3 and 0<beta<pi/2, h in (0,pi/3], c>0, phase in (-pi,0); no arctangent branch crossing occurs. Neighbor dot products are above -1. Direct cross-product sum has zero transverse components and z component N sin² beta sin(2h).

The desired half-phase angle is the strict midpoint between atan(cA tan h) and h. Monotonic tan on (0,pi/2) proves cA<cB<1 and betaB in (0,betaA). A connector from z to first vertex followed at the end by its exact reverse makes a conjugated loop; opposite edge cross products cancel in W. For the rotated southern loop z dot first vertex is -cos betaB>-1, although the connector becomes ill-conditioned in the excluded betaB=0 limit. Proper rotation preserves the positive eigenphase and rotates W; pi about x makes W point -z. Each conjugated loop is exp(i gammaB Z), hence their product exp(i 2 gammaB Z) equals exp(i gammaA Z) as a full SU(2) matrix. This depends on determinant one, exact anchor return and the positive-eigenphase convention; it is not equality of arbitrary matrices merely from an intensity.

Path B has zero W, path A positive z W. For real fixed kappa and n the phase difference is -kappa n_z W/2. An unwrapped bound eps gives the component bound 2eps/W. Three orthogonal known axes and the same n,kappa give the Euclidean norm bound by Pythagoras. Modulo-2pi measurements alone allow aliases and do not bound arbitrary coupling; context drift across comparisons also invalidates combining components.

For an isotropic unit n held constant within each whole path, n_z is uniform on [-1,1]. Its characteristic integral is (1/2) integral exp(-i kappa W x/2) dx = sin(kappa W/2)/(kappa W/2), continuously one at zero. This is signed real coherence; visibility magnitude is absolute sinc. Path B has exactly unit extra coherence irrespective of its per-path n. Context resampling within B subloops would invalidate cancellation. Neither the ensemble nor the contextual coupling or actual phase readout is derived. The source correctly calls this coherence, avoiding a negative-visibility claim.
