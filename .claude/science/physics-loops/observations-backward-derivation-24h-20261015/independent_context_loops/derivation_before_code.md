# Independent mathematical reconstruction

Finite transport: move exp(-i theta_i A_(i-1)/2) through U_(i-1)...U_1 using A_(i-1)*(U_(i-1)...U_1)=(U_(i-1)...U_1)*A_0. Induction gives the asserted single right twist with summed real theta. On a closed path, the base product stabilizes A0 and is diagonal in its ± eigenbasis. For fixed n, lambda*n.(a-b) is a coboundary. G(b)UabG(a)†=Uab*exp[-i lambda*(n.a-n.b)*A/2]. Cross-product edge sums generally survive. Edge-dependent n destroys that telescoping unless it is genuinely a difference of vertex scalars.

Latitude: adjacent normalized spinor overlaps are w=cβ²+exp(iΔ)sβ², where cβ=cos(beta/2),sβ=sin(beta/2). The shortest rotor gives minus their argument. Factor w=exp(iΔ/2)[cos(Δ/2)-i cos(beta)sin(Δ/2)]. Since Δ/2=pi/N<pi/2, its real part after factoring is positive. Summing gives gamma=-pi+N atan(cos(beta)tan(pi/N)) modulo 2pi. At both poles all axis transports are identity; the selected continuous phase branch differs by -2pi at the south pole. Cross-edge transverse components sum to zero and Wz=N sin²(beta)sinΔ. Taking N to infinity yields gamma=-pi(1-cos beta) and added phase -kappa*pi*sin²(beta).

Uniform spherical context: projecting onto any fixed unit direction gives a uniform variable in [-1,1], so its characteristic function is sinc. Independent edges factor the characteristic function; constant context combines the vectors first. They are different assumptions. For latitude, |v|²=4 sin²(beta)sin²(pi/N)[1-sin²(beta)sin²(pi/N)]. Taylor log sinc x=-x²/6+O(x⁴) implies the stated -kappa²*pi²*sin²(beta)/(6N)+O(N^-3). Under kappa_N=kappa0*sqrtN the leading term is finite and the higher terms vanish. Negative sinc is a signed characteristic function, not negative visibility.

Zero-mean isotropic covariance immediately gives Var(sum kappa*n_i.v_i/2)=kappa²/12 sum Cij vi.vj. This is not enough to calculate the characteristic function unless its distribution is separately supplied.

Record mixture: conditional on a complete exogenous context sequence and a uniform seed independent of it, the full-branch reservoir theorem gives product Bernoulli probabilities for the resulting prescribed p_t. A fresh seed each trial is unnecessary. But using the block04 map at p=0 or1 is undefined as written; p(n) includes these endpoints, notably q=0 at alpha=0 or pi. Remedy: define p=1 as forced port0 with unchanged seed and p=0 as forced port1 with unchanged seed, and likewise leave the complementary local state unchanged. This preserves the moment formulas; alternatively restrict to strictly interior p. With that qualification, integrating cos(alpha-qX) and its square gives the stated mu and Var(p). The total-covariance formula gives Var(p) for distinct trials sharing one context, and zero for independent contexts. Total variance yields the displayed count expressions. Conditional reservoir independence fails if the context sequence is selected using the hidden seed rather than independent of it.

Renewal law: for last-segment start i=1, weight rho^(j-1). For i>=2, renewal at i has weight (1-rho), no later renewal contributes rho^(j-i), while all earlier renewal patterns average to F_(i-1). Independent new mark means the segment characteristic Phi factors. This proves the exact recursion. A zero mean initial uniform mark remains the same across k steps with probability rho^k; after any renewal it is independent and zero mean, giving Cij=rho^abs(i-j). The rule presumes independent Bernoulli renewal decisions and independent replacement marks.

Continuum: couple n_i to the same marked Poisson path. On each interval a0 cross a1=integral a0 cross a'(s) ds; replacing a0 by a(s) costs <=integral M² t dt=M²h²/2. Replacing n0 by n(s) costs in expectation <=integral 2M P(at least one event by t)dt <=M h²/ell. After N intervals and factor |kappa|/2, the claimed Lh bound follows. Unit norm marks and bounded |a'| suffice; no second derivative is needed. |exp(ix)-exp(iy)|<=|x-y| transfers the bound to characteristic functions. Sampling the Poisson path gives the exact retain-or-uniform transition with rho=exp(-h/ell), even when multiple renewals occur. Fixed rho as h shrinks changes correlation length; fixed physical ell does not. The bound concerns the extra twist only, not a demonstrated finite-step error for the base spinor holonomy.

All identities are conditional. Context ensemble, renewal probability, length parameter, coherence and detector map are supplied, not derived by symmetry, M2(C), or lattice locality. Apart from the endpoint-domain clarification above, no mathematical defect found before author-code inspection.
