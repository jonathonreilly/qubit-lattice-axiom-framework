For any finite transitive preorder, nonnegative decreasing box feasibility is equivalent to U_i>=0 and L_j<=U_i on every ordered pair. Taking each t_i as the maximum of zero and all downstream lower endpoints proves sufficiency. This independently gives exactly the author's later pair-obstruction formula. For interval order, reflexive pairs can be added without changing the maximum; the implementation separately includes each run's own lower endpoint. Strictly disjoint closed intervals give the declared order. Overlapping intervals are deliberately not ordered.
