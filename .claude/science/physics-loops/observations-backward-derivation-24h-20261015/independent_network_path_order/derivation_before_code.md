# Independent pre-code derivation

For resolvent R=(z²I+K)^-1 at large positive real z, w^T R e=sum_n (-1)^n mu_n z^(-2n-2). The first nonzero term is (-1)^d mu_d z^(-2d-2). Symmetry makes the other cross factor identical, and the rank-one denominator is 1+O(z^-2). Thus response difference starts theta*mu_d² z^(-4d-4). Resolvent moment coefficients correspond exactly to the entire impulse series coefficients, so earliest nonzero moment index is n=2d+1 and impulse degree 2n+1=4d+3 with the stated factorial/sign. No asymptotic inversion principle is needed. If all D first moments vanish, Cayley-Hamilton makes all higher moments vanish. The reachable Krylov subspace is invariant under K and unchanged by theta ww^T there; its driven trajectory is therefore identical for all time. This is exact algebra, not noisy numerical identifiability.

Positive-spring graph: K^k entries sum all k-step walks, allowing diagonal stays. Before graph distance no walk exists; at exactly distance no stay/detour can occur and every shortest edge product has the same sign (-1)^distance. Hence the nearer endpoint of w=e_i-e_j determines d when distances differ. Equal distance subtracts two positive-magnitude sums and may cancel, either finitely or at every order under symmetry. In a connected bipartite graph, adjacent endpoints have opposite distance parity and triangle inequality bounds their difference by one; it is exactly one, so cancellation cannot occur at the minimal distance. This graph interpretation requires the positive-spring single-edge structure, not arbitrary signed stiffness or arbitrary w.

Remainder: moments satisfy |e^T(K^n-Ktheta^n)e|<=||K||^n+||Ktheta||^n<=2M^n for unit e. After the leading index 2d+1, sum n=m+r with m=2d+2. Factor 2M^m|t|^(2m+1)/(2m+1)!, bound factorial ratio by (2r)! and sum the remaining terms to cosh(sqrt(M)|t|). This gives the displayed bound for every finite real t, including negative t by absolute value. It is deliberately loose and can dominate the leading term; it does not by itself certify detectable order in bandlimited noisy data.

For block09 K and rotated Kprime, w=(0,1,-1) has mu0=0; mu1=-1 and +1/5 respectively. Thus with the same calibrated theta, the chain leading coefficient is 25 times the triangle coefficient. Rotating w along with the hidden coordinates restores orthogonal equivalence for the perturbed matrices and all responses. The breaking of equivalence comes from independently fixed intervention coordinates, not the unperturbed observation.
