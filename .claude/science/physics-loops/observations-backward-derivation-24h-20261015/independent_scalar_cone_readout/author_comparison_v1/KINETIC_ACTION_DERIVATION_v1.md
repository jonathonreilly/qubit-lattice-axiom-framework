## 1. General action and principal response

Use signature (-+++), X=-gE^{mu nu} phi_mu phi_nu/2, and

    S = integral sqrt(-gE)[M^2 RE/2 + P(phi,X)] + Sm[A(phi)^2 gE].

M>0, A>0, P is sufficiently smooth, alpha=d log A/d phi. A rolling timelike background has X>0. The scalar equation is

    nabla_mu(P_X nabla^mu phi) + P_phi + alpha Tm = 0.

Because nabla_mu X=-nabla^nu phi nabla_mu nabla_nu phi, its scalar second-derivative coefficient is

    G^{mu nu}=P_X gE^{mu nu}-P_XX nabla^mu phi nabla^nu phi.

In the local rest frame of the background gradient, the scalar principal dispersion is

    (P_X+2X P_XX) omega_E^2 - P_X |k_E|^2 = 0,
    cs^2 = P_X/(P_X+2X P_XX).

The regular positive-energy/hyperbolic branch used here has P_X>0 and D=P_X+2X P_XX>0. Tensor propagation instead uses the Einstein metric null cone. A positive conformal matter metric has the same null cone; rescaling local clocks and rulers by the same A does not alter the dimensionless cs. A tensor/light agreement therefore supplies no value for P_XX.

Under a smooth field relabeling phi=f(chi) with f' nonzero, X_phi=f'^2 X_chi. The transformed Lagrangian has Q_X=f'^2 P_X and Q_XX=f'^4 P_XX. The dimensionless ratio 2X P_XX/P_X and cs are unchanged at matching states. This is a candidate-coordinate invariance, not a derivation of a measured field coordinate.

The formula concerns a separated scalar principal mode. A static Yukawa range, a finite-frequency group velocity, a tensor mode or a material resonance is not automatically this mode. Mixing, noncanonical matter couplings, higher derivatives and multiple fields require a new principal analysis.

## 2. A physical metric channel without imposing canonical kinetics

Consider linear scalar perturbations of a homogeneous timelike background, with no linear anisotropic stress in the Einstein frame and no traceless-spatial integration kernel in the nonzero spatial Fourier sector. A general P(phi,X) has this property just as a canonical scalar does. The traceless Einstein equation gives Phi_E=Psi_E. Under gJ=A^2gE,

    Psi_J=Psi_E+delta chi, Phi_J=Phi_E-delta chi,
    J=(Psi_J-Phi_J)/2=delta chi=alpha delta phi,
    chi=log A.

Thus a measured gravitational-slip scalar channel can carry the scalar phase without first choosing P=X-V. Local alpha must be nonzero and smooth; zero coupling is a genuine missing-readout case. Smooth multiplication by alpha changes lower-order terms, not the principal characteristic surface of a nonzero high-frequency scalar signal.

For a local frozen-background plane component exp(i(kz-omega t)) with real k,omega nonzero, the physical metric is

    dsJ^2=-(1+2Psi_J)dt^2+(1-2Phi_J) dx^2.

In the stated linearized Riemann convention,

    R_0i0j=delta_ij partial_t^2 Phi_J + partial_i partial_j Psi_J,
    RT=R_0x0x=R_0y0y=-omega^2 Phi_J,
    RL=R_0z0z=-omega^2 Phi_J-k^2 Psi_J.

The candidate physical readout is therefore

    J = [(1+k^2/omega^2) RT - RL]/(2k^2).

It cancels the unknown common Einstein potential rather than assuming that potential negligible. The pure-conformal approximation RL/RT=1-k^2/omega^2 alone does not do that; it must not be used when the common potential is unbounded. The exact cancellation above is for the frozen linear plane model. Applying it in an expanding/inhomegeneous spacetime needs a declared curvature/drift remainder, mode identification, known propagation direction, local clock/ruler registration and calibrated relative tidal components. Ordinary interferometer strain is not assumed to provide the two independent tidal amplitudes. A common nonzero multiplier does not alter a single component's phase; different channel transfers can contaminate the cancellation.

This is not yet a measurement protocol or scalar detection. Full-sky metric tomography, local tidal reconstruction and propagation records have different contracts. The current GW polarization literature is to be checked for the contract it actually uses.

## 3. Finite-frequency mass elimination is conditional

Suppose a correctly identified scalar mode on one effectively constant environment obeys

    omega_i^2=cs^2 k_i^2+mu^2+e_i,
    |e_i|<=E_i, k_2^2>k_1^2.

This is a declared local dispersion model, not a consequence of a single arrival time. Subtracting two measured pairs removes a common mu^2:

    |cs^2-(omega_2^2-omega_1^2)/(k_2^2-k_1^2)|
        <=(E_1+E_2)/(k_2^2-k_1^2).

Errors in registered squared frequencies and wavenumbers must be propagated too; no nonzero lower bound on their difference means no finite bound of this form. A one-frequency phase velocity cs^2+mu^2/k^2 does not isolate cs. Real material/curvature dispersion belongs in e_i with a justified bound, not an ignored correction.

## 4. What a luminal scalar would establish

On a connected open state patch with X>0, P_X>0 and D>0, exact cs=1 at EVERY state implies P_XX=0 throughout that patch. Locally on a product subpatch,

    P(phi,X)=K(phi) X - V(phi), K(phi)>0.

The coordinate psi=int sqrt(K(phi)) dphi makes the kinetic term canonical. Neither K normalization, V, A, the absolute scale nor the origin of the observed state is selected by luminality alone. This is local equivalence within the declared class; it is not a fundamental-action selection theorem.

If |cs^2-1|<=delta<1 uniformly on a fixed-phi positive-X interval, then

    |partial_logX log P_X| = |cs^-2-1|/2
        <= delta/[2(1-delta)],
    |log[P_X(phi,X2)/P_X(phi,X1)]|
        <= delta |log(X2/X1)|/[2(1-delta)].

This bounds kinetic variation over that interval. Finitely many samples need extra regularity/enclosure evidence to supply a uniform premise. An observed cosmological trajectory is not an open two-dimensional field-state patch.

## 5. Same linear histories, different nonlinear actions

Take any finite list of smooth state curves X=X_j(phi). For a canonical reference P0=X-V(phi), define the explicitly selected alternative

    P_lambda=P0+lambda F(phi,X) product_j [X-X_j(phi)]^3.

F is smooth. For every listed curve, the deformation and all partial derivatives of total order at most two vanish. The full action perturbation about that background, including the metric dependence of X, starts at cubic order. The same background and quadratic action, hence the same linear scalar and metric equations, survive on each listed curve when A and matter are unchanged. This is a positive construction of alternatives, not an exhaustive impossibility statement. Small lambda preserves regular hyperbolicity locally near compact portions where the reference has a strict margin. No global stability or ultraviolet completion is asserted.

A full-Einstein witness is available without supplying a background stress by hand: the massless canonical scalar admits aE proportional to t^(1/3), phi=sqrt(2/3) M log(t/t0)+phi0 and X=M^2/(3t^2). These give curves X=C_j exp(-sqrt(6) phi/M) with distinct positive C_j. Verify all Friedmann/Raychaudhuri/scalar equations and the deformation jets directly. Test matter can retain the same A; finite-density dust is not part of this witness.

At a point of a listed curve away from its intersections with others, the transverse third derivative can be nonzero. In a separately declared local decoupling example with constant rolling q, X0=q^2/2 and deformation lambda(X-X0)^3, writing phi=qt+pi gives a cubic term lambda q^3 (partial_t pi)^3. Its first nonlinear equation has a contribution 6lambda q^3 pi_t pi_tt to the convention pi_tt-Laplacian pi. This test-field example illustrates a nonlinear discriminator; it is not itself an exact flat-gravity solution with nonzero scalar energy. No empirical bispectrum or wave-mixing detection is claimed.

The single-history nonlinear freedom is established prior art (KLT2017/2019). The finite-product example is to be checked as the campaign's concrete scope countercontrol, not advertised as a novel discovery. It prevents a successful linear inverse from silently promoting its canonical input to a derived action.

## 6. A field-label route that may be circular

In an independently specified Einstein metric with separately measured matter stress and nonzero trace, conformal coupling would give

    omega_nu = nabla_mu Tm^mu_nu/Tm = partial_nu log A.

Closure and equality of this one-form across matter species would be useful conditional consistency tests. They do not establish a new observable merely by definition. If gE is obtained by first choosing A and transforming the conserved physical-frame stress, the same relation follows from that choice: using it to derive A would be circular. A measured physical metric alone does not independently provide the Einstein metric, M, stress split or field label required by a P(phi,X) stress portrait. Keep this proposed shortcut unretired unless a noncircular measurement or inverse closes that gap.
