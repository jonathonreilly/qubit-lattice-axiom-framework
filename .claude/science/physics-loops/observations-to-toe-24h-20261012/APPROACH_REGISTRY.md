Materially different live mechanisms include a dispersive massive tensor,
a massless metric with one composition-dependent scalar, a massless metric with
a positive finite/continuous scalar spectrum, and classical static/refreshed
phase noise as clock/channel rivals. Different Gaussian states share the same
retarded response but need not share fluctuations. No one family has been
selected as a TOE. The amplitude/matter and cosmological bridges remain research
targets, not implicit Standard Model or cosmological derivations.
