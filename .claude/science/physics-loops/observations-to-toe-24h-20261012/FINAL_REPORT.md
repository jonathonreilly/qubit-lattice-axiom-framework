Its homogeneous future convergence argument is restricted to the stated expanding positive-fluid system. It does not select the observed finite-time initial state, and its convergence result is not transferred to the later induced-curvature or exponential actions.
3. The curvature/fluid construction gives an explicit positive kinetic sector and exact ideal-fluid family under its supplied normalization. Local positivity and a homogeneous solution do not establish the full perturbation spectrum, quantum stability or an explanation of the vacuum scale.
4. For the original stiff potential, the canonical weak-scalar theorem assumes bounded scaled states u=k²v and p_u=kw. It proves a uniform coefficient expansion on fixed compact sets, H_J²/(H_v² H_GR²)=1+k C₁+O(k²), with an exact cancellation at ideal matched states. At fixed nonzero transverse field x, the potential-curvature contribution grows as 4x²/(k²H_GR²). This is not a full eigenmode or a theorem that the finite-k histories obey the assumed scaling. The conditional transverse limit retains a curved field metric.
