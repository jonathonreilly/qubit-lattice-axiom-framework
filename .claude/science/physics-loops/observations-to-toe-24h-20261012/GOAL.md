## Scientific target

Work backward from reproducible observations to the smallest connected set of dynamical structures and candidate premises that can explain them, then run those candidate theories forward into independent tests. The ambition is a candidate TOE; its existence, uniqueness and success are open questions. A 24-hour budget cannot certify them.

The search direction is observations -> empirical constraints and invariants -> competing mechanisms -> candidate laws and premises -> forward predictions -> falsification or revision. Deriving a preferred lattice's consequences is one optional comparison, not the organizing direction. Keep at least three materially distinct live families while scientifically useful. New definitions, proofs, dynamics, geometries and candidate physical assumptions are authorized.

Target domain: quantum correlations and interference; matter and gauge interactions; neutrino/mass structure; clocks, propagation and universal free fall; gravity and cosmological observations. Distinguish directly recorded spectra/counts/timing from parameter estimates conditioned on QFT, GR or cosmology. Domain coverage is a requirement map, not a claim that every sector will be solved.

Success witnesses include a new explicit construction linking previously separate observations; a theorem identifying which structural ingredient an observation requires within a stated model family; or a frozen quantitative prediction/envelope that discriminates candidate mechanisms using data not used to fit that prediction. Record underdetermination honestly. Reproducing standard physics is a control, not itself new theory. A useful new assumption should have an equation, a physical forward map, a reason it helps, and a possible falsifier.

Forbidden shortcuts: building the desired observation into a definition and calling it a prediction; choosing a numerical prefactor from its comparison target; treating a fitted value or standard interpretation as raw data; interpreting a search failure as an impossibility theorem; declaring TOE completion from note/PR/pass counts; unrecorded model changes after test exposure.
