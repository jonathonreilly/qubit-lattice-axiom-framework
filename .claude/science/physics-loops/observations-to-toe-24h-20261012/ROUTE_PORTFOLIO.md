# Inverse reconstruction portfolio

Status: open. Initial possibilities await focused prior-art and data checks.

1. Neutrino energy/baseline spectra -> minimum coherent phase/evolution mechanism. Start from observed spectra, not oscillation-fit parameter summaries. Compare standard coherent mixing with explicit physically admissible alternative evolution laws; recover the standard result as a control and seek a cross-experiment discriminator.
2. Clocks, free fall and propagation -> a common geometry/coupling mechanism. Keep scalar, vector, tensor and relational alternatives explicit; derive actual measured timing/redshift/acceleration channels and include source and apparatus effects.
3. Quantum correlations/composition -> operational state and probability structure. Preserve freedom-of-setting and detector assumptions; distinguish classical-local, quantum and broader no-signalling families without pretending finite data uniquely select an ontology.
4. Gauge/matter observations -> interaction structure and chiral content. Work from charges/cross-sections/amplitudes and consistency with unitarity, anomaly conditions and observed spectra; those consistency premises must be named.
5. Cross-domain synthesis -> one candidate law with shared parameters, several forward observables and a distinct falsifier. A catalogue of independent fits is insufficient.

Novelty search and exact hypothesis comparison are required before shipping a new claim. Record search revision, actual matching source read, closest result, new mechanism and first decisive computation per selected route.
