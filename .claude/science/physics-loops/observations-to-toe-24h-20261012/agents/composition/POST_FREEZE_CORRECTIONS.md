# Post-freeze corrections and scope

The original PROTOCOL.md, MODEL_FREEZE.json and V1 results are preserved. Their hashes are in V1_RESULTS_FREEZE.json. This addendum does not rewrite their history.

1. The original selected solar Be/Ti source, Schlamminger et al. 0712.0607v1, did not contain the numerical solar coefficient sought. Its galactic-center/space-fixed result is not a solar result. Wagner et al. 1207.2442v1, Table 3, supplies the earlier solar Be/Ti summary.
2. Ross et al. 2602.02815v1 was discovered during that source repair. It is a **model-development holdout selected after the original source failed**, not an ex ante or blind holdout. The first full paper read was recorded at 2026-10-12T17:05:11.209640+00:00; its central value was exposed before MODEL_FREEZE.json at 17:09:50.757116 UTC. The paper was already publicly released on 2026-02-02. Its measured amplitude was excluded from calibration fitting, and the earlier electron-branch witness grid was unchanged. Model/box/calibration rules were frozen before computing numerical held-out responses. These distinctions are necessary; this exercise supplies no blind-prediction evidence.
3. The published 0.66(0.61) fm/s² summary is used only for a **conditional published-summary comparison**. The local interval [-0.5356,1.8556] fm/s² is its central marginal Gaussian interval using 1.96 standard errors. It is not a joint three-measurement confidence set, and the value 4.856 for the t=.01 witness is a standardized residual under this summary approximation and frozen nominal sensitivities. It is not an independently established exclusion significance. The source/data response audit and independent normalization review remain separate obligations.
4. MICROSCOPE describes its systematic contribution as an upper bound. Modeling it as an additive Gaussian nuisance with scale 1.5e-15 is our declared summary approximation, not a prior supplied by the authors. A bounded additive nuisance |b|<=1.5e-15 gives a separate sensitivity check in MODEL_AND_RESULTS.md.
5. The V1 programs check extensionless stop markers, whereas the campaign supervisor uses STOP_REQUESTED.json. All subsequent execution must use run_v2.py, which checks both .json and extensionless STOP_REQUESTED/USER_STOP_REQUESTED markers plus the immutable deadline before starting the frozen script. Old scripts and outputs remain unchanged for reproducibility. These jobs take seconds; no detached process or long-running worker was launched.
6. This exactly massless canonical scalar is a distinct comparison model. A positive spectral continuum or finite mediator set developed elsewhere in the campaign is not identical to it. A connection requires an explicit new forward model, normalization, source response and state covariance; none is silently substituted here.
