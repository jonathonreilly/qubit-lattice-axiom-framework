# Precision of the actual-background response

The v1 readouts interpret serialized shortest decimals as exact arithmetic
inputs. They have independently checked equations and interval membership,
not thirty-digit physical accuracy. The near-null eta and dot-G values on
weak split branches are dominated by rounded-parameter conventions; even
their signs change when the same stored values are reconstructed as exact
binary64 numbers. No robust sign, measured drift, or physical tiny bound
follows. The independent report preserves both conventions and all original
failed controls. All working-interval memberships are unchanged by this
representation comparison, but numerical-history error remains unbounded
by it. In particular split k=1e-6 is only about2e-12 above the lower
one-reported-error Cassini boundary; that is stored-point membership alone.

The conditional local-background, weak-body and sufficiently long-range
response premises remain unresolved. These results do not establish a
combined empirical fit or acceptance.
