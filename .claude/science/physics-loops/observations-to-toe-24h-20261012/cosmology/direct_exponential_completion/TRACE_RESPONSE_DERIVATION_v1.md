# Initial response to the observed gas trace

Parent /root derives a local consequence of the regular limiting equations.
It concerns an instantaneously matched chosen state, not all later times.
Let B=1+2(x²+y²)/3, x²=R0 rho_dust, y²=R0 rho_gas,
u=p_u=0, p_x=-3x/2,p_y=-2y and Pi=R0 P_gas. Write tau_g=y²-3Pi.
Pressureless dust cancels from this gas trace. Direct substitution in the
limiting lowered u equation gives

    C_u=Tbar/2,
    B Tbar=x²+4y²/3,
    W_u=-x²-2y²/3,
    (exp(-u)/2) R0 tau_total=(x²+y²-3Pi)/2,
    p_u'=-2 tau_g/B.

Thus a positive gas trace initially accelerates u in the negative direction.
The effect survives k→0 in the scalar dynamics, although its effect on the
physical expansion is proportional to k. This connects the supplied massive
electron/neutrino thermodynamics to the response; it introduces no new fitted
parameter or observation.

There is also an initial slope identity for the physical coefficient C_exp.
At this matched instant C_exp=0. Define E=W+B Tbar/2 and Delta=E-sigma0.
The trace-work term in E' vanishes at p_u=0, so E'=-3B Tbar, and
Delta'=-tau_g by fluid conservation. Differentiating the full coefficient,
including its physical-clock term exp(-u)p_u, then gives

    C_exp' = 2 Delta'/(3B)+p_u' = -8 tau_g/(3B).

Primes use N_J in the limiting system. Omitting the clock derivative would
give the wrong factor. An exactly ideal radiation gas has tau_g=0 and both
derivatives vanish, consistently with the preserved ideal orbit. For an
arbitrary later thermal state neither instantaneous matching nor these two
short formulas is assumed. The gas approximation and initial matching are
supplied, not derived from a microscopic TOE. This is a local mathematical
response identity; it is not a new measurement or history-convergence bound.
