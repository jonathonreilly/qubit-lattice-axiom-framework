# The cancellation is a stationary maximum of the local Newton coefficient

Parent /root continuation before first execution, 2026-10-13.
Same conditional premises and notation as HAND_DERIVATION_v1.md.
This addition is an analytic proposal awaiting independent check.

On a fixed theory, not along a fixed-k family,

    d k/d phi=2 sqrt(k) beta_D,
    d z/d phi=2 sqrt(k) z(1+z),
    beta_D=-k(1-3k)z.

At the proposed null family beta_D=-(1+k) and
z=(1+k)/[k(1-3k)], the chain rule yields

    beta_D'=-2 sqrt(k)(1+k)(7+3k)/(1-3k).

Differentiating d ln G_N/d phi=2 sqrt(k)[1+beta_D/(1+k)]
and only then substituting the null family gives

    d^2 ln G_N/d phi^2=-32k/(1-3k)<0, 0<k<1/3.

Thus the vanishing first derivative at this point does not imply constant
G_N on a finite scalar interval. In canonical local coordinate,
ln[G_N(phi_0+delta_phi)/G_N(phi_0)]
=-16k/(1-3k) delta_phi^2+O(delta_phi^3).
No time trajectory or observed scalar velocity is supplied. A time derivative
of G_N is zero at the crossing only to this instantaneous order, while a
finite observation over evolving phi needs its trajectory and apparatus model.
