# A separate common-operator flavor candidate

Status: exploratory conditional hypothesis, 2026-10-12. This is a new physical
identification to test, not a consequence of the scalar construction. It does
not change the adopted premise registry or claim novelty for Jacobi matrices.

The positive spectral scalar construction has a canonical Laguerre Jacobi
operator with diagonal 2n+s and adjacent entries -sqrt((n+1)(n+s)). Test the
stronger hypothesis that its N=3 truncation also fixes neutrino flavor
eigenvectors, with electron weak flavor at endpoint n=0 and muon/tau at n=1,2.
The mapping of those internal slots to physical flavors is supplied. It must
earn its place through forward comparisons, rather than being inferred from
the mathematical existence of a three-dimensional matrix.

For ordered eigenvalues x1<x2<x3, let U diagonalize this real symmetric matrix
and choose normal mass ordering. Electron probabilities are its positive
quadrature weights wi=|U_0i|^2. Supply standard coherent relativistic flavor
propagation. The physical mass map is separately tested as m_i^2=m_*^2 x_i^p,
with p=1 and p=2 fixed controls and p in [.1,10] an explicitly additional
candidate parameter. A common mass-squared offset is unobservable in vacuum
oscillations and may be added; absolute masses remain open. A real chain gives
zero CP-odd oscillation invariant. A complex cycle would be a new candidate.

Freeze s in [.01,10]. Use only the Daya Bay 3158-day published standard-family
fit summary sin^2(2theta13)=.0851 +/- .0024 to select s, through
4w3(1-w3). This is a theory-conditioned fitted calibration input, not raw
counts or an arbitrary-law use of the Daya Bay chi-square grid. Its quoted
uncertainty and a central +/-1.96 sigma interval are propagated as a diagnostic,
not as a complete parameter confidence region. The Daya Bay summary assumes
its solar-sector inputs; that conditional dependence must be retained.

Do not fit theta12 or theta23. Predict w2/(1-w3) and |U_13|^2/(1-w3) for later
comparison with pinned primary oscillation summaries or an appropriate full
likelihood. Their approximate known values motivated this line and are not
blind targets. No empirical exclusion follows from an unsourced approximate
number. The mass-gap ratio for each fixed p is a separate forward output;
fitting p to that ratio later must be recorded as a second candidate.

All three-flavor identifications, the coherent propagation equation, normal
ordering, endpoint flavor assignment, and proposed shared operator are explicit
premises. The scalar and neutrino dimensional scales are independent unless a
subsequent version explicitly equates them. Using a common s alone would link a
static/noise power to mixing probabilities; it would not derive the Standard
Model, the number of generations, matter masses, or a TOE.
