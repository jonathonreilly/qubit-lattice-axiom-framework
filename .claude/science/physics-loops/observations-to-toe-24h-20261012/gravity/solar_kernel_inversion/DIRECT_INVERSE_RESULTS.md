## Distinguishing a working fit from a physical explanation

The common response was fitted to all three curves, the external field box
and the solar quadrupole box. The mapping of Newtonian to physical external
field uses a spherical-parent relation; isolated disks, fixed baryonic
reconstructions, stellar ratio, geometry and circular-speed identification
remain supplied. Sampled E optimization and identical high-field response
columns leave substantial nonidentifiability. None is resolved by t=0.

The exact static action has been derived and checked. Its simplest promotion
to two independent Lorentz scalars fails the positive-energy kinetic test.
A single-scalar alternative has a different disk and solar equation, so the
fits do not transfer to it. These failures delimit the route; they do not
exclude all gravity theories or all new assumptions.

Both D responses are now carried unchanged to the same six preselected
out-of-fit development galaxies. Their weights and targets are fixed before
those fields. Stage1 computes source fields, stage2 performs actual candidate
refinement/boundary checks, and only then stage3 scores observations. A poor
transfer result will be preserved before any new response fit.
