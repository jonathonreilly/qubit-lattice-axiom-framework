## Independent derivation of the proper-clock response

For a small plane perturbation at fixed finite positive frequency and speed, the coordinate geodesic equation around resting endpoints is xi_tt=-c² grad psi. The harmonic particular displacement is the stated i n c² psi/(omega c_s). Expand each Euclidean null distance to first order about the unperturbed arm. Only the arm projection of the endpoint displacement enters. The outgoing and returning durations therefore give the stated combination of the mirror at t-L/c and beam splitter at t and t-2L/c. Independently integrating the beam splitter's rate adds the integral of psi; its velocity dilation begins at second order.

Writing x=omega L/c and r=gamma_s mu, the mirror term relative to psi_b(t) has phase exp[ix(1+r)], and the early beam splitter has phase exp(2ix). The clock integral gives exp(ix)sinc(x). These phases and the flight-time prefactor reproduce exactly

    H = exp(ix)sinc(x)
        + i r/(2x) [2 exp(ix(1+r))-1-exp(2ix)].

For x near zero, direct series expansion gives

    H = 1-r² + i x (1-r²)(1+r/2) + O(x²),

which also shows that the displayed apparent 1/x singularity is removable. At r=+1 or -1 the flight term is exactly minus the clock term. At mu=0 only the clock term remains. Equal-arm subtraction cancels that common clock contribution.
