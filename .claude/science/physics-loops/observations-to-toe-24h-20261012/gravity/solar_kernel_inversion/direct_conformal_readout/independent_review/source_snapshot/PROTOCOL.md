# New conformal matter/light completion: frozen questions

Direct parent authorship, 2026-10-12. Status: conditional-support.
The scalar-force readout of the local vector model has no transverse
trace-free plane tide and leaves photons/clocks unspecified. Actual
interferometer and light-deflection observations motivate this next model
decision. No empirical data will be fitted by this block.

Supply an explicit new physical metric for matter and electromagnetism,

    g_tilde_mu_nu = exp(2 phi/c^2) eta_mu_nu,
    phi=e.u,

where the existing homogeneous local vector field action is kept in its
preferred Minkowski coordinates and c is the electromagnetic limiting
speed. Matter uses -m c^2 integral exp(phi/c^2) sqrt(1-v^2/c^2)dt.
Maxwell electromagnetism is minimally coupled to this metric in four
spacetime dimensions. This is an added model premise, not a derivation of
gravity or an assertion of general covariance of the preferred field action.

First derive the nonrelativistic coupling and exact particle source factor;
do not silently carry the old mass-conserved radiation source to all orders.
Then derive the linear physical Riemann tide and free-mirror motion for
phi/c^2=psi_s(t-n.x/c_s). Derive a round-trip proper-clock optical arm
response from null flight times and endpoint trajectories, at first order
in psi and all omega L/c, with harmonic particular motions specified.
Keep zero-initial-condition transients separate from monochromatic response.

Test the following limits before empirical interpretation: luminal scalar
gives a transverse breathing tide and no transverse trace-free part;
an arm parallel to a luminal plane wave has zero round-trip response;
transverse and small-arm limits match local proper-length deviation;
the exact equal-arm signal differs from simultaneous Euclidean endpoint
length at finite frequency; c_s distinct from c yields linked longitudinal
and breathing components rather than an arbitrary pure polarization label.

Use independent numerical null-flight root solving with displaced endpoints
and an integrated proper clock, not merely the same transfer formula.
Verify O(psi^2) residual scaling as amplitudes halve on fixed geometries.
Check the weak metric curvature symbolically and the static null-path
conformal cancellation. The photon action may be conformally invariant even
though massive clocks are not: derive each statement explicitly.

No GR arrival-time or polarization Bayes factor may be transferred without
the candidate's waveform/propagation/noise likelihood. Light bending is a
separate constraint: determine whether this completion changes unparameterized
null trajectories at all. A new metric completion may expose a new failure;
checkpoint it and choose the next physical bridge, keeping the original
campaign deadline unchanged.
