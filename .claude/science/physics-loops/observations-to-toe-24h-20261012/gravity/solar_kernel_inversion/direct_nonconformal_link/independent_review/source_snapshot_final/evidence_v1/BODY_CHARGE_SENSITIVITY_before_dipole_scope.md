# Body charges: the extra premise and its measurable consequence

Status: conditional-support. Parent derivation following an independent
source-order finding. The main eccentric formula now explicitly assumes the
formal point action with self terms discarded and no residual body-dependent
charge sensitivities. Weak self gravity and constant self energies alone do
not establish that premise. The original wording is preserved in evidence_v1.

To display the missing effect, supply small constant residual active charges
q_a=m_a(1+eta_a) on the same Newtonian centre-of-mass Kepler trajectory. This
is a sensitivity diagnostic, not a self-consistent composite-body action:
passive charges, binding masses and changed conservative orbit also need
reconstruction if eta is to be a physical prediction.

With X1=(m2/M)r and X2=-(m1/M)r,

    D_self=sum q_a X_a=m_red (eta1-eta2) r.

The far-zone source derivative acquires n.D_self''/c_s. Its angular cross
terms with monopole and quadrupole vanish by parity; the new nonnegative
power is

    P_dipole=G_N |D_self''|^2/(3c_s^3).

For the supplied Kepler orbit, r''=-G_N M r/r^3. With delta_eta=eta1-eta2,
the orbit average gives

    <P_dipole>=G_N m_red^2 delta_eta^2 a^2 Omega^4/(3c_s^3)
                * (1+e^2/2)/(1-e^2)^(5/2).

This follows from the same dt weighting and I_3 in the preceding note; it is
checked by direct Cartesian acceleration/time integration separately from
the original mono/quadr calculation. For a circular orbit the ratio to the
candidate's scalar quadrupole is

    P_dipole/P_scalar_quadrupole=(5/16)delta_eta^2(c_s/v_orb)^2.

Thus small |eta_a| compared with1 is not enough. A sufficient parametric
hierarchy for negligible dipole is |delta_eta|<<v_orb/c_s, or an independently
established absence of residual charge contrast. The main formula uses the
latter as an explicit candidate premise, not a result about real bodies.

Relative to the supplied same-orbit GR quadrupole, with s=c_s/c,

    R_dipole=(5/96)delta_eta^2 s^-3 (c/(a Omega))^2
        *[(1+e^2/2)(1-e^2)]/[1+73e^2/24+37e^4/96].

For eta contrast1e-3, v_orb/c=1e-3 and the circular-calibrated s, the
circular additional ratio is about0.153. The paired diagnostic computes the
exact number. This is an illustrative sensitivity choice, not a measured
body charge. It shows why even a weak-body calculation must state the
charge/order assumption before it is applied to observations.

Neither this additive supplied-orbit diagnostic nor zero residual eta proves
a complete extended-body limit. Internal pressure/stresses, self-field
regularization, passive coupling, environmental response and conserved total
stress remain open physical work. Strong-body tests cannot use the earlier
mono/quadr coefficient without that reconstruction.
