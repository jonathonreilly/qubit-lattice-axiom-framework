## Supplied action and physical identifications

Use signature(-+++), x^0=ct, G_*>0, n finite dimensionless scalars phi^A,
constant positive-definite symmetric K_AB and constant covector alpha_A.
Supply one matter action S_m[gtilde,Psi] with

    S_g = c^3/(16 pi G_*) integral d^4x sqrt(-g_*)
          [R_* - 2 K_AB g_*^(mu nu) partial_mu phi^A partial_nu phi^B],
    gtilde_mu_nu = A^2 g_*mu_nu,  A=exp(alpha_A phi^A).

All matter, including freely falling weak probes, clocks and vacuum Maxwell
fields, uses gtilde. At infinity phi=0, A=1 and g_*=eta. There is no scalar
potential, derivative matter coupling or environmental screening. Einstein
geometry, this field content and universal coupling are new candidate premises.
They are not proved uniquely necessary by the three observed channels.
The matter action's microscopic content, quantization, masses and couplings
are still supplied and unresolved.

Write v^A=(K^-1)^(AB)alpha_B and a=alpha_A v^A>=0. Under phi=S chi,
K'=S^T K S and alpha'=S^T alpha, so a is unchanged. Thus many positive scalar
fields do not permit cancellation of this universal linear charge norm.
This says nothing about nonuniversal, screened, massive or indefinite-metric
alternatives. With constant K and alpha, only the v direction is sourced at
linear order; orthogonal free scalar modes can carry independently supplied
initial data but are not generated by the common weak source at this order.

Define T_*^(mu nu)=2c/sqrt(-g_*) times delta S_m/delta g_*mu_nu. Variation
of the displayed action gives

    G_*mu_nu = 2 K_AB partial_mu phi^A partial_nu phi^B
              -g_*mu_nu K_AB partial_rho phi^A partial^rho phi^B
              +(8 pi G_*/c^4) T_*mu_nu,
    Box_* phi^A = -(4 pi G_*/c^4) v^A T_*,
    nabla_*mu T_*^(mu nu) = alpha_A T_* partial^nu phi^A.

For example, integration by parts in delta S_g/delta phi gives
c^3 K_AB Box phi^B/(4pi G_*); delta S_m/delta phi^A is
alpha_A sqrt(-g_*)T_*/c. The signs and factors therefore agree. The scalar
stress on the right of Einstein's equation has divergence
2K_AB(Box phi^A)partial_nu phi^B. Inserting the scalar equation cancels
the matter divergence exactly. Matter stress alone is not conserved in the
Einstein coordinates. The physical metric's matter equations instead imply
nabla_tilde_mu T_tilde^(mu nu)=0. This addresses a source identity, not a
proof of global solutions or compact-body regularity.

## Linear modes, signs and common cone

At the flat zero-scalar background, scalar stress is quadratic. The linear
metric equation is G_L[h]=(8pi G_*/c^4)T_*. Its divergence vanishes as an
identity. In vacuum, trace reversal and harmonic gauge give Box hbar=0.
For a nonzero null Fourier vector, the solution space of the ten-component
linear Einstein operator has dimension six; four independent coordinate
gauge directions leave two physical tensor polarizations. Off the null cone
the kernel has only those four gauge directions. The paired symbolic runner
checks these ranks directly rather than counting selected TT components alone.

The second Einstein variation is
-c^3/(32pi G_*) integral h_mu_nu G_L^(mu nu)[h] d^4x. On a TT wave,
G_L=-Box h_TT/2; integration by parts gives

    S_TT=c^2/(64pi G_*) integral dt d^3x
          [(partial_t h_TT)^2-c^2(grad h_TT)^2].

This and positive K give positive quadratic energies for the two tensor and
n scalar modes. No general nonlinear positive-energy or stability theorem is
claimed. All these vacuum linear modes obey the same null characteristic.
Maxwell's metric is conformal to g_*, so its geometric-optics null cone is
the same. This common propagation cone is a consequence of the supplied
two-derivative action, not a speed inferred by independently fitting each wave.
It does not derive the matter sector's dispersion relations or a cosmological
history from the repository's lattice rules.

## Static motion and light bending measure different combinations

For a weak point exterior with inertial source mass M at leading order,
let U_*=-G_* M/r. Solving the linear equations gives

    phi^A=v^A U_*/c^2,
    h_*00=-2U_*/c^2,  h_*ij=-2U_*delta_ij/c^2.

Multiplication by A^2 then gives

    gtilde_00=-1-2(1+a)U_*/c^2+O(c^-4),
    gtilde_ij=[1-2(1-a)U_*/c^2]delta_ij+O(c^-4).

A slowly moving weak test mass feels U_N=(1+a)U_*. Thus

    G_N=G_*(1+a),
    gamma=(1-a)/(1+a),
    f_scalar=(G_N-G_*)/G_N=a/(1+a)=(1-gamma)/2,
    f_tensor=G_*/G_N=(1+gamma)/2.

The word fraction refers to the attractive Newtonian force in this restricted

the transverse gradient of 2G_*M/(c^2 sqrt(b^2+z^2)) along the unperturbed ray.

## The source stress needed for a tensor waveform

Radiation reaction is adiabatic; no compact-star timing model is supplied.

An accelerating particle stress alone cannot source a harmonic tensor field.
At Newtonian order the required combined spatial stress is dust momentum
flux plus the field stress

    sigma_ij=(partial_i U_N partial_j U_N
              -delta_ij |grad U_N|^2/2)/(4pi G_N).

    integral tau_ij = integral(rho v_i v_j+sigma_ij)=I_ij''/2.

integral sigma_ij=-G_N m1 m2 r_i r_j/r^3. The point self terms are excluded

tuning a scalar propagation speed. Published pure-polarization comparisons

to the previously exposed galaxies. The nonlinear response N,D of that
