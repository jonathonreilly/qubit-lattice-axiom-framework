# From measured detector response to the candidate's matter readout

Direct parent block opened October 12, 2026, after the matter/radiation
derivation and before numerical controls. The original campaign deadline
and direct authorship are unchanged.

## Observation and import boundary

The GW170814 primary paper, DOI10.1103/PhysRevLett.119.141101, sections V
and VII, compares coherent detector responses under pure polarization
models while retaining a GR phase model. Its tensor preference motivates
checking what this candidate's force actually does to test masses.
It is exposed development context, not an independent test of this model.
No published Bayes factor or inferred source mass will be transferred to
the candidate. No strain data have been fitted or held out in this block.

## Hypotheses to test

Inherit only the homogeneous vector field, phi=e.u, the reciprocal
nonrelativistic force -grad phi, constant coefficients and weak waves.
Derive the local tidal matrix for a plane wave before adding a readout.
For a restricted mechanical readout, supply initially free test masses,
linear perturbations, equal arms and simultaneous Euclidean length
measurement. Treat optics, suspensions and electronic transfer functions
as missing; do not call mechanical length a calibrated LIGO strain.

Derive exact finite-arm endpoint displacement, its long-wavelength limit,
and the response of a network to one or two distinct wave speeds.
Check whether vector field components imply vector or tensor tidal
polarizations, and state the domain of any scalar-pattern degeneracy.

## Controls fixed before execution

- Symbolic differentiation of a general plane-wave scalar potential.
- Numerical integration of endpoint acceleration against the derived
  harmonic displacement for rotated arms and directions.
- Long-wavelength convergence and a finite-arm counterexample to extending
  the scalar-pattern degeneracy outside its assumptions.
- A synthetic detector-network calculation distinguishing one-speed
  rank-one response from two-speed response after a single delay alignment.
- Deliberate wrong-sign and transverse-force alternatives must fail.

Synthetic geometries are controls, not empirical validation. New readout
premises remain labelled. No formal audit, global no-go or claim of a TOE.

Before execution, the finite-arm control is specifically interpreted as
testing the extension of frequency-independent F beyond long wavelengths.
There is no supplied finite-frequency breathing or optical model here, so
that control cannot compare two complete optical-polarization models.
