# Observed matter neutrality to a charge family

Direct parent research. Status: conditional-support. This block starts from
electrically charged matter, weak charged-current mixing, neutrino masses
and laboratory neutrality summaries. It asks what they constrain before
claiming that a charge pattern follows from microscopic axioms.

Supply a local four-dimensional chiral gauge theory with Lie algebra
su(3)+su(2)+u(1), one Higgs doublet with nonzero neutral vev, the usual
quark/lepton representation multiplicities, and optional right-handed
neutrinos. These are explicit observed-pattern/EFT hypotheses. Supply the
usual perturbative anomaly cancellation requirement, ordinary spin
structure and no additional anomaly-cancelling sectors. Do not infer gauge
groups, three colors/generations, Yukawa values or a native chiral phase.

Personally solve all relevant hypercharge relations with the Higgs charge
left free until normalization. Compare: one family without nu_R; Dirac
nu_R with connected inter-family mixing; disconnected flavor graphs; and
an allowed Majorana or Weinberg mass term. Keep the global form and
primitive compact U(1) charge lattice separate from the Lie-algebra
anomaly calculation. A small real algebraic parameter is not automatically
a representation of a specified compact gauge group.

prior work. Their admitted charge/readout premises are not reconstructed
