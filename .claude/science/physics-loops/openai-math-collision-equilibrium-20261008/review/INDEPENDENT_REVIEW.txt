1. Collision model and full invariant space — note:42-79 and 120-156.
   Correct for the declared complete collision set on {-1,0,1}^3, with a
   positive rate for every reversible pair. A shared input/output species
   forces equality of the other species by momentum conservation. A repeated
   input (v,v) could only scatter to (v+w,v-w); conservation of squared speed
   forces 2|w|^2=0. Thus every nontrivial reaction has four distinct species,
   and its stoichiometric vector has squared norm four.

   The coordinate-rectangle collisions are actual conserved exchanges; when
   a rectangle degenerates, its mixed-difference identity is tautological.
   Their invariant identities remove all mixed terms. Interpolation on the
   three coordinate values leaves h=a+u.x+theta_x x^2+theta_y y^2+theta_z z^2.
   The exchanges (+/-e_x)<->(+/-e_y) and (+/-e_x)<->(+/-e_z) force the three
   quadratic coefficients equal. Conversely these five functions are
   conserved, and evaluation at zero and signed coordinate axes proves
   independence. Hence S-perp=row(A), rank(A)=5 and dim(S)=22. This is a
   complete proof; it does not depend only on a numerical rank test.

2. Positive global ODE, entropy minimizer and convergence — note:160-237.

   mass M. On the positive region each loss term for coordinate i is bounded
   by kappa_r M f_i, giving f_i(t)>=f_i(0) exp(-M K t). This prevents finite
   time contact with the boundary, while mass bounds prevent finite time
   blowup. The compact nonnegative moment fiber contains the given positive
   state. Entropy is continuous there and strictly convex. Moving a proposed
   boundary minimizer toward the positive state gives a strictly negative
   epsilon log(epsilon) term, which dominates its O(epsilon) terms; thus the
   minimizer is interior. Its constrained gradient is log(f*) in row(A),
   yielding the claimed Gibbs exponential. Beta need not be positive on this
   finite velocity set, and no unwarranted restriction is imposed.

   The identity Hdot=-sum kappa_r (x_r-y_r) log(x_r/y_r) has the correct sign
