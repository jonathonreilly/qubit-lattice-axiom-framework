Substantive mathematical checks
------------------------------
1. External theorem and hypotheses (note 73-90, 100; Main.lean 60-69).
   The accepted theorem has d>=3 and ell>=1 and provides both DynamicsConverges
   and, for every beta beyond a positive threshold, a translation-invariant
   KMS state with real spin-Z expectation at least ell/8. Specializing to
   d=3, ell=1 is valid. Comparator selection and the stated scope agree with

2. Qubit orientation and coupling (note 100-119; production Model.lean 195-223).
   The production matrix unit acts as |a><b|, and magnetic levels are k-ell/2.
   At ell=1, S+=(|1><0|), giving (S_x,S_y,S_z)=(X,-Y,-Z)/2. Conjugation by
   X therefore sends all three external spin matrices to the standard Pauli
   matrices divided by two. The unordered-edge definition selects exactly
   one orientation of each nearest-neighbor bond. Consequently
   Phi(H_0)=-sum sigma_x dot sigma_y/4 and H_J=kappa Phi(H_0), kappa=-4J>0.
   No factor two from directed bonds or sign reversal is missing.

3. Infinite-volume algebra bridge (note 100-114).
   The production construction is on l2 of finitely supported level
   configurations. For each finite Lambda, split a configuration into its
   restriction to Lambda and its outside restriction. The resulting Hilbert
   space is a direct sum, indexed by outside configurations, of copies of the
   ordinary finite tensor space. Every Lambda-local matrix acts as the same
   matrix on each copy. Its operator norm is therefore exactly the ordinary
   finite matrix norm, and its representation is faithful. The generated
   local algebra is the union of finite tensor matrix algebras; its norm
   closure is the quasilocal algebra in the note. This verifies the asserted
   isometric identification rather than assuming a reference representation
   supplies an arbitrary tensor norm.
   The product of on-site conjugations is compatible with identity embeddings
   and is isometric, so it extends uniquely to the completion. Applying it
   twice is the identity. It commutes with translations because the same X is
   used at each site. The note correctly avoids assuming an infinite-product
   unitary on the particular reference Hilbert space.

4. Dynamics and KMS scaling (note 121-134; Model.lean 225-256).
   The imported dynamics uses exp(+itH) A exp(-itH), with expanding free boxes,
   matching the note. The finite-volume intertwining passes through the norm
   limit by isometry. With beta_ext=kappa beta, the function
   F_J(z)=F_ext(kappa z) preserves boundedness, continuity, analyticity and both
   KMS boundary identities, since kappa is positive. Thus beta_c=beta_0/kappa,
   not kappa beta_0. Pullback by Phi^{-1} preserves the state conditions and
   translation invariance. Finally Phi(S_z)=Z/2 implies
   omega_J(Z)=2 omega_ext(S_z)>=1/4 with zero imaginary part. The theorem is
   existence of an infinite-volume ordered state, not uniqueness, finite
   zero-field Gibbs magnetization, preparation or a numerical critical point.

5. One-flip and Gaussian identities (note 144-169).
   The two-spin Pauli dot matrix has diagonal entries 1,-1,-1,1 and exchange
   entries 2 in the one-down subspace. Subtracting the all-up energy therefore
   gives -2J times the graph Laplacian on the one-flip sector. On Z^3 its
   symbol is -4J sum(1-cos k_a), hence D=-2J and the quartic coefficient is
   -D/12. The 3x3x3 periodic graph has 27 sites and 81 distinct bonds; its
   six neighbors are distinct, so the runner's geometric Laplacian convention
   agrees with its independently assembled bond action.
   Integration by parts yields I_2=I_0/(2a), I_4=3I_0/(4a^2). Summing the
   three quartic terms and multiplying by a/12 gives 3/(16a) relative to the
   leading Gaussian. The note correctly integrates a polynomial correction,
   not a nonintegrable exponential with positive quartic tail.

6. Paper-only coefficients and order of limits (note 171-191).
   The paper's introduction defines the free-box Hamiltonian with each bond
   once, pressure normalized by beta times volume, and magnetization as the
   right derivative of the thermodynamic pressure. Its displayed corollary is
   S-m_S = zeta(3/2)/(8*pi^(3/2)) (beta_ext*S)^(-3/2)
           +3*zeta(5/2)/(128*pi^(3/2)) (beta_ext*S)^(-5/2)
           +o((beta_ext*S)^(-5/2)).
   A direct finite-volume conversion, followed by the stated limits, gives
     p_J,beta(h_sigma)=kappa p_(1/2),kappa*beta(2h_sigma/kappa),
     m_sigma(beta)=2 m_(1/2)(kappa*beta).
   Therefore beta_ext*S=beta*(-2J), and doubling the deficit gives exactly
   the two coefficients printed in the note. Fixed J<0 also converts the
   remainder to o(beta^(-5/2)). The note preserves volume first, positive field
   to zero second, and low temperature last, and does not identify this
   pressure-derived magnetization with an arbitrary state from Theorem 1.
   The Gaussian calculation alone does not establish the interacting
   many-body remainder; the packet states that limitation correctly.
