# Mathematical routes
1. Direct continuous-time binary worldlines: successful. A finite-measure real symmetric compatibility kernel gives one compact spatial operator for all lengths at each temperature. Even moments are handled by Hilbert–Schmidt norms, avoiding an unjustified diagonal-kernel trace formula.
2. Two-purity contraction: successful. Re-proved the contraction, cross-temperature identities, dyadic induction and interpolation covering every even length, then bounded the ratio of physical Gibbs weights.
3. Exact integer closed walks and rational Taylor tails: successful. The primary certificate uses checked rotation orbits. Independent unreduced return counts and separate Hilbert–Schmidt moment calculations reproduce all 257 moments for each seed size.
4. Gauss-constraint elimination and local plaquette action: successful for all even lengths. Finite enumeration separately detects orientation mistakes and verifies both frozen sectors.
5. Finite Trotter transfer matrices: diagnostic only. Exact finite identities were tested; slow finite-slice convergence was not promoted to a limiting proof. The direct worldline construction removes that need.

These are complementary steps of one proof, not independent physical walls or five different completed theorems. Other proof strategies were not exhausted; no impossibility conclusion is asserted.
