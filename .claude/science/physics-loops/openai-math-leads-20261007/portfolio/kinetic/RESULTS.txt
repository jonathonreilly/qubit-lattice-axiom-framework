1. 364 + 149 + 363: a concrete finite collision construction

Definition, independently checked algebra
Let V={-1,0,1}^3, with one species X_v per velocity. Group unordered pairs
{i,j} by v_i+v_j and |v_i|^2+|v_j|^2. Join every two distinct pairs in each
group by reversible reactions X_i+X_j <-> X_k+X_l, both rate constants 1.
This gives 27 species, 288 reversible pairs / 576 directed reactions. Every
nontrivial complex has two distinct species: equal input velocities have no
other pair with the same momentum and energy, by strict convexity.

Let nu=e_k+e_l-e_i-e_j and F(f)=sum nu(f_i f_j-f_k f_l). Exact rational
stoichiometric rank is 22. The five rows (1,v_x,v_y,v_z,|v|^2) annihilate every
column and have rank 5, so they span all additive collision invariants.
An independently implemented elimination modulo 1000003 also has rank 22;
together with the five exact upper-bound identities this checks the rank.
The model avoids the content-exchange obstruction by actually changing velocity
labels during collisions. It consequently needs its own interpretation if
records are to carry immutable content.

For every alpha,u,beta, f_v*=exp(alpha+u.v-beta|v|^2) satisfies pairwise
flux balance. We checked it exactly with the rational state
f_v*=2^(v_x)3^(v_y)5^(v_z)7^(-|v|^2). The H-function
H(f)=sum_i f_i(log f_i-1) obeys
  dH/dt = -sum_reversible_pairs (x-y) log(x/y) <= 0,
  x=f_i f_j, y=f_k f_l.
This follows by inserting log f into the stoichiometric derivative, not from
an assumed thermodynamic arrow. Equality at positive f means log f is in the
five-dimensional invariant span, hence precisely the Gibbs form above.

positivity holds for every finite time. Polynomial local Lipschitz continuity

compactly supported nonnegative f0, and smooth bounded-derivative L2 fields

smallness or symmetry. Its key mechanism is cancellation of signed retarded
impulses before taking absolute values, followed by momentum-increment bounds.

We tested an exact finite positive-jump encoding of magnetic motion. For a
velocity-chain generator A with nonnegative rates,

If A|v|^2=0 pointwise and Av=v cross B/sqrt(1+|v|^2), then v dot Av=0,
so every nontrivial jump rate is zero. This is an exact restriction on this
