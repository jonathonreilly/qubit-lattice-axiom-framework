# Conditional finite-register to full original history lift

This is a root precomparison mathematical observation, not adoption of the still-unchecked growing-volume proof. The author's new proof has not been read. If the SAME trace-norm final quantum/register error is uniform over every finite dyadic time partition and original-mark/count register, it may already control the complete unnormalized original history instrument in total variation. It does not control normalized states conditioned on rare individual histories.

For each fixed finite n,S, both microscopic and target original dynamics preserve N between births and increase N by exactly two at a birth. Bare Omega starts with N=n and the carrier has at most2n occupancy, so the actual history contains at most floor(n/2) original marks; this is a carrier identity, not an imposed event cap. Histories lie in a finite union of ordered time simplices times finite original-label words. Each process defines a positive trace-class-valued measure with total trace one and final quantum state as its payload, embedded in the same separable rotor Hilbert space.

The difference has a trace-class Bochner density relative to the sum of the scalar trace measures. Trace class is separable here and these explicit finite-event instruments also admit density matrices against the usual ordered-time Lebesgue measures plus the no-event atom. Conditional expectation on increasing dyadic-bin sigma algebras converges in L1(trace class), by approximation by simple functions and scalar martingale convergence. The trace-class variation norm is therefore the increasing limit of norms of the finite coarse register outputs. A bound uniform in the binning carries through this limit unchanged. Mark words and terminal quantum operators must be retained in each coarse register; counts alone do not suffice.

Potential subtlety: the comparison must use the SAME measurable bins/words and Hilbert embedding for both processes, and the finite-register proof must allow the full word register, not merely counts or one bin independently. Deterministic boundaries and equal-time events have zero jump-time measure at fixed epsilon because rates are finite, but the no-event atom remains. The argument does not assert convergence of probability densities pointwise, conditional trajectories, physical-time intensity, unbounded energy or actual source/clock selection.
