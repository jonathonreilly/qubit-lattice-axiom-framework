These are explicit counterexamples in fixed declared classes, not global model/axiom exclusions.

N1: actual supplied hard-core/integer-rotor model and the precise topologies. N2: other regularity topologies, weighted boundary bounds, carriers and laws remain possible. N3: all quantum/law/state/coupling imports explicit. N4: exact landed input identities, no retained promotion. N5: finite controls and written operator proof have separate scope. N6: field cutoff, volume limit, finite-monitor instruments and total energy are distinguished. N7: microscopic/local-source/clock residuals remain. N8: no audit or foundation update.

The formal no-go N5 infeasibility certificate is not applicable to this positive theorem. No universal impossibility, independent-wall count or axiom pressure is inferred from either counterexample.
