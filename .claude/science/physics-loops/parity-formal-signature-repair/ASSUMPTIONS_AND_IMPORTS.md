# Assumptions And Imports

- No new axiom is introduced.
- `cpt_exact_note` remains the only source dependency for the free staggered `H_0` parity identity.
- The derivative-label signs are explicit formal bookkeeping: `chi(partial_0)=+1`, `chi(partial_i)=-1`.
- The PR does not claim an actual staggered-lattice derivative representative, a complete lattice operator basis, or a lattice-action no-go.
- The finite-lattice computation remains limited to the free staggered hopping Hamiltonian on checked even periodic tori.
