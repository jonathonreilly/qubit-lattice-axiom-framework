# Assumptions And Imports

## Load-Bearing Inputs

- Standard 4x4 Dirac algebra in the chiral basis.
- Formal parity action `P_Dirac = gamma^0`.
- Abstract derivative sign character:
  `partial_i -> -partial_i`, `partial_0 -> partial_0`.
- Exhaustive finite enumeration of the four SME-style dim-5 bilinear
  structures named in the note.

## Not Load-Bearing

- Actual lattice derivative representatives.
- Conjugation of lattice derivatives by `P_inv * epsilon`.
- Any lattice-action coefficient no-go.
- Any physical Lorentz-violation exclusion theorem.
