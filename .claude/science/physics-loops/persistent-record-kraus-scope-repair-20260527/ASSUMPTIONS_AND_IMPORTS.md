# Assumptions And Imports

## Consumed Inputs

- A finite-dimensional system Hilbert space.
- A finite record basis.
- A normalized isometry `W`.

These are definitions of the bounded theorem surface, not framework
imports.

## Not Consumed

- Persistent-record overlap-kernel dynamics.
- Minimal axiom rows.
- Kraus/Choi literature as a load-bearing citation.
- Born-rule downstream assumptions.
