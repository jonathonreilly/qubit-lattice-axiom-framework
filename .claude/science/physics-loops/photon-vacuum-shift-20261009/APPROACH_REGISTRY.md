# Mathematical approaches

- Dispersion geometry: strict lattice chord deficit controls multiparticle
  reference denominators; exact continuous-momentum inequality.
- CCR/normal ordering: cancel the extensive vacuum term before estimates,
  preserving the finite exchange remainder and interference.
- Gaussian covariance: integrate even kernel powers to bound every full-cosine
  interference term at two insertions.
- Compact variational spectral measure: live alternative, not pursued here;
  requires an actual ground-state/trial-state comparison and residue control.

Different oscillator sizes are diagnostic cases, not distinct proof families.
