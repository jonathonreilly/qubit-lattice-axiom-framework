# Assumptions And Imports

- Allowed input: finite cubical balls generated by the runner at `R = 2, 3, 4`.
- Allowed input: the runner's fixed diagonal convention for triangulating each
  boundary square.
- Forbidden import: PL Schoenflies / Alexander cone theorem for arbitrary PL
  balls.
- Forbidden import: Perelman, Moise, van Kampen, Alexander trick, or
  mapping-class-group facts.
- Forbidden import: Kawamoto-Smit homogeneity as a derived physical-closure
  premise.
- No observed values, fitted constants, literature values, or new axioms are
  load-bearing.
