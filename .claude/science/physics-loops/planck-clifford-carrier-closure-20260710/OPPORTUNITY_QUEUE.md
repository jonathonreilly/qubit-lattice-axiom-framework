# Opportunity Queue

This is a single-target deep attempt, not an unattended multi-lane campaign.
Within the target, the ranked opportunities are:

1. Derive an invertible substrate/Clifford intertwiner on `P_A`.
2. Find a canonical invariant irreducible summand of the full exterior
   Clifford action and determine whether it can replace `P_A` without a new
   selector.
3. If both fail, land the exact representation-mismatch obstruction and
   demote the positive note so downstream consumers cannot treat the explicit
   `C^4` construction as a substrate derivation.

Ranks favor direct blocker reachability, executable exact linear algebra, and
small branch blast radius.
