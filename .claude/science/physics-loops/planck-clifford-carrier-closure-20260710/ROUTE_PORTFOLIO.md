# Route Portfolio

## Orthogonal attack frames

| Attack frame | Intended movement | Hard-residual pressure | Decisive test |
|---|---|---:|---|
| representation intertwiner | constructive theorem or exact incompatibility | 3 | compare the substrate `1 + 3` spatial action on `P_1` with the `2 + 2` action induced by Clifford bivectors; solve for a nonzero invertible intertwiner |
| canonical exterior Clifford action | intrinsic full-cell construction | 3 | build `c(v)=epsilon(v)+iota(v)` on `Lambda^* W`, test `P_1` invariance, and classify the 16-dimensional module and its commutant |
| selector plus descent | combine the strongest `P_A` selector with an induced action | 2 | after granting `P_A`, test whether any substrate-native odd maps preserve it and satisfy the Clifford relations |
| commutant/minimal-ideal route | search for a canonical irreducible summand without naming `P_A` | 3 | classify minimal left ideals of the full-cell Clifford action and test invariance under the substrate automorphism/commutant group |
| exact no-go family | prove that the stated representation type forbids the claimed equivariant identification | 3 | character/Casimir mismatch and zero intertwiner space |

The representation-intertwiner route is first because it directly tests the
auditor’s missing implication and can either produce the needed map or refute
the claimed compatibility. The exterior-action and minimal-ideal routes are
independent checks against a false negative caused by the chosen `P_A` model.

## Fan-out synthesis after execution

- The representation-intertwiner route closes negatively: exact Casimir
  mismatch `1+3` versus `2+2`, with simultaneous-intertwiner nullity zero.
- The canonical exterior-action route constructs a genuine full-cell `Cl_4`
  action, but `P_A gamma P_A=0`, the packet leaks to degrees zero and two, and
  the full module has multiplicity four.
- The joint-algebra route gives `M_8(C)` with multiplicity two, so the smallest
  Clifford-plus-exterior-spin block is eight-dimensional.
- The native cubic-taste route avoids the `1+3` representation but leaves
  eight explicit Pauli-string time operators and a two-copy `Cl_4` module on
  `C^8`; it still needs an unsupplied multiplicity projector and event-cell
  bridge.
- The graph-first route supplies a `gl(4)` commutant/base surface. That is
  freedom to assign matrices, not a theorem selecting a Clifford coframe map.

All frames agree that a positive repair requires a changed representation
premise, not merely a stronger selector for the same `P_A` packet.
