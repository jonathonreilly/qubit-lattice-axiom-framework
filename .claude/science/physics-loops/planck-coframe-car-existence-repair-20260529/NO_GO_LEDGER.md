# No-Go Ledger

## Fixed-Inner-Product Daggered CAR Under Arbitrary Similarity

The broad route is false. A nonunitary similarity transform preserves Clifford anticommutators and the word-span facts, but generally changes adjoints against a fixed standard inner product. Therefore fixed-inner-product daggered CAR is not a consequence of P1 alone.

## New Hermitian Premise

Not used. Adding a compatible Hermitian/self-adjoint premise would be a new premise path rather than a repair of the existing row.
