- Symmetry-only substrate-to-`P_A` forcing fails: `P_3` and other rank-four
  equivariant packets survive the same stated symmetry tests.
- Abstract first-order coframe unconditionality fails: Hodge maps `P_1` and
  `P_3` while preserving the symmetry package.

  Hodge-dual without an action-source domain.

This block does not dispute those no-gos. It records the surviving route as
conditional on the link-local action-source premise.
