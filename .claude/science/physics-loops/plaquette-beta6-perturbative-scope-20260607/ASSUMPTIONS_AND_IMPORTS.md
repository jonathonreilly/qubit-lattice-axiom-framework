# Assumptions And Imports

- Supplied packet: beta=6 Wilson normalization, `w_1..w_16`, MC comparator,
  and F2 comparator.
- Runner-local arithmetic: finite truncations, tadpole fixed points, Pade grids,
  tadpole-Pade grids, and residual diagnostics.
- Not supplied by this branch: retained/effective-bounded authority rows for
  the NSPT packet or comparator surfaces.
