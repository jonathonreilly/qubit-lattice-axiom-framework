# Assumptions And Imports

## SU3 dabc

Allowed inputs:

- Gell-Mann generators `T^a = lambda^a / 2`.
- Trace normalization `Tr[T^a T^b] = delta^{ab}/2`.
- Definition `d^{abc} = 2 Tr[{T^a,T^b}T^c]`.

Forbidden imports:

- Physical SM color identification.
- QCD amplitude or gluon-sector readings.
- Physical quark-color cubic-Casimir interpretation.

## Record Unbounded Schema

Allowed inputs:

- Record finite additivity after a supplied readout context and supplied disjoint produced records exist.
- Finite `Z^3` site lists.

Open imports:

- Production of nonzero records.
- Unit normalization or positive lower bound.
- Supplied readout context.
- Arbitrarily large nonzero produced-record availability.
