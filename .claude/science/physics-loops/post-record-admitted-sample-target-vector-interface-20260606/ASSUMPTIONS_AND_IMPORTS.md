# Assumptions And Imports

## Allowed premises

- admitted finite post-record sample;
- supplied statistic set;
- exact finite counting.

## Open imports

- The sample is admitted observation data.
- Weights and selection rules remain supplied.
- The sample alone does not select a production kernel.
- The sample does not derive a Born law.
- Stable settings are not selected dials.
- Independent audit owns any future verdict.
