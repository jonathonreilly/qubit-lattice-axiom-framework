# Route Portfolio

## Route A: Count reversal no-go

Status: executed.

Prove directly that `count(w) = count(reverse(w))`, and that count pushforwards
of finite laws are invariant under reversal. This prunes the route from
post-record counts or count certificates to a physical arrow.

## Route B: Transition orientation witness

Status: executed inside the runner.

Show that oriented transition counts reverse by transposition. For asymmetric
histories, the count state is identical while forward and reversed empirical
kernels differ. This isolates the missing bridge: orientation selects the
kernel direction; counts do not.
