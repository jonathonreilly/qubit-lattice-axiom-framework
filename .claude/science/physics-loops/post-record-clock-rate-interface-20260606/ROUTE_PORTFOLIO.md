# Route Portfolio

## Route A: Event Index Versus Physical Clock

Status: selected and implemented.

Show that append histories determine event index and prefix order, while many
strictly increasing clock maps preserve the same history and induce different
rates.

## Route B: Supplied-Clock Conditional Rates

Status: support side of the theorem.

Once a clock map is supplied, durations and rates are exactly computed from the
record stream.

## Route C: Hamiltonian/Transfer Derivation

Status: rejected for this block.

Append/count algebra does not derive a carrier Hamiltonian, transfer matrix, or
physical time step.

## Route D: Dial Selection From Event Rates

Status: rejected for this block.

No event-index theorem selects a generation/Koide dial location.
