# Goal

Build a finite classifier for what bounded/conditional audit rows may claim
from the evidence they supply after post-record histories and counts are typed.

The target is a ladder, not a verdict engine. It should separate:

- exact append/count support;
- finite-law p-values;
- law-scoped concentration certificates;
- simulation-only support;
- stable-setting support;
- selected-dial support;
- bounded production-dynamics support;
- independent audit verdict ownership.
