# No-Go Ledger

## NG-001: Expectation to p-value

Expectation-only evidence cannot certify p-values. It needs a finite law,
exact enumeration, or law-scoped concentration certificate.

## NG-002: Simulation to calibration

Simulation-only evidence is support-only. It cannot be treated as a calibrated
p-value without a finite certificate or a declared statistical protocol.

## NG-003: Stable setting to selected dial

A stable setting under a supplied map or flow is not a selected dial value. A
selector, invariance, score, target prior, or rule must be supplied.

## NG-004: Branch-local classifier to audit verdict

This branch cannot apply audit verdicts. The independent audit lane owns
verdicts and effective status.
