# No-Go Ledger

## Counts-Alone Probability

Finite realized counts do not select a unique predictive probability law.
Multiple incompatible laws can assign positive likelihood to the same finite
history.

## Counts-Alone Born

Born weights require pre-record state/effect/instrument structure. Counts can
audit a supplied Born model over repeated trials but cannot derive the trace
functional.

## Counts-Alone Rate

Record append/count dynamics is event-indexed. A time metric, clock, or
transition rate is a separate bridge.

## Counts-Alone Dial Selection

Finite count histories do not select a generation/Koide dial location. Dial
stability or selection needs a separately specified dynamics or prior.
