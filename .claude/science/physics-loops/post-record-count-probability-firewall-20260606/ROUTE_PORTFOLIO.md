# Route Portfolio

## Route A: Counts-Alone Probability No-Go

Status: selected and implemented.

Show that finite realized counts have multiple compatible predictive laws and
therefore cannot select a unique probability/Born law.

## Route B: Empirical Frequency As Model Estimator

Status: bounded support only.

Frequencies can estimate parameters after an iid or other statistical model is
admitted. This route is not a derivation of the model.

## Route C: Born From Pre-Record Instrument

Status: outside this block.

Born weights belong to the pre-record/instrument layer and require the
effect-probability bridge. Counts can later be compared with the supplied
weights.

## Route D: Dial Selection From Frequencies

Status: rejected for this block.

No finite count theorem selects the generation/Koide dial. A dial setting can
be stable or statistically fitted only under additional model assumptions.
