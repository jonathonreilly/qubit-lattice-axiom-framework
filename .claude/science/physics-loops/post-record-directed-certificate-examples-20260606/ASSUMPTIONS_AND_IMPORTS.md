# Assumptions And Imports

## Allowed premises

- finite supplied law on words;
- supplied bridge id, law id, orientation convention, and clock/order id;
- supplied kernel/transfer/instrument id when a certificate uses dynamics
  language;
- supplied directed statistic or event;
- exact finite enumeration with rational arithmetic;
- upstream arrow-orientation firewall and supplied-orientation bridge
  interface.

## Open imports

- The physical arrow is not derived here.
- The production kernel is not derived here.
- The clock, rate, Hamiltonian, transfer operator, instrument, or boundary
  condition is not derived here.
- The Born law is not derived here.
- Stable settings are not selected dials.
- Generation/Koide dial location is not selected or forced.
- Independent audit owns any future verdict or effective retained status.

## Pre-record/post-record interpretation

The law carries probabilities. The post-record word or site carries realized
markers, atoms, and counts. This branch uses that split as an interpretation
discipline, not as a new axiom.
