# No-Go Ledger

- Count pushforwards are invariant under word reversal, so count-only
  certificates cannot orient a physical arrow.
- A supplied empirical kernel is valid input for a certificate but is not
  selected by the count state.
- Directed examples do not select a production kernel, Hamiltonian, transfer
  operator, instrument, clock, rate, or Born law.
- A stable setting or stable location is not a selected generation/Koide dial.
- Certificates are law-scoped and bridge-scoped; they do not transport across
  laws, orientations, clocks, kernels, or statistics without rechecking.
