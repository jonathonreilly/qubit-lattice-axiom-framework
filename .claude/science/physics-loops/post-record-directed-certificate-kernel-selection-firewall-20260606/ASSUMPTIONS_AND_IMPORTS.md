# Assumptions And Imports

## Allowed premises

- finite sources, kernels, laws, and directed statistics;
- exact rational enumeration;
- upstream directed-certificate examples;
- upstream supplied-orientation bridge and arrow-orientation firewall.

## Open imports

- A production kernel must be supplied or selected by a separate rule.
- A clock, rate, Hamiltonian, transfer operator, instrument, or boundary
  condition is not derived here.
- A Born law is not derived here.
- A physical arrow is not derived here.
- Generation/Koide dial location is not selected or forced.
- Independent audit owns any future verdict.

## Interpretation discipline

The law or production model carries probability. Post-record words carry
realized information. Realized records can test a supplied model but do not
select the model without additional selection authority.
