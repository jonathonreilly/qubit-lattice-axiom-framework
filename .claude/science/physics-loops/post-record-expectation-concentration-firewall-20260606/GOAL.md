# Goal

Determine whether post-record expected frequencies can themselves deliver
concentration, finite p-values, or audit calibration.

The target is narrow. The branch does not ask whether concentration theorems
exist under additional hypotheses. It asks whether the record/dynamics surface
already derives those hypotheses from expected post-record count bookkeeping.

Expected outcome: an exact no-go if two finite post-record laws can share the
same expected counts and one-time marginals while giving different tail
probabilities and p-values.
