# No-Go Ledger

## NG-001: Expectation to concentration

Route:

```text
expected post-record frequency
  => concentration / p-value / audit verdict
```

Verdict: no-go.

Reason: the iid fair law and perfectly correlated fair law share the same
expected counts and same one-time marginals at horizon four, but assign
different probabilities to extreme imbalance and different p-values to the
same observed word.

Scope: finite two-letter horizon-four counterexample. It does not rule out
conditional concentration under supplied independence, mixing, martingale, or
other explicit hypotheses.

## NG-002: Expected frequency to generation/Koide dial

Route:

```text
expected frequency
  => dial-location certification without supplied score/rule/law
```

Verdict: no-go in this block.

Reason: the branch contains no supplied score/rule/kernel that would select a
dial location, and the counterexample shows that expectation-level bookkeeping
does not determine the finite law needed for calibrated selection.
