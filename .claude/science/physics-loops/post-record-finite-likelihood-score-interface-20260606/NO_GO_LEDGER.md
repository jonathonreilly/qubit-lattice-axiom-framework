# No-Go Ledger

## Record Alone Derives Candidate Models

Rejected. Post-record data supply a realized word. A probability law over
possible words is an additional model input.

## Likelihood Ordering Is Model Selection

Rejected. Likelihood ordering is a score under supplied models. Selection
requires a supplied decision rule, threshold, loss, or prior policy.

## Posterior Weight Is Framework Authority

Rejected. Posterior weights require a supplied prior and update rule. They do
not apply audit verdicts or retained status.

## Score Interface Selects The Dial

Rejected. A dial candidate can be scored only after it supplies a finite law.
The score interface does not force a stable dial location.
