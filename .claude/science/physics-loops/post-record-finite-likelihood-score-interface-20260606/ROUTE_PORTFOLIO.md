# Route Portfolio

## Route A: Likelihood Vector

Evaluate each supplied finite law at the realized record word.

Status: pursued and verified.

## Route B: Likelihood Ratios

Compare two supplied models by exact rational ratios when the denominator is
nonzero.

Status: pursued and verified, with zero-denominator guard.

## Route C: Supplied-Prior Bayes Weights

Normalize `pi_m L_m(w*)` over a supplied model list and supplied prior.

Status: pursued and verified as a conditional score, not a selected verdict.

## Route D: Derive The Candidate Family From Record

Try to infer the model list from post-record append/count structure.

Status: rejected. Record gives realized words and counts, not the space of
candidate probability laws.

## Route E: Select The Dial

Use score ordering to force a generation/Koide dial.

Status: rejected. A score can evaluate a supplied dial candidate only after the
candidate law and decision rule are supplied.
