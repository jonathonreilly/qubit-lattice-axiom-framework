# Literature Bridges

No external literature is required for this finite certificate. The p-value
validity statement is verified by direct enumeration over the finite word
space, not by importing an asymptotic theorem.

Potential later bridges:

- exact tests and randomized p-values for sharper discrete calibration;
- likelihood-ratio tests for supplied finite model classes;
- martingale or concentration bounds for long horizons where enumeration is too
  large.

Those later bridges would be additional supplied-method interfaces, not
derivations from Record alone.
