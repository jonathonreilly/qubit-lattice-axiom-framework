# Assumptions And Imports

## Assumed

- A finite post-record alphabet.
- A supplied target prior `pi`.
- A supplied reset strength `alpha` with `0 < alpha < 1`.

## Not Supplied By Record

- The target prior.
- The reset strength.
- The physical source of the target or reset kernel.
- Clock/rate, Hamiltonian/action/coupling, Born/instrument, or dial-selection
  bridges.
