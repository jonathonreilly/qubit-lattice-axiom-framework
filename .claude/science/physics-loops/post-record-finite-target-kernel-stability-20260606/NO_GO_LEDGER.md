# No-Go Ledger

## Stability Selects Target

Rejected. Every supplied finite target prior has a reset kernel that makes it
stable.

## Record Derives Alpha

Rejected. The contraction strength is an extra dynamics parameter.

## Target Stability Forces Dial

Rejected. A dial target can be stabilized after it is supplied, but the
stability theorem does not choose the target.

## Reset Kernel Derives Physical Rate

Rejected. Event-step contraction does not supply a physical clock.
