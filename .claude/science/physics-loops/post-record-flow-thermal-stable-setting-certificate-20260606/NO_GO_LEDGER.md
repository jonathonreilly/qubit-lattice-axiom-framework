# No-Go Ledger

- Stable setting does not imply selected dial.
- A separatrix is a stable feature only under the supplied map; it is not a
  physical selector.
- Thermal or score roots are scoped to the supplied evaluator.
- Bounded obstruction rows remain bounded obstructions.
- Generation/Koide stable features are not forced values.
- Record does not derive the flow, score, thermal rule, selector, kernel, clock,
  rate, or physical arrow.
