# Assumptions And Imports

## Supplied inputs

- Current audit-ledger snapshot.
- Upstream selector/dial row subdivision.
- Upstream flow/thermal stable-setting certificate.
- Supplied dial coordinate examples, including `Q(r)=(1+2r)/3`.
- Supplied stability predicates and exact finite/algebraic checks.

## Not derived here

- The physical selector for a dial value.
- A generation/Koide dial value from Record.
- A measure, prior, source law, score, flow, map, kernel, clock, or arrow from
  Record.
- Equal weighting in the records/objectivity conditional route.
- Audit verdicts or effective retained status.
