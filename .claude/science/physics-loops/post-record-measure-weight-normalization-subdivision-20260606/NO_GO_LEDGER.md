# No-Go Ledger

- Normalized measure is not selected dial.
- Source measure, prior, trace state, and channel weights are supplied imports,
  not consequences of Record.
- A generation/Koide row with weights is not a forced value.
- A source unit is not selector authority without a selector rule.
- Finite normalization does not derive Born law, production dynamics, clock,
  rate, kernel, or physical arrow.
