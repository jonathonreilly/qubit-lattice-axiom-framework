# Assumptions And Imports

## Assumed

- Finite post-record word data.
- Supplied candidate probability laws when scores are computed.
- Optional supplied priors or thresholds when posterior/decision examples are
  evaluated.

## Not Supplied By Record

- The candidate model family.
- The prior.
- The loss, threshold, tie-breaker, or decision rule.
- The admissibility rule for adding or excluding candidate laws.
- The physical origin of the candidate probabilities.
- A generation/Koide dial setting.
