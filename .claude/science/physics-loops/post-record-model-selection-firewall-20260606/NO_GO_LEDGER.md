# No-Go Ledger

## Score To Canonical Model

No-go. A score vector lacks a prior, loss, threshold, tie-breaker, and
candidate admissibility rule.

## Bayes Without Prior

No-go. Different supplied priors can reverse the posterior argmax for the same
likelihood vector.

## Maximum Likelihood Without Tie/Admissibility Rule

No-go. Distinct laws can tie at the observed word, and extending the candidate
family can change the winner.

## Score To Dial

No-go. A generation/Koide dial candidate can be evaluated only after it supplies
a candidate law and a supplied selection/stability rule.
