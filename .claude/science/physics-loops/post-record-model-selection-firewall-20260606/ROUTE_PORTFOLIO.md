# Route Portfolio

## Route A: Prior Reversal

Use the same likelihood vector with two different supplied priors to reverse
the posterior argmax.

Status: pursued and verified.

## Route B: Tie Without Tie-Breaker

Construct two distinct finite laws with equal likelihood on the observed word.

Status: pursued and verified.

## Route C: Family Extension

Add a normalized candidate law concentrated on the observed word and show the
maximum-likelihood winner changes.

Status: pursued and verified.

## Route D: Threshold Dependence

Use the same likelihood ratio with two supplied thresholds to obtain different
decisions.

Status: pursued and verified.

## Route E: Force Dial Selection

Try to infer a generation/Koide dial from the score vector alone.

Status: rejected by Routes A-D.
