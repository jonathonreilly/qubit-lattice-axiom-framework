# No-Go Ledger

- Count or realized-record data do not derive a production kernel.
- Bounded finite scans do not imply continuum or unbounded retained status.
- Detector readout tables do not derive the physical instrument or source
  formation law.
- Persistent record overlap tables do not derive the record-writing law,
  preservation law, or physical overlap kernel.
- A stable setting is not a selected dial.
- A generation/Koide stable location is not a forced value.
