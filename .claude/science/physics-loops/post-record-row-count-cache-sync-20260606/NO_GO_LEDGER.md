# No-Go Ledger

- Do not convert audit statuses or effective statuses in the ledger.
- Do not claim a stable setting is a selected dial.
- Do not claim finite current-snapshot row counts are invariant mathematical
  facts; they are exact only for the current ledger snapshot.
- Do not derive physical measure, selector authority, Born law, production
  dynamics, clock/rate, or physical arrow from Record in this block.
