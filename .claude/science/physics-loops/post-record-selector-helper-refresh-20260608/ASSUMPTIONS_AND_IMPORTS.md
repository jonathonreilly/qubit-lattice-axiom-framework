# Assumptions And Imports

## Load-Bearing

- Current ledger row enumeration is read-only.
- The measure/weight helper supplies the `selector_tangent_readout_weight` lane split.
- The selector prototype only certifies finite supplied weights and a supplied positive tangent metric/Hessian.

## Open Imports

- No Record-derived selector/tangent/readout carrier is supplied.
- No Record-derived readout map or weights are supplied.
- No Record-derived positive tangent metric/Hessian is supplied.

## Not Used

- No audit verdict is applied.
- No audit data file is edited.
- No physical measure, Born law, production dynamics, or generation/Koide dial is derived.
