# Assumptions And Imports

Allowed inputs:

- Supplied finite carrier, supplied nonnegative weights, and supplied positive
  tangent metric/Hessian.
- Retained upstream finite weight-normalization subdivision.

Forbidden inputs:

- No selector authority.
- No Record-derived carrier, readout primitive, tangent metric, or Hessian.
- No physical measure, Born law, production dynamics, or physical arrow.
