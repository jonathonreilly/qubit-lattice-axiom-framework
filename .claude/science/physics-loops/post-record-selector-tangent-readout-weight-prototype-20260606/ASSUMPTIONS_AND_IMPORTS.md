# Assumptions And Imports

## Supplied inputs

- Current audit-ledger snapshot.
- Upstream measure/weight normalization subdivision.
- Supplied finite readout/tangent carrier.
- Supplied positive tangent metric or Hessian.
- Supplied readout/projection weights.

## Not derived here

- Selector authority or physical selector rule.
- Physical readout/preparation primitive.
- Tangent metric, Hessian, Born law, source law, or physical measure from
  Record.
- Generation/Koide dial value.
- Production dynamics, kernel, clock, rate, or physical arrow.
