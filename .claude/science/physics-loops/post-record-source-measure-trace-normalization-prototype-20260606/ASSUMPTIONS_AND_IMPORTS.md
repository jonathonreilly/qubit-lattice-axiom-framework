# Assumptions And Imports

## Supplied inputs

- Current audit-ledger snapshot.
- Upstream measure/weight normalization subdivision.
- Supplied finite carrier.
- Supplied positive trace/reference measure.
- Supplied nonnegative source weights with positive total.
- Supplied observable on the same finite carrier.

## Not derived here

- Physical identification of the trace reference as pre-record reference.
- Born law, prior, source law, selector, or physical measure from Record.
- Production dynamics, kernel, clock, rate, or physical arrow.
- Audit verdicts or effective retained status.
