# No-Go Ledger

## Stable setting is not selected dial

The user's constraint is load-bearing. This block may identify rows where a
stable setting could be supported under a supplied surface, map, score, rule,
or kernel. It must not claim that stability alone selects the dial value.

## Keyword bucket is not a verdict

The runner classifies rows for triage. It does not certify the row's theorem,
audit status, physical truth, or downstream closure.

## Record does not supply physical arrow here

The post-record ledger can carry realized information/counts. This block does
not derive a physical arrow, Hamiltonian, transfer kernel, instrument, clock,
or rate from Record.

## Generation/Koide remains unfixed

No generation or Koide dial is selected or forced. Those rows remain stable
location candidates only under supplied surfaces and rules.
