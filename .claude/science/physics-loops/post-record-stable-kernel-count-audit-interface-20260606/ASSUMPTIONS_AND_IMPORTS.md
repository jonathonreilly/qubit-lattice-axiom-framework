# Assumptions And Imports

## Assumed

- A finite record alphabet.
- A supplied initial law and transition kernel.
- A finite horizon.
- A supplied count statistic, tail direction, and optional threshold.

## Not Supplied By Record

- The kernel.
- The statistic or threshold.
- The physical probability source.
- Concentration or asymptotic approximation.
- Audit verdict authority.
- Clock/rate, Born/instrument, Hamiltonian/action/coupling, or dial selection.
