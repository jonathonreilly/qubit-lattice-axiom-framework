# Goal

Build the concentration side of the post-record audit interface.

The aim is not to prove concentration from Record. The aim is to make the safe
contract explicit:

```text
supplied finite law or supplied concentration theorem
  + statistic/event
  + law-scoped certificate
  => conservative audit flag under that law
```

This lets bounded and conditional audit rows consume calibrated concentration
evidence without treating expected post-record counts as probabilities.
