# Assumptions And Imports

## Supplied Inputs

- Finite projective ladder levels.
- Projection maps between adjacent levels.
- A family-lift rule naming the stable predicate.

## Open Imports

- Derivation or external justification of the family-lift rule.
- Independent audit before effective retained status.

## Not Imported

- No density limit is imported from this finite ladder.
- No unbounded retained authority is applied.
- No dial, kernel, selector, target, or weight is selected.
