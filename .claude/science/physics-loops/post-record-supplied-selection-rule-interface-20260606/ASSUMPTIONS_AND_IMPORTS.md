# Assumptions And Imports

## Assumed

- A finite candidate set.
- A supplied exact rational score map.
- A supplied tie-priority order or decision rule.
- A positive score gap if margin stability is claimed.

## Not Supplied By Record

- Candidate generation.
- The score map.
- Tie priority, threshold, loss, or decision rule.
- The physical source of scores.
- Born weights, instruments, Hamiltonians, actions, clocks, or rates.
- A generation/Koide dial score landscape.
