# No-Go Ledger

## Record Derives Scores

Rejected. Record supplies post-record words and counts, not a physical score
landscape over candidate models or dials.

## Record Derives Selection Rule

Rejected. Tie-priority, threshold, loss, and decision policies are separate
inputs.

## Stability Without Margin

Rejected. The runner checks strict stability only below half a positive score
gap; at the boundary, strict margin can be lost.

## Supplied Selector Forces Dial

Rejected. The selector can choose a dial only inside the supplied candidate
score surface.
