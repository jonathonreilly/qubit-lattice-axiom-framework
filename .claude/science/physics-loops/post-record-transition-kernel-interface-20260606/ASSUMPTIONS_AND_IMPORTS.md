# Assumptions And Imports

## Load-Bearing Inputs

- Existing finite post-record history/count theorems.
- A supplied finite initial law on the record alphabet.
- A supplied row-stochastic finite transition kernel.
- Finite probability arithmetic.

## Non-Inputs

- No derivation of the kernel.
- No derivation of Markov property or stationarity.
- No clock/rate bridge.
- No Born/instrument bridge.
- No Hamiltonian/action/coupling selector.
- No generation/Koide dial selector.

## Status Impact

Exact-support for the supplied-kernel interface. Physical stochastic dynamics
remains conditional on the supplied kernel and its assumptions.
