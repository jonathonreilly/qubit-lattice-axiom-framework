# No-Go Ledger

## Kernel Derivation

The finite append/count layer does not derive the transition kernel.

## Markov/Stationarity

The Markov property and stationarity are model assumptions, not consequences of
Record.

## Clocked Rates

Transition probabilities are not transition rates until a clock or time
denominator is supplied.

## Born/Instrument Origin

If the kernel is meant to be Born/instrument-derived, the pre-record bridge is
separate.

## Dial Selection

The supplied-kernel interface has no generation/Koide dial selector.
