# Literature Bridges

No external literature is required. The two-state stationary vector and
contraction identity are verified by direct rational arithmetic.

Potential later bridges:

- multi-state Markov-chain ergodic theorems under explicit irreducibility and
  aperiodicity assumptions;
- physical derivation of kernels from supplied instruments or transfer
  operators;
- clock-rate conversion from event-step kernels to physical rates.

Those later bridges are additional premises, not derivations from Record
alone.
