# No-Go Ledger

## Record Derives Kernel

Rejected. Post-record append/count dynamics does not supply transition
probabilities.

## Stability Selects Kernel

Rejected. Different supplied kernels on the same two-state alphabet have
different stable locations.

## Equal-Letter Forces Dial

Rejected. Equal-letter is a stable location for a symmetric supplied kernel,
not a proof that the physical kernel is symmetric.

## Stability Derives Rate

Rejected. The discrete transition kernel gives event-step dynamics, not a
physical clock or transition rate.
