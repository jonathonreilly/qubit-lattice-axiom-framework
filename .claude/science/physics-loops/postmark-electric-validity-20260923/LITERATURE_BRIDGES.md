# Literature bridges

| Source | Mathematical tool considered | What transfers | What does not transfer without a new proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Teschl, *Jacobi Operators and Completely Integrable Nonlinear Lattices*](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~gerald/ftp/book-jac/) | Jacobi operators, Weyl theory, extensions, moment problems | Vocabulary and methods for the unbounded path operator | Essential self-adjointness or extension selection for these 15-residue quadratic coefficients |
| [Sahbani, periodic perturbations of unbounded Jacobi operators](https://journals.bg.agh.edu.pl/OPUSCULA/2016.36.6/OpMath.2016.36.6.807.pdf) | Essential self-adjointness for specified periodic Jacobi families | Candidate theorem-search direction | Coefficients/hypotheses are not matched; failure to meet Carleman's sufficient hypothesis is inconclusive |
| [Bouten, Gohm, Gough, and Nurdin, Trotter-Kato quantum Markov limits](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjqt/s40507-015-0024-2) | Core/graph convergence and compact-time semigroup limits | A proof template if a fixed limiting generator and common domain are established | The present leading term is `C H2,infinity` with `C` varying; hypotheses not yet shown |
| [Arenz, Burgarth, Facchi, and Hillier, dynamical decoupling of unbounded Hamiltonians](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.06143) | Averaging with unbounded generators | Warns that domain and remainder control matter | Pulse decoupling model is not this flux-dependent hopping evolution |
| [Continuous-variable dynamical decoupling and homogenization](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.05908) | Quadratic continuous-variable homogenization | A possible comparison for scaled-coordinate dynamics | Different state space and assumptions; no conclusion transferred |

These are method leads only. The block imports no literature theorem as a premise.
