First decisive check: derive a long-tau estimate while the coefficients vary over the physical interval and the boundary is active, then look for a uniform limit or separated subsequence. Premise risk is moderate because the exact model comes from open #8831; invalidation scope is limited to this output and sector. Review cost is high without an analytic error estimate.
