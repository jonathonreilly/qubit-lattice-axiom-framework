## Exercise 2: First-Principles Reduction

Minimal physical driver: `y_t` must be an invariant physical top response to a
canonical Higgs fluctuation, not a response to an arbitrary source coordinate.
The same Cl(3)/Z3 surface must provide the top observable, the Higgs/source
observable, the action or transfer authority linking them, and the pole/LSZ
normalization.

The observable must identify the top response to the physical scalar carrier.
Acceptable positive data would be one of:

- a same-surface EW/Higgs action with `dS/ds = sum O_H`, canonical `O_H`, and
  strict physical `C_ss/C_sH/C_HH(tau)` pole rows;
- a W/Z physical-response packet with accepted action, production W/Z rows,
  same-source top rows, matched covariance, strict non-observed `g2` or another
  allowed absolute pin, `delta_perp`, and final W-response rows;
- a Schur/Feshbach pole packet with pole coordinate, `K'(pole)` or equivalent,
  source projection numerator, canonical bridge, and FV/IR/contact authority;
- a neutral H3/H4 packet with physical neutral transfer/off-diagonal primitive
  authority and source/canonical-Higgs coupling.

Invariant under source reparametrization: zero/nonzero qualitative support,
rank statements, and dimensionless ratios whose numerator and denominator are
measured on the same certified source with matched covariance and absolute
normalization.  Base Block118's selected finite axis is invariant support for
the candidate direction, but Block122 verifies that it is not invariant
action/LSZ/source-overlap normalization.  Not invariant: raw source slopes,
finite source coordinates, renamed taste-radial aliases, contact-shifted
source-source rows, or a number called `y_t` before the canonical source and
physical normalization are fixed.

Measured/proved without imports: current PR230 can prove support and exact
boundaries.  It cannot import `v`, observed targets, `H_unit`, `alpha_LM`, or
unit normalizations to fix the absolute top-Yukawa readout.

## Exercise 3: Literature Search Record

Literature is route context only:

- FMS/gauge-invariant BEH: Maas, Sondenheimer, and Toerek,
  `arXiv:1709.07477`, clarifies why physical Higgs/W/Z statements need
  gauge-invariant composite/observable identification.  It does not supply a
  PR230 same-surface `O_H` row.
- GEVP spectral residues: Blossier et al., `arXiv:0902.1265`, supports the
  need for spectral/GEVP matrix elements in lattice spectroscopy.  It does not
  supply PR230 `C_sH/C_HH(tau)` pole rows.
- Moment determinacy: Carleman/Stieltjes and Curto-Fialkow flat-extension
  literature give tests for when finite moments determine measures.  The
  current finite PR230 rows do not pass as physical pole/LSZ authority.
- Feshbach-Schur maps: mathematical physics Feshbach-Schur literature supplies
  a template for effective pole equations.  PR230 still lacks strict pole
  coordinate, derivative, and residue rows.

## Exercise 4: Mathematics Search Record

Candidate mathematics not admitted as proof input yet:

- operator-algebra/GNS/OS reconstruction: useful for specifying transfer
  authority, but current rows do not satisfy a physical OS transfer certificate;
- total positivity/Perron-Frobenius: useful for neutral primitive tests, but
  Block116 finds no physical neutral transfer or H4 coupling;
- Schur complement/Feshbach: useful for a pole-row contract, but current rows
  are finite inverse-block support;
- flat extension/moment problem: useful for excluding finite-proxy promotion;
- holonomic/source-response routes: only admissible after a current-surface
  `O_H`/h-source exists.
- invariant finite-axis selectors: useful for selecting a candidate direction,
  but insufficient for action/LSZ/source-overlap authority without additional
  physical rows or an accepted action theorem.
