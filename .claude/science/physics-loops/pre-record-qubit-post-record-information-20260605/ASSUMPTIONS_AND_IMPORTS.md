# Assumptions And Imports

## Framework premises

- `MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-05.md`: Lattice, Quantum, Record.
- Quantum supplies one qubit per site / one-site algebra `M_2(C)`.
- Record supplies durable realized-outcome registration, `K`/CPT-orbit readout
  once a context is supplied, and finite scalar additivity over disjoint
  records.

## Standard mathematics / finite quantum mechanics

- Finite-dimensional density matrices and projective instruments.
- Selective and nonselective projective-update algebra in the finite example.
- Pointer-basis copy/re-read check and the finite no-cloning boundary exhibited
  by the runner.

## Not assumed as closed

- A physical record-production dynamics.
- Decoherence, arrow, time metric, or physical persistence mechanism.
- Operational frequency/typicality identification for Born weights.
- Equal-letter generation prior or Koide value.

## Exact theorem premise boundary

The exact Record-typing theorem does not require a physical instrument,
decoherence model, re-readability theorem, or Born-frequency typicality. It uses
only the current Record wording that the realized outcome is a `K`/CPT orbit in
a supplied finite readout context and that Record supplies no probability,
weighting, or normalization.

Repeat-read/copyability remains useful dynamics intuition, but it is not a
load-bearing premise for the exact type separation.
