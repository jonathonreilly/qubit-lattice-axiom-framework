Status: executed.

Show that the pre-record qubit, the record-event instrument, and the post-record
information token are different object types. Verify the distinction in a
finite qubit example.

## Route B: Full no-broadcasting theorem

Status: deferred.

Could strengthen the copy/re-read boundary with a full theorem that only
commuting families are broadcastable. Not needed for this bounded artifact.

## Route C: Generation prior stability dynamics

Status: next opportunity.

Use the typed firewall to compare equal-letter and dimension priors under an
explicit post-record update/renormalization rule. This is the likely next
positive route for the Koide dial.

## Route D: Record-formation dynamics

Status: open.

Try to derive or constrain the physical instrument `{K_r}` from local lattice
and record-stability requirements. This remains a larger dynamics target.
