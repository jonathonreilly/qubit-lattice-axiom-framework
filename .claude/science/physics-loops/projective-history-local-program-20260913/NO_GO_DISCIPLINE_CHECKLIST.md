# No-go discipline applicability

The claim is a positive programmed realization on a declared prepared family,
with an upper resource bound and a scalar data-history error bound. It does
not assert a necessary exponential cost, probability uniqueness or an axiom
wall. The off-code comparison exhibits two particular measures and is not
an impossibility theorem for robust encodings or physical compilers. No
route-exhaustion or N1-N8 negative certification is claimed or fabricated.
A general no-go would require a separate quantified proof and full packet.
