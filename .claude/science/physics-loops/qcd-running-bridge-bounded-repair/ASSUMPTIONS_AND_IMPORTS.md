# Assumptions And Imports

- No new axioms are introduced.
- The branch is stacked on the plaquette bounded repair because this QCD bridge
  remains blocked until the plaquette row is audit-accepted.
- The bridge uses standard SM 2-loop RGE infrastructure and quark-mass
  thresholds as explicit bounded inputs.
- `alpha_s(M_Z)` / PDG values are comparators and uncertainty envelopes, not
  fitted proof targets.
