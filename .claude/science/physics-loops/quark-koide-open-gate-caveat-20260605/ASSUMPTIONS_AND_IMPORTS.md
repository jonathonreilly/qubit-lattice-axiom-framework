# Assumptions And Imports

## Allowed Inputs

- Existing `C_3`/Koide algebra authorities already linked from the source note.
- Existing packet-local quark and charged-lepton central-value comparators.

## Explicit Caveat

The relation `Q = sum(x_k^2) / (sum x_k)^2` and
`|b|^2/a^2 = (3Q - 1) / 2` is only defined when `sum x_k != 0`, equivalently
when the mean `a` is nonzero.

## Forbidden Inputs

- No quark mass derivation.
- No scheme/scale convention derivation.
- No transfer of charged-lepton BAE to quarks.
- No new axiom or audit verdict edit.
