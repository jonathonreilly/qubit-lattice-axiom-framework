# No-Go Ledger

- Finite post-record algebra does not supply a nontrivial connected reversible
  dynamics: `Aut(C^O)` is finite and `Der(C^O)=0`.
- Append/count updates are not reversible automorphisms on `N^O` for nonempty
  suffixes.
- A Markov stable point is not selected by the finite record alphabet; it is
  selected by the supplied generator/rates.
- The block does not derive record-production dynamics, probabilities, Born
  frequencies, transition rates, a clock metric, or a dial selector.
