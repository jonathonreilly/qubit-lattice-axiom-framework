# No-Go Ledger

## No-go: Record alone forces `r=1/2`

Record gives finite additivity over supplied realized records. It explicitly
does not provide weighting, probability, normalization, dynamics, or occupancy.
Any direct force claim would violate the 2026-06-05 minimality policy.

## No-go: Lueders sharpening stabilizes Koide

The map `r -> 2r^2` becomes `s' = 2s`. It fixes `s=0`, but the multiplier is
`2`, so `s=0` is repelling.

## No-go: Heat-kernel crossing is attraction

The supplied path `r(t)=tanh(t)^4` crosses `r=1/2` at
`t=atanh(2^(-1/4))` with positive derivative. It is a transit, not an
attractor.

## No-go: All entropy notions select the same point

Two-sector entropy ascent stabilizes `s=0`; real-mode entropy ascent
stabilizes `s=1`. The partition/functional is load-bearing.

## No-go: Born probability from record readout

Calling record-sector readout vectors probabilities requires a separate
normalization/Born gate. The classifier uses ratios and normalized coordinates
as structural observables, not as derived Born probabilities.
