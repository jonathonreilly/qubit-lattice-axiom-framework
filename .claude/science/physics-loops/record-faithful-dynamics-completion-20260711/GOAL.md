# Goal

Attack the framework's highest-leverage TOE gap: the missing law that connects
Admissibility, record formation, time evolution, and probabilities.

The campaign will not try to select a Hamiltonian directly from the static
availability rule. It will first distinguish three objects that prior lanes
sometimes place on the same footing:

1. a local continuous-time Hamiltonian interaction `H`;
2. a strictly causal unitary tick/QCA `U`;
3. a record-forming completely positive instrument `J = {J_r}`.

The working architecture to test is:

```text
Admissibility + prior records
          |
          v
local record instrument J  -- outcome weights --> record frequencies
          |
          +-- absorbing classical record sectors
          |
          +-- coherent no-record dilation block U
                         |
                         v
                  continuum generator H
```

This is a target, not a premise. The campaign succeeds only if the arrows are
derived or their minimal additional content is isolated without renaming the
desired Dirac carrier as "faithfulness."

## Nature-grade success criteria

- The physical evolution object is unambiguous.
- The July-10 SWAP--Laplacian completion is excluded for a record-observable
  reason, not because its spectrum is disfavored by assumption.
- The admissibility-to-carrier map is exhaustive enough to survive a hostile
  countermodel.
- Time ordering and probability weights arise on the same process surface.
- Any surviving freedom is stated explicitly: coupling, rate, phase, boundary,
  and continuum-renormalization data may not disappear into vocabulary.
- The output feeds a single controlled-continuum-limit campaign rather than
  separate SM, gravity, and measurement imports.

## First-block decision sequence

1. Quotient laws by all record-history and readout consequences.
2. Show why a raw record-preserving Hamiltonian cannot be the formation law.
3. Derive the normal form of a local instrument with permanent classical
   records and a coherent no-record dilation.
4. Classify cubic-equivariant faithful neighbor response on the qubit carrier.
5. Attempt a strengthened record-faithful scalar/SWAP countermodel.

The discriminator is decisive: either the scalar branch survives faithfully,
or the vector/Clifford branch is forced under a short, independently motivated
premise list.
