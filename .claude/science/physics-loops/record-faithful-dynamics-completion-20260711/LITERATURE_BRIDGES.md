## Simultaneous 3D QCA reading

Primary context, not a framework premise:

- D'Ariano, Erba, and Perinotti,
  [Isotropic quantum walks on lattices and the Weyl equation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.00826),
  classify cell-dimension-two isotropic walks and locate the three-dimensional
  Weyl solutions on the body-centered-cubic Cayley graph.
- Raynal,
  [Simple derivation of the Weyl and Dirac quantum cellular automata](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.05890),
  starts from the body-centered-cubic graph and rederives the two homogeneous,
  local, isotropic, unitary two-component automata by a Gram-matrix route; it
  does not independently select that graph.

These papers corroborate that adjacency choice and internal carrier dimension
are load-bearing. They do not derive a simple-cubic framework tick, a CAR
realization, a Record coupling, or the physical process selector, and are not
load-bearing dependencies of the scalar CAR-QCA theorem.
