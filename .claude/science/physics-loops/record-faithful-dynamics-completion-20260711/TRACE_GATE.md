# Trace Gate

two channels: scalar-even and vector-odd. Spectral record-faithfulness would
remove the scalar channel and give the Clifford-vector carrier, but that bridge
is not yet supplied. The campaign therefore partially closes the upstream
blocker shared by the kinetic, Born, measurement, arrow, action-selection, and
continuum lanes. It does not yet close Lorentz, SM, or GR recovery.
