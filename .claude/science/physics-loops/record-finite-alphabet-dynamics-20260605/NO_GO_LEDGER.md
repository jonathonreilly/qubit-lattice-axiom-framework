# No-Go Ledger

- Fixed-atom append is not a monoid endomorphism of `O*`; it is a state update
  in a right action.
- Coarse counts do not preserve arbitrary scalar readouts when fine readout
  weights differ inside a coarse fiber.
- Post-record realized updates are integral counts; ensemble expectations with
  fractional weights are pre-record or ensemble objects.
- The finite-alphabet grammar does not choose the next atom, a probability
  kernel, a rate, a Hamiltonian, or a dial setting.
