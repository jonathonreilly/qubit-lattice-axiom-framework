# No-Go Ledger

- `[H, Pi_S] = 0` alone does not imply redundant record formation.
  The runner keeps three counterexamples: `H=0`, a system-only pointer phase,
  and a nonzero commuting interaction with the environment held in an eigenstate.

- Reusing the same coherent fragment can erase the record.
  Persistence requires fresh, idle, decoupled, or equivalent completed-fragment
  hypotheses.
