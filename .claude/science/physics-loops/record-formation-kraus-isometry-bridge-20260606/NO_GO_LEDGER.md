# No-Go Ledger

## General Persistent Dynamics To W

Current block does not derive `W` from arbitrary persistent-record dynamics.
The earlier finite Kraus source note identifies this as open. This block only
provides `W` once stable projectors, orthonormal labels, and an ideal
pointer-label write are supplied.

## Post-Record Counts To Probabilities

Post-record word/count dynamics applies after a record atom is realized. It
does not derive branch weights, transition probabilities, Born typicality, or
production rates.

## Dial Selection

The projective record-write bridge has no term that selects a generation or
Koide dial value. Dial work remains a stable-location problem under separately
specified dynamics, not a forced selection.
