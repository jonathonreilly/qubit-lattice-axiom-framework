# No-Go Ledger

- `H=0` and environment-blind commuting Hamiltonians show that
  `[H_int, Pi_S] = 0` alone cannot be sufficient for redundant record
  formation.
- A coherent controlled rotation repeatedly applied to the same fragment is
  reversible; persistent completed fragments need fresh-fragment writing,
  decoupling, or an irreversible/decohering mechanism.
- The number-conserving OS transfer class check only establishes a conserved
  charge/pointer class, not record production by itself.
