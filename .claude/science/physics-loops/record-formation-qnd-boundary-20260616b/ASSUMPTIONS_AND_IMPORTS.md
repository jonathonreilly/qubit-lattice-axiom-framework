# Assumptions And Imports

Allowed framework premises:

- `minimal_axioms`: Lattice, Quantum, Record language.
- Record axiom finite additivity and durability after registration.

Explicit bounded model inputs:

- Quantum-Darwinism reading of a record as redundant objective pointer imprint.
- Finite `S + E_1..E_n` qubit carrier.
- Controlled-copy Hamiltonian `g sigma_z(S) sum_k sigma_x(E_k)` with `g > 0`.
- Recording time `t = pi/(4g)`.
- Fresh-fragment, idle-completed-fragment, or decoupling condition for
  persistence.

Not imported or claimed:

- Record-production dynamics from the Record axiom.
- A physical OS transfer that writes fragments.
- Coupling magnitude, beta value, action, gauge bosons, or continuum limit.
