# Assumptions And Imports

## Inputs

- Lattice: `Z^3` site set.
- Record: durable realized outcome and finite scalar additivity over finite
  disjoint records.
- Record typing firewall: post-record atoms/counts are separate from
  pre-record probabilities.
- Supplied finite readout context/alphabet `O`.

## New local construction

- Free monoid `O*` of finite record words.
- Free commutative count monoid `N^O`.
- Explicit `Z^3` line embedding for arbitrary finite `N`.

## Not imported

- No measurement/decoherence dynamics.
- No Born frequency theorem.
- No physical record-production rate.
- No time metric.
- No completed infinity.
- No observed values or fitted selectors.
