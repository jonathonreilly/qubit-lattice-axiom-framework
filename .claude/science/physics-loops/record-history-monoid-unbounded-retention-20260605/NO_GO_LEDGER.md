# No-Go Ledger

## Coherent history qubit

No. The post-record history sector is a word/count object. It is not one
pre-record quantum state over the whole history.

## Completed infinity

No. The theorem proves arbitrary finite length, not an actually infinite word.

## Record-production dynamics

No. The theorem classifies the post-record algebra once records are registered.
It does not prove records are physically produced at any rate.
