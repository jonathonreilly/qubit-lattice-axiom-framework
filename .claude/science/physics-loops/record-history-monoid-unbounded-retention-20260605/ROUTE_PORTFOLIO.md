# Route Portfolio

## Route 1 - free monoid and count monoid

Status: executed.

Use standard finite-word algebra and count projection to define the post-record
history sector.

## Route 2 - unbounded finite retention from `Z^3`

Status: executed.

Use the infinite lattice line `(i,0,0)` to show that every finite `N` has `N`
distinct record slots.

## Route 3 - completed infinity

Status: rejected.

The framework theorem only needs unbounded finite histories. Completed infinite
records would require a separate topology/limit object and are out of scope.

## Route 4 - physical record production

Status: rejected for this block.

Record-production dynamics is explicitly outside the Record axiom and remains a
separate open bridge.
