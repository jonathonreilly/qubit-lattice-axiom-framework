# Assumptions And Imports

## Allowed Inputs

- Pre-record instrument kernel gate: a supplied instrument/Born bridge can
  give one-shot probabilities.
- Post-record histories are realized words/counts.

## Constructed Inside This Block

- Two exact two-record joint laws with the same one-step marginals.
- Count/frequency distributions for each joint law.
- Exact variance comparison showing typicality data differ.

## Open Imports

- IID or sequence-law premise.
- Typicality/convergence theorem.
- Physical generator and clock/rate unit.
- Generation/Koide dial-value derivation.

## Forbidden Inputs

- Treating one-shot probabilities as IID by default.
- Treating an empirical finite word/frequency as the probability law.
- Claiming physical rates, generator, or dial selection.
