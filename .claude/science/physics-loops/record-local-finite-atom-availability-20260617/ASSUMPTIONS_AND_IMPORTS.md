# Assumptions And Imports

No new axiom is introduced.

Framework inputs:

- Lattice: site set `Z^3`.
- Quantum: one-site algebra `M_2(C)`.
- Record: durable realized-outcome registration and finite additivity after a
  readout context is supplied.

Declared finite context:

- one-site diagonal `{P_0,P_1}` readout algebra;
- entrywise `K` in that declared basis;
- unit-count functional on the finite Boolean context.

Open imports not closed here:

- physical readout-context selection;
- measurement/decoherence instrument;
- production or realization of records;
- probabilities, IID typicality, rates, and dial selection.
