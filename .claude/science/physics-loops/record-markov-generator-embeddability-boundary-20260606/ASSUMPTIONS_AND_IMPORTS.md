# Assumptions And Imports

## Stacked Inputs

- PR #2797 / `RECORD_PRODUCTION_KERNEL_BOUNDARY_2026-06-06.md` supplies the
  branch-local distinction between post-record append/count dynamics and
  production kernels.

## Reproved Here

- Lazy two-state kernel is embeddable after a rate and time are supplied.
- Swap stochastic kernel has negative determinant and is not a finite real
  exponential.
- Reset stochastic kernel is singular and not a finite matrix exponential.
- Rate and clock interval are not separately fixed by a single transition
  matrix.

## Not Imported

- Physical clock metric.
- Physical Markov generator.
- Born/IID law.
- Dial selector.
