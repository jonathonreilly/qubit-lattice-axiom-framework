# No-Go Ledger

- **Record atoms imply probabilities.** Rejected. Post-record atoms are
  realized information; future-record probabilities need a production kernel.
- **Kernel implies Markov rate.** Rejected by the embeddability boundary.
- **Generator implies physical rate.** Rejected. A clock interval and rate/unit
  normalization are separate inputs.
- **Eta/Koide/generation dial forcing.** Rejected. This classifier contains no
  dial field.
