# No-Go Ledger

## Rejected Shortcuts

- Pre-record qubit state alone does not determine a production kernel; the
  supplied instrument/readout context is load-bearing.
- A post-record atom is not the probability vector that preceded it.
- A one-shot probability vector does not provide IID frequencies or typicality.
- Instrument probabilities do not supply a physical Markov generator or
  clock/rate unit.

## Open Repairs

- Derive or admit a measurement/instrument bridge.
- Derive or admit the Born trace rule or equivalent probability-origin rule.
- Supply an IID/typicality premise if frequency claims are needed.
- Supply a physical generator and clock/rate unit for dynamics claims.
