- Finite record alphabet.
- Positive atom dimensions or multiplicities `d_i`.
- A chosen invariance/stable-target principle:

  - or an explicit target prior `pi_s`.

- No fitted selector.
- No Born-frequency typicality theorem.

## Boundary

named. It cannot choose the physical dial position from Record alone.
