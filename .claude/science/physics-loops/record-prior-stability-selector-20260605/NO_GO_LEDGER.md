# No-Go Ledger

## Record-alone selector

Record alone cannot select `s=0`, `s=1`, or any other prior. The minimal axiom
explicitly supplies no weighting, normalization, probability, dynamics, or
occupancy rule.

## Stability-alone selector

Bare stability cannot choose the dial position. For every fixed `pi_s`, the
reset chain

```text
p -> (1-alpha)p + alpha pi_s
```

has the same contraction form and a unique attracting fixed point.

## Koide-forcing route

The equal-letter endpoint is stable under atom-symmetric post-record dynamics,
but that does not force the physical charged-lepton selector. The physical
dynamics/invariance premise remains open.
