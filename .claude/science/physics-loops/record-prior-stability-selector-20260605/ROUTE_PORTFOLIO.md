# Route Portfolio

## Route 1 - finite invariant prior theorem

Status: executed.

Prove that atom-permutation invariance plus normalization selects the uniform
prior over record atoms. For the two-sector generation alphabet this is
`s=0`.

## Route 2 - microstate coarse-graining theorem

Status: executed.

Prove that uniformity over one singlet microstate and two doublet microstates
coarse-grains to `(1/3, 2/3)`. This is the `s=1` dimension/Born endpoint.

## Route 3 - stable Markov dial family

Status: executed.

Show that each fixed `pi_s` has a row-stochastic detailed-balance reset chain
with unique attracting fixed point `pi_s`. This proves stable setting without
forcing a unique endpoint.

## Route 4 - physical dynamics derivation

Status: open.

Derive, from framework-native dynamics rather than a supplied selector, whether
post-record atom symmetry or pre-record microstate symmetry is physically
selected for the charged-lepton/generation use case.
