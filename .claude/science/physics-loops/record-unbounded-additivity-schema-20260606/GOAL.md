# Goal

Clarify what Record unlocks for unbounded retained-style reasoning without
overclaiming dynamics.

The intended result is a narrow conditional-support schema:

- fixed finite record prefixes are bounded by their chosen length;
- the Record axiom ranges over arbitrary finite disjoint collections;
- with supplied nonzero produced records, finite additivity gives no global cap
  across finite collection size;
- production, probability, IID, rates, and dial selection remain separate.
