# No-Go Ledger

- Fixed-prefix boundedness is not a global Record cap. A chosen finite `N`
  bounds that prefix, but the axiom schema ranges over arbitrary finite
  disjoint collections.

- Unbounded finite-count accumulation is not a production law. Record does not
  create the next atom or supply a probability distribution.

- Normalized empirical frequencies are post-record readouts from realized
  counts. They are not Born weights, IID typicality, or a future kernel.

- The unbounded schema does not select a Koide/generation dial value.
