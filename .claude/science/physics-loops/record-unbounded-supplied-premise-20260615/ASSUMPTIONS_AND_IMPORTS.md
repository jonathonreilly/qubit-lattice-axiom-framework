# Assumptions And Imports

Kept:
- Record finite additivity over finite disjoint collections;
- fixed finite-prefix readout identities;
- supplied nonzero record atoms;
- supplied readout context.

Not derived:
- production of arbitrarily many nonzero records;
- unbounded availability of produced records;
- probability, IID, rate, clock, dial, or capacity law.

Downstream consumers must carry the supplied-record premise.
