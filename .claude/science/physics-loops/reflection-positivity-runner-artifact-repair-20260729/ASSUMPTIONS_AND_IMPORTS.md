# Assumptions and imports

- The audited row has no load-bearing repository dependencies and no open
  imports.
- The mathematical chain is frozen at the auditor-accepted finite-volume,
  two-slice, pure-gauge `SU(N)` scope.
- The current renderer budget is 20,000 characters for each live stdout
  surface; the primary cache supplies the expected normal-mode size.
- The registered helper is repo-local, separately executable, and does not
  import the primary runner, its cache, target booleans, or audit state.
- No empirical value, fitted selector, literature theorem, new axiom, or
  framework primitive is introduced.

Counterfactual check: combining both streams inside the primary process would
exceed the primary stdout budget and risk clipping middle N1--N8 locators.
Keeping separate authenticated packet paths preserves both streams without
changing theorem evidence.
