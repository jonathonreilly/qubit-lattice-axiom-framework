# Assumptions And Imports

## In Scope

- Staggered-only fermion operator `M = M_KS + mI`, with `m > 0`.
- Wilson plaquette gauge half with compact SU(3) Haar measure and positive
  beta.
- Standard OS/STW/Menotti factorisation identities, used as literature credit
  for the lattice reflection-positivity manipulation.

## Out Of Scope

- Fermion Wilson term `M_W`.
- Symmetric-canonical `M_W = r*d*I` determinant-positivity bridge.
- Broader canonical staggered+Wilson determinant positivity.
- Any audit verdict or effective-status assignment.
