# Assumptions And Imports

## Used Directly

- Record finite additivity over finite pairwise-disjoint record collections,
  only as set additivity over supplied record labels.
- Record K/CPT orbit invariance, only as K-evenness for a supplied readout
  context.
- The explicit extra restriction `H_char`: the tested phase part is a
  determinant-character / log-character group homomorphism of multiplicative
  sector phases.
- Elementary algebra: group-additive real-valued functions are odd, and
  even plus odd forces zero.

## Not Imported As Proof

- No physical readout context is derived.
- No claim is made that Record additivity alone implies `H_char`.
- No claim is made that non-homomorphic K-even phase data is absent.
- No audit registry, queue, ledger, or front-door status is edited.

## Hostile Counterexample Kept Load-Bearing

`I(S) = sum_{j in S} cos(theta_j)` is finite-additive over disjoint record
collections, K-even, and phase-dependent. It is not an `H_char`
group-homomorphic phase readout. This is the reason the result is bounded
support rather than a full Record-alone closure.
