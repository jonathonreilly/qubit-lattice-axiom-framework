# No-Go Ledger

## Record-Alone Phase Erasure

Claim rejected: Record finite additivity over disjoint records plus K/CPT
evenness erases all phase-sensitive scalar readouts.

Reason: `I(S) = sum_{j in S} cos(theta_j)` is finite-additive over disjoint
record collections and K-even, but remains phase-dependent. Therefore Record
alone cannot justify the previous broad wording.

## Non-Homomorphic Readouts

The current theorem does not touch non-homomorphic, K-even phase readouts. Any
future downstream use that needs their absence must supply a separate bridge.
