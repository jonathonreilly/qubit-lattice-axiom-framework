# Route Portfolio

## Route A - Record-alone proof

Attempt: prove Record finite additivity plus K/CPT orbit invariance erases all
phase dependence.

Disposition: rejected. The counterexample `sum_j cos(theta_j)` is
Record-additive over disjoint records, K-even, and phase-dependent.

## Route B - H_char scoped theorem

Attempt: make the hidden multiplicative phase-additivity premise explicit as
`H_char`, then prove even plus group-additive phase content vanishes.

Disposition: selected. This directly repairs the conditional audit objection
without pretending Record alone supplies a phase homomorphism.

## Route C - Physical readout derivation

Attempt: derive that the physical strong-CP / AC_phi_lambda readout surface
must satisfy `H_char`.

Disposition: left open. This is a stronger science bridge and is not proved by
the current branch.
