# Goal

Repair the audited-conditional residual-environment all-weight convolution row by removing the hidden normalized-trivial-channel premise from the load-bearing theorem.

The target is not to prove `kappa_(0,0)(beta)=1`. The target is to make the theorem's actual claim the unnormalized per-weight identity

```text
R_beta^env chi_(p,q) = (1 / lambda_env(beta)) C_(Z_beta^env) chi_(p,q)
```

with `lambda_env(beta)>0` independent of the actual trivial Peter-Weyl coefficient.
