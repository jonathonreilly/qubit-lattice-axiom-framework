## Synthesis

Route A is selected. It removes both circularities: the old benchmark numbers
become non-load-bearing examples, and the Schur source is restricted from the
already constructed microscopic shell source rather than defined as
`Lambda f_*`. Route B supplies a self-contained derivation of the continuum
static-conformal identities but remains an explicit sector hypothesis after
lattice replacement. Route C becomes the claim firewall: the four minimal
axioms do not supply a gravity action or physical source/readout map. Route D
is retained only as regression coverage.
