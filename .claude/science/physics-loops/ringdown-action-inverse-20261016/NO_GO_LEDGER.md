# Claim boundary

This unit proposes a constructive positive conditional theorem, not a no-go.
Its altered-force examples are explicit witnesses with specified outputs;
none quantifies over all alternative theories or all network evolutions.
No claim that no framework primitive or physical route can close the remaining
measurement work is made. Degenerate cases are outside this theorem's domain.
The no-go checklist is therefore not invoked for this source unit.
