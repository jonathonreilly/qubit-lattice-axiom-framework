# Assumptions And Imports

## Current-Surface Inputs

- The spin factor carries `gamma5`.
- The generation factor carries the `C3` cyclic shift and singlet/doublet
  partition.
- A rooting map that is claimed native to the current symmetric surface must be
  `C3`-equivariant unless another theorem supplies the symmetry breaking.

## Open Imports Exposed

- Non-equivariant physical `C3` breaking remains open.
- A T-odd K-reality origin remains open.
- A spin-sector polarization rule remains open if used to expose a generation
  breaker.

## Imports Not Added

- No continuum rooting theorem is assumed.
- No physical spin-generation entangling map is admitted.
- No audit status is modified.
