# No-Go Memory

- The accepted minimal axioms do not choose a Hamiltonian, transfer operator,
  staggered kinetic branch, or time metric. Reintroducing any of these as an
  axiom consequence requires a separate approved bridge.
- Positive reciprocal roots of the classical recurrence do not by themselves
  construct a physical one-particle transfer kernel, its inner product, or a
  Grassmann-to-Fock map.
- A random-link scan is not the positivity proof. The all-matrix proof must
  remain the anti-Hermitian modal decomposition.
- These recorded walls delimit rejected routes for this block; they are not a
  universal theorem that no separate physical construction can succeed.
