# Assumptions And Imports

- Wilson convention: `S_W = -B + constant`; hence `exp(-S_W)` is proportional to
  `exp(B)`.
- Plane exponent: `B_0 = beta Re Tr[U_+ U_-^dag]`; plane action contribution is
  `S_{W,0} = -B_0` up to the usual constant.
- `Z_N` finite-Haar sums are exact for the finite group checks.
- `U(1)` plane-kernel positivity is certified from the positive defining series
  for `I_n(beta)`, with an explicit positive tail bound.
- The `U(1)` angular-grid reflected-Gram check is numerical quadrature only.
- `SU(2)` is Monte Carlo evidence only; it is not an exact nonabelian proof.

Open imports / residuals:

- Full interacting `SU(N)` finite-Haar proof is not supplied here.
- Fermion-sector transfer positivity is outside this branch.
- Independent audit remains the status authority.
