# Assumptions And Imports

## Retained

- The runner-defined cubical-ball family at `R = 2, 3, 4, 5`.
- Pure finite combinatorics over boundary quads, fixed diagonal
  triangulations, tetrahedra, faces, edges, and Euler characteristics.

## Removed From The Claim

- PL Schoenflies / Alexander cone uniqueness.
- Alexander trick and `MCG(S^2)`.
- van Kampen.
- Perelman and Moise.
- Kawamoto-Smit physical-closure/homogeneity.

Those topics may be cited as literature elsewhere, but this repaired row does
not depend on them.
