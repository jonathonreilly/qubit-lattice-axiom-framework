# Assumptions And Imports

## Allowed Inputs

- Exact Route-2 endpoint algebra and granted T-side values:
  `beta_T/alpha_T=-1`, `alpha_T/alpha_E=-2`.
- Current Route-2 naturality, E-center lift, Rconn typed bridge, O_h
  covariance, Schur quadratic, box-size, and minimal-axiom notes.
- Exact rational arithmetic.

## Forbidden Inputs

- Observed quark masses.
- Fitted Yukawa values.
- CKM or J target values.
- Nearest-rational endpoint fitting.
- Adopting `rho_E=21/4`, `q_E=15/8`, `c_TE=-8/9`, `lambda=9/4`, or
  `b/a=1449/704` as a proof input.

## Open Imports After Block96

- A nonlinear E-center readout primitive.
- An inverse-square center-lift law producing `q_X ~ w_X^-2`.
- A typed source/readout primitive deriving `c_TE=-8/9` or
  `lambda=q_E/q_T=9/4`.
- A typed Route-2 metric/source primitive deriving the target metric tensor.
