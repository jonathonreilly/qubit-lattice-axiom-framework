- Exact SU(3) channel fractions `F_adj=8/9`, `F_singlet=1/9`.
- Channel readout family `R_phys(kappa)=F_adj+kappa F_singlet`.
- Block70 current-projector idempotence, narrowing `kappa` to `{0,1}`.
- Block68 endpoint orientation sign support for positive `q_E`.

## Forbidden Inputs

- The observed or desired value `rho_E=21/4` as a selector premise.
- A fitted disconnected-current coefficient.
- A new singlet-annihilation axiom hidden in wording.
- An audit verdict.

## Open Import

An exact singlet-annihilation or disconnected-current-zero theorem selecting
`kappa=0` over `kappa=1`.
