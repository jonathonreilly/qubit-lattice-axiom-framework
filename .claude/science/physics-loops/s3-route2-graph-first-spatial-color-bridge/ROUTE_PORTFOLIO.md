## Selected Block58 Route

The selected route is a steelman, not a strawman:

1. Grant graph-first selected-axis `SU(3)` as a typed `N_c=3` from `d=3`.
2. Put it on the same spatial 3-dimensional axis space.
3. Decompose `End(R^3)` into scalar, antisymmetric, and spin-2 sectors.
4. Ask whether the exact `8/9` fraction lands on the Route-2 E/T2 readout.

The runner shows that `8/9` is only the total traceless-adjoint fraction of
`End(R^3)`. The Route-2 readout lives inside the spin-2 `E+T2` split, whose
internal fractions are `3/2`, `2/3`, `3/5`, and `2/5`, with no sign supplied.

## Fan-Out Synthesis

- Algebraic frame: total adjoint dimension does not equal an E/T2 center
  response.
- Symmetry frame: cubic spin-2 splitting distinguishes `E` and `T2`, but
  graph-first `SU(3)` counts the whole traceless adjoint.
- Sign frame: dimensions are positive; `c_TE=-8/9` needs an orientation or
  signed functional.
- Trace frame: this route can only prune a color shortcut, not close the
  Route-2 endpoint.

The strongest next route is therefore constructive and same-domain: derive the
missing signed E/T2 source/readout functional directly.
