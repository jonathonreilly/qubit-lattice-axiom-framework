# Assumptions And Imports

## Allowed Inputs

- `q_T=5/6` under the granted T-side values.
- O_h per-arm weights `w_E=1/3`, `w_T=1/2`.
- Current covariance, Schur quadratic, naturality, center-excess, and minimal
  axiom notes.
- Exact rational arithmetic.

## Forbidden Inputs

- Observed quark masses.
- Fitted Yukawa values.
- CKM or J target values.
- Nearest-rational endpoint fitting.
- Adopting the inverse-square law as an approved primitive.

## Open Imports After Block97

- A source/readout theorem deriving `q_X w_X^2=5/24`.
- A nonlinear E-center readout primitive not covered by current quadratic
  invariant surfaces.
