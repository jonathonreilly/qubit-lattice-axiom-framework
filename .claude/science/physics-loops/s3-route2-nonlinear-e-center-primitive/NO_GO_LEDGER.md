# No-Go Ledger

- Carrier/naturality route: exact carrier and T-side candidates leave
  `rho_E` free.
- E-center-blind route: shell/T-side/channel-preserving/low-rational
  constraints do not see the E-center column.
- Record registration/positivity route: fixes norm or one-sided bounds, not
  the E-readout direction.
- Source-domain color route: `c_TE = -R_conn` would force `21/4`, but the
  typed color-to-support endpoint bridge is missing.
- Quadratic covariance route: same-domain `kappa = 3/2` exists, but an
  `O_h`-invariant quadratic leaves the E:T1 ratio free; common powers miss the
  target except the not-derived inverse-square law.
- Tensor/gate primitive route: endpoint-fitted affine maps are broad
  restricted-class members, not unique physical gate primitives.
- Determinant/log/registrable routes: useful algebra inside supplied contexts,
  but no E-center distinguishing context or inverse-square law is derived.
