- Block127 source-readout isometry sufficient theorem.
- Block125 source-Hessian channel-coupling no-go.
- Block126 source-readout unit calibration no-go.
- Exact finite `P_R` readout map as a finite row/codomain surface.

## Conditional Countermodel Premise

For the sake of the no-go, assume a typed map exists:

```text
Phi_ET : Block121 source-Hessian components -> finite P_R E/T output rows.
```

The question is whether that typing alone forces unit preservation.

## Forbidden Inputs

- `c_TE=-8/9` as a proof input.
- `rho_E=21/4` or `q_E=15/8`.
- Quark-data target values.
- Fit-derived selector choices.
- Endpoint-value reversal into the source/readout scale.

## Exposed Import

```text

```

on the normalized source/readout scalar line.
