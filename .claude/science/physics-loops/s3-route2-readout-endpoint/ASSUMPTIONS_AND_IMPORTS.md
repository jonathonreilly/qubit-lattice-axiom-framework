# Assumptions And Imports

## Allowed Premises

- Exact Route-2 endpoint algebra.
- Granted T-side stretch values `q_T=5/6` and
  `gamma_T(shell)/gamma_E(shell)=-2`.
- Exact color fraction `F_adj=8/9`.
- Positive E-center branch for the target.
- Exact rational arithmetic.

## Forbidden Inputs

- Observed quark masses.
- Fitted Yukawa values.
- CKM/J or endpoint-data target fitting.
- Nearest-rational selection from live endpoint data.
- Treating any E-center shear-breaking primitive as already supplied.

## Open Imports After Block33

- An E-center shear-breaking primitive.
- A source-domain rule that evaluates or types `q_E`.
- A direct magnitude theorem for `|c_TE|=F_adj`.
