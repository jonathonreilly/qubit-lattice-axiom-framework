```text

```

```text

[[1, 0, rho_E, 0],
```

- Fitted Yukawa, CKM, or J targets.
- Live endpoint nearest-rational selection.
- Treating eta-floor membership as a unique selector.
- Treating factor rigidity as endpoint selection.
