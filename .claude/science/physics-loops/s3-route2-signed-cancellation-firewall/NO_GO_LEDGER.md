# No-Go Ledger

- Minimal E-channel naturality leaves `rho_E` free.
- Record/positivity leaves readout direction free.
- Positive one-pole or one-inverse cones cannot reach `9/4`.
- Density-square `p=-2` would work, but current named authority bank does not
  supply it.
- This block adds: an affine signed one-pole fit exists exactly, but all such
  fits require a negative coefficient and therefore a new signed selector.
