# Assumptions And Imports

## Used

- Exact restricted Route-2 readout form from the exact readout-map note.
- Exact endpoint carrier columns with center offset `1/6`.
- Exact rational arithmetic over row coefficients.
- Existing Block115 direct E-center boundary only as downstream context.

## Not Used

- Observed endpoint values.
- Fitted live readout coefficients.
- The target endpoint triple as a selector.
- A source-row degree law.
- A physical T-row primitive selecting `(1,-2,2)`.

## Open Imports

- A theorem deriving `beta_T=-alpha_T`.
- A theorem deriving `alpha_T/alpha_E=-2`.
- A theorem or accepted primitive selecting the full relative T row
  `(alpha_E, alpha_T, beta_T)=(1,-2,2)`.
