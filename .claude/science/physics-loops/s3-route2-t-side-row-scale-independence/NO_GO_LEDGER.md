# No-Go Ledger

- Carrier columns provide endpoint evaluation sites but not row coefficients.
- The conditional time/slice family begins after `P_R` is supplied.
- T-row scaling preserves `rho_T` and `q_T` while changing `s_TE`.
- E-shell rescaling preserves `rho_T` and `q_T` while changing `s_TE`.
- Changing `beta_T` at fixed shells preserves `s_TE` while changing
  `rho_T` and `q_T`.
- Therefore a row-shape theorem and a shell-scale theorem are independent
  unless a stronger primitive selects the full relative row.
