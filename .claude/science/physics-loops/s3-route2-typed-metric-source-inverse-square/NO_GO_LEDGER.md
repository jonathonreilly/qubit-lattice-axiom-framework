# No-Go Ledger

- Carrier-only Route-2 endpoint data leave `rho_E` free.
- E-center-blind endpoint constraints cannot derive `rho_E = 21/4`.
- Registration and positivity conditions fix readout norm or bounds, not the
  E-row direction.
- Direct Fierz/color fraction is a separate typed bridge and is not derived as
  a Route-2 center ratio.
- O_h equivariance and quadratic invariant forms do not force
  `q_E/q_T = 9/4`.
- Constant, direct projector-weight, quadratic, and single-inverse monomial
  laws miss the endpoint covariance.
