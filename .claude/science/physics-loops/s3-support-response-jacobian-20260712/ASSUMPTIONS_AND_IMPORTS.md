# Assumptions and Imports

## Framework foundation check

The repo's minimum axiom set (`A_min`) is exactly Lattice, Qubit,
Admissibility, and Record. The approved primitive registry contains the scale
reference, kinetic isotropy, and realized-state primitives. This block adds no
axiom or primitive and does not claim that the finite ADM evaluation protocol
is selected by that foundation. None of the approved primitives is
load-bearing for the scoped finite computation.

## Scoped finite-protocol definitions and conditions

- The standard finite nearest-neighbor negative Laplacian with zero Dirichlet boundary.
- The explicitly displayed seven-site center/arm basis.
- The explicitly displayed finite conformastatic metric/readout algorithm.
- Frozen theorem-domain parameters `size=15`, shell radius `4`, probe radius
  `4.25`, source step `0.005`, and stencil step `0.04`.
- Three explicit probe coordinates, cubic nearest-mode interpolation, a strict
  radius-4 exterior cut, the nonzero-entry radial-average convention at
  tolerance `1e-12`, and the `(3,3,0)` anchor orbit.
- Standard finite-dimensional linear algebra, centered differences, and affine-map uniqueness.

## Forbidden load-bearing inputs

- copied `Theta` endpoint values;
- fitted local-`O_h` or finite-rank source-family coefficients;
- observed targets or literature values;
- the definition-only tensor-prototype note as theorem authority;
- an assumed exact tensor-observable or physical GR bridge.
