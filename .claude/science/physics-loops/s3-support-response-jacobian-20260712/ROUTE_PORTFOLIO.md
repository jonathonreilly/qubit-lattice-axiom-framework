## Selected synthesis

The direct finite replay and exact support algebra are complementary. The
local identity closes the denominator exactly. The self-contained replay
closes the numerator at the declared finite-protocol scope. Affine uniqueness
then makes the computed quotient the coefficient, and hence the derivative,
of the constructed endpoint interpolant only. A `3 x 3`
source/stencil control grid is a falsifier for accidental zero/sign behavior.

The upstream-reuse route is specifically not used because it would preserve
the `audited_renaming` failure mode.
