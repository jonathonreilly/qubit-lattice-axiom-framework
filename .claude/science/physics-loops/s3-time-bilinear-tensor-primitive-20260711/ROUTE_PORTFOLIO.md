## Stuck fan-out synthesis

The sequential fan-out separates algebra from semantics.  R1 and R2 show the
old coordinate assumptions are not the decisive wall: their strongest exact
forms are compatible with the current lattice geometry.  R3-R5 each require a
tensor/source/readout identification absent from the accepted premise set.
R6 proves that the homogeneous algebra cannot fix the overall scale once the
carrier ray is assumed.  It does not turn its scaled matrices into physical
models. R7 can be a bounded bridge but cannot close a premise-set derivation.

The selected block therefore combines R1, R2, and the narrowed R6.  This is structurally
distinct from the prior carrier-orbit registry cycle, which asked whether an
E/T-distinguishing operator had been enumerated.
