# Opportunity queue

This is a single-target interactive run, not an unattended campaign.  The
ranked in-target opportunities are:

1. **Finite-replay bounded theorem** — high retained-positive probability;
   no observational inputs; existing compute path; narrow blast radius.
2. **Analytic non-affinity/error theorem** — medium probability; useful if the
   replay needs a sharper obstruction; substantially larger proof burden.
3. **New equivariant physical tensor observable** — low near-term probability;
   multiple open semantic bridges and would not be the same audited object.

The queue is exhausted when item 1 passes review or when its hidden inputs
cannot be retired and item 2 has isolated the exact obstruction.
