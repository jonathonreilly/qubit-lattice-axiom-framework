# Preserved scope and failed candidates

There is no general no-go conclusion. The annular exponential candidate passes trace/radial equations but has a nonzero scalar elliptic residual. The full-action planar profile has zero planar redshift and nonzero true spherical radial redshift. They are explicit local existence/residual witnesses, not exclusions of all scalar or network theories.

Prior code/export failures and the independent mixed-Subs representation failure are preserved in the archive. They did not select physical assumptions. High-wave-number synthetic designs have small singular values; finite-noise reconstruction remains an open measurement problem.
