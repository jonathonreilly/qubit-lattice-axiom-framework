# Artifact and route selection

The milestone combines the complete necessary inverse, its original-equation
rank witness and a preserved failure of a narrower test. Historical nearby
coefficient variants stay in the archive. They do not receive separate PRs.

Next useful research routes, in priority order:

1. Work directly from primitive records and their allowed errors, rather than
   declaring derivative errors. First discriminator: construct smooth bounded
   primitive perturbations and measure their effects on the required derivative
   features; determine what bandwidth or regularity evidence is necessary.
2. Test clock/distance registration jointly with the inverse. First
   discriminator: exact coordinate/unit changes versus physical changes in a
   common action and common recorded fields. Do not count a clock convention as
   an action derivation or supply a preferred clock to resolve a degeneracy.
3. Cross-environment action compatibility remains a downstream consumer.
   Use only histories surviving the original equations and full measurement
   residuals; do not compare fitted potentials before establishing correspondence.

No numerical success probabilities, row counts or PR counts are used to rank
these routes. The first route attacks the most immediate remaining bridge
between the present mathematical inverse and usable observations.
