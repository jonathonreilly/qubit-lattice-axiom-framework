# Target contract

Derive a necessary finite-error kinetic-curvature test evaluated on primitive lensing and actual-event redshift records, eliminating explicit J,Z and bare-density initial readouts. This is narrower than deriving the action or validating it in nature.

Domain: the stated general-Q conformal-dust linear sector, fixed smooth regular background, K=C'+hC nonzero, full angular convention, complete regular rays, calibrated observation map, compact annular time support and valid continuum error envelopes. Completion requires ordered adjoints, a nonzero original-action record witness, deterministic error arithmetic and independent checks. Choosing a potential, background, gain, initial state, time history or measurement identity does not count as deriving it.
