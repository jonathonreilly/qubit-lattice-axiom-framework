# Independent cluster-cap value judgment

Recommendation: one coherent conditional primitive-photon kinetic-testing milestone is scientifically distinguishable from the two preceding units, provided the corrected full-ray fixture and explicit observation/error contract are integrated. The Bernstein norm refinement is supporting evidence within that milestone, not a separate milestone or publication. This is a value/scope proposal, not an audit verdict, novelty certification, canonical-source approval or landing decision.

## What the preceding units already do

The fully read local canonical PR9641 note constructs background/reception-gain identification from registered repeated-cone fields and gives a primitive weak residual for the transport/background coefficient relation. Its canonical action and all-original-equation checks remain conditional; its primitive residual is not a general-Q kinetic-curvature test. It already establishes the general tactic of composing photon maps and adjoints. Repeating that tactic alone would not warrant a new unit.

The fully read local PR9644 note admits general Q and identifies normalized transverse kinetic curvature from intermediate P,J,Z responses under rank/regularity assumptions. Its finite-error test removes derivatives of those measured fields, but it still assumes those latent metric/scalar/matter fields are supplied. The same-background family demonstrates that transport/background information alone does not fix the kinetic coefficient.

## Concrete new reduction

The new root composition starts with that kinetic residual, uses Euler to remove J, and uses the momentum/Euler commutator on K=C'+hC nonzero to remove Z. It then substitutes the Born/path-corrected photon primitives and transfers the remaining derivatives to fixed test/candidate weights. The evaluated functional uses projected lensing psi and actual-event redshift z; it does not require an explicit J or Z readout, dust-density initial constant, or separate numerical reconstruction of their noisy time derivatives. Those are meaningful explicit input obligations removed from this necessary candidate test.

This is a stronger interface than simply reporting that latent fields can in principle be reconstructed from derivatives: it provides a bounded-error primitive integral functional and a corrected original-action constructed discrimination witness. A noncanonical mode yielding a nonzero canonical-candidate moment prevents the functional from being merely a vacuous compatibility identity on the chosen fixture. The exact norm enclosure makes its deterministic error statement inspectable rather than relying on a wildly loose unshifted monomial bound. None of these observations proves practical sensitivity.

The conditional algebra is plausibly coherent: in d tau r²drdOmega, Euler elimination yields v=wJ'+wZ; the commutator yields Z=-N/K; subsequent adjoints reverse order and include radial2/r and the D-adjoint scale-factor derivative. These are substantive composition requirements. This value assessment is not a replacement for the other independent checker's complete primitive-operator review or final canonical source reading.

## What remains imported and must not be retired

- General-Q scalar, Einstein gravity, universal conformal dust, linear/sector and background hypotheses; the test is necessary, not a derivation of the action/species or a sufficient solution certificate.
- Fixed candidate histories a,q,G,R,F,E,U and derivatives, positivity/regularity and K nonzero on support. K=0 remains a chart exclusion. PR9641's canonical scalar/Hamiltonian residuals cannot certify a general-Q candidate. A general-Q joint background/gain existence or uniqueness theorem is not inherited merely because momentum/Euler transport is shared.
- An open reception-time region, registered source distances and observer/source clock/path corrections. Radial depth on one cone does not provide that region. Independently isolated ISW can encode one temporal derivative; corrected drift can encode another combination. Neither exception supplies all required histories automatically.
- Candidate gains: dividing the final adjoint weights by supplied gains does not reconstruct or calibrate those gains. Calibration/background/geometry errors affect the weights and need separate bounds. The common amplitude/normalization freedom is not removed.
- Full-sky recovered harmonic coefficients and their continuum errors, or an appropriately multiplied pointwise bound; masks, sampling, intrinsic-shape effects, quadrature and finite-time coverage need their own justification.
- Finite-test null spaces, off-history action freedom, state/history origin, absolute normalization and nonlinear validity. No uniqueness or observed rejection follows from this finite witness.

The old support failure is an important limitation discovered by checking complete rays rather than source events. Preserve its algebra/norm results but label its physical witness invalid. Present the corrected negative-eta support as a repair, not as if the original calculation had already validated a physical ray. The angular-error clarification likewise changes the interpretation of numerical radii, even though it leaves kernels unchanged.

## Proposed scope/title

A suitable narrow claim is: “A necessary finite-error test of general scalar kinetic curvature using registered photon primitives.” Keep one theorem/derivation, a source-bound corrected witness, the norm certificate, adverse-history evidence and explicit open observational obligations together. No separate norm PR is justified. This improves the conditional test interface; it does not complete the observation-to-action chain.
