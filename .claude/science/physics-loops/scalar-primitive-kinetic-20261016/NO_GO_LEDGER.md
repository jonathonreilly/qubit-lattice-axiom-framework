# Preserved failures and domain limits

No universal no-go or exhausted-search claim is made. Old eta[-2,-1],r[1,2] source support allowed rays to cross eta=0; its formal moments/norms survive as algebra but not a physical photon witness. The fixed safe replacement eta[-4,-3],r[1,2] was declared before recomputation. The action, candidate and mode were unchanged.

The initial harmonic-only witness hid an actual wZ-sign mutation. Added arbitrary nonharmonic probes challenge that operator without mislabeling the probes as action solutions. Old normv1 failed uncancelled polynomial conversion; v2 produced valid enclosures but falsely rejected integer ZZ domains with a QQ-only predicate. v3 corrected the checker; v4 recomputed the physical domain. All attempts are retained.

Two-dimensional harmonic-error budgets were originally ambiguously named; the final claim explicitly distinguishes coefficient errors from arbitrary pixel errors. K=0, missing time coverage, masks, background/gain uncertainty and nonlinear errors remain exclusions or missing suppliers, not empirical rejections. Geometric one-cone counterexamples are not full-action impossibility witnesses. Formal N-gate exhaustion thresholds are not invoked.
