# No-Go Ledger

Retained separable APS/Wald/Gauss source route:

- Positive Born/Gauss source is orientation-even: `[+1,+1]`.
- APS eta and positive Wald carrier are source-neutral spectators: `[0,0]`.
- The required signed source is orientation-odd: `[+1,-1]`.
- The target vector appears only when the explicit cross term is added.

This is a retained-route no-go, not a no-go for every possible future
source-section theorem.
