# No-go ledger

| Route | Exact obstruction | Scope | Reopen condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| graph from factorization | same base supports nonisomorphic path and complete graphs | bare finite factorization | supply a graph-selection premise or theorem on a richer surface |
| graph from full permutation naturality | empty and complete graphs are both invariant | identical-factor naturality | add a discriminator beyond base automorphisms |
| local Hamiltonian from local factors | every graph support and `H=0` are available | operator selection | derive a physical operator from additional law content |
| unitary evolution from Hilbert kinematics | unitary group and nonunitary CPTP semigroup coexist | physical dynamics selection | supply physical time action plus reversibility/norm preservation |
| Born readout from the inner product | normalized contextual `p=2` and `p=4` rules coexist | bare readout selection | supply operational probability/noncontextuality premises |
| collapse of live framework into Hilbert base | Lattice is separate premise; dynamics and Born are withheld | current framework | reviewed framework change or retained bridges |

The ledger does not say richer routes are impossible. It prevents reopening
unchanged-base entailment without a genuinely new premise or discriminator.
