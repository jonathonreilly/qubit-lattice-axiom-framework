# No-Go Ledger

- Record/post-record counts do not derive a physical time metric or rate.
- Stone uniqueness is transfer-relative and tau-relative; the transfer alone
  does not set the absolute time unit.
- The staggered action has an exact time-space exchange intertwiner, so
  RP-admissibility alone does not select the physical temporal axis.
- Raw tensor products admit independent commuting transfers as mathematical
  comparators; excluding them as physical clocks needs an additional source
  rule.
