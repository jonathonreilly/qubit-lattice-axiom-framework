# Assumptions And Imports

## Repo Surface

- `docs/repo/ACTIVE_REVIEW_QUEUE.md` supplies the active review gate.
- `SINGLE_CLOCK_STONE_FINITE_DIM_UNIQUENESS_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md`
  supplies context for the valid narrow Stone theorem.

## Reproved In This Block

- Fixed positive `T` and fixed `tau` reconstruct a unique generator.
- The same `T` with a different `tau` gives a rescaled generator.
- Tensor-product positive transfers can define distinct commuting
  one-parameter groups.
- A product transfer has a unique summed generator only after that product
  transfer and time scale are supplied.

## Not Imported

- Unique reflection-positive axis.
- Unique transfer construction.
- Physical time-unit normalization.
- Exclusion of tensor-factor / commuting transfer clocks.
