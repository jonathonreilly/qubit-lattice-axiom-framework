# No-Go Ledger

- Direct `H_unit` scalar-singlet to full `SU(2)_L` fundamental doublet:
  no nonzero equivariant bridge exists, `Hom_SU(2)(trivial,fundamental)=0`.
- Scalar/radial carrier to thermal field-content count:
  a radial coordinate is one real direction and cannot certify the four real
  components of the full high-temperature doublet.
- Neutral-ray selection before a supplied doublet:
  a neutral ray is a ray inside a doublet, not a derivation of the doublet.
