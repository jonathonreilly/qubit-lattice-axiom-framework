# Goal

Harden the `SM_GSTAR_I12` empirical thermal comparator by proving that the
`nu_R` nonthermalization conclusion does not depend on exact textbook
prefactors.

The target is bounded support only: exact rate/Hubble constants are made
non-decisive across an adversarial prefactor grid, while the empirical small
neutrino mass and parametric thermal/cosmology scalings remain explicit inputs.
