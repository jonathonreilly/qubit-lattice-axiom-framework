# Assumptions And Imports

## Repo Surface

- `docs/repo/ACTIVE_REVIEW_QUEUE.md` supplies the review gate text.
- Existing free two-point SO(4) notes are context only; this block does not
  consume them as proof inputs.

## Reproved In This Block

- Spatial signed-permutation symmetry leaves two diagonal quadratic kinetic
  invariants: `p_tau^2` and `p_x^2+p_y^2+p_z^2`.
- 4D signed-permutation symmetry leaves one diagonal quadratic invariant.
- A spatial-cubic invariant anisotropic kinetic form fails time-space rotation.
- Quartic spatial cubic artifact checks do not determine the marginal
  quadratic time normalization.

## Not Imported

- Euclidean kinetic normalization.
- 4D hypercubic symmetry.
- Full SO(4) continuum covariance.
- OS reconstruction or interacting all-`n`-point covariance.
