# No-Go Ledger

## N1: Multi-Loop Consistency Does Not Force CAR

Adjacent braid/Coxeter consistency forces all adjacent exchange generators to
carry the same `Z_2` sign, but the sign can be `+1` or `-1`.

## N2: Single-Valued Abelian Exchange Character Does Not Select The Sign

Both the trivial and sign characters are single-valued one-dimensional
characters.

## N3: CAR Predicate Is The Missing Selector

Adding `chi(s_1)=-1` selects the fermion sign, but that is exactly the selector
this route was supposed to derive.
