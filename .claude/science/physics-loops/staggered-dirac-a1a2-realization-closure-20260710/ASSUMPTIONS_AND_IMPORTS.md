node:

   cubic rotations, with no preferred site.
2. Qubit — one-site possibility algebra `M_2(C)`; a compatible `Cl(3,0)`
   presentation adds no primitive structure; no preferred possibility.

   nearest-neighbor rule whose available possibilities vary with neighbor
   conditions.
4. Record — records form; each present record locks exactly one admissible
   possibility; at most one record per site; permanence; content-only finite
   additive scalar readout.

The approved kinetic-isotropy and realized-state primitives are not theorem
premises. They were checked as rescue routes at their source boundaries; they
supply neither a kinetic-law selector nor a derivation of one from the four
axioms.
