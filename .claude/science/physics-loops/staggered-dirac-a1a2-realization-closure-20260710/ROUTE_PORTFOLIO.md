## Orthogonal attack record

### R1 — symmetry/naturality

The strongest symmetry rescue was imposed directly: nonzero, Hermitian,
number conserving, nearest neighbor, translation invariant, proper-cubic
invariant, and invariant under a common one-site frame change. The edge law
`I-SWAP` satisfies all of it. Its one-flip generator is the cubic graph
Laplacian, with symbol `2 sum_mu(1-cos k_mu)` and only the origin in its zero set. The
named symmetries therefore do not uniquely select first-order staggered
kinetics.

### R2 — Admissibility semantics

The positive route tried to identify changing local availability with the
kinetic coefficient carriers. A complete countermodel instead defines
availability by the top eigenspace of the unordered sum of recorded neighbor
projectors, with the whole projective line at scalar ties. It is fixed,
neighbor dependent, translation/proper-cubic covariant, and common-frame
covariant, yet it carries no kinetic coefficient map. The source axiom's own
boundary says Admissibility is not dynamics, so the identification is extra.

### R3 — Clifford/cocycle

Pairing three Pauli generators with the three lattice axes and supplying a
naive first-order Dirac vertex would allow spin diagonalization. But those are
exactly the absent data: the current Qubit axiom says the compatible
`Cl(3,0)` presentation adds no further primitive structure. The already
retained bounded kinetic theorem leaves gauge-inequivalent plus and minus flux
classes once its stronger sign-link surface is licensed.

### R4 — approved primitives and Record

Kinetic isotropy supplies only `c_t=c_s`; realized-state supplies only an
evaluation slot at a supplied state. The countermodel includes an explicit
nonempty permanent record history with additive readout, so Record does not
select the kinetic branch either.

### R5 — spectral selection

Requiring a first-order law with eight isolated corner zeros would eliminate
the graph Laplacian, but no current premise states that requirement. Using it
here would assume the target discriminator. It remains a viable future premise
only if independently derived and registered.

### R6 — complete countermodel

This route gives one model of all four current axioms together with a highly
symmetric nonzero physical matter law whose exact infinite spectrum disagrees
with the staggered conclusion. That is sufficient to refute universal
selection. The finite runner independently checks the local algebra,
degenerate Admissibility cases, record history, exchange interaction,
Fourier/corner formulas, flux split, and wrap-holonomy sharpener.
