# No-Go Ledger

## Matter-Content Battery Alone

The matter-content carrier battery does not select K1 over K0. Both branches
carry the same retained-surface package, so the selector must consume spectral
or thermal data rather than carrier counts.

## Z Geometry Alone

The retained Z-certificate separates K1 and K0 geometrically, but it explicitly
performs no selection by itself. It must be composed with a finite-species-
density/thermal clause.

## CL Without Z

FSB-K minus the Z hypothesis is false as a universal statement: the quadratic
point-zero comparator violates the T^4 law. The Z geometry leg is load-bearing.

## Current Block Boundary

This block does not close P-KIN wholesale. It selects within the licensed
two-class surface only after C1 reaches retained grade.
