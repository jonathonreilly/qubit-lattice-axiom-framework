# No-Go Ledger

## Born Marginals Alone

Statement: retained one-copy Born/Gleason weights plus finite scalar additivity
on the two-registration quotient do not force `m(j,k)=p_j p_k`.

Witness: for `p=1/2`, both
`(1/4,1/4,1/4,1/4)` and `(1/2,0,0,1/2)` are finite-additive joint laws with
the same one-copy marginals. The diagonal two-copy density matrices
`diag(p^2,p(1-p),(1-p)p,(1-p)^2)` and `diag(p,0,0,1-p)` make the same boundary
Born-realizable.

Scope: prunes only the route "derive factorization from one-copy Born
marginals alone." It does not refute physical repeated-registration
independence, record-stack stationarity, or a future preparation theorem.
