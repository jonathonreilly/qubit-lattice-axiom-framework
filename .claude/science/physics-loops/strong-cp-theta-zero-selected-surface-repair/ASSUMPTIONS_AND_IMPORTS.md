# Assumptions And Imports

No new axiom is introduced.

The repair makes these selected-surface hypotheses explicit:

- no bare theta slot on the selected action surface;
- real positive scalar mass line `M_u = m_u I`, `M_d = m_d I`;
- paired finite anti-Hermitian staggered spectrum;
- real Wilson gauge action;
- nonnegative topological-sector weights.

These are hypotheses of the bounded selected-surface theorem, not claimed framework derivations.
