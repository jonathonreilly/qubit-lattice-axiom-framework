## Independent source reconstruction and edge checks

For a direct derivation of (14), introduce an auxiliary real indicator source lambda. Let w_(V(tau)) denote the complete unnormalized conditional density from (1), and write

    L(s,t,tau,lambda) = log integral exp(sF+tG+lambda 1_B) w_(V(tau))(a,z) da.

At lambda=0, partial_lambda L is the normalized event probability. A single tau derivative inserts the score A_c. Taking the three ordinary linear-source derivatives lambda,s,t gives the fourth joint cumulant of 1_B,F,G,A_c. No derivative of A_c with respect to those source parameters occurs, because the score is source independent. This verifies the displayed identity by a generating-functional path rather than merely assuming that successive normalized derivatives add one cumulant argument. It does not authorize a second boundary derivative without the corresponding score-derivative terms.

The output-inclusive boundary convention is necessary. Consider a level-zero kernel with its intermediate output owned outside R, but some fine input readers inside R. Set every fine input to I and that exterior output to -I. The kernel belongs to C_R because of its selected input reader, and at reference it contributes phi_c=2. An estimate checking only exterior input links would incorrectly assign zero reference cost. The stated B_ext includes this hidden output, so the final proof does not make that error.

The checked edge cases are consistent: u=0 gives the product-Haar conditional and zero boundary score; M=0 gives H_u=1; repeated/inverse path occurrences are bounded by their full word multiplicities; an arbitrary exterior is permitted in (7) because A_ref is retained; and taking R to be the full torus recovers a volume-sized prefactor rather than falsely producing a component-sized estimate. For local coercivity, even a complete factor may read outside J_R, but a missing factor cannot be supplied by the global energy bound alone.
