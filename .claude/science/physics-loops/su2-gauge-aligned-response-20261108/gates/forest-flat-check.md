### Independent twisted closed-form reconstruction

Set S=L^2 and z_mu=exp(i k_mu). Lift a closed Bloch one-form a to the integer lattice. Elementary plaquette commutations make its discrete path integral Psi well defined on the universal cover. The functions

    Psi(x+S e_mu) - z_mu Psi(x) = b_mu

are constants, because their discrete gradients vanish. Commuting two translations gives

    (z_nu-1)b_mu = (z_mu-1)b_nu.

If some z_mu is not one, adding b_mu/(z_mu-1) to Psi sets every b_nu to zero. Thus a=dPsi with a genuinely Bloch potential. The actual composed Q gives terminal differences (z_mu-1)Psi(root). Their vanishing forces the root value to zero. Vanishing a on the fine tree forces Psi to equal that root value everywhere in the cell, so a=0 and a1=0.

If every z_mu=1, the b_mu are the torus periods. Put c_mu=b_mu/S and psi(x)=Psi(x)-c dot x. Then psi is periodic and a=c+dpsi. Every paired path has its prescribed lifted net displacement; hence Q1 Q0 c=S c while Q1 Q0 dpsi=0 on terminal roots. The terminal condition forces c=0, after which the tree condition kills dpsi. This explicitly retains, then removes, the harmonic torus modes. It also covers Bloch boundary points with z_mu=-1.

### A second independent flat-gap proof supplied by Energy

This proof of coercivity did not use Bloch matrices. Set each retained link a_e=exp(i epsilon x_e dot sigma), keep tree links I and terminal links I, and apply the checked compact inequality. Its left side is epsilon^2 ||x||^2/2+o(epsilon^2); its energy is epsilon^2 <x,Hmin x>/2+o(epsilon^2). Dividing and taking epsilon to zero gives

    Hmin >= C(L,kappa)^(-1) I.

This holds uniformly in volume and all permitted internal trees. For the periodic trees, the Bloch variational g is therefore at least this constant (finite-volume momenta are dense in the continuous Brillouin torus and the fibers are continuous). This supplies an independent path-and-plaquette check of the load-bearing gap, not merely another evaluation of the same Fourier derivation. It does not evaluate an efficient numerical constant.

Terminal identity cannot be dropped from this energy-to-identity inequality. A compatible nonidentity flat terminal field can have W0=gamma0=gamma1=0 while its rooted quotient has nonidentity root-to-root transport. This is outside the theorem's stated domain, not a counterexample to it.
