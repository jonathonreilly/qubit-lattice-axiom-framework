## 1. A rough core under an arbitrary exterior

Use the actual two-step compact quotient with terminal V=I, beta=u, both
kernel strengths kappa*u, and fixed L,kappa as in the supplier. Write
T_x=sum_{e owned by x}(1-w(a_e)). Fix a chord threshold 0<delta<=2 and
a deterministic nonempty set S of terminal cells. Its fixed event B_S says
that EVERY cell of S owns at least one link with chord distance >=delta.
This is not the event that S is an entire rough component.

Let C_*,q_* in (20),(27) be the uniform radial-profile constants and let
t_*=c_E/2>0. Choose an integer h, independent of S,u,volume and exterior,
large enough that

    C_*(2h+1)^4 q_*^h <= delta^2/4.                    (1)

Set R=N_h(S), and condition on any compact quotient configuration z outside
R. No smallness condition is imposed on z. For every x in S, N_h(x) is
contained in R, including on a periodic torus. Apply (27) with A=S,
Omega=R, s_x=h, and t=t_*. Since B_S implies T_S>=delta^2 |S|/2,
Markov's inequality gives in this same normalized regional law

    p_S(z):=P_R(B_S|z)
      <= min(1, exp[t_* C_* |S|-t_* u delta^2 |S|/4]). (2)

In particular, for u>=max(1,8C_*/delta^2),

    p_S(z) <= eta(u)^|S|,
    eta(u)=exp[-t_* u delta^2/8] < 1,                 (3)

uniformly over the entire exterior. The buffer width can be large; no
efficient numerical constant is asserted. A whole-torus R has empty
exterior and the same proof applies. Arbitrary dependence among the cells
of S and among collar variables is allowed. This estimate does not bound
the probability of a rough cell conditional on all its immediate neighbors.

## 2. The exact normalization that is compared

Let W_R(a_R,z;V) contain every original energy/kernel factor reading J_R,
with all original radial normalizers, and integrate against product Haar.
The terminal variations below are around V=I. Define

    Z_R = integral W_R da_R,
    Z_bad = integral 1_{B_S} W_R da_R,
    Z_good = integral 1_{B_S^c} W_R da_R.

Then, exactly, Z_R=Z_bad+Z_good and

    z_S=Z_bad/Z_good=p_S/(1-p_S),
    L_S=log(Z_R/Z_good)=-log(1-p_S).                  (4)

Here "good" means only that not every cell in S is rough. Neither the
buffer nor the other cells are restricted to a chart. This precise complement
is essential: (4) is not a ratio against an all-small-field regional integral.
Z_good>0 because a product cap around identity misses B_S. Thus every
logarithm in (4) is a real logarithm of an actual positive normalized integral.

When eta(u)<=1/2, (3),(4) imply

    0<=z_S<=2 eta(u)^|S|,  0<=L_S<=2 eta(u)^|S|.       (5)

Outside factors common to the two integrals cancel exactly. All conditional
covariances generated by the regional integration remain in its logarithm.
This comparison uses a full compact denominator. Replacing it by a
chart denominator would change the theorem.

## 3. Two sources and two distinct terminal variations

For bounded real original sources F,G independent of V, use the exact
reconstruction U=U(a,h) and include its orbit integral:

    Z_R(V;s,t)=integral da_R dh W_R(a_R,z;V)
                              exp[sF(U(a,h))+tG(U(a,h))].

Define Z_bad and Z_good by their same fixed indicators inside this integral.
The fixed quotient exterior is z; the Haar orbit variables h are integrated,
including their source-only factor. At zero sources this extra integral is
one. This definition preserves the conditional orbit covariance for
gauge-variant sources. It does not describe conditioning on original links
instead of quotient coordinates.

Consider four labeled derivatives (partial_s,partial_t,D_c,D_d), with c,d
distinct terminal links and fixed tangent vectors v_c,v_d at identity. Their
log-weight insertions at zero sources are F,G,A_c,A_d, where

    A_c=kappa*u <v_c,M_{1,c}>

if the final kernel c is selected in W_R, and A_c=0 otherwise. A final
kernel excluded from W_R is a common outside factor and cancels from (4).
Every selected input-dependent normalizer remains in the measure; its output
independence explains its absence from this particular score. There is no
cross-score derivative for distinct links. F,G and the event have no explicit
V dependence.

Let I be a nonempty subset of the four derivative labels and k=|I|. Compact
differentiation of the normalized event probability gives exactly

    D_I p_S = kappa_{k+1}(1_{B_S}, (X_i)_{i in I}),     (6)

under the corresponding full sourced regional law, evaluated here at zero
sources and terminal identity. Source and boundary derivatives each occur
at most once in this formula. Repeated terminal-link derivatives would
require score-derivative terms and are not covered by (6).

Put ell=(||F||_infinity,||G||_infinity,
         kappa*u |v_c|,kappa*u |v_d|).
In every term of the cumulant partition formula, the unique block containing
the indicator retains a factor p_S. The remaining variables are bounded.
Therefore

    |D_I p_S| <= b_k p_S product_{i in I} ell_i,
    (b_1,b_2,b_3,b_4)=(2,6,26,150).                  (7)

These constants are sum_{j=1}^{k+1} S(k+1,j)(j-1)!; no independence or
replacement of a conditional covariance by zero is used.

Apply the ordinary mixed chain rule to L_S=-log(1-p_S). With P_4 the set
of partitions of the four labels, its exact expression is

    D_{1234} L_S = sum_{pi in P_4}
       (|pi|-1)! (1-p_S)^(-|pi|) product_{B in pi} D_B p_S. (8)

For 0<=p_S<=1/2, (7),(8) give the explicit uniform bound

    |D_c D_d partial_s partial_t L_S|
      <= C_4 kappa^2 u^2 |v_c||v_d|
                        ||F||_infinity||G||_infinity eta(u)^|S|,
    C_4=sum_{pi in P_4} (|pi|-1)! 2^|pi|
                                  product_{B in pi} b_{|B|}. (9)

The conservative constant follows from p_S^|pi|<=p_S and
(1-p_S)^(-|pi|)<=2^|pi|. If a score is absent the corresponding actual
derivative is zero; the displayed upper bound remains valid. Derivatives
are taken before applying the estimate at V=I. No extension of (3) to a
nonidentity terminal neighborhood has been presumed.

Equivalently, the left side of (9) is the full regional fourth cumulant
minus the fourth cumulant with the fixed event B_S^c imposed, using the
same original sources, scores and orbit variables in both measures.
This is an exact local normalization-response difference. It is not the
fourth derivative of a raw, unnormalized rare-event integral.

## 4. What the comparison supplies and what is still needed

Equations (3),(5),(9) supply an exponentially small, source-complete local
compact-core correction, uniformly over arbitrary fixed quotient exterior.
The fourth derivative costs only the displayed u^2 factor. All source-only
orbit terms and every regional kernel normalizer survive.

The core exclusion B_S^c is not the all-good chart, buffers can overlap, and
averaging these ratios over a common exterior also differentiates that
exterior law. No product formula for ratios with overlapping readers is
asserted. A family of these pointwise local bounds is not yet a connected
logarithm expansion for the full posterior. In particular (9) has no decay
in the separation of the four insertion supports. Those composition and
connected-cancellation estimates, and the complete subtraction for a
different coarse conditional denominator, remain open. Nothing here proves
an obstruction to them. Independent checking of this consequence is pending.
