    rho_J(dU0 dU1)=Q_J(0)^(-1) q_u(U0) K0(U1|U0)
                         product_(e in O) k1,e(I|U1) dU0 dU1.  (D1)

The terminal-root fine forest may have arbitrary permitted internal trees.
Its exact quotient leaves all non-tree fine links and all intermediate
links, with M=3L^8+1+4L^4 group coordinates and k=3M scalar coordinates per
cell. At zero sources the root-fixed gauge orbit has product probability
Haar, independent of the quotient law nu_J. This follows directly from
the Haar-bijective forest map: q_u, K0 and every remaining identity-output
kernel are invariant under this same root-fixed orbit group. Removing J
does not change that invariance or the Jacobian.

Use periodic axial nearest-neighbor cell distance d1 and its connected
graphs for Gaussian paths and tree lengths. The compact shell neighborhoods
N_r use periodic l-infinity distance d_inf. These metrics obey
d_inf<=d1<=4d_inf. All finite reader radii can be converted at this fixed
cost. The marginal chart supplier already uses d1; below we conservatively

    A_c=kappa u <i v_c dot sigma_Pauli,M1,c>,
    A_d=kappa u <i v_d dot sigma_Pauli,M1,d>.            (D2)

## 3. The missing compact primitive budget

Fix the Gaussian constants beta,R0,p0,K_g and the forest threshold H_f.
For each compact label use S_g=N_R0(Gamma_g); either metric's R0 collar

    W_K=(8K+6D+13+2R0)^4,
    Delta_K=max(1,(16K+12D+25)^4-1),
    t_K=K exp[beta W_K+(log C_epsilon)V_K
                          +k log(K_g) H_K-c_J u q/4].          (D6)

We claim the actual compact part of (F6) obeys

    h_comp<=W_K t_K/(1-Delta_K^2 t_K)                 (D7)

whenever Delta_K^2 t_K<1. To prove this, let T be the union of core cells
of g. Its d_conn-components are exactly g's cores, because distinct cores
are separated beyond d_conn and each core is connected in that graph.
Thus T determines the partition; there is no additional set-partition
factor. Gamma-overlap connectivity implies T is connected in the graph
joining cells at d_inf distance <=2(d_conn+D+2)=8K+6D+12, whose degree
is at most Delta_K. A connected n-cell set containing a fixed cell has
at most Delta_K^(2(n-1)) deterministic depth-first spanning-tree encodings:
the walk has 2(n-1) steps, and its visited set recovers the set. Given T,
the shell choices number at most K^(number of cores)<=K^n.

If x belongs to S_g, some core cell is within d_inf radius d_conn+D+2+R0
of x. Summing that possible root costs at most W_K; possible repeated

The positive exponent costs are O(K^4)+O(log K)=O(log(u)^4), whereas

    h_comp<=exp(-c_r p^2)<=H_f/2.                     (D8)

The Gaussian producer already proves h_weak<=C sigma p^3 and

## 4. Connected correction and the full marginal response

Apply the proved abstract density-forest theorem at this exact match. Its
base-disjoint forest labels are first regrouped by full-reader overlap,
retaining their internal base compatibility, before the hard-core log.
The no-compact families have identical indexing in Z_* and Z_a and cancel

    log(Z_*/Z_a)=sum_Y Psi_Y(zeta),
    sup_x sum_(Y contains x) exp[(beta/2)|Y|]
                  sup_(|zeta|<r)|Psi_Y|<=C exp(-c_r p^2).       (D9)

Every term contains a compact label. Every source parameter it reads brings

    |kappa4_(nu_J)(F,G,A_c,A_d)-kappa4_(chart,J)(F,G,A_c,A_d)|
      <=C ell_F ell_G kappa^2 u^2 |v_c||v_d|
                           exp(-c_r p^2) exp[-(beta/2)tau_4].  (D10)

This is a bound on exact connected coefficients for bounded measurable
quotient F,G. It includes chart-law and regional-normalization marks.
It does not replace the source-dependent chart logarithm by zero.

For C2 singlets the already proved actual marginal cube covariance bound
has a uniform positive Hessian gap times u. Restricting its fixed cube to

Singlet invariance of F,G gives DF(0)=DG(0)=0. Taylor's formula and their

    ||F-EF||_s,||G-EG||_s<=C_s/u,
    ||grad F||_s,||grad G||_s<=C_s/sqrt(u),
    ||A_e-EA_e||_s<=C_s |v_e|sqrt(u),
    ||grad A_e||_s<=C_s |v_e|u.                        (D11)

## 5. Original sources and the actual conditional normalizer

For original local C2 singlet sources F(a,h),G(a,h), keep their complete
root-fixed orbit source factor E_h exp(sF+tG). Set

    f=E_h F, g=E_h G, C_FG=E_h(FG)-fg.                (D14)

These are local C2 quotient singlets. The endpoint readers of h are the
same as for the exact forest reconstruction; no new gauge path is inserted.
Differentiating the full joint orbit factor at zero sources gives exactly

    kappa4_(rho_J)(F,G,A_c,A_d)
       =kappa4_(nu_J)(f,g,A_c,A_d)
                         +kappa3_(nu_J)(C_FG,A_c,A_d).         (D15)

The second term is not deleted. The density match and (D9) work with three
labels by omitting one source vertex. On the chart the centered C_FG has
weight u^-1, its gradient weight u^-1/2, and two scores have weights
sqrt(u). For each 1|2 cut, the centered third cumulant is its crossing
covariance of a singleton and a pair. The same product estimates and a
three-representative spanning tree give C exp(-alpha_1 tau_3/2).
The compact correction is C u^2 exp(-c_r p^2) exp(-beta tau_3/2).
Thus this whole third cumulant is O(1) with three-support tree decay.

If the orbit reader sets H_F,H_G are disjoint, independent Haar gives
C_FG=0 identically; orbit invariance of either source also suffices. If
they overlap, enlarge source regions T_F,T_G to include their endpoint
owner cells. These regions then intersect, their union has bounded diameter,
and is a valid reader for C_FG. A three-region connecting tree can be
extended a bounded distance to meet both original source regions. Hence

    |kappa4_(rho_J)(F,G,A_c,A_d)|
      <=C |v_c||v_d|[u^-1+1_(H_F intersects H_G)]
                         exp[-mu' tau_4(T_F,T_G,S_c,S_d)].     (D16)

Its weighted two-boundary sum has the same prefactor. This allowance is
not a claimed nonzero leading coefficient; it preserves the conditional
