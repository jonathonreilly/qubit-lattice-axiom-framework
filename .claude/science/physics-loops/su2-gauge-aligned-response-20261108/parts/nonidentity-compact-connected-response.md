Every surviving radial normalizer remains. The seven J kernels have been
integrated out completely, including their normalizers.

    sigma=u^(-1/2), p=log(2+u)^A, rho=sigma p, A>5/2.             (N2)

## 2. Reinstantiating the exact compact insertion identity

    c T_R-K_E T_B<=Ebar_R<=K_E(T_R+T_B).                      (N6)

This holds under arbitrary compact quotient exterior. Its physical density

occurs in each regional numerator and denominator. No whole-volume

         (1+alpha)^K tau>2M(4K+1)^4, M=3L^8+1+4L^4,

Here I_R is the entire regional chart integral, and N^out is one integral
over its compact complement with the selector. It is not the complement

    H_s=(union_s N_dconn(A))\(union_s R_j(A)).                 (N9)

Every assigned exterior source reader lies there and outside every other

## 4. Chart moments without an identity symmetry

At q=I the complete-energy Hessian has a uniform positive gap. Local
third derivatives, finite reader range and bounded incidence imply the
same gap, times u, on a sufficiently small centered product cube; the
Haar log has bounded local derivatives and can be absorbed by increasing
u_0. The same estimates give row bounds Cu and finite range. Thus the
Neumann-cube and mixed one-form proof in the existing nonlinear-chart
supplier applies to this actual S and this cube. It gives

    |Cov(f,g)|<=C u^(-1) sum_(X,Y) exp[-alpha d1(X,Y)]
                            ||grad_X f||_2 ||grad_Y g||_2.      (N18)

That analytic proof uses the Hessian, row/range bounds and cube faces,
not symmetry of S or its expectation. Its constants do not grow as the
cube shrinks. The new regional radial-profile proof independently gives
||x_i||_s<=C_s sigma for every fixed finite s under this same full chart
law. It uses exact Haar cap division and the same-law tower under product
restrictions. We do NOT assert E x_i=0.

Now impose uniform local C2 bounds and simultaneous conjugation invariance
on the quotient functions F,G as functions on the original a variables.
Their differential at the all-identity field is zero: an invariant linear
functional on each adjoint R3 copy is zero. Since a* is O(epsilon) near I,
Taylor expansion of F(a* exp(ix)) gives, after centering,

    ||F-EF||_s<=C_s w, ||grad F||_s<=C_s sqrt(u) w,
    w=epsilon sigma+sigma^2,
    ||A_c-EA_c||_s<=C_s |dot V_c| sqrt(u),
    ||grad A_c||_s<=C_s |dot V_c| u,                         (N19)

and similarly for G,d. Indeed F-F(a*) is bounded by
C(epsilon sum_readers|x_i|+sum_readers|x_i|^2), and its gradient by
C(epsilon+sum_readers|x_i|). Scores have bounded divided first derivatives;
their value at a* is removed before the moment estimate. These statements
need no background symmetry and do not treat a* as a mean.

For centered four labels with weights (w,w,sqrt(u),sqrt(u)), Hölder and
the product rule give every product its product weight and every product
gradient at most C sqrt(u) times that weight. Across a 1|3 cut the fourth
cumulant is Cov(singleton,triple) minus its three pairings. Across a 2|2
cut it is Cov(pair,pair) minus the two cross pairings. Every remaining
pairing crosses the cut at least once. Apply (N18) to that crossing and
Hölder to its partner. Each cut bounds the cumulant by
C u w^2 exp[-alpha d1(S_I,S_J)] times the tangent norms. Since
u w^2=(epsilon+sigma)^2<=2(epsilon^2+u^(-1)), a longest edge of a minimum
spanning tree on four support representatives gives (N4) for the chart,
with exponent alpha/3 and a bounded reader-diameter loss. Combining with
(N17) gives the full compact statement, since u^3 exp(-c_r p^2) is bounded.

For any 0<=eta<mu the weighted sum over distinct c,d in O obeys

    sum_(c!=d) exp(eta tau_4)|kappa4_nuV(F,G,A_c,A_d)|
       <=C_eta (epsilon^2+u^(-1)) exp[-b_eta d1(S_F,S_G)]       (N20)

for unit tangent vectors. A connecting tree bounds the distance from a
fixed F representative to G and to the two score anchors by tau_4 plus
a fixed diameter constant. Sum the two resulting four-dimensional
exponentially decaying anchor factors. Periodic volume only reduces this
sum relative to the infinite lattice bound.

## 5. Original sources and the conditional-density subtraction

For original local bounded C2 singlet sources on the full hidden chain,
set f=E_h F, g=E_h G and C_FG=E_h(FG)-fg under the root-fixed product Haar.
These remain local C2 singlet functions of a, independent of V as functions.
Integrate the whole joint orbit source factor E_h exp(sF+tG). Exact
differentiation gives, at zero sources and this fixed background,

    kappa4_rhoV(F,G,A_c,A_d)
       =kappa4_nuV(f,g,A_c,A_d)+kappa3_nuV(C_FG,A_c,A_d).       (N21)

The second term is retained. Three labels in (N16) give its compact/chart
correction C u^2 exp(-c_r p^2) exp(-beta_f tau_3/2). On the chart C_FG
has centered weight w and gradient weight sqrt(u)w; the two scores have
weights sqrt(u). The 1|2 covariance cuts and a three-representative tree
give the valid, deliberately unimproved bound C u w=C(1+epsilon sqrt(u))
with tree decay. We do not assume Gaussian odd-cumulant cancellation in
this step or claim that this upper power is sharp.

If the original orbit reader sets H_F,H_G are disjoint, product Haar gives
C_FG=0 identically. Otherwise their endpoint-extended source regions T_F,T_G
meet and have bounded union diameter. A tree meeting that union and the
two scores can be extended a bounded distance to both regions. Therefore

    |kappa4_rhoV(F,G,A_c,A_d)|<=C |dot V_c||dot V_d|
      [epsilon^2+u^(-1)+(1+epsilon sqrt(u)) 1_(H_F intersects H_G)]
                          exp[-mu' tau_4(T_F,T_G,S_c,S_d)].    (N22)

It has the corresponding weighted boundary-pair sum. This is a quantitative
allowance for the exact orbit term, not a claim of nonzero leading behavior.

For p_J=N_V/Q_J(V_O), the density of the seven terminal outputs conditional
on their exterior, suppose ALL specified terminal outputs are within the
same small identity neighborhood. The fully pinned numerator's hidden law
is handled by restoring the seven kernels. At identity their leading
energies are nonnegative, so E_full,I^lead>=E_J,I^lead gives the same
supplied compact gap for every allowed tree. The new mode comparison uses
only this gap and the complete local factor bounds, whose full-template
incidence constants already cover restoration. The compensated regional,
Gaussian and connected arguments above therefore apply with the restored
count K_surv+7 and that law's own mode and shifted energy minimum.

The exact partial-terminal identity for distinct terminal links is then

    D_c D_d partial_s partial_t log p_J
      =kappa4_piV(F,G,A_c,A_d)
                  -1_(c,d in O) kappa4_rhoV(F,G,A_c,A_d).      (N23)

Every term uses its own normalized law. Outputs in J do not enter Q_J.
Applying (N4) or (N22) separately to both terms proves the same bounds for
this coefficient with epsilon=max over ALL terminal outputs. No cancellation
between the two laws is assumed. Fixed-score generating functions reproduce
this mixed coefficient because distinct source/output labels have zero
mixed log-weight derivatives; they are not equal to finite terminal
variations. The frozen-center device therefore loses no physical derivative.
