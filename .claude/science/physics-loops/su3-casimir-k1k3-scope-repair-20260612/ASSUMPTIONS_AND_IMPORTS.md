# Assumptions And Imports

## Load-Bearing

- `cl3_color_automorphism_theorem`: supplies the algebraic SU(3) action on
  the 3-dimensional symmetric base subspace `V_3` and the Gell-Mann trace
  normalization.
- Schur's lemma, total antisymmetry of `f^{abc}`, and the standard SU(N)
  Casimir closed form are pure mathematical context; the note also derives
  `4/3` directly by the trace identity.

## Explicitly Not Load-Bearing

- Physical SM color identification of `V_3`.
- One-gluon-exchange, quark self-energy, scattering, confinement, or other
  physical readouts of the color factor.
- K4 / C1-C5 readouts from the historical broader parent surface.

No new axiom, observed target value, fitted selector, or unit convention is
introduced.
