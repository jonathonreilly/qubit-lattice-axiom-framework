# No-Go Ledger

## Pruned In This Block

- T-oddness alone selects K-reality.
- A C3-invariant K-odd Hermitian operator beyond `A=i(C-C^2)` supplies the
  selector.
- C3 averaging or central-sector Record data canonically retains the
  noncommuting bridge.
- Scalar finite signatures of a unit bridge choose its orientation.

## Not Pruned

- A physical C3-breaking source vector from same-source dynamics.
- A valid spin-generation entangled carrier that produces a generation doublet
  vector.
- A holomorphic/Pfaffian determinant action theorem.
