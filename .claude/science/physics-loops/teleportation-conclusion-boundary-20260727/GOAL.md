# Goal

## Exact Target Contract

**Target statement:** the finite teleportation planning packet does not entail
unconditional nature-grade closure.

**Domain:** the explicit `2 x 8 x 4` selector family, signed rows at even sides
`4..12`, and controller/detector requirement formulas.

**Allowed premises:** those finite packet entries and ordinary finite
combinatorics/propositional logic.

**Forbidden weakening:** assigning `unconditional_closed = False` as an input;
treating a timeout as a physical result; claiming a global teleportation no-go.

**Completion witness:** exhaustive selector residuals plus extensions that
preserve all finite scaling/requirement premises while violating the universal
scaling or device-existence conclusion.

**Does not count:** reprinting fixed booleans, proving only threshold arithmetic,
or claiming the three underlying Nature-grade obligations are solved.
