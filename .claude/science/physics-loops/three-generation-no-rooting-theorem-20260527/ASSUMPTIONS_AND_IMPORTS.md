# Assumptions And Imports

- Internal finite carrier: `C^8` with basis labelled by `{0,1}^3`.
- Internal Clifford generators: `XII`, `ZXI`, `ZZX`, which satisfy `Cl(3)`.
- Projection family: nonempty proper coordinate projections onto BZ-corner/taste basis subsets.
- Internal taste action: the three bit flips generating transitive `(Z_2)^3`.
- Not imported: arbitrary-subspace representation theory, path-integral rooting, physical species semantics, Standard Model generations, CKM, chirality, or substrate physicality.
