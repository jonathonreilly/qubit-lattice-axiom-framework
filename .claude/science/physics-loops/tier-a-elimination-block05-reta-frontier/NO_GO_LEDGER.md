# No-Go Ledger

- Periodic/torsion `q*pi` phase sources cannot supply the literal rational
  radian bridge; retained no-go already covers this bin.
- Canonical `2*pi` packaging of the fixed-locus rational produces
  `4*pi/9` or `4*pi/3`, not `2/9` or `2/3`.
- Homogeneous self-consistency/readout maps cannot isolate a nonzero off-locus
  member. They either select zero/on-locus or leave the full line free.
- The map `Phi = S_sum` is not a derivation until the map itself is licensed;
  otherwise it is R-eta in holonomy coordinates.
- Record-preserving dynamics conserve `(r, delta)` on the checked surface and
  do not relax the value to the target.
