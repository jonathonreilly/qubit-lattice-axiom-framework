## Discharged Shortcuts

- `d^2 = 0` preserves closedness under exact branch moves; it does not make
  every branch cochain closed.
- The closed-branch carrier is conditional on `dn = 0`.
- The defect witness proves that closure is needed; it does not prove closure.
- Record/readout and admissibility do not select a defect-free sector.
- The G3 phase-insertion work cannot bypass defect discipline.

## Remaining Imports

constraint or dynamical route for G1, an action-side phase insertion for G3,
nonabelian record/readout registration for G2, and final theta-bar assembly.
