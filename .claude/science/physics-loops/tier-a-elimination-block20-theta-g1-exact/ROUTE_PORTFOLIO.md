## Fan-Out Synthesis

The exactness route is tempting because it gives `dn=0` immediately from
`d^2=0`. The obstruction is that theta needs the quotient
`ker d2 / im d1`, not just the kernel. Exactness removes the quotient and
therefore the odd-support intersection charge. The next live G1 route must
derive closed non-exact sectors or suppress defects dynamically while keeping
the nontrivial sector labels.
