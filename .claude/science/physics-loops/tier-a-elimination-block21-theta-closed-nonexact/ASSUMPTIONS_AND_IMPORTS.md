# Assumptions And Imports

## Allowed Current Premises

- Minimal axioms: Lattice, Qubit, Admissibility, Record.
- Approved primitive treatment through the audit premise registry.
- Existing theta support packets as current-surface route material.
- Exact finite cochain algebra already present in the block20 and 4D-carrier
  runners.

## Forbidden Imports

- Observed neutron-EDM or target theta value.
- Fitted selector, axion assumption, or phenomenological suppression premise.
- New topological-sector, bundle, branch/section, action-class, or
  sector-readout primitive.
- Registry edit or audit verdict.
