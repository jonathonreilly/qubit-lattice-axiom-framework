## N7 — Steelman against a phase no-go

A hostile reviewer should reject any assertion that these local limitations
exclude photons. The compact Gaussian comparison fails precisely because
it discards winding structure; a controlled decomposition can retain that
structure. The exact short-time bridge already has a positive interior
Hessian uniform in volume. Combine a bound on joint compact endpoint events
with its local derivative estimate, or build nonlocal periodic correctors,
and a fixed-g response may remain positive despite microscopic virtual
defects. The known isotropic compact-gauge literature supports treating
that possibility seriously, but its hypotheses must be matched to this
Hamiltonian. The actionable next obligation is the actual coupled winding
and endpoint law, not another calculation of the same local obstruction.
