# Compact Hamiltonian response and short-time phase tools

This is a personal derivation checkpoint for the supplied compact U(1)
rotor Hamiltonian at fixed interaction strength. It proposes three useful
steps toward the phase question: a cofinal free-box limit of equal-time
angle measurements, an exact electric-response criterion for visible soft
excitations, and short-time path estimates whose constants survive growing
spatial volume. The uniform positive response, compact long-time phase and
full photon field remain open. No axiom update is warranted by these results.

The notes form three reviewable groups:

- [Electric jump representation](notes/BLOCK01_WORKING_ANISOTROPIC_SELF_ENERGY_AND_TIME_BLOCKING.md),
  [spectral response](notes/BLOCK02_ELECTRIC_RESPONSE_AND_GAUSSIAN_REFERENCE_TESTS.md),
  [phase correctors](notes/BLOCK03_WEIGHTED_PHASE_CORRECTOR_AND_MAGNETIC_TESTS.md)
  and [local density bounds](notes/BLOCK04_LOCAL_DENSITY_AND_FINITE_LOOP_TEST_FAMILY_BOUND.md)
  identify the actual response and the limitation of fixed local loop tests.
- [Quantum ground-state comparison](notes/BLOCK05_GROUND_STATE_CORRELATION_COMPARISON.md)
  derives the growing-free-box angle-marginal limit. It keeps the compact
  double-cover restriction and does not identify unrestricted thermal traces.
- [Compact Gaussian comparison](notes/BLOCK06_COMPACT_STABILITY_COMPARISON_READING.md),
  [positive Wilson mixtures](notes/BLOCK07_POSITIVE_WILSON_MIXTURE_AND_WEAK_REGION_COMPARISON.md)
  and [short-time bridge action](notes/BLOCK08_SHORT_TIME_BRIDGE_AND_ENDPOINT_ACTION.md)
  investigate phase proof mechanisms while preserving the winding sectors.

The short-time result is for the actual coupled continuous-time Hamiltonian,
not a product of isolated magnetic factors. The Wilson mixture controls
specified weak auxiliary plaquettes under the coupled classical gauge law;
its variance variable has infinite mean and is not a physical unwrapped
field. The source counterexample concerns one displayed raw-curl bound in
arXiv2509.19176v1, not its final theorem.

Eight finite programs challenge formulas by separate constructions. Formula
faults, actual failed attempts and corrections are preserved. The [verification
record](review/FOCUSED_VERIFICATION.json) binds the final sources and outputs;
[numerical history](review/NUMERICAL_HISTORY.md) states the limits. These are
author checks, not an independent scientific review or audit.
