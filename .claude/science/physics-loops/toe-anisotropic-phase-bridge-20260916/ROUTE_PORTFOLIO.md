# Routes actually examined

The negative-claim checklist records the seven genuinely different analytic
formulations attempted. Their products are: exact operator identities,
variational correctors, positive-kernel bounds, correlation inequalities,
a source counterexample, an annealed mixture comparison and continuous-time
bridge estimates. Numerical programs challenge these arguments; they are
not another proof family or a replacement for derivation. The phase target
remains open across the portfolio.
