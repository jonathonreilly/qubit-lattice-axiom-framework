# Electric response as a precise phase target

Personal derivation proposal, 2026-09-16. This uses the supplied pure-gauge rotor Hamiltonian, not an axiom-derived law. The spectral identities are standard perturbation and sum-rule machinery derived below. They identify a sufficient response estimate for low-energy excitations; that estimate has not been proved uniformly in volume. Neither the identities nor the finite checks establish a photon phase.

## 1. Domain and the actual response

On a finite connected free spatial cubic box, let D be link incidence, C spatial curl, and c_p=C*1_p. The physical electric lattice is Gamma_Z=ker(D) intersect Z^E; integer contractibility identifies it with the lattice generated by the plaquette cycles. Work in ell2(Gamma_Z), with

    H=(g²/2)||E||²+(1/g²)sum_p[1-(S_(c_p)+S_(-c_p))/2], g>0.

The electric energy is proper on this finite-rank lattice and the jump operator is bounded. Thus H has compact resolvent. The positive jump semigroup is irreducible on Gamma_Z, so its lowest eigenvalue E0 is simple and its normalized electric-coordinate ground vector psi is strictly positive. In angle coordinates the same problem is a scalar elliptic operator with a real smooth potential on the physical quotient torus. Its lowest eigenfunction there may also be chosen strictly positive. These are two separate positivity facts, not an identification of the two coordinate wavefunctions.

For a real link vector f in the cycle space ker(D), define the physical electric observable A_f=(f,E), and the flat electric-offset family

    H_f(t)=H-g² t A_f+(g² t²/2)||f||².                 (1)

Its ground energy is denoted e_f(t). This is an offset of the electric kinetic term, distinct from a spatial Wilson-loop flux insertion and from the bare plaquette stiffness. The linear perturbation is K-form bounded with arbitrarily small relative bound, so the isolated simple ground branch is differentiable twice (indeed analytic near zero). Electric inversion gives <A_f>=0. With R=(H-E0)^(-1) on psi-perp, perturbation theory yields

    e_f''(0)=g²||f||²-2g4 <A_f psi,R A_f psi>.        (2)

For f=0 all expressions vanish; assume f!=0 below. Define

    chi_f=2<A_f psi,R A_f psi>,
    rho_f=e_f''(0)/(g²||f||²),
    eta_f=1-rho_f=g² chi_f/||f||².                   (3)

The diamagnetic inequality for a scalar kinetic operator with flat vector potential on the quotient torus gives e_f(t)>=e_f(0): take the pointwise modulus of any trial wavefunction; the kinetic form decreases and the real multiplication potential is unchanged. Its modulus remains in the physical quotient space. Therefore rho_f>=0. Equation(2) gives rho_f<=1, so 0<=eta_f<=1. For nonzero f in the free-box cycle space, A_f psi is nonzero because psi is positive on the entire electric lattice; hence eta_f>0 at each fixed finite box. This strict finite-box inequality does not provide a lower bound uniform in box size.

Longitudinal f=D*v has A_f=0 on the physical sector and rho_f=1. On a periodic spatial torus, a harmonic electric direction is also constant in a fixed global-flux sector. Such constraint or superselection directions must be excluded. The free-box contract above avoids the latter ambiguity; its long-wavelength test vectors must remain in the nonzero cycle space.

## 2. Sum rule and the conditional soft-energy conclusion

Put K0=H-E0 and let nu_f be the spectral measure of A_f psi under K0, supported on positive energies. Direct commutation with the integer shifts gives

    m_1:=integral omega dnu_f(omega)
       =<A_f psi,K0 A_f psi>
       =(1/(2g²))sum_p (Cf)_p² <cos F_p>,            (4)

    m_-1:=integral omega^(-1)dnu_f(omega)
       =eta_f||f||²/(2g²).                           (5)

The factor1/2 in(4) is the ground-state double-commutator identity. The multiplication operator cos F_p is (S_(c_p)+S_(-c_p))/2 in electric coordinates. Its expectation is positive by positivity of psi and at most1. Hence

    m_1<=||Cf||²/(2g²).

All quantities are finite: H has a positive finite-volume gap above its simple ground state, psi belongs to Dom(K), and the bounded magnetic potential makes the required electric quadratic forms finite. The double-commutator calculation can first be made on finite electric support and extended by this form control.

If Delta_f is the bottom of the positive spectral support visible to A_f, then omega²>=Delta_f² on that support. Equations(4)-(5) imply the exact upper bound

    Delta_f²<=m_1/m_-1
       <=||Cf||²/[eta_f||f||²].                      (6)

There is also a useful statement about the susceptibility weight rather than merely the first visible level. For every Omega>0,

    integral_(omega>Omega) omega^(-1)dnu_f
       <=m_1/Omega².

Thus if eta_f>=eta_*>0 and Omega=a||Cf||/(sqrt(eta_*)||f||), a>1, at least1-a^(-2) of the total susceptibility lies below Omega. This follows from the same positive spectral measure and does not assume a quasiparticle pole.

A sufficient next theorem is therefore:

    For one fixed supplied g>0 and a sequence of free boxes with
    transverse test vectors f_L, establish eta_(f_L)>=eta_*>0,
    while ||C f_L||/||f_L|| tends to zero.             (TARGET)

This would force observable low-energy spectral support. It would still not by itself establish two photon polarizations, linear dispersion with a nonzero limiting residue, Gaussian higher correlations, Lorentz invariance, a matter phase, or an axiom-selected model. Identifying a complete Gaussian field requires more than this sufficient gaplessness criterion.

The earlier Gaussian trial-state energy bounds do not prove TARGET: a trial energy upper bound has a nonzero error already at t=0, so differentiating that inequality twice cannot bound the exact ground-energy curvature. A volume-extensive error also cannot be removed by dividing an energy estimate by volume and then differentiating an unproved response limit.

## 3. Why a globally Gaussian electric-coordinate reference is too strong

The correctly blocked isolated integer increment already has tails heavier than every fixed Gaussian. Let a=T/g²>0. Its Skellam mass obeys, by keeping the event of n positive jumps and no negative jumps,

    p_T(n)>=exp(-a)(a/2)^n/n!, n>=0.                 (7)

For any c>0 and finite C, this lower bound eventually exceeds C exp(-c n²). Thus replacing the complete increment law by a globally quadratically confined electric density is not an exact comparison. A central-window approximation or a macroscopic Gaussian limit remains possible.

The quadratic killing in the interacting process does not simply restore Gaussian tails to the exact electric ground vector. Along any fixed plaquette cycle in a finite free box with P>=1, set psi_n=psi(n c_p). The positive ground-state recurrence gives

    [2g² n²+P/g²-E0] psi_n
       =(1/(2g²))sum_q[psi(n c_p+c_q)+psi(n c_p-c_q)]
       >=(1/(2g²))psi_(n-1).

Since E0>=0 and |c_p|²=4,

    psi_n>=psi_(n-1)/(4g4 n²+2P)
          >=psi_0 (4g4+2P)^(-n)/(n!)².               (8)

The positive recurrence also ensures its left coefficient is positive wherever used. Equation(8) excludes an upper bound psi(n c_p)<=C exp(-c n²) with fixed c>0 and finite C. Its squared mass has the corresponding factorial-fourth-power lower bound. In particular no exact globally uniformly convex potential on the cycle coordinates, with positive quadratic coercivity, can represent these complete ground probabilities relative to counting measure. This conclusion is only about that proposed reference class in electric coordinates. It does not exclude an angle-field representation, a large-field decomposition, a blockwise central-limit theorem, or the Coulomb phase.

For a single plaquette this is the familiar discrete rotor/Mathieu recurrence. No novelty is claimed for its factorial tails. Its purpose here is to prevent importing a globally uniformly convex Gaussian perturbation theorem into a carrier that violates its hypotheses.

## 4. Next decision

The exact positive path representation remains available. A viable continuation must either control the non-Gaussian electric tails together with the coupled response, prove TARGET through a uniform offset-energy estimate, or use a different compact phase representation. The direct one-link dual estimate, the independent Poisson limit and the Gaussian trial compression do not supply that estimate. These are dependencies of one unresolved thermodynamic problem, not multiple independent axiom walls.
