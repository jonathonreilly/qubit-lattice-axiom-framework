## 2. One cube and its corner data

**Theorem 1 (cube commutant and lifts).** For `H_C=⊕_σ⊗_w E_w[σ]` and `G_C=∏_w U(5)_w`,
\[
H_C=\bigoplus_{\boldsymbol\mu}V_{\boldsymbol\mu}\otimes M^C_{\boldsymbol\mu},\quad
M^C_{\boldsymbol\mu}=\bigoplus_\sigma\bigotimes_wM^w_{\mu_w}[\sigma].
\]
The `G_C` commutant consists exactly of `⊕_{μ⃗} 1⊗x_{μ⃗}`. Such an operator also commutes with all full local gauge actions. A cube rotation `R` is implemented by a unitary `L_R`, unique up to phase, with `Ad L_R` a group action and `L_R H_C[σ]=H_C[Rσ]`. On an invariant type tuple, `L_R=Π_R⊗L_R^M`, where `Π_R` permutes the representation factors. If `R^n=1`, then `L_R^n=c1` and `(L_R^M)^n=c1` for one scalar `c`.

*Proof.* Sector decomposition and irreducibility for the product group give the commutant by Schur's lemma. Outside ends at a corner commute with cube operators. The lift permutes link modes and link factors, hence transports sectors and composes as an automorphism. On an invariant tuple, remove the representation-factor permutation; the remainder commutes with `G_C`, so Schur gives `1⊗L_R^M`. Since `Ad L_R^n=id` on the full cube matrix algebra, `L_R^n` is scalar; `Π_R^n=1` gives the last claim. □

**Theorem 2 (corner data).** For `1≤p≤4`, the multiplicity of some type `μ₀` in `(Λ^p C⁵)^{⊗3}` contains a standard two-dimensional `S₃` module `q`. A three-cycle has eigenvalues `ω,ω²`, `ω³=1≠ω`. *Proof.* Two distinct vectors of `Λ^p C⁵` give three tensors with one vector distinguished. Their span is the three-point permutation representation, possibly tensored with sign; it contains the standard module. Since permutations commute with `U(5)`, some `U(5)` multiplicity contains it.

For the line, take distinct `p`-subsets `x_i=Y∪{i}` for `p≤3`, with `|Y|=p−1` disjoint from `{1,2,3}`, and `x_i={1,…,5}\{i}` for `p=4`. Form the symmetric and alternating signed permutation sums of the three distinct wedge monomials. Both are nonzero and each transforms by a character. The colour-invariant operator `E₁₂=Σ_k f†_{1k}f_{2k}` maps each term to a basis vector indexed by the unordered pair in slots 1,2 and the remaining subset. The two terms for a fixed remaining subset have coefficients `s,s'∈{±1}`. One of the two character sums has coefficient `s+s'` or `s−s'` nonzero. Thus `E₁₂` is nonzero on one character subrepresentation. A constituent irreducible on which it is nonzero supplies `μ₁,L`; the nonzero intertwiner is injective. Permuting the ends gives every ordering. Distinct `S₃` isotypes are orthogonal. □

## 4. Equivariant cube gate

Fix an even cube `C`. For each diagonal `P={w₁,w₂}`, put type `μ₀` at its ends and `μ₁` elsewhere. In `σ*` define `E_P` and `X_P` as the `+1` and `−1` eigenspaces of `Z_{w₁}Z_{w₂}` on `q_{w₁}⊗q_{w₂}⊗(⊗_{w∉P}L_w)`. They each have dimension two. Representation factors are suppressed in this notation.

**Theorem 4 (cube gate).** For even `m=2p`, `1≤p≤4`, there is a unitary `g_C` commuting with `G_C` and invariant under every cube rotation. For every diagonal `P`, it fixes `V_{μ⃗_P}⊗E_P` and exchanges `V_{μ⃗_P}⊗X_P` with a subspace in `H_C[σ*+hex_P]⊕H_C[σ*−hex_P]`.

*Proof.* The diagonal stabilizer `D₃` preserves the type tuple, `E_P` and `X_P`. On joint eigenlines `v_{ss'}` of `Z_{w₁},Z_{w₂}`, write the `r` eigenvalues as `θ_{ss'}=κa_sb_{s'}`. The ratio `ρ=a_+/a_-` is `ω` or `ω²`. The half-turn exchanges the endpoints, and `rh=hr^{-1}` as an automorphism, so `θ_{ss'}θ_{s's}=c` for a scalar `c`. In particular `θ_{++}²=θ_{--}²`. The case `s≠s′` gives `θ_{++}²=c=θ_{+-}θ_{-+}`, hence `a_+b_+=a_-b_-`. Alternatively, Theorem 1 gives `(θ_{++}/θ_{--})³=1`; the ratio is therefore `1`. Thus `b_+/b_-=ρ^{-1}` and `θ_{+-}/θ_{-+}=ρ²≠1`.

In `σ*+hex_P`, the two diagonal corners retain `q` and each hexagon corner contains `μ₁` by Theorem 2. The tensor product of their nonzero multiplicity spaces has an `r` eigenvector `φ`. On `q_{w₁}⊗q_{w₂}⊗φ`, the `r` eigenvalues include all three cube roots of one scalar. Theorem 1 makes this scalar the same as for `X_P`. Hence an `r`-equivariant isometry `f` takes `X_P` into that target. Extend `F=1⊗f` by zero. Set `J_P=(F+Ad L_h(F))/√2`. The two ranges lie in the orthogonal sectors `σ*±hex_P`, so `J_P*J_P` is the projection onto the source. `J_P` commutes with the gauge group and is invariant under `r,h`, hence `D₃`.

Transport `J_P` to the other three diagonals. Stabilizer invariance makes transport well defined. Their target sectors are pairwise distinct and differ from `σ*`. Their source spaces for different diagonals are orthogonal: at a corner on one diagonal but not the other they carry `q` versus `L`, either in different types or orthogonal by Theorem 2. The corresponding `E` spaces are also orthogonal to all sources and targets. Let `Π` project onto the sum of the sources and target ranges and put
\[
g_C=1-Π-i\sum_P(J_P+J_P^*).
\]
On each source–target pair this is `−i` times a swap, and it is identity elsewhere. It is unitary, gauge invariant and rotation invariant, and has the stated action. □

## 7. Conjugation and the meaning of R-Φ

**Theorem 7 (band conjugation).** Let `W_B∈GA1-P-5`, let `ω` be an empty-matter D1 state of `U_B=W_BW_EM_oM_e`, and let `G` be a finite product of GA1-P-5 sublayers. Put `W'_B=GW_BW_EG*W_E^{-1}` and `ω'=ω∘Ad G*`. Then `W'_B` is in the class, `ω'` is an empty-matter D1 state, and
\[
\Phi:\pi(A)\Omega\longmapsto\pi'(GAG^*)\Omega'
\]
extends to a unitary intertwining ticks and even translations and mapping `H_phys` onto `H'_phys`. Every band is carried to a band with the same fibre.

*Proof.* The class is closed under the displayed finite product. The local automorphism `Ad G` is invertible and gauge and translation covariant. It preserves empty matter. The GNS inner products are identical after conjugation, and inverse conjugation supplies dense range. On empty matter the new link tick is `G(W_BW_E)G*`; this proves stationarity and tick intertwining. Gauge covariance gives the physical-space claim. □

**Corollary 7.1 (R-Φ is frame dependent).** R-Φ is not invariant under these admissible conjugations. Theorem 3's band lies in `closure π(𝒩)Ω`; its image under `Φ` is Theorem 5's orthogonal band.

*Proof.* Theorem 3's generators are neutral. Theorem 5 proves orthogonality after conjugation. □

The mode has one fibre component, travels only along `−1`, and carries a cube hexagon loop attached to a corner multiplicity flip. It has neither a Clifford cone nor a pair of photon polarizations. It is the conjugate of a neutral band. Thus R-Φ alone does not identify a gauge boson. A frame-independent criterion, or a physical rule fixing the frame, is not proposed here.

## 8. Endpoints and other open targets

**Theorem 8 (oriented sector frozen at `m=1,9`).** Let `m∈{1,9}`. If a finite link region `R` contains the support of a link-only gauge-invariant operator `A`, and `P_R` projects onto `σ→` on all links of `R`, then `AP_R=P_RAP_R`. Consequently, in any empty-matter D1 state carried by `σ→`, `H_phys=closure π(𝒩)Ω`; no nonzero physical band there satisfies R-Φ. Conjugation by a finite product of GA1-P-5 sublayers maps a state carried by `σ→` to a state carried by `σ→`.

*Proof.* Decompose `A` into components with changes `δ_ℓ` of tail numbers. Local centre invariance requires zero divergence of `δ` at each site. In `σ→` every tail is at its maximum: 1 for `m=1`, 5 for `m=9`. A component acting nontrivially on `P_R` therefore has `δ≤0`. A nonzero finite, nonnegative, divergence-free flow `−δ` would contain a directed path continuing indefinitely in positive coordinate directions, impossible in finite support. Thus only `δ=0` acts on `P_R`. Both `P_R` and `P_RAP_R` are local gauge invariant, and the latter commutes with all end numbers. The empty-matter spanning theorem `GA1_5.md` Theorem 6.1 gives the physical-space equality. For sector preservation (referee closure, GA15PHI_REFEREE.md), §3: let `W_s=∏_{X∈ℱ_s}g_X` be a GA1-P-5 sublayer and fix finite `R`. Put `R₁=R∪⋃{X:X∩R≠∅}`, finite by local finiteness, and `G_R=∏_{X∩R≠∅}g_X`. The other gates commute with `P_R`, so `W_s^*P_RW_s=G_R^*P_RG_R`. Every gate in `G_R` is supported in `R₁`; applying the first claim to `g_X` and `g_X^*` shows that each commutes with `P_{R₁}`. Since `P_{R₁}≤P_R`,
\[
P_{R₁}G_R^*P_RG_RP_{R₁}=G_R^*P_{R₁}P_RP_{R₁}G_R=P_{R₁}.
\]
A projection `Q` with `P_{R₁}QP_{R₁}=P_{R₁}` satisfies `Q≥P_{R₁}`. If `ω` is carried by `σ→`, then `ω(W_s^*P_RW_s)≥ω(P_{R₁})=1`. Thus `ω∘Ad W_s^*` is carried by `σ→`; induction gives the finite-product claim. □

The support condition matters.
