## 2. Geometry

**Theorem 2.1 (G1–G3).** Every link belongs to exactly one tiling cube, to two of its faces, and to two mixed plaquettes. At `x`, its three even incident links point along `ε_i e_i`, and the odd ones along `-ε_i e_i`. The site stabilizer in `𝒢` is cyclic of order three; a cube stabilizer is the 24-element proper rotation group. Even faces, odd faces and mixed plaquettes are separate orbits, with respectively 6, 6 and 12 per cell.

At each site pair even axis `i` with odd axis `i` for `P₀`, or with odd axis `i+σs(x) mod 3` for `P_σ`, `σ=±1`. These three pairings are covariant. Their curves are infinite: `P₀` gives lines and `P_{±1}` gives translation-invariant curves.

*Proof.* Along an `i`-link precisely one of the even or odd cube intervals contains both endpoints; the other two coordinates select unique intervals. In each transverse plane, the two incident plaquettes have opposite transverse base parity, so one is a cube face and one mixed. The incident directions follow from the parity of the base.

For a rotation `Q` about `c₀`, fixing the origin up to a `2Z³` translation requires `Q(1,1,1)=(1,1,1)`, leaving the three cyclic permutations. Every proper cube rotation fixes each specified cube after an allowed even translation. Under `Qe_i=δ_i e_{π(i)}`, in-plane base parities follow their axes; the normal parity can be flipped by a half-turn about an in-plane axis. Cyclic permutations and quarter-turns give the stated orbits and counts.

The transformed signs obey `ε_{π(i)}(gx)=δ_i ε_i(x)` and `s(gx)=sign(π)s(x)`, proving covariance of the pairings. For `P₊`, start at the origin along `+e₁`: the next five steps are `−e₃,+e₂,+e₁,−e₃,+e₂`. At `(2,2,−2)` the original local pattern recurs and the curve is translated by that nonzero even vector. Site transitivity and cyclic rotations cover all `P₊` curves; reversing the cyclic shift gives the same result for `P₋`. □

**Theorem 2.2 (G4 and D).** Let `ℱ` be a cell-invariant family of pairwise disjoint finite link sets. At any site, a nonempty intersection of a member with the six-link star is a single link, the even triple or the odd triple. Thus every link-only gauge-invariant gate with such a support commutes with each parity-split charge `q_v^e=∑_{even ℓ∋v}p_{ℓ,v}` and `q_v^o=∑_{odd ℓ∋v}p_{ℓ,v}` on `L5-U`.

*Proof.* The order-three site stabilizer cycles each triple. Disjointness makes its images of a star intersection equal or disjoint. An invariant intersection is either triple or all six; a noninvariant intersection has at most two elements, and a two-element one must contain one of each parity. The latter produces one of the three covariant pairings of Theorem 2.1. Cell transitivity then forces the support containing a link to contain its whole infinite pairing curve. An all-six intersection similarly propagates from neighbour to neighbour and makes the support infinite. Both contradict finiteness.

Gauge invariance gives commutation with `Q_v=N_v+∑_{ℓ∋v}p_{ℓ,v}`. A link-only gate commutes with matter and all ends outside its support, hence with the sum of end numbers in its star intersection. The classification then gives both parity-split commutators. □

## 3. Loops and layers

**Theorem 3.1 (L and P).** Every ordered closed-walk operator `W(c)` is gauge invariant and is carried by a cell lift to `W(gc)`. For a plaquette, `W(c⁻¹)=W(c)†` on `L5-U`; on `L5-O`, `W(c)` is Hermitian and `W(c⁻¹)=W(c)`. The proposed class defines gauge-invariant, cell-covariant quasi-local automorphisms and contains `W^{cube}` for all real `θ_e,θ_o`.

*Proof.* At each visit to a vertex the head and tail indices transform by inverse site actions; their contraction is invariant. The link-reversal formulas in §1 give the lift identity. Plaquette links are distinct and commute across links; reversing their ordered product proves the adjoint identities, with four minus signs on `L5-O`. Thus `B_p` is independent of boundary orientation. Locally finite commuting finite-support gates define a local automorphism by the finite product of gates meeting the observable; each gate commutes with gauge transformations. Covariance follows from the invariant family and gate assignment. Cube link sets within a tiling are disjoint, and cube rotations permute the six `B_p`; therefore the cube layer satisfies the class conditions. □

The mixed plaquettes form one orbit, and every link meets two of them. A disjoint-support sublayer cannot contain one gate on every mixed plaquette. Theorem 2.2 bars a disjoint covariant sublayer from transferring end charge between tilings. Overlapping supports with commuting gates are not ruled out. Nearest-neighbour compilation remains GAP 2.

## 4. Completeness with a neutral algebra

**Theorem 4.1 (C0).** In the region of §1,
\[
H_σ=\bigotimes_{v\in S}\operatorname{Star}_v^σ,\quad
\operatorname{Star}_v^σ=\Lambda^{N_v}\mathbb C^5\otimes\bigotimes_{ℓ\ni v}\Lambda^{p_{ℓ,v}}\mathbb C^5
=\bigoplus_λ V_λ\otimes M^v_λ[σ],
\]
where only region links occur and a fixed fermionic ordering is used. Then
\[
𝒩_R=\bigoplus_σ\bigotimes_{v\in S}\operatorname{End}_{U(5)}(\operatorname{Star}_v^σ),\qquad
\operatorname{End}_{U(5)}(\operatorname{Star}_v^σ)=\bigoplus_λ 1_{V_λ}\otimes\operatorname{End}(M^v_λ[σ]).
\]

*Proof.* Number commutation makes the operator block diagonal in `σ`. The fixed-number exterior-power decomposition gives the site tensor product. Haar averaging over the product group factorizes on elementary tensors, and Schur's lemma gives the displayed site commutants. □

**Theorem 4.2 (CL).** If sectors differing by one move `σ′=σ+e_a−e_b` both contain a site type `λ`, then the `λ`-multiplicity component of `E_ab` between them is nonzero.

*Proof.* `E_ab,E_ba,E_aa−E_bb` form an `sl₂` triple commuting with the site `U(5)`. Fix all other occupations and `p_a+p_b`. On the finite-dimensional `λ` multiplicity space the triple decomposes into irreducible unitary `sl₂` modules. Adjacent weights have nonzero raising maps within each irreducible. For weight `h=p_a−p_b`, if `h≥−1` every summand containing `h+2` contains `h`; if `h≤−1` every summand containing `h` contains `h+2`. Hence the map is respectively onto or injective, and is nonzero when both spaces are nonzero. □

**Theorem 4.3 (C; conditional completeness).** Let `𝒢_R` be the unital *-algebra generated by `𝒩_R`, the plaquette loops wholly in the region and the oriented hop pieces. For a tuple of site types `λ⃗`, let `S(λ⃗)` be the sectors in which every type occurs. Connect two sectors when one plaquette or hop move takes one to the other. Then `𝒢_R=A_R^G` if and only if every nonempty `S(λ⃗)` is connected, **CONN_R**.

*Proof.* In rishon variables a plaquette loop is, up to a fixed sign, the product of its four colour-contracted corner moves `E^v_{out,in}`; an oriented hop is the product of its two matter/end corner moves. Regrouping is valid in the fixed fermion ordering. Each corner is an intertwiner, and Theorem 4.2 makes the component between sectors sharing the relevant site types nonzero. Their tensor product is nonzero.

The commutant of `𝒩_R` consists of operators block diagonal in sector and site-type tuple, arbitrary on `⊗_v V_{λ_v}` and scalar on `⊗_v M^v_{λ_v}[σ]`. Commutation with a nonzero plaquette or hop component equates the arbitrary matrices at adjacent sectors. If CONN_R holds, these matrices depend only on `λ⃗`; they are exactly the product-group algebra. The finite-dimensional double-commutant theorem gives `𝒢_R=A_R^G`. If a graph is disconnected, the projection onto one component of a fixed type commutes with all generators but is not in the product-group algebra, so equality fails. □

For `L5-O[S_+]`, the same Haar argument gives neutral factorization by end chiralities, using `S_+=(16,16)⊕(16′,16′)`; the analogue of CL is open.

**Region-wise counterexample.** On a star containing six links and no plaquette or complete matter hop, gauge-invariant end exchanges supply a noncommutative neutral algebra; no contained plaquette or hop generates it. For example, in a sector with three equal one-rishon end occupations, exchanges of ends `(a,b)` and `(b,c)` act in different orders on three distinct colour labels. This is a star-support convention, distinct from the induced-region convention in Theorem 4.3. It does not decide generation on larger regions.

**CONN attacks.** A1: the draft asserts that single-site moves connect local weights, citing CL and highest weights without giving that connectivity proof; this step is GAP. In any case four simultaneous plaquette corners need not synchronize. A2: even when a specified box has trivial first homology and a difference decomposes into boundaries and hop paths, intermediate sectors must retain every type; that is unproved. A3: the draft invokes Howe duality and a matroid exchange property without rebuilding them; even granting those assertions, local detours alter the other three corners and need synchronization. None proves CONN.

## 5. Coherent magnetic symbols

**Theorem 5.1 (E0, E, E′).** Put `c_m=min(m,10−m)/10`. There is a diagonal-`U(5)`-invariant reference state on `Λ^m C¹⁰` with `ω_ref(U^{jk})=−c_m δ_jk` and `ω_ref(r^{x†}Tr^x)=(m/10)tr T`. Rotating one end independently on every link gives a gauge-covariant product family labelled by `V_ℓ∈U(5)` with
\[
ω_V(U_ℓ)=c_m V_ℓ,\quad ω_V(W(∂p))=c_m^4\operatorname{tr}V_{∂p},\quad ω_V(B_p)=2c_m^4\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}V_{∂p}.
\]
For every function `f`, `ω_V(f(p_x,p_y))` is independent of `V`; all single-end gauge invariants are functions of the end number. With `tr T^aT^b=δ^{ab}/2`, `C₂(Λ^p C⁵)=3p(5−p)/5`; at `m=5`, `(p−5/2)²=25/4−(5/3)C₂`.

*Proof.* For `m≤5`, take the normalized trace on the `m`-particle exterior power of the five orbitals `(e_j^y+e_j^x)/√2`. Its one-body density is `(m/5)` times the projector on that subspace. CAR give the stated negative off-diagonal expectation and the end density `(m/10)1`. For `m≥5`, fill all ten modes except `10−m` holes in the orbitals `(e_j^y−e_j^x)/√2`; its density is `1−((10−m)/5)Π_-` and gives the same formulas. Both states are invariant under simultaneous end rotations.

An end rotation changes the link symbol to any `c_m V` and diagonal invariance makes the family covariant under independent endpoint gauge rotations: in the draft convention `ω_V∘Ad(g)=ω_{V^g}`, with `V^g_ℓ=g_y⁻¹V_ℓg_x`. The plaquette uses four distinct link factors, so the product state factors its expectation. End rotations commute with the number projections; irreducibility of each `Λ^p C⁵` and Schur's lemma give the invariant end algebra. The standard exterior-power Casimir formula `p(5−p)6/10` gives the last identity. □

For smooth Hermitian gauge potentials with links `V_ℓ=exp(iaA_ℓ)` on a fixed box, the plaquette expansion and a Riemann sum give
\[
\sum_p\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}(1-V_{∂p})
=\tfrac a2\int\!d³x\sum_{i<j}\operatorname{tr}F_{ij}²+O(a²).
\]
Indeed `V_{∂p}=exp(ia²F_{ij}+O(a³))`; the trace's linear imaginary term has zero real part. Tiling faces retain half of each orientation in each periodic cell, so their sum has half this leading coefficient. For equal `θ`, the cube generator has field-dependent symbol `−θ c_m⁴(a/2)∫∑tr F²+O(a²)`. A coefficient proportional to `1/a`, with a suitable sign, is needed for a finite nonzero limiting energy. Unequal `θ_e,θ_o` change the coefficient accordingly. This establishes a magnetic quadratic form, not the full electric-plus-magnetic energy.

**Theorem 5.2 (`L5-O` exact symbol).** On `S_+`, the joint eigenstate of the ten commuting `U^{aa}` from `GA2_Y2.md` Theorem 3.4 has link symbol `D=diag(s_a)`, `s_a=±1`, and vanishing end-generator expectations. End rotations give `DRᵀ`, `R∈SO(10)`; product-state plaquette symbols are the ordered products of these matrices, with two backward minus signs. The end-chirality symbol is constant on this family.

*Proof.* The end action rotates the corresponding vector index, while the chirality commutes with Spin(10). Distinct link factors and the product state give the loop symbol; a plaquette has two reverse traversals. □

**GAP (small-field identification on the `L5-O` coset).** If `det D=−1`, every single-link symbol has determinant `−1`, so `V_ℓ=exp(iaA_ℓ)` with `A_ℓ∈so(10)` cannot parametrize it. A flat-background or determinant calculation and its gauge-field dictionary are needed before importing the displayed smooth-field expansion. The draft does not supply them. In both laws, exact symbols do not make the operators unitary or their fluctuations small; `GA2_Y2.md` Theorems 2.1 and 3.2 give the stated finite-register obstructions under their hypotheses.

## 6. Bands and propagation

**Theorem 6.1 (R).** For `L5-U[m]`, let `ω` be a D1 state with `ω(N_x)=0` for all sites and let `𝕍=W_BW_E`. Then `H_phys` is the closed span of link-only gauge-invariant local vectors `π(A)Ω`, and
\[
\widetilde U\,π(A)Ω=π(𝕍^*A𝕍)Ω
\]
for every such `A`.

*Proof.* On a finite region containing the relevant supports, positivity gives `ρ=P₀ρP₀`, where `P₀` projects onto empty matter. Decompose a gauge-invariant local operator `A` by its changes `d_x` in matter numbers. Summing the local `U(1)` Gauss-charge commutators shows `∑d_x=0`, since each supported link has fixed total rishon number. Every component with a negative `d_x` kills `P₀`; every nonzero `d` has a negative entry. Thus `AP₀=P₀AP₀=A_link P₀`, where `A_link` is its vacuum matrix element and is link-only and gauge invariant. This proves the span statement.

Every number-conserving matter hop annihilates empty matter, so the matter cube unitaries and their adjoints act as identity on `P₀`. The link layers preserve `P₀`. Hence `U_B P₀=𝕍P₀` and `P₀ U_B=𝕍P₀`, which gives `(U_B*AU_B−𝕍*A𝕍)P₀=0`. The corresponding GNS vectors coincide. Empty matter times the tracial link state supplies a gauge-invariant stationary D1 state for every member. □

**Theorem 6.2 (F).** If every gate of `W_B` is supported on the links of a single tiling cube, every physical band of an empty-matter D1 state is `V=1` on a connected `C¹` neighbourhood of any node. It does not propagate there.

*Proof.* Every link lies in exactly one cube across both tilings. Thus `𝕍=∏_C𝕍_C` on disjoint link sets, including the electric gates. The gauge-invariant algebra of any finite collection of cubes is finite dimensional and invariant under its adjoint action. By Theorem 6.1 its GNS image is a finite-dimensional `Ũ`-invariant subspace. These subspaces exhaust a dense subspace of `H_phys`, so the physical tick has pure point spectral measure supported on a countable set `Λ`.

For a band with image in `H_phys`, the eigenvalues of `V(K)` lie in `Λ` almost everywhere. The real and imaginary parts of `tr V(K)^r` are `C¹` near the node and take countably many values almost everywhere. A nonzero derivative would make such a function strictly monotone along lines in a small box, contradicting that property by Fubini. They are therefore constant on the connected neighbourhood. Newton identities make the characteristic polynomial constant; at the node all eigenvalues are 1, and a unitary with that spectrum is the identity. □

**Theorem 6.3 (S1, S2, S).** For `m∈{2,4,6,8}` and all allowed matter angles and `τ_E`, the proposed class contains a layer and a locally gauge-invariant D1 state with a band in `H_phys∩H_even`,
\[
V(K)=e^{-iK\cdot(1,1,1)},\qquad N_j=1.
\]
The band propagates under the §1 definition. Its vectors are generated by neutral link operators. This does not prove a propagating flux band or the nearest-neighbour admissibility required by GA1-P.

*Proof.* For each tiling let `a(v)` be the opposite vertex in the cube containing `v`. Point reflection through the cube centre identifies the six link ends at `v` with those at `a(v)`, preserving colour and intertwining their site `U(5)` representations. For a pair of end-number sectors matched by that reflection, decompose their stars into `V_λ⊗M_λ`. On equal-type pairs exchange only the two multiplicity factors, using the reflected identification, and act as identity on the representation factors; act as identity on unequal types or unmatched sectors. This gives a unitary gate on the twelve incident links. It preserves every end number and commutes with both site gauge groups and all other site groups. Gates of one antipodal pairing act on disjoint *end factors* and are even, so they commute even where their link supports overlap. Point reflection is carried to point reflection by the cell group, so the gate family is covariant. The even-cube and odd-cube pairings give two proposed sublayers `S_e,S_o`; set `W_B=S_oS_e`.

In the sector `σ*` with every end number `p=m/2`, the star commutant is noncommutative: exchanges of three equal-occupation end factors commute with `U(5)` but the exchanges `(ab)` and `(bc)` act in different orders on three distinct `p`-subsets of the five colours. Such subsets exist for `p=1,2,3,4`. Thus some type `λ₀` has multiplicity `d≥2`.

The electric layer is scalar on `σ*`, the matter gates fix empty matter, and each swap preserves the product of equal maximally mixed multiplicity states. Thus the state is D1 and stationary.

Choose `a∈End(M_{λ₀})` with `tr a=0` and `tr(a*a)=d`. Let `A_v` be `1⊗a` on that type in sector `σ*`, zero elsewhere. The vectors `u_b=π(A_{2b})Ω` are orthonormal by the star product state. For odd `m`, no cell-invariant *sharp uniform end-number sector* of this form exists: cell symmetries reverse a link and would require both end numbers to be `m/2`. **Theorem 6.4 (T).** Place one pair of Hermitian anticommuting involutions `X_C,Z_C` on each tiling cube, with different cubes commuting. Let `W=W_oW_e`, where each sublayer flips its target cube by `X_C` controlled by parity of a finite covariant set of `Z` operators on opposite-tiling cubes. Then no power `W^n`, `n≥1`, translates a nontrivial finite Pauli product by a nonzero vector `2γ`, even up to sign.

*Proof.* Parity-controlled flips are products of CNOTs. Encode finite `X` products by a pair of Laurent polynomials over `GF(2)`, one for each tiling. The induced matrix `M` has determinant 1. If a nonzero Pauli product translates by `2γ`, at least its `X` or `Z` part is a nonzero finite-support eigenvector of `M^n` or its inverse transpose with eigenvalue `z^γ`. The characteristic equation gives `tr(M^n)=z^γ+z^(−γ)` in either case. Rotations act on the indices with a possible fixed monomial shift on the odd sublattice; covariance conjugates the matrix by that shift, so the trace is invariant. Distinct Laurent monomials are linearly independent, hence `{Qγ,−Qγ}={γ,−γ}` for each proper cubic rotation `Q`. Each order-three body-diagonal rotation must fix `γ`: mapping it to `−γ` is incompatible with its odd order. Fixing all body diagonals forces `γ=0`, a contradiction. □

## 7. Open attacks and consequences

**GA1-5-Φ.** Is there a member of the proposed class and a D1 state with a propagating R-Φ band in `H_phys` on `L5-U[m]`? Attack 1: cube-supported gates fail in every empty-matter D1 state by Theorem 6.2; this does not cover other states. Attack 2: disjoint covariant support cannot move end charge between tilings by Theorem 2.2; commuting overlapping gates and matter remain possible. Attack 3: the one-qubit Clifford construction has no translational Pauli glider by Theorem 6.4; this does not exclude other bands or multiple modes per cube. No compatible equivariant anticommuting-pair construction for the latter is supplied.

**GA1-5-O.** Is there a proposed-class member and a D1 state with a propagating physical band on `L5-O[S_+]`? Attack 1: empty matter is not generally fixed; the Majorana hop can create pairs (`LW1_5.md` B8b-5). Attack 2: link states in the joint pair-creation kernel make empty matter hop-inert, but compatibility of that kernel with a covariant multiplicity-swap construction is not established. The draft's claimed non-invariance of the kernel does not follow merely from the complex structure used to define it. Attack 3: the tracial state is stationary and gauge invariant, but the draft does not show that its Majorana hop dynamics move the proposed multiplicity operator to a single translate. These are failed constructions, not impossibility proofs.

The swap example is conditional on the broad proposed class and says nothing about the VC1 vacuum. Theorem 6.2 does not say every link state is fixed: cube evolution may be nontrivial, although physical bands at nodes are flat. The magnetic symbol is a possible input to Y2-W only with its normalization, state choice, fluctuations and missing electric comparison explicit. No continuum convergence follows.
