## 3. Tools: four lemmas, rebuilt

**Setting S.** Sites are `Z³` with nearest-neighbour links `ℓ = (x, x+e_i)`. Each site carries a finite-dimensional graded matter factor `F_x` (CAR modes, Jordan–Wigner order fixed). Each link carries a Hilbert space `H_ℓ`, an ordinary tensor factor. A compact group `K_x` acts at each site by even unitaries on `F_x` and on the `x`-end of each link at `x`, and trivially elsewhere; actions at distinct sites commute. A finite region `R` is a finite set of matter factors and link factors; `S(R)` is its sites together with all endpoints of its links; `𝔄(R)` is the algebra of `R` (a full matrix algebra when `R` is finite and all factors are finite-dimensional); `𝔄^G(R) = 𝔄(R) ∩ 𝔄^G`. Both live laws are of this form (`LW1_5.md` §2), and so are `L5[SU(2), s]` (`GB1QL.md` §1.5) and the families of section 5.

**Lemma T0 (local commutation; ported).** If `A ∈ 𝔄^G(R)` and `x ∈ S(R)`, then `A` commutes with the in-region part of every gauge unitary at `x`. *Proof.* The gauge unitary at `x` is (in-region part) times (out-of-region part); both are even, the second commutes with `A`, and `A` is fixed by the product (`GB1Z2L5.md` Theorem 2 proof, first paragraph). □ Local invariants are dense in `𝔄^G` (`GB1Z2L5.md` Theorem 1).

**Lemma T1 (extremal witness).** In Setting S, let `T_x ⊆ K_x` be a torus, and decompose `F_x` and each `H_ℓ` into joint weight spaces of the torus generators (for `H_ℓ`, joint weights `(w, w′)` of the `x`-end and `y`-end actions). Let `Ψ = ⊗_x ψ_x ⊗ ⊗_ℓ ψ_ℓ` be a product of joint weight vectors, with weights `w*_x` and `(w*_{ℓ,x}, w*_{ℓ,y})`. Suppose that for each finite `R` there are real linear functionals `λ_x` on the weights (`x ∈ S(R)`) such that:

- (W1) for each matter factor `F_x` in `R`, `w*_x` is the unique maximizer of `λ_x` over the weights of `F_x`, and its weight space is `Cψ_x`;
- (W2) for each link `ℓ = (x, y)` in `R`, `(w*_{ℓ,x}, w*_{ℓ,y})` is the unique maximizer of `λ_x(w) + λ_y(w′)` over the joint weights of `H_ℓ`, and its joint weight space is `Cψ_ℓ`.

Then the vector state of `Ψ`, restricted to `𝔄^G`, is a character.

*Proof.* Fix `R` and let `Ψ_R` be the restriction of `Ψ` to the factors of `R`. Put `Λ_R = Σ_{x∈S(R)} λ_x(G^{in}_x)`, where `G^{in}_x` is the tuple of in-region torus generators at `x`. On a product of weight vectors, `Λ_R` takes the value `Σ_{x} λ_x(w_x) + Σ_{ℓ=(x,y)} (λ_x(w_{ℓ,x}) + λ_y(w_{ℓ,y}))`, sums over matter factors and links of `R`. Each summand is at most its witness value, with equality only at the witness weight, by (W1)-(W2). So the top eigenvalue of `Λ_R` is attained exactly on the tensor product of the witness weight spaces, which is `CΨ_R`. By Lemma T0, every `A ∈ 𝔄^G(R)` commutes with `Λ_R`, so it preserves `CΨ_R`: `AΨ_R = c(A)Ψ_R`. Then `⟨Ψ, ABΨ⟩ = c(A)c(B)` for `A, B ∈ 𝔄^G(R)`, and `c(1) = 1`. Two local invariants lie in a common `𝔄^G(R)`. Multiplicativity and unitality pass to the norm closure by density and continuity of the state. □

**Corollary T1.1 (instances).** Each invariant algebra below has a character.
(a) `L5-U[m]`, every `1 ≤ m ≤ 9` (rebuilds `GB1Z2L5.md` Theorem 2 and `ZM1INV.md` R1). Take `λ_x(e_j) = c_j + ε_R h(x)`, with `0 > c_1 > c_2 > … > c_5`, `h(x) = x_1 + x_2 + x_3`, and `ε_R > 0` small for `R`. (W1) holds at empty matter. On a link the end weights are occupation sets `A, B ⊆ {1,…,5}` with `|A| + |B| = m`; the maximizing multiset takes colours `1, 2, …` twice each. For `m = 2k`, `A = B = {1,…,k}` is the unique maximizer. For `m = 2k+1` the two ends tie on colour `k+1`; the tilt adds `ε_R(h(x)|A| + h(y)|B|)` with `h(y) = h(x) + 1`, so the unique maximizer is `A = {1,…,k}`, `B = {1,…,k+1}`, the oriented state of `ZM1INV.md` R1.
(b) `L5-O[S_+]`: all `c_j < 0`, empty matter, empty links (the even empty vector lies in `S_+`).
(c) The `R_s` register of `GB1QL.md` §1.2 with gauge group `SU(2)` acting on all matter modes (pure gauge, or doublet matter with no spectators), every `s ≥ 1`: take the `J³` weight with `λ = −1` at every site; the doublet maximizer is `n_4 = 0, n_5 = 1`; on a link the unique maximizer is the end-weight pair `(−s/2, −s/2)`, attained only in `H_s`, with a one-dimensional joint weight space.
(d) The spin-`S` U(1) quantum link with nonnegative-number matter (rebuilds `GB1Z2L5.md` Theorem 7).

**Lemma T2 (characterless pieces).** (a) A unital C*-algebra containing self-adjoint unitaries `X, Z` with `XZ = −ZX` has no character. (b) If a unital C*-algebra with no character embeds unitally in `𝔄^G`, then `𝔄^G` has no character. (c) Suppose `𝔄^G` contains operators `F_{ab}`, `1 ≤ a, b ≤ s`, with `F_{ab}* = F_{ba}`, `[F_{ab}, F_{cd}] = δ_{bc}F_{ad} − δ_{ad}F_{cb}`, each `F_{aa}` with integer spectrum, and `Σ_a F_{aa} = n·1`. If `s ∤ n`, `𝔄^G` has no character.

*Proof.* (a) `χ(X)² = χ(Z)² = 1` and `χ(X)χ(Z) = −χ(Z)χ(X)` force `χ(X)χ(Z) = 0`, a contradiction. (b) Compose. (c) A character takes each element into its spectrum: if `χ(A) ∉ spec A`, then `A − χ(A)1` is invertible and its image `0` is invertible, which is false. From the commutator, `χ(F_{aa}) − χ(F_{bb}) = χ([F_{ab}, F_{ba}]) = 0`. So all `χ(F_{aa})` equal one integer `c`, and `sc = n`. □

**Corollary T2.1 (two gauge-singlet modes).** If some site carries two CAR modes on which every `K_x` acts trivially, `𝔄^G` has no character. *Proof.* With Majoranas `γ_1, γ_2` of the first mode and `γ_3` of the second, `X = iγ_1γ_2` and `Z = iγ_2γ_3` are even, gauge invariant, self-adjoint unitaries, and they anticommute. Apply T2(a)-(b). □ One singlet mode is not enough: its even algebra `span{1, n}` is commutative.

**Lemma T3 (infinite registers).** (a) *U(1), Kogut–Susskind link.* Let `H_ℓ = ℓ²(Z)` with `E e_n = n e_n`, `U e_n = e_{n+1}`, the gauge action at the two ends generated by `∓E`, any matter, and a link algebra containing `e^{iθE}` and `U`. Then `𝔄^G` has no character. (b) *SU(2), Kogut–Susskind link.* Let `H_ℓ = L²(SU(2))` with the gauge action `U ↦ h_y U h_x^{-1}` and link algebra `B(H_ℓ)`. Then `𝔄^G` has no character. (c) If `H` is an infinite-dimensional separable Hilbert space, `B(H)` has no tracial state.

*Proof.* (a) `e^{iθE_ℓ}` commutes with every Gauss generator; the oriented plaquette `U_p = U_1U_2U_3*U_4*` is invariant; `e^{iθE_1}U_pe^{−iθE_1} = e^{iθ}U_p`. For `θ ∉ 2πZ` this is `ab = λba` with invertible `a, b` and `λ ≠ 1`; a character would give `χ(a)χ(b)(1 − λ) = 0` with `χ(a), χ(b) ≠ 0`. (This is `GB1Z2L5.md` Theorem 5's pair, now on an infinite register.) (b) Let `a = (−1)^{k_ℓ}`, which acts as `f ↦ f(U_ℓ ↦ −U_ℓ)` because `H_k` consists of degree-`k` harmonic polynomials (`GB1QL.md` §1.1); it commutes with left and right translations, so it is invariant. For a plaquette `p ∋ ℓ`, let `b` be multiplication by `s_p = 1` where `tr X_p ≥ 0` and `−1` elsewhere. It is invariant, since `tr X_p` is. Since `U_ℓ` occurs once in `X_p` and `−1` is central, `aba` is multiplication by `s_p` with `tr X_p` negated, which equals `−s_p` off the Haar-null set `{tr X_p = 0}`. So `aba = −b` in `B(H)`, and Lemma T2(a) applies. (c) `H ≅ H ⊕ H` gives isometries `S_1, S_2` with `S_1S_1* + S_2S_2* = 1`; a tracial state `τ` would give `τ(1) = τ(S_1*S_1) + τ(S_2*S_2) = 2τ(1)`. □

**Remark T3.1 (the convention matters at `s = ∞`).** If instead each link algebra is the unitized compacts `K(H_ℓ) + C1` (a choice in the spirit of the nonunital field algebras of Grundling–Rudolph, arXiv:1108.2129), the map killing `K(H_ℓ)` is a character of each link algebra. Its tensor products are consistent under inclusion, so the pure-gauge link algebra and its invariant subalgebra have a character. So at `s = ∞` "has a character" is decided by a link-algebra convention that no working-set entry fixes. PS-6r fixes finite registers, so the question arises only for a law that changes PS-6r (section 7, text C5).

**Lemma T4 (the centre-parity hypothesis, isolated).** Call (CP) the following property of a law: for each site `z` there is a unitary `c_z`, implementing an element of `K_z` on `F_z` and the `z`-ends, such that `Ad c_z` negates every odd matter operator at `z`, fixes all other matter, `Ad(c_z²)` is the identity, `c_{z+2β}` is the translate of `c_z`, and, for every link `ℓ = (x, y)`, `c_x c_y` acts on `H_ℓ` as a scalar. Under (CP), and for updates and D1 states in the sense of `LZ1.md` §1, the conclusions of `LZ1.md` Theorems 2.1-2.2 hold: every band in the odd sector is identically `1` on a connected `C¹` neighbourhood of any node.

*Proof.* (CP) is exactly what `LZ1.md` §1 uses: "the product of their adjoint actions over a finite set containing all matter sites and both ends of all links in its support equals matter parity θ: each matter generator gets one sign, while each allowed link operator gets an even number." If `c_xc_y` is scalar on `H_ℓ`, `Ad(c_xc_y)` fixes every link operator. The rest of `LZ1.md` Theorems 2.1-2.2 uses only the commuting involutive adjoint actions, their tick invariance (gauge invariance of the update), their translation covariance and the D1 state. □ Instances: `L5-U[m]`, where `c_z = e^{iπQ_z}` and `c_xc_y = e^{iπm}` on `Λ^m C^{10}`; `L5-O[S_+]`, where the two end actions of `−1` multiply to a scalar (`RCSPO.md` Theorem 1 proof, citing `CG16D.md` Theorems 2-3).

**Proposition 1.2 (V1c, independent ends).** Let `L5-U[•]` be `L5-U` with each link register the full rishon Fock space `Λ(C⁵_x ⊕ C⁵_y)` (all rishon numbers `0,…,10`; the hop and Gauss generators of `LW1_5.md` §2 unchanged). ### 4.4 Row 4: the cube-face factorization

**What the proof uses.** `GB1ISO.md` Theorem 2.1: "Each link belongs to one cube in 𝒞 ... Thus, on empty matter, `U_KS=⊗_C(e^{-iθB_C}e^{-iτ_EA_C})`" (features 4a, 4b, 4c). **Definition 4.1 (the swap layer on `R_s`).** Fix `s ≥ 1`. Let `Σ*` be the sector in which every link lies in `H_1 = V ⊗ V` (`V ≅ C²`, the two end factors). For a tiling `T` and an antipodal pair `{v, a(v)}` of a cube of `T`, put `t = a(v) − v ∈ {±1}³` and identify the end factors of `star(v) = ⊗_{ℓ∋v} V^{(ℓ,v)}` with those of `star(a(v))` by the translation `τ_t` (it maps `(v, v ± e_i)` to `(a(v), a(v) ± e_i)` with orientation kept, and acts as the identity on internal factors). Decompose `star(v) = ⊕_λ V_λ ⊗ M_λ` under `SU(2)_v`. The gate `S_{v,a(v)}` acts, on the block where all twelve links of the two stars lie in `H_1`, by exchanging the multiplicity factors `M_λ` of the two stars through this identification and by the identity on the `V_λ` factors and on all other factors; off that block it is the identity. Let `S_T = ∏_{pairs of T} S_{v,a(v)}` and `W^{sw} = S_oS_e`. This is the construction of `GA1_5.md` Theorem 6.3 and Proposition S-odd (translation identification), transcribed to `R_s`.

*Properties.* (i) Each gate is unitary and commutes with every local `SU(2)` action: on the block it acts only on multiplicity factors, and the block projection is a function of the `E²_ℓ`. (ii) Gates of one sublayer commute: two gates sharing a link act on different end factors of it inside the joint block, and each preserves every `E²_ℓ`; off the joint block one of them is the identity. (iii) Cell covariance: a translation or a rotation `g` about the cell centre maps cubes of `T` to cubes of `T` and antipodes to antipodes. ## 6. LW2-G, separate branch: a fixed register with internal structure

### 6.1 Three readings of "internal structure", and the one worked here

(i) *Many flavours at fixed `s ≥ 2`.* The register of `LU[s, m]` with `s` fixed: each end carries a flavour index on which no site frame acts. (ii) *An internal chain.* The flavour slots of one end form a chain, and the law adds gauge-invariant internal moves `Σ_j(r^{x†}_{j,a}r^x_{j,a+1} + h.c.)` along it. Each move commutes with `U(5)_x` (the colour index is contracted), so (ii) is (i) with an internal update; every result below is kinematic and holds for both. A chain that joins the two ends must pass a rishon from a slot carrying the colour of `x` to one carrying the colour of `y`, which is a link-matrix entry `U^{jk}`; so a connecting chain adds nothing beyond (i). (iii) *D-theory proper*: an extra direction of extent `β`, with reduction when the correlation length is much larger than `β` (`LIT_QLM.md` §§1, 5, 11). With the register fixed, `β` is fixed, and the reduction is not a limit inside the law; `LIT_QLM.md` §11 already records: "The finite local register does not remove the growing extra extent L′." Reading (iii) also turns sites into columns, which changes PS-5. This file works (i)-(ii) and names (iii) and the q-deformed registers (section 6.4) without working them.

### 6.2 The same questions for (i)-(ii)

**Theorem I1 (fixed internal flavours).** Fix `s ≥ 2` and `1 ≤ m ≤ 10s − 1`, and let the law be `LU[s, m]`, with or without internal chain moves. Then:

*Proof.* (a), (b), (d), (e) are the cited results. (c), "if": for `m = 2qs`, Lemma T1's witness (colours `1,…,q` full at both ends) uses a site-independent functional, so a cell lift, which carries the gauge action at `x` to that at its image with the same group element and acts on links by colour- and flavour-preserving rishon permutations, maps the top weight space to itself; the witness is fixed up to a phase. "Only if": the end-flavour operators `F^X_{ab} = Σ_j r^{X†}_{j,a}r^X_{j,b}` of one end commute with both end actions and with matter, so they lie in `𝔄^G`; they satisfy the `gl(s)` relations, each `F^X_{aa}` has integer spectrum, and `Σ_aF^X_{aa} = p_{ℓ,X}`. ### 6.3 What the internal branch buys, and what it costs

With fixed internal flavours the law escapes exactly one family of negatives: the character obstruction and the steps that consume a fine character (row 2 and row 3's first step), and only when `s ∤ m`. It keeps the trace obstruction, the parity mechanism behind flat odd bands, Lemma 5 of the Clifford route, and the cube-face factorization of the magnetic layer. The cost is structure: a flavour index on which no site frame acts and whose generators `F_{ab}` are exact local invariants. Its reason inside the law is only the escape itself; section 7 grades this under test 4. A second cost: when `s ∤ m` the invariant algebra contains the non-commutative flavour algebra of each link, so any readout target (GB1's Z₂ source, a coarse register) must also absorb these local degrees of freedom. Whether that helps or hurts a readout is not decided here.

### 6.4 Branches named, not worked

- **D-theory proper (reading (iii)).** Changes PS-5 (columns of sites) or PS-1 (an extra direction); its reduction needs a correlation length much larger than a fixed `β`, which is a vacuum property, not a register property. Not worked.
- **q-deformed registers.** Zache–González-Cuadra–Zoller (arXiv:2304.02527) regularize Kogut–Susskind by "deforming the defining symmetry algebra to a quantum group" `SU(2)_k`, with finite local spaces and convergence to Kogut–Susskind as `k → ∞`. PS-6 speaks of a frame *group* acting on registers; a quantum-group symmetry would need a new reading of PS-6. Not worked; recorded as LW2-QDEF.
