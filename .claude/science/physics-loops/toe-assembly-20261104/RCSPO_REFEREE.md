**G2. Proposition 4.4's commutation (RCSPO line 7, line 101). *Proof of the closure.* Let `ω^{(z)}_ℓ∈Spin(20)` be the product over the five planes `(2j−1,2j)` of the `z`-end of the rotation by `π`, so that `w_{ℓ,z}:=ρ_ℓ(ω^{(z)}_ℓ)=∏_je^{iπL^{2j−1,2j}_{ℓ,z}}`.
