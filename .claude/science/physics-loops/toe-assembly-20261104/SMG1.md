## 3. Quadratic obstruction and minimality

**Theorem 3.1.** On a ring `Z_L`, `L≥2`, let `Q=(i/4)Σ A_{xα,yβ}γ_{x,α}γ_{y,β}+const`, with real antisymmetric `A`, be invariant under global `S` and one-site translation. Then `A_{xa,yβ}=0` for `a≤8`, `β≠a`; each eigenspace of `Q` has dimension divisible by 16. On `Z`, for finite-range translation-invariant `A`, every one of these eight flavour symbols has zeros at `k=0,π`.

*Proof.* Each quadratic transforms by `ρ_αρ_β`, with `ρ_9=ρ_10=1`. The eight `ρ_a` are distinct and nontrivial, so only equal flavours survive among terms involving them. Translation invariance and antisymmetry make each row sum of the flavour matrix zero. Consequently `Γ^a=Σ_xγ_{x,a}` commutes with `Q` and `{Γ^a,Γ^b}=2Lδ_ab`. The eight normalized operators generate a nonzero quotient of `Cl₈(C)≅M₁₆(C)`, hence `M₁₆(C)`, because that algebra is simple, so each invariant eigenspace is a sum of 16-dimensional modules. For the infinite chain, the symbol of `iA` is `−2Σ_{r>0}A_{0a,ra}sin(kr)`, proving both zeros. \(\square\)

The symmetry alone does not forbid same-flavour intersite bilinears. One-site translation is essential: the dimerized hop `Σ_x (1+(−1)^x)(i/2)γ_{x,a}γ_{x+1,a}` is `S`-invariant and gaps that flavour. The alternating-sign form `Σ_x (−1)^x(i/2)γ_{x,a}γ_{x+1,a}` in O304 is gapless on `Z` (referee closure, SMG1_REFEREE.md). Translation of the **generator** is the hypothesis; the two ordered hopping layers need not give a one-site-translation-invariant tick.

**Theorem 3.2 (restricted minimum).** For `ν=2n>0` Majoranas, let `U⊂F₂^ν` consist of even words and act by the corresponding sign flips. If it forbids all nonzero quadratic operators and an invariant Hermitian operator has a unique ground vector, then `ν≥8`. The construction of Theorem 2.2 attains eight.

*Proof.* A unique ground vector is a common eigenvector of all implementers. Anticommuting implementers cannot share such a vector; hence `U⊂U⊥` and `k=dim U≤ν/2`. The coordinate functionals `s_a∈U*` are distinct, otherwise the corresponding quadratic is invariant. Self-orthogonality says `Σ_a s_as_aᵀ=0`. For `ν=2` the distinct functionals fill `F₂`, whose sum has nonzero diagonal. For `ν=4` they fill `F₂²`, whose sum has nonzero off-diagonal. ## 4. Product tick and Floquet spectrum

**Theorem 4.1.** At `θ=0`, on a finite segment or ring, `U=e^(−iH₀)` and `H₀` has unique ground vector `Ψ=⊗_x(ψ_x⊗|0⟩_{5,x})⊗⊗_ℓ|+⟩_ℓ`, with gap at least `Δ₀`. It is gauge- and `S`-invariant. *Proof.* The terms are even, commute, are nonnegative, and have unique local kernels. Their individual gaps are `14τ`, `τ₅`, and `τ_E`. If `p_j` are their kernel projections, `H₀≥Δ₀Σ_j(1−p_j)≥Δ₀(1−∏_jp_j)`. The kernel product is the projection onto `Ψ`. All factors are fixed by their gauge and symmetry actions. For the infinite ground-state uniqueness, choose a local lowering matrix unit `A=|κ⟩⟨e|` for any factor with excitation energy `λ>0`. All other terms commute with it, so `ω(A*[H₀,A])=−λ ω(|e⟩⟨e|)≥0`. The product GNS space is spanned by finite excitations, whose energies are sums of the local excitation energies. This gives the gap and simple kernel. Each local gate fixes the product state, so the tick does too. \(\square\)

**Lemma M** (referee closure, SMG1_REFEREE.md). On the 1D carrier, let `S` act by `γ_g` on modes 1-4 of every site and `U(1)₅` by `Ad e^{iφN₅}`, `N₅ = Σ_x n_{x,5}`. Let `𝔐` be generated by the mirror Majoranas `γ_{x,a}` (`a ≤ 8`) and `𝔏` by `γ_{x,9}, γ_{x,10}`. (i) Every local operator invariant under `S × U(1)₅` is a finite sum `Σ_i M_iL_iΛ_i`, with `M_i ∈ 𝔐` `S`-invariant, `L_i ∈ 𝔏` `U(1)₅`-invariant and `Λ_i` a link operator. (ii) A non-constant `S`-invariant mirror monomial has degree at least 2, and degree 2 only for a same-flavour pair `γ_{x,a}γ_{y,a}`. (iii) A non-constant `U(1)₅`-invariant light monomial (in `c_{x,5}, c†_{x,5}`) has even degree at least 2. (iv) Hence genuine mixing (`M_i` and `L_i` both non-constant) has degree at least 4, and degree 4 only as a same-flavour mirror pair times a number-conserving light pair.

*Proof.* (i) Each `g_x` is even and lives on modes 1-4, so `γ_g` fixes `𝔏` and the links; `N₅` is even and lives on mode 5, so `Ad e^{iφN₅}` fixes `𝔐` and the links. The averages `E_S = 16^{−1}Σ_gγ_g` and `E_U = (2π)^{−1}∫dφ Ad e^{iφN₅}` therefore commute and act on each factor of a product `mlλ` of monomials separately. An invariant `X` equals `E_SE_U(X) = Σ E_S(m)E_U(l)λ`. (ii) `γ_g` multiplies a mirror monomial by `(−1)^{u·f}`, where `u` is the support of `g` and `f ∈ F₂⁸` counts the factors of each flavour mod 2. Invariance for all `g` means `f ∈ C^⊥ = C`, whose words have weight 0, 4 or 8. Degree 1 or 3 would need a word of weight 1 or 3; degree 2 needs `f = 0`. (iii) Invariance means equal numbers of `c` and `c†`. (iv) follows. ∎
