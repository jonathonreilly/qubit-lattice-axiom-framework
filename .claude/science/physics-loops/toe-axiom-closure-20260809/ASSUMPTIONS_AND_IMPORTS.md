# Assumptions and Imports

## First-principles reset

Canonical `A_min` is Lattice, Qubit, Admissibility, and Record. The August 5
Admissibility wording supplies a nearest-neighbor-determined and
nearest-neighbor-varying probability distribution over local possibilities;
it does not supply the distribution's form or values. Qubit supplies the
one-site algebraic possibility structure. Neither sentence identifies
possibilities with effects, provides an effect grading, or declares an
eligible menu family.

The approved scale-reference, kinetic-isotropy, and realized-state primitives
are registered foundation content. None is load-bearing in Block 1's finite
one-site mathematics, and none supplies a probability selector.
