# Goal

At the supplied reflected twenty-two-edge gravity action and spatial momentum
`(pi/2,0,0)`, construct the actual finite boundary state of the odd
gauge-bordered Laurent pencil and decide whether it admits a positive,
source-faithful one-step or even-step transfer.

## Exact target contract

| field | contract |
|---|---|
| Target statement | Separate zero/infinite endpoint chains from finite dynamical modes, construct the stable TT response realization, and either exhibit a positive Hermitian boundary metric preserving the same TT source/readout response or give a finite infeasibility certificate. |
| Quantifiers/domain | The supplied reflected action at `mu=1/1024`, odd `y/z` reflection sector, and spatial momentum `(pi/2,0,0)` only. |
| Allowed premises | The four current axioms, the supplied Block-74 action and maps, the Block-181 polynomial/residue computation, and standard finite-dimensional linear algebra. |
| Forbidden weakenings | Inspect-and-select the desired root; silently change the action, source, observable, moments, contour, or inner product; infer a result for Block 180's distinct nonlinear action. |
| Required edge cases | Zero roots introduced by clearing Laurent denominators, infinite roots from the singular leading coefficient, all finite nonzero roots, source-dark versus source-visible modes, one-step and even-step positivity. |
| Completion witness | Explicit descriptor/rank data and a reproduced positive metric with residual bounds, or a dual/eigenvalue/residue certificate showing the metric equations are infeasible at the declared scope. |
| Does not count | Another raw momentum scan, a restatement of Block 181's Hankel signs, an axiom edit, a TOE score change without obligation retirement, or an untested physical interpretation. |

The search is neutral. Gravity, other actions, other momenta, nonlinear
backgrounds, continuum limits, and exact spatial blocking remain outside this
contract unless separately computed.

## Outcome

The descriptor endpoint split is `(7,14,7)` and the scalar response has no
zero/infinite endpoint pole. The fixed three-mode TT response fails the
positive H-self-adjoint metric problem at one and two steps, but admits an
explicit positive non-self-adjoint strict-contraction metric. The exact target
is therefore completed with a narrowly qualified infeasibility certificate
and a constructive counterexample to broader negative wording.
