# Independent Panel Return

Three independent roles challenged the campaign: scalar/cyclic mathematics,
physical Ward/source typing, and TOE portfolio ranking.

Consensus:

1. The signed TT residues nevertheless define a unique strictly positive
   scalar Herglotz measure. The exact cyclic unitary is infinite dimensional;
   finite all-power realization fails narrowly.
2. The full action-derived edge matrix is the higher-value object. Global
   rank-five positivity and the explicit Ward line promote the route from a
   scalar fit to a conserved-source stationary representation.
3. Paired Weyl gluing materially closes provenance: the action, reflection,
   and declared cross-Ward border select the full-line moments.
4. The full six-coordinate KKT extension is slice dependent. Only the
   conserved rank-five source form is gauge-invariant; only three constant
   one-time currents are conserved at every frequency.
5. Bilateral Toeplitz positivity is not OS transfer, positive energy,
   quantization, or Record dynamics. Full-circle spectrum and nonzero
   covariance tails make that boundary explicit.
6. The highest next investment is momentum/sector and IR extension, with a
   separate quantum/Record dynamics attack. Scalar metric/dilation scans are
   now low leverage.

The panel materially changed the campaign twice: it first demoted an abstract
scalar defect-space dilation, then reranked the action-derived full matrix to
first place and narrowed the physical source claim after the transverse-slice
counterexample.
