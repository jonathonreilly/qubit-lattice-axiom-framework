# Literature Bridges

No external literature result is used as a proof premise.  The terminology
“Dirac--Kahler,” “DeWitt,” “Fierz--Pauli,” “Ward,” and “Osterwalder--Schrader”
labels algebraic structures whose relevant identities are either executed by
the runner or explicitly left open.

Before any future literature bridge is promoted, it must be translated into a
finite lattice equation, assigned an authority status, and checked at the
same generic and exceptional momentum strata as the local theorem.
