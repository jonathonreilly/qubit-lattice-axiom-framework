  exact unresolved compatible-positive-involution target.
- Block 114 supplies an exact positive reflection-real involution as a control,
  not as evidence that the added temporal parity constraint is satisfiable.

  rational/algebraic residuals and inertia certificates carry the claim.

  four-dimensional lift remain open interfaces and are not inferred from a
  finite-carrier positive matrix.
