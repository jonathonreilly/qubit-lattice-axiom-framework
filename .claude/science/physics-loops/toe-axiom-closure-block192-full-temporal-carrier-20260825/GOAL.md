# Goal

Close the first empty Block-191 arrow by constructing one temporal history
carrier that contains both frozen temporal sectors, acts on the full
16-dimensional exterior fiber, intertwines the exact Block-190 action at every
incoming and outgoing endpoint, and is compatible with reflection.

The target chain is

`full action -> temporal mode injection -> reflected positive history -> fixed grade-three conditioned source -> actual conditioned Gram -> adjacent write`.

The carrier tournament is frozen before target evaluation:

1. the minimal uniform periodic circle containing both momenta (`L=24`);
2. one four-dimensional real antisymmetric temporal complex with exact
   frequencies `sin(pi/6)=1/2` and `sin(pi/4)=sqrt(2)/2`;
3. a continuous-frequency reflected kernel if both finite routes fail.

This order was bound by the independent panel after the route set was frozen
and before any Block-192 target residual was evaluated.

The frozen source is Block 191's grade-three PVM and connected response.  It
may not be replaced, rephased, or reselected to rescue a carrier.  The carrier
may be designed on D1 and H1, one point from each temporal sector.  D2, D3,
H2, and the new cross-sector point X1 are held out.

X1 is frozen as incoming momentum
`(pi/6, pi/4, 0, pi/6)` and transfer
`(pi/3, pi/6, pi/2, pi/12)`, so its outgoing temporal momentum is `pi/4`.
It tests an actual transition between the two temporal sectors.

Success requires all of the following in one route: exact full-fiber mode
intertwiners at every endpoint; a single reflection mapping each mode to its
reverse; an action-derived positive history for every fixed outcome; recovery
of the frozen connected response as the conditioned-versus-unconditioned
metric derivative; exact total Ward/recoil; and congruence of that actual
conditioned Gram through the adjacent write isometry.  Equal normalized
density matrices, a direct sum with no cross-sector transition, or a chosen
positive sign alone do not count.

Block success would establish a candidate source/history/write vertical
slice.  It would not derive Born probabilities, select the realized outcome,
prove permanent Record dynamics, retire a TOE obligation, or amend an axiom.
Those remain separately gated.
