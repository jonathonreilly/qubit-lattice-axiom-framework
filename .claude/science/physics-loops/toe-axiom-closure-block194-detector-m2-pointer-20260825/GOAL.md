# Goal

Block 194 tests the shortest currently available path from the exact Block-193
source response to a physical event instrument and an explicit binary pointer.
It does not choose a connector from the measured tangent.

Let the frozen grade-three event projectors be `E_st`, generated by commuting
Hermitian involutions `O1,O2`.  On the D4 form carrier define the bounded real
detector family

`J(n) = i Gamma_t sum_{a=1}^3 n_a Gamma_a^+`, `n in R^3`.

The family is fixed before response.  `n` is a covariant detector one-form,
not a direction claimed to arise from the symmetric vacuum.  First solve the
linear nondemolition constraints

`[J(n),O1]=[J(n),O2]=0`.

The campaign continues only if the real solution space has dimension exactly
one.  Normalize the resulting ray so `J^2=I`; its sign is only the
`+ <-> -` pointer relabelling.  Require Hermiticity, temporal-reflection
oddness, proper-cubic covariance of the pair `(event context,n)`, and no
response data in the classification.

For the unique ray define `K_st=E_st J=J E_st` and the sector involution

`S_J = [[0,J],[J,0]]`.

Its projectors `P_+/-=(I +/- S_J)/2`, refined by the `E_st`, must reproduce a
complete positive eight-projector instrument.  Add a pointer `M2` with
`Q_+/-=(I +/- Z)/2` and freeze the controlled writer

`W_J=P_+ tensor I_2 + P_- tensor X`.

Starting from the `Q_+` pointer state, joint readout `(E_st,Q_sigma)` must
reproduce the eight instrument probabilities exactly.  Require
`W_J^dagger W_J=I`, `Q_+Q_-=0`, a nonidentity pointer flip on the minus branch,
and static readability.  This is a one-step code, not yet autonomous
formation or permanent Record dynamics.

Only then contract the same Block-193 propagated TT tangents at the frozen
phase-zero contact `x=x_source`.  The discovery gate is real normalized TT
rank two at both D1 and H1.  Any failure stops the family.  There is no scan of
alternate `K`, polar response directions, phases, positions, detector axes,
sources, event partitions, or normalizations.  Held-outs D2/D3/H2/X1 open
only after the discovery conjunction passes, and they must pass without refit.

A positive result closes detector selection plus one-step binary realization;
it still does not close formation, permanence, physical time, Born forcing,
or an end-to-end TOE lane.  A negative result is bounded to this degree-two
timelike/spatial detector family and must preserve the Riesz/intertwiner and
other physical detector families under N1--N8.
