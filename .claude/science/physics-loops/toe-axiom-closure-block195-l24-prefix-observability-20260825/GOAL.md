Block 195 tests whether the exact one-step Block-194 detector can become a
complete two-polarization observable over the already fixed L24 history.  It
does not assume that the Euclidean action is a physical time-evolution law.

`A = I_24 tensor (m I + i S_sp) + D tensor Gamma_t`

and its ordinary-transpose reflected construction.  Using only that action,
its declared reflection, the fixed Block-194 event PVM and M2 dilation, and
the literal Block-193 source deformation, derive all of the following as one
typed object:

1. a named physical system space and normalized initial state;
2. one action-selected completely positive instrument for each prescribed
   L24 crossing, with eight outcomes `(s,u,sigma)` and
   `sum_alpha M_alpha^dagger M_alpha = I`;
3. exact cylinder probabilities for every prefix, with coarse-graining of a
   later outcome returning the earlier prefix probability;
4. fresh M2 cells whose earlier orthogonal labels are unchanged by every
   later update; and
5. covariance under the already fixed temporal reflection and proper-cubic
   transformations.

An OS quotient or transfer construction counts only if its positive form,
null-space descent, translation action, normalization, and uniqueness up to
declared physical equivalence are derived.  The campaign may not set
`U=exp(-i H Delta t)`, choose a Wick rotation, import a transfer matrix,
select a ready state from the desired answer, or assume that one Euclidean
lattice step is physical time.  Multiple inequivalent compatible channels
fail the selection clause just as surely as no channel.

If any dependency clause fails, stop before all source-response and history
word calculations.  Package only the exact dependency boundary, apply current
No-Go Discipline before negative wording, and pivot to the frozen
Regge-D4-L24 intertwiner feasibility gate.

## Full-Period Observability Gate

Only after the dependency gate passes, freeze the derived channel, the
every-step L24 cadence selected by the action, the Block-194 detector ray and
pointer code, and the literal source.  For every complete outcome word `w`,
derive

`p_X(w;h) = Tr I_w,rho_X(h)`

and its two exact source tangents at `h=0`.  Stack every word and every fixed
event/pointer label; no favorable word may be selected.  An exact compressed
recursion is allowed only with a proof that it equals the full cylinder law.
The two-column response matrix must have real rank two at both D1 and H1,
equivalently a strictly positive exact two-by-two response Gram determinant.

There is one horizon, `L=24`, and one cadence.  No alternate horizon, cadence,
history subset, detector, axis, event context, phase, source, or response
weight may be tried after seeing the result.  A failure at either discovery
point seals D2/D3/H2/X1.

If discovery and held-outs all pass, quantify exactly which local Record
formation and persistence clauses are now instantiated and which still need
physical input.  No axiom amendment, obligation retirement, retained status,
or TOE percentage movement is preregistered.
