# No-Go Ledger

At registration, the only negative is known exploratory input: a globally
invertible single-cover gauge-carrier equivalence with the frozen link/vertex
placements is rank deficient at `z_t=-1`.  It is not a no-go for:

- the preregistered doubled/half-lattice placement carrier;
- larger-support or larger-orientation induced carriers;
- a different independently derived cell complex or gauge placement;
- a noninvertible chain map with an independently justified quotient;
- full OS/GNS/CAR or process-tensor reconstruction;
- gravity, Born forcing, Records, the minimal axioms, or the TOE.

If the full graded target fails, N1 and N7 remain presumptively failed while any
of those mechanisms is live.  The allowed negative disposition is only
`partial-attempt-with-named-untested-routes` unless a later complete N1--N8
packet proves otherwise.

The additional temporal-only-cover obstruction is equally narrow: it closes
only a cover graded in `t` while leaving spatial link/vertex placements on the
coarse torus.  It is not evidence against the frozen full 16-grade target.
