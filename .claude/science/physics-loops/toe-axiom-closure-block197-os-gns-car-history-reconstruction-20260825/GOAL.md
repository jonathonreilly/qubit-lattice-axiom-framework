# Goal

Block 197 tests the earliest load-bearing gate in the panel-selected route to
Block 195's missing `W2` obligation: an action-selected inter-crossing history
law.  It asks whether the literal Block-192 periodic `L=24`, `m=2/7`
fermionic action has the reflection-positive Berezin seed required for an
OS/GNS/CAR reconstruction before attempting a quotient, transfer, event
factor, or CPTP law.

## Exact target contract

**Target statement.**  Starting only from the frozen Block-192 action,
reflection, first-half cut, and Block-194 event data, construct the exact
positive-time degree-one Berezin form.  Require reflection covariance,
Hermiticity, and positive semidefiniteness on both adjacent physical plane
classes.  If it passes, continue in order through null-ideal descent,
positive composed translation, CAR/Fock reconstruction, an exact
`C^32` Block-194 event-factor intertwiner, and a unique action-derived CPTP
history law.  If it fails, stop at the first exact principal-minor witness and
test the preregistered convention/spin-structure repairs.

**Domain.**  Exact arithmetic over the algebraic field generated by the
frozen radii.  The decisive discovery sector is the actual radius-one sector
among Block 192's nine endpoint radii.  The temporal carrier has 24 sites and
the positive half has 12 sites.

**Allowed premises.**  Literal tracked inputs from Blocks 190, 192, 194, and
195; the finite-circle Grassmann algorithms and free infinite-time CAR/Fock
identities are method templates only.  They do not supply positivity for the
Block-192 carrier.

**Forbidden weakenings.**  No inserted channel, boundary density matrix,
Kraus complement, cadence, detector axis, fitted reflection phase, response-
dependent enlargement, replacement of the Berezin form by the old Schur-
graph Gram, or relabeling a finite-circle correlation as a semigroup.  A
parity-doubled carrier and a global process tensor remain separate route
families rather than silent repairs.

**Required edge cases.**  Both adjacent plane classes, both global real
reflection signs, radius zero as a control, the actual radius-one discovery
sector, the antiperiodic-plus-transported-seam repair, and exact compatibility
of a scalar circle twist with both frozen temporal momenta `pi/6` and `pi/4`.

**Completion witness.**  Passing Gates T1--T5 below, including a unique Choi
equivalence class and fixed event-factor/source descent.  A positive Gram,
one compatible channel, or an untyped Hilbert contraction does not count.

## Gates and stop rules

### T1 -- literal finite-circle Berezin seed

For radii zero and one and adjacent plane classes `j=0,1`, build the exact
phase-real action from `temporal_matrices(24)`, the plane-shifted frozen
reflection, and the corresponding 12-site embedding.  Compute the degree-one
positive-time form directly from the covariance.  Require exact action
covariance, Hermiticity, and PSD.  Certify PSD by exact LDL or all decisive
principal minors; certify failure by one exact negative principal minor or
negative exact vector norm.  Independently reproduce the disclosed radius-one
witness without importing its value.

Any exact failure at an actual Block-192 radius fires the stop before quotient,
translation, event factor, source response, or held-outs.

### T2 -- convention and plane battery

On T1 failure, test both adjacent plane classes, both orientations of the
positive half, and the only global real signs that preserve Hermiticity.
Show algebraically whether a global sign can change the decisive even-order
minor.  Verify that the old right-Schur Gram remains positive while the
Berezin form fails, so the two objects are not conflated.

This gate is not an exhaustive theorem over arbitrary internal reflection
completions or doubled carriers.

### T3 -- spin-structure repair and carrier compatibility

Reconstruct the known antiperiodic-wrap plus transported-seam reflection on
`L=24` and check the local/degree-one positive forms on both adjacent plane
classes.  Test periodic wrap plus transported seam and antiperiodic wrap plus
uniform phase as negative controls.

Then derive, without numerical fitting, the scalar boundary twist required by
each frozen temporal mode.  A common scalar twist `tau` must satisfy
`tau=exp(-i k L)` for both `k=pi/6` and `k=pi/4`.  Determine whether any
integer `L` can make the common twist antiperiodic; specialize to `L=24`.
Changing the twist or carrier is a repair candidate, not a closure of the
literal Block-192 target.

### T4 -- conditional reconstruction

Only if T1 passes: prove null-ideal invariance; reconstruct positive composed
translations from independently calculated multi-lag forms; establish the
all-grade Wick/CAR-Fock identity; exhibit an exact invariant `C^32` factor
carrying all eight Block-194 rank-four effects, reflection labels, proper-
cubic action, and source tangent; derive the Choi/Kraus law and prove CP, TP,
causal prefix identities, and uniqueness modulo declared equivalence.

Any failure or inequivalent completion stops before response.

### T5 -- response and held-outs

Only after T1--T4 pass may D1/H1 be opened and required to reproduce the
registered TT rank-two response without refit.  Only then may D2/D3/H2/X1 be
opened.  Earlier failure leaves all response data sealed.

## Claim boundary

Failure of T1 would prove only that the literal frozen periodic/uniform
Block-192 carrier does not seed the declared OS/GNS/CAR construction.  It is
not a no-go for a parity-doubled carrier, open/infinite time, a different spin
structure with a rebuilt source carrier, an action-derived global process
tensor, centered-symbol gravity, axioms, or the TOE.  No axiom amendment,
obligation retirement, retained status, or TOE percentage movement is
preregistered.
