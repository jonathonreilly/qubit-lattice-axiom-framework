history-law obligation, but repairs the post-Block-198 handoff before testing
it.  The first target is an action-derived history functional on the fixed
Block-194 event operations.  A process tensor, memory dimension, or boundary

## Exact target contract

**Target statement.**  Starting only from the frozen periodic `L=24`,
`m=2/7` action/cut, the Block-194 eight-projector PVM and M2 writer, and the
literal source tangents, determine whether the action defines a normalized,
strongly positive event-word functional with identity insertion and ordinary
event coarse-graining.  If it does, compute its word-Hankel rank, derive the
minimal predictive realization, and test the resulting restricted causal
process before any full-comb or response claim.  If it does not, classify the
missing action-to-operation or boundary datum exactly rather than supplying
one.

**Domain.**  Exact rational/algebraic arithmetic on the fixed twelve-site
positive half / twelve-site coarse circle and the full `C^32` event fiber.
All nine frozen squared spatial radii are required:
`0, 3/4, 1, 5/4, 3/2, 2, 3, (7+sqrt(3))/4,
(10+sqrt(3))/4`.

**Typed objects.**  The campaign keeps separate:

1. the normalized cyclic Gaussian/Grassmann functional of the quadratic
   action;
2. the open-cut Schur or right-graph boundary kernel;
3. field/covariance moment sequences;
4. the eight-dimensional commutative Block-194 effect system;
5. Lüders CP operations, identity insertion, the M2 pointer operation, and
   the TT source tangents; and

   object.

Equality of dimensions or reproduction of one static trace does not identify
objects of different type.

**Allowed premises.**  Literal tracked inputs from Blocks 192--198 and the
current minimal axioms.  Older finite process/decoherence-functional runners
are test templates only.  Their states, Born traces, operation alphabets,
boundaries, and process values are not imported.

**Forbidden weakenings.**  No supplied density or boundary fixed point,
chosen time arrow, fitted phase, response-selected insertion map, pointer
reset, fresh-environment assumption, post-positivity memory enlargement,
replacement of the indefinite Block-198 moments by the positive Block-195
moments without a derived event map, or promotion of a direct-sum Gram to a
tensor-product history.  No process is called tomographically complete on
`M_32` if only the fixed PVM span was tested.

**Completion witness.**  Gates T0--T6 below, culminating in one unique,
mutually restrictive full-L24 event-history family derived from the action,
with positive and causal restrictions, identity insertion, the fixed
PVM/M2/source descent, and no imported boundary.  One compatible process,
one positive covariance Hankel matrix, or a minimal linear recurrence alone
does not count.

## Gates and stop rules

### T0 -- event-operation type census

Reconstruct the eight effects, their ranks, orthogonality, completeness, and
commutative span.  Construct exact superoperator/Choi representatives of the
eight Lüders maps, their nonselective dephasing sum, and identity insertion.
Report the dimensions of the effect and declared operation spans.  Type the
M2 writer and both TT source tangents separately.  If no declared operation
system can accept the source or distinguish omission from measurement, a
full process-tensor claim is sealed, but a fixed-PVM closed-history test may
continue.

### T1 -- action functional and boundary objects

Derive the normalized cyclic Gaussian/Grassmann functional and the open-cut
boundary kernels directly from the frozen action.  Bind Berezin ordering,
reflection, orientation, and periodic gluing.  Prove which objects are unique
from `A` and which require a state, cut orientation, or insertion map.  Do not
identify the positive right-Schur Gram with the fermionic Berezin form.

### T2 -- exact moment taxonomy and predictive rank

Derive the full nonwrapped and circular scalar Green sequences at every
frozen radius.  Prove recurrence, Hankel ranks, seam residual, positive
`C`-family inertia/defect, and indefinite `M`-family inertia/defect.  State
separately recurrence order, block-Hankel rank, tensor-network bond lower
bound, and any CP-memory lower bound.  None is called process memory before
T3.

### T3 -- action-to-event insertion map

Attempt to derive, without extra choices, a linear involution-preserving map
from the fixed event operations and identity insertion into the action
functional.  It must reproduce the Block-194 one-shot marginal, distinguish
identity insertion from PVM dephasing, type the M2 operation and source
tangents, respect proper-cubic/reflection covariance, and define every word
needed at three crossings.  If the map is absent, nonunique, or requires an
unregistered boundary/time choice, stop T4--T6 and report its exact affine or
type residual.  Do not call this automatically an axiom defect.

### T4 -- conditional restricted history positivity

Only if T3 passes: construct the decoherence matrix on a spanning set of
fixed-PVM history words through three crossings.  Require Hermiticity,
normalization, strong positivity, ordinary coarse-graining, and identity-slot
restriction.  Reject any construction containing the Block-198 indefinite
moment Hankel as an injective positive compression.  Freeze memory from the
derived word-Hankel rank before solving a realization.

### T5 -- conditional causal process and full-L24 extension

Only if T4 passes: solve the exact restricted Choi/comb constraints, causal
trace recursion, boundary fixed-point/gluing condition, and uniqueness modulo
declared process-MPO gauge.  Prove mutually consistent extension through all
24 slots; uniqueness at three crossings alone is insufficient.  Promotion to
a full `M_32` process requires a separately derived informationally complete
operation basis.

### T6 -- conditional response and held-outs

Only after T0--T5 pass may D1/H1 response be opened, followed by sealed
D2/D3/H2/X1 held-outs without refit.  The resulting law must retain the fixed
PVM, M2 write, both TT source directions, all nine radii, and the stated
symmetries.

the executed derivation contract.  It is not a no-go for other insertion
maps, a cyclic quasifree history, an open-time CAR vacuum, a different

preregistered.
