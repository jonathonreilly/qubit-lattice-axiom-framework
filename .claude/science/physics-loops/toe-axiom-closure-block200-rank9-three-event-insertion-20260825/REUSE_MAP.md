## Independent-check architecture

The checker will rebuild Q, the exterior PVM, M2, and operation coordinates
without importing project runners.  It will verify Q024 both by direct Schur
complement and inversion of the covariance principal block; derive rather
than identify the event intertwiner; evaluate Wick/Pfaffian minors with a
separate sparse control; check every 512 branch value; and keep ordinary
nonnegative cylinders distinct from a coherent `512 x 512` strong-positivity
claim.

The likely bottleneck is the new 32D event-to-Grassmann intertwiner.  Exact
source differentiation stays sealed because combining its 216 terms with 512
branches and nine radii would be premature expression swell.
