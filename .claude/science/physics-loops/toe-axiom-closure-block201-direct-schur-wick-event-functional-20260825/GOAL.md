slot order, the conjugate-leg order, and the ordinary exterior-form adjoint.

For each boundary subset `B` of `{0,2,4}`, construct the exact Schur kernel
`Q_B^P`.  With no fitted state, filling, boundary, phase, character, scale, or
depth normalization, evaluate

`a_B(alpha_1,...,alpha_n) = det(Q_B)^(-1)
 sum_(epsilon in {0,1}^n) (-1)^(n-|epsilon|)

strongly-positive coherent history, a causal process, or a Born/Record law.
