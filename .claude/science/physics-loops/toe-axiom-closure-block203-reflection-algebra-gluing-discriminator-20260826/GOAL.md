# Goal

Block 203 runs the cheapest decisive successor to Block 202 without assuming
that the periodic carrier is a supertrace, that either reflection sign is
physical, or that a positive Euclidean form is already a Record state.

The first target is a neutral discriminator on the D1, squared-radius-zero
sector of the literal Block-192 action.  It types and compares

`reflection sign x observable algebra x trace type`,

with reflection sign in `{frozen, opposite}`, observable algebra in
`{full CAR, fermion-even CAR}`, and trace type in `{ordinary, graded}`.  The
periodic `L24` action is the evidentiary carrier.  An antiperiodic seam is a
changed-spin-structure control and may not be substituted for it.

## Stage A: exact algebra/gluing selector

Before events or histories are loaded:

1. freeze the cut, seam orientations, Grassmann order, involution, CAR
   generators, parity, and the two candidate reflection anti-automorphisms;
2. derive their action on the full and even observable algebras and decide
   whether the two signs are physically inequivalent there;
3. derive the exact two-half Berezin re-gluing functional for the original
   periodic action, including its determinant and parity-resolving source
   insertions, and determine whether it matches an ordinary trace, a graded
   trace, neither, or both;
4. repeat only as a labeled antiperiodic control to distinguish a derived
   selector from a chosen convention;
5. test normalization and positivity on a complete D1 parity-resolving
   projector basis: vacuum, each one-particle/odd sector, and the pair/even
   sector; and
6. require one unique cell, modulo algebraic equivalence, to be simultaneously
   same-action, exactly re-gluing, normalized, and positive.

Positivity alone may reject cells but may not select the already disclosed
opposite-sign cell after the fact.  If the signs coincide on the declared
physical algebra, the sign tournament collapses and exact gluing/positivity
becomes the selector.  If zero or more than one inequivalent cell survives,
Stage A stops with underdetermination or incompatibility; no convention is
chosen.

## Stage B: typed Record descent

Only after a unique Stage-A cell survives may a separately hashed stage load
Block 194's fixed `C32` PVM.  It must derive an exact unital positive descent
from the selected boundary algebra to those effects, fix all sector weights
without a filling or parity postselection, and satisfy `omega(I)=1` and the
eight D1 values without outcome renormalization.

Only that completed Stage-B object can retire an action-to-one-shot-event
obligation.  Prefix/history consistency remains a later gate.

## Stop and scope rules

The first exact failed projector, nonunique compatible cell, imported sector
selector, or failure of exact same-action re-gluing stops the block.  A
periodic/graded incompatibility would constrain this carrier; it would not be
a no-go for antiperiodic, open/infinite-time, sector-selected, Nambu, process,
or gravity routes.

The discriminator alone earns zero obligation retirement and zero TOE
percentage movement.  Gravity, formation/permanence, Born forcing, a causal
process, axiom amendment, retained status, and a complete TOE remain sealed.
