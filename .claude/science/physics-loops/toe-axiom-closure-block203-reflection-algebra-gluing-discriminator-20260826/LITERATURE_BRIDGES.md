# Literature Bridges

- Osterwalder-Schrader reflection positivity motivates the boundary form but
  does not select its global sign or turn it into a Record state.
- Fermionic coherent-state path integrals distinguish ordinary thermal traces
  from parity-inserted graded traces through temporal seam conditions.  The
  present runner must derive that distinction for the exact finite action.
- CAR-state positivity is tested on positive operators, including parity
  projectors that lie in the even observable algebra.

These are method bridges only; no external theorem substitutes for the exact
same-action calculation.
