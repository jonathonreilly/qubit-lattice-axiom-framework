# Goal

Block 204 tests the shortest live action-to-Record seam left by Block 203:
whether the fixed Block-194 eight-projector Record operator system is a
canonical positive restriction of the exact periodic action functional, or
whether the apparently positive Record weights instead belong to a distinct
one-particle OS boundary state.

The target is neutral.  A mathematical PVM, a maximally mixed density, and an
abstract CP map do not by themselves identify the physical possibility space
or derive Admissibility's distribution.

## Stage A0: carrier and functor typing

Before any eight-outcome weight is evaluated, reconstruct from source:

1. the Block-192 linear Dirac--Kahler carrier and its eight equivalent
   two-component Clifford blocks at the D1 zero-radius point;
2. the Block-203 two-mode CAR/exterior lift selected by exact periodic
   two-half gluing;
3. the Block-194 two-sector `C32` carrier and its eight nonzero rank-four
   projectors; and
4. every upstream-supplied map, if any, between those objects.

The runner must distinguish a direct-sum multiplicity decomposition from a
fermionic tensor/exterior lift.  It may not second-quantize a space that is
already being used as an exterior event carrier without declaring the new
carrier and its physical interpretation.

## Stage A1: canonical Record restriction

Only if the typing is coherent, classify the following frozen routes:

- literal same-carrier identification;
- functorial exterior/fermionic second quantization of the Block-194 PVM;
- compression to the exactly-one-particle sector;
- a unital positive/CP pullback to the Block-203 CAR algebra; and
- the independently action-derived OS boundary marginal as a labeled control.

A periodic descent passes only if one route is uniquely fixed by the supplied
action, symmetries, sector typing, and Record refinement; preserves the unit
and every fixed event/coarse event; uses no number/parity postselection,
filling, outcome renormalization, maximally mixed input, or fitted map; and
makes the exact periodic functional nonnegative on all eight event atoms.
An arbitrary POVM or a map that erases all outcome distinctions does not pass.

For the functorial CAR route, test the vacuum, one-particle, and multi-port
occupation sectors without constructing a dense `2^32` matrix.  In
particular, determine whether the natural lifts of distinct one-body event
projectors remain orthogonal and complete, and whether the one-particle
compression is an action-derived sector or an added law.

## Stage B: OS boundary-state control

Whether or not periodic descent passes, separately reconstruct the positive
Block-192 OS internal marginal and its two-sector extension.  Determine
symbolically whether the eight Block-194 weights are normalized and positive
for all action-derived relative sector weights, and identify exactly which
additional bridge would be needed to call this state the physical local
possibility distribution rather than a one-particle boundary diagnostic.

This control may establish a reusable positive probability candidate.  It may
not be relabeled as a descent of the periodic graded functional, a realized
event, a formation rule, a permanent Record, a Born theorem, or a TOE closure.

## Stop and scope rules

The first category mismatch, nonunique positive map, shared-vacuum failure,
multi-port overlap, imported sector restriction, or negative event atom stops
the periodic-descent claim.  If the result is negative or wall-bounded, the
complete N1--N8 discipline packet is mandatory and all alternative Record,
AP, open/infinite-time, process, gravity, and axiom routes remain live.

No axiom edit is authorized by this discriminator alone.  TOE percentages
move only if a named action-to-one-shot-Record obligation is actually closed.
