# Literature Bridges

- Fermionic Fock functoriality sends direct sums of one-particle spaces to
  tensor products of exterior algebras; it does not automatically turn a
  one-body PVM into the same exclusive many-body outcome menu.
- Restriction of a one-body PVM to the exactly-one-particle sector restores
  its ordinary projective interpretation, but the physical selection and
  normalization of that sector are separate obligations.
- A positive OS boundary form can supply a normalized state on a declared
  boundary carrier, but OS positivity alone does not identify that carrier
  with Admissibility's local possibility domain or a Record context.

These are method bridges only.  The runner must derive the finite-dimensional
facts for the exact upstream PVM and action; no external theorem substitutes
for that calculation.
