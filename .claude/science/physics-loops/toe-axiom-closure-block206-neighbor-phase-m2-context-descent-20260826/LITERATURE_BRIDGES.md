# Literature Bridges

- Finite-dimensional analytic perturbation and openness turn an exact first
  nonzero normalized coefficient into a finite nonconstant positive germ.
- PVM marginalization supplies the port-free pointer effects only after the
  joint probability functional is typed; it does not select an apparatus.
- Translation covariance supplies the source/detector phase.  Proper-cubic
  covariance requires simultaneous transport of state, effects, displacement,
  and labels.
- A local quantum instrument can realize a conditional pointer but does not by
  itself derive the nearest-neighbor source map or autonomous actualization.

These bridges license mathematics and terminology, not retained authority or
axiom content.
