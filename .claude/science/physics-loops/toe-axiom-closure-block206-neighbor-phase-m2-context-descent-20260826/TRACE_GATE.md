  four coarse ports, and port-free `M2` pointer as distinct objects.
- A normalized series coefficient is computed only after numerator and trace
  denominator recurrences are both included.

  truncated series is not itself a state.
- `sum_st p_(st,sigma)` is the port-free pointer law.  No single favorable
  `st` may substitute for that marginal.
- The six displacements are physical neighbor positions only after the source
  contact is local; a momentum label alone is not `eta`.
- The ordered `(p,q)` dependence must be inverse-Fourier reconstructed.  A
  bijection between free 24-frame orbits is not a local map.
- Scalar shell data and adjoint-`M2` Bloch data are separate representation
  classes; only an explicitly derived Hom may supply the `T2` source shear.
- Proper-cubic covariance transports source, state, effect, displacement, and
  labels together; partial rotation is invalid.
- No formation, history, gravity, axiom necessity, retained status,
  obligation retirement, or TOE movement follows from a conditional pointer.
- The five N5 lines must begin exactly with `per_element:`, `per_site:`,
  `per_mode:`, `per_block:`, and `lattice_wide:`.
