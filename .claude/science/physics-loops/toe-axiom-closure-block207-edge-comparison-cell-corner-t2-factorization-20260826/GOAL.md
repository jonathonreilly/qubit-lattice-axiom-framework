# Goal

Block 207 tests the proposed local Admissibility architecture in its sharpest
action-native form: oriented lattice directions compare neighboring site
contents, while an elementary cell combines fixed corner contributions.  The
target is the exact Block-206 H1 second-TT source and its positive port-free
binary `M2` probability germ.  The campaign asks whether the existing action,
rather than a fitted decoder, supplies the missing local context map.

## Frozen inherited objects

Keep the complete Block-206 H1 construction unchanged: `L=24`, mass `2/7`,
the `12|12` cut, ordinary-transpose right-Schur state, literal forward and
actual reverse source, TT column index `1`, fixed complete eight-effect PVM,
port-free binary pointer, and all 24 proper-cubic frames.  Keep

```text
incoming p = (pi/6, pi/3, 0, pi/6)
transfer q = (pi/3, pi/2, 0, 0).
```

H2 is sealed and may not be evaluated until the H1 local ownership gate is
positive.  No minimal-axiom or claim-registry edit is permitted during the
factorization test.

## Frozen candidate factorization

Write `X_a` for the matter translation and `Y_a` for the geometry/transfer
translation in the exact Laurent action.  The only permitted elementary
factors are those already present in the parent action:

```text
D_in,a  = (X_a - X_a^-1)/(2 i)
D_out,a = (X_a Y_a - X_a^-1 Y_a^-1)/(2 i)
C_a     = (X_a Y_a + X_a^-1)/2.
```

`D` is the oriented edge comparison and `C` is the fixed endpoint/corner
average.  For a spatial off-diagonal pair `i<j`, retain the action's exact
face/corner operator

```text
H_ij = - C_i C_j (c_i a_j + c_j a_i)/sqrt(2)
V_ij = m H_ij + i (H_ij D_in + D_out^T H_ij).
```

No alternative stencil, adjustable corner weight, scalar prefactor, source
adjoint, favorable port, or orbit lookup may be substituted.

For scalar weights `r_s` on the eight signed cube corners
`s=(s_x,s_y,s_z) in {+/-1}^3`, preregister the mixed corner moment

```text
Q(r) = (sum_s s_x s_y r_s,
        sum_s s_y s_z r_s,
        sum_s s_x s_z r_s).
```

The coordinate order is `(xy,yz,xz)`, matching the Block-206 `T2` shear.
The same definition may be applied componentwise to `M2` content, but scalar,
central, Bloch, and exterior-algebra objects must remain separately typed.

## Stage A: cubic representation and local support

Rebuild all 24 proper-cubic actions on oriented edges, six face directions,
eight corners, and `T2`.  Compute exact character/Hom dimensions and an
explicit basis.  Establish whether `Q` is the unique scalar-corner-to-`T2`
intertwiner up to scale, and classify reversal parity.  Decompose every
forward and actual-reverse Laurent source term into the frozen `C_i C_j` and
`D` factors, recording path length, corner multiplicity, cancellations, and
whether the radius-three support is a composition of unit-edge factors.

A product of nearest-neighbor factors is not automatically one-step
Admissibility.  Report separately: strict radius-one dependence, one-cell
edge/corner assembly, and multistep locality.

## Stage B: action selection and exact reconstruction

Construct the complete action-generated feature matrix before inserting the
H1 TT coefficients.  Solve exact linear systems for the forward and actual
reverse sources against that feature matrix.  Report rank, nullity,
coordinates, and residual.  A pass requires:

1. zero exact residual in both directions;
2. coefficients fixed by the action and H1 tensor, not fitted to the final
   probability;
3. one covariant rule across all 24 frames and translations;
4. the same rule distinguishes the Block-206 same-`q`, different-`p` source
   collision; and
5. orientation reversal gives the actual reverse rather than an adjoint
   surrogate.

Project any induced six-neighbor Bloch feature onto the two Block-206
odd-shell/even-shell intertwiners.  The action may select one class or one
fixed combination.  Merely showing that both classes span `T2` is inherited,
not progress.

## Stage C: physical `M2` and Record-context gate

The exterior-algebra action factorization closes only operator locality.  To
close the Admissibility context seam, derive a collision-free map from actual
neighboring one-site `M2` conditions to the edge/corner features.  The map
must not assume access to an unknown quantum state as a classical value; it
must be an action-defined comparison/instrument or a law-level function on
the supplied conditions whose probabilities are Record-testable.

Classify the outcome exactly:

- **complete H1 local ownership:** action-selected `M2` edge/corner map,
  forward/reverse reconstruction, all collisions and covariance green;
- **operator-factorization support:** native edge/corner action identity is
  exact but the `M2`/Record comparison map remains unselected;
- **candidate mismatch:** the frozen local feature span has nonzero residual
  or fails covariance/collision/reverse gates.

Only the first outcome opens H2.  A failure is scoped to this frozen
factorization and cannot become a broad Admissibility no-go without N1--N8.

## Axiom and TOE gate

No axiom edit follows from a missing downstream comparator, corner content,
or action choice.  An edit requires the previously registered complete
same-input threshold: two physically complete local laws satisfying the same
source, event, formation, covariance, and readability inputs with no remaining
discriminator.  Formal obligation retirement and TOE percentage movement are
zero unless a named end-to-end obligation actually closes.
