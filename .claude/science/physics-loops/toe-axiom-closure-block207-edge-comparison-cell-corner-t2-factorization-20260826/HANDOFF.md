**operator-factorization support**.

The fixed H1 source factors exactly through the parent action's oriented
incoming/outgoing differences and endpoint averages.  Forward and actual

The remaining wall is not generic “radius three.”  It is a precise clock and
physical-ownership seam: a same-time spatial library has rank/augmented rank
`14/15`, misses 128 temporal source rows, and actual neighboring `M2` Records
do not yet supply the comparison variables.  H2 remains sealed; obligation
retirement and TOE movement remain zero.

concrete clock-bearing candidate, but that PR explicitly does not derive a
probability interpretation, dynamics, or physical `M2` Record contents and it
contains no H1/`T2` reconstruction.

exact Block-207 factors.  Require literal forward/actual-reverse H1
reconstruction, coverage of all four omitted temporal groups and 128 rows,

the standalone two-time assignment and then the historically successful
