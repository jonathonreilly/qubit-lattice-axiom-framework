  vectors, TT shear, source vertex, probability effect, and Record contents as
  distinct typed objects.

  probability rule or a readable comparison outcome.

  strict radius one only if the final site law depends on six immediate
  conditions with no hidden state.

  be fixed upstream, never chosen after seeing H1.

  tensor, state, and effect together.
- Actual reverse is evaluated from the outgoing endpoint and `-q`; an adjoint
  equality is not a substitute.
- H2, formation, history, axiom necessity, retained status, obligation
  retirement, and TOE movement stay closed until their explicit gates pass.

  `per_site:`, `per_mode:`, `per_block:`, and `lattice_wide:`.
