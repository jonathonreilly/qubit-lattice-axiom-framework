# Mutation Plan

The primary runner must reject one load-bearing mutation for every check
family, including mutations that:

- stale the authority or alter the immutable registration;
- change one Clifford square, anticommutator, or complex-structure sign;
- retain only the favorable realification orientation;
- conflate real transpose with complex adjoint;
- call a trace-zero generator a state or effect;
- erase a negative eigenvalue or renormalize an indefinite cell object;
- corrupt Choi positivity, trace nonincrease, or joint normalization;
- insert `E_P=P`, repeat certainty, Lüders update, or trace-probability
  semantics as an axiom consequence;
- switch the projective menu or operation order after seeing H1;
- replace actual reverse with adjoint;
- supply `p`, `q`, a Fourier phase, TT shear, port, or frame lookup to the
  local compiler;
- drop one temporal group or one of the 128 inherited temporal rows while
  claiming full rank;
- hide the same-`q`, different-`p` collision;
- inspect the global Schur environment inverse or far-Record marginal;
- identify a conditional candidate instrument as a framework-selected law;
- open H2 without complete H1 ownership; or
- claim axiom update, obligation retirement, retained status, or TOE movement
  without the registered completion witness.

If a scoped negative or bounded-with-walls result is reported, the primary
cached stdout must include substantive `per_element:`, `per_site:`,
`per_mode:`, `per_block:`, and `lattice_wide:` lines, and the source note must
carry the complete N1--N8 record.  The independent checker must reconstruct
the core using a nonidentical representation and mutation set.
