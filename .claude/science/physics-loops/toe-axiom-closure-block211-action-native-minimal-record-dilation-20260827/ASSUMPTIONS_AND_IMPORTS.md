- Lattice, Qubit, Admissibility, and Record exactly as stated on current
  `origin/main`.
- Standard finite exact matrix, CP, Choi, Naimark/Stinespring, finite-group
  representation, and combinatorial mathematics.

- Block-208 H1 fixture, Clifford realifications, cell Choi operator, endpoint
  compiler, radial decoder, temporal relay, and source reconstruction.

  machinery; their proposed standing supplies no retained premise weight.
- PR #7749 and PR #7750 are comparison inputs only, pinned to their inspected
  tips.  Neither is authority for a positive bridge.

## Zero-weight open conditions

- identification of native action/link data with actual neighboring `M2(C)`
  possibilities;
- selection of first corner harmonic over readable higher harmonics;
- selection of one versus repeated cell depth;
- physical placement/serialization of the orthogonal register;
- formation site/rate, clock, and general multi-event collision semantics;
- extension from the fixed H1 mode/fixture to the total law domain.

These conditions may be closed by calculation, left open, or used to falsify
the direct family.  None may be silently imported.
