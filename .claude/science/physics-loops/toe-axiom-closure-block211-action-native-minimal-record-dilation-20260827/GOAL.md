## Frozen candidate families

### A. First-harmonic action solder

Write the H1 `T2` shear as the symmetric off-diagonal tensor `H(h)`.  Search
all proper-cubic intertwiners from `T2` into the eight-corner Bloch field,
resolved by Boolean/Walsh corner degree and endpoint reversal.  The direct
candidate is

```text
b_c = alpha H(h)c,
rho_c = (I+b_c.sigma)/2.
```

The normalization `alpha` must be fixed by the existing odd radial decoder;
it may not be fitted to H1 after evaluation.  The full equivariant section
space, its higher-harmonic alternatives, and positivity are evaluated beside
the degree-one family.  A degree-one restriction counts as physical selection
only if the reproduced action/cell has zero coupling to the discarded corner
harmonics.

For endpoint phases, test the unique first-harmonic pure `M2` orbit of each
native unit link factor.  The runner must form the factor from the local
one-hop action representation; accepting the global momentum angle itself as
an input is a failure.

### B. Direct cell-induced effect laws

Let `Phi` be the strict unital qubit channel whose Choi operator Block 208
reproduces.  For signed cubic axes `n`, freeze

```text
Q_n^(d) = (Phi^d)^*(P_n/3),  d in {0,1,2},
P_n=(I+n.sigma)/2.
```

Depth zero is a non-action control.  Depths one and two test whether one direct
cell use is selected or whether distinct same-input readable laws survive.
No inverse channel is applied physically; an exact inverse may be used only by
the downstream statistic decoder when its determinant is nonzero.

### C. Orthogonal Record register

For each passing endpoint effect menu construct its canonical square-root
Naimark isometry.  Tensor the two endpoint isometries and attach the 36 labels
to product-orthogonal binary Record patterns.  Test two register notions
separately:

1. Hilbert-dimension minimum: the least number of qubit sites capable of 36
   mutually orthogonal labels;
2. literal geometric covariance: binary patterns carried only by one or two
   copies of the six signed-neighbor shell, with proper rotations acting by
   the actual shell permutation and endpoint reversal swapping the copies.

An arbitrary six-bit code is a control, not a geometric bridge.

### D. Blank-to-locked attachment

The branch register begins on blank sites.  A passing attachment locks only
the local orthogonal bit at each used site, never writes a second Record at a
site, and never removes or changes an old Record.  Test a simultaneous block
write and a strict-neighbor append-only serialization.  A finite unitary on a
fixed blank/record direct sum is retained as a hostile control, not as the
only permitted architecture.

## One / many / none decision

Let `S_211` be the complete preregistered family above, modulo Kraus basis,
unused-environment unitary, conjugate realification, and Record-code relabeling
that leaves every readable probability invariant.

- `one`: exactly one readable probability law passes every joint gate;
- `many`: at least two same-input readable probability laws pass, with the
  physical discriminator stated explicitly;
- `none`: no member passes, narrowly falsifying this direct bridge family.

Only a nonempty law with no supplied state solder, effect depth, Record code,
or formation attachment counts as complete H1 local ownership.  Two complete
same-input laws with no remaining physical discriminator trigger the existing
axiom-update evidence gate.  Anything weaker moves no TOE percentage.

H2 is sealed.  No axiom, audit ledger, premise registry, governance surface,
or citation manifest may be edited in this campaign.  `review-loop` is
disabled by explicit user instruction.
