# Preflight Witnesses And Disclosure

No Block-211 target calculation has been run before this registration.  In
particular, no corner-harmonic Hom rank, first-harmonic uniqueness result,
cell-pulled POVM spectrum, depth-one/depth-two probability comparison,
Naimark isometry, equivariant binary-code census, register lower bound,
append-only write trace, post-Record H1 residual, readable-law cardinality,
axiom decision, obligation retirement, or TOE movement has been computed.

Known inherited facts are disclosed:

- Block 208 supplies a strict CPTP/unital qubit cell in both conjugate
  readings, a normalized 36-outcome endpoint compiler, a normalized
  12-outcome temporal relay, exact H1 forward/reverse reconstruction, and an
  explicit sharp/unsharp and radial-section nonselection boundary.
- Block 208's 36 candidate one-qubit output states are full-rank and pairwise
  overlapping.  They are not 36 perfectly distinguishable single-shot
  Records.
- Standard finite-dimensional Naimark/Stinespring mathematics guarantees a
  dilation once a POVM is supplied.  Mere existence is not the target; the
  action/cell selection, physical register placement, and Record attachment
  are the target.
- The rank-one locked-output normal form and the strict-neighbor single-front
  construction are exact candidate machinery.  They do not derive this
  effect law, this outcome register, a formation site/rate, or a physical H1
  state solder.
- The in-flight Block-209 PR reports a positive three-direction `D3(g,V)`
  form and Schur plane restrictions, but no carrier map to the Block-208
  corner-Bloch field.  It is a comparison candidate and cannot be used as a
  metric selector without that missing intertwiner.
- The in-flight Block-210 PR excludes one fixed 12-outcome common-weight
  refinement on its different six-context bench.  It does not exclude the
  present 36-outcome quantum instrument; it reinforces the need to test a
  genuinely joint, context-sensitive register rather than a fixed 4/12-way
  classical mixture.

The campaign therefore asks whether the direct cell and local action remove
the supplied choices.  It does not count an admitted dilation, an arbitrary
code, or a calibrated inverse statistic as physical selection.
