# Goal

route selected by the post-Block-218 five-physicist panel.  It tests whether
the four shared parity bits of the mixed Record front can be generated by one
bounded-local, branch-labeled law before permanent commitment.

## Decision classes

- `positive-size-lifted-factorization`: one unchanged bounded-local syndrome
  law realizes the exact parity support through held-out (L=8), while any
  commit/event/rate imports remain named.
- `positive-autonomous-local-commit`: the stronger case in which the same
  local law also owns conflict resolution and safe permanent commitment.
- `multiple-complete-laws`: two fully typed same-input rules survive and
  differ on a readable finite-history cylinder.
- `none-in-frozen-family`: only the radius-two, sixteen-transient-symbol
  family is excluded after N1--N8.

No axiom edit, audit verdict, obligation retirement or TOE percentage change
is authorized merely by a finite support result.  `review-loop` is disabled
by user instruction.
