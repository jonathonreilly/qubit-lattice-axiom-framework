# Literature Bridges

These are comparison surfaces, not imported conclusions.

- Bravyi, Hastings and Verstraete, *Lieb-Robinson bounds and the generation of
  correlations and topological quantum order*, arXiv:quant-ph/0603121:
  finite-speed correlation growth forbids treating a size-independent
  constant-depth circuit as an unbounded parity correlator.
- Verstraete, Wolf and Cirac, *Quantum computation, quantum state engineering,
  and quantum phase transitions driven by dissipation*, arXiv:0803.1447:
  local frustration-free checks can define dissipative preparation, but the
  physical branch process and convergence assumptions must remain explicit.
- Ticozzi and Viola, *Stabilizing Entangled States with Quasi-Local Quantum
  Dynamical Semigroups*, arXiv:1112.4860, and *Steady-State Entanglement by
  Engineered Quasi-Local Markovian Dissipation*, arXiv:1304.4270:
  quasi-local stabilizability depends on the allowed initial subspace and
  locality structure; GHZ-like targets do not justify an arbitrary-input,
  scalable claim.
- Kowalski and Mosteiro, *Time and Communication Complexity of Leader Election
  in Anonymous Networks*, arXiv:2101.04400: irrevocable anonymous leader
  election without size knowledge has a genuine finality obstruction, so a
  hidden first-site selector or torus counter is forbidden here.
