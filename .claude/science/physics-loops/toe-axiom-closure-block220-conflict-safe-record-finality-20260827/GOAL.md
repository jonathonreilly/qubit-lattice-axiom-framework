parity formation to an actual permanent Record.  It separates a conditional,
event-seeded coverage/echo compiler from the stronger autonomous law that
must arbitrate every locally generated competing seed.

## Decision classes

- positive-event-seeded-compiler: the tier-1 compiler succeeds with every
  event/site/rate import explicit.
- positive-autonomous-finality: one unchanged local law also resolves all
  reachable competing seeds and owns the first-commit branch family.
- representation-capacity-boundary: the rank-76 complement lacks the required
  symmetry representation for the frozen controller alphabet.
- frozen-multiseed-boundary: the tested finite-state arbitration grammar has
  an exact conflict or nontermination witness; broader reservation, wave,
  continuous-time and larger-block routes remain live.
- multiple-complete-laws: two same-input autonomous laws survive and disagree
  on a readable finite-history cylinder.

Only positive autonomous finality can be considered for Record-obligation
retirement, and even it must still be joined to event occurrence, rate,
renewal and repeated histories.  Review-loop is disabled by user instruction.
