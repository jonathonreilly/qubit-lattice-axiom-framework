# No-Go Ledger

No broad finality, anonymous-leader, Record, Admissibility or axiom no-go is
authorized.  Any negative is confined to the exact carrier representation or
finite controller grammar tested here.  A negative release requires the
no-go-discipline N1--N8 checklist.

## N1 -- alternative routes

Attempt or preserve revocable reservation, returning coverage/echo,
same-bit certificate merge, opposite-bit rollback, stochastic emergent
leader, event-seeded growth, larger radius, larger or continuous alphabets,
multisite handshakes, coherent instruments, continuous-time jumps, explicit
boundaries, and the independent gravity-action route.

## N2 -- independent walls

Use exactly six collapsed walls from Block 219: W_F conflict-safe permanent
finality; W_E physical event/context/effect/site authority; W_Q hazard/rate;
W_R renewal/rearming; W_H projectively consistent readable histories; and W_I
controlled infinite-volume stopping/local fixation.  A carrier or arbitration
failure concerns only a selected subfamily of W_F and cannot imply the other
walls.

## N3 -- hidden-wall scan

Search for coordinates, fixed phase, selected origin, supplied root or
boundary, torus-size counter, synchronous clock, host scheduler, global
success bit, globally fresh IDs, shared random tape, hidden leader, untyped
environment, postselection, reset, rate, renewal and physical-time imports.

## N4 -- residual matching

- A representation mismatch excludes only the proposed single-block
  controller representation.
- A unique-seed positive result keeps site, occurrence and rate supplied.
- A finite graph counterexample excludes only the exact rule and tested
  quantifiers.
- Nontermination under an unfair schedule does not refute a fair-schedule
  theorem.
- A fair-MEC trap does refute the corresponding almost-sure termination
  claim.
- A concurrent-root failure does not erase a valid conditional compiler.

## N5 -- rhetoric audit

- per_element: every carrier ray, branch effect and transition entry;
- per_site: every site and local neighborhood in the frozen graph family;
- per_mode: both content bits, all parity components, complement and all
  proper-cubic orientations;
- per_block: complete L=4, L=6 and held L=8 transition systems within the
  stated initial domain;
- lattice_wide: only an analytic all-even-period proof with controlled
  infinite-volume meaning, never finite enumeration alone.

## N6 -- partial-closure paths

Preserve the event-seeded compiler, exact rollback, carrier representation,
normalized first-commit histories and any reusable safe-diamond theorem even
if autonomous multi-seed arbitration fails.

## N7 -- steelman

The strongest positive route is a bounded local protocol whose candidate
roots remain revocable, whose coverage certificates merge on same-bit
collision, whose opposite-bit collisions roll back before permanent write,
and whose only closed fair recurrent classes are single-bit full-component
Records.
