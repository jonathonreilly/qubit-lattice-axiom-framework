# Repair Preregistration

## Trigger

The first H/P/L/S/F compiler is invalid as a local Markov surface. Its host
simulator retained `root` and `first_port` after genesis, and identical full
controller states could take different next steps. Its frozen JSON also
named transitions without serializing their executable guards and effects,
so the held-out checker did not consume the same rule. Those findings erase
the proposed compiler theorem; only the representation character and abstract
cleanup lemmas survive.

This repair target is frozen before implementing or running a held-out
period-8 check.

## Frozen repair carrier

Use five bit-coloured roles:

- ordinary waiting pointer `P_{b,d}` on `A1+E+T_axis`;
- ordinary active head `H_{b,d}` on `A1+E+T_axis`;
- immutable root/first-port marker `R_{b,d}` on the projectively twisted
  direction representation `A2+E+T_other`;
- closed scalar `L_b` on `A1`;
- failure eroder `S_b` on `A2`.

Per complement parity this consumes

`3A1+2A2+3E+T_other+2T_axis`,

dimension 20, and leaves `E+5T_other`, dimension 17. Across both complement
parities the 40 controller rays plus the two inherited transient rays leave
34 noncode dimensions. Proper-cubic signs on the twisted root representation
are projective phases and must cancel in projectors and CP branches.

## Frozen local algorithm

Genesis supplies exactly one `R_{b,d0}` at the event site and initializes the
matching neighbor as `H_{b,-d0}`. The root marker remains physically present
and retains the first port. The ordinary H/P depth-first walk explores the
punctured parity graph with the root excluded. An active head treats an `R`
encounter as its parent only when the root's stored port and the head's exact
inverse port point to one another; every other `R` encounter is a cross edge.
When the unique initial child returns through that exact relation, it becomes
`L_b` and the root writes `LOCK_b` directly. Matching `LOCK/BG` then floods
over `L_b`. Any mismatch, malformed state or conflicting Record launches
`S_b`, which erases every reachable `R/P/H/L` reservation back to its original
transient bit before decaying.

The graph lemma required for success is that the displacement-two parity
component with one root vertex removed remains connected for every tested
and claimed transverse period. Period 4's parallel labelled ports remain
distinct and require exact inverse-port matching.

## Frozen executable evidence contract

1. Serialize a complete finite transition DSL, not transition names: state
   alphabet, local read support, pattern predicates, exact port relations,
   guards, writes, branch weights, priority/exclusion rules, QND/default
   actions and genesis map.
2. Hash the canonical executable table bytes. The primary and independent
   runners must both consume those bytes; changing an executable branch must
   change the digest.
3. No post-genesis transition may read host `root`, `first_port`, visited,
   covered, stack, size, coordinates or history. Those may exist only in the
   validator oracle used to classify an execution after the table has run.
4. Construct an explicit equivariant carrier/intertwiner or an equivalently
   exact matrix certificate, then construct every declared local Kraus branch
   and check completeness and proper-cubic/complement covariance.
5. On periods 4 and 6, exhaust every transient word, root and first-port
   genesis. Check success iff consensus, no premature/opposite Record, exact
   mixed rollback, and state-collision determinism.
6. Enumerate every reachable overlapping enabled-transition peak, including
   commit/cleanup/QND interactions. Require a strong diamond or a declared
   exclusive guard on the exact reachable domain. Malformed-domain claims
   must match the frozen Goal scope.
7. Freeze the executable table and primary training result in a commit before
   any structurally independent held-out period-8 execution.
8. Reject hidden-state, prose-only-sidecar, table-bypass, twisted-root,
   inverse-port, punctured-connectivity, ignore-mismatch, eroder/commit,
   premature-commit, Record-overwrite, weight and digest mutations.

## Decision classes

- `positive-markov-event-seeded-compiler`: every gate above passes and the
  frozen held-out consumer independently reproduces the result.
- `twisted-root-representation-boundary`: the proposed projective root orbit
  does not admit the required exact equivariant embedding or CP covariance.
- `frozen-local-table-boundary`: one frozen executable grammar has an exact
  safety, rollback, determinism, connectivity, critical-peak or held-out
  witness. This does not exclude higher-block, coherent or continuous-time
  finality.

No autonomous-law, obligation-retirement, TOE-percentage or axiom claim is
authorized by a repaired supplied-root compiler.
