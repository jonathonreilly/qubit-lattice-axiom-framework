# Preregistration

No Block 221 executable probe may be written or run before this file is
committed.

## Frozen question

Can the unused normal-direction members of Block 220's already embedded P/H/R
orbits support one reversible, covariant ancestry zipper that distinguishes an
own-root cross edge from a foreign same-bit root before either root can
commit?

The tested one-site family is intentionally singular. It is not a search over
arbitrary labels or transition tables.

## Frozen physical allocation

For each supplied normal `n` and bit `b`, Block 220 currently activates the
four tangent directions of P, H and R. Block 221 may additionally activate the
12 existing normal-direction states. The on-site partition changes from 34
named rays plus rank-94 `X_n` to 46 named rays plus rank-82 `X_n`. No new orbit
or carrier dimension is allowed.

The candidate assigns one normal state as the collision anchor

`A_b = R_(b,+n)`

and the remaining five as a cyclic zipper alphabet

`Z^0_b,...,Z^4_b = (R_(b,-n),P_(b,+n),P_(b,-n),H_(b,+n),H_(b,-n))`.

Proper rotations transport `n` and complement flips `b`. Any A2 sign must be
carried as an explicit separately indexed Kraus phase; it may not be erased as
metadata.

## Frozen microgrammar

1. A noninverse same-bit H/R contact atomically changes H to `A_b`; R keeps
   its physical tangent direction. Every H/R commit becomes a guarded star
   transition and is disabled while an `A_b` is adjacent to that R.
2. The anchor spawns `Z^0_b` on the unique parent P whose physical direction
   points to the anchor. Each backward step leaves the next zipper colour on
   that parent. The actor stack is the only permitted ancestry source.
3. Reaching an R with the original anchor also adjacent certifies an own-root
   cross edge. The zipper must restore every P pointer from physical
   adjacency, return the anchor to H with the collision dart as its last
   scanned direction, and leave the original R unchanged.
4. Reaching an R without the original anchor certifies distinct roots. The
   actor root is atomically changed to S before the return wave leaves its
   neighbourhood. The return wave reaches the original anchor, changes it to
   S and rolls both reservations back before any state is reused.
5. Direct-root and width-two parallel-dart cases require explicit rules. Two
   simultaneous anchors, zipper/zipper contacts or any malformed phase contact
   must roll back or remain safely frozen; they may never default to commit.
6. Randomness is forbidden in safety rules. It may be considered for liveness
   only after deterministic safety and fair-MEC exclusion pass.

## Stage A: alias/capacity gate

The primary must reconstruct the Block 220 carrier and exact two-root L4
counterexample, then enumerate the physical ancestry stacks generated by the
unchanged table. It must also enumerate simple candidate stacks on the
periodic parity-component grids for widths 2 and 3. A restoration step is
invalid if a zipper site has zero or more than one adjacent next-colour site,
including parallel-dart and path-chord cases.

Training widths are 2 and 3, corresponding to L4 and L6. The candidate and
runner bytes freeze before width 4/L8 is inspected by an independent consumer.
If a training alias exists, the one-site family stops immediately. If only a
held alias exists, the frozen family fails without retuning.

## Stage B: executable safety gate

Only after Stage A survives may the exact microgrammar be appended to a copy
of the frozen Block 220 table. Exhaust all 576 same-bit and 768 opposite-bit
mixed L4 two-root starts under every reachable action order. Required:

- zero reachable Record on any mixed start;
- no hidden-state collision or scheduler-dependent ownership;
- exact rollback of both input bits on failure;
- no branch-closed fair nonterminal maximal end component;
- no regression on the full Block 220 single-root L4/L6 domains;
- all borrowed states erased or restored before reuse.

Then run L6 training. Freeze the grammar before any independent L8 execution.

## Stage C: physical instrument gate

If the classical grammar survives, compile literal pair/star Kraus factors on
the 46+82 partition, prove completeness on actual projectors, transport under
all 24 proper rotations and complement, expose every projective phase, and
require a structurally independent reconstruction with nonidentical
mutations.

## Decision classes

- `positive-one-site-two-phase-finality`: all stages pass, still subject to
  event-site/rate/renewal and retained-status gates;
- `scoped-five-colour-zipper-alias`: a frozen local ambiguity or ABA witness;
- `scoped-one-site-capacity-boundary`: the grammar needs a state or orbit not
  present in 46+82;
- `scoped-classical-safety-failure`: a false/opposite commit or nonterminal
  fair MEC survives;
- `scoped-physical-certificate-failure`: classical semantics survive but the
  covariant CP realization fails;
- `inconclusive`: only resource or timeout failure.

No negative decision is a broad no-go for permanent Records, higher blocks,
oriented-edge memory, stochastic liveness, coherent arbitration or
continuous-time laws. No decision alone justifies an axiom amendment.
