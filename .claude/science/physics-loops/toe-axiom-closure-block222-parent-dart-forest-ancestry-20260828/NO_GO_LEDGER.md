# No-Go Ledger

No negative is authorized before the exact local carrier and concurrency
counterexample are frozen.  If a negative is reached, `no-go-discipline`
must be rerun before any source note ships.

Live escape families that must remain explicit:

- an explicit two-arm higher-block forest whose two ancestry paths meet or
  return separate root acknowledgements;
- a larger finite block with visible overlap constraints and no fixed dimer
  phase;
- rollback-first coalescence with a remote-head safety proof;
- stochastic liveness after deterministic false-commit exclusion;
- coherent arbitration or a continuous-time generator;
- a physical-law supplier route through the open metric/matter PR stack;
- the direct registered AC occupancy-grain obligation.

Forbidden broad phrases include “one-site ancestry is impossible”,
“permanent Records are impossible”, and “the axioms must change”.
