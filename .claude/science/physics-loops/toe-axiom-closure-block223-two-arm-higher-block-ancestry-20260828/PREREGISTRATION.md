## Frozen physical object

Reuse Block 222's exact supplied-normal onsite partition without adding a
qubit, hidden root identity, epoch, coordinate, size, fixed dimer phase, or
independent edge tensor factor:

```text
74 named rays + transported rank-54 X_n = 128.
```

The new object is a genuine overlap-visible higher-block pattern, not a new
edge degree of freedom.  A collision seam is an adjacent ordered pair of
onsite `A` endpoints on a labelled tangent edge.  Each endpoint is locally
joined to exactly one Block-222 `H_(p,q)` front whose `q` dart points to that
endpoint and whose `p` dart retains its own parent.  The two `A` endpoints,
their reciprocal collision darts, and their two attached fronts form one
radius-two seam block.  Every neighboring block must agree on the shared
onsite rays and labelled darts.  No scheduler or proof-side query index is a
physical datum.

The Block-222 residual `[1,0,2,0]` may time-multiplex at most three additional
scalar/projective control phases per complement parity.  Any directional
orbit, independent per-edge bit, unbounded overlay count, root label, or
off-carrier role is a capacity failure rather than an implicit import.

## Frozen two-arm semantics

For one quiet collision seam:

1. Launch one pointer-reversal front along the exact parent dart from each
   collision endpoint.  Each old parent becomes a trail carrying both its
   exact parent and return-child darts, as in Block 222.
2. A front may claim its next parent site only when the complete bounded star
   contains no other seam, front, trail, acknowledgement, conflict, Record, or
   reused root guard.
3. Any front/front, front/trail, trail/trail, acknowledgement/foreign-trail,
   or already-claimed-root contact enters deterministic `CONFLICT`.  No attempt
   is made to decide whether the contact came from the same or another query.
   Conflict propagates only along the visible marked subgraph and rolls every
   participating endpoint back before reuse.
4. Therefore a single same-tree collision is safe: its two parent paths meet
   and the query rolls back without Record.  A concurrent overlap is also
   safe by the same rule.
5. A quiet foreign-tree collision has disjoint parent paths.  Each front
   reaches a distinct guarded root.  Each root sends an acknowledgement back
   along its exact return-child trail.  A positive foreign classification is
   enabled only at the original adjacent `A--A` seam after both endpoints
   simultaneously receive acknowledgements through their own attached
   fronts.
6. An acknowledgement cannot detach, jump trails, accept endpoint equality
   for dart equality, or complete at a scalar `A` not joined to its own seam.
   Any malformed or second contact is conflict and rollback.
7. Record commit remains disabled throughout this classifier.  A positive
   classification is only permission to enter the inherited guarded
   merge/erode phase; it is not itself a Record.

Randomness is forbidden in all safety transitions.  Stochastic backoff may be
examined only after deterministic false-classification exclusion is exact.
