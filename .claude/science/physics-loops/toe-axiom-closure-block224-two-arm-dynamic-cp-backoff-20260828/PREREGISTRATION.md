## Frozen parent and scope

The Block-223 object is immutable in this campaign:

```text
onsite partition: 74 named rays + rank-54 X_n = 128
controller roles per complement parity:
  U;

  H_(p,c) (sixteen ordered parent/return-child dart pairs);

```

No new onsite ray, overlay bit, edge tensor factor, query identity, epoch,
coordinate, component size, preferred root, fixed dimer phase, scheduler owner,
clock tick or proof-side history is allowed.  The 47-signature quotient and
its all-conflict mapping may not be retuned after a dynamic failure.

This block distinguishes three objects that must not be conflated:

1. deterministic branchwise safety of the local controller;
2. a literal completely positive trace-preserving instrument conditional on
   an explicitly supplied local parameter `0 < p < 1`;
3. physical selection of that parameter, event occurrence, scheduler/rate,
   renewal and continuum time.

Only the first two are in the constructive target.  The third remains an
explicit supplier even if the first two pass.

## Frozen dynamic candidate A: symmetric quench and exact rollback

`P_p` is a quiescent parent dart.  `R_d` is a guarded root with its exact
return child.  `A--A` is the active reciprocal collision seam.  `H_(p,c)` is
a rootward front carrying both its next-parent dart `p` and return-child dart
`c`.  `T_c` is its trail.  `L_c` is the only rollback/acknowledgement wave and
retains the exact child dart.  `S` is a failed seam endpoint.  `LOCK` and `BG`
remain Record sectors and are read-only throughout this classifier.

The candidate rows are defined by behavior, not by a proof-side query label:

1. **Quiet advance.**  A front `H_(p,c)` may replace a quiescent `P_r` at dart
   `p` only while its complete edge-centred radius-two support contains no
   other `A`, `S`, `H`, `T`, `L`, root acknowledgement or Record.  The old
   front becomes `T_c`; the new front is `H_(r,inverse(p))`.
2. **Quiet root turn.**  A front meeting the root guard through its exact dart
   does not overwrite the guard.  The edge-centred radius-two row restores the
   front's stored `P_p` and launches `L_c` on its return-child trail.  Thus the
   root datum is never lost.
3. **Return.**  `L_c` moves only through the exact child dart.  Each move
   restores the current site to the parent dart that is visible on the unique
   marked parent-side continuation and transfers `L` to the child trail.  At
   its own `A` endpoint it produces an acknowledgement only while the
   reciprocal neighboring endpoint is still the same quiet seam.
4. **Positive seam.**  A positive classifier state requires two seam-bound
   acknowledgements at the original adjacent endpoints, zero live `H/T/L/S`
   in either marked arm, two distinct guarded roots, and no Record in the
   complete seam neighbourhood.  It grants permission to the inherited
   guarded merge/erode phase but does not write `LOCK` or `BG`.
5. **Symmetric contact quench.**  For every frozen conflict signature, the
   actor and every visible target participant are quenched in one separately
   indexed edge-centred radius-two row.  Each `H_(p,c)` restores `P_p` and
   launches `L_c` on its own child; each `T_c` becomes `L_c`; each `R_d`
   retains `d`; and each contacted `A` becomes `S`.  No row chooses a winner.
6. **Failed seam.**  Failure reaches both adjacent endpoints through the
   visible seam.  An endpoint returns to its prelaunch `P` or `R` role only
   after every attached exact-dart controller has returned and the complete
   radius-two guard is quiescent.  A missing or failed mate forces a returning
   acknowledgement into rollback rather than success.
7. **Erase before reuse.**  `A/S/H/T/L/R` guards cannot be relaunched or
   overwritten by a new query.  A site becomes reusable only after its exact
   base role has been restored and every controller that names one of its
   darts has disappeared.

The runner must construct the candidate as an explicit finite row table.  It
may not certify these prose rules by restating them.  In particular, every
row must expose its input signature, support, output signature, exact dart
transport, priority, and deterministic or stochastic squared weight.

If a target-side `T`, `H`, `R` or `A` cannot start symmetric rollback without
losing a restoration datum, if a marked parent-side continuation is not
unique in the complete visible state, or if one physical input signature
requires two different safety outputs, candidate A stops immediately.  The
runner must print the first complete witness and may not repair the grammar in
the same block.

## Stage A -- transition compiler and hostile-schedule safety

Independently reconstruct the Block-223 carrier, 47-signature quotient and
first reciprocal witness.  Generate all local rows from the frozen behavior
above, canonicalize them under all 24 proper-cubic rotations and complement,
and reject duplicate physical inputs with different outputs.

Model-check every width-two forest/query pair and one canonical representative
of every width-three contact signature.  Add held fixtures for the first
reciprocal witness, three mutually contacting seams, contact during launch,
contact during root turn, contact during return, failed-mate return, and seam
endpoint reuse.  Explore every enabled asynchronous action until a terminal
state or a repeated state-action class is reached.

Required safety invariants:

- no `LOCK` or `BG` write and no positive classification before two bound
  acknowledgements plus component quiescence;
- all same-tree and contacting queries reject; a quiet distinct-root query
  still succeeds;
- exact restoration of every parent, return-child, seam and root dart;
- zero orphan `A/S/H/T/L` roles in every terminal state;
- no guarded root or controller site is reused early;
- identical complete visible states have identical successor distributions;
- safety is true on every stochastic branch, not merely in expectation.

Any first failure is a scoped dynamic result.  It is not a broad higher-block,
finality, Record, or axiom no-go.

## Stage B -- literal physical instrument

Only if Stage A passes, compile the exact same rows into separately indexed
partial-isometry/Kraus branches on the actual onsite partition.  For each
normal and support:

- named roles are rank-one sectors and `X_n` is rank 54;
- unmatched sectors receive the default identity branch;
- deterministic rows have squared weight one;
- the backoff row has exactly two branches with squared weights `p` and
  `1-p`, retained symbolically as a supplied family `0 < p < 1`;
- many-to-one classical outputs use separate environment-labelled Kraus rows,
  never a coherent row sum;

  `sum K^dagger K = I` exactly by sector dimension;
- all outputs stay in the 74+54 carrier, all Record sectors are QND, and all
  A2/projective phases transport under the 24 rotations and complement.

The compiler must enumerate all actual input sectors for pair, edge-centred
radius-two and guarded-star supports or give an exact factorized dimension
certificate.  A dimension count alone cannot excuse a semantic input alias.

## Stage C -- conditional liveness

First construct the deterministic transition graph and list every closed
nonterminal strongly connected component or maximal end component.  The known
lockstep `launch -> contact -> rollback -> relaunch` cycle must be reproduced.

Then replace only the quiescent relaunch with an independent local Bernoulli
backoff family `0 < p < 1`.  The probability law is an input to the instrument,
not a consequence of Admissibility.  Build the finite Markov decision process
for every Stage-A fixture and test almost-sure absorption against every
declared nonanticipating fair scheduler.  A positive escape branch, one
favorable scheduler, or the special value `p=1/2` is insufficient.

Report separately:

- branchwise safety;
- finite-fixture almost-sure absorption;
- finite expected absorption time, if established;
- absence or presence of a size-uniform lower rate;
- infinite-volume fixation, event occurrence, clock and physical law
  selection, all of which remain open unless independently proved.

The first closed nonterminal end component under the declared scheduler class
stops candidate A and triggers the precommitted deterministic-coalescence
pivot.
