## Frozen exchange-symmetric seam controller

Let `(a,b)` be the two physically adjacent seam endpoints.  Endpoint order is
only a labelled-edge chart: exchange maps every row to its partner and grants
neither endpoint priority.  The three scalar roles give exactly these nine
ordered pair states:

| pair | meaning |
|---|---|
| `AA` | discovery; no acknowledgement |
| `SA`, `AS` | the indicated arm has acknowledged |
| `SS` | success cleanup selected; no confirmation |
| `US`, `SU` | success cleanup; the indicated arm confirmed |
| `UU` | abort cleanup selected; no confirmation |
| `AU`, `UA` | abort cleanup; the indicated arm confirmed |

The controller rows are frozen as follows:

```text
SA + ack_b -> SS                 AS + ack_a -> SS
SS + conflict -> UU
US + conflict -> AU             SU + conflict -> UA
US + confirm_b -> SUCCESS       SU + confirm_a -> SUCCESS
AU + confirm_b -> ABORT         UA + confirm_a -> ABORT
```

The exchange partners of every asymmetric row are mandatory.  The second
acknowledgement atomically selects success cleanup; the second confirmation
atomically performs the terminal action.  There is no tenth persistent pair
state.  Conflict dominates until the final terminal action.  A single
acknowledgement, a single confirmation, endpoint equality, or absence of a
locally visible mark never suffices.

`SUCCESS` here is permission to enter the inherited guarded merge/erode and
Record-writing phase.  `ABORT` consumes the supplied collision event.  Neither
macro-output is itself credited as a permanent Record in this block.

## Exact distributed-memory invariant

or a dart.  Until an arm's confirmation is consumed, every collision, parent,
return-child, trail, root, contact, and cleanup dart remains present in a
connected higher-block pattern of `R/P/H/T/L` roles.  Consuming a dart-bearing

   incidence;

3. a Y center never compresses three pending incidences into one
   `H_(p,c)`, `T_c`, or `L_p` role;
4. extra children remain as marked neighboring roles until independently
   confirmed;
5. `U/S/A` endpoints cannot be claimed or generically decayed while an exact
   attached mark remains;
6. contact joins the full visibly connected marked union; it does not choose
   a surviving query, first arrival, root, or port;
7. all schedules may differ in service order only if their complete visible
   terminal normal form and classification are identical.

The runner must explicitly reproduce two negative controls:

- **parallel-port control:** on periodic width two, consuming acknowledgements
  returning through ports `0` and `2` leaves an identical scalar latch but

These controls reject only endpoint-only or central-only compression.
Neighbor-retained distributed memory remains live and must be tested rather
