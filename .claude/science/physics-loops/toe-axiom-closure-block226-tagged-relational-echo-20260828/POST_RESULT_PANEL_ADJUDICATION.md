## Adjudication

The failed three-site row leaves a visible discovery `H` one site outside its
support.  The shortest materially new mechanism is therefore a full-state
four-site contact row

```text
H-T-T-T -> P-H-T-L
```

plus an explicit rootward cleanup echo for contacts farther down a wake.  It
uses existing `P/H/T/L` roles and makes the same complete-state, critical-pair,
CP, and fair-component obligations immediately testable.

Component coalescence remains attractive for confluence, but exact set-valued
root incidence may grow with component size and opens a new finite-carrier
problem.  Serial scanning adds a covariant token, termination, and hidden
order/owner risks.  Neither is presently a shorter first test.
