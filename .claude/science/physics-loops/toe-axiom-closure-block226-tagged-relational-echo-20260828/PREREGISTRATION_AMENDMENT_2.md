# Preregistration Amendment 2: local interior-contact seed

This amendment is committed before any Block 226 runner is written or
executed.  It freezes the minimal local seed required to turn the panel's
abort codeword into an executable row.

On an oriented rootward-to-seamward marked arm, an interior contact with a
live `T` uses the complete labelled radius-two triple

```text
T_rootward -- T_contact -- T_seamward
```

and atomically writes

```text
H_(p,c) -- T_contact -- L_seamward.
```

The rootward neighbor's missing parent incidence is recovered from the exact
visible forest neighbor in the same source cylinder; the contacted `T` keeps
its labelled abort tag; and the seamward `T` becomes the matching `L`.  All
foreign participants in the frozen contact signature are quenched in the
same separately indexed row.  If the rootward neighbor is already the exact
discovery `H_(p,c)`, it remains the `H` of the seed rather than being
duplicated.

At a short-arm boundary where the complete `H-T-L` word does not fit, the
direct-root/one-edge/radius-two degeneracy row must either preserve every dart
and atomically enter `S-S`, or stop with the first complete witness.

No global abort mode, remote write, instantaneous component recolouring, or
proof-side path signal is allowed.  In particular, the rootward marked
remainder beyond the new `H` is not declared clean.  It must be restored by
rows enabled from the visible seed.  If the seamward abort echo succeeds while
that remainder leaves an orphan `H/T/L`, the candidate stops with
`scoped-tagged-echo-orphan-or-restoration-failure`.

This amendment freezes one minimal local physical realization of the tagged
echo.  Failure excludes this seed grammar only; serial branch scanning and
other preregistered distributed encodings remain distinct until tested.
