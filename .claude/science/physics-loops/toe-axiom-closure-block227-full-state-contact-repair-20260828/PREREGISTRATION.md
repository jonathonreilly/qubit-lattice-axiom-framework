# Block 227 Preregistration

No Block 227 primary runner may be written or executed before this packet is
committed.

## Frozen parent and locality contract

Reconstruct the Block-226 `74+54=128` carrier inventory, nine seam-pair
capacity states, labelled parent/child darts, executable full-state `Row`, and
all eleven frozen row families.  The parent result is a boundary, not a
selected law: only its exact carrier, row semantics, positive direct/one-edge
fixtures, and counterexample are inputs.

Every Block-227 row must bind and transform:

- onsite roles and every support dart;
- the seam pair, attached bindings, terminal mode, and incident foreign roles
  and darts;
- executable local guards whose truth is evaluated by `Row.enabled`;
- endpoint-exchange, proper-cubic, complement, normal, and projective orbit
  metadata;
- one deterministic squared weight before any CP claim.

For every stored transition, `target == Row.apply(source)`.  Every stored
history is consecutive.  A guard may inspect only the displayed bounded
support and its incident labelled darts.  In particular, `NO_FOREIGN` means
no foreign participant incident to that support; it may not inspect the whole
arm or component.  No proof-side contact position, path length, future event,
or scheduler order enters a state or guard.

The support bound is fixed independently of arm length: at most four
consecutive arm sites plus the exact incident foreign tip and its two-site
wake.  A larger guarded Y star may be used only in Stage B and still has a
fixed radius.  Any row whose support grows with the arm stops the route.

## Frozen single-contact grammar

Write arms rootward to seamward.  `F` below denotes an exact incident foreign
`P-H-T` front terminating on the subscripted ordinary `T` through its labelled
dart.  `F` is quenched by the matching contact row only.

| name | complete local word | output | role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `D_T` | `P H T T` | `H T T T` | quiet discovery with explicit seamward context |
| `D_A` | `P H T A` | `H T T A` | quiet discovery at the seam boundary |
| `C0` | `H T T_F T` | `P H T L` | nearest contact repairs the preceding `H` atomically |
| `CQ` | `H L T_F T` | `P H T L` | clean-return/contact collision becomes tagged abort |
| `CF` | `T T T_F T` | `T T L T` | remote contact seeds an oriented rootward certificate |
| `M` | `T T L` | `T L T` | move the certificate exactly one site rootward |
| `K1` | `H L T L` | `P H T L` | join a clean return and abort certificate one `T` apart |
| `K0` | `H L L T` | `P H T L` | join adjacent clean/abort certificates |
| `B` | `H T L T` | `P H T L` | move tagged abort seamward and restore one site |
| `A` | `H T L A` | `P P P S` plus reciprocal `S-S` | atomically consume provenance and enter failure barrier |

Retain quiet root turn `R-H-T -> R-H-L` and clean return
`H-L-T -> P-H-L`, but compile complete source cylinders rather than leaving
an implicit three-site wildcard.  In particular:

- discovery is exactly `D_T` or `D_A`; it is disabled on `P-H-T-L` and on an
  incident `F` in its displayed support.  Without this four-site context,
  `P-H-T-L-T-A -> H-T-T-L-T-A -> H-T-L-T-T-A -> P-H-T-L-T-A` is a live
  cycle;
- ordinary clean return on `H-L-T-X` excludes `X=L` and an incident `F` in
  its displayed support;
- `K1` owns `H-L-T-L` and `CQ` owns `H-L-T_F-T`;
- root turn may race a remote contact because that contact is outside its
  displayed support; it may not be suppressed by a global absence guard.
  Exact complete variants preserve every off-support participant;
- all endpoint/boundary degeneracies either restore every labelled dart in
  one row or print the first complete failing source.

`CLEAR(support,darts)` below always means absence only on the named sites and
incident labelled darts.  It never means absence elsewhere on the arm or
component.  Priority cannot make overlapping physical projectors disjoint.
Rows that occupy the same complete local cylinder are compiled into disjoint
inputs; actions on overlapping but distinct supports remain real critical
pairs and must join.

## Stage A -- exhaustive single-contact compiler

Generate every simple labelled arm of lengths zero through eight, every port
and turn, every reachable discovery/root-turn/good-return position, and every
single incident foreign contact on a live `T` wake.  Execute every asynchronous
ordering of enabled rows after the contact source is supplied.

The reachable graph must include and join:

```text
R H T T_F T:  C0  versus root-turn then CQ
R H T T L A:  root-turn then K1 versus M then B at the seam boundary
R H T T L T:  root-turn then K1 versus M then B in the interior
R H T L T:    root-turn then K0 versus B
H L T^k L:    every interleaving of good-return and rootward M motion
```

Required Stage-A results:

1. every complete input has one output distribution and terminal obligation;
2. every stored transition and chain satisfies the full-state contract;
3. each race commutes or has one bounded common join with identical full
   visible normal form;
4. every supplied single contact reaches reciprocal `S-S` abort, restores the
   exact root/path/seam darts, and leaves zero `H/T/L/A/S` orphan roles outside
   the failed barrier;
5. quiet no-contact arms still reach and hold the clean relational arrival;
6. support/ray count is constant in arm length and uses no `LOCK/BG` scratch;
7. direct-root, one-edge, width-two-labelled controls retain every dart.

The first failed complete input, history pair, join, restoration, or support
bound stops the block.  It cannot be repaired in the same block.

## Stage B -- multi-contact, Y, and parallel compiler

Only after Stage A passes, freeze its rows.  Add no new onsite role.  Execute:

- every pair of remote contacts and every ordering of their two `L`
  certificates on arms through length eight;
- certificate adjacency, crossing, catch, duplicate incident contact, and
  contact during `CQ/K1/K0/B/A`;
- width-two ports reaching the same neighbor but retaining distinct labelled
  darts;
- all twelve labelled parent/two-child Y triples and four cleanup subsets;
- good return on one child concurrent with remote contact on the other;
- canonical dynamic representatives of all 47 width-three contact signatures
  and the first reciprocal crosswire orbit, extending only if a quotient cell
  contains multiple complete physical outcomes.

Every enabled critical pair/triple must commute or join to one identical full
visible normal form.  A surviving choice of first contact, first child, port,
root, or certificate is failure.  If two `L` certificates require an onsite
counter or support that grows with separation, stop and pivot.

The first fixtures are frozen before execution.  They include adjacent
contacts in `R-H-T-T_F1-T_F2-T-T-A` and separated contacts on
`R-H-T^8-A` at the third and seventh live `T`.  A second participant may not
silently disappear from a complete `C0/CQ/CF/B` source, and a row may not
rewrite a site still carrying that participant.  The first stranded
`H-T-L-L`, blocked abort front, or order-dependent surviving contact prints
both full histories and stops Stage B; it is not repaired post hoc here.
