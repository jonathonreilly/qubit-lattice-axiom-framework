## Allocation and rerank rule

Allocate `65%` to product compilation, `20%` reserved for immediate
component-coalescence preregistration on a real wall, `10%` to monitoring the
gravity/transfer pincer, and `5%` to preserving the probability-form bridge.
Serial scanning receives no execution budget in this block.

Recheck after each decisive stage or three hours, whichever comes first.  A
new PR reranks this block only if it retires a named TOE obligation with fewer
supplied physical structures than the finite product test.  A bounded
mathematical limit alone improves route confidence but does not do that.
