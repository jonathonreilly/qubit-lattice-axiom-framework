coalescence / 1 gravity / 0 probability-first / 0 serial`. The panel interprets
the one new cell as deterministic cleanup-front coalescence, while keeping the
general component algebra as the immediate fallback rather than claiming it
is already necessary.

## Block 229 target

Freeze the one visible merge cell before execution, then test:

1. every contact subset through length ten, followed by longer adversarial
   arms and translated overlaps;
2. triple/quadruple adjacent and separated certificates, requiring the same
   result for every parenthesization and schedule;
3. a symbolic local rank independent of arm length and contact count;
4. full labelled-dart lifting, equal-neighbor parallel ports, Y triples, and
   reciprocal crosswire controls;
5. exact local CP and strong-fair component absorption.

Pivot immediately to distributed set-valued incidence if the merge is not
associative, needs support growing with component diameter, loses a root/dart,
overflows the fixed carrier, or creates a nonjoinable translated cell.

The panel explicitly rejects a capacity or axiom conclusion: the failed graph
has only 13 states, all support remains at most four, and no new carrier state
is demanded. It requires a real length-independent rank because the current
diagnostic rank fails on `M` and `G_T`; finite acyclicity cannot substitute.
