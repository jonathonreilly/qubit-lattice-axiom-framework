# Mathematical routes

The exact normal form exposes the differential current-commutator coefficient.
Supported free packets plus their complete projected tail give initial bounds.
An exact Duhamel comparison gives the growing-time upper bound. Fixed-vector
spectral averaging and H1 source control give the sharp power threshold;
a separate fixed-tau error estimate gives the inverse-energy lower bound.
The boost/boost contact expansion and periodic auxiliary rotation address a
different bracket and domain. None implies a full symmetry representation.
