# Mechanisms and evidence roles

Boost matches the finite-spin spatially growing-flow theorem, explicit dyadic
graph estimates and C1 state/operator integration; it avoids assuming analytic
vectors for arbitrary local or rapid almost-local elements. Gap uses finite
ODE locality, cone-split polynomial-time seminorms and Schwartz gap filtering,
including both scalar vacuum columns and both derivation graph extensions.
Fock uses four independent clocks, capped connected clustering, three relative
lattice sums, complete finite-energy spectator bounds and Gram recursion;
separated-tensor density is proved in its defined regular target span only.
Weighted uses finite padded Jacobi and translation-orbit coefficient moments;
conservation removes the weighted residue, leaving a complete finite site test.
Integrated uses the rapid almost-local moving-vector product rule and existing
Banach-group uniqueness before canonical equality and maximal spectral sums.
Each mechanism is judged at its displayed hypotheses. Hashes and control-row
counts establish provenance and sensitivity, not the analytic conclusions.
