# Completed approaches and remaining routes

1. The actual annular positive-frequency forcing is separated from the whole
   effective spectrum. Its resolvent gives a freely evolving dressed vector
   plus a rapidly decreasing transient. Positive spectral reweighting and the
   prior continuum theorem yield the ordered outgoing correction limits.
2. The actual residual is a longitudinal triplet satisfying one global mean
   constraint. A cutoff mean-zero singlet is an approximate range vector.
   Eight-cone radial bounds quantify its norm and approximation error.
3. Pointwise forcing resolvents and the full transverse integral give
   polynomial weighted growth. A noncommuting integration identity, both
   endpoints and the full differentiated error yield explicit all-time bounds.
4. Uniform Hilbert control of the effective solution converts those bounds
   into simultaneous correction-norm limits. Symmetric-form cancellation and
   every free/cross/correction term give all-nine actual expectation bounds.

The full-inverse shortcut fails for the actual initial residual. The earlier
valid exponential estimate is preserved; the polynomial derivation improves
it without changing its evidence. The separate H_* threshold probe may test
sharpness of a free primitive, but has no accepted outcome in this milestone.
