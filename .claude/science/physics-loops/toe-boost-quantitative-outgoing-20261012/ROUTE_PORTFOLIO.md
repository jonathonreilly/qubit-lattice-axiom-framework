# Evidence roles and independence

The complete K and Q notes are the analytic theorem artifacts. Each received
a separate source reconstruction before its reviewer opened new author
controls. Reviewers had the theorem formulas and necessary prior reviews;
these are source-shared checks, not blinded discovery. Root proposed the
mechanisms and is a contributor, not their independent reviewer.

K's primary retains all38 original finite controls. Q's primary embeds all
three complete original helpers in separate named functions, without importing
an external sidecar or executing an opaque string:27 corrector,16 polynomial,
8 mean controls. They are finite algebra/ODE/majorant controls. Analytic cone
bounds, continuum measures and growing-time limits rest on the full proofs.
Separate reviewer controls use independent finite implementations. Author
replays establish reproduction. None is formal Lean verification.
