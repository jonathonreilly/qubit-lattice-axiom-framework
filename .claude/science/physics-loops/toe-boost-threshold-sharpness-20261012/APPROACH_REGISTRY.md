# Completed argument families

1. Physical parity and the global mean projection give a longitudinal square
   with one rank-one subtraction. The source resolvent and zero atom are exact.
2. All eight torus cones give the low-energy pushforward density. Uniform
   boundary subtraction and scalar inversion identify the entire local measure,
   including the exclusion of hidden singular mass. Signed square-root branches
   and exact atom removal give the positive threshold density.
3. The free primitive spectral multiplier gives a sharp coefficient and a
   uniform absolute squared-norm remainder; finite meshes cannot supply it.
4. A time-adapted approximate-range vector bounds general weighted residual
   primitives. Noncommuting Duhamel acts on that already small primitive.
5. Hilbert-valued Fubini retains the full varying residual, while the exact
   forcing error remains separate. Both triangle inequalities give explicit
   actual two-sided bounds at small effective times and sufficiently small lambda.
