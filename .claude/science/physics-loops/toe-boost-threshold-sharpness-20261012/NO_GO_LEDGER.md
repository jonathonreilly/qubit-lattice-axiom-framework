# Preserved failures and scope boundaries

Positivity alone does not choose the rank-one sign. A negative-axis resolvent
limit gives an atom, not the real-axis density. Omitting that atom's subtraction
changes the leading primitive scale. A full complementary inverse is not L2.
A fixed finite mesh has a different lambda-first limit.

A coarse operator-norm comparison of propagators loses the small-lambda scale.
The new proof compares primitives instead. Neither the noncommuting factor nor
the time-varying residual or exact forcing error can be dropped. No universal
no-go is proposed. Every earlier boost counterexample remains unchanged.
