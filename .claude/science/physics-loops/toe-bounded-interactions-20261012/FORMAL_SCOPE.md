# Two finite formal artifacts and their limits

CliffordInteraction.lean is an abstract ring/complex-algebra derivation under
explicit normalized square and anticommutation hypotheses. Its successful saved
execution and axiom reports are archived with exact compiler/runtime records.
The initial wrong import failed and the import repair is explicit. The two
FalseControls proofs fail; that establishes failed proof attempts, not the
negations of their altered formulas. Concrete faithfulness, nonzero degree and
norm require the ordinary CAR argument. The unnormalized cell factor n_1,c
squared is separate from the normalized formal identity.

CompressedResolvent.lean proves the noncommutative difference identity with a
left inverse of 1+a v and a right inverse of 1+w b. No inverse of a compressed
resolvent on its outside-band kernel is taken. The reversed-sign and
omitted-term rational examples are literal proved counterexample conjunctions.
They are separate from failed proof attempts and do not examine all noncommuting
order mutations. The complete later scope review supersedes only the original
pending review state; original seals and interrupted stub remain unchanged.

Neither source proves Hilbert/Fock representation, selfadjointness, spectral
gaps, domains, inverse existence or bounds, continuum comparison, norm orbit
convergence, actual energy, adiabatic control, ground selection or empirical
physics. Saved Lean execution is historical evidence at its exact scope.
No Lean rerun is requested by the new identity tools or integration driver.
