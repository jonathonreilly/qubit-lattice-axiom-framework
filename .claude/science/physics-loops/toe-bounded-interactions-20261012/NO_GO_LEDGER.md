# Literal scope of negative and limiting evidence

- A fixed positive actual-energy cap does not force a ground density: the
  preserved finite/low excitation examples concern that implication.
- Rough ordinary compact cells can fail the operator or form-domain premise
  needed for an unbounded-generator manipulation; this does not obstruct
  bounded CAR/cocycle dynamics.
- Full CAR convergence preserves parity invariance but not fixed even-sector
  support; the target is full Fock.
- The separately appended fixed two-mode nonzero linear-ramp example has
  positive liminf of tau squared times actual excess. This excludes a faster
  uniform power over that broad class. It says neither every V nor the actual
  supplied chain has that lower bound, and says nothing against all protocols.
- Clifford altered proof attempts failed. They are not formal counterexamples.
  Compressed-resolvent rational counterexamples are proved exact conjunctions
  for two specific altered identities, not universal factor-order tests.

All witnesses, assumptions and counterexamples remain in their original
complete sources. This ledger summarizes scope; it is not a new proof or
exhaustive route census. N1/N5 remain pending.
