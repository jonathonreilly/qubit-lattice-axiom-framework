# Conditional driven recovery of the evolved bus memory

**Proposed conditional theorem, pending root review.** The accepted evolved-memory preparation can be followed, at a selected time in the accepted bus window, by a finite driven schedule which approximately returns the entire memory E to vacuum and restores the arbitrary original C state, including its correlations with D/R up to the specified old-plant unitary. The same positive complements give this construction **under the additional, explicitly supplied amplitude range `0<=u<=2`**. The original range `0<=u<=1` does not authorize this schedule. All literal old H terms remain installed with coefficient one. The construction has a common modeled incident bound `3J_*+g` and a common per-site first-derivative bound; physical implementation needs the independent calibration and pointwise certificates below.

This is a conditional, externally scheduled endpoint recovery, not native control, autonomous stopping, erasure, a generic-plant theorem, or indefinite apparatus reuse. The full comparison is on D,C,E,R. A microscopic drive is an additional retained-output interface. No numerical runner was used: the load-bearing checks below are exact operator identities and Duhamel bounds. ## 1. Accepted input contract and extra permission

Use the accepted finite bus instance and its positive-block ledger, in the same physical units with hbar=1:

    K=H_D+A,  A=sum_j h_j,
    0<=h_j<=c_j I,  Gamma_j=c_j I-h_j>=0,
    C0=sum_j c_j,  Gamma=sum_j Gamma_j=C0 I-A,
    max_x sum_(j:x in supp h_j) c_j <= J_* .          (1)

The c_j and C0 are the actual chosen complement coefficients, not substituted upper bounds in a phase. All physical onsite/edge reserves and counterterms are included. The parent supplies a common J_* over its allowed accuracy family. Only D's designated old port and the new C sites meet added bus blocks. Write the actual unreserved decomposition

    A=R_b I+A_C+xi V_p,
    A_C=A_0+xi V_s+xi^2 A2.                         (2)

Here R_b is the actual scalar reserve denoted R in the parent; R without a subscript below denotes the passive reference register. A_C acts only on C, V_p couples the old port to C, and A2 is the **full physical** nearest-neighbor density counterterm, including off-code action. Fix full-space bounds

    v_port>=||V_p||,  v>=||V_p+V_s||,  a2>=||A2||.     (3)

No commutation of H with V_p, or among the summands of A_C, is assumed. H commutes with A_C because their registers are disjoint.

Retain the parent's supplied independent vacuum memory E, one qubit per C qubit, its vacant ordinary-neighbor disjoint matching, full positive matching SWAP controls, phase-referenced positive onsite rotations, and zero storage generator/isolation throughout the entire experiment. The arbitrary initial state is sigma_DCR tensor |0_E><0_E|, with possible joint readiness error eta_ready. Unlisted storage dynamics or couplings are implementation errors, not silently zero physical costs.

The **new permission** is to drive those same Gamma_j with a common envelope in `[0,2]`. Thus, during preparation or recovery,

    K_u(t)=K+u(t)Gamma+G(t)
          =H+f(t)(A_C+xi V_p)
                 +[R_b f(t)+C0 u(t)]I+G(t),
    f=1-u in [-1,1].                               (4)

Every actual added block remains positive: the h_j stay installed and u Gamma_j and G are separately nonnegative. In particular `u=2` gives a negative coefficient of the unreserved A, **not** negative physical control blocks and not a negative H. The installed K is positive by its paid reserve, so K_u is positive. The range extension and supply of the complements on every actual support, including the old-port support, are physical conditions.

Let F be the accepted smooth monotone ramp on `[0,1]`, flat at its endpoints, with `F(x)+F(1-x)=1`, `m_F=max|F'|`. Choose chi to be the parent's specific nonnegative single-peaked flat-endpoint bump with integral one, `m0=max chi`, `m1=max|chi'|`; thus `integral|chi'|=2m0`. Set

    gamma_F=integral_0^1 |2F(x)-1| dx <=1.          (5)

It is a fixed dimensionless waveform constant. A computable upper bound one suffices in every estimate, so no integration oracle is needed.

## 2. Preparation and selected runtime, with full memory output

The accepted preparation uses durations `r_on,t_s,t_p,r_off`, with

    alpha_on=r_on/2,  alpha_off=r_off/2,
    tau_p=r_on+t_s+t_p+r_off,
    U_C=exp(-i alpha_on A_C),
    A_u,p=t_s+t_p+alpha_on+alpha_off,
    Phi_p=C0 A_u,p+R_b(alpha_on+alpha_off)+pi n_S/4. (6)

Here n_S is the number of source qubits. Let Q relabel the original C factors as E, preserving all D/C/R correlations, and U_E be U_C on the relabelled factors. With exact readiness the preparation's ideal isometry is

    exp(-i Phi_p)|Omega_phi>_C tensor
        [(exp(-i tau_p H)_D tensor U_E tensor I_R)Q]. (7)

The source rotation axes during preparation are `phi_l+omega_l alpha_off`. In particular, the known unitary U_E is part of the output and cannot be deleted by convention.

Select a runtime `t=T+s`, `0<=s<=w`, `T=t_*/xi^2`. The accepted strong-bus output and (7) give

    exp[-i(Phi_p+chi_b(t))]|Psi_C(t)> tensor
      [(exp(-isH) W exp[-i(T+tau_p)H]
                        tensor U_E tensor I_R)Q],
    |Psi_C(t)>=|0_bus> tensor product_l
                     |Alpha_l(phi_l-omega_l t)>,
    chi_b(t)=R_b t+c_old xi^2 t+theta0.            (8)

The notation `|Alpha_l(theta)>` is the N-fold equatorial product `( |0>+exp(i theta)|1> )/sqrt(2)`. Every source factor is retained. The original runtime phases still contain `+omega_l T`; no additional `+omega_l tau_p` is inserted. Formula (8) is a product of the known C output and the arbitrary, generally entangled D/E/R state. It is an approximate full-output statement, not an assertion that the actual runtime output factorizes exactly.

In unhalved trace norm the accumulated error through this endpoint is bounded by

    eta_ready + delta_bus
    +xi r_on v_port+r_off(xi v+xi^2 a2)
    +2 integral_p ||E_p(t')||dt'
    +2 integral_runtime ||E_r(t')||dt'.          (9)

The accepted bus supplies delta_bus uniformly over the old/reference input, with E included in that reference. Both original global scalar phases remain explicit in (8).

## 3. Five recovery stages

Choose finite positive durations `r_a,t_q,t_m,r_z`. The middle negative ramp has **the same duration r_on and the same F as the initial preparation on-ramp**. Let recovery time start at the endpoint t of (8). The chronological schedule is:

| Stage | Duration | Common complement u | Extra gate |
|---|---:|---|---|
| a: isolate known C | r_a | F(x) | zero |
| q: rotate sources to vacuum | t_q | 1 | positive inverse rotations |
| m: restore memory register | t_m | 1 | matching full SWAPs |
| n: inverse memory evolution | r_on | 1+F(x) | zero |
| z: return to installed K | r_z | 2[1-F(x)] | zero |

Here x is local stage time divided by its duration. All derivatives match at the boundaries. Set `alpha_a=r_a/2` and

    tau_q=r_a+t_q+t_m+r_on+r_z,
    A_u,q=r_a/2+t_q+t_m+3r_on/2+r_z.              (10)

At the recovery endpoint u=G=0 and the installed Hamiltonian is again exactly K.

On stage a compare against `H+f A_0+[R_b f+C0 u]I`, omitting only `f[xi(V_p+V_s)+xi^2 A2]`. On the known C factor of (8), the unreserved bus part and the source ferromagnetic parts of A_0 annihilate the vector, while the source field advances each phase. The source phase at the end of a is therefore

    theta_l=phi_l-omega_l(t+alpha_a).             (11)

Define `Y_theta=cos(theta)Y-sin(theta)X`. Stage q uses, on each source qubit,

    Q^-_theta=(pi/4)(I-Y_theta)>=0,
    G_q(t')=chi(t'/t_q)/t_q sum_(l,j) Q^-_(theta_l),
    exp(-i Q^-_theta)|alpha_theta>
                         =exp(-i pi/4)|0>.        (12)

To check the sign and phase exactly, the parent's forward gate is `Q^+_theta=(pi/4)(I+Y_theta)`, with `exp(-iQ^+)|0>=exp(-i pi/4)|alpha_theta>`. The two commute and sum to `(pi/2)I`; hence their exponentials multiply to `exp(-i pi/2)I`, proving (12). Moreover `Q^-_theta=Q^+_(theta+pi)`, so the parent's supplied positive phase-referenced rotation family already contains this gate. This is a positive inverse **up to its explicitly retained scalar**, not the unavailable negative generator `-Q^+`. At the end of q the entire C register, including bus/router sites, is vacuum in the comparison.

Stage m applies on each matching pair

    G_m(t')=pi chi(t'/t_m)/(2t_m) sum_v (I-S_v).

The pairs commute and `exp[-i(pi/2)(I-S_v)]=S_v` exactly, with no extra endpoint scalar. Consequently, on every initial vector including arbitrary D/C/R entanglement,

    |0_C> tensor U_E Q|psi>_DCR
           -> (U_C tensor I_DR)|psi>_DCR tensor |0_E>. (13)

The unknown information is transferred back; it is never measured or erased.

On both remaining stages compare against the **entire** `H+f A_C+[R_b f+C0 u]I`, omitting only `xi f V_p`. Their signed areas satisfy

    integral_n f=-r_on/2=-alpha_on,
    integral_z f=r_z integral_0^1(2F-1)=0.       (14)

Since A_C is a fixed operator and commutes with H, their C propagator is exactly `exp(+i alpha_on A_C)`, which cancels the U_C in (13), including every phase, noncommuting C-only interaction and off-code term. No operation on unknown E data beyond the supplied SWAP is needed. No spectral inverse, recurrence, tomography, or exact-degeneracy oracle is used.

## 4. Endpoint theorem, phases and errors

The recovery comparator phase is

    Phi_q=C0 A_u,q+R_b(alpha_a-alpha_on)+pi n_S/4. (15)

The complete ideal final isometry, acting on the original D/C/R input with E initially appended in vacuum, is

    V_final|psi> = exp(-i Phi_tot)
       [(V_D tensor I_CR)|psi>_DCR] tensor |0_E>,
    V_D=exp[-i(s+tau_q)H] W exp[-i(T+tau_p)H],
    Phi_tot=Phi_p+chi_b(t)+Phi_q
       =C0(A_u,p+A_u,q)+R_b(t+alpha_off+alpha_a)
                      +c_old xi^2 t+theta0+pi n_S/2. (16)

This proves the desired information-restoration contract: the original C state and its correlations are restored **under the specified unitary on D**, with the reference unchanged, and E is independent vacuum. It does not assert that applying a nontrivial V_D leaves all D/C correlations numerically identical to their input values. The laboratory duration is `tau_p+t+tau_q`. The old free time before W is `T+tau_p`; the old free time after W is `s+tau_q`. Moving the recovery duration to the right of W would generally be wrong when `[H,W]!=0`. All scalar parts of H remain inside these literal old-time flows.

As an exact scalar cross-check, `A_u,p+A_u,q+t+alpha_off+alpha_a=t+tau_p+tau_q`. Thus an installed constant present in both C0 and R_b, such as the unchanged old positivity reserve R_s, acquires its full elapsed-time phase in (16).

For stage a the isometry Duhamel error is at most `alpha_a(xi v+xi^2 a2)`. After the swap, the absolute signed area is

    integral_(n,z) |f|=alpha_on+gamma_F r_z.     (17)

The corresponding isometry error is at most `xi v_port(alpha_on+gamma_F r_z)`. This estimate holds even on an arbitrary restored unknown C state entangled with D/R. Thus the recovery's unhalved trace contribution is at most

    e_q=r_a(xi v+xi^2 a2)
          +xi(r_on+2 gamma_F r_z)v_port
          +2 integral_q ||E_q(t')||dt'.         (18)

No actual trajectory is assumed to stay in a code, vacuum, or symmetric subspace. Duhamel evaluates each residual on the comparison trajectory, and its other propagator is unitary. The physical A2 norm, not its code compression, appears. Combining (9) and (18) by unitary propagation and the triangle inequality gives, for every sigma_DCR,

    ||rho_final - V_final sigma V_final^*||_1
       <= min(2, eta_ready+delta_bus+b1 xi+b2 xi^2+2 E_tot),
    b1=(2r_on+2 gamma_F r_z)v_port+(r_off+r_a)v,
    b2=(r_off+r_a)a2,
    E_tot=integral_p||E_p||+integral_runtime||E_r||
                                      +integral_q||E_q||. (19)

Initial readiness is counted once, and the same ideal original-input comparator is used throughout; there is no extra actual-marginal substitution. Marginalizing (19) proves the E vacuum, C restoration and D target estimates with the same tolerance. For exact ready pure inputs the underlying amplitude error is the corresponding sum of the isometry errors (half each displayed weak trace term), the supplied bus amplitude error, and integrated generator errors. Equation (16) fixes its scalar convention. Density operators alone do not expose a global phase, but that is not a reason to delete it.

## 5. Modeled strength, slew, global action and work

Given fixed positive gate margin g and per-site first-derivative allowance S_*, choose

    r_a >= m_F J_*/S_*,
    r_on >= m_F J_*/S_*    [already required in preparation],
    r_z >= 2 m_F J_*/S_*,
    t_m >= max(pi m0/g, sqrt(pi m1/S_*)),
    t_q >= max(pi m0/(2g), sqrt(pi m1/(2S_*))).  (20)

The original r_off,t_s,t_p satisfy their accepted analogous bounds. The extra factor two in r_z pays the full amplitude excursion from two to zero. Each ramp has per-site derivative at most `|u'|J_*`; only one matching or onsite pulse meets a site during the separate gate stages. Their maxima are `pi m0/t_m`, `pi m1/t_m^2` and `pi m0/(2t_q)`, `pi m1/(2t_q^2)` respectively. Therefore the modeled added incident loads and derivatives satisfy

    J_added <=3J_*+g,       S_added<=S_* .       (21)

Both the installed h_j and the separately driven u Gamma_j are counted. In the ramp where u reaches two their separate bound is `J_*+2J_*`; the gates occur at u=1 and cost at most g. E has only its supplied matching gate in the nominal model. These are common constants independent of accuracy and source count when the parent common J_* premise holds. They are not bounds on the total global generator, the original old load, or unmodeled microscopic controls.

With `C_Gamma=||Gamma||<=C0`, `S_Gamma=sum_j||Gamma_j||<=C0`, exact conservative global recovery prices are

    peak control norm <=2 C_Gamma+g n_C,
    integral_q ||u Gamma+G||
          <=C_Gamma A_u,q+pi n_C+(pi/2)n_S,
    summed local complement action = S_Gamma A_u,q,
    integral_q ||d(u Gamma+G)/dt'||
          <=4 C_Gamma+2n_C pi m0/t_m+n_S pi m0/t_q. (22)

The complement's total variation is four: it rises from zero to two and returns to zero. A separate normalized-trace ledger, with s_H and s_A the normalized traces of H and A on their carriers, is

    s_H+s_A+u(C0-s_A)
       + n_C pi chi(t'/t_m)/(4t_m) [SWAP],
       or + n_S pi chi(t'/t_q)/(4t_q) [rotation]. (23)

Only the indicated pulse term is present in its stage. Their scalar integrals are pi n_C/4 and pi n_S/4. The SWAP's full nonscalar exponential combines with that scalar to give exact S; equation (23) is not the comparator phase (15). Every C-only scalar retained in U_C cancels with its exact inverse; the actual R_b and C0 terms remain as in (16).

For the exact nominal closed driven model, the controls vanish at the two recovery endpoints and the work identity is

    Tr[K rho_end]-Tr[K rho_start]
       =integral_q Tr[rho(t') d(u Gamma+G)/dt']dt'. (24)

Its absolute value is bounded by the last line of (22). There is no prescribed sign or claim of free reset energy. During recovery `||K_u||<=h+3C0+g n_C` is a sufficient full finite bound for `h>=||H||`; hence first and raw second moments are bounded by that value and its square. The same `beta^j e` controls the reference-valued first/second centered moment after tracing D,C,E, by testing against every reference operator of norm at most one. ## 6. Physical calibration and timing costs

Every implementation error in (19) is a **full-space Hermitian generator** error on D,C,E, including installed coefficients, control axes, storage drift, omitted neighbors, unintended old-port couplings and switching. It acts over its actual interval. An installed or memory-storage error of full norm at most nu throughout the experiment contributes at most

    2 nu(tau_p+t+tau_q)                           (26)

to the trace bound. Long storage remains a demanding supplied resource.

For each complement block, a realized term `(u+delta u_j)(Gamma_j+Delta Gamma_j)` differs from u Gamma_j by a norm no greater than

    |delta u_j| ||Gamma_j||
       +u ||Delta Gamma_j||+|delta u_j| ||Delta Gamma_j||. (27)

Sum these bounds and integrate, then multiply by two for trace error. A constant additive coefficient-operator error is weighted by the actual area `A_u,p+A_u,q`; a relative block error is additionally weighted by its block norm. The extended amplitude is included through u, which can equal two. The cross term in (27) is not omitted. Pointwise positivity, the allowed amplitude range, incident strength and derivative bounds of an actual implementation require separate certificates or explicit fixed slack; a small integrated error alone proves none of them.

For a source rotation axis error delta theta,

    ||Q^-_(theta+delta)-Q^-_theta||
       =(pi/2)|sin(delta/2)| <= (pi/4)|delta|.

Thus the full recovery rotation trace allowance is at most `(pi/2)sum_(l,j)|delta theta_(l,j)|`. For common phase error at all n_S>0 sites, request accuracy `2 e_phase/(pi n_S)` to spend e_phase; when n_S=0 there is no rotation-phase budget. Equation (11) requires a certified unit complex phase from the final selected t, alpha_a and omega_l. For nominal real errors, before reduction modulo 2pi,

    |delta theta_l| <= |delta phi_l|
       +(t+alpha_a)|delta omega_l|
       +|omega_l|(|delta t|+|delta alpha_a|)

One may compute unit phases directly without an argument branch. The accepted exact-target support certificate or rational-target repair remains in force; no exact-zero oracle is added by recovery.

Then (11) simplifies exactly to `theta_l=phi0_l-omega_l(s+alpha_a)`. Recovery-axis arithmetic need not subtract two large T-dependent phases. ## 7. Order of choice and the meaning of a specified runtime

There are two distinct claims.

**Fixed already selected hardware and stop time.** Given the accepted finite instance, its selected `t=T+s`, the specified prep durations and the additional control permission, equations (10)–(29) give an explicit finite recovery and its actual error. No later choice of xi is invoked to improve that already fixed experiment. If the computed residual exceeds a requested tolerance, this instance does not satisfy that tolerance by this bound. An arbitrary prescribed wall-clock stopping time is not automatically realizable as the bus's T.

**An accuracy family whose runtime may be selected.** Fix a desired complete trace tolerance e, common J_*,g,S_* and profiles. Choose the accepted bus size and unscaled coefficients for nominal `delta_bus<=e/4`, retaining permission to reduce xi. Choose the fixed durations using (20) and the parent's inequalities, with the inverse ramp reusing r_on. Compute b1,b2 from the full-space unscaled bounds. Further require

    0<xi<=min(existing bus bounds,
               e/[8(1+b1)], sqrt(e/[8(1+b2)])). (30)

Then `b1 xi+b2 xi^2<=e/4`. Require `eta_ready<=e/4` and `E_tot<=e/8`, which spend the other two quarters and give (19)<=e. All b's are dimensionless: a duration multiplies an energy. Fix this final xi **before** recomputing T, A_C, R_b, C0, U_E, bus phases and preparation/recovery axes and scalar phases. Choose any specified s in the certified window. The physical nominal K and its norm bounds do not depend on those coherent axes, and the local gate norms do not depend on angle. Thus there is no phase-duration fixed point. No polynomial total-runtime claim is needed here.

If moments are requested, either first select a suitably tightened trace budget and verify it against the final beta, or retain the explicit finite-instance inequalities (25) and improve errors only by controls/calibration available after fixing K. One must not infer a uniform moment tolerance from a trace tolerance when beta grows with the family. Further xi reduction changes T and the phase prescriptions; it is not compatible with promising the same previously fixed runtime without rechecking feasibility.

## 8. Exact failure checks and scope boundaries

**Omitting the inverse memory area is wrong.** Already at xi=0, take a C mode with `A_C=nu|1><1|` and initial `|+>`. Preparation stores the relative phase `exp(-i alpha_on nu)`. Merely rotating the known output to vacuum and swapping back leaves that phase, and any final ramp of additional positive signed area a leaves trace distance

    2|sin[nu(alpha_on+a)/2]|

from the original C state. It can be order one while all weak-port errors vanish. Equation (14), not a claim that U_E is harmless, removes it. This is a counterexample to that shortcut, not a universal impossibility under the smaller control range; special recurrences or other supplied gates could give different routes.

**A zero signed area does not cancel the old-port coupling.** The final return ramp has zero integral f, but H need not commute with V_p. For example, with `A_C=0`, `H=Omega Z_D`, `V_p=X_D X_C`, its first-order interaction-picture term contains `integral f(t') exp(2i Omega t')dt'`, not just `integral f`. As a function of frequency k=2Omega, the Fourier integral for the monotone symmetric return profile `f=2F-1` has zero value at k=0 but derivative `i integral t' f(t')dt'`, which is nonzero: `(t'-r_z/2)f(t')` is nonnegative and strictly positive on a set of positive measure. Thus for sufficiently small nonzero Omega the Fourier integral is nonzero. This exact analytic discriminator explains why (17) uses absolute area and why literal H cannot be reversed by the algebraic complement identity. The example tests an invalid cancellation inference; it is not a new strong-bus instance or a universal no-go claim.

**Restoration is at the declared endpoint.** The installed K resumes immediately with C in its unknown original state, and can subsequently alter C and its correlations. E remains isolated in the nominal model, but no permanent halt or unlimited repeated-use theorem follows. A subsequent cycle needs its own schedule, calibration, storage and accumulated-error analysis. For a separately biased old drift, the existing bias-transfer qualifications are still necessary; recovery does not turn H+bias into H.

The original supplies of complement interactions, matching vacancies and isolation, pure memory, phase/timing references, precise coefficients and external controls remain explicit. The additional `[0,2]` amplitude range is the only control-range enlargement used. The ideal E returns to vacuum because known C purity is swapped into it while unknown information is restored to C; no unknown information is destroyed. Work and complete outputs of an actual microscopic driving apparatus are not supplied by a classical waveform. Root controls acceptance.
