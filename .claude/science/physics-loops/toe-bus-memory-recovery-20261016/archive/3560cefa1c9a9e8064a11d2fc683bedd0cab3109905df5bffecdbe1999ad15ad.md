This does not make an arbitrary signed K+H_app+G positive. If K>=0 it is
positive. Otherwise a reserve already included in K may establish positivity;
no reserve is silently appended here. Adding a new reserve would change the
specified generator and must be declared with its mean, scalar, phase and
timing costs.

One can check the apparatus's transient whole-space gap without using it as a
preparation mechanism. For gamma>0 the two-qubit calculation gives

    delta n_u+gamma(I-S_u) >= d_gamma(n_u+n_e),
    d_gamma=(delta+2gamma-sqrt(delta^2+4gamma^2))/2>0.          (21)

On the one-excitation pair block the two eigenvalues are those of
[[delta+gamma,-gamma],[-gamma,gamma]]. On |11> the left value is delta and
2d_gamma<=delta; |00> has value zero. Summing (21) and using (2) proves a
strict transient apparatus gap above the all-C/E vacuum. At gamma=0 the
degenerate free E memory makes this gap zero. No gap of the complete arbitrary
K generator or gap-based state preparation is inferred.

Under the whole-term operator-norm convention, each selected pair has added
load 2gamma(s), peaking at 2pi/t_s on each of its two sites. No old site has a
new reset edge. The boundary's original loads, including Lambda at q0 and its
unchanged K load, remain installed. Entry adds at most 2pi/t_p at v0. If the
Pauli dictionary is used instead, (8) has three two-site coefficients of
magnitude gamma/2 and a scalar gamma/2 per pair; it is a different accounting
dictionary, not an alternative full-term norm.

## 7. Mean, variance, covariance, work and finite information capacity

For any initial sigma_DCR the same installed generator has exact moments

    mu_initial = Tr(K sigma_D)+Tr(H_app sigma_DC),
    Var_initial(H0)=Var_sigma(K)+Var_sigma(H_app)
       +Tr sigma {K-mu_K I,H_app-mu_app I}.                  (22)

The symmetrized covariance in (22) need not vanish: the old and clock factors
are allowed to be correlated, and K and H_app need not commute. The free
reservoir H_E=0 has zero specified energy contribution, but its supplied pure
state is not thermodynamically free by implication.

At any intermediate time, with G equal to the active pulse, the actual total
generator is H_tot=H0+G. Its exact variance is

    Var(H_tot)=Var(H0)+Var(G)
                  +Tr rho {H0-mu_0 I,G-mu_G I}.             (23)

No covariance is dropped. Conservative full-space instantaneous allowances are

    beta_s(s)=h+B+2n gamma(s),  beta_p(s)=h+B+2omega_p(s),
    |Tr(H_tot rho)-a| <= beta_(s or p)(s),
    Tr[(H_tot-aI)^2 rho] <= beta_(s or p)(s)^2,
    Var_rho(H_tot) <= beta_(s or p)(s)^2.                   (24)

Thus both the installed and driven energy spreads are paid. A physical
identification of these engineered generators with measured energy remains a
separate premise, not supplied by positivity alone.

Since the controls vanish at each stage's endpoints, closed unitary evolution
obeys the exact work identity

    Delta Tr(H0 rho) = integral Tr(dot G(s) rho(s)) ds.

In particular the absolute changes are bounded by 4n pi/t_s and 4pi/t_p,
respectively. They need not be energy losses. The Markov source's formula
-r(t)Tr(H_app rho), its jump interpretation and its dissipative Q bounds do
not apply. The external controller's own Hamiltonian, clock preparation and
energy storage have not been microscopically constructed; these norm/work
allowances price the supplied driven system, not an omitted autonomous driver.

At the ideal reset endpoint, the H0 moments are precisely the old K moments
of sigma_D. At the ideal final entry endpoint of (18),

    mu_* = Tr(K sigma_D)+alpha0,
    v_* = Var_(sigma_D)(K)+q.                              (25)

Although E retains correlations, these scalar formulas follow from (3) and
free K aging, not from a product assumption on initial sigma. If the actual
complete output differs in trace norm by eps, let beta=h+B. On that SAME
installed H0, with controls now off,

    |mu_actual-mu_*| <= beta eps,
    |Tr[(H0-aI)^2(rho_actual-rho_*)]| <= beta^2 eps,
    |Var_actual(H0)-v_*| <= 3 beta^2 eps.                   (26)

The last inequality adds beta^2 eps for the second moment and 2beta^2 eps for
the squared centered means. For either passive record Q=R or Q=ER, duality
also gives, j=1,2,

    ||Tr_DC[((H0-aI)^j tensor I_Q)(rho_actual-rho_*)]||_1
                         <= beta^j eps,                  (27)

with E traced as well when Q=R. Equation (27) is a linear reference-valued
mathematical record, not a nonlinear conditional variance or an energy reader.
Raw uncentered moments require ||H0|| and ||H0||^2, or the explicitly restored
a-dependent terms. A small trace error has not made high-energy accuracy
free. For desired scalar mean and variance allowances E_mu,E_v>0, it suffices
to impose additionally eps<=min(E_mu/beta,E_v/(3beta^2)).

The exact ideal reset stores the entire n-qubit C state in n reservoir qubits,
including its entanglement with D/R. For orthogonal clock basis inputs with
the same old state, the ideal E outputs are orthogonal. An exactly unitary
replacement with a pure initial environment therefore needs at least 2^n
environment dimensions. This construction supplies that dimension exactly.
For an independent maximally mixed C it transfers n log 2 entropy; it does
not destroy information. The dimension statement is for exact replacement,
not a new approximate lower bound for (15).

E generally cannot be reused as a clean reservoir after one use. Undoing its
information transfer restores the corresponding clock information; discarding
E as a subsystem does not prepare it again. Repeated uses need fresh pure
memory or a separately priced restoration mechanism. Engineered H_E=0 and
zero post-pulse couplings allow finite retained storage within this stipulated
model, but physical memory noise and later reading require their own contract.

## 8. Calibration, actual time and remaining physical operations

All nominal evolution above is on the complete D,C,E space. If the joint ready
input is within eta_ready in trace norm of sigma_DCR tensor vacuum_E, and the
actual full Hermitian generator has an integrable perturbation F(s), then

    eps_total <= min(2, eta_ready+2B t_s+2G_e t_p
                                      +2 integral ||F(s)|| ds). (28)

This follows from initial-state contraction and unitary Duhamel. F may break
excitation conservation or couple an unintended sector; a small restriction
only to the vacuum or one-head subspace is not sufficient for (28). R stays
passive; adversarial couplings to an uncontrolled reference are outside that
model. A finite F bound on the actual whole space is a supplied calibration
certificate, not inferred from a few pulse samples.

For amplitude errors delta_gamma_u(s) on individual pair generators, and
delta_omega(s) on entry, a sufficient contribution is

    4 sum_u integral |delta_gamma_u(s)| ds
                          +4 integral |delta_omega(s)| ds. (29)

This counts every one of n simultaneous controls. It is compatible with the
finite peak and slope allowances in Section 6. For example, shifting the
whole reservoir envelope by d in a common fixed comparison interval, extending
it by zero outside its support, gives

    integral |gamma(s-d)-gamma(s)| ds <= (2pi/t_s)|d|,

and hence the pulse-mismatch contribution 8n pi|d|/t_s. Entry similarly costs
8pi|d|/t_p. These are conservative sufficient bounds; they do not cover a
change of total elapsed time unless that change is also included. Comparison
at a wrong endpoint time dt can separately cost up to 2||H0|| |dt| in trace
norm for post-pulse free evolution (or use the actual time-indexed target).
The full scalar is retained for phase-sensitive map statements.

A general idle gap of duration g may conservatively add 2B g when the desired
comparison retains K but omits H_app; a gap on the ideal reset vacuum is the
special exact case explained after (19). No undefined gap is hidden in t0.
Unwanted reservoir free terms, residual memory couplings, imperfect switching,
finite control precision and a finite later storage interval are examples for
which a declared integral in (28) is required. If a fixed storage disturbance
has norm bound nu for time T_store, the additional trace allowance is at most
2nu T_store relative to the stipulated decoupled model.

No terminal measurement is required for the reset/preparation endpoint. A
subsequent classical flag, physical energy measurement, nonzero-duration reader
or autonomous pulse scheduler has not been constructed by these equations.
Its actual backaction and full elapsed time cannot be replaced by a promised
instantaneous channel. The result is therefore a finite externally scheduled
unitary supplier of the prepared state, with both its environment and its
remaining physical interfaces visible.

## 9. Failed shortcuts, selected evidence and exact closure

Three tempting shortcuts fail. First, the boundary leaves no extra neighbour
at q0, so an unlisted central reset port is not available; Section 2 uses the
clock sites themselves. Second, a dirty reservoir does not reset C under
SWAP: it places that dirty input into C. Third, requiring both a reset clock
and the unchanged pure reservoir would erase arbitrary orthogonal inputs by a
unitary, which is impossible by preservation of inner products. The retained
E target in (12) is essential even when only (13) is wanted later.

A further rejected inference would transfer the Markov trajectory or its
monotone energy balance to these pulses. Only the common endpoint objective is
reproduced with the error (18). This note makes no native cooling, detailed
balance, thermalization, infinite reuse or broad no-go claim.

The independently written selected exact control in `selected_control.py`
addresses one concrete risk: a purported reset endpoint that silently loses
old/clock/reference correlations or returns dirty memory as if pure. It checks
the complete toy ready-domain SWAP isometry, a specified correlated input and
missing-SWAP/dirty-memory/common-phase mutants. It also checks the explicit
matching at one L=8 layout. Its plan, input and source are sealed before its
single execution. This finite discriminator is not a simulation of the
boundary many-body dynamics, a numerical proof of (14), or an all-L geometry
test. General geometry and the arbitrary-reference bound are the analytic
arguments above. Actual results, including any failure, belong in the report
and full preserved streams rather than being anticipated here.

The construction removes the supplied Markov-generator/bath realization
premise for this finite reset endpoint. It replaces it with n explicit fresh
pure qubits, a collision-free NN matching, degenerate isolated memory and
calibrated positive finite pulses plus an external schedule. It preserves
every old term and every clock term during those pulses. It closes neither
the native supply of purity/vacancies/controls nor autonomous scheduling,
permanent storage, a classical reader, arbitrary crowded-layout access or a
fixed-cap efficiency theorem. These are actual remaining interfaces, not
assumptions implicitly certified by the endpoint mathematics.
