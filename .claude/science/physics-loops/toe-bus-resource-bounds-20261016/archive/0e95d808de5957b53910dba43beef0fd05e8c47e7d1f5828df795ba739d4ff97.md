## 1. Finite invariant-envelope corrector

Keep the exact parent's Hermitian K0,V,A2, isometric J with K0J=JF and J*VJ=0. Suppose finite subspaces H1 subset H2 of the actual Hilbert space both contain Ran J, reduce K0, and satisfy

    V Ran J subset H1,
    V H1 subset H2,
    A2 Ran J subset H2.

Let P=JJ*, Q=I-P. Assume only dist(spec(K0 restricted to H2 intersect Q),spec F)>=delta>0. No gap is required in the orthogonal complement of H2. For every F eigenspace define Z1 by the restricted Q resolvent. Because H1 is invariant and contains VJ, Z1 has image in H1. Define D by the unchanged secular compression of J*(VZ1+A2J). This D is Hermitian and commutes with F. Define Z2 by the restricted H2/Q Sylvester equation and the same nonzero P/P divisors; set equal-energy P/P blocks to zero. The three support assumptions guarantee there is no omitted right-hand-side component outside H2.

Then the equations are identities in the actual full Hilbert space, not projected dynamics:

    K0 Z1-Z1 F=-VJ,
    K0 Z2-Z2 F=JD-VZ1-A2J,
    (K0+xi V+xi^2 A2)(J+xi Z1+xi^2 Z2)
       -(J+xi Z1+xi^2 Z2)(F+xi^2 D)
      =xi^3(VZ2+A2Z1-Z1D)+xi^4(A2Z2-Z2D).

VZ2 and A2Z1 may leave H2; their full norms are explicitly charged in the residual. Unitary Duhamel therefore gives the same bare-input, all-output bound as parent equation28 for t<=t_*/xi^2+w. No invariant-envelope assumption on the actual perturbed Hamiltonian is made. Parent Frobenius bounds hold with this restricted delta and the existing connected P/P divisor bound. The finite moment corollary is separately algebraic and does not itself create this gap.

A minimal discriminator should include an exactly resonant but unreachable spectator: K0=diag(0,1,0), J=e0, V=|0><1|+|1><0|, A2=0, H1=H2=span(e0,e1). Here Z1=-e1, D=-1, Z2=0 despite zero full Q/P gap. Adding V12=V21=1 violates V H1 subset H2; extending H2 then exposes a genuine second-order resonant obstruction. Merely removing reachable resonances from an inverse would be invalid.

## 2. Actual source envelopes without a spin-spectrum ansatz

For q length-N sources let S be the tensor product of their fully symmetric spaces, dimension (N+1)^q. Let G_r be the span of O_A v for v in S and operators O_A supported on at most r of the qN source sites, including arbitrary operator products across different sources. Then

    dim G_r <= (N+1)^q sum_(s=0)^r binom(qN,s)4^s.

Use the span, not an assertion that these vectors are linearly independent. Each adjacent source permutation maps this span to itself by conjugating O_A and fixing every symmetric v. Thus each source Laplacian sum(I-SWAP) preserves G_r. Each total source number also preserves G_r: commute it through O_A, whose commutator is still supported on A, and use that S is invariant. Hence the actual free source Hamiltonian reduces G_r, including nonsymmetric components; no free-fermion formula for the Heisenberg chain is used.

Let B_r be the actual bus subspace with at most r excitations. Number-conserving XY hopping, including the branched router, preserves B_r. Put

    H1=H_old tensor B_1 tensor G_1,
    H2=H_old tensor B_2 tensor G_2.

The old port term changes bus number by one, without source action. A source/bus exchange changes bus number by one and acts on one source site. Therefore VJ is in H1 and V H1 is in H2. Every source counterterm in A2 is at most two-local, so A2J is in H2. These are precisely the hypotheses in section1. For B actual bus sites,

    dim H2 <= d [1+B+binom(B,2)] (N+1)^q
                    [1+4qN+16 binom(qN,2)].

For fixed d,q and B polynomial in N this is polynomial in N. It is an ambient dimension bound, not a full computational implementation. A finite basis and its matrix elements must be made effective without enumerating 2^(qN) vectors if computational complexity is claimed. Local operators on at most four sites between Dicke basis vectors suggest direct binomial formulas; this remains a requested check.

## 3. Quantitative separation inside the envelope

At rho=0 the actual bus core is an open XY path and routers are isolated, as in the parent. Restricting to <=2 bus quanta still yields subset sums of core eigenvalues and integer router occupations. Source energies in G2 have form sum omega_l k_l+JF a with a>=0. A Q state has either bus excitation or nonsymmetric source content, so every difference from a code energy remains a nonconstant affine form in mu_j,nu,JF. The number of pair forms is at most M=(dim H2) D_c, D_c=d(N+1)^q; this conservative bound counts multiplicities.

An elementary source-gap estimate may suffice for the nonzero JF coefficient. Write l=N(N-1)/2, N>=2. Every permutation is a product of at most l adjacent swaps. For arbitrary psi, telescoping that product gives

    ||(I-U_pi)psi||^2 <= l^2 sum_j ||(I-SWAP_j)psi||^2
                      =2 l^2 <psi,L_unit psi>.

Averaging over all permutations gives 2||(I-P_sym)psi||^2 on the left, hence L_unit >= l^(-2)(I-P_sym). For sums over disjoint sources, every positive source eigenvalue a is at least l^(-2). This crude N^-4 lower bound is sufficient; no sharp spectral-gap theorem is imported.

Suppose the existing admissible parameter box has coordinate side lengths at least w0>0, with all closed subboxes strictly inside the cone-valid positive regions. Every affine form has some coefficient magnitude at least a0=min(1,l^-2). A slab |f|<=eta occupies at most fraction 2eta/(a0 w0), by conditioning on a coordinate with such a coefficient. With M forms and

    delta0=a0 w0/(8M),

the union of slabs |f|<=2delta0 occupies at most half the box. A nonempty open complement gives a rational choice with every difference >2delta0; certified finite enclosures can find one with all differences >delta0. This proves a specified sufficient restricted gap, not a random-success claim. No arbitrary exact-zero test is used.

The source bound also applies if the chosen invariant envelope contains extra, unreachable sectors. They only enlarge M. For rho>0, the same full-operator bound R rho for all router edges preserves at least delta0-R rho within H2, because H2 remains invariant under number-conserving routing and the zero-routing core is isospectral as weights vary. Choose R rho<delta0/2. Calibration must stay in the original weight box and retain its own actual derivative bounds.

## 4. Remaining checks and scope

The compressed source self-energy identity is load bearing: the parent's inverse c+L was defined on the full source. It should suffice on G1 (which also reduces global SU(2) because local supports are rotated without enlarging them), but the raising/lowering derivation and its one-hole coefficients must be checked explicitly. Reuse the parent's connected two-action classification only after verifying the compressed inverse acts identically on every reached vector. A2 may leak into G2 and must remain in Z2.

The code's P/P divisor classification is unchanged and still requires every stated strong spectral premise. The newly accepted quantitative bias selection may later supply those divisors, but is not needed to state this finite conditional lemma. Native initialization, phase and timing reference, positive occupied-edge permissions, actual full-space norm estimates, all controller outputs and separate energy/release budgets remain unchanged.

Even a polynomial restricted gap does not alone prove polynomial compiler runtime: actual box widths, calibration derivatives, routed responses, source coefficients and geometry must all be tracked. Long routing paths may produce very small Green functions. No favorable total scaling should be stated until these independent factors are bounded. No claim about exact final halting or reset/reuse follows.
