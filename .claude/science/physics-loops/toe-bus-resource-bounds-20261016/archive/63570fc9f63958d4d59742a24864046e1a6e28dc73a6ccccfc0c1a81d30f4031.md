## 1. The restricted equations really are full-space equations

Write `P=JJ*`, and let `P_i` project onto the proposed `H_i`. Since `K0` is Hermitian, `K0 J=JF`, and `H_i` reduce `K0` while containing `Ran J`, the subspaces

`Q_i = H_i intersect Ran Q`

reduce `K0`, and `H_i=Ran J direct-sum Q_i`. In particular the inverse of `E-K0|Q_2` preserves `Q_1`. This last fact follows from the orthogonal reducing decomposition `Q_2=Q_1 direct-sum (Q_2 intersect H_1^perp)`; it is not an assumption that a projected inverse preserves arbitrary supports.

`J*VJ=0` and `V Ran J subset H_1` put every `VJ Pi_E` in `Q_1`. Define

`Z1 Pi_E = (E-K0|Q_2)^-1 VJ Pi_E`,

with that inverse included into the actual Hilbert space. Then `Ran Z1 subset Q_1` and

`K0 Z1-Z1 F = -VJ`

holds in the entire Hilbert space. The secular compression

`D = sum_E Pi_E J*(VZ1+A2J) Pi_E`

is Hermitian: each V contribution is a diagonal block of `J*V (E-K0|Q_2)^-1 VJ` with a self-adjoint real resolvent. The A2 contribution is Hermitian too. By definition `[D,F]=0`.

The second right-hand side `R=JD-VZ1-A2J` lies in `H_2`: this uses **both** `VH_1 subset H_2` and `A2 Ran J subset H_2`. Its equal-energy P/P blocks vanish. In `Q_2/P`, solve by the certified restricted spectral gap; in unequal-energy P/P blocks divide by the parent’s classified nonzero connected energy differences. Set the equal-energy P/P blocks to zero. The resulting `Z2` has image in `H_2`. Its omitted exterior rows are exactly zero on both sides because `H_2` reduces K0. Thus

`K0 Z2-Z2 F = R`

is another full-space identity even if `K0|H_2^perp` has eigenvalues equal to code energies.

Multiplying `Z=J+xi Z1+xi^2 Z2` gives exactly

`(K0+xi V+xi^2 A2)Z-Z(F+xi^2 D)=xi^3 R3+xi^4 R4`,

`R3=VZ2+A2Z1-Z1D`, `R4=A2Z2-Z2D`.

There is no projection in these residuals. In particular VZ2 and A2Z1 can leave `H_2`. The endpoint identity

`U J-J Ueff = U(J-Z)+(UZ-ZUeff)+(Z-J)Ueff`

and unitary Duhamel imply the parent’s bound

`2xi||Z1||+2xi^2||Z2||+t(xi^3||R3||+xi^4||R4||)`.

For `t<=t_*/xi^2+w`, `xi<=1`, the same `xi C_corr` majorant follows. Neither Z nor the physical input must be an isometry/dressed input. Tensoring a passive reference leaves these operator-norm bounds unchanged.

The abstract Sylvester statement is an existence statement. Effective construction in this bus still uses the parent’s known connected P/P support and zero classifications; the restricted gap alone is not an equality oracle for an arbitrary F.

## 2. The actual bus and source envelopes meet the hypotheses

Let S be the tensor product of the q fully symmetric length-N source spaces. Choose a four-element operator basis at each source site. The vectors `O_A v`, with `|A|<=r` and v in a Dicke-product basis of S, span `G_r`; hence

`dim G_r <= (N+1)^q sum_(s=0)^r binom(qN,s)4^s`.

This is an upper bound on a span, with duplicates and dependencies allowed. It is not a claim that separated sites are available as new physical controls: these local operators only label a mathematical basis.

For an adjacent source swap U, `U O_A v=(U O_A U*)v`; its support has the same size. Thus every such swap and each source Laplacian preserve `G_r`. Hermiticity gives reduction. For the number of a particular source,

`N_l O_A v = O_A N_l v + [N_l,O_A]v`,

and the commutator has support contained in A. This proves number invariance, including operators spread across different sources. Independent global SU(2) rotations of each source conjugate local operators without increasing support and preserve S; consequently `G_r` also reduces these rotations and their generators.

The full branched XY bus conserves excitation number, so its actual subspace `B_r` of at most r quanta reduces the bus Hamiltonian. No branched free-fermion assertion is needed. Therefore `H_1=H_old tensor B_1 tensor G_1` and `H_2=H_old tensor B_2 tensor G_2` reduce K0 and contain the code.

The old-port term changes bus number by one and has no source action. A source exchange changes bus number by one and applies one local source operator. Multiplying a one-local source operator by an operator supported on at most one site gives support on at most two sites, including the case of different sources. Hence `VJ subset H_1` and `VH_1 subset H_2`. Every individual term of the actual A2 in parent equation (20) is at most two-local on a source, so `A2J subset H_2`. It need not lie in H1.

The proposed dimension bound follows from `dim B_2=1+B+binom(B,2)` (terms beyond the available number of sites are zero):

`dim H_2 <= d [1+B+binom(B,2)] (N+1)^q [1+4qN+16 binom(qN,2)]`.

This is polynomial in N for fixed d,q and polynomial B. In the exact parent B is already fixed by the old plant. It says nothing favorable about scaling in the old-qubit count, since d itself is the full old Hilbert-space dimension.

## 3. The source inverse must be restricted, but the coefficient survives

`c+L|g_1` is invertible.

To see this, suppose an L-eigenspace in g1 has eigenvalue alpha with `c+alpha=0`. Because g1 reduces SU(2), that eigenspace is a sum of complete spin multiplets. Comparing it with the code state whose source excitation is n+1 gives energy difference `lambda-omega_l+alpha=0`, contradicting the restricted gap. The alpha=0 symmetric case is covered as well.

Define the new operator

`T_c = inclusion_g1 (c+L|g_1)^-1 projection_g1`.

It commutes with the source SU(2) generators and source number. On the symmetric part it is `1/c`. The parent’s actual ladder argument then applies: `S_- chi_k=sqrt(k(N-k+1)) chi_(k-1)` and `S_+ chi_N=0`. `P_s sigma_+^1 T_c sigma_-^1 P_s`

`= k(N-k+1)/(N^2 c) + b_N(c) k(k-1)/N^2`,

where `b_N(c)=N/(N-1)<chi_N,T_c chi_N>`.

Every one-hole computational basis vector equals `sigma_-^j |N>`, so the entire one-hole sector lies in g1. It also reduces L. Computing b_N from that full N-dimensional one-hole matrix therefore gives exactly this compressed coefficient; it does not require the inverse on all `2^N` source states.

## 4. The source-gap and slab constants check

Let `ell=N(N-1)/2`, N>=2. Every permutation can be sorted using at most ell adjacent swaps, so write its unitary as a word of length m<=ell. Telescoping `I-U_1...U_m` gives a sum of prefix unitaries times `(I-U_j)`. Applying Cauchy–Schwarz to this sum, then bounding the multiplicity of each adjacent swap by ell, yields

`||(I-U_pi)psi||^2 <= ell^2 sum_j ||(I-SWAP_j)psi||^2`.

Because a swap is a self-adjoint involution, the sum on the right is `2<psi,L_unit psi>`. Averaging the left over all permutations gives `2||(I-P_sym)psi||^2`. Therefore

`L_unit >= ell^-2 (I-P_sym)`.

This proof uses group averaging to establish an inequality; it does not propose enumerating N! permutations as an algorithm. For disjoint sources, the L_l commute and are positive, and the kernel of their sum is the tensor-product symmetric space. Every positive eigenvalue of the unit-scale sum is consequently at least ell^-2, including after restriction to G2.

At rho=0, the open bus core alone has the parent’s subset-sum spectrum. Restriction to at most two bus quanta selects the allowed subsets and router occupations; it does not change the source coefficients. Since G2 reduces every source L_l and N_l, simultaneous source eigenvectors still give differences

`sum_(j in S) mu_j + r nu + J_F a + (E_b-E_c) + sum_l omega_l z_l`.

## 5. Effective source bases without exponential computational vectors

The proposal leaves this as a requested implementation argument. The following is an exposed reviewer refinement, not a claim that the root had implemented it.

Use unnormalized Dicke vectors `D_k=sum_(|x|=k)|x>` and local matrix units with rational entries. For an operator O supported on a fixed set of s sites,

`<D_k,O D_l> = sum_(a,b in {0,1}^s) O_ab 1_(k-|a|=l-|b|) binom(N-s,k-|a|)`,

where the binomial coefficient is zero outside its allowed range. The formula follows by counting the identical outside bit string required by the inner product. For multiple sources the expression factors source by source. Thus the Gram matrix of all `O_A D_k` spanning vectors can be assembled from binomial integers without enumerating `2^(qN)` basis vectors. For G2, products of two labels involve at most four sites. Exact rational Gaussian elimination selects an independent spanning subset; exact rank decisions here are on explicitly rational Gram matrices, not on arbitrary computable target entries. Normalization can be done with certified algebraic square roots afterward.

The free source action is also effective in this basis: a swap conjugates the local-operator label, and source number acts through its commutator plus the known Dicke excitation. Their coefficients can be reduced to the chosen basis using its nonsingular Gram matrix. One- and two-local images of code vectors are already among the labels. Tensor this with the old supplied spectral basis and the bus basis of at most two quanta. This supplies finite reduced matrices and reached right-hand sides without constructing exponentially many source components.

For fixed q and fixed local-action order the list sizes, binomial bit lengths and exact linear algebra are polynomially bounded in N. That limited source-representation statement is separate from the precision cost of the supplied old data, spectral search, calibration, routed responses, corrector magnitudes and final runtime.
