# Dependency and inequality ledger, before final derivation

Scope: one fixed finite strongly regular old H, fixed B/target/time unit/window, the accepted fixed bus graph, computable strictly certified fixed parameter boxes. The reachable-envelope proposal was unaccepted on exposure; root subsequently supplied acceptance d83e5d3c, with mandatory restricted source inverse and full residual norms. No shrinking-bias uniformity is assumed.

Put n=N+1, q=d(d-1)/2, m=2(d-1), b=m+4q. All constants in this ledger are independent of N and epsilon, but may depend very badly on the fixed old data. They must have supplied or computably certified positive lower/finite upper bounds. No numerical value or favorable size is inferred from compactness alone.

| Quantity | Sufficient scaling | Actual reason |
|---|---|---|
| source fluctuation | N>=C_N epsilon^-2 | Accepted complete-output source estimate |

| unit source nonzero gap | >=n^-4 | Accepted adjacent-permutation inequality |

Independent expansion gives p=18q^2+65q+30 and 2+4p=72q^2+260q+122. This is a very large sufficient exponent, not an optimum. For q=1 it is454.

Remaining derivation obligations: uniform fixed calibration/core-hop certificates; strict finite parameter grid with a guaranteed margin; source inverse implemented on the full one-hole sector, never the full source; all-error xi inequalities with explicit constants; a concrete input-precision prescription and separate exact Gram arithmetic conditioning; actual old/source phases and positive reserves; exact boundary for shrinking H+sC.
