# No-go ledger

- C1: in the class of one-qubit, star-supported, cubic-covariant Pauli
  stabilizer rules, no point excitation is both three-dimensionally mobile and
  fermionic, and with soldered labels no nonzero Chamon charge has rank-three translation mobility; zero/local excitations are excluded. Scope
  and routes around the claim are in the note's N1–N8 gate.
- C2: with two qubits per site and soldered spin, no free-single-site-variable-preserving operator moves
  a charge in three dimensions. Rules without free local variables have no
  three-dimensionally mobile nonzero charge.
- C3: three axis qubits, soldered, C4/C3-fixed strings. No maximal rule has
  bounded nonzero code dimension together with a three-dimensionally mobile
  charge. Unsoldered labels do allow one (eight interleaved toric codes).
- C4: For the stated soldered rotation-fixed class, rank growth and the alphabet lemma exclude bounded code dimension together with free-variable-preserving charge motion. Among the 36 supplied maximal unsoldered C4/C3-fixed rules selected by equal nonzero log2 code dimension at L=4 and L=6, every nonzero same-site charge pattern for which the radius-1 tube solver finds legs from 0 to 4e_x,4e_y,4e_z in the radius-5 box has T-junction sign +1. The finite rank selection is not an asymptotic classification of all bounded-dimension rules. Other supports, strides and non-maximal rules remain open.
