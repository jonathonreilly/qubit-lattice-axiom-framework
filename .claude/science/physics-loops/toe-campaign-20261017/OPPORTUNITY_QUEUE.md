00. **The order-4 BKSF codes: an anomaly or an error?**
   - Under the bare action all three C2 have eta = -1, and every W-twist keeps the number of -1 odd.
   - A nondegenerate fermion admits only even-minus patterns on D2 (abelian extensions).
   - Decide whether the bare D2 action has a symmetry-localization obstruction (an H^3-type anomaly) or
     whether lambda = theta*eta fails for some C2.
   - The same constraint explains why w2 on all of O (which contains D2) is hard for a nondegenerate
     single species. It might be impossible, which would be a genuine no-go for one-state spin-1/2
     fermions in these codes.

   and its spin-1/2 action from Admissibility, or must they be supplied? A related question: a
   Lieb-Schultz-Mattis-type analysis of which site contents admit a symmetric vacuum.
0. ~~A single species with w2 on all of O.~~ Impossible on general grounds. -1 lies in the commutator
   subgroup of 2O (already in Q8), so no one-state fermion can carry w2 on O. Cubic spin-1/2 needs a
   doublet. **New target:** an O-covariant code whose emergent fermion is a doublet (two degenerate states,
   for example two species exchanged or mixed by rotations) carrying w2. That is the electron-like case.
1. **Which realization does the dynamics select?** (partly answered: Majorana-only hopping selects the
   trivial class, in both families)
   - The spin class is a property of the rotation's action on excited states. The on-site phase
     W = prod exp(-i pi B/4) toggles eta by w2 and is invisible in the ground space.
   - Determine which realization is a symmetry of natural hopping Hamiltonians (Majorana vs
     number-conserving) in both families.
   - Determine whether a U(1) charge with a spin-charge relation (Spin^c-like) forces the w2 choice.
     This is the sharpest route to an electron-like spin-1/2 prediction.
