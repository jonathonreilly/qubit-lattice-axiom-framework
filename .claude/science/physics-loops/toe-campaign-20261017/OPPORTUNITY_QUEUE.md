# Opportunity queue (ranked)

1. **C2 — two qubits per site (spin, soldered) x (flavour).** This is the B3
   site.
   - Classify covariant NN Pauli rules.
   - Look for a charge that is mobile in three dimensions. Compute its
     exchange (Levin–Wen) and the rotation fractionalization C4^4 = +-1 on an
     isolated charge (a spinorial label).
   - This tests the escape mirrored from the free-tick B3 note.
2. **C3 — block rules on staggered 2x2x2 blocks** (the B6 spinorial class),
   with block-centred covariance.
3. **C4 — beyond Pauli on one qubit per site.**
   - Covariant NN commuting projectors in general, starting with rank-one
     conditions and "site given neighbours" forms.
   - Equivalently, whether any covariant NN rule pins a site's own possibility
     under soldering.
4. **C5 — generations.** Count the species and foliation labels of the
   surviving rules against the C3 axis labels. Chamon's four foliations are
   body diagonals, which are exactly the C3 axes of O.
5. **Planon statistics in the Chamon rule** (known in the literature;
   reproduce only if a later block needs it).
