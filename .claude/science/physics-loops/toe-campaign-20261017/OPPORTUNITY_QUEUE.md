# Opportunity queue (ranked)

1. **C3 — beyond Pauli: covariant nearest-neighbour commuting projectors on
   one qubit per site.**
   - Both C1 and C2 are Pauli-only. The axiom's possibility domain is all of
     M_2(C), so general rank-one or projector conditions are the natural next
     class.
   - First decisive check: classify covariant nearest-neighbour projector
     conditions on the star that commute with all translates (frustration-
     free, commuting).
   - Question: does any such rule have a three-dimensionally mobile
     excitation without free local variables?
2. **C4 — block rules on staggered 2x2x2 blocks** (the B6 spinorial class),
   with block-centred covariance. The C2 example's eight charge types are
   labelled by position mod 2, the same eight corners.
3. **C5 — spin-unsoldered two-qubit sites** (253,215 candidate strings),
   for completeness.
4. **C6 — generations.** Count species and foliation labels against the C3
   axis labels. Chamon's four foliations are the body diagonals, which are the
   C3 axes of O.
5. **Coherence of record-writing motion.** In the C2 example, compute
   whether the record-writing hops can be made reversible with a dynamics on
   the free variables. This would make the "no coherent motion" reading
   precise.
