(1) COMMUTING, CODE DIMENSION: CONFIRMED (stronger than claimed)
  - L=4, all 720 orderings, half-edge model <B, R, S>: all generators commute; the signed group is consistent
    (no -I); log2 dim = 384 - 381 = 3. The edge model gives 192 - 189 = 3, the same.
  - L=6: log2 dim = 3 for a representative of every one of the 60 codes. The note checks only one code per
    orbit type.
  - S_p = cyclic product of the four A's. Its sign does not depend on the start point or the direction (all 720
    orderings, all 3 planes), and it is Hermitian. So "the product of the four A around a plaquette" is
    well defined, and it equals the companion note's flux-0 generator.

(2) EXACT COVARIANCE: CONFIRMED
  - The site action is (v,d) -> (Rv, Rd), with Pauli labels unchanged. Under it,
    P A^o_(v,a) P^-1 = A^{Ro}_{R(v,a)}, including phase. Here Ro = (R d1, ..., R d6), and the rotated edge
    has tail Rv if R(+a) is positive, and R(v+e_a) otherwise.
  - Checked for every vertex and axis at L=4, all 24 rotations and all 720 orderings: 3,317,760 checks,
    0 failures.
  - B_v -> B_{Rv} and R_e -> R_{Re} hold exactly.
  - The intertwining also holds at the state level. Enc|z_e> = |z_e z_e> on the two halves, so
    P_site Enc = Enc P_edge, with no phases.

(3) STABILIZERS AND ORBITS: CONFIRMED
  - The stabilizers were computed directly. For the site action, I permuted the half-edge generators and
    tested membership. For the edge action, I permuted the edge generators. Item (2) was not used.
  - The 720 orderings fall into 60 codes, and the half-edge and edge partitions are identical.
  - For all 60 codes, the site stabilizer equals the edge stabilizer:
      12 codes: orbit 4,  stabilizer D3 = {E, 2 C3, 3 edge C2}
      12 codes: orbit 6,  stabilizer {E, 1 axis C2, 2 edge C2}

  - The stabilizer of the identity ordering is the same at L = 4, 5, 6 and 8, in both models.

(4) TRANSFER: SOUND, WITH WORDING CORRECTIONS
  Direct tests in the half-edge model, with phases:
  a. Levin-Wen T-junction, straight A-strings of length 2, L=6: -1 for all 20 triples in all 60 codes.
     The toric-code control (A = XX only) gives +1 for all 20.
  b. Sign sectors, L=4. All 8 translation-invariant S-sign sectors are consistent. In the flux-0 and flux-pi
     sectors, every stabilizing rotation maps the signed group to itself under the bare site permutation.
     In each of the 6 mixed sectors, 44 codes have a stabilizing rotation that needs a correction. The edge
     model gives the same numbers, as the bare-permutation note states.
  c. Pair test (check_4b, L=8), for all 108 (code, C2) pairs (36 D3-type, 36 D2-type including the 12 axis
     C2, 36 Z2-type), in the flux-0 and flux-pi sectors.
     Method:
       - S = S0 C, where S0 is an A^o hop string from x to Rx, and |x - Rx|_inf = 4.
       - C is solved over GF(2) on the radius-1 boxes around x and Rx so that syn(S) = syn(P_R S P_R^-1).
       - Each accepted S carries gauge-invariant charge 1 at x and at Rx, and has no excitation outside the
         boxes.
       - lambda = eigenvalue of S^dag (P_R S P_R^-1) on the signed code space.
       - Two x per pair, and 7 random solutions per x (nullspace shifts).
     Result: lambda = +1 in every trial, in:
       - the half-edge model with R = +ZZ (the note's model);
       - the half-edge model with R = -ZZ;
       - the edge model (cross-check).
     Controls (half-edge site action, toric code):
       - zero flux: +1 under the vertex C2 and under the plaquette-centred C2;
       - pi flux: +1 under the vertex C2 and -1 under the plaquette-centred C2.
     So the test can return -1.
     With theta_f = -1 from (a), eta_f(C2,C2) = -1 for every C2 of every code under the bare site action.
     This reproduces the bare-permutation note's spin-1/2 result directly in the site-local model.
  d. Repetition sign. Uniform R = -ZZ (with B = +Z^6 and S unchanged) is also consistent and symmetric, and
     gives lambda = +1. So the uniform sign does not matter. Axis-dependent R signs would break the
     axis-permuting rotations, because P maps R_e to R_{Re} with the sign of the source edge's axis. The note
     fixes R = +ZZ, which is fine.

  Corrections and caveats for the note's transfer item:
  i. Fermion charge in the half-edge model. B_v alone is NOT the gauge-invariant charge.
     - A single X on a half-edge flips B_v together with R_e and some S_p. A local operator can therefore
       move the fermion's "B-defect" onto a broken repetition bond.
     - In check_4b, most symmetric solutions S have even B-parity at x and at Rx, yet carry charge 1 measured
       by Q_Omega = prod_{v in Omega} B_v * prod_{e inside Omega} R_e (the Z flux through the boundary).
     - Suggested wording: the point charge is a B_v or R_e violation, and the charge in a region is measured
       by Q_Omega, not by prod B_v. This does not change any conclusion; sectors are local-unitary invariant.
  ii. "Verbatim" should keep the realization caveat of the bare-permutation note's item 6.
     - P_R W, with W = prod_v exp(-i pi B_v/4), is also a site-local realization here. B_v is a one-site
       operator, so W is site-local. It gives the trivial class.
     - The spin-1/2 class therefore belongs to the bare site action, not to the site-local code. The note's
       item 4 lists "the spin-1/2 fractionalization class under bare rotations", which is correct, but it
       should say the dressing remark transfers as well.
  iii. "Holds verbatim" should be read as "within the declared encoding family".
     - "No O-invariant code, minimal orbit four" is a statement about the half-edge images of the 60 BKSF
       codes.
     - Other six-qubit or site-local Pauli codes, such as GSE with on-site Clifford rotation actions, are not
       covered. N1 already says this.
  iv. Methodological warning for any runner. A naive pair test (S = A^o string, S^R = its image) does NOT
     work for most pairs.
     - P A^o_e P^-1 = A^{Ro}_{Re}, which equals the A^o hop times endpoint vertex terms. So S and S^R have
       different syndromes.
     - In check_4.py this left 84 of 108 pairs undecided (T not in the group), with none at -1.
     - The syndrome-symmetrized method in (c) is required. If the runner's pair test (if any) uses
       hops only, it covers only the 24 pairs where the hops are P_R-clean.
     - Windows must also not let the correction bridge x and Rx. In my first attempt, overlapping boxes let
       C annihilate the pair. The gauge-invariant charge check above guards against this.

PRIOR WORK

  - Setia, Bravyi, Mezzacapo, Whitfield, "Superfast encodings for fermionic quantum simulation",
    Phys. Rev. Research 1, 033033 (2019), arXiv:1810.05274: the citation is correct.
    - The GSE places d/2 qubits on each vertex of even degree d, with one local Majorana per incident edge.
      On the cubic lattice this is 3 qubits per site, the same total count as edge BKSF (3 edges per site).
      Source: web search snippets only; full text unreachable from this sandbox.
    - The note's description "qubits attached to vertices" is accurate.
  - Suggested additions:
    (a) The half-edge encoding doubles the qubit count relative to both edge BKSF and GSE. GSE is the
        economical site-local BKSF-type encoding.
    (b) In GSE, a site rotation permutes the local Majoranas. On 3 qubits that is generally an on-site
        Clifford, not a bare qubit permutation. So the "bare action" question there is different, as N1 notes.
  - Novelty: the repetition (half-edge) splitting is elementary, as the note says. The "new" observation, that
    the site permutation reproduces the edge permutation, holds by construction once the qubits carry
    direction labels. Calling it an observation rather than a result is appropriate. I found no prior
    statement of it, but my literature search was limited.
