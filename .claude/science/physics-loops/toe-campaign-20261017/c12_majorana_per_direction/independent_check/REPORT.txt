Conventions: gamma_{v,d} with d = (+x,+y,+z,-x,-y,-z) -> JW index 0..5; B_v = i*gamma_0..gamma_5 = Z1Z2Z3
exactly. S_p = A(v,a)A(v+a,b)A(v+b,a)A(v,b) (the ordered product; cyclic shifts and reversal give the same
operator). Sectors = signs (s_yz, s_zx, s_xy). Group membership with sign comes from an exact echelon tableau,
and its rank was cross-checked against numpy GF(2) elimination.

(1) SECTORS. CONFIRMED.
  L=4 and L=6: all 8 sectors give Hermitian, pairwise commuting generators, the group does not contain -I,
  and log2 dim = 3. The same holds at L=2 and L=8.
  Remark (outside the note's stated scope, but relevant to "8 translation-invariant sectors"): on ODD L
  (3, 5, 7) only +++ is consistent. The other 7 sectors, including the uniform --- sector, contain -I, and
  +++ has log2 dim 2. So "8 sectors / two uniform sectors / dim 3" holds on even tori only.

(2) T-JUNCTION. CONFIRMED.
  At L=5 (legs of length 2), L=6 (2), L=7 (3) and L=10 (4), every straight leg commutes with all S_p and
  flips B at {o, end} only. W1W2W3 = s W3W2W1 gives s = -1 for 20 of 20 triples. The bosonic control gives
  +1 for 20 of 20.
  The result is structural: each leg holds exactly one Majorana at the junction, gamma_{o,d}, and these are
  pairwise anticommuting and distinct.

(3) ROTATION COVARIANCE. CONFIRMED, and it holds more widely than stated.
  - All 24 rotations x 32 admissible eps preserve the signed groups of +++ and --- at L=4 AND L=6.
    Every generator image was checked, with its sign.
  - B_v -> +B_{Rv} exactly.
  - Non-uniform sectors go to the sector with permuted orientations.
  - The 32 non-admissible eps never preserve the group, because they send B -> -B.
  - The 24 improper elements (O_h \ O), with the same determinant-type eps condition, also preserve the
    uniform groups in 1536 of 1536 cases. So the code is O_h-covariant with unitary on-site Cliffords; the
    note understates this.
  - The check is close to a tautology. With translation-invariant eps, every plaquette contains each of its
    Majorana directions exactly twice, so S_p -> (prod eps^2) S_{Rp} = S_{Rp} for ANY eps, and
    B -> (prod eps)(sgn) B. The 24 x 32 scan therefore confirms a two-line argument.

(4) GROUP LAW. CONFIRMED.
  - Sizes over the 1024 (C4, C3) eps choices: 48 occurs 128 times, 192 occurs 384 times, 768 occurs 512
    times. All of them contain -1.
  - In the 128 order-48 groups the kernel onto O is exactly {+1, -1}. Every axis-C2 lift squares to +1 and
    every edge-C2 lift squares to -1.
  - The squares are forced. Over all admissible lifts, axis C2 squares are +1 (24) or not +-1 (72), never
    -1. Edge C2 squares are -1 (48) or not +-1 (144), never +1.
  - Addition: all 128 groups have element orders {1:1, 2:7, 3:8, 4:24, 6:8}, with no element of order 8.
    The even part is A4 x Z2. The group is A4 x| Z4, the extension pulled back from sgn: S4 -> Z2. It is
    NOT the spin double cover 2O (which has order-8 elements and axis-C2 lifts of order 4), and NOT GL(2,3).
    The note's phrase "central extension of O by fermion parity" is true but unspecific; it should name
    this class.

(5) INTERPRETATION ATTACK.
  - Locality: legitimate. Every (R, eps) is implemented by an explicit 8x8 on-site unitary (built and
    checked), which is a 3-qubit Clifford, composed with a permutation of site blocks. That is a strictly
    on-site symmetry. However, 736 of the 768 on-site Cliffords are ENTANGLING: every non-identity
    rotation needs one. A bare permutation of the 3 site qubits implements only the identity rotation.
  - "One species": fine as worded. B-violations are the only point charge; plaquette violations form
    loops. The single species maps to itself.
  - Overreach in the interpretation section. "The species count is a statement about the symmetry action"
    is not established. The comparison changes two things at once:
      (a) the encoding: 3 qubits per site with Majoranas per direction, versus BKSF qubits on edges;
      (b) the action: entangling on-site Cliffords, versus bare permutations.
    The BKSF companion's independent check had already allowed per-edge single-qubit Cliffords without
    success. It is not shown that the BKSF code itself becomes O-invariant under some local Clifford
    action. So the evidence is: this different encoding, with a different kind of action, is covariant.
  - The Majorana-level extension is not the emergent fermion's fractionalization; see (7). The note
    correctly makes no fractionalization claim.
  - Scope caveat: (1) needs even L; see the remark there.

(6) PRIOR ART. The "new here" claim is weak.
  Access caveat: arxiv.org, APS, Semantic Scholar and INSPIRE are blocked by this session's egress policy.
  The judgments below come from search-engine abstracts and snippets, not full texts.
  - The code is standard. It is the Majorana-per-edge-end ("Gamma-model") bosonization on the cubic
    lattice: Bravyi-Kitaev 2002 in Majorana form, the GSE of Setia-Bravyi-Mezzacapo-Whitfield 2019, and
    Levin-Wen PRB 67, 245316 (2003), whose exactly soluble 3D examples use string hopping operators of
    this type (I recall that structure but could not open the full text). Here the vertex parities are
    imposed as stabilizers, which gives Z2 gauge theory with a fermionic charge.
  - Closest prior art, which the note does not cite: K. Li and H. C. Po, "Higher-dimensional Jordan-Wigner
    transformation and auxiliary Majorana fermions", PRB 106, 115109 (2022), arXiv:2107.14083. Its
    abstract describes a JW/bosonization on 2D and 3D lattices of even coordination that "keeps internal
    and spatial symmetries manifest", worked out for the CUBIC lattice, built from a "Gamma model" of
    auxiliary Majoranas, with an analysis of how the symmetries act. Also relevant: H. C. Po,
    arXiv:2107.10842 (symmetric JW, 4-coordinated lattices). Manifest point-group covariance of this
    construction with on-site Majorana-permutation actions is therefore essentially known.
  - The citation of Wu-Arovas-Hung is off target for the cubic case. Their 3D example is the DIAMOND
    lattice with 4x4 Gamma matrices, as is Ryu's. Neither is the cubic six-Majorana code.
  - Honest residual novelty: (i) the explicit exact finite check; (ii) the explicit order-48 group count
    and its identification as A4 x| Z4, which may or may not be in Li-Po (not verifiable here); (iii) the
    repository-internal contrast with the BKSF orbit result. Recommendation: cite Li-Po and Levin-Wen,
    downgrade "new here", and state that (3) follows from a two-line structural argument.

(7) FRACTIONALIZATION eta_f(C2, C2). Result under the prescribed pair test.
  Setup:
  - The eps come from each of the 128 consistent order-48 groups, with both lifts of every C2.
  - All 3 axis C2 and all 6 edge C2 rotations, about the origin site.
  - 5 positions x per C2 class, with separation at most 4 (below half the torus).
  - 8 paths per pair: 6 Manhattan orders plus 2 detours.
  - Sectors +++ and ---, at L = 10 and L = 12.
  - Pair creator S = product of A along the path. S and S^R were verified to have exactly the syndrome
    {B_x, B_Rx} and no plaquette violations.
  Result: lambda = sign(S^dag S^R) = -1 in EVERY case (L=10: 28672 axis and 61440 edge evaluations; the
  same at L=12). Conjugate elements agree.
  => eta_f(axis C2) = eta_f(edge C2) = -lambda = +1.
  Hand derivation for axis C2 about z, x = (1,0,0), path through the origin: S = A1 A2 and S^R = A2 A1 =
  -S, so lambda = -1 independently of eps.
  This is the trivial pattern on the order-2 data. It is not spin-1/2-like (2O would give -1, -1), and it
  is not the Majorana-level pattern (+1, -1). The naive reading "U_R^2 = (-1)^F, so the edge C2 is
  Kramers-like on f" is wrong here.
  Controls:
  - (7b) Admissible but group-inconsistent eps for an edge C2: 16 of 32 choices give a lambda that depends
    on the pair or path. The consistent-group requirement is load-bearing.
  - (7c) A creator dressed with flux, S = gamma_{x,d} gamma_{Rx,Rd} (8 violated plaquettes), gives
    lambda = +1 for an axis C2. So lambda is canonical only when S creates the bare B-violations.
  Note on the campaign's preliminary result. The c12 README reports "axis C2 lambda = +1" at L = 8. After
  finishing, I looked at gse_pair2.py. Its solver drops the commutation constraint for plaquettes within
  L_inf <= 1 of the endpoints, so it can return flux-dressed creators. That reproduces exactly the +1 of
  (7c). Its separations of 3 to 5 at L = 8 also reach half the torus. I expect the bare-fermion answer to
  be lambda = -1, eta = +1 for both classes.
  Caveat: eta = -lambda assumes the standard relation lambda = theta * eta for a C2-exchanged pair. I did
  not fix the fractionalization class beyond the C2 squares (C4 and C3 data were not computed).
