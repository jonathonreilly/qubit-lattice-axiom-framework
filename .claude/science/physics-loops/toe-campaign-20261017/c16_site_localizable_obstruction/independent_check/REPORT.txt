ITEM 2 (kernels): CONFIRMED
  - Over GF(2): dim Z^2(O;Z2) = 25, dim B^2 = 23, so H^2(O;Z2) = Z2^2. The cocycles w2 (from the
    SU(2) section) and w1 cup w1 are independent modulo B^2, so they span it.
  - w1, the sign of the permutation of the six directions, equals the S4 sign on the four body
    diagonals. It is 1 on C4 and on edge C2, and 0 on axis C2 and on C3.
  - Test for "the central -1 is a commutator of the extension", i.e. no 1-dim rep with z -> -1:
      class 0: no, w1^2: no, w2: yes, w2+w1^2: yes. So the kernel is {0, w1^2}.
    Second method: u(g) = i^{w1(g)} has coboundary exactly (-1)^{w1 cup w1}, so w1^2 is the
    Bockstein of the sign character.
  - Squares of the lifts of the involutions, written as (axis C2, edge C2):
      0: (+,+)    w1^2: (+,-)    w2: (-,-)    w2+w1^2: (-,+)
    These four patterns are distinct, so the involution squares detect the class.
  - Subgroups: there are 30 subgroups in 11 conjugacy classes. The restriction of w2 forces local
    dimension >= 2, i.e. -1 lies in the commutator subgroup of the preimage in 2O, exactly for:
      D2(axis), D2(1 axis + 2 edge), D4, T and O (commutator subgroups of order 2, 2, 4, 8, 24).
    It does not for C1, C2(axis), C2(edge), C3, C4 and D3. For D3 the commutator subgroup is Z3.
    This matches the note.
  - Axis D2: dim H^2 = 3, and all 8 square patterns occur, one per class. A 1-dim rep exists
    exactly for (+,+,+), (+,-,-), (-,+,-) and (-,-,+), the patterns with an even number of -.
    w2 restricts to (-,-,-). This matches the note.

ITEM 3 (odd orbits): CONFIRMED
  - The only subgroups of odd index are D4 (index 3) and O (index 1). This also follows from
    Lagrange: 8 must divide |H|.
  - Precision: the conclusion is a necessary condition. An O-invariant odd-charge pattern needs a
    charge term with an odd orbit. Having such a term does not mean that violating its orbit alone
    gives a realizable pattern. See wording item W4.

ITEM 4 (shells): CONFIRMED WITH ONE CONVENTION TO STATE
  - I built all 8 irreps of 2O as explicit matrix representations:
      A1; A2 = w1; E via O -> S3; T1 = vector; T2 = T1 x A2;
      E1/2 = defining SU(2); E5/2 = E1/2 x A2; G3/2 = Sym^3 (spin 3/2).
    All are homomorphisms, the characters are orthonormal, and sum dim^2 = 48. These are the
    standard Bethe/Koster labels (Gamma6 = E1/2, Gamma7 = E5/2, Gamma8 = G3/2). On the C4 lift with
    trace +sqrt2: chi(E1/2) = +sqrt2, chi(E5/2) = -sqrt2, chi(G3/2) = 0.
  - Induced representations, with g the continuous lift of the right-handed minimal rotation
    (trace > 0 for C4 and C3) and chi_k(g) = exp(2 pi i k / n):
      six directions (stabilizer lift Z8):
        k=0  A1+E+T1      k=2,6  T1+T2      k=4  A2+E+T2
        k=1,7  E1/2+G3/2  k=3,5  E5/2+G3/2
      eight octants (Z6):
        k=0  A1+A2+T1+T2   k=2,4  E+T1+T2
        k=1,5  E1/2+E5/2+G3/2   k=3  2 G3/2
      twelve face diagonals (Z4):
        k=0  A1+E+T1+2T2   k=2  A2+E+2T1+T2
        k=1,3  E1/2+E5/2+2G3/2
    Every entry of the table and of the claim scope is correct, and so is "6 = 2 + 4".
  - Convention (needs stating): the k labels for the six directions depend on which generator of
    Z8 is used. Replacing g by -g = g^5 swaps k=1 <-> 5 and 3 <-> 7, so the table would then read
    E1/2 for k = 3, 5. The runner takes "the first element of maximal order", which is not
    documented; it happens to pick a trace-+sqrt2 generator, so its output matches mine. The
    octant and face-diagonal groupings do not depend on the generator.

ITEM 1 (obstruction logic): CONFIRMED WITH CORRECTIONS (the hypotheses need tightening)
  The argument is correct as standard SET reasoning. The local space at the fixed point is 1-dim,
  so the localized operators are phases, the U(1) class of the projective action is trivial, and
  the Z2 class maps into the kernel. a x a = 1 reduces the gauge freedom to nu_a = +-1, which keeps
  the involution squares well defined. That is what gives the "even number of -" rule. Gaps:
  (G1) "The violated-term pattern fixes the local state" needs more than "commuting Pauli
       Hamiltonian". The terms must generate the full stabilizer group, with no nontrivial local
       logical operators (local indistinguishability). A spectator qubit with no term, or a
       gauge-type local degeneracy, would make the local space bigger than 1.
  (G2) The realization must be a linear action of H, or the note must say which extension is
       meant. The Majorana-per-direction realizations form A4 x| Z4, an extension of O by fermion
       parity with class w1^2 (companion note). The measured "class" then includes that
       contribution. This does not affect "not w2", because both 0 and w1^2 are in the kernel, but
       the class should be named relative to the realization.
  (G3) Given its hypotheses, the content of item 1 is close to a tautology: a 1-dim projective rep
       is U(1)-trivial. The load-bearing hypothesis is the existence of an H-invariant violated-term
       pattern carrying a. This is a real condition, and it fails for the bare permutation (see
       item 5). The note should name it as the condition to verify.

ITEM 5 (application): CONFIRMED WITH CORRECTIONS (one overclaim)
  Confirmed (half-edge model, L=4, Paulis with phases, 3 orderings x 24 rotations about v=0):
  - The bare P_R maps B_v to +B_v: 0 failures.
  - V_R P_R maps B_v to +B_v: 0 failures. Here V_R is the on-site CZ network on direction pairs
    whose relative order differs between o and Ro.
  - V_R P_R maps every generator near v (B, R and S^o) to + a generator of the same type:
    0 failures out of 13,608 checks. Analytically, V_R P_R A^o_e = +A^o_{Re} exactly, and S_p is
    unchanged under dihedral reordering of its four A's.
  So for the on-site Clifford realization the term set is preserved, the single-B_v pattern is
  invariant, and item 1 applies. The same holds for the Majorana-per-direction signed permutation,
  which maps generators to +generators by that note's construction.
  Pair test: with lambda = theta*eta and theta = -1, lambda = -1 for all nine C2 means eta = +1 on
  both classes, which is the class-0 pattern. w1^2 would give eta(edge C2) = -1, i.e.
  lambda(edge) = +1; w1^2 is trivial on axis C2 and nontrivial on edge C2. Confirmed.
  "Never w2 on O" follows, and also never w2+w1^2.

  OVERCLAIM: "B_v is mapped to itself by every rotation realization ... (bare permutation, ...) so
  item 1 applies to every such realization."
  - B_v -> B_v is necessary but not sufficient. Item 1 also needs the whole term set mapped onto
    itself, so that the pattern {B_v only} is invariant.
  - For the BARE permutation this fails. Exact edge BKSF, L=4: 720 orderings give 60 codes, with
    stabilizers D3 (12 codes), order 4 (12) and C2 (36). The intertwining P_R A^o_e = A^{Ro}_{Re}
    held with 0 sampled failures.
      * (code, C2) pairs for which SOME ordering's plaquette term set is P_R-invariant:
        D3 36/36, C2-codes 24/36, D2 12/36 (72/108 in total, matching the companion note).
      * Codes with an ordering whose term set {B} u {S^o} is invariant under the WHOLE
        stabilizer: only 24 of the 36 C2-codes. None of the 12 D3 codes (the C3 never preserves
        it) and none of the 12 D2 codes.
      * L=8, clean hop string from v. P_R sends the clean single-fermion pattern to one with
        plaquette violations within distance 2 of v in:
          D2 axis C2   12/12     D2 edge C2   19/24     D3 C3   24/24
          D3 edge C2   24/36     C2-code edge C2   10/12
        (3 orderings x up to 4 codes per type.)
  - So under the bare action, item 1's hypothesis is not established for the D3 and D2 codes. For
    the D2 codes the pair data (-,-,-) together with item 2 imply that at least one premise fails:
    (i) no D2-invariant charge pattern exists, (ii) localization fails, or (iii) lambda = theta*eta
    fails. Candidate (i) is now concrete, because no ordering's clean pattern is D2-invariant.
    This matches the note's own "does not decide which assumption fails" and contradicts its
    "item 1 applies to every such realization".
  - Also: "Any vacuum-preserving realization maps B_v to +-B_v" is false in general. A
    code-preserving Clifford can send B_v to B_v times other stabilizers. It holds for on-site
    (support-preserving) realizations that map terms to terms.
  - The runner does not test item 5 at all (it has no B_v or Clifford code). The "exact algebra"
    of item 5 is unchecked by the runner; this check covers it.
