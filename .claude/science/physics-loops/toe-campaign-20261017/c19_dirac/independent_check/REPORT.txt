ITEM 1  Selection rule (no Dirac mass from site-rotation-invariant terms)     VERDICT: CONFIRMED
        (and stronger than stated). N1's wording on Majorana masses needs correction.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why it holds, convention-free:
  - Both k* and -k* are fixed by all 24 rotations (exactly two fixed momenta; fixed_momenta()).
  - On the Bloch space at either momentum, the rotation acts by the same 12 x 12 signed permutation W_R.
    The internal shell is E1/2 + E5/2 + 2 G3/2, and E1/2 and E5/2 each occur once.
  - So the node doublets are fixed vectors: the unique E1/2 and E5/2 subspaces. One sits at k* and the
    other at -k*, whatever t2 and X are.
  - Two consequences follow:
    - E_N = -2 - sqrt2 t2 exactly, independent of X. X vanishes on a one-copy isotypic subspace because
      it is I*K-odd.
    - m_D / m depends only on the mass matrix (1.4041 for the runner's M), not on t2 or X.
  - The character ratio between the two doublets is +1 on the w1-even and -1 on the w1-odd rotations,
    i.e. A2, in either 2O lift (spin_bloch.log). This is convention-free.
  - The E1/2 / E5/2 labels themselves swap between the two valid lifts, and between the twists i^{w1}
    and (-i)^{w1}.
  - Complex conjugation maps the E1/2 subspace onto the E5/2 one (checked).

Numbers:
  - Exact bases (orbit counts) of O-invariant staggered (momentum Q) terms:

        range               real   imaginary
        on-site                4           2
        range <= 1 site       22          20
        |sh|_1 <= 2           76          74
        |sh|_inf <= 1         88          74

    The maximum coupling between the k* doublet and the -k* doublet is 8.6e-16 over all of them.
    The coupling map has rank 0 (selection.log).
  - Momentum-0 O-invariant terms, same ranges, real and imaginary: the diagonal blocks are scalar to
    1.3e-15 and the off-diagonal blocks vanish.
  - Without translation symmetry: random complex Hermitian operators of site range <= 2 on the L = 4
    torus, symmetrized over the 24 site rotations only. The k* <-> -k* blocks are <= 3.3e-14, and the
    diagonal blocks are scalar to 4e-14 (realspace.log).
    So the rule is pure Schur on site-O. It needs neither translation symmetry nor a particular momentum.
    Only momentum Q connects k* and -k*: momentum-0 terms cannot couple them, and no other momentum can.
  - Finite strength (not only first order): O-invariant staggered A1 terms of strength 0.1 to 1 keep the
    folded levels at k* 4-fold (pure real or pure imaginary terms).
    A complex mixture splits them 2 + 2, and each doublet stays a Weyl node with flux +-1 (deg4b.log).
    So no gap opens at any strength.
  - Real A2 terms give zero at EVERY range (on-site 1, range-1 13, |sh|_1<=2 55, cube 61; max 3e-17).
    The reason: K maps one node doublet onto the other. The coupling of a K-even operator is then a
    symmetric invariant bilinear form on a pseudoreal irrep, and that must vanish.
    Imaginary A2 terms (5 / 29 / 95 / 101 dims) give a coupling map of real rank 2.

Majorana masses (N1), my own BdG; Bloch BdG = real-space L = 4 BdG (majorana.log):
  - Twisted-invariant pairing (pair matrix A2 under the untwisted PSG):
    - With momentum Q (on-site 5 dims, range <= 1 29 dims) it is intra-node, i.e. a Majorana mass.
    - The BdG with mu = E_N is gapped near k*. min|E| = the node pairing (1.39e-3 at amplitude 1e-3), and
      it is attained at k* (majorana2.log).
    - It keeps all 24 twisted site rotations. Translations survive only when dressed with U(1)
      rotations.
    - With momentum 0 it is forbidden (E1/2 x E5/2 contains no A1).
  - Untwisted-invariant pairing:
    - Momentum Q is forbidden.
    - Momentum 0 (on-site 2 dims, range <= 1 20 dims) gives a nonzero inter-node pairing, but the nodes
      stay gapless: BdG zero modes sit at |q| ~ Delta/v (e.g. 6e-18 at |q| = 0.048). This is the BCS
      versus FFLO pattern of Weyl superconductors.
  - So under the twisted action the protection needs number conservation. Under the untwisted action
    (class w2 + w1^2) no pairing tested gaps the nodes.

ITEM 3  Massive Dirac fermion                                VERDICT: CONFIRMED WITH CORRECTIONS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Confirmed:
  - The imaginary A2 staggered on-site space has dimension exactly 5. Real ones (1 dim) give zero.
  - The two node doublets go to E_N -+ m_D exactly, with the centre shift 1e-15.
  - m_D is exactly linear in m: the 4-state node block is closed. m_D/m = 1.4041 for the runner's M,
    3.304 and 2.798 for my elements.
  - Labels: the massless node states are E1/2 + E5/2 under the twisted O. Each massive doublet is the
    spin-1/2 irrep of 2T (|chi|^2 = 1, overlap with SU(2) spin-1/2 = 1), and its site-T D2 commutator
    is -1.

Corrections (substantive for the dispersion):
  (a) The runner's dispersion test is not discriminating.
      - It compares at |dk| <= 0.02 with m = 0.05 (m_D = 0.070, m_D/v_W = 0.64), i.e. q << m_D/v.
      - A FLAT band passes it: max relative deviation 6.4e-4 < 1e-2.
      - So the "5e-4" is a statement about E ~ m_D near q = 0. It is not isotropy, and not the sqrt form.
  (b) The runner uses v = vel/2. The Dirac velocity is the Weyl velocity v_W = vel. That wrong
      velocity also passes the test (5.2e-4).
  (c) Proper asymptotic test (dirac.log), q = x m_D/v_W with x <= 4. Decompose E = E_N + s(q) +- r(q):
      - r(q) -> sqrt(v_W^2 q^2 + m_D^2), with error ~ m.
        Runner's H: 4.8e-6 at m = 1e-4 and 4.8e-4 at m = 1e-3. Mine: 6.4e-6 and 6.4e-4.
      - The angular spread is 2.8e-6 at m = 1e-4.
      - There is an extra isotropic scalar term s = c q^2: c = 0.0275 for the runner's H, 0.035 for mine.
        It breaks particle-hole symmetry.
      - So: E - E_N = c dk^2 +- sqrt(v^2 dk^2 + m_D^2) + O(m dk^2, dk^3).
      - The Dirac regime needs m_D << v^2/c (0.44 for the runner's H) and q << the k.p range.
      - At the runner's m = 0.05 the form fails beyond tiny q: an 88% deviation at q = 4 m_D/v = 2.6.
        At m = 1e-2 it is 5%.
  (d) "Pseudoscalar" means A2 under the rotations only. The 5-dim space splits under site inversion:
      - 3 I-even elements give a real node coupling z;
      - 2 I-odd elements give z with relative phase i (mass_parity.log).
      So the mass is a complex number m e^{i phi}: rank 2, with a scalar (P-even) and a pseudoscalar
      (P-odd) part. The phase phi is the chiral (axion) angle, and the w1-odd rotations send z -> -z.
      A generic M breaks the site inversion (realspace.log). An I-even M keeps it.
  (e) Spin labels, convention-free:
      - The two massless doublets differ by A2, and each is spinorial: D2 commutator -1.
      - With U(1) rephasing allowed (number conservation), the 2T label among its three 2D irreps is
        conventional. The invariant is the w2 class (D2 commutator -1).

ITEM 4  Symmetry of the massive model                         VERDICT: CONFIRMED (and strengthened)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On L = 4, m = 0.05, random M (realspace.log):
  - Kept: the 12 T rotations about a site and the 32 even translations.
  - Broken: all 12 w1-odd site rotations, all 32 odd translations, and the site inversion (also composed
    with an odd translation).
  - p - Rp (p = (1/2,1/2,1/2)) is an odd translation exactly for the w1-odd R.
  - All 24 maps n -> R(n - p) + p commute with H. A geometric check confirms that each one moves the
    corner points by the rotation about p.
  - So "together the 24 rotations about a cube centre" is right, modulo the kept even translations.

Projective class under the cube-centre O (a convention-free U(1) invariant):
  - C2x C2y C2x^-1 C2y^-1 = -1 on the whole one-fermion space, i.e. w2 (spin-1/2).
  - The untwisted cube-centre action lifts to 2O and the i^{w1}-twisted one does not. The k*-phase of
    the odd translation supplies the i^{w1}.
  - Each massive doublet is invariant under the cube-centre O, with D2 commutator -1, and is E1/2 or
    E5/2 depending on the lift (realspace.log; spin_bloch.log, both doublets).
  - So the massive Dirac doublets are spin-1/2 under the full cube group about a cube centre, not only
    under 2T. The note can say this.

Mechanism:
  - On the node space the odd translations act as the chirality operator: relative phase -1 between the
    doublets (spin_bloch.log).
  - The w1-odd site rotations act as a spin rotation times the chirality operator.
  - Their product, the cube-centre rotation, is a pure spin rotation. That is why it survives the mass.
  - So "the w1-odd rotations play the role of the shift symmetries" is fair. The closer analogue: both
    the odd translations and the w1-odd site rotations act as gamma5 up to a spin rotation.

Consistency: site C4z T_x C4z^-1 T_y^-1 = -1. This is C17's caveat.

    - But that is only 0.22 to 0.46 of 2 m_D, because the 24 extra Weyl points are also gapped by the

  - Generic obstruction seen: extra Weyl crossings between the node bands on the lines (pi/2)(1, a, -a),
