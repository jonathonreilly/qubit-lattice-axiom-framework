(a) GENERAL PAIR TEST: CONFIRMED, all 108 (code, C2) pairs, both uniform sectors, lambda = +1

  My implementation
  - Window: an R-invariant set of edges, the distance-1 neighbourhood of a lattice path x -> Rx together with
    its R-image. (The runner uses a bounding box.)
  - Constraints, solved over GF(2):
      * B_x and B_Rx flipped, every other B unflipped;
      * no plaquette flipped unless a corner is within L-infinity distance 1 of x or Rx;
      * <S,g> = <S^R,g> for every generator g.
  - Checks on each solution: the B syndrome is exactly {x, Rx}; O = S^dag S^R commutes with all generators;
    O decomposes in the generator span (local and contractible); lambda is the sign of O in the signed
    group G_s.
  - Several solutions per x: the particular solution plus random nullspace vectors.

  Your question: is "same syndrome as S^R" enough, or can a symmetric local excitation contaminate lambda?
  - Why the condition is the right one.
      * It is exactly what makes S|vac> a P_R-eigenstate: O commutes with all generators and is local,
        so O is in +-G and P_R S|vac> = lambda S P_R|vac>.
  - Why it is the pair's sector invariant.
      * Two solutions differ by K = S S'. K has no B syndrome, and an R-symmetric plaquette syndrome
        confined to N1(x) and N1(Rx). Fluxes form closed loops, so modulo stabilizers
        K = K_x K_x^R h, with h a stabilizer element.
      * Then O' = +-O times (stabilizer factors) times [K_x, K_x^R].
      * The stabilizer factors carry sign +1. Reason: P_R preserves the signed group, and
        (S^dag S^R)^R = (S^dag S^R)^dag = S^dag S^R.
      * So lambda can change only through the commutator [K_x, K_x^R]. This commutator does not depend on
        the stabilizer representative, and it is +1 when the two clusters can be cleaned to disjoint
        supports, i.e. when x and Rx are well separated (the note's separation >= 3).
  - Numerical confirmation.
      * Random nullspace sampling found zero spread among all solutions.
      * This holds even at separations 1 and 2, where the argument alone does not guarantee it.
  - Conclusion: no contamination observed or possible at the separations used.

  - Hence "class on the whole stabilizer = w2 restricted" holds for every code. This uses (a) for all
    three C2s of each order-4 code, which is exactly what the 36 newly closed pairs supply.
  - Caveat to keep: this is the class under P_R. Under P_R W the class is trivial (the note already says so).
