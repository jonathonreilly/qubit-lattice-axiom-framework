Reranking after the massive covariance/defect milestone:

   value because it exposes the additional electric and magnetic defect

   but should not be inferred from a theorem for uniformly massive matter.
4. Native law/axiom selection: still open; neither current result supplies a
   new contradiction that would justify forcing an axiom update.

First derivation targets:
- The paired finite determinant is a finite Fourier polynomial in link angles.
  Bound its link-current degrees and prove exact integer current conservation.
- Integrate the Villain character expansion to obtain C^* k+j=0 for U(1),
  or C^* k+j=Nq for finite Z_N, with closed q. Keep signed coefficients.
- Use orthogonal projection and the maximum of a shifted lattice Gaussian to
  compare constrained Gaussian sectors without an exponential volume factor.
- Determine which bounds concern a positive conditional ensemble and which
  only bound signed terms. No defect probability may be assigned to a signed
  expansion without a positive representation.
- Test the required source forms and the role of fermion-current screening;
  if that does not close the infrared estimate, pursue the matched transfer
  route and retain the exact unsolved obligation.

Potential useful elementary bound: for four-spin paired determinants each
link Fourier current is bounded by 2m in magnitude. If N>2m, then on edges
where q is nonzero, |Nq-j|>=(N-2m)|q|. This suggests an electric-sector
stiffness window involving (N-2m)^2/beta. It is not yet a phase or positive
defect-density theorem for the determinant-coupled ensemble.
