  response. Likewise a local wrapping susceptibility need not diagnose

- Positive coordinate amplitudes and identical coordinate marginals can
  coexist with arbitrarily small quantum fidelity. Restricting to diagonal
  loop dressings can also miss nonzero charged order.
- A discarded-register coarse state is generally mixed. Its ordinary
  complementary fidelity can equal one while the needed transition norm
  vanishes. Coarse-graining needs the operator-preservation/locality gates.
- Positive local statistical weights do not imply a positive physical
  transfer operator. The deformed Z4 two-plaquette fixture has an exact
  negative physical eigenvalue. Even a positive one-step link transfer
  need not have an entrywise-positive continuous-time semigroup.
- A finite clock with approximate persistent formation does not select
  an exact native event law. The exact-capture obstruction is restricted
  to the explicitly stated continuous semibounded closed-system setting.
- Common-cone consistency needs its collision and gravitational premises.
  Neither one-loop attraction nor a supplied relativistic effective theory
  chooses the universal metric or derives emergent relativity from axioms.
