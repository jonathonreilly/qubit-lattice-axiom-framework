# Approach registry — cycle 1

Target contract: the dimension of the soldered bond-symmetric factors on
one proper orbit of `(1, 2, 3)`, and the dimension after far-end sum 1.

Family used: exact orbit enumeration and rational row reduction.

Family not used in cycle 1: character tables of the cubic group.

Cycle 2 target contract: whether order-blindness leaves only the
exhibited modulus-3 line.

Family used: the cap-sign formula on each modulus, exact product means,
and one mixture moment.

Family not used in cycle 2: a general element of the bond space.

Cycle 3 target contract: which members of the cap span meet the
product-mean identity.

Family used: the correlation table of the caps and two witness moments.

Family not used: a bond factor outside the span. That remains open.

Cycle 4 target contract: whether a soldered order-blind factor on this
orbit can sit outside the span of the caps `a·d = 1, 2, 3`.

Family used: the opposite-cap formula on each negative level, exact
product means, and one named witness bond.

Family not used: a slice outside the six oriented caps. That remains open.

Cycle 5 target contract: whether a soldered order-blind factor on this
orbit can have a slice outside the six cap signs.

Family used: the transverse-cross formula on the caps `a·e_z = -3` and
`b·e_z = 3`, extended by proper rotations, with one witness slice.

Family not used: the 48-point chiral pair. That remains open.

Cycle 6 target contract: the dimension of the soldered bond-symmetric
factors on the 48 signed permutations of `(1, 2, 3)`, and the dimension
after far-end sum 1.

Family used: exact orbit enumeration and rational row reduction.

Family not used: an order-blindness cut on that space. That remains open.

Cycle 7 target contract: whether a soldered order-blind kernel on this
orbit remains outside the six cap lines and the modulus-3 transverse
cross.

Family used: the transverse-cross formula at absolute heights 1 and 2,
rational row reduction of the nine kernels, and one mixture moment.

Family not used: a kernel outside the three transverse crosses. That
remains open.

Cycle 8 target contract: whether a soldered order-blind kernel on this
orbit remains outside the six cap lines and the three matched
transverse crosses.

Family used: the swapped-absolute transverse cross at heights 1, 2,
and 3, rational row reduction of the twelve kernels, the height-3 sum,
and one cross-height moment.

Family not used: a kernel outside those twelve. That remains open.

Cycle 9 target contract: whether the one-cap-pair ansatz has a kernel
outside the cap line and the two transverse crosses.

Family used: bond-orbit count on each pattern, the cap line, and the
matched and swapped crosses, with one cross-pattern moment.

Family not used: a kernel with two live directions. That remains open.

Cycle 10 target contract: whether two live directions can keep
neighbour-product mean 1.

Family used: the level-`+3` cap plus the matched cross on `a·d = -3`,
and the moment of the two level-3 cap lines.

Family not used: a kernel outside the cap-pair span. That remains open.

Cycle 11 target contract: whether a slice-zero kernel supported on one
height signature can be order-blind outside the eighteen cap-pair
kernels.

Family used: the 21 reversal classes of endpoint heights, the
pair-moment quadrics, and the cap and cross basis.

Family not used: a sum of two height signatures. That remains open.

Cycle 12 target contract: whether an unequal height signature beside
one partner can be order-blind outside the eighteen cap-pair kernels.

Family used: the pair-moment squares on each of the twelve unequal
signatures and each of the other twenty signatures.

Family not used: an equal-height signature beside an opposite
signature. The square test does not decide it.

Cycle 13 target contract: whether an equal-height signature beside
one opposite or equal-height partner can be order-blind outside the
eighteen cap-pair kernels.

Family used: the direct sum on all 210 pairs, the three equal-height
quadrics, and the same-modulus and different-modulus normal forms.

Family not used: the extra slice direction on `(-3, -3)`, `(-3, 3)`,
and `(3, -3)`, where the joint kernel has dimension 10.
