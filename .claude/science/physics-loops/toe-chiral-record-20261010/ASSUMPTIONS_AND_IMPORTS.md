# Assumptions and imports — cycle 1

- The proper cubic rotations are the determinant-+1 signed permutation
  matrices. The runner enumerates them. It does not import the count 24.
- Bond reversal identifies `(direction, left, right)` with
  `(-direction, right, left)`.
- Soldered covariance applies the same rotation to the direction and to
  both records.
- Factors are rational. No positivity, order-blindness, or normalising
  constant beyond the far-end sum is assumed in the dimension count.
- No formation site, probability, or rate is imported.
- The three approved primitives are not used. This count does not call
  the scale reference, kinetic isotropy, or a realized state.
