# No-go ledger — cycle 1

No no-go is claimed.

Cycle 1 prunes only the reading that the linear bond space is the
exhibited one-parameter family.

Cycle 2 prunes the reading that order-blindness leaves only that
modulus-3 line. The witness is the modulus-1 line, the modulus-2 line,
and the mixture moment `25/24`. It does not say that no further
order-blind factor exists.

Cycle 3 prunes mixtures inside the cap span. The witnesses are the moment
`13/12` and the three-neighbour moment `-3`. It does not say that no
factor outside the span is order-blind.

Cycle 4 prunes the reading that no soldered order-blind factor sits
outside the positive cap span. The witnesses are the three opposite-cap
lines and the bond from `(1, -2, -3)` to `(1, 2, 3)`. It does not say
that no slice outside the six oriented caps is order-blind.

Cycle 5 prunes the reading that every soldered order-blind slice lies
in the six cap signs. The witness is the transverse-cross slice at
`(-2, 1, 3)`. It does not say that no further kernel exists.

Cycle 6 prunes only the reading that the 48-point bond space is the
exhibited one-parameter family. It does not say that order-blindness
cuts the dimension.

Cycle 7 prunes the reading that every soldered order-blind kernel on
this orbit lies in the six cap lines and the modulus-3 transverse
cross. The witnesses are the height-1 and height-2 crosses and the
mixture moment `23/24`. It does not say that no kernel remains outside
the three crosses.

Cycle 8 prunes the reading that every soldered order-blind kernel on
this orbit lies in the six cap lines and the three matched transverse
crosses. The witnesses are the three swapped crosses and the moment

Cycle 9 prunes the reading that a one-cap-pair support still hides a
kernel outside the cap and the two crosses. Each pattern has dimension
3 and is spanned by those three kernels. It does not say that a kernel
with two live directions is impossible.

Cycle 10 prunes the reading that two live directions force the
two-neighbour moment off 1. The witness is the level-`+3` cap plus the
matched cross on `a·d = -3`. The two level-3 cap lines have moment
`7/6`. It does not say that a kernel outside the cap-pair span exists.

Cycle 11 prunes the reading that a non-opposite height signature still
hides an order-blind kernel. The witnesses are the moment sums `-1` and
`1`, whose uniform averages are `-1/24` and `1/24`. It does not say
that a sum of two signatures lies in the cap-pair span.

Cycle 12 prunes the reading that one partner can revive an unequal
signature. All 240 pairs force the unequal monomials to zero. It does
not say that an equal-height signature beside an opposite signature
is zero. The square test leaves that pair open.

Cycle 13 prunes the reading that one opposite or equal-height partner
can revive an equal-height signature. The joint kernel on every pair
is the direct sum, and the equal-height quadrics vanish only at the
origin. It does not say that the extra direction in the triple
`(-3, -3)`, `(-3, 3)`, `(3, -3)` is zero. That joint kernel has
dimension 10.

Cycle 14 prunes the reading that the complement line on that triple
is order-blind. The moment on `±e_x` at `(-3, -2, -1)` is `2`, with
uniform average `1/12`. It does not say that a mixture of the line
with the three signature kernels has the same moment.

Cycle 15 prunes the reading that a rational mixture of the line with
those three kernels can cancel every two-neighbour moment and leave
the eighteen. The complement coefficient and the equal-height block
are both forced to zero. It does not say that a kernel using a fourth
signature is inside the eighteen, and it does not cut the 48-point
alphabet.

Cycle 16 prunes the reading that every far-end normalised soldered
factor on the 48-point chiral pair is order-blind. The height-linear

moment `71/50`. It does not classify the order-blind subset, and it
does not re-derive the chiral-cap line.

strictly positive order-blind line on the 48-point alphabet. The
transverse-sign cap at coefficient `1/2` has product mean 1 on all 729
partner patterns, and two bonds forbid every scale of the chiral cap.
It does not classify a factor outside both lines.

Cycle 18 prunes the reading that every strictly positive order-blind
factor on this alphabet lies in the span of the chiral cap and the
transverse-sign cap. The sign-product cap at coefficient `1/2` has
product mean 1 on all 729 partner patterns, and three bonds to

outside those three kernels, and it does not classify the
