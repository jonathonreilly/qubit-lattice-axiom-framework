# Opportunity queue

1. Decide whether every quadruple of positive integers (t, u, r, s) gives
   partner rank 10, at every chirality coefficient, for t times the plus
   swapped-dot generator plus u times the minus swapped-dot generator
   plus r times the plus cap and s times the minus cap. The case t = u
   is settled. Do not claim the ten-kernel coefficient span is settled.
   Do not claim the 300-dimensional affine space is classified. Do not
   return to the modulus-3 triple.
2. Formation site, probability, and rate remain outside the axioms. Do not
   invent a hazard law for them.
