# Opportunity queue

1. Decide whether every chirality coefficient on the sum of one
   swapped-dot generator and both cap generators has partner rank 10.
   That sum has both a nonzero swapped-dot coordinate and a nonzero
   scaled coordinate. One swapped-dot generator plus the cap of the
   same span has partner rank 7 only at chirality coefficient 0, and
   one swapped-dot generator plus the cap of the other span has partner
   rank 10 at every chirality coefficient. Do not claim the ten-kernel
   coefficient span is settled. Do not claim the 300-dimensional affine
   space is classified. Do not return to the modulus-3 triple.
2. Formation site, probability, and rate remain outside the axioms. Do not
   invent a hazard law for them.
