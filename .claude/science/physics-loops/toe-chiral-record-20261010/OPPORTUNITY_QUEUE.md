# Opportunity queue

1. Decide which rational mixtures of two opposite-height bases, at two
   distinct absolute heights, have every neighbour-product mean equal
   to 1. A sum across heights can be live in two directions. Do not
   claim the 300-dimensional affine space is classified. Do not return
   to the modulus-3 triple.
2. Formation site, probability, and rate remain outside the axioms. Do not
   invent a hazard law for them.
