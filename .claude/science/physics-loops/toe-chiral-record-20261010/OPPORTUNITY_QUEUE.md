   coefficients and a nonzero excluded coefficient can pair with a
   nonzero ten-kernel vector and still have every cross moment equal
   to 0. Each plane of one parity kernel and one excluded kernel is
   settled. Oblique plus matched passes against itself at heights 1

   coefficient span is settled. Do not claim the 300-dimensional
   affine space is classified. Do not return to the modulus-3 triple.
