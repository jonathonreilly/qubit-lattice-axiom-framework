# Opportunity queue

1. Decide whether every positive integer m gives partner rank 10, at
   every chirality coefficient, for one swapped-dot generator plus m
   times the cap of each ratio span. The case m = 3 has partner rank
   10. Do not claim the ten-kernel coefficient span is settled. Do not
   claim the 300-dimensional affine space is classified. Do not return
   to the modulus-3 triple.
2. Formation site, probability, and rate remain outside the axioms. Do not
   invent a hazard law for them.
