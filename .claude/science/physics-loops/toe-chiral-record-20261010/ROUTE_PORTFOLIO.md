# Route portfolio — cycle 1

Taken: enumerate the soldered bond-symmetric factors on one proper orbit
of `(1, 2, 3)` and row-reduce the far-end sums.

Not taken in cycle 1: the 48-point chiral pair and other alphabets.

Cycle 2 takes the cap-sign ansatz on moduli 1, 2, and 3, and the
two-neighbour moment of a mixture. It does not take a general factor.

Cycle 3 takes linear combinations of those three signs. It does not take
a bond factor outside that span.

Cycle 4 takes the opposite cap `a·d = -m` for each modulus `1, 2, 3`.
It does not take a slice outside those six oriented caps.

Cycle 5 takes one transverse-cross kernel on the modulus-3 caps. It
does not take the 48-point chiral pair.

Cycle 6 takes the soldered bond-orbit count on the 48 signed

Cycle 7 takes the transverse-cross formula at absolute heights 1 and 2.
It does not take a kernel outside those three crosses.

Cycle 8 takes the swapped-absolute transverse cross at heights 1, 2,

Cycle 9 takes the six one-cap-pair supports. It does not take a kernel

Cycle 10 takes the sum of the level-`+3` cap and the matched cross on

Cycle 11 takes one reversal class of endpoint heights. It does not take

Cycle 12 takes one unequal height signature plus one partner. It does
not take an equal-height signature beside an opposite signature.

Cycle 13 takes one equal-height signature plus one opposite or

the triple `(-3, -3)`, `(-3, 3)`, `(3, -3)`.

Cycle 21 takes the chirality, transverse-sign, and sign-product caps
