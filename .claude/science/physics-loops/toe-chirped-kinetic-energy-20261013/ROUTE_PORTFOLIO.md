# Accepted routes and scope

Root V2: fixed BV profiles, qualitative secular localization, weighted full-input
comparison and one-time law/mean/W1. Independent850: resource-uniform secular
bound and corresponding fixed-characteristic/mean errors, qualitative profile-W1.
Root's later assessment of that strengthening is separate. Fixed-partition
normalization and cross-block estimates are supporting checks. Fixed-accuracy
frequency approximation alone is a preserved failed shortcut. This source
package proposes no new frontier route and runs none of these calculations.
