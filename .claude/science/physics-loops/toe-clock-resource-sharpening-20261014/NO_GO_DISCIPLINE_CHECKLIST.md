# No-go scope

This is a positive conditional refinement, not a no-go packet. No no-go PASS or impossibility claim is supplied. Retained limitations concern this construction: full runtime, high-arity clock-data coupling, separate clock-site cost, supplied preparation/calibration/readout and effective data. They are not universal obstructions.

The archived alternative kernels, ordinary Fejer comparison, path/battery/clock citations and unsuccessful source-access or metadata attempts retain their scopes. The current sufficient bandwidth gain is not used to claim total-cost dominance over those alternatives. Future physical-supply work requires its own theorem and evidence.
