# Complete argument families

1. Schwartz operator filters restrict full transfer; lowering-word kernels
   give finite-energy bounds. The exact remainder gives vacuum factorization.
2. Scalar source differentiation retains every dispersion commutator and
   spectator factor. Strong Hilbert differentiation gives its global norm;
   off-collision localization gives its rapid local decay.
3. The entire source inherits a closed Fourier support. Creation maps converge
   strongly by Cook on the linear span of smooth products; adjoints converge
   only weakly at this step.
4. Convex separation and a normalized two-time phase control every integration
   by parts coefficient. Both lattice Schur sums are uniform for s>=t>=1,
   including s=t and unbounded s/t.
5. Reverse the finite-interval scalar integral. Compactness is used at fixed t
   before sending its upper time to infinity; the source filter order is kept.
   Fourier dominated convergence then identifies the final strong limit.
6. Positivity and energy intertwining give the compressed/uncompressed detector
   limits. A positive weighted sum and ordered singular resolvent limits state
   the further exact exhaustion criterion, without proving its kernel vanishes.
