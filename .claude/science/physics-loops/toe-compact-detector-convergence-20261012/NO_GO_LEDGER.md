# Failed steps retained at their actual scope

Vacuum-vector momentum support does not imply full operator support. A gap
does not alone give the vacuum remainder for a chosen annihilator tuple.
Pointwise pair separation cannot be summed over unrestricted spectator
coordinates. Creation convergence does not imply strong adjoint convergence.
Moving-time compactness and a bare forward 1/t estimate do not prove PC.
Deleting the source Fourier filter or convex containment loses the demonstrated
kernel estimate. An arbitrary joint time/resolvent limit can be wrong.

These are scoped proof failures or algebraic counterexamples. The product
qubit example tests a missing vacuum-remainder hypothesis, not the full N1
microscopic setting. No general completeness impossibility is asserted here.
