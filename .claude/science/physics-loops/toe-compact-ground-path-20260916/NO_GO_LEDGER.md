# Negative-claim boundary

No phase impossibility or axiom-level obstruction is proposed. The packet
contains two bounded positive countercontrols: an explicit constrained
two-state path kernel with a negative eigenvalue, and an explicit isolated
binary plaquette with a polynomial small-g bad-flux probability. They
challenge specific shortcuts, not every possible comparison or extension.

The photon response, native selection, global winding and microscopic
current identifications are unproved consumers or scope limits. Their
absence is not evidence that they are impossible. The conservative
[N1-N8 record](NO_GO_DISCIPLINE_CHECKLIST.md) does not claim a formal gate
PASS or manufacture five refutations of these open questions.
