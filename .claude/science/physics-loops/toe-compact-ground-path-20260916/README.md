# Compact ground paths and dilute coarse currents

This draft proposes volume-uniform joint magnetic and temporal excursion
bounds for a supplied three-dimensional compact U(1) rotor Hamiltonian at
fixed positive coupling. Reflection positivity and chessboard estimates
combine with Brownian path bounds and an extensive variational energy
bound. A reversible diffusion estimate constructs subsequential
infinite-volume continuous-path laws. Their coarse compact currents are
exactly conserved and have exponentially bounded component sizes in an
explicit sufficiently small-coupling range.

The Hamiltonian is supplied; the framework axioms do not select it in
this packet. The results are author-proposed and personally checked.
Independent review and canonical audit registration are pending. Neither
a photon phase nor a TOE nor an axiom update is claimed.

Read the four parts as one argument:

1. [Ground-path magnetic event bound](notes/BLOCK01_SAMPLED_MAGNETIC_EVENT_BOUND.md):
   Hamiltonian, sector, variational estimate, actual spatial/time reflection
   positivity, chessboard geometry and a sampled event estimate.
2. [Whole-interval magnetic excursions](notes/BLOCK02_CONTINUOUS_TIME_MAGNETIC_EXCURSIONS.md):
   all-time supremum events and the even-power trace bound for a constrained
   kernel that need not be positive semidefinite.
3. [Infinite-volume continuous paths](notes/BLOCK03_SPATIAL_PATH_LIMIT.md):
   forward/backward martingale cancellation, tightness, local compact bridge
   specification and transfer of excursion bounds.
4. [Temporal oscillations and coarse closed currents](notes/BLOCK04_TEMPORAL_OSCILLATIONS_AND_COARSE_CURRENTS.md):
   winding-sensitive link events, exact cubical current conservation and
   finite current components at a declared fixed coarse time scale.

Evidence is summarized in [the personal review](review/PERSONAL_REVIEW.md).
The source-to-goal relation is in [TRACE_GATE.md](TRACE_GATE.md); scope and
unresolved conditions are in [the claim certificate](CLAIM_STATUS_CERTIFICATE.md)

The decisive unsolved consumer is a uniform lower bound on the transverse
electric susceptibility, or a directly controlled physical source limit.
Joint rare-event bounds alone are not a cluster expansion of the smooth
field coupled to the defect current.
