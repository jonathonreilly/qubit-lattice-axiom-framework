# Preserved invalid shortcuts

All mixed-product kernels cannot be replaced by powers of one lowering
operator. The creator's output-energy padding cannot be omitted. Different
masses do not guarantee distinct velocities. Full operator-norm estimates
for remaining factors lose the decay needed by the Gram recursion. Two
isometries alone do not make their scattering-matrix composition isometric.
These are proof-boundary controls, not lattice no-go theorems or N-gate claims.
