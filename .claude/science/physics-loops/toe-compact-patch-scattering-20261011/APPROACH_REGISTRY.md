# Mathematical approach families

1. Joint energy/current filtering: exploit the supplied continuous momenta
   before using geometric translations. Split flow parameters into a bounded
   region and a Schwartz tail; do not require an exponential moment.
2. Centre decomposition: finite characters project only on the stipulated
   finite-order mass component. A global projection is unnecessary and false
   without that spectrum.
3. Direct creator properties: exact vacuum graphs and almost locality permit
   Cook convergence without unproved geometric transfer isolation.
4. Fixed-chart comparison: discard a packet only through its zero vacuum
   action and separated commutators; no small operator-norm claim is used.
5. Covariance: the direct-property creator class is preserved by fixed
   microscopic flows even when a transformed local seed is not local.

The Fock inner-product factorization and full-space density families remain
underexplored in this milestone and are explicit next obligations.
