# Premises and dependencies

S1 supplies actual commuting, vacuum-preserving finite-range microscopic H and
K=P/beta flows. S2 supplies isolated positive mass-Casimir eigenspaces. S3
supplies a common finite-order centre action on those components only. S4
supplies compact single-lift charts and separated packet velocities. None is
derived from the framework's minimal physical axioms or changed by this PR.

BDN's LR bound, finite-region approximation and smooth packet estimates are
matched primary imports. The finite-product Cook argument is reconstructed
from directly proved creator properties. BDN's stronger geometric isolation
condition need not hold, and its Fock-isometry conclusion is not imported.

The EX discussion separately assumes a unique vacuum and positive H-gap. Those
additional assumptions make EX equivalent to full-GNS completeness; EX itself
is unproved. They are not silently added to the compact scattering theorem.
N1/B1/M1 provide the motivating exact source contract, not retained authority.
Their unchanged byte identities were checked through PR9476 head0cc560e.
