# Exact construction contract

Given the explicitly supplied S1–S4, construct a dense class of one-particle
creators on each selected compact mass/centre patch. Prove strong incoming and
outgoing products for every fixed finite family of separated packet velocities,
fixed-chart creator independence, permutation symmetry and the V action.
No uniform limit as separation vanishes is part of this contract. Nonzero
multiparticle norms, Fock extension and full-GNS exhaustion are later obligations.
