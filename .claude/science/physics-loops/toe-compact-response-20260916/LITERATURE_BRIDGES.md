# Literature bridge and limits

The source-bound note uses a conventional finite convex Gibbs covariance inequality after verifying the actual time-grid Hessian and noise normalization. The Gaussian comparison uses the source-variation mechanism already written on main and a published diagonal discrete Riesz theorem. The actual carrier, anisotropic rates, magnetic marginal, time weights and smallness regime are derived in this pack.

[Domelevo and Petermichl](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.03796) supply the dimension-independent diagonal operator bound. [Carlen, Cordero-Erausquin and Lieb](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.0709) supply context for the covariance tool. Neither source supplies a compact Hamiltonian photon phase, the native framework model, or the field-dependent integer-chart density. Their mathematical results are used with checked domains, not as a substitute for those physical obligations.
