# Route portfolio

The central route is source variation of the actual lifted Hamiltonian bridge. A positive entrywise majorant improves the initial maximum-in-time bound to space-time Lp Hessian and cubic bounds. This supplies the operator hypotheses needed for a real comparison theorem.

The complementary route is integer current geometry. A joint bad-cell bound, rather than independence, controls enlarged filling boxes. It constructs a coarse lift outside finite regions but deliberately stops before assigning its probability density.

The next route is an exact compact winding/region representation with normalized source weights. This is prioritized over further corollaries of the comparison theorem. Alternative active targets remain a direct fixed-g transverse susceptibility lower bound and model-selection/finite-clock matching. No failed method is counted as an axiom obstruction.
