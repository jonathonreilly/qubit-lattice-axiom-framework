## Review units

1. [Actual compact sine scores](notes/COMPACT_SINE_SCORE_DOMINATION_AND_STATIC_CURL_LIMIT.md).
   Positive electric-walk weights prove a volume-independent exponential
   bound for time-dependent sine observables at finite temperature. Exact
   static curl shifts give a quantitative Gaussian limit when a weighted
   cosine contact average concentrates. A supplied stationary ergodic
   thermal Gibbs path state supplies that concentration for smooth diffuse
   curl tests. Principal flux, general time dependence, ground-state passage
   and a photon pole are not thereby obtained.
2. [Exact cover and source identities](notes/EXACT_COMPACT_COVER_AND_SOURCE_IDENTITIES.md).
   Every interval winding, the initial fundamental cell, Haar normalization
   and the final temporal winding remain in the exact trace. Background
   derivatives retain their contact and mixture terms. A free circle checks
   the failure of inheriting convexity or cubic bounds by dropping winding
   fluctuations. These are standard finite-graph tools, without a novelty
   claim for covering kernels or Ward identities.
3. [Sparse-loop counterexample](notes/SPARSE_LOOPS_WITH_NONGAUSSIAN_FILLING_FLUCTUATIONS.md).
   An explicitly constructed stationary ergodic symmetric current has
   arbitrarily rare isolated unit loops and diagonal bounded covariance.
   Its diffuse local-filling observable nevertheless converges to a product
   of four Gaussians plus independent Gaussian noise, with fourth cumulant
   78rho^4 and exploding real MGFs. This refutes a specific inference from
   sparse geometry, not the Hamiltonian law or the framework axioms.
