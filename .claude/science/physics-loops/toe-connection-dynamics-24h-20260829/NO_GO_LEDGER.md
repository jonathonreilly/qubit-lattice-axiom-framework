- A same-`L` diagonal q=4 shortcut is false inside the fixed `J=1,K=0` block:
  the exact block has all six off-diagonal entries nonzero.  Sufficiency of the
  full `(L,J)` state remains open.

- O01 even `K=0` and O10 odd `K=0` cannot share the same scalar multiplier.

  is one directed order-four strand cycle.  Its K=1,2,3 blocks are nonsymmetric.
- Seven odd V3 labels are not seven scalar O10 coefficients; the honest
  `Hom_O(3)(V^5,V^3)` obligation has dimension 91 unless exact cup factorization
  is proved.

  decomposition and cannot test taste splitting.
