# Preserved failures and distinctions

The normalized slow-only two-dimensional example retains a nonvanishing mean error and divergent second-moment error for the same K, including reserve. It rejects that route only. Earlier product-comparator mismatches remain scoped to that different comparator. Small ordinary norm of the bare fast branch does not permit dropping its energy-weighted contribution. No proof permits replacing the full high propagator by free auxiliary evolution. One-excitation inverse data cannot substitute for the full inverse. Bare-H norm cannot replace augmented h_*. A convenient reserve majorant cannot replace actual v in phases or moments. Exact minimal real ceiling is not automatically effectively computable. These statements do not constitute a universal no-go or confer a no-go gate PASS.
