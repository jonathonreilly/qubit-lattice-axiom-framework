# Assumptions and imports

The chosen one-dimensional free massive chain, m,v>0, its actual normalized
current, chosen quasifree state and even-spin/CAR realization are explicit
premises. Concrete and Local Even supply the two direct scientific imports.
ENERGY and SHARP give comparison context; relevant coordinate/alias identities
are proved again in the source. The result imports no model selector, interaction,
higher-dimensional charge theorem or new physical axiom. General Hilbert/spectral
and CAR inductive-limit machinery is prior art; no generic novelty is claimed.
