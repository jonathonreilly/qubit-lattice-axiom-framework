# Derivation

The matched shift obstruction gives a nontrivial unitary. Faithfulness plus
cyclicity supplies a local defect vector. Unitary operator order gives the
sharp factor1/4; the local H domain and spectral tail yield finite-energy
weight. Both terms of the exact operator identity are retained for preparer
conversion. The full-block index estimate gives an optional quantitative
operator defect. Actual positive detector range gives kernel inclusion, with
chosen-family equality only under the separately checked projection theorem.
