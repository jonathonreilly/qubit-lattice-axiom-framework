# Scoped failed shortcuts

Whole-product energy compression does not factor into single-particle masks.
Vacuum support does not give full operator support. Disjoint velocity sets may
have overlapping convex hulls. Arbitrary detector menus may miss selected
directions. Positive support need not have a lower frame bound. Uncontrolled
joint time/resolvent limits fail. After time convergence, positive partial
supports can make some other iterated orders valid; the author's earlier
unfrozen blanket objection was corrected and preserved in its operation record.
These are algebraic/proof-boundary examples, not physical no-go models.
