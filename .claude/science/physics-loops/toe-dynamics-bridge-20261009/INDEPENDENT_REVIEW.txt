7. WHAT COMPOSES, AND THE SPATIAL INTERFACE THAT IS STILL MISSING

The channel limit plus fixed-count averaging genuinely composes to the
parent's supplied well-mixed finite occupation process. A subsequent
finite-time large-count limit can give the homogeneous mass-action ODE,
with the stated ordered limits or a selected finite-N error schedule.
The estimates themselves do not give a simultaneous physical scaling.

They do not yet instantiate the spatial kinetic premise T=v·grad:
(a) Interchange moves all labels by the same graph rule, not by their
    velocity-dependent streaming x -> x+v dt.
(b) The averaging here is over global count fibers. Rapid global mixing
    can erase spatial variation instead of producing local equilibrium.
(c) One occupied register per graph slot fixes the local particle number;
    a position/vacancy or multi-particle-per-cell construction is needed
    to represent an independently varying spatial mass density.
(d) Gate spacing h, interchange epsilon, particle count N, the kinetic
    Knudsen epsilon, and diffusive delta denote different scales. A spatial
    composition must specify their relation and control streaming,
    local mixing, statistical fluctuations and any nonlinear linearization
    errors on the intended time interval, with suitable uniform constants.

The next load-bearing mathematical object is thus an explicit spatial
microscopic model whose population/large-count evolution approximates the
supplied streamed kinetic equation. Identifying graph adjacency alone is
insufficient. This review does not supply that object, infer nonlinear
Navier–Stokes, select physical viscosity, or promote supplied interactions
to consequences of the minimal native axioms. Both reviewed notes correctly
retain these boundaries and name their physical suppliers.
