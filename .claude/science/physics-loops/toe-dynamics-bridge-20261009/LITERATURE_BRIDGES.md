# Literature screen

Bellouquid, arXiv:nlin/0306036, was inspected through the main theorems and
assumption (1.9). Its specific initial-data bounds are not silently replaced by
arbitrary nonconstant data; no nonlinear-limit theorem is imported here.

Carlen, Carvalho and Loss, arXiv:1804.10280v2, studies quantum Kac collisions
and propagation of chaos. Amini and Chalal, arXiv:2605.06973v1, studies a
Lindblad mean-field limit. Their abstracts were checked as context; their full
hypothesis packages have not been imported. Repeated-interaction and kinetic
corrector methods are established mathematical machinery. No priority claim is
made for those methods.

The new work provides exact constants and controlled constructions for the
specified repository models. Existing OpenAI-backed collision results on main
remain usable at their exact checked interface without duplicate external review.
