## 1. A failed fixed-kappa estimate

Let ell=sigma/2=5*10^-7 and v=1/40. The original reference variance bound for spectral mass outside the inner band is

```text
2 kappa^2/(v ell)^2.
```

At kappa=10^-8 this is 1.28, so it gives no useful lower bound on inner-band mass. The old numerical delta_m<13*10^-9 is not uniform in the requested family. Likewise the old separate installed point-amplitude ceiling 8*10^-5, obtained from variance at kappa=10^-12, cannot simply be retained at the upper endpoint. It is not a Markovian exponential-decay or linewidth completion-time argument.

## 2. Actual full-line reference resolvent and spectral measure

Use the scalar full-line free chain h=2JI-J(S+S*) and an emitter at E0 with contacts V=|L>-exp(i phi)|L+2>. It is only the exact reference spectral problem; the installed physical clock remains a half-line with the original gate. For E(k)=2J(1-cos k), k in (0,pi), put vg=E'(k). The retarded free kernel is

```text
R_f(n,m) = (-i/vg) exp(ik|n-m|),
Sigma_f = V* R_f V = (-i/vg)[2-2cos(phi)exp(2ik)],
Df = E(k)-E0-kappa^2 Sigma_f = x+i kappa^2 gamma,
s=Re Sigma_f, gamma=-Im Sigma_f,
x=E(k)-E0-kappa^2 s.
```

These signs agree with the actual retarded convention in the installed source. The scalar emitter resolvent is 1/Df. With

```text
g_plus=exp(-ikL)(1-exp(i phi)exp(-2ik)),
g_minus=exp(ikL)(1-exp(i phi)exp(2ik)),
a=|g_plus|, b=|g_minus|,
gamma=(a^2+b^2)/(2vg),
```

Stieltjes inversion on the compact interior band gives

```text
d mu/dk = vg kappa^2 gamma/[pi (x^2+kappa^4 gamma^2)]
        = kappa^2(a^2+b^2)/(2pi |Df|^2).
```

The two directional contributions are nonnegative. The desired one is `kappa^2 b^2/(2pi |Df|^2)=|yref|^2/(2pi)`, where `yref=kappa conjugate(g_minus)/Df`. The underlying emitter vector is normalized, so the full spectral measure has mass one, including any outside-band point masses. No completeness of scattering states over the entire spectrum is assumed. Positive gamma and the regular resolvent boundary value exclude a singular part on the inner band, as in the qualified installed argument.

The source's bounds on the wider fixed band are independent of kappa because Sigma_f depends only on the unchanged chain and loop:

```text
|Sigma_f| <= 160,  |Sigma_f'| <= 560,
gamma >= 19, vg >= v, |E''| <= 2J < .061.
```

On the inner band |k-k0|<=ell, a<=2ell=10^-6 and b<=2. Hence

```text
gamma <= (4+10^-12)/(2v) < G=81,
|s'| <= S=560, |gamma'| <= G1=560.
```

Writing kappa_min=10^-12 and kappa_max=10^-8, define

```text
Delta = v ell-160 kappa_max^2 > 0,
X = 4*10^-8.
```

At the inner-band endpoints x_-<=-Delta and x_+>=Delta. Throughout that band `|x| <= 2J ell+160 kappa_max^2 < X`. Also `x'=vg-kappa^2 s' > v/2`. These elementary scalar comparisons are to be checked exactly. No resonance is presumed centered exactly at E0 after its real self-energy shift.

## 3. A phase integral with its correction

For each positive kappa set `theta(k)=atan[x/(kappa^2 gamma)]`, taking the continuous principal arctangent. Since gamma>0, it is smooth throughout the inner band. Direct differentiation gives the exact identity

```text
theta' = kappa^2(gamma x'-x gamma')/(x^2+kappa^4 gamma^2),

d mu/dk = theta'/pi
       + [kappa^4 gamma s' + kappa^2 x gamma']
                       /[pi(x^2+kappa^4 gamma^2)].
```

The correction can have either sign; a lower mass bound subtracts its absolute integral. Monotonicity of theta is not required. At the endpoints, `atan(y)>=pi/2-1/y` for y>0 gives

```text
(theta_+-theta_-)/pi >= 1-2 kappa^2 G/(pi Delta).
```

Using x'>v/2 and gamma>=19,

```text
integral_inner 1/|Df|^2 dk <= 2pi/(19 v kappa^2),
integral_inner |x|/|Df|^2 dk
  <= (2/v) log[1+X^2/(19^2 kappa^4)].
```

The first is the exact real-line quadratic integral after the monotone substitution. The second follows by integration over [-X,X] of `|x|/(x^2+(19 kappa^2)^2)`. Thus, denoting the inner-band mass by m_inner,

```text
1-m_inner <= 2 kappa^2 G/(pi Delta)
           +2 kappa^2 G S/(19v)
           +(2 kappa^2 G1/(pi v))
                         log[1+X^2/(19^2 kappa^4)].
```

A deliberately loose uniform upper expression replaces kappa^2 outside the logarithm by kappa_max^2, replaces kappa^4 inside its denominator by kappa_min^4, and uses pi>3. No monotonicity of their product is needed. The logarithm is below 80: it suffices to check the exact rational inequality `1+X^2/(19^2 kappa_min^4) < (8/3)^80`, since e>8/3. The resulting rational mass-loss ceiling is

```text
Bmass = 2 kappa_max^2 G/(3 Delta)
       +2 kappa_max^2 G S/(19v)
       +(2 kappa_max^2 G1/(3v))*80.
```

An informal calculation puts Bmass near 4.3214*10^-7. The exact planned check will test `Bmass+10^-12 < 4.4*10^-7`. The extra 10^-12 will cover the directional fraction below. Unlike the variance estimate, this direct spectral-density bound includes all outside-band spectral mass without requiring a global density formula near thresholds or a separate classification of outside-band bound states.

## 4. Common envelope and conditional full seed overlap

The source cutoff chi is one on the inner band and goes linearly to zero at the endpoints of the full band. Its normalization is

```text
N_kappa^2 = integral chi^2 |yref|^2/(2pi),
f_kappa = chi conjugate(yref)/(sqrt(2pi)N_kappa).
```

On the inner band b>3/2, so the wrong-direction fraction is at most `(a/b)^2 <= (4ell/3)^2 < 10^-12`. Consequently

```text
1 >= N_kappa^2 >= (1-10^-12)m_inner
                >= 1-Bmass-10^-12 > 1-4.4*10^-7,
Aref = integral chi |yref|^2/(2pi N_kappa) is in [N_kappa,1].
```

The upper normalization uses the actual total spectral mass. The lower Aref bound uses chi>=chi^2; its upper bound is Cauchy-Schwarz. The normalization is positive real. For each kappa this is one old-independent mathematical envelope of the supplied product seed, not a new external packet preparation or a profile common to all different kappa values.

The exact installed Schur formulas are algebraic identities at any positive kappa. To apply them to this family, their inequalities must be freshly checked at kappa_max, rather than imported with the original numeric labels. With the original rational constants,

```text

a_max=2sigma, b_min=2sin(2k0)-2sigma, b_max=2,
eta=a_max/b_min, gamma0=b_min^2/(4J),
sPQ=a_max*206*delta, sQP=delta*206*b_max,

            +6 kappa^2 delta*206*b_max*(1+eta_p).
```

Every positive upper expression here is monotone in kappa provided xi<1. The proposed exact check is `eta_p(kappa_max)<4*10^-6` and `epsilon_off(kappa_max)<10^-14`, together with the full closed block gap, `gamma0(1-xi)>19`, and inverse-factor inequalities. The exact packet isometry still comes from the past-limit decay argument and selfadjoint intertwining.
