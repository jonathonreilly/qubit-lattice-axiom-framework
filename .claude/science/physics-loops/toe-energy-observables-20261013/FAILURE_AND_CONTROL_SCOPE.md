# Failure and control evidence

The archive preserves exact selected scientific/provenance evidence and its
earlier limitations. The chronology names metadata failures, prefreeze timing
caveats and truncated renderings without converting them into successful first
attempts or scientific negative results. Older active/pending statuses remain
historical rather than being silently removed from preserved bodies.

The publication worker's actual construction and bookkeeping logs live in the
off-repository frozen preparation package. They report source operations only.
Generated identity and publication programs were not run by the worker; no
runtime baseline, corruption-control result, canonical cache, graph delta or
staged compatibility result is claimed in advance.

Future root execution must retain exact streams and real outcomes. The four
proposed byte controls deliberately alter a temporary archive, publication,
metadata or parent input. They must fail for their stated integrity reason after
successful baselines; an unrelated startup failure is insufficient. Any partial
installation or failed command remains visible and prevents silent overwrite or
automatic retry. Original scientific countercontrols remain separate evidence.
