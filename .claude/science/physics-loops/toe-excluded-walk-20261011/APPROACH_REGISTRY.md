# Approaches and dispositions

1. All-channel resolvent plus explicit central coarea: proves the regular
   denominator and threshold coefficient without deleting virtual channels.
2. Rank-two compression plus a checked stationary theorem: avoids assuming
   that an infinite contact-coupling limit commutes with scattering limits.
3. Direct band/Duhamel comparison: controls arbitrary band packets at fixed
   time and at the displayed longer times without smooth-profile assumptions.
4. Canonical density commutator: supplies the finite-rank current correction
   rather than identifying the free current with an unspecified placement.
5. Coherent angular interference and quantitative Cook tails: gives a nonzero
   fourth-order finite-time witness, with order-seven form-factor regularity.

The contact-only packet and six-derivative estimate do not provide that
witness; their exact failed mechanisms remain in the source. Global projection
reweights K. Restriction and compression of g+F must be distinguished.
