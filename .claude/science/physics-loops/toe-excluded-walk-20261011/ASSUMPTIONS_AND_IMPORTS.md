# Assumptions and mathematical imports

- Supplied model: three-dimensional lattice Weyl symbol, continuous clock,
  antisymmetric two-record sector, normalized full-torus measure and exact
  coincidence exclusion. These are not derived from minimal physical axioms.
- Supplied energy placement: compressed additive symmetric local densities.
  Its bounded current is derived from the first-moment commutator on its
  stated Sobolev domain; a general placement needs a separate calculation.
- Central region: positive-positive channel, |K|<=E/4 and sufficiently small
  positive E for the sharper bound. All eight relative corners are retained;
  closed mixed channels still contribute virtually. Other total corners have
  different threshold behavior.
- Kato Theorem 1 (1957): bounded self-adjoint trace-class perturbation, checked
  separately in each fiber. Completeness concerns fiber absolutely continuous
  spaces, not all globally dispersing fiber eigenstates.
- Yafaev Section 2 (1992): injective Hilbert-Schmidt augmentation satisfies the
  kernel, compactness and boundary hypotheses. The active 2-by-2 reduction
  yields the normalized stationary contact formula almost everywhere.
- Analysis: finite-dimensional spectral calculus, local coarea and implicit
  functions, dominated convergence, Fourier integration by parts for whole-line
  W^(r,1) densities. Their relevant conditions are displayed in the proof.
- Packet: smooth compact K/E envelopes, nonvanishing scaled current variance,
  order-seven incoming/outgoing contact and current-form-factor bounds, and
  one global exclusion normalization. Uniform scaled derivatives do not mean
  uniform unscaled energy derivatives at zero.

E*, derivative suprema and the maximal small-parameter interval are not
numerically certified. Their finite existence is proved under these premises.
