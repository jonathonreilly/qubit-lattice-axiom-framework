# Checked mathematical bridges

[Kato, Theorem 1 (1957)](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/pjab1945/33/5/33_5_260/_pdf)
supplies fiberwise trace-class wave operators and completeness. The five-page
primary was read; the finite-rank perturbation verifies its hypotheses.

[Yafaev, Section 2 (1992)](https://www.numdam.org/item/AIHPA_1992__57_4_361_0.pdf)
supplies the stationary representation under its actual kernel and operator-
ideal assumptions. Relevant formulas were visually checked on journal
pp.369-371; the separate threshold reviewer also inspected p.368. The theorem
used does not require a constant-sign perturbation despite the paper title.

The threshold, canonical-current and packet estimates are derived here.
The bounded repository statement search and exact source scope are preserved
in evidence. This is no claim of worldwide priority or exhaustive literature
coverage. Neither imported theorem supplies a physical scattering clock.
