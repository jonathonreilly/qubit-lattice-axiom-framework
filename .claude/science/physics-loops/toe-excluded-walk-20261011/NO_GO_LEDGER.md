# Preserved counterexamples and scope restrictions

This milestone proposes positive conditional theorems, not a universal no-go.
The all-pi total corner has no contact-induced scattering; the central K=0
current vanishes. A noncentral mixed-channel example has small pair energy
with order-one individual energies. A pure contact-channel packet changes
only phase. Opposite coherent phases reverse the constructed current change.
These facts prevent broader interpretations and remain in the proof.

No conclusion of impossibility is inferred about other placements, corners,
models, physical clocks or many-body limits. Their hypotheses are not covered.
