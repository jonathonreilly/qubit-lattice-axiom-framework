# Target contract — block 1

Target: for the supplied four-dimensional free-box finite-clock Villain link/image
law at fixed integer N and fixed beta in a sufficiently dilute two-defect window,
establish a specified stationary thermodynamic state's full macroscopic flux and
score characteristic-functional limit, with Gaussian covariance containing a
nonzero Maxwell part. The order of limits and all six face components must be fixed.

Allowed premises: current minimal framework axioms/approved primitives in their
actual scope; explicitly supplied Villain law and counting lattice geometry;
current source mathematics with checked hypotheses and open review status tracked.
No new framework axiom, fitted coupling, or selected native law is implicit.

Forbidden weakenings: N growing with mesh, beta tending to infinity with mesh,
conditioning away a defect species, only two-point convergence, replacing the
quantized support by all real fields, or assuming the terminal Gaussian theorem.

Required cases: arbitrary finite real combinations of smooth compact tests;
longitudinal/exact and coexact sectors; finite-volume normalization and both source
signs; exhaustion/state matching; lattice zero modes and free-boundary conventions.

Completion witness: complete acyclic proof with a vanishing higher-source remainder,
identified nondegenerate covariance, and the same physical state/observable throughout.
A finite coefficient or numerical Gaussian fit is not this witness. A counterexample
must lie within the stated domain. A method failure is only a method failure.

Value: removes the fixed-clock full-field gap left by current main's Haar full-score
and clock smoothing theorems. It does not select the model from the native axioms,
supply matter/gravity, or certify a TOE.
