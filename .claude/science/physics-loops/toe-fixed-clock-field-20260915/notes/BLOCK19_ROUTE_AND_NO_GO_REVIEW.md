# Block19 route and no-go review

PROVISIONAL personal review; no independent audit or axiom update.

N1 — Routes. Exact Gauss/connection algebra simplifies the local band
problem. Scaled-form compactness supplies leading stability before
quasimode coefficients are used. Controlled off-band corrections supply
the remainder. Smooth flux envelopes and the finite-chain lower bound
separately test regulator growth. These are constructive matching routes.

N2 — Wall independence. Slow-level failure at insufficient cutoff is a
regulator consequence of the same ring's Fourier uncertainty, not a new
independent physical wall. The rate-free floor remains valid and is weaker
than resolving the O(g) levels. A ring is not a proof of the cubic phase.

N3 — Hidden premises. Four sites, two spinless CAR particles, eta=(0,1,0,1),
unit hopping, integer electric links and no magnetic plaquette are fixed.
The matter ground label is simple only in a chart about pi, which suffices;
no globally isolated band or globally periodic frame is assumed. Hard
cutoffs retain the exact charge-dependent allowed interval.

N4 — Matching. Every E0 offset enters A and C. The local unitary removes
the connection exactly and leaves a constant C' matrix. A deleted-offset
Hamiltonian has a different g^2 coefficient and is labeled adverse, not an
equivalent gauge. The actual full-field and reduced-coordinate CAR matrices
agree. The older source's formal leading coefficient is credited, while
the proof, next coefficient and cutoff distinction are new in this block.

N5 — Rhetoric. The gap is a finite-ring GLOBAL holonomy level of order g.
It is not a photon mass, charged particle mass or thermodynamic phase gap.
The strong cutoff condition is sufficient for the O(g^3) error, and its
optimality is not asserted. Full Gaussian-state necessity is not confused
with a complete finite-bandwidth spectral classification.

N6 — Partial closure. The ring slow spectrum, first two coefficients,
loop/flux limits and leading cutoff scale are established provisionally.
General periodic three-dimensional Berry matrices, twist-minimum geometry,
volume dependence and interacting charged excitations remain open. The
normal-mode 1/g electric scale is not removed by this ring's 1/sqrt(g) scale.

N7 — Steelman. Keeping the exact local Gauss connection avoids an apparent
derivative-coupling obstacle: the entire matter family has a common local
momentum basis. Increasing the cutoff at the stated rate recovers the slow
oscillator without changing any axiom. Other ring coefficients or references
can change the cancellation; that would require a new calculation.

N8 — Cross-cycle echo. The periodic-holonomy parent separated its formal
rate from its proved floor; this block discharges that specific obligation.
Leading spectral stability prevents the mistake of interpreting a small
residual as an ordered-eigenvalue count. Simon's correction on degenerate
parity is read; this one-dimensional simple ladder does not import the
uncorrected degenerate theorem. No author check is called independent audit.

Disposition: provisional positive slow-spectrum theorem with a narrow
cutoff obstruction. Broad phase or axiom no-go FAIL/not claimed.
