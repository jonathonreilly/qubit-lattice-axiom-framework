# Block22 route and no-go review

Provisional personal review. No independent audit or axiom change.

N1 — Routes. Ground-vector compactness, direct bounded translation/multiplier
convergence, normal energy-form convergence, fixed-time Duhamel comparison,
and ordinary Fourier Riemann sums connect the operator, state and observable.
Their roles differ; none is counted as an independent audit of the others.

N2 — Independence. The result depends on Blocks20–21 and applies only to their
declared example. It adds physical observable content to their level theorem;
it does not independently establish a finite-coupling charged phase.

N3 — Premises. Integer rotors, exact neutral Gauss space, the paired Wilson
Hamiltonian, unit gauge weights, fixed a, chosen arithmetic volume subsequences,
g->0 FIRST, and fixed physical supports/times are explicit. A global charge
creation operator is not treated as a physical local observable.

N4 — Matching. E is scaled by g, magnetic sine by 1/g. Normal transverse modes
survive; charge offsets and slow harmonic electric fields vanish at this scale.
Their absence from the limiting local covariance is proved from their distinct
scales, not from deleting the corresponding degrees of freedom. The original
ground remains Gauss-constrained. Symmetric unitary products retain the canonical
commutator convention. The first-time operator order fixes the sign in exp(-iwt).

N5 — Rhetoric. Gaussianity is claimed for a limiting characteristic functional,
not inferred from two-point agreement. Two-field unbounded correlations require
the additional energy-form argument, which is supplied. No arbitrary higher
unbounded moments or long-time uniformity are inferred from bounded probes.

N6 — Partial closure. The iterated physical local state and finite-time free
response are identified. Fixed-positive-g thermodynamic dynamics, confinement
or pairing control, interacting poles and a joint regulator/volume rate remain
open. The bare photon and Weyl metrics in this example are different.

N7 — Steelman. Alternating global twists do not prevent common local correlations.
Taking g to zero first gives a controlled comparator; a viable positive-g phase
would need additional estimates rather than contradicting this result.

N8 — Cross-cycle echo. The ring's slow gap, the periodic fixed-box ladder and
the massless lattice Maxwell frequencies answer different questions. They are
not three proofs of a photon at finite coupling. The full-field finite-clock
Euclidean problem in Blocks1–6 is also a distinct model/limit and remains open.

Checks: real-space curl and independent Fourier covariance matrices agree within
3.34e-15 at L3 and L4, with exactly52 and126 positive modes and the required
gradient/harmonic kernel. The explicitly shared noncubic Block21 fixture gives
the joint bounded-characteristic error2.11e-5 and the fixed-time magnetic-response
error0.00180 at g=.05; both decrease along the checked sequence. Electric and
magnetic variances are0.25074 and0.99841 versus limiting0.25 and1. The mixed probe
and spin covariance is0.000110. Residuals are below1.77e-11. These are diagnostics,
not proofs of asymptotic rates or an independent cubic many-body calculation.
No initial checker failure occurred. The extra unbounded-field justification
was added during proof review before the final result was checkpointed.

Disposition: provisional positive iterated-limit state theorem; no broad no-go.
