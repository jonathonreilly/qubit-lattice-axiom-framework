## Exact target and present wall

For the supplied four-dimensional finite-clock Villain law, prove the full
physical flux/score characteristic-functional scaling at fixed finite N and
fixed beta in a stated nonempty Coulomb window. Both integer defect species
must be included. Construct or identify the state and the covariance in the
same limit. A nonzero Maxwell part must concern the physical observable.
Growing N with the mesh, sending beta to infinity with it, deleting a defect
species, or projecting added white noise into a synthetic Maxwell field do
not count. Neither a two-point estimate nor any finite list of activity
coefficients supplies the complete characteristic functional.

The exact positive hybrid dynamics exists, but its pointwise curvature can
be negative and its exponential rates have nonsmall second moments. A
bounded alternative rate removes the latter issue without yet giving the
required observable response estimate. The coupled expansion retains the
right source and phase; its signed, all-order nonlinear summation remains
open. These are mathematical method obstacles, not evidence against the
supplied physics model or a reason to change a framework axiom.

Progress must supply a new usable estimate, construction, or counterexample
with its quantifiers. Completion needs the physical state and all-order
limit. A genuine axiom conclusion would instead require a native,
matching-premise inconsistency or independence result; this supplied-law
campaign alone cannot establish that conclusion.

## Reduction and the smallest useful tests

The physical requirement is a diffuse-source limit. A global all-tilt
Hessian, global pointwise curvature, or absolute row bound for every kernel
is stronger and can be deleted as a proof requirement. The model, source,
two species, fixed parameters, and state cannot be deleted.

Separate three problems: control the vacuum measure; control its response
to the actual macroscopic source; identify the covariance and state. A
vacuum pair estimate can help the first without solving the other two.
The smallest useful tests are one free four-cube for carrier/phase algebra,
a short chain of four-cubes for compatible multiple components, and the
infinite projection symbol for an infrared norm claim. More large Monte
Carlo runs would not decide these exact questions.

The new reduction is to use theta_P at an electric root and theta_Q at a
magnetic root. Their cosines are exactly identical. This produces root
physical-energy bounds and half-activity reserve inequalities for all finite
component shapes. Next test the surviving signed networks, rather than
automating a component expansion whose central estimate is missing.
