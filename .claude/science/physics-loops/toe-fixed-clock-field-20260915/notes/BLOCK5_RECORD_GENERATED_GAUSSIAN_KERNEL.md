# A record-generated full-domain kernel with growing support

Personal positive construction, 2026-09-15. Supply identifications of all
local possibility algebras with a common M2(C). Under a change of that
common presentation, all contents transform simultaneously. This comparison
of fibers, the classical formation-kernel interpretation, and the iid
priority process are declared model inputs. No physical matter/kinetic
identification, Born rule, or all-foundation witness is claimed.

## 1. Define the measure without a selected matrix basis or star

For a partial six-neighbour record profile eta, take every ordered word w
of lengths0,1,2,3 in its recorded contents, with repetitions allowed.
The empty word is I. Let Z_w be independent circular complex Gaussians
with mean0 and E|Z_w|^2=1. Define

    B_eta=sum_(length(w)<=3) 2^(-length(w)) Z_w w(eta),
    K(eta,.)=Law(B_eta).                              (1)

There are at most259 words. This is a Borel probability kernel on the
FULL algebra for every partial profile, including arbitrary nondiagonal,
non-Hermitian and singular entries. It is a pushforward of an explicitly
supplied coefficient probability; no Frobenius norm or output density is
needed to define it. The numerical weights and Gaussian law are supplied
law content, not a selection from the axioms.

Permuting neighbor slots merely permutes identically distributed word
coefficients, so the kernel respects all slot permutations and the lattice
symmetries. A real algebra automorphism takes a word to the same word in
the transformed inputs and acts on each central coefficient either by the
identity or by complex conjugation. Circular Gaussian coefficients have
the same law after that conjugation. Thus (1) is equivariant under EVERY
simultaneous real algebra automorphism, not just a finite matrix sample.

It varies with the conditions. If the sole recorded neighbor is0, B=Z_I I.
If it is I, the scalar coefficient variance is
1+1/4+1/16+1/64=85/64. Both laws are normalized probabilities; full support
on the entire algebra is not required at these central profiles.

## 2. Its support is exactly the algebra generated by the inputs

Let W_l be the complex span of words of length at most l. If W_l=W_(l+1),
right multiplication by any input preserves W_l, so every longer word is
already in W_l. Starting at dim W_0=1, a strict increase at every step
through l=3 reaches dimension4, the full dimension of M2(C). Consequently
W_3 is the entire unital complex algebra generated by the recorded inputs.

The coefficient Gaussian in (1) has full support in its finite coefficient
space. A linear pushforward has support exactly its closed image. Hence

    supp K(eta,.)=Alg_C(I, recorded contents).          (2)

This may be the center, a two-dimensional commutative algebra, a proper
three-dimensional triangular algebra, or all M2(C). No density on an
incorrect-dimensional carrier is asserted. Adding records enlarges the
input algebra, so support grows monotonically under record append.

## 3. Global irreversible realization and an actual noncentral seed

Use independent exponential activation times at blank sites, and at every
site independent Gaussian marks for all259 possible slot words. At its
activation the site evaluates (1) using only already present records,
then permanently locks the resulting matrix. The bounded-degree decreasing
priority proof used in Block3 gives finite ancestor graphs almost surely
and a consistent global process, also with any supplied compatible initial
records. Evaluation on a finite ancestor graph always produces finite
matrix entries; no global moment bound is assumed or claimed.

A formed output belongs to its formation-time input algebra. Later input
algebras contain that algebra, since existing records remain unchanged.
Thus (2) proves ongoing as well as formation-time support, one record per
site, and permanent content. This is a classical kernel on recorded matrix
labels; it is not a CPTP copying operation on unknown quantum states.

For an explicit compatible noncentral initial condition, place records

    (0,0,0)=A, (1,0,0)=A,
    (0,1,0)=B, (1,1,0)=B,
    A=diag(1,-1), B=[[0,1],[1,0]],

and leave other sites blank. Each seeded site has one A and one B neighbor.
The matrices I,A,B,AB span M2(C), so every seed lies in the support
determined by its initial neighbor profile. This initial condition is
supplied state data, not an empty-start event or an invariant preferred state.

Let x=(2,0,0) activate first among the relevant new sites, then y=(2,1,0),
with their other initially blank neighbors activating later. This event has
strictly positive probability: for example require T_x in(0,1), T_y in(1,2),
and the finitely many other blank neighbors' clocks greater than2. At x,
the only recorded neighbor is A, so the new matrix is

    U=u I+v A,
    u=Z_0+Z_2/4, v=Z_1/2+Z_3/8.

Here E|v|^2=17/64>0, so v is nonzero almost surely. At y, the recorded
neighbors are B and U. Since det[U,B]=4v^2 is nonzero, the four matrices
I,U,B,UB span the full algebra. The conditional formation law at y
therefore has full topological support on M2(C). This is reachable support
inside the declared global model, not a merely algebraically possible
profile whose compatibility was left unchecked.

If one starts empty instead, every formed record remains central by the
companion naturality theorem. The seed example preserves the distinction
between law covariance and state invariance. Transforming the entire seed
by a lattice symmetry and a common algebra automorphism transforms its
process law accordingly; no fixed seed is claimed invariant.

## Scope

The independent-fiber hypothesis of the companion theorem does not apply
to this construction. Products of neighboring contents consume the supplied
common-algebra identification. An alternative with explicit transport maps
would have to specify and transform those maps as additional data.
The kernel's support and process construction do not identify a physical
quantum readout or satisfy the approved matter kinetic primitive by fiat.
No claim that these inputs are selected by the existing axioms, or that
a new primitive is needed, follows from this positive example.
