# Goal

Derive, from the four axiom sentences and the three approved primitives
alone, whether sequential record formation on `Z^3` has a unique
lattice-covariant law, and if not, exhibit an exact witness pair together
with the minimal separating clauses (not adopted).

Exact target contract:

- Lemma C: no total order on `Z^3` is invariant under a non-identity
  proper cubic rotation about a site.
- Theorem E: sequential formation products are order-blind iff the
  adjacent-pair exchange identity holds.
- Classification: interior isotropic binary order-blind kernels are
  exactly the independent (constant) rules.
- Witness: a covariant varying kernel with two orders whose exact joints
  differ on path3, the plaquette, and `2x3`.

Success is a bounded theorem with an exact runner. The physical order and
the physical rule remain unselected.
