# Target contract

User request: review the repo, identify work most likely to advance the TOE or
expose a required axiom update, and run a 24-hour campaign.

Primary target: determine whether the supplied staggered linear gravity
constraint system has a nontrivial local nonlinear completion at the next field
order, consistently coupled to matter or record-clock content.

Domain: declared translation/proper-cubic-covariant local polynomial constraints
on the existing vertex/bond/face placement. Freeze the full ansatz, field degree,
support radius, lapse timing, equivalence relations and bracket-order convention
before computation. Start at coordinate radius one; explore radius two only
after pricing the first system. A finite torus must be large enough to eliminate
wraparound aliases; local coefficient identities or exact certificate lifts
are preferred. An ansatz-relative failure is never every-model impossibility.

Allowed premises: current four axioms and registered primitives within their
source grants. Canonical continuous tensor fields, their Poisson structure,
the matter carrier, action, clock, timing and derivative scheme are separately
supplied comparator hypotheses unless derived. No primitive or axiom is adopted.

Completion witness: explicit cubic lapse correction, quadratic momentum
correction and field-dependent structure functions whose entire declared
bracket system closes to the stated order, plus checked continuum/degenerate
controls; OR a complete enumerated ansatz and exact infeasibility witness.
Partial equations, an unresolved stronger/equivalent lemma, or a numerical rank
sample are useful research checkpoints, not closure.

Forbidden weakenings: silently expand M2 to continuous geometry, project away
the offending source, tune an empirical value, check only uniform lapses, omit
cross/Jacobi brackets, mistake an equality at h=0 for nonlinear closure, or
call finite samples an all-lattice proof.

Edge cases: zero modes, singular kinetic ratios, face timing/pairing factors,
boundary/torus aliases, null terms, disconnected sectors, state-dependent
structure functions and source/birth impulses.

Downstream value: decide what the present fixed-grid gravity candidate needs
beyond its linear spectrum, then pursue the best unresolved matter/record
supplier. Exact axiom pressure must distinguish a contradiction with supplied
axioms from failure of an optional comparator or insufficient premise content.
