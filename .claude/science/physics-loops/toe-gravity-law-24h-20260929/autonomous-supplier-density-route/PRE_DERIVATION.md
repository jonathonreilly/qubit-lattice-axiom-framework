# Pre-derivation: remove volume costs without hiding pulse-time costs

The finite-volume supplier serializes N center gates per bin, so its pulse width and global clock norms scale with N. The star-overlap graph has degree18. A proper19-coloring exists on every finite torus; on Z3 a27-color coordinate-mod3 pattern works (and is periodic on sides divisible by6). Same-color source/record stars are disjoint. Use a fixed number of color slots per bin and independent clocks with the same color program. Arbitrary finite torus coloring may depend on the torus; this is a supplied program, not a uniform infinite-state selection.

At a fixed product field box R, original complete magnetic terms and jumps are finite-dimensional bounded local interactions. A colored collision map differs from its local Lindblad exponential by O(tau²), and overlaps rather than total center count should determine the accumulated error on a finite regional instrument. Registers at unobserved centers are retained in the apparatus but may be traced when comparing the source. At observed centers the full original label is appended, with its coherent sign sum intact.

Uniform rotor-box transfer is not already stated by PR9399's order of limits. Its connected locality constants are independent of field cutoff because electric terms commute and the bounded whole-word norms are uniform. Both full rotor and compressed models can first be localized to a common finite spatial patch, uniformly over cutoff. On that patch the actual prepared-input weighted Dyson argument then removes the field box. All-time localization must use the same finite subdivision/normal-CP argument, not a norm-continuity assertion for the rotor electric group. This offers a noncircular order: choose spatial patch from locality first, then choose R from the finite-patch prepared comparison. For unbounded local source forms, apply higher moment bounds and bounded spectral truncation before this trace comparison.

For scheduled triangles at the color slots, the integral of the uniform local interaction strength over time is O(n times colors), independent of pulse width s. For ideal translation clocks, tracing clock coordinates gives an average over exact characteristic programs with independent initial offsets y_a. One may compare each perturbed program with the deterministic ideal program by Duhamel on the latter's evolved observable. The commutator bound is then supplied by the ideal program only; no velocity bound on all bad clock configurations is needed. At a good individual clock, integrated pulse mismatch is O(n sigma/s). At a bad clock it is at most O(n), since both positive pulse trains have total area bounded by n over the horizon/circle. Expected mismatch per center is therefore O(n(sigma/s+theta)), where theta is the packet's local bad-position probability. A summable spatial weight around the observed support replaces the global Ntheta union bound. Fejer smoothing similarly costs n rho1 per center. This has to be proved for the multi-time record instrument, including its quantum output, not just a scalar expectation.
