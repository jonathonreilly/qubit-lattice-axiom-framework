# Independent Abel observations before the extension proof

The author disclosed only that its final packet includes a connected Abel
extension with a lambda->0 loss. I have not opened that extension or its
proof. These observations record what can be checked independently.

For a bounded positive source of mass<=M and a contractive output channel,
fixed-fast-U convergence extends immediately to an Abel integral at EACH
fixed lambda>0: truncate at U, use compact-horizon convergence, and price
both omitted tails by2M exp(-lambda U)/lambda. This requires the same source,
output space and original instrument on both sides; it gives no lambda=0
conclusion or simultaneous lambda/epsilon rate.

A spatial sum requires more. A positive gain source alone copies spectator
mass and has no disjoint-support cancellation. A complete bounded insertion
of the form D[B_a]^*O=B_a* O B_a-{B_a*B_a,O}/2 annihilates tests disjoint
from its support. If actual local propagators obey a uniform finite-speed
bound, summing insertion norms over source centers in dimension3 is at most
C(1+u)^3 after splitting at a radius proportional to u and summing the
exponential exterior. Abel integration then costs a polynomial inverse
power, typically lambda^-4 plus lower powers. The exact complete insertion,
source-state dependence, retained marked outputs and any omitted Hamiltonian
terms must be verified before assigning that estimate to the author target.

Neither observation supplies unweighted waiting-time tightness, a stationary
Dirichlet identity in a changing microscopic state, or a joint physical-time
limit. The polynomial LOWER survival tail reconstructed in PRE is compatible
with finite Abel response at every positive lambda and with many low waiting
moments. No external theorem or computation is imported here.
