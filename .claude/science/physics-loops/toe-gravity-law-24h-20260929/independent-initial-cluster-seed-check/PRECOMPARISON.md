For any finite-depth circuit with fixed cone radius and local gates I+O(epsilon),
an initial-vacuum B occupation satisfies ||n_b Y Omega||<=C epsilon; an initial
filled A vacancy satisfies the analogous bound. Backwards conjugation sends
each such commuting projector to a bounded fixed cone. On the actual bare
ambient product Omega, projectors with disjoint backward cones have exactly
factorizing expectations; restricting Omega to its physical Gauss sector does
not destroy its original product property. This is independence of disjoint
input tensor factors, not a classical event assumption for later dynamics.

If an occupied connected animal has m B sites, remove those whose cones meet
the root A-hole cone and greedily choose pairwise-disjoint cones among the
remainder. At most nu vertices are excluded by each choice, for fixed geometry
nu. A weaker count max(0,floor(m/nu)-1) is safe if nu also covers the root
exclusion. Dropping all unchosen commuting occupation projections bounds the
joint event by the root-hole probability times that many one-site B factors.
A rooted-animal count bounded by degree^(2m) then yields the announced form.

For epsilon<=epsilon0 chosen sufficiently small, the geometric bound decays
in m after division by epsilon², uniformly in volume/spin. It is an INITIAL
state statement only. Exact source Y Omega is a mathematical frame of bare
preparation, not a proposed replacement physical input.
