# Root precomparison notes: local finite-spin response compatibility

Before any new author contract/proof/route conclusion. Exposure is the root's
assigned target and the already checked one-hole local rotor response and global
weighted finite-spin response. These are candidate independent mechanisms and
pitfalls, not a new theorem or an adopted source premise.

The global Q4 comparison cannot be applied directly to an arbitrary-background
local state or its Gauss completion. The exterior completion may carry large
fields and may leave the original spin box. An unweighted operator estimate
for Delta_S would also grow with volume. Both are genuine missing steps.

A plausible alternative is a local diagonal interaction picture. All terms
of Delta_S commute, have finite support and are diagonal. Conjugating a local
operator by exp(i Delta_S t) enlarges its support by a fixed radius, not an
iterated chain: terms originally disjoint from that operator commute with all
the other Delta terms. The conjugated Hbar_S remains bounded by744 on W1,
with hole motion at most two per factor. Its link/charge action near a moving
hole can depend on nearby fields, but never on arbitrary remote fields.

Comparing its local coefficients with the rotor can use the exact spin-weight
bound and the phase commutator. For a word supported near the hole, only finitely
many Delta terms change; their difference is bounded by a constant times the
squared total absolute field in a fixed enlarged neighborhood divided by C.
The two-hop terms can change occupations/gates of nearby A centers, so these
terms must be included, not only the two shifted links. A finite-radius field
Q_D has bandwidth at most two under the rotor H, commutes with loss, and the
previous bounded-band proof works with gamma=0 even in arbitrary B backgrounds.
It gives polynomial finite-time Q_D moment growth independently of volume.
No global total-field moment is needed for this finite-time statement.

A spatially restricted comparison process could then be used as a PROOF
reference, with actual outputs outside the restriction controlled by exact
hole-propagation Dyson tails. In the diagonal interaction picture the norm
bound and finite propagation remain uniform in S. The local differences
cost a polynomial in T times C^-1 sqrt(Tr Q_D^4 rho), plus buffer tails.
One must verify that any restriction used in the proof has a valid passive
terminal-plus-mark output or directly control the bounded local instrument;
it cannot silently delete actual physical paths from the claimed model.

Returning to the laboratory picture matters. Delta_S acts on every region,
even far from the hole. Its distant factors disappear only after the exterior
quantum system is traced out. A full-global-vector comparison cannot discard
them. On retained local X, the relevant Delta terms lie in a fixed thickening
of X and can be bounded with regional field moments. The immediate postmark
state requires the correct output-side Delta, since a birth changes W and
local gates. Coherent edge branches cannot be rephased independently without
accounting for their actual density. It may be possible to align only the
unobserved exterior unitary, separately at each original recorded time; that
is a proof purification freedom, not a changed mark or observed operation.

A rotor absorption tail could then bound the actual spin tail after one fixed
large horizon, using a LOCAL survival comparison with all original mark
probabilities. This would extend bounded local first-event outputs to all times
without proving spin waiting/field moments. The state preparation requirements,
order of S, buffer, count and time limits, and all finite-volume restrictions
must be explicit. A result at each fixed buffer/count is not automatically a
uniform thermodynamic microscopic limit.

In particular, the previous local cap N_B(D)+|Q_D|<=K with fixed K is not a
positive-density source estimate as the buffer grows. At fixed density K can
grow like radius cubed while the known reference decay worsens exponentially
in K. Even a successful local-spin lemma will still require a genuine
source-weighted cluster/localization argument; it cannot be called M4.
