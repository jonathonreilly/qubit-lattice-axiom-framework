## Independent route and things that must be proved

2. Independent local pin mechanism: a bad interior particle either pays the diagonal or belongs to an actual graph pair with a third occupied point at bounded distance. For each residual occupation that point pins a bare annihilation amplitude. At a physical boundary its nine-forward-bond anchors live on different rectangles, not one common periodic cube. A corner spectator may not be an endpoint of a given bond orientation at any admissible anchor, so blindly placing the old pin is invalid.
