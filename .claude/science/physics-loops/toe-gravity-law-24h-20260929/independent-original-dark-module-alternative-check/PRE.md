# Precomparison: full aligned-charge reduction and depth

Author brief exposure: a physical large-torus preparation first detected
by the augmented G/formal-source observation at H power L/4−1, mixed
full-star exits only through moving-hole terms, and aligned cross-sign
blocks only axial with norm at most6. The complete new proof is unread.

Root independently reconstructs the local source reduction: at an aligned
full-star input, F_hF_h* has only its six returns. A negative same-hole
Fa*Fa term must initially hop to an empty B outside the full star. If
its return removes a B inside the star, that output is bright; otherwise
the aligned star remains unchanged. It cannot produce a mixed FULL
star at the same hole. Shared-B moving-hole terms can change the
shared B charge to the departing A charge. To flip the output star
polarity, there can be only one shared B; a diagonal distance-two A
neighbor shares two and leaves one unchanged. Thus only six axial
neighbors contribute. Each literal two-hop block is a partial
isometry with norm at most1, giving total norm at most6 by row/column
Schur bounds. Need check all inputs, signs and physical field inverses.

For the depth candidate, locality gives only a bound until the first
actual minority charge/vacancy is reached; the exact charge capacity
and first nonzero full source observation require inspection. A unique
extremal axial path can prevent cancellation at first detection, but
basis escape by itself does not prove full-module observability. If
H has the existing volume-independent norm and observation is bounded,
vanishing first m powers yields a factorially small finite-time
observation bound for arbitrary prepared inputs as m grows. This is
not an actual-source ensemble statement or a uniform-energy bound.
