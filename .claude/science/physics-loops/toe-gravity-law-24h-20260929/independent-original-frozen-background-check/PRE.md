# Precomparison: fixed-background generic observability

Author exposure: complete supported H columns reduce to the magnetic
K* K on hole amplitudes for fixed B pattern/all occupied A+, provided
no B vacancy lies within distance3 and hole neighbors are plus. Flat
physical holonomy gives squared Fourier sums and a one-vertex
Vandermonde minor. I have not read the full new proof.

Independent preliminary reconstruction: characters restricted to A
are equivalent under simultaneous addition of pi to all three k
components. Their squared sums are nonidentical trigonometric
polynomials unless the unsquared sums agree up to one global sign,
since the analytic ring is an integral domain. Comparing coefficients
of each independent exp(±i phi_j) then gives precisely that equivalence.
Generic finite flat holonomies therefore have simple spectrum on A.
Every Fourier eigenvector is nonzero at every A vertex, making one
vertex cyclic via a Vandermonde matrix. A physical flat holonomy must
be exhibited in the actual Gauss cycle torus. An arbitrary independent
phase on every configuration-graph edge is not an allowed substitute.

The essential source check remains the COMPLETE canceled H columns,
including negative same-hole terms, charge permutations and outgoing
rows outside the support. A fixed-background compression alone cannot
exclude a full eigenvector. I suggested the author inspect direct sums
over B patterns where output patterns stay orthogonal; opposite
polarities are not automatically separable and their flat spectra agree
by k to −k. Any later extension requires separate actual column checks.
