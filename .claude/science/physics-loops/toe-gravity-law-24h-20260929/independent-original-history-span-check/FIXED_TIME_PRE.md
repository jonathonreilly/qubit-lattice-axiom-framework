# Precomparison: fixed-time rank through history determinants

The author's brief, not the complete new proof, has been read. Root
proposed testing initial-vs-postbirth generator factors and supplied an
unproved total-x-flux shortcut. The author found a stronger literal
no-four-cycle electric-support argument. Root independently reconstructed
that geometry and the determinant logic in discussion before this freeze.
No blind independence claim is made.

At minimal generator degree m_w=wL/4, total l1 saturation means every
individual field increment lies on the target's nonzero edges with
the target sign. Initial NB0 Q has only scalar or four-plaquette
components because all A charges are plus and both temporarily moved
B charges must return. Scalars fail maximal degree4 field gain; a
plaquette uses a four-cycle absent from the target support. The
actual support is the long x ring with distinct y preparation trees,
a disjoint special z tree and the final three-edge star (or first-hop
one-edge star). No hidden occupied-but-zero-field edge belongs to it.
Thus every leading term with a g0 generator factor is zero, while
the nonnegative postbirth coefficient remains nonzero. The complete
coherent marks and loss must be retained when verifying saturation.

For ratios g0=s(1−a), gi=sa beta_i, all beta_i>0 and sum beta=1,
leading amplitude Xi_w is a nonzero row constant times s^m_w a^m_w.
For d distinct a_j in(0,1), the leading determinant is Vandermonde
in x_j=a_j^(L/4), with a nonzero product of x_j and row constants.
The finite-order joint Taylor remainder makes it nonzero at all
sufficiently small positive s for each fixed d. This does not
require convergence of an infinite Taylor expansion.

Each determinant along these fixed complete-history rays is holomorphic
below the real axis and exponentially bounded in the fourth quadrant.
The checked scalar boundary uniqueness therefore gives a null zero
set. An invertible d-by-d pairing matrix implies that any nonzero
finite-dimensional test combination pairs with at least one actual
history; continuity in ratios gives positive density integral. Taking
a countable intersection over d and the two bounded output maps gives
common-time positive definite finite compressions and infinite rank.
Positive definite every finite compression still does NOT establish
faithfulness on the whole infinite-dimensional X or Y.
