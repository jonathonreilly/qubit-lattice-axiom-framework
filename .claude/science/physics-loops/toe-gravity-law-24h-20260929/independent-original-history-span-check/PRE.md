# Independent precomparison: actual history support

Author exposure: lower-half-plane bounded-loss Dyson holomorphy, open-time
integrated history support equals complete history span, single resolvent
words, and a scalar history-ratio ray yielding almost-everywhere positive
actual source diagonal when that diagonal is nonzero once. The complete
new proof has not yet been read. Root previously cautioned that a fixed
sum of positive real gaps has no interior in the multivariate negative
imaginary tube; fixed-time density does not follow from tube uniqueness.

Independent intended reconstruction: write each positive source diagonal
as a finite-mark sum of squared history amplitudes integrated over an
open simplex. If it is nonzero at one time, at least one interior history
ratio has nonzero amplitude. Vary total complex time at that fixed ratio.
Semibounded h with bounded dissipative perturbation should give a
locally bounded holomorphic scalar amplitude in the lower half-plane,
continuous to positive real times. If its nonzero boundary function
vanished on a positive-measure real set, boundary uniqueness after
local conformal mapping would force it to vanish identically. At a
nonzero ray time, continuity in ratios gives positive integral. This
proves almost-everywhere time positivity only if all bounded insertions,
semigroup bounds, sector positivity, and source indexing are exact.
It supplies neither vector-span density at one time nor a lower bound
uniform in the winding index. Those are separate claims.

For complete history span, Laplace transforms of individual positive
time gaps give resolvent words, and uniqueness of the vector-valued
Laplace transform can recover their span. Unbounded repeated Taylor
words are not an automatically equivalent substitute.
