# Precomparison warning: state-invariant sector versus observable cochain

Before reading the new full proof, root identified a possible quantifier gap. The saturated-B, W2 sector is invariant under leading state evolution, and its compression R intertwines the full adjoint B2 with its compressed Hamiltonian derivation: R B2=ad_H R. But a globally sector-supported observable need not be a B2 eigenoperator. Original bare-j gains can pull that observable back onto an incoming higher-hole sector. A general bounded normal superoperator S need not preserve the kernel of R, so R S need not induce a map on the compressed observable algebra.

Accordingly a nonzero compressed frequency shift immediately obstructs only a quotient-descending/sector-preserving cochain. It does not, on its own, rule out an arbitrary full-carrier solution [B2,S]=T. A full conclusion needs a construction of approximate eigenoperators AND bounded dual test functionals for the full generator, or a proof that the specified class of local S descends. Normalizable physical angle packets are necessary for the rotor fiber argument but do not alone repair this incoming-gain issue. This warning was sent to the author before completed proof exposure; no claim that the author actually makes this error is recorded.
