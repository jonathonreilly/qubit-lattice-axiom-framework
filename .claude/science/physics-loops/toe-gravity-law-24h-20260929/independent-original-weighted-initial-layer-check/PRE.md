## Reconstruction and likely temporal distinction

The contract's fixed positive-time weighted-hole target is not immediate
from the known unweighted defect density. Since S is of order epsilon^-1,
the crude bounded-spin weight would lose two powers of epsilon. The original
microscopic hopping has coefficient epsilon^-3, so a direct positive
weighted moment inequality is not a useful uniform estimate either.

A local near-identity normal form is a plausible way to prove only an
initial-layer result at t=O(epsilon^2). If a finite-depth circuit Y removes
W-changing Hamiltonian terms through enough orders, write schematically
Y H Y*=delta epsilon^-4 W+delta epsilon^-2 Z2+Z4+O(epsilon),
[Z2,W]=[Z4,W]=0, and Y j Y*=j+epsilon B+O(epsilon^2).
This statement itself must be checked from the actual local normal form;
a global fixed-volume spectral rotation does not supply uniform locality.
On scaled time s=t/epsilon^2, the penalty still oscillates rapidly but
commutes with the diagonal weighted-hole observables. The W-preserving
Z2 and original j dynamics act at bounded scale. The cross dissipator has
coefficient epsilon on that scale. Retaining its gain and anticommutator,
Cauchy/Young inequalities can plausibly bound its weighted source by the
weighted-hole moment plus epsilon^2 times an ordinary field moment.
The W-changing Hamiltonian remainder costs still smaller powers if its
weighted local-word norm is bounded.

For a rigorous moment closure it is preferable to avoid silently enlarging
a fixed radius ball at every derivative. Put q_e=1+|E_e|, and consider the
suprema over all sites a and all links e of <w_a q_e^2>, together with the
supremum over e of <q_e^2>. A bounded-range W-preserving term near a can
move the local hole only within its support; a term far from a commutes
with w_a. Each literal normalized shift changes E_e by at most one, so
q_e after any bounded-length word is comparable to q_e before it. Finite
local word coefficients, including E(E+/-1)/S(S+1), are uniformly bounded.
Thus a finite family of such local terms should close these two suprema,
without replacing a global volume factor by a ball volume. A fixed-ball
weight is then bounded by a fixed multiple of the sum of q_e^2 inside it.
The two-support moment permits arbitrary separation of a and e; this is
important for avoiding an infinite growing-radius hierarchy.

The anticipated estimate is x(t)<=C epsilon^2 exp(C t/epsilon^2), with a
similar bounded-rate estimate for the field moment on the fast timescale.
It gives x<=C_T epsilon^2 for t<=T epsilon^2. If that is the actual claim,
it does not establish the fixed-T contract. A logarithmically longer layer

1. Actual local normal-form coefficients and transformed remainder must
   belong to a weighted operator algebra uniformly in S, including normalized
   spin boundary zeros. Ordinary operator norm alone does not control the
   unbounded-in-S field weight.
2. Each leading term must really preserve the hole number on its local
   support, or have its nonzero W grade explicitly accounted for. A global
   block statement without a local decomposition is insufficient.
3. The diagonal electric part must be kept with its exact coefficient.
   Diagonal E polynomials commute with the physical diagonal weights;
   replacing them by a bound proportional to S^2 would destroy uniformity.
4. The full original coherent jump and the full same-mark cross dissipator,
   including loss, must remain. A resolved replacement or grade measurement
   cannot be used as a proof device for the coherent ensemble.
