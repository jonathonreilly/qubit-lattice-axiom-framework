This is a precomparison, not acceptance.

On a finite safe torus, the physical Gauss carrier decomposes into cycle-phase fibers with finite-dimensional matter matrices. H(theta) is Hermitian on real phases and has finite Laurent entries; G is diagonal nonnegative. The nondecaying subspace of exp[-i delta H-kappa G/2] at each phase is the maximal H-invariant subspace in ker G, equivalently ker of the finite observation stack G^(1/2)H^j, j=0,...,d-1. Replacing G^(1/2) by G preserves that kernel. Cayley-Hamilton makes this finite. The fiber contraction strongly decays on its orthogonal complement. Direct-integral dominated convergence identifies strong decay for physical vectors if the dark subspace vanishes almost everywhere, or if their actual source is fiberwise orthogonal to it. An isolated dark phase cannot disprove that assertion, and a generic-rank test on arbitrary source columns cannot prove actual overlap/orthogonality.

A plausible independent reconstruction of the slab mechanism starts from the complete canceled hole blocks, not an adjacency proxy. For layers h=y+z mod L, the output two layers above the highest occupied input layer receives only the +2 off-hole block when four consecutive layers above are empty. The four-hole-hop paths factor through the intervening B layer into two neighboring-match sums; same-hole B reshuffles cannot contribute there. Any injectivity proof must retain charge permutations, all B masks and blocked endpoints in both maps.

For one full alternating staircase cycle, the ratio of its two unitary matchings moves the vacancy and permutes the 2L-1 remaining signed charges. After L(2L-1) iterations both the vacancy and all charges return; the accumulated field is the ring cycle multiplied by its total charge Q_loop. That charge is an odd sum of +/-1, hence nonzero. The potential -1 eigenvalue of the matching ratio can therefore occur only on finitely many flux equations exp(i Q_loop Phi)=(-1)^[L(2L-1)], a measure-zero set if Phi is a nontrivial physical cycle coordinate. This must be verified for winding cycles and physical Gauss/tree conventions; a fixed symmetric-charge fiber does not suffice.

If a B vacancy breaks the cycle, dark input coefficients adjacent to it vanish. The matching-sum equation propagates zero through the resulting chain using invertible individual charge-transfer maps. This can give injectivity even for coherent charges, if all kernel rows and dynamic mask domains are actually included. Top-layer injectivity then removes one layer and induction excludes an eigenvector confined to such a proper slab. It does not make the slab invariant, prove absorption of a state initially in it, exclude a delocalized dark vector, or control any actual source survival mass.

The full criterion remains a distinct hard algebraic question: a proved nonzero maximal minor of the actual Laurent observation stack establishes generic full rank; a sampled numerical rank does not. If every such minor vanishes, a rational-function kernel over the Laurent fraction field exists, but a sourced dark eigenbranch still requires the correct invariant module and actual source overlap. No uniform decay rate, growing-volume estimate, finite-spin transfer or microscopic field-energy condition follows from a bare a.e.-phase result.
