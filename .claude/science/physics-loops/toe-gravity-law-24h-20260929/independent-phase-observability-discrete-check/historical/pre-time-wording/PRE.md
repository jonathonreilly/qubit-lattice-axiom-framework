## Actual canceled law

With one A hole h, the rotor coefficient H=C+[F,F*] gives

P_h H P_h=F_h F_h*−sum_(dist(a,h)=2)F_a*F_a,
P_a H P_h=[F_a,F_h*].

C cancels the other diagonal terms. Distinct-intermediate paths cancel. A dark input has every B neighbor of h occupied; hence for a hole-moving path the reverse F_h*F_a ordering at the shared B is blocked and the surviving F_a F_h* coefficient is positive. The original common loss is G=2 times the number of vacant B neighbors of h; coherent original signs are not resolved. These are operator identities with charges and field translations, not vacancy-only identities. Same-hole B reshuffles remain and must not be omitted from an eigenvector equation.

## Independently proposed spectral criterion

Choose integer tree flows for each physical charge word and cycle coordinates z_e on the finite torus. The physical rotor carrier is a direct integral of finite matter spaces, dimension fixed at finite L,k, with Laurent H(z) and phase-independent diagonal G. Let B project onto bright matter words. Form O(z) by stacking B H(z)^j for0<=j<d, where d is the entire fiber dimension. Cayley-Hamilton makes its kernel H-invariant and equal to the maximal invariant subspace inside ker G. For real phases H is Hermitian; the dissipative matrix has an imaginary eigenvalue precisely when an H eigenvector is dark. Thus strong decay in the full rotor Hilbert space is equivalent to O having full rank almost everywhere, by finite-dimensional stability and dominated convergence.

The generic rank is its rank over the Laurent fraction field. A nonzero exact maximal minor suffices; finite sampled numerical ranks do not. If generic rank is deficient, choose a nonzero rational kernel vector and clear denominators: a Laurent-polynomial physical section lies in the dark invariant kernel almost everywhere and is a normalizable, finite cycle-support state. It need not be a single H eigenvector. This gives a genuine nondecaying Hilbert state, but says nothing about overlap with actual Omega-derived source sections unless that overlap is separately established. Different charge words have different tree reference flows; the common Fourier embedding must preserve Gauss exactly.

## Independently proposed staircase/slab argument

Use the cyclic height r=y+z mod L. Assume a dark eigenvector has hole support in an interval of at most L−4 layers; this conservative margin prevents reverse two-hop paths from wrapping from the opposite end. At its highest nonzero layer m, look at output hole layer m+2. Same-hole reshuffles and all diagonal terms multiply zero input at that output layer. The only remaining terms are the two-forward-step block of F F*, with fixed B mask and actual charge/field translations. Its factorization should be the two incidence maps from A-hole layer m to B-vacancy layer m+1, then to A-hole layer m+2. Both are sums of y and z path maps. This must be established before using injectivity; replacing either sum by a chosen edge would miss interference.

At fixed x each such incidence comparison runs along an alternating2L-site staircase ring, or an open chain if an occupied-B mask/domain deletes a necessary vertex. Open chains have endpoint equations and should be injective without a phase condition. On a closed ring, normalize one path map to get I+U, where U transports the vacancy by two sites with a charge permutation and physical phase. After L steps the vacancy returns but the2L−1 occupied charges are cyclically permuted. After L(2L−1) steps all charges return, each charge has traversed the complete ring once, and the phase is exp(±i Q_ring Theta_ring), with odd nonzero Q_ring=sum of2L−1 charges. A kernel of I+U would require this phase equal to1 because L is even. A finite union of such nontrivial phase hypersurfaces is null. This is the point needing exact orientation, charge-orbit and broken-domain verification, not a scalar holonomy assumption.

If both incidence maps are injective for those generic phases, the highest layer vanishes, and descending induction excludes a slab-supported dark eigenvector. This only excludes a stated support family; a dark vector spanning all cyclic layers or coherently mixing evolving masks is not automatically excluded. Cross-mask highest-layer outputs remain separated because every hole move preserves the B mask. The absent same-hole terms must be justified by the output support, not by pretending masks are static.
