# Independent selective check of the scattering-channel gap

Root derived this classification from the actual frozen native-stability
Hamiltonian before reading any scattering-route proof or code. The author's
progress brief supplied the proposed perfect-matching projection and reported
the same64-graph control; the present justification is the following elementary
classification, not a claim of blind independence of the question or count.

In the four-particle occupation basis let P select configurations whose induced
eighteen-neighbor graph admits two disjoint edges, and Q its complement. For
any four-vertex simple graph without two disjoint edges, either it has an
isolated vertex, or its edges form a star with one degree-three vertex.
Proof: pick edgeab; every other edge touches a or b. If both endpoints have
neighbors outside{a,b}, avoiding disjoint edges forces their common outside
neighbor and a triangle; the fourth vertex must then be isolated. Otherwise
all nonisolated edges have a common center, hence absent isolated vertices the
graph is the three-leaf star.

For degrees0,1,2,3 the diagonal polynomial(degree−1)(degree−2)/2 is1,0,0,1.
Consequently every Q occupation word has D>=1. The checked full-carrier
Hamiltonian is H=mu D+S+W with S,W positive. Orthogonal compression gives
QHQ>=mu Q on the actual hard-core sector, without any pair-boson replacement.
On a finite torus this immediately yields||(QHQ−E)^(-1)||<=1/(mu−E) for real
E<mu. On the infinite N=4 sector the same quadratic-form bound holds on finite
occupation support; any use of its resolvent still needs the stated operator
realization/domain. Finite-range finite-branching particle moves make this
sector bounded, but the author's explicit operator bound is not yet inspected.

A separate exact64-graph enumeration confirms the minimum diagonal value1
on all27 graphs without a perfect matching. It is a finite classification
control, not enumeration of the full many-body lattice.

This selectively checks the proposed closed-channel gap only. It does not yet
check the nine-bond Fourier symbol, finite-core PHQ support, actual E-pair
coupling, threshold limiting resolvent, absence of resonances, scattering
length/matrix or the many-body phase. Those remain open for subsequent source
and independent examination. No formal review PASS or audit is issued.
