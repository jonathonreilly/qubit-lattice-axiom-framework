Strength tests: a genuine many-pair comparison plus an effective variational
solution implies the leading-energy target; the scalar energy coefficient
alone does not recover a state/observable embedding. The direct extensive
pair-density-matrix eigenvalue statement is the selected condensation
target itself. Mesoscopic coherence and finite-mode weak commutators do not
imply that statement: their bounds remain subextensive at fixed nu. A
diagonal sign gauge is not required by any phase target and its failure is
only a route-local test.

No global campaign stop is inferred. These are distinct mathematical
formulations and mechanisms, not labels for agents or artifact types.
