# Route families and exact scope decisions

1. Charge-preserving spectral filtering: ATTEMPTED, candidate completed in
SECTOR_FLUX. The full-ground survey theorem was insufficient as stated for
arbitrary exact N. The detailed U(1) two-cut construction admits a direct
neutral-vector proof. Full-space LR and exact local factors avoid projecting
locality onto the charge sector. Centering the slab charge pays the translated
cut's phase; no flux-path gap is assumed.

2. Exact discrete symmetry: ATTEMPTED, completed in EXACT_PARITIES. Every
literal row has a fixed staggered character on even tori. Translation
anticommutes with its corresponding parity at odd N. The complete coherent
rows, rather than a chosen dimer graph, produce eight orthogonal partners.
The continuous-dipole and odd-seam extensions fail at an actual nonzero
matrix element. These failures delimit these extensions only.

3. Direct affine twist and density variational response: ATTEMPTED. Averaging
opposite twists cancels the current without a real-state assumption. The
actual all-state row bound gives O(N/L^2), useful for N=o(L^2), inadequate as
a fixed-positive-density small gap in three dimensions. The density f-sum
needs actual homogeneous response weight; the flux construction does not
supply it. No generic no-go on a homogeneous collective phase follows.

The all-N EOS, paired local mass and low pair-gradient energy suggest routes
to static low-momentum concentration, but not by themselves a macroscopic
mode or a lower bound on the relevant homogeneous density variance. No new
proof of those stronger targets is asserted. A dressed-pair trial, a response
lower bound, a groundspace analysis or an actual correlation estimate remains
a possible route. The three real families above are not relabeled into a
five-family exhaustive no-go packet. No such packet PASS is requested.

Scope audit: the odd-N statement requires even periods and translation;
continuous dipole-U(1) is not a symmetry; the flux alternative requires the
finite N/L arithmetic; unique-sector and unique-full ground are distinct;
zero-energy partners and positive-frequency density modes are distinct.
These are hypotheses and logical boundaries, not five asserted independent
walls to a physical phase. Their mutual independence is not claimed.

All lower energies in other charge sectors are an intentional hostile test
for SECTOR_FLUX: they are harmless only because every filter vector and local
twist preserves N exactly. Removing that neutrality invalidates the sector
proof. Removing the fixed local norm/range invalidates its volume-uniform
propagation estimate. The literal main source supplies both for this H0.

The strongest next objection is that all low energy delivered by these
arguments could lie in a ground multiplet or couple only to the extended
flux operator. That possibility is not excluded. Closing it requires an
actual local matrix-element or homogeneous susceptibility estimate; it is
not a labeling convention and is not claimed to require a new axiom.
