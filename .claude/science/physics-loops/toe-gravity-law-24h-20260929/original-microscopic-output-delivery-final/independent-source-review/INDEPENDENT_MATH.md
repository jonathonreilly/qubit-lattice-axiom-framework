# Independent reconstruction at the frozen source

This is the reviewer's analytic check, not an execution of the author's primary or a second simulation. It uses the supplied local operators, finite matrices and elementary inequalities directly. The finite primary results below were also reconstructed combinatorially without importing its functions.

1. Put [W,F]=F and T=-(F+F*). The anti-Hermitian first homological coefficient is S1=-F+F*, since [S1,W]=-T. Direct expansion gives [S1,T]=2[F,F*], hence D2=C+[F,F*]. Adding the supplied C cancels the occupied-A same-center term except for the diagonal E(E+k)/Cspin term. The remaining three families are F_a F_a*, F_a*F_a(Q_a-1), and overlapping [F_a,F_c*]. Each preserves total W and vanishes on the fully occupied local A subspace. On cubic geometry their conservative per-hole path counts are 36, 18*36, and 18*2*2, totaling 756. Every path changes sum|E| by at most two. Symmetry of absolute matrix entries converts the resulting row bound into the stated form bound with 1600W. The field-polynomial piece commutes with sum|E|. These facts are special to the actual compensation, not generic normal-form assumptions.

2. For [W,J_r]=rJ_r, the grade-average of D[J]*W is sum_r r J_r*J_r. The first derivative [S1,j] has grades zero and minus two; positive jump grades therefore start at epsilon squared. The offgrade drift R is O(epsilon^-1). With K1=i epsilon^4 I_W(R)/delta, i delta epsilon^-4[W,K1]=-R: the sign in the source is correct. K2 cancels (1-P)B K1. Since the leading B_-2 preserves grades, P B_-2 K1=0. The surviving local error is O(epsilon^2), not O(epsilon). Bare Omega has zero vacancies, and each local rotated vacancy costs the square of an O(epsilon) commutator. Integrating the corrected observable therefore supplies both the O(epsilon^2 N) vacancy bound and the nonnegative negative-grade activity budget. No gap, thermal state, global norm closeness of Y, or volume-independent global spectral perturbation constant enters.

3. The weighted Schur norm controls [sum|E|,A] because each matrix entry pays only the field displacement of A. Original hops, diagonal normalized compensation, the grade maps and each finite-cone gate have uniform bounds in that norm. Thus the field observable's growing norm never enters the remainder constants. Leading averaged field drift is bounded by epsilon^-2 times W plus a bounded interaction density. The same two correctors apply; physical return gains another epsilon from the rare-hole offgrade expectation. This yields the displayed first-field estimate. Translation invariance belongs to the original law and Omega; it is not assigned to the colored circuit.

4. The negative-grade budget first bounds the SUM of squared J_-1 amplitudes. Since J_-1=j+O(epsilon^2), it bounds the sum for bare j in rotated coordinates. Pullback gives Y*jY=j-epsilon[S1,j]+O(epsilon^2). Only its first-order truncation is translation covariant, so division by N is legitimate only after that pullback. For the full derivative, [S1,j]=jF_a-F_a j+[F*,j]. The latter commutator has grade minus two and kills fully occupied A input; F_a j has an input vacancy. Their squared contribution in the physical state is O(epsilon^2). This reconstructs the local integrated O(epsilon^2) squared error after dividing the amplitude by epsilon. Factoring the gain difference and applying time Cauchy gives O(epsilon), uniformly for extensions sharing the same marginal. It gives no free anticommutator estimate on arbitrary tests.

5. For cumulative means/history functions, the grade-zero jump is epsilon(Bhat-D)+O(epsilon^3), where Bhat=jF_a and D=F_a j occupy disjoint A-occupancy blocks. Thus A*A=Bhat*Bhat+D*D. The complete offgrade loss potential has the same two-corrector cancellation. Simultaneously recording all translated copies is legitimate classical bookkeeping of one event history: updates may be many-to-one, but their full Kraus column has identity loss. A mark updates at most |F| copies. Only physical translation covariance is used after return. This yields the stronger signed O(epsilon^2) history balance, including O(epsilon^3) bin endpoints, without replacing joint quantum/history correlations by conditional rates.

6. Compress a local test by filled A sites and by the field cutoff on all links incident on its matter sites. The first compression makes it commute with each vacancy projector. The fast D_vac commutator and bare jump dissipator then have two-sided hole support, giving epsilon^-2*O(epsilon^2). The first cross map has odd grade and costs epsilon^-1*O(epsilon). The diagonal electric piece costs K(R^2+R) by the exact coupled scaling. Gentle projection costs R^-1/2 and the circuit costs epsilon. Balancing R^-1/2 with hR^2 gives R approximately h^-2/5 and exponent 1/5. Restricting an arbitrary common-carrier test first to the spin box makes this valid also at R>S. Finite-rank cutoff compactness plus asymptotic equicontinuity gives a uniform subsequence; finite discarded paths are individually continuous. None of this determines a quantum generator or a unique limit.

7. The spin coefficient satisfies (1-sqrt(1-x))^2<=x for 0<=x<=1, and m(m+s)/(S(S+1))<=|m|/S. Outside the box the zero extension has squared error one, also bounded by |m|/S. Telescoping a fixed word with rotor partial permutations on the right shifts the first-moment weight by only a fixed integer. This proves the common-carrier quadratic-form estimate for the whole coherent word after Cauchy, without dephasing its branches. Gain factorization then costs S^-1/2. Uniform state convergence and the original O(epsilon) gain estimate identify the same subsequence's currents. Passing the exact register gain-minus-loss equation uses a bounded append map, not an unbounded count observable. Its limiting marginal is Lipschitz because the rotor source words are bounded.

8. For a neutral test O and negative-grade V, VP_U=0 and [O,P_U]=0 on a support containing both. Hence the loss expectation is at most sqrt(Tr sigma V*V)*||V||*||O||*sqrt(Tr sigma Q_U). With the integrated epsilon^4 budget and pointwise epsilon^2 hole bound, multiplication by epsilon^-2 leaves O(epsilon). Grade minus two squares and the neutral D terms are smaller; their coherent gain cross terms are retained and supported on holes on both sides. The full offgrade map is handled by the exact registered generator's two correctors. Covariance yields D_micro*(Y*OY), not D_micro*O. The stated dressing boundary is essential and correct.

9. A linear post-event field weight has exact output norm at most 1+|Z|S. Its multiplication of the squared amplitude error gives O(epsilon^2 S)=O(epsilon). The comparison word changes fields by at most two per branch, so its weighted activity is controlled by the proved first field moment. Weighted gain factorization gives O(sqrt(epsilon)). A quadratic weight instead has price O(S^2); this argument supplies no vanishing error. The text presents this as a limit of its proof, not as an impossibility theorem.
