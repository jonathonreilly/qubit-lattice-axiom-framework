# Narrow spin-input correction to the mean/field corroboration

The historical `check_mean_field_algebra.py` and `MEAN_FIELD_ALGEBRA_RESULTS.json` are preserved unchanged. Root's subsequent complete read identified a genuine finite-control scope defect: `loopbirth=next(iter(seed_births))` was constructed using rotor words. Its field on the seeded negative edge is -2, so it is not a spin1 input. The historical spin routines checked only output fields, allowing vacuous checks for this input. The other six named inputs have |E|<=1. In particular all six full W1 current rows, the boundary controls starting from the legal circulation seed, and the coherent Omega/one-hole input are unaffected. All rotor controls also remain valid.

The separate runner `check_mean_field_spin_inputs.py` preserves the historical input for diagnosis and supplies an explicit legal replacement: from the same circulation seed, hop from a_loop=(1,0,1) to a_loop+(1,0,0), then make the original resolved + birth on a_loop+(-1,0,0). Both intermediate states are physical Gauss states with |E|<=1, and this word occurs nontrivially in the original spin1 source. The corrected control asserts validity of EVERY input to a spin operation, including intermediate linear supports.

Only affected spin controls were rerun.
