In one finite physical W=1 rotor phase fiber let K=ker G, M be its full mixed-star projector, and A=projection onto the remaining full aligned stars, with no local A-charge-cone condition. Let N=intersection_(j=0,...,d-1) ker(G H^j), the largest H-invariant subspace inside K, and Pi_N its orthogonal projection. Define N_A separately as the largest H-invariant subspace contained in Ran A. Static N intersect Ran A need not be invariant; only N_A is safe for invariant-module decompositions.

For the complete original positive-grade source stack B_h,mu=-F_h j_mu F_h P0, a nonzero word uses three distinct initially empty B neighbors. Output star occupation rises by three. On a K output the input therefore had exactly three occupied star sites, and the three newly occupied charges include nu and minus nu. Thus no aligned full-star output occurs. The checked formal frame gives

    c M <= P_K sum_(h,mu) B_h,mu B_h,mu* P_K <= C M,
    (c,C)=(56,144) resolved, or (40,288) coherent.

The input-star-three projections may be inserted because all other outputs are removed by P_K. Complete arbitrary physical W0 inputs are meant; a state-weighted actual input is not the identity in this formula. Therefore

    Pi_N B=0  iff  M Pi_N=0  iff  N subset Ran A
              iff  N=N_A.

For Z=-i delta H-kappa G/2, delta,kappa>0, Pi_N reduces both terms. Its complement has strictly decaying finite-dimensional semigroup, because any imaginary-axis eigenvector would have Gv=0 and be an H eigenvector. Contractivity excludes imaginary Jordan growth. Phasewise almost-everywhere absorption gives unweighted phase-integrated strong convergence by dominated convergence; no uniform rate, weighted residence, finite-spin transfer or continuous microscopic forcing follows.

The actual-history condition is weaker:

An exact operator route for the stronger formal condition is

    rank [O_G; M] = rank O_G,  O_G=[G; GH; ...; GH^(d-1)].

Over the actual Laurent fraction field, it is equivalent to row-span containment and, after clearing denominators, an identity

    p(theta) M = sum_j C_j(theta) G H(theta)^j

for one nonzero Laurent polynomial p and actual Laurent matrix coefficients C_j. This would suffice outside its null zero set without proving full rank O_G. It is not presently derived. Fixed-phase kernel containment is also equivalent to a finite positive-form inequality M<=C(theta) O_G*O_G, but its constant need not be uniform or integrable.

A further direct identity is the formal-source cyclic subspace inside N:

    span_(j=0,...,d-1) H^j Pi_N Ran B = N orthogonal-minus N_A.

Its orthogonal complement inside N consists exactly of vectors whose whole H orbit is orthogonal to B. The frame says every such orbit vector lies in A, giving N_A. This formal cyclicity statement neither makes actual histories dense nor proves N=N_A.
