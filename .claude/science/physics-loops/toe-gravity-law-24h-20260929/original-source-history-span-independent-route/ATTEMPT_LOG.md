This is not an exhaustive failed-route/no-go packet.

- Direct symmetry restriction: total charge, number and Gauss restrictions
  reproduce the already declared physical sector. Spatial symmetries permute
  actual labels rather than fixing each history. No extra common invariant
  excluding a physical input was found.
- Exact winding cutoff: the complete actual winding proof disproves extending
  the no-birth zero-winding invariant through births. Finite bandwidth of one
  product is not a bound on all waiting histories.
- High-density frozen masks: a no-event frozen occupation configuration may
  still have incoming birth amplitudes. I did not establish annihilation by
  every incoming mark and did not promote this to an unreachable subspace.
- First-hop interference: the separately checked eight-word kernel is a
  formal physical input. It need not be invariant under KD/Q/R or other
  marks; it cannot be used as an actual unreachable example.
- Taylor/Krylov shortcut: individual finite-order derivatives on finite words
  lie in a history span, but a converse would require a separate cyclicity
  argument. The proved bounded-resolvent characterization avoids it.
- Fixed-total-time continuation: invalid as a direct multivariable inference;
  varying one constrained gap changes another imaginary part oppositely.
  The proof retains only open-gap support equality and uses a distinct
  one-variable fixed-RATIO ray for scalar almost-every-time positivity.

Useful outcome: actual integrated-source support identity, common-time
unbounded winding diagonals, and bounded-word formulation of the genuinely
remaining coherent reachability statement. Full P3 density remains open.
