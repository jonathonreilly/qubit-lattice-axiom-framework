# Fixed-time rank: supplemental analytic target and discriminator

The exact target is infinite rank, at a common dense-G_delta full-measure
set of positive source times, of the actual dark-compressed source and
actual first-hop output at one fixed finite graph, b and original label
string. The stronger intermediate target is strict positivity of every
finite compression to the first d vectors of the fixed winding families.
Full closed-span faithfulness at fixed time is not targeted or inferred.

Inputs are the source-bound frozen proofs10eb8ccd and809a5838, together
with the actual landed initial-sector scalar/plaquette form and complete
winding proof633126b7. No change of initial state, observed labels,
Hamiltonian, loss, electric measure or limit order is permitted.

Prederived discriminator: the literal nonzero electric support of each
chosen winding word contains no four-cycle. At the first possible total
Taylor degree, l1 saturation therefore forbids any initial no-birth Q
factor, since the initial-sector operator is scalar plus plaquette shifts.
The leading polynomial has no initial-gap variable. Varying the fraction
of total time spent after the first birth gives powers a^(wL/4), with an
exact generalized Vandermonde determinant. Holomorphy of the determinant,
not the density matrix itself, is the proposed positive-time extension.

The root independently suggested a related signed-Jx discriminator, whose
fixed-mark assertion remains unproved and is not used. The author supplied
the sparse electric-support argument and disclosed it before writing this
proof. Root then reconstructed its proposed topology and Vandermonde
mechanism. That exposure is not an independent check of the final proof.
No computation or resource job is planned; a focused proof check is needed
before downstream reuse. Original deadline and output scope remain.
