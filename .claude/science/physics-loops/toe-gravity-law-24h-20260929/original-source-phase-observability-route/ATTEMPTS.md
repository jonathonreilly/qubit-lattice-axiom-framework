# Attempt record and exact stopping condition

This records analytical attempts on the complete original W1 rotor sector. No new coefficient/law, random background, phase distribution, proxy graph or stationary source was supplied.

1. **Full Laurent observability.** The physical Gauss decomposition gives actual finite Laurent matrices. Cayley–Hamilton makes the full observation stack exact, and rank deficiency yields a finite-field invariant dark vector. This is a useful certificate format, but the full-rank minor is still missing. At k=n−1 even its necessary Krylov depth grows at least ceil(n/6)−1, with exponentially large charge fibers. A shallow sparse rank sample was therefore not run. Generic connectivity or an arbitrary independently weighted matrix cannot replace these actual coefficients.

2. **Actual charge monodromy.** The opposite y/z inward matchings differ by a unitary that circulates the hole and permutes 2L−1 charges. Following all charges through L(2L−1) steps gives the exact odd-charge field circulation. It supplies one simultaneous physical pi-z witness and almost-everywhere injectivity of the canceled highest-layer block. Broken occupied rings are chains with injective end rows. This closes the proper-slab dark-EIGENVECTOR question. The literal sparse control checks that primitive monodromy only, not the theorem or full observation rank.

3. **Trying to close the periodic wrap.** The slab peeling proof needs an actual outer row. With only three omitted layers, a lower-side −2 hop can reach the same row as the upper-side +2 hop. With all layers occupied, no outer row exists at all. The invertibility of the forward block then gives a coupled periodic recurrence, not zero amplitudes. The odd cycle charge does not on its own show that the eigenvalue-dependent recurrence has no dark solution. Replacing that unresolved recurrence by the two-matching monodromy would drop diagonal/same-hole B reshuffles and the other transverse paths. This attempted extension was stopped rather than asserted.

4. **Fixed B-mask and scalar-charge shortcuts.** Hole-moving terms preserve the B mask, but same-hole terms need not. For example, with dark hole h, occupied c=h+e_x, occupied A site a=h+2e_x, and empty b=h+3e_x, the actual −F_a*F_a word first moves q_a to b and then q_c to a. It leaves the hole at h but replaces occupied c by b, with both original field shifts. Its axial shared-center path is unique. Hence the full law does not preserve the old mask. Similarly, the symmetric-charge fiber at flat phase need not be invariant at general phases. Neither restriction is a valid full-sector proof. The original-mask tag in the proved highest-layer factorization is only a direct-sum proof device after cancellation.

5. **One complex phase or perturbation around a flat mode.** A proved nonzero Laurent minor at any nonzero complex point would suffice, but no actual one was obtained. Assigning large edge phases does not supply independent diagonal potentials or independent weights for the many configuration edges. The same physical link variable occurs in many charge-dependent paths, so generic structural controllability is an extra theorem, not a permitted assumption. The known flat-phase dark mode may spread over all hole layers/masks when perturbed; the slab result excludes only a branch that remains confined to the declared support.

6. **Source-specific alternative.** A full-rank certificate would handle every normalizable source. If rank is deficient, actual failure still requires overlap with the complete original continuously generated source. A Laurent null vector and a complete legal original zero-waiting source word with nonzero Laurent overlap would certify such failure, by strong continuity and the positive original trajectory expansion. No such pair is found. Absence of overlap with sampled or zero-waiting words alone would not prove stability under intervening electric/H4 evolution.

The exact remaining theorem is either full almost-everywhere rank of the actual observation matrix or orthogonality of its dark projector to the actual full effective-history source orbit. This is target-strength, not a proved terminal lemma. Even either rotor statement would leave quantitative long-age control, finite-spin transfer and continuously forced epsilon-dependent source uniformity separate. No further computation is justified by the current analytic reduction.
