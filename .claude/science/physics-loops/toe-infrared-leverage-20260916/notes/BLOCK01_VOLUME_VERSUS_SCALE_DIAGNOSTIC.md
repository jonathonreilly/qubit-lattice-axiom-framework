# Volume and infrared scale are separate questions

This diagnostic is an exact identity inside the supplied charged rotor model, not a separate scientific milestone or axiom no-go. At fixed g>0, write P_e=g E_e. Exact Gauss law implies, for every finite vertex test f and every real t,

    exp[i t sum_e (D* f)_e P_e] = exp[i t g sum_x f_x Q_x]

on the physical space. Both observables are bounded and local if f has finite support. In a free transverse Maxwell gauge state the left expectation is one. A free matter state with a nontrivial distribution of Q(f) has a right characteristic function different from one for some t. Thus merely increasing volume at fixed g cannot yield the decoupled local Maxwell/free-matter state from PR8160, with those same observables and fixed normalization. This conclusion does not require convergence of unbounded electric moments.

An infrared rescaling changes the probes and may also induce a running effective coupling. The identity above does not rule out an emergent free or weakly interacting macroscopic limit. Proving that limit requires the appropriately scaled charge/current correlations and their coupling to the gauge field. The Coulomb term in the new variational compression preserves this distinction.
