# Scope and reachability

This is upstream conditional support; a downstream physical consumer has not been selected. The target is a single-time energy-law theorem for each fixed normal state and a separate mean/W1 theorem for finite initial energy. The arbitrary-state covariance proof is not a quasifree reduction. Root's exact pure-state distance and the independent fixed-mixed-state asymptotic extension remain distinct. Compact-Fock, CAR and finite-rank detector results use different test classes.

Actual-cell transfer assumes the exact common energy, M23 and weighted trace convergence of preparations. Positive production is required for the explicit varying-state counterexamples. The negative packet is FAIL/partial-narrowing, not exhaustive impossibility. No new physical primitive or retained status is proposed.
