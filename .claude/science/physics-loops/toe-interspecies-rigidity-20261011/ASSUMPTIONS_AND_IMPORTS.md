# Premises

Exact: C1 charges and energies on connected open domains, full affine span of
both velocity images, and every local regular AB shell identity. Measure and
current consequences require their own full-support, continuity, current
identification, conservation and individual scalar-domain symmetry premises.

Approximate: six conditioned labeled anchors, finite velocity diameter and a
uniform normalized tangent or unnormalized wedge defect. Integration costs
intrinsic path length. The current corollary assumes derivative control for
the current components themselves; small charge values alone do not supply it.
Neither conclusion fixes a positive common quadratic coefficient.
