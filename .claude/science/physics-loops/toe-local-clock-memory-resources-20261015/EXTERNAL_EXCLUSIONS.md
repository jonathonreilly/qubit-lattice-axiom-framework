# Explicit local-only external-paper exclusions

EXTERNAL_ASSET_EXCLUSIONS.json is the authoritative per-copy inventory. It
lists 146 excluded aliases representing 69 unique assets: three full primary
PDFs and 66 extracted pages. Each row gives the original local path, byte
length, SHA256, primary URL, type and reason. This includes duplicate copies
inside the two assessment archives and the local purification literature
origin. No excluded PDF/page bytes are in this draft's content-addressed store.

The primary sources are Ticozzi–Viola, arXiv1403.8143 / DOI10.1038/srep05192;
Kaye, arXivquant-ph/0703194v3; and Barenco et al., arXivquant-ph/9503016v1.
URLs are retained in every asset row. These external mathematical methods
are credited prior art; local files remain available at their recorded paths.

The original freezes and source maps are preserved unchanged. Their historical
paper/page members are resolved by exclusion rows, not silently dropped or
labelled missing evidence. This packet reconstructs all included authored
evidence. It expressly does not reconstruct the excluded external papers.
Checking those original paper bytes later requires the local external inputs.

Download/extraction programs, receipts, short operational stdout/stderr,
recovery records, authored discussion, all candidate/review/root bodies and
controls remain included. No failed receipt was excluded to obtain a clean
record. The archive boundary is publication scope, not a scientific repair.
