## 2. Supplied finite model and exact conserving unitary

Let S have finite dimension d, let K=K* be its supplied Hamiltonian, and let V be any supplied unitary on all of S. A passive arbitrary reference R may be entangled with S. Reservoir B is initially independent. Write the exact spectral decomposition

    K=E0 I+sum_(a=1)^r Delta_a P_a,
    Delta_a>0,  P0+sum_(a=1)^r P_a=I,
    E0=min spec K,   r<=d-1.                         (1)

The Delta_a are distinct positive energies above E0, and P_a may have arbitrary rank. This is a coordinate description: the actual K, including E0 I, is never subtracted or rescaled in the conservation law or target. No commensurability of the Delta_a is assumed. For r=0 take B one-dimensional and the implementation V itself; the rest concerns r>=1.

For an integer L>=1, choose r finite ladders, each with basis n_a=0,...,L+1, and supply

    H_B=b0 I+sum_a Delta_a N_a,
    b0>=0,  D_B=(L+2)^r.                            (2)

Positive means nonnegative. The default b0=0 gives an actual zero-energy ground. A supplied strictly positive b0 can be installed and paid explicitly. If positive total free energy is also desired for a K with E0<0, taking b0>=-E0 suffices, without changing K. This reserve changes the actual mean and free phase; it is not a costless shift of the physical zero.

Set v0=0 and va equal to the a-th unit vector of Z^r. Label a joint basis vector from P_a S and reservoir coordinate n by `m=n+v_a`. For any label m whose coordinates all lie in 1,...,L+1, every system energy subspace occurs, at reservoir coordinate `m-v_a`. The corresponding complete label space is isomorphic to S. Define the unitary U_L(V) to equal V under that identification. On every remaining incomplete label space define it to be the identity.

These spaces are mutually orthogonal and partition the entire finite S/B Hilbert space, so U_L(V) is exactly unitary, including every boundary state. On label m the actual total free Hamiltonian is the scalar

    E0+b0+sum_a Delta_a m_a.

Consequently

    [U_L(V), K tensor I+I tensor H_B]=0.           (3)

Distinct labels may have equal energies because of integer relations among the Delta_a. That causes no difficulty: a block decomposition may refine an eigenspace. No classification of those relations and no equality oracle for arbitrary total energies is required. Finite cyclic wrapping is not used.

For finite-real existence the spectral data in (1) exist. An effective prescription can take them, with their projectors/basis and certificates, as supplied data. Bare computable matrix entries are not silently treated as an exact-degeneracy or continuously selected eigenbasis oracle. Alternatively a supplied full orthonormal eigenbasis can use one coordinate for each eigenvector except a specified ground vector, retaining zero or repeated gaps as separate nonnegative ladders; r=d-1 then works without grouping equal energies. Exact matching of those supplied energies to the physical K is still necessary for exact (3).

## 3. Complete reservoir output and solved accuracy bound

Prepare the explicit coherent pure vector

    b_L(n)=sqrt(2/(L+1)) sin(pi n/(L+1)), n=1,...,L,
    b_L(0)=b_L(L+1)=0,
    beta_L=b_L tensor ... tensor b_L.              (4)

Every S input tensored with beta_L is supported on complete label spaces, because adding any v_a to a coordinate vector in 1,...,L stays in 1,...,L+1. On this input U_L(V) has the exact action

    sum_(a,b) P_a V P_b tensor T^(v_b-v_a),        (5)

where bilateral coordinate shifts are used only to describe the action on the embedded vector. Its actual final coordinates remain in 0,...,L+1. The physical operation is the finite block unitary, not a truncated bilateral shift.

For the zero-extended sine vector, the finite path eigenvector identity gives

    <b_L,T b_L>=cos(pi/(L+1)),
    d_L=||T b_L-b_L||=2sin(pi/[2(L+1)]).           (6)

Indeed `b(n-1)+b(n+1)=2cos(pi/(L+1))b(n)` with zero endpoints; multiplying by b(n) and summing proves the overlap. Negative shifts have the same difference. On the bilateral comparison space set

    C_+=sum_a P_a tensor T^(v_a),   C_-=C_+*.

These are unitaries and (5) is `C_-(V tensor I)C_+`. For every unit vector psi_SR, orthogonality of the P_a makes

    ||(C_+-I)(psi tensor beta_L)||^2
       =sum_(a>=1) ||P_a psi||^2 d_L^2 <= d_L^2.

Apply the same estimate to C_- on V psi and use the triangle inequality. The full isometry estimate is

    ||U_L(V)(I_S tensor |beta_L>)-V tensor |beta_L>||
        <=2d_L=4sin(pi/[2(L+1)]).                 (7)

Reference tensoring preserves it. Purification and the pure-state trace inequality therefore give, for every rho_SR,

    ||(U_L(V) tensor I_R)(rho_SR tensor beta_L beta_L*)
                  (U_L(V)* tensor I_R)
            -(V tensor I_R)rho_SR(V* tensor I_R)
                          tensor beta_L beta_L*||_1
        <= eta_L=min(2,8sin(pi/[2(L+1)]))
        <=min(2,4pi/(L+1)).                        (8)

This is an unhalved trace-norm estimate on **S,B,R together**. It is stronger than a reduced-system channel claim. The actual B output is generally changed and correlated; the unchanged product beta_L is a comparison state, not an assertion of exact catalyst return.

For a desired 0<epsilon<=1 it suffices to choose

    L>=max(1,ceil(4pi/epsilon)-1).                 (9)

For fixed supplied S,K this gives dimension O(epsilon^-r), free reservoir energy range O(epsilon^-1) and no gate-duration bound. These are loose sufficient costs, not optimality claims. If S itself grows as an accuracy-dependent bus, neither r nor its spectrum is a fixed constant; (9) must not be promoted to a polynomial resource bound for the original plant.

All target scalar phases are retained in V. In particular replacing V by `exp(-i Phi)V0` multiplies its action on every occupied complete block by that phase. The chosen identity on unoccupied incomplete blocks need not share it; this does not delete the phase from (7).

## 4. Exact energy ledger and the meaning of approximate return

Let `D=sum_a Delta_a`, `D2=sum_a Delta_a^2`, and `mu=(L+1)/2`. Symmetry of (4) gives

    mean_initial(H_B)=b0+mu D,
    ||H_B-b0 I||=(L+1)D,
    Var_initial(H_B)=sigma_L^2 D2,
    sigma_L^2=(2/(L+1)) sum_(n=1)^L
           (n-mu)^2 sin^2(pi n/(L+1))
              <=(L-1)^2/4.                       (10)

The variance formula is a finite exact sum; its bound follows because every supported n lies within (L-1)/2 of mu. No continuum approximation or unbounded reservoir is used. The initial second moment is the square of the displayed mean plus the displayed variance.

For the initially independent rho_SR and B, the total free mean is `Tr(K rho_S)+mean_initial(H_B)` and its variance is `Var_(rho_S)(K)+sigma_L^2 D2`. Equation (3) preserves the complete total-energy distribution, including every moment, exactly. The actual final system and reservoir generally have nonzero energy covariance, which must be retained in the final variance.

Let rho'_S be the implemented system output and define the intended energy transfer to S by

    W_V(rho_S)=Tr[(V* K V-K)rho_S].

Then exact balance and (8), using any `h_c>=||K-cI||`, yield

    mean_final(H_B)-mean_initial(H_B)
             =-Tr[K(rho'_S-rho_S)],
    |mean_final(H_B)-mean_initial(H_B)+W_V(rho_S)|
             <=h_c eta_L.                        (11)

For example `h_c=(max spec K-min spec K)/2` is valid at the mid-spectrum c. This is an expectation ledger for the supplied conserved observable, not a claim of deterministic classical work, a work measurement, or thermodynamic erasure cost. Measuring the reservoir energy initially would destroy the prescribed coherence and change the operation.

The product comparator in (8) has unchanged reservoir mean, but (11) allows a nonzero finite energy transfer. There is no contradiction: the centered reservoir norm grows as `(L+1)D/2`, so eta_L times this norm is generally O(D), not o(1). Trace proximity alone cannot justify dropping a reservoir energy change, its variance, or its covariance with S. An independent desired moment tolerance must be priced with the relevant actual finite observable norms or with (11); it does not follow for free from epsilon state error.

## 5. Phase coherence, age and calibration

The useful resource is the supplied coherent vector (4), not just its energy distribution. The nearest-shift overlap is `cos(pi/(L+1))`, approaching one quadratically in 1/L, while its state-level shift error is d_L. For phases theta=(theta_1,...,theta_r), define

    beta_theta=exp[-i sum_a theta_a N_a] beta_L,
    D_theta=P0+sum_a exp(-i theta_a)P_a,
    V_theta=D_theta V D_theta* .                   (12)

The shifted-vector estimate now is `T^(v_a) beta_theta` close to `exp(i theta_a) beta_theta`, and the proof of (7) gives the same eta_L for the comparator

    V_theta rho V_theta* tensor beta_theta beta_theta* . (13)

Thus a phase reference error is an actual conjugation of the target, not an irrelevant reservoir phase. If every phase error has a chosen representative of magnitude at most delta, `||V_theta-V||<=2delta`, giving at most 4delta additional unhalved joint trace error when the retained comparison reservoir is the actual known beta_theta.

If the required comparison reservoir is specifically beta_L instead, use the full readiness error. Centering `sum theta_a N_a` at `mu sum theta_a` gives

    ||beta_theta-exp(-i mu sum_a theta_a) beta_L||
          <=sigma_L (sum_a theta_a^2)^(1/2),
    ||beta_theta beta_theta*-beta_L beta_L*||_1
          <=2 sigma_L ||theta||_2.                 (14)

The inequality `||(exp(-iX)-I)psi||<=||X psi||` proves this, and product independence gives its variance. Apply actual U_L to that ready-state difference and telescope with (8): eta_L plus the trace bound in (14) suffices. Do not add the separate 4delta comparison again unless using a different comparison convention. Equation (14) tracks its scalar explicitly at vector level.

Free storage for a known time tau produces theta_a=Delta_a tau and the actual global phase `exp(-i b0 tau)`. It therefore rotates the target frame as

    V_theta=exp(-iK tau) V exp(+iK tau).            (15)

If S also freely evolves for tau before applying U_L(V), exact commutation (3) gives the full comparison `exp(-iK tau)V rho V* exp(+iK tau)` with freely evolved reservoir, **not** generally `V exp(-iK tau)rho exp(+iK tau)V*`. The latter order can be requested by lifting `exp(+iK tau)V exp(-iK tau)`, or by supplying the correctly phased reservoir at the declared gate time. Both require the stated timing/phase data. This is why an endpoint unitary does not supply an actual-time actuator automatically.

An unknown extra storage time delta tau costs at most `2 sigma_L sqrt(D2)|delta tau|` in full reservoir readiness trace norm, up to the explicitly tracked scalar mean phase, and thus by telescoping the same addition to (8). For L>1 the explicit sufficient request `|delta tau|<=e_phase/[(L-1)sqrt(D2)]` spends at most e_phase. With e_phase=O(epsilon), L=O(epsilon^-1) and fixed D2 this gives an O(epsilon^2) timing request, not a necessary lower bound. For L=1 the packet is an energy eigenvector and its free change is only scalar. Initial phase, spacing and storage-time errors can be combined by the explicit coordinate allowance

    |delta theta_a|<=|delta theta_(a,prep)|
       +|tau| |delta Delta_a|+Delta_a |delta tau|
       +|delta Delta_a| |delta tau|.

Use its Euclidean norm in (14). If a physically different phase convention/comparator is used, its distinct error must be stated. Neither a high mean energy nor these numerical tolerances prepare a phase reference.

For an actual joint ready-state trace error eta_ready and an implemented unitary U_hat satisfying `||U_hat-U_L(V)||<=nu`, the complete output trace error against (8)'s target is at most

    eta_L+eta_ready+2nu.                           (16)

An additional target-unitary approximation `||V_hat-V||<=nu_V` costs at most 2nu_V; the block lift itself has `||U_L(V_hat)-U_L(V)||<=nu_V` for unitary V_hat. Approximate nonunitary blocks require a certified unitary completion before the exact conservation statement applies.

A concrete coefficient-precision request is also available. Approximate each of the L nonzero amplitudes of each single-ladder b_L to complex absolute error delta, with `sqrt(L)delta<=1/2`, and normalize each resulting vector. Its vector error is at most `2sqrt(L)delta`; a tensor-product telescope gives reservoir vector error at most `2r sqrt(L)delta` and trace error at most `4r sqrt(L)delta`. Hence `delta<=eta_ready/[4r sqrt(L)]` is sufficient for this particular product preparation model. An absolute-complex-error request of 2^-b can use `b>=ceil(log2(max(2sqrt(L),4r sqrt(L)/eta_ready)))`. This counts requested precision, not evaluation runtime. A physical joint readiness certificate is still needed; evaluating these numbers does not manufacture the state or guarantee independence from S.

Exact nominal conservation is distinct from physical calibration. If the reservoir spacings have errors delta_a, then `||Delta H_B||<=(L+1)sum_a|delta_a|+|delta b0|`. For any full free-generator error Delta G and a unitary implementation error nu,

    ||[U_hat,G+Delta G]||
       <=2||G-cI||nu+2||Delta G||,
    G=K tensor I+I tensor H_B.                     (17)

This bounds a calibration defect, not exact conservation. Arbitrarily small numerical spacing errors do not in general retain exact resonance. A native implementation respecting an exact symmetry or a supplied exact matching certificate remains a stronger premise. Requested coefficients/phase precision are finite numerical requests, not an oracle wall-time or physical manufacture guarantee.

## 6. A positive onsite qubit reservoir, with the gate still unsynthesized

There is an elementary encoding which avoids hiding the ladder energy range in one increasingly strong onsite block. Let M=L+1. Encode a single ladder basis state n in M qubits by

    |n>_unary=|1>^tensor n tensor |0>^tensor(M-n),
    H_(B,a)=Delta_a sum_(j=1)^M n_(a,j).          (18)

The prefix-state span is invariant under this positive onsite Hamiltonian, and its restriction is exactly Delta_a N_a. Use r such registers. There are r(L+1) qubits, every free-field incident load is at most max Delta_a, independent of L for fixed K, and the encoded sine product has exactly the energy ledger (10). A nonzero reserve b0 can be included as one actual scalar onsite contribution. This is a conditional qubit model; vacancy, isolation and those onsite fields remain supplied.

On S tensor the encoded ladder subspace install the abstract U_L(V), and on its orthogonal complement take the identity. The code projector commutes with the full free generator, so this extension remains exactly unitary and energy conserving on **all** physical qubit states. No penalty or omitted non-code energy is needed for this endpoint statement.

This does not make the gate local. Its spectral projectors, complete-label selection and conditional V can be global across S and all ladders. No positive nearest-neighbor Hamiltonian realizing it, bound on its incident interaction load, pulse action, slew or duration has been supplied. A compact binary encoding uses only `ceil(log2 D_B)` qubits abstractly, but likewise supplies no common local-strength ledger. The unary observation prices the free reservoir Hamiltonian only. Nor does an abstract Hermitian logarithm of U_L(V) solve locality, retention of literal K during an actual gate interval, switching interaction energy or a halting interface.

## 8. Exact discriminators and limits of reuse

One current hand reconstruction checks the full bookkeeping without a numerical runner. Take `K=Delta |1><1|`, Delta>0, target V the Hadamard, L=2, and reservoir `beta=(|1>+|2>)/sqrt(2)` in levels 0,...,3. On system input |0>, the actual full lifted vector is

    (|0,1>+|0,2>+|1,0>+|1,1>)/2.                (21)

Its overlap with `|+> tensor beta` is 3/4, so its exact joint unhalved trace distance is `sqrt(7)/2`. The system marginal is `[[1/2,1/4],[1/4,1/2]]`, at distance 1/2 from |+><+|. The reservoir marginal is the equal mixture of beta and its one-step downward shift, with purity 5/8. Thus a reduced channel estimate cannot be substituted for the full-output estimate.

The reservoir mean drops from `3Delta/2` to Delta while S gains Delta/2. Reservoir variance rises from Delta^2/4 to Delta^2/2; final system variance is Delta^2/4 and covariance is `-Delta^2/4`. Total variance remains exactly Delta^2/4. These values follow from the four amplitudes in (21). Dropping the covariance would give the wrong conserved second moment. Giving the packet phase theta yields system off-diagonal `exp(i theta)/4`, confirming the sign of (12).

Three failed shortcuts remain explicit:

* A finite cyclic down-shift is unitary but does not conserve the supplied positive energy at its wrap. For a qubit flip it would send `|0,0>` to `|1,L+1>`, gaining `(L+2)Delta`. The finite-label identity completion avoids this boundary.
* A reservoir diagonal in its energy basis with the same populations as (4) has the same mean and variance but supplies no such coherence. With a ground-state qubit input the total initial state commutes with total free energy. Any exactly conserving unitary keeps that invariance, and the reduced qubit output is energy diagonal. Its distance from |+><+| is at least one. This is a concrete failure of an energy-moments-only readiness certificate, not a prohibition on coherent reservoirs.
* Exact unchanged reservoir return cannot be inferred from (8). In the concrete ground-to-excited qubit flip, exact return with the same reservoir mean would add Delta to the total mean, contrary to (3). Growing reservoir range makes approximate state return compatible with finite mean-energy transfer; it does not erase that transfer.
