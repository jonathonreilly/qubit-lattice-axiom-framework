# Finite energy carrier: derivation to test next

Block3 proves a spatial approximation with the actual continuous battery retained,
including a separate cap comparison. Next address the continuous carrier itself.
Do not silently combine an unrelated finite battery with that Fourier proof.

Candidate exact finite-fiber construction: let A be any finite-dimensional ambient
system Hamiltonian with finite distinct spectrum Sigma, size q. Supply a finite
battery preparation supported on m energy eigenvalues E_j (with arbitrary coherent
amplitudes). Let Q_set={E_j+a: a in Sigma} and B_set={Q-b:Q in Q_set,b in Sigma}.
Then |Q_set|<=m q and |B_set|<=m q². If 0<=A<=B_max and E_j in[b,b+w], the
battery spectrum lies in[b-B_max,b+w+B_max]; b>=B_max makes it nonnegative.
Incommensurate energy spacings are allowed: this is a finite irregular spectrum,
not a regular ladder that rounds physical gaps.

For each total energy Q in Q_set, W_Q psi=sum_a Pi_a psi tensor |Q-a> is an
isometry of the ENTIRE system Hilbert space onto its total-Q fiber. Every energy
Q-a lies in B_set by construction. For an arbitrary ambient bare seed B, the
finite spectral lift sum_ab Pi_b B Pi_a tensor T_(a-b)^finite therefore obeys
V_B W_Q=W_Q B. The actual prepared domain and every subsequent jump/no-jump
history remain in these complete fibers. Complete seeds retain all effects and
zero refusal on that domain; feedback retains its actual non-scalar effect.
Total modeled energy is exactly conserved. No positive outcome is discarded.

Avoid adding a hidden global Q selector to the implementation. Define T_u^finite
as literal partial translation on the finite energy set. The spectral lift itself
has no Q-projector multiplying it. Outside the complete safe fibers, the complete
law uses its existing one-per-edge eligibility refusal complement; feedback uses
the actual compressed loss. Positivity of the complement follows by viewing the
finite set as a compression of translations on the additive group generated by
the finitely many energy differences (or the union of the prepared energy cosets).
This infinite group is proof machinery only; the actual constructed battery is
finite. The exact safe domain is verified through W_Q, not stipulated as a
runtime global control. Existing native old-Record/N/code arguments should carry
through the same spectral lift, but check them before claiming.

This construction changes the prepared battery from the source sine packet to a
specified finite energy superposition. It does not yet reproduce the sine-packet
channel, matter coherences, energy distribution or old finite transport census.
It removes an infinite-dimensional resource for a conditional finite-preparation
family only. Although the energy-level count is at most m q² and may be encoded
using ceil(log2(m q²)) qubits, a physical local Hamiltonian and the prescribed
irregular energies are still supplied; no axiom selects them. Global A dressing
also remains. A large-dimensional spectrum can be expensive to specify even
when its qubit count is modest.

Key compatibility question: finite irregular partial translations generally have
no scalar Fourier coordinate with E=-i partial_tau and exact translation
invariance on all battery inputs. The Block3 locality proof cannot simply be
copied. Need either a controlled approximation of the source continuous packet
by such finite energy sets with retained-state embedding, or a finite-battery
construction whose scalar Fourier/cap structure allows the same error proof.

Possible alternatives to investigate after the exact finite-fiber proof: a regular
fine energy grid with approximate conservation; a finite additive-group/Folner
construction matching finitely many translation overlaps; a finite basis Galerkin
approximation in the capped L2 energy interval with a stated strong/trace error;
or direct finite-time Hamiltonian/spectral approximation with source-local error.
Do not claim all these routes are independent walls or already attempted.
