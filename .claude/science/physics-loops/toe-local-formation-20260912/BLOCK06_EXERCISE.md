## 0. Exact wall

Determine whether the real scalar alpha in the supplied infinite canonical
Gaussian native pi-flux model is zero. Nonzero is enough; positivity is a
stronger optional target. With the source normalization h>0, write
D_A=H+B_A>=h/4, R_A=-D_A^-1, g=gamma_0,
B_A=i g gamma(d_A), J_A=2i gamma(d_A). A runs over the fifteen
two-subsets of the six center legs. The ordered disjoint-pair adjacency T has
ninety nonzero entries. The exact existing identity is

    8 alpha = Re(<x,T x> - <x,T p>),
    x_A=R_A Omega, p_A=g R_A J_A R_A Omega.

The source direct overlap already exceeds 160/h^2. Its Ward correction is
undetermined. Existing finite-moment, quartic, and correlated primal/dual
certificates cross zero. Positive vacuum overlaps, positive zero-energy
scattering amplitudes, and a nonzero one-particle norm do not settle alpha.

Allowed: the named supplied native model, canonical state, exact CAR and
magnetic symmetries, the conditional uniform impurity gap and quasilocal
coefficient construction. Forbidden replacements: a flat finite bath as the
native infinite bath, an impurity vacuum as the original vacuum, removal of
Ward boundary terms, positivity of an ordered product of inverses, or a
physical axiom interpretation of this model-specific scalar. Degenerate h=0
is excluded by the source definition. If alpha=0, Block5 still supplies a
bounded infinite sixth-return multiplier, but not a uniform finite-size
bound without convergence rates. If alpha is nonzero, its sign is immaterial
to the leading sixth-return coefficient, which involves alpha squared.

Progress means a genuinely new identity with all terms retained, a rigorous
bound with a native hypothesis check, or a counterexample to an overbroad
proof mechanism. Rephrasing the desired sign as an unproved positivity lemma
does not count as closure. Neither outcome by itself forces an axiom change.

## 2. Minimum requirement

Delete a numerical value, positive sign, a complete phase, and a universal
axiom conclusion from this block's target. The smallest remaining object is
one real scalar formed from fifteen bounded impurity resolvents and the
canonical vacuum. There are two independent issues: algebraic cancellation
of the ninety words and quantitative native contractions. The first can be
tested in tiny CAR representations; only the actual infinite model settles
the second. An alpha sign proof cannot be inferred from a generic finite
fixture. The fastest useful refutation is a rational representation that
preserves the proposed algebra but violates its proposed inequality.
