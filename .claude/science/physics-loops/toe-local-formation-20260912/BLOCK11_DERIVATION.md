## 6. Finite native preparation and an arbitrarily long current window

No restriction of an infinite physical cycle-code state is assumed. Let
Omega be a finite open virtual box with its y interval starting at an even
integer. Every odd-y x candidate has its detour in the adjacent lower
even-y row. Then the protected finite graph is connected and its hopping
matrix h_Omega is the compression of the infinite protected h. Let
C_Omega be the compression of (11.8). Its spectrum nu_j stays in [1/4,3/4].
Construct the finite quasifree density with these occupations and condition
on even total fermion parity. Its preparation succeeds with probability

    (1+product_j(1-2nu_j))/2 >= (1-2^(-M))/2,

where M=|Omega|. In the occupation eigenbasis the conditioned covariance
remains diagonal. Direct summation over the other independent occupations
gives

    nu_j^(even)-nu_j =
      -2nu_j(1-nu_j) product_(l!=j)(1-2nu_l)
       / [1+product_l(1-2nu_l)].

Consequently

    ||C_Omega^(even)-C_Omega|| <= delta_M,
    delta_M=2^(-M)/(1-2^(-M)).                           (11.11)

Parity conditioning need not preserve Gaussianity. Only the covariance
bound is used. Map this even finite density into the original connected
native cycle code by the imported faithful finite dictionary. This gives
an actual finite physical initial state and avoids a boundary/parity
compatibility shortcut. Its protected two-point function evolves by h_Omega
even though its higher correlations need not be Gaussian.

For each observed bond endpoint at graph distance at least d from the
outside of Omega, the vectors h_Omega^n delta_v and h^n delta_v agree for
n<d, with the finite vector extended by zero. Both Hamiltonian norms are
at most 6t. For 0<=s<=T define a=6tT and R_d(a)=sum_(n>=d) a^n/n!.
The exponential series gives

    ||exp(i s h_Omega)delta_v-exp(i s h)delta_v|| <= 2R_d(a).

The norm of C is at most one. Applying this bound to the two vectors in
each covariance entry and adding (11.11) yields the uniform entry error

    |C_finite(s)_(v,w)-C_infinite_(v,w)| <= 4R_d(a)+delta_M.

This comparison includes arbitrary times up to T, not just a selected
momentum eigenstate in a periodic finite box. An oriented bond-current

for the two-site cell average in each direction.

so R_d(a)<=2(ea/d)^d. For 0<epsilon<=1, it suffices to choose

    d >= ceil(max(12 e tT, log_2(16/epsilon),1)),
    M >= ceil(log_2(4/epsilon))                         (11.13)

to make (11.12) at most epsilon. In particular epsilon<=1/8192 preserves
