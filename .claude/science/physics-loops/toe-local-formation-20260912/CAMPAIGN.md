## Ranked campaign

Ranks are judgments of expected information and downstream usefulness, not probabilities of success. Each block can return a construction, counterexample or precise remaining obligation.

### 1. Native formation compatibility: the first decisive block

**Question:** can a specified native state/control family implement its complete event instrument using a single content law on actual nearest-neighbor Record conditions?

Start with the product-ready finite path apparatus of #8036 and the charged-history interface of #8065. Define physical coordinates, preparation domain, event menus, complete outcome set, allowed schedules, state changes and dwell before testing. A virtual graph edge or endpoint star is not automatically a physical nearest-neighbor edge.

For each legal forming event at x and supported history h, compute its conditional content law q_x(.|h,formation). Separately calculate the complete local condition eta_x(h), including all neighboring Record contents and occupancy. The target is a single covariant F with q_x=F(eta_x) over the entire declared family. The matrix state, hidden program index or remote posterior cannot silently enter eta.

First discriminator: find two reachable histories within the **same preparation and control domain** that have the same eta but different q. Preserve both probabilities, the physical coordinates and positive history weights. Test outcome pairs, no-event/failure branches, recorded signs and transported observables. Distinguish sequential conditioning from the marginals of a jointly formed layer.

**Success:** an explicit F with a general sufficiency proof, closure under the specified dwell and preservation of old Records; conditional resources remain named. **Counterexample:** a positive-probability same-condition pair inside that exact domain. **Inconclusive:** arbitrary preparations outside the domain, a hidden difference in neighboring preparation Records, conditioning on an impossible event, or assuming all formation orders are allowed.

A generic remote-steering example would repeat known concerns. The new work must bind the actual native apparatus or establish a reusable characterization of the schedule/state restrictions it requires. An all-mask or arbitrary-order theorem is useful only if those stronger hypotheses are labeled and their physical necessity is separately tested.

**Constructive escapes to develop immediately after a failure:** local preparation/program Records; a causal formation schedule that carries sufficient conditions forward; a constrained invariant matter family; a larger encoded multi-site apparatus; or a global joint-history law whose local event marginals satisfy Admissibility. Changing the law's domain is recorded as a change of target, not a proof that the original apparatus worked.

Downstream consequence: determines whether the current native instrument can become a process on the declared ontology, rather than merely a conditional quantum calculation.

### 2. Persistent formation with continuing matter and priced resources

Build one explicit process on increasing finite physical regions, using the successful interface from block 1 or clearly stated inherited conditions.

Require permanent old Records, complete outcome accounting, fresh-site consumption, preparation and energy budgets, and nonzero propagation/current in a surviving region. Study fixed nonzero formation density/rate where meaningful, and any necessary scaling of that rate separately. A rate sent to zero before observation or a region fully recorded before propagation is not the target.

The main finite autonomous clock recurs and undoes its write at t=2. Repeating that known failure has no value. Explore a propagating controller with a growing fresh Record trail, an explicitly absorbing local process, or another mechanism that actually supplies persistence. Finite unitary dilation alone is insufficient. Any irreversible law or absorbing boundary is a supplied hypothesis until derived.

Use exact small physical-carrier matrices as a check against a separate CAR/bit-action construction. For larger systems use locality/error bounds only after verifying finite interaction range, bounded strength, state and time domains. Lieb–Robinson machinery is a mathematical tool, not a physical clock supplier: [Bravyi, Hastings and Verstraete](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0603121).

**Success:** a family with controlled local-observable error on a growing time/length window, positive surviving dynamics, priced resources and consistent histories. **Counterexample:** a reachable rewrite, local-condition collision, loss of propagation or a resource requirement inconsistent with the declared scaling. **Inconclusive:** only a finite pulse, postselected success, global fidelity estimate with uncontrolled volume dependence, or an abstract apparatus without physical placement.

## What would justify changing an axiom?

The campaign does not presuppose that the current axioms are adequate or inadequate.

| Finding | Valid consequence | Required next step |
|---|---|---|
| A chosen apparatus, schedule, state or approximation fails | Reject or change that construction | Test the strongest same-axiom alternative |
| Two complete models of the actual axioms give different target physics | Axioms alone do not uniquely entail that target | Decide whether a downstream law, boundary data or an axiom revision is the intended theory; an axiom edit is not logically forced |
| A necessary empirical target contradicts a theorem covering every model of specified axioms and explicitly justified auxiliary assumptions | At least one premise or identification in that set must change | Identify the smallest supported inconsistent subset and which alternatives remain |
| Search or computational resources run out | The stated search remains incomplete | Preserve strongest result and reopen condition |

An axiom-pressure packet must state the exact axiom clause, quantifiers, physical target, proof/certificate and auxiliary assumptions. It must test whether a local carrier, preparation Record, different allowed formation order, global-history coupling, multi-site encoding, convention change or corrected observable avoids the contradiction. Unknown relationships between obstructions remain unknown; do not count them as independent.

Use the repository's N1–N8 discipline for negative claims. Its route-count requirement is a submission rule, not proof of exhaustiveness. No negative or positive label supplies mathematical truth. Bell locality must not be inferred from the word nearest-neighbor: factorization and measurement-setting assumptions must be separately established before importing [Bell's theorem](https://cds.cern.ch/record/111654).

Only a concrete evidence packet can recommend an axiom change. The campaign itself does not amend the axioms or primitive registry. Existing conditional routes remain available unless a matching proof excludes them.
