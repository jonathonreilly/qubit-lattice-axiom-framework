## Present answer and decisive campaign contracts

No reviewed source supplies a full local permanent-record formation law that
composes with native matter. This is a missing derivation, not a proof that no
such law exists. The nearest-neighbor determination condition is left unsupplied
for native event probabilities. It is not disproved merely by L†DL fill-in,
the plane's diagonal potential, or initially unrecorded preparation variables.
The latter become a defect only if two allowed preparations with identical
relevant conditions must be governed by different content kernels.

**Priority 1 — actual-axiom local-sufficiency discriminator on the native carrier.**
Target a fixed proposed native formation model and its allowed preparation
domain, across all legal histories, at a specified forming physical edge site.
Define precisely the nearest-neighbor conditions from Record content and
occupancy; separately declare what the global-history law supplies. Prove or
refute factorization of the complete event-content kernel through that local
condition map, including failure/refusal outcomes and conditioning on formation.
Start with the product-ready native filter; compare preparation/control histories
with identical local Records using exact Kraus probabilities, then test whether
those histories are actually admissible under the same proposed global law.
Success: a covariant local kernel with a sufficiency proof on the quantified
domain, and no readout of unrecorded variables. Counterexample: an extendible
pair of same-condition histories with distinct content probabilities, both
inside that fixed domain; this refutes that model's factorization only.
For an axiom-underdetermination conclusion, construct full Z3 models satisfying
all actual axioms and the same claimed supplied conditions but differing on the
target; a finite probability table or a model violating Admissibility is weaker.
Inconclusive: the pair differs in relevant preparation Records, is outside the
common domain, or depends on an unproved global extension.
Keep preparation Records and an explicit global-history law live. The root's
reported main state-sufficiency theorem permits them and forces no core edit;
that sixth science source was not reread under this five-note scope cap.

**Priority 2 — compose permanence, renewed capacity and native matter.**
Target one explicit candidate process on physical Z3 M2 sites, with one fixed
covariant nearest-neighbor content kernel, permanent single-use Records and a
defined schedule/rate domain. Couple it to a native path family of arbitrary
finite length, initially using the proved quadratic filter rather than claiming
general interactions. Include preparation Records if needed, all failures and
fresh-site consumption. Distinguish derived components from supplied conditions.
Success: consistent finite restrictions/global history construction; Record
preservation at every later event; operational native Kraus matching on every
ready input/reference; and the priority-1 sufficiency condition proved at each
formation event. Show how controller/energy resources and fresh capacity scale.
Counterexample: a reachable legal step changes an old Record, needs unavailable
fresh capacity, violates the stated local kernel, or fails complete-instrument
matching. The finite clock's recurrence already defeats its use alone as the
permanence mechanism; do not rerun that as new progress.
Inconclusive: only an abstract CPTP dilation, selected-success map, finite
arrival window, scalar plane measure or nonlocal externally programmed gates
are supplied. Preserve that partial result and its exact supplier gap.
Downstream value: a positive result connects matter preparation to an actual
Record process; a negative result identifies a concrete candidate restriction
to relax without claiming a universal no-go or silently changing the axioms.
