## 1. Make the native local condition sufficient

Use PR 8089 at `0b00d351b4c6d4ef6fd737925f5ae403e086cc95`. Start with two
adjacent candidate plaquettes, their protected connected hopping graph,
vertex-parity effects and an even two-particle Slater preparation. Fix the
actual spatial placement and the allowed dwell/event schedule before deriving
probabilities. Include old Records, preparation Records, program Records and
all six neighboring positions in the local condition. Distinguish extra
native quantum state from the framework's declared current Record state.

The first target is a commuting diagram: physical preparation and native
conditioning give a parity mean `m_v(h)`; the actual current neighboring
Records give `eta_v(h)`; a single function `g` must satisfy
`m_v(h)=g(eta_v(h))` over the declared domain. Then the native mark law is
`p_z=(1+z kappa g(eta_v))/2`. Prove permanence and the same factorization
after the second noncommuting event and its dwell, not only initially.

For the Slater route, use the exact Gram update from PR 8089 and the quadratic
dwell `C(t)=exp(-i h t) C exp(i h t)`. A stored occupation alone does not
establish closure: its time derivative depends on off-diagonal covariance.
Derive exactly which covariance entries, preparation parameters or transported
program data the chosen family requires. A global covariance calculator is
an accounting device until its physical Record implementation is supplied.

**Successful output:** one explicitly embedded invariant family, a single
measurable local law, an update rule on physical Records and a proof for all
legal histories in that family. Quantify the cost of new program Records and
the allowed time/horizon. An exponential finite probability bank is already
available abstractly in PR 8088; a new result must implement its native
dictionary or replace that cost with a proved smaller construction.

**Decisive failure of that family:** two positive-weight reachable histories
with the same complete local condition and different `m_v`, under the same
declared preparation/control domain. Record their actual probabilities and
all neighbor contents. A hidden change in prepared quantum state outside
that domain, an impossible branch or omitted program neighbor is not such
a failure. Equal global Record maps with distinct hidden input states must
be labeled as an ontology/identification issue, not passed off as a complete
framework counterexample.

**Stop rule:** once a collision is established, cease scanning equivalent
examples. Attempt the smallest causal program or invariant-family repair.
Retain multi-site encodings and alternative occurrence laws as live routes
unless a matching theorem excludes them. Do not infer a universal axiom
obstruction from the selected carrier's failure.

## 2. Put growth and the infrared result on one Hamiltonian

Compare PR 8087 at `d568433b0fb323b8d72cc76f362ed3ead6b0b8d8` with PR 8086
at `a3e5529b4d74f618df416747c39bf150b885e495`. The former removes candidate
hopping edges to protect Records; the latter's star coefficient uses the
full cubic free symbol, its vacuum covariance and specific defects.

Derive the actual protected graph's Bloch symbol and its low-energy set,
including possible Fermi surfaces, nodal degeneracy and projector weights.
Rebuild the defect operator and gap estimate for that same graph and chosen
state. Only then ask whether a Ward identity yields a positive local star
coefficient and whether the spectral measure has the required infrared
power. A finite numerical sign or visual similarity of dispersions does not
transfer the theorem. The filled-state nonlinear counterexample stays live.

**Successful output:** a common Hamiltonian/state/Record family with a
conditional infrared theorem and controlled local-observable dynamics on
growing windows. **Decisive failure:** an exact nodal, gap or symmetry
mismatch that invalidates the proposed transfer, together with the specific
hypothesis it breaks. This would reject that assembly, not every protected
graph or interacting route.

## 3. Price informative formation under that common dynamics

the starting algebra. Specify whether activation depends only on geometry
or also on marks. Establish a current bound over the intended formation
window, including repeated attenuation at incident vertices and conditioning
correlations. Conserved total number and a nonexplosive clock alone do not

preparation and any energy-conserving dilation. Keep mean matter-energy
change separate from total work and memory costs. A finite abstract unitary
extension is insufficient to close those physical resource obligations.
