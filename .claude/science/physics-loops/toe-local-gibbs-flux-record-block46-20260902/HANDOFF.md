Block46 is preregistered to test a strict-free, action-derived probability law
rather than attach another independent Record law.

Expected decisive outcomes:

- If star occupation full-conditionals depend only on link moduli while the
  cube Record transcript sees flux, the exact axiom/program decision is between
  a richer cell/coherent Record-event bridge and an explicit physical-action
  selector. A merely site-local diagonal probability clause cannot carry the
  loop bit on this bridge.
- If the star already sees flux, the local Record-selector route stays live and
  the phase-sensitive term must be isolated.
- If the source result duplicates landed work or needs a nonquadratic term, the
  block is killed with no source artifact.

No canonical axiom wording may be edited here. Any eventual proposal must say
which registered multi-site events exist, how their probabilities descend to
literal Record contents, and how the physical action feeds them; otherwise it
only restates that a probability distribution exists.
