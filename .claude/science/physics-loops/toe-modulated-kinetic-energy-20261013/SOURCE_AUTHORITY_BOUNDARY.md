# Source authority and integrity limits

External root program/package hashes and a separate exact review/acceptance are mandatory before installation. The driver verifies the immutable original 174 bound members plus its self-excluded receipt, all prepared bytes, current procedures, actual branch/base and canonical parents. Installed payload and exact staged blobs remain pinned through both phases. Root must explicitly accept the normalization correction, parent maps and standalone M23 boundary.

The identity tool embeds hashes for its archive map, edits, parent bindings, graph proposal and review status. It checks compressed and decompressed objects, all original source members, seven immediate source closures, exact core and canonical maps, complete n=3 diffs and the required normalization repair. Every input read is declared. Original absolute paths are archive aliases, never filesystem reads by the canonical tool.

The tool cannot independently authenticate a simultaneous replacement of itself and all embedded anchors. Root's external program/package acceptance supplies that boundary. Four future corruption cases target archive bytes, published note, transformation metadata and canonical parent separately; they are byte-integrity controls, not mathematical tests. Future external root records are guarded by the driver without circular self-pins.

No archived program is imported or executed by the identity tool. Nested provenance and historical numerical evidence are not a recursive audit. Hash equality supplies no independent proof or retained status.
