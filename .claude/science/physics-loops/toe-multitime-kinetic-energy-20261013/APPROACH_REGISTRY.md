# Approach registry

Existing analytic machinery: finite-rank CAR Gaussian/squeeze control,
auxiliary Weyl word overlaps, finite Gram identities, bounded spectral
approximation for Lüders histories, and conditional common-H0 comparison.
The original domain and weighted-work premises stay exposed. Collapsed states
are not assumed Gaussian and arbitrary operator words are not replaced by a
classical process. No new mathematical route is introduced by preparation.

Source machinery: deterministic byte archives and strict reverse unified
diffs; exact core concatenation; owner containment; literal runner rendering;
AST syntax only; exact path, link, whitespace and stage inventories. These
mechanisms check artifacts, not the theorem. Proposed runtime programs remain
separate source and cannot be imported by the identity runner.
