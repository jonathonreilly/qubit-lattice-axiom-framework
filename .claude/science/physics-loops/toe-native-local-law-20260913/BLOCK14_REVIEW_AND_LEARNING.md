limit for a contractible box. The proof controls charged physical states;
the finite tests do not substitute for its compactness argument. A finite
scaled cutoff produces a real boundary error. Primitive integer generation
excludes extra flux copies; real span alone is inadequate. The source is

potential has a manifold of flat minima. Matter can select a nonidentity
holonomy even while every plaquette remains flat. A charged four-site ring
already distinguishes the energy at identity (-2) from the minimum over
holonomies (-2 sqrt(2)). It is a controlled counterexample to transferring
the open-box comparator unchanged, not an obstruction to photons.
