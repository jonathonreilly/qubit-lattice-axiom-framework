and its common positive metric; that upstream source is proposed, not audited.

handles exact-node finite-grid degeneracy. The real harmonic electric
projection is fractional in general; no independent fractional integer-link
state is asserted. The slow charged effective Hamiltonian remains an open
construction with possible Berry and affine-lattice effects.
