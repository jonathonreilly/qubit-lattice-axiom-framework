# Ranked opportunities

1. Physical native local-law sufficiency: literal two-cycle history, causal
projector relay, then a scalable sufficient program or invariant family.
2. Common growth/infrared Hamiltonian: actual protected Bloch spectrum,
covariance and defects before transferring the positive-star machinery.
3. Autonomous informative formation: current over growing windows, finite
collision errors, preparation, control, total energy and memory cost.
4. Reciprocal sourcing: common native matter, geometry variation, clock,
normalization and constraints.
5. Law selection or axiom pressure when a new quantified discriminator
makes it more useful than the constructive routes.

No global exhaustion. After a finished/failed campaign, record its learning,
refresh this ranking, and immediately pursue the next useful campaign through
the deadline. PR count and passing checks do not measure TOE progress.
