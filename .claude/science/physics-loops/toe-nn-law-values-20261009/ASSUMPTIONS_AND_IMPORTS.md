# Assumptions and imports — cycle 3

Supplied by the axioms, and used only as named:

- sites `Z^3`, nearest neighbors, proper cubic rotations
- one-site algebra `M_2(C)`
- one fixed nearest-neighbor admissibility rule, covariant under those rotations
- the distribution's values are not axiom content

Declared for this cycle, not an axiom:

- the pair is a density on `C^2 ⊗ C^2`
- its Pauli coefficients are the directed pattern `p, q, v, u, t`
- elementary settings are unit vectors in `R^3`
- signed-axis settings are the six vectors `±e_i`, a separate comparison

Not used: the dynamics clause, the trace rule, a Hamiltonian, the sum rule,
literature comparator values, and the three registered primitives
`scale_reference_primitive`, `kinetic_isotropy_primitive`, and
`realized_state_primitive`.

The parent bond note's nullspace is cited as the origin of the pattern. It
is not a step in the square proofs. Cycle 2's menu assumptions are unchanged
and are not inputs here.

Counterfactual: if the pair is not a density, Theorems 1–5 do not apply.
Cycle 2 already bounded one non-density menu. Formation site, probability,
and rate remain unsupplied.

- one-site algebra `M_2(C)`

- the pair law is the normalized weight on one edge
- settings in the CHSH comparison are part of the comparison
- local randomness is independent across the two wings

Not used: a two-qubit density, the dynamics clause, the trace rule, a
Hamiltonian, the sum rule, literature comparator values, and the three
registered non-axiom primitives.

other wing, Theorem 4 does not apply. Those are the open routes, not
corrections of the menu arithmetic.
