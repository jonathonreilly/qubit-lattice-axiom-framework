two-qubit density forces the singlet" under that density hypothesis.

Cycle 2 does not claim that every representation misses CHSH square 8.
The density corners and the supplied dynamics clause still reach it. The
scoped statement that did prove is narrower: local `[-1,1]` readouts of the
six-axis product-class pair have CHSH square at most 4, and the coordinate
square is at most `8/9`.

N7's steelman is accepted as an out-of-domain escape, not as a hole in that
arithmetic. The escape is the next queue item: the directed six-parameter
family, still inside the density hypothesis.

Read, and not treated as closing this route:

- The Bell note's records-only bound `|CHSH| ≤ 2` assumes a formation kernel
  with ancestry conditions. It is not the `(p,q,r)` square.
- The structural CHSH note bounds deterministic `±1` responses by 2. Cycle 2
  re-proves the `[-1,1]` atom bound because the coordinate readout is not
  `±1`. The sixteen sign pairs are only a control.
- The September census leaves `(p,q,r)` free and is not a CHSH evaluation.

Live escapes: drop locality of the readout, or return to the density
representation, or derive the endpoint `w=-1` from a different clause.
Formation site and rate remain unsupplied by the axioms.
