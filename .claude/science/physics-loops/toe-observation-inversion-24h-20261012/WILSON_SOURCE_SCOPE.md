# Source scope and data roles

The dated Nature URL returned a 27-page publisher preview rendering, not the full five-page article. Its abstract verifies alpha inverse137.035999206(11); it does not supply raw data or a reproduced likelihood. The HAL clade.pdf file is the same collaboration's Moriond2021 proceedings, not the Nature paper: extraction equation2 and systematic table1 were read. Nature says raw data and analysis code are available from the corresponding author on reasonable request; this campaign has not requested them or contacted anyone.

Parker arXiv1812.04130v1 pages1-2 gives h/mCs=3.0023694721(12)e-9 m²/s, inversealpha137.035999046(27), 0.16ppb statistical and0.12ppb systematic alpha uncertainties, and alpha²=(2Rinfinity/c)(mCs/me)(h/mCs). This is an extracted on-shell coupling under atomic/recoil/mass/Rydberg theory, not a direct microscopic charge readout. The published experiment was blind; the campaign's use of its known public value is not blind.

Fan2209.13084v2 equation6 gives g/2=1.00115965218059(13), a measured frequency ratio after trap/cavity corrections; equation8's inversealpha is extracted from that same measurement with SM theory and cannot be counted as independent calibration. Use Fan's single result, not an unjustified average with2008. Current PDG2026 electron listing gives an average1.15965218062(12)e-3 and separately the Fan result, noting unresolved independent-alpha discrepancies. Its existence does not establish the covariance needed to combine experiments here.

Volkov2404.00649v2 equation3 gives universal total C10=5.891(61); no-lepton-loop subset6.828(60) is distinct. Equation5 inversealpha[ae,Volkov]=137.0359991595(155) includes the same Fan datum; it is only a published theory-comparator encoding. Aoyama etal2412.06473v3 later revise the no-fermion-loop SetV to6.800(128), resolving that old discrepancy; this is not a total C10. Do not use either extracted inversealpha as a second independent datum or casually average subset and total coefficients.

For the fixed Wilson target, magnetic ratios are comparators; mass and charge are calibrated; LHAASO's already pinned timing bound is an external conditional comparator. Recoil determinations remain separate, sharing possible mass/Rydberg calibrations whose full joint covariance is unavailable. No joint fit, confidence exclusion, supplied-alpha derivation, or nature observation from the synthetic lattice controls is claimed.


H.E.S.S.2411.08189v1 (2024-11-12) was checked beyond its abstract: main text pages4-6 and supplement TableI. The headline0.3–40TeV range is electron-like candidate events, not established40TeV electrons. The collaboration deliberately does not subtract the residual hadron population. Above3TeV a dominant hadronic contribution cannot be excluded, and the highest-energy flux is an upper limit on true electrons; an exponential electron cutoff above a fewTeV is not excluded. Thus this campaign rejects the tempting conversion of40TeV into a robust charged-particle survival bound. The representative electron component below about3TeV still depends on the electromagnetic-shower instrument response and MSSG background-shape interpretation. Its main data set has265574 electron-like events. The published energy-scale systematic is10%; detector/nuisance covariance and event directions are not yet supplied for our model. No new Wilson exclusion follows.
