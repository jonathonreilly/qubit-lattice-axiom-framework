# No-go scope

This is a positive conditional construction, not a no-go packet. No no-go PASS or universal impossibility is asserted. The fixed-width error floor concerns the stated estimate with width and smoothing order held fixed. It does not forbid other clocks, parameter choices or approximation methods.

Wrong-interface controls retain their stated scopes. Reversed hopping, omitted phase/background, invalid endpoint or inappropriate normalization diagnostics do not exclude different constructions. Resource growth and open physical suppliers are conditional interfaces, not new impossibility theorems.
