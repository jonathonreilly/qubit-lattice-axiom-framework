# Exact mathematical premises

The abstract object is an automorphism of a spin-chain tensor algebra with
uniform interval tails and a small map norm on the full interior block algebra.
Supersite dimensions are finite and fixed for each chain. The error is not a
single-site or selected-state approximation. RWW's near-inclusion construction,
finite Choi index formula and stability theorem are matched primary imports.
NSY's microscopic Lieb–Robinson and thermodynamic-limit statements supply the
Hamiltonian application. Their PDFs and extracted texts remain in the external
research directory with exact hashes and official source URLs; reference
metadata is included without redistributing complete third-party papers.

The tube specialization supplies a finite-range uniformly bounded interaction,
fixed time and a full-algebra identity with an integer shift. It does not
derive that identity from N1's representation/scattering premises, adopt a
native Hamiltonian law, or identify automorphism evolution with Records.
