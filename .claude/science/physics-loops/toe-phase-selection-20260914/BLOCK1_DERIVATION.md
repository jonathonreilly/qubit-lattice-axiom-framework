# Block 1: two penalty limits select different finite-penalty operators

Personal working derivation, 2026-09-14. Conditional construction, not native
dynamics, an independently checked theorem, or a phase proof. The starting
definitions are reproduced in `upstream/BLOCK14_MONOPOLE_KINETIC_DERIVATION.md`
and `upstream/BLOCK14_HARD_LIMIT_MAPPING.md`, with exact source hashes in the
campaign source map.

Let F be the oriented face-edge incidence and D the cube-face incidence of a
rectangular three-dimensional box containing all of its cells. DF=0. A
physical clock flux is b in {-1,0,1}^P with Db=0 modulo three. Define
Q=Db/3. The prior Hamiltonian has off-diagonal transitions

    b' = principal(b + sigma F e_l),
    m = (b'-b-sigma F e_l)/3,
    rate = t exp(-mu ||m||_2^2),  sigma=+1,-1,

and diagonal 2t per original edge plus (3K/2) sum b_p^2 + lambda sum Q_c^2.
Multiplicity of original link moves with the same physical flux action is
retained. The diagonal is not replaced by the configuration's escape rate.

Let P0 project onto integer-neutral fluxes Db=0. At fixed finite box, the
low-energy operator as lambda tends to infinity is P0 H_(lambda=0) P0 on
its range. This follows from the positive penalty with a nonzero discrete
gap outside P0; it does not require a gap inside P0. A quantitative spectral
bound and its volume dependence still need to be written below.

For one edge l, write f=F e_l and r=|supp f|. The mismatch on each incident
face is either 0 or -sigma f_p. The latter occurs exactly when
b_p=sigma f_p and the shift wraps from that endpoint to its opposite. If
both b and b' are neutral, Dm=0. On the edge star of a full rectangular 3D
box, the equations Dm=0 force m=k f on this support: successive faces
around the edge are joined by a cube, whose equation equates their oriented
coefficients. A bulk star is a cycle; a boundary star is a path, with the
same equality propagated along it. Thus k is either 0 or -sigma.

The two allowed neutral transitions are consequently:

    no face wraps:       b' = b + sigma f,       rate t;
    every face wraps:    b' = b - 2 sigma f,     rate t exp(-r mu).

Mixed wrap patterns create a nonzero integer charge and are removed by P0.
This statement needs modification on degenerate lower-dimensional complexes,
on complexes with a disconnected edge link, or with identified cells. On a
periodic cubic lattice with sufficiently large periods, the local cycle
argument applies, but global flux/holonomy restrictions must still be retained.

On the dual spin-one links, define normalized truncated shifts
E+|-1>=|0>, E+|0>=|1>, E+|1>=0, and E-=(E+)*. Put
B_l=product_p E_p^(f_p), over incident faces, where exponent +1 denotes E+
and -1 denotes E-. Then the projected kinetic operator is

    P0 H_mu P0 = 2t |E| P0 + (3K/2) sum_p E_p^2
                - t sum_l [B_l+B_l* + exp(-r_l mu)(B_l^2+(B_l*)^2)]

on the neutral sector. In the bulk r_l=4; at a flat boundary r_l=3; at a
corner edge r_l=2. The displayed E_p in the electric potential is the
diagonal spin-one electric operator, not the raising operator.

The second harmonic is present for every finite mu. It is precisely the
all-wrap transition, not a correction generated by virtual charged states.
In contrast, taking mu to infinity first removes it, leaving the ordinary
single-step spin-one plaquette Hamiltonian. The subsequent joint infinite
penalty limit may agree, but a finite-mu hard-spatial-penalty model is not
the prior temporal-hard model. A proof or phase diagram for one must be
matched to the other before use.
