## 1. Target and supplied hypotheses

The native probability question is whether local matrix contents can create
an algebraic direction absent from all their neighbors. Block25 proves
preservation for arbitrary commutative input algebras under simultaneous
GL2(C) covariance. Its random-word construction preserves every generated
algebra, but its stabilizer argument does not cover a noncommuting triangular
tuple. Here an orbit-degeneration argument covers precisely that gap after
adding a regularity hypothesis. An explicit singular kernel tests the need
for that hypothesis. Neither result selects a physical probability law.

Let M=M2(C) with its ordinary finite-dimensional topology. For a fixed mask
of recorded neighbors, let eta=(A1,...,Am) in M^m, 0<=m<=6. A common algebra
identification between neighboring contents and the output is supplied.
Write A(eta) for the unital complex subalgebra generated by the tuple, with
A(empty)=C I. Suppose eta -> K_eta is a probability kernel on M satisfying

    K_{S eta S^-1} = (Ad_S)_* K_eta,  S in GL2(C).                 (1)

Assume also compact-input uniform tightness: for each compact C subset M^m
and epsilon>0 there is a bounded output set B with

    sup_{eta in C} K_eta(M minus B) < epsilon.                   (2)

Weak continuity of the probability kernel is a sufficient condition for (2).
Indeed, at each input a continuous cutoff supported on an output ball has
expectation >1-epsilon; continuity gives an input neighborhood on which that
expectation stays >1-epsilon. Finitely many such neighborhoods cover C, and
the largest of their output balls gives (2). No output moments are required.

The current minimal-axiom memo explicitly imposes translation and proper
cubic covariance, but does not explicitly impose (1), (2), a common-fiber
comparison, or this probability topology. These are candidate-law conditions.
The phrase "no possibility is privileged" is not silently enlarged into
these particular mathematical hypotheses. All statements below are conditional
on the conditions written above. A fixed mask prevents any inference about
continuity between unreadable blank sites and recorded zero contents.

The domain must contain the orbit-degeneration limit used below. A law
defined only on irreducible tuples need not extend to that limit, and the
theorem does not impose such an extension on a restricted-domain proposal.

## 2. Proposed support theorem

For every eta under (1)-(2),

    K_eta(A(eta))=1, hence supp K_eta is contained in A(eta).     (3)

The second assertion follows because every finite-dimensional linear
subspace A(eta) is closed. Thus (3) also restricts supported realizations of
zero singleton probability; it is stronger than a statement about sampled
values alone. It does not shrink the full possibility ontology M.

First classify unital complex subalgebras of M. Up to similarity they are

    C I; diagonal matrices; span{I,E12}; upper triangular matrices; M. (4)

Here is an elementary exhaustive proof. If an algebra contains a matrix with
two distinct eigenvalues, its two spectral idempotents belong to the algebra.
In a diagonalizing basis these are E11 and E22. Sandwiching any other algebra
element between them isolates its off-diagonal entries. If both directions
occur, all four matrix units occur and the algebra is M. If just one occurs,
the algebra is triangular; if neither occurs it is diagonal. If no element
has distinct eigenvalues and the algebra is noncentral, subtracting the
eigenvalue from a noncentral element and changing basis gives E12. For any
Y in the algebra with Y21=c nonzero, E12 Y has eigenvalues c and 0, a
contradiction. Every Y is therefore upper triangular and has equal diagonal
entries, so the algebra is exactly span{I,E12}. This proves (4).

For the three commutative cases, (1) alone suffices; (2) is not needed.

* Diagonal case: diag(r,1), r>0, fixes the input. Its adjoint action scales
  Y12 by r and Y21 by 1/r. An invariant probability on C under a nontrivial
  dilation is concentrated at zero: the disjoint dilated annuli all have
  equal probability, which must be zero. Countably many annuli cover C\{0}.
  Applying this to both marginals proves that the output is diagonal.
* Central case: every S fixes the input. The diagonal argument first makes
  the output diagonal. Conjugation by I+E12 produces upper entry Y22-Y11;
  invariance then forces Y22=Y11. Thus the output is scalar.
* Jordan case: U_n=I+n E12 fixes the input for every integer n. For
  Y=[[a,b],[c,d]],

      U_n Y U_n^-1=[[a+n c, b+n(d-a)-n^2 c],[c,d-n c]].

  Unless c=0 and d=a, its norm tends to infinity as n tends to infinity.
  Invariance says mu(ball_R)=integral 1_{ball_R}(U_n Y U_n^-1) dmu(Y).
  Bounded dominated convergence makes this mu(ball_R intersect span{I,E12}).
  Let R grow. The output lies in span{I,E12} almost surely.

For the triangular case, all inputs are upper triangular. Put S_t=diag(t,1)
for 0<t<=1. The tuple eta_t=Ad_{S_t} eta tends to its diagonal tuple eta_0.
The input path including eta_0 is compact. If Y has law K_eta, equivariance
gives Y_t=Ad_{S_t}Y with law K_eta_t, and (Y_t)21=Y21/t. Uniform tightness
along this path forces Y21=0 almost surely. More explicitly, if
Pr(|Y21|>epsilon)=p>0, every output radius R is exceeded with probability
at least p for t<epsilon/R, contradicting (2). The output is upper
triangular, proving (3) in this final proper case. The full-algebra case is
tautological. Conjugating back completes the proof for every input tuple.

This proof needs tightness only along the specified degenerating orbit paths.
It does not require a density, absolute continuity, differentiability,
analyticity, a selected matrix metric, or finite variance.

## 3. Sharpness: a Borel algebra-covariant escape

Consider two actual input contents P and B obeying

    P^2=P, tr P=1, B!=0, B=P B(I-P).                            (5)

Then B maps the one-dimensional ker P isomorphically onto ran P. Let C be
its inverse from ran P to ker P, extended by zero on ker P. Equivalently C
is the unique solution of

    C=(I-P) C P, B C=P, C B=I-P.                               (6)

Existence is the inverse of a nonzero map between one-dimensional spaces;
uniqueness follows from the prescribed domain, range and inverse. Equations
(5)-(6) are similarity invariant, so C(Ad_S P,Ad_S B)=Ad_S C(P,B). This
construction does not select an inner product. On any coordinate patch with
B_ij!=0 it has the explicit rational expression

    C=(I-P) e_j e_i^T P / B_ij.                               (6a)

Indeed B e_j spans ran P and e_i^T B annihilates ran P. Multiplying (6a)
on either side by B gives the projections in (6). Its prescribed range and
kernel complete the check. Uniqueness makes the patches agree, so C is
continuous on (5) and Borel there. The coordinate formula supplies no preferred
physical basis: its output is the intrinsic inverse already defined in (6).

For P=E11 and B=t E12 with t nonzero,

    A(P,B)=upper triangular matrices, C(P,B)=t^-1 E21.          (7)

The deterministic law delta_C escapes the generated algebra. It cannot be
extended with uniform tightness to the finite input limit (E11,0). Its output
norm grows as 1/|t|. This exhibits what the trivial simultaneous stabilizer
of the triangular tuple alone leaves possible, consistent with Block25's
explicit restriction of its earlier general theorem to commutative algebras.

A full-domain permutation-covariant Borel kernel is available. In the m
recorded slots, collect every ordered pair (i,j) satisfying (5). If this
finite set is nonempty, use the uniform mixture of delta_{C(Ai,Aj)}; otherwise
use delta_{sum_i Ai/6}. The predicates, cardinality and unique inverses are
Borel and similarity invariant, and permuting slots just permutes the finite
set. Thus this is a total local Borel kernel satisfying (1), including proper
cubic covariance, which violates (3). A mixture with the full-domain word
kernel below can additionally retain its support on irreducible tuples.
Neither law is asserted to be selected by the current axioms.

## 4. A regular kernel attaining the allowed support

Block25 already supplies the construction; this section records its role
as a sharpness witness, not a new construction. Fill the six algebraic slots
with the recorded matrices and zeros for missing factors, without reading
blank sites. Let W_w(eta) range over all words of lengths 0 through 3, with
W_empty=I. For positive supplied c0,c1,c2,c3 and independent standard circular
complex Gaussian coefficients z_w, define

    Y_eta = sum_i Ai/6 + sum_{|w|<=3} c_|w| z_w W_w(eta).       (8)

There are 1+6+36+216=259 Gaussian marks. This law is weakly continuous by
the shared-mark polynomial coupling, and has uniformly bounded moments on
compact input sets. Similarity acts on each word; permutation of the six
slots permutes the iid marks within each length. Its support is the span
V3 of these words. Since the mean lies in V3, the affine support is V3 itself.

Starting with V0=C I, the word spans increase. If Vk=Vk+1 they are stable
under multiplication by all generators, hence already equal A(eta). If no
equality has occurred by k=3, dimension has risen from 1 to at least 4 and
V3=M. Therefore V3=A(eta). The Gaussian law is nondegenerate on this span
because every coefficient c_|w| is positive. It attains exactly the maximum
support allowed by (3), including all of M at irreducible tuples.

The arbitrary positive coefficients and Gaussian mark law remain supplied.
Neither maximal support nor regularity singles them out; different positive
coefficient choices already give different admissibility laws with the same
support, locality and covariance. No optimization over these coefficients
has been justified as an axiom-derived law selector.

## 5. Formation consequence and its limits

Suppose every initial Record content lies in a common proper unital algebra
B, common-fiber comparison is as above, and each formation uses a kernel
obeying (1)-(2). On any history with well-founded local dependencies,
induction keeps every new Record in B: the neighbors generate a subalgebra
of B, and every admissible supported output is in that subalgebra by (3).
The finite-ancestor constructions of Block25 meet this history condition.
The theorem is not extended to an unspecified infinite backward dependency
chain or to a physical instrument reading unknown live qubits.

One seed matrix A can at most generate span{I,A}; Cayley-Hamilton makes this
commutative. Two noncommuting seed matrices are not sufficient for full M:
(E11,E12) generate only the triangular algebra. Two matrices with no common
invariant line, such as (E12,E21), do generate M. If they occupy opposite
neighbors of a blank site, (8) gives that site's forming content full M
support. Thus an irreducible relational seed gives an explicit allowed
bootstrap; regularity does not prohibit full-algebra probability laws.

This is an algebraic restriction on this candidate class, not a physical
quantum no-go. Scalar or triangular contents may encode other structures via
additional readout maps; no theorem here excludes such encodings or derives
their physical validity. No initial empty state, seed distribution, clock,
observable map, interaction, matter sector or gravitational law is selected.
The result supplies a precise hypothesis test: a proposal claiming to create
full matrix support from a common proper algebra must identify which premise
of this theorem it escapes. It does not force a change to the current axioms.
