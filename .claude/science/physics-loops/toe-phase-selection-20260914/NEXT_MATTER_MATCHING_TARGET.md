# Next matter matching target

The new positive charge-energy floor must not be reused as an electron-mass
claim. The proved sector is a pure-gauge representation with a prescribed
nonzero local external charge profile. Its transfer commutes with every local
gauge transformation and preserves that profile.

A dynamical matter construction changes the Hilbert space and its gauge
representation. A gauge-invariant matter excitation may satisfy a combined
Gauss constraint in which the electric divergence is balanced by matter
charge. The total gauge-charge label can then be zero even though the
excitation carries the desired physical charge. The present pure-gauge
projection and its conditional temporal averaging cannot simply be copied
without rederiving their hypotheses for that larger transfer operator.

The next decisive task is to specify the actual matter operator, hopping term,
combined gauge action and physical charge observable, then derive its
spectral measure and a dispersion or mobility witness. Test a matrix element
that moves the matter endpoint while preserving the combined Gauss law.
A merely static insertion, nonzero external-sector floor, or supplied
coefficient in a hopping Hamiltonian is not that witness. This identifies a
concrete matching task; it rules out no possible matter construction and
supplies no axiom-forcing contradiction.
