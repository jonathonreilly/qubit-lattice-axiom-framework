# Personal first review of the fixed-law bridge

The full compact-U(1) flux characteristic has an exact positive auxiliary real-MGF representation, checked on actual three/four-cube Hodge matrices. Three distinct one-charge-cube representations agree in twelve cases; using a characteristic function instead of the real MGF gives a clear wrong-sign discrepancy. This verifies the finite identity only.

A separate preconditioned-flow lemma is being developed to establish Gaussianity of diffuse gradient sources with an ell3 remainder. Its hypotheses retain both Hessian contraction and a uniform third-order influence bound. A collective rank-one cosine interaction shows why the latter cannot be omitted. The application uses explicit free-cube cochain reflection and carrier derivative estimates, still to be challenged. The intended scalar periodic Riesz bound has a visible inverse omission in the published primary theorem display; its correctly specified analytic input needs another source/proof check.

No fixed-beta covariance homogenization, angle/auxiliary state match, finite-clock Gaussian phase, independent check, new PR, or retained status has been established. The preconditioned lemma and its pending checks are the current hard work; no formal promotion is requested.
