# Narrow counterexamples and retained failures

Zero mass gives a second zero mode and destroys this vacuum-reduced inverse. Fixed positive mass has exponentially small opposite-end response under the displayed path contraction; changing mass and paying the shrinking gap avoids that family-specific failure. Neither result is a general no-go for local simulators.

Useful one-excitation entries do not bound the complete inverse used by the two-excitation corrector. Mere positivity without a quantitative entry lower bound does not certify polynomial coefficients. Multiple clock heads leave n_j n_k cross terms; a vacuum clock does not launch. Omitting the nonscalar kappa n_p leaves a plant defect. A single XX axis lacks the complex X/Y source freedom. A dressed-state estimate omits both bare-input endpoint corrections.

Linear time sampling produces the wrong schedule for the binomial clock. Discarding clock or reserve phase changes a coherent isometry. Arbitrarily oversized n or Delta satisfies a lower accuracy condition while invalidating upper resource powers tied to a sufficient choice. A generic many-body waveform is not directly a two-qubit one-port control.

A product Gibbs controller marginal can have coherent old/reference correlations: its marginal distance 2delta is not generally a valid full replacement bound; the safe joint allowance is g(delta). The exact unconditional old marginal cannot be replaced by a normalized good-event state or a nominal earlier state. Small state error with growing B_full does not establish accurate energy moments, returned auxiliary energy, final isolation or reuse. Fixed temperature/gap cannot keep a growing whole controller arbitrarily pure.

Finite runtime is not permanent stopping, finite matrix checks are not arbitrary-size/time proof, and polynomial auxiliary prices do not bound a general waveform compiler or produce native cooling/release hardware.
