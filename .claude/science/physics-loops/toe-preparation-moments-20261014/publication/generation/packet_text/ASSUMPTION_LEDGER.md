# Assumptions and paid limits

| Premise | Role and obligation |
|---|---|
| Exact finite multi-port K, Delta>0, full auxiliary space and raw be8 facts | Imported from the one canonical mathematical parent; arbitrary original H is retained. |
| Identical installed K and scalar for actual and comparator preparation | Required for sensitivity and later same-K conservation; no later fresh-vacuum substitution. |
| Full old/reference marginal bound | Supplied joint trace-norm certificate; one-site marginals or auxiliary populations do not imply it. |
| Local auxiliary excitation probabilities or certified upper bounds | Supplied preparation information, with total defect at most their sum; correlations and P-Q/Q-Q coherence remain allowed. |
| Optional exact vacuum/nonvacuum block diagonality | Separate extra premise; population control alone does not dephase. Any implementation, clock, reset and approximation are additional costs. |
| Reference-valued records | Retain epsilon_R, including Delta*kappa_vac*epsilon_R and scalar-dependent mean terms. Scalar variance statements do not imply an operator-valued variance. |
| Normalization | Scalar variance statements concern normalized states. Unnormalized positive blocks retain homogeneous linear moment statements only; rare normalization needs its probability. |
| Restricted chain family | Complete 79d Appendix supplies the chosen reserved 3-by-3 strip before H: 2M+1 original roles, 4M fresh auxiliaries, 6M+1 active sites, 8M edges, 9(M+1) box sites. No arbitrary occupied-site packing. |
| Strength, baseline and physical time | Same physical time and original H; growing Delta, scalar/reserve, ideal variance, norm, precision and timing remain charged. No common-cap or optimality claim. |
| Physical preparation/reader/energy interpretation | Remain supplied or open. This source composition supplies no new preparation apparatus, native dynamics, reset or physical collapse. |

Generic kappa already includes Delta; sharper kappa excludes it. Generic D is the excited block, sharper D0 the centered original operator, appendix D a scalar norm coefficient and appendix D0 the penalty prefactor. The wrapper makes these local conventions explicit without renaming accepted proof text.
