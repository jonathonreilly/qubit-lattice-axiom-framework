# Failure and source-exposure record

All original failed routes, false/pending fields, precisions and exposure
records remain byte-for-byte in the accepted source archive. Later acceptance
records sit beside them. The parent V2's earlier failed reconstruction is
preserved as a historical failure in its own publication evidence; its
successful repair is a tooling reference, not this package's validation.

This package tests two wrong assembly-diff conventions and four corrupted
byte inputs in a temporary root. All six intentionally return 1 for their
expected reasons after four successful baselines. These are integrity controls,
not mathematical counterexamples. No automatic retry or failed-return
reinterpretation is performed. Complete new tool outcomes remain in the
preparation's execution_logs and scratch_identity records.

Initial filename/metadata displays were truncated. Necessary specific sources
were read separately, and full result arrays were parsed against exact
manifests. Such parsing is not claimed as manual review of every raw array.
The final AUTHOR_READ_COVERAGE records precise source/program coverage.
No new scientific derivation, formal check, model change or audit verdict is
supplied. Historical core coverage is reused at unchanged pins.
