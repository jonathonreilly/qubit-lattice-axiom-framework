# Mathematical approaches

| Construction | Purpose and outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Physical kernel and projected band | Keeps the dressing mode, excludes the auxiliary singlet, and fixes the small prepared amplitude. |
| Two spectral-cutoff correctors | Controls the dressing orbit and the nonzero-spectrum complement separately, with explicit time dependence. |
| Canonical current and energy conservation | Retains finite-rank placement terms and the amplitude-sensitive quadratic error. |
| One globally normalized free-band packet | Preserves the actual momentum weights on a nonempty three-dimensional support. |
| Volterra equation and resolvent | Gives exact coherent memory and the dressing spectral measure. |
| Cone density and endpoint compression | Proves absolute continuity without substituting finite-bath behavior. |
| Complete wave operators | Gives a norm-one outgoing transverse datum and bounded weighted-current limits. |
| Regulated outgoing resolvent | Identifies the selected outgoing datum and gives an explicit weighted lower bound using its moment and cone mass. |
| Joint scale choice | Uses the finite all-time estimate at growing times, with all normalized errors tending to zero. |

These are constructive supplied-model arguments. None establishes a stochastic
encounter law or exhausts the possibilities for many-body transport.
