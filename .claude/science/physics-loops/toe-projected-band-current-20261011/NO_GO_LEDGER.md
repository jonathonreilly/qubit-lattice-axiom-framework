# Scope of negative findings

No no_go or route-exhaustion theorem is proposed. The finite-bath recurrence
example and the necessity of the ac-membership and observable-invariance
hypotheses are preserved as bounded mathematical examples. The primary does
not infer continuum relaxation from finite matrices or uniform all-state
approximation from fixed-vector bounds. Limits on these proofs are not
claims that another physical mechanism is impossible. No N-gate execution
or negative route certificate is asserted.
