# Goal

Run a 24-hour first-principles campaign from current main and the exact
unretained prior-art cutoff. The campaign seeks the shortest path to a
positively retained end-to-end Record-native law, while accepting an exact
minimal law or axiom decision if the positive route cannot be completed.

No existing percentage moves merely because a construction passes. Audit
retention and obligation retirement are separate events.

## Exact Target Contract

Target statement:

Construct one exact law, or one exact operational-equivalence class of laws,
that starts from the current Lattice, Qubit, Admissibility, Record, and
Qualification surface and fills the live Record/Born fields:

1. a positive physical state or preparation interface;
2. a normalized effect or event law with Born-form history weights;
3. actual Record formation and permanent content;
4. autonomous continuation with concurrency and overlap semantics;
5. a full-lattice or exact projectively consistent history domain;
6. an explicit physical-time boundary rather than a simulator ordinal; and
7. a typed handoff to matter and the gravity source/state interface.

An alternate completion witness is an exact, newly strengthened
underdetermination or incompatibility result that survives the no-go
discipline and identifies the smallest extensional law datum, state-type
clarification, or approved-primitive decision required for a positive theory.

## Quantifiers And Domain

The preferred positive target is translation and proper-cubic covariant on
Z cubed, strict nearest-neighbor when represented as a local law, total on its
declared causal domain, and exact for arbitrary finite histories. A genuinely
global history law is admissible only if it proves its local Admissibility
projection, projective consistency, intervention typing, and Record readout.

## Allowed Premises

- the four current axioms and three approved primitives;
- finite-dimensional operator algebra and standard probability theory;
- named mathematical theorems with their import role exposed;
- contingent boundary data if typed as contingent rather than derived;
- reconstructed unlanded branch results as support-only prior art, never as
  authority or retained dependencies.

## Forbidden Weakenings

- another finite single-front construction standing in for total law closure;
- another sampled-law pair that repeats the July and August nonselection
  results;
- a supplied density operator, menu, scheduler, seed, preparation label, or
  gravity source called derived;
- a current-map Markov law silently conditioned on an unrecorded live factor;
- a normalized branch family without actuality, concurrency, or physical
  history semantics;
- a local rule without overlap or collision analysis;
- a simulator tick called physical time;
- a positive-semidefinite mathematical form called a physical state without
  the preparation, quotient, and Record interfaces;
- route-confidence or unretained PR progress counted as TOE percentage
  progress.

## Required Edge Cases

- blank and malformed Record maps;
- multiple controllers or fronts and overlapping neighborhoods;
- incompatible preparations with the same current Record map;
- zero-probability and certain events;
- shared effects across different physical registrations;
- translations and all 24 proper cubic rotations;
- boundary and infinite-volume consistency;
- schedule permutations and critical pairs;
- clean versus dirty ancillary sectors;
- flat and weakly curved gravity-interface limits if that bridge is reached.

## Completion Witness

Positive completion requires an acyclic dependency graph, exact construction,
hostile controls, a runner whose final line is TOTAL: PASS=n FAIL=n, and an
independently checkable claim boundary. A law-selection or axiom-decision
completion must state the exact datum and show why every smaller currently
available route fails or remains equivalent to the target.

## Prior-Art Cutoff

Current main already contains the July complete sampled-law pair and later
August local-law and Born-factorization diagnostics. The unretained stacked
campaign goes farther:

- Block 63 constructs a state-dependent Record/Born/history candidate.
- Block 64 compiles it to a strict-NN arbitrary-horizon single front.
- Block 84 constructs a seed-grown full-lattice cylinder law and a twin.
- Block 87 totalizes one finite-state Markov law.
- Block 94 proves current-Record-map insufficiency for the prepared-live
  family and leaves four live routes: generated matter Record, preparation
  Record, complete-history or law-side conditioning, or a causal-prestate
  distinction.

This campaign begins at that four-route fork.

## Panel Contract

A five-physicist panel is used at initial route selection, after a major
theorem or countermodel, after two consecutive non-terminal blocks, and on a
time ceiling while route value remains ambiguous. Each panel must answer
whether the current approach is worth continuing and whether a higher leverage
TOE investment is available. The second panel caught a scope overclaim, a
comparator mismatch, and a route-bundling error, so the current ceiling is two
hours. Frequency widens only after two redundant unanimous panels.

## Campaign Accounting

The checkpoint-zero percentages in STATE.yaml stay fixed until independent
retention retires an obligation. Candidate construction can change route
confidence and the opportunity queue without changing those values.
