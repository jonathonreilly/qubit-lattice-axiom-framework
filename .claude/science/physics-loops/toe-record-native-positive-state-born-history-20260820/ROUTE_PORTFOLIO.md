## Initial Panel Synthesis

All five perspectives agreed on the negative portfolio cuts:

- another generic full-Z3 Record law is already constructible and unselected;
- another global history-positive functional duplicates Cycle 30 and later
  cylinder work;
- a two-write reflected PSD witness is not discriminating. For equal binary
  marginals `p`, the exchange-symmetric reflected-positive fiber contains the
  full interval `p^2 <= a <= p`, with product only at `a=p^2`;
- a live causal prestate is premature while current-ontology generated and
  preparation-Record routes remain open; and
- gravity remains externally owned.

The positive synthesis combines the two top recommendations. Route B's
preparation Record supplies the physical intervention carrier; Route A's
endogenous controller supplies a current-ontology genesis attempt. The new
content must be exact tag screening or strong lumpability under the same
strict-NN kernel, followed by a preparation-affinity discriminator. A generic
Choi-positive map, an externally supplied mixture table, or one more PSD
coupling is a duplicate and fails the value gate.

The skeptical panel's stronger requirement is retained: the preparation write
should descend from one independently specified finite action and resource
debit. If the first block can only insert the write table, it records that
supplier honestly and pivots to the action-to-write derivation rather than
calling the route closed.

## Panel Decision Rule

The selected route must have a concrete first return within one deep block:
an exact lemma, construction, counterexample, or dependency reduction. If its
terminal residual is equivalent to selecting the complete law by fiat, mark
it blocked-equivalent and pivot. Two consecutive non-terminal returns force a
new panel and a route change unless a new mechanism has appeared.
