# Mathematical mechanisms

Mass uses time averages, mixed-lowering finite-energy estimates, unequal-time
LR localization, Cook estimates with every spectator and comparison of widths.
Centre uses Hilbert-valued line control plus massive Hessian transversality;
exact squared-energy slabs independently check the geometric step. A separate
full-line rapidity argument identifies the supplied scalar boost domain.
Joint uses mixed connected histories, self-adjoint Taylor remainders, absolute
multiple series and finite time subdivision, followed by finite padded Jacobi,
one-site basis tests and a local divergence classification.
These are mathematical mechanisms; hashes and predicate counts are bookkeeping.
