# Sources and limited reuse

The mass note adapts the named Dybalski hep-th/0412226v4 mechanism and the
relevant BDN arXiv:1412.2970v2 lattice arguments at explicitly checked scope.
Relativistic locality, Poincare representation and massless/Fock conclusions
are not silently imported. EVIDENCE_MAP and EXTERNAL_SOURCE_INVENTORY bind the
official source URLs, versions, downloaded bytes and every extracted/rendered
external artifact; the originals remain external and are not deleted.
The centre scalar-domain proof is self-contained; earlier free-Dirac sources
provide historical context only. The joint proof invokes no new analytic-
vector integrability theorem. No new exhaustive novelty search or paper read
was performed in preparation.
