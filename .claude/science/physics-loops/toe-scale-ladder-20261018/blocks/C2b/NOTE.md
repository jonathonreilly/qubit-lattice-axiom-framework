## 1. The candidate statement

Notation. The tick–edge frame is `(t, x, y, z)` on `Z³ × Z_τ`. O is the Lattice axiom's proper cubic rotations of the spatial axes. A *sector* is any field species that the supplied law carries. That includes the gauge or plaquette sector, matter, and any further species, each with its own dimension-4 quadratic (kinetic) form in the infrared.

- **KI+_w (universal kinetic form).** There is one symmetric form `Q` on the tick–edge frame such that the dimension-4 kinetic form of every sector is the one that sector builds from `Q`:
  - a scalar uses `Q^{μν} ∂_μφ∂_νφ`;
  - a spinor uses `ψ̄ γ^a e_a^μ ∂_μ ψ` with `e eᵀ = Q`;
  - a gauge field uses `Q^{μρ} Q^{νσ} F_{μν} F_{ρσ}`.

  O-covariance makes `Q = diag(q_t, q_s, q_s, q_s)`. KI+_w says the ratio `q_s/q_t` is **one number for all sectors**. The registered primitive (`c_t = c_s` for matter) then fixes that number to 1.
- **KI+_G (symmetric law, the candidate proper).** The supplied law is covariant under B4⁺ = ⟨O, R_{tx}⟩ with `a_τ = a`, where `R_{tx}` is the quarter-turn `t → x → −t`. The law here means the full Euclidean lattice action of all sectors: kinetic terms, couplings, and every irrelevant or regulator term. On each sector the group acts through the lift the sector's index structure requires: a vector on `γ_μ`, a 2-form on `F_{μν}`, and no B4⁺-charged taste index (C2's H1 and H2). This is the time-direction analogue of Admissibility's covariance clause. It is not a re-axiomatisation of time and it names no dynamics.
- **KI+_tick.** In tick and edge units the common cone is 1 edge per tick.

**Logical relations (proved, section 3).**
- KI+_G implies KI+_w and KI+_tick, at tree level and order by order (given H1–H4).
- KI+_w together with the registered KI implies KI+_tick at tree level.
- KI+_w does not imply KI+_G, and it is not radiatively stable (section 4).
- KI+_G restricted to matter is the registered KI under its "regulator block hypercubic-symmetric" clause.

**Why B4⁺ is the weakest symmetric form (exact; `kip_group_theory.log`).** We look for a symmetry group G with O ⊆ G ⊆ B4, realised as lattice point symmetries. G must leave
- a one-dimensional space of invariant symmetric rank-2 tensors (n_S = 1);
- no antisymmetric invariant (n_A = 0, so there is no extra `ψ̄γ_{[μ}∂_{ν]}ψ`);
- only `F²`, plus `FF̃` if G is proper, among the quadratic forms in `F` (n_F).

Exact character averages over all 7 subgroups of B4 that contain O:

| order | proper | mixes t with space | n_S | n_A | n_F | sufficient |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 24 (O) | yes | no | 2 | 0 | 3 | no |
| 48 (three groups) | mixed | no | 2 | 0 | 2 or 3 | no |
| 96 | no | no | 2 | 0 | 2 | no |
| **192 (B4⁺)** | **yes** | **yes** | **1** | **0** | **2 (F², FF̃)** | **yes** |
| 384 (B4) | no | yes | 1 | 0 | 1 | yes |

A group that never moves the time axis leaves `(c_t, c_s)` free (n_S = 2). That restates the B4/S4 transitivity no-go on main: the extra generator *is* the content. The smallest sufficient group is unique, and ⟨O, R_{tx}⟩ = B4⁺. The improper B4 adds nothing for velocities; it only removes `FF̃`, which carries no velocity. So time reflection does not need to be imported beyond the Lattice axiom's proper rotations.

**Check against registered premises (no contradiction found).**
- *Lattice.* KI+_G uses the proper rotations O and translations, and adds `R_{tx}`, which acts on the emergent tick. It does not alter the spatial structure.
- *Qubit, Record.* KI+ is silent on both.
- *Admissibility.* Admissibility "is not a dynamics axiom" and defines no time metric. KI+ constrains the *supplied* dynamics, exactly as the registered KI does, and supplies no dynamics.
- *`kinetic_isotropy_primitive`.* KI+ implies it; there is no conflict. It is a strict extension of reading K_m. It is the content of reading K_full, which C2 identified as one reading of "the regulator block on which loops are computed is hypercubic-symmetric". Choosing that reading by ruling is barred by `AXIOM_MINIMALITY_POLICY.md` section 1, so only an owner act could make it the meaning.
- *`scale_reference_primitive`.* That primitive carries units only and KI+ supplies none, so they are orthogonal. Together they give the size of the residual Lorentz violation (section 3).
- *`realized_state_primitive`.* KI+ is a law-level covariance and fixes no state, so it passes the "law privileges no states" clause. It does **not** exclude a realized state that breaks B4⁺ (H4): a medium or a background. Infrared speeds quoted in such a state are state data and are not fixed by KI+.
- *Strict-license note* ("Matter sector only ... gauge-sector plaquette-coefficient anisotropy ... separate rows"). KI+ fills exactly that separate row, as a premise rather than a derivation.
- *Curvature-member common-cone note* (2026-10-18). That note gets equal matter cones in the continuum from a supplied one-relabelling-algebra closure with gravity. ## 3. What KI+ implies

**(a) Tree level, dimension 4 (proved).**
- KI+_w: `c_γ² = q_s/q_t = c_ψ²` by definition. This is a restatement, not a derivation.
- KI+_G: since n_S = 1, the matter form is `∝ δ_{μν}`. Since n_F = 2 with `FF̃` velocity-free, the gauge form is `∝ F_{μν}F_{μν}`. Hence `c_γ = c_ψ = a/a_τ = 1` edge per tick.

**(b) Radiative stability.**
- *Under KI+_G (standard symmetry argument; not re-proved here):*
  - With a B4⁺-covariant regulator, every effective-action coefficient is B4⁺-invariant. The dimension-4 bilinears are unique, so δ(μ) = 0 at every μ, perturbatively, given H1–H4.
  - On C2's one-loop flow, η = 1 is an exact zero of `β_η` for every `e` (`kip_flow.log`). So δ(cutoff) = 0 gives δ(l) = 0 for all l.
  - Lattice artifacts are present but harmless. B4⁺-invariant dimension-6 operators such as `Σ_μ ψ̄γ_μ∂_μ³ψ` exist. In loops they feed dimension-4 terms only through the unique invariant, so they renormalise `Z` but not δ.
  - The toy shows this directly. The symmetric action has power-divergent, non-logarithmic contributions to `Z_0`, and `Z_0 = Z_s` to 6e-10.
  - The residual Lorentz violation is then dimension 6. Its size is `~ κ (E a)²`, with `a⁻¹ = M_Pl` from the scale reference and κ = O(0.05–1). Example values are 0.0486 for the walk's cubic coefficient at b_gold and 1/3 for the B4 note's estimate.
- *Under KI+_w only (toy computation, section 4):*
  - **Statement.** If the dimension-4 forms are universal but some irrelevant term breaks B4⁺, the one-loop matching at the cutoff gives δ_th = C (g²/16π²) ε. Here ε is the size of the B4-breaking irrelevant coefficient and C ≈ 0.36 (T3, per unit ε) to 0.93 (RT) in the toy. This is not suppressed by any power of `a`.
  - This is the Collins–Perez–Sudarsky–Urrutia–Vucetich mechanism, PRL 93, 191301 (2004), as recalled; its coefficients were not re-checked.
  - C2's flow then carries 0.78 δ_th to the IR. At QED-like coupling with ε ~ 0.05 that gives δ_IR ~ 7e-6, about 10 orders above an illustrative 1e-15 bound (`kip_flow.log`; illustrative, toy coefficient).
  - So **KI+_w is not a sufficient extension.** The extension must constrain the whole law, including its irrelevant terms. That is the answer to the question of whether KI+ avoids the Collins issue: KI+_G does, KI+_w does not.
  - Lattice-QCD practice is consistent with this, as recalled: on anisotropic lattices the gauge and fermion anisotropies must be tuned separately (e.g. Klassen, NPB 533 (1998) 557), while isotropic hypercubic lattices need no Lorentz tuning at dimension 4.

## 4. The Collins-type toy (numerical; `kip_collins_toy.runall.log`)

**Model.** Euclidean lattice with `a = 1`. A real scalar φ has tree form `Σ p̂_μ²`. A Wilson fermion has `S⁻¹ = iΣγ_μ s_μ(k) + M(k)` and Yukawa coupling `g φψ̄ψ`. At one loop, `Π(p) = g² ∫ tr[S(k)S(k+p)]` (from `Γ = −Tr ln(D + gφ)`). We write `Π = Π(0) + g² Σ_μ Z_μ p_μ² + …`, so that `δ_φ ≈ (g²/2)(Z_s − Z_0)`.

**Variants.** All share the same dimension-4 fermion form, `c_t = c_s = 1`, so KI+_w holds in every one.
- SYM: fully B4-symmetric.
- T3(ε): `s_0 = sin k_0 (1 − ε/3 + (ε/3)cos k_0)`, which is `k_0 − (1+ε)k_0³/6`. The difference from the spatial `sin k_j` is a pure dimension-6 time/space mismatch.
- RT: Wilson coefficients `r_t = 0.5`, `r_s = 1`, a dimension-5 mismatch.

**Quadrature.** A product Gauss–Legendre rule with a sinh map in each axis. The node set is symmetric under B4. The p-derivatives use a 5-point stencil with `h = 0.1 m`. Results are compared between N = 72 and 96 nodes per axis (96 and 128 for m a < 0.003).

**Results.**
- **SYM:** `|Z_0 − Z_s| ≤ 6e-10` at every m a from 0.4 to 0.003 (rounding level).
- **Log coefficient:** the slope `dZ_0/d ln(1/m)` falls toward the continuum value 1/(4π²) = 0.02533. That value was fixed beforehand by a hand Feynman-parameter calculation and agrees with γ_φ = 2g²/16π². The last slope is 0.02621, within 3.5%, and it decreases monotonically, consistent with O(m a) Wilson corrections.
- **T3, ε = 0.5:** `Z_0 − Z_s` runs −3.9e-4, −8.9e-4, …, −2.219e-3 as m a goes 0.4 → 0.003. The step ratio is 0.51, so it converges, to about −2.25e-3. For ε = 0.25 it converges to about −1.15e-3. The ratio is 1.95, so the response is linear in ε.
- **RT:** the anisotropy converges to about −0.0117 by m a = 2e-4.
- In every B4-breaking variant the anisotropy **grows** as m a decreases and tends to a nonzero constant. A lattice artifact would instead vanish like a power of m a.
- Writing `δ_φ = C (g²/16π²) × (mismatch)`, the run gives **C ≈ 0.36 per unit ε** for T3 (the ε = 0.5 and ε = 0.25 runs agree, 0.355 and 0.364) and **C ≈ 0.93** for RT with `r_s − r_t = 0.5`.

**Not claimed.**
- These are toy coefficients, not those of QED or of the supplied carriers.
- The fermion's own one-loop speed shift was not computed; generically it does not cancel the scalar's.
- The quadrature has no certificate.

## 6. Minimality and cost (honest classification)

- **Not derivable from the registered surface (witness under matching premises).** C2's W2 has models A and B that share Lattice, cubic covariance, a common tick with `a_τ = a`, KI (K_m, bare matter action) and the scale reference. They have c_γ/c_ψ = 1 and 6/5. So even KI+_w is independent of the registered premises.
- **Not a definitional choice.**
  - A unit definition can absorb one dimensionful conversion, such as ρ or `a`. It cannot absorb a dimensionless ratio of two sectors' speeds.
  - Reading the registered text as K_full would be a definitional move in form. But policy bars resolving the ambiguity by ruling, and its content differs from K_m (W2 separates them).
- **Classification.** KI+_G is a **widening amendment of `kinetic_isotropy_primitive`**:
  - its scope grows from the matter kinetic form to the whole supplied law;
  - it implies the registered primitive;
  - it adds no new *kind* of premise. It is a dimensionless structural covariance with no mass ratio, coupling, phase or selector, the same category as the registered primitive and the Lattice/Admissibility covariance clauses;
  - it removes `N_sectors − 1` free speed ratios and forbids B4⁺-odd irrelevant terms in all sectors.
  - The last point is a real constraint on supplied dynamics, larger in scope than "a graining ratio". The registry note's phrase "supplies only the kinetic form ratio" would no longer describe it.
  - If adopted *in place of* KI, the count of primitives is unchanged and the content is strictly larger.
- **Alternative routes, which trade premises rather than remove them.**
  1. The HKT one-algebra closure with a supplied gravity sector gives equal matter cones in the continuum (curvature-member note, 2026-10-18). It needs supplied gravity clauses and unit generator normalisations. It fails on the lattice at O(a) or O(a²), so it supplies no cutoff protection.
  2. A dynamical attractor: C2 and §3(b) show the log flow is too slow.
  3. A derivation of KI+_G from a single structure, for example a photon built as a bilinear of the matter field on the same tick. If found, that would retire the extension; it is not available on main.

## 7. Falsifiers

- **F1 (empirical).** A detected energy-independent (dimension-4) difference between the maximal speeds of the photon and any charged species would refute KI+_G. Bounds of order 1e-15 or tighter are recalled in C2 §5 and not re-verified. Medium and background effects in the realized state would have to be excluded first. KI+_G with the scale reference predicts δ = 0 at dimension 4 and residuals `∝ (E/M_Pl)²`, which is about 7e-33 × κ at 1 TeV. An E-independent signal versus E² growth is the discriminator.
- **F2 (derivational, carriers).** A derivation that the framework's realized photon or matter carrier cannot sit in a B4⁺-covariant law on a common tick would make KI+_G unsatisfiable by the framework's own carriers. Examples: an intrinsically continuous-time emergent photon, or a QCA tick with no tick–edge quarter-turn covariance. The extension would then be inconsistent with them rather than a constraint on them.
- **F3 (internal, supplied walk).** A derivation that the physical clock is two-tick, or that π-modes are physical IR species, leaves no walk member satisfying KI+ (§5 caveat 1).
- **F4 (symmetry breaking).** A derivation that the vacuum, as the realized state, spontaneously breaks B4⁺ (H4 fails) would make KI+ irrelevant to IR speeds.
- **F5 (computational).** A one-loop computation in an explicitly B4⁺-covariant lattice model with H1–H4 in which δ ≠ 0 would contradict §3(b). The toy found none, at the 6e-10 level.
