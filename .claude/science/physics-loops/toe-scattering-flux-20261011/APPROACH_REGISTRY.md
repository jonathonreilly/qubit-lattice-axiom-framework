# Mathematical approaches

| Formulation | Mechanism | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Real-space source and bond currents | Discrete continuity and weak annular flux | Exact normalization with parity interference retained. |
| Regular lattice resolvent | Scalar reduction and matrix coarea/stationary phase | Fixed-energy large-radius flux and threshold cross section. |
| Normalized shell beams | Rank-one scattering and disjoint output projections | Positive order-lambda-squared finite-packet transfer. |
| Flux bilinear form | Weighted variance and rank-two observable difference | Exact form coefficient and coercivity. |
| Contact ellipsoid | Full fourth moments and quadratic-form nullspace | Complete current tensor, active rank and explicit bounds. |

All are constructive routes. Universal kinetic closure is a distinct next
problem; this milestone makes no route-exhaustion claim about it.
