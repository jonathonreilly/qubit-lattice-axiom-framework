# Literature bridge

W. Shaban and B. Vainberg, Radiation Conditions for the Difference Schrodinger
Operators, arXiv:1812.06390. Source URL and PDF digest are in the proof. The
Fourier setup, Theorem 1, Lemma 2, Theorem 4, equation (13), and proof through
(21) were checked, with equation (13) visually checked on page 8. Only the
regular strictly convex scalar expansion is imported. The matrix extension
is reconstructed with its hypotheses. No continuum theorem is substituted
for the lattice flux calculation. The original paper is not redistributed.
