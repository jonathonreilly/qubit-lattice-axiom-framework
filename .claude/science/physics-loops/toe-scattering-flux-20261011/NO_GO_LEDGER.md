# Scope of negative findings

No no_go or route-exhaustion theorem is proposed in this milestone. The source
preserves constructed dark/contact vectors, parity zeros and differing ordered/
paired normalizations as explicit examples. Its positive flux and tensor claims
are restricted to their declared model and limits. Statements about omitted
kinetic conclusions describe what this proof establishes, not impossibility
of future mechanisms. No N-gate certificate is asserted as executed.
