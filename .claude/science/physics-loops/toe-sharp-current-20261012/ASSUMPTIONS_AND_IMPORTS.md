# Assumptions and imports

Mathematical scope: the supplied one-dimensional free massive chain, m,v>0,
0<b<a, its actual coefficient energy and normalized primitive actual current,
complex-linear exact-energy isometries and the real subclass, and the specified
spectral bands. The proof addresses all such isometries without imposing fiber
decomposability or translation covariance. It does not assume a preferred map.

The direct scientific imports are the concrete-chain spin/CAR/current realization
and local-even-continuum scaling/reality/CAR conventions, pinned in the author
source inventory and to be bound to published note bytes. The energy-coordinate
attainer is rederived in full, so no energy-adapted continuum theorem is imported.
Energy/state/dyadic work is related chronology and context only.

Ordinary spectral calculus, isometry norm preservation, direct integrals and CAR
norm estimates are standard mathematics. General OAR/wavelet methods are prior
art; this packet makes no generic novelty claim. The result is not an interacting
additive-momentum or higher-dimensional lane contribution.
