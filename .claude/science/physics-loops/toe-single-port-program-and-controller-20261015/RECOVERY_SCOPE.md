# Selected recovery scope

The four primary proof/review bodies are stored once as exact note intervals. The accepted clock proof remains an exact interval in its canonical parent note, and its separate review remains recoverable from the exact canonical gzip object, with stored and decompressed identities separately checked. Neither an unavailable interval nor a different byte encoding is silently substituted.

SOURCE_MANIFEST and ARCHIVE_INDEX deduplicate selected current source/decision/metadata/receipt bytes by SHA256. Current source freezes are checked against all their listed members; RECOVERY_SELECTION identifies which identities are recoverable in this new packet and which historical dependencies were not recursively copied. An identity check is not a complete content reading or mathematical revalidation.

Actual compiler author metadata and limited literature-retrieval history are preserved as recorded, including the earlier web-extraction archival limitation and the PDF's provenance-only role. Actual decisive-review and root-acceptance sources/argv/streams/exit receipts are preserved. N/run.py root receipts have no separate LAUNCH; none is manufactured. Source-only draft metadata runs use their own actual recorder and captures.

Old large publication archives, graph products and prior scientific outputs are not replayed or duplicated wholesale. The old mediator and Gibbs/publication-template sources are not hidden new mathematical parents. The template reuse map concerns code, not transfer of scientific claims.
