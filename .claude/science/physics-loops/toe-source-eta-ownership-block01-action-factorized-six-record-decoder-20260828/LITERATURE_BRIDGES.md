# Literature Bridges

Finite-range lattice operators can acquire wider effective support under
composition; exact support-factor equality is the relevant algebraic test.
Equivariant Hom classification counts allowed covariant maps but does not
select a physical member. A coordinate embedding of the cubic group into the
M2 automorphism group is additional structure unless derived from the physical
condition. These are mathematical bridges, not authority for an Admissibility
law.
