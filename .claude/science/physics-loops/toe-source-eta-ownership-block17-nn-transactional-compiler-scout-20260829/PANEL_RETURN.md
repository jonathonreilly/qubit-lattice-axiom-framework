## Aggregate decision

1. Finish Block 17 exactly once by packaging the independently reproduced
   false-green boundary: useful geometry survives, but the candidate has
   unsourced payloads and unbound gate maps.
2. Do not build a Block-17b compiler variant.
3. Run a pure permanent-Record occurrence and joint-cylinder campaign.
4. Keep activation hazards `lambda_x(R)` distinct from conditional mark laws
   `p_x(v|R)`:

   ```text
   (L F)(R) = sum_(x not in dom R) lambda_x(R)
              sum_v p_x(v|R) [F(R union {(x,v)})-F(R)].
   ```

5. Test both the single-oriented-Record seed and the Block-16 three-Record
   gapped seed.  The former is a target change; the latter is a compound event
   unless hidden intermediate carriers are supplied.
6. Use two dimensionlessly inequivalent rate pairs.  Comparing `Q` only with
   `cQ` tests a choice of units, not physical nonselection.
7. Hand gravity only an explicitly timed candidate current.  Require exact
   local conservation and an identified source observable before claiming a
   gravity advance.

The panel's hardest warning concerns infinite volume.  Positive homogeneous
nucleation at every blank site gives infinite **global** event rate, so it
cannot be described by one global exponential holding time.  That does not by
itself rule out a standard finite-range interacting particle system: a local
Poisson graphical construction may still be well defined on every finite
spacetime cylinder.  The successor campaign must test that distinction rather
than silently assume either conclusion.
