## Efficiency rule

At every campaign boundary, rerank against the shortest path to a positively
retained end-to-end theory. Continue a route only if it can retire at least one
of: physical occurrence selector, microscopic Record lock, physical clock, or
local conserved-source join. Pure enumeration, additional arbitrary hazards,
and broader quotient searches are lower leverage unless they directly attack
one of those obligations.
