## Rank 1 — action or transfer generator cone

This route should begin with the action or transfer object, not with a desired
beta value. The minimum useful result is:

1. a typed map from the admitted action/transfer data to monotone Record-history
   weights or generator entries;
2. positivity, normalization, covariance, and append-only permanence;
3. the full projected cone of same/other recorded-neighbor gains;
4. a proof of whether the projection is empty, one ray, or more than one ray
   modulo one common rate;
5. the equal-Z local-cylinder odds and local-process obligations for every
   surviving ray.

Ordinary detailed balance cannot be imposed naively on the same append-only
state graph. A forward append has no Record-erasing reverse, so full-support
detailed balance would force its rate to zero. The viable action route must
instead use an oriented history measure, an absorbing transfer construction, an
enlarged state space, or another explicitly non-erasing bridge. Existing
fixed-placement or conditional action evidence is not, by itself, a Record
history selector. In particular, open connection PR #7803 provides
positive fixed-placement reinforcement but no normalized permanent-Record
history or action-to-Record decoder, so it is an input rather than closure.

Kill or pivot rules:

- empty projected cone: the proposed action-to-Record bridge fails;
- one ray: run the complete collision/process/discriminator retirement test;
- multiple rays: report only action-family underselection and enumerate the
  surviving physical inputs;
- reverse occupied-to-null transitions required: pivot, because they violate
  append-only Record permanence in the current object.

## Rank 2 — autonomous reusable bath

The present construction imports a fresh vacuum ancilla and disposal after each
site visit. A stronger route must specify:

- a reusable many-body bath Hilbert space and state;
- one autonomous unitary or controlled repeated-interaction law;
- no hidden fresh-reset oracle;
- the induced relation-factor coupling quotient and cadence;
- correlations between visits and the resulting process class;
- QND, lock, formation, permanence, and local-infinite existence.

The route succeeds as a selector only if the bath dynamics derives one beta ray
without inserting the coupling ratio or cadence as a bath parameter.

## Rank 3 — distributed pointer encoder

A strict one-site M_2 carrier cannot host seven mutually orthogonal pointer
states, but that does not close distributed encodings. The bounded target is a
local or finite-range encoder that supplies:

- six readable mark sectors plus blank;
- nondemolition neighbor queries at the stated algebra;
- target append and permanent lock;
- covariance under simultaneous slot-label rotations;
- a reduced occurrence generator whose gain cone can be classified.

Encoding capacity alone is technical hardening, not a beta selector. This route
rises above rank three only if the encoder algebra constrains the same/other
gain ratio.

## Rank 4 — explicit minimal-axiom intensity decision

Current approved primitives do not contain the extensional beta law. An owner
may nevertheless decide to supply one. The honest governance path must:

- state the exact formation intensity/kernel and its domain, separately from
  the already conditional normalized mark distribution;
- distinguish supplied content from a physical derivation;
- update the primitive registry and dependency records;
- identify which prior imports are retired;
- preserve falsifiable downstream consequences and avoid calling beta=1
  canonical merely by minimality.

No claim that a new axiom is required is licensed by Block19.

## Portfolio stop conditions

Stop and reopen N1–N8 if a route changes any of:

- positive-real pair factors or the allowed Hamiltonian family;
- pointer carrier or QND algebra;
- fresh versus reusable bath semantics;
- one-site versus compound event arity;
- Markov versus memory-bearing history law;
- the one-common-rate equivalence;
- local/cylinder versus global process obligations.

The fastest genuine retirement remains rank 1: an admitted action/transfer
construction whose complete monotone generator cone is exactly one beta ray
modulo global scale.
