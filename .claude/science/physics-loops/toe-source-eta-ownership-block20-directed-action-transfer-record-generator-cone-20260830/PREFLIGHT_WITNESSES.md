## Full-support detailed-balance zero-edge lemma

Let `R' = R^(x,f)` differ from `R` by one append. Suppose a Markov kernel or
rate generator obeys detailed balance with a full-support stationary law
`pi`:

~~~text
pi(R) q(R,R') = pi(R') q(R',R),       pi(R),pi(R')>0.
~~~

Permanent Record lock requires the reverse erasure edge `q(R',R)=0`.
Therefore

~~~text
pi(R) q(R,R')=0,
q(R,R')=0.
~~~

Thus a nonzero append-only generator cannot itself be the literal
full-support reversible dynamics on the same visible Record graph. The
August 11 Perron kernel is stronger still: entrywise positivity makes both
directions positive before any deletion.

This lemma rejects only that literal same-graph identification. It leaves
oriented history measures, absorbing or killed transfers, nonreversible
bridges, and larger reversible systems with hidden archive/environment
degrees of freedom live. Choosing one of those directions is additional
bridge content, not a consequence of detailed balance.
