- `per_element`: exact group, source-coordinate independence, provenance,
  coefficient, channel, and parameter identities;
- `per_site`: blank/same/opposite/perpendicular sectors, append, lock, and

Each line must say `checked and not executed` where appropriate. Boilerplate
labels with no scientific content fail the gate.
