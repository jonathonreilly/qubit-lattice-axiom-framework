# Assumptions and Imports

## Approved baseline used

The minimal axioms supply:

- cubic `Z^3` sites, translations, nearest-neighbor adjacency, and proper cubic
  rotations;
- one-site possibility domain `M_2(C)`;
- one fixed local condition-dependent probability-distribution requirement;
  and
- local Records that form, lock one possibility, are permanent, and alone are
  readable.

The primitive registry was checked. Scale reference, kinetic isotropy, and
realized-state reference supply no POVM weights, branch instrument,
Record-to-live preparation, rate, clock, source, or gravity.

## Conditional science imported

- Block09's fourteen direction/corner product-state probability formula,
  H1/H2 tensors, proper-cubic target, and symbolic open-family moment;
- Block11's open-family full-algebra QND context and its preserved
  consumable/classical exits; and
- Block16--21 only as route and scope context, not as load-bearing proof of the
  Block22 construction.

Every imported result keeps its existing status. No conditional parent is
promoted.

## Explicit construction inputs

1. one selected isolated anchor;
2. one supplied Ready radial pointer word;
3. one consumable six-qubit live state;
4. one atomic radius-four higher-body event;
5. a selected grouping of twenty-six output sites into one event packet;
6. the displayed Lüders branch choice;
7. a STOP complement; and
8. declaration that each of the twenty-six Locked output sites has formed a
   local Record.

Removing or changing an item changes the construction domain.

## Derived in Block22

- the unique full-Hilbert fourteen-effect extension of the Block09 product law;
- all exact spectra, strict positivity, completeness, and `14 x 64`
  projector-square-root sectors;
- all 24 proper-cubic effect and code transformations;
- one common onsite spin representation across all 32 primitive qubits;
- six Ready and 84 Locked orthogonal product words;
- the five simultaneous front/outcome orbit sizes;
- isolated template/centroid anchor recovery;
- exact instrument CP/TP, branch probabilities, Lüders poststates,
  arbitrary-reference control, STOP, and Locked identity;
- CP-map covariance despite a nontrivial Kraus-gauge phase;
- the complete-`M_2` identity-channel branch-effect boundary; and
- the commuting orthogonal CNOT escape.

## Not imported or derived

- a scalar bit intrinsic to one site;
- Ready/live preparation from a strictly prior Record;
- autonomous packet discovery or anchor selection;
- overlap arbitration, cleanup, relay, liveness, or finite-history process;
- nearest-neighbor compilation or global translation-invariant dynamics;
- a map from fourteen outcomes to Block19's six occurrence marks;
- formation location, rate, cadence, physical clock, or empirical frequency
  theorem;
- Born weights as a consequence of the minimal axioms rather than the
  displayed candidate trace law;
- action-selected intensity, conserved source/debit current, or gravity;
- axiom necessity, axiom amendment, approved-primitive change, audit verdict,
  obligation retirement, or TOE percentage movement.

## Semantic boundary

Each output site locks one radial qubit possibility. The event label is decoded
from the joint pattern of twenty-six local Records plus their selected packet
membership and lattice positions. Because `P_0(r)=P_1(-r)`, no single local
density matrix carries an anchor-free logical bit.
