# Preflight witnesses

No Block23 target runner, cache, or target mutation has been created or
executed.  These desk witnesses must be independently rederived after the
preregistration commit.

## Geometry witness

The Block22 support has maximum axial extent four.  Translating a successor
by nine lattice units leaves at least one unit between its axial extreme and
the old block.  Exact set enumeration gives no old/successor intersection at
`R=9`; the six differently directed successor supports are also pairwise
disjoint.  Rotations permute their centers and supports.  The union contains
224 sites and reaches axial radius thirteen.

## Covariant state-preparation witness

For normalized outcome direction `u_b`, the symmetric trace-free tensor
`Q_b=u_b u_b^T-I/3` has eigenvalues `2/3,-1/3,-1/3`, hence is invertible and
`Q_b n` is nonzero for every live direction.  It obeys
`Q_(g b)=g Q_b g^T`, so normalization gives exact Bloch-vector covariance.

For an axis `b=e_i`, `v_n(b)=n` on the parallel pair and `v_n(b)=-n` on the
four perpendicular live sites.  For a corner `c`, the `e_j` factor is the
normalized vector with zero `j` component and components `c_j c_k` on the
other axes.  These formulas are generated by `Q_b`; they are not an outcome
lookup table.

The all-radial-zero Blank blocks and the target family transform under the
same onsite spin action used by Block22.  The full old Record is necessary:
`f` chooses the successor block and Ready word, while `b` chooses the live
state.  Outcome `b` alone is not a spatial sufficient statistic.

## CP/TP and QND witness

The 84 old `C_(f,b)` projectors are mutually orthogonal.  Summing the separate
Kraus effects gives `sum A_(f,b)^dagger A_(f,b)=P_valid`; the orthogonal STOP
projector completes the identity.  Different `rho_b` need not be orthogonal.

Each commuting Record projector commutes with both controlled Kraus
operators, and direct Heisenberg expansion gives
`Lambda^dagger(C_(f,b))=C_(f,b)`.  This preserves the old classical Record
algebra and every Locked sector.  It does not preserve arbitrary
noncommuting pointer observables or promise an unchanged reduced state for a
coherent superposition of distinct Record sectors.

On a valid Locked-plus-Blank input, preparation changes exactly one successor
block.  On reapplication that block is not Blank and STOP is identity.  The
separate Kraus family dephases coherent superpositions of different Locked
labels; only the commuting physical Record algebra is claimed QND.

A coherent sum of all controlled partial isometries is not an equivalent
covariant implementation.  Under a fixed-axis quarter turn, fixed `+z` and
`-z` Record sectors acquire different target/Blank phases.  Separate Kraus
phases cancel branchwise in the CP map; one summed Kraus operator has no
single global phase.  This is a finite-use classical-Record channel, not a
recurring Blank supply or a full code-algebra isometry.

## Kernel witness

Substitution of the six `v_n(b)` vectors in the Block22 effect coefficients
gives six exact signed-entry values and positive row sums.  The axis and
corner formulas in GOAL.md normalize separately to one-half probability in
the axis class and one-half in the corner class.

Antipodal inputs prepare the same state, and signed axis effects occur in
equal pairs.  The resulting seven-ray quotient has block form

```text
A = (1/12) I_3 + (5/36) J_3,
B = (1/8) J_(3x4),
C = (1/6) J_(4x3),
D = (sqrt(2)/16) I_4 + (1/8-sqrt(2)/64) J_4.
```

Axis-sum-zero vectors have eigenvalue `1/12`; corner-sum-zero vectors have
eigenvalue `sqrt(2)/16`; class-constant vectors reduce to
`[[1/2,1/2],[1/2,1/2]]` with eigenvalues one and zero.  This avoids a dense
symbolic eigensolve.  Exact detailed balance gives weights `1/6` per axis ray
and `1/8` per corner ray.  Strict positivity implies primitivity and uniqueness.

That uniqueness is only for the seven internal outcome rays.  Retaining `f`
produces six closed front sectors; retaining the translated anchor produces
spatial drift.  Neither is a finite irreducible stationary Record process.

## Principal risks

1. Calling `b` alone sufficient while silently using `f` to choose a site.
2. Supplying a successor Ready word or winning ray outside the old Record.
3. Hiding the six-block Blank star or treating it as autonomously generated.
4. Calling commuting-Record QND identity on the complete pointer `M_2` algebra.
5. Using target-state nonorthogonality to invalidate a control-orthogonal
   partial isometry, or overlooking control orthogonality entirely.
6. Checking label covariance without rotating physical Blank/target states.
7. Inserting the fourteen-by-fourteen transition table instead of deriving it.
8. Running a dense symbolic eigensolver after the invariant subspaces are known.
9. Calling a two-event cylinder an arbitrary history or physical rate law.
10. Letting a preparation failure contaminate the passed Block22 writer or the
    separate mixed-front campaign.
11. Coherently summing branch Kraus operators whose rotation phases differ.
12. Calling the abstract reversible kernel a realized stationary process when
    the same all-six-Blank eligibility cannot directly append event three.
13. Refitting the frozen sign of `Q_b` after seeing the transition kernel.
14. Promoting seven-ray uniqueness to the 42-state front process or the
    append-only spatial history.
