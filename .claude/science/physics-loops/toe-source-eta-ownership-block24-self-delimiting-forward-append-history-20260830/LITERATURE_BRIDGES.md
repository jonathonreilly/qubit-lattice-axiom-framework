# Block24 literature bridges

No external literature result is load-bearing. The proof uses explicit finite
Kraus algebra, orthogonal product states, a finite stochastic kernel, and an
elementary induction of consistent cylinder probabilities.

Standard terms such as CPTP, QND, and projective consistency are descriptive;
the runner must check the displayed identities directly. Kolmogorov-extension
language is not needed for the awarded terminal, which concerns every finite
cylinder only.

A later physical-process campaign may benefit from interacting-particle-system
or quantum-cellular-automaton literature, but importing that material now would
not shorten the exact Block23-to-history bridge.
