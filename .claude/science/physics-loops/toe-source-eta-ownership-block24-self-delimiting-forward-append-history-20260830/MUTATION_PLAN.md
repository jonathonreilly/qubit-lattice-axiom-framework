# Block24 hostile mutation plan

Executed altered models must include at least the following families.

## Geometry and control

- displacement `9 -> 8`;
- forward `f -> -f`;
- host-selected fixed target;
- next-outcome-selected target;
- drop one source control and collide two source labels;
- activate or modify a lateral block;
- accept a nonblank/Locked forward target; and
- overwrite the current source pointer.

## Channel algebra

- omit `K_STOP`;
- use `I-alpha P_valid` with `alpha!=1`;
- drop one next-outcome branch;
- coherently sum separate Record branches;
- replace one prepared live factor by Blank;
- use the future outcome in preparation;
- perturb one positive-root sector;
- replace the physical contraction by a stored transition scalar; and
- transpose/conjugate the arbitrary-reference factor incorrectly.

## Recurrence and scope

- mark the old source eligible after its forward block is Locked;
- mark the translated new source ineligible despite a new forward Blank;
- reuse the same consumed Blank block;
- break one last-outcome prefix marginal;
- infer depth `n+1` without applying stochasticity to depth `n`;
- make backward or lateral content affect the anchored branch;
- allow two shared-target tips while claiming a global projector; and
- label the compound range-nine step as nearest-neighbor, physical time,
  source, gravity, substrate generation, audit retention, or TOE movement.

Coverage guards may check frozen hashes, AST shape, exact counts, and explicit
scope strings, but they must not be counted as altered-model rejections.
External controls must likewise be reported separately.
