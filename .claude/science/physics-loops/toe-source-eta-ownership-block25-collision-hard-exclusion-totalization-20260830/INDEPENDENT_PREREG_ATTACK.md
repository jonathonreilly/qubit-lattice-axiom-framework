the following shortcuts:

- calling geometric disjointness a quantum commutation proof;
- checking only the already-known exact shared-target pair;
- treating nonorthogonal radial product states as classical labels;
- choosing a coordinate-minimal, lexicographic, colored, or ordered winner;
- importing a clean arbitration bit or ancilla;
- applying two writers to one physical factor;
- omitting STOP/identity mass in a blocked sector;
- changing an old Record or charging no Blank for a success;
- proving only finite examples without an arbitrary-finite graph argument;
- changing behavior when the finite window is padded; or
- promoting a sharp-sector failure to all mixed-front arbitration.

The checker should stop Stage B entirely if Stage A finds a certified
noncommuting pair. Continuing to construct graph sectors after that witness
would hide the actual type failure.
