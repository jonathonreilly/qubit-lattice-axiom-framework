# Block25 mutation plan

The executed source must reject altered models which:

1. omit one constrained current-pointer factor;
2. omit one selected-Blank live or pointer factor;
3. compare block labels instead of physical site/factor maps;
4. treat every nonzero product overlap as commuting;
5. treat only exact shared targets as conflicts;
6. move one factor to a fake physical coordinate;
7. activate one vertex of a conflicting pair;
8. choose a lexicographic or coordinate-priority winner;
9. apply both writers on an overlapping output site;
10. overwrite or dephase an older classical Record beyond the declared sector
    read;
11. consume zero or two Blank blocks per successful append;
12. omit the STOP/identity completion;
13. use an outcome or target label unavailable to the runtime;
14. drop an arbitrary-reference factor;
15. test fewer than all proper cubic frames;
16. change an interior decision under legal window padding;
17. claim collision liveness from safe hard exclusion;
18. claim Blank production, rate, gravity, axiom change, audit, obligation, or
    TOE movement; or
19. widen one noncommuting witness into a general arbitration no-go.

Each mutation must alter the model or claim surface actually used by the
corresponding predicate. Count-only and stored-answer mutations do not qualify.
