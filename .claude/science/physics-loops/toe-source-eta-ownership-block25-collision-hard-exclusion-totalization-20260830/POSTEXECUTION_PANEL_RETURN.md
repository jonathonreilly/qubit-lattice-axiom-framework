## Sharpened route

The winner is not a supplied static coloring. It is an endogenous,
state-carried hard-core lease sector using the existing qubit alphabet.

1. Prove the **B0 certificate**: every finite locally certified lease set whose
   complete Block24 target/control/read-write footprints are pairwise disjoint
   supports the tensor-product Block24 channel, exact CPTP/QND/covariance,
   projective consistency, unique ownership, and one declared Blank debit per
   append.
2. Attack **B0 autonomy**: construct a finite-range covariant
   mark--claim--grant--append/renew-transfer law. Losing tips remain unchanged,
   ownership is physically readable, leases remain invariant, and every finite
   conflict component resolves without coordinate priority, supplied infinite
   tiling, external clock, hidden scheduler, or fresh oracle.
3. If positive, derive cadence and a locally conserved current carrying Blank,
   lease, waste, and any explicit collision-token flux.
4. Only then join that selected current to the action/connection/gravity stack
   and test Ward/Bianchi consistency.

The certificate alone is conditional multi-front progress. Endogenous lease
acquisition/renewal is the TOE-level step.

## Decisive falsifier and stop rule

Stop B0 at the smallest two-tip or three-lease certificate showing any of:

- accepted leases share a target, control, or dependency factor;
- symmetry-identical local data cannot encode a unique owner, producing double
  leasing or permanent deadlock;
- legal local update orders induce different channels; or
- CPTP, QND, or covariance requires an unrecorded global phase, priority,
  clock, or fresh status packet.

After the minimal pair, cycle, and projective-consistency tests, either prove
the invariant family/general channel or stop. Do not grow a fixture catalogue.
