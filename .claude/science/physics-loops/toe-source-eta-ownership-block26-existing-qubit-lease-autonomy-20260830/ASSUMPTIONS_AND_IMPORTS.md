The source may derive tensorization, covariance, QND action, resource debit,
the deterministic symmetry implication, and normalized physical append-channel
mixtures. It may not silently derive the lease list, collision weights, joint
formation rule, actual firing, rate, fairness, or indefinite renewal.

Counterfactuals:

- Removing pairwise carrier disjointness must break the direct tensor-product
  certificate on at least one Block25 overlap.
- Removing the exchanging symmetry must remove the deterministic equal-grant
  inference, even though a particular deterministic rule may still deadlock.
- Replacing the anticorrelated kinematic one-owner state by independent equal
  marginals must admit zero-owner and double-owner outputs, without implying
  either state is a physical Record law.
- Removing a selected channel's STOP completion must invalidate the claimed
  full-space CPTP mixture rather than being hidden by a valid-sector check.
