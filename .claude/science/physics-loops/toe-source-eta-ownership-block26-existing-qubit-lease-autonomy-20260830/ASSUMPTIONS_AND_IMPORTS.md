joint formation rule, actual firing, rate, fairness, or indefinite renewal.

  certificate on at least one Block25 overlap.

  inference, even though a particular deterministic rule may still deadlock.

  must admit zero-owner and double-owner outputs.

  recorded-owner preparation rather than being relabeled as free randomness.
