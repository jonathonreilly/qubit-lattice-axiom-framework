## Why the visible-cause campaign was demoted

The matrices

```text
Q_lambda = (1-lambda)/16 J_4 + lambda/4 I_4
```

have classical nonnegative ranks `1` and `4`.  That is a resource statement,
not a selection law.  The Record stack already contains `84` mutually
orthogonal Locked words, and no current premise says the physical realization
must be a prior visible classical Record or must minimize its alphabet.
Quantum/entangled, hidden-classical, direct-joint, alternate-carrier, and
autonomous-process routes remain live.

A further exact distinction matters.  For a sole visible classical mediator,
requiring every fixed-cause singleton response to remain uniform forces the
product table `q_0`.  But a visible cause changes the physical neighboring
condition, so that fiberwise screening requirement is an additional premise.
Requiring only the cause-forgotten singleton marginal lets both supplied
tables survive.  Block29 therefore does not bake either reading into its
primary theorem.

## Physical boundary and dispatch

This is one pair turn followed by zero, one, or two connected straight
appends.  It is not a second returned-pair use.  The immediate lateral outputs
cannot form the next returned pair, and reusing the original pair carrier
activates STOP because two target Blanks are now Locked.

Therefore:

- dual pass: package only a direct connected-cylinder corollary, then attack
  a covariant successor/handoff from visible outputs to a later returned pair
  and its autonomous eligibility/resource law;
- one-sided failure: presume implementation error until an independent exact
  `q`-dependent obstruction is isolated;
- common factor or marginal failure: route that named wall without coupling
  inference;
- growing-memory inference: forbidden at finite depth two; and
- timeout/incomplete: no science inference.

The gravity PR remains downstream.  Additive one-tip currents cannot see this
fork; a later gravity join needs a normalized conserved pair-sensitive source
and a lawful decoder.

## Axiom threshold

No Block29 result can force an axiom update.  That would require either two
full lattice-wide autonomous resource-complete models with identical physical
boundary data and different decoded odds, or an exhaustive impossibility
theorem across direct, classical, quantum, hidden, compiler, history, and
autonomous process classes, followed by N1--N8.  Even then the conclusion is
only that an additional premise is needed if unique prediction is required;
whether it belongs in an axiom, primitive, or downstream law is governance.
