## Operator target

The final runner must carry exact typed projectors through every contraction.
For each first-output configuration `alpha`, construct the literal
eight-pointer projector `R_alpha`.  At its two Record-selected future centers,
construct the full-Blank projector `B`, its exact complement `I-B`, and the
four tensor sectors `D_ij`.  The branch control is the physical tensor product

```text
C_alpha,ij = R_alpha D_ij.
```

The four sectors for fixed `alpha` must be pairwise orthogonal and sum to
`R_alpha`; controls with different `alpha` must be orthogonal by their actual
pointer-word overlaps.  Projector ranks must follow from the represented
local factors.  Operator meaning may not be carried only by strings, fresh
symbols, scalar coefficients, or bit labels.

Expand each imported Block24 append into a canonical block-action map.  When
two complete append carriers overlap, merge them to exactly one action per
physical block: identity/identity collapses to identity, physical/identity
collapses to the physical factor, and physical/physical is rejected.  The
merged map must reconstruct both imported append factor lists and must be the
object consumed by the contraction.  A same-cardinality carrier tuple or a
string-valued overlap resolution is insufficient.

For every future branch, contract the actual merged factors to an
operator-valued Gram

```text
L^dagger L = t C_alpha,ij,
```

where `t` is derived from the imported Block24 factors.  For every composed
branch `M=L K`, recontract the imported Block28 branch, require its Gram
control to equal its declared `Pi_s,t`, reconstruct the exact intermediate
eight-pointer output, prove `R_alpha K=K`, and retain the commuting future
resource sector.  The required fixed-sector identity is

```text
sum_future M^dagger M = F_lambda,s,t,alpha Pi_s,t D_ij,
```

as an equality of both the physical control and its derived coefficient.  A
comparison of two scalar copies of `F` is forbidden.

The active future sum must be reconstructed from the complete operator-valued
sector rows and reduce exactly to the explicit `P_out = sum_alpha R_alpha`.
The complement STOP must contain that same active-sum object and contract to
`I-P_out`; it may not be justified only by a fresh symbolic projector.

The arbitrary-reference check must tensor the actual future branch, STOP, and
composed-cylinder descriptors with an explicit identity factor and obtain the
corresponding matrix-unit delta through the same contraction path.  The
covariance check must transport and compare the composite controls, resource
sectors (including `D10 <-> D01` under side exchange), merged append maps,
active sum, STOP, and `M=L K` descriptor—not detached coordinates alone.
