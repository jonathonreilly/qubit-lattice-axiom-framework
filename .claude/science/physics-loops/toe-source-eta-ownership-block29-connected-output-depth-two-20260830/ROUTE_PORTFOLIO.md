# Block29 route portfolio

## Selected Stage 0

Representation-neutral connected output-to-input composition.  It is the
smallest nontrivial check that the Block28 Records really feed the existing
Block24 history machinery rather than ending at a detached probability table.

## Ranked next routes

1. **Physical successor/handoff, then autonomous state-driven reuse.**  On a
   dual pass, derive how the actual laterally exiting Records become a new
   returned-pair input.  Immediate pair reuse is ill-typed: the two exits do
   not form the next returned pair, and the original carrier STOPs after two
   targets become Locked.  The handoff must be a function of visible outputs
   and declared resources before eligibility, repeated invocation, renewal,
   or cadence can be tested.
2. **Targeted local compiler.**  Use only if this block finds one named
   `M_2(C)` factor or output-conditioned control failure.
3. **Extensive Record history.**  Use only after an arbitrary-depth theorem
   proves growing state or entropy demand; finite depth two is insufficient.
4. **Representation resource classification.**  Classical nonnegative rank,
   quantum memory, hidden memory, and visible-Record debit may be reported
   separately, but no current premise minimizes any of them.
5. **Selector/source and gravity.**  Defer until both candidates have a
   normalized autonomous process and a conserved pair-sensitive current.

## Rejected immediate route

A visible-classical four-state cause is not an existence prerequisite.  It
would impose orthogonality, visibility, copyability, conditional
independence, and causal order not supplied by the current axioms.  Existing
Record code capacity also makes a bare four-label fit low leverage.

Two fresh disjoint pair carriers are also rejected as the primary route: their
tensor-product cylinder is automatic and does not connect an output to a
future input.
