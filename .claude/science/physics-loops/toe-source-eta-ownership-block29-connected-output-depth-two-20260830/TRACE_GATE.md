pair, derive or select `q`, identify a physical cause, prove autonomous
invocation or arbitrary-depth reuse, renew Blank resources, supply a
scheduler or cadence/rate, compile a nearest-neighbor law, attach gravity,
amend an axiom, set an audit verdict, retire an obligation, or move a TOE
percentage.

  covariant physical successor/handoff campaign;
- exactly one fails: independently isolate an exact `q`-dependent comb
  obstruction before stating any scoped selector;

  and make no coupling inference; and
- incomplete or timeout: no science inference.
