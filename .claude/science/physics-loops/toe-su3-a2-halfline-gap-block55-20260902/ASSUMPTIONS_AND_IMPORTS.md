# Assumptions And Imports

Allowed repository surfaces:

- the exact `SU(3)` character recurrence for `J`;
- the exact Wilson coefficient identity and positivity for `beta >= 0`;
- the existing geometric saddle profile and half-slice contraction lemmas;
- the scalar Bessel local-CLT lemma, subject to direct verification of every
  bound used here;
- elementary finite Weyl-group algebra, Fourier inversion, Taylor remainder
  estimates, compact self-adjoint spectral theory, and Perron-Jentzsch.

Not allowed as proof inputs:

- sampled W85 tables or fitted asymptotic constants;
- the numerical value `0.1938058` as a limiting eigenvalue ratio;
- a finite packet substituted for the infinite operator;
- the finite-grid eigenvector-tail claim as a uniform theorem;
- physical `beta=6`, an action-selection premise, continuum Yang-Mills, or an
  infinite-volume four-dimensional mass-gap interpretation;
- a new axiom, primitive, or audit verdict.

The exact Wilson action is conditional input to this pure-gauge theorem.  Block
54's separate action/normalization decision remains open and is not smuggled
into this block.
