# Goal

Close the native discrete `SU(3)` half-line transfer-gap parent, or retire the
highest-leverage route with an exact named residual.  The primary attack rewrites
the Wilson character coefficients as the killed continuous-time six-neighbor walk
in the shifted `A_2` Weyl chamber and uses the finite Weyl reflection formula,
rather than another finite Bessel-determinant sweep.

The campaign is pure gauge.  It does not touch fermions, gravity, general
Born/Record semantics, or another worker's open branch.

## Completion witness

At least one of the following must be proved at theorem grade:

1. an explicit uniform Wilson-to-saddle estimate on a growing
   `p,q = O(sqrt(beta))` window, with a proof-side constant and true tail strong
   enough to complete the registered W85/operator-remainder route; or
2. a legitimate bypass: pointwise Perron gap for every finite `beta`, plus a
   common-space compact convergence theorem to a positivity-improving limiting
   operator, sufficient to prove one uniform half-line gap without fitting a
   threshold or a limiting eigenvalue.

An exact reflection identity alone is an intermediate lemma, not completion.
Finite grids, extrapolated ratios, or an asymptotic formula without a uniform
tail do not close the target.
