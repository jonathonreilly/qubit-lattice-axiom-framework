# Negative-claim applicability

This source proposes a bounded positive theorem. It does not assert a no-go,
a universal failed-extension result, or impossibility of physical realization.
The explicit matrix examples witness failure of a particular algebraic
identity after changing its hypotheses. Failed estimates identify the proof
steps replaced by the successful accepted argument.

No N1--N8 packet PASS is claimed or synthesized. The primary identity tool's
five resolution lines report scientific nonexecution only. If a later claim
asserts a broader negative result, it requires its own evidence and review.
