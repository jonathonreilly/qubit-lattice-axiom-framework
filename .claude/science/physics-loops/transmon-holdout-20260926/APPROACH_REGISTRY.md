# Mathematical approaches
Fourier/unitary operator reduction: proves the conditional zero-offset square/rotor identity, including the scalar shift. Numerical spectral construction: two basis constructions agree on fixed-parameter device predictions; this is floating evidence rather than an interval theorem. Local inverse calibration: three starts find one common root; global root uniqueness remains open. Finite sensitivity sampling: sixteen corner refits and a charge grid; no continuum enclosure. Underexplored: rigorous global inversion/error bounds and a derived finite-microscopic correction with coefficients fixed independently of this spectrum.
