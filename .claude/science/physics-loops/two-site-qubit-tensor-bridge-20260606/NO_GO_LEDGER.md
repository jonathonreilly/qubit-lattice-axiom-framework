## Locality Alone Does Not Force Tensor Composition

Authority:
`TENSOR_COMPOSITION_REQUIRES_LOCAL_TOMOGRAPHY_BEYOND_LOCALITY_NARROW_NO_GO_NOTE_2026-06-03.md`

Content:
faithful commuting local `M_2(C)` copies/no-signalling do not force the
ordinary generated `M_2(C) tensor_C M_2(C) ~= M_4(C)` composite. The duplicate
sector `M_4(C) oplus M_4(C)` is the exact counterexample.

Effect on this PR:
the bridge explicitly uses retained finite-block tensor-product authority
rather than deriving tensor composition from locality alone.
