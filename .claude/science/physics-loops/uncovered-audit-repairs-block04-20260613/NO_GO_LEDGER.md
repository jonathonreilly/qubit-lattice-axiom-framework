# No-Go Ledger

## Signed-Gravity Arbitrary Cutoff Label

Rejected. The nonzero T3 eta label is not true for arbitrary spectral cutoffs.
For `Lambda=m+r` and `0<a<1/2`, the exact formula is
`eta=1_{r>=a}-1_{r>=1-a}`. Ordinary cutoffs with `r<a` or `r>=1-a` can give
`eta=0`.
