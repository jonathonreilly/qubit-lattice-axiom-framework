# Literature Bridges

No literature search or literature theorem is load-bearing in this block.
The finite determinant identity, chain rule, log-determinant Hessian, and
linearized Einstein symbol are written explicitly and checked independently
by the runner.

External literature could provide context for induced gravity, the conformal
factor, York projection, or Regge gauge modes, but importing that context would
not strengthen the exact theorem proved here and is therefore omitted.
