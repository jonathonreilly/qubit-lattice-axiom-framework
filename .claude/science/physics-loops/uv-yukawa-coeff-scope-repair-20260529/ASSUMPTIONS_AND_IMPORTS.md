# Assumptions And Imports

## Load-Bearing Inputs

- Finite SU(3) generator algebra.
- Finite one-link Haar contraction identity.
- Finite Dirac gamma/Fierz algebra.
- Retained bounded representation dimensions `N_c=3`, `N_iso=2`.

## Removed From Load-Bearing Surface

- Canonical plaquette/`u_0` expansion-domain authority.
- Perturbative convergence proof.
- Strong-coupling non-convergence proof.
- `g_bare` closure, staggered-Dirac realization, and shared tadpole transport.
- Physical top-Yukawa readout and precision claims.

coefficient governs the physical framework surface.
