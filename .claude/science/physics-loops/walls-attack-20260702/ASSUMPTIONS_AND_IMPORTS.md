### Boundary Exposed

Every length-`k` word has closed form `2^e r^(2^k)`, where `e` is the binary
integer encoded by the positions of `f`. Hence the exact dial set is
`{0} union {2^(-e/(2^k - 1)) : e = 0,...,2^k - 1}`. These positive dial
points densify in `[1/2,1]` but never enter `(0,1/2)`. The residual is
therefore the word-supplier question: what physical structure supplies or
bounds the composition word?
