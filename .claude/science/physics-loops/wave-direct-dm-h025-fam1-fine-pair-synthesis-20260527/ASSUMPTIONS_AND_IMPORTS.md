# Assumptions And Imports

## In Scope

- Fam1 seed0 and seed1 H=0.25 control logs.
- Retained Fam1 seed0 and seed1 control notes.

## Out Of Scope

- Coarse high/low band provenance.
- Coarse-to-fine seed-ordering reversal.
- Uneven late-gain-compression mechanism.
- Portability, family-wide, third-seed, or weaker-strength claims.
