authority, selector, or observable map is added.  The carrier below is only
the explicit finite numerical object under test.

## Forbidden promotions

- no conversion of cell/layer schedule rate into a calibrated laboratory speed;
- no claim that the finite wave field is physical gravity;
- no inheritance of Lane 4–8b results or the old single-point magnitude;
- no continuum, universal, mechanism, or unique scaling inference;
- no audit status or ledger edit from this source branch.

The exact parameter identity `D=range/schedule_rate` and
`measurement=onset+D+buffer` is ordinary algebra.  It proves only the timing
coupling, not a physical no-go.
