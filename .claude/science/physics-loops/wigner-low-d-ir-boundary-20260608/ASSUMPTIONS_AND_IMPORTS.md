# Assumptions And Imports

Load-bearing:

- finite-dimensional Hermitian charge-block Hamiltonian generated by the runner;
- Gibbs-state construction from that finite Hamiltonian;
- nearest-neighbor lattice dispersion formula used directly by the runner.

Open, non-load-bearing:

- finite-temperature no-SSB theorem for `d <= 2`;
- Ward/order-parameter normalization;
- physical Noether current theorem beyond finite charge commutation.

No new axiom is introduced.
