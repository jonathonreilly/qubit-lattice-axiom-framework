# Assumptions And Imports

Load-bearing hypotheses in the new theorem:

- supplied standard Wilson plaquette action;
- canonical generator trace normalization `Tr(T_a T_b)=delta_ab/2`;
- standard small-`a` plaquette expansion;
- continuum Yang-Mills coefficient convention.

Not assumed as derived:

- Wilson action-surface selection from the three axioms;
- `g_bare=1`;
- physical `beta=6`;
- exclusion of improved or alternative action surfaces.

The theorem computes only the coefficient relation forced inside that supplied
surface.
