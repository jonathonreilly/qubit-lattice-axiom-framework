BG's continuum interpolation/extension/averaging sentence does not specify an extension that reproduces the original lattice site values: taking cell means of an interpolant generally changes those values. This is a gap, not a demonstrated growth of the optimal constant.

   The rest of the reduction does not hide another power of L if this input is supplied. Write x=||D_U omega||, y=||H_s|| and p=||P_N phi||. Repair, **unproved**: construct an exact discrete extension on each admissible fan, with both norms and all mean corrections bounded independently of L and fine depth, then glue with bounded overlap.
