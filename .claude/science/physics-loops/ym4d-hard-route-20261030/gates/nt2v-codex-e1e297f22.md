## Defects, ranked by what they break

A covariance limit alone does not justify clipping. For an elementary obstruction, let X=epsilon^(-1/2) with probability epsilon and 0 otherwise, and put the same X at a pair. Then Cov(X,X)=1-epsilon, whereas Cov(min(X,b),min(X,b))=b² epsilon(1-epsilon) for every fixed b once epsilon<b^(-2). Taking epsilon=g⁴ even respects X<=2/g² for a scaled bounded energy. The unbounded covariance survives and every fixed clipped covariance vanishes. This is a counterexample to the missing inference, not a Wilson counterexample. **Repair [unproved for either producer]:** include (GS_j)(c) at W=1 with its stated uniform constants, or directly require a uniform integrability bound for the products X_p X_q and the means. Then F-3, F-2 and the two FT values do give a strict bounded-observable difference. It is one scalar *difference*, obtained from two pair values, together with tail/error estimates—not just one unconstrained covariance number.

2. **The proof of FT silently needs uniform moments of the flat chart coordinates.** Exact lines: `library/nt2-flat-fibre-criterion.md:60–62`; compare the actual inputs `parts/upper-chain.md:628–631,885`.
