**F1. Lines 153, 188 (I6 and the constants claim). Repairable.** I6 says that A1's constants and admissibility floors are "uniform above their stated geometric floors". `a1-balaban.md` §8 does not state this in those words. Its §6 (lines 1264–1284) and its order list (lines 1341–1348) fix \(a_5\) after the "localization length \(R_1M_1\)", with \(C\) enlarged to include \(R_1M_1\). Hence \(\varepsilon_A\) depends on \(L\) alone only if that length is auxiliary and fixed at its floor. SA 9916 supports this reading: for Theorem 1 the length "may be fixed at its least admissible value". So does a1-balaban line 1267: \(S_*\) is uniform over every gap above its minimum. Repair: state this reading in I6 and cite those lines. Otherwise, say that \(\varepsilon_P\) may depend on that one fixed length. Theorem P82's conclusion does not change. \(C^\circ\) and the rates do not involve A1. The consumer allows \(\bar\varepsilon\) to depend on \(\mathfrak M\) (SA 9734, 9916).
