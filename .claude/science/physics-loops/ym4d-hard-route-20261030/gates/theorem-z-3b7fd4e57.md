## Defects

**D1. The constants of the annuli are contradictory, and the failure is a per-level factor. Severity: fatal to the proof as written; repair exists (below).**
`library/quiet-boundary-rarity.md:19` (iii), `:27`, `:79`. Claims: thickness t_0 := 2 W_*/L + 2 r_S; W_* := C R/T^2; a chain crosses an annulus in at least W_*/R hops, and this beats the surface count L^{3 (l-j)}. Why it fails:
- A closed layer of thickness t fits between two rough cells only if they are more than about t apart. So duality gives chains at hop range R >= t_0. Hops are then W_*/R <= W_*/t_0 < L/2, for any W_*. (Read t_0 in level-(l+1) cells, as line 19 literally says, and it is worse: the layer is thicker than the annulus, 2 W_* + 2 L r_S level-l cells against W_*.)
- W_* = C R/T^2 says hops = C/T^2 = C L^4/a_*^2. With c_0 proportional to log L, what the count needs is hops >= 3 log L/(c c_0 T^2), about 8.1 L^4/(c a_*^2), free of log L. Against L/2 available, the shortfall is of order L^3/(c a_*^2). c is at most (1/16)/C_T = 1/512.
- This is not a matter of choosing constants. A level-j layer must lie in the shadow of a level-(j+1) layer, by the Markov property itself, so the room for it is L theta_{j+1} level-j cells, and hops <= L theta_{j+1}/theta_j. To have hops of order L^4/a_*^2 one needs theta_{j+1}/theta_j of order L^3/a_*^2 at each level: **a factor per level, compounding in the thickness of the top layer.** With theta constant, L^{3 m} exp(-c (x_l + c_0 m) T^2 L/2) is unbounded in m = l - j once m > m_0, with m_0 proportional to x_l T^2 L/(3 log L). Since m runs up to l and x_l stays near x_* at coarse levels, no x_*(L) fixes this uniformly in the number of levels. Numbers in N4.
- Consequence: at low levels, crossing chains of a fixed shell exist somewhere on the surface with probability near one. So the sum over B in (M) carries exp(sum_j S_j eps_j), which is the surface excess the stop from outside was introduced to avoid (line 61, hostile point (12)). Also "at most 2" at line 79 is wrong in form even at one level: the sum over B is a product over surface elements, exp(eps |boundary tube|). That part is harmless, as it is absorbed in 𝔈 |tube|.

**D4. The class path lemma is false: topological sectors. Severity: high, repair not obvious.**
