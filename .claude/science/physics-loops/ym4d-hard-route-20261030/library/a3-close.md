## 1. Premise (1): the critical reference U_0 lies in Reg

**Statement.** Let \(\mathbf B\) be a separated sequence (SA 9593) with gap \(\rho\ge\rho_0'\), \(\mathbf V_0\) a real \(\varepsilon_1\)-regular datum, and \(U_0=U^\circ(\mathbf V_0)\) the axial representative of A1 (`a1-balaban.md` (8.2)). Let \(M,c_{\rm reg}\) be the constants of `p82-curved.md` Theorem P and \(W:=R_1M_1\) the walk length of Balaban's propagator theorems used in the gauge theorem of `a1-balaban.md` §3.8. Put \(M'=c_{\rm reg}M+2W\). There are \(C_L\) and \(a_L>0\), depending on \(L\) only, such that if \(\rho_0'\ge 4LW+2L\) and \(C_LM'\varepsilon_1\le a_L\), then \(U_0\) satisfies (Reg\(_\alpha\)) of `p82-curved.md` §1 with
\[
\alpha=C_LM'\varepsilon_1 .
\]
So [Reg at \(U_0\)] holds with \(\alpha\le\alpha_*\) as soon as \(\varepsilon_1\le\alpha_*/(C_LM')\), which is the consumer's ceiling of `p82-curved.md` (a radius chosen after the lengths).

**Counterexample attempt.** Reg asks for a derivative bound on a gauge potential, and curvature alone does not give one: on a cube of side \(m\xi\) at mesh \(\eta\), a field with \(|\partial U-1|\le\varepsilon\eta^2\xi^{-2}\) can have, in every gauge, transverse differences of size \(\varepsilon\xi^{-2}\log(\xi/\eta)\) (the \(L^\infty\) failure of the Calderon-Zygmund bound; this is `p82-curved.md` test 2.4). So a counterexample at \(U_0\) would have to violate the current bound at some bond. The only places where criticality does not control the current directly are the layer-0 bonds (fixed by the datum), the free inward bonds and the bonds next to \(\partial\Omega_0\). At layer 0 the scale is \(\eta\), and the current is at most \(2(d-1)\) plaquette differences over \(\eta^3\), so it is controlled by curvature (`a1-balaban.md` §8, base case). At the others, (8.2) bounds \(J(U_0)(b)\) at every bond, since the reset (6.6) bounds \(r(U)\), a supremum over all bonds. No counterexample.

**Proof.** Four steps.

*(i) Balaban's class.* `a1-balaban.md` (8.2) (gated ADVANCE conditional on F0, `gates/a1-balaban-gate.md` §§4, 8; F0 is `flat-port.md`, gated in row 99) gives at every plaquette and bond of \(\Omega_0\)
\[
|\partial U_0(p)-1|\le B\varepsilon_1\eta^2\mathsf L(p)^{-2},\qquad |J(U_0)(b)|\le B\varepsilon_1\mathsf L(b)^{-3}.
\]
Balaban's class \(\mathcal U_k(\{\Omega_j\},\alpha_0)\) (CMP 99a (1.7)-(1.9), read on the scan of p. 77) is: \(|U(\partial p)-1|<\alpha_0L^{-2j}\) for \(p\in\Omega_j\), and \(|(D^{\eta*}_U\partial U)(b)|<\alpha_0L^{-2j}(L^j\eta)^{-1}\) for \(b\in\Omega_j\), where \(p\in\Omega_j\) means that some corner lies in \(\Omega_j\). In level-\(k\) units \(L^j\eta=\xi_j\) and \(L^{-2j}=\eta^2\xi_j^{-2}\). A corner in \(\Omega_j\) has scale at least \(\xi_j\), so \(\mathsf L(p)\ge\xi_j\) and \(\mathsf L(b)\ge\xi_j\). Hence the curvature half of (8.2) gives (1.7) with \(\alpha_0=B\varepsilon_1\). For (1.9): Balaban's \(\eta^{-2}D^*_U\partial U\) is a matrix whose anti-Hermitian part is \(J(U)\) at every bond whose six plaquettes lie in \(\Omega_0\); its Hermitian part is bounded by \(C\varepsilon^2\mathsf L^{-3}\) through \(\mathrm{Re}\,W-1=-\frac12(W-1)^*(W-1)\) for unitary \(W\) (`a1-balaban.md` §2, checked in its gate). So (1.9) holds at those bonds with \(\alpha_0=C(B\varepsilon_1+B^2\varepsilon_1^2)\le 2CB\varepsilon_1\). Below, only bonds with all six plaquettes in \(\Omega_0\) are read.

*(ii) Geometry for \(j\ge1\).* Let \(Z\subset\Omega_0\cap(\mathcal L_j\cup\mathcal L_{j+1})\) be a cube of side \(c_{\rm reg}M\xi_j\), \(j\ge1\). Let \(\square\) be the union of the cubes of side \(W\xi_j\), aligned with the level-\(j\) blocks, that meet \(Z\). It is a cube of side \(M''\xi_j\le M'\xi_j\), \(M''\) a multiple of \(W\). Balaban's enlargement \(\tilde\square\) (CMP 99a p. 98: the cover of the outermost nested cube, whose boundary lies at distance \(2W\xi_j\) from \(\square\)) satisfies \(\tilde\square\subset Z+4W\xi_j\) (\(\ell^\infty\) neighbourhood). Since \(Z\subset\Omega_j\) and \(\operatorname{dist}_\infty(\Omega_{j-1}^c,\Omega_j)>\rho\xi_{j-1}=\rho\xi_j/L\), the condition \(\rho>4LW+2L\) gives \(\rho\xi_j/L>4W\xi_j+2\xi_j\), so \(\tilde\square\subset\Omega_{j-1}\), at distance more than \(2\xi_j\ge2\eta\) from \(\Omega_{j-1}^c\). Thus every plaquette and every bond, with its six plaquettes, meeting \(\tilde\square\) lies in \(\Omega_0\).

*(iii) Proposition 6 at level \(j\).* Balaban's proof of CMP 99a Proposition 6 (pp. 98-99) first drops the members \(\Omega_{j'}\), \(j'>j\): the class conditions at a coarser scale are stronger, so \(U_0\) lies in the class of the truncated sequence \(\Omega_0\supset\dots\supset\Omega_j\) with the same \(\alpha_0\). After rescaling \(\xi_j=1\), the only use of the hypotheses is (1.7)-(1.9) on \(\tilde\square\subset\Omega_{j-1}\), at scale \(\xi_{j-1}\), which costs \(L^2\) (curvature) and \(L^3\) (current); this is his (1.128) and (1.132). The membership \(\square\subset\Omega_j\), \(\square\not\subset\Omega_{j+1}\) is used only to place \(\tilde\square\) in \(\Omega_{j-1}\), which (ii) supplies directly. Its port is `a1-balaban.md` §3.8, last paragraph ("Proposition 6's local gauge needs no critical background"; reference 1, flat inverses only), gated ADVANCE conditional on F0. Under \(7dL^2M''\alpha_0\le c_1\) it gives a gauge \(u\) on \(\tilde\square\) with \(U_0^{u^{-1}}=e^{i\eta A}\) and, on \(\square\supset Z\), in level-\(j\) units (his (1.134), (1.136)),
\[
\xi_j|A|+\xi_j^2|\nabla A|<14dL^2B_1M''\alpha_0\le 28\,C\,dL^2B_1BM'\varepsilon_1,
\]
with \(\nabla\) the ordinary difference. This is (Reg\(_\alpha\)) on \(Z\).

*(iv) \(j=0\), no current needed.* Let \(Z\subset\Omega_0\cap(\mathcal L_0\cup\mathcal L_1)\) have side \(n\eta\), \(n=c_{\rm reg}M\). In complete axial gauge from a corner of \(Z\) (\(U_1\equiv1\); \(U_2=1\) on \(x_1=0\); \(U_3=1\) on \(x_1=x_2=0\); \(U_4=1\) on \(x_1=x_2=x_3=0\)), the link \(U_\mu(x)\) is the holonomy of a loop bounding a staircase of at most \(3n\) plaquettes of \(Z\). By unitarity the holonomy is within the sum of their deviations of \(1\): \(|U(b)-1|\le 3nB\varepsilon_1\), since \(\mathsf L(p)\ge\eta\). For \(U=e^{i\eta A}\) with eigenvalue angles \(\pm\theta\), \(|\theta|\le\pi\), one has \(|U-1|=2|\sin(\theta/2)|\ge(2/\pi)|\theta|\), so \(\eta|A|\le\frac{3\pi}{2}nB\varepsilon_1\) and \(\eta^2|\nabla A|\le2\sup\eta|A|\le3\pi nB\varepsilon_1\). This is (Reg\(_\alpha\)) at \(j=0\) with \(\alpha=5\pi c_{\rm reg}MB\varepsilon_1\).

Taking \(C_L\) the larger of the two constants proves the statement. \(\square\)

**Order of constants.** \(L\); flat constants (F0); \(W\); \(M,c_{\rm reg},\alpha_*\) (`p82-curved.md`); \(\rho_0'=\max\{\rho_0,4LW+2L\}\), so the gap is fixed after \(M\) and \(W\), as in the outline's order (SA 9916) and `gates/a1-balaban-gate.md` D2; then \(B\) (A1); then \(\varepsilon_1\le\min\{\alpha_*,a_L\}/(C_LM')\). Depth, mesh, volume and region shape enter nowhere. Torus floors: \(\tilde\square\) has side \(O(M'+W)\xi_j\), within the outline's second floor (SA (1b)(ii)).

**Label.** Premise (1) is **discharged as an implication**: [Reg at \(U_0\)] follows from A1 (8.2) and the Proposition 6 port, both gated conditional on F0, and F0 (`flat-port.md`, gated row 99). The two-layer condition \(Z\subset\mathcal L_j\cup\mathcal L_{j+1}\) is not needed for existence; it only fixes the unit \(\xi_j\). What this does not cover: complex references, the independent-current clause of SA 10133-10137, and Balaban's Laplacian and Hölder members; P82° at real critical references reads none of them.

**Counterexample attempts.** (a) *Reference margin at the boundary of a tower.* The restricted configuration has a different current at the new action boundary (`gates/a3-derivative-gate.md` §10, Lemma 10.2 repair, \(|J_W|\le C\varepsilon_1\eta^{-3}\)). The tower clauses are imposed only on \(Z\) with two layers of \(M\)-cubes removed (SA 9646), so this bond set is excluded. No failure. (b) *Reference margin at internal seams of a tower.* The current is a property of the configuration, not of the constraint, so at a bond whose six plaquettes lie in \(Z\) the tower configuration's current equals the ambient one whenever the tower configuration is the restriction (Lemma R below). No failure. (c) *Layered domains.* When \(X\) meets a seam of the ambient sequence, a tower of \(Z\subset X\) can be coarser than the ambient sequence on part of \(Z\); then the tower configuration is not a restriction, and its datum's tests carry factors up to \(L^{2(q-j_{\rm amb})}\). This is why SA 6(d) imposes wider gaps and layered radii in that case. Not a counterexample, but Lemma R does not reach it. (d) *Nonconvexity.* The outline itself notes (SA 12371-12376) that endpoint admission does not admit the segment between two removed substitutions; (BGR32) along one analytic curve avoids this. Not a counterexample to (10.2), which is stated along the curve.

**Lemma R (reference admission of the real critical background, single-layer domains).** Let \(U_0\) be the critical background of a separated sequence, regular at radius \(B\varepsilon_1\) by (8.2), with \(\rho\ge\rho_0'\) as in Section 1. Let \(X\) be a region with \(X\subset\Omega_j\). Then for every \(\alpha_0\ge C_R\varepsilon_1\) and every \(\alpha_1>0\), the real triple \((U_0,0,J(U_0))|_X\) satisfies every clause of the core \(\mathcal U^\circ_j(X;\alpha_0,\alpha_1)\) (SA 9646) with margin \(\tfrac12\): each defining quantity is at most \(\tfrac12\) of its bound. Here \(C_R\) depends on \(L\) only; the constant \(C\) of the local-gauge clause is taken \(\ge C_LL^3\) (it depends on \(L\) only, as the outline's constants do), and \(M\varepsilon_1\) is small.

*Proof.* Work in level-\(j\) units: bare spacing \(\eta_j=L^{-j}\), level-\(j\) scale \(1\). Conversion from level-\(k\) units: a length \(\ell\) becomes \(\ell L^{k-j}\), so a current (dimension length\(^{-3}\)) is multiplied by \(L^{3(j-k)}\).

*Simple conditions.* On \(X\subset\Omega_j\) every corner scale is at least \(\xi_j\). By (8.2), \(|\partial U_0-1|\le B\varepsilon_1(\eta/\xi_j)^2=B\varepsilon_1\eta_j^2\), and \(|J(U_0)|\le B\varepsilon_1\xi_j^{-3}\), which is \(B\varepsilon_1\) in level-\(j\) units. \(A'=0\). For a cube of side \(O(LM)\) in \(X\), Section 1 steps (ii)-(iii) at level \(j\ge1\) (the enlargement lies in \(\Omega_{j-1}\) because the cube lies in \(\Omega_j\)), or step (iv) if \(j=0\), give a real gauge with \(|A|,|\nabla A|\le C_LL^3MB\varepsilon_1\) in level-\(j\) units. With \(\alpha_0\ge4B\varepsilon_1\) and \(C\ge C_LL^3\), each simple clause holds with margin \(\frac12\): for instance \(C_LL^3MB\varepsilon_1\le\frac14CM\alpha_0\).

*Tower clauses.* Fix \(Z\subset X\) of level \(p\le j\), a rule, and \(n\le p\); let \(\mathbf B'\) be the tower of \(Z\) cut at depth \(n\), with members \(T'_q\), \(q\le n\). The tower is separated (gap \(\rho\), first rule; \(M_1'\), second rule; SA 9646 and Theorem 6(a)). Since \(T'_q\subset Z\subset X\subset\Omega_j\subset\Omega_q\) for \(q\le n\le j\), the restriction identity (9.3) of `a1-balaban.md` §9.2 (gated ADVANCE, `gates/a1-balaban-gate.md` §6) applies: \(U_{\mathbf B'}(M_{\mathbf B'}(U_0|_{T'_0}))=U_0|_{T'_0}\) modulo \(\mathcal G^{\rm res}(\mathbf B')\). For \(A'=0\) the tower configuration is \(\Phi_{M_{\mathbf B'}(U_0)}(0)\), that background (SA 9646). So \(U_n(Z;U_0)\) is gauge equivalent to \(U_0|_Z\). On \(Z\) with two layers of \(M\)-cubes removed, every bond has its six plaquettes in \(Z\), so the tower configuration's current there is the ambient current \(J(U_0)\). Both bounds are gauge invariant. Now compare with the clause:
- curvature: \(B\varepsilon_1L^{-2j}\) against \(\alpha_0L^{-(j-p)}\eta_p^2=\alpha_0L^{-j-p}\); the ratio is \((B\varepsilon_1/\alpha_0)L^{p-j}\le\frac12\) for \(\alpha_0\ge2B\varepsilon_1\);
- current, in level-\(n\) units: \(B\varepsilon_1L^{3(n-j)}\) against \(\alpha_0L^{-(j-p)}L^{2(n-p)}=\alpha_0L^{2n-j-p}\); the ratio is \((B\varepsilon_1/\alpha_0)L^{n+p-2j}\le\frac12\).

Existence of every tower configuration is the existence half of A1 for \(\mathbf B'\), whose datum \(M_{\mathbf B'}(U_0)\) is \(CL^2B\varepsilon_1\)-regular by the converse in Theorem 1. The same holds with \(U\) in place of \(\mathbf U\), since they coincide. Take \(C_R=\max\{4B,CL^2B\}\). \(\square\)

**Lemma T (SA Theorem 5(a): tower clauses from the simple conditions).** Let \(Z\) be a region of level \(p\) (a union of aligned level-\(p\) cubes), and work in level-\(p\) units (\(\eta_p=L^{-p}\), \(\xi'_q=L^{q-p}\)). Let \(U\) be real, \(\mathbf U=e^{i\eta_pA'}U\), with, on \(Z\),
\[
|\partial U-1|,\ |\partial\mathbf U-1|<\alpha'_0\eta_p^2,\qquad |A'|,\ |\nabla_UA'|<\alpha'_1,\qquad \alpha'_0+\alpha'_1\le c_T/2 .
\]
For \(n\le p\) and either rule, let \(\mathbf B'\) be the tower of \(Z\) cut at depth \(n\), and \(Z^\circ=\{x\in Z:\operatorname{dist}_\infty(x,Z^c)>e_n+2\xi'_n\}\) (\(e_n\) as in Lemma J (a); \(Z^\circ\) contains \(Z\) minus two layers of cubes of side \(\ge(e_n+2\xi'_n)/2\), e.g. the \(M\)-cubes of \(\pi_{p-1}\) once \(M\ge L(\rho+2)\), resp. \(M\ge L(3M_1'+2)\)). Then:
(a) \(w\mapsto U_n(Z;e^{i\eta_pw}U)\) is defined and holomorphic on \(\{\sup|w|<c_TL^{p-n}\}\);
(b) on \(Z^\circ\), \(|\partial U_n(Z;\mathbf U)-1|\le K^\sharp(\alpha'_0+\alpha_1'^2)\eta_p^2\), and in level-\(n\) units \(|J(U_n(Z;\mathbf U))|\le K^\sharp(\alpha'_0+\alpha_1'^2)L^{2(n-p)}\); the same holds with \(U\) in place of \(\mathbf U\), with \(K^\sharp\alpha'_0\).
Here \(c_T,K^\sharp\) depend on \(L\) (and on \(\rho\) through A1's constants), not on \(p,n,Z\) or the depth.

*Proof.* *Step 1 (real fields).* Let \(U'\) be real with \(|\partial U'-1|\le a\eta_p^2\) on \(Z\), \(a\le c_T\). On the tower layer of level \(q\) this is radius \(a(\xi'_q)^2=aL^{-2(p-q)}\le aL^{-2(p-n)}\) in the tower's local units. The plaquettes-of-averages recursion (`a1-balaban.md` §3.5 and §7.6 for the actual mean, gated) and the converse of Theorem 1 (used in `a1-balaban.md` §9.2) make the datum \(M_{\mathbf B'}(U')\) \(\varepsilon\)-regular with \(\varepsilon=C_1L^2aL^{-2(p-n)}\). The tower is separated by construction (gaps \(>\rho\xi'_q\), first rule; \(\ge LM_1'\xi'_q>\rho\xi'_q\), second rule; members unions of aligned cubes of side \(\mathfrak M\xi'_q\), resp. \(M_1'\xi'_q\), a multiple of \(\mathfrak M\xi'_q\)), and A1 places no condition on the shape of \(T'_0=Z\). A1 (8.2) then gives the real background \(U^\circ(M_{\mathbf B'}(U'))\) regular at radius \(B\varepsilon\) in the tower's local units. On \(Z^\circ\subset T'_n\) (Lemma J (a)) the local scale is \(\xi'_n\), so there \(|\partial U^\circ-1|\le B\varepsilon(\eta_p/\xi'_n)^2=BC_1L^2a\,\eta_p^2\) and, in level-\(n\) units, \(|J|\le B\varepsilon=BC_1L^2aL^{2(n-p)}\); bonds of \(Z^\circ\) have their six plaquettes in \(Z\).

*Step 2 (curvature of the real and imaginary fields).* For a plaquette with links \(U_i\) and \(a_i=A'(b_i)\), moving the links to the right gives \(\partial\mathbf U=\exp\big(i\eta_p[a_1+\operatorname{Ad}(U_1)a_2+\operatorname{Ad}(U_1U_2)a_3+\operatorname{Ad}(U_1U_2U_3)a_4]+O(\eta_p^2|A'|^2)\big)\partial U\). Since \(\operatorname{Ad}(U_1U_2U_3)=\operatorname{Ad}(\partial U)\operatorname{Ad}(U_4^{-1})\), the bracket is \(\eta_p(D_UA')_p+O(|A'||\partial U-1|)\), with \(D_U\) the covariant curl. Taking logarithms and using both curvature bounds: \(|D_UA'|\le C(\alpha'_0+\alpha_1'^2)\). \(U\) is real, so \(\operatorname{Ad}(U)\) commutes with \(X\mapsto X^*\), and \(D_UA'_{\rm R}=\frac12(D_UA'+(D_UA')^*)\); likewise for \(A'_{\rm I}\). The same expansion for \(U_{\rm R}=e^{i\eta_pA'_{\rm R}}U\) and \(U_{\rm I}\) gives \(|\partial U_{\rm R}-1|,|\partial U_{\rm I}-1|\le C_2(\alpha'_0+\alpha_1'^2)\eta_p^2\). This is SA 15890-15898, checked here.

*Step 3 (holomorphy and a crude bound on a large ball).* Put \(B(w)=\frac1i\log[M_{\mathbf B'}(e^{i\eta_pw}U)M_{\mathbf B'}(U)^{-1}]\). The analytic-remainder bootstrap of the averages (`gates/a1-balaban-gate.md` §1: \(a_{m+1}\le(1+C\alpha t_m^2)a_m+Ct_{m+1}a_m^2\), \(t_m=\xi'_m\)) closes with \(a_0=\sup|w|\) as long as \(24C\sup|w|\,\xi'_n\le1\), and then gives \(|B(w)(c)|\le2\xi'_q\sup|w|\le2c_TL^{q-n}\) for a row of level \(q\le n\). So for \(\sup|w|<R:=c_TL^{p-n}\), with \(c_T\le\min\{1/(24C),r_{\rm B}/2\}\), \(B(w)\) is holomorphic and lies in Theorem 3's polydisc. By definition (SA 9646) \(U_n(Z;e^{i\eta_pw}U)=\Phi_{M_{\mathbf B'}(U)}(B(w))\), which is holomorphic there and, by `a1-balaban.md` (9.2) (gated), regular at radius \(C_4(\varepsilon_0+r_{\rm B})\), where \(\varepsilon_0=C_1L^2\alpha'_0L^{-2(p-n)}\le r_{\rm B}\) is the datum radius of Step 1. Fix an element of \(Z^\circ\), and let \(\phi(w)\) be \(\eta_p^{-2}(\partial U_n-1)\) at a plaquette, or \(L^{2(p-n)}J(U_n)\) at a bond (level-\(n\) units). Then \(\phi\) is holomorphic on \(\{\sup|w|<R\}\) with \(|\phi|\le S:=2C_4r_{\rm B}L^{2(p-n)}\).

*Step 4 (three real points).* For \(g(t)=\phi(tw)\) and \(\sup|w|\le R/2\), Cauchy's estimate on the disc of radius \(R/\sup|w|\) gives \(|\phi(w)-\phi(0)-D\phi(0)w|\le2S(\sup|w|/R)^2\). \(D\phi(0)\) is complex linear, \(w=A'_{\rm R}+iA'_{\rm I}\) and \(\sup|A'_{\rm R}|,\sup|A'_{\rm I}|\le\sup|A'|\). Hence
\[
|\phi(A')|\le2|\phi(0)|+|\phi(A'_{\rm R})|+|\phi(A'_{\rm I})|+6S(\alpha'_1/R)^2 .
\]
At real arguments \(\Phi\) is the real background over the actual averages (Theorem 3), so Steps 1-2 bound the three real values by \(BC_1L^2C_2(\alpha'_0+\alpha_1'^2)\). The remainder is \(6\cdot2C_4r_{\rm B}L^{2(p-n)}\alpha_1'^2/(c_T^2L^{2(p-n)})=12C_4r_{\rm B}c_T^{-2}\alpha_1'^2\): the factor \(L^{2(p-n)}\) of the crude bound cancels against the radius \(R^2\). Take \(K^\sharp=4BC_1C_2L^2+12C_4r_{\rm B}c_T^{-2}\). \(\square\)

**Lemma J.** (a) \(\{x\in A_b:\operatorname{dist}_\infty(x,A_b^c)>e_q\}\subset T^b_q\), where \(e_q=\sum_{i<q}(\rho\xi_i+\mathfrak M\xi_{i+1})\le(\rho+L\mathfrak M)\xi_q/(L-1)\le\rho\xi_q\) [second rule: \(e'_q\le3M_1'\xi_q\)].
(b) (G1), (G2), (G3) hold, and \(\Omega'\) is a separated sequence with gap \(\rho\) and cube size \(\mathfrak M\).
(c) Put \(S_b=\{x\in A_b:\operatorname{dist}_\infty(x,A_b^c)\le e_{j_b}\}\subset{\rm col}_b\). Off \(\bigcup_bS_b\), \(x\in\Omega'_q\iff x\in\Omega_q\) for every \(q\). On \({\rm col}_b\cap W\), \(\Omega'_q\cap{\rm col}_b=W\cap{\rm col}_b\cap T^b_q\) for \(q\le j_b\), and \(\Omega'_q\cap{\rm col}_b=\emptyset\) for \(q>j_b\).
(d) A row of \(\Omega'\) or of \(\Omega\) with an end point in \(S_b\) has level \(\le j_b\) and lies in \({\rm col}_b\). Dropped and added rows have both end points in \(S_b\).
(e) The mismatch set \(\mathcal C\) (union of the double blocks of dropped and added rows) lies in \(S_b^+=\{x\in A_b:\operatorname{dist}_\infty(x,A_b^c)\le e_{j_b}+3\xi_{j_b}\}\subset{\rm col}_b\). No old row and no seam datum of \(\Omega\) outside \(\bigcup_bS_b^+\) is changed.

*Proof.* (a) If \(\operatorname{dist}(x,(T^b_q)^c)>\rho\xi_q+\mathfrak M\xi_{q+1}\), the aligned level-\((q+1)\) cube of side \(\mathfrak M\xi_{q+1}\) containing \(x\) is at distance \(>\rho\xi_q\) from \((T^b_q)^c\), so \(x\in T^b_{q+1}\). Points of \((T^b_q)^c\) lie in \(A_b^c\) or, by induction, in \(\{\operatorname{dist}(\cdot,A_b^c)\le e_q\}\); since \(\operatorname{dist}(\cdot,A_b^c)\) is 1-Lipschitz, \(\operatorname{dist}(x,A_b^c)>e_{q+1}\) gives \(\operatorname{dist}(x,(T^b_q)^c)>e_{q+1}-e_q\). Summing, \(\sum_{i<q}\xi_i\le\xi_q/(L-1)\) and \(\sum_{i=1}^q\xi_i\le\xi_qL/(L-1)\); \(L\mathfrak M\le\rho\) and \(L\ge3\) give \(e_q\le\rho\xi_q\). [Second rule: \(e'_q=2M_1'\sum_{i=1}^q\xi_i\le3M_1'\xi_q\).]

*Key inclusion.* For \(q>j_b\): \(\Omega_q\cap A_b\subset A_b\setminus{\rm col}_b\), since \({\rm col}_b\cap\Omega_{j_b+1}=\emptyset\). And \(\operatorname{dist}(\Omega_q\cap A_b,\ A_b^c\cup S_b)\ge\max\{(n_c\mu-\rho)\xi_{j_b},\ \rho\xi_{q-1}\mathbf 1_{q\ge j_b+2}\}\). The first term: \(x\in\Omega_q\cap A_b\) has \(\operatorname{dist}(x,A_b^c)\ge n_c\mu\xi_{j_b}\), points \(z\) of \(A_b^c\cup S_b\) have \(\operatorname{dist}(z,A_b^c)\le e_{j_b}\le\rho\xi_{j_b}\), and the distance function is 1-Lipschitz. The second: the segment \([x,z]\) contains a point \(y\in{\rm col}_b\) (continuity of \(\operatorname{dist}(\cdot,A_b^c)\) along it) with \(|x-y|_\infty\le|x-z|_\infty\); and \({\rm col}_b\subset\Omega_{j_b+1}^c\subset\Omega_{q-1}^c\), so \(|x-y|_\infty>\rho\xi_{q-1}\). (b) \(\Omega'_0=\Omega_0\cap\bigcap_bA_b=W\), and \(\Omega'_q\subset\Omega_q\): (G1). *Cubes:* \(\Omega_q\) and \(T^b_q\) (\(1\le q\le j_b\)) are unions of aligned \(\mathfrak M\xi_q\)-cubes; so is \(A_b\) for \(q\le j_b\), since \(\mu\xi_{j_b}/(\mathfrak M\xi_q)=(\mu/\mathfrak M)L^{j_b-q}\) is a positive integer power of \(L\). For \(q<j_b\), \(\operatorname{dist}((T^b_q)^c,T^b_{q+1})>\rho\xi_q\) by construction [second rule: \(\ge LM_1'\xi_q>\rho\xi_q\) as \(M_1'\ge\rho\)]. (c) For \(x\in W\setminus\bigcup S_b\): \(\operatorname{dist}(x,A_b^c)>e_{j_b}\ge e_q\) (\(q\le j_b\)), so \(x\in T^b_{\min(q,j_b)}\) for all \(b,q\) by (a). (d) A row of level \(q\) of either sequence is a bond in \(W\) with an end point in the \(q\)-th member and none in the \((q+1)\)-th. A level-\(j_b\) bond of \({\rm col}_b\) with an end point in \(T^b_{j_b}\) is a row of both, same datum \(V_{j_b}\) (its type may change from interior to outward; the constraint \(\mathcal M^{j_b}U(c)=V_{j_b}(c)\) does not). A level-\(j_b\) bond with no end point in \(T^b_{j_b}\) is dropped; both its end points are in \(A_b\setminus T^b_{j_b}\subset S_b\). (e) Double blocks of rows of level \(\le j_b\) have diameter \(\le2\xi_{j_b}\); with (d) this gives \(\mathcal C\subset S_b^+\), and \(e_{j_b}+3\xi_{j_b}\le(\rho+3)\xi_{j_b}<n_c\mu\xi_{j_b}\). The rest is (d). \(\square\)

## 4. Premise (4): M1, the multiplier normalization

**The issue** (`gates/a3-derivative-gate.md` §12 item 5). Theorem 4 (SA 9740) states \(|\dot\omega(c')|\le Ce^{-c\delta_0d_{\mathbf B}(y_{c'},y_c)}|e|\) "in the unweighted colour or matrix value norm", but the multiplier \(\omega\) of Theorem 3 (\(F'(\mathcal H(B))=K^{\mathsf T}_{\mathcal H(B)}\omega(B)\)) depends on the pairing in which \(K^{\mathsf T}\) is taken. (1b)(i) does not fix that pairing; SA 11761-11770 declares "the actual cell-weighted pairings" with "datum cell weights \(\mu_a\)", and SA 10012-10016, 11172, 11735 switch between raw and per-length rows. `a3-derivative.md` (34) proves the bound for the counting row pairing.

**Lemma M.** Fix the fine pairing \(\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_{\rm f}=\eta^4\sum_b\operatorname{tr}\) and a datum pairing \(\langle b,\omega\rangle_\mu=\sum_c\mu_c\operatorname{tr}(b(c)\omega(c))\), \(\mu_c>0\). Let \(\omega_\mu\) be the multiplier defined with the transpose \(K^\sharp\) in these pairings, and \(\omega_1\) the one for \(\mu\equiv1\) (counting).
(i) \(\omega_\mu=\mu^{-1}\omega_1\) row by row; the force \(K^\sharp\omega_\mu=K^{\mathsf T}\omega_1\) is the same. This holds for raw and for per-length rows (\(\omega_{\rm len}=\ell\omega_{\rm raw}\), \(\ell={\rm diag}(\xi_c)\)).
(ii) Take \(\mathbf V_0=\mathbf 1\) (so \(U_0=\mathbf 1\), SA Theorem 1) on a separated sequence of depth \(k\) whose layer 0 contains a bare plaquette \(p\subset\Omega_0\) at distance \(\ge3L\eta\) from \(\Omega_1\) (for instance \(\Omega_0=T_\ell\) and \(\Omega_1\supset\dots\supset\Omega_k\) nested cubes with the gaps of SA 9593, on a torus large enough; Theorem 4 claims uniformity over all such sequences). Let \(c\) be a bond of \(p\), \(e\in\mathfrak g\), \(\dot\omega=\partial_s\omega(se_c)|_{s=0}\). Then, in raw rows, \(\operatorname{tr}(e\,\dot\omega_1(c))\ge\kappa|e|^2\), where \(\kappa>0\) is the fixed normalization of the plaquette action (\(\kappa=1\) in the outline's convention \(F''(0)=D^*D\) at \(U_0=\mathbf 1\)). Hence \(|\dot\omega_1(c)|\ge\kappa|e|\) and \(|\dot\omega_\mu(c)|\ge\kappa\mu_c^{-1}|e|\).
(iii) Consequently, Theorem 4's multiplier member, with \(C\) independent of depth, holds for \(\omega_1\) (raw or per-length rows) and fails for \(\omega_\mu\) whenever \(\mu_c\) at level-0 rows tends to \(0\) as the depth grows. For the physical cell weight \(\mu_c=\xi_c^4\) (level-\(k\) units) it fails at the level-0 row \(c\) by the factor \(\eta^{-4}=L^{4k}\) (raw rows) or \(\eta^{-3}=L^{3k}\) (per-length rows).

*Proof.* (i) \(\langle K^\sharp\omega,A\rangle_{\rm f}=\langle\omega,KA\rangle_\mu=\langle\mu\omega,KA\rangle_1\), so \(K^\sharp\omega=K^{\mathsf T}(\mu\omega)\), and the equation \(F'=K^\sharp\omega_\mu\) is \(F'=K^{\mathsf T}(\mu\omega_\mu)\). Since \(K\) is onto (P82° (a), or `a3-derivative.md` §6), \(K^{\mathsf T}\) is injective, so \(\mu\omega_\mu=\omega_1\). The fine weight cancels. Per-length rows change \(K\) to \(\ell^{-1}K\) and the multiplier to \(\ell\omega\), with the pairings unchanged; the argument is the same.

(ii) At \(\mathbf V_0=\mathbf 1\): \(U_0=\mathbf 1\), \(\mathcal H(0)=0\), and \(F'(0)=0=K_0^{\mathsf T}\omega(0)\) gives \(\omega(0)=0\). Differentiate \(F'(\mathcal H(B))=K^{\mathsf T}_{\mathcal H(B)}\omega(B)\) and \(\mathcal Q^{\rm st}(\mathcal H(B))=\mathsf b(B)\) at \(B=0\) in the direction \(e_c\), with \(h=\partial_s\mathcal H(se_c)|_0\): \(F''(0)h=K_0^{\mathsf T}\dot\omega_1\), and \(K_0h=D\mathsf b(0)e_c\). The term \((DK[h])^{\mathsf T}\omega(0)\) vanishes. At level 0 the raw row is the bond logarithm: \(\mathcal Q^{\rm st}(A)(b)=\eta A(b)\) at \(U_0=\mathbf 1\), so \(K_0=\eta\) there. The bonds of \(p\) are interior level-0 rows (layer 0, no outward rows at level 0), and \(\mathsf b(B)=B\) at interior rows. The block factor of an outward level-1 row with outer end \(z\) reads \(B\) only on the bare bonds inside the block \(B(z)\), which lies within \(2L\eta\) of \(\Omega_1\); \(p\) is at distance \(\ge3L\eta\). So \(D\mathsf b(0)e_c=e_c\): \(\eta h(c)=e\), and \(h(b_i)=0\) on the other three bonds \(b_i\) of \(p\). Pair the first equation with \(h\):
\[
\operatorname{tr}(e\,\dot\omega_1(c))=\langle K_0h,\dot\omega_1\rangle_1=\langle h,K_0^{\mathsf T}\dot\omega_1\rangle_{\rm f}=\langle h,F''(0)h\rangle_{\rm f}=\kappa\,\eta^4\sum_{p'}|(dh)_{p'}|^2\ \ge\ \kappa\,\eta^4|(dh)_p|^2 .
\]
At \(U_0=\mathbf 1\) the Wilson Hessian is the curl form: \(U_{p'}=\exp(i\eta^2(dh)_{p'}+O(\eta^3h^2))\) and \(1-\frac12\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}\) is quadratic in the exponent. Here \((dh)_p=\pm\eta^{-1}h(c)=\pm\eta^{-2}e\), so the right side is \(\kappa|e|^2\). Cauchy-Schwarz gives \(|\dot\omega_1(c)|\ge\kappa|e|\), and (i) gives the weighted bound.

(iii) The first claim is `a3-derivative.md` (34) (counting, raw; gated ADVANCE as a conditional implication), and per-length counting multipliers are smaller by \(\xi_c\le1\). For \(c=c'\), \(d_{\mathbf B}=0\), so the member asks \(|\dot\omega(c)|\le C|e|\), while (ii) gives \(|\dot\omega_\mu(c)|\ge\kappa\mu_c^{-1}|e|=\kappa L^{4k}|e|\) for \(\mu_c=\eta^4\). \(\square\)

**Consumers.** In the real-critical chain the multiplier is read only in convention-free combinations:

**Verdict.** M1 is **settled**: Theorem 4's multiplier member is true exactly in the counting datum pairing (raw or per-length rows), which is what `a3-derivative.md` proves, and false, by an explicit hand-checked lower bound, in the cell-weighted datum pairing of SA 11761 read with Theorem 4's unweighted value norm.
