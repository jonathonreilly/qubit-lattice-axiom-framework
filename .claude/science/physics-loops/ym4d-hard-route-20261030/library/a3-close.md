## 1. Premise (1): the critical reference U_0 lies in Reg

**Statement.** Let \(\mathbf B\) be a separated sequence (SA 9593) with gap \(\rho\ge\rho_0'\), \(\mathbf V_0\) a real \(\varepsilon_1\)-regular datum, and \(U_0=U^\circ(\mathbf V_0)\) the axial representative of A1 (`a1-balaban.md` (8.2)). Let \(M,c_{\rm reg}\) be the constants of `p82-curved.md` Theorem P and \(W:=R_1M_1\) the walk length of Balaban's propagator theorems used in the gauge theorem of `a1-balaban.md` §3.8. Put \(M'=c_{\rm reg}M+2W\). There are \(C_L\) and \(a_L>0\), depending on \(L\) only, such that if \(\rho_0'\ge 4LW+2L\) and \(C_LM'\varepsilon_1\le a_L\), then \(U_0\) satisfies (Reg\(_\alpha\)) of `p82-curved.md` §1 with
\[
\alpha=C_LM'\varepsilon_1 .
\]
So [Reg at \(U_0\)] holds with \(\alpha\le\alpha_*\) as soon as \(\varepsilon_1\le\alpha_*/(C_LM')\), which is the consumer's ceiling of `p82-curved.md` (a radius chosen after the lengths).

**Counterexample attempt.** Reg asks for a derivative bound on a gauge potential, and curvature alone does not give one: on a cube of side \(m\xi\) at mesh \(\eta\), a field with \(|\partial U-1|\le\varepsilon\eta^2\xi^{-2}\) can have, in every gauge, transverse differences of size \(\varepsilon\xi^{-2}\log(\xi/\eta)\) (the \(L^\infty\) failure of the Calderon-Zygmund bound; this is `p82-curved.md` test 2.4). So a counterexample at \(U_0\) would have to violate the current bound at some bond. The only places where criticality does not control the current directly are the layer-0 bonds (fixed by the datum), the free inward bonds and the bonds next to \(\partial\Omega_0\). At layer 0 the scale is \(\eta\), and the current is at most \(2(d-1)\) plaquette differences over \(\eta^3\), so it is controlled by curvature (`a1-balaban.md` §8, base case). At the others, (8.2) bounds \(J(U_0)(b)\) at every bond, since the reset (6.6) bounds \(r(U)\), a supremum over all bonds. No counterexample.

**Proof.** Four steps.

*(i) Balaban's class.* `a1-balaban.md` (8.2) (gated ADVANCE conditional on F0, `gates/a1-balaban-gate.md` §§4, 8; F0 is `flat-port.md`, gated in row 99) gives at every plaquette and bond of \(\Omega_0\)
\[
|\partial U_0(p)-1|\le B\varepsilon_1\eta^2\mathsf L(p)^{-2},\qquad |J(U_0)(b)|\le B\varepsilon_1\mathsf L(b)^{-3}.
\]
Balaban's class \(\mathcal U_k(\{\Omega_j\},\alpha_0)\) (CMP 99a (1.7)-(1.9), read on the scan of p. 77) is: \(|U(\partial p)-1|<\alpha_0L^{-2j}\) for \(p\in\Omega_j\), and \(|(D^{\eta*}_U\partial U)(b)|<\alpha_0L^{-2j}(L^j\eta)^{-1}\) for \(b\in\Omega_j\), where \(p\in\Omega_j\) means that some corner lies in \(\Omega_j\). In level-\(k\) units \(L^j\eta=\xi_j\) and \(L^{-2j}=\eta^2\xi_j^{-2}\). A corner in \(\Omega_j\) has scale at least \(\xi_j\), so \(\mathsf L(p)\ge\xi_j\) and \(\mathsf L(b)\ge\xi_j\). Hence the curvature half of (8.2) gives (1.7) with \(\alpha_0=B\varepsilon_1\). For (1.9): Balaban's \(\eta^{-2}D^*_U\partial U\) is a matrix whose anti-Hermitian part is \(J(U)\) at every bond whose six plaquettes lie in \(\Omega_0\); its Hermitian part is bounded by \(C\varepsilon^2\mathsf L^{-3}\) through \(\mathrm{Re}\,W-1=-\frac12(W-1)^*(W-1)\) for unitary \(W\) (`a1-balaban.md` §2, checked in its gate). So (1.9) holds at those bonds with \(\alpha_0=C(B\varepsilon_1+B^2\varepsilon_1^2)\le 2CB\varepsilon_1\). Below, only bonds with all six plaquettes in \(\Omega_0\) are read.

*(ii) Geometry for \(j\ge1\).* Let \(Z\subset\Omega_0\cap(\mathcal L_j\cup\mathcal L_{j+1})\) be a cube of side \(c_{\rm reg}M\xi_j\), \(j\ge1\). Let \(\square\) be the union of the cubes of side \(W\xi_j\), aligned with the level-\(j\) blocks, that meet \(Z\). It is a cube of side \(M''\xi_j\le M'\xi_j\), \(M''\) a multiple of \(W\). Balaban's enlargement \(\tilde\square\) (CMP 99a p. 98: the cover of the outermost nested cube, whose boundary lies at distance \(2W\xi_j\) from \(\square\)) satisfies \(\tilde\square\subset Z+4W\xi_j\) (\(\ell^\infty\) neighbourhood). Since \(Z\subset\Omega_j\) and \(\operatorname{dist}_\infty(\Omega_{j-1}^c,\Omega_j)>\rho\xi_{j-1}=\rho\xi_j/L\), the condition \(\rho>4LW+2L\) gives \(\tilde\square\subset\Omega_{j-1}\), at distance at least \(2\xi_j/L\ge 2\eta\) from \(\Omega_{j-1}^c\). Thus every plaquette and every bond, with its six plaquettes, meeting \(\tilde\square\) lies in \(\Omega_0\).

*(iii) Proposition 6 at level \(j\).* Balaban's proof of CMP 99a Proposition 6 (pp. 98-99) first drops the members \(\Omega_{j'}\), \(j'>j\): the class conditions at a coarser scale are stronger, so \(U_0\) lies in the class of the truncated sequence \(\Omega_0\supset\dots\supset\Omega_j\) with the same \(\alpha_0\). After rescaling \(\xi_j=1\), the only use of the hypotheses is (1.7)-(1.9) on \(\tilde\square\subset\Omega_{j-1}\), at scale \(\xi_{j-1}\), which costs \(L^2\) (curvature) and \(L^3\) (current); this is his (1.128) and (1.132). The membership \(\square\subset\Omega_j\), \(\square\not\subset\Omega_{j+1}\) is used only to place \(\tilde\square\) in \(\Omega_{j-1}\), which (ii) supplies directly. Its port is `a1-balaban.md` §3.8, last paragraph ("Proposition 6's local gauge needs no critical background"; reference 1, flat inverses only), gated ADVANCE conditional on F0. Under \(7dL^2M''\alpha_0\le c_1\) it gives a gauge \(u\) on \(\tilde\square\) with \(U_0^{u^{-1}}=e^{i\eta A}\) and, on \(\square\supset Z\), in level-\(j\) units (his (1.134), (1.136)),
\[
\xi_j|A|,\ \xi_j^2|\nabla A|<7dL^2B_1M''\alpha_0\le 14\,C\,dL^2B_1BM'\varepsilon_1,
\]
with \(\nabla\) the ordinary difference. This is (Reg\(_\alpha\)) on \(Z\).

*(iv) \(j=0\), no current needed.* Let \(Z\subset\Omega_0\cap(\mathcal L_0\cup\mathcal L_1)\) have side \(n\eta\), \(n=c_{\rm reg}M\). In complete axial gauge from a corner of \(Z\) (\(U_1\equiv1\); \(U_2=1\) on \(x_1=0\); \(U_3=1\) on \(x_1=x_2=0\); \(U_4=1\) on \(x_1=x_2=x_3=0\)), the link \(U_\mu(x)\) is the holonomy of a loop bounding a staircase of at most \(3n\) plaquettes of \(Z\). By unitarity the holonomy is within the sum of their deviations of \(1\): \(|U(b)-1|\le 3nB\varepsilon_1\), since \(\mathsf L(p)\ge\eta\). For \(U=e^{i\eta A}\) with eigenvalue angles \(\pm\theta\), \(|\theta|\le\pi\), one has \(|U-1|=2|\sin(\theta/2)|\ge(2/\pi)|\theta|\), so \(\eta|A|\le\frac{3\pi}{2}nB\varepsilon_1\) and \(\eta^2|\nabla A|\le2\sup\eta|A|\le3\pi nB\varepsilon_1\). This is (Reg\(_\alpha\)) at \(j=0\) with \(\alpha=5\pi c_{\rm reg}MB\varepsilon_1\).

Taking \(C_L\) the larger of the two constants proves the statement. \(\square\)

**Order of constants.** \(L\); flat constants (F0); \(W\); \(M,c_{\rm reg},\alpha_*\) (`p82-curved.md`); \(\rho_0'=\max\{\rho_0,4LW+2L\}\), so the gap is fixed after \(M\) and \(W\), as in the outline's order (SA 9916) and `gates/a1-balaban-gate.md` D2; then \(B\) (A1); then \(\varepsilon_1\le\min\{\alpha_*,a_L\}/(C_LM')\). Depth, mesh, volume and region shape enter nowhere. Torus floors: \(\tilde\square\) has side \(O(M'+W)\xi_j\), within the outline's second floor (SA (1b)(ii)).

**Label.** Premise (1) is **discharged as an implication**: [Reg at \(U_0\)] follows from A1 (8.2) and the Proposition 6 port, both gated conditional on F0, and F0 (`flat-port.md`, gated row 99). The two-layer condition \(Z\subset\mathcal L_j\cup\mathcal L_{j+1}\) is not needed for existence; it only fixes the unit \(\xi_j\). What this does not cover: complex references, the independent-current clause of SA 10133-10137, and Balaban's Laplacian and Hölder members; P82° at real critical references reads none of them.

## 2. Premise (2): A1's pointed-core admission (10.2)

**What (10.2) is.** `a1-balaban.md` (10.2) is the outline's admission criterion (BGR32) with its reference margin (SA 12313-12333): for the actual outer, inner and removed substitution curves \(X_\nu\) of the pointed window (BGR31), every clause \(\|\mathcal R_a\|<r_a\) of the assigned pair core, hereditary tower clauses included, satisfies \(\|\mathcal R_a(X_\nu)-\mathcal R_a(X_0)\|<\epsilon_ar_a\) on the retained disc and \(\|\mathcal R_a(X_0)\|\le(1-\epsilon_a)r_a\). The source of the requirement is the last sentence block of Obligation A1 (SA 3825-3828), "for the pointed window deduction"; its proof mechanism is (BGR33) with the room estimate of SA 15879-15990 and (1b) Theorems 5 and 6(d). It is `sa-ledger.md` rank 4 ("WF substitution room", rated extreme) together with the ledger's "1b Theorem 5" row.

**Scope finding: real-critical nonlinear A3 does not read (10.2).** The reads of the chain, from the files and their gates:
- `a3-profile.md` (Theorem 2, first half): P81 (orbit, regularity), P82, D1.
- `a3-derivative.md` §§6-7, 10 (Theorem 4 first paragraph, Theorem 3's pair, Theorem 6(c)): P81 at the strength listed in `gates/a3-derivative-gate.md` §12 item 4 (orbit and uniqueness, local gauges and current bounds, holomorphic \(\Phi\) with complex regularity and a uniform chart radius), P82° (Lemma 8.1), (G1)-(G3), datum admission of the interpolation \(e^{itB_0}\mathbf V^J\) (a datum-ball statement, Theorem 3's polydisc).
- `p82-curved.md`: [Reg at \(U_0\)] (Section 1 above), the flat package.
- `sa-2.8-terms.md` (the 2.8 consumer): I2 = P81 with the complex regularity of \(\Phi\) on the datum polydisc (`a1-balaban.md` (9.2); `gates/a1-balaban-gate.md` positive finding (c)), and the multiplier only through \(\lambda_\Lambda=0\) and \(\lambda\cdot Dq\).

None of these reads a pair core \(\mathcal U^\circ_j(X;\alpha_0,\alpha_1)\), a tower configuration at complex \(A'\), an independent current \(\mathbf J\), or a substitution curve \(X_\nu\). So (10.2) is not a premise of real-critical nonlinear A3; it is a premise of the pointed window (Requirement B2, (PW1)-(PW3), sa-ledger rank 4). The list in `FINISH.md` ("open on this obligation") should move it there. This changes what A3 waits for; it does not close (10.2).

**Counterexample attempts.** (a) *Reference margin at the boundary of a tower.* The restricted configuration has a different current at the new action boundary (`gates/a3-derivative-gate.md` §10, Lemma 10.2 repair, \(|J_W|\le C\varepsilon_1\eta^{-3}\)). The tower clauses are imposed only on \(Z\) with two layers of \(M\)-cubes removed (SA 9646), so this bond set is excluded. No failure. (b) *Reference margin at internal seams of a tower.* The current is a property of the configuration, not of the constraint, so at a bond whose six plaquettes lie in \(Z\) the tower configuration's current equals the ambient one whenever the tower configuration is the restriction (Lemma R below). No failure. (c) *Layered domains.* When \(X\) meets a seam of the ambient sequence, a tower of \(Z\subset X\) can be coarser than the ambient sequence on part of \(Z\); then the tower configuration is not a restriction, and its datum's tests carry factors up to \(L^{2(q-j_{\rm amb})}\). This is why SA 6(d) imposes wider gaps and layered radii in that case. Not a counterexample, but Lemma R does not reach it. (d) *Nonconvexity.* The outline itself notes (SA 12371-12376) that endpoint admission does not admit the segment between two removed substitutions; (BGR32) along one analytic curve avoids this. Not a counterexample to (10.2), which is stated along the curve.

**Lemma R (reference admission of the real critical background, single-layer domains).** Let \(U_0\) be the critical background of a separated sequence, regular at radius \(B\varepsilon_1\) by (8.2), with \(\rho\ge\rho_0'\) as in Section 1. Let \(X\) be a region with \(X\subset\Omega_j\). Then for every \(\alpha_0\ge C_R\varepsilon_1\) and every \(\alpha_1>0\), the real triple \((U_0,0,J(U_0))|_X\) satisfies every clause of the core \(\mathcal U^\circ_j(X;\alpha_0,\alpha_1)\) (SA 9646) with margin \(\tfrac12\): each defining quantity is at most \(\tfrac12\) of its bound. Here \(C_R\) depends on \(L\) only, the simple local-gauge clause holds with the constant \(C\) of that clause taken \(\ge C_RL^3\), and \(M\varepsilon_1\) is small.

*Proof.* Work in level-\(j\) units: bare spacing \(\eta_j=L^{-j}\), level-\(j\) scale \(1\). Conversion from level-\(k\) units: a length \(\ell\) becomes \(\ell L^{k-j}\), so a current (dimension length\(^{-3}\)) is multiplied by \(L^{3(j-k)}\).

*Simple conditions.* On \(X\subset\Omega_j\) every corner scale is at least \(\xi_j\). By (8.2), \(|\partial U_0-1|\le B\varepsilon_1(\eta/\xi_j)^2=B\varepsilon_1\eta_j^2\), and \(|J(U_0)|\le B\varepsilon_1\xi_j^{-3}\), which is \(B\varepsilon_1\) in level-\(j\) units. \(A'=0\). For a cube of side \(O(LM)\) in \(X\), Section 1 steps (ii)-(iii) at level \(j\) (the enlargement lies in \(\Omega_{j-1}\) because the cube lies in \(\Omega_j\)) give a real gauge with \(|A|,|\nabla A|\le C_LL^3MB\varepsilon_1\) in level-\(j\) units. With \(\alpha_0\ge4B\varepsilon_1\) and \(C\ge2C_LL^3B/4\), each simple clause holds with margin \(\frac12\).

*Tower clauses.* Fix \(Z\subset X\) of level \(p\le j\), a rule, and \(n\le p\); let \(\mathbf B'\) be the tower of \(Z\) cut at depth \(n\), with members \(T'_q\), \(q\le n\). The tower is separated (gap \(\rho\), first rule; \(M_1'\), second rule; SA 9646 and Theorem 6(a)). Since \(T'_q\subset Z\subset X\subset\Omega_j\subset\Omega_q\) for \(q\le n\le j\), the restriction identity (9.3) of `a1-balaban.md` §9.2 (gated ADVANCE, `gates/a1-balaban-gate.md` §6) applies: \(U_{\mathbf B'}(M_{\mathbf B'}(U_0|_{T'_0}))=U_0|_{T'_0}\) modulo \(\mathcal G^{\rm res}(\mathbf B')\). For \(A'=0\) the tower configuration is \(\Phi_{M_{\mathbf B'}(U_0)}(0)\), that background (SA 9646). So \(U_n(Z;U_0)\) is gauge equivalent to \(U_0|_Z\). On \(Z\) with two layers of \(M\)-cubes removed, every bond has its six plaquettes in \(Z\), so the tower configuration's current there is the ambient current \(J(U_0)\). Both bounds are gauge invariant. Now compare with the clause:
- curvature: \(B\varepsilon_1L^{-2j}\) against \(\alpha_0L^{-(j-p)}\eta_p^2=\alpha_0L^{-j-p}\); the ratio is \((B\varepsilon_1/\alpha_0)L^{p-j}\le\frac12\) for \(\alpha_0\ge2B\varepsilon_1\);
- current, in level-\(n\) units: \(B\varepsilon_1L^{3(n-j)}\) against \(\alpha_0L^{-(j-p)}L^{2(n-p)}=\alpha_0L^{2n-j-p}\); the ratio is \((B\varepsilon_1/\alpha_0)L^{n+p-2j}\le\frac12\).

Existence of every tower configuration is the existence half of A1 for \(\mathbf B'\), whose datum \(M_{\mathbf B'}(U_0)\) is \(CL^2B\varepsilon_1\)-regular by the converse in Theorem 1. The same holds with \(U\) in place of \(\mathbf U\), since they coincide. Take \(C_R=\max\{4B,CL^2B\}\). \(\square\)

**Label and what stays open.** Lemma R is an implication from (8.2), (9.3) and Section 1, all gated conditional on F0. It supplies the reference half \(\|\mathcal R_a(X_0)\|\le\frac12r_a\) of (10.2) for real critical references on single-layer domains, the first case of SA 6(d). Open, hardest first:
1. the displacement half (BGR33) along the actual complex substitution curves, which reads (1b) Theorem 5(b) (tower curvature and current Lipschitz in \(A'\) with decay \(e^{-c\delta d^\flat}\)) and the room estimate of SA 15879; this is the first open step, and it is not attempted here;
2. the tower clauses at complex \(A'\) (Theorem 5(a); the three-real-point argument of SA 15890-15915 reduces them to real fields \(e^{i\eta A'_{\rm R}}U\), \(e^{i\eta A'_{\rm I}}U\), which are not critical, so restriction does not apply and Theorem 2's profile bound is needed);
3. the layered case of SA 6(d) (domains meeting seams).

Premise (2) is therefore **not closed**. It is moved off the real-critical A3 list (it is not read there) and onto ledger rank 4, with its reference half proved in the single-layer case.
