## Summary of verdicts

1. **The literal finger of Proposition 3.2 does not violate (D).** It sits at level 0, more
   than $(R+24)\mathfrak M\eta$ from $\Omega_1$. Every carrier $E_c$ of height $j\ge1$ lies
   within $\ell_j$ of $\Omega_j\subset\Omega_1$. So $U_r=1$ on every carrier bond.
2. **The same mechanism one level up does violate (D).** A thin island (or finger) of
   $\Omega_j$, $j\ge1$, narrower than 12 big blocks and wider than $26/L$ big blocks, has a core
   that no class cube reaches. A single bond with holonomy $\mathrm{diag}(-1,-1,1,\dots,1)$

   (Proposition 1). The same pair breaks the account's display (4.5:eq-admissible-pairs-1)
   at level $j$, and the literal averaging map of the row is not defined there.
3. **Bałaban's restricted class does not exclude it.** His outer-region rule (CMP 99b
   pp. 394, 396) restricts only $\Omega_0$. His towers obey CMP 96 (2.1)–(2.2), which allow
   thin $\Omega_j$ for $j\ge1$. So (D) is false on that class too, and on the account's class
   $\mathfrak S$. Statement T of `library/b98p5.md` is false on $\mathfrak S$ as written.
4. **(D) holds on two smaller classes** (Section 3): on the account's inclusion of the CMP 109

## 1. The objects

**The class.** CA-I 12949–12967 (Definition 4.5:def-admissible-pairs), with the cube class of
CMP 99b (3.35) taken at side $12\mathfrak M$ as the account states ("the number $O(1)$ may be
taken equal to 12", CA-I 12955–12957; CMP 99b p. 409: "the number $O(1)$ in the condition
(3.35) can be taken as equal to 12"). Write $\ell_q=L^q\eta$, $\eta=L^{-k}$. A *class cube of
index $q$* is a set $\square$ of fine sites with these properties:

- it is a union of level-$q$ big blocks (blocks of side $\mathfrak M\ell_q$ of the level-$q$
  big-block grid), and it is a cube of side $12\mathfrak M\ell_q$;
- $\square\subset B^q(\Lambda_q)\cup B^{q+1}(\Lambda_{q+1})=\Omega_q\setminus\Omega_{q+2}$;
- $\square\cap B^q(\Lambda_q)=\square\cap(\Omega_q\setminus\Omega_{q+1})\neq\emptyset$.

Here $\Omega_{k+1}=\Omega_{k+2}=\emptyset$. A *bond of $\square$* has both end points in
$\square$. (S1) asks, for each class cube, a $G$-valued gauge $u$ on $\square$ with
$U_r^u=e^{i\eta A}$ on the bonds of $\square$, $\ell_q|A|<a_0$ and $\ell_q^2|\nabla A|<a_0$
there. As a set of fine sites, a cube of side $s$ is a product of four runs of $s/\eta$
consecutive sites; two of its sites differ by at most $s-\eta$ in each coordinate.

**Bałaban's restricted class.** CMP 99b p. 396 (page image read): "Here $O(1)$ will mean a
number $\geq 10$. The set $\Lambda_0$ in the above condition means $\Lambda_0=\Omega_0\setminus\Omega_1$,
where $\Omega_0=\{$a union of big blocks of the lattice $T_1$, such that their distances to
$\Omega_1$ are $\le RM\}$." P. 394 also allows "a thinner layer of the big blocks surrounding
$\Omega_1$". The levels $j\ge1$ follow CMP 96 (2.1)–(2.2): each $\Omega_j$ is "a sum of big
blocks" with $(L^j\eta)^{-1}\mathrm{dist}(\Omega_j^c,\Omega_{j+1})>RM$. No thickness is imposed
on $\Omega_j$, $j\ge1$. So the restricted class is the account's class with one extra rule:
$\Omega_0$ is a neighbourhood of $\Omega_1$ made of level-0 big blocks.

**Carriers and (D).** As in `library/b98p5.md` §3: a row $c$ of height $j\ge1$ is a level-$j$
bond with $c_-\in\Lambda_j$; $E_c=B^j(c_-)\cup B^j(c_+)$, all fine bonds in that double block.
(D): every elementary plaquette $p\subset E_c$ has
$\|U_r(\partial p)-I\|_F\le8\sqrt N L^2a_0(\eta/\ell_j)^2$, with $\|\cdot\|_F$ the
unnormalised Hilbert–Schmidt norm.

## 2. Task (1): the thin finger against (D)

### 2.1 The literal finger does not reach a carrier

Proposition 3.2 of `library/print-inverse.md` puts the finger $F$ in $\Omega_0$ at distance
more than $(R+24)\mathfrak M\eta$ from $\Omega_1$, and changes one bond $b_0$ in its middle.

*Claim.* No carrier $E_c$ of height $j\ge1$ contains a bond of $F$.

*Proof.* Take a row $c$ of height $j\ge1$. Its end point $c_-$ lies in $\Lambda_j\subset\Omega_j$.
$\Omega_j$ is a union of level-$j$ blocks, so $B^j(c_-)\subset\Omega_j\subset\Omega_1$. The
other block $B^j(c_+)$ is a translate by $\ell_j$, so every site of $E_c$ lies within
$\ell^\infty$-distance $\ell_j$ of $\Omega_j$. For $j=1$ this is $L\eta<(R+24)\mathfrak M\eta$.
For $j\ge2$ the gap (CA-I 12717) puts the $\ell_j$-neighbourhood of $\Omega_j$ inside
$\Omega_{j-1}\subset\Omega_1$. In both cases $E_c$ misses $F$. ∎

So at the finger's configuration $U_t$, $U_r=1$ on every carrier bond and (D) holds with
left side zero. The finger breaks the level-0 propagator, not the averaging of rows of height
$\ge1$.

### 2.2 The same mechanism at a level $j\ge1$: Proposition 1

**Proposition 1.** Let $d=4$, $G=SU(N)$ with $N\ge2$, $L\ge3$ odd, $\mathfrak M\ge L$, $R\ge R_1$
and $k\ge1$. Let $w$ be an integer with
\[
 wL\ge26,\qquad w\le11 .
\]
(For $L=3$: $w\in\{9,10,11\}$; for $L\ge27$ every $1\le w\le11$.) Take the fine periodic
lattice of physical side $P$, a multiple of $\mathfrak M$ with $P\ge(w+26)\mathfrak M$
($\ell_k=1$). Let $F$ be a cube of side $w\mathfrak M$ that is a union of $w^4$ level-$k$ big
blocks. Take the tower
\[
 \Omega_0=\dots=\Omega_{k-1}=T_\eta,\qquad \Omega_k=F
\]
(for $k=1$ one may instead take $\Omega_0$ to be the union of level-0 big blocks within
distance $R\mathfrak M\eta$ of $F$). Let $x_0$ be a level-$k$ site with
$x_{0,\mu}-f_\mu\in[w\mathfrak M/2-1,\,w\mathfrak M/2)$ for each $\mu$, where $f_\mu$ is the
least $\mu$-coordinate of $F$. Put $b_0=(x_0,x_0+\eta e_1)$ and
\[
 V=\exp(i\pi H),\quad H=\mathrm{diag}(1,-1,0,\dots,0),\qquad
 V=\mathrm{diag}(-1,-1,1,\dots,1)\in SU(N).
\]
Let $U_r(b_0)=V$, $U_r=1$ on every other bond, $\mathfrak a=0$, $J=0$, $U=U_r$. Then:

(a) the tower is admissible (CA-I 12712–12724), and it is in Bałaban's restricted class;

(b) $(U,J)$ satisfies (S1), (S2), (S3) for every $a_0,a_1,a_\gamma>0$;

(c) the row $c_0=(x_0,x_0+\ell_ke_1)$ has height $k$, and the plaquette $p_0$ with corners
$x_0$, $x_0+\eta e_1$, $x_0+\eta e_1+\eta e_2$, $x_0+\eta e_2$ lies in $E_{c_0}$ with
$\|U_r(\partial p_0)-I\|_F=2\sqrt2$ and $|U(\partial p_0)-1|_{\rm op}=2$;

(d) hence (D) fails at $(c_0,p_0)$ whenever $8\sqrt N a_0<2\sqrt2$, and the display
(4.5:eq-admissible-pairs-1) fails at level $k$ whenever $a_0+a_1<1/2$;

(e) the literal straightened map $q_{c_0}=Q_k(U,\eta A,c_0)$ of `library/b98p5.md` (5)–(6) is
not defined at $U$: one of its logarithms has argument $V^{-1}$, outside the identity chart.

*Proof.* (a) Each $\Omega_j$ is a union of level-$j$ big blocks: $T_\eta$ is, as $P$ is a
multiple of $\mathfrak M=\mathfrak M\ell_k$, which is a multiple of $\mathfrak M\ell_j$; $F$ is
by choice. For $j\le k-1$, $\Omega_j^c=\emptyset$, so
$\mathrm{dist}(\Omega_j^c,\Omega_{j+1})=+\infty>R\mathfrak M\ell_j$. There is no gap condition
past $k$. CA-I 12723–12724 admits $\Omega_j=T_\eta$. For $k\ge2$, $\Omega_0=\Omega_1=T_\eta$,
which is Bałaban's $\Omega_0$ for this $\Omega_1$. For $k=1$ the alternative $\Omega_0$ is his
$\Omega_0$ by definition, and its gap to $F$ is $R\mathfrak M\eta$ up to one block, so take the
neighbourhood radius $(R+1)\mathfrak M\eta$ if strict inequality is wanted.

(b) Since $\mathfrak a=0$ and $J=0$, (S2) and (S3) hold. For (S1) we show that $x_0$ lies in no
class cube. Then $b_0$ is a bond of no class cube, $U_r=1$ on every bond of every class cube,
and $u=1$, $A=0$ satisfy the strict bounds.

- Index $q\le k-2$ ($k\ge2$): $\Omega_q\setminus\Omega_{q+1}=T_\eta\setminus T_\eta=\emptyset$,
  so no class cube has index $q$.
- Index $k$: a class cube would lie in $\Omega_k\setminus\Omega_{k+2}=F$. It has $12\mathfrak
  M/\eta$ sites per coordinate run, and $F$ has $w\mathfrak M/\eta<12\mathfrak M/\eta$. None
  exists.
- Index $k-1$ (or index 0 with the alternative $\Omega_0$ when $k=1$): the cube contains a
  site $z\notin F$. So for some $\mu$, $z_\mu<f_\mu$ or $z_\mu\ge f_\mu+w\mathfrak M$. Two sites
  of the cube differ by at most $12\mathfrak M\ell_{k-1}-\eta=12\mathfrak M/L-\eta$ in each
  coordinate. But
  $\min\{x_{0,\mu}-f_\mu+\eta,\;f_\mu+w\mathfrak M-x_{0,\mu}\}\ge w\mathfrak M/2-1$, and
  $w\mathfrak M/2-1\ge12\mathfrak M/L$ because $\mathfrak M(wL-24)\ge2\mathfrak M\ge2L$. So
  $|x_{0,\mu}-z_\mu|>12\mathfrak M/L-\eta$, and the cube does not contain $x_0$.

$P\ge(w+26)\mathfrak M$ keeps all these coordinate comparisons inside one period.

(c) $x_0$ and $x_0+\ell_ke_1$ are level-$k$ sites of $F$ (the second because
$x_{0,1}-f_1+1<w\mathfrak M-\eta$), so both lie in $\Lambda_k=F$ and $c_0$ is an interior row of
height $k$. The block $B^k(x_0)$ contains every fine site within $(L^k-1)\eta/2\ge\eta$ of
$x_0$ in each coordinate, so $p_0\subset B^k(x_0)\subset E_{c_0}$. Orient
$U(\partial p_0)=U(x_0,x_0+\eta e_1)\,U(x_0+\eta e_1,x_0+\eta e_1+\eta e_2)\,
U(x_0+\eta e_2,x_0+\eta e_1+\eta e_2)^{-1}U(x_0,x_0+\eta e_2)^{-1}=V$. Then
$V-I=\mathrm{diag}(-2,-2,0,\dots,0)$, with Frobenius norm $2\sqrt2$ and operator norm 2.

(d) The right side of (D) is $8\sqrt NL^2a_0L^{-2k}\le8\sqrt Na_0$. The right side of the
display is $4(a_0+a_1)L^{-2k}<2$ when $a_0+a_1<1/2$. Round 1 fixes
$a_*=\rho/(16\sqrt NK_*)$, far below both thresholds.

(e) Write $c^{(m)}=(x_0,x_0+\ell_me_1)$, $1\le m\le k$. Since $x_0$ is a level-$k$ site, it is
a level-$m$ site for every $m$, and $c^{(m)}$ is the first coarse bond of the straight path of
$c^{(m+1)}$. Recursion (6) of `library/b98p5.md` forms $W_m=\mathcal M^m(U)$ and evaluates
$c_0$ from $W_{k-1}$ on the straight path of $c_0$, so it needs $W_1(c^{(1)})$. By
`library/b98.md` (5),
$\mathcal M(U)(c^{(1)})=\exp\big(\sum_{x\in B(x_0)}L^{-d}\log\{U(\Gamma_{c^{(1)},x})T^{-1}\}\big)T$
with $T=U(c^{(1)})$, the product along the straight fine path, whose first bond is $b_0$; so
$T=V$. Take $x=x_0+\eta e_2\in B(x_0)$. Its comb path is the one-step connector
$(x_0,x_0+\eta e_2)$, the straight segment from $x$ to $x+\ell_1e_1$ (transverse coordinate
$x_{0,2}+\eta$, so it avoids $b_0$), and the one-step connector back to $x_0+\ell_1e_1$. None
of these is $b_0$, so $U(\Gamma_{c^{(1)},x})=1$ and the argument of that logarithm is
$V^{-1}$. $V^{-1}-I$ has eigenvalue $-2$, so the series $\sum_n(-1)^{n+1}(X-I)^n/n$ diverges
at $X=V^{-1}$, and $V^{-1}$ has eigenvalue $-1$, on the cut of the principal logarithm. The map
is therefore not defined at $U$ in the chart that (5) and `library/b98p5.md` (7)–(8) use. ∎

**What Proposition 1 shows.** (S1)–(S2) do not imply (D) on the account's class $\mathfrak S$,
and not on Bałaban's restricted class either. Statement T of `library/b98p5.md` asks the map
to be defined and holomorphic at every pair of $\mathfrak S$ with $a_0,a_1\le a_*$; part (e)
refutes it. This is a counterexample to the *extension of the averaging remark to all of
$\mathfrak S$*, and to the account's display at 12968–12973 at levels $j\ge1$. It is not a
counterexample to CMP 98 Proposition 5, which assumes the plaquette bound CMP 98 (52), nor to
CMP 99b, whose p. 409 localisation needs the side-12 cubes $\square^5$ around every small cube
of $\mathcal D_j$ and so tacitly needs $\Omega_j$ thick (this last point is PRINT-INVERSE's;
row 428 owns it, and this note does not pursue it).

**Remark (the reading of $O(1)$).** P. 396 says "$O(1)$ will mean a number $\geq10$", and (3.35)
scales the bound with "a size of $\square$". If the class held cubes of *every* side
$S\mathfrak M\ell_q$, $S\ge10$, each with bound $S\mathfrak M\alpha_0/\ell_q$, a cube of index
$k-1$ and side about $(wL+2)\mathfrak M\ell_{k-1}$ would cover the island, and Proposition 1
would fail. That reading is not the account's: CA-I fixes side 12 and bound $a_0$, and
p. 409 fixes "$O(1)$ … equal to 12". Under that other reading (D) would hold only with a
constant of order $L^3\mathfrak M$; this note does not pursue it.
