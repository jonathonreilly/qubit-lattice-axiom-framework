   A(V) = Σ_ω A_ω over the tree walks of §5, absolutely. With
   datum-independent majorants a_ω(b,b'),

       max{ sup_b Σ_{ω,b'}, sup_{b'} Σ_{ω,b} } a_ω(b,b') e^{δ_W D(ω;b,b')} ≤ B_W.   (85)

2. Step domains have ℓ^1-diameter ≤ ρ_w in level-k units. A footprint
   is their union; consecutive domains meet, so its interior is
   connected. A_ω(b,b') = 0 unless b, b' lie in the footprint at
   distance ≥ 1 from its boundary.
3. A_ω is a holomorphic function on 𝒫_F, F its complete read set

4. Equal nest traces and data on F give equal indices, domains,

   Every gauge transformation transports A_ω by the endpoint
   representations. The lattice isometries that preserve the grids and
   the contour and pivot choices of the cells (the fixed cell
   convention) transport A_ω under convention (M).

+ ⟨A, DRD^*A⟩ + ⟨A, Q^*aQ A⟩, 𝓛 = I - D G'R D^* (p. 421).

**Lemma 3.1 (Ward identity).** Let f_V(A) = S(e^{iηA}V), with S the
holomorphic continuation (1/2)(tr W_p + tr W_p^{-1}) of the Wilson
action, V near the real group, J(V) = ∇f_V(0), Δ^W_V = ∇²f_V(0).
Define the local operator 𝒞[J] from site functions to bond functions by

    ⟨B, 𝒞[J]λ⟩ := -(i/2) ⟨J, [B, λ + λ^+]⟩,   λ^+(b) := Ad(V(b)) λ(b_+).   (3.1)

Then Δ^W_V D_V = 𝒞[J(V)], with D_V λ(b) = η^{-1}(λ^+(b) - λ(b_-)).
Signs follow the chart and gauge conventions fixed below; other
conventions change only signs in (3.1).

*Proof.* For a site function λ put u_t = e^{itλ}. Gauge invariance of
traces of plaquette products holds for complex configurations and
gauges, so f_V(A_t) = f_V(A) along the curve defined by
e^{iηA_t(b)} = u_t(b_-) e^{iηA(b)} V(b) u_t(b_+)^{-1} V(b)^{-1}. Write
X = ηA(b), Y = tλ(b_-), Z = tλ^+(b). The Baker-Campbell-Hausdorff
series gives X_t = X + Y - Z - (i/2)[X, Y + Z] + R, where every term of
R has degree at least two in t or at least two in X. Hence
d/dt A_t at t = 0 equals -𝒟_A λ with 𝒟_A λ = D_V λ + (i/2)[A, λ + λ^+]
+ O(|A|^2|λ|). Differentiating f_V(A_t) = f_V(A) in t gives
⟨∇f_V(A), 𝒟_A λ⟩ = 0 for all small A. Differentiating this in A at
A = 0 in direction B gives ⟨Δ^W_V B, D_V λ⟩ + (i/2)⟨J(V), [B, λ+λ^+]⟩
= 0. Δ^W_V is symmetric. ∎

Consequences. At the true current, for all A, λ:

    ⟨A - Dλ, Δ^W(A - Dλ)⟩ = ⟨A, Δ^W A⟩ - c_J((A, λ)),
    c_J((A, λ)) := 2⟨A, 𝒞[J]λ⟩ - ⟨Dλ, 𝒞[J]λ⟩.                          (3.2)

So B15's Ward term V_π = 𝓛^*Δ^W𝓛 - Δ^W is the polarization of
-c_J((A, G'RD^*A)). This is the "two-bracket identity" B15 uses; (3.1)
writes it out. For an **independent** current J, (3.2) defines c_J and
V_π[J] (polarized), linear in J. Since |λ^+| ≤ (1 + Cα_1)|λ|,

    |⟨B, 𝒞[J]λ⟩| ≤ C sup(m^3|J|) ‖m^{-1}B‖_{2,loc} ‖m^{-2}λ‖_{2,loc},          (3.3)

summed over bounded overlaps. No derivative of J occurs.

**Lemma 3.2 (size of the true current).** On the class of §1, with
ε := C_L M_1 α_0,

    sup_b m_b^3 |J(V)(b)| ≤ C_L (ε + α_1) =: γ_V,                           (3.4)

at every bond of Ω_0 whose plaquettes meet Ω_0, and on every set F at
the bonds whose plaquettes lie in F, for data in 𝒫_F.

*Proof.* Real part: B15 (63) in a gauge of (3.35)-(3.36). At bonds of
the bare layer whose plaquettes leave Ω_0, use O's boundary assumption
|P_p - I| ≤ ε η^2 s_p^{-2} = ε (`b102.md` 1610, 1883): each of the
at most six incident plaquettes contributes at most C|P_p - I| η^{-3}.
Complex part: e^{iηB} e^{iηA'} = e^{iη(B ⋆ A')}, with B ⋆ A' =
B + A' + O(η|A'||B|). So J(V) = (I + O(α_1))^T [J(U) + Δ^W_U A' + R_2(A')].
Here |Δ^W_U A'| ≤ |q_U^* q_U A'| + |V_U A'| < α_1 m^{-3} + C ε α_1 m^{-3}
by (3.38) and (H12.3). The remainder R_2 is the quadratic part of the
same finite plaquette expansion as B15 (63), with A' in place of A_0:
|R_2| ≤ C(|A'||∇A'| + |A'|^3 + η|∇A'|^2) ≤ C α_1^2 m^{-3} by (3.37). ∎

**Lemma 4.0 (smooth distance).** For each pillar z put
ψ_z(x) := Σ_y χ_y(x) d_B(c_y, z), c_y the centre of pillar y. Then
|ψ_z - d_B(·, z)| ≤ r_0 + 4L, |Dψ_z| ≤ C_L/m and |D²ψ_z| ≤ C_L/m².

*Proof.* χ_y(x) ≠ 0 forces d_B(c_y, x) ≤ r_0 + 4L; this gives the
first bound. Since Σ_y χ_y ≡ 1, Σ_y Dχ_y ≡ 0 and Σ_y D²χ_y ≡ 0. So
Dψ_z(x) = Σ_y (Dχ_y)(x) [d_B(c_y, z) - d_B(x, z)], and likewise for
D², with the same constant d_B(x, z) subtracted. At most ν_0(L)
cutoffs are nonzero near x, and each bracket is at most r_0 + 4L. ∎

  Its gauge restricts to Q. The bound at the class index exceeds the
  bound at ξ(Q) by at most L, absorbed in C_L.
- (b) `h-walk.md` Lemma C (C1)-(C3) hold for Balaban's nests at walk

  cut-down at block size M' and gap ρ'. (C5) is replaced by
  `h-walk-fix.md` Lemma N.
- (c) Let κ be a walk cube and F ⊃ κ^♯. The restriction of a datum of
  𝒫_F to the cut-down S^κ obeys (3.35)-(3.36) for S^κ at the radius

    ‖X^{-1}‖_{Y*→Y} + ‖T^{-1}‖_{E*→E} + ‖W^{-1}‖_{row→row*} + ‖K_b^{-1}‖_{𝒳*→𝒳} ≤ C_g.   (4.1)

Here a_g, γ_g, C_g depend on L, N and the floors only. Every inverse is
holomorphic in (V, J, j) and real at real data.

*Proof.* Step 1: U real, J = 0, j = 0. The recursive masses of (3.24)
lie between two positive constants fixed by a and L. So (H11.3) gives
⟨ψ, A_s ψ⟩ ≥ c_L(‖Dψ‖² + ‖m^{-1}ψ‖²). For the scale weights, let m̃ be m smoothed

large the conjugated form stays coercive. This gives
‖m^{-2}G'g‖_2 + ‖m^{-1}DG'g‖_2 ≤ C‖g‖_2 (the scale-conjugated form of

G': L² → Λ and, by transposition, Λ* → L². H11's scalar Gram bound
‖G'Q'^*μ‖_2 ≥ c_L ‖μ‖_top bounds N, with the scale factors of

bounds its norm. ‖K_0‖_{E→E*} ≤ C_L because K_0 is a finite stencil
plus DRD^*, and ‖RD^*A‖_2 ≤ ‖D^*A‖_2 ≤ C‖A‖_E.

|⟨A, V_π[J]A'⟩| ≤ C γ_g ‖A‖_E ‖A'‖_E. By N3 (two legs of C^(2) on one

Neumann series around W_0^{-1}.

**Lemma 4.3 (local inverse; replaces `h-walk.md` Lemma L).** Under the
hypotheses of Lemma 4.2, for F ∈ 𝒳* supported near the pillar z and
every pillar y,

    ‖K_b^{-1} F‖_{𝒳,y} ≤ B_L e^{-τ_L d_B(y,z)} ‖F‖_{𝒳*},
    τ_L := min{1, (2 C_g C_K)^{-1}},  B_L := C_L C_g,                       (4.2)

with C_K the constant in (4.3). τ_L and B_L depend on L, N and the
floors only.

*Proof.* Let 0 < τ ≤ τ_L and θ := τ ψ_z (Lemma 4.0). We claim

    ‖e^θ K_b e^{-θ} - K_b‖_{𝒳→𝒳*} ≤ C_K τ.                               (4.3)

The entry at (x, x') is multiplied by e^{θ(x)-θ(x')} - 1. This is the
commutator estimate of `h-walk.md` Lemma K, with e^θ in place of the
cutoff h̃_κ and τ in place of C/M_w. Its four cases carry over with the
gate's repairs. Multiplication blocks (identity, masses, 𝒞[J], the
coefficient j): a base-point shift costs Cτ. First-order blocks: the
factor (e^{Δθ} - 1)/η across a fine bond is ≤ Cτ/m, against the m^{-1}
part of the norm. Second-order blocks (A_s, Δ^W, the pairing
⟨D^*A, A_s λ⟩): the discrete product rule leaves a first-order term
with coefficient ≤ Cτ/m and a zeroth-order term with coefficient
≤ C(τ/m)² + Cτ/m², by Lemma 4.0; the pairing is split as in
`gates/h-walk-gate.md` §5, so no third difference of λ occurs. Averaging
blocks (Q, Q', C^(2)): the oscillation of e^θ over a carrier of
diameter C_L m is ≤ C_L τ, times the absolute row and column sums of
N1 (H10) and N3. A merely Lipschitz weight would fail on the graph
norm (`gates/h-walk-gate.md` §5); θ has two bounded differences, so it
does not.

By (4.1) and (4.3), e^θ K_b e^{-θ} = K_b (I + K_b^{-1}(e^θ K_b e^{-θ} - K_b))
with the bracket of norm ≤ C_g C_K τ ≤ 1/2. So
‖e^θ K_b^{-1} e^{-θ}‖_{𝒳*→𝒳} ≤ 2C_g, on the finite lattice where all
these matrices exist. For F supported near z, θ ≤ τ C_L on its
support, so ‖e^θ F‖_{𝒳*} ≤ C_L ‖F‖_{𝒳*}. On pillar y, θ ≥ τ d_B(y,z)
- τ C_L, and multiplication by e^{-θ} on that pillar is bounded with
its two differences. Hence (4.2). ∎

So the analytic input of the whole walk construction reduces to the
global bound (4.1). That bound reads only coercivity (N1), a bounded
right inverse (N2), the second jet (N3) and the complex averages (N4).
No decay estimate of O (H13) or B14 is used, and no conjugation of a
graph-norm space by a Lipschitz weight.

## 6. Assembly of (84), the bound (85), and clauses 2-4

**The terms.** For level-k walk cubes κ meeting the index set, and tree
walks ω,

    A^loc_κ := θ_κ C_♭^T (𝖦 - a) C_♭,
    A^G_ω  := -C_♭^T [T^(1)_ω]_{νν,act} C_♭,
    A^E_ω  := -C_♭^T 𝒟[j^(1)_ω] C_♭,                                     (6.1)

with θ_κ evaluated at the midpoint of the left index (convention (M)),
"act" the level-k rows touching Λ, and A^E_ω re-rooted at its last
trunk cube. By (1.1), Σ_κ θ_κ = 1 at every location ((M), (m1)),
the linearity of 𝒟 in its coefficient, and §5 item 1,

    A(V) = Σ_κ A^loc_κ + Σ_ω (A^G_ω + A^E_ω),   absolutely.                 (6.2)

**Step domains.** For a cube κ of ω put Dom(κ) := κ^♯ enlarged by 4L
in level-k units. It contains the cell X_κ, the reads of the local
inverse (κ^(4)), the boxes λ^(4) supplying j^(0) to K_κ (Lemma N2:
λ^♭ ⊂ κ^♯), the plaquettes read by J(V), and the stencils of C_♭, 𝖦
and 𝒟 (N5: C_♭ reads B(e_±) ∪ B(e), side ≤ 2L). For a level-k cube
its ℓ^1-diameter is at most 4(8L^2 M_w + 8L) ≤ 40L^2 M_w = ρ_w, and
cubes of lower level are smaller. In a tree walk every cell overlaps
its parent, so consecutive domains meet; the footprint
|ω| := ∪ Dom(κ) has connected interior.

**Clause 2 (support).** The output of T^(1)_ω lies in supp h̃_{κ_0}
⊂ κ_0^(2), and its input is read on supp θ_{κ_n}; C_♭ moves arguments
by at most 2L. The cubes κ_0, κ_n of a nonzero A^G_ω are level-k cubes,
since active rows lie at distance > R_1 M_1 from lower layers. So
A^G_ω(b, b') ≠ 0 forces b ∈ κ_0^(2) + 2L and b' ∈ κ_n^(1/4) + 2L + L.
Both lie in the footprint at distance ≥ (4L^2 - 3)M_w ≥ 1 from its
boundary. A^E_ω lives within C_L of the coefficient rows in
supp θ_{κ_n}, and A^loc_κ within 2L of supp θ_κ. Same margin.

**Kernel bounds.** C_♭ has entries ≤ C(d,L) and range ≤ 2L, 𝖦 is
block diagonal with entries ≤ C_{L,N} (N5; `ol.md` (11.6)), and 𝒟
obeys (HD) with constant C_D. With (5.1), at unit row scale,

    |A_ω(b, b')| ≤ C_A (C_T M_w^{-1/3})^{|ω|} e^{-(τ_L/8) d_B(ω; b, b')},
    C_A := C_L (B_1 + C_D C_J γ_V + 1),                                   (6.3)

where d_B(ω; b, b') is the least d_B-length of a tree through b, b'
meeting every cell (`gates/h-walk-gate.md` §8 support check).

**Comparison of metrics.** Every density of d_B is ≥ 1 in level-k
units, and ℓ^1 ≤ 2 ℓ^2 in four dimensions. A tree for d_B(ω; b, b')
meets every cell, hence every domain. Approximating it by a lattice
tree costs at most C(|ω| + 2) in length. So

    D(ω; b, b') ≤ 2 d_B(ω; b, b') + C(|ω| + 2).                          (6.6)

This holds for either reading of CMP 96's distance (§1): if D is
measured in local units, D ≤ C d_B directly.

**The majorants and (85).** Put

    a_ω(b, b') := C_A e^{C τ_L} (C_T e^{Cτ_L} M_w^{-1/3})^{|ω|} e^{-(τ_L/16) D(ω; b, b')}
                  × 1[b, b' obey the support condition of clause 2].       (6.4)

By (6.3) and (6.6), |A_ω| ≤ a_ω. The majorant depends on the geometry
of ω's domains and on b, b' only, not on the datum, the nest outside
the footprint, the mesh, the depth, the volume or M. Take
δ_W := τ_L/32. Fix b. Its roots: at most ν_0(L) cubes. Tree walks of
size m from a root: at most C_0 C_1^m. Indices b' allowed by the
support condition, for given ω: at most C_L M_w^4. Hence

    sup_b Σ_{ω,b'} a_ω(b,b') e^{δ_W D(ω;b,b')}
       ≤ ν_0 C_L M_w^4 C_A e^{Cτ_L} Σ_m C_0 (C_1 C_T e^{Cτ_L} M_w^{-1/3})^m ≤ B_W,      (6.5)

once M_w ≥ (2 C_1 C_T e^{Cτ_L})^3. The column sum is the same,
counting tree walks backwards from their last cube (`gates/h-walk-gate.md`
§§7-8). This is (85).

conventions. So this construction also gives, at small independent

precision, for this assignment. Whether it meets every clause of
CA-I (W') (its domain 𝒰(1) ⊃ P' ∪ P_{2ρ}, its exact constants) is not
