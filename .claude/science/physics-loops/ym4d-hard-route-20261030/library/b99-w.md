## 1. What is attacked, and the readings used

the precision (84) of `b99.md` is

    Δ^(k) = 𝖦 + (Q G_1 Q^*)^{-1} - a - 𝒟[𝔧],    𝔧 = H_1^* J.     (1.1)

Here 𝖦(B) = ‖𝔤(B)‖², with 𝔤(B)(y,x) the linear part of

CMP 99b (3.128)-(3.129), so G_1 carries both corrections of B15: the

## 2. Result

**Theorem W-b (Lemma W as an implication).** Fix d = 4, N ≥ 2, odd
L ≥ L_0. There are, as functions of L and N only: floors M', ρ', a walk
scale M_w (a power of L), constants δ_W > 0, B_W, ρ_w = 40 L^2 M_w,
and a ceiling a_W > 0 (decreased once more after C_D of (HD) is fixed),
such that the following holds for every M_1 ≥ M_w and R_1 ≥ 8L^3
(powers of L), every k ≥ 0, mesh, volume and nest pair as in §1, and
M_1 α_0, α_1 ≤ a_W.

1. The inverses in (1.1) exist on the whole class, and
   A(V) = Σ_ω A_ω over the tree walks of §5, absolutely. With
   datum-independent majorants a_ω(b,b'),

       max{ sup_b Σ_{ω,b'}, sup_{b'} Σ_{ω,b} } a_ω(b,b') e^{δ_W D(ω;b,b')} ≤ B_W.   (85)

2. Step domains have ℓ^1-diameter ≤ ρ_w in level-k units. A footprint
   is their union; consecutive domains meet, so its interior is
   connected. A_ω(b,b') = 0 unless b, b' lie in the footprint at
   distance ≥ 1 from its boundary.
3. A_ω is a holomorphic function on 𝒫_F, F its complete read set
   (footprint), and uses no datum off F.
4. Equal nest traces and data on F give equal indices, domains,
   majorants and terms, also across tori related by a grid translation.
   Every gauge transformation transports A_ω by the endpoint
   representations. The lattice isometries that preserve the grids and
   the contour and pivot choices of the cells (the fixed cell
   convention) transport A_ω under convention (M).

Nothing depends on the later expansion size M. The terms are real at
real data.

## 3. The unfolded system for Balaban's operators

Notation as in `b102.md` H9-H12 and `b99.md` B15: physical bilinear
pairings (η^4 per fine bond or site, s^4 per row), local scale m = s
of the layer, D = D_V, A_s = Δ'_a = D^*D + Q'^*aQ' with the recursive
masses of CMP 99b (3.24), Dirichlet condition on Ω_0^c (3.27). Adjoints
are bilinear transposes, also at complex V. Q is the multi-layer comb
average of (3.16) (Q_j on Λ_j) and C^(2) its second-order term, with
the per-length normalization fixed in B15's formula reconstruction, so
that (3.128) reads ⟨A, G_1^{-1}A⟩ = ⟨𝓛A, Δ^W 𝓛A⟩ - 2⟨C^(2)(𝓛A), H^*J⟩
+ ⟨A, DRD^*A⟩ + ⟨A, Q^*aQ A⟩, 𝓛 = I - D G'R D^* (p. 421).

**Lemma 3.1 (Ward identity).** Let f_V(A) = S(e^{iηA}V), with S the
holomorphic continuation (1/2)(tr W_p + tr W_p^{-1}) of the Wilson
action, V near the real group, J(V) = ∇f_V(0), Δ^W_V = ∇²f_V(0).
Define the local operator 𝒞[J] from site functions to bond functions by

    ⟨B, 𝒞[J]λ⟩ := -(i/2) ⟨J, [B, λ + λ^+]⟩,   λ^+(b) := Ad(V(b)) λ(b_+).   (3.1)

Then Δ^W_V D_V = 𝒞[J(V)], with D_V λ(b) = η^{-1}(λ^+(b) - λ(b_-)).
Signs follow the chart and gauge conventions fixed below; other
conventions change only signs in (3.1).

*Proof.* For a site function λ put u_t = e^{itλ}. Gauge invariance of
traces of plaquette products holds for complex configurations and
gauges, so f_V(A_t) = f_V(A) along the curve defined by
e^{iηA_t(b)} = u_t(b_-) e^{iηA(b)} V(b) u_t(b_+)^{-1} V(b)^{-1}. Write
X = ηA(b), Y = tλ(b_-), Z = tλ^+(b). The Baker-Campbell-Hausdorff
series gives X_t = X + Y - Z - (i/2)[X, Y + Z] + R, where every term of
R has degree at least two in t or at least two in X. Hence
d/dt A_t at t = 0 equals -𝒟_A λ with 𝒟_A λ = D_V λ + (i/2)[A, λ + λ^+]
+ O(|A|^2|λ|). Differentiating f_V(A_t) = f_V(A) in t gives
⟨∇f_V(A), 𝒟_A λ⟩ = 0 for all small A. Differentiating this in A at
A = 0 in direction B gives ⟨Δ^W_V B, D_V λ⟩ + (i/2)⟨J(V), [B, λ+λ^+]⟩
= 0. Δ^W_V is symmetric. ∎

Consequences. At the true current, for all A, λ:

    ⟨A - Dλ, Δ^W(A - Dλ)⟩ = ⟨A, Δ^W A⟩ - c_J((A, λ)),
    c_J((A, λ)) := 2⟨A, 𝒞[J]λ⟩ - ⟨Dλ, 𝒞[J]λ⟩.                          (3.2)

So B15's Ward term V_π = 𝓛^*Δ^W𝓛 - Δ^W is the polarization of
-c_J((A, G'RD^*A)). This is the "two-bracket identity" B15 uses; (3.1)
writes it out. For an **independent** current J, (3.2) defines c_J and
V_π[J] (polarized), linear in J. Since |λ^+| ≤ (1 + Cα_1)|λ|,

    |⟨B, 𝒞[J]λ⟩| ≤ C sup(m^3|J|) ‖m^{-1}B‖_{2,loc} ‖m^{-2}λ‖_{2,loc},          (3.3)

summed over bounded overlaps. No derivative of J occurs.

**Lemma 3.2 (size of the true current).** On the class of §1, with
ε := C_L M_1 α_0,

    sup_b m_b^3 |J(V)(b)| ≤ C_L (ε + α_1) =: γ_V,                           (3.4)

at every bond of Ω_0 whose plaquettes meet Ω_0, and on every set F at
the bonds whose plaquettes lie in F, for data in 𝒫_F.

*Proof.* Real part: B15 (63) in a gauge of (3.35)-(3.36). At bonds of
the bare layer whose plaquettes leave Ω_0, use O's boundary assumption
|P_p - I| ≤ ε η^2 s_p^{-2} = ε (`b102.md` 1610, 1883): each of the
at most six incident plaquettes contributes at most C|P_p - I| η^{-3}.
Complex part: e^{iηB} e^{iηA'} = e^{iη(B ⋆ A')}, with B ⋆ A' =
B + A' + O(η|A'||B|). So J(V) = (I + O(α_1))^T [J(U) + Δ^W_U A' + R_2(A')].
Here |Δ^W_U A'| ≤ |q_U^* q_U A'| + |V_U A'| < α_1 m^{-3} + C ε α_1 m^{-3}
by (3.38) and (H12.3). The remainder R_2 is the quadratic part of the
same finite plaquette expansion as B15 (63), with A' in place of A_0:
|R_2| ≤ C(|A'||∇A'| + |A'|^3 + η|∇A'|^2) ≤ C α_1^2 m^{-3} by (3.37). ∎

**The system.** Fix an admissible nest S, a pair P = (V, J), J
independent, and a row coefficient j. Spaces as in `h-walk.md` §4:
𝒳 = E ⊕ Y ⊕ Y ⊕ row*, E: ‖∇_V A‖² + ‖m^{-1}A‖²; Y = (h, λ, μ) with the
graph norm ‖m^{-2}λ‖² + ‖m^{-1}Dλ‖² + ‖A_s λ‖² on λ, ‖m^2 μ‖_top on μ;
row*: Σ_c s_c^6 |ν_c|². Put X(h, λ, μ) = (h + A_s λ, A_s h - Q'^*μ,
-Q'λ), BA = (D^*A, 0, 0), ΠY = λ, and

    q((A, λ)) = ⟨A, (Δ^W + Q^*aQ) A⟩ + ⟨D^*A, A_s λ⟩ - c_J((A, λ))
                - ⟨A - Dλ, Δ^(2)[j](A - Dλ)⟩,                              (3.5)

with ⟨B, Δ^(2)[j]B⟩ := 2⟨C^(2)(B), j⟩_rows. K_b = K_b[S, P, j] has the
rows of `h-walk.md` §4 with these blocks:

    A: q_AA A + q_Aλ ΠY - B^*Z + Q^*ν;   Y: Π^*q_λA A + Π^*q_λλ ΠY + XZ;
    Z: -BA + XY;                          ν: QA.

Every block is a finite stencil or a comb row/pillar average, with
coefficients holomorphic in V and affine in (J, j). K_b is symmetric
for the bilinear pairing.

**Lemma 3.3 (identification).** Suppose X, T and W := Q T^{-1} Q^* are
invertible, where L = Π X^{-1} B and T = q_AA + q_Aλ L + L^* q_λA +
L^* q_λλ L. Then K_b is invertible, (4.1)-(4.2) of `h-walk.md` give
K_b^{-1}, and

    L = G'RD^*,   T = Δ^W + DRD^* + Q^*aQ + V_π[J] - 𝓛^*Δ^(2)[j]𝓛,
    [K_b^{-1}]_{νν} = -W^{-1},   [K_b^{-1}]_{Aν} = T^{-1}Q^*W^{-1}.         (3.6)

At J = J(V): T|_{j=0} = G^{-1} of CMP 99b (3.120), and
[K_b^{-1}]_{Aν}|_{j=0} = H of (3.126). At J = J(V), j = H^*J:
T = G_1^{-1} of (3.128), [K_b^{-1}]_{νν} = -(QG_1Q^*)^{-1}, and
[K_b^{-1}]_{Aν} = H_1 of (3.129).

*Proof.* The elimination is `h-walk.md` Lemma U, whose algebra Codex
redid line by line (`gates/h-walk-gate.md` §4, first failing step
none). It uses only: X symmetric, X^{-1}(s,0,0) has λ-part G'Rs (from
the three scalar equations, with N = (Q'G'^2Q'^*)^{-1} existing by
`b102.md` H11), R symmetric for the site pairing, and the definition
(3.5). Then A_s G'R D^*A = RD^*A gives ⟨D^*A, A_s LA⟩ = ⟨A, DRD^*A⟩,
and A - DLA = 𝓛A. So q((A, LA)) = ⟨A, TA⟩ with T as displayed. At the
true current, (3.2) turns ⟨A, Δ^W A⟩ - c_J((A, LA)) into ⟨𝓛A, Δ^W 𝓛A⟩,
which is (3.120) and, with the C^(2) term at j = H^*J, (3.128). The
blocks then give (3.126) and (3.129) by their minimizer
characterization QH_1 B = B, RD^*H_1B = 0 (CA-II 9096-9099). ∎
