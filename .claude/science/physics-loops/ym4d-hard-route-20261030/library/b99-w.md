## 1. What is attacked, and the readings used

**The operator.** By CA-II 8427-8430, 9095-9101 and 10050-10067,
the precision (84) of `b99.md` is

    A(V) = C_♭^T Δ^(k) C_♭,
    Δ^(k) = 𝖦 + (Q G_1 Q^*)^{-1} - a - 𝒟[𝔧],    𝔧 = H_1^* J.     (1.1)

Here 𝖦(B) = ‖𝔤(B)‖², with 𝔤(B)(y,x) the linear part of
(1/i) log[(e^{iB}V)(y,x) V(y,x)^{-1}], the exponential gauge of CMP 109
(2.5) (CA-II 9047-9085). C_♭ solves Q^♭B = 0 in the pivot bonds
b_0(e) (CA-II 9808-9810, 10084-10088). Q, G_1, H_1, a are those of
CMP 99b (3.128)-(3.129), so G_1 carries both corrections of B15: the
Ward term V_π and the constraint term V_2. 𝒟[f] is the symmetric
operator of the block-local form ⟨D̃^(2)(B,B), f⟩, D̃^(2) = hC^(2),
under the account's hypothesis (HD) (CA-II 10035-10067). J = J(V) is
the Wilson current of V. The index set is the free bonds touching Λ
(or of Ω_{k+1}, CA-II 8424); restriction to it is a compression and
changes nothing below (`h-walk.md` (H4)).

## 2. Result

**Theorem W-b (Lemma W as an implication).** Fix d = 4, N ≥ 2, odd
L ≥ L_0. There are, as functions of L and N only: floors M', ρ', a walk
scale M_w (a power of L), constants δ_W > 0, B_W, ρ_w = 40 L^2 M_w,
and a ceiling a_W > 0 (decreased once more after C_D of (HD) is fixed),
such that the following holds for every M_1 ≥ M_w and R_1 ≥ 8L^3
(powers of L), every k ≥ 0, mesh, volume and nest pair as in §1, and
M_1 α_0, α_1 ≤ a_W.

1. The inverses in (1.1) exist on the whole class, and
   A(V) = Σ_ω A_ω over the tree walks of §5, absolutely. With
   datum-independent majorants a_ω(b,b'),

       max{ sup_b Σ_{ω,b'}, sup_{b'} Σ_{ω,b} } a_ω(b,b') e^{δ_W D(ω;b,b')} ≤ B_W.   (85)

2. Step domains have ℓ^1-diameter ≤ ρ_w in level-k units. A footprint
   is their union; consecutive domains meet, so its interior is
   connected. A_ω(b,b') = 0 unless b, b' lie in the footprint at
   distance ≥ 1 from its boundary.
3. A_ω is a holomorphic function on 𝒫_F, F its complete read set
   (footprint), and uses no datum off F.
4. Equal nest traces and data on F give equal indices, domains,
   majorants and terms, also across tori related by a grid translation.
   Every gauge transformation transports A_ω by the endpoint
   representations. The lattice isometries that preserve the grids and
   the contour and pivot choices of the cells (the fixed cell
   convention) transport A_ω under convention (M).

Nothing depends on the later expansion size M. The terms are real at
real data.
