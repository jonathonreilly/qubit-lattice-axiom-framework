## 0. Result

**What is proved.**

1. *(CI-form) for the regular gas, for every potential generated by a term of the class kinds* [P, as an implication]. At a main-step input structure σ of level i and at every complex input pair P′ ∈ 𝒪_σ (§1.3), the regular gas built from the fibre precision 𝖠(P′) and from the potentials of the kinds (W), (S), (Z°), (E, R), (B°) (§5.1) has activities and local logarithms with SA's unmarked-majorant bounds (UM24) and (UM30), the Kotecký–Preiss criterion (UM27) with strict room (B1), holomorphy in P′ on the fixed-U fibre, and these bounds uniformly on the ordinary product box (B4, ordinary part). This is Theorem UM(P′) (§6), Theorem B1(P′) and Theorem B4(P′) (§7). The inputs are Theorem V′ of NL1-L (old factors), Theorem B of PR-lay with IN1 Proposition A.5 and Theorem A.6 (the Gaussian), and the per-cube size inputs that PAN already uses ((L-W), (L-S), (L-Z), (L-reg), (L-B°)).
2. *The localisation that feeds UM1* [P]. Lemma 5.2 turns each class term into potentials with exact locality and the bound o_f(𝔟)(e^{κ̄} − 1)^{−|Y∖X_f|}. Proposition 5.3 gives (UM1) with amplitude a^{cl} ≤ 2(𝔟/R)N_{cl} + a_W, where 𝔟/R = A₁ℓ^{𝔭₀−q} → 0. So a ≤ a_*/2 is a ceiling on γ after M.
3. *(CI-form)(ii) at P′* [P from PR-lay]: Lemma 4.1 and Proposition 4.2 give (UM4)–(UM6) at P′, with K = K₀, ε ≤ min{ε₀, 1/(3DM⁴)} and C₀^{UM} ≤ 1, on the coarse discs |s_Δ| < e^{κ̄}.
4. *LF1(4) at P′* [P, as an implication]: Proposition 8.1 gives Re Ψ_ω(ξ; P′) ≤ C_ΨN₀(ω) at every P′ ∈ 𝒪_τ from LF1's per-site bounds (Size-isl), (Loc-isl). These read the pair only through admitted carried terms and through κ(P′). So no inclusion of the ST1 domain in SA's U^c_i is needed (§8.3).
5. *CI by induction over structures* [P, as a reduction]: Theorem CI(σ) (§10) gives CI at σ from CI before σ and items 1–4, by Proposition CI-ind of NL1-L. It covers every regular-gas activity at a main-step structure with no exempt term, and every island exponent at its formation.

**What is not proved: the first remaining Outstanding statement.**

**Lemma TD ((UM1) for the exempt terms)** [O], §9. Call a term *exempt* if it is attached to the live large-field region: a standing island (I), a kept form (K), a factor or term kept by a drop-out, or a boundary term whose minimising exempt tree has a nonempty island family. The statement: the part Σ_{f∈𝔉^{ex}}[F_f(P′; 𝟏, B) − f₀(P′)] of the gas exponent has an exact local decomposition Σ_{W}V^{ex}(W; P′, B) over nonempty sets W of fine cubes of the gas region, natural candidate the cube Möbius form (5.5), such that

  |V^{ex}(W; P′, B)| ≤ a^{ex}e^{−λ_Td_f(W)}  for every P′ ∈ 𝒪_σ and every real B in the box on W,   (TD)

with λ_T ≥ λ_V and a^{ex} → 0 as γ → 0 after M. With (TD), (CI-form) for the regular gas holds for all seven kinds. Without it, I found no way to put the dependence of exempt terms on the gas fluctuation into SA's potential format (UM1). Three attacks, each failing for an exact reason (§9.2):

- *A1. Switch Möbius* (SA L17913–17920; ST1 L884). The only bound the switch interface gives on a Möbius piece is (e^{κ̄} − 1)^{−1} per new cube. An exempt support contains a cell of side ≥ Ř_i fine cubes, so it has at least 8Ř_i³ boundary cubes. Decorations hung on them multiply the bound of a single-cube potential by at least exp(4e^{−κ̄}Ř_i³ − e^{−κ̄}(10LŘ_i + 2)/2). Even granting SA's amplitude e^{−cMŘ_i} (SA L17927–17930), the gain is only e^{−(κ̄+cM+1)(10LŘ_i+2)}. Since Ř_i ≥ (log θ_{i−1} − 1)^r → ∞ while κ̄, M, L are fixed before γ, the bound fails. Setting the switches to 1 off the gas removes the decorations but leaves two-cube pieces at any mutual distance with no decay.
- *A2. PAN shells* (PAN Proposition 6.1). There is no entropy, but the n-th shell piece reads N_{n+1}(X_f) ∩ 𝒞_Λ, at least 8(Ř_i + 2n)³ cubes. (UM1) asks for e^{−λ_T(8(Ř_i+2n)³−1)}, and the shell bound is e^{−cMŘ_i − κ̄n}.
- *A3. Cube Möbius on the screened polydisc* (NL1-L Proposition S). Iterated Schwarz gives (2𝔟/R_f^{scr})^{|W|}, and R_f^{scr} is capped by R_cap ≍ e^{−Cκ̄}/g (NL1-L L399). For W = {Δ₁, Δ₂} at fine-cube distance D, (UM1) asks for e^{−λ_TD}, while the bound is ≍ b_fg²𝔟²e^{2Cκ̄}, independent of D. A per-cube radius would need a row-by-row response bound. DR's (16) (ST1 L119–124), read as (4.1) of NL1-L (L259–264), bounds the response by the sup of the source and the distance to its *whole* support.

The exact gap is this. ST1's response interface (switch holomorphy plus a sup-norm bound) does not encode multi-point decay. A row-resolved or walk-resolved form of DR, (DR-row) of §9.3, with a composition lemma, would. That is the four-dimensional form of Y3's per-string hull products (Y3 L52582–52585). The conditional-law route of PAN and TT does not need Lemma TD: PAN's (P-an) is a bond-pair bound (PAN L367–375), for which shells suffice.

**Imports that carry their own status** (§12.2): the structural identities of the actual gas (heredity, normalised functional, wall factorisation; IN1 (I-3), [SS]), used for exactness and heredity, not for the bounds; the per-cube sizes (L-reg) of renormalised regular members, which contain SA's (OSF3) renormalisation assertion (SA L19666–19669: "These summations do not prove (OSF3)"); (W-in′), a reading of Balaban's 𝐏 at P′; LF1's per-site bounds (Size-isl), (Loc-isl) (SA L18078–18095, [SS]); and SC1's insertion slot.

**Two corrections to the statement of (CI-form) in NL1-L L518.** (a) (CI-form)(i) reads "every old factor ... satisfies the conclusions of Theorem V′ at P′ with radius R". That is enough for the class kinds (Proposition 5.3). It is not enough for the exempt kinds: Theorem V′'s conclusions are a sup-norm statement on a polydisc, and §9.2 shows that no localisation built only on them gives (UM1). (b) The first open item inside (CI-form) is no longer ⟦PR-lay⟧: Corollary B.6 of PR-lay supplies (ii) at every P′ obeying (I-G) (PR L374–376). It is Lemma TD.

## 1. Adopted design, the domain, and the restated interface

### 1.1 The radius table (Tq0)

**[design]** The radius table is SA's: r_ι(m) := C_ιθ_m^{−1/2}(log θ_m)^q, ι = 0, 1 (SA L6033, as NL1-L L19 quotes it; IN1 L44). The lift is λ ≡ 1 (Proposition U of NL1-L, NL1-L L221–233). NL1-L recommends this design (NL1-L L239: "(Tq0) is simpler than (Tε) with smoothed lifts and lifted cores, and it is SA's radius table"). Under (Tq0) every formation statement of ST1 and ST3 holds for γ below the ceilings (U1)–(U3) (NL1-L L221–226). C₀, C₁ are row-16 constants, chosen after M and κ̄ (C4 L40, quoted at NL1-L L119).

Notation at the main step of level i (NL1-L §1.2, L57–58): ℓ := log θ_{i−1}, g := θ_{i−1}^{−1/2}, 𝔟 := A₁ℓ^{𝔭₀} the window radius in scaled units, R := c_Rℓ^q with c_R := 1 (NL1-L Lemma R, L389). Since q > 𝔭₀ + 2r (IN1 L347), 𝔟/R = A₁ℓ^{𝔭₀−q} → 0 as γ → 0.

### 1.2 Design clause (I-G)

**[design]** (I-G) of PR-lay (PR L201–206), with λ_σ ≡ 1: at every main-step input structure σ, every input pair P′ = (U, A′, 𝐉′) of the consumer's domain satisfies, on every bond b of Ω₀^σ, with m = m_σ(b) the level of b in the nest in force and l(b) its local scale,

  l(b)|A′(b)|, l(b)²|∇_UA′(b)| < (1 + β_s)r₁(m),  l(b)³|𝐉′(b)| < (1 + β_s)r₀(m).   (1.1)

PR-lay observes that (1.1) is SA's design (SA L6141–6152, read below in §3) and that it holds as soon as the domain retains the pointwise records formed at the structures between the last raising of each bond and σ, which is ST1's chain-domain rule (ST1 L84, "𝒟_{a|σ} imposes every record born between a and σ"; PR L340). PR-lay's Lemma B.3 then gives κ(P′) ≤ ϱ_c(γ) with ϱ_c(γ) := (1 + β_s)max{C₀, C₁}sup_mθ_m^{−1/2}(log θ_m)^q → 0 (PR L332–336; with λ = 1 the factor C_λ(log θ)^{2r_R} is absent).

### 1.3 The domain 𝒪_σ

Fix a main-step input structure σ of level i: the transition (i − 1, out) → (i, in) of ST1 (NL1-L L57). Let U be its real background (a function of the retained variables R).

**Definition 1.1.** 𝒪_σ is the set of complex input triples P′ = (U, A′, 𝐉′) with the fixed real factor U that lie in the round-3 chain domain 𝒟_{a|+} with every working core and every ceiling of ST1 hypothesis 2 (ST3 L399), as in Theorem V′ (NL1-L L377), and that satisfy (1.1). 𝒪_σ(U) denotes this fibre; activities are holomorphic on it and Borel in U (ST1 L101).

By §1.2, (1.1) is implied by the chain-domain rule for chains that start no later than the structures at which the relevant pointwise records were formed. So 𝒪_σ is the ST1 chain domain at σ, under (Tq0). [design, as PR-lay §6.3 argues; not re-proved here]

### 1.4 The interface CI, restated

**CI (restated)** [the statement this draft serves]. For every carried activity f formed at a structure σ by NL1 or LF1, its declared domain contains 𝒪_σ, and on it f has the interface of ST1 L101 (bounded by b_f, holomorphic in (A′, 𝐉′) with U fixed, Borel in U and near stored variables, jointly holomorphic in far variables and switches, real gauge covariant) with:

- *(order)* no constant fixed before κ reads b_f;
- *(where b_f enters)* b_f enters only Theorem 14's output bound (77) (ST1 L884: "Φ_Y(s, z) − f₀ ... bounded by 2b_f") and PAN's ceiling X_P (PAN L367–371, through N^♮).

This is NL1-L's correction of ST3's wording (NL1-L L534: "What ST1 and (L) need is weaker and true: no constant fixed before κ reads b_f"; ST3 L256 had "fixed before κ and independent of M, κ̄").

### 4.3 Holomorphy of the Gaussian functional in the pair

Put c_K := 1/(K + K³) and ε′(K) := min{ε₀(K), 1/(20K²(K + 1)³(K + K³)), 1}, ρ′(K) := min{ρ₀(K), c_K/8}. These are functions of K only. In the rest of this draft ε₀ and ρ₀ denote ε′(K₀) and ρ′(K₀); IN1's (A.9)–(A.11) and Theorem A.6 hold verbatim with these smaller numbers, which are still determined before κ (IN1 Theorem A.7, row 8′).

**Lemma 4.3.** [P] Fix Z₀ and s with |s_Δ| < e^{κ̄}. Let F(P′; b) be Borel on 𝒪_σ(U) × ℝ^m, holomorphic in P′ for each b, with |F(P′; b)| ≤ F₀e^{ρ|b|²}, ρ ≤ ρ₀. Then P′ ↦ 𝒥_{C(s;P′),Γ(s;P′)}[F(P′; ·)] is holomorphic on 𝒪_σ(U), jointly with s, and real when P′, s and F are real.

*Proof.* *Step 1 (the matrices).* 𝖠(P′) is a fixed function of (A′, 𝐉′) at fixed U, holomorphic on the class (PR-lay (I-C)(H3), PR L189–191: "each 𝖠_ω is a fixed function, holomorphic in the complex variables (A′, 𝐉) at fixed U on the class"; the walk series converges absolutely and locally uniformly by (H1)–(H2)). The decoupled matrix 𝖠(P′)^s has entries 𝖠(P′)(x, y)s^{D(x,y)}, polynomial in s (IN1 Definition A.3). Its compression has real part ≥ 3a/4 (Proposition 4.2(i)), so C(s; P′) is holomorphic (locally a Neumann series). The inverse root R(B) := (1/π)∫₀^∞y^{−1/2}(y + B)^{−1}dy is holomorphic in B on {Re B ≥ a}: the integrand is holomorphic in B and bounded by y^{−1/2}(y + a)^{−1}, which is integrable, so Lemma 5.5 of PAN (PAN L206–212) applies with the measure y^{−1/2}dy. So Γ(s; P′) is holomorphic.

*Step 2 (the density).* By (FG6) (SA L13959–13962), under (FG1) with ε ≤ ε₀(K) and ρ ≤ ρ₀(K), 𝒥_{C,Γ}[F] = ∫F dμ_D with D = C + CΓΓ^𝖳C and dμ_D(b) = (2π)^{−m/2}(det D)^{−1/2}e^{−b^𝖳D^{−1}b/2}db (FG2)–(FG3). At the reference, D₀ = C₀ + C₀Γ₀Γ₀^𝖳C₀ ≥ C₀ ≥ ‖C₀^{−1}‖^{−1} ≥ 1/K₀ and ‖D₀‖ ≤ K₀ + K₀³, so D₀^{−1} ≥ c_{K₀}. Write D − D₀ as the five-term telescoping sum (C − C₀) + (C − C₀)ΓΓ^𝖳C + C₀(Γ − Γ₀)Γ^𝖳C + C₀Γ₀(Γ − Γ₀)^𝖳C + C₀Γ₀Γ₀^𝖳(C − C₀). With ‖C‖, ‖Γ‖ ≤ K₀ + 1 (for ε ≤ 1) each term is at most (K₀ + 1)³ε, so ‖D − D₀‖ ≤ 5(K₀ + 1)³ε. These are operator norms; no dimension enters. Since ε ≤ ε₀ ≤ 1/(20K₀²(K₀ + 1)³(K₀ + K₀³)), ‖D − D₀‖‖D₀^{−1}‖ ≤ ½. So D is invertible, ‖D^{−1}‖ ≤ 2K₀, and ‖D^{−1} − D₀^{−1}‖ ≤ ‖D^{−1}‖‖D − D₀‖‖D₀^{−1}‖ ≤ c_{K₀}/2. For real b, Re(b^𝖳D^{−1}b) = b^𝖳(Re D^{−1})b ≥ b^𝖳D₀^{−1}b − (c_{K₀}/2)|b|² ≥ (c_{K₀}/2)|b|².

*Step 3 (the integral).* Let 𝒦 be a compact neighbourhood of a given (P′, s). On 𝒦 the map (P′, s) ↦ D is continuous, so |det D|^{−1/2} ≤ c(𝒦) < ∞; the branch of (det D)^{−1/2} is the continuation from the positive root at D₀ along the segment from D₀, which stays in the ball of Step 2 (SA L13934). So for (P′, s) ∈ 𝒦 and real b,

  |F(P′; b)| |dμ_D/db| ≤ F₀c(𝒦)(2π)^{−m/2}e^{(ρ − c_{K₀}/4)|b|²} ≤ F₀c(𝒦)(2π)^{−m/2}e^{−(c_{K₀}/8)|b|²},

since ρ ≤ ρ₀ ≤ c_{K₀}/8. The right side is integrable and does not depend on (P′, s). For fixed b the integrand is holomorphic in (P′, s), because the density is holomorphic in D and D in (P′, s). Lemma 5.5 of PAN gives holomorphy of 𝒥 on the interior of 𝒦. Reality: at real data C, Γ, D and F are real, (det D)^{−1/2} > 0 by continuity from the reference along real D, and the integral is real. ∎

## 5. The potentials at a fixed complex input pair

### 5.1 The kinds, class and exempt

The step-i exponent is P = Σ_{f∈𝔉}[F_f(𝟏, y) − f₀] with the seven kinds of PAN §3.1 (PAN L101–113): (W) Wilson and Jacobian remainder and the coupling-profile difference; (S) strip; (Z) δ-elimination factors; (E, R) regular terms of every level; (B) boundary terms of every origin; (K) kept forms; (I) standing islands. At a complex input pair P′ ∈ 𝒪_σ, the same terms are formed by NL1-L (4.2)–(4.4) with P′ in place of P (Theorem V′).

Let 𝒵 := 𝒵_{i−1} be the live large-field region (standing islands, live components, drop-outs). By (G-gap) (PAN L163, from SA L17320–17325), every cube of 𝒞_Λ is at d_c ≥ G := 10LŘ_i from 𝒵.

**Definition 5.1.** A term f is *exempt* if its set X_f meets 𝒵, or if it is a boundary term whose minimising exempt tree (SA L6191–6195, as PAN L154 quotes it) has a nonempty island family 𝖥. Write 𝔉^{ex} for the exempt terms and 𝔉^{cl} := 𝔉 ∖ 𝔉^{ex}. The class kinds are (W); (S); (Z°) (the step's own z-factors and the carried factors near Λ); (E, R) (including healed-family terms without a standing object in their domain, SA L17996–17997 as PAN L108 quotes it); (B°) (boundary terms with 𝖥 = ∅ and X_f ∩ 𝒵 = ∅). The exempt kinds are (I), (K), the factors and terms kept by drop-outs (SA L17654 as PAN L75 quotes it), and (B) with 𝖥 ≠ ∅.

### 5.3 Two lemmas

**Lemma 5.1 (amplitude on the box).** [P from (H-V′)] Let f have a two-sided presentation (|F_f| ≤ b_f on 𝕊_{X_f} × 𝔻_R). For 0 ≤ r < R put o_f(r) := sup{|F_f(P′; s, ζ) − f₀(P′)| : s ∈ 𝕊_{X_f}, |ζ|_∞ ≤ r}, with f₀(P′) := F_f(P′; 𝟏, 0). Then o_f(r) ≤ 2b_f r/R.

*Proof.* Fix s and ζ with 0 < |ζ|_∞ ≤ r and put u := ζ/|ζ|_∞. φ(t) := F_f(P′; s, tu) − F_f(P′; s, 0) is holomorphic on |t| < R and |φ| ≤ 2b_f. By (L5), F_f(P′; s, 0) = f₀(P′) for every s (NL1-L Theorem V′: "(L5) ... for each fixed P′"). Lemma 5.1 of PAN (Schwarz, PAN L181–186) gives |φ(|ζ|_∞)| ≤ 2b_f|ζ|_∞/R. ∎

**Lemma 5.2 (switch Möbius localisation).** [P from (H-V′); the argument is ST1's proof of (77), ST1 L884, and SA L17913–17916] Let f have a presentation as in (H-V′) with X_f connected. For a finite set Y of fine cubes with Y ⊇ X_f, and t ∈ [0, 1]^{Y∖X_f}, let s^Y(t) ∈ 𝕊_{X_f} be 1 on X_f, t on Y ∖ X_f and 0 off Y. Define

  f_{X_f}(P′; ζ) := F_f(P′; s^{X_f}, ζ) − f₀(P′),
  f_Y(P′; ζ) := ∫_{[0,1]^{Y∖X_f}} ∏_{Δ∈Y∖X_f}∂_{t_Δ} F_f(P′; s^Y(t), ζ) dt  (Y ⊋ X_f).   (5.2)

Then:

- (a) *Exactness.* Σ_{Y⊇X_f} f_Y(P′; ζ) = F_f(P′; 𝟏, ζ) − f₀(P′), a finite sum.
- (b) *Locality.* f_Y depends on ζ only through its values on the bonds of I in cubes of Y.
- (c) *Connectivity.* f_Y = 0 unless Y is connected. If Y contains no bond of I, then f_Y = 0.
- (d) *Bound.* For |ζ|_∞ ≤ r, |f_Y(P′; ζ)| ≤ o_f(r)(e^{κ̄} − 1)^{−|Y∖X_f|}.
- (e) *Holomorphy and reality.* f_Y is holomorphic in (A′, 𝐉′, ζ) on 𝒪_σ(U) × 𝔻_R and real at real (P′, ζ).

*Proof.* (a) Let 𝒞 be the finite set of all fine cubes and T := 𝒞 ∖ X_f. The function g(t) := F_f(P′; s(t), ζ), with s(t) = 1 on X_f and t on T, is smooth on [0, 1]^T. Apply g(1_A ∪ 0) − g(0_A ∪ 0) = ∫_0^1∂_{t_Δ}g dt_Δ once in each variable of T, and expand by Fubini. This gives the finite identity g(𝟏) = Σ_{W⊆T}∫_{[0,1]^W}∂^Wg(t_W, 0_{T∖W})dt_W. The term W = ∅ is g(0) = F_f(P′; s^{X_f}, ζ). Subtract f₀ from both sides and put Y := X_f ∪ W.

(b) For t ∈ [0, 1]^{Y∖X_f}, supp s^Y(t) ⊆ Y, so K_{X_f}(s^Y(t)) ⊆ Y. By (L3) (Theorem V′), F_f(P′; s^Y(t), ζ) depends on ζ only on the bonds of I in K ⊆ Y. The same holds for its t-derivatives and their integral.

(c) Suppose Δ ∈ Y ∖ X_f does not lie in the component of Y that contains X_f. For every t, K_{X_f}(s^Y(t)) is contained in that component, so it does not contain Δ. Changing t_Δ changes s only off K, so (L3) gives ∂_{t_Δ}F_f = 0, and f_Y = 0. If Y contains no bond of I, then by (b) F_f(P′; s^Y(t), ζ) does not depend on ζ, so it equals its value at ζ = 0, which is f₀(P′) by (L5) for every t. All its t-derivatives vanish, and for Y = X_f the difference vanishes.

(d) Fix t ∈ [0, 1]^{Y∖X_f} and 0 < ρ < e^{κ̄} − 1. On the polycircle {u : |u_Δ − t_Δ| = ρ for Δ ∈ Y ∖ X_f}, |u_Δ| ≤ 1 + ρ < e^{κ̄}, so s^Y(u) ∈ 𝕊_{X_f} and |F_f(P′; s^Y(u), ζ) − f₀| ≤ o_f(r). The Cauchy formula on the polycircle gives |∏∂_{t_Δ}F_f(P′; s^Y(t), ζ)| ≤ o_f(r)ρ^{−|Y∖X_f|}. Integrate over the unit cube and let ρ ↑ e^{κ̄} − 1. For Y = X_f the bound is the definition of o_f.

(e) F_f is jointly holomorphic in (A′, 𝐉′, s, ζ) (Theorem V′). Its t-derivatives are holomorphic in (A′, 𝐉′, ζ) and continuous in t on the compact cube. Integration over a compact cube preserves holomorphy (Lemma 5.5 of PAN with a bounded majorant on compact subsets). At real P′ and real ζ, F_f is real for real s by (L4), so its real t-derivatives are real. ∎

### 5.4 UM1 for the class kinds other than (W)

Convention [reading of SA L14295–14296, "Lengths d_f and d_c are tree lengths in fine and coarse cube units"]: for a connected set Y of fine cubes, d_f(Y) ≤ |Y| − 1, because a spanning tree of Y in the *-adjacency graph has |Y| − 1 edges of unit length. If SA's convention counts edges with another fixed length, every λ_V below is multiplied by that fixed factor.

**Definition 5.2 (older potentials of class kinds).** For a finite connected set Y of fine cubes and real B on the bonds of I in Y with |B(b)| < 𝔟,

  V^{cl}(Y; P′, B) := Σ_{f∈𝔉^{cl}∖𝔉_W : X_f⊆Y} f_Y(P′; B).   (5.3)

By Lemma 5.2(b), V^{cl}(Y) reads B only on Y. So the box on the bonds of Y is the only cutoff needed (SA L14315–14316). By Lemma 5.2(a), summed over all Y, Σ_YV^{cl}(Y) = Σ_{f∈𝔉^{cl}∖𝔉_W}[F_f(P′; 𝟏, B) − f₀(P′)].

**Proposition 5.3 (UM1 for the class kinds).** [P from (H-V′), Lemmas 5.1–5.2 and (Size)] Let λ_V > 0 with κ̄ ≥ λ_V + 2 and κ ≥ λ_V + 1. Then for every connected Y, every P′ ∈ 𝒪_σ and every B in the box on Y,

  |V^{cl}(Y; P′, B)| ≤ a^{cl}e^{−λ_Vd_f(Y)},  a^{cl} := 2(𝔟/R)N_{cl}.   (5.4)

*Proof.* By Lemma 5.2(d) and Lemma 5.1 with r = 𝔟 (and ω_f in place of 2b_f for (E, R), where (Size) is stated with ω_f),

  |V^{cl}(Y)| ≤ (𝔟/R)Σ_{f: X_f⊆Y}ω_f(e^{κ̄} − 1)^{−|Y∖X_f|}.

Since κ̄ ≥ λ_V + 2 ≥ 1, e^{κ̄} − 1 ≥ e^{κ̄−1}, so (e^{κ̄} − 1)^{−1}e^{λ_V} ≤ e^{λ_V−κ̄+1} ≤ e^{−1}. With d_f(Y) ≤ |Y| − 1 ≤ |X_f| + |Y ∖ X_f|,

  e^{λ_Vd_f(Y)}|V^{cl}(Y)| ≤ (𝔟/R)Σ_{f: X_f⊆Y}ω_fe^{λ_V|X_f|}e^{−|Y∖X_f|} = (𝔟/R)e^{−|Y|}Σ_{f: X_f⊆Y}ω_fe^{(λ_V+1)|X_f|}.

Charge each f with X_f ⊆ Y to one cube of X_f. The last sum is at most |Y|·sup_ΔΣ_{f:X_f∋Δ}ω_fe^{(λ_V+1)|X_f|} ≤ |Y|N_{cl}, since λ_V + 1 ≤ κ. Finally |Y|e^{−|Y|} ≤ e^{−1} < 2. ∎

*Size of the amplitude.* 𝔟/R = A₁ℓ^{𝔭₀−q} → 0 (q > 𝔭₀). N_{cl} is fixed after M. So a^{cl} ≤ a_*/4 is a ceiling on γ after M (supremum form, SA L19485).

## 9. The exempt kinds: Lemma TD

### 9.1 The statement

**Lemma TD (UM1 for the exempt type)** [O]. There are a rate λ_T ≥ λ_V and an amplitude a^{ex}, with a^{ex} → 0 as γ → 0 after M, and for every main-step input structure σ and every P′ ∈ 𝒪_σ an exact local decomposition

  Σ_{f∈𝔉^{ex}}[F_f(P′; 𝟏, B) − f₀(P′)] = Σ_{∅≠W⊆𝒞_Λ}V^{ex}(W; P′, B),

with each V^{ex}(W; ·, ·) holomorphic in (A′, 𝐉′) on 𝒪_σ(U), real at real arguments, and reading B only on the bonds of I in W, such that for every real B in the box on W

  |V^{ex}(W; P′, B)| ≤ a^{ex}e^{−λ_Td_f(W)}.   (TD)

The natural candidate is the cube-Möbius potential (5.5), which is exact, local, holomorphic and real by §5.7; for it (TD) is a bound to be proved. Attacks A1 and A2 below try other exact local decompositions.

With (TD), (A1) of Theorem UM(P′) holds for all four types, with a := a^{cl} + a_W + a^{ex} + w_⋆, and Theorems UM(P′), B1(P′), B4(P′) hold for all seven kinds.

*The expected mechanism (TD′).* For one exempt f, the dependence of F_f on the fluctuation of a gas cube Δ passes through the response, from Δ to the reads of f, over at least d_c(Δ, X_f) ≥ G cubes. A walk-resolved response would give |f_W| ≤ C_Tb_f(C_T𝔟/R)^{|W|}e^{−μ_TM·St(W; X_f)}, where St(W; X_f) is the least total length of a forest whose trees each meet X_f and which together meet every cube of W. Turning (TD′) into (TD) needs a count over the exempt geometry, as in PAN Lemmas 7.2–7.6 (PAN §7.2–§7.6). It must control the length that a spanning tree of W spends crossing X_f, which (TD′) does not pay. I did not carry out that count. Lemma TD is stated in the form (UM1) needs.

### 9.2 Three attacks, and why each fails

All three use only what the interface supplies at P′: Theorem V′ (holomorphy on 𝕊_{X_f} × 𝔻_R with |F_f| ≤ b_f, locality (L3), (L5)), NL1-L Proposition S (the screened radius, capped at R_cap), and DR's bound (16) as (4.1). In each, take an exempt f whose X_f contains the hull of one standing island. The hull contains a cube of side at least Ř_i fine cubes: a component of 𝒵 contains a cell of its level, and cells are at least Ř_i cubes wide (PAN L163, L165 (G-sep)). So the boundary layer ∂X_f := N_1(X_f) ∖ X_f [reading: components of 𝒵 are unions of cells of their level, as the boxing map produces them, SA L17320–17325 as PAN L163–165 quote it] has |∂X_f| ≥ (Ř_i + 2)⁴ − Ř_i⁴ ≥ 8Ř_i³ cubes. Recall G = 10LŘ_i and Ř_i ≥ (ℓ − 1)^r with r ≥ 2 (PAN L395). The amplitude on the box is, at best, o_f ≤ b_fe^{−cMŘ_i} for some fixed c > 0. This is SA's main-step amplitude away from the box (SA L17927–17930: "Cg_k^{−1}e^{−cMŘ_k}"); I grant it in each attack, although (4.1) gives it only for sources away from the stored rows.

**A1 (switch Möbius, SA L17913–17920 and ST1 L884).** The pieces f_Y of Lemma 5.2 are exact and local, with |f_Y| ≤ o_f(e^{κ̄} − 1)^{−|Y∖X_f|}. To form a gas potential on W ⊆ 𝒞_Λ one sums over the Y with Y ∩ 𝒞_Λ = W: V(W) := Σ_fΣ_{Y∩𝒞_Λ=W}f_Y. Fix W and one path π of fine cubes from X_f to W, of length ≥ G. For every set D of cubes of ∂X_f ∖ π, Y := X_f ∪ π ∪ W ∪ D is connected and has Y ∩ 𝒞_Λ = W. The interface bound on V(W) therefore contains the sum

  Σ_D o_f(e^{κ̄} − 1)^{−|π|−|W|−|D|} = o_f(e^{κ̄} − 1)^{−|π|−|W|}(1 + (e^{κ̄} − 1)^{−1})^{|∂X_f∖π|} ≥ o_f(e^{κ̄} − 1)^{−|π|−|W|}exp(½e^{−κ̄}(8Ř_i³ − |π|)),

using log(1 + x) ≥ x/2 for 0 ≤ x ≤ 1. Take |π| = G + 1 and W a single cube, so d_f(W) = 0. (TD) asks for a^{ex} → 0, while this bound is at least b_fexp(4e^{−κ̄}Ř_i³ − (κ̄ + cM + 1)(10LŘ_i + 2)), using (e^{κ̄} − 1)^{−k} ≥ e^{−κ̄k}. Since κ̄, M, L, c are fixed before γ and Ř_i → ∞ as γ → 0, the exponent tends to +∞. *Exact reason:* each decoration costs only (e^{κ̄} − 1)^{−1}, the switch radius, and an exempt support has ≳ Ř³ places to hang decorations, while its amplitude is small only like e^{−cMŘ}. *Variant A1′ (switches set to 1 off 𝒞_Λ).* Then decorations outside the gas are resummed, but a piece on W = {Δ₁, Δ₂} ⊆ 𝒞_Λ need not be connected inside the gas: both cubes may be joined to X_f through the non-gas region, where no switch is paid. Its interface bound o_f(e^{κ̄} − 1)^{−2} does not depend on D := d_c(Δ₁, Δ₂), while (TD) asks for e^{−λ_TD}. Since the gas boundary adjacent to the non-gas region can extend over the whole torus, D is unbounded. *Exact reason:* switches only see the cubes where they are not set to 1.

**A2 (PAN shells, PAN Proposition 6.1).** Shells have no entropy: F_f(𝟏, B) − f₀ = Σ_{n≥n₀}g_n^f with |g_n^f| ≤ 2o_fe^{−κ̄n}, n₀ := d_c(X_f, 𝒞_Λ) − 1 ≥ G − 1 (PAN L224–238; for n < n₀ the shell reads no gas bond and g_n^f = 0 by (L5)). But g_n^f reads B on all of N_{n+1}(X_f) ∩ 𝒞_Λ. In a history whose gas region surrounds the island beyond the gap, this set contains S_{n+1}(X_f), with at least 8(Ř_i + 2n)³ cubes. A tagged domain must contain the cubes its potential reads, and a covering tree of a set of k cubes has length ≥ k − 1 (SA L14424–14425: "take a fine covering tree for Y"). So (UM1) for g_n^f asks for a^{ex}e^{−λ_T(8(Ř_i+2n)³−1)}, against the bound 2b_fe^{−cMŘ_i}e^{−κ̄n}. For n = n₀ the exponent comparison is λ_T·8(Ř_i + 2G − 2)³ against cMŘ_i + κ̄G, and the left side wins as Ř_i → ∞. *Exact reason:* a shell is a four-dimensional region whose volume grows like the cube of the island size, while the shell bound decays only linearly in its radius. Splitting a shell further brings back A1 or A3.

**A3 (cube Möbius on the screened polydisc, NL1-L Proposition S).** (5.5) needs no switches. For W = {Δ₁, Δ₂}, the function (t₁, t₂) ↦ f_W(P′; t₁B_{Δ₁} + t₂B_{Δ₂}) vanishes when t₁ = 0 or t₂ = 0. It is holomorphic as long as the fluctuation stays in the polydisc where F_f(P′; 𝟏, ·) is holomorphic. The largest polydisc the interface supplies has radius R_f^{scr} ≤ R_cap ≍ e^{−Cκ̄}/g: the source must stay in DR's source ball and in the chart ball (NL1-L L399: "R_f ≤ s_D/(C_Ac_Tge^{κ̄}) ≍ e^{−Cκ̄}/g whatever the distance"). Lemma 5.4 of PAN gives |f_W| ≤ 8b_f(𝔟/R_f^{scr})², which is at least 8b_f(𝔟C_Ac_Tge^{κ̄}/s_D)². This does not depend on D = d_c(Δ₁, Δ₂). (TD) asks for e^{−λ_TD}, so the bound fails for D > (2ℓ + Cκ̄ + 2log(1/𝔟))/λ_T. Per-cube radii would repair this. A polydisc with radius Re^{μ′Md_c(Δ,X_f)} on cube Δ would give the factor ∏_Δ(𝔟/R)e^{−μ′Md_c(Δ,X_f)}. But (4.1) (NL1-L L259–264) bounds the response by ‖S‖_src, the sup over all source rows, times e^{−μd_+(b, supp S)}, the distance to the *whole* support. On a graded polydisc the source is ≍ gRe^{μ′Md_max} on far cubes and the nearest source cube is at distance ≍ MG. So (4.1) gives only ≍ gRe^{μ′Md_max − μMG}, which exceeds the room once d_max > (μ/μ′)G, and it exceeds the cap anyway. *Exact reason:* the interface's response bound is a sup-norm bound, not a row-by-row (weighted) bound, and the fluctuation cannot be complexified beyond R_cap.

### 9.3 What would prove Lemma TD

All three attacks fail for one reason. The interface encodes decay of the response only through switch radii (one fixed factor per cube) and through one distance to the whole source support. Multi-point decay needs the response resolved by rows or by walks, for example:

**(DR-row)** [proposed input for ST1's DR; not stated by ST1]. For sources S = Σ_{c∈W}S_c with S_c supported on the rows of cube c, the Möbius pieces h_W := Σ_{W′⊆W}(−1)^{|W∖W′|}h(s; P′, Σ_{c∈W′}S_c) satisfy n_b(h_W) ≤ C_D^{|W|}∏_{c∈W}‖S_c‖_src·e^{−μ·St(W ∪ {b})}, with St the least length of a tree meeting b and every cube of W.

This is the four-dimensional form of Y3's device (Y3 L52582–52585), where each coefficient or string carries the product of the parameters over its own hull. (DR-row) would also need a composition lemma for f(Π_f(·)) that avoids the factorial growth of partition sums. A forest-formula form would do this. I did not attempt it.

*Remark (the conditional-law route does not need TD).* PAN's Theorem P (PAN L367–375) bounds Σ_{X∋b,b′}sup|P_X|, a bond-pair statement, and TT Lemma 3.1 needs only that (PAN L45). Shells suffice there. TD is needed only because SA's regular gas asks for (UM1), a per-domain statement with tree-length decay.

*Remark (moving exempt terms out of the gas).* Evaluating exempt terms at a background localised away from the gas would make them independent of B. But the difference from the actual background is again a function of B attached to the same exempt support (SA L17562: "Each change of background produces differences that localisation turns into boundary terms"). So the obstruction moves; it does not disappear.
