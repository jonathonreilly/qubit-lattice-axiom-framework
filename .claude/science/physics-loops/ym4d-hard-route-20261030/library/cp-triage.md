## Conventions for the inventory

Each numbered substatement is a proposed lane boundary, not a newly registered
lemma. The cited definition supplies its full domain and quantifiers.
`P` means port a proof already written in the account or quoted as a published
theorem. `S` means complete a written sketch or a printed-without-proof claim.
`N` means an additional estimate or construction identification, with no full
proof located in the sources examined. A printed citation is not classified P
when the account expressly says its proof was not printed.

Sizes count residual deliverables at the named interface, excluding repeated
upstream premises. They are lower-bound planning estimates, not complexity
theorems. Some must split further after their actual maps are fixed.
An item can contain useful independent work while its complete closure depends
on other items. The dependency table after the inventory makes this distinction.
