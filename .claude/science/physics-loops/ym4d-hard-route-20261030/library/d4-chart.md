## 3. The old common chart

\[
 \Omega_l^0\cap C=\Omega_l^\circ\cap C,
 \qquad \Omega_l^1\cap C=C\setminus Z_l''(C).
 \tag{1}
\]
All other determining members agree.

\[
 \Gamma_m^0=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb T^{(m)}:
 x,y\in\Omega_0^0,\ \{x,y\}\cap\Omega_m^0\ne\varnothing,
 \ \{x,y\}\cap\Omega_{m+1}^0=\varnothing\}.
 \tag{2}
\]
The new rows use \(\Omega^1\) in this same formula (SA 5612–5629).
Only \(\Gamma_j\) and \(\Gamma_l\) can change.

Therefore the old and new determining problems are not the same fixed-sequence problem.

## 4. The transition estimate, with its missing inputs exposed

There are two contributions, even before averaging.
Set \(v=u_1u_0^{-1}\).
Transport the stage-one pair with \(u_0\), and call it \((\widetilde W_1,\widetilde K_1)\).
Then the exact identities are
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 W_1-W_0
 &=v(x)(\widetilde W_1-W_0)v(y)^{-1}
   +[v(x)W_0-W_0v(y)]v(y)^{-1},\\
 K_1-K_0
 &=\operatorname{Ad}v(x)(\widetilde K_1-K_0)
   +(\operatorname{Ad}v(x)-I)K_0.
 \end{aligned}                                                     \tag{4}
\]
They follow by inserting and subtracting \(v(x)W_0v(y)^{-1}\) and \(\operatorname{Ad}v(x)K_0\).
Thus the link estimate needs the covariant difference of the assigned gauge.
Bounding its condition number alone does not bound that difference.

Fix an old tower \(e\) and a norm weight \(\lambda_e(b)\ge\eta\).
Write \(\|w\|_{*,e}=\sup_b\lambda_e(b)|w(b)|\).
Use constant weight \(L^{d-p}\) for `d4-stage.md`'s single-layer norm in level-\(p\) units.
For the layered version, use Theorem L's local tower scale \(\lambda_e(b)=\xi'_e(b)\).
Take every following supremum over \(D_1^+\), \(\zeta\), and the indicated bonds.
Using matrix operator norms, define
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 A_e&=\sup\frac{\lambda_e(b)}\eta|\widetilde W_1(b)-W_0(b)|,\\
 G_e&=\sup\frac{\lambda_e(b)}\eta|v(x)W_0(b)-W_0(b)v(y)|,\\
 B&=\sup_x\max\{|v(x)|,|v(x)^{-1}|\},\qquad
 E_e=B^2A_e+BG_e.
 \end{aligned}                                                     \tag{5}
\]
Assume these raw maps exist and are jointly holomorphic there, before testing the old tower.
Suppose \(\sup|W_0U_\tau^{-1}-I|\le q_0<q<1\) and \(E_e<q-q_0\).
Equation (4) gives \(\sup|W_1U_\tau^{-1}-I|<q\).
Both old link logarithms therefore exist, and
\[
 \sup\|w_1-w_0\|_{*,e}\le\frac{E_e}{1-q}.                       \tag{6}
\]
Indeed, \(\|X^r-Y^r\|\le r q^{r-1}\|X-Y\|\) when \(\|X\|,\|Y\|\le q\).
Apply this estimate termwise to the convergent logarithm series.
Its sum is \((1-q)^{-1}\).
Unitarity of \(U_\tau\) removes the remaining right factor.
This proves (6) without assuming the stage-one logarithm first.

The bound has no factor counting earlier parameters.
Its hypotheses already take the supremum over all those parameters.
In particular, \(A_e\) compares complete earlier compositions, not only the last response.
Equation (4) retains the independent-current and assigned-gauge obligations.
It supplies no uniform majorant for \(A_e,G_e,B\) in this history.

## 5. The old (R5) clause, for each erosion rule

For every allowed old \(Z,p\) and \(1\le d\le p\), let \(\mathbf B_{\tau,e}'\) be its fixed determining set.

\[
 b_{n,\tau,e}=\frac1i\Log\!\left[
 M_{\mathbf B_{\tau,e}'}(W_n)
 M_{\mathbf B_{\tau,e}'}(U_\tau)^{-1}\right],\qquad
 U_{d,\tau,e}^{(n)}=
 \Phi_{M_{\mathbf B_{\tau,e}'}(U_\tau)}(b_{n,\tau,e}).              \tag{7}
\]
Neither \(\mathbf B_{\tau,e}'\) nor the real reference is replaced by its stage-one version.

Here is the precise conditional admission proof.
Retain the fixed-reference averaging and continuation hypotheses of `c1-repr.md` Section 8.
For layered towers retain `wf-room-2.md` Theorem L, including memberwise containment in the old ambient sequence.
For each tower assume the resulting ball \(\|w\|_{*,e}<R_e\) satisfies
\[
 \|b_{\tau,e}(w)\|\le C_{A,e}\|w\|_{*,e},\qquad
 C_{A,e}R_e<r_{B,\tau,e}.                                      \tag{8}
\]
Here the ball of radius \(r_{B,\tau,e}\) lies in the assigned continuation domain.
The raw-average constant includes both endpoint factors.

\[
 \|w_0\|_{*,e}<R_e/4,\qquad E_e/(1-q)<R_e/4.                   \tag{9}
\]
Equation (6) puts \(w_1\) and the joining segment inside the half ball.
Equation (8) puts \(b_{1,\tau,e}\) inside the continuation ball.
Thus (7) exists for that old tower.
Cauchy's estimate on the larger ball also gives
\[
 \|b_{1,\tau,e}-b_{0,\tau,e}\|
 \le 2C_{A,e}\|w_1-w_0\|_{*,e}
 \le 2C_{A,e}E_e/(1-q).                                       \tag{10}
\]
The argument applies to rule I with its own data.
It applies to rule II with its own data.
It proves their (R5) clauses under Section 4's hypotheses and (8)–(9), for every old tower.

\[
 \sup_{Q\in\mathcal A_{\rm later}}|F(Q)|
