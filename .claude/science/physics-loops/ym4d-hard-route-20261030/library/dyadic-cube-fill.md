**Why.** (LNC) was routed through "a patch above eps_1^2 is covered by smaller patches ... Lemma RF with variable scale is not written". Every small-energy lemma (Lemma U, Lemma F, Lemma RF) stops at a patch of energy above eps_1^2. The lemma below does not. It uses only that a plaquette holonomy is a product of conjugates, and Cauchy-Schwarz over nested meshes.

## 1. Lemma C-lin: the cell fill is linear in the boundary size

**Lemma C-lin (discharged, given Lemma C).** *Fix K_max. There is C_1 = C_1(K_max) such that: for Q a lattice rectangle of dimension m = 2, 3, 4 with sides in [n, 2 n], and boundary data with theta_p <= K/n^2 on boundary plaquettes, K <= K_max (for m = 2: boundary holonomy angle at most K), there is an extension with the exact boundary links and max theta_p <= C_1 K / n^2.*

*Proof.* m = 2 is the exact disc formula: uniform flux theta/N, N >= n^2. For m = 3, 4 and K >= K_0 := 2^-12: Lemma C gives E(K_max)/n^2 <= (E(K_max)/K_0) K/n^2. For K < K_0, redo Lemma G without Lemma E. The tree gauges g_D, g_P have link angles at most 6 K/n, 4 K/n. On the rim h = g_P g_D^-1 has edge differences at most 10 K/n, and the rim has edge diameter at most 16 n, so h stays within 160 K <= 1/8 of a constant h_0. Write h = h_0 e^w with |w| <= C K and edge differences of w at most C K/n, extend w radially over the top face, w-tilde = rho (w o pi), edge differences at most C K/n. Replacing g_P by (h_0 e^{w-tilde})^-1 g_P gives one boundary gauge with link angles at most C_2 K/n, for every n >= 1. Then in the proof of Lemma C: |(da)_p| <= K/n^2 + 16 (C_2 K/n)^2 <= C K/n^2, the interpolant has |A| + |dA| <= C K on the unit cube, and A_ext has comass at most C (|A| + |dA| + |A|^2) <= C K. So every fine plaquette has angle at most C K/n^2. QED

*So a cell whose boundary holonomies are small has energy at most C K^2, not C. This is the only new input to Lemma C.*

## 2. Lemma CF: flat fill of a cube at any energy

**Lemma CF (discharged at the level of each term, given Lemmas H, C-lin; corner bookkeeping below).** *There is an absolute C_F such that for every SU(2) field U on a lattice box Q_{2H} := [-2 H, 2 H]^4, H >= 1, there is a field U-hat on Q_{2H} with: U-hat = U on and outside a box boundary inside Q_{2H} \ Q_{(3/2) H}; U-hat pure gauge on Q_H; and*

    sum_{p in Q_{2H}} theta_p(U-hat)^2 <= C_F sum_{p in Q_{2H} \ Q_{(3/2)H}} theta_p(U)^2.        (CF)

*No hypothesis on U. No smallness, no parents, no topology.*

*Proof.* Let E be the right sum.

*(a) The surface.* Take R := product of [-r_mu, r_mu] with r_mu integer in [(3/2) H, 2 H]. Averaging over r: the plaquettes tangent to a facet {x_k = +-r_k} sum to e_S <= C E/H, and those in a ridge {x_k = +-r_k, x_rho = +-r_rho} sum to e_R <= C E/H^2, for some choice of r, by Markov on both averages. The ridge bound is why the box has independent sides: a mesh keeps the ridges at every scale.

*(b) Nested meshes.* In each coordinate rho, choose partitions P_0 := all integers, P_{j+1} subset of P_j, with gaps in [2^j, 2^{j+1}], keeping the end walls. Passing from P_j to P_{j+1} keeps either the even or the odd interior walls, and I take the choice that at most halves b_j^rho := the sum of theta_p^2 over plaquettes p on facets, with normal-in-the-facet rho and x_rho(p) an interior wall of P_j. Coordinates are independent, as each plaquette on a facet has one such rho. So b_j^rho <= 2^-j e_S. The mesh M_j on boundary R is the product. For a 2-cell f of M_j, Lemma H and Cauchy-Schwarz give theta(f)^2 <= (sum_{p in f} theta_p)^2 <= 4^{j+1} sum_{p in f} theta_p^2. Summing, since p lies in at most two such f,

    S_j := sum_{f in M_j} theta(f)^2 <= C 4^j ( 2^-j e_S + e_R ) = C ( 2^j e_S + 4^j e_R ).

*(c) The complex.* Let n_j := 2^j, R_0 := R, R_{j+1} := R_j shrunk by n_j on each side, and stop at J with 2^J about H/8, so R_J still contains Q_H. Tile R_j \ int R_{j+1} by a product mesh of sides in [n_j, 2 n_j] with walls at the two surfaces, nested with the mesh on the next surface. Let Pi send each mesh vertex of level j to a vertex of M_j on boundary R near its sup-norm radial projection, so that adjacent vertices at level j have images joined by a path of length at most C n_j on one or two adjacent facets; Pi is the identity at level 0. *Every coarse edge carries the U-transport along that fixed short path, placed on its first link, with other links 1.* Then the holonomy of any coarse 2-cell at level j is the U-holonomy of a closed short loop on boundary R. Boundary R is a 3-sphere, so the loop bounds a union of at most C 2-cells of M_j and of M_{j+1}, and its angle is at most the sum of their theta(f). Hence its square is at most C times the sum of their squares, with bounded multiplicity. *No fine link of U is copied, and no lattice map is needed; this removes the corner problem.* At level J put a gauge on boundary R_J by a tree: since boundary R_J is simply connected in the mesh, all coarse edges then have angle at most C max theta(f), M_J having boundedly many cells. Make every link inside R_J trivial, with radial links carrying that gauge.

*(d) Filling.* By dimension, as in Theorem S: 2-cells by the exact disc, then 3-cells, then 4-cells, by Lemma C-lin at scale n_j, all earlier links kept. On a cell tau let Theta(tau) be the largest holonomy angle among the coarse 2-cells on its boundary. Then its plaquettes have angle at most C Theta(tau)/n_j^2, and it has at most C n_j^4 plaquettes, so its energy is at most C Theta(tau)^2. The outer surface at level 0 is U itself, with n_0 = 1, where the cell has no interior link.

*(e) Sum.* Summing over cells of level j gives at most C S_j, by bounded multiplicity, including S_{j+1} from the inner surface. So the energy in R is at most C sum_{j <= J} ( 2^j e_S + 4^j e_R ) <= C ( H e_S + H^2 e_R ) <= C E. Off R nothing changed, and those plaquettes are already in E or are outside Q_{2H}. QED

*What was checked by hand.* Uniform flux f: S_j = C 2^j f^2 H^3, total C f^2 H^4, so the bound is sharp in order. A thin vortex sheet through the cube: pierced 2-cells carry theta = pi, so S_j about pi^2 H/2^j, far below the bound. One instanton of small size: boundary R avoids it by the average, with small E. So (CF) is consistent with the three cases where I know the answer. **Hostile, what I would attack:** step (c) at ridges, where a radial projection folds a coarse 2-cell over a ridge. The loop still bounds a union of boundedly many M_j cells on two adjacent facets, because boundary R is locally a cone over its ridge structure, but the count of those cells is not displayed. And the first layers: at n_0 = 1 a cell of Lemma C-lin has Theta up to pi, covered by K_max. I found no failure. **It has not been read by anyone else, and it is new, so it should be gated before it carries weight.**

*Consequence.* Lemma F is not needed for a flat cube, and Prop 10's single step does not need Lemma U. Dense steps still need a target with the parents' holonomy, so Lemma RF is not replaced.

## 3. Prop LE: minimisers have bounded local energy, uniformly in everything

*Setting.* V any level-l field, class or not. c = (U, V_1, ..., V_{l-1}) a chain in its fibre with quiet data and no marks in the region considered. 𝖠 := A + P/x-tilde_{l-1} the modified energy of Theorem Z, Step 5, for any pi. R_1 := (K_1/T)^{1/2} in level-l blocks, K_1 absolute as in the fill. E_y(r) := the energy of U on the cube of radius r blocks about y.

**Observation (kernel; Text 5.2).** A box with at least m_* labelled cells is dropped, and all its parent bonds are Haar. So on a set of boxes all made dropped, at a cost of about m_* C T^2 per box for m_* cells of flux 2 T at level l - 1, **the parents are no constraint at all**, whatever V is there, rough or not. These new cells are at level l - 1, where the top bracket has coefficient x-tilde_{l-1} - x-tilde_{l-1} = 0 on Delta. This is why a rough V costs nothing here.

**Prop LE (implication at the level of each term; given Lemma CF, the observation, Lemma TE).** *Let w := 4 R_1, and let c minimise 𝖠 on the fibre of V. For y and r with no marks and no frozen bonds within the cube of radius r + w about y:*

    E_y(r) <= theta E_y(r + w) + C_d T^2 (r + w)^4,        theta := (C_F + 1/8)/(C_F + 1) < 1,        (LE-1)

*for all r in that range and, with no marks at all on the torus,*

    E_y(1 / mu) <= C_E T^2 mu^-4,    mu := (1 - theta)/(2 w),    C_E absolute,        (LE-2)

*for every y, every V, every torus, with no dependence on |X|. Hence, with alpha := log_2(1/theta) > 0,*

    E_y(1) <= C T^{alpha/2} << eps_1^2 for T small:        **no high patch anywhere, at any V.**        (LE-3)

*Proof of (LE-1).* Choose the surface of Lemma CF in the shell of width R_1, in fine units n := R_1 L^l, and run dyadic layers up to scale n. Inside, take the field of Theorem S at scale n: all coarse links trivial, 2-cells capped, flux at most C/n^2. In level-l units that is C/R_1^2 = C T/K_1 per plaquette, so energy at most C T^2 per block. Drop every box touched, by the observation. A straddling box takes its m_* cells where U has least energy, at a cost at most C (m_*/L^4) E(box) + C m_* T^2. Upper levels of the chain are unchanged, since the top-level parents of dropped boxes are Haar. Dyadic layers cost at most C_F E(shell), as in (e): the sum runs to n, and H e_S is the shell energy.

*The penalty in P.* New labelled cells at level k <= l - 2 occur only in dyadic layers at scale about L^k, and there are at most C S_j / T^2 <= C L^k e_S / T^2 <= C L^{k-l} E(shell) / (R_1 T^2) of them. So their term in P/x-tilde_{l-1} is at most (x-tilde_k - x-tilde_{l-1})/x-tilde_{l-1} times C L^{k-l} E(shell)/R_1, summable over k since x-tilde_k - x-tilde_{l-1} <= (l - k) C(L) by the flow, at most C(L) E(shell)/(x R_1). Removing rough cells of c changes P by the removed loss, at most b(x_k) per removed cell, and it is local by (P4) of `library/threshold-paid-reference.md`. By Lemma TE this is at most eps(x) A^Z(c) in the region, with a removed penalty of the favourable sign. So minimality gives E(r) + E(shell) <= C_F E(shell) + C_d T^2 (r + w)^4 + (1/8) E(shell) + small E(r). QED.

*Proof of (LE-2).* Let W(y) := integral over r of mu e^{-mu r} E_y(r) dr, finite on a finite torus. (LE-1) for all r gives W <= theta e^{mu w} W + C_d T^2 integral mu e^{-mu r} (r + w)^4 dr, and theta e^{mu w} <= 1 - (1 - theta)/2, so W <= C T^2 mu^-4 /(1 - theta). And E_y(1/mu) <= e W. *This replaces a recursion from X outward, which would have read log |X|.* QED

*Proof of (LE-3).* The same step with Lemma CF at H = 2^i blocks and all boxes of the cube dropped: a_i <= theta a_{i+1} + C T^2 16^i, a_i := E_y(2^i). Unrolling from 2^m = 1/mu: a_0 <= theta^m (a_m + C T^2 16^m) <= C (mu)^{alpha} <= C T^{alpha/2}, since T^2 mu^-4 is absolute. QED

**Hostile.** (1) *Does dropping break the chain below?* Lower levels in a dropped box are unconstrained by that box's parents and are read as usual. (2) *A straddling box:* written. (3) *Is a minimiser available?* Labels make P lower semicontinuous only up to the smooth cut; read an almost-minimiser with error below every term. (4) *Marks.* With open marks in the region, deep marks can raise the fine energy by a factor L^{4 (l - k)} per block near them, and (LE-2) as written is false there. Prop LE-M below treats this. (5) *Frozen bonds:* the surface runs outside them, and they are quiet; the fill keeps them by the copy rule of `library/quiet-boundary-rarity.md`. Written for one wall only.

**Prop LE-M (leak from deep marks; implication at the level of this paragraph; not gated).** *Fix j <= l - 1. Let y be such that a cube of radius delta_j level-(j+1) blocks about y misses C_j(M) and the frozen bonds at levels at most j. Then the cube of radius r_j := C R_j level-(j+1) blocks about y has energy at most C_* T^2 r_j^4 (x-tilde_j / x-tilde_{l-1}), with C_* absolute, R_j := R_1 x-tilde_j / x-tilde_{l-1} and delta_j := C_delta(L) R_j.* At j = l - 1 this is (LE-2) in a ball free of clusters, whatever the marks are outside it.

*Proof, by induction on j.* Run (LE-1) at level j: fill at scale R_j in level-(j+1) blocks, and drop only level-(j+1) boxes. **Dropping at level j + 1 keeps V_{j+1}, so the chain above and every mark above level j are unchanged,** and marks at levels at most j are absent from the zone. In 𝖠 a dropped box now costs C_d T^2 x-tilde_j / x-tilde_{l-1} in energy units, as its new labelled cells at level j carry the coefficient x-tilde_j - x-tilde_{l-1}. New rough cells at levels k <= j in the dyadic layers cost at most C (x-tilde_j / x-tilde_{l-1}) E(shell) / R_j <= C E(shell), which is why R_j carries the ratio. So (LE-1) holds at level j with theta as before, up to a constant in C_F. *A priori bound:* a cube of radius delta_j level-(j+1) blocks is covered by (delta_j L / r_{j-1})^4 cubes of radius r_{j-1} level-j blocks, and those are in the zone at level j - 1, so by induction its energy is at most (delta_j L)^4 C_* T^2 (x-tilde_{j-1} / x-tilde_{l-1}). Unrolling m := C log L steps from delta_j to r_j, theta^m (L delta_j / r_j)^4 x-tilde_{j-1} / x-tilde_j <= 1/4, since delta_j / r_j = C_delta(L)/C carries no ratio of x, as x-tilde_{j-1} / x-tilde_j <= 2, so the a priori term is at most (1/2) of the claim and C_* does not grow with j. *Base j = 0:* a plaquette has energy at most 4, so a cube has energy at most C (L delta_0)^4, and m is larger by log(1/T), a number after a_*. *In level-l units the zones sum:* the distance from C_(l-1)(M) that is asked is at most sum_j delta_j L^{j + 1 - l} <= C_delta(L) R_1 sum_m (1 + m C(L)/x) L^{-m} <= 2 C_delta(L) R_1. QED at this level. *So the leak is confined by a sum over levels of L^{-m}, with no factor per level.*

## 4. Prop DX: no dense box far from X (route; level of a paragraph)

**Prop DX (implication at the level of this paragraph; given (LE-1) to (LE-3), Prop LE-M, Lemma RF at non-dense layers).** *In the setting above with V class on the cube of radius r_* := C T^-5/2 log(1/T) blocks about y, c has no dense box in the cube of radius r_*/2, so energy at most K T^2 per box, and no cell of Z, by one step of Prop 10.*

*Route.* A dense cluster not reaching the sphere is filled as in (CI-N), layers non-dense, Lemma RF with the same parents: gain (K/4) T^2 per dense box, a contradiction. A cluster crossing the sphere {r} is capped: Let v(r) be the number of cells of side R_1 in Q_r meeting a dense box. Fill the dense clusters inside as above. At each cell crossing the sphere use the fill of (LE-1) locally, with all its boxes dropped. Its cost is an absolute constant per cell: (LE-2) bounds the shell, and Theorem S at scale R_1 bounds the rest. So (K/4) T^2 v(r) <= C ( v(r + R_1) - v(r) ), so v grows exponentially at rate K T^2 / (C R_1), while v(r) <= (r/R_1)^4. That is a contradiction at r_*. *Why walls are no problem here:* a dropped wall can hide a relative holonomy between U and the trial, which a cut at block scale could not afford; the crude cell at scale R_1 asks no relative gauge at all, as T R_1^2 = K_1 is the scale at which any two class fields differ by a bounded cell fill. *What I worked out and rejected:* a band of width R_1 around everything, since it costs C T^2 per block, more than a thin tentacle's gain; so only the crossing cells are crude. *The seam, between crude cells and Lemma RF.* Let 𝒯 be the union of crossing cells. Every cell off 𝒯 has no dense box, so it has no dropped box, and the patches of Lemma RF there have the same parents. Run the colours of Lemma RF on patches missing an inner part of 𝒯. This gives one field F, of energy at most C_f (E(U) + E(trial)) on the annulus about 𝒯. Then fill 𝒯 from its boundary by the dyadic layers of Lemma CF, on the surface chosen in that annulus, up to scale n, and inside by trivial links, with a last layer crude at scale n: at most C per cell, and C_F E_annulus(F) for the layers. All boxes of 𝒯 are dropped. Lemma CF's proof reads nothing about the shape of the surface but bounded geometry at scale n, and 𝒯 is a union of cells of side n. *Not written:* the ridge count for a union of cells. *Last step:* one step of Prop 10 at a cell of Z in a non-dense region, in a ball. Prop 10's bump is read at a minimal lift, (f), so a local form of (f') is needed, on a ball with its own shell as data, and such data are low energy, not quiet. Not written.

**So (LNC) has a route with every step named, at the level of this paragraph; it is not proved.** Its r_* is large: d_x, t_0 must exceed C r_*, a number after a_*, which the order of constants allows. Nobody has read any of this.
