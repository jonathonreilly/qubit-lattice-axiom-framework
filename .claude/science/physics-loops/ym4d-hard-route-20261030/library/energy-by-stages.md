## 1. Objects

Fix a level i, data 𝔡 = (d_l on Sigma_l, l <= i - 1), quiet, and frozen level-i bonds with data, if any, as in `library/quiet-boundary-rarity.md`. Write s_j for the level-j unit in fine lattice spacings.

*The functional with no frozen bonds at its own level.* For a level-m field W on **all** level-m bonds, of flux at most T_2:

    Phi_m(W) := inf { A(U) : (U, V_1, ..., V_m = W) a chain with the data d_l, l < m, on Sigma_l, and no cell above T }.

So Phi_0 = A, and the note's functional is the minimum of Phi over one level's frozen bonds:

    𝔸_{m+1}^𝔡(V') = inf { Phi_m(W) : W in the fibre of V', W = d_m on Sigma_m, flux W <= T }.        (1.1)

**Frozen bonds at level m are not data of Phi_m.** They are data of (1.1) only. This is what makes the recursion below close.

*Free and frozen cells.* A level-j cell is *frozen* if every bond of it is frozen, *free* otherwise; a mixed plaquette is free. Under (N), (B) and clearance:

*Cells with their enlargement.* A_c(U) is the fine energy on c + 4 s_j, as in Prop 10 of `library/relative-density-bound.md`. kappa := min(c_b/16, 1/(2 C_T)), with C_T of Lemma TE. *A dense box:* a box of side L cells with energy above K T^2, as there. All energies below are of the current field.

## 4. Restoration, and the recursion

**Lemma REST (implication, given (LIFT-1), (QD); level of each term).** *Let U be as in the Setting and U^- its stage output, with steps s, E(s) := E_n at step s. There is a level-j field W^+ in the fibre of W, equal to d_j on Sigma_j, flux at most T, with*

    Phi_j(W^+) <= Phi_j(W^-) + (1/8) sum_s E(s) + C K (r_*/L)^(3/2) T^2 #{s}.        (4.1)

*Proof.* W^- differs from an admissible field in two ways. *Frozen bonds:* on frozen bonds in bands, by a mismatch M. Let H_F be M on frozen bonds, extended to free bonds of Q_(n+2) by the retraction of (EXT-d), times a cut-off at scale r_n, as written at the end of Section 7. **It must not be zero on free bonds:** that first form of mine lost a factor r_n^2 (Section 8, item (9)). With it, ||d H_F||^2 <= C m_0 sum_s (a_n + target energy)(s), with m_0 <= C K/kappa the number of steps meeting a region of diameter 6 r_*, by the count in Section 7. *Parents:* mismatch s on parents whose lines meet a step's region: a fraction f <= C (r_*/L)^3 of their lines. As in hostile (a) of Prop 10, |s| <= C f^(1/2) ||F||_(box) <= C f^(1/2) K^(1/2) T. The dense step's parents are restored as there. H_P := the bump of item (f), spread on all free lines of the parent's block. Near a wall this is half of the lines at worst, as bricks are whole blocks, so a parent has either all of its lines frozen, and then no mismatch once H_F is applied, or at least half free. W^+ := W^- e^(H_F + H_P).

*Energy at the end, with no multiplicity.* Let S be the union over steps of Q_(n+3)(s). A cell of S at the end is either unchanged since the start, or last changed at one step s'. Unchanged: it lies in Q_(n+3)(s) off Q_(n+1)(s) for some s, and at time s it was as now, so all such cells together carry at most sum_s (a_(n+1) + a_(n+2))(s) <= sum_s E(s)/C_*. Last changed at s': all such cells carry at most the energy after step s', at most E(s')/C_*. So the fine energy, and by (J) the level-j energy, of W^- on S is at most 2 sum_s E(s)/C_*. By (3.1) and (QD), (a_n + target energy)(s) <= E(s)/C_* + C eta_0 E(s).

*Cost.* By (LIFT-1) for H_F: N_(supp H_F)(W^-)^2 is at most the energy of W^- on S plus the cells beyond. Beyond Q_(n+2)(s) the weight at distance r_n is at most exp(-rho_1 r_n), and cell energies at the end are at most kappa T^2, so that tail is at most C r_n^3 exp(-rho_1 r_n) kappa T^2 <= C eta_0 E(s), by the choice of R_1. So the cost of H_F is at most C_3 ( 1/C_* + eta_0 ) sum_s E(s), with C_3 absolute. Take C_* >= 32 C_3 and eta_0 <= 1/(32 C_3). For H_P, the cost per affected parent is C ||F||_(box) |s| + C |s|^2 <= C K f^(1/2) T^2, and the number of affected parents is at most C #{s}. QED

*So the frozen bonds are restored at the level where they are data, with the action below read through one function; no bump at a general fine field, no fixed-link Lemma RF, no reference minimiser.* Since each step gains at least (kappa/4) T^2 and L is large against r_*, K/kappa, the last term of (4.1) is at most (1/16) sum_s E(s).

**Theorem AE-T (recursion; implication, given Sections 2, 3, 4).** *For a level-(j+1) field W of flux at most T_2, and any fine U with V_(j+1)(U) = W and data d_l, l <= j, let Xi_(j+1)(U) := A(U) - Phi_(j+1)(W). Then*

    Xi_(j+1)(U) >= (1/16) sum_(s in stage j) E(s) + Xi_j(U^-),    Xi_0 := 0.        (4.2)

*Proof.* (1.1), then (4.1), give Phi_(j+1)(W) <= Phi_j(W^+) <= Phi_j(W^-) + (3/16) sum_s E(s). And A(U) - A(U^-) >= (1/4) sum_s E(s), by (3.2). Add A(U^-) - Phi_j(W^-) = Xi_j(U^-). QED

*Finiteness and the order of proof.* (4.2) is proved upward in j, for all fields at once, and the fields U^- are built downward, starting from the given U. Phi_0(W_0^+) = A(W_0^+) is finite, so Phi_1 is finite at the top of stage 0, and (LIFT-1) carries finiteness to Phi_1(W^+), and so on. The data on levels below j are not moved, so the recursion's hypotheses hold at each stage. Level j + 1 data of U^- differ from W near the steps, which is why top and data are named separately: Phi_j has no frozen level-j bonds in it, and the next stage's top is W^-, which is class.

**Corollary (class minimum and non-concentration with data; implication).** Xi_i >= 0 for every chain to V_i of flux at most T_2. Let U be a minimiser of A over all fine fields with data 𝔡 and top V_i. Then (4.2) forces no step at any stage, so Z is empty at every level. So the minimiser is regular, with flux at most T/4 on free cells: **a forced rough cell does not exist for data with (QD).** This is the reference margin that `parts/hard-core--active-energy.md` isolates, obtained as a conclusion. ## 5. Every rough cell pays, with no factor of depth
