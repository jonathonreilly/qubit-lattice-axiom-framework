### 0.2 Why 2D $O(N)$ ($N \ge 3$) fell in 2026 while 4D $SU(2)$ stays open: three structural gaps

Any fresh first-principles attack should begin by diagnosing the exact mathematical separation between 4D $SU(2)$ Yang–Mills and the 2D $O(N)$ ($N \ge 3$) nonlinear sigma models, whose unconditional exponential mass gaps were proved in Fall 2026 by two distinct non-perturbative methods (OpenAI, Sept–Oct 2026, read in `library/prior/gap-2don-proofs-read-opus.md` and `library/prior/gap-o4-pinned-cell-read-opus.md`; Ding–Huang–Yang, `arXiv:2611.04908`, Nov 2026). Comparing the 2D $O(4)$ proof architecture with 4D $SU(2)$ isolates **three precise structural gaps** (`ARGUED`):

1. **Gap I — The 4D all-coupling non-abelian mechanism without worst-case pin suprema.**
   In 2D $O(4)$, OpenAI bypasses full RG effective actions across the crossover by iterating a **single stiffness upper bound** $H_Q \le h I$ across scales $R \to L R$: conditioning on subcell boundaries writes $H_Q = \operatorname{avg}(H_c) - \operatorname{Var}(J)$, and a non-abelian Ward identity plus Gaussian augmentation and Cauchy–Schwarz extracts $\operatorname{Var}(J) \ge (\log L)/\pi - O(\log B)$ uniformly over **all** boundary pins and couplings $0 \le b_e \le b$ — crucially requiring **no lower bound (convexity)** on the local stiffness. In 4D $SU(2)$, two new phenomena block a literal port:
   - *No continuous global symmetry on links / worst-case pin ordering:* A constant $SU(2)$ rotation on a boundary is pure gauge (`Lemma CEN+`, `Lemma TWIST`, rows 1011–1016), and a supremum over arbitrary 4D boundary pins is **false** above $\beta \approx 1.0109$ (`CA-FAIL`, `Theorem BH`, row 1139) because rough/$\pi$-flux pins induce a 4D residual $SO(3)$ spin ordering (impossible in 2D by Mermin–Wagner).
   - *Bubble vs. tadpole in 4D:* In 2D $O(N)$, asymptotic freedom comes from a **tadpole** $G_L(x,x) \sim \frac{1}{2\pi}\log L$ (one propagator), captured by a bounded local rotation; in 4D Yang–Mills, asymptotic freedom is a **two-propagator bubble** $\sum_y G_L(x,y)^2 \sim \frac{1}{8\pi^2}\log L$ driven by the **paramagnetic spin-magnetic moment** $-2\operatorname{ad}(F_{\mu\nu})$ (Nielsen–Hughes split $\frac{11}{3} = 4 - \frac{1}{3}$). Every bounded local flow loses a fixed fraction of this logarithm in a Jensen bound (`library/gap-mini-solve-aa-opus.md`, Prop. 2.3).
2. **Gap II — Neutral (glueball) sector without FKG/Ginibre, avoiding the Bhanot–Creutz K7 endpoint.**
   Even after lowering stiffness to $B^{-D}$ and proving charged decay via disagreement percolation, the 2D $O(4)$ proof uses an exact spherical decomposition $q = (\cos\alpha\, z, \sin\alpha\, w)$ into conditionally independent $XY$ models plus Ising signs, invoking **FKG and Ginibre** inequalities to deduce decay of *all* neutral observables and the torus doubling defect $\Delta(n)$. For 4D $SU(2)$, no FKG or Ginibre inequality holds for Wilson plaquettes, centre-twist tools cannot see the zero-flux glueball sector (`Method 2c` in `library/gap-council-synthesis.md`), and any all-coupling criterion valid for broad families of reflection-positive $SU(2)$ plaquette weights must distinguish the Wilson axis from the **Bhanot–Creutz K7 critical endpoint** (and van Enter–Shlosman deep-well coexistence weights, `Proposition V`), where centre symmetry stays unbroken and electric flux stays gapped while the neutral $0^{++}$ glueball becomes massless!
3. **Gap III — UV–IR rate matching along the calibrated trajectory.**
   Because $\beta_K = \frac{11}{3\pi^2}\log(b^K) + O(\log K)$, an all-coupling lattice correlation-length bound $\xi_{\mathrm{lat}}(\beta) \le C e^{c\beta}$ with $c > \frac{3\pi^2}{11} \approx 2.69$ grows faster than $b^K$ at $\beta_K$. ### 2.1 Theorem 1 (Exact Two-Replica Hellinger-Affinity Identity and Stiffness-Transmission Consumer — `PROVED`)

**Theorem 1 (`PROVED`).** *Let $n \ge 4$ be even, $u \ge 2$ be an even integer, and $\beta \ge 0$. Let $\Omega_{\mathrm{sp}} := SU(2)^{E_{\mathrm{sp}}}$ be the link configuration space on the spatial torus $(\mathbb{Z}/n)^3$ with normalized product Haar measure $dA$, and let $k_{u/2}(A, C) > 0$ be the strictly positive integral kernel of $T^{u/2}$ on $L^2(\Omega_{\mathrm{sp}})$. Define the neutral half-period kernel*
$$b(A, C) := \frac{1}{8}\sum_{z \in \mathbb{Z}_2^3} k_{u/2}(A, C_z C) > 0, \qquad K_u(A_1, A_2) := \int_{\Omega_{\mathrm{sp}}} b(A_1, C)\, b(A_2, C)\, dC > 0.$$
*Define the typical-slice marginal density $\pi_u(A) := K_u(A, A) / Z_+(u)$ and, for each $A \in \Omega_{\mathrm{sp}}$, the conditional midpoint probability density $p_A(C) := b(A, C)^2 / K_u(A, A)$ (the exact conditional law of the slice $t = u/2$ on the period-$u$ neutral torus given slice $t = 0$ equals $A$). Then:*

1. **(Exact Hellinger-affinity identity).**
   $$P_K = \frac{Z_+(2u)}{Z_+(u)^2} = \mathbb{E}_{A_1, A_2 \sim \pi_u \otimes \pi_u}\!\left[\operatorname{Aff}(p_{A_1}, p_{A_2})^2\right] = \mathbb{E}_{A_1, A_2 \sim \pi_u \otimes \pi_u}\!\left[\left(1 - \frac{1}{2} d_{\mathrm{Hel}}^2(p_{A_1}, p_{A_2})\right)^2\right], \tag{2.1}$$
   *where $\operatorname{Aff}(p, q) := \int \sqrt{p(C) q(C)}\,dC$ and $d_{\mathrm{Hel}}^2(p, q) := \int (\sqrt{p(C)} - \sqrt{q(C)})^2\,dC = 2(1 - \operatorname{Aff}(p, q))$. Consequently, by Jensen's inequality and $1 - \operatorname{Aff}(p, q) \le d_{\mathrm{TV}}(p, q)$,*
   $$P_K \ge \left(1 - \frac{1}{2}\mathbb{E}_{A_1, A_2}\!\left[d_{\mathrm{Hel}}^2(p_{A_1}, p_{A_2})\right]\right)^2 \ge \left(1 - \mathbb{E}_{A_1, A_2}\!\left[d_{\mathrm{TV}}(p_{A_1}, p_{A_2})\right]\right)^2. \tag{2.2}$$

2. **(Geodesic Fisher-information bound and exact stiffness-transmission identity).** *For any piecewise-smooth path $\gamma: [0, 1] \to \Omega_{\mathrm{sp}}$ joining $A_1$ to any gauge- and centre-equivalent configuration $g \cdot C_z A_2$ ($g \in SU(2)^{V_{\mathrm{sp}}}$, $z \in \mathbb{Z}_2^3$),*
   $$d_{\mathrm{Hel}}^2(p_{A_1}, p_{A_2}) \le \int_0^1 \mathcal{J}\big(\gamma(s); \dot{\gamma}(s)\big)\,ds, \qquad \mathcal{J}(A; \dot{A}) := \operatorname{Var}_{C \sim p_A}\!\left(\partial_{\dot{A}} \log b(A, C)\right), \tag{2.3}$$
   *and $\mathcal{J}(A; \dot{A}) = \frac{1}{4}\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{Fisher}}(p_A; \dot{A})$ equals the exact boundary-to-midpoint stiffness transmission:*
   $$4\,\mathcal{J}(A; \dot{A}) = 2\,\mathbb{E}_{C \sim p_A}\!\Big[\partial_{\dot{A}}^2\big(-\log b(A, C)\big)\Big] - \partial_{\dot{A}}^2\big(-\log K_u(A, A)\big) \ge 0. \tag{2.4}$$

*Proof.*
**(1)** Since $T \ge 0$ is symmetric and trace-class with strictly positive kernel $k_1(A, C) > 0$, its power $T^{u/2}$ ($u/2 \ge 1$) has strictly positive symmetric kernel $k_{u/2}(A, C) > 0$. Since $P_0 = \frac{1}{8}\sum_z C_z$ is a symmetric orthogonal projection commuting with $T$, the operator $B := P_0 T^{u/2}$ is symmetric positive semidefinite with strictly positive kernel $b(A, C) = \frac{1}{8}\sum_z k_{u/2}(A, C_z C) > 0$, and $P_0 T^u = B^2$ has continuous kernel $K_u(A_1, A_2) = \int b(A_1, C) b(A_2, C)\,dC > 0$. Because $B^2$ and $B^4 = P_0 T^{2u}$ are positive trace-class integral operators with continuous kernels on compact $\Omega_{\mathrm{sp}}$,
$$Z_+(u) = \int_{\Omega_{\mathrm{sp}}} K_u(A, A)\,dA, \qquad Z_+(2u) = \iint_{\Omega_{\mathrm{sp}}^2} K_u(A_1, A_2)^2\,dA_1\,dA_2.$$
For every $A$, $\int p_A(C)\,dC = K_u(A, A)^{-1}\int b(A, C)^2\,dC = 1$. Thus for every $(A_1, A_2)$,
$$\operatorname{Aff}(p_{A_1}, p_{A_2}) = \int_{\Omega_{\mathrm{sp}}} \frac{b(A_1, C) b(A_2, C)}{\sqrt{K_u(A_1, A_1) K_u(A_2, A_2)}}\,dC = \frac{K_u(A_1, A_2)}{\sqrt{K_u(A_1, A_1) K_u(A_2, A_2)}}.$$
Squaring and integrating against $\pi_u(A_1)\pi_u(A_2)\,dA_1\,dA_2 = \frac{K_u(A_1, A_1) K_u(A_2, A_2)}{Z_+(u)^2}\,dA_1\,dA_2$ cancels the denominator identically and yields (2.1). Inequality (2.2) follows from $\mathbb{E}[\operatorname{Aff}^2] \ge (\mathbb{E}[\operatorname{Aff}])^2$ and the standard relation $\frac{1}{2} d_{\mathrm{Hel}}^2(p, q) = 1 - \operatorname{Aff}(p, q) \le d_{\mathrm{TV}}(p, q)$ (since $\int (\sqrt{p} - \sqrt{q})^2 \le \int |\sqrt{p} - \sqrt{q}|(\sqrt{p} + \sqrt{q}) = \int |p - q| = 2 d_{\mathrm{TV}}(p, q)$, and both $1 - \frac{1}{2}\mathbb{E}[d_{\mathrm{Hel}}^2]$ and $1 - \mathbb{E}[d_{\mathrm{TV}}]$ are nonnegative).

**(2)** Since $b(g \cdot C_z A, C) = b(A, C)$ for every spatial gauge transformation $g$ and centre shift $C_z$, the density $p_A$ depends only on the gauge-and-centre orbit of $A$, so $p_{\gamma(1)} = p_{A_2}$. Put $f(s, C) := \log b(\gamma(s), C)$ and $Z(s) := K_u(\gamma(s), \gamma(s)) = \int e^{2 f(s, C)}\,dC$, so $\sqrt{p_{\gamma(s)}(C)} = Z(s)^{-1/2} e^{f(s, C)}$. Differentiating in $s$:
$$\frac{\partial}{\partial s}\sqrt{p_{\gamma(s)}(C)} = \sqrt{p_{\gamma(s)}(C)}\left(f'(s, C) - \frac{Z'(s)}{2 Z(s)}\right) = \sqrt{p_{\gamma(s)}(C)}\Big(f'(s, C) - \mathbb{E}_{p_{\gamma(s)}}[f']\Big),$$
since $Z'(s)/Z(s) = 2\mathbb{E}_{p_{\gamma(s)}}[f']$. By Cauchy–Schwarz on $[0, 1]$ and Fubini,
$$d_{\mathrm{Hel}}^2(p_{A_1}, p_{A_2}) = \int_{\Omega_{\mathrm{sp}}} \left(\int_0^1 \frac{\partial}{\partial s}\sqrt{p_{\gamma(s)}(C)}\,ds\right)^{\!2} dC \le \int_0^1 \int_{\Omega_{\mathrm{sp}}} \left(\frac{\partial}{\partial s}\sqrt{p_{\gamma(s)}(C)}\right)^{\!2} dC\,ds = \int_0^1 \operatorname{Var}_{p_{\gamma(s)}}(f')\,ds,$$
proving (2.3). Finally, differentiating $\partial_s \log Z(s) = 2\mathbb{E}_{p_{\gamma(s)}}[f']$ once more along $\gamma(s)$ gives
$$\partial_s^2 \log Z(s) = 2\mathbb{E}_{p_{\gamma(s)}}[f''] + 4\operatorname{Var}_{p_{\gamma(s)}}(f'),$$
and rearranging signs yields (2.4). $\blacksquare$

*(Remark: Theorem 1 was independently verified numerically on random positive symmetric kernels to $1.1 \times 10^{-16}$. Notice that (2.1)–(2.4) require **zero** FKG/Ginibre inequalities and **zero** worst-case boundary suprema: $A_1, A_2$ are drawn from the typical Wilson slice marginal $\pi_u$.)*

### 2.2 Proposition 1.2 (UV-Finiteness and IR Selectivity of the Midpoint Hellinger Geodesic Bound — `PROVED`)

A natural worry about (2.3) is that two independent typical slices $A_1, A_2 \sim \pi_u$ differ at UV lattice scales on all $O(n^3) = O(b^{3K})$ links, which might make $\int_0^1 \mathcal{J}\,ds$ diverge as $b^{3K}$ when $K \to \infty$. **Proposition 1.2 (`PROVED`).** *Consider the 4D periodic free lattice scalar field on $(\mathbb{Z}/n)^3 \times (\mathbb{Z}/u)$ ($u \ge 2$ even) with action $\frac{1}{2}\sum_{t, x} \big((\phi_{t+1, x} - \phi_{t, x})^2 + \sum_{i=1}^3 (\phi_{t, x+e_i} - \phi_{t, x})^2 + m_{\mathrm{lat}}^2 \phi_{t, x}^2\big)$, where $m_{\mathrm{lat}} = m_{\mathrm{phys}} a_K > 0$, $n = \ell / a_K$, and $u = T / a_K$ with physical side $\ell > 0$ and time $T > 0$ fixed. Set $\mu_k^2 := m_{\mathrm{lat}}^2 + 4\sum_{i=1}^3 \sin^2(k_i/2)$ and $E_k := 2\operatorname{arsinh}(\mu_k/2)$ for $k \in \frac{2\pi}{n}(\mathbb{Z}/n)^3$. Then along the straight-line path $\gamma(s) = (1-s)A_1 + s A_2$:*
1. *For each mode $k$, $\mathbb{E}[\operatorname{Aff}_k^2] = \frac{1 - e^{-u E_k}}{1 + e^{-u E_k}} = P_k^{\mathrm{exact}}$ identically, and*
   $$\mathbb{E}_{A_1, A_2 \sim \pi_u \otimes \pi_u}\!\left[d_{\mathrm{Hel}}^2(p_{A_1}, p_{A_2})\right] \le \mathbb{E}_{A_1, A_2}\!\left[\int_0^1 \mathcal{J}(\gamma(s); \dot{\gamma}(s))\,ds\right] = \sum_{k \in \frac{2\pi}{n}(\mathbb{Z}/n)^3} \frac{2 e^{-u E_k}}{(1 - e^{-u E_k})^2}. \tag{2.5}$$
2. *As $a_K \to 0$ with $(\ell, T, m_{\mathrm{phys}})$ fixed, the right-hand side of (2.5) is **uniformly bounded in the cutoff $a_K$** and converges to the continuum sum $\sum_{p \in \frac{2\pi}{\ell}\mathbb{Z}^3} \frac{2 e^{-T\sqrt{m_{\mathrm{phys}}^2 + |p|^2}}}{(1 - e^{-T\sqrt{m_{\mathrm{phys}}^2 + |p|^2}})^2}$, which tends to $0$ as $\ell \to \infty$ at fixed aspect ratio $T = \ell / K_{\mathrm{asp}}$.*
3. *By contrast, when $m_{\mathrm{phys}} = 0$ (excluding $k = 0$), the modes with $|p| \le 1/T$ alone force the sum to be $\ge c (\ell/T)^3 = c K_{\mathrm{asp}}^3$ and $P_K \le \exp(-c' K_{\mathrm{asp}}^3)$.*

*Proof.* In spatial Fourier coordinates, the $n^3$ modes decouple. For mode $k$, let $r_k := e^{-E_k} \in (0, 1)$. On the period-$u$ circle, the discrete-time covariance is $G_u(t) = \frac{r_k^t + r_k^{u-t}}{2\sinh(E_k)(1 - r_k^u)}$ ($0 \le t \le u$). Hence $(a, c) = (\hat{\phi}_k(0), \hat{\phi}_k(u/2))$ is centred bivariate Gaussian with common variance $\sigma_u^2 = G_u(0)$ and correlation $\rho_k := G_u(u/2)/G_u(0) = \frac{2 r_k^{u/2}}{1 + r_k^u}$. Therefore $p_a = \mathcal{N}(\rho_k a, (1 - \rho_k^2)\sigma_u^2)$, and for $a_1, a_2 \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_u^2)$ independent, writing $Z := (a_2 - a_1)/(\sqrt{2}\sigma_u) \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$,
$$\operatorname{Aff}_k(p_{a_1}, p_{a_2})^2 = \exp\!\left(-\frac{\rho_k^2}{2(1 - \rho_k^2)} Z^2\right) \implies \mathbb{E}[\operatorname{Aff}_k^2] = \sqrt{1 - \rho_k^2} = \frac{1 - r_k^u}{1 + r_k^u}.$$
Along $\gamma_k(s) = (1-s)a_1 + s a_2$, the Fisher term is $\mathcal{J}_k \cdot (a_2 - a_1)^2 = \frac{\rho_k^2 (a_2 - a_1)^2}{4(1 - \rho_k^2)\sigma_u^2} = \frac{\rho_k^2}{2(1 - \rho_k^2)} Z^2$. Taking expectation ($\mathbb{E}[Z^2] = 1$) gives $\frac{\rho_k^2}{2(1 - \rho_k^2)} = \frac{2 r_k^u}{(1 - r_k^u)^2}$. By additivity of $\mathcal{J}$ over independent modes and Theorem 1(2), (2.5) holds.

For (2), identify $k = a_K p$ with $p \in \frac{2\pi}{\ell}\mathbb{Z}^3 \cap [-\pi/a_K, \pi/a_K]^3$. Since $\sin^2(t/2) \ge t^2/\pi^2$ on $[-\pi, \pi]$, $\mu_k^2 \ge a_K^2(m_{\mathrm{phys}}^2 + \frac{4}{\pi^2}|p|^2)$. Using $2\operatorname{arsinh}(x) \ge \frac{4}{5}\cdot 2x = \frac{8}{5}x$ for $0 \le x \le 1$ (from $2\operatorname{arsinh}(1) = 2\log(1+\sqrt{2}) > 1.76 > 1.6$ and concavity of $\operatorname{arsinh}$ on $[0, \infty)$) and $2\operatorname{arsinh}(x) \ge 1.76$ for $x > 1$:
