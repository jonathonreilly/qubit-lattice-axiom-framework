For the normalized spatial zero mode $q$, the infinite-time covariance solves $(2+\mu^2)C(k)-C(k-1)-C(k+1)=0$ away from zero. **HOLDS for a single projected spectrum. FAILS for the unprojected quotient-torus definition used in §2.1.** The first mismatch is already at line 42 if $Z$ denotes an ordinary torus. Write $Z_N(u)=\operatorname{Tr}(P_0T^u)$ for the zero-spatial-twist, electric-neutral trace of row 1367 line 11. In the tensor trace use $P_0\otimes P_0$ on the two copies. Each $Z_N$ is a centre-twist average. For example, on an abstract positive transfer space with a neutral vacuum and a single charged excitation of relative weight $a\in(0,1)$ at time $t$, $x_N=0$, while the ordinary swap has $r=a/(1+a+a^2)>0$. Changing the time alone cannot repair the missing projector. The projected purity is already on file in row 1356, `gap-mini-mneutral-fable.md` 48–49, 80–81, complementing the full swap identity I3.

For $a_i=(\lambda_i/\lambda_0)^t$ in the *same* neutral sector, put $x=\sum_{i>0}a_i$, $y=\sum_{i>0}a_i^2$. Substitution gives

$$
r_N=\frac{Z_N(t)^2-Z_N(2t)}{Z_N(t)^2+Z_N(2t)}
=\frac{2x+x^2-y}{2+2x+x^2+y}.
$$

The denominator is positive and the expression decreases with $y$. Evaluating at $x^2$ and at zero gives exactly the bounds of lines 188–191,

$$
\frac{x}{1+x+x^2}\le r_N\le\frac{2x+x^2}{2+2x+x^2}\le x.
$$

The original $x/(1+x)$ lower bound fails for one excited level, where $y=x^2$ and the corrected lower bound is attained.

The lower bound printed in row 1402 tends to zero for large $x$. To obtain the converse claimed at 192, use $y\le x$ as well. The purity $p=(1+y)/(1+x)^2\le1/(1+x)$ gives $r_N=(1-p)/(1+p)\ge x/(2+x)$. This is the min-entropy comparison of `gap-compress-opus.md` 48–58 applied to the projected spectrum. Thus $r_N\le2/23$ suffices for $x\le4/21$, and $x\le4/21$ implies $r_N\le92/533$. The ratio varies with $y$ even at $x=4/21$. “About 0.16” is no exact threshold. The odd probability is $(1-p)/2=r_N/(1+r_N)$, whereas $r_N$ is the odd-to-even weight ratio.
