### Step 6. "Zero wall" -- the claim FAILS in the sense asked (no e^{c n^3} change of measure)

What is true: in both structures the four-corner identities (2.3), (2.8) and the angle bounds (2.5), (2.11) are exact, with no prefactor; no spatial plaquette is changed; no end is randomised. In this sense they differ in kind from row 1574's pair rotation, whose rotated ends are hot on the whole slice at every coupling (Lemma NOISE) and whose weights move by up to exp(36 beta n^3).
What is not true: the interpolating measures P_{l,l} carry a codimension-one coupling defect on 3n^3 temporal plaquettes per seam/cut, and its free energy is extensive.
- Structure II, exact in the free field (from the determinants of Theorem 2.3): per transverse mode log Z_cut(0,0) - log Z_cut(1,1) = -log tanh(omega/2) + O(e^{-v omega}), so the corners differ by Theta(n^{D-1}) = Theta(n^3) in D = 4. For Wilson, log Z_cut(0,0) - log Z_cut(1,1) = 2 log c_0 - 2 log lambda_0 + o(1), and c_0 = <M1, psi_0>^2 is a free-boundary overlap, of order e^{-sigma n^3} (ARGUED; no proof given here).
- Structure I: phi(l) = log Z_2seam(l,l) is exactly convex (the exponent is linear in l along the diagonal), phi(0) = phi(1), phi'(0) = E_{P_00}[sum F_0 + sum F_v] = -12 n^3 beta <Re U_p^temp> < 0. At leading order in beta (cumulant expansion at beta -> 0, where Var F_e = beta^2 and <Re U_p> = beta/4 + O(beta^3); ARGUED, not a proof) phi(l) - phi(0) = -3 beta^2 n^3 l(1-l) + O(beta^3 n^3), i.e. -(3/4) beta^2 n^3 at l = 1/2.
So the defect measures differ from the physical measure by densities of size e^{Theta(n^3)}. The exact identities cancel this, but any bound on h(l) that is imported from the physical measure (lambda = 1) by density comparison loses up to e^{12 beta n^3} (two layers of 3n^3 plaquettes, each changed by at most 2 beta). A bound on h without loss needs a method that works directly in the defect measures uniformly in the layer coupling (cluster expansion does this at small beta; nothing in the note does it on [2,3]). "Zero wall" is therefore correct only as "zero spatial wall and no randomised ends"; as "no e^{c n^3} change-of-measure loss" it is not established and the wording should be corrected to: exact identities whose interpolating Gibbs measures carry an extensive, deterministic, gauge-invariant coupling defect.
