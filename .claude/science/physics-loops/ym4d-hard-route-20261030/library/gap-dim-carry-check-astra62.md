### A signed quotient on two admissible Wilson grids

Here is a sign for the actual neutral excited trace on the omitted pair, with its range. Fix a finite periodic spatial cube $B$ of side at least five. $$
                 s_B(2q;\beta,\beta)<s_B(q;2\beta,\beta/2).       \qquad (1)
$$

The fine and coarse sides have the same physical time period and spatial box. The inequality is stated for transfer powers; taking $q\ge3$ gives ordinary nondegenerate periodic tori. The proof uses the existing electric-kernel comparison of row 863, *gap-crux-skeleton-max* §3.1–3.2, and the plaquette electric eigenfunction of row 1145, *gap-solve2-astra62* at (21).

For $X=\sum_{p\subset B}x_p$ and $M=\#\{p\subset B\}$, $K_b^{1/2}e^{aX}K_b^{1/2}$ lies between $e^{-aM}K_b$ and $e^{aM}K_b$ in quadratic-form order. $$
 s_B(1;\beta,\beta)\le e^{2\beta M}C_B\beta^4,\qquad
 (\lambda_1/\lambda_0)_{(2\beta,\beta/2)}
       \ge e^{-4\beta M}u(\beta/2)^4 .
$$
Choose $\beta_B$ small enough that the square of the first bound is strictly below the second for all $0<\beta<\beta_B$.
