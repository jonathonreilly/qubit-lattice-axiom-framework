## (ii) Lemma EVEN and the paired-link vacuum

**(a) HOLDS.** For positive trace-class $B$ of nonzero trace, $p_j=\|\Pi_j B^{1/2}\|_{\rm HS}^2/\operatorname{Tr}B$. Here $\Pi_j$ is the spectral projection of the Haar-preserving circle at integer frequency $j$, with half-turn equal to swap. The projections resolve the identity. Summing $j^2\ge1$ on $j\ne0$ gives $E j^2\ge1-p_0$, also for an infinite moment. Commutation of $B$ with the whole circle is unnecessary.

**(b) HOLDS.** Write $B/\operatorname{Tr}B=w|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|+B'$, with $B'\ge0$ of trace $1-w$. Thus $p_0\le w\|\Pi_0\Psi\|^2+1-w$. Haar averaging $\exp(isG)$ projects onto its zero mode. Its diagonal matrix element is precisely the stated mean overlap.

**(c) HOLDS.** Let $p_i$ now denote the eigenvalues of the one-copy density $\rho$. Then $P=\sum p_i^2\le p_{\max}\sum p_i=p_{\max}$. A product of top one-copy vectors for $B=\rho^{\otimes2}$ has weight $p_{\max}^2$. Inserting this in (b) gives the claimed $1-e/P^2$. At $e=84/625$, parity gives $P\ge1-2e=457/625$. The exact lower bound is

$$
  1-\frac{84\cdot625}{457^2}
   =1-\frac{52500}{208849}
   =\frac{156349}{208849}>\frac{187}{250}=0.748 .
$$

The last cross-products differ by $32487>0$.

**Reading — HOLDS AFTER CORRECTION.** The first wrong inference is “is the conjunction” at line 181. Small moment implies the parity bound and a large mean vacuum overlap. There is no converse estimate on the high even harmonics. For instance weight $1/m$ split between $\pm2m$ and the rest at zero has zero odd weight, zero-mode weight tending to one, and second moment $4m$. This example tests the inference from a winding law alone. A product-state realization follows below. Check 1585(iii) gives the Hilbert–Schmidt identity behind the distinction.

For a positive rank-one example take two replicas of $x\in\mathbb R^2$ with standard Gaussian reference. Keep their mean fixed and rotate their difference by $s$. This preserves the product measure and swaps at $\pi$. Applying the Gaussian quantile to each uniform coordinate conjugates it to a measure-preserving circle on a doubled Haar two-torus, up to null sets. In one copy let $f_m(x)=c_m(1+m^{-1/2}\cos(mx_1))$ with $\|f_m\|_2=1$ and $m\ge2$. The density $|f_m\rangle\langle f_m|$ has purity one. Its vacuum pair tends to the invariant constant in $L^2$, so its mean overlap tends to one. Write $D$ for the rotation derivative. Up to sign, $D(f_m\otimes f_m)$ is $(x_2-y_2)(f'_m\otimes f_m-f_m\otimes f'_m)/2$. The prime differentiates the first coordinate. Independence, $\mathbb E(x_2-y_2)^2=2$ and $\langle f_m,f'_m\rangle=0$ give $\mathbb E j^2=\|f'_m\|_2^2=c_m^2m(1-e^{-2m^2})/2\to\infty$. This example tests the algebraic reading of EVEN. The Wilson answer requires its own spatial law.

3. At angle $s$, put $\alpha=\cos^2(s/2)$, $\gamma=1-\alpha$, $h_s=\alpha^4+\gamma^4$. Apply the gauge averages of 1581 Lemma 1 (check 1583 Step 3) to the whole packing.
