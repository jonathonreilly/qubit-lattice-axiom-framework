### Step 5 (done). Attack A: transfer operator, Perron-Frobenius, reflection positivity

**5.1 The even two-slice law (PROVED).** E(u,v) = (A^t(u,v) + A^t(u,Jv))/2 > 0, so w_e := E^2/Z_e, Z_e = Tr(T^a P_e) = (1 - p) Z, is a probability law; w_e = (w + w o Theta + 2 r w_tw)/(2(1 + r)), and w_e o Theta = w_e. R o Theta = -R, so E_(w_e)[R] = 0, and since O^2 = R^2 E^2 with Tr(T^a P_o) = p Z:
  p/(1 - p) = E_(w_e)[R^2] = Var_(w_e)(R),
  sum_(b in u) E_w|Delta G_b|^2 = 4 (1 - p) E_(w_e)[ |grad_u R|^2/(1 - R)^2 ] = 4 (1 - p) E_(w_e)[ |grad_u R|^2 (1 + R^2)/(1 - R^2)^2 ].
Proof: A = E(1 + R), |grad X|^2 = 4|grad R|^2/(1 - R^2)^2, so A^2 |grad X|^2 = 4 E^2 |grad R|^2/(1 - R)^2; the Theta-image form uses (A o Theta)^2 = E^2 (1 - R)^2 and E_w|grad X|^2 = E_w[rho^2 |grad X|^2] (4.1 b); average the two. QED.
So (VAR-SLAB) is exactly a Poincare inequality for the odd function R under the even law w_e: its left side is Var_(w_e)(R), its right side is (up to the factor (1 - R)^(-2), which lies in (1/4, infinity)) the Dirichlet energy of R. (FR) is that Dirichlet energy. This is the exact form of 4.2.

**5.2 The twist-indicator form (PROVED).** The even slab law with bottom u and top {v, Jv} is the mixture of the two slab laws mu_(u,v), mu_(u,Jv) with weights A^t(u,v), A^t(u,Jv). Let sigma = +1 on the first component and -1 on the second. Then R(u,v) = E_mix[sigma], and M_s := E_mix[sigma | layers 0..s] = R_(t-s)(w_s, v) is a martingale from M_0 = R(u,v) to M_t = sigma. The u-dependence of the mixture is only through the first-layer weight, so
  grad_b R(u,v) = beta Cov_mix(f_b, sigma),   sum_b |grad_b R|^2 <= beta^2 Gamma(u,v) (1 - R^2),
with Gamma(u,v) the operator norm of the covariance matrix of the first-layer forces (f_b)_(b in u) under the mixture (Bessel inequality). Reading: (FR) asks that the bottom layer of the slab barely knows whether the top is v or Jv; the size of Cov_mix(f_b, sigma) is the information about sigma carried down to the first layer.
Trivial and near-trivial bounds: Gamma <= 3|B| gives back 24 beta^2 |B|. If sup Gamma = Gamma_* is volume-free, then the sum over b in u of E_w|Delta G_b|^2 is <= 2 beta^2 Gamma_* (1 + E_w cosh X), with E_w cosh X = (r + E_w rho^3)/2 (the identity E_w[1/rho] = E_w[rho^3] follows from (T2) with F = rho). Gamma_* is a sup over boundaries and is not known to be volume-free at order one (OPEN).

**5.3 Spectral and Perron-Frobenius content (PROVED / ARGUED as marked).**
1. (PROVED) Let Q(u, dw) = A_T(u,w) psi_0(w) dw/(lambda_0 psi_0(u)) be the ground-state-transformed one-step chain; it is reversible for psi_0^2 and commutes with J. Its top eigenvalue on odd functions is e^(-E_-). So the flux energy E_- is the spectral gap of Q on odd functions: 1 - e^(-E_-) = inf over odd h of (Dirichlet form of Q)(h)/||h||^2. The flux half is a Poincare inequality for Q on odd functions; (FR) reads the same gap through the Langevin Dirichlet form instead.
2. (PROVED) For even s >= 2 and t - s >= 0 (Cauchy-Schwarz with T^t = T^(s/2) P_o T^(t-s) P_o T^(s/2); T^(s/2) maps the point mass at u to the smooth function A^(s/2)(., u), so O^s(u,u) is finite): |O^t(u,v)| <= lambda_-^(t-s) (O^s(u,u) O^s(v,v))^(1/2). Any bound on R or grad R through this route needs a bound on lambda_-/lambda_0, i.e. on E_-.
3. (ARGUED) Perron-Frobenius gives lambda_- < lambda_0 only qualitatively (psi_0 > 0 is even). Birkhoff's contraction gives lambda_-/lambda_0 <= tanh(Delta/4) with Delta the projective diameter of T, which is at least of order beta times the number of time-like plaquettes of one layer (3|B|): useless for large |B|. Reflection positivity applies to p = Tr(T^a P_o)/Tr(T^a) directly; passing through (FR) only adds the ratio and the Langevin gap.

### Step 6 (done). Attack B: direct bounds (sheet, coupling, cluster, curvature)

**6.1 The sheet form (PROVED).** For a sheet Sigma of time-like plaquettes inside the slab, homologous to the cut (the L_2 L_3 plaquettes of one layer that contain the cut links, moved by changes of variables), let W_Sigma = prod over p in Sigma of e^(-2 beta x_p). Then rho = E_(mu_(u,v))[W_Sigma] for every such Sigma, and
  Delta G_b(u,v) = beta Cov_(mu_(u,v))(f_b, W_Sigma)/rho   for every Sigma not containing p(b).
Proof: flipping the interior slices from the sheet's layer upward is a Haar-preserving change of variables that maps mu_(u,Jv) to mu_(u,v) reweighted by W_Sigma/rho; J leaves the other plaquettes unchanged. For two disjoint homologous sheets, W_Sigma W_Sigma' is a bounding twist, so E_mu[W_Sigma W_Sigma'] = 1. QED.
Obstruction (identity PROVED, size ARGUED): the same change of variables gives E_mu[W_Sigma^2] = rho E_(mu_(u,Jv))[prod over Sigma of e^(2 beta x_p)] >= rho exp(2 beta sum over Sigma of E x_p) (Jensen); with a mean plaquette near 0.6 at beta = 2.3 this is about rho e^(2.8 L_2 L_3), while Cov(f_b, W_Sigma) is of order e^(-t E_-). Cauchy-Schwarz loses e^(c L_2 L_3). Averaging W_Sigma over N disjoint sheets gives Var(Wbar) = (E W_Sigma^2 - 1)/N + 1 - rho^2 (PROVED from the two moments above), and N <= (t - 1) L_1, so the first term still dominates. The smallness of Delta G_b is a cancellation inside a likelihood ratio with exponentially large variance: shared assumption SA3 of row 899 (READ gap-wall-assume-max.md:458).

**6.2 Coupling and Dobrushin (ARGUED).** A coupling of mu_(u,v) and mu_(u,Jv) gives |Delta G_b| <= 2 beta P(the coupled first layers differ at b). Disagreement starts on the whole cut sheet and must cross t layers to reach b. Any single-site or vertex-block Dobrushin coupling has worst-case influence caps 2/3 and 0.775 at order one, and a background-uniform layer bound caps at 4 G_3 = 1.0109 > 1 (facts stated in the brief from row 839, checked; not re-read here). So the disagreement per layer is not contracted, and the method gives nothing at beta_W in [2.3, 2.6]. It is also group-blind (it would pass for frozen Q_8 and 2T, where the slab with top Jv carries a domain wall and Delta G_b is not small; Step 7).

**6.3 Clustering in the slab at rate m (ARGUED, GUIDE arithmetic).** A weaker true bound would come from time clustering of the boundary force: |Delta G_b|^2 <= 2|G_b(u,Jv) - g_b(u)|^2 + 2|G_b(u,v) - g_b(u)|^2 for any g_b(u). Its natural size is the full dependence of the boundary force on the far slice, about beta^2 e^(-2 m t) per link, which includes the even (glue) changes of v, not only the flip. GUIDE at the seed box (|B| = 8000, beta = 2.3, mass m = 1.2 in lattice units, t = 5): 3|B| beta^2 e^(-2 m t) is about 0.8 times a local constant, against the needed 4 lambda p_* of about 8e-4 lambda. So clustering at rate m is too weak by about 10^3/lambda at the seed box; it would need t of about (log|B| + log(1/p_*))/(2m) = (9.0 + 8.5)/2.4, about 7.3 lattice units, i.e. a about 15 instead of 10. And proving such clustering in the slab under a sheet perturbation at order one is the glue half of the gap problem itself (OPEN).

**6.4 Integration by parts and curvature (ARGUED).** The Ward identity sum over the plaquettes p containing a time-like link g of E_mu[grad_g x_p] = 0 (left invariance of Haar) moves the force between plaquettes; it does not make Delta G_b small. ### Step 7 (done). Fact tests: U(1) Coulomb, frozen Q_8 and 2T, and SU(2)

The zero mode is a particle on a circle with energies E_1 k^2, E_1 = L_1/(2 beta L_2 L_3), k the electric flux. For small tau, X'(psi) = (pi/(2 tau)) tanh(pi psi/(2 tau)) and psi has variance tau under w, so
  sum over both slices of E_w|Delta G_b|^2 = 2 (L_1/(L_2 L_3)) E_w[X'(psi)^2] = 2 pi^2 beta^2 L_2 L_3/(t^2 L_1) (1 + O(tau^(1/2))).
Per direction-1 link, |Delta G_b| is about pi beta/(t L_1). Reading: the twist is an unabsorbed half winding of the photon zero mode, spread over the cross-section and over the time t; each link feels it with strength about 1/(t L_1).

**7.3 SU(2) confining (Step 4).** The twist is absorbed (thick vortices condense, r -> 1): the sum is about 8 p Dbar with Dbar about 3 L_1 at strong coupling, exponentially small in a sigma L_1.

**7.4 What the tests say about (FR) (ARGUED).** (FR) at the needed size is a confinement statement: it holds when the twist is absorbed by the vacuum (SU(2) confining), and it fails when the twist survives as a delocalized object (U(1) zero-mode winding, frozen-group domain wall), whose summed response is of order beta^2 L_2 L_3/(t^2 L_1). ### Step 8 (done). What remains of the Langevin gap at order one, and slab structure

**8.1 Status (READ).** lambda is proved volume-uniform for beta_W < 1/4 (8/27 on tori with periods in 82Z), rows 818/829 (gap-finishline-opus.md:191); at order one it is behind wall W2 (gap-wall-assume-max.md:353); for Villain U(1) at beta >= 2^14 it tends to 0 (Theorem V).

**8.2 Only an odd, two-slice gap is used (PROVED).** Let lambda_odd be the best constant in Var_(w_e)(F) <= lambda_odd^(-1) E_(w_e)|grad F|^2 over smooth F(u,v), gauge invariant in each slice, with F o Theta = -F. By 5.1, p/(1 - p) = Var_(w_e)(R) with R odd. The swap (u,v) -> (v,u) preserves w_e and R (E, O are symmetric kernels), so E_(w_e)|grad R|^2 = 2 E_(w_e)|grad_u R|^2; and (1 - R)^2 < 4 gives E_(w_e)|grad_u R|^2 <= FR_u/(1 - p), FR_u := sum over b in u of E_w|Delta G_b|^2. Hence
  p <= 2 FR_u / lambda_odd.
This is (T6) with the full gauge-invariant gap replaced by the odd gap of the even law, at the price of a factor about 4.

**8.3 Lemma ODD-0 (PROVED).** At beta = 0, lambda_odd >= 3 L_1, with equality for one winding line chi_l(v).
Proof. At beta = 0 the kernel is 1, so w_e is Haar on both slices. Expand a gauge-invariant F in products of spin-j_b matrix elements. Gauge invariance at a vertex needs an even number of half-integer spins there, so the half-integer-spin links form a mod-2 cycle H. J acts by (-1)^(number of links of H in the cut); F odd forces H to cross the cut an odd number of times, so H has odd winding in direction 1 and crosses each of the L_1 parallel cuts: at least L_1 links. Each carries Laplacian eigenvalue 4 j(j + 1) >= 3 on the unit S^3. QED.
Also PROVED at every beta: for one winding line, |grad_b chi_l|^2 = 1 - chi_l^2 on each of its L_1 links (f(U) = <U, M> on the unit S^3), so its Rayleigh quotient is L_1 (1 - E chi_l^2)/E chi_l^2. It is small only when the line holonomy is close to the centre.
Reading (ARGUED). lambda_odd is the confinement-sensitive part of the Langevin gap. It collapses for the rivals by a collective odd mode: in U(1) Coulomb, cos(Phibar_u - Phibar_v), Rayleigh quotient of order L_1/(L_2 L_3); in frozen groups, the sector sign z(u) z(v), tunnelling-small. For the averaged SU(2) line F = (L_2 L_3)^(-1) sum_l chi_l, the Rayleigh quotient is L_1 (1 - q)/(L_2 L_3 cbar), q = E chi^2, cbar = (L_2 L_3)^(-2) sum over pairs (l, l') of E[chi_l chi_l'] (diagonal included): small exactly when winding lines are correlated across the transverse plane (centre order in a spatial direction). In confinement cbar is about q/(L_2 L_3) and the quotient is about L_1 (1 - q)/q, i.e. 3 L_1 at q = 1/4 (strong-coupling size).
