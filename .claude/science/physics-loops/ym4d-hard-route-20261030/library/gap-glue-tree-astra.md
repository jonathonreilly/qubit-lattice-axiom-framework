**Carried variable (definition).** On the spatial three-torus of side \(R\), let \(T_{a,R}\) be the positive Wilson transfer operator on the gauge-invariant space. \[
 Q_a(R)=\operatorname{Tr}\!\left(P_+\widehat T_{a,R}^{\,bR/a}\right)-1
       =\sum_{j\in +,\ j\ne0}e^{-bR E_j(a,R)}. \tag{V}

The even sector includes all centre-blind excitations, not just one plaquette observable. Existence, trace class, the top eigenvector, and the compatibility of \(P_+\) are leaf T.
