### Step 4. Lemma LR (local equivariant rules) and (I-loc) for Q and Q' (01:55Z)

**Lemma LR.** Let X, Y be countable sets with free actions of Z^4 (translations tau_v), H = hZ^4 a subgroup. Let u_0 in R^X (or C^X) be H-invariant, O = {u : sup_x |u(x) - u_0(x)| < r}, and F: O -> C^Y with
- (E) F(tau_v u) = tau_v F(u) for v in H;
- (L) for each y in Y a finite patch P(y) of X, P(tau_v y) = tau_v P(y), such that F(u)(y) depends only on u restricted to P(y).
For N in hZ define X_N = X/NZ^4, Y_N = Y/NZ^4, pi_N the quotients, pi_N^* f = f o pi_N. Suppose N is so large that each patch P(y) u {y} maps injectively into the torus (period larger than the patch diameter), and let F_N be the torus rule that, at ybar, lifts the patch, evaluates F's formula on the lifted values and projects back. Then:
- (a) pi_N^* F_N(ubar) = F(pi_N^* ubar) for ubar in O_N.
- (b) For N | N' (both admissible), with p = pi_{N'N}: X_{N'} -> X_N: F_{N'}(p^* ubar) = p^* F_N(ubar).
- (c) If F is differentiable at u_0 (each F(.)(y) is a function of the finitely many variables of P(y)), then DF_{N'}(p^* ubar_0) p^* = p^* DF_N(ubar_0), hence by Lemma P (i) of row 987, DF_N(ubar_0) = Per(DF_{N'}(p^* ubar_0)); the kernel K = DF(u_0) on Z^4 has finite range, commutes with H, and DF_N(ubar_0) = sum_{g in NZ^4} K(x, y+g), a finite sum.
- (d) If G: O' -> C^Z is another such rule with group H' and F(O) in O', then G o F is such a rule with group H n H' and patches P_G(z) unioned over P_F; for N admissible for both, (G o F)_N = G_N o F_N.

*Proof.* (a) Fix y over ybar. By injectivity of the patch embedding, the lifted patch values of ubar are (pi_N^* ubar) restricted to P(y); by (L) the torus rule returns F(pi_N^* ubar)(y). Independence of the lift: another lift is tau_v y with v in NZ^4, a subgroup of H since h | N; by (E) and the NZ^4-invariance of pi_N^* ubar, F(pi^* ubar)(tau_v y) = F(pi^* ubar)(y). (b) pi_N = p o pi_{N'}, so pi_N^* = pi_{N'}^* p^*. By (a) twice, pi_{N'}^* F_{N'}(p^* ubar) = F(pi_N^* ubar) = pi_N^* F_N(ubar) = pi_{N'}^* p^* F_N(ubar); pi_{N'}^* is injective. (Note p^* preserves sup norms, so p^* O_N is in O_{N'}.) (c) Differentiate (b) at ubar_0 along a direction f; p^* is linear and fixes the base point (p^* ubar_0 = ubar_0' by H-invariance of u_0). Uniqueness in Lemma P (i). Finite range of K is (L); commutation with H is (E) differentiated at the H-invariant u_0. The formula for DF_N is (a) differentiated. (d) Composition of (E) and (L); the torus identity from (a): pi^* G_N(F_N ubar) = G(pi^* F_N ubar) = G(F(pi^* ubar)) = pi^*(G o F)_N(ubar), with injectivity of pi^*. QED

This is finite and elementary (functions of finitely many variables, the chain rule, injectivity of pull-back); it is Lean-formalizable as written. PROVED.
