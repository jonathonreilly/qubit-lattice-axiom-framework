**Proved (geometry).** Use coordinates $(t,x,y,z)$. The two central boxes are $A=\{0,1\}\times S$ and $B=\{2,3\}\times S$, where $S=\{0,1\}^3$. For two distinct faces let $\rho_{ij}$ be the supremum of the absolute correlation over centred unit-variance real functions of their respective full data in the joined law. ## A lower bound in the joined Wilson law

**Proved.** Reflect in the join plane $t=3/2$. This exchanges the two central boxes, their lateral arms in pairs, and the two end caps. The slices at $t=1,2$ both have spatial vertex set

$$
D=S\ \cup\!\bigcup_{j\in\{\pm x,\pm y,\pm z\}}(S+2d_j).
$$

Keep all its spatial nearest-neighbour bonds $E(D)$. The temporal links in the reflection cut form a matching, one per site of $D$. Every straddling plaquette is the square over one bond of $E(D)$. In particular the four bonds $(s,s')$ above go from $S$ to an arm. After each half has been integrated up to its spatial face array, its weight is the same positive bounded function $H$. It is invariant under vertex gauges on that array by changing gauge on the half. At fixed matching links, change the upper frames to make all those links identities. Haar invariance removes the frame changes from the half integrals. Each crossing plaquette now pairs a spatial bond with its reflected copy.

Write $a(h)=\int e^{h q(g)}dg$ and let $C_h$ denote the one-bond convolution with $e^{h q}/a(h)$. Vary the coupling of the selected square over $e=(s,s')$ to $h$, working with $e^{h q}$ and keeping the original Wilson constants outside this variation. Up to fixed positive factors its mass divided by $a(h)$ is

$$
\left\langle H,\left(C_h^{(e)}\otimes\!\bigotimes_{b\in E(D)\setminus\{e\}}
C_\beta^{(b)}\right)H\right\rangle .
$$

Use the reflection estimate of 1348 (17) and 1485 at this cut. On spin $k/2$, the electric multiplier is $r_k(h)=I_{k+1}(h)/I_1(h)$, with $r_0=1$. Each $r_k$ is nonnegative and nondecreasing for $h>0$. In the proof in 1485, $I_{n+1}/I_n$ is the mean of $u$ under the exponential tilt of $(1-u^2)^{n-1/2}du$ on $[-1,1]$, with derivative equal to its variance. Taking the product over $1\le n\le k$ gives the assertion. Consequently the displayed quadratic form is nondecreasing in $h$. The finite-volume mass is differentiable by boundedness of $q$. Its logarithmic derivative at $h=\beta$ proves

$$
\mathbb E q(p_j)\ \ge\ a'(\beta)/a(\beta)=r_1(\beta)>\tfrac7{15}.
$$

The last strict inequality is 1485's rational bound from GL4, $r_1(\beta)>\beta/(4+\beta^2/6)=1380/2929>7/15$ at $\beta=23/10$. The final cross products are $20700>20503$. This application treats the corner plaquette as one marked summand in the complete action. [1348, (17)](gap-blockstep3-astra62.md) · [1485, bound following (10)](gap-k2-wilson-interface-astra62.md)

**Proved (strict violation).** The two negative exits each have covariance at least $(3/5)r_1(\beta)$ with $F$ and the two positive exits at least $(4/5)r_1(\beta)$. The four normalized covariances therefore satisfy

$$
\sum_{j\in\{-y,-z,+y,+z\}}\mathbb E[F G_j]
\ \ge\ \frac{14}{5}r_1(\beta)
\ >\ \frac{14}{5}\frac7{15}
\ =\ \frac{98}{75}>1 .
$$

For the pairwise suprema one may read $X_{s_k}$ alone on each pair. Each of the four perpendicular lateral exits then has $\rho_{ij}\ge r_1(\beta)$. Permuting or reversing the transverse axes gives the same bound from every lateral face to its four perpendicular lateral neighbours. Even positive face weights cannot make all the weighted row sums less than one. Choose a lateral face with smallest weight $h_i>0$. Its sum $\sum_{j\ne i}(h_j/h_i)\rho_{ij}$ is at least $4r_1(\beta)>28/15$. This last statement concerns weights constant per face.

## Each of the sixteen Wilson masses

**Proved (exact join).** Write $\zeta\in(\mathbb Z/2)^3$ for a temporal twist and $\tau\in\{v,2v\}$. Measure $m,\tau$ in fine links of the Wilson step under test. Put the stacks on the last time bond and last spatial bond of each selected direction. With aligned side-two boxes, every negatively signed fine plaquette crosses two box cuts, and each group of four fine corner plaquettes has one sign $\epsilon_P^\zeta$. Row 1348 (1) with row 1475 §5 gives, separately at each label,

$$
Z^\zeta_\tau=c_2^{V_\tau}
\int \exp\!\left(\sum_E v_E^{\mathsf T}Q_Ev_E+
       \sum_P\epsilon_P^\zeta T_P[v_{E_1},v_{E_2},v_{E_3},v_{E_4}]\right)
       \prod_E dv_E\,d\nu_\tau,\qquad c_2=e^{-96\beta}z_2 .
$$

Here $z_2$ is the unshifted open-box mass, $V_\tau$ counts boxes, and $\nu_\tau$ is the product of open-box laws and the Haar laws of the relative crossing links. Joining regroups this integral by Fubini with the common links integrated once. An insertion of $F G_j$ uses the same variables in the same integral. Place a translate of the tested join away from the time twist seam. Then all its marked plaquettes have sign $+1$.

**Proved (disjoint-path test in every twist).** On $m^3\times\tau$, fix one spatial axis $d=y$ or $z$. Vary the coupling of **every** temporal-$d$ plaquette to $h\in[2,\beta]$ and keep all other couplings at $\beta=23/10$. For this argument use the unshifted log mass

$$
L_\zeta(h)=\log\int\exp\!\left(\sum_p h_p\eta_p^\zeta q(U_p)\right)dU ,
$$

where $\eta_p^\zeta$ is the fine plaquette sign. The shift back to the physical convention is the same scalar function of $h$ for every twist. There are $N=m^3\tau$ plaquettes of the selected orientation. Its signed mean is the same at each translate. Indeed a translation carries the sign stacks into homologous stacks. Multiplying links by a centre sign moves them back, preserving Haar and the signed plaquette insertions. Explicitly, moving a stack one time layer flips the spatial $d'$-links on its cut at the intermediate time slice. Moving its spatial cut one site flips the time links on that intermediate spatial slice at the stack time. Other plaquettes keep their signs, by cancellation in pairs. Compose these changes for the selected twist directions $d'$. The orientation-dependent couplings respect these translations. Thus $L_\zeta'(\beta)/N=\mathbb E_\zeta q(p_j)$ for a marked plaquette of this orientation away from the time seam. For the untwisted torus, normalized electric convolution gives

$$
e^{L_0(h)}=K\,a(h)^N\operatorname{Tr}(T_h^\tau),\qquad
T_h=M\left(\bigotimes_{b} C_{\lambda_b}^{(b)}\right)M .
$$

Here the trace is on the gauge-invariant spatial slice space, $M$ multiplies by the square root of its unshifted spatial Wilson weight, and $\lambda_b=h$ on $d$-bonds and $\beta$ elsewhere. To obtain this formula, integrate the temporal links of each layer as vertex gauges between its two spatial arrays. This inserts the Haar projection onto the invariant space. The projection commutes with $M$ and the bond convolutions. Split each spatial weight between its adjacent layers and take the periodic trace. The removed scalar per $d$-bond is $a(h)$, with all other removed scalars in $K>0$, independent of $h$. The finite product convolution is positive trace class by GL3 and its factorial tail. The bounded multiplication on either side preserves the trace-class property. Each product multiplier is nondecreasing in $h$, so $T_\beta\succeq T_2$. The min-max principle orders their individual eigenvalues and hence the traces of their positive integer powers. Also $(\log a)''$ is the variance of $q$ under the one-bond tilt. It follows that

$$
\frac{L_0(\beta)-L_0(2)}{N(\beta-2)}
 \ \ge\ \frac{\log a(\beta)-\log a(2)}{\beta-2}
 \ \ge\ r_1(2)>\frac{2}{4+4/6}=\frac37 .
$$

For each twist there are at most $3m^2$ signed plaquettes. Changing their signs changes the exponent pointwise by at most $6\beta m^2$. Thus $|L_\zeta(h)-L_0(h)|\le6\beta m^2$ at **each** endpoint. Convexity of $L_\zeta$ follows from the nonnegative variance of the sum of the signed temporal-$d$ traces. Its left secant now gives

$$
\mathbb E_\zeta q(p_j)
\ \ge\ \frac{L_\zeta(\beta)-L_\zeta(2)}{N(\beta-2)}
\ >\ \frac37-\frac{12\beta}{(\beta-2)m\tau}
\ =\ \frac37-\frac{92}{m\tau}.
$$

At $m=512v$, for aligned even $v\ge8$, $m\tau\ge8192>35\cdot92=3220$. The last bound exceeds $3/7-1/35=2/5$. Local gauge invariance holds for each signed law. The same fixed $F$ and the same four $G_j$ therefore give

$$
\sum_j\mathbb E_{\zeta,\tau}[F G_j]>\frac{14}{5}\frac25=\frac{28}{25}
\qquad\text{at every one of the sixteen labels.}
$$

Only the untwisted transfer was ordered in this argument. The sectorwise comparison used positive integrals and a bound on the number of signed plaquettes. In particular no positivity of an individual twist insertion as an operator is needed.
