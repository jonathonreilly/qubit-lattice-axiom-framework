## Theorem SL and its corollaries

With a background $F=\phi k$ and a Gaussian curl fluctuation $Z$ of covariance $g_P I_3$, the part of $\mathbb E|F+Z|^4$ quadratic in $F$ is $2|F|^2\mathbb E|Z|^2+4\mathbb E(F\cdot Z)^2=10g_P|F|^2$. The saddle displacement caused by $f-s$ starts at order $B^3$ and the Hessian difference at order $B^2$.
