### 1.3 Definition

A **fact** is a statement F about the Wilson law. An **input** is a triple (F, Q, C): F; the consumer variable Q that F bounds (the purity P_K, a thermal sum, or the sixteen masses of library/gap-k2-tool-design-fable.md 3.1); and a consumer C on file together with the proved inequality that takes F to the bound C reads. A selective input must read three things.
- **(P-law) the law.** Inside one family (group and dimension fixed, beta varying), F changes its truth value where the family changes phase. A property decided by the Lie algebra, by the representation, or by a count of free modes is constant on the family. It cannot be the input.
- **(P-scale) the physical scale.** F has content at lattice sizes N(beta) that grow with beta. Equivalent form on file: the hypothesis fails when beta grows at a fixed lattice (Remark STAB, row 1386).
- **(P-carry) a step.** F is, or contains, a statement that moves a bound from size N to 2N or from beta to beta + delta, with its loss stated (Remark PAIR, row 1386: "the missing object is the carry, not the gain").
In one sentence: the input must read an upper bound, in law, on a response of boxes of fixed physical size, in the consumer's variables. The colour bracket and the mode count enter only through the size of the gain per step.
