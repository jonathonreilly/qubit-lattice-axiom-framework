### 1.1 The Consumer `(PUR-io)` and the Selectivity Constraint

Fix the release witnesses (an odd block factor $b$ and calibration level $g_0$, as in check 1453) and a side $\ell \in 1024\mathbb N$. At cutoff $K$, put $n = \ell b^K$ and $u = n/512$. $$Z^z(v) = \operatorname{Tr}(C_z T^v), \qquad Z_+(v) = \frac{1}{8}\sum_{z \in \mathbb Z_2^3} Z^z(v) = \operatorname{Tr}(P_0 T^v), \qquad P_K = \frac{Z_+(2u)}{Z_+(u)^2}.$$
The seed `(PUR-io)` asserts $P_K \ge 457/625$ at infinitely many $K$ for one $\ell$ and one $g_0$ (`library/gap-k2-tool-design-fable.md` §3.1; checked in check 1465; Lean formalization of seed-to-gap in `lean/gapchain`). By Proposition NEG (`library/gap-mech-direction2-fable.md`, row 1386), every proved statement on file with content at order-one coupling and late cutoffs is lattice-local, an identity, or a negative, and holds in 5D as well. Because 4D compact $\mathrm{U}(1)$ is massless at weak coupling (Guth; Fröhlich–Spencer) and 5D $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ is believed to be deconfined at weak coupling, any proof of `(PUR-io)` must include at least one **selective** step failing for 4D compact $\mathrm{U}(1)$ (Wilson and Villain) and 5D $\mathrm{SU}(2)$.

## 2. Theorem 1: Non-Gibbsianness and Constrained-System $\mathbb Z_2$ Obstruction for Global Local-Rule K-II Classes

Throughout this section we identify $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ with the group of unit quaternions $q = q_0 + q_1\mathbf i + q_2\mathbf j + q_3\mathbf k \in S^3 \subset \mathbb H$, with $\operatorname{Re}(q) = q_0$, $\operatorname{Im}(q) = q_1\mathbf i + q_2\mathbf j + q_3\mathbf k$, $\bar q = q^{-1} = q_0 - \operatorname{Im}(q)$, and Euclidean norm $\|q\| = \sqrt{q_0^2 + q_1^2 + q_2^2 + q_3^2}$ (so $\operatorname{Re}(U) = \frac{1}{2}\operatorname{Tr}(U)$ in $2\times 2$ matrix conventions and $\|U - I_2\|^2 = 2(1 - \operatorname{Re} U)$).

### 2.1 General Gauge-Covariant Contour-Mean Blocking Kernels and Row 1468's `Tube` Class

Let $T_{bM} = (\mathbb Z / bM \mathbb Z)^4$ be a fine 4D torus ($b \ge 2$) and $T_M = (\mathbb Z / M\mathbb Z)^4$ the coarse torus with coarse bond set $E_1$. A **gauge-covariant contour-mean blocking map** $Q_\kappa$ of strength $\kappa = a\beta > 0$ maps a fine density $D(W)$ on $\mathrm{SU}(2)^{E_0}$ to
$$(Q_\kappa D)(V) = \int_{\mathrm{SU}(2)^{E_0}} D(W) \prod_{c \in E_1} \frac{\exp\big(\kappa \operatorname{Re}(M_c(W) V_c^{-1})\big)}{Z_1(\kappa \|M_c(W)\|)}\, dW, \qquad Z_1(r) = \int_{\mathrm{SU}(2)} e^{r \operatorname{Re} g}\, dg,$$
where for each coarse bond $c = (y, \mu)$, $M_c(W) = \frac{1}{|\mathcal P_c|}\sum_{\gamma \in \mathcal P_c} \operatorname{hol}_W(\gamma) \in \mathbb H$ ($\|M_c(W)\| \le 1$) is a uniform average of holonomies along finite lattice paths from the base site $b y$ to $b(y + e_\mu)$ (or of a gauge-covariant finite-range smoothed field $\Phi(W)$ along those paths). This class contains:
- Row 1468's map $Q_2$ ($b = 2$, $384$ paths $e = (\xi, \pi)$, $\xi \in \{0,1\}^4, \pi \in S_4$);
- Centred odd-$b$ contour-mean maps $Q_b$ (plain form of Sussman–Alpoge's `kernelImage`, $\xi \in \{-\frac{b-1}{2},\dots,\frac{b-1}{2}\}^4$);
- Straight-product decimation $D_\kappa$ ($|\mathcal P_c| = 1$, single straight path of length $b$, $\|M_c(W)\| \equiv 1$);
- Finite-step stout/gradient-flow-preconditioned maps $Q_{2,m,\delta}^{\mathrm{GF}}$ (Section 4).

Crucially, the normalization factor $Z_1(\kappa \|M_c(W)\|)^{-1}$ is **independent of $V$**. For the Wilson density $D_0(W) = e^{-\beta S_W(W)}$ ($S_W(W) = \sum_p (1 - \operatorname{Re} W_p)$), the **constrained fine measure** at a coarse field $V \in \mathrm{SU}(2)^{E_1}$ is the probability measure on $\mathrm{SU}(2)^{E_0}$:
$$d\mu^V(W) := \frac{1}{D_1(V)} \exp\Big(-\beta S_W(W) + \kappa \sum_{c \in E_1} \operatorname{Re}\big(M_c(W) V_c^{-1}\big)\Big) \prod_{c \in E_1} Z_1(\kappa \|M_c(W)\|)^{-1}\, dW,$$
where $D_1(V) := (Q_\kappa e^{-\beta S_W})(V)$.

Recall from row 1468 §4.2 (`library/gap-k2-tool-design-fable.md`) and row 1471 §1, §3 (`library/gap-k2-end-class-fable.md`):
- A local rule $\Phi = \{\Phi_X\}$ assigns to each connected coarse bond set $X \subset E_1$ a bounded gauge-invariant function $\Phi_X(V)$ depending only on $V|_X$, with weighted norm ($\theta \in (0, 1]$, $r > 0$, $x > 0$):
  $$\|\Phi\|_{x,r} := \sup_{c \in E_1} \sum_{X \ni c} e^{r |X|} \sup_{V} \frac{|\Phi_X(V)|}{1 + \theta x E_X(V)}, \qquad E_X(V) := \sum_{p \subset X} (1 - \operatorname{Re} V_p).$$
- A density $D_1$ on $T_M$ **realizes $\Phi$ with site constant $e$ and wrap error $\eta_M$** if for all $V \in \mathrm{SU}(2)^{E_1}$,
  $$\Big|\log D_1(V) + e |T_M| + \sum_{\substack{X \subset E_1 \\ \operatorname{diam}(X) < M/2}} \Phi_X(V)\Big| \le \eta_M.$$
- $D_1 \in \mathrm{Tube}(\Phi^*, \rho; \eta_M)$ if $D_1$ realizes some $\Phi$ with $\|\Phi - \Phi^*\|_{x,r} \le \rho$ and wrap error $\eta_M$.
- **Lemma W of row 1471** (checked in check 1473) proves that for any $0 < q < r$,
  $$\sup_{c \in E_1} \sum_{X \ni c} e^{q |X|} \|\Phi_X\|_\infty \le M(\theta x, r - q)\,\|\Phi\|_{x,r}, \qquad M(\theta x, r - q) \le 1 + \frac{3\theta x}{e(r - q)}. \tag{2.1}$$

### 2.2 Theorem 1A: Constrained Long-Range Order Refutes `Tube(Phi*, rho; eta)` `[PROVED BY HAND]`

**Theorem 1A `[PROVED BY HAND]`.** *Fix $\beta > 0$, $\kappa > 0$, and a gauge-covariant contour-mean blocking map $Q_\kappa$. Suppose there exist a periodic coarse field $V^*$ (of cell period $p_0 \mid M$), a coarse bond $c_0 = (y_0, \mu_0)$ with translation-symmetry orbit $\mathcal O \subseteq E_1$ of size $|\mathcal O| = |T_M|/p_0^4$ (under the symmetries of $\mu^{V^*}$), and a unit imaginary quaternion $\mathbf e_* \in S^2 \subset \operatorname{Im}\mathbb H$ such that the single-bond observable*
$$S_c(W) := \operatorname{Im}\big(M_c(W)(V_c^*)^{-1}\big) \cdot \mathbf e_* \in [-1, 1]$$
*satisfies on $T_M$ for arbitrarily large $M \in p_0\mathbb N$:*
1. *(Exact $\mathbb Z_2$ oddness and orbit homogeneity) There is a measure-preserving involution $\mathcal I$ of $\mu^{V^*}$ permuting the orbit $\mathcal O$ ($c \mapsto \tau c$) such that $S_c(\mathcal I W) = -S_{\tau c}(W)$ and $A_c(\mathcal I W) = A_{\tau c}(W)$ (where $A_c(W) := \operatorname{Re}(M_c(W)(V_c^*)^{-1})$), and the joint law under $\mu^{V^*}$ is invariant under a transitive group of translations on $\mathcal O$ (so in particular $m_{\mathcal O} := \frac{1}{|\mathcal O|}\sum_{c \in \mathcal O} S_c$ satisfies $m_{\mathcal O} \stackrel{d}{=} -m_{\mathcal O}$);*
2. *(Uniform LRO) $\mathbb E_{\mu^{V^*}}[m_{\mathcal O}^2] \ge c_* > 0$ uniformly along an unbounded sequence of torus sides $M$.*

*Then:*
- *(i) **(Essential discontinuity / van Enter–Fernández–Sokal jump.)** Put $m_0 := \frac{3}{4}\sqrt{c_*/2}$, $\varepsilon := \frac{1}{16} m_0$, and $\delta := \min(\frac{\varepsilon}{\kappa}, \frac{m_0}{8})$. For every radius $R \ge 1$ and every $M$ in the sequence with $M > 2R$ and $|\mathcal O| \ge \frac{16 \log(4/c_*)}{\kappa \sin h \sqrt{c_*/2}}$ (where $B_R := |\mathcal O \cap \Lambda_R(c_0)|$ and $h := \min(\frac{\varepsilon}{2\kappa B_R}, \frac{7}{32}\sqrt{c_*/2}, \frac{1}{2})$), there exist two coarse fields $V^{(+)}, V^{(-)}$ on $T_M$ agreeing identically on the ball $\Lambda_R(c_0)$ ($V^{(+)}|_{\Lambda_R} = V^{(-)}|_{\Lambda_R} = V^*|_{\Lambda_R}$), together with single-bond perturbations $V^{(\pm)}_\delta$ changing only bond $c_0$ to $V_{c_0}^* e^{\delta \mathbf e_*}$, such that the single-bond cross-difference*
  $$\Delta_{c_0}(V^{(+)}, V^{(-)}) := \big(\log D_1(V^{(+)}_\delta) - \log D_1(V^{(+)})\big) - \big(\log D_1(V^{(-)}_\delta) - \log D_1(V^{(-)})\big)$$
  *satisfies the $R$- and $M$-independent lower bound*
  $$\Delta_{c_0}(V^{(+)}, V^{(-)}) \ge c_1(c_*, \kappa) := \frac{3}{64}\min(1, 2\kappa)\, m_0^2 = \frac{27}{2048}\min(1, 2\kappa)\, c_* > 0. \tag{2.2}$$
- *(ii) **(Refutation of row 1468's weighted `Tube` class.)** For any fixed $(\Phi^*, \rho, x, r, \theta)$ with $\|\Phi^*\|_{x,r} < \infty$, $\rho < \infty$, $r > 0$, $\theta \in (0, 1]$, and any wrap error sequence $\eta_M \to 0$ as $M \to \infty$, $D_1 = Q_\kappa(e^{-\beta S_W})$ does **not** belong to $\mathrm{Tube}(\Phi^*, \rho; \eta_M)$ for large $M$.*

*Proof.*
**Step 1 (Perturbed coarse fields).** For any $h > 0$, define $V_{\mathrm{all}}^{(+h)}$ by replacing $V_c^*$ with $V_c^* e^{h \mathbf e_*}$ on every bond $c \in \mathcal O$ (leaving $V_c^*$ unchanged for $c \notin \mathcal O$). For any $W$ and any bond $c \in \mathcal O$, using $e^{-h\mathbf e_*} = \cos h - \sin h\,\mathbf e_*$,
$$\operatorname{Re}\big(M_c(W)(V_c^* e^{h\mathbf e_*})^{-1}\big) = \cos h\,\operatorname{Re}(D_c(W)) + \sin h\, S_c(W), \qquad D_c(W) := M_c(W)(V_c^*)^{-1}.$$
Note that for $0 < h \le 1$, $\cos h - 1 = -2\sin^2(h/2) \in [-h^2/2, 0]$, so perturbing $V_c^*$ by $e^{h\mathbf e_*}$ changes the exponent at bond $c$ by $\kappa \sin h\, S_c(W) + \kappa(\cos h - 1)\operatorname{Re}(D_c(W))$. To eliminate the $\cos h - 1$ tilt cleanly, work directly with the exact log-Laplace functional of $V^{(+h)}_{\mathrm{all}}$ vs $V^{(-h)}_{\mathrm{all}}$:
Let $A_c(W) := \operatorname{Re}(D_c(W)) \in [-1, 1]$. For $h > 0$ with $h \le \frac{1}{4}\sqrt{c_*/2}$:
$$\psi_M(h) := \frac{1}{|\mathcal O|} \log \frac{D_1(V^{(+h)}_{\mathrm{all}})}{D_1(V^*)} = \frac{1}{|\mathcal O|}\log \mathbb E_{\mu^{V^*}}\!\Big[\exp\Big(\kappa |\mathcal O|\big(\sin h\, m_{\mathcal O} - (1 - \cos h)\bar A_{\mathcal O}\big)\Big)\Big],$$
where $\bar A_{\mathcal O} := \frac{1}{|\mathcal O|}\sum_{c \in \mathcal O} A_c(W) \in [-1, 1]$.
Since $0 \le m_{\mathcal O}^2 \le 1$ and $\mathbb E_{\mu^{V^*}}[m_{\mathcal O}^2] \ge c_*$, reverse Markov gives
$$c_* \le \frac{c_*}{2} + \mathbb P_{\mu^{V^*}}(m_{\mathcal O}^2 \ge c_*/2) \implies \mathbb P_{\mu^{V^*}}(|m_{\mathcal O}| \ge \sqrt{c_*/2}) \ge \frac{c_*}{2}.$$
By symmetry ($m_{\mathcal O} \stackrel{d}{=} -m_{\mathcal O}$), $\mathbb P_{\mu^{V^*}}(m_{\mathcal O} \ge \sqrt{c_*/2}) \ge c_*/4$. On this event, using $1 - \cos h \le h^2/2$, $|\bar A_{\mathcal O}| \le 1$, and $\sin h \ge \frac{7}{8} h$ for $h \le 1/2$:
$$\sin h\, m_{\mathcal O} - (1 - \cos h)\bar A_{\mathcal O} \ge \sin h\sqrt{c_*/2} - \frac{h^2}{2} \ge \sin h\Big(\sqrt{c_*/2} - \frac{4}{7} h\Big).$$
Restrict $h \le \frac{7}{32}\sqrt{c_*/2}$, so $\frac{4}{7}h \le \frac{1}{8}\sqrt{c_*/2}$. Then
$$\psi_M(h) \ge \frac{7}{8} \kappa \sin h \sqrt{c_*/2} - \frac{\log(4/c_*)}{|\mathcal O|}. \tag{2.3}$$
Now define $\phi_M(s) := \frac{1}{|\mathcal O|}\log \mathbb E_{\mu^{V^*}}[\exp(\kappa s |\mathcal O| m_{\mathcal O} - \kappa (1 - \cos h)|\mathcal O| \bar A_{\mathcal O})]$ as a function of $s \in \mathbb R$ at fixed $h$. Since $\phi_M(s)$ is convex in $s$, $\phi_M(0) \le \kappa (1 - \cos h) \le \frac{1}{2}\kappa h^2 \le \frac{1}{16}\kappa \sin h\sqrt{c_*/2}$, and $\phi_M(\sin h) = \psi_M(h)$, convexity gives
$$\kappa \mathbb E_{\mu^{V^{(+h)}_{\mathrm{all}}}}[m_{\mathcal O}] = \phi_M'(\sin h) \ge \frac{\phi_M(\sin h) - \phi_M(0)}{\sin h} \ge \frac{13}{16}\kappa \sqrt{c_*/2} - \frac{\log(4/c_*)}{|\mathcal O|\sin h}.$$
By exact translation invariance on the orbit $\mathcal O$, $\mathbb E_{\mu^{V^{(+h)}_{\mathrm{all}}}}[S_{c_0}] = \mathbb E_{\mu^{V^{(+h)}_{\mathrm{all}}}}[m_{\mathcal O}]$. Hence for $|\mathcal O|$ large enough that $\frac{\log(4/c_*)}{\kappa |\mathcal O| \sin h} \le \frac{1}{16}\sqrt{c_*/2}$,
$$\mathbb E_{\mu^{V^{(+h)}_{\mathrm{all}}}}[S_{c_0}] \ge \frac{3}{4}\sqrt{c_*/2} = m_0. \tag{2.4}$$

**Step 2 (Removing the field inside $\Lambda_R(c_0)$ by Radon–Nikodym).** Fix $R \ge 1$ and put $B_R = |\mathcal O \cap \Lambda_R(c_0)|$. With

## 3. Theorem 2: Uniform-in-Exterior Large-Field Percolation Obstruction for Bare-Plaquette Two-Region Expansions at $\beta \in [2, 3]$ `[PROVED BY HAND]`

Theorem 1 shows that global-in-$V$ local-rule classes (uniform constrained mixing and, conditionally on Proposition 1C, row 1468's `Tube(Phi*, rho; eta)`) cannot contain $Q_\kappa(e^{-\beta S_W})$. The classical alternative in constructive gauge theory (Balaban; Sussman–Alpoge) is a **two-region (small-field / large-field)** expansion, splitting the lattice into disjoint $b^4$ blocks $B_y = by + \{0, \dots, b-1\}^4$ and declaring a block **hot** ($H_y = 1$) if it contains a bare plaquette exceeding a cut $\epsilon$:
$$H_y(W) := \mathbf 1\Big\{\max_{p \subset B_y} \big(1 - \operatorname{Re} W_p\big) > \epsilon\Big\},$$
where $p \subset B_y$ means all four vertices of $p$ lie in $B_y$. We now prove that at order-one coupling $\beta \in [2, 3]$, bare-plaquette hot blocks **provably percolate** (and exceed Kotecký–Preiss convergence thresholds by orders of magnitude) **uniformly over all exterior configurations**.

### 3.1 Lemma 2.1: Exact BFS Non-Tree Plaquette Ordering Inside Disjoint $b^4$ Blocks `[PROVED BY HAND]`

$$N(b) := 4(b-1)b^3 - (b^4 - 1) = 3b^4 - 4b^3 + 1 \tag{3.1}$$

$$e_k \in p_k \qquad \text{and} \qquad e_k \notin p_j \quad \text{for all } 1 \le j < k \le N(b). \tag{3.2}$$

1. $(v - e_\nu, v)$: since $\nu = \nu_{\max}(v)$, this link belongs to $\mathcal T$;
2. $(v - e_\nu + e_\mu, v + e_\mu)$: since $\nu > \mu$ and $(v+e_\mu)_\nu = v_\nu \ge 1$, we have $\nu_{\max}(v+e_\mu) = \nu$, so this link also belongs to $\mathcal T$;
3. $(v - e_\nu, v - e_\nu + e_\mu)$: its base vertex $v - e_\nu$ has strictly smaller $L_1$-norm $|v - e_\nu|_1 = |v|_1 - 1 < |v|_1$;
4. $e = (v, v + e_\mu)$ itself.

For every $j < k$, every non-tree link of $p_j$ is either $e_j$ (distinct from $e_k$) or has base vertex of $L_1$-norm strictly less than $|v(e_j)|_1 \le |v(e_k)|_1$ (hence cannot equal $e_k$). ### 3.2 Lemma 2.2: Exact Uniform-in-Exterior Single-Link Hot-Plaquette Lower Bound `[PROVED BY HAND]`

**Lemma 2.2 `[PROVED BY HAND]`.** *In 4D $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Wilson lattice gauge theory at coupling $\beta > 0$, let $e \in E_0$ be any fine link and $p$ any plaquette containing $e$. Let $\mathcal F_{-e}$ denote the $\sigma$-algebra generated by all links other than $e$. Put $r := 6\beta$. Then for every $\epsilon \in (0, 1)$, uniformly over all exterior configurations $W_{-e}$:*
$$\mathbb P_\beta\big(1 - \operatorname{Re} W_p > \epsilon \mid \mathcal F_{-e}\big) \ge q(\beta, \epsilon) := \frac{\int_\epsilon^2 e^{-r t}\sqrt{t(2 - t)}\, dt}{\int_0^2 e^{-r t}\sqrt{t(2 - t)}\, dt}. \tag{3.3}$$
*Moreover, writing $f(\epsilon) := \sqrt{\epsilon(2-\epsilon)}$, $s(\epsilon) := \frac{1 - f(\epsilon)}{1 - \epsilon}$, and $d := 1 - \epsilon$, we have the elementary closed-form lower bound*
$$q(\beta, \epsilon) \ge q_{\mathrm{lb}}(\beta, \epsilon) := \sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\, \frac{r^{1/2} e^{-r\epsilon}}{1 - \frac{3}{8r}}\Big[f(\epsilon)\big(1 - e^{-r d}\big) + \frac{s(\epsilon)}{r}\big(1 - (1 + r d) e^{-r d}\big)\Big]. \tag{3.4}$$

*Proof.* Conditioned on $W_{-e}$, the six plaquettes incident on $e$ have staples $S_1, \dots, S_6 \in \mathrm{SU}(2)$ (with $S_1$ the staple of $p$, so $W_p = W_e S_1^{-1}$). Writing $V := W_p = W_e S_1^{-1} \in \mathrm{SU}(2)$ (which preserves Haar measure), the conditional density of $V$ is proportional to $\exp(\beta \operatorname{Re}(V S_1 S^\dagger)) = \exp(\tilde r \operatorname{Re}(V V_0^{-1}))$, where $S := \sum_{j=1}^6 S_j$, $\tilde r := \beta \|S\| \in [0, 6\beta]$, and $V_0 := S S_1^{-1} / \|S\| \in \mathrm{SU}(2)$ (arbitrary if $S = 0$).

We show $\mathbb P_{\tilde r, V_0}(1 - \operatorname{Re} V \le \epsilon) \le \mathbb P_{r, I_2}(1 - \operatorname{Re} V \le \epsilon)$ in two elementary steps:
1. *Alignment to $V_0 = I_2$*: For fixed $\tilde r \ge 0$, let $H$ be the hyperplane in $\mathbb R^4$ bisecting $I_2$ and $V_0$, and $\rho_H$ reflection across $H$ (an isometry of $S^3$ swapping $I_2 \leftrightarrow V_0$). Put $C := \{V \in S^3 : 1 - \operatorname{Re} V \le \epsilon\}$. On $C \cap \rho_H(C)$ the densities of $V$ and $\rho_H(V)$ pair identically; for every $V \in C \setminus \rho_H(C)$, $V$ is strictly closer to $I_2$ than to $V_0$, whereas $\rho_H(V) \in \rho_H(C) \setminus C$ is strictly closer to $V_0$ than to $I_2$, so $\operatorname{Re}(V V_0^{-1}) < \operatorname{Re}(V)$ while $\operatorname{Re}(\rho_H(V) V_0^{-1}) = \operatorname{Re}(V)$. Hence $\int_C e^{\tilde r \operatorname{Re}(V V_0^{-1})} dV \le \int_C e^{\tilde r \operatorname{Re}(V)} dV$.
2. *Monotonicity in $\tilde r \le r = 6\beta$*: At $V_0 = I_2$, $\frac{d}{d\tilde r}\mathbb P_{\tilde r, I_2}(\operatorname{Re} V \ge 1 - \epsilon) = \operatorname{Cov}_{\tilde r, I_2}(\mathbf 1_{\{\operatorname{Re} V \ge 1-\epsilon\}}, \operatorname{Re} V) \ge 0$ by Chebyshev's covariance inequality for nondecreasing functions on $[-1, 1]$.

Under Haar measure on $\mathrm{SU}(2) \cong S^3$, $\operatorname{Re} V = \cos\theta$ has density $\frac{2}{\pi}\sin^2\theta\, d\theta$ on $[0, \pi]$. Substituting $t = 1 - \cos\theta \in [0, 2]$ ($dt = \sin\theta\, d\theta$) gives (3.3).

For (3.4):
- *Denominator upper bound*: For all $t \in [0, 2]$, $\sqrt{2 - t} \le \sqrt{2}(1 - \frac{t}{4})$ (squaring gives $2 - t \le 2 - t + \frac{t^2}{8}$). Hence
  $$\int_0^2 e^{-r t}\sqrt{t(2 - t)}\, dt \le \sqrt{2}\int_0^\infty e^{-r t}\Big(t^{1/2} - \frac{t^{3/2}}{4}\Big)\, dt = \sqrt{2}\Big(\frac{\sqrt\pi}{2 r^{3/2}} - \frac{3\sqrt\pi}{16 r^{5/2}}\Big) = \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2}}\, r^{-3/2}\Big(1 - \frac{3}{8r}\Big).$$
- *Numerator lower bound*: On $[0, 1]$, $f(t) := \sqrt{t(2-t)}$ is strictly concave ($f''(t) = -(t(2-t))^{-3/2} < 0$), so on $[\epsilon, 1]$ it lies above its chord $f(\epsilon) + s(\epsilon)(t - \epsilon)$ joining $(\epsilon, f(\epsilon))$ to $(1, 1)$. Integrating explicitly gives
  $$\int_\epsilon^2 e^{-rt}\sqrt{t(2-t)}\, dt \ge \int_\epsilon^1 e^{-rt}\big(f(\epsilon) + s(\epsilon)(t-\epsilon)\big)\, dt = \frac{e^{-r\epsilon}}{r}\Big[f(\epsilon)(1 - e^{-rd}) + \frac{s(\epsilon)}{r}\big(1 - (1+rd)e^{-rd}\big)\Big].$$
Dividing the numerator lower bound by the denominator upper bound gives (3.4). $\square$
