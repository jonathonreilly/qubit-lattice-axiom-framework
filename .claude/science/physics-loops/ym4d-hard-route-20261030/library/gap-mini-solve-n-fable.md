## 3. Lemma FKG-NO: the SU(2) plaquette-trace field is not associated at small beta (PROVED given NS0; a corollary of EP-NO)

**Statement.** Let the 4D periodic torus contain S_0 (sides as in EP-NO, for example all sides at least 5). Order [-1, 1]^(plaquettes) coordinatewise and call a function increasing if it is non-decreasing in every x_p. Put F' := prod over p in M of (1 + x_p)/2 and G' := prod over p in A + C of (1 + x_p)/2, with S_0 = M + (A + C) the partition of EP-NO. Both are increasing, with values in [0, 1]. Then:
(a) under Haar measure (beta = 0), Cov_0(F', G') = -2^(-183) 2^(-92) = -2^(-275) < 0;
(b) for every choice of couplings 0 <= beta_p <= 2^(-291), Cov(F', G') < 0, under the Wilson measure with these couplings and in the centre-even state.
So the law of (x_p) is not associated (the FKG inequality fails for a pair of increasing functions) in this range. For U(1), the same pair satisfies Cov >= 0 at every beta >= 0 (see the remark below).

**Proof.** (a) Expand: F' = 2^(-|M|) sum over S subset of M of prod over p in S of x_p, and G' likewise over T subset of A + C. M and A + C are disjoint, so FG-type products have no repeated factor. Claim: E_0[prod over p in S of x_p] = 0 for every S with S non-empty and S a proper subset of S_0. Indeed, every link of S_0 lies in exactly two plaquettes of S_0, and S_0 is connected through shared links (EP-NO inputs, K1 of its GUIDE). If S is non-empty and proper, some link l of S lies in exactly one plaquette of S. Otherwise every link of S would lie in two plaquettes of S, so S would be closed under link adjacency inside S_0 and equal S_0. That plaquette contains l once, so the product is affine in the entries of U_l, with the other links independent of U_l under Haar measure. Since int U dU = 0, the mean is 0. Hence E_0[F'] = 2^(-|M|) (only S empty), E_0[G'] = 2^(-|A+C|), and E_0[F' G'] = 2^(-92) (1 + E_0[prod over S_0 of x_p]) = 2^(-92) (1 - 2^(-183)), because S union T = S_0 only for S = M, T = A + C. So Cov_0(F', G') = -2^(-92) 2^(-183).
(b) EP-NO (b) bounds the conditional density of the 184 links of S_0, given all other links, by exp(+-2208 beta_max) relative to product Haar measure. For every H supported on these links with 0 <= H <= 1 this gives |E[H] - E_0[H]| <= delta := exp(2208 beta_max) - 1. Apply it to F', G', F'G' (each in [0, 1]), writing E F' = E_0 F' + e_1 and E G' = E_0 G' + e_2 with |e_i| <= delta: Cov(F', G') <= Cov_0(F', G') + delta + (E_0 F' + E_0 G') delta + delta^2 <= -2^(-275) + 4 delta for delta <= 1. Since 2208 < 2^12 and e^u - 1 <= 2u for 0 <= u <= 1, beta_max <= 2^(-291) gives 2208 beta_max < 2^(-279), delta <= 2^(-278) and 4 delta <= 2^(-276) < 2^(-275). The centre-even state is a positive mixture of Wilson measures with couplings +-beta_p (EP-NO (c)), and the bound uses only |beta_p|. QED

**Remark (U(1), RECALLED).** For compact U(1) with x_p = cos theta_p, F' and G' expand into products of cosines with positive coefficients, and products of cosines expand into cosines of integer combinations with positive coefficients. So Ginibre's inequality gives Cov(F', G') >= 0 at every beta >= 0. This settles only this pair; full FKG association for U(1) plaquette cosines is not claimed.
