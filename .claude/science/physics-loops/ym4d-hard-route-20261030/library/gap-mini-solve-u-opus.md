**Observation 3.3 (READ R-e; what chapter 9 can transport). ARGUED.** Chapter 9 transports, on each fixed torus, connected Schwinger functions of p >= 2 separated curvature test functions between consecutive cutoffs, with summable increments. Why the separation-free part is there (ARGUED, not a claim about the account's text). The Log h part compares the two runs' last-lattice integrals. By Duhamel along the segment between the two last-lattice actions, the difference of their p-point connected functions is a (p+1)-point connected function with one insertion of the action difference, summed over the last lattice. It decays in the separation of the p sources only if the interpolated last-lattice theories cluster, which is the infrared gap at the reference coupling. So a clustering form of hypothesis (iv) would not be a cheaper input: it contains the crux.
