## Hand checks behind the surviving statements

**Vacuum factors and the marginal map.** With the positive normalized top eigenfunction, define H(x)=exp(V(x)/2)Omega(x). The eigenvalue equation gives

`H(x)=lambda_0^(-1) integral exp(beta <x,y>) m(dy)`, and `m(dx)=exp(-V(x))H(x)dx`.

This directly checks the density and BLK's ratio: changing d slice links changes the last integral's integrand by a factor between exp(-2 beta d) and exp(2 beta d). For two slices count spatial plaquettes, the central temporal plaquettes once even if both endpoints change, and one outer temporal plaquette per changed link. Holley–Stroock gives the displayed C_D. The half-infinite interpretation additionally uses the named transfer limit.

For MARG put y=u.w. Isometries preserve the x and w gradient norms. At one spatial vertex, the map from its three unit Lie-algebra directions to the six incident link tangents has squared operator norm six; hence the pullback's temporal gradient is bounded by six times the sum over those links. Summing vertices gives `D_u <= 12 D_w`. Simultaneous gauge invariance gives the same bound `D_u <= 12 D_x`. Thus `D_u <= 6(D_x+D_w)` and `D_F <= 7(D_x+D_w)`. Integrate the lifted inequality and take the fixed-box transfer limit. Only this forward map is used. EQ then gives `C_J <= 7 max(2 Cbar,K+8 Cbar K kappa^2 n')`, exactly the factor 14 in the entropy coefficient. A block contains at most `6 m^2(m+1)^2 <= 6(m+1)^4` plaquettes and at most `4m(m+1)^3 <= 4(m+1)^4` links; these justify the two deliberately loose counts.

**First-link expansion, SEP, FOREST.** Quaternion Haar covariance is `integral q_i q_j dq = delta_ij/4`. Applying it to the linear functions t(a_i u) gives the second-order coefficient beta^2/8 times `k+2 sum_(i<j)t(a_i a_j^(-1))`; odd terms vanish. With six staples, choose(6,2)=15. Opposite staples in each of three transverse axes give three planar rectangles; the other twelve are bent. For SEP, a unit sphere geodesic gives `t_p''=-t_p`; differentiation under the finite compact integral gives `Hbar''=E H''+Var(H')`. At the flipped kept link, the k all-kept plaquettes equal -1, and each of the k' others is at most 1, giving `Ric-Hess Hbar <= 2-beta(k-k')`. For FOREST, assign independent Haar vertex gauges from one root per tree. Their edge differences have product Haar law; invariance and transitivity identify the marginal. These are separate facts, not an exhaustive classification of skeletons.

**HBE, HESS, DEF-ABS.** The invariant diffusion preserves invariant functions, so their log-densities have horizontal gradients. Bochner gives `I'(t)=-2 E[f_t (|Hess log f_t|^2+(Ric-Hess H)(grad log f_t,grad log f_t))]`. The HBE hypothesis yields `I'<=-2 rho I` and entropy integration gives alpha=rho. Substituting both DEF-ABS premises instead yields `I'<=-rho I`, hence alpha=rho/2. Smooth positive densities on the finite compact connected product justify entropy convergence; approximation extends to the usual test class. For a plaquette word, move all increments to its end: `W(t)=W prod_i exp(t Z_i)`. Its velocity is W sum Z_i and its covariant acceleration is `W sum_(i<j)[Z_i,Z_j]`. On the unit S^3, Hess t=-t g and `|grad t|=sqrt(1-t^2)`; `[Z_i,Z_j]` has norm at most `2|Z_i||Z_j|`. This verifies HESS including its sign. Differentiating the conditional log normalizer supplies the additional nonnegative force variance.

## GUIDE code and data check

The embedded code was read in full (617–940). The Hamiltonian is beta times one half-trace per positively oriented plaquette on an even periodic four-torus. `staples` has the correct forward/backward paths. At fixed staples S, the update chooses X with scalar density proportional to `sqrt(1-X_0^2) exp(beta |S| X_0)` and sets U=X conjugate(S/|S|), which is the required conditional law. The tiny-field branch replaces the exponential proposal by its uniform limit for beta|S|<1e-6, so this is a numerical limiting approximation there. The Hessian follows by differentiating `U1 exp(t xi1) U2 exp(t xi2) exp(-t xi3) U3^-1 exp(-t xi4) U4^-1`. Its four diagonal blocks are -beta t_p times the identity. The six off-diagonal prefix/middle/suffix products, their inverse-link minus signs, and their transposes in the script match this expression. At the identity configuration this becomes `-beta d* d`; omitting the off-diagonal blocks would instead give `-6 beta Id` and fail S2. Thus that control distinguishes the required Hessian from a diagonal proxy.

`gauge_matrix` is the differential `xi_l=Ad_(U_l^-1)eta_s-eta_t`. The dense P is the orthogonal projection if G has full rank, which holds at generic sampled configurations; stabilizers make this inverse invalid at special backgrounds such as the flat field. The sparse version regularizes G*G by 1e-10, so its P is approximate, not an exact projector. With an exact projection, the shifted operator is `P Hs P + 12 beta P`; subtracting the shift recovers the largest horizontal eigenvalues in the measured positive range.
