## Decision

**Literal (BUB) is dead as a selective sign for the tested straight block map.** The compact Wilson U(1) plaquette tadpole already gives a positive margin at arbitrarily weak coupling. Even the *strict dressed sign on a bounded coupling window* occurs in compact U(1), by the two-edge variance bound. The size of a dressed margin uniform all the way to $\beta=\infty$ is a separate open comparison.

**The level form is a candidate statistic. Calling it the exact first statement of M-D fails.** The missing mathematical steps are entry into an independently defined class, a uniform blocking step on that class, and a terminal implication for the neutral thermal trace. The weak-coupling sources do not supply those steps for $J$. This check establishes no progress on the seed or mass gap. The mathematical bounds in this check use hand arguments.
