**Lemma PC-c — HOLDS (D178–236).** At each site let $L=\delta^*\delta$, $M$ exchange the two ends on each axis and $J$ be the all-ones matrix. The degree is $h=2d-2$ and $L=(2d-1)I+M-J$. Constants, antisymmetric pairs, and mean-zero symmetric pairs exhaust the $2d$ end coordinates. Their eigenvalues are $0,h,2d$, with multiplicities $1,d,d-1$. This agrees with the earlier four-dimensional calculation.

To recover the factor eight, subtract the site mean $k_i$ from each $f_i$, giving columns $u_i$ of a matrix $U$. On an edge $a\to b$ the change in $2S(f_i)$ is the exact difference $\operatorname{Im}[k_i(\overline{u_i(b)}-\overline{u_i(a)})]$. Projecting onto cycles therefore gives $\lambda\theta$ from the entries $\tfrac12\operatorname{Im}(UU^*)_{ab}$. Orthogonal projection is a contraction. Each unoriented edge contributes two conjugate entries to the full squared matrix norm. Consequently
$$
 \lambda^2\|\theta\|^2 \le \tfrac18\|UU^*\|_{HS}^2
 =\tfrac18\|U^*U\|_{HS}^2
 \le \frac{\|T\|_{HS}^2}{8h^2}.
$$
Indeed $0\le hU^*U\le U^*LU$, the Gram matrix $T$ up to the inner-product convention. For positive $B\le A$, $\operatorname{tr}(A^2-B^2)=\operatorname{tr}((A-B)A)+\operatorname{tr}(B(A-B))\ge0$. This checks the orientation, projection and norm in D224–236, including $\theta=0$ or $\lambda=0$. On the infinite lattice use finite support or finite gradient norms and exhaust whole sites. After site centering the columns are square summable by the same spectral bound. An arbitrary exact part of the prescribed field is absorbed into $\delta\phi$.
