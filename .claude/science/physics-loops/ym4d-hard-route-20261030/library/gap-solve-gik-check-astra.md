## Row 1197. The two twist identities

**G1 — HOLDS (G 87–98).** At site $u$, rotate the first two real components by $\alpha_u$ and keep the other components $z_u$ fixed. This is an orthogonal map, so it preserves each sphere measure and their product. On $uv$ the inner product becomes

$$
z_u\cdot z_v+\rho_u\rho_v\cos(\theta_u-\theta_v+\alpha_u-\alpha_v).
$$

Subtract the original term and multiply by $-\beta$. This gives G 95 with its stated sign. At $\rho_u=0$ the angle is immaterial and the actual spin is fixed. Summing proves the cost identity for any fixed site field, including for $O(N)$ with an $(N-2)$-component $z$.

**G2(a) — HOLDS AFTER CORRECTION at G 123–124.** Multiplying the two diagonal matrices into $V$ fixes $z$ and sends $w$ to $e^{i\alpha}w$. Conjugation preserves Haar. The fixed set of the whole circle, or of a single angle outside $2\pi\mathbb Z$, is $w=0$. For a specified angle in $2\pi\mathbb Z$ every link is fixed. At $\alpha=0$, the link with $z=0,w=1$ is a counterexample to the unrestricted single-angle reading.

**G2(b,c) — HOLDS (G 125–139).** The inverse of a conjugated link is the conjugate of its inverse. Multiplying the four factors and cycling the first $r_1$ to the end inserts, in order, $r_1^{-1}r_2,r_2^{-1}r_3,r_3^{-1}r_4,r_4^{-1}r_1$. Constant angles give identity insertions. Diagonal links commute with every insertion and the insertion product telescopes. This is the corner identity of row 1013. For the explicit signs, put $A=V_1V_2,\ B=V_4V_3$. Half the trace is the real part of

$$
(z_1z_2-w_1\bar w_2)(\bar z_4\bar z_3-\bar w_4 w_3)
+(z_1w_2+w_1\bar z_2)(\bar z_4\bar w_3+\bar w_4 z_3).
$$

The two negative terms come from the first product. The second gives the last four positive terms at G 132. Each $w_j$ contributes $+\alpha_j$ and each $\bar w_j$ contributes $-\alpha_j$. This checks all eight terms, including when an amplitude vanishes.
