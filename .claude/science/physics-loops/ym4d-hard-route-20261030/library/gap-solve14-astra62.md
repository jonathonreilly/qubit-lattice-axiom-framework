## A twist ratio from two ordinary completions

**PROVED.** Let \(G=(\mathbb Z/2)^3\). On the ordinary time circle of period \(2t\), observe just the two arrays \(Y_0,Y_t\) of the preceding short spatial words.  \zeta_h=\mathbb E_\nu\sum_z\sqrt{w_z w_{z+h}},\qquad

## An adjoint axis from earlier time layers

**PROVED (rectangle input).** On an ordinary periodic Wilson rectangle, for coordinate rectangles with sides \(h,k\) shorter than their periods,
\[
       \|1-U_{h\times k}\|_{L^2}\le C\sqrt{hk/\beta},\qquad \beta\ge1 . \tag{4}
\]
Here the norm inside the expectation is the Euclidean quaternion norm. Row 1153 proves the one-plaquette deficit bound and the width-one version, using the reflection-positivity method of Bachas. To extend the width, turn the \(h\) direction into time, of total period \(p>h\). Multiplication by the spatial length-\(k\) transporter is an isometry \(V\) from scalar states into states with the two endpoint color indices, using normalized Hilbert–Schmidt norm in those indices. Gauge averaging transports the indices at the two endpoints on the \(h\) steps. The Wilson rectangle expectation is consequently
\[
 \frac{\sum_{i,j}\lambda_i^{p-h}\nu_j^h |V_{ji}|^2}{\sum_i\lambda_i^p}
\]
with scalar and charged transfer eigenvalues in the same normalization. Both transfers are positive by the projected convolution construction. The weights \(\lambda_i^p|V_{ji}|^2/\sum_i\lambda_i^p\) sum to one. Jensen on the nonnegative ratios gives \(Ew_{h,k}\ge(Ew_{1,k})^h\). Zero scalar eigenvalues do not contribute as \(h<p\). Apply the same argument in the other direction. For large \(\beta\), \(Ew_{1,1}\ge1-C/\beta>0\), hence \(1-Ew_{h,k}\le Chk/\beta\). A larger absolute constant handles bounded \(\beta\). Also \(\|\operatorname{Ad}q-I\|_{\rm F}\le C|q-1|\). Thus commuting two consecutive straight coordinate runs of lengths at most \(s\) costs at most \(Cs/\sqrt\beta\) in \(L^2\) Frobenius norm of transports. Prefixes, suffixes, inversions and conjugations preserve this bound. No independence of rectangles is used. **PROVED (single period, ordinary Wilson measure).** Let the spatial side be \(n\to\infty\), let \(\beta=O(\log n)\) (fixed \(\beta\) is allowed), and take a time period \(2t\ge4\). ## Can a field-dependent choice of the surface remove that cost?

**PROVED (entropy after forgetting the selected surface).** In one of the two time arcs prescribe, for each transverse site \(y\), a simply connected patch of at most \(A\) normal faces with simple boundary, interior to its ribbon.              p_*=\min\{1,\exp[-c\beta m/A+C A m]\}.                      \tag{18}
\]
Indeed the windings of the relative sign cochains are common across the lines of each endpoint, since the cochains are spatially closed. \]

**PROVED.** For example let a marked normal face in each patch be located by moving at most \(K\) sites in each normal coordinate between neighboring transverse sites, with fixed \(K\).
