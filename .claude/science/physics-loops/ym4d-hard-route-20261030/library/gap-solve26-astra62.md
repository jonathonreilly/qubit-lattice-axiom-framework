**PROVED (smooth conditional densities in a fixed number of variables).** Use the original Wilson link law including temporal links. \[

**PROVED.** In a bounded normal chart around the identity, \(\nu_\beta\) in the \(z\)-variables tends locally smoothly to a positive constant multiple of Lebesgue measure.
