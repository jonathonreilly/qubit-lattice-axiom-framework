## Which states carry the short-period trace?

**PROVED, given row 1153 (5).** Put \(v=N/m^3\), \(c=\pi^2/15\), and \(Y_i=mE_i/v\). Take any \(m\to\infty\) with \(m^4\beta^{-1/4}\log(2+\beta)\to0\) on the physical grid. For fixed \(0<x<3c<y\), the band \(B=\{x\le Y_i\le y\}\) carries all but an exponentially small fraction of the short-period trace. At the fixed physical time its *absolute* contribution satisfies
\[
 \lim_j \frac{m}{Tv}\log\sum_{i\in B}e^{-TE_i}=-x.                  \tag{1}
\]
In particular the states responsible for the leading free-gas multiplicity already have vanishing energy-weighted count at every fixed admissible \(L\). This identifies a portion of the count in the question without using a physical mass gap.

**PROVED.** Give state \(i\) probability \(p_m(i)=e^{-mE_i-g_m}\). For every fixed \(u<1\), the limit of \(v^{-1}\log\mathbb E_m e^{uvY}\) is \(c[(1-u)^{-3}-1]\), by the two instances of (5). Its derivative at zero is \(3c\). Choosing small fixed \(u<0\) and \(u>0\) gives exponential left and right tail bounds outside \(B\). For the physical upper bound, use \(\sum_{Y_i\ge x}e^{-TE_i}\le\exp[g_m-(T/m-1)xv]\). For the lower bound take \(x<z<x+\delta<y\) and use the Gibbs law at \(m(3c/z)^{1/4}\). The same two tail inequalities, with the rescaled time, put probability tending to one on \(x<Y<x+\delta\), so this interval contains at least one state. Its physical weight is at least \(\exp[-(T/m)(x+\delta)v]\). Since \(m/T\to0\), letting \(\delta\downarrow0\) proves (1). Row 1153 (8) already gives the unweighted entropy inside fixed bands. Formula (1) uses a different speed, \(Tv/m\), for their physical weight.

**PROVED, given the same short-period law.** A physical Gibbs state lives on an asymptotically much rarer set than the complement just estimated. For any fixed \(d>0\), put
\[
 H_d=\{(T-m)E_i\le (c+d)v\}.
\]
Its complement contributes at most \(\exp[-(d+o(1))v]\) to the unnormalized physical trace. The rank of \(H_d\) is \(\exp(o(v))\). Indeed for each fixed \(A>0\),
\[
 \log |H_d|\le g_{Am}+Am(c+d)v/(T-m).
\]
Divide by \(v\), pass to the upper limit and then send \(A\to\infty\). The vacuum is in \(H_d\), so
\[
   \lim_j v^{-1}\log p_m(H_d)=-c .
\]
In particular even the full exponential rate for this rare event is fixed by the vacuum alone. It does not determine how much its excited states weigh at \(T\).

\]

\]

Apply these bounds to the covariance with \(1-\cos\theta\). Chord is at most angle. This proves the coupling estimate.

**PROVED.** Couple two chains with left endpoints \(x,x'\) and common right endpoint \(y\) by updating a common uniform site and using the preceding one-site coupling. **PROVED (a small-ball bound in the gauge quotient).** For two spatial link fields write

**PROVED (smoothing with the exact Wilson vacuum).** On the full product of link spheres, let \(q_b(x,z)\) be the product of one-link kernels \(e^{b x_e\cdot z_e}/\Phi(b)\), relative to Haar, with \(b>0\) a smoothing parameter unrelated to the box width. \]
relative to \(\mu\). It is self-adjoint, positive semidefinite, Markov, and commutes with gauge and all the stated finite symmetries. ## What the adjoint field knows about the winding sectors

**PROVED (reading the temporal twist from adjoint Wilson data).** At either physical period \(p=T,2T\) let \(b\in G=(\mathbb Z_2)^3\) specify a temporal centre twist, with partition function \(Z_b\).   \mathbb P_{\rm mix}(\widehat b\ne b)\le C n^4 e^{-c\beta n^2}.            \tag{29}
\]
The error is absolute and uniform in the unknown relative partition functions. Thus the sector weights of the temporal **boundary conditions** are accessible from the adjoint Wilson ensemble. **PROVED (uniform unnormalized tilted estimate).** Write \(a_p^b=1-w_p^b\) with the boundary sign included in the plaquette trace, and \(dM_b\) for the twisted measure *divided by the untwisted partition*, of mass \(Z_b/Z_0\le1\). ## Eliminating the full-period conditional from the recovery

**PROVED (explicit kernels on an open collar).** In (27) and (31) every auxiliary lift, reference and reconstruction can instead be drawn from the following local kernel. **PROVED (estimate with only the true path as input).** Fix an actual Wilson path \(O\) and its adjoint collar. For one open draw the curvature disagreement \(B\) relative to \(O\) has probability on the exact mod-two \(2\)-cochains of the collar proportional to

                     \exp\!\left[-2\beta\sum_{p\in B} w_p(O)\right].
\]

             \mathbb P(K\subset B)\le (C e^{-c\beta})^{|K|}.                \tag{32}

**PROVED (components at the free boundary).** Take adjacency through every elementary three-cube of the interval, including the spatial ones on its boundary.
