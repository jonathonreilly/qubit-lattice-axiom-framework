What the theorem covers (row 782):
- (U-a) 4D U(1), Villain weight, beta above an existential threshold (108 beta_1): perimeter lower bound on rectangular Wilson loops, and non-summable field-strength two-point correlations.
- (U-b) Same for the Wilson cosine weight: claimed by Frohlich and Spencer, derivation omitted.

What the test needs (T1, T3 of `gap-crux-fp.md` 274-283): a comparison model M' in the class of the tool, at a coupling where **Seed(M') fails**, where Seed is a statement about the thermal sum S(a; (2a)^3) on **periodic** tori (line 272). So every use of T1/T3 needs the step

  (BRIDGE) the infinite-volume fact (no area law, or non-summable correlations, on Z^4 with fixed boundary) implies that Seed(M') fails on periodic tori.

(BRIDGE) is not proved anywhere I found (see Section 1). A contrapositive route exists: Seed(M') on tori gives, by Theorem S, a uniform gap on periodic cubes; one then needs "uniform torus gaps imply exponential clustering in the fixed-boundary infinite-volume state of M'". For SU(2) at fixed beta row 700 is cited for the torus-to-limit passage (`gap-crux-fp.md` 456); for U(1) with fixed boundary links nothing is written. So even at large beta, T1/T3 with U(1) as M' is PROVED GIVEN (BRIDGE), not a theorem.

For Z_2 the same bridge is needed, but the Z_2 test is mostly used on the flux half (twist ratios on a torus), where a direct torus argument is short (a Peierls bound for the twist free energy on a torus at large beta). That is also not written in the lane.

Two further points from the sources, used below:
- (FIX) `library/gap-seed-check.md` L191-201 (check of row 698) proves the failure of the periodic seed for Z_2 at each fixed size n as beta -> infinity (by hand), and sketches the same for U(1). Its own verdict (L201): these "do not show that the seed fails at a fixed coupling for every size". T3 needs the failure at every size at one coupling. So on the torus even the large-beta form of T3 is not written for either model.
- (OBS) "No area law" (a perimeter lower bound) does not by itself contradict a gap. A Higgs-like phase has a gap and a perimeter law. The FS fact that contradicts a seed is the non-summable field-strength correlation, through Theorem S, a uniform torus gap, and (BRIDGE). The perimeter bound contradicts a seed only through a "seed implies area law" step for U(1), which is not written (for SU(2) it goes through the twist ratios and Tomboulis-Yaffe-Kanazawa, whose U(1) form is Kanazawa's Theorem 4, GUIDE here).
- (RANGE) For the continuum target (GAP)/(PHYS-SEED) the bare coupling tends to infinity. An existential large-beta threshold then covers the needed range. For a fixed-beta lattice verdict at beta of order one (R1, beta up to about 2) it does not: there the U(1) and Z_2 facts are numerics.
