## Inputs, defects and decision

They are sufficient inputs with distinctive content. Their two-sided formulations can be stronger than necessary. A single normalized moment suffices as above. **None is equivalent to (LOG).** [Row 1391](gap-univk-rate-fable.md) §5.5 asks for a bound on log *total* zero-source masses after the same extensive subtraction on two tori. The following are separations of the comparison hypotheses. They make no claim to realize a Wilson history.

Neither a failed shell upper bound nor a failed cell-moment bound by itself refutes (LOG) or forces a large endpoint difference.
