## 3. The exponent of a label is affine or it can be made so: Lemma ENDS and Theorem G (PROVED, elementary; unchecked)

Setting. G is a countable set of labels. For h in G, (Omega_h, mu_h) is a measure space with mu_h >= 0. The two ends of the dial move are the chart measures tau_h^A and tau_h^(B|A) (objects of B, ramps and stop-level production slots of A), finite and not both zero.

**(POS-2)** For every h in G there are a measurable f_h >= 0 and real measurable Phi_h^A, Phi_h^B on {f_h > 0} with d tau_h^A = f_h e^(Phi_h^A) d mu_h and d tau_h^(B|A) = f_h e^(Phi_h^B) d mu_h.
In words: both ends are nonnegative on the chart and have the same support up to mu_h-null sets. Put Delta_h := Phi_h^B - Phi_h^A on {f_h > 0}.

**3.1 Lemma ENDS (necessity).** If some assembly formula P_h has (S-form) of row 1398 3.5 at the two points (lambda, eps) = (0, 0) and (1, 0) of one fibre, then (POS-2) holds. If (POS-2) fails for a label of G on a set of positive mu_h measure, then (S-form) fails for every formula with these ends.
Proof. (S-form) gives the densities f_h exp(real, finite) at both points with the same f_h, which is (POS-2). QED
So (POS-2) is the part of (S-form) that no choice of interpolation can avoid. It is a statement about two end terms. It contains no dial.

**3.2 Theorem G (the geometric array).** Assume (POS-2). Let Z be a finite set and Delta_h = sum_(z in Z) W_h^(z) with real measurable W_h^(z) (for example the class shares of the account, or Z a single point). For real t = (t_z)_z put
  rho_h[t] := f_h exp( Phi_h^A + sum_z t_z W_h^(z) ),   F_G(t) := sum_(h in G) integral rho_h[t] d mu_h   in (0, infinity].
Then:
 (a) (ends) rho_h[0,...,0] and rho_h[1,...,1] are the densities of tau_h^A and tau_h^(B|A).
 (b) (exponent form, no non-affine part) on every line t = t^0 + eps e_z the family has the form of Lemma SEMI (row 1398 3.1) with a_h = W_h^(z), phi_h = 0 and kappa = 0. So log F_G is convex on every such line where it is finite. More: F_G is the Laplace transform of a positive measure on R^Z, so log F_G is convex jointly in t on the convex set where F_G is finite, and real analytic in its interior.
 (c) (first variation) in the interior of the finiteness set, (d/d lambda) F_G(lambda, ..., lambda) = sum_z (d/d t_z) F_G at the diagonal point.
 (d) (incidence) if W_h^(z) = 0 for the labels that do not read the class z, then rho_h[t] does not depend on t_z for those labels.
Proof. (a) is the definition. (b) F_G(t) = integral e^(<t, w>) d nu(w), with nu the image of the measure sum_h f_h e^(Phi_h^A) mu_h on the disjoint union of the charts under the map (W^(z))_z. Holder's inequality gives log-convexity (this is Lemma EXP of row 1395 4.5 in several variables). A Laplace transform of a positive measure is analytic in the interior of its domain. (c) is the chain rule for an analytic function. (d) is the definition. QED

**3.3 Consequence for the row's first question.** For the geometric array the complete non-affine exponent of every label is zero. The per-label lower bound on the second derivative holds with constant 0, which is at most C N_w^4/R_z^2. (S-sum) is empty. The slot-by-slot questions of row 1398 3.5 and the defects 1 and 3 of check 1399 (inverse covariance, determinant, activity norm against exponent remainder, normaliser floor) do not arise, because no operator is re-read along the path: the path interpolates the logarithm of the complete end densities.
What the geometric array needs in place of them is its own majorant (Section 4). The geometric array is the account's array whenever the account's P_h reads every terminal entry in the exponent with frozen evaluation maps (the form printed at Y 81185-81208); I could not confirm this for every entry (2.1 (iv)). If the account's P_h reads some entry differently, the two paths differ between the ends, and the account's majorant on its dial domain is then a statement about another path.
Check 1399 (L93) lists what a re-interpolation must supply: common end points ((a)), the admissible fibres ((b), all lines), a first-variation identity for the new path ((c)). These hold. Its fourth item, uniformity over labels, centres and both tori, is a property of (POS-2) and of the majorant, not of the path.

## 4. The retained end point estimate from the two ends alone: Theorem R (PROVED, elementary; unchecked) (written 2026-11-22T16:54Z by date -u)

Setting of Section 3 with Z a single point: g(lambda) := log F_G(lambda), F_G(lambda) = sum_(h in G) integral f_h e^(Phi_h^A + lambda Delta_h) d mu_h. So F^A := F_G(0) and F^(B|A) := F_G(1) are the retained masses of the two ends. Assume (POS-2) and 0 < F^A, F^(B|A) < infinity.

**4.1 Theorem R.**
 (a) (pointwise form) If delta_- <= Delta_h <= delta_+ holds mu_h-almost everywhere on {f_h > 0} for every h in G, then delta_- <= log F^(B|A) - log F^A <= delta_+.
 (b) (moment form) Let S > 0 and g(-S), g(1+S) <= Lambda. Then
   | log F^(B|A) - log F^A | <= ( Lambda - max(g(0), g(1)) ) / S  <=  ( Lambda - g(0) ) / S.
 (c) (mean form) If F_G is finite on a neighbourhood of [0, 1], then log F^(B|A) - log F^A = integral_0^1 <Delta>_lambda d lambda, where <.>_lambda is the mean in the probability measure proportional to sum_h f_h e^(Phi_h^A + lambda Delta_h) mu_h on the disjoint union of the charts.
Proof. (a) e^(delta_-) f_h e^(Phi_h^A) <= f_h e^(Phi_h^B) <= e^(delta_+) f_h e^(Phi_h^A) pointwise; integrate and sum. (b) g is convex on [-S, 1+S] by 3.2 (b). Since 1 = (1+S)/(1+S) + 0 S/(1+S), convexity gives g(1) - g(0) <= (g(1+S) - g(0))/(1+S) <= (Lambda - g(0))/(1+S). Since 0 = (-S)/(1+S) + 1 S/(1+S), it gives g(0) - g(1) <= (Lambda - g(1))/(1+S). If g(1) >= g(0), the first line gives |g(1) - g(0)| <= (Lambda - g(1) + g(1) - g(0))/(1+S), so |g(1) - g(0)| <= (Lambda - g(1))/S. If g(0) > g(1), the second line gives the same with g(0) in place of g(1). The second inequality of (b) is trivial. (c) g is analytic near [0, 1] by 3.2 (b) and g' = <Delta>_lambda. QED

4.2 **Relation to what is on file.** (b) is Lemma RENYI of row 1395 4.6 (with c = 0: g(1+S) - g(1) = log E_(nu_B)[r^S], g(-S) - g(0) = log E_(nu_A)[r^(-S)]) applied on the disjoint union of the label charts at the ramps of A, restricted to G. Row 1395 could not feed that lemma on V_w, where the two label measures can be mutually singular (III 11895-11900). On the charts the two ends have one reference measure, and (POS-2) is exactly the hypothesis that makes the density ratio exist. (a) is the chart form of the pointwise bound; it differs from (DENS-K) of row 924, which is a bound for the full densities on V_w: (a) concerns retained labels only, on charts, at the ramps of A, and gives total masses only.

4.3 **Compared with Theorem S of row 1398.** Theorem R (b) needs: (POS-2); the majorant Lambda at the two real points lambda = -S and lambda = 1+S of the geometric line; one of the two end floors. It does not need: the fibres, the first variation (S0), holomorphy, the connectedness argument, (S-sum), a curvature constant. With class radii R_z, the admissible S is min_z R_z, because every slot has one class and at lambda = 1+S a slot of class z has moved by S |W_u| beyond the convex hull. So the factor e^(s_1) - 1 with s_1 = sum_z 2/R_z (row 1398 3.4, a sum over the recent classes with their multiplicity k - b + 1) becomes 1/min_z R_z (a maximum of the recent loads, no multiplicity). With the constants of row 1398 3.4:
   | log F^(B|A) - log F^A | <= ( E_+^sharpsharp + C' ) N_w^4 max_(z recent) l_(n(z)) / E_1 .
This is PROVED GIVEN (POS-2), the end floor F^A >= e^(-C' N_w^4) or the same for F^(B|A), and (MOM-G): F_G(-S), F_G(1+S) <= e^(E_+^sharpsharp N_w^4) with S = E_1/max_(z recent) l_(n(z)).

4.4 **Where (MOM-G) can come from (ARGUED, not proved).**
 (i) From the account's majorant, if the account's array is the geometric one (every terminal entry read in the exponent, frozen evaluation maps, the form of Y 81185-81208). Then F_G(1+S) is the account's sum at the real array x^B + S (x^B - x^A), where "the total dial of every slot stays at most 2 E_1" (III 17456-17459), and the majorant A^sharp of III 17508-17512 applies if it holds on that joint real set and not only on the fibres of the dial domain. For the labels of G only recent classes move (incidence, III 13664-13686, as used in row 1398 2.4).
 (ii) From pointwise class shares. (PW): for every h in G there are numbers N_z(h) >= 0 with |W_h^(z)| <= K l_(n(z)) N_z(h) on {f_h > 0}. Then by 4.1 (a)
   | log F^(B|A) - log F^A | <= K sup_(h in G) sum_z l_(n(z)) N_z(h),
 and no majorant, floor or radius is needed. By 4.1 (c) the supremum can be replaced by the supremum over lambda in [0, 1] of the mean sum_z l_(n(z)) <N_z>_lambda.
 What the account prints toward (PW): the loads are suprema over the slice data of the two-run differences of the stored families, with tree decay, S^+_i[F] = sup_X e^(kappa_F d(X)) sup |F^B - F^A| (III 12017-12023, 12045-12056); the resolved members obey "value per cell <= |eps| K(d) y_n" per cell of Gamma^0_n in the region (III 13733-13739); the live arrest obeys |D_W^z| <= K_G N(W) Delta_n^c (Y 129655-129658); the class-n normaliser pieces obey |delta L^(n)(X'')| <= (d^op_n/C^T_cl) C_F L-bar(X'') (III 12729-12733), which is an absolute bound on an additive piece, so it needs no floor when the normaliser is read as a sum in the exponent. These are bounds per slot or per cell at a common datum. A supremum over the data is a pointwise bound at every real point of the chart.
 Units (check 1399 defect 5): N_z(h) counts cells of zone n(z), which are level-n(z) cells. For a label of G and a class z = (b, t) with b >= k - a_0 the crude count is N_z(h) <= L^(4(k-b)) N_w^4, and there are at most k - b + 1 reading levels t. So the crude form of (ii) is K N_w^4 sum_(b >= k - a_0) (k - b + 1) L^(4(k-b)) l_b. It has the volume factor L^(4(k-b)) where row 1398 3.4 has q_a^(-(a_0-1)). 4.5 **Is this equivalent to the open target? No.** (POS-2) contains no comparison of cutoffs: it holds for two unrelated positive families on one support. (MOM-G) is an upper bound, of the kind of (H1). (PW) is a comparison statement, but it is strictly stronger than the retained estimate it implies (two halves of a chart with Delta = +M and -M and equal masses have ratio 1 and violate (PW)), and it does not give (LOG): rarity (H3), the representatives (H4), the ramp move and the two-torus carry are untouched.
