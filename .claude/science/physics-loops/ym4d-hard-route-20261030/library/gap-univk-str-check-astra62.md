## Comparison of the hypotheses

**The prescribed (S-form) with (S-sum) and corrected (STR) are independent.** This holds even with a constant ramp. L50's “weaker” is false as an ordering of these hypotheses. Once the full curvature bound is supplied, Theorem S gives an alternative sufficient estimate for the recent move. Thus the claimed reduction of STR itself at L114 is false even after repairing the curvature budget.

1. **S-form and S-sum can hold while both STR alternatives fail.** Take one actual class of required radius $R>4$, coordinate $u=\lambda+\varepsilon$, and two positive label weights $\tfrac12\exp(\pm2u/R-3u^2/R^2)$. At any $\lambda\in[0,1]$ absorb the constant into $f_h$ and the linear term into $a_h$. The remaining $-3\varepsilon^2/R^2$ has modulus at most 3 on the disc. All fibre and diagonal majorants are uniform. At $\lambda=0$ the sum is $e^{-3u^2/R^2}\cosh(2u/R)$. Its log has second derivative $-2/R^2$ at zero and it vanishes at $u=i\pi R/4$. Both tests fail on the required fibre. This example even satisfies the repaired total-exponent requirement.
2. **STR can hold while S-form fails.** On the prescribed two single-point charts use affine weights $\tau_1(u)=(1+u)/(4R)$, $\tau_2(u)=1-\tau_1(u)$. Both physical endpoints have positive weights. The retained sum is identically 1, hence satisfies both STR tests with any admissible radius. At $\lambda=0,\ \varepsilon=-3/2$, inside the real half fibre, $\tau_1<0$. No nonnegative base times a real exponential represents this given label. The example addresses L120 even for affine input slots. A new representation of the total would change the claim.
3. **Neither is equivalent to (LOG).** Use check 1397 L107's one good label $F(u)=1+4u(1-u)/R^2$. The two endpoint masses are 1 on both tori, so LOG holds with zero error, but STR fails. It also fails S-form because at $\lambda=0$ the negative root $(1-\sqrt{1+R^2})/2$ lies strictly between $-R/2$ and 0. The label has positive mass at 0 and zero at that root, incompatible with fixed support and a finite real exponent. Polynomial dependence by itself would prove nothing. In the reverse direction keep the good mass identically 1 on both tori, with constant ramps, and add a discarded physical B mass $e^{V b_K}-1$ on the small torus alone, as in check 1397 L70. The two full log differences per unit volume are $b_K$ and 0 (in last-lattice volume units at fixed stop depth). No common subtraction puts both within $Ce_K$ if $b_K/e_K\to\infty$. Both path conditions hold. This is a separation of implications for the comparison data. The Wilson model is undecided.
