### 1.1 What is proved, exactly

- **Objects.** B a periodic spatial box, sides at least 3. u, v two spatial-link configurations (two neighbouring time slices). The layer model: one SU(2) spin h(x) per site (the temporal link), bond variable y_b = (1/2) tr(h(x) v_b h(x+e_i)^(-1) u_b^(-1)), partition function I(u, v) = integral of prod_b exp(-beta (1 - y_b)) (READ `gap-crux-fp.md:373-377`). c_nu flips the sign of the links of direction nu that cross one plane.
- **Identity L (exact; READ `gap-crux-fp.md:381-386`).** Z[k]/Z = E( I(u, c^k v)/I(u, v) ), E the torus measure. The signed mean of the layer twist ratio is the twist ratio of the 4D torus.
- **The form that the wall blocks.** (AS-bg) at beta_1: sup over all boxes, all (u, v), all beta <= beta_1 of abs(log(I(u, c_nu v)/I(u, v))) <= C (abs(B)/b_nu) exp(-m b_nu) (READ `gap-crux-fp.md:412`). In coefficient form: kappa_1 = (1/2) sup over (u, v) of abs(1 - I(u, c_nu v)/I(u, v)) (READ `gap-compress-opus.md:173`). In covariance form: N(x, y) = sup over O in O(4) and over all horizontal-edge backgrounds of abs(E_mu_k[q_x . O q_y]) <= C r^d(x,y), which is the only input of Lemma R to the area law (READ `gap-arealaw2-opus.md` Step 1 and 6.4).
- **Proposition TB2 (row 839; HOLDS AFTER CORRECTION by row 849).** For the O(N) nearest-neighbour model with weight exp(beta sum s_x . s_y) on a periodic box with sides (b, c, d), b even: 1 - Z_anti/Z_per >= 2 [(m_Lambda^2/(2N) - b/(24 beta c d))_+]^2, m_Lambda^2 = 1 - (N/beta) G_Lambda. On even cubes: liminf (1 - Z_anti/Z_per) >= [(1 - N G_3/beta)_+]^2/(2 N^2) (READ `gap-compress-check-astra.md:69-98`). Given: the infrared bound of Frohlich, Simon and Spencer, which the row 849 check READ in the primary source (CMP 50, Section 2, Theorems 2.2-2.3). I did not open that paper.
- **Use.** At the flat background u = v = 1 the layer model is that O(4) model at beta = beta_W, and the flip is the antiperiodic seam. So kappa_1 >= m^4/64 (1 - o(1)) and (AS-bg) is false for every beta_1 > 4 G_3. The exact threshold is 4 G_3 (about 1.0109; the decimal is not a proved cutoff). The hand bound G_3 < 1/3 gives failure for beta > 4/3, which covers the window (READ `gap-compress-check-astra.md:100, 147`).
- **Covariance form (row 834, 6.4; row 857 Section 9: HOLDS).** No bound N(0, x) <= C r^d(0,x) with finite C and r < 1, uniform in the background and the volume, holds for beta_W > 4 G_3.
- **What is not proved (the check's limits; READ `gap-compress-check-astra.md:100, 240, 258, 264`).** (a) No lower bound on the Doob coefficient eta_1 = sup_u TV(P(u, .), P(Ju, .)); a three-state example separates eta_1 from kappa_1. (b) Nothing on thick slabs kappa_t, t >= 2. (c) Nothing on the mean over the true law. (d) It excludes a background-uniform two-point decay bound for one slab, "not the 4D area law or every conceivable use of slabs" (READ `gap-arealaw2-check-astra.md:374-375`). (e) Nothing on the critical coupling 0.936 (GUIDE).

### 1.3 Two small results for the table

**Lemma W1-abs (PROVED; two lines).** Ising ring of t sites, coupling K, J the global flip. Identity L reads Z_anti/Z_per = E[e^(-2K s s')] for one bond (s, s'). As t tends to infinity, P(s s' = -1) = 1/(1 + e^(2K)), so E[e^(-2K s s')] = 1 (the deficit tends to 0), while (1/2) E abs(1 - e^(-2K s s')) = tanh K.
Proof. E[e^(-2K s s')] = e^(-2K) e^(2K)/(1 + e^(2K)) + e^(2K)/(1 + e^(2K)) = 1. E abs(1 - e^(-2K s s')) = (1 - e^(-2K)) e^(2K)/(1 + e^(2K)) + (e^(2K) - 1)/(1 + e^(2K)) = 2 tanh K. QED
Reading: the bound p(a) <= (1/2) E_w abs(1 - ratio) of Proposition TB (b) has a floor tanh K at every length, in a model with a gap. The absolute value destroys an exact cancellation between typical ratios below 1 and rare ratios far above 1. For the gauge centre flip the layer integral over h softens this at order-one coupling (the seam can move), but not at weak bare coupling (table line 1c).

**Lemma W1-t (the thick-slab coefficient and wall-to-wall rectangles; ARGUED: it is the proof of TB2 (i), (iii) with one standard input not written here).** Let t >= 1, and let the slab have t layers of temporal links between two flat slices (all wall links 1), on a periodic spatial box with sides (b, c, d), b even, b in direction nu. Let s(x) = h_1(x) h_2(x) ... h_t(x) in SU(2) = S^3 be the wall-to-wall line at the spatial site x. Then

1 - A^(t)(1, J1)/A^(t)(1, 1) >= 2 [ gbar/(2N) ]^2,   N = 4,   gbar = (cd)^(-2) sum over x in cross-section 0, y in cross-section b/2 of E_slab[ s(x) . s(y) ],

and s(x) . s(y) = (1/2) tr(s(x) s(y)^(-1)) is the Wilson rectangle with temporal sides at x and y, closed along the flat walls.
Argument. (i) Take nu as the transfer direction of the slab. The state space is the configurations of one cross-section. Let R negate the top-layer temporal links of the cross-section. Plaquettes inside the cross-section contain two such links, plaquettes in the (t, nu) plane contain one from each of two neighbouring cross-sections, so R commutes with the transfer operator T_nu. The seam of J is the insertion of R (move the seam by h_t -> -h_t on a half-space, as in Step 3 of Proposition TW, READ `gap-crux-fp.md:398`). So the ratio is Tr(R T_nu^b)/Tr(T_nu^b) = 1 - 2 w_o. (ii) Each component of M = sum over the cross-section of s(x) is a multiplication operator of norm at most cd, and it is R-odd. With T_nu >= 0 the Holder step of TB2 (iii) (READ `gap-compress-check-astra.md:93`) gives gbar <= 2N sqrt(w_o). QED. The input not written here: T_nu of the Dirichlet slab is a positive operator (the usual Wilson transfer operator in direction nu, with the wall links fixed; standard, not found written in the lane).
Reading. For t = 1 this is TB2 without the infrared step. For every t it says: **kappa_t small forces the wall-to-wall rectangles t x (b/2) of the flat Dirichlet slab to be small on average.** So the thick-slab escape (1b) is exactly a confinement statement between flat walls. U(1) in the Coulomb phase fails it at every t (GUIDE: a perimeter law at fixed t), as the fact test requires. The converse (area decay of these rectangles gives kappa_t small) is not shown.

### 1.4 W1 in one paragraph

W1 is a theorem about one point of the background space, the flat pair, at one layer. It is group-blind and action-robust. It blocks sups. Its three live assumptions are: the sup over backgrounds (1a), the thickness one (1b), and the choice of what is conditioned on (1e). The absolute value (1c) is needed by the wall but dropping it returns the flux half. The scale (1d) is shared with every wall.
