## Step 2. Wall W2: the annealed block condition (AN1) is the worst case in disguise

### 2.1 What is proved, exactly

- **Setting (READ `gap-annealed-opus.md:17-22, 56-76`).** Wilson measure mu on a periodic torus, any centre signs. A finite family A of link blocks B_a. Gamma_a is the block heat bath. The chain picks a block uniformly at random and resamples it; every U_s has law mu. A transport field xi^a_(j,v) solves the response identity (2.1); R^a_jc = sup over unit v of abs(xi_c). (AN1): for every finite block sequence and every set of jumps at distinct times, E[prod R(U_(s_i)) given the blocks] <= prod k. (AN2): the numbers k satisfy the block criterion (BC-gen) with margin kappa.
- **Theorem AG (HOLDS, row 852 Section 1).** (AN1) and (AN2) give: L^1 gradient contraction, block gap kappa/2, Langevin gap, exponential clustering of local functions. Uniform in the torus and in the centre signs. It does not give the seed comparison (READ `gap-annealed-opus.md:124`: OPEN).
- **The wall (row 852 Section 3; READ `gap-annealed-check-astra.md:69-81`).** Take the schedule a_1 = ... = a_m = a with the same jump (j, c) at every time. The exterior Y does not move, and the m refreshed blocks are independent given Y. So E[prod] = mu_ext(r(Y)^m) with r(y) = gamma_a(R^a_jc(., y) given y). Letting m grow: **(AN1) holds if and only if r(y) <= k^a_jc for almost every exterior y.** For the continuous Poisson fields and the full-support Wilson law this is the sup over y. So literal (AN1) is Lemma Q's quenched criterion: worst case over the exterior, mean over the block.
- **The second wall (row 852 Section 4; READ `:93-97`).** (P3) with a bad event that depends on the exterior only, of probability p > 0, fails for every eps < 1: the same schedule gives p <= eps^m for all m. In general (P3) is equivalent to p_a(y) = gamma_a(Bad_a given y) <= eps for almost every y.
- **What survives (row 852; READ `:87, 97, 113, 175`).** (a) The path-sum premise of the Remark at `gap-annealed-opus.md:76` is a sufficient replacement for (AN1) in Theorem AG (a): bounds only on the products that occur along label paths, averaged over the random schedule. A label path cannot repeat the jump j -> c under successive updates of a (after landing in B_a the next update of a kills it). "No Peierls supplier for it is proved here." (b) "A criterion charging distinct space-time regions or only surviving label paths would require a new statement and proof." (c) Theorem LW (Lyapunov weights) HOLDS; the cycle obstruction claimed for it is "an unproved heuristic; it establishes no impossibility result for LW".
- **Controls (HOLD).** Periodic Villain U(1) at beta >= 2^14 admits no (AN) with a volume-uniform margin for any fixed finite shape (Theorem UA, given Theorem V). The massless Gaussian field has margin at most 0 (Proposition GFF-AN, after its domain correction).
- **Target form blocked.** A proof of a volume-uniform block or Langevin gap at order-one beta_W through moment bounds (AN1) or a Peierls bound (P3) quantified over all schedules.

So v o R = v and k(R phi, omega, R phi') = k(phi, omega, phi'): R is a unitary involution that commutes with T. The seam replaces one factor k(phi, omega, phi') by k(phi, omega, R phi'). So Z_anti = Tr(T^b R) and Z_anti/Z_per = w_e - w_o, with w_o = Tr(P_o T^b)/Tr T^b, P_o = (1 - R)/2. Since b is even and T is self-adjoint, Tr(P T^b) is the squared Hilbert-Schmidt norm of P T^(b/2), so w_e, w_o lie in [0, 1], and 1 - Z_anti/Z_per = 2 w_o.
Step 3, Holder. For each of the N = 4 components, M = sum over the cross-section of s^a(x) is a multiplication operator with norm at most cd and R M R = -M. Put S = T^(b/4), A = P_e S^2, B = P_o S^2; both are nonnegative (squares of the self-adjoint P_e S, P_o S). Then Tr(M T^(b/2) M T^(b/2)) = Tr(M (A + B) M (A + B)) = 2 Tr(M A M B), because M A M is supported on the odd subspace and M B M on the even one. And abs(Tr(M A M B)) is the squared Hilbert-Schmidt norm of A^(1/2) M B^(1/2), at most abs(abs(M))^2 (Tr A^2)^(1/2) (Tr B^2)^(1/2) = abs(abs(M))^2 (Tr P_e T^b)^(1/2) (Tr P_o T^b)^(1/2). Summing the components and dividing by Tr T^b: (cd)^2 gbar <= 2N (cd)^2 (w_e w_o)^(1/2) <= 8 (cd)^2 w_o^(1/2).
Step 4. w_o >= gbar^2/64, so 2 w_o >= gbar^2/32; and kappa_t >= (1/2) abs(1 - rho_t(1, 1)). QED
