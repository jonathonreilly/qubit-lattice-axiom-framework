## Route 2: construct the missing gauge from curvature

I next tried direct axial reconstruction on each required native cube.
At layer zero, unit-spacing differences make this a useful construction.
That case is already in `pi-admit`, section 5.1, and Y 63523–63528. It is not repeated.
At higher layers the same argument loses the ratio of the layer spacing to the fine spacing.
The following exact calculation isolates that loss.

Work on a cubical lattice patch of side $`S=K\xi`$ and spacing $`\eta`$.
Here $`K`$ is a fixed positive integer, $`\xi/\eta=L^n`$, and $`0<aK<1`$.
Use a diagonal Hermitian generator $`T`$ of $`\mathrm{SU}(2)`$, normalized by $`\|T\|=1`$.
For $`x_2=m\eta`$, put $`s(x_2)=(-1)^m`$ and define

```math
A_1=0,\qquad A_2(x)=\frac{a x_1}{\xi^2}s(x_2)T,
\qquad A_3=A_4=0,
\qquad U_\mu(x)=e^{i\eta A_\mu(x)}.
```

This is the axial representative with trivial horizontal links and trivial vertical links on $`x_1=0`$.
All matrices commute. The only nontrivial plaquettes satisfy

```math
U_{12}(x)=\exp\!\left(i a\frac{\eta^2}{\xi^2}s(x_2)T\right),
\qquad
\|U_{12}(x)-1\|\le a\frac{\eta^2}{\xi^2}.
```

The potential is small in layer units when $`aK`$ is small.
Its axial first difference is

```math
\xi\|A_2\|\le aK,
\qquad
\xi^2\|\nabla_2 A_2(x)\|=\frac{2a x_1}{\eta}.
```

Thus this reconstruction gives no depth-independent first-difference ceiling at fixed $`a>0`$.
At points with $`x_1\ge S/2`$, the last quantity is at least $`aK L^n`$.
The curvature bound alone does not validate this particular gauge algorithm.

This is deliberately **not** a counterexample to gauge existence or to an actual source.
Indeed, let $`f(x_2)=0`$ for even $`m`$ and $`f(x_2)=\eta`$ for odd $`m`$.
Then $`f(x_2+\eta)-f(x_2)=\eta s(x_2)`$.
The gauge

```math
v(x)=\exp\!\left(i\frac{a x_1 f(x_2)}{\xi^2}T\right)
```

gives exactly

```math
A_1^v=-\frac{a f(x_2)}{\xi^2}T,\qquad A_2^v=A_3^v=A_4^v=0,
\qquad
\xi\|A^v\|\le a\eta/\xi,\quad
\xi^2\|\nabla A^v\|\le a.
```

The displayed gauge follows by multiplying the commuting bond exponentials with $`v(x)v(x+\eta e_\mu)^{-1}`$.
It confirms that the failure concerns axial reconstruction, not the sought regular-pair class.

**Failure of route 2.** The attempted elementary construction does not control the required first differences uniformly.
A successful replacement must construct the good gauge on the actual whole cube, using the source's extra information.
The supplied Bałaban gauges do this on their admitted cubes; their transfer is already available in route 1.
This calculation neither replaces those proofs nor supplies admission on an uncovered cube.
