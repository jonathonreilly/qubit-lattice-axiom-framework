## 1. Kill test first: the two product-tagged lifts

**Criterion.** Freeze an admitted actual coefficient input, its nodes and its original mask.
Vary one permitted switch, $`s(\Delta)=t`$, holding the others at one.
Use a direction $`v`$ in the actual raw-datum image.
A nonzero difference at an allowed $`t`$ refutes I10-LIFT at this frozen test setting.
If the stage cannot hold this input and direction fixed while varying $`t`$,
the coefficient test concerns I-INCIDENCE only; the full substituted test remains necessary.

The two sides here are $`G_{0,r}(s)Q^*q_{0,r}(s)\ell^{-1}`$, $`r\in\{Y,I\}`$.
Neither side is Y's directly decorated raw lift.
I 15276–15316 supplies the primitive/composite rule.
Y 33528–33568 supplies its admitted native primitive families.
Both source results retain their hypotheses.

Let $`G_r^-`$ and $`q_r^-`$ be the sums of the original primitive coefficients
whose complete carriers avoid the permitted cube $`\Delta`$.
Include every coefficient read and every original mask restriction in these sums.
Write $`G_r^+=G_r-G_r^-`$ and $`q_r^+=q_r-q_r^-`$.
Squarefree primitive tags give, exactly,

```math
F_r(t)v=C_{0,r}v+tC_{1,r}v+t^2C_{2,r}v,
\qquad F_r(t)=G_{0,r}(t)Q^*q_{0,r}(t)\ell^{-1},
```

```math
\begin{aligned}
C_{0,r}&=G_r^-Q^*q_r^-\ell^{-1},\\
C_{1,r}&=(G_r^+Q^*q_r^-+G_r^-Q^*q_r^+)\ell^{-1},\\
C_{2,r}&=G_r^+Q^*q_r^+\ell^{-1}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
```

These formulas follow by multiplying the two affine sums.
Normal convergence permits the grouping and finite physical row contraction.
No switched inverse identity is used.

Grant the unswitched operator identification, as the assigned leaf does.
Put $`D_a=C_{a,Y}-C_{a,I}`$.
Then $`D_0+D_1+D_2=0`$, and the hand test at $`t=1/2`$ is

```math
[F_Y(1/2)-F_I(1/2)]v
 =\left(\frac34D_0+\frac14D_1\right)v.
\tag{2}
```

The coefficient test can also be written without derivatives:

```math
\begin{aligned}
C_{0,r}v&=F_r(0)v,\\
C_{1,r}v&=\frac{F_r(1)-F_r(-1)}2v,\\
C_{2,r}v&=\left[\frac{F_r(1)+F_r(-1)}2-F_r(0)\right]v.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
```

All three values lie in the stated switch disc.
They are formal evaluations at the frozen coefficient input, not automatically three reached stage configurations.

There is a useful exact cancellation in the old test's placement.
Suppose an actual $`v`$ is supported on rows in $`\Delta`$, and read an output bond in $`\Delta`$.
Every contributing $`q`$ carrier then meets $`\Delta`$ at its input.
Every contributing $`G`$ carrier meets it at its output.

The old nonzero $`t-t^2`$ discrepancy is absent.
This does not assert that such a row and cube are allowed at this entry.
An exempted cube cannot be used for this test.

**Outcome: inconclusive on the actual item-10 image.**

### Route 2: characterize the sums with cut cubes by an inverse equation

A primitive is multi-affine in its finitely many switches.
Its values on all binary masks determine its coefficients.
Indeed, for $`O(s)=\sum_A s^AO_A`$,

```math
O_A=\sum_{B\subset A}(-1)^{|A|-|B|}O(\mathbf1_B).
\tag{9}
```

Substitution proves (9).
If $`C\not\subset A`$, the coefficient of $`O_C`$ is zero.
Otherwise it is $`(1-1)^{|A\setminus C|}`$, hence one exactly when $`C=A`$.
This is a written finite identity, not an enumeration.
It suggests comparing each original primitive at every binary mask, then composing.
Equality of only the composite's binary values would be insufficient, since its degree can be two.

The attempted characterization was a common inverse on the retained region.
The source's exact positive parametrix exposes the extra term this needs.
Y 31975–32006 gives

```math
\mathcal M=\sum_x\omega_x\chi_x I_x\chi_x,
\qquad
\mathcal A\mathcal M=I+\mathcal T.
```

Keep only patches whose complete reads survive a binary cut.
Denote their index set by $`J`$.
The same multiplication gives

```math
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal M_J&=\sum_{x\in J}\omega_x\chi_x I_x\chi_x,\\
\mathcal A\mathcal M_J&=P_J+\mathcal T_J,\qquad
P_J=\sum_{x\in J}\omega_x\chi_x^2,\\
\mathcal T_J&=\sum_{x\in J}\omega_x[\mathcal A,\chi_x]I_x\chi_x.
\end{aligned}
\tag{10}
```

The full partition sums to one. The retained subfamily need not do so.
Even in the favorable case where mask restriction commutes with every union product,
and the restricted Neumann series converges, its sum satisfies

```math
\mathcal A\mathcal M_J(I+\mathcal T_J)^{-1}
 =I-(I-P_J)(I+\mathcal T_J)^{-1}.
\tag{11}
```

This follows by writing $`P_J+\mathcal T_J=(I+\mathcal T_J)-(I-P_J)`$.
It is not the identity operator unless the displayed defect vanishes.
In particular, $`P_J`$ is a sum of cutoff squares, not automatically the indicator of a new inner region.
With Y's additional combined carriers, even the favorable product restriction needs checking.

To use this route on the actual source, one must match these defects after all row injections and primitive compositions.
Neither paper states that the two masked sums solve that common boundary problem.
Renormalizing the surviving cutoff squares, changing the local boundary operator, or re-filing their errors would change the assignment.

**Route failure.** Binary-mask uniqueness cannot be applied without the defect comparison in (11).
The exact local inverse at switches one is already available; it does not remove this defect after a cut.
This failure rejects the proposed characterization, not I-INCIDENCE.
