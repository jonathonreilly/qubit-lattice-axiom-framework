## 3. One stopping lemma: I10-LIFT

```math
\widehat H_{0,N}(\sigma)
 =G_{0,N}(\sigma)Q^*q_{0,N}(\sigma)\ell^{-1}.
\tag{5}
```

The lemma asks for equality with the prescribed lift,

```math
\widehat H_{0,N}(\sigma;\zeta)v
 =H_{0,I}(\sigma;\zeta)v,
```

On a zero-current datum chart, (6) identifies the first datum derivative of the prescribed response.
If a stage uses only a restricted datum image, equality is required on that image and its actual directions.
A failure outside it does not refute the lemma.

This statement concerns just the lift.
Even a proof would leave the other slots of the seven-term equation and the bounds (4) to match.
The following three routes attempted to supply (6).

### Route 1: port the complete native response directly

The native inverse, coarse inverse and lift all exist on N's admitted class.
One can therefore first try its already constructed response, without rebuilding any words.
This replaces the left side of (6) by N's directly decorated $`H_{0,N}`$.
The replacement is invalid: (1)–(2) separate that lift from (5).
The obstruction is the multiplicity of one switch, not a norm or boundary loss.

This remains visible on positive-level rows whenever a complete averaging row fits inside one cut cube.
Let $`\widehat Q=\ell Q`$ and let $`e_cv`$ be raw datum on that row.
Every output bond read by $`\widehat Q(c,\cdot)`$ then lies in the same cube.
The same carrier argument gives

```math
[\widehat QH_{0,N}(t)e_cv](c)=tv,
\qquad
[\widehat Q\widehat H_{0,N}(t)e_cv](c)=t^2v.
\tag{7}
```

Here $`\widehat QH_0=I`$ is used only before switching.
This is a conditional row test, not a claim that this row occurs in an actual stage's source image.
It shows why treating every bare row as never-cut would not establish a general identity either.

A common change of the switch cannot preserve the primitive values and cure (7).
At a nonzero primitive entry incident to that cube, preservation requires the new switch to equal $`t`$.
The union-tagged lift would then still carry $`t`$, whereas the composite carries $`t^2`$.
Equality forces $`t\in\{0,1\}`$.
The prescribed complex disc is not restricted to those values.

**Failure:** the available N response theorem supplies the wrong decoration for this direct transfer.

### Route 2: use physical uniqueness and analytic continuation

### Route 3: rebuild only the composites, keeping the original tagging rule

```math

 =\sum_{\nu(A)+\nu(B)=m}G_A^NQ^*q_B^N\ell^{-1},
```
