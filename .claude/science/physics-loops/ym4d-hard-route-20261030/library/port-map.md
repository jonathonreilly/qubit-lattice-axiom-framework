## 0. Four facts that shape the map

**F1. The account's Theorem 0 is narrower than T0.** CA Theorem 0 (ii-ℝ⁴) gives limits along one joint torus subsequence, translation and hypercubic invariance, OS positivity and reconstruction of 𝓗^curv with H ≥ 0 and a unitary W₃ action. It does not assert "temperedness, field operators, Wightman functions; the spectrum condition … beyond H ≥ 0; uniqueness of Ω and clustering; a mass gap; O(4) invariance (that is clause (vi))" (CA-I 2706–2710). Clause (vi), O(4), is conditional on the far-sphere flux hypothesis (IR-fl), "derived nowhere in this book" (CA-I 3024–3030; CA-III 110830–110835, 111006–111012). Clause (iv) does not assert "that the limit fails to be Gaussian" (CA-I 2815). So T0's E1 (O(4)), E4 (clustering), the vacuum and cone, the Wightman theory, NT2, and the volume-first limit over **all** periodic Gibbs states are not in the account. Our frozen LG1, SR1, CL1, PH1, RO1, WR1 are the parts beyond it. Porting stops at the account's clauses (0), (i), (iii), (ii-𝕋-b), (ii-ℝ⁴), (iv).

**F2. The account's own lists, not the scan, give the status.** The scan's PROVED rows include statements the account calls open. Checked examples: 5.6:prop-polydisc-holomorphy (scan PROVED; CA-II 46167 "Proof outstanding."; listed CA-II 60283–60286); 4.21:prop-relative-machine (scan PROVED; CA-I 61325 "Proof outstanding."); 5.13:prop-sun-correlation (scan PROVED; listed outstanding CA-III 111200–111201); 9.1:lem-glued-minimisers (scan PROVED; listed incomplete CA-III 111454–111456); 4.22:lem-probe-summand and 4.22:prop-foreign-operation (scan PROVED; proved only from Definition 4.22:not-live-region-geometry "with its proof outstanding", CA-I 86238, 87671). Some open statements are not numbered statements at all: Definition 4.22:not-live-region-geometry (CA-I 85575), Remark 4.24:rem-covariance-decay (CA-I 105181), Remark 6.3:lem-last-scale-constants ("The statement of this lemma is not written out here", CA-II 63365–63367), Remark 5.11:lem-sixth-family (CA-II 59622–59624), the continuum kernel (CA-III 111247–111248). And "PROVED" in the scan often means "proved as an implication" from named hypotheses (for example CA-I 19578–19600 assumes three Balaban sentences "stated … without proof"). Table 3 is therefore built from CA-III 111167–111560 and the per-chapter lists, each entry checked at its source line.

**F3. Which clauses lie on T0's chain.** By the account's dependency list (CA-III 110790–110846): (0) and (i) are one induction; (iii) rests on them; (ii-𝕋-b) on (iii-a) and lattice reflection positivity; (ii-ℝ⁴) on (iii-b), reflection positivity and (0); (iv) on the correlation theorem, (0) and chapter 8. (ii-𝕋-a) rests on (v), and (vi) on (ii) and (v-e). T0 uses subsequential limits (T0 item 1) and the two-point law (T0 item 5). So clauses (v), (ii-𝕋-a) and (vi), and with them every leaf of CA chapters 9 and 10 and Lemma 7.3:lem-first-passage-offsets (used only for (ii-𝕋-a), CA-II 68350–68355), are **off T0's chain** (consistent with `tp1.md` L593).

**F4. Where the account assumes rather than proves.** It assumes Balaban's stage theorem, CMP 122a (1.60)–(1.70), as a hypothesis of Theorem 4.18:thm-preparatory-boundary (CA-I 54985–54998), and Remark 4.14:rem-supply-scope (CA-I 49495) places background inclusion and drop-out spaces outside its implication (lead's reading, `sa-index/ca-4.12-4.19/README.md`).
