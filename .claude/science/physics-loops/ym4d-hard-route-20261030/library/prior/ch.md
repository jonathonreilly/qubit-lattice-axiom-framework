## 1. Exact target and consumers

The account's scalar target is `5.6:prop-polydisc-holomorphy`, II:46131–46165. Fix \(\kappa_1\ge1\), a size bound \(b\), and the actual datum class \(\mathcal D_b\). Under \(\delta_1M\ge\kappa_1\), with \(M\) chosen after \(\kappa_1\), and one sufficiently small last coupling ceiling, the assertion is

\[
F_t(\sigma;\mathfrak d)=t(\mathsf R(\sigma;\mathfrak d)),\qquad
F_t(\cdot;\mathfrak d)\text{ holomorphic on }P_{\mathcal V(t)}(e^{\kappa_1}),\qquad
|F_t(\sigma;\mathfrak d)|\le\mathfrak b.

Here \(\mathfrak b\) is the supremum of the original term on its analytic domain. “Here holomorphic means continuous and separately holomorphic” (II:46148). The assertion is datum by datum: “no measurability, continuity or analyticity” in the datum is asserted (II:46155–46156). The proposition already asks only for the **open** polydisc. II:46157–46159 gives the closed-polydisc version as a sufficient condition. Thus `cwch.md:151`'s phrase “the proposition's stronger closed-domain formulation” must not be read as its actual requirement.

For each fixed datum there must be relatively open reached-tuple neighborhoods \(\Omega_i\), with compatible single-valued jointly holomorphic actual links, such that

\[

## 3. First open input, exactly

The first unresolved supply in the **recorded localized-link route** is CH4-Aux, not abstract composition, a Cauchy estimate or a new walk norm. Its exact existing statement is `ch-bridge.md:270–275` (also `open-registry.md:23`):

> **CH4-Aux (the first localized auxiliary-stage supply).** Under CH's admitted source-free inputs, without its excluded complex-membership hypotheses, there is one positive last coupling ceiling \(\gamma_4\) with the following property. For every admitted history, datum \(\mathfrak d\in\mathcal D_b\), and actual localization triple with all relevant running couplings at most \(\gamma_4\), the prescribed link-4 germ and every auxiliary minimizer/current readout \(e\in\mathscr T_3\) have compatible single-valued holomorphic continuations near every tuple reached for \(\sigma^{(4)}\in P_4\). The link uses exactly \(\mathbf B_k(Y_3)\) and the original primitive tags. Each auxiliary map uses its own prescribed determining system and the raw data induced by that link, retaining its branches and boundary conditions. With \(w_3=\mathcal E_4(\sigma^{(4)};\mathfrak d)\), they satisfy
> \[
> |\partial U_{p,X}(w_3)(x)-I|<r_{e,2}(x),\qquad
> |J_{p,X}(w_3)(x)|<r_{e,4}(x)
> \]
> at every required location, with the continuation domains and cube gauges required to define those same auxiliary maps. The ceiling depends only on the earlier constants and \(b\), and is independent of history, depth, age and volume. The branches must agree with the prescribed germs wherever initially defined; no replacement response or reduced current is allowed.

Here \(\mathscr T_3\) includes every auxiliary label \((p,X)\), evaluation location and full stencil tested at link 3's input. Each \(r_{e,a}\) is the **entire printed right-hand side**, including coefficient, \(\mathsf L_0\) power, coupling radius and units (`ch-bridge.md:258`). This is an input even at outer parameter zero. It is necessary for CH, not sufficient for CH: later links, the other stage clauses, final membership and uniform outer room still follow in order (`ch-bridge.md:277`; `cwch.md:163,372`). This does not assert that CH4-AUX is logically indispensable to every possible scalar proof of \(H_\circ\).
