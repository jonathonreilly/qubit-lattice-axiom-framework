# 2. (R-core) and the restored box

There are three related assertions in Y; keeping their domains separate gives the requested read.

1. **Extension test and elementary extension.** The main operation's item (2), Y 63836, asks whether exterior (V_k) has an extension into Λ\(^{pr}\) with curvature (<2\epsilon_k). If the exterior plaquettes obey |∂V\(_k\)-1| (<\epsilon), Y says an extension exists with |∂V\(_k\)-1| (<CM^2\epsilon); the stated construction gauges axially on ∂\(^+\)Λ\(^{pr}\) and sets the interior field to 1. The converse use is only a failure payment: some exterior plaquette has defect at least (cM^{-2}\epsilon_k). The quoted elementary construction is at Y 63836; no separate detailed proof passage is given there.
2. **Reference minimizer (V_\Lambda).** Y 63864 summarizes: “for exterior data with |∂V\(_k\)-1|<\epsilon one has |∂V_\Lambda-1|\le C(M)\epsilon.” The precise nearby statement is Proposition `minimiser in a box` at Y 71488–71489. Hypotheses: exterior curvature (<\epsilon), with (C(M)\epsilon\le a_\Lambda); in the application ε is at most a constant times ε\(_k\), a small-coupling ceiling. The continuation class requires curvature (<a_\Lambda) on level-(k) plaquettes whose corners all lie within distance 1 of Λ\(^{pr}\). On that class there is one critical gauge orbit of minima, analytic in the data, and its representative has defect ≤(C(M)\epsilon). Read spatially, the stated controlled plaquettes include ones inside the box, straddling its face, and outside but with corners within the stated distance-1 collar; Y does not extend this particular minimizer bound to arbitrarily distant exterior plaquettes. The exterior-data hypothesis itself is on plaquettes made up of exterior bonds. The next proof paragraph, Y 71491–71497, locates the argument as a contraction in an (M)-dependent ball, with an axial-gauge Hessian lower bound and a tree-gauge Poincaré inequality; it also identifies the cited Bałaban bound (cM^{-5}). Y 71560–71572 gives a more detailed minimizer formulation with exterior extension data and a bound on (S(P)).
3. **Fluctuation cutoff and island statement.** At Y 63849, the multi-scale fluctuation field (B) lives on bonds of the shells and has cutoff |(B)| (<\delta'_k/g_k). At Y 63856, (V'_k=V_kV_\Lambda^{-1}) is the reference fluctuation and χ(Λ\(^{pr}\)) imposes |\(\tfrac1i\log V'(b)\)| (<M_0\epsilon_k) on box bonds; (M_0) is chosen large after (M). At an island Y 76681–76685 says the reference has the exterior bounds of the passed extendibility test and surrounding density cubes, and on the restored box the four links are compared with the reference under the retained cutoff to get (C(M)\epsilon_i). Y 76686–76687 adds: “the newly made layer uses its own regularity guard.” It does not say there that every tested plaquette with an edge in the box is bounded by the same reference estimate.

- **Frame change.** Y 77682–77687 says frame changes act at block endpoints and preserve plaquette defect. - **Tree gauge.** Y 63849 places the multi-shell tree T_0 in fluctuation bonds from levels k_flat through k; axial contours run around nested boxes. Each shell tree uses the gauge freedom at each shell site except one, and extra bonds crossing inner boundaries and the outer box join the shell trees into one tree. Y 76698–76699 says the reverse tree gauge preserves the plaquette bounds.
- **What “conjugates the holonomy” is about.** Y 76675–76689 is explicitly about carrying an already obtained holonomy bound through later moves of the actual determining datum. Gauge draw/frame change/tree gauge conjugate that holonomy; a late refresh retains the old datum in the inner slot while the fresh field is used by outgoing rows; a reference-law draw changes the datum and is handled by its own reference/cutoff bounds. The conjugation sentence is a statement about the holonomy values and their bound, not a complete list of all sites or row variables transformed by each move.

# 4. (R-ord): output tests, cells, and loop

At the output of step (t), Y 62050(c) explicitly retains

> “\(\chi_{t,\mathcal S}=\prod_{\square\subset\Omega_t}\chi_t(\square)\).”

Thus the tests carried outside the integral operations are for every test cube contained in Ω\(_t\), rather than every cube on the torus. Y 62078 says the step cube is also a level-(t) density cube, with the same background and threshold; old tests inside Ω\(_t^c\) pass into the integral operation. Y 62001 gives cube size and says it lies well inside a cell. Neither this cited statement nor Y 62050(c) says the test cubes partition each level-(t) cell; that part of (R-ord) is not located in the cited Y passages.

For the loop read, Y 65419–65424 says the datum uses determining links on rows and, on a completed bond, the average of the lower field over its full double block; retained averaging constraints make those values agree with the fields in the tests. Y 76654–76660 likewise says to use the full lower blocks for each completed bond before bounding the datum plaquette holonomy. The density test itself is defined at Y 1150–1152. Y states the ordinary defect as order ε\(_q\) at Y 76667–76668; the specific (2.1\epsilon_l) constant in `cov-global.md` comes from its `cov-x` formulation, not those Y sentences.

# 5. (R-last): healing test and cubes

Y 62105 says a healed component has passed every test; Y 62106 says an island is precisely the case where only the last χ\(_t\) test fails, on the new field on (X) less a margin. For a quiet component that heals, Y 76692–76697 gives the coverage split: the **old top test enlarged by four cells** covers the outer six cells, because the complement of the top member is inset by two; the final positive test on the **six-cell inset** supplies the interior; together they give the top test on the new domain. Y 76700–76701 says the full original localized read is used even where it crosses the two test-place sets. Therefore the old test is carried on its former top-test cubes with the four-cell enlargement to cover the new outer band; the interior is supplied by the final positive test on the six-cell inset. The text does not identify the old test as being carried on every cube inside that inset.
