## Direct answer

Among the sources checked, no **published** theorem supplies the requested step at the target’s order-one Wilson coupling (the lane’s comparison window is bare Wilson `β≈2.3–2.6`) for four-dimensional continuous non-Abelian Yang–Mills: a periodic-box even thermal sum of excited states, or an order-one slab/half-space correlation or transfer gap that gives that sum. There are close results below the window, in finite groups, conditional criteria, and Higgs or scalar models. They do not close `GLUE-STEP` or row 895’s open seed.

The closest literal slab result is the 2025 preprint of Sky Cao, Ron Nissim and Scott Sheffield. It derives Wilson area law for `G=SU(N), U(N), SO(2N−2)` in the ’t Hooft-scaled model on `Z^d` / large periodic tori, when `β<β*_G`, with `β*_{SU(N)}=β*_{U(N)}=1/[8(d−1)]` and `β*_{SO(N)}=1/[16(d−1)]−1/[8N(d−1)]`. In `d=4`, the SU/U bound is `β<1/24`; SZZ’s preceding sufficient threshold is half of theirs. Their route checks a Dobrushin–Federbush condition: after conditioning the top/bottom boundaries of each height-one slab, the crossing links form a variable-environment nonlinear sigma model, and exponential correlation decay uniformly in those boundaries implies area law. This is a true non-Abelian group-sensitive slab supplier in its stated small-β regime, but it is an area-law input, not a periodic even thermal sum or a stated Yang–Mills transfer gap; the interval does not reach the lane’s `β≈2.3–2.6`. Its geometry is not just the single plaquette’s range: the Bakry–Émery curvature criterion is applied to the slab spin system, with group and dimension entering its constants (the SU/U threshold happens to be independent of `N` in this normalization). [Cao, Nissim and Sheffield, arXiv:2509.04688](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04688).
