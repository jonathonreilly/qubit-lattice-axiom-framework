## Convention and scope

Throughout, `βW` means the coefficient in the requested fundamental SU(2) Wilson weight

`dμ(U) ∝ exp[(βW/2) Σp Re Tr(U_p)] ∏e dUe`,

with `Tr` the ordinary two-dimensional trace. For SU(2), `Tr(U)` is real. A bound `βW < b` is strict where printed as strict; it says nothing about the endpoint. “Area law” below means exponential suppression in the lattice area of a Wilson loop, possibly with perimeter or constant prefactors. A mass gap or exponential decay of local correlations alone is not labelled an area law unless the cited theorem supplies the implication.

## Answer to the threshold question

Yes. Among the sources read, Durhuus–Fröhlich (1980) already states the 4D SU(2) strong-coupling area-law range `βW<2/3` as a “more refined estimate.” Its appendix explicitly derives only `βW<1/3` and does not show the refinement’s derivation. The later row 825 proof supplies a checked proof through the full strict range `0≤βW<2/3` (with its own stated setup and constants); it is not the first statement of that range in the literature. None of the sources read states the endpoint `βW=2/3` as included. This note is literature context only and does not confer audit, proof-retention, or other repository status.

RESULT: Durhuus–Fröhlich (1980) already states `βW<2/3` for 4D SU(2), but the accessible detailed derivation establishes only `βW<1/3`; row 825 provides a checked proof of the stated refined range.
