## Scope and answer

Target: four-dimensional Wilson SU(2), with `G_ev` the centre-even small thermal sums on cubes and the three lane seeds `Z_per` (electric-flux sum), `Z_full` (magnetic-flux sum), and `Z_kappa` (equal electric and magnetic flux sum), at one physical size uniformly in the cutoff; see `library/gap-skeleton-final.md` §§13.15–13.17. The question is whether a proved inequality bounds one of those sums in terms of a known quantity, and whether it yields a means of control at the correlation-length scale.

**Answer:** I found no proved inequality that directly bounds `Z_per`, `Z_full`, `Z_kappa`, or `G_ev` by a known small quantity for four-dimensional Wilson SU(2) at the order-one **GUIDE** effective coupling relevant to the lane. The closest rigorous tools are an exact Fourier/duality relation among flux sectors, reflection-positivity inequalities for individual twists and Wilson loops, a conditional size-dilation theorem for magnetic vortex free energies, and an exact blocking identity for positive weighted magnetic-twist sums with a small-coupling blocked denominator. Each either changes observable, needs the desired smallness as a premise, concerns a different flux sum, or leaves an uncontrolled coupling/free-energy term. None establishes the proposed seed or a vacuum transfer-operator mass gap.

The lane's `gap-seed-lit.md` and `gap-crux-lit-2.md` already contain substantive reviews of Kanazawa/TY and Tomboulis/Ito–Seiler. The entries below give the source-level statements most relevant to the present question and new citation-following result. Quantitative regime labels and any numerical values below are **GUIDE** as requested. Group symbols such as `SU(2)` and `Z_N`, and bibliographic identifiers, are conventional labels rather than numerical estimates. No simulation or computer-assisted calculation is used as proof.

| **Kanazawa, generalised Tomboulis–Yaffe inequality.** For a finite periodic hypercubic lattice, a centre-twisted partition function `Z^-` and the untwisted `Z` obey a reflection-positivity bound on a fundamental Wilson loop: in SU(2), `〈W(C)〉 ≤ GUIDE(2) { GUIDE(1/2)(1 − Z^-/Z) }^(A_C/(L_mu L_nu))`. The result extends to SU(N) and suitable one-plaquette actions. There is no small-beta hypothesis in this inequality. | **Other:** reflection positivity converts an individual magnetic-vortex free-energy ratio into a Wilson-loop bound. It does not provide the small ratio. | Closest to M1 only if one already knew exponentially small twist sensitivity. It bounds a loop observable, not a sum over electric flux, the full/kappa seed, centre-even cube sums, or the vacuum gap. The required twist decay at order-one **GUIDE** effective coupling is not supplied. | Full arXiv HTML theorem and hypotheses read. Kanazawa, arXiv:[0808.3442](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.3442), DOI [10.1016/j.aop.2009.04.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aop.2009.04.002). |
| **Kanazawa, weighted twist-sum blocking identity.** For `G=SU(N)` with positive character coefficients and any positive weights on a subgroup of the centre, `𝒵_Λ=Σ_g A^g Z^g_Λ` satisfies an exact identity relating `𝒵_Λ/Z_Λ` to a coarser-lattice ratio. The coarse numerator is at chosen small character couplings `α c'_r`; the denominator uses `ᾱ_Λ c'_r`, with `|α−ᾱ_Λ|=O(1/|Λ|)`, for sufficiently large volume. The proof uses the thermodynamic pressure and a strong-coupling expansion at the coarse coupling. This is an equality, not a monotonicity inequality. | **M2-like:** exact change of scale / variables, but no one-way flow or known bound on the resulting ratio. | It does cover arbitrary positive **magnetic twist** weights, potentially useful for `Z_full` only if the lane's definitions match those weights. It does not make the electric transform or equal-electric/magnetic `Z_kappa`; the denominator discrepancy and the nonperturbative prefactor remain unknown at the target coupling. Small-coupling control on the coarse lattice does not evaluate the original ratio. | Full arXiv HTML, including theorem and proof, read. Kanazawa, arXiv:[0805.2742](https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.2742), DOI [10.1016/j.physletb.2008.11.017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physletb.2008.11.017). |
| **Mack et al., magnetic-flux free-energy inequalities.** The paper proves inequalities between magnetic-flux free energies on tori with different diameters. Its abstract states a conditional implication: if magnetic-flux free energy vanishes under large uniform dilation, it decreases exponentially in cross-section area, a sufficient condition for permanent static-quark confinement; it also reports a linear-potential conclusion for three-dimensional U(N) at sufficiently large separation. The abstract does not specify the complete hypotheses or coupling range. | **Other:** size comparison / conditional free-energy decay; not M1–M4 as posed. | This is the most direct size-monotonicity lead, but it is conditional on the large-dilation behavior and concerns magnetic flux free energy and static Wilson confinement. It does not bound the finite-size electric, full, or kappa sums, `G_ev`, or a vacuum correlation gap in four-dimensional SU(2). | Abstract only; exact assumptions and inequalities not independently checked. Mack et al., “Inequalities for magnetic-flux free energies and confinement in lattice gauge theories,” DOI [10.1016/0550-3213(82)90470-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(82)90470-9). |
| **’t Hooft electric/magnetic flux duality.** For pure SU(N) gauge theory in a periodic box, electric and magnetic flux sectors are related by an exact duality equation following from Euclidean invariance; the paper discusses their distinct confinement properties as temperature varies. This is an exact relation, not an ordering or upper bound by a known small sector sum. | **M2-like / exact duality:** Fourier/rotation relation between sectors. | It can trade information between electric and magnetic sectors under the symmetry and geometry hypotheses; absent an independent sign/order/size estimate it cannot bound their sum. Equality of electric and magnetic flux at a symmetric geometry does not imply smallness. No mass-gap conclusion for the lane follows. | Abstract only. G. ’t Hooft, “A property of electric and magnetic flux in non-Abelian gauge theories,” DOI [10.1016/0550-3213(79)90595-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(79)90595-9). |
| **Exact flux Fourier transform and modern SU(2) measurement.** The ’t Hooft partition function is the finite `Z_N` Fourier transform of magnetic-twist partition functions over temporal twists. At equal-cycle `T^4`, Euclidean rotation gives an exact duality equation. Morikawa–Suzuki implement this numerically for SU(2), zero theta, and report light non-electric fluxes in the ordinary confining regime. | **Other:** exact character/Fourier transform; numerical sector measurement is GUIDE only. | The transform identifies which combinations are electric sectors; it is not an inequality controlling them. The paper's SU(2) phase evidence is numerical and at selected finite parameters, so cannot discharge a seed or proof obligation. It does not establish a cutoff-uniform estimate. | Full paper HTML read for definitions, duality, and numerical-method distinction. Morikawa & Suzuki, arXiv:[2501.07042](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.07042), DOI [10.1093/ptep/ptaf072](https://doi.org/10.1093/ptep/ptaf072). |

Two consecutive forward-citation rounds therefore added no relevant rigorous seed-sum result; search stopped.

## RESULT

No proved bound was found that controls `G_ev`, `Z_per`, `Z_full`, or `Z_kappa` by known data for four-dimensional Wilson SU(2) at the target order-one **GUIDE** effective coupling; the nearest exact statements concern individual magnetic twists, conditional magnetic-flux size decay, Fourier duality, or Wilson-loop confinement and do not establish the lane's seed or mass gap.
