## Target statement and what would count

For the product of unit round 3-spheres (one factor per link), let

\[
 d\mu_\beta=Z_\beta^{-1}e^{-S_\beta}d\mathrm{vol},\qquad
 S_\beta=\beta\sum_p(1-\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}U_p),
\]

and let `f` be smooth and gauge invariant. Route B asks, uniformly in the periodic volume for every `β` in a fixed compact order-one Wilson interval, for constants `θ<1`, `κ>0` such that

\[
 \int \operatorname{Hess}S_\beta(\nabla f,\nabla f)\,d\mu_\beta
 \ge -\theta\int\!\|\operatorname{Hess}f\|^2d\mu_\beta
       -(2-\kappa)\int\!|\nabla f|^2d\mu_\beta.\tag{G2}
\]

The product Ricci tensor is `2g`. The weighted Bochner identity therefore turns (G2) into

\[
 \int(L_\beta f)^2d\mu_\beta\ge(1-\theta)\int\!\|\operatorname{Hess}f\|^2d\mu_\beta
                       +\kappa\int\!|\nabla f|^2d\mu_\beta,
 \quad L_\beta=-\Delta+\nabla S_\beta\cdot\nabla.
\]

On the mean-zero gauge-invariant sector this gives a Langevin/Poincaré spectral gap at least `κ`. It is not itself a transfer-matrix gap or a twist estimate; the additional implication is the end bridge in `gap-ac-lat.md` / `gap-tu-retry.md` and must retain its stated hypotheses. At `β=0`, the left side of (G2) is zero, so (G2) holds trivially for any `0<κ≤2` and `θ≥0`. The interesting question is uniform extension to the order-one interval, not existence at the product Haar point.

## Assessment for row 812

1. The literature does give a clean proof of the needed Poincaré inequality at strong coupling: (G2) follows with no Hessian-square reserve from the SZZ pointwise Bakry–Émery condition after normalizing β and the metric. It therefore supplies a valid base interval, not the compact order-one interval asked by Route B.
2. Integrated/multiscale curvature ideas exist in scalar and discrete spin settings (Polchinski flow, susceptibility criteria, Helffer–Sjöstrand resolvents). Each requires a special flow or correlation/coercivity hypothesis that has no established counterpart for gauge-invariant functions on `SU(2)^E` under the Wilson law.
3. No result found proves an averaged inequality that pays negative Wilson-action Hessian against the Hessian-square term with `θ<1`, `κ>0`, uniformly in volume at order-one Wilson β. No gauge-theory application of the positive Ricci curvature of SU(2)/S³ beyond the strong-coupling range was found.
4. Thus the literature search supplies context and the subcritical base, but does not close (G2) at order one or the (G2)-to-twist bridge. Row 812 still needs its stated hand proof or a hand-checkable counterexample.
