## Target in the outline

The indexed outline identifies row 941 as the one-box target: the SU(2) Wilson vacuum-slice law `ν` on `B=(24a)^3`, at `β=2.3–2.6`, with `α(a−2) ≥ 16 + 3 log a`. The requested alternatives are `(LSI-vac)_α` or `(HC-vac)_α`.

Row 931 Step 6 defines `(LSI-vac)_α` as

`Ent_ν(h) ≤ E_ν[|grad h|²/h]/(2α)`

for smooth gauge-invariant densities `h`. It pairs this with `(SG-step)_σ`, a sub-Gaussian bound on the one-step fiber force under conditional laws. Theorem E is an entropy-contraction consequence of those two premises. The open Step 6 implication `(E-to-HC)` asks whether these premises yield `(HC-vac)_α'`. The outline itself flags the obstruction: entropy contraction does not control chi-square/Rényi-2 divergence, the Markov-kernel Dirichlet form need not be the gradient form in the LSI, and the exponent must move with time. It recalls that Gross’s continuous-time theorem gives a proportional rate in a Hamiltonian limit, and that strong data processing alone gives hypercontractivity only in a large-exponent limit (Ahlswede–Gács 1976).

The source-side outline assumptions do not already include the desired inequality. The SA index marks the positive-energy self-adjoint semigroup in Theorem 0(b)(v) as assumed; AK1–AK3 and PV3 likewise take reconstruction/semigroup inputs. The indexed PV conclusion identifies `ker H` with `L²(X,λ)` conditionally and explicitly says that this identification yields no strictly positive lower spectral bound on `(ker H)⊥`. None states LSI or hypercontractivity of `ν`.

## Disposition

Among the sources read, no primary result proves `(LSI-vac)_α` or `(HC-vac)_α` for the SU(2) Wilson vacuum slice on the specified one-box `B=(24a)^3` at `β=2.3–2.6`, with `α(a−2)≥16+3 log a`. No source proves row 931’s `(E-to-HC)` from its specific `(LSI-vac)` and `(SG-step)` hypotheses for the continuous compact-link kernel. Gross’s theorem addresses the matching continuous-semigroup route only after one has the matching generator and LSI; finite-state discrete-time results do not directly cover the target state space or its two different Dirichlet forms. The 2D all-coupling SU(N) LSI is an encouraging structural analogue, but its dimension, measure, and theorem-to-transfer connection differ.

What remains uncovered is therefore specific: an LSI or hypercontractive estimate for the *ground-state vacuum-slice law* with controlled dependence on this box and coupling, or a valid continuous/discrete theorem that converts the row 931 entropy contraction to the required moving-exponent `L²→Lq` bound for this kernel. Search was bounded to the sources and refs listed above; “not found” here is not a literature-wide nonexistence assertion.
