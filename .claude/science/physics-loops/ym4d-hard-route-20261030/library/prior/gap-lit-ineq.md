## Scope and exact crux (repo source)

The task asks whether an inequality monotone in a usable parameter exists for the four-dimensional Wilson SU(2) lattice gauge theory, including after a centre split, and whether it can yield a correlation-length-scale dichotomy. In §13.16 the relevant alternatives are a small centre-even thermal sum on every cube (`G_ev`) or one of three seeds (periodic/electric, full/magnetic, and kappa/equal electric-magnetic twist sum) at one physical size, uniformly in cutoff. A seed would need to propagate to `G_ev`; the `G_ev`-only step is explicitly open there. Section 13.17 calls a parameter correlation inequality a possible M4 route, alongside M1–M3 or a stated fifth mechanism. I use those labels only as the repo's organizing vocabulary, not as scientific status.

The local source record already has useful, adjacent theorems. Sussman and Alpoge's outline defines the four-dimensional Wilson SU(2) DLR model and proves link-reflection positivity for bounded half-space observables; it then proves a finite-torus chessboard estimate by reflection Cauchy–Schwarz (outline lines 2094–2117, 2834–2942; fetched outline `/tmp/gap-lit-ineq-20261111/sa_ym4.tex`). Credit for that account and outline: Ethan Sussman and Levent Alpoge, with Claude and Codex as authors of their text. This supplies reflection positivity/chessboard input, but the displayed estimate is not parameter-monotone and is not itself a `G_ev` or seed dichotomy. I searched the supplied CA-I index for items 4.12–4.19 for correlation/monotonicity claims; it exposed no target inequality beyond the already-indexed account. Both user-requested sources were fetched once: the outline (32,992 GUIDE lines) and CA-I (166,711 GUIDE lines); neither whole file was read.

## Direct answer

I found no proved correlation inequality for four-dimensional Wilson SU(2), nor for a centre-projected/split SU(2) measure, that is monotone in a parameter and supports a sharpness argument for the vacuum gap or the `G_ev`/three-seed criteria. There are rigorous SU(2) reflection-positivity and twist-comparison inequalities, and there is a sharpness theorem for a finite-group Ising/Potts lattice-gauge model. Their observables and representations do not supply the needed dichotomy. Ginibre/GKS monotonicity is rigorous for appropriate abelian gauge cones and spin systems, but no extension to the non-Abelian Wilson SU(2) law was located. Abdesselam's recent talk frames the non-Abelian extension as a conjecture, not a theorem.

## Results, closest to the crux first

### SU(2) reflection positivity, chessboard, and twist inequalities

**Sussman–Alpoge outline (the local route).** Model: four-dimensional periodic Wilson SU(2) DLR states at GUIDE `β>0`. The outline proves link-reflection invariance/positivity and a finite-torus chessboard inequality for positive bounded slab insertions, from a positive one-bond convolution kernel and reflection Cauchy–Schwarz. Means: reflection positivity plus chessboard estimate (an M4-like tool only if a separate parameter criterion is found). Application: this is the exact model and dimension, and it supports Wilson-loop/twist comparisons; as stated it gives no monotonicity in β and no criterion for the centre-even thermal sum or any seed. Read status: full for the cited outline ranges, guided by `library/sa-index/chunk-02.md` and `chunk-03.md`; CA-I §§4.12–4.19 index searched, not full-read. Source: Sussman–Alpoge, `YM4_sketch_500.tex`, commit `31a47c543775` (private GitHub raw source; fetched to `/tmp` as requested). The local account should be credited as above; it is not an independent literature result.

**Fröhlich–Israel–Lieb–Simon (FILS).** The general reflection-positivity framework systematizes infrared bounds and chessboard estimates for reflection-positive statistical-mechanics models; the chessboard estimate bounds joint probabilities of reflected local events by the corresponding replicated expectations. These tools prove phase transitions in model-specific settings, but FILS is a method framework, not a monotonicity theorem for Wilson SU(2). Model/range: the paper states general abstract hypotheses and illustrates them on long-range lattice models; it has no four-dimensional SU(2) Wilson-coupling range. Means: reflection positivity/chessboard, requiring a separate observable and parameter criterion for M4. Applicability: the local outline supplies the needed RP/chessboard property for the target Wilson law, but neither FILS nor that property alone provides the `G_ev`/seed dichotomy. Read status: full open preprint read at theorem/method level. Source: Fröhlich–Israel–Lieb–Simon, “Phase Transitions and Reflection Positivity. I. General Theory and Long Range Lattice Models,” [DOI 10.1007/BF01940327](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01940327); [open IHES copy](https://repo-archives.ihes.fr/FONDS_IHES/I_Prepublications/FROHLICH/1978-1980/P_78_224/P_78_224_web.pdf).

**Tomboulis–Yaffe and Kanazawa.** For finite periodic pure SU(N) Wilson lattice gauge theory at positive Wilson coupling, reflection positivity bounds the absolute expectation of a Wilson loop in nonzero N-ality by a power of a center-twist/center-vortex free-energy ratio. The SU(2) case is the original theorem; Kanazawa extends it to SU(N). When the twist free-energy ratio is sufficiently small with transverse area, this yields Wilson-loop area decay (confinement/string tension). Kanazawa's separate Theorem 5 is for reflection-positive classical spin systems with a global symmetry group G and a decomposable, G-invariant two-point observable Γμ(x;n), with GUIDE `Lμ=2^k n`: it bounds `|<Γμ(n)>|/<Γμ(0)>` by a prefactor times `[1−∫G dg <O(g)[V]>]^(n/Lμ)`. Thus if the twist-wall expectation in brackets is sufficiently small at scale Lμ, the two-point function decays exponentially and gives a nonzero mass gap. Means: exact twist comparison plus reflection positivity (a finite-size M4-style dichotomy in the spin theorem). Applicability: the gauge theorem is directly SU(2), but concerns nonzero N-ality Wilson loops and center twists, not the vacuum-sector gap; the spin theorem's global symmetry and two-point observable are not the local gauge theory's centre-even thermal trace. No implication to `G_ev` or the three seed sums at order-one effective coupling follows. Read status: Kanazawa full arXiv text, including Theorems 2 and 5; original TY paper only through Kanazawa's statement and citations. Source: [Kanazawa, arXiv:0808.3442](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.3442), [DOI 10.1016/j.aop.2009.04.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aop.2009.04.002); original Tomboulis–Yaffe, *Finite temperature SU(2) lattice gauge theory*, Commun. Math. Phys. 100 (1985), 313–341, [DOI 10.1007/BF01206134](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01206134).

**Mack–Petkova center comparison.** For pure SU(2) lattice gauge theory, the Wilson-loop expectation is bounded by a corresponding Z2 gauge-theory Wilson-loop expectation with a rescaled coupling (normalization as stated in the cited comparison); consequently SU(2) confinement follows whenever the comparison Z2 theory confines. A related theorem in two-dimensional lattice gauge-Higgs models gives area decay for fractional static charges for compact G with a nontrivial center and matter neutral under that center, at arbitrary real-action couplings. Means: comparison inequality, not a monotone sharpness parameter. Applicability: this is a direct SU(2)-to-centre comparison for string observables; the Z2 model's loop transition and the observable mismatch prevent it from proving a uniform SU(2) gap or any of the three thermal seeds. Read status: Mack–Petkova publisher abstract only (publisher page refused full access); the related two-dimensional result was read from the open DESY report. Source: [Mack–Petkova, DOI 10.1016/0003-4916(79)90346-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-4916(79)90346-4); [Aizenman–Fröhlich, DESY report](https://www-library.desy.de/preparch/desy/scan/1978/desy78-040.kek.pdf).

**Fröhlich–Tomboulis–Ukawa correlation inequalities.** The paper derives Wilson-loop inequalities for Z2 and U(N) lattice gauge theories and applies them to area decay above a specified temperature/coupling regime; its discussion of SU(2) is a possible application, not a proved SU(2) monotonicity theorem. Means: correlation inequalities in the abelian/discrete or U(N) settings. Applicability: U(N) does not mean SU(N), and these loop estimates do not concern the SU(2) vacuum trace or the centre-even/seed sums. Read status: publisher abstract only; the accessible publisher PDF route refused full access. Source: Fröhlich–Tomboulis–Ukawa, “Correlation inequalities and quark confinement in lattice gauge theories,” [DOI 10.1016/0550-3213(81)90421-1](https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(81)90421-1).

**Duncan–Schweinhart sharp deconfinement.** For q-state Potts hyperlattice gauge theory in codimension two on Z^d, a plaquette random-cluster representation transfers sharpness of the dual Potts transition to the Wilson-loop deconfinement transition. The result is unconditional for Ising lattice gauge theory; for q>2 it uses a slab-regularity conjecture. Means: random-cluster representation, duality, and sharpness (M4). Applicability: the Ising gauge model is the Z2 centre control, not continuous SU(2); random-cluster positivity/sharpness is not established for the Wilson SU(2) plaquette measure, and the theorem controls loop asymptotics rather than the vacuum gap or flux-summed seeds. Read status: full open author PDF for theorem statements and proof structure, with abstract cross-checked against arXiv. Source: [Duncan–Schweinhart, arXiv:2308.07534](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.07534), [DOI 10.1007/S00220-025-05338-X](https://doi.org/10.1007/S00220-025-05338-X).

### Inequalities that really are monotone, but outside the target law

**Ginibre/GKS in finite abelian gauge theory.** For finite Abelian gauge group Z_n with Wilson action on Z^4, the loop observables in the relevant positive-definite cone satisfy Ginibre's correlation inequality; it gives nonnegative correlations and monotonicity of cone observables as plaquette couplings increase. In Z2 all Wilson loops are real and both Griffiths inequalities apply. For U(1), the first inequality applies to the real cone observables, but complex Wilson loops need not lie in that cone; the second inequality for Wilson loops can fail and even reverse in two-dimensional U(1). Means: positive-definite-character cone/Ginibre; an M4-type monotone parameter exists for those observables. Applicability: no SU(2) transfer is known; the decisive cone/product closure uses Abelian characters and loses validity for noncommuting holonomies. The Z2 instance remains only a finite-centre comparison/control. Read status: full relevant sections of Li–Zhou; Ginibre original theorem not independently read. Source: [Li–Zhou, arXiv:2404.17071](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.17071), [DOI 10.1007/JHEP08(2024)154](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP08(2024)154); Ginibre, [DOI 10.1007/BF01646537](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01646537), cited as Commun. Math. Phys. 16 (1970), 310–328.

**Villain U(1) extension.** For the compact U(1) Villain lattice gauge model, Wilson-loop expectations are monotone in the plaquette couplings by Ginibre's inequality; the paper also proves a limiting relation from suitable Wilson/Manton actions to Villain interactions for compact connected groups. The latter is a convergence result, not a non-Abelian Ginibre inequality. Means: Abelian Fourier-positive kernel. Applicability: the U(1) monotonicity does not transfer to non-Abelian Wilson SU(2); the authors explicitly point to noncommutativity and the lack of a spanning-surface product formula for non-Abelian Wilson loops. No route to `G_ev` or the three seeds is provided. Read status: full relevant statements and discussion. Source: [Chevyrev–Garban, arXiv:2404.09928](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.09928), [DOI 10.1007/s10955-025-03420-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10955-025-03420-1).

**Non-Abelian GKS proposal.** Abdesselam's talk proposes inequalities for invariant observables in non-Abelian spin and lattice-gauge systems, expressed under the non-interacting measure and intended to imply finite-coupling GKS analogues; the large-power test case is said to be provable. The abstract labels the general inequalities a conjecture. Means: if proved in the required Wilson SU(2) observable cone, this is the most direct possible M4 candidate. Applicability: currently none as a theorem for Wilson SU(2), its centre split, `G_ev`, or the seeds; no sharpness dichotomy is established. Read status: talk abstract and recording description only; no slides/paper found in the searched open indexes. Source: [HIM 2025 talk page](https://www.mathematics.uni-bonn.de/him/video-recordings/tp_2025_02_ws_3/youtube/malek-abdessalam-on-correlation-inequalities-for-non-abelian-spin-systems-and-lattice-gauge-theories). Abdesselam's [arXiv:2207.07603](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.07603) proves related stable-polynomial inequalities for O(N)/CP^(N−1) invariant observables in a zero-coupling setting, not SU(2) lattice gauge theory (abstract read only).

### Sharpness and comparisons for other spin or gauge models

**Duminil-Copin–Tassion; Duminil-Copin–Raoufi–Tassion; Aizenman–Barsky–Fernández.** Sharpness theorems for Bernoulli percolation, ferromagnetic Ising, and FK random-cluster/Potts models give exponential subcritical decay and the corresponding supercritical order-parameter conclusion, under their respective finite-range/transitivity and positivity assumptions. ABF's differential inequalities cover Ising-type one-component ferromagnetic spin systems (including φ⁴ examples) and Bernoulli percolation, not non-Abelian gauge measures. Means: FKG/positive association, pivotality/decision-tree or differential inequalities (M4). Applicability: these arguments need a monotone product/FK/random-current representation; no such representation with the required positive association is known for continuous Wilson SU(2). Centre projection couples the remaining variables and does not supply the required FK law. No `G_ev`/seed dichotomy follows. Read status: DCT and DCRT primary arXiv papers read in relevant theorem statements; ABF publisher abstracts/metadata only. Sources: [DCT, arXiv:1502.03050](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03050); [DCRT, arXiv:1705.03104](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.03104); [Aizenman–Barsky, DOI 10.1007/BF01212322](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01212322); [Aizenman–Barsky–Fernández, DOI 10.1007/BF01007515](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01007515).

**Aizenman–Simon; Messager–Miracle-Solé–Pfister; Lees–Taggi.** Aizenman–Simon compare the plane-rotor (XY) and Ising ferromagnets with the same pair couplings, obtaining an ordering of their critical inverse temperatures and a lower bound on the XY transition. Messager–Miracle-Solé–Pfister prove correlation inequalities and uniqueness results for the plane-rotator ferromagnet; related Messager–Miracle-Solé inequalities compare two-point functions across reflection planes in reflection-positive ferromagnets. Lees–Taggi and later reflection-positive-measure work prove site-monotonicity/positivity results for O(N) loop or spin correlations and quantum Heisenberg/XY examples. These are spin/loop representation results; they are not inequalities for gauge-link plaquettes. Means: comparison/reflection positivity (some M4 ingredients, but not a coupling dichotomy here). Applicability: no direct adaptation to four-dimensional Wilson SU(2) centre sectors is supplied; the needed variables, order structure, and observables differ. Read status: Aizenman–Simon open scan read; Lees–Taggi arXiv abstracts read; Messager et al. citation/abstract-level only. Sources: [Aizenman–Simon, DOI 10.1016/0375-9601(80)90493-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/0375-9601(80)90493-4); [Messager–Miracle-Solé–Pfister, bibliographic record](https://eurekamag.com/research/082/730/082730372.php); [Lees–Taggi, arXiv:2002.12666](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.12666).

**Simon–Yaffe perimeter bound.** In an arbitrary lattice gauge theory satisfying the paper's setup, the Wilson-loop expectation has an exponential upper bound in loop perimeter. The bound does not imply an area law or exponential decay of vacuum-sector correlations. Means: reflection positivity/transfer positivity estimate; no varying sharpness parameter. Applicability: valid as a general gauge bound, including non-Abelian settings where hypotheses hold, but it is the wrong direction/scale for the SU(2) gap criteria. Read status: full short open paper. Source: Simon–Yaffe, [DOI 10.1016/0370-2693(82)90815-2](https://doi.org/10.1016/0370-2693(82)90815-2).

**Garban–Sepúlveda; Chatterjee; Basu–Ganguly.** Garban–Sepúlveda prove low-temperature (large GUIDE β) Wilson-loop bounds for four-dimensional U(1) Villain gauge theory, quantifying topological-defect effects; this is Abelian and a Wilson-loop/string result. Chatterjee proves an absolutely convergent string-trajectory expansion for strongly coupled SO(N) lattice gauge theory in the large-N limit, in any dimension; the expansion yields loop estimates and a gauge-string duality. Basu–Ganguly give a more geometric description of those SO(N) strong-coupling trajectories and an example showing that a Wilson-loop area-law lower bound does not hold in full generality in any dimension. Means: defect/spin-wave estimate for U(1), and exact controlled expansion/large-N parameter (M2-like) for SO(N). Applicability: none gives a uniform GUIDE order-one-coupling SU(2) result or an estimate for the centre-even thermal sum/seed; Basu–Ganguly is also a caution against assuming universal loop lower bounds. Read status: abstracts and theorem summaries only. Sources: [Garban–Sepúlveda, arXiv:2107.04021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.04021); [Chatterjee, arXiv:1502.07719](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.07719), [DOI 10.1007/s00220-019-03353-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-019-03353-3); [Basu–Ganguly, arXiv:1608.04379](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.04379), [DOI 10.1002/cpa.21788](https://doi.org/10.1002/cpa.21788).

**Shen–Zhu–Zhu log-Sobolev; Chandra–Chevyrev–Hairer–Shen stochastic quantization.** Shen–Zhu–Zhu prove at strong coupling, under explicit assumptions including GUIDE `|β| < 1/[16(d−1)]` for SU(N) and GUIDE `d>1`, uniqueness/ergodicity and Poincaré/log-Sobolev inequalities for lattice SU(N) Yang–Mills, with exponential decay of a class of correlations and a strictly positive mass gap in that regime. For GUIDE `d=4`, this is GUIDE `|β|<1/48`: a genuine four-dimensional SU(2) lattice gauge mass-gap result in a strong-coupling range. It does not provide the requested order-one effective-coupling route. The 2026 Piazza–Shen preprint proves a volume-uniform log-Sobolev inequality for two-dimensional SU(N) Wilson measure at each fixed GUIDE β. Chandra et al. construct stochastic quantization/gauge-orbit dynamics for Yang–Mills or Yang–Mills–Higgs in two and three continuum dimensions. Means: functional inequalities / Markov dynamics; the SZZ strong-coupling theorem supplies correlation decay only under its explicit hypotheses. Applicability: it does not establish the `G_ev`/seed criterion in the crux's coupling window, and the continuum Markov-process construction itself is not a correlation inequality or a mass-gap proof. Read status: SZZ and Piazza–Shen abstracts only; Chandra et al. abstract only. Sources: [Shen–Zhu–Zhu, arXiv:2204.12737](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.12737), [DOI 10.1007/s00220-022-04609-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-022-04609-1); [Piazza–Shen, arXiv:2610.35286](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35286); [Chandra et al., arXiv:2201.03487](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.03487), [DOI 10.1007/s00222-024-01264-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00222-024-01264-2).

## Forward-citation rounds and search limits

I searched arXiv, INSPIRE/open bibliographic indexes, Semantic-Scholar/OpenAlex-style indexes, publisher/open-access copies, and author pages using the named starting works and their cited-by records. Round one followed forward citations from the Mack–Petkova/Fröhlich–Tomboulis comparison line, TY/Kanazawa, Ginibre/abelian gauge inequalities, and the percolation/random-cluster sharpness line. It added the Abdesselam conjectural non-Abelian GKS program and the 2D SU(N) log-Sobolev preprint, but no proved SU(2) correlation inequality or target dichotomy. Two forward-citation rounds therefore added no relevant proved result beyond the finite-group/abelian/spin cases recorded above. Search is bounded by open-source access; Mack–Petkova and several older journal items were abstract/secondary-source only. Where an access refusal was encountered, I stopped at that copy and used available open metadata or an alternate open copy; no paywalled text was reconstructed.

**Unresolved limitations.** This is not an exhaustive proof that no such inequality exists. The named list was searched broadly, but older papers with inaccessible full texts and not-yet-indexed work may contain additional statements. No numerical or computer-assisted evidence was used. No paper text has been copied into the checkout beyond short theorem summaries.

RESULT: No proved parameter-monotone correlation inequality was found that applies to four-dimensional Wilson SU(2), its centre split, `G_ev`, or the periodic/full/kappa seeds and yields the needed sharpness dichotomy; the nearest rigorous SU(2) tools control twist sensitivity and Wilson-loop confinement, while Ginibre/GKS monotonicity and random-cluster sharpness remain restricted to abelian, discrete, or spin models.
