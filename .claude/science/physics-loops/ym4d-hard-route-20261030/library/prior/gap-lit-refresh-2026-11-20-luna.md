## New or updated relevant public results (2026-11-10 through 2026-11-19)

- **Deep Bhattacharjee, Ushashi Bhattacharya, James Montgomery Lockwood, Tristan Romanov, Ikailo John Sessford, and Sanjeevan Singha Roy, “The Yang–Mills Mass Gap from One Bound at One Scale.”** Submitted Nov 10, posted Nov 12, 2026; Preprints.org manuscript `202611.1282`, v1: [source](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202611.1282). The abstract claims a mass gap for four-dimensional lattice Yang–Mills at strong coupling for every compact simple group, and says a mass gap and unique vacuum for torus continuum theories follow from a one-scale partition-function bound. It explicitly leaves that bound open at weak coupling and says unrenormalized loop variables have a trivial limit. It therefore does not establish the claimed continuum mass gap along the weak-coupling/continuum tuning. The host identifies this version as not peer-reviewed. Quote: “That bound is open at weak coupling.”

- **Zeng Chunping, “From Four-Dimensional Wilson Lattice Gauge Theory to Quantum Yang–Mills Theory, with a Conditional Mass-Gap Theorem.”** Hexagon preprint `2611.00543v1`, version 1 dated Nov 17, 2026: [source](https://hexagonmath.org/2611.00543v1). It gives a human-readable account of the OpenAI/Yang–Mills-group construction and states a conditional mass-gap route. It proves a logical boundary that clustering alone need not imply a spectral gap, and states equivalence between a gap and a uniform exponential clustering rate under the paper's setup. Its proposed mass-gap route still requires new infrared input; the positive gap is not proved. This is a conditional result, not a solution of the mass-gap problem. Quote: “A positive mass gap is not proved.”

- **Kei-Ichi Kondo, “Confinement–Higgs Complementarity Viewed through Relative Quantum Reference Frames.”** arXiv:`2611.19519`, submitted Nov 14, 2026, `hep-th` and `hep-lat`: [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19519). The paper gives exact continuum and lattice identities interpreting scalar–color observables from gauge-fixing-free SU(2) gauge–scalar lattice simulations, discusses a candidate nonlocal separation line, and compares it with Fradkin–Shenker continuity and Osterwalder–Seiler analyticity. It is a confinement–Higgs/complementarity result for a gauge–scalar model; it does not claim a Yang–Mills mass gap or a quantitative exponential-clustering rate.

- **Zeng Chunping, “From Four-Dimensional Wilson Lattice Gauge Theory to Quantum Yang–Mills Theory: Construction with Proofs from Certified Assumptions, and the Record of Its Independent Validation.”** Hexagon preprint `2611.00492v1`, version 1 dated Nov 15, 2026: [source](https://hexagonmath.org/2611.00492v1). This exposition of the October existence construction explicitly records that a positive mass gap and uniqueness of the infinite-volume limit are not proved. It supplies no new unconditional gap or confinement theorem, but records a new reduction of the gap problem to one uniform rate and a counterexample to deriving the gap from the registered inputs alone. The source describes the scope of the construction, not a new mass-gap result. Quote: “A positive mass gap, uniqueness of the infinite-volume limit, uniqueness of the vacuum of the mixed theory, and order-by-order agreement of the OPE with perturbation theory are not proved.”
