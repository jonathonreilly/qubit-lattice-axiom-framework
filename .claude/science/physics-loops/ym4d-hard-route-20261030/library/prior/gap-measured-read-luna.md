## String tension, flux tubes, and Lüscher terms

- **MEASURED string tension:** the continuum dimensionless scale is fixed by the Wilson-loop or torelon energy in pure gauge simulations; e.g. Teper’s `r₀√σ` above. In Teper’s SU(2) data, Wilson β=2.0–2.85, `a√σ` falls from `0.69(5)` at β=2.0 to `0.0636(12)` at β=2.85; representative values are `0.2686(21)` at 2.4 and `0.1834(26)` at 2.5. The finite-volume winding-string extraction uses `a m_l=(a²σ)L−π/(3L)`. Teper estimates this correction contributes about 4% to σ and explicitly cautions that its assumed simple bosonic-string universality class had numerical evidence but was not yet convincing at that precision. Refereed review source; underlying lattice studies cited there.
- **MEASURED periodic-string correction (SU(2), 3+1D):** Lucini and Teper fit winding strings longer than 1 fm and obtain `c_L=0.98(4)` for the coefficient of the periodic-string `1/L` correction, versus the simple bosonic-string value `π/3≈1.047`. They say this is within 2σ of the bosonic value. The 3+1D simulations use Wilson action at β=2.55, `a√σ≈0.159`, L=8–32 (longest string about 2.3 fm); shorter lengths are excluded from acceptable fits. This is a closed-string coefficient, not the static-potential coefficient. Refereed PRD 64, 105019. [Lucini & Teper, “Confining strings in SU(N) gauge theories”](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.64.105019).
- **MEASURED static-potential term (SU(3), 4D):** Lüscher and Weisz’s multilevel measurement with the standard Wilson action at β=6.0 reports the force over `r≥0.5 fm` is linear in `1/r²`; the largest-distance four points agree with a straight line to ≤0.12% errors. Their slope-derived `c(r)` errors are ≤2.5%. They confirm the large-distance `γ/r` term with the bosonic-string coefficient `γ=−π/12` in `V(r)=σr+μ+γ/r+…`, with string behavior visible surprisingly early. No single fitted `γ` uncertainty is quoted in the summary used here, so the data precision should not be restated as a coefficient error. Refereed JHEP. [Lüscher & Weisz, JHEP 07 (2002) 049](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/0207003).
- **Status distinction:** `−π/(12r)` for an open static string in four dimensions and `−π/(3L)` for a closed periodic string follow from the same two transverse bosonic modes but differ by geometry. They are effective-string measurements/interpretations at long distances, not a mass-gap theorem.

Engels–Scheideler later measured the pseudo-specific-heat peak on 4⁴–16⁴ lattices: for L≥8, `β_co=2.23(2)`, peak `C_V=1.685(10)`; their conclusion was that it is not an ordinary phase transition. Refereed PRL / PLB. [Creutz, Phys. Rev. **MEASURED, mixed fundamental–adjoint action:** Bhanot and Creutz studied the four-dimensional SU(2) action over the `(β_F,β_A)` plane. The SO(3) and Z₂ first-order lines meet at a triple point approximately `(β_F,β_A)=(0.55±0.03,2.34±0.03)` and continue to a critical endpoint around `(1.57±0.05,0.78±0.05)`. The ordinary Wilson axis is `β_A=0`; its specific-heat peak is described as a “shadow” of this nearby critical endpoint, not as a first-order transition on that axis. Refereed PRD. [Bhanot & Creutz, Phys. Rev. D 24, 3212 (1981)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.24.3212). The coordinate estimates are reproduced in the accessible lattice-gauge text of Gattringer–Lang; they are the source’s approximate phase-diagram values, not a direct precision determination from the abstract.

## Center vortices, monopoles, and dual-superconductor claims

These are **CONTESTED mechanism evidence**. Their reported data are numerical and some statements are robust empirical correlations under specified gauges/projections. The identification of the selected objects as *the* gauge-invariant cause of confinement is not established by those measurements.

- **Center vortices — MEASURED, gauge-fixing/projection dependent:** Engelhardt, Langfeld, Reinhardt and Tennert use SU(2) Wilson-action configurations and center projection after center-gauge fixing. For sufficiently large β they test the dimension-two scaling law `ρa² ∝ exp[−(6π²/11)β]`, reporting that the planar piercing density scales. In physical units, with the scale set by `√κ=440 MeV`, they report `ρ≈2 vortices/fm²`; the measured pair correlation has attractive range `0.4±0.2 fm`. Their scaling window for the comparison is `2<β<2.8` in the authors’ convention `β=1/g²` (so this is not the standard SU(2) Wilson `β_W=4/g²`), with 10⁴ and 12⁴ lattices for the distribution comparison. The projected Wilson loop reproduces nearly the full string tension in the cited center-gauge literature. The authors distinguish a gauge-invariant thick-vortex description from the gauge-fixed projected P-vortices, and say the underlying thick-vortex interpretation is an assumption. Refereed PLB. [Engelhardt et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/9801030); see also [Greensite, “Some Current Approaches to the Confinement Problem” (2009)](https://www.actaphys.uj.edu.pl/fulltext?page=3355&series=Reg&vol=40).
- **Abelian monopoles / dual-superconductor scenario — MEASURED but projection-dependent:** Bornyakov–Müller-Preussker use pure SU(2) Wilson action, maximal Abelian gauge (MAG), and DeGrand–Toussaint monopole currents. Their runs at β=2.4, 2.5, 2.6 use 24⁴, 24⁴, 28⁴ lattices (a≈0.12, 0.085, 0.06 fm), 20 independent configurations per β, simulated annealing with 10 gauge-copy starts and comparison to a standard iterative gauge-fixing algorithm. In their MAG definition, the continuum abelian string-tension ratio is `σ_ab/σ=1.03(4)` and the properly defined IR-monopole density is `ρ_mon^IR=0.65(2) σ^(3/2)`, yielding mean separation `d=1.15(1)/√σ=0.52(1) fm`. They explicitly find total monopole density diverges toward the continuum due to UV loops; only the IR/cluster-selected density scales. Lattice 2001 conference proceedings / arXiv preprint. [Bornyakov & Müller-Preussker](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/0110209).
- **Earlier monopole density:** Bornyakov et al. (1991) report evidence of scaling for a MAG-projected SU(2) monopole density, `ρ=(66Λ_L)^3`, and suppression below a critical box size. This is a gauge-fixed density and is not a universal physical density absent the projection and infrared definition. Refereed PLB. [Bornyakov et al.](https://inspirehep.net/literature/303750).
- **Mechanism reading:** center dominance, Abelian dominance, monopole condensation and dual Meissner expulsion are interpretations of gauge-fixed/projection-dependent observables. The robust statements in these papers are correlations, scaling in chosen definitions, and projected-string-tension reproduction. The gauge-invariant confinement question remains the physical area law / center-symmetry structure, not the uniqueness of a particular projected defect mechanism. Greensite’s review treats vortices, monopoles, calorons and Gribov/Coulomb confinement as active approaches, not settled equivalences. [Greensite 2009 review](https://www.actaphys.uj.edu.pl/fulltext?page=3355&series=Reg&vol=40).

## Landau-gauge propagators and the Gribov region

All propagator observations below are **MEASURED but gauge-dependent**: they concern gauge-fixed gluon and Faddeev–Popov ghost two-point functions, not gauge-invariant glueball correlators.

- **SU(2), Cucchieri–Mendes:** large-volume Wilson-action simulations in Landau gauge derive bounds involving the smallest nonzero Faddeev–Popov eigenvalue and find the infinite-volume `D(0)` finite and nonzero in 4D; the ghost propagator is consistent with tree-level `1/p²` rather than infrared enhancement. The 2008 study uses the then-largest SU(2) lattices, identifies finite-volume effects as the main issue, and reports the conclusion for 3D and 4D. Refereed PRD. [Cucchieri & Mendes, “Constraints on the infrared behavior…”](https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.2371) and [“Infrared behavior and infinite-volume limit…”](https://arxiv.org/abs/0812.3261).
- **SU(2)/SU(3) comparison, Cucchieri et al.:** matched pure-gauge Landau-gauge lattice parameters gave similar gluon and ghost propagators down to roughly 1 GeV. This is a gauge-dependent comparison and does not imply gauge-invariant SU(2)/SU(3) equivalence. Refereed PRD. [Cucchieri, Mendes, Oliveira & Silva](https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.3367).
- **SU(3), Bogolubsky et al. (2009):** standard Wilson single-plaquette action at β=5.70 (`a≈0.17 fm`), volumes L⁴ with L=64,72,80,88,96; gluon propagators use up to 67 configurations on 96⁴ and ghost data L=64,80. Minimum momentum about 75 MeV. Landau gauge is fixed using simulated annealing (closer to global maxima than basic over-relaxation, but not a unique gauge copy). They observe an infrared gluon-propagator plateau / nonzero `D(0)` and finite ghost dressing `J(q²)=q²G(q²)`, consistent with decoupling-type functional solutions, not an enhanced-ghost scaling law. Refereed PLB. [Bogolubsky et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.0736).
- **Gribov region:** the lattice Landau condition has multiple local extrema (Gribov copies); a gauge-fixing algorithm picks among them. Cucchieri–Mendes and Bogolubsky et al. discuss sensitivity to smallest Faddeev–Popov eigenvalues, distance to the Gribov horizon, and copy selection. The propagator numbers therefore must be reported with gauge-fixing prescription and volume. Their results are evidence about Landau-gauge Green functions; they do not by themselves prove or disprove a gauge-invariant confinement mechanism. [Bogolubsky et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.0736); [Greensite, “Some Current Approaches to the Confinement Problem”](https://www.actaphys.uj.edu.pl/fulltext?page=3355&series=Reg&vol=40).

## Large-N behavior

**MEASURED:** Lucini–Teper–Wenger’s improved-operator Wilson-action calculations for N=2–8 find low-lying glueball mass ratios compatible with earlier work and fit `1/N²` corrections. Their 2004 continuum extrapolation gives `m(0++)/√σ=3.307(53)` at N=∞. The later Athenodorou–Teper Wilson-action study N=2–12 finds `3.072(14)` and `m(2++)/√σ=4.599(14)` at N=∞. The modern analysis has different ensembles/operator basis and substantially smaller errors; it supports a smooth large-N limit, not a proof of confinement or a continuum QFT. **MEASURED:** Lucini–Teper–Wenger’s deconfinement fit is `T_c/√σ=0.596(4)+0.453(30)/N²` for N=2–8; a later fit in another analysis reports `0.5949(17)` at infinity. These are finite-temperature lattice extrapolations with Wilson action. [LTW 2004](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/0404008), [Athenodorou–Teper 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00364), [LTW deconfinement](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-lat/0307017).

RESULT: The measured record supports a finite, nonzero lowest gauge-invariant glueball mass in units of `√σ` in continuum-extrapolated pure SU(2) and SU(3) lattice studies, alongside a finite confining string tension and `T_c/√σ≈0.71` (SU(2)) / `≈0.63–0.65` (SU(3)); the specific mechanism is not settled by the data, and projected vortex/monopole and Landau-gauge propagator evidence is gauge-dependent or disputed.
