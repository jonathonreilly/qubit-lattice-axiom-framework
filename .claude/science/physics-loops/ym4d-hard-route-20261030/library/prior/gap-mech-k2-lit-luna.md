### What is and is not on record

The conventional identity is elementary and established: for two copies, the expectation of the swap on region A is Tr(ρ_A²); in a path integral, the swap glues the two replicas cyclically across A. Gauge theories require an entanglement prescription because the physical Hilbert space does not factor by spatial region; Donnelly’s edge-sector construction and the lattice-gauge replica papers address that issue. Those results establish a replica twist / branched geometry.

The proposed stronger statement is different. Row 1529 embeds two SU(2) copies into H=Sp(1)×Sp(1)⊂Sp(2), uses a unit S⁴ vector to reduce Sp(2) to H, and lets a matrix J exchange the two factors. Its own Lemma INN proves (by hand, unchecked) that the kappa→∞ limit gives the periodic two-replica purity and that the ratio starts at one for kappa=0. But its seam uses the matrix J∈Sp(2) to exchange the two Sp(1) factors, while the global Higgs sign n→−n used to define the twist is stated not to lie in SO(5). The proposal therefore contains the requested inner factor-exchange mechanism, but its purity limit is only at κ→∞. Proposition BASE gives a high-temperature-in-kappa estimate with error O(n³u(C(e^kappa−1))^u), for every β and dimension, which is non-selective. The route stops at HT: it assumes the purity bound for every kappa along the family. The unproved transition is not a derived block step and, as row 1529 itself says, contains the seed near the coupling of interest. Therefore the row is a useful exact reformulation and candidate observable bridge, not progress on K-II under the lane’s equivalence/selectivity rules.
