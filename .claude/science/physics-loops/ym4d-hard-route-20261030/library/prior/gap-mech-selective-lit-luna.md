### Other requested mechanisms screened

- **Monopoles and (Z_2)/centre vortices.** Rigorous results found are either the conditional Mack–Petkova/Tomboulis–Yaffe free-energy inequalities above, strong-coupling expansions, or finite/discrete-group results. No open primary source read here proves an order-one 4D SU(2) monopole/vortex bound whose output controls a centre-neutral correlator or PUR-io. Center-flux inequalities can control charged Wilson loops once their free energy is estimated; no such neutral-transfer bridge was found.
- **Stochastic quantization and curvature/LSI.** Shen–Zhu–Zhu is the direct Bakry–Émery/LSI theorem. It holds in all d>1 under an explicit strong-coupling threshold. The lane's row-955 literature review also records the later strong-coupling Dobrushin/LSI manuscripts and their limits; none covers [2,3]. No dimension-specific curvature theorem for the pure 4D order-one Wilson ensemble was found.
- **Large N and master fields.** Chatterjee's and Jafarov's factorization results (as summarized in Shen–Zhu–Zhu's primary introduction) assume sufficiently small coupling and finite-volume/N-dependent controls; Cao–Nissim–Sheffield give the stated small-β area-law ranges. Factorization is not a bound on the centre-neutral transfer-trace ratio at fixed SU(2), nor is it a 4D-only selective estimate. Cao, Park & Sheffield's 2025 all-β planar-map expansion is likewise an identity/representation for Wilson loops, not a decay bound.
- **Dimension/group contrast.** Guth and Fröhlich–Spencer rigorously show why weak-coupling 4D compact U(1) is an adverse comparison. The 5D SU(2) weak-coupling no-gap statement in this lane is a physical belief/phase-diagram expectation, not a theorem identified in this source set. None of the positive candidate theorems above gives a proved 4D-vs-5D discriminator in the requested coupling band.

## Conclusion

No published or open-arXiv/open-journal estimate located satisfies all R1 conditions: pure 4D SU(2)/SU(N), order-one βW in [2,3] or the late calibrated trajectory, a uniform large-distance estimate, a proved step genuinely using both 4D and non-Abelian structure (and failing in 4D compact U(1)), and a centre-neutral readout capable of bounding PUR-io. The all-coupling Tomboulis paper is only a claimed candidate with a published proof critique; the proved positive results found are conditional, charged-observable, model-general, or confined to strong/weak-coupling regimes outside the target. No claim of a new theorem or status follows.

RESULT: R1 remains open on the literature examined; no qualifying selective order-one estimate or direct centre-neutral PUR-io input was found.
