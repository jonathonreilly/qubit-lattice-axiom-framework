- **The key arithmetic (p. 8), quoted:** "at a reference depth K, the preliminary mixing length in physical units is a_K X(beta_K) = L^((1 - eta/(2 pi))K + o(K)). Matching the effective densities to finer cutoffs is controlled by the error parameter C_H M^2 L^(-(2 - upsilon)K) in a physical square of side M, where upsilon > 0 can be chosen arbitrarily small. Thus M can be much larger than the mixing length while this error tends to zero. Fixing one sufficiently large K fixes a successful physical square once and for all; its decay criterion passes to every finer cutoff."

**First reading of the answer (to be confirmed in Sections 7-8).** The seed is NOT obtained at the terminal coupling H after the RG. It is obtained at the **bare** coupling beta_K of a reference depth K, on the original lattice of spacing a_K = L^(-K), by Proposition 2.1 directly (route (a) of Step 1). Proposition 2.1's length X(beta_K) is in physical units L^((1 - eta/2pi)K) — it *grows* with K (the one-loop rate would give physical length O(1); the factor-2 loss 4 pi vs 2 pi makes it grow), but by less than L^K. The RG's job is only the *comparison*: the effective densities at depth K and at every finer depth N > K, restricted to one physical square of side M, differ by C_H M^2 L^(-(2-upsilon)K), which is small for M up to about L^((1 - upsilon/2)K), and so can be chosen with a_K X(beta_K) << M << L^((1-upsilon/2)K) when upsilon < eta/pi. So the band (from the UV trajectory to the seed size) is crossed **at one fixed cutoff, by the stiffness flow of Proposition 2.1**, and the RG + chessboard comparison transports the finite-box defect from depth K to depths N > K **at a fixed physical box**, i.e. it is a cutoff-uniformity step, not a band-crossing step. The trade that makes it close is the strict inequality between two exponents: the mixing-length exponent 1 - eta/(2 pi) < 1 and the error contraction exponent 2 - upsilon > 2 x (that), i.e. eta > pi upsilon. In the lane's terms (reading, unchecked): Proposition 2.1 is a (K-flow) seed at one coupling, uniform in the coupling; Sections 5-8 are a cutoff-removal of a box criterion, the 2D form of "the seed carried to finer cutoffs" of `parts/gap.md` L22-23 ((SEED) "with a carry to finer cutoffs").
