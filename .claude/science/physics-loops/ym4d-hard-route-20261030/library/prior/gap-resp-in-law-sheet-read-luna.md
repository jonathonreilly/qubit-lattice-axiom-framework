## Question and notation

A bound for a shared rigid rotation, a supremum over all boundary data, free-boundary correlation decay, or one-point decay under special pins is not the requested statement. The neutral/singlet question concerns invariant local observables, including energy-type observables.

## Decision for RESP-TR

No source read states the requested entrywise-per-boundary-site response averaged over the O(4) model’s own boundary law, or a two-sample distance between cell laws with both boundary samples drawn from that law. OpenAI’s O(4) Theorem 3.1 is the nearest singlet result: it controls neutral local covariances at zero field for free subgraphs and sufficiently large unpinned tori, but its proof uses association and monotonicity and does not control pinned boundary response. The O(n) paper controls only vector two-point functions. Pei and Ding–Huang–Yang provide special-pin, odd-observable, or auxiliary-loop estimates; none fills the response gap. The standard weak-to-strong and Yoshida criteria do not give the requested result: the former is discrete-spin and begins with boundary-uniform weak mixing; the latter is for unbounded ferromagnetic spins and is explicitly uniform over boundaries.
