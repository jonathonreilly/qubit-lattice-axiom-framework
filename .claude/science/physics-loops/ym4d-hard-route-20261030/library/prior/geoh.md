The following order is a risk estimate: test the statements on which a proof could fail before doing routine ports. Dependencies still apply; later lanes consume the earlier geometric invariants. Each entry names its first hard step, not a proposed proof.

3. **Live (a), strip realization.** Specify the birth strip and the single-cell owner, then preserve both under one complete T/R cycle with a standing interrupted mark. Start with that cycle; an abstract disjoint family is insufficient.
4. **Live (b), persistent collar.** Follow a strip through the interruption that leaves the smallest collar. Check the next T site and every R integration site on the same history. This can fail before any contour summation matters.
5. **O5, BOX matching.** Identify the actual nonlinear source on the main-box chart and classify every exterior/straddling stencil. Then check the first/unfinished formation case. Use the new guarded first-transition arguments where they match. Do not launch duplicate analytic admission work owned by DOM/DR.

8. **Live (e), contours and anchors.** On the resulting actual grading, prove read-to-label reach at every cut link, including a read through a live strip or hub. Only then port the conditional cut-family sum and check its cell/depth constants. This supplies geometry to the separately registered O-kept-reader/O-probe/O-foreign consumers; it does not take over those rows.
9. **Live (d), incidence counts.** With the strip and collar invariant fixed, prove the mixed-level probe incidence bound using its exact boundary convention. Keep cell ownership, probe multiplicity, pocket packing and adjacent-anchor multiplicity as distinct claims.
10. **O1, remaining rule identification.** Compare the revised operation and its selectors with the new compulsory closed-endpoint ancestral class, move by move. This dictionary is needed by the earlier lanes, but its remaining standalone work should be a short identification if the rules agree. If they differ, expose the difference before those lanes proceed. Do not reprove waiting or tracing.
