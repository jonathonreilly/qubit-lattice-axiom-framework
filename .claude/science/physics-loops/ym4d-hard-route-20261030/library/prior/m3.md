component's complete reads, not only a block-diagonal matrix. Reader
factorization is the resulting independence of components. An arbitrary

### 5.3 Main risks and already failed routes

* **The local diagonal can survive the cut.** A bound on untagged a or
  on m_c is already available and does not fix this. Q and Q* are
  block-local, so their entire carriers, not only their anchors, must be
  covered. GW1's argument for a lift does not automatically cover this
  bare mass vertex.
* **Tagging does not preserve operator identities.** One union tag of
  a concatenated primitive differs from a product of tags of its factors
  when footprints overlap. `layer-pkg.md:912–947` already gives the scalar
  word diagnostic. Changing tags, or using `R=I-P` again after tagging,
  changes the switched function. Recovery at one cannot identify it
  away from one.
* **The old field reduction is unavailable at arbitrary switches.** K10
  uses the G-tilde equation, while the representation proof needs the
  `P_0G_0` equation and inverse/constraint identities. They are equivalent
  at one, not for the assigned tagged sums. Analytic continuation from
  `sigma=1` cannot repair this; one switch value is not an open set.
* **A new rider must be an explicit consumer choice.** Requiring equality
  with K10's existing `J_def(sigma)` for every sigma, while retaining
  the current definitions and locality clause, hits the existing
  zero-switch contradiction. The proposed lane can instead construct
  the missing reduced extension consumed by the assumed layer package.
  It must say so, and establish the link identification on that basis.
  It cannot silently claim that it repaired K10's old off-one identity.

**Recovery decision.** Attack only the tagged reduced assignment and this
identification. Reuse the checked symbolic obstruction and word machinery.
If a consumer is found to require equality with K10's unaltered off-one
definitional current, record that precise incompatible requirement and stop
that identification. Do not weaken locality or manufacture a switched

open independently of this digest.
