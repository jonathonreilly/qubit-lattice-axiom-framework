## Shared roots appearing in multiple boxes

- **A1–A4 background, fibre, response, and substitution admission** (`YM4_1400` lines 1966–2173; `rg:primitive-closure-premises` at 4153–4175): NB states the underlying background inputs; RS, B, LF, RC and CP need the actual histories, fibres, domains, and substitutions to meet those inputs in their own applications. The primitive clause quantifies over every actual determining region, stored-field fibre, allowed parameter domain, and actual history.
- **Actual pair envelope `hi:pair-sup-envelope`** (label 73364; primitive clause 4158–4162): B, LF and CP use the common-core history envelope on actual supports/fibres; the bound concerns the complete actual \(V_X\), including sources and counterterm normalization, not a selected background alone.
- **Kept Gaussian integral `hi:reference-gaussian-integral`** (label 74966; primitive clause 4160–4168): B and LF require an integrable majorant in the actual stored coordinates; LF's event-energy inequality must leave this reserve available. CP uses the resulting marked-mass control when converting to normalized moment errors.
- **Owned event-energy witnesses `hi:event-energy`** (label 76743): LF needs witnesses for each actual owned event and drop-out fibre; B needs the associated retained-kernel/common-majorant control; CP needs the Gaussian marks and unspent reserves to bound weighted exceptional defects.
- **Read and analytic-core coverage `hi:read-coverage`** (label 77502): B requires actual later-level read inclusion; LF and CP use the same actual history/read and analytic-domain admission as their component integrations and marked laws.
- **Actual one-step formation and common domains** (RS p. 832 and pp. 841–844): RS supplies formed regular/marked outputs and compatible cuts/domains; B and LF consume those outputs and domains; RC needs the actual regular formation and history bounds; CP's conditional law and marked errors depend on the formed, normalized presentation. The overview explicitly says the complete history-dependent NB domain checks remain with RS, B and LF (overview lines 318–321).
- **Shared formation and closure estimates (overview p. 835)**: the overview's RT entry says the formation and closure estimates left open there are supplied by RS, B, LF and RC. YM4_1400 lines 81869–81880 gives a nearby marked-use capacity condition: the majorant must cover histories where all marked uses occur, and actual formation must assign simultaneous uses to a common site/action budget.
- **Marked/source budgets and normalized partition control** (YM4_1400 lines 81869–81880 and 106913–106972): CP needs joint marked defects, while the same history-mass/source-budget inputs appear in B/LF's retained and event estimates. Normalizing an unnormalized estimate additionally requires a partition floor; the weighted-moment proposition does not supply one.
