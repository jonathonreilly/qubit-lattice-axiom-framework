## Step 1. The six declarations

Notation: b = their odd blocking factor (their `L`); K = cutoff level, mesh b^-K; β = bare Wilson β = g^-2 (standard normalisation, row 1449 Step 1a); `flowCouplings p β j` = their effective inverse coupling after j block steps from bare β (`TotalDensityFlow.coupling` agrees with it while the run stays above the floor, `total_flow_of_above_total`).

**1.1 `runDomain`** (YangMills/ContinuumLimit/RunAboveTheFloor.lean L43-44):
`{β | y + 2 * T.B < β ∧ ∀ j < k, y + 2 * T.B < flowCouplings (T.stepParameters y) β j}`.
A floor condition only: the bare β and every effective coupling before level k lie above y + 2B. **No upper bound on β.**
The same file's `RunTable` (L23-37) carries `descent : HasLinearDescent ... lam c₅` and `abs_flow_step_le` (L105-110): |x_{t+1} - x_t| ≤ 2B while the run is above the floor. As used in `bare_root_with_window_data` and `tuned_first_passage_index`, descent reads lam (K - i) - c₅ ≤ x_i - x_K for i ≤ K. So on a run, lam (K-i) - c₅ ≤ x_i - x_K ≤ 2B (K-i): the effective coupling falls at a rate between lam and 2B per step.

**1.2 `IsTunedAt T y P K β`** (YangMills/ContinuumLimit/FirstPassageOfATunedPair.lean L13-15): the flow from β stays ≥ y at every level j < K and its level-K value is exactly P. `tuned_first_passage_index` (same file L57-113): if tuned at K with P = `passageThreshold Bs g` and K ≥ `runDepth lam c₅`, there is a first-passage index K' ≤ K with K - K' < runDepth, i.e. the flow first drops to the threshold within a bounded number of steps of K.

**1.3 `bare_root_with_window_data`** (YangMills/ContinuumLimit/ReferenceRoots.lean L9-23, statement). For every Q holding at admissible parameters: ∃ b_0, ∀ odd b ≥ b_0, ∃ parameter table T, floor y, constants (lam, cr, Bs ≥ 2, γ > 0, ...), with a one-loop average slope and `RunTable`, and

> `∀ H : ℝ, ∃ (M : ℕ+) (δ : ℝ), 8 ≤ torusSide L M ∧ 0 < δ ∧ ∀ᶠ K : ℕ in atTop, ∀ g₀ ∈ Ioo (0:ℝ) δ, ... IsLeast {t | 0 < t ∧ referenceResponse L M K (t ^ 2)⁻¹ = fixedPhysicalLevel g₀} g ∧ b ∈ runDomain T y K ∧ IsTunedAt T y P K b ∧ H ≤ P`

with g = `renormalizedBareCoupling L M K g₀`, b = g^-2, P = flow coupling at level K. **This holds at every late K (`∀ᶠ K in atTop`), uniformly in g₀ ∈ (0, δ).** It controls the least root from one side only: the bare β_K is in the run domain, and the effective coupling at level K (unit physical scale) is at least H. The reference level M (torus side 2b^M) and δ depend on H. The proof (L24-95) builds a tuned trial bk at each level with flow value at least H (`all_cutoff_trial`), shows the least root lies at or above bk (`leastBareAt_of_trial`: the response is continuous, tends to 0 cold, and the trial is on the high side of the level), and then monotonicity of the tuned branch (`tuned_branch_of_le`) gives P ≥ flow value of bk ≥ H.

**1.4 `NoEscapingLevel T y L M`** (YangMills/ContinuumLimit/FixedReferenceLevelBound.lean L13-18): for every level r > 0, every strictly increasing k_j and bare b_j in the run domain, tuned, with `referenceResponse L M (k j) (b j) = r` for all j, the level-k_j flow value cannot tend to +∞. That is: **at a fixed positive reference response, the effective coupling at unit physical scale stays bounded along every sequence of cutoffs** (no escape to the cold end). It is proved from stage decay (`noEscapingLevel_of_stageDecay`, L22-79): if the level-K flow tends to ∞, the reference covariance at a stopped level is at most r/2, contradicting the level r.

## Step 2. Decision (a): β_K at every late K

**Lemma BND (ARGUED from the Lean statements quoted; elementary; not a Lean theorem of the release; unchecked).** Fix the data of `fixed_observable_continuum_of_no_escape` for one odd b ≥ b_0: T, y, the H used there, M = M(H), δ, and g₀ ∈ (0, δ). Let β_K be the least-root bare coupling and P_K = flow value at level K from β_K. Then there are K_0 and W' < ∞ with

  H ≤ P_K ≤ W' for every K ≥ K_0.

Proof. The lower bound is `bare_root_with_window_data` (eventually in K). For the upper bound, suppose sup_{K ≥ K_0} P_K = ∞. Pick a strictly increasing k_j ≥ K_0 with P_{k_j} → ∞. Every hypothesis of `NoEscapingLevel T y b M` holds along (k_j, β_{k_j}) with r = `fixedPhysicalLevel g₀` > 0: β > 0, run-domain membership and `IsTunedAt` from `bare_root_with_window_data`, and the response equals r because β_{k_j} is a root. So NoEscapingLevel gives False. NoEscapingLevel T y b M holds for this very (T, y) and every M (it is the hypothesis `hNo := hTable KrRun lam cr hRun M` in `fixed_observable_continuum_of_components`, Formulation/FixedObservableContinuum.lean L161-163). QED

**Corollary BND-1 (linear bracket at every late K; ARGUED).** For K ≥ K_0: H + lam K - cr ≤ β_K ≤ W' + 2 B K.
Proof: β_K - P_K = x_0 - x_K. `RunTable.descent` (as applied at ReferenceRoots.lean L60-64) gives the lower bound; `abs_flow_step_le` (RunAboveTheFloor.lean L105-110) summed over K steps gives the upper bound, the run being above the floor by `IsTunedAt`.

**Corollary BND-2 (sharp one-loop slope at every late K; ARGUED).** β_K / log(1/a_K) → 11/(3π^2), a_K = b^-K, along **all** K, not only along the extracted K_j.
Proof. Put W* = max(W', Bs + γ^-2 + 1) and g* = (W* - Bs)^{-1/2} ∈ (0, γ], so `passageThreshold Bs g* = W*` (definition `g^-2 + B`, Construction/WindowOfTheParameters/FirstPassageOfASequenceForm.lean L5) and `CouplingConditions p Bs ... g*` holds (`bare_root_with_window_data`, clause `∀ g ∈ Ioc 0 γ`). For late K, β_K > W* (Corollary BND-1) and x_K = P_K ≤ W*, so the first index K'' ≤ K with x_{K''} ≤ W* exists and is ≥ 1; (K'', β_K) is then a first-passage pair at threshold W* (same construction as `tuned_first_passage_index`, FirstPassageOfATunedPair.lean L85-113, with Nat.find). By descent, lam (K - K'') - cr ≤ x_{K''} - x_K ≤ W* - H, so K - K'' ≤ (W* - H + cr)/lam, a bound independent of K; in particular K'' → ∞. Then `asymptotically_free` at g* gives |β_K / log(1/a_{K''}) - 11/(3π^2)| ≤ δ(K'') → 0, and log(1/a_K)/log(1/a_{K''}) = K/K'' → 1. QED
Caveat for the reader of the Lean text: (K'', β_K) has the bare coupling of level K at cutoff K''; it is used only to read off the slope, not as a lattice to which the five-bound theorem is applied. The precise field name and statement of `asymptotically_free` (in `TotalDensitySystem.CouplingConditions ... .passage`) and of `IsFirstPassagePair` are checked in Step 3.

**Verdict on (a).** The release states control of the least root at every late K only from below (H ≤ P_K, run-domain membership, `∀ᶠ K`). The upper control at every late K is not stated, but it follows in a few lines from two statements the release proves for the same table (`bare_root_with_window_data`, `NoEscapingLevel`), with no new estimate (Lemma BND). With it, the bare coupling at every late cutoff lies in the chain's range β_0 ≤ β_K ≤ B_0 log(ℓ/a_K) for every B_0 > 11/(3π^2) (BND-2), and even with the explicit B_0 = 2B/log b + 1 (BND-1). So **(a) holds, as an ARGUED corollary of the Lean statements**, pending the Step 3 check of the two definitions named in the caveat.

## Step 5. Decision (d): what the Lean construction fixes at the physical scale

**What is fixed exactly.** At every late K: `referenceResponse L M K β_K = fixedPhysicalLevel g₀`, the centred tr F^2 covariance of two bumps of radius 1/8 at distance 3, on the torus of side 2b^M, equals its leading perturbative value at coupling g₀ (row 1449 Step 1a). M = M(H) and δ = δ(H) are chosen after H (ReferenceRoots.lean L20-21), with H = max(X, Bs + γ^-2 + 1) in the public proof (FixedObservableContinuum.lean L99-100).

**What that pins.** By `bare_root_with_window_data` and Lemma BND, the effective inverse coupling at the unit physical scale, P_K = x_K, lies in a band [H, W'] at every late K. H is large (above the record constant X and above Bs + γ^-2 + 1), and W' exists but is not explicit (NoEscapingLevel is a non-quantitative "cannot tend to ∞"). Lean does not prove that P_K converges; it may wander inside the band. Lean's flow is controlled only above the floor: `runDomain` requires every effective coupling to exceed y + 2B, and `RunTable` gives its bounds only while the run stays above y.

**Consequences for a lattice seed on this trajectory.**
1. The Lean unit of length is ultraviolet. The effective coupling there is 1/P_K ≤ 1/H, weak. This is the same kind of unit as the account's first-passage unit (g ≤ γ_H, effective inverse coupling above 5 by check 1331; `gap-shrink-box-fable.md` 1.3 and Lemma N). So the seed cannot be proved at a physical size of order one in Lean units. It must hold at a size ℓ beyond the passage of the flow from the band [H, W'] to an order-one coupling: GUIDE, one loop, ℓ ≳ b^{(W' − β_c)/s} with β_c of order one and s = 11/(3π^2) log b. The constant is unknown because W' is not explicit.
2. That passage takes place below Lean's floor y. No statement in the release reaches it. The seed's region, order-one effective coupling on the blocked lattice, is outside what the Lean RG controls, so no Lean flow bound can stand in for the seed or for the infrared carry. This agrees with row 1353 ("a step must ... read first-passage data") and with Theorem T of row 1332, where the infrared carry is a separate open hypothesis.
3. Because the band [H, W'] is the same at every late K, one ℓ serves every late cutoff if the seed holds at all (GUIDE). That is why the target can be stated at every late K with one existential ℓ (Step 6), rather than along the unknown subsequence.

**Addendum to 1.5 and 1.1 (definitions checked after Step 5).**
- `StageDecayAt Tb Kr y` (YangMills/ContinuumLimit/StoppedCovarianceEstimate.lean L9-20): for a fixed physical side ell ≥ 8, A ≥ 1, test spacing vt > 0 and every ε > 0, eventually in q: whenever a scored tower follows the flow from β to level k above the floor, and the level-k effective inverse coupling lies in [q^92, A q^92], the covariance of two separated tr F^2 tests at spacing (6/5) q on the torus of side ell·q is at most ε. So: **a cold effective coupling at level k forces a small tr F^2 covariance.** `stopped_covariance_vanishes` (CovarianceAtTheStoppingLevel.lean L44-...) proves it at all response parameters, from the stopped Wilson covariance, score moments, terminal second moment and torus tail. This is the only physics input of NoEscapingLevel, and hence of the upper half of Lemma BND.
- `HasLinearDescent ℓ lam c₅` (Construction/ResponseInTheExpansion/RunningCouplingSlope.lean L10-12): lam (j − i) − c₅ ≤ x_i − x_j for i ≤ j while the flow stays above the floor. This is the direction used in Corollary BND-1 and in the bound K − K'' ≤ D of BND-2.

## Step 6. The restated gap target, and next rows
