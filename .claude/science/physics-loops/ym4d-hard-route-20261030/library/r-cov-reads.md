## 3. Pair and coordinate conventions

Write \(p=(\mathbf U,\mathbf J)\).
The current is an independent coordinate.
For a real gauge map \(g\),
\[
 p^g=(\mathbf U^g,\operatorname{Ad}(g)\mathbf J),\qquad
 \mathbf U^g(b)=g(b_-)\mathbf U(b)g(b_+)^{-1}.
 \tag{1}
\]
SA 5729–5736 bases each current at the bond's initial site.
At a physical evaluation, the pair is \((U,J_{\Omega_0}(U))\).
The subscript retains only the inner plaquettes (SA 10129–10137, 17396).
It cannot be dropped at boundary bonds.

The stage background uses the determining sequence.
The carried activities, profiles and tests retain the action sequence (SA 17433–17447).
Write \(U_n=U^{(n)}_{k,X}\) for the background after \(n\) preparation stages.
The notation does not identify backgrounds belonging to different sequences.

The displayed continuation rule is
\[
 p_{s,\lambda}
 =\big(e^{i\eta\lambda H(s)}\mathbf U,
          \mathbf J+\lambda\mathsf j(s)\big).
 \tag{2}
\]
This is (R8)–(R10), SA 5748–5811, before the recorded node gauge.
The current response comes from the constraint multiplier.
Changing units multiplies potentials by \(L^{-m}\) and currents by \(L^{-3m}\).
Replacing the second entry of (2) by \(J(e^{i\eta\lambda H(s)}\mathbf U)\) changes the prescribed read.

Stored coordinates have three different types.
Group links use left and right endpoint actions.
Stored forms \(a_l=\mathcal A_l^{\rm s}\) use the adjoint action at their initial sites (SA 6160–6173).
The shell variable uses one scale: \(V''=e^{ig_kB}V_0\), even on lower shells (SA 17591–17598).
Frame labels and switches are scalar data.

Two distinctions change the earlier proposed comparison.
The new reference coordinate \(V'_k\) and the old shell coordinate \(B\) are different variables.
Also, the general-pair patch form is only part of a splitting-dependent decomposition:
\[
 \Phi_\omega=\Phi_\omega^\circ-\tfrac12q_X+V_X.
 \tag{3}
\]
SA 18057–18059 requires only the complete sum to descend to the pair.
Thus a discrepancy of \(q_X\) alone does not refute the complete read.

## 5. The returning-gauge test

Here is the exact compatibility equation that the reads must satisfy.
Let \(\widehat p_f\) be a factor's pair before its returning gauge \(r_f\).
Its returned pair and its native stored coordinate are
\[
 p_f=\widehat p_f^{\,r_f},\qquad
 \widehat a_f(b)=\operatorname{Ad}(r_f(b_-)^{-1})a(b).
 \tag{4}
\]
This notation records a coordinate change; it does not supply the missing maps \(r_f\).
Both entries of \(\widehat p_f\) undergo the same return.

Suppose the native read transforms by \(h_f\), and let primes denote evaluation at \((p^g,T_g\xi;s)\).
Since \((W^u)^v=W^{vu}\), the returned action is
\[
 d_f=r'_f h_f r_f^{-1}.
 \tag{5}
\]
Indeed, \(p'_f=(\widehat p_f^{h_f})^{r'_f}=p_f^{d_f}\).
Also, \(a'=\operatorname{Ad}(d_f)a\) gives
\[
 \widehat a'_f
 =\operatorname{Ad}((r'_f)^{-1}d_fr_f)\widehat a_f
 =\operatorname{Ad}(h_f)\widehat a_f.
 \tag{6}
\]
Thus a native covariant factor has a covariant returned read.
The argument applies to the independent current as well.

For this transport proof, factors sharing \(a(b)\) must induce the same adjoint action there.
Equality of their matrices is stronger than necessary; a central sign has trivial adjoint action.
Group links require compatible actions at both endpoints.
One fixed real family \(d_g\), independent of the localisation parameters, would define the substitution in (10).
If it is independent of the integration variables, Haar and Lebesgue measures are preserved.
Left and right unitary translations preserve Haar measure.
The adjoint representation of SU(2) preserves Lebesgue measure on its three-dimensional Lie algebra.
Invariance of the recorded tests would then preserve \(S_\omega\).

The elementary stages of this check do work.
For a block gauge draw, use \(u'=\ell u v^{-1}\), where \(\ell\) lifts the coarse values of \(v\).
Then \((W^v)^{u'}=(W^u)^\ell\), and the absolute gauge factor is unchanged.
For a rooted tree, its transport obeys
\[
 c_{S^v}(x)=v(o)c_S(x)v(x)^{-1}.
 \tag{7}
\]
Tree-normalising the reference and data therefore leaves only the root conjugation \(v(o)\).
This preserves the \(B\)-test and the vacuum side of the last-shell test.
It also transports the reference denominator with its numerator.
These identities explain why the uncompensated witness in `r-cov-10.md` is insufficient.

The unresolved step is equality of (5) for the **actual parameterised reads** sharing each stored coordinate.
SA 7913 prescribes fixing former stored tops during node-gauge correction.
It does not specify every \(r_f(s,\lambda)\) used in SA 17903–17925.
SA 10356–10402 specifies the decoupled response once its walk data are assigned.
SA 10446–10464 identifies it with a background at physical parameters only.
That identification cannot determine all the returning gauges at arbitrary parameters.
