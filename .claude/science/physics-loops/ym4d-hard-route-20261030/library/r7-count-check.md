**Repair (proved here under input (C)).** (C): the action member `Lambda^a_j` is a union of level-`j` cells (`M Rcheck_j`-cubes of the fixed level-`j` cell grid), for `1 <= j <= k`.

*Lemma R.* Under (C), the number of rim sites of level `j` is at most `81 #_j(Z^a_j)`, and so `sum_j #_j(rim_j) <= 81 L^4/(L^4-1) sum_n #_n(Gamma^a_n) <= 81 L^4/(L^4-1) * 35410 sum_n #_n(Gamma_n)` under the inputs of Theorem B.

Then Steps 2 and 3 of Theorem A, and Theorem B, apply verbatim (they bound `sum_j #_j(Z^a_j)`). ∎
