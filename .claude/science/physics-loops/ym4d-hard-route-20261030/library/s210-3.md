First failing step: none. The steps I redid are these.

*Distance.* For $X$ Hermitian and traceless, $\|X\|$ is a multiple of an Ad-invariant inner-product norm on su(2). So $r(W)=\min\{\|X\|:e^{iX}=W\}$ is the geodesic distance of a bi-invariant metric. It has the triangle inequality, and $r(We^{iX})\le r(W)+\|X\|$. The cut-off $\theta=\tilde\theta\circ r$ is smooth. The function $r$ fails to be smooth only at $\pm1$, and $\tilde\theta$ is constant near $0$ and near $\pi>5\delta/3$.

*Fixed point.* $F_H(W)=U$ holds if and only if $W=Ue^{isH}$ with $s=\theta(Ue^{isH})$. The map $g(s)=\theta(Ue^{isH})$ sends $[0,1]$ into itself. Also $|g(s)-g(s')|\le{\rm Lip}(\theta)\,r(e^{i(s'-s)H})\le{\rm Lip}(\theta)h|s-s'|$, and ${\rm Lip}(\theta)h<1/2$. So Banach's theorem gives one fixed point. Also $\partial_s(s-g(s))\ge1/2$, so the implicit function theorem gives a smooth inverse.

*Jacobian.* Put $\theta_0=\theta(W)$. Then
$F_H(W)^{-1}F_H(We^{i\epsilon X})=\exp\{i\epsilon[{\rm Ad}_{e^{i\theta_0H}}X-d\theta_W(X)H]+O(\epsilon^2)\}$.
So the left-trivialised differential is ${\rm Ad}_g-H\otimes d\theta_W$, with $g=e^{i\theta_0H}$. The determinant lemma gives $\det({\rm Ad}_g)\,(1-d\theta_W({\rm Ad}_g^{-1}H))=1-X_H\theta(W)$. Here $\det{\rm Ad}_g=1$ and ${\rm Ad}_g^{-1}H=H$. The determinant is positive, so it is the Haar Jacobian. Right translation by $B$ keeps Haar measure, so $\int f(V)\,dV=\int f(T_H(W))J_H(W)\,dW$.

*(8).* If $r(W)\le4\delta/3$, then $F_H(W)=W$. Otherwise $r(F_H(W))\ge r(W)-h>7\delta/6>a$. For the second identity, use $\|XY-1\|\le\|X-1\|+\|Y-1\|$ for unitaries and $\|e^{iA}-1\|\le\|A\|$. If $\theta(W)<1$, both tested norms are below $5\delta/3+\delta/6<2\delta$. If $\theta(W)=1$, the two tested matrices are equal. The unshifted-bond bound is $\|WBQ^{-1}-1\|\le r(W)+h+\|LQ^{-1}-1\|<2\delta$.

## 2. What (10) asks, and in which variables

**Records and erasure.** A history $h$ is its record of outcomes (SA L17407-17413). Its clusters are as at SA L17447. Fix a cluster $\mathfrak c$ of $h$ whose root $X$ heals at level $k$. As in SA L17451, $h$ carries no cluster that heals below $k$. Every other cluster is *retained*. The erased record $h^-$ keeps every outcome of $h$ outside $\mathfrak c$. At every place of $\mathfrak c$ and every level $j\le k$, it records the never-large outcome: each test made there passes (the *fill*). From level $k$ on, $h$ and $h^-$ carry the same label (SA L17451).

**Remark 2.1 (raw variables cannot carry (10)).** In the measure layer of SA L17482, the stored variables are raw fields. There, a retained third test at a bond $b$ reads $V_k(b)(V^{\rm bg}_{k,h}(b))^{-1}$. The background $V^{\rm bg}_{k,h}=\mathcal M^kU_t(\mathbf V)$ is formed on $\Omega_t(h)$, and $\Omega_t(h^-)\ne\Omega_t(h)$ at the place of $\mathfrak c$. If $V^{\rm bg}_{k,h}(b)\ne V^{\rm bg}_{k,h^-}(b)$ at one tested bond, then the two raw indicators $\mathbf 1\{|\log(V_k(V^{\rm bg})^{-1})(b)|<\delta_k\}$ differ on a set of $V_k(b)$ of positive Haar measure. So (10) must use coordinates in which the tested quantities are themselves variables. Section 3 defines them.

**What (10) is used for.** The product step (2) of `s210.md` needs the *supports* and the *reference measures* to split over clusters. SA's gas (L17966-17980) keeps every density in $\exp\sum_YV_{\mathfrak X}(Y)$. So in this note (10) is an identity of indicators. Densities are treated in Section 5.

## 3. The erasure/replay map

**Definition 3.1 (formula coordinates).** At step $t=k'+1$ of either history, the variables are the following.

- (F1) Raw stored fields: $V_q$ on $\Omega_{q+1}^c$, for each $q<t$, with surviving delta functions read as definitions (SA L17536, L18035). The measure is Haar.
- (F2) Fluctuation coordinates: $A_{k'}(b)=\tfrac1i\log(V_{k'}(b)(V^{\rm bg}_{k'}(b))^{-1})$ at the nonpivot bonds of $\Omega_t$ where no strip substitution is made. Pivot values are not coordinates; they solve the averaging constraint (SA L7481, L8278-8285). The measure is Lebesgue times the fixed Haar density $\sigma_{\rm H}$.
- (F3) Strip coordinates: $W'(b)$ at the cut-off and fully shifted strip bonds, with $V_{k'}(b)=T_{H(b)}(W'(b))$ as in `s210.md` (6). The measure is Haar.
- (F4) The new field $V_t$, until step $t+1$ splits it on $\Omega_{t+1}$ by (F2).

**Definition 3.2 (reconstruction maps).** $\Phi_h$ sends formula coordinates to raw fields: $V_{k'}=e^{iA_{k'}}V^{\rm bg}_{k',h}$ on (F2) bonds, $V_{k'}=T_{H_h}(W')$ on (F3) bonds, pivots from the constraint, and (F1) unchanged. Here $V^{\rm bg}_{k',h}$ and $H_h=\log(V^{\rm bg}_{k',h}(V^{\rm loc})^{-1})$ are formed on the label of $h$. The map $\Phi_{h^-}$ is the same with $h^-$ in place of $h$. The two maps differ only through $V^{\rm bg}$, that is, through $H$ and the (F2) translation.

**Supplied rules.** SA states these only in words. They are adopted here as definitions. Each one agrees with the cited text, and none changes a measure on its support.

- (L1) *Localised strip background.* $V^{\rm loc}_{k'}$ is the averaged background $U^\circ_W$ of SA Theorem 6(c) (L9783). Each component $W$ of $\Lambda_t^c\cap\Lambda_{k'}$ has its own problem: the joined sequence and joined datum of $W$ (stored data on old bonds, the lift of $V_{j_a}$ on collar bonds). No plaquette and no averaging constraint meets two components, so a problem posed on their union splits exactly.
- (L2) *Zones.* Each strip bond is in exactly one zone. K: the bond is read by a passed test of threshold at most $\delta_{k'}$; it is left unsubstituted, so it carries (F2). C: the bond is not in K, and a failed test of threshold at most $\delta_{k'}$ reads it; it gets the cut-off substitution. F: every other strip bond gets the full shift, $\theta\equiv1$. The zone is read from the record (SA L17459). With this rule, no bond of zone F is read by a test of threshold at most $\delta_{k'}$.
- (L3) *Pivots in old tests.* An old test $\chi_{k'}(\square)$ with $\square\subset\Omega_t^\sim\setminus\Omega_t$ is either carried with SA's pivot value or omitted. On the support, the two choices give the same measure, by Lemma 2.2(a) (SA L17368).
- (L4) *Fill.* At the places of $\mathfrak c$, $h^-$ records a pass for every test that the never-large record makes there. These tests are activated by the replay.

**Inputs (cited from SA, not rebuilt here).**

- (SUP) Superposition and the margin (SA L17443-17455). Distinct components of $Z_j$ are at least one level-$j$ cell apart. Hulls, layers, boxing and the collar/rim rounds act componentwise. One cell exceeds the reach of every test, pivot and collar. The window lemma holds; it is `s210.md` (1), proved there.
- (L2.2) Lemma 2.2 (a), (b) (SA L17368-17380): old tests and gauge indicators equal $1$ on $\Omega_t^\sim$ on the support; off $R_t$, $\chi^{(k')}$ implies $\chi'_{k'}$.
- (EX) On the support of the local tests of a level, the true background, every localised background used by a test, and $V^{\rm loc}$ exist (SA Theorems 1, 6; ledger A1, checked rows 81, 95).
- (M) On the support, $\|H\|+\|V^{\rm loc}Q^{-1}-1\|<\delta_{k'}/6$ and ${\rm Lip}(\theta)\|H\|<1/2$ at every strip bond (SA L17518 gives $|H|\le C\varepsilon e^{-\check R}$; L17380 gives $|V^{\rm bg}Q^{-1}-1|\le C(A_0/A_1)e^{-c\check R}\delta$).
- (CAR) Carriage (SA L17562): the tests, gauge factors and delta functions carried by an integral operation read no current background, and the operation's support $S_\omega$ is a set of its own variables (SA L18033, L18101).

## 4. Theorem E: (10) holds on the supports

Let $G^h$ be the set where (EX) and (M) hold for the label of $h$ at every level. By (EX) and (M), $G^h$ contains the support of the raw plaquette tests $\chi_q(\Omega_q)$, $q\le k$, of $h$ (SA L17383). These tests are factors of the indicator product of $h$. Define $G^{h^-}$ in the same way. There, the fill's plaquette tests are factors.

**Theorem E.** Assume (SUP), (L2.2), (EX), (M) and (CAR), with rules (L1)-(L4). Let $m$ be a move of a retained cluster $\mathfrak r$ at step $t$, and let $o$ be an outcome of $m$. Then there is one set $D_{m,o}$ of formula coordinates with these properties.

- (a) $\mathbf 1_{D^h_{m,o}}(\Phi_h(e,\xi))=\mathbf 1_{D_{m,o}}(\xi)$ on $G^h$, and $\mathbf 1_{D^{h^-}_{m,o}}(\Phi_{h^-}(e,\xi))=\mathbf 1_{D_{m,o}}(\xi)$ on $G^{h^-}$.
- (b) $D_{m,o}$ reads formula coordinates only within one level-$t$ cell of the level-$(t-1)$ region of $\mathfrak r$. The guard of $m$ is the same in $h$ and $h^-$. The guard is whether $m$ occurs, together with its cubes, zones, rounds, class and localised problems.
- (c) Each test that the replay activates (L4) reads only formula coordinates within one cell of the places of $\mathfrak c$. None of these is a coordinate of $\mathfrak r$.

Hence (10) holds for every retained move and outcome. The indicator products factor as

$$
\prod I^h=\mathbf 1_{\mathfrak r}(\xi_{\mathfrak r})\,\mathbf 1_{E}(\xi_E)\,\mathbf 1^h_{\mathfrak c}(\xi^h_{\mathfrak c};\xi_\partial),\qquad
\prod I^{h^-}=\mathbf 1_{\mathfrak r}(\xi_{\mathfrak r})\,\mathbf 1_{E}(\xi_E)\,\mathbf 1_{\rm fill}(\xi^-_{\mathfrak c};\xi_\partial).
\tag{E}
$$

Here $\xi_\partial\subset\xi_E$ are the exterior coordinates within one cell of the places of $\mathfrak c$. They are disjoint from $\xi_{\mathfrak r}$.

**Proof of (a).** The proof goes through the indicators by type. Each step names the set $D_{m,o}$.

1. *Tests on raw stored fields.* These are $\chi_t(\square)$ and $\chi'_{k'}(\square)$ for $\square\subset\Omega_t^c$, and the old tests on $\Omega_t^c\setminus\Omega_t^\sim$. They read raw fields within $O(1)$ test cubes. The localised backgrounds $U_{t,\square}(V_t)$ read $V_t$ near $\square$ only (SA L17320). These fields lie in $\Lambda_t^c\subset\Omega_{t+1}^c$, so they stay (F1) at every later step. $\Phi_h$ and $\Phi_{h^-}$ are the identity on (F1) and (F4). So $D_{m,o}$ is the same raw set. This case does not use $G$.
2. *Old tests and gauge indicators on $\Omega_t^\sim$.* By (L2.2)(a) they equal $1$ on the support of the step-$t$ tests and constraints. This holds in both histories. Put $D_{m,o}$ equal to the whole space, as (L3) allows. On $\Omega_t^{\sim-1}\setminus R_t$, $\chi'_{k'}$ equals $1$ on the support of $\chi^{(k')}$ by (L2.2)(b). It is treated in the same way.
3. *Tests in fluctuation coordinates.* These are the third test on cubes in $\Omega_t^{\sim-1}$, and the collar and rim tests of every round. Each one reads $A_{k'}$ at nonpivot bonds, and its only global object is $V^{\rm bg}$ (SA L17320, L17336). By totality (SA L17346), the test is the indicator that $V^{\rm bg}$ and the logarithm exist and that $|A_{k'}(b)|<a_b$ at the bonds read. Here $a_b\in\{\delta_{k'},\delta_{k'}/(4\Theta_1),\theta_c\delta_{k'}\}$. On $G^h$, $V^{\rm bg}_{k',h}$ exists. The logarithm exists off a Haar-null set, and the coordinate is $A_{k'}(b)$ itself. So $D_{m,o}=\{|A_{k'}(b)|<a_b\ \forall b\}$, or its complement for a failed outcome. The same holds in $h^-$ on $G^{h^-}$. If such a test reads a strip bond, case 4 applies. A passed test puts the bond in zone K, and a failed test puts it in zone K or C (L2). Every threshold $a_b$ is at most $\delta_{k'}$, so `s210.md` (8) covers zone C.
4. *Strip tests.* Take a strip bond $b$. In zone K, $b$ carries (F2), and its low-threshold tests are as in 3. Its $2\delta_{k'}$ test equals $1$ on its kept condition: $\|e^{iA}V^{\rm bg}Q^{-1}-1\|<a_b+\|H\|+\|V^{\rm loc}Q^{-1}-1\|<2\delta_{k'}$ by (M). This is the unshifted-bond remark of `s210.md` 5.1. In zone C, by `s210.md` (8) and (M), a test of threshold $a\le\delta_{k'}$ is $\mathbf 1\{r(W'(b))<a\}$, and the $2\delta_{k'}$ test is $\mathbf 1\{\|W'(b)V^{\rm loc}(b)Q(b)^{-1}-1\|<2\delta_{k'}\}$. In zone F, $T_H(W')=W'V^{\rm loc}$ exactly, and no low-threshold test reads $b$ (L2). Products and complements keep these identities. So failed-cube indicators of $R_t$ are covered. $V^{\rm loc}$ is a function of the (F1) coordinates on its component $W$ (L1, R2). Also $W\subset\Lambda_t^c$ is connected, so it lies in the component of $\mathfrak r$. Its sequence and datum are therefore the same in $h$ and $h^-$ (SUP). $Q$ reads $V_t$ near $\square$ only. So $D_{m,o}$ is the same set in both histories.
5. *Operations $\mathbf R$ of retained components.* These are the stages with tests at $\delta'_j$, the frame sum, the trees, the test on $B$, the last-shell test, the fates and the inner histories. Their tests use backgrounds localised in the component (SA L17592). By (CAR), each support $S_\omega$ is a set of the component's own variables. The class conditions read the component's own boxes in its window (SA L17576-17580). By the window lemma (`s210.md` (1)), no creation read there has healed. So each $D_{m,o}$ is the same.
6. *Undefined objects.* In cases 1, 4 and 5, every object is localised. Its existence is the same local event in both histories. In case 3, the only global object exists on $G^h$ and on $G^{h^-}$.
7. *Pivots.* No test of cases 3-4 reads a pivot value (SA L17320). A pivot inside $\Omega_t$ enters only through $\Phi$, that is, through densities. A surviving delta function on $\Omega_t^c$ fixes an (F1) pivot from (F1) data and $V_t$ in its own double block (SA L8283-8285). That is a local map, the same in both histories.

**Proof of (b).** The cubes tested, the regions $\Omega,\Lambda,S$, the rounds, the collar and rim sets, the cut-off zone, the class and the fates are all functions of the record. They read it within one cell of $\mathfrak r$ (SUP; SA L17459 records the three bond sets in the trace). The record of $h^-$ equals that of $h$ off the places of $\mathfrak c$, and these places are at least one cell from $\mathfrak r$ at every level (SUP). The reads of cases 1-5 have reach less than one cell (SUP).

**Proof of (c).** By (L4), the activated tests sit at the places of $\mathfrak c$. They are tests of cases 1 and 3 of the never-large record, so their reach is less than one cell. Their coordinates are $\xi^-_{\mathfrak c}$ and exterior coordinates. Coordinates in the gap between $\mathfrak c$ and $\mathfrak r$ may be read by both, but no move of either writes them. That is allowed: shared, unchanged exterior parameters need no edge in `s210.md` Section 3.

**Proof of (E).** In both histories, multiply the indicators over all moves. By the definition of $G^h$, $\prod I^h=\mathbf 1_{G^h}\prod I^h$. Replace each retained factor by $\mathbf 1_{D_{m,o}}$ using (a). The raw plaquette tests that imply $G^h$ are unchanged factors (case 1), so the factor $\mathbf 1_{G^h}$ can be dropped again. The exterior tests away from $\mathfrak c$ are cases 1 and 3 of the never-large record, the same in both histories. Collect the rest by (b) and (c). $\square$

**What Theorem E does not show.** It is an identity of indicators and guards. It says nothing about the densities: Jacobians, actions, Gaussian weights, normalisers. Those are treated in Section 5.
