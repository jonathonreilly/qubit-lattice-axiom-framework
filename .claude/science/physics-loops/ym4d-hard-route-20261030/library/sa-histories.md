### 3.4 The induction and constants

SA L19408–19524 defines a class of **presentations**, including actual formation, domains, coherence and mass, then states Theorem 2.11 with a next-step tube hypothesis which the theorem expressly does not return. It also explains why activity norms alone cannot imply decay with age: an unremoved marginal action is a counterexample. The constant-order and scalar-summation arguments are conditional on the earlier estimates fitting that order. They do not create a missing local chart, cancellation or multiplier estimate.

There is a second obstruction to importing our zero-temperature test verbatim. In our notes the finite base measure, label rule and thresholds are independent of the bare coupling; that is why the elementary Laplace limit yields SEP or SP. SA L17508 and L19410 instead make moves depend on the frame through thresholds, gauge laws, reference laws and cube sizes. Its threshold is `epsilon_j = g_j p_0(g_j)`, with creation penalty of order `p_0(g_j)^2`, not a fixed `t T^2`. As the inverse coupling tends to infinity, the threshold and event vary and `p_0(g)^2/t` tends to zero. Thus SA's stated mass rates do **not** entail a positive fixed-threshold SP/SEP gap by the limit in rarity-x (3.1). A transfer would have to fix the measure/events and check rates separately.

## 6. Each local stop: stated, proved, outlined or skipped?

### 6.1 SEP: marked sector versus the identical residual sector

**Literal SEP: skipped. Related cancellation and compatible-history deletion: outlined.** Our statement is

`m(D) - m(D \ gamma) >= a_I eps_q^2 T^2 |gamma_I| + a_II T^2 |gamma_II|`,

with essential minima of the same coupling-free measure and a supported deletion preserving the exact residual active set (`rarity-fresh-x` §§2–3). SA states no such sector-minimum comparison. It retains a real core action, replaces a local law by a normalized reference, and erases *healed history clusters* with unchanged standing part (L17451, L17854–17884, L18877–18949). These are different comparisons.

The x gate's D1 correctly locates the classical problem as a signed excess plus a compatible fill; finite-coupling relative entropy is a second problem. Its identification of an empty-sector minimum with a class lift would itself require the global minimum identification, so that sentence must not be imported as an established equality. SA Proposition 2.4 only asserts minimality within a class. The c gate's four-atom example also matters: full-law product rarity does not imply residual-sector deletion. Neither SA's ordinary stability nor a repaired RAR proves SEP automatically.

### 6.2 SP: the two-step, one-hole, one-window essential gap

**Literal SP: skipped. No proof of it occurs in the compared outline.** `rarity-repair` §7 asks for a positive infimum of `a_exit(w)-a_all(w)` over the original nonempty regular-top windows. The numerator fixes the actual singleton branch and crossing; the denominator retains every branch. SA has no corresponding excluded-line event or two-step window comparison.

Our `sp-decide` §2 does prove the restricted low-window case: when the displayed scale condition holds and `0 <= w <= epsilon/2`, a supported pure-gauge reference gives `a_all=0` and the hole gives gap at least `11T^2/24`. This is actual restricted progress, not merely a proposal. For the remaining windows the signed minimum shift remains. SA's absolute failed-test witnesses are analogous to the numerator's absolute hole cost; its reference-action cancellation suggests what must be added, but proves no SP statement. The change to local windows in `sp-nonlinear` §3 is explicit and does not settle the original global windows.

### 6.3 QR: the signed quadratic reserve

**Literal QR and its deleted-row inverse comparison: skipped. Quadratic positivity around a class reference: stated/outlined, but weaker.** QR is the bound

`|ell_p H_h y_*| + sqrt(max(0, <y_*,(G_0^{-1}-G_h^{-1})y_*>)) <= 1/2`.

It controls both the marked-plaquette value of the changed minimizer and the possible lowering of its minimum. SA's Proposition 2.4, stored Schur bound and nonnegative `q_X` (L17656–17662, L18052–18059, L18303–18313) do not contain this comparison of two constraint maps. Positivity of one Hessian cannot supply its signed second term.

`sp-decide` (4.3)–(5.2) derives the exact constrained quadratic minimum and the `T^2/8` reserve conditional on QR. `qr-decide` §§2–5 then proves the finite inverse perturbation and spreading inequalities, reducing QR to the geometric DC/RC bounds with explicit constants. This is useful machinery for checking a quadratic part of 2.8 after identifying the *actual* operators. It is not a proof of SA's nonabelian multiscale cancellation, nor unconditional QR for our literal routing.

### 6.4 RC and the routing

**Literal RC: skipped. A distinct shell-routing construction: outlined.** Our current is

`R(c)=q_c(avg_{R_c} J_cx - avg_{D_c} J_cx)`.

RC bounds its coefficient `l^1` norm, including the changed normalization and routed endpoint contours (`qr-decide` §7). SA L17598 specifies stable trees around nested boxes; L18363–18391 describes shell reference paths and spanning surfaces for loop estimates. `sp-nonlinear` §2 provides a **proposed explicit routing** for an admitted singleton cubical collar. It proves `||R(c)||_1 <= 12a/L^2` and an adequate displayed scale, but leaves literal collar identification and its dependence on L/T unresolved. ### 6.5 Local chart and supported reference

**SA states both kinds of input and outlines a local class construction; it does not prove our required whole-numerator comparison.** Totality (L17346), Proposition 2.4 (L17656), the reference law (L17604–17608), the triangular envelope (L18753–18789) and OH5 (L23483–23504) distinguish the relevant roles. A supported reference needs more than a formal stationary point: its denominator must be positive, its prescribed tests must pass, and the actual measure in its neighbourhood must be controlled.

Our present source is stronger than fresh-c's original stop in two limited respects. `sp-nonlinear` §5 gives a fixed local gauge/logarithm chart for every field below a specified *absolute* action cutoff, with total local Taylor errors. Its §6 constructs a supported all-lines reference and pays the high-action complement. However, it expressly leaves an energy-controlled global extension of the numerator and a **sharp** reference of energy at most `E(H_0 y)+1/64` open. Its crude supported reference cannot replace the sharp one. SA's class minimum and OH5 do not fill those gaps for our low-action, possibly topologically nontrivial histories.

The central-twist example in `sp-nonlinear` §4 rules out pricing a global identity-period-chart exit merely by positive action. It does not refute SA's nonwrapping box chart, which keeps exterior data and does not require a global trivial period. SA has a legitimate way to avoid that particular counterexample; its remaining domain inclusions still require proof.

### 6.6 AR: the absolute annular reset

**Literal AR: skipped. A weaker shell extension: outlined.** AR requires one constant `C_AR` independent of radius, a radius-dependent smallness threshold, exact agreement with U inside `B_(2r)`, identity outside `B_(3r)`, and control of the **sum** of all transition plaquette actions by the input annular action (`sp-nonlinear` §7).

SA L17583 and L18363–18391 instead uses an axial/shell extension with a pointwise curvature loss of order `C M^2` or `C s^2`, to make existence tests pass. Such a sup-norm extension is not the radius-uniform L2 action reset. Squaring a curvature loss and summing transition volume does not remove its radius dependence. Likewise the gauge gluing at L17664–17671 controls admitted potentials and differences, not AR for an arbitrary small-action annulus. No displayed argument supplies AR's constant or exact endpoint requirements.

AR is a proposed way to close our numerator extension. It is not proved necessary for 2.8: SA may instead close its specially prepared-shell comparison directly. Proving AR would still leave the sharp reference, routing admission and the signed comparison, as `sp-nonlinear` itself records.

## 7. Where our gate findings also apply, with quoted source locations

The shared defects below are **missing analytic steps in an asserted actual-history closure**, not counterexamples to the final Wilson conclusions. SA's explicit conditional OSF/OH deductions survive them. The zero-influence claim, growing-ball entropy absorption and `(1+p)^N <= 2` from fresh-c are not repeated in the corresponding SA arguments.

1. **Signed cancellation is still an obligation, not absolute energy positivity.** SA L17874 says “The ten resulting terms”; L17874–17889 describes their types and suppression without listing their actual expressions and domain verifications. This is the unfinished step of 2.8. The lesson of x gate D1 and c gate D3/D6 applies: a local coercive reference and a positive core cost do not alone compare two constrained actions. SA correctly writes the missing signed identity, so the defect is incomplete verification of that identity's controlled remainder, not omission of the reference subtraction. **Shortest repair, unproved:** expand the ten terms with their actual determining sets, weights and data, and bound their sum against the retained reference at every allowed evaluation. Do not replace this by another absolute lower bound.

2. **The actual conditional chart and its measure still have to be supplied.** SA L18773 says “Jacobian is bounded by”, followed by `J_0^{d_j}`; L18751–18789 uses pivot/triangular chart bounds to integrate the complete Gaussian envelope. This is the same kind of missing application identified in c gate D1/D2/D4: a Gaussian integral in declared coordinates is elementary, while admission, coverage, multiplicity and the actual determinant are model inputs.
