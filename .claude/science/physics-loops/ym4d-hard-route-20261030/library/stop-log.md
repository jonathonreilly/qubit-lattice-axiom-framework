stability budget is already stated for every stopped run at Y 95982–95992.

## The exact conditional input

## Where the logarithms go

             \sum_{j=0}^{k-1}(\log x_j)N_{j+1}+R_k,
\qquad |R_k|\le CN_0.
\tag{7}
$$

This is a stated vacuum requirement. It is **not** a bound by final
volume: $CN_0$ must not be replaced by $CN_k$. Nor should (7) be used
to estimate the common vacuum separately in the upper and lower bounds.

 \big[n_{j-1}(T_\ell)-n_{j-1}(Y_j)\big],
\quad
0\le n_{j-1}(T_\ell)-n_{j-1}(Y_j)
 \le3(L^4-1)\#_j(Y_j^c).

This is a **positive** cost in the normalized density. It is not
claimed to cancel. Missing activities and stage scalars have the

## C1, C3 and the mirror consumer

**C1.** The fixed radius and constant functions in the table are justified
as hypotheses by Y 96550, with the geometric realization at
Y 96075–96096. Uniformity at just one reference coupling in the short
D1 statement, Y 2359–2369, cannot replace that quantifier.

In particular `hi:one-zone-test`, Y 77391–77414, is a conditional test:

$$
|\Psi_\omega(z)|\le C|z|p_n n^\sharp(\omega),\qquad
|z|<r_n,\qquad r_n\le\frac{c\ell_{\min}}{2Cp_n}.
\tag{13}
$$

The last inequality is an **upper** limit on an admissible test disc.
It supplies no lower bound for $r_n$ and no fixed ordinary source ball.
The uniform ordinary source formation/domain input is still needed.
For that input the stated ordinary excess is $\bar f_*=C(1+\varrho)$,
with no accumulated level factor (Y 96513–96526).
One must not replace it by an arbitrary one-zone estimate and silently
identify its parameter with the physical source norm.

**C3.** E1 controls scalar increments and excludes dependence on the
unused cutoff (Y 2492–2500). The next coefficient is formed from the

For the entire history law one additionally needs Statement 3.8:
fixed supply rules, transported partitions, and common frame/weight
prefixes (Y 86054–86071; already SA 22042–22059).

and transported initial data. Equality of their prefixes gives equality
of the next formed coefficient and operation. Self-calibration then
sets the same next $\theta_j=x_j$. Induction identifies every shared
prefix. The terminal reference coupling only decides when this common
run stops. This is the conditional prefix argument; verifying its actual

and Gibbs states, and explicitly does not identify two RG constructions
