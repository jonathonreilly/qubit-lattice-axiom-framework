## N7 — Steelman

A hostile reviewer could argue that in an already derived local derivative
expansion, the affine subspace is the local Hessian sector by definition, so
its orthogonal complement is naturally “nonlocal.” That argument would defeat
this boundary if the repo supplied a retained operator theorem proving that
the chosen affine coordinate exhausts all local terms and that the complement
has a support/range meaning. The current scalar theorem supplies neither.
Thus the steelman identifies a valid future closure route but does not refute
the narrow statement about affine projection alone.
