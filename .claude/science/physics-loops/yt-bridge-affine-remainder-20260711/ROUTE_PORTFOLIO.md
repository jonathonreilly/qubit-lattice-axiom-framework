## Stuck fan-out synthesis

Five orthogonal frames were tested: axiom derivation, authority reuse,
universal transport algebra, interval certification, and negative
underdetermination. Only the universal transport route removes every observed,
fitted, and physical-boundary input while still deriving a nontrivial kernel
bound. It does not solve the action-to-transport bridge, so trace reachability
is partial rather than full physical closure.

## New structure

For

`y'=c y(-d y^2+G+e q)`,

the exact endpoint kernel obeys

`K'/K=2cdy^2`,

`K''/K=4c^2d y^2(G+eq)`.

The cancellation of the explicit source and gauge terms from the first
logarithmic derivative is the dramatic step. It gives a profile-independent
curvature estimate and a falsifier for the historical adjoint sign.
