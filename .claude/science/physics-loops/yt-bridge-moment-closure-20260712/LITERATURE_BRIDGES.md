# Literature Bridges

No literature review or external numerical input is used.  The proof is
self-contained finite-dimensional Pauli algebra plus elementary calculus.

The finite-dimensional Kubo/Duhamel first-variation formula is used as standard
mathematical infrastructure.  The runner instantiates and checks its
commutator kernel at matrix level but does not claim to derive the general
time-dependent first-variation formula.  No literature theorem or value is a
physics input.
