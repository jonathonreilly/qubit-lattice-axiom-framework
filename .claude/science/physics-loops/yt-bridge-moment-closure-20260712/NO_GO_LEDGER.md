## New exact boundary

`Lattice + Qubit + Admissibility + Record` admits an on-site qubit response kernel
`K(s)=sin(pi s)`.  The nonnegative profiles
`phi_±=1±(6s^2-6s+1)` have the same `(I_2,c_2)=(1,1/2)` but response
difference `4(pi^2-12)/pi^3 != 0`.  Therefore affine first-order response and
first-order two-moment closure are not derivable from the current axiom
surface.

Scope guard: this does not rule out a future positive theorem on an enlarged,
retained dynamics/source/readout surface.
