# Assumptions And Imports

## Explicit Inputs

- Primitive finite-record sources are real observables.
- The retained `hw=1` C3 basis is the real generation/record basis.
- `C` is represented by a real cyclic permutation matrix.
- Connected-source authority has already removed `B_a`.

## What If Wrong

- If the physical source can be an imaginary Hermitian generator in this record
  basis, `B_y` remains open.
- If the top-line assignment is not nontrivial, `B_x` still does not close Y_T.

## Forbidden Imports

No `H_unit`, old Ward route, `y_t_bare`, observed masses, PDG, `alpha_LM`,
plaquette/u0, Planck, alpha_s, or fitted selector is used.
