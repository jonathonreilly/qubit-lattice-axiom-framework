# No-go ledger

Prior durable boundaries:

- The exact Fierz result supplies a representation-dimension fraction, not a
  physical scalar normalization.
- The one-Higgs color-singlet insertion is proportional to `I_color`; a direct
  Hilbert-Schmidt interpretation gives singlet weight one, not zero.
- VEV subtraction changes a c-number expectation, not the color insertion.
- A connected color-matrix source quotient cannot be lifted to a scalar source
  whose only varied coordinate is `h` and whose color identity is fixed.
- Pole-row purity does not fix absolute scalar normalization.

New route under test: sharpen these into the superoperator identity
`rank(P_adj)/dim End(V) != ||P_adj(M_phi)||^2/||M_phi||^2` for
`M_phi proportional to I`.
