# Route portfolio

Five orthogonal first-principles attack frames were tested sequentially.

| Route | Hard-residual pressure | Proposed trace | Result |
|---|---:|---|---|
| SU(`N`) equivariant scalar-to-adjoint map | 3 | direct blocker closure | `Hom_SU(N)(1, adj)=0`; an invariant scalar insertion is `c I` and its adjoint projection vanishes. Selected artifact route. |
| Projector rank versus projector action | 3 | direct blocker closure | `Tr_super(P_adj)/N^2=(N^2-1)/N^2`, but `P_adj(I)=0`; the old step confuses a subspace rank fraction with evaluation on the physical operator. |
| Connected correlator / VEV subtraction | 2 | negative route pruning | `J(h)=hI` keeps `dJ/dh=I`; connected cumulants do not create a traceless source direction. |
| Isotropic operator-ensemble average | 2 | blocker isolation | `8/9` can arise as an average adjoint energy only after adding isotropic covariance on all of `End(V)`; that covariance is an extra, unsupplied premise and is incompatible with a source confined to `C I`. |
| Pole-residue / LSZ normalization | 2 | negative route pruning | rank-one pole purity and masses are invariant under scalar rescaling; a canonical same-surface kinetic/residue theorem is still required. |

The selected route is the combined equivariant-map and projector-rank/action
argument because it directly addresses the auditor's quoted class-F step and
is independent of phenomenological inputs.
