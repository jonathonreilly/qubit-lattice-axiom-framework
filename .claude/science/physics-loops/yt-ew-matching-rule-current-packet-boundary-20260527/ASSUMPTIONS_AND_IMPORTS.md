# Assumptions And Imports

## In Scope

- Exact finite color-channel algebra at `N_c = 3`.
- Current-packet inventory of selector routes `S1`, `S2`, `S3`.

## Out Of Scope

- Global finite-`N_c` non-exactness.
- Exhaustive no-go over all future selectors.
- External OZI theorem or glueball-spectrum computation.
- Observed `y_t`, `v`, `m_H`, or fitted empirical `R_conn`.
