# Assumptions And Imports

## Explicit Assumptions

- A1/A2 are treated as the current physical qubit-on-`Z^3` substrate.
- The transfer matrix has isolated finite-volume vacuum, top, and W pole rows
  on the source surface being considered.
- The same source coordinate `h` drives the top and W rows on the neutral
  radial carrier.
- The FH derivative of `-log(Lambda_X/Lambda_0)/a_t` is the pole-mass response.

## Conditional Import

- The physical top Yukawa deformation is the primitive RN/Fisher source
  intervention for normalized `O_top`.

## What If This Import Is Wrong

Then the lambda family remains:

```text
y_33(lambda) = lambda/sqrt(6).
```

The FH top/W ratio can still measure `lambda`, but the current proof route
does not select it.
