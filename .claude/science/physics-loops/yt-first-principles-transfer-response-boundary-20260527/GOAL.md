prove the transfer/Feynman-Hellmann pole-response bridge end to end and test
whether it forces the missing top coefficient.

Outcome:

- transfer pole-response identity is exact support;

  exact support;

  the top sector matrix element;

  `dM_t/dell = A/sqrt(12)` on the same surface as
  `dM_W/dell = g_2 A/2`.
