# No-Go Ledger

## Pole-Row Normalization No-Go

Status: no-go.

Statement: strict single-pole source-Higgs rows and Gram purity certify a
common pole, but they do not fix absolute scalar/source normalization.

Reason: under `s -> mu s` and `H -> lambda H`, all pole-residue equations and
the effective-mass ratio remain valid. The normalization ratio between
`K_Y(0)=8/9` and `K_Y(1)=1` is absorbed by `lambda^2=9/8`.

Scope: this does not rule out canonical `O_H`/LSZ derivation, W/Z response
bypass, direct top correlator measurement, or a future same-surface theorem.
