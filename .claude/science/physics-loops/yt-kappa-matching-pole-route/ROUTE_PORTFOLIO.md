normalization.

Verdict: blocked. The identity is invariant under `s -> mu s` and
`H -> lambda H`.

## Route 2: Effective Mass Selects Normalization

Hypothesis: the common exponential decay fixes the physical Yukawa readout.

Verdict: blocked. Effective mass uses `C(t)/C(t+1)` and cancels amplitudes.

## Route 3: Residue Ratio Selects kappa

Hypothesis: `Res(C_sH)/sqrt(Res(C_ss) Res(C_HH))` distinguishes the
connected-trace readout.

Verdict: blocked. For a rank-one pole this normalized ratio is one for all
operator normalizations.

## Route 4: Canonical LSZ Positive Route

normalization on the accepted source/action surface.

Verdict: still viable and now sharper. This block shows pole rows cannot
replace that theorem.
