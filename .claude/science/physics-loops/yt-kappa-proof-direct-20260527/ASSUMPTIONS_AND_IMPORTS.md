# Assumptions And Imports

## Explicit Assumptions

- Qubit/Cl(3) on `Z^3` is the active substrate premise.
- `Q_L` has six color-isospin components.
- The democratic unit vector on those six components has component amplitude
  `1/sqrt(6)`.
- The physical top Yukawa coefficient is a source/action or pole-response
  coefficient, not merely an operator-ray label.

## Open Assumptions

- The physical top Yukawa deformation is the primitive no-hidden-record source
  intervention targeting normalized `O_top`.
- Alternatively, an accepted finite same-surface top/W transfer/action backend
  exists and yields coefficient-bearing FH rows with no `kappa` input.

## Forbidden Proof Inputs

`H_unit`, `yt_ward_identity`, `y_t_bare`, observed top/W/Z masses, PDG targets,
fitted selectors, `alpha_LM`, plaquette/u0, Planck, and alpha_s are not allowed
as load-bearing proof inputs for local `kappa`.
