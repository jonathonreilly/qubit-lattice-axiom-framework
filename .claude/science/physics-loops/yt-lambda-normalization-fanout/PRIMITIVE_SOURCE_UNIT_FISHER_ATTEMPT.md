# Primitive Source-Unit Fisher Attempt

Target:

```text
prove lambda=1 for the physical top source.
```

Result: positive conditional support.

The primitive signed-record RN family

```text
R_h(epsilon)=exp(h epsilon)/cosh(h)
```

has origin score `epsilon` and Fisher information `1`. The scaled family

```text
R_h^lambda(epsilon)=exp(h lambda epsilon)/cosh(lambda h)
```

has score `lambda epsilon` and Fisher information `lambda^2`, so primitive
unit Fisher/source normalization forces `lambda=1`.

For the six-component normalized top trilinear, the same argument gives

```text
O_top=sum_i O_i/sqrt(6)
lambda O_top has Fisher norm lambda^2
unit source coordinate -> lambda=1
```

This proves the scalar unit once the source/action convention applies to the
physical top Yukawa deformation. It does not by itself derive that convention
from A1+A2; that remains the single open bridge.
