# Assumptions And Imports

## Explicit Assumptions

1. The local substrate is the current qubit-on-`Z^3` axiom memo.
2. The LSP projective theorem supplies ideal sharp-projective measurement
   readout, not a universal source-action rule.
3. Y_T signed records are local Pauli signed readouts.
4. The current source/action packet is bounded support.
5. The top/W Feynman-Hellmann response ratio algebra is support-closed.

## Implicit Assumptions Tested

1. Projective outcomes do not fix source-action units.
2. Normalized rays and projective probabilities can be invariant while action
   tangents scale by `lambda`.
3. A strict same-source response certificate can bypass source-coordinate
   rescaling if it measures both top and W pole responses on one surface.

## Imports Not Used

No `H_unit`, old Ward chain, `y_t_bare`, observed top/W mass, PDG target,
`alpha_LM`, plaquette/u0, Planck, alpha_s, or fitted selector is load-bearing.
