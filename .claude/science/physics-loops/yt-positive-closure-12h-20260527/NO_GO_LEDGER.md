# No-Go Ledger

- Formal transfer/Feynman-Hellmann first principles alone do not force
  `kappa`; sector matrix elements remain load-bearing.
- Current same-source/W-row/symbolic-top support cannot be promoted into a
  coefficient-certified top row by algebra alone; finite transfer
  counterfamilies vary `kappa`.
- C3 spectral projectors alone do not determine source responses; derivatives
  of `a(h), x(h), y(h)` are load-bearing.
- Unit/Fisher source normalization fixes source scale, not direction inside
  the C3 tangent space.
- LSP projective readout supplies instruments for supplied projectors; it does
  not select the C3 source tangent or top line.
- Positivity/orientation support selects C3 and an oriented splitter, not the
  physical `Y_T` source tangent.
- `B_x` source direction alone does not select the physical top coefficient:
  `P_0` gives `2/sqrt(6)`, nontrivial lines give `1/sqrt(6)`.
- Mass-ordering under `B_x` selects `P_0`, not a nontrivial line.
- New in this block: same-surface matrix-element factorization algebra alone
  does not certify the physical top row. It needs accepted generator
  factorization and nontrivial top-line authority.
