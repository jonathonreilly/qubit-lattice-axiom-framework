Cycle 14 prunes a narrow shortcut left open by the hard-boundary support
block. The prior artifact showed that nearest Fisher boundary selection from
the symmetric C3 RN/Fisher baseline would choose `P_nt` and conditionally feed
the `A/sqrt(12)` block-support theorem. The new no-go shows that the current
boundary geometry does not itself make nearest-face selection physical:
purity/minimum-rank, positive-source-asymptote, and response-maximum rules on
the same endpoint data select `P_0`.

This trace does not close the target. It only prevents future work from
promoting nearest-face support without an accepted physical readout theorem.
Positive closure still requires that theorem plus same-surface generator
factorization, another accepted zero-singlet top-block law, or accepted strict
same-source top/W pole rows with contact, FV/IR, and model-class controls.
