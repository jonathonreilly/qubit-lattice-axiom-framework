# Assumptions And Imports

## Used As Support

- `POSITIVITY_ORIENTATION_SELECTS_C3_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-23.md`
  supplies group-theory C3 subgroup selection under an admitted orientation
  criterion.
- `QUARK_C3_ORIENTED_WARD_SPLITTER_SUPPORT_NOTE_2026-04-28.md` supplies the
  C3-oriented Hermitian splitter normal form.
- Current Y_T C3 source-direction and LSP boundary packets supply the active
  blocker context.

## Not Imported

No observed masses, target coefficients, old Ward/H_unit route, plaquette/u0,
Planck, alpha_s, or fitted selector is used.

## What If Wrong

If orientation did close the source direction, the support packets would need
to derive not only `C3` or `B_y`, but the physical top line, source-generator
matrix element, and `1/sqrt(6)` coefficient. They do not.
