# Literature Bridges

No external literature is load-bearing in this block. The relevant fact is
standard finite-dimensional representation theory:

- the SU(N) fundamental is irreducible;
- any endomorphism commuting with all SU(N) transformations is scalar
  identity;
- traceless nonzero generators are adjoint insertions, not singlet insertions.

The runner uses an elementary torus-plus-permutation check instead of citing
Schur's lemma as an external authority.
