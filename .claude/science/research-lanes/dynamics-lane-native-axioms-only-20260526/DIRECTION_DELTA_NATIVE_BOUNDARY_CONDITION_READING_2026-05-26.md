## Question

Boundary conditions are a standard physics route for fixing continuous

## Boundary conditions native to A1+A2

The following are boundary-condition-class constraints in the retained
framework:

1. **C₃-orbit closure**: `C³ = I` (cyclic clock generator). Any function on
   the C₃ orbit transforms as `f(C·x) = ω^k·f(x)` for `k ∈ {0,1,2}` on the
   irreps. This fixes the DISCRETE structure (the C₃ characters at angles
   `0, 2π/3, 4π/3`); it does **not** constrain the CONTINUOUS offset `δ`.

2. **Cl(3) self-adjointness**: operators `O: Cl(3) → Cl(3)` are
   self-adjoint w.r.t. `⟨A,B⟩ = Tr(A†B)`. Constrains the operator class but
   has no resolution for the U(1) phase generated by the pseudoscalar `i`.

3. **CP-evenness (retained)**: real `D`, `θ = 0`; cosines only. This is a
   reality boundary — it forbids complex phases in the COEFFICIENT
   structure but doesn't fix the COSINE ARGUMENT.

4. **Koide cone (retained, lepton)**: `|z|/a₀ = 1/√2 ⟺ Q = 2/3`. This is a
   **radial** boundary on the C₃ irrep amplitude. The azimuthal `δ` is
   independent of the radial magnitude — fixing `Q` fixes `|z|` but not
   `arg(z)`.

5. **Hermiticity of the mass operator**: the lepton mass operator must be
   Hermitian (real eigenvalues `m_k`). For `m_k = 1 + √2·cos(2πk/3 + δ)`,
   this is automatic for any real `δ` — no constraint on `δ`'s value.

6. **Positivity of `m_k²`**: automatic since `m_k²` is a square. No
   constraint on `δ`.

7. **Unitarity of the C₃ action**: `C·C† = I`. Forces `|ω| = 1`; not a
   constraint on `δ`.

8. **C₃-twist boundary on Z³ (hypothetical)**: a global C₃ twist of the
   spatial Z³ lattice would impose phase quantization at C₃ characters.
   But this is the standard C₃ character story, giving `cos(2πk/3)` — not
   `cos(2πk/3 + δ)` with a free `δ`. Imposes nothing on `δ`.

## Direction δ result

**No native boundary condition in A1+A2 + retained content binds `δ`.**

The C₃ generation sector decomposes into:

- **Discrete part**: the irrep label `k ∈ {0,1,2}` and the corresponding

amplitude. It does **not** fix the **azimuthal** part. The `δ` parameter
is the azimuthal complement of the radial Koide constraint — they are
**independent kinematic data** in the retained framework.
