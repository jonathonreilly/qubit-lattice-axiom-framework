This is review metadata, not an audit verdict or a new physical premise.
The five companion notes state conditional mathematical models. The Qubit
axiom does not supply a single-particle amplitude model, a two-qubit site,
a tick, a spin soldering, or a physical species identification.

## No-Go Discipline Gate

### N1 — Distinct routes and actual disposition
1. Nonconstant determinant: the Laurent-unit argument and the absence of a
   fixed lattice vector exclude winding for the specified symmetry groups.
2. Smaller symmetry: the exact O/T/D4/D3 cell classifications and the D2
   product exhibit distinguish frozen, axial and three-directional motion.
3. Larger support: the exact range-two helper is executed. Range-three and
   higher claims are deferred to complete characteristic-zero certificates.
4. Different coin action or dimension: the three alternative soldering
   actions are checked on the cell. The two-qubit Clifford construction is
   an escape under a supplied larger coin. General irreducible-coin freeze
   remains a research task; absence of a spinorial summand is not absence
   of a tensor spinor factor with compensating projective flavor.
5. Blocking or continuous time: block-centred covariance admits the supplied
   eight-amplitude construction. Continuous-time walks are outside the
   finite-support single-tick classification. Nearest-site numerical
   failures do not establish a no-go.

Formation routes are separate: zeros in a rule, order-sensitive formation,
nonlocal dependence, infinite alphabets and observables of infinite support
are outside the finite positive local model. No claim is made that these
routes cannot produce correlations. The algebraic proof applies only to
normalized fixed-order sequential draws, and then to their common law.
