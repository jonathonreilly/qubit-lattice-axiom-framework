Pre-run criteria (written before any computation), bosonized-sites probe, 2026-10-26.

Construction under test (supplied, standard): sites are plain qubits (occupation Z_v); one link qubit per lattice bond;
bosonized edge operator A_e -> X_a X_b * L_e with L_e a BKSF-type link string, B_v -> Z_v; Gauss constraint
G_v = Z_v * prod_{e at v} Z_e. Flux values Phi_p = prod of bosonized A around a face are conserved register values.

(1) ALGEBRA (exact, GF(2) with phases). PASS iff on the single cube complex AND on the periodic 4^3 torus:
  (a) every bosonized A_e, B_v commutes with every G_v;
  (b) bosonized A/B pairwise (anti)commutation equals the Majorana-bilinear (anti)commutation for every pair;
  (c) face loop products of bosonized A commute with everything (conserved), and choosing the sector
      Phi_p = -(fermion loop value) reproduces the face phase -1 with uniform positive hops;
  (d) the copy sign s = i R1 R2 R3 R bosonizes to a G-commuting operator (even), path independent in the sector.
  FAIL of (a) or (b) => P1 dead.
(2) BAND. PASS iff (i) many-body spectra of bosonized R, cube tick exp(-i eps R) and s in the face-phase sector equal the
  fermionic ones (with face phase -1) on the single cube to < 1e-10; (ii) the periodic single-particle tick with the
  sector's signs has a Dirac point at K=0 with speed sin(eps)/sqrt3 (rel. err < 2% from finite differences) and the
  copy sign splits it into exactly one light copy with copy mass on; (iii) the zero-flux sector does NOT give R^2 = 1.
(3) PI-FLUX. Report E/site of the half-filled NN sea on 4^3 (min over 8 boundary twists) for pi, zero and >=300 random
  Z2 sectors. CONFIRM iff pi is lowest of all sampled. Honesty clause: the flux commutes with the tick; selection
  requires a least-energy (state-preparation) principle that must be named as supplied or not.
(4) KLEIN. Compute the gauge-invariant on-site Pauli algebra of a bosonized site and the commutant of the hop algebra.
  COMPATIBLE iff a Z2xZ2 with three nontrivial sectors commuting with the hop survives on physical states; BROKEN otherwise.
(5) RULE COUNT. Count clauses deleted vs added honestly (including new link qubits, vertex ordering choice, constraint).
Verdict: SIMPLIFIES AND WORKS iff (1),(2) pass and net clause count drops with no new supplied choice;
WORKS BUT NO SIMPLIFICATION iff (1),(2) pass but deletions are offset by new supplied items; FAILS iff (1) or (2) fails.
