Construction. Site qubit v: B_v -> Z_v (occupation record unchanged). One link qubit per bond e=(a,b):
A_ab = -i g_a g_b -> X_a X_b L_e, L_e = X_e * prod Z(bonds before e at a) * prod Z(bonds before e at b).
Gauss constraint G_v = Z_v * prod_{bonds at v} Z_e. Bond hop c^dag_a c_b + h.c. = (i/2) A_ab (B_a - B_b); longer

1. Theorem (exact, GF(2) with phases).
 (a) Every bosonized A_e, B_v commutes with every G_v (cube: 12+8 vs 8; 4^3: 192+64 vs 64).
 (b) The full (anti)commutation table of bosonized generators equals that of the Majorana bilinears
     (190 pairs on the cube, 32640 on 4^3; 0 mismatches).
 (c) Fermion face loops are scalars (+1); bosonized face loops Phi_p commute with every generator, so each Phi_p is a
     conserved Z2 register value; the bosonized algebra in sector {phi_p} is the fermion algebra with hop signs
     whose face products are phi_p. Face phase -1 <=> sector phi_p = -1 with uniform positive hops: no phase written.
 (d) The copy sign s = i R1R2R3R (R_i the unit axis hops) bosonizes to G-commuting path words; it is
     path independent within a fixed flux sector.
 (e) Commutant of the hop algebra in the Pauli group = the constraint group (G_v, face loops, 3 Wilson loops):
     cube 13 = 13, 4^3 193 = 193.
