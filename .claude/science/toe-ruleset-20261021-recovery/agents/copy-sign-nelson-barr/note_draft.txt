# CL-1's copy sign gives a tree-level Nelson-Barr block only if the light-doublet x mirror pairing is absent, and the copy sign does not forbid that pairing

Claim type: bounded_theorem. Labels: [T] exact, [F] finite diagnostic, [S] estimate or reading.
Runner: runner.py, TOTAL: PASS=14 FAIL=0, 3.9 s, 73 MB peak, 4.2 k characters, seeded, AUDIT_TIMEOUT_SEC = 120.
Tested under a unique filename (scratchpad copy_sign_nelson_barr_test_run.py, deleted). Axioms and CL text unchanged.
No rule adopted. Criteria: criteria_pre_run.txt, written before code.
Deviations from the pre-run criteria (results, not hidden):
- (1) The criterion "odd under all 12 kept antiunitaries" was ill-posed. The product of two kept antiunitaries is a
  unitary rotation, and the turn is among them. So an operator odd under all 12 is turn-neutral, and on Pi+ every
  s-odd operator is turn-charged. The strict count is 0. The operative test is breaking: the perturbed tick keeps
  none of the 24 T x R. That test is what B2 records.
- (1) Unplanned finding: s is NOT an exact symmetry of the tick. It commutes with U(K) only at K = 0 (A1).
- (3) The pre-run "portal" class (ii) was refined while coding. Multiplicative Hermitian dressings, including a
  real-symmetric portal matrix, give exactly zero theta (D1). Only flavour-generic real coefficient matrices give theta
  (D2).

## Question
Does s act as the NB Z2, with the s = -1 copy (the mirror) as the heavy vector-like partner? Then a spontaneous
T-odd, turn-breaking, s-odd vacuum would give J != 0 with theta-bar = 0 at tree level.

## 1. Cell operators at K = 0 [T]/[F]
- A1 [T]/[F]. Properties of s:
  - s^2 = 1, and s commutes with the turn tt;
  - on Pi+, s = -tau3: the light q 2 pair has s = +1 and the mirror-copy q 1 pair has s = -1;
  - s commutes with U(0) (1e-15), but [s, U(K)] grows as K^2 (1.7e-5 at |K| ~ 1e-2, 1.7e-7 at 1e-3) and is O(1) at
    generic K.
  So s is a label of the node, broken by the update at second order in K (dim-5 class). It is not an exact Z2.
- B1 [T]. The s-odd Hermitian operators on Pi+ form an 8-dim space (32 on the whole cell). They connect light q 2 to
  mirror q 1, so EVERY one carries turn charge (turn-neutral part 0). This is A0's requirement met automatically: a
  light-mirror s-odd vacuum carries family triality. Each family-fixing T x C2' splits the space 4 odd + 4 even.
- B2 [F]. Breaking. A generic s-odd Pi+ term, added symmetrically to the copy-mass tick, keeps NONE of the 24 T x R
  (lam 0.1 and 0.3). A purely T x C2'(1,-1,0)-odd term also keeps none. Control: a T x C2'(1,-1,0)-even s-odd term keeps
  exactly that element.
- So (1a) holds. s-odd, turn-charged cell operators exist that break every family-fixing antiunitary. The generalized-CP
  lemma (cp-source-new-tick) then no longer forces J = 0.
- Answer to P3's original "turn-neutral" request: on Pi+ there are none. s-odd implies turn-charged here, so the
  menu's falsifier wording ("no turn-neutral s-odd operator") would have killed M2 wrongly. Its update to
  "turn-charged" (A0) is the right one.

## 2. Free 3-family NB toy [T]/[F]
Supplied:
- light d (s = +1) and mirror D (s = -1, three copies, mass M x 1, locked as in spontaneous-family-breaking C);
- real light Yukawas m_u, m_d ~ 1e-2 M (seeded);
- F = (u a^T + v b^T)/sqrt2 in the D_L d_R slot, with real directions and spontaneous phases alpha, beta.

Results:
- C1 [T]. With the light_L x mirror_R slot G = 0, det M_full = det(m_d) M^3, so arg det lies in {0, pi} (max distance
  8e-14 over 300 seeds x 5 ratios). theta-bar_tree is 0 or pi. Which one is the sign of det m, a discrete choice
  (spontaneous-turn-cp B found pi in 17/40).
- C2 [F]. Integrating out the mirror gives m_eff m_eff^+ = m (1 + F^+F/M^2)^-1 m^T, which is complex. Median |J| is
  3.1e-4, 6.8e-4, 1.5e-3, 2.3e-3, 2.2e-3 at |a|/M = 0.3, 0.5, 1, 2, 3, so the criterion J > 1e-5 passes everywhere.
  These are O(1)-random textures; they are not compared with 3.08e-5.
- C3 [F]. J goes as (|a|/M)^2: 3.5e-7, 3.4e-5, 1.3e-3 at 0.01, 0.1, 1. This is NB's coincidence: |a| ~ M is needed.
- C4 [T]. One s-odd vector (b = 0) or equal phases gives |J| = 2e-17, because mirror rephasing removes the phase. Two
  s-odd fields with a relative spontaneous phase are needed.
  [T, analytic] With the turn and s, the relative-phase potential at renormalizable order is
  2 mu cos D + 2 lam cos 2D, plus D-independent terms (phi1* phi2 is turn-neutral and s-even). For lam > 0 and
  |mu| < 4 lam r1 r2 its minimum cos D = -mu/(4 lam r1 r2) is generic, so the CP breaking is genuine, not Z2-equivalent.
  The common phase is an accidental U(1) at this order (phi^3 is s-odd), so a pseudo-Goldstone appears. Not quantified.
- C5 [T]/[F]. THE STRUCTURAL GAP. The light x mirror singlet is unique (16 x 16bar has one singlet, A1). Its Spin(10)
  form pairs Q with Q' AND d^c with d'^c with one coefficient matrix, so G = F^T. Then:
  - theta_tree is O(1) (median 0.75 rad), so NB is lost;
  - at m = 0 only 1 of 3 light families stays massless (vs 3 with G = 0): the vacuum gives light doublets mass ~|a|.
  s does not forbid G. Both slots are s-odd, carry the same turn charge (2 - 1) and the same hand pairing. The NB zero
  therefore needs the s-odd vacuum to sit in a gauge-non-singlet direction of 16 x 16bar (a 45/210 piece that weights Q
  by zero and d^c by nonzero). That is a chosen gauge-structure direction, the analogue of spontaneous-turn-cp's
  hand-added Z2.

## 3. One-loop theta-bar [T]/[F]/[S]
The proxy is dM = eps x (s- and gauge-allowed products of tree matrices: m C2 F^+F in the light slot, m C1 F^+ in the
G slot, F C3 F^+ on the mirror), with real coefficient matrices C and eps = lam/16pi^2.
- D1 [T]. Aligned C = 1 keeps arg det real (2e-14). A generated G = m F^+ gives det = det m det(M - F F^+), which is real.
- D2 [F]. Flavour-generic real C gives theta linear in eps (ratio 10.0 per decade), with median slope 0.25 at
  |a| = M. theta < 1e-10 then needs lam <~ 6e-8. This is the standard NB quality problem (Dine-Draper, Vecchi; cited,
  not rederived). s does not solve it: its O(K^2) breaking (A1) adds a further, cutoff-suppressed source, which is
  not computed.

## 4. The 't Hooft vertex of the SMG-gapped mirror [T] + argument
Toy: Grassmann zero modes d_L, d_R, D_L, D_R (2 each), with the mirror gapped ONLY by a quartic vertex g e^{i phi_g}
(no bilinear).
- E1 [T]. With G = 0 the instanton amplitude is exactly det(m) x g, independent of F to all orders (|A(F) - A(0)| = 0).
  The d_L zero modes can only be soaked by m, which uses every d_R. So NB's F-independence SURVIVES an SMG-only mirror
  at tree level.
- E2 [T]. The vertex phase enters one-for-one: a phase shift of 0.700 for phi_g = 0.700. So theta-bar =
  theta - arg det m - phi_g x (number of vertices per instanton). With G != 0 a g-independent det F det G term appears
  (|dA| = 2.4), and F leaks.
- Argument [S]. The mirror is coloured, so the rotation that removes the vertex phase (far-wall-smg A4's "phases
  rotate away", shown in a colourless 1+1D proxy) is QCD-anomalous in 3+1D. It moves phi_g into theta instead of
  removing it.
  - phi_g is in {0, pi} iff the far-wall dynamics keeps an antiunitary that is odd on E.B. The W = 0 bulk keeps T
    (far-termination C1), and FREEZE-class layers keep T x C2'. Both are E.B-odd, so the tree vertex is real, up to a
    discrete pi.
  - The spontaneous s-odd vacuum breaks all such antiunitaries. It reaches the mirror amplitude only through loops
    (class 3, mirror self-energy F C3 F^+), so the vertex risk is a loop-level risk of the same lam <~ 1e-7 size, not
    a tree-level one.
  The question stays OPEN until the far-wall SMG is built.

## 5. Estimates [S]
- F1. Far-termination geometry. Light lives on the near wall and the mirror on the far wall, with overlap weight ~0.168
  per layer (far-termination N4, toy m0 = 0.6). At |a| = M, median |J| is 3e-6 at Ls = 4 and 1e-11 at Ls = 11, the depth
  that its Crab bound needs if the far layer carries a T-odd term. The overlap that suppresses the dispersion leak also
  suppresses F. NB needs the s-odd vacuum to live where both copies have weight, which is the cell picture (CL-1's copy
  mass, both copies on one cell) rather than the slab picture.
- Count tension (spontaneous-turn-cp result 1). The NB vacuum breaks the turn (B1) at |a| ~ M, the mirror-gap scale.
  But the N_f = 0 mod 3 anomaly argument needs the turn unbroken at the mirror-gap scale, i.e. v << Lambda_mirror.
  C3 says J falls as (|a|/M)^2, so a factor-10 hierarchy costs 1e-2 in J. Unresolved.
- Domain walls. Spontaneous Z3 (turn), Z2 (s) and T walls form, so the breaking must precede inflation (as in #10047).

## Answer
PARTIAL, and CONDITIONAL.
- What works:
  - s-odd, turn-charged vacua that break every family-fixing antiunitary exist on the cell (1a). They automatically
    carry the family triality A0 demands.
  - With the NB slot G = 0, the mirror acts as the heavy vector-like partner. theta-bar_tree is in {0, pi} exactly and
    J is unsuppressed for |a| ~ M.
  - The F-independence survives an SMG-only mirror (E1).
- What fails:
  - (i) s is not an exact Z2 of the update; it is broken at O(K^2), so the NB protection is approximate.
  - (ii) s does NOT forbid G. The unique 16 x 16bar singlet fills both slots (G = F^T), which gives O(1) theta and
    massive light doublets. The NB zero needs a chosen gauge-structure direction of the vacuum.
  - (iii) The one-loop quality bound is lam <~ 6e-8, the same as standard NB.
  - (iv) The SMG vertex phase enters theta-bar one-for-one. It is real at tree level only through the kept E.B-odd
    antiunitary of the far-wall dynamics, and is loop-exposed.
- So CL-1's copy sign supplies the heavy partner and the Z2 labels at the node, but not the forbidden slot. M2 does
  NOT yet remove the hand input that spontaneous-turn-cp needed. It replaces "hand-added Z2 + vector-like D" by "s at
  the node + a vacuum direction that skips Q x Q'".

## Route (not a wall)
Find a rule that puts the s-odd vacuum in the SU(2)-singlet slot only. Candidates, untested:
- a chirality-graded or polar-split reason that the doublet pairing needs a second hop while d^c x d'^c needs none;
- the far wall's extra (q2, +) 16, whose pairing with one (q1, -) could absorb Q';
- an s-odd vacuum built as a composite of right-handed components only.
Also compute the s-breaking O(K^2) term's contribution to theta-bar.
