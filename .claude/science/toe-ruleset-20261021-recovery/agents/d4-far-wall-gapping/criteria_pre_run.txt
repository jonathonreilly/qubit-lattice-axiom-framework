d4-far-wall-gapping: pre-registered criteria (written 2026-10-26 before any run of this probe)

Question: does the far-wall mirror gapping (Spin(12) Casimir on two 16s; Cayley completion of the charge-4 term)
carry over to the D4 matter law with the 4-mode spinor register per event?

(1) Symmetry. Build W(F4) (1152) with spinor lifts S (Spin(4) image, as in d4-zoom-chirality).
  PASS-1 if all of:
  a) every proper lift commutes with g5 and its chirality blocks have det 1 (to 1e-12), so the antisymmetric Weyl form
     eps is invariant and the 10-channel pair B_a = psi^T (M_a x eps) psi on the far-wall Weyl field is invariant;
  b) every improper lift anticommutes with g5 (maps the far-wall term to the opposite chirality = wall swap);
  c) det S = 1 and S unitary for all 1152, and the Fock lift of S commutes with the on-site Cayley 4-form on the 4 spinor
     modes (to 1e-12);
  d) W(F4)+ invariant counts on Sym^2 V, Sym^4 V, Sym^3 V x V equal the SO(4) counts (1, 1, 1): no dim-6 term allowed.
  FAIL-1 if any of a), c), d) fails.

(2) On-site spectrum with the D4 register. Far-wall Weyl field = 16 x 2 D4 spinor components (P- block).
  PASS-2 if: the operator identity (1/2) sum_a {B_a, B_a^+} = 144 - 2 S(S+1) - C2_10 - J^2 (S = Weyl-spin SU(2), the
  Lorentz SU(2) of that chirality) holds on N <= 2 and on the full N = 4 sector (sparse, 35960 states) to 1e-10, and sparse
  Lanczos in N = 4 gives the predicted lowest level; then (with the cited spin10-two-flavour rep theory) the unique
  Lorentz- and Spin(10)-singlet state E0 = -144 g, gap 4 g, survives. Full Dirac register: H_+ + H_- unique product, gap 4 g.
  FAIL-2 if the identity fails or the Lanczos level disagrees.

(3) Chain proxy. 1+1D root-line reduction of the D4 register: 4 spinor modes per event, Dirac hop i t psi_x^+ Gamma psi_{x+1}
  + h.c., Gamma = i gamma(u_t) gamma(n), on-site g Phi (Cayley) or g Re Omega; twisted boundary (pi/2,pi/2,-pi/2,-pi/2).
  Gate: spectrum equals the cayley-mirror-gap proxy chain (L = 4, all sectors) to 1e-9.
  Classify Delta(L) for L = 4..Lmax (Lmax = 6 live if memory-safe), merged with the embedded cayley-mirror-gap L = 7, 8:
   OPEN if Delta(Lmax)/Delta(4) >= 0.9; CLOSING if Delta(Lmax)/Delta(4) within 5% of the free ratio; else INCONCLUSIVE.

Verdict: GAPPING CARRIES OVER if PASS-1, PASS-2 and Cayley OPEN at some g on the D4 reduction;
         PARTIAL if PASS-1 and PASS-2 but (3) not OPEN, or the 3+1D far wall is untested (state which);
         FAILS if (1) or (2) fail.
