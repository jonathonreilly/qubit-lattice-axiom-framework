Question: does the adopted gravity sector (CL-3 hinge sum, tick fraction and pair tick
offsets as hinge-sum response variables, lapse in the step) carry over to the D4 event set
sliced along a root?

Carrier (fixed before running): D4 events, Delaunay 16-cell tiling, each 16-cell coned from its
centre (the deep holes = the Voronoi 24-cell vertices; same W(F4)-symmetric "D4 cone" as main's
#9714/#9772/#9774, up to rotation and scale). Flat edges: event-event roots |r| = sqrt2,

S = sum_tri A (2 pi - sum theta); metric reading = line-integrated h_ab d^a d^b/(2l);
non-metric edge modes integrated out (Schur complement) -> Q_eff(K).

    |c - 1| <= 1e-3 at ka = 0.05, both polarisations, >= 3 spatial directions; |c-1| ~ (ka)^2.

 N2 one G: G_N (static) / G_T (TT coefficient) = 1 within 2e-2.
