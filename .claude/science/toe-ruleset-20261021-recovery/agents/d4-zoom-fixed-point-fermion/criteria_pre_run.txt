d4-zoom-fixed-point-fermion: pre-registered criteria (written 2026-10-26 BEFORE any computation)

SYNTHESIS UNDER TEST
"Matter's history weight on the D4 event set is the fixed point of the D4 zoom (block averaging over D4 cells,
factor 2), sliced along a root."
Expectation stated by the dispatcher: W(F4) gives all-orders protection of dim-6 (no imposed radiative condition),
and the zoom removes the nearest-neighbour fermion's Lee-Wick pair, giving full reflection positivity.

PLANNED CONSTRUCTION (fixed before the run)
- Blocking kernel (factor 2, D4 -> 2 D4): coarse field = (1/16) sum_x w(x - 2X) phi_x, with w = 1 at 0, 1/2 on the
  24 roots (facets of the coarse Voronoi cell, shared by 2 cells), 1/8 on the 24 norm-4 vectors (vertices of the
  coarse Voronoi cell, shared by 8 cells). Fourier f(q) = w~(q)/16. This is the Voronoi-cell average with boundary
  sharing. Required: W(F4)-invariant (all 1152 elements), each of the 16 cosets D4/2D4 has total weight 1,
  f = 0 on pi D4* \ 2 pi D4*.
- Fixed point: G*(p) = sum_{l in 2 pi D4*} Pi(p+l)^2 (-i g.(p+l) + m)/((p+l)^2+m^2) + alpha, Pi(q) = prod_{j>=1} f(q/2^j).
  D* = G*^{-1}. alpha = 1/2 and 1 (inert for the spectrum).
- Prior expectation (support argument, stated before running): the limit smearing has support 2V (V = Voronoi cell),
  time half-width sqrt2 = 2 slice spacings, so the slice correlator is a pure positive exponential sum only for
  n >= 4; site RP on all n, m >= 1 is NOT guaranteed by the support argument. Expected: "buffered" exact positivity.

 1c W(F4) covariance rho(Ap) = A rho(p), sigma(Ap) = sigma(p) to < 1e-10 at random non-grid p.
 1d exponential locality of D*: fitted decay kappa > 0.5 per unit length along root and short directions.
 1e GW relation and gamma5-hermiticity to < 1e-12; overlap of D* with m0 = 1/(2 alpha) returns D* (< 1e-12).
 1f no doublers: G* finite and D* != 0 everywhere except p = 0 (min |D*| over grid minus origin > 0.1/alpha... reported).
 1g lowest band from the root-slice correlator equals sqrt(k^2+m^2) to < 1e-6 (axis, face, body, generic k; m = 0, 0.3).
 1h dim-6: k^2 coefficient of v - 1 < 1e-5 in all directions (consequence of 1g).

   Full RP if wrong-sign fraction < 1e-8 (numerical floor); buffered RP if PSD only for i,j >= 2.
 - Truncations R = 1..4 (D4 shells |x|^2 <= 2R) of D*: Laurent pole test (parents' machinery): number of complex

 Verdict for (2): WORKS if exact fixed point is full-RP AND some finite R has no complex pair and no ghost on all
 sampled k; PARTIAL if exact (or buffered) RP holds only at the fixed point and every finite truncation/rung keeps
 a complex pair or ghost; FAILS if the exact fixed point has a negative-norm or complex branch.
