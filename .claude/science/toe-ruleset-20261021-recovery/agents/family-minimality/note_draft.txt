# No adopted rule prefers the smallest anomaly-free family count: fewest changes is blind to content, the minimal FREEZE winding carries one copy, and the vacuum energy falls with each family

Claim type: bounded_theorem. Exact statements are labelled [T], finite diagnostics [F] and readings [S].
Runner: runner.py, TOTAL: PASS=8 FAIL=0, 0.3 s, 131 MB peak, 4.3 k characters of stdout, seeded, AUDIT_TIMEOUT_SEC = 120.
It was tested under a unique filename (scratch/family_minimality_test_run.py). Axioms unchanged. No rule adopted.

## Question

With a FREEZE-class tick (nonzero turn-graded winding) the far-wall bookkeeping of floquet-pi-slab allows N_f in
{3, 6, 9, ...}. slab-parity showed that the odd layer count is not forced. Even granting it, does any ADOPTED principle prefer
the smallest anomaly-free count, with no 3 put in? Three candidates were tested:
- (a) CL-2's fewest changes / least information (the info-min-law paragraph);
- (b) the FREEZE-class winding itself: does the winding of one tick fix how many copies its wall carries?
- (c) the vacuum energy of the mirror-gapped far wall: is it monotone in N_f, and does a stability criterion bound N_f?

Repo first. A git grep of origin/main (docs, scripts) for "smallest anomaly-free", "minimal family count", "family-count
minimality" and "minimal number of families" found nothing, and no open PR covers it. This note builds on floquet-pi-slab
(D1 index), slab-parity (C1: the adopted tick has W = 0), anomaly-family-count (Omega as a per-family 16 pairing),
freeze-mirror-smg (A, C1: bare-label term thresholds) and the register's CL-2 and CL-3 texts.

## Pass criteria

The criteria were written before any code ran (criteria_pre_run.txt). PASS = an adopted rule selects N_f = 3 with no 3
inserted. Predicted: FAIL for (a), (b) and (c). Every line matched its prediction.

## A. CL-2 fewest changes

- A1 [T]/[F]. For independent families (product odds and product support) the fewest-changes transport cost is additive:
  c(N) = N c(1). The product of optimal couplings attains N c(1), and any coupling's expected changes are at least the sum of
  its marginal couplings'. Checked by LP on a seeded two-mode family: c(N)/N = 0.4240 for N = 1, 2, 3.
- A2 [T]. In a stationary vacuum (odds unchanged by the half-tick) staying has zero changes, so CL-2's cost is 0 for every
  N_f. The vacuum cannot rank family counts.
- A3 [S]. CL-2's text prices the transport of a given content under a given half-tick. Through a passing it "does not count
  new modes". The axioms put one qubit per site, so families are composite cell copies (composite before atomic). No clause
  of CL-2, or of CL-1, which supplies the cell content, prices their number. N_f is a supplied multiplicity.
- A4 [T]. Extended reading, not adopted: "the content minimises expected changes".
  - Over the anomaly-free set its minimiser is N_f = 0 (no matter).
  - 3 appears only with a supplied "N_f >= 1", and only on the 16-pair Omega route.
  - With FREEZE's bare-label charge-4 term (freeze-mirror-smg: it exists only for N_f >= 4, and Spin-Z4 needs
    2 N_f = 0 mod 16) the smallest count is 8, and 24 if the turn mod 3 also applied.
  - So even the extended minimality is route-dependent.

## B. FREEZE-class winding

- B1 [T]. A per-family node pair (light with hand +, mirror with hand -) has winding f(q,+) + f(q',-).
  - For q = q' this is 0.
  - For q != q' it is exactly one of the six vectors +-(3 e_a - (1,1,1)), all of L1 norm 4.
  - W_F = (-2, 1, 1) is one of them. So FREEZE already has the minimal nonzero per-family winding of its class, and no
    smaller FREEZE-class tick exists.
- B2 [F]. Take the odd (Ls = 7) slab with this minimal-winding tick.
  - The near wall holds exactly one Weyl doublet at (K = 0, 0) and one at (R, pi), each 2 states in turn sectors (1, w),
    i.e. q 2. The far wall holds the q 1 doublet, in sectors (1, w^2).
  - So the near wall carries ONE light copy in total across sectors. The pi state is FREEZE's own pinned partner.
  - The three turn sectors are turn charges, not copies.
- B3 [T]. N_f stacked identical families give W_tot = N_f W_F, checked at N_f = 2 and 3.
  - W_F = (1, 1, 1) mod 3, so the anomaly-free set of floquet-pi-slab D1 is exactly "W_tot = 0 mod 3 in Z[Z3]"
    (both are {3, 6, 9, 12} on 1..12).
  - A "minimal nonzero winding" rule therefore picks N_f = 1, which is anomalous.
  - "Minimal anomaly-free winding" (W_tot = 3 W_F) is the supplied minimality restated in winding language.
  - CL-1's adopted tick has W = 0 (slab-parity C1), so no adopted tick carries any of this.

## C. Vacuum energy of the gapped far wall

- C1 [T]/[F]. Reduced analogue: Omega' = sum eps_ijkl B_ij B_kl, with B_ij the antisymmetric pair of two 4s. It has charge 4
  and is SU(4)-invariant (both exact).
  - H = -(Omega' + h.c.) on 8 modes has a unique ground state at E0 = -3.4641 and a gap of 1.4641.
  - Two decoupled families (Kronecker sum, 2^16 sparse) give E0(2) = -6.9282 = 2 E0(1), with the same gap.
  - This is an analogue only. The real 16 Omega (2^32 per family) is not diagonalised.
- C2 [T]. With a family-diagonal mirror term, E0(N) = N E0(1) is strictly DECREASING in N_f and the gap is independent of
  N_f. anomaly-family-count's Omega is a per-family pairing inside a 16, so it is family-diagonal.
  - Energy minimisation would therefore prefer MORE families, without bound, and no stability bound arises.
  - CL-3 weighs only departures from the vacuum, and its Hamiltonian constraint subtracts the vacuum phase. So the far
    wall's vacuum energy carries no weight in the adopted rules at all.

## Answer

FAIL, as predicted. No adopted rule selects N_f = 3.
- (a) CL-2 prices transport, not content. It is exactly zero in the vacuum for every N_f and additive off it. Extended to
  content selection it would pick N_f = 0, and the smallest nonzero count it gives depends on the supplied gapping route
  (3 or 8).
- (b) The minimal FREEZE-class winding puts one copy on each wall. The winding does not fix N_f. Anomaly freedom is the
  divisibility W_tot = 0 mod 3, and "smallest" is not in any clause.
- (c) The vacuum energy falls linearly with each family and the gap does not depend on N_f. The direction is wrong, there
  is no bound, and CL-3 gives the vacuum no weight.

What would select 3 (proposal space, untested): a rule that minimises total turn winding subject to anomaly freedom and
nonzero matter. Its only content is "smallest", and it fails gate item 1 in spirit unless it predicts something else. The
measured N_nu = 2.984 +- 0.008 (given light neutrinos in every family) remains the only selector of 3 over 6.

## Gate (anti-smuggling)

No rule is proposed. All three candidates were tested as adopted (or as explicitly flagged extensions of adopted) rules, and
none inserts a 3.
