# Zoom rungs of the spin-3/2 Margolus tick give no three distinct light copies

Claim type: bounded_theorem.
Primary runner: `scripts/zoom_rungs_of_the_spin_three_halves_margolus_tick_give_no_three_distinct_light_copies_2026_10_21.py`.

The [candidate-law register](CANDIDATE_LAWS.md) has zero premise weight, and no law there produces fermion generations. This note tests whether generations can arise from zooming: copies of a light mode appearing at the one-block rung or at the coarse-cell rung of a supplied tick. The [minimal axioms](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md) choose none of the ticks used. The result is negative. In the current tick no rule-level mechanism gives three non-degenerate light copies, let alone the measured hierarchy. Every check passes, and several PASS lines record that a candidate mechanism fails against measurement.

## Statement

1. One block. For rotation-covariant multi-step Margolus ticks, every level at K = 0 and at K = pi(1,1,1) has multiplicity 4k. Copies come in pairs, so an odd count such as 3 cannot occur at this rung.
2. Coarse cell. The fine corners {0, pi}^3 fold to coarse K = 0 in rotation orbits of sizes 1, 3, 3, 1, indexed by the number n of components equal to pi. Every triplet is exactly degenerate. For the mass tick W(mu), cos w_n = cos^2(mu/2)(3-2n)/3 - sin^2(mu/2); at mu = 0.1 the orbit levels are 0.100, 1.2345, 1.9124 and pi, so the triplets sit at the cutoff.
3. Zoom. Two fine ticks make one coarse tick with coarse momentum 2K, and the coarse gap is twice the fine gap per coarse tick. The physical mass is the same at every rung, so zooming makes no new light copy.
4. One copy per orbit. For the family cos w = A S - B, cos w_n is affine in n, so cos w0 - 2 cos w1 + cos w2 = 0. This has no root with w1/w0 = 206.77 and w2/w1 = 16.817 when w2 is at most pi. In the small-w limit the ratio w2/w1 tends to sqrt 2.
5. Copy symmetry. Under S3 (3 = A1 + E) the commutant is span{1, J} and the masses are {a+2d, a-d, a-d}, never three distinct values. Proper cube rotations include axis transpositions, so a copy set permuted by the spatial axes is forced into this degenerate pair. C3 alone gives circulants a + bP + b*P^2, three parameters for three masses and no prediction. A real structure on C3 again forces a degenerate pair; three distinct masses need a complex phase that is not a multiple of pi/3.

## Derivation

Items 1 and 2 follow from Schur's lemma for the 24 spin-3/2 corner actions: their commutant on a block has dimension 4, and the orbit of a fine corner under rotation is an orbit of identical spectra. Item 3 follows because two applications of W(mu) at momentum K equal one coarse step at 2K with the gap doubled per coarse tick. Item 4 is the second difference of an affine function of n. Item 5 is the commutant of the permutation representation of S3 or C3 on three copies. On the square-root-mass circulant, the Koide ratio is Q = 1/3 + (2/3)|b|^2/a^2, independent of the phase.

## Checks

The runner prints 18 PASS or FAIL lines, and its output ends `TOTAL: PASS=18 FAIL=0`. Section A uses the supplied tick:

- the commutant of the 24 spin-3/2 corner actions has dimension 4;
- 30 random covariant multi-step ticks show only multiplicity 4 at K = 0 and K = pi(1,1,1);
- the coarse-cell orbit sizes 1, 3, 3, 1 and identical spectra within each orbit;
- the W(mu) coarse-cell levels match the closed form (deviation 1.3e-15), giving 0.1000, 1.2345, 1.9124 and 3.1416 at mu = 0.1;
- one zoom step gives a coarse gap of 0.20000 against 2 mu = 0.2;
- the A S - B family has no root carrying the lepton ratios: the second difference is negative on (0, pi/16.817] and behaves as -140 w1^2 near 0. This PASS records a failed mechanism.

Section B checks the copy-symmetry algebra: the S3 commutant has dimension 2, the C3 commutant has dimension 3 and reconstructs any three masses (for the leptons |b|/a = 0.707100 and phase 0.222230 rad modulo 2 pi/3), a real structure forces a degenerate pair, and Q is independent of the phase on the circulant. It then compares with measurement. Lepton Q is within 1 sigma of 2/3 (0.666661 +- 0.000007), while up-type (0.849) and down-type (0.731) quark Q is not. This PASS records that no species-blind Q = 2/3 mechanism fits.

Section C checks rung ladders against measured masses. Each PASS here records a failed mechanism:

- a rung-independent rule forces a constant ratio, but the lepton ratios differ by a factor of 12.30;
- a geometric ladder obeys Koide only at a ratio of (23+5 sqrt 21)/2 = 22.96 per rung;
- suppression m ~ exp(-c 2^n) predicts a log-ratio of 2, against estimates of 1.889, 1.298 and 0.788 for leptons, up and down quarks;
- a universal two-parameter rung map fitted to the leptons (c = 0.5294, m* = 42.5 GeV) misses every quark step by a factor between 2.5 and 25 (0.18, 0.04, 3.65 and 0.40 times the measured value for c from u, t from c, s from d and b from s).

Section D checks mixing. Each PASS here records a failed mechanism. If up and down quarks are both circulant in one copy basis, the CKM matrix is a permutation, with |V_us| = 0 against 0.22500 +- 0.00067. Circulant charged leptons with diagonal neutrinos give sin^2 theta13 = 1/3, against 0.0220.

Section E is a reference check. The Brannen form with r = 1/2 and delta = 2/9 reproduces both lepton ratios within 0.1 percent (206.770 against 206.768, and 16.8180 against 16.8170). It uses two supplied constants for two ratios, and no tick here produces either. The printed Koide estimate of the tau mass from the electron and muon is 1776.97 MeV, against 1776.86 +- 0.12.

## What is supplied and the boundary

Supplied: the tick, the spin-3/2 corner action, the two partitions, the mass parameter, the assignment of one copy per rung or orbit, and measured PDG and NuFIT central values. Quark masses mix renormalisation schemes, but every quark failure is at the factor-of-2 level or worse. The orbit no-go in item 4 covers only the A S - B family, not every covariant tick.

Not established: any tick-level mechanism giving three non-degenerate light copies, the Koide r, its phase delta, or mixing. The coarse-cell rung supplies the count 3 and no rung supplies the hierarchy. A rule that gave three distinct copies would need an odd copy structure, a C3 with a complex phase, and a misalignment between the up and down bases, without breaking spatial isotropy. Such a rule is not built or tested here.
