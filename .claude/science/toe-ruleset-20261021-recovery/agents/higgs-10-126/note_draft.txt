# The Spin(10) gapping term leaves the 126 channel free, so 10 + 126 Yukawas are unfixed and give no non-fitted mass relation

Claim type: bounded_theorem. Labels: [T] exact, [F] finite diagnostic, [E] estimate against measurement.
Runner: runner.py, TOTAL: PASS=11 FAIL=0, 3 s, 290 MB peak, 4.1 k characters of stdout, seeded, AUDIT_TIMEOUT_SEC = 120.
It was run under a unique filename (scratch/higgs_10_126_test_run.py). Axioms unchanged. No rule adopted.

## Question

higgs-gapping-channel showed that if the Yukawa source is only the forced 10 channel, then |V_CKM| = 1, which is
falsified. In the minimal SO(10) escape, 16 x 16 = 10 + 126 (+ 120) with both 10 and 126bar Yukawas. This note asks:
- Is the 126 channel present in the rules, or forced by them, with a fixed ratio to the 10?
- If it is, does that fixed ratio give a mass relation that was not fitted?
- What can be tested without family structure?

Repo first. I searched origin/main for prior 10 + 126 or Georgi-Jarlskog work and found none. This note builds on:
- spin10-two-flavour: the so(12) identity, F = 144 - 2S(S+1) - C2_10 - J^2, and the unique ground state;
- higgs-gapping-channel: the oscillator 16, the SM reading of weights, and the single-10 exclusion;
- spin10-breaking: the 16 x 16 Casimirs 9, 21, 25;
- composite-sixteen: exchange K^2 = 64, 16, 0 on 10, 120, 126, so the 126 is also inert there, which is consistent with
  the result here.

## A. Invariant four-fermion terms on two 16s (exact)

- A1 [T]. The matrices C Gamma^(1), C Gamma^(3) and C Gamma^(5) restricted to the 16 span the 10, the 120 and the 126.
  - Their ranks are 10, 120 and 126.
  - The (1) and (5) forms are symmetric and the (3) form is antisymmetric.
  - So Sym^2 16 = 10 + 126 and Lambda^2 16 = 120.
- A2 [T]. Pauli statistics ties flavour symmetry to the Spin(10) channel. The decomposition is
  Lambda^2(32) = (10, S=0) + (126, S=0) + (120, S=1), with dimensions 10 + 126 + 360 = 496 and C2_10 = 9, 25, 21.
  - Every summand appears once, so there are exactly THREE independent number-conserving invariant quartics.
  - In terms of the pair-count operators Q_r = sum_{i<j} Pi_r(ij), these are g_10 Q_10 + g_126 Q_126 + g_120 Q_120.
- A3 [T]. Each of the three is a Casimir function. These were checked as operator identities on the 496 pair states:
  - Q_10 = -(C2_10 - 45N/4)/16 - (S^2 - 3N/4)/8 - N(N-1)/64;
  - Q_126 = (C2_10 - 45N/4)/16 - 3(S^2 - 3N/4)/8 + 9N(N-1)/64;
  - Q_120 = N(N-1)/2 - Q_10 - Q_126.
  With Tr M_a M_b^+ = 16 delta_ab, the combination 32 Q_10 - 20 J equals 2[144 - 2S(S+1) - C2_10 - J^2]. That is the
  spin10-two-flavour identity, rederived here.
- A4 [T]. ANSWER TO (1): the forced term -(g/2) sum {B_a, B_a^+} = -16 g Q_10 + (one-body terms) has coefficients
  (-16 g, 0, 0) on (Q_10, Q_126, Q_120).
  - At fixed N, Q_10 = N(N-1)/2 - Q_126 - Q_120, so the forced term equals +16 g (Q_126 + Q_120) + f(N).
  - So the 126 is "present" only in this Fierz-trivial sense. It is repulsive, and its weight is exactly equal to the
    120's.
  - At fixed N the physical couplings are the two differences. The forced term fixes g_10 - g_120 = g and
    g_126 - g_120 = 0.
  - Any attractive 126 channel needs the 126-120 splitting to be nonzero. That splitting is a free invariant which no
    rule sets, and the forced term sets it to zero.
  - On the "fewest change" ordering: the 10 is also the lowest-Casimir channel (9 < 21 < 25), and the 126 is the
    highest. Nothing in the rules prefers it.
- A5 [T]. Charge-changing terms.
  - The Z4 centre acts as 16 -> i 16, so it forbids Delta N = +-2 (the same reason 16 x 16 has no singlet).
  - Delta N = 4 has exactly one invariant, the 10-channel Omega. The reasons:
    - (psi Gamma psi)^2 = 0 for commuting psi (the pure-spinor identity, error 4e-16), so Sym^4 16 has no singlet;
    - the antisymmetrised 126 square vanishes identically;
    - the 120 square is proportional to the 10 one (rank 1, invariance error 1e-15).
  - So even the non-conserving next term carries no independent 126.
- Next order (six-fermion and higher) is not computed. Every quartic is already a Casimir function, so higher terms that
  are polynomials in the Casimirs add couplings without fixing a 126/10 ratio.

## B. Levels (exact)

- B1 [T]. B_r^+|GS> carries good S, C2_10 and J quantum numbers, so it is an exact eigenstate of H = -g F whenever it is
  nonzero. The levels above the ground state are:
  - the 10 at 10 g;
  - the 126 and the 120 at 26 g each, degenerate (the A4 statement seen in the spectrum).
- They are nonzero for two reasons:
  - the ground-state pair counts are Q_10 : Q_126 : Q_120 = 9 : 27 : 84 (sum 120);
  - sum [B_r, B_r^+] is proportional to J, which is 0 at the ground state (Schur on the irreducible 32).
- So the 126 composite exists, but it is heavier than the 10 and not bound by any attractive channel. The forced term
  gives it no Yukawa, Hubbard-Stratonovich boson or condensate.

## C. The Clebsch -3 (exact)

- C1 [T]. At the neutral-doublet weight, the 126 has weight multiplicity 3, of which exactly one direction is a colour
  singlet, for each hypercharge.
  - Relative to the 10, its (e,e^c):(d,d^c) and (nu,nu^c):(u,u^c) entries carry the factor -3, with the ratio of ratios
    equal to -3.000 for both H_d and H_u, and equal over the three colours.
  - This is the Georgi-Jarlskog / Babu-Mohapatra Clebsch, derived from the explicit oscillator 16 rather than quoted.

## D. Mass sum rules and counting

The mass sum rules use the supplied Babu-Mohapatra form: M_d = H + F, M_e = H - 3F, M_u = r(H + sF), M_nuD = r(H - 3sF).
- D1 [T]. Suppose each family's mirror carries its own 10 and 126 channels, with any couplings and VEVs. Then M_u and M_d
  are both diagonal and |V_CKM| is a permutation. ADDING THE 126 DOES NOT REPAIR MIXING UNLESS THE CHANNELS ARE
  FAMILY-OFF-DIAGONAL, and family structure is the families wall.
- D2 [F]. Generic complex case.
  - Parameters: H real diagonal (3), F complex symmetric (12), r (1) and s (2), so 18 real.
  - The Jacobian of the 13 charged observables (9 masses, 3 CKM moduli, J_CP) has rank 13 at 5 seeded points.
  - So there is no local relation among charged-fermion observables: the fit has no charged-sector prediction.
  - The neutrino sector (126 VEVs giving type I/II seesaw) adds 2 to 4 parameters against 5 measured leptonic numbers.
    The literature's predictions there (for example theta_13 and delta from b-tau convergence, Bajc-Senjanovic-Vissani)
    are fits with supplied textures. They are cited, not computed.
- D3 [F]. With real (CP-conserving) couplings there are 11 parameters and rank 11 < 13, so two relations exist
  (J_CP = 0 is one of them). The rules force neither real couplings nor any value of Y10, Y126, r or s.
  - The forced term has <B> = 0 exactly, so the rules supply no VEV of either channel.
  - Every VEV ratio is a free supplied number.
- D4 [E]. ANSWER TO (3), testable without families. The only family-blind content is the Clebsch. A family whose masses
  come from a single channel must have m_e/m_d = 1 (pure 10) or 3 (pure 126).
  - Supplied SM running values at 2e16 GeV (Xing-Zhang-Zhou 2008) give m_e/m_d = 0.41, m_mu/m_s = 4.57, m_tau/m_b = 1.69.
    Each is more than 25% from both 1 and 3, so NO family is single-channel.
  - The Georgi-Jarlskog texture (1/3, 3, 1) is a supplied family texture, not derived. It deviates by 24%, 52% and 69%
    under SM running. Supersymmetric running brings it closer, per the literature; this is not computed here.
  - With two channels per family (h_i, f_i), any pair (m_d, m_e) fits, so per-family ratios test nothing.

## Verdict

1. The 126 is not forced. The general invariant has a free 126 coefficient (the 126-120 splitting). The forced term sets
   that splitting to zero, which leaves the 126 repulsive and degenerate with the 120 (26 g). There is no fixed Y126/Y10.
2. The 10 + 126 sum rules make CKM != 0 possible only with family-off-diagonal channels, and the rules have no family
   structure (D1). Even then, 18 parameters cover the 13 charged observables at full rank (D2). The rules fix none of
   Y10, Y126 or the VEV ratios.
3. No falsifiable non-fitted mass relation exists. The rule "Yukawas = 10 + 126 of the forced term" reduces to the
   single-10 rule (falsified upstream) unless a free 126 coupling is added. With that coupling added it is UNTESTED: the
   Clebsch -3 is forced, but every observable use of it needs a family texture.

Next-wall pointer (proposal only): a 126 Yukawa needs both a rule that splits the 126 from the 120 attractively and a
family-off-diagonal channel. Both route back to the families wall. The cleanest family-blind test left is the forced
Clebsch -3 in any family-local rule. It already fails for every family (D4), so a family-local rule of either channel
type is excluded by the GUT-scale ratios as well as by CKM.
