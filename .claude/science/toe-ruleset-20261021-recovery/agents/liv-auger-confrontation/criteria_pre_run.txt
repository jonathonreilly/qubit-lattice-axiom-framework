Pre-run criteria (written 2026-10-26 before runner.py was written or run)

Quantity: R = |delta_pred| / |delta_bound|, where delta_pred is the rule-fixed proton coefficient
converted to the Auger convention E^2 - p^2 = m^2 + delta_{had,2} E^4 (eV^-2), and delta_bound = 1e-57 eV^-2
(Auger 2022, arXiv:2112.06773, 5 sigma). R is evaluated for (a) the sky average over directions and
(b) the least-constrained direction (smallest |c_p|), with the parton weighting at both a low scale
(2 GeV) and the cell scale (LO evolution).

Decision rule, fixed in advance:
- The bound applies only if the predicted sign lies in the sign range the bound was derived for, and
  under the composition model it assumed. If the sign is outside that range, the comparison is "not covered",
  and the verdict is taken from the bound with the matching sign if one exists (else CONSISTENT, no bound).
- EXCLUDED: sign covered, and R >= 10 for the sky average AND R >= 10 in the least-constrained
  direction, at both weighting scales. The Auger n = 2 number is an order-of-magnitude rescaling
  (their Eq. 6.4), so a factor-10 margin is required.
- IN TENSION: sign covered and 1 < R < 10, OR R >= 10 only for some directions or one weighting scale,
  OR exclusion only under an assumption the data disfavour (e.g. pure-proton composition).
- CONSISTENT: R < 1 everywhere, or no bound applies to the predicted sign/order.
Any new kinematic consequence found on the way (e.g. composite vacuum emission) is reported
separately as [E], with its own verdict against the data, never folded silently into R.
