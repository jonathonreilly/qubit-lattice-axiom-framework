Question: is the U(1)_Y covariant anomaly density of the one-family 16 the lattice divergence d*j of a LOCAL,
gauge-invariant current j (Luscher's locality condition, tested in momentum space), while a lone L doublet's is
not? Follow-up to luscher-local-current (4^4: finite-range fits inconclusive).

Set-up (fixed before running):
- Kernel copied from overlap-nonabelian / luscher-local-current: Wilson kernel r = 1, m0 = 1.6, H = g5 X,
  D = 1 + g5 sign H, q_Y(x) = tr sign H_Y(x,x); A = sum m Y q_Y; 16 = Q (6, 1/6), L (2, -1/2), u^c (3, -2/3),
  d^c (3, 1/3), e^c (1, 1); lone doublet = L alone.
- Background class: AXIS-RESTRICTED factorised U(1) fields, U1 = U3 = 1, U2 = exp(i th2(x1)), U4 = exp(i th4(x3)).
  Field strengths F12 = F(x1) = th2(x1+1) - th2(x1), F34 = G(x3). H commutes with translations in x2, x4, so it is
  EXACTLY block diagonal in (p2, p4): L^2 blocks of dimension 4 L^2. sign H is computed EXACTLY per block (dense
  eigh), which replaces the sparse/Zolotarev approximation proposed in the earlier note (no rational error).
  Validation control: block method = full dense 4D method on 4^4 to 1e-12.
- Lattices: L = 6, 8, 10, 12, 16 (20 if time allows) on the Mac mini; L = 6 re-verified in the laptop runner.

Measurements:
- M1 (order-2 kernel, one charge Y = 1): q2 = sum Pi(x1-y, x3-z) th2(y) th4(z) from random-phase th2, th4
  (all momenta incl. zero modes), 4-sign combination + Richardson in eps (eps = 0.02, 0.04). F-kernel
  Q~(P1,P3) = Pi~ / ((e^{iP1}-1)(e^{iP3}-1)); position kernel Q(a,b) by exact partial sums of Pi;
  gamma_meas = sum_ab Q (zero-momentum value). Luscher-split current kernels of Q - gamma delta:
  K1(a,b) = sum_{a'<=a} [Q(a',b) - delta_{a'0} R(b)], K3(a,b) = delta_{a0} sum_{b'<=b} [R(b') - gamma delta_{b'0}],
  R(b) = sum_a Q(a,b) (so d*K = Q - gamma delta exactly).
- M2 (finite field, the 16 and the doublet): single-mode backgrounds F = eps cos(p x1), G = eps cos(k x3),
  eps = 0.3; R_c(p,k) = 4 * (Fourier coefficient of A_c at (p,k)) / eps^2 for c = 16, L. Diagonal p = k = 2 pi n/L
  on all L, plus row k = 2 pi/L on L = 16 (and full grids on L <= 12). eps-scaling check (0.3 vs 0.15).
- M3 (zero momentum exactly): unit flux in the 12- and 34-planes (charge unit 1/6) on 6^4, full dense; indices
  for 6Y = 1..6.
