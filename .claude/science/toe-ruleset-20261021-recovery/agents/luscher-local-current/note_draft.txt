# The U(1)_Y anomaly of the one-family overlap passes Luscher's zero-momentum condition, but finite-range current fits on 4^4 cannot test locality

## Question

Wizard synthesis route A (w5): the chiral overlap theory with the field-dependent projector P_hat exists with a
gauge-invariant measure if the anomaly density of the anomaly-free content is q = d*k with k a LOCAL
gauge-invariant current (Luscher 1998/99 cohomology, supplied). overlap-nonabelian found the 16's density cancels
through cubic order but not at order 4 (sum Y^5 = 5/9, sum_doublets Y^3 = -1/9). Is that order-4 residual a local
divergence, while a lone doublet's density is not?

## Repo check

- No main note constructs Luscher's local current or measure term (git grep "cohomolog"/"Luscher" on origin/main:
  the hits are the WZ/Fujikawa staggered notes and ABJ index notes, which do not touch it).
- Open PRs used: #10049 (overlap-from-walk: Luscher P_hat, free-kernel locality exp(-0.72 d)), #10051
  (overlap-nonabelian: order-4 residual, kernel copied here), #10052 (overlap-colour). Narrows overlap-from-walk's
  sentence that the 1e-3 zero-sum remainder "is the divergence term": it is consistent with that, but this probe
  could not certify locality on 4^4.

## Set-up

- Kernel and conventions copied from overlap-nonabelian: X = D_W - m0, m0 = 1.6, H = g5 X, D = 1 + g5 sign H,
  q_Y(x) = tr g5 D(x,x) = tr sign H(x,x). A = sum m Y q_Y; the 16 has (m, Y) = Q (6, 1/6), L (2, -1/2), u^c (3, -2/3),
  d^c (3, 1/3), e^c (1, 1); the lone doublet is L alone.
- Factorised backgrounds: (A1, A2) depend on (x1, x2) and (A3, A4) on (x3, x4). Then F12 and F34 are the only field
  strengths, Luscher's density is T = F12 F34 / (4 pi^2), and a quartic local basis is small enough to write down.
- Fit: k = combination of gauge-invariant plaquette monomials F12^n1 F34^n2 with factors at L1 offset <= r;
  basis columns are d*_mu of each monomial; optional extra column T. 12 backgrounds at eps = 0.2 (8 train,
  4 held-out); ridge 1e-8 on scaled columns (1e-6 for the quartic basis).

## Results

Exact and integer:
- K1 [T]: GW 1e-14, g5-hermiticity 8e-16, P_hat projector 3e-15, tr g5 D(x,x) = tr sign H(x,x).
- C0 [T]: in all 12 trivial-sector backgrounds sum_x A = 0 for BOTH the 16 (3e-13) and L (2e-13). In small smooth
  backgrounds the integral cannot separate them (each index is 0).
- C1 [F, integers]: unit flux in both planes for charge 1/6 plus a perturbation; sum_x T = 36 (charge 1). Indices of
  6Y = 1, 2, 3, 4 are -1, -4, -9, -16 = -(6Y)^2, and sum_x q = -2 index exactly. Lone L: integrated anomaly -18, so no
  periodic current k exists at all (local or not).
- C1b [F]: e^c (6Y = 6, plaquette angle 3 pi/4) is NOT resolved on 4^4 (index 0, gap 0.014) nor on 5^4 (-35, gap
  0.009; scratch check, not in the runner). With the predicted e^c integer, the 16's flux integral is 0 (1e-13); with

  measured here; overlap-nonabelian's (SU(2) x U(1))/Z2 half flux (fluxes <= 3) measured it.

Against the pre-set criteria:
- P1 (16 residual falls >= 3x from r = 0 to 1): NOT MET (1.02 -> 0.62). The bilinear basis is the wrong order for

## Answer

| question | answer |
|----------|--------|
| does the 16 integrate to zero? | trivial sector: yes, exactly, but so does L; flux sector: yes from the integers (e^c predicted, not resolved) |
| does the lone doublet's integral fail? | yes in flux: -18 (no current of any kind) |
| is the 16's order-4 residual a local divergence? | NOT decided by this probe: on 4^4 (smallest momentum pi/2, period 4) finite-range fits are size- and sample-limited and do not separate local from nonlocal currents |
| what decides it, then? | [S] Luscher's U(1) theorem (supplied): a local, gauge-invariant, topological density q (sum_x dq = 0 under all variations) is alpha + beta F + gamma eps F F + d*k with k local; for the overlap with admissible fields alpha = beta = 0, and gamma is the zero-momentum (flux-sector) integral. Each charge gives q_Y = 2 Y^2 T + d*k_Y, so A = 2 sum m Y^3 T + d*(sum m Y k_Y). For the 16, sum m Y^3 = 0, so the order-4 residual is part of d*(sum m Y k_Y): local by the theorem, not a new obstruction. The finite content checked here is that premise: the trivial-sector zeros and the flux integers. |

## What this establishes, and what remains

- Establishes [T/F]: the lattice identities; the index integers 6Y = 1-4; the lone doublet's nonzero flux integral;
  the 16's eps^4 law; that 4^4 finite-range fits cannot carry Luscher's locality test (a method negative: do not
  re-run it at 4^4).
- Does NOT establish: locality of k numerically; the measure term itself; admissibility (max |F| = 0.79 here, well
  above Luscher's proof bound); the gauge-field integral; any non-abelian statement. For SU(2) x U(1) the
  construction is known only to all orders of perturbation theory (Luscher 2000, Kikukawa et al., supplied); the
  non-perturbative non-abelian measure is open in the literature.
- Next test that could decide numerically: the momentum-space form of the same question. For one charge, measure the
  order-e^2 kernel G(p_a, p_b) on L >= 8 (factorised backgrounds keep it a 2D x 2D problem) and check
  G(p) - 2 Y^2/(4 pi^2) = p_hat . K(p) with K bounded as p -> 0; the lone doublet keeps a 1/p term. That needs sparse
  sign(H) (Zolotarev) rather than dense eigh.
