# The spin-3/2 Margolus tick has no exact SU(2) on its two copies

Claim type: bounded_theorem.
Primary runner: `scripts/spin_three_halves_margolus_tick_has_no_exact_copy_su2_2026_10_21.py`.

The [candidate-law register](CANDIDATE_LAWS.md) has zero premise weight. This note asks what internal symmetry acts on the two Dirac copies of the supplied spin-3/2 Margolus tick. That tick has one fermion mode per site and block unitary W(mu) = cos(mu/2) W + i sin(mu/2), applied on even and then odd 2^3 blocks. The [minimal axioms](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md) do not choose this tick. The answer is that the tick carries no SU(2) on its copies. Its only block-local internal symmetry is U(1) when mu is nonzero, and U(1) x U(1) when mu = 0. An SU(2) appears only at long wavelength, and the lattice breaks it at O(k^2).

## Statement

1. Hidden Clifford algebra. Set Gamma_a = sqrt 3 W_a and Gamma_(a+3) = i Gamma_a tau_a, with tau_a = diag(2c_a - 1). These are six anticommuting Hermitian involutions on C^8. The odd-block hop is D^dag Gamma_a D = cos K_a Gamma_a + sin K_a Gamma_(a+3), so the massless tick is a product of two unit 6-vectors. The product Gamma_1 ... Gamma_6 equals -i eps, where eps = (-1)^(x+y+z) is the sublattice parity.
2. Internal symmetry. The six generators generate all 8x8 matrices. Hence for mu nonzero only scalars commute with every U(K), and the block-local symmetry is U(1)_V. At mu = 0 the tick generates only the even half of the algebra, so the commutant is span{1, eps}, which is U(1)_V x U(1)_eps. At a single K the commutant is U(4) x U(4), but that restates the band degeneracy and changes with K.
3. Copies at long wavelength. The tick fixes beta = W, alpha_a = i Gamma_(a+3) beta and gamma5 = i alpha_1 alpha_2 alpha_3. These give C^8 = Dirac(4) x copy(2), with Pauli matrices sigma_i on the copies and sigma_3 = eps gamma5. The emergent copy symmetry is U(2) with mass and U(2)_L x U(2)_R without. The lattice breaks every block-local sigma_i and P_L sigma_i at O(k^2).
4. Chirality. The only exact lattice remnant is eps = gamma5 sigma_3, an axial charge with opposite sign on the two copies. A block-local inversion P with P gamma5 P^-1 = -gamma5 exists, so the content is left-right symmetric (vector-like). Nothing in the tick selects one chirality.
5. Mass. The commutator norm of P_L sigma_1 with U(0) is exactly sin mu. The mass keeps the vector copy SU(2) at k = 0, breaks the chiral one and breaks U(1)_eps. This is the ordinary breaking caused by a Dirac mass.
6. Discrete structure. Each rotation factorises as L(R) = S_(1/2)(R) rho(R) with rho(R) in the copy U(2). The copy doublet carries |chi| = (2, 2, 0, 1, 0) on the classes (E, C2face, C4, C3, C2edge). This is the two-dimensional irrep E of the cube group, which factors through S3, the permutations of the three axes. Since rho is irreducible, no copy generator commutes with all 24 rotations. Bands are exactly fourfold, with no threefold degeneracy. A block-local particle-hole map exists. The species are two Dirac copies gapped by mu at quasi-energy 0, plus two massless ones at quasi-energy pi and K = pi(1,1,1), with speed cos(mu/2)/sqrt 3.

## Derivation

Six anticommuting involutions on C^8 generate the full matrix algebra Cl_6 = M_8, and their even part is the commutant of their product, which is proportional to eps. The massless tick is a product of two elements of the span of the Gamma's, hence even, so its block-local commutant contains eps; a mass term adds the identity component and an odd part, which removes eps. The continuum algebra (beta, alpha_a) generates Cl_4 = M_4, so its commutant in M_8 is M_2, the copy algebra. Removing beta leaves the commutant of the alpha's, which is M_2 + M_2 on the two chiralities. Block-local generators of the copy algebra commute with U(K) only at k = 0, and the leading commutator is quadratic in k.

## What is supplied and the boundary

Supplied: the tick, its spin-3/2 rotation action (the S3 copy representation depends on it), the sign convention for left and right, and the choice to count only block-local matrices as internal symmetries. The operators beta and alpha are read off the tick rather than supplied, and the copy algebra is their canonical commutant.

Not established: an exact copy SU(2) under a K-dependent or multi-block dressing (this is not settled), any gauge group, any chirality, and any weak-interaction or generation structure. The copies behave like staggered-fermion tastes permuted by the axes, but there are only two of them. Measurement shows a parity-violating weak interaction and three light neutrino species (LEP gives 2.984 +- 0.008). A vector-like doublet with no exact chiral SU(2) does not reproduce either on its own. The two massless species at quasi-energy pi would be an observable mismatch unless something removes them; that issue belongs to the tick itself.
