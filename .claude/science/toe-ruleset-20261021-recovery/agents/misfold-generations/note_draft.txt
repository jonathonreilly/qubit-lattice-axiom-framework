# Misfolds of the cube fold do not form three generations: the quadratic tick binds nothing, and only the point misfold is stable

Claim type: bounded_theorem. Labels: [T] exact, [D] finite diagnostic.
Runner: runner.py, TOTAL: PASS=13 FAIL=0, 2.4 s, ~125 MB peak, 4.5 k characters of stdout, seeded, AUDIT_TIMEOUT_SEC = 120.
It was run under unique filenames (scratch/misfold_generations_test_u1.py, _u2.py). Pass criteria were written before
any run (criteria_pre_run.txt). Run u1 failed one check (E2: I had expected exact Slater-Condon zeros for the larger
classes; the computed elements are nonzero), and the check was rewritten to state the computed result.
Axioms unchanged. No rule is proposed.
Repo check: no note on main uses "misfold". Builds on fold-selection-3d (open PR #9978) and fold-defect-dm (A: the fold
parameter space is contractible, so no fold defect is topological).

## Question

The three-generations reset (2026-10-23) proposed that the families are three stable misfold classes of the one cube
fold, distinguished by the dimension d_m = 1, 2, 3 of the misfolded region, each carrying the same content, with
neutral transitions between them forbidden. Pre-set prediction: three classes. A different count is a result.

## Model and extension

The model is fold-selection-3d's, unchanged. The energy is the tick's own quasi-energy, which is exact for a quadratic
model. At the fold limit, mu = -2 eps, the even cubes decouple: H = a R_E. The vacuum fills R = -1 in each even cube,
and the point misfold (a cube with 5 units) costs a = |mu|.

The question needs a "misfolded region", which the model does not define. Extension used: the region X holds the
odd tiling's fold, meaning R_O restricted to X, half filled at its negative levels. The remaining sites of each even
cube hold the lowest levels of R_E restricted to them, and their exact zero modes are filled freely. Each filling
gives a charge q = units - N/2; odd q is odd parity.

Elementary move: any number-conserving one-body unitary on one cube's 8 modes. This is the class the cube tick
generates. "Locally stable" means two things: no such move on any cube lowers E, and E is not above the q-sector
minimum.

## Results

A1 [T]. Interlacing. A 7-site cube fragment keeps the levels -1, -1, -1 (these are exact R eigenvectors), one zero
mode, and +1, +1, +1. So removing one site from an even cube costs exactly a.

A2 [T]. The point misfold costs a and sits in the 4-state level, split 2 + 2 by the copy sign. It is passive under
every one-cube move, and it is the q = 1 sector minimum.

A3 [T]. A cube holding 7 units (q = 3) costs 3a, the same as three separated points. Its binding is 0, so it is
marginal.

B1 [T+D]. The elementary misfolds take their sites from 2^d_m distinct even cubes. So E = 2^d_m a exactly, for every
q: 2a, 4a and 8a, ratios 1 : 2 : 4. The zero modes number Z = 2, 4, 8. These values hold on 4^3 and on 6^3.

B2 [D]. On every touched cube a one-cube move lowers E by a/2. The region orbital leaves the cube mixed and not
passive. No elementary misfold is locally stable.

B3 [T]. The q-sector minimum of a quadratic tick is |q| a, reached by separated point misfolds. The q = 1 classes sit
1a, 3a and 7a above it.

B4 [D]. The odd-cube domains on 8^3 (lines of 1, 2, 3 cubes, a 2x2 sheet, a 2x2x2 block) cost 8.0a, 13.7a, 19.4a,
24.1a and 43.3a. Each has a lowering move.

B5 [T]. RESULT: there is one stable class, against a prediction of 3.

C1 [T+D]. At mu = -eps the q = 1 minimum is the band edge |mu|, an extended state. All the classes lie above it, in the
order point < bond < plaquette < cube. A free tick's many-body spectrum is the sumset of its one-body levels, so
nothing binds at any mu.

D1 [T+D]. The contents differ between classes:
- The point has one 4-state level (2 + 2).
- The bond has 2 zero modes plus region levels at +-0.577a.
- The plaquette has 4 zero modes plus region levels at +-0.816a.
- The cube has 8 zero modes.

E1 [T]. The Loewdin cofactor formula agrees with Fock space.

E2 [T+D]. RESULT: the largest one-body element (site density, nearest-neighbour hop or link current) between q = +1
states is:

| Pair | max \|<P\|O\|X>\| |
|---|---|
| point-bond | 0.38 |
| point-plaquette | 0.14 |
| point-cube | 0.017 |
| bond-plaquette | 0.30 |
| bond-cube | 0.035 |
| plaquette-cube | 0.10 |

None vanishes by symmetry or topology.

E3 [T]. No neutral selection rule protects the classes. Together with fold-defect-dm (A), this means fold defects
carry no topological label.

## Reading

The hypothesis fails at steps 2, 4 and 5 (F1, F2, F4) inside this model, for three reasons:
- The fold model is quadratic, so energies add and nothing binds.
- Extended misfolds are excited, decaying configurations that cost 2^d_m a.
- Their contents differ, and they mix with the point misfold through neutral one-body operators.

Step 3 gives an ordering E(1) < E(2) < E(3) with ratios 1 : 2 : 4, but it is the ordering of unstable states.

What would be needed: an interaction that binds (none exists in CL-1's tick), plus a reason why exactly three bound
classes exist. Any supplied binding term (a cube charging U(n_c - 4)^2, cube-cube V) sets the count through its sign and
size, so the count would be chosen, not forced. That fails the anti-smuggling gate unless the term is derived.
