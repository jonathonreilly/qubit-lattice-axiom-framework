# The body-diagonal turn anomaly fixes the family count mod the odd turn order, not mod the dimension

Claim type: bounded_theorem.
Primary runner: `scripts/body_diagonal_turn_anomaly_fixes_the_family_count_mod_the_odd_turn_order_not_mod_the_dimension_2026_10_23.py`.

The three-fold-turn lemma (families-mod-3) gives N = 0 mod 3 for the family number under a supplied mirror term. Its Z3 is the turn of the cube about a body diagonal, and on the cube that order equals the dimension. This note asks which of the two the 3 tracks: it replaces the Z3 by the actual body-diagonal turn of the d-cube for d = 2, 3, 4 and recomputes the obstruction. The [candidate-law register](CANDIDATE_LAWS.md) has zero premise weight; the [minimal axioms](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md) are unchanged and fix no d other than 3.

## Statement

Carry the lemma over unchanged: the twisted turn group Z_n; light copy charge q_l, mirror copy charge q_m; the mirror's single-copy top form on M modes keeps the twist k only if M(q_m + k) = 0 mod n; the gapped set S = {Gamma-b, R-a, R-b} carries the per-family index m(q_l + k) mod n, with m doublets per family; N is obstructed if no kept k cancels N m (q_l + k). Take the cell-supplied content m = M = 2^(d-1), half the 2^d corner modes. Then, over every charge pair q_l != q_m:

- d = 2: no proper turn other than 1 fixes the diagonal. The natural analogues, both of order 2 = d, are the pi turn -1 (proper; it reverses the diagonal; on fermions T^2 = (-1)^F, group Z4^F) and the diagonal reflection (improper). No N is obstructed.
- d = 3: the C3 turn (order 3, proper). N = 0 mod 3, reproducing families-mod-3.
- d = 4: the order-4 body-diagonal turn (the axis 4-cycle) has det -1, so it is a rotoreflection; no proper order-4 turn keeps the diagonal axis. As Z4 or Z8^F (best case, treated as unitary) it obstructs no N. The proper turns that keep the axis, C3 on three axes (order 3) and -C3 (order 6), give N = 0 mod 3.

So the allowed N are not the multiples of d in d = 2 or d = 4. The obstruction is N = 0 mod the odd part of the proper turn's order: the lemma's 3 is the cube's turn, which equals d only at d = 3.

## Derivation

Turns. The stabilizer of the body diagonal in the signed-permutation group is the axis permutation group S_d, and the axis d-cycle has order d and det (-1)^(d-1). It is a proper turn only for odd d. The runner lists the proper orders: d = 2 fixing {1}, reversing {2}; d = 4 fixing {1, 2, 3}, reversing {2, 6}.

Charges. The turn permutes the 2^d corner modes. Its charge counts are {0^4, 1^2, 2^2} at d = 3 (the families-mod-3 A4 value), {0^6, 1^3, 2^4, 3^3} for the 4-cycle and {0^8, 1^4, 2^4} for C3 in d = 4. Every axis permutation fixes the windows Gamma and R, so the two-window gapped set exists in every d. The spin lift removes T^n = (-1)^F for odd n only.

Mechanism. When n is a power of 2 and M = m = 2^(d-1) >= n, the top-form condition keeps every k and the index m(q_l + k) vanishes mod n. The twist is not selected and nothing is obstructed. For odd n, 2^(d-1) is a unit mod n, so k = -q_m is fixed and N(q_l - q_m) = 0 mod n follows. The sensitivity scan at n = 4 needs an odd per-family multiplicity m (and M != 0 mod 4) to get mod 4. The toy's multiplicity 2 gives at most mod 2. The cell never supplies an odd m.

## Comparison with Dobrescu-Poppitz (2001, supplied)

In 6D the SU(2) global anomaly (pi_6(SU(2)) = Z12) needs the excess of doublets of one 6D chirality over the other to be 0 mod 6. Their anomaly-free assignment gives 3 - 1 = 2 per generation (Q in three colours against L), so N = 0 mod 3. AGREE on the conclusion and on the allowed set {3, 6} in 1..8: both have a per-family coefficient that is a unit in Z3. They DISAGREE on the origin. DP's 3 is the 3-part of pi_6(SU(2)) together with the colour count. Ours is the turn order with power-of-two cell content.

Zoom in DP's role: space plus one zoom direction is d = 4, an odd spacetime (4+1D) with no Weyl fermions, and pi_5(SU(2)) = Z2 has no 3-part. DP needs two extra directions with 6D chirality. Zoom is a coarse-graining index with no turn relating it to space, so it cannot play that role as is. A second direction (the layer) plus 6D chiral content would have to be supplied. In our class, five spatial directions would give mod 5 from the 5-cycle and mod 3 only from its 3-cycles (toy-algebra extrapolation only).
