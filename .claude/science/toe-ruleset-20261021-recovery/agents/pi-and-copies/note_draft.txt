# Partition-neutral Margolus ticks cannot both gap the pi-cone and carry a mass

Claim type: bounded_theorem.
Primary runner: `scripts/partition_neutral_margolus_ticks_cannot_both_gap_the_pi_cone_and_carry_a_mass_2026_10_21.py`.

The [candidate-law register](CANDIDATE_LAWS.md) has zero premise weight. This note concerns the supplied spin-3/2 Margolus tick, which has one fermion mode per site and applies a block unitary on even 2^3 blocks and then on odd blocks shifted by (1,1,1). It has a massless species at R = pi(1,1,1) and two equal-mass Dirac copies at K = 0. The [minimal axioms](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md) choose neither the tick nor the readings. Convention: the half-tick block unitary is V(a) = cos a W + i sin a = W e^{iaW}. "Privileges no partition" is read as invariance under one of eight partition exchangers. Under that reading, no covariant term found here gives a mass and also gaps the pi-cone, and no covariant term splits the two copies.

## Statement

1. Formula at R. With even mass a and odd mass b, U(R) = -cos(a-b) + i sin(b-a) W and U(0) = cos(a+b) + i sin(a+b) W. The K = 0 mass is a+b, and R sits at pi +- (a-b).
2. Covariant block algebra. The commutant of the 24 spin-3/2 corner actions is span{1, W, F, B}: the identity, the edge hop, the face-diagonal hop and the body-diagonal hop. (F, B, W) act as Pauli matrices on a two-state index. W and B anticommute with the cone's velocity matrices, so they are Dirac masses; F commutes with them, so it is not a mass. The corner parity Gamma = D(R) acts on this algebra as conjugation by F.
3. Exchangers. Translation by (1,1,1) with the gauge (-1)^x, and inversion through a site, each optionally combined with Gamma or with complex conjugation, give eight operations. Each maps even-block covariant unitaries onto odd-block ones and acts on the block unitary as conjugation by 1, F, B or W.
4. Single ticks. Take a partition-neutral single tick whose K = 0 term is a nonzero pure Dirac mass (no F part). Then U(R) = lambda 1, with lambda^2 equal to the product of the two K = 0 levels. So R sits either at the light centre (an extra light species) or exactly at its pi-point (gapless).
5. Edge-hop mass schedules. For any schedule V(m_t), U(0) = exp(i W sum m_t) and U(R) = (-1)^(n/2) exp(i W sum (-1)^t m_t). Every exchanger symmetry therefore forces mass times pi-gap = 0. A mass sign alternating every tick gives U(0) = U(R) = 1, which is massless and ungapped.
6. Staggered tick. The half-tick masses (b, -b) are invariant under four exchangers and gap R by 2b. Adding the mass, (mu/2 + b, mu/2 - b), breaks all eight. Its bands are cos w = ((cos 2b + cos mu)/2) S - (cos 2b - cos mu)/2, with S the mean of cos K_a, and fill [mu, pi - 2b]. The light band is sqrt(mu^2 + c^2 k^2) with c^2 = (cos 2b + cos mu)/6, and the tick keeps 24/24 rotation covariance. Its site-inversion image is the same rule with mu -> -mu. The only thing that names a partition is the sign of mu relative to b. With b = (pi - 1/2)/2 every mode lies within the switching-law domain |w| <= 1/2 rad per tick, at speed c = 0.142.
7. Rejected route. Rotating the involution toward F, cos h W + sin h F, is neutral under site inversion and gaps R by 2h, but it makes the cone speed direction-dependent at first order, with anisotropy about 2.8 h.
8. Copies. Under the 24 rotations the K = 0 Bloch space is two copies of G3/2, with commutant 1_4 x M_2. Every covariant tick sampled has rest levels in groups of four, so the two copies have equal rest mass. The Dirac masses form an 8-dimensional space 2 A1 + 2 A2 + 2 E whose rotation-invariant part is span{W, B}. Both of these give the two copies equal mass, and every copy-splitting mass breaks the rotations.

## Derivation

Item 1 follows from D(R) = Gamma and Gamma W Gamma = -W, so the odd half-tick at R is V(b) with W replaced by -W. Item 5 follows from V(m) = W e^{imW} and W^2 = 1: products of edge-hop masses collapse to a single exponential in W, with the signs at R alternating. For item 4 the runner works through an explicit decomposition: a partition-neutral tick is (v, alpha(v)) with alpha conjugation by 1, F, B or W. Item 4 is verified on root-found samples rather than proved in closed form. Item 8 follows from Schur's lemma: since the K = 0 space is 2 x G3/2, any rotation-invariant operator acts as the same 4x4 block on both copies.
