criteria_pre_run.txt -- raw-xmax-flowed-protons, written 2026-10-26 BEFORE any data fetch or runner run.

Question: under the rules as written (dim-6 LIV, nuclei broken up below <= 46 EeV, protons above ~5 EeV in
the flowed state with one constituent at x_lead -> 1), do the RAW air-shower observables of a pure
flowed-proton beam above 46 EeV fit Auger's (and TA's) raw <Xmax> and sigma(Xmax)?

Method fixed in advance:
M1. Raw data: FD/hybrid <Xmax>, sigma(Xmax) (stat + sys) in the bins at/above lg(E/eV) = 19.5, and the
    proton / iron predictions of EPOS-LHC, Sibyll 2.3(c/d), QGSJet-II-04 at the same energies, quoted from
    published tables/figures (values marked "read from figure" where not tabulated). SD-DNN Xmax moments
    (arXiv:2406.06315) used as the higher-statistics cross-check. Muon number (Rmu) reported only where
    published at these energies.
M2. Toy model: a seeded Monte Carlo generalized Heitler-Matthews cascade (leading particle with elasticity
    kappa, interaction length lambda_I(E), multiplicity N(E), 1/3 of non-leading energy to EM subshowers with
    Xmax_EM = X_inj + X0 ln(E_g/xi_c)), calibrated ONLY on the standard proton and iron predictions (the toy
    reproduces model-predicted p/Fe <Xmax> difference and sigma pattern; the absolute scale is anchored to the
    hadronic-model proton prediction). Flowed proton = toy proton with first interaction modified by
    x_lead taken from the composite-dispersion / liv-surviving-observables results (no new fit).
M3. Bracket of the flowed first interaction (supplied, not derived): cross-section factor f_sigma in
    [1/3, 2], elasticity kappa_1 in [kappa_p, 1 - (1 - x_lead)/2] (lower bound = standard), multiplicity
    factor f_N in [0.3, 1.5]. Every corner is reported; the most favourable corner (shallowest, narrowest)
    is the one that decides.
M4. Model systematics: hadronic-model spread of the proton prediction (max - min over the three models)
    plus Auger's quoted Xmax systematic, added linearly (conservative for the rules).

Decision rule (pre-registered), at the bin(s) above ~46 EeV:
- EXCLUDED: the most favourable corner predicts sigma(Xmax) above the measured value by > 3 combined sigma
  (stat + sys + model spread in quadrature) AND/OR <Xmax> above by > 3 combined sigma.
- TENSION: the most favourable corner misses one moment by 2-3 combined sigma, or misses by > 3 only in a
  corner-independent way that depends on the toy calibration.
- CONSISTENT: both moments within 2 combined sigma in at least one corner of the bracket.
- sigma(Xmax) is expected to be the more model-robust moment; if the verdict differs between <Xmax> and
  sigma(Xmax), sigma decides and that is stated.
Physics prior stated in advance (to be tested, not assumed): a leading-parton projectile is a
single-particle (no superposition) shower; it should be at least as deep and at least as fluctuating as a
proton unless f_sigma is large AND kappa_1 small AND f_N large.
