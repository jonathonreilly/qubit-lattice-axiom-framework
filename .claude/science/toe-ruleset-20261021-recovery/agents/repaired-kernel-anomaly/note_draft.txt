# The repaired time-even overlap kernel keeps the family anomaly cancellation in SU(2) x U(1) and SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1) backgrounds, but needs a finer lattice for flux three

## Question

new-tick-recheck repaired the overlap kernel for the adopted family-class tick: X is built from the tick's T-even pieces,
which is the Wilson form with v_s = 1/sqrt3 and r_s = 1/3. The U(1) indices 0, 1, 4 on 4^4 were unchanged, but the
non-abelian and colour backgrounds were not rerun. Do the anomaly checks of overlap-nonabelian and overlap-colour survive
on the repaired kernel?

## Set-up (only the kernel changes)

- X = sum_mu [(v_mu g_mu - r_mu) T_mu U / 2 - (v_mu g_mu + r_mu) T_mu^+ U^+ / 2] + (sum r - m0), with
  v = (1/sqrt3, 1/sqrt3, 1/sqrt3, 1) and r = (1/3, 1/3, 1/3, 1). H = g5 X, D = 1 + g5 sign H, P_hat_- = (1 + sign H)/2.
- m0 = 0.5. It was chosen by a U(1)-only pre-scan over m0 in {0.2, ..., 0.6} (scratch/m0scan.py, scan5.py). At m0 = 0.5
  every |f| <= 2 on 4^4 and f = 1 on 3^4 is resolved, with the largest minimum gap (0.167). It is a resolution choice;
  no anomaly quantity entered it. K0: free D has exactly one zero on 8^4, and GW holds to 3e-15.
- Backgrounds: the same seeds and draw order as the two probes, so the link fields are the old ones. The content, Y values,
  contrast sets, fluxes, Z2/Z6 global forms and thresholds are copied.

## Results

Resolution (K1) [F]: a component flux f = 3 in both planes on 4^4 is NOT resolved at any m0 in the window. The pre-scan
gave index 0 at m0 = 0.2-0.4 and -3 at m0 = 0.5-0.6; the old kernel at m0 = 1.6 gave -9. The plaquette angle is 3 pi/8.
The weaker spatial Wilson term (r_s = 1/3) means the kernel needs smoother fields. On 5^4 (angle 0.75) f = 3 gives -9 at
m0 = 0.4, 0.5 and 0.6.

SU(2) x U(1)_Y:

- NA2a [F] 4^4, (SU(2) x U(1))/Z2 half flux:

  - e^c (f = 3) gives -1, not -9 (gap 0.006). The 4^4 sum is 8.
  - So the copied criterion NA2 is NOT met on 4^4. The cause is resolution, not anomaly.
- NA2b [F] 5^4 singlets in the same flux class: u, d, e, nu = -4, -1, -9, 0. The doublets stay on 4^4, because dim 5000
  exceeds the 3072 cap. The cross-lattice family sum Y index is 0, and L alone gives 5/2.
  - [S] This combines integers of one flux class measured on two lattices. It is weaker than the old same-lattice test.
- NA3: Q has an odd index (-1); 3 Q + L = -8, even.
- NB3 [F] 3^4 scan. The lone doublet scales as eps^2.01 (Y) and eps^2.01 (SU(2)); the family as eps^3.94 and eps^3.96.
  - For comparison, the old kernel gave 1.97/2.01 and 4.02/4.03.
  - The family/lone ratio at eps = 0.05 is 1.1e-2 (Y) and 1.7e-3 (SU(2)); the old values were 1.6e-2 and 2.1e-3.
- NB4 [T]: the charge sums are unchanged.
- NC1, NC2, ND1 [T]: GW 5e-14 and the split 3e-14. Pseudo-reality and the Y parities hold to 5e-14. The Weyl-phase lemma
  holds to 4 digits.
