# No adopted rule fixes the Floquet slab's layer parity: the zoom ladder keeps one orientation, the vacuum fold is blind to inversion, and adopted half-ticks carry no turn winding

Claim type: bounded_theorem. Exact statements are labelled [T], finite diagnostics [F] and readings [S].
Runner: runner.py, TOTAL: PASS=7 FAIL=0, 3.7 s, 63 MB peak, 4.8 k characters of stdout, seeded, AUDIT_TIMEOUT_SEC = 120.
It was tested under a unique filename (scratch/slab_parity_test_run.py). Axioms unchanged. No rule adopted.

## Question

floquet-pi-slab found that a slab alternating FREEZE (F) and its inverse (F^+), with label gates, puts the q 1 mirror in the
other copy only for an odd layer count, which restores N_f in {3, 6, 9}. The parity and the label bond pattern were chosen.
Does anything in the adopted rules force them? Three candidates were tested:
- (a) the layer direction is the zoom ladder (CL-1 split, b = 2, belonging to the even tiling, polar);
- (b) both slab ends terminate on the same selected cube fold (fold-selection-3d);
- (c) a layer is a half-tick of one tiling, so the count is fixed by the tick's own period.

Repo first. A git grep of origin/main (docs, scripts) for "odd layer count", "slab parity" and "layer count parity" found
nothing, and no open PR covers it. This note builds on floquet-pi-slab, fold-selection-3d, twist-local-single,
polar-split-reason and the register's CL-1 Split paragraph.

## Pass criteria

The criteria were written before any code ran (criteria_pre_run.txt). PASS = an adopted rule forces odd parity, together
with the reason it is not a choice in disguise. Predicted: FAIL for (a), (b) and (c), and (d) conditional only. Every line
matched its prediction except one runner check (B2), whose spectrum comparison was wrong. The spectrum of CL-1's real tick
is closed under conjugation, so it cannot tell inversion apart from a swap. The check was replaced by the exact
translation identity before the final run.

## 0. What the parity is

- 0 [T]/[F]. W(F^+) = -W_F. For any layer pattern, W = n W_F with n = #F - #F^+. The label gates commute with the turn, so
  they add nothing.
  - Checked for patterns with n = 1 and n = -3.
  - Checked for alternating stacks with Ls = 2..9, which give W = (Ls mod 2) W_F.
- The bulk gap forces alternation (floquet-pi-slab C2), so n is in {0, +-1}, and "odd Ls" means "n = +-1".
- The question is therefore: what fixes the net orientation n?

## A. Zoom ladder

- A1 [T]. The split's site map y = 2x sends the 8 sites of every parent cube of either tiling to least corners of child EVEN
  cubes. The child tiling is fixed by the split itself ("a split belongs to the even tiling"), whatever the parent's tiling
  was, so no tiling bit passes from rung to rung.
- A2 [T]/[F]. The split passes the tick forward: half of a half-tick before the passing and the other half after, with no
  inversion. polar-split-reason shows the polar direction is the same at every generation. So a rung-indexed stack has
  constant orientation, i.e. it is all F.
  - Then W = N W_F: (-4, 2, 2), (-6, 3, 3) and (-8, 4, 4) at N = 2, 3, 4.
  - The bulk has a nonzero two-layer winding (-4, 2, 2) at every k_l, so it is gapless at 0 and pi.
  - The ladder cannot supply the alternation at all, let alone its parity.
- A3 [S]. The rung count from the finest (matter) role block to the coarsest (edge register) block is log2 of block sizes
  that CL-1 supplies (B_f, b_e). Splits are triggered by edge lengths, so the count is dynamical, and no clause fixes its
  parity.

## B. Termination on the cube fold

- B1 [T]/[F]. Theorem. If U has no eigenphase at 0 or pi, the vacuum of U^+ is the particle-hole complement of the vacuum of
  U (C -> 1 - C). Each cube's total goes n -> 8 - n, and its variance is unchanged.
  - So the fold (which tiling's cubes hold definite totals) is the same for F and F^+.
  - On CL-1's two-tiling tick (4^3 torus, eps = 0.3, R^2 = 1 on both tilings), (Var N_even, Var N_odd) = (1.822, 0.310) for
    both U and U^+ at mu = 0.2.
  - At the fold limit mu = 2 eps it is (2.0, 0.0).
- B2 [T]/[F]. Control with power.
  - mu -> -mu is the tiling swap: translation by (1,1,1) maps R_e to R_o exactly, and U(-mu) is the translated tick
    conjugated by one half-tick (identity to 1e-15). The swap flips the fold, to (0.310, 1.822).
  - Inversion does not flip it.
  - The inverse tick's steps are -(eps -+ mu/2), so its naive "mass angle" is -mu, yet its fold is unchanged. The "sign of
    mu" in fold-selection-3d must therefore be read as sign(mu/eps). This sharpens that note.
- B3 [T]. Every layer of the F/F^+ slab has the same fold, so the termination "both walls end on the selected fold" holds for
  every Ls and fixes nothing about n.
  - A tiling (fold) bit is a conjugation, and a determinant winding is conjugation-invariant, so W never sees it.
  - Odd parity would follow only if F and F^+ layers sat on opposite folds, and B1 shows they do not.

## C. Layer = half-tick

- C1 [T]. Each of CL-1's half-ticks (the even step on R_e and the odd step on R_o(K)) commutes with the site-gauged (111) turn
  on the axis (commutator 0).
  - By floquet-pi-slab B1, each half-tick has W = 0, and so does the whole tick, with or without the mass angle. All four
    windings come out (0, 0, 0).
  - So any stack of adopted half-ticks has W = 0 for every count and parity, which gives q_mirror = q_light.
- C2 [T]. FREEZE is a five-layer e.o.e.o.e circuit (twist-local-single) with no turn-commuting layering at any depth
  (floquet-pi-slab B2).
  - So its half-ticks carry no sector winding of their own, and the unit of W is the whole FREEZE tick.
  - The period fixes the number of half-ticks per tick (5). It does not fix the number of FREEZE ticks in the stack.

## D. Consistency reading (not an adopted rule)

- D1 [T]. F^+ is no regauging of F by any K-continuous, turn-commuting unitary, since that would keep W, and
  W(F^+) = -W_F.
  - So the inverse layers form a supplied orientation field. CL-1 has one universal step eps and no inverse tick, and n is
    the net charge of that field.
  - The only parity-fixing condition found is "the slab's compactified tick has the winding of the update's own tick".
  - With the adopted CL-1 tick (W = 0) this forces n = 0, which is EVEN (q_mirror = q_light), the wrong parity.
  - With FREEZE as the update it would force n = 1.
  - So odd parity is equivalent to adopting a W != 0 (FREEZE-class) update tick. That is a CL-1-level choice, not a
    consequence of any adopted clause.

## Answer

FAIL, as predicted. No adopted rule forces odd n or the label bond pattern.
- (a) The zoom ladder keeps one orientation, which gives an all-F stack with a gapless bulk.
- (b) The vacuum fold is exactly blind to inversion, so same-fold termination holds for every Ls.
- (c) The adopted half-ticks carry W = 0, so half-tick bookkeeping gives q_mirror = q_light at any count.
- The one natural consistency condition, applied to the adopted tick, selects EVEN.

Minimality, 3 over 6: NO. None of the candidates touches the family count.

## Gate (anti-smuggling)

No rule is proposed. "The slab's net orientation is +1" or "the update tick is FREEZE-class" would each produce odd n. The
first is a choice whose only motive is the count. The second is the real open decision: adopting FREEZE as CL-1's tick.
It would have to pass the gate on predictions of its own, such as FREEZE's pinned pi node and the pi partners on both walls,
which are UNTESTED.

## Smuggling review

Supplied:
- FREEZE;
- the F^+ layers (an orientation field outside CL-1);
- the label bond pattern;
- g, eps, the mu values and Ls;
- free fields.

Forced:
- W = n W_F;
- the split's erasure of the tiling bit;
- the forward split passing, which keeps orientation constant;
- the inversion blindness of the vacuum fold (exact);
- the tiling swap as a conjugation;
- W = 0 for the adopted half-ticks;
- the even parity selected by the consistency reading with the adopted tick.

Not shown:
- the copy-mass factor's winding (expected 0 by the B1 lemma if it commutes with the turn; not built here);
- interactions;
- any rule that would make the update tick FREEZE-class.
