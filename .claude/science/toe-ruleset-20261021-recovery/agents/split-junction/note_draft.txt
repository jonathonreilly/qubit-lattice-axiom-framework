# A geometry-triggered cube split with a stationary partition keeps Lambda exactly

Claim type: bounded_theorem.
Primary runner: `scripts/geometry_triggered_cube_split_with_a_stationary_partition_keeps_lambda_exactly_2026_10_21.py`.

The [candidate-law register](CANDIDATE_LAWS.md) has zero premise weight. Its cube split had a kick of rho^2/rho* in Lambda at every split, which needed splits adiabatic to about 1e-123. This note asks which split rule keeps Lambda and what a split does to the matter on the cube tick. The [minimal axioms](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md) choose neither the carrier nor the split rule.

## Statement

In the supplied carrier, Lambda = 12A/l^2 - rho is a conserved charge. A split pinned to a tick count changes it; a split triggered when a cube's edge reaches the threshold, with the 4-volume of the split tick shared between parent and children at the stationary value, keeps it, and the expansion rate steps by (3/16)(HL)^2 instead. On the cube tick, the light modes cannot all keep band and energy through a split, a midpoint split keeps flips at about 0.04 K^2, and a half-filled sea is heated by about 20% per split by every local map tried.

## Derivation

T1, Lambda as a charge. The per-cell Lagrangian l f(u) - E, with f = -24u artanh u + 12 sqrt(1-2u^2) asin(u^2/(1-u^2)), has energy function u f' - f = -12 A(u). So Lambda = 12A/l^2 - rho is the charge of translations in 4-volume.

T2, pinned split. Momentum is continuous across it, f'(u_c) = k f'(u_f), so Lambda changes by 12[k^2 A(u_f) - A(u_c)]/L^2, at leading order (rho^2/3)[1/rho*(L) - 1/rho*(L/k)]. The kick is a difference of a function of cell size, so staged refinements telescope and no schedule avoids it. Matter lost at the split goes into Lambda one-for-one.

T3, triggered split. When the step sits in configuration space rather than at a fixed time, the refraction (Weierstrass-Erdmann) corner condition makes the conserved charge continuous. Matter lost at the split then changes H, not Lambda. A jump in vacuum density at the split still enters one-for-one; the register's clause that measures the matter phase from the vacuum phase on the same edges sets that jump to zero. Gating the split on the matter state brings the pinned kick back.

T4, record 4-volume against step. Splitting by k in space and 1/j in tick keeps the record 4-volume iff k^3 j = 1 and the step iff j = k, so both hold only at k = 1. At a fixed record 4-volume a 2^3 split lengthens the tick 8-fold and multiplies sin eps by 16. This rules out the register's earlier "halve the ticks to keep the step".

T5, energy no-go. The massless light band obeys cos w = cos^2 eps + sin^2 eps <cos K_i> exactly. A band-following split at fixed step raises each light mode's energy by (3/4)[sum K_i^4/(24K^2) - sin^2 eps K^2/72], positive in every direction. Compensating it needs opposite-sign components, that is partners, so no covariant split keeps both band and energy of every excitation.

T6, heat-free split. An isometric split mapping the half-filled sea to itself must map positive bands to positive and negative to negative. The polar part of the corner map's sign blocks does so, but its range grows as 1/gap, the Compton length of the lightest sea species.

Midpoint split. The parent applies half its even half-tick read from its own edges, each corner mode passes, shared evenly, to that corner of the 8 children, and the children apply the other half. At the midpoint the light-band projector does not depend on the step, which is why flips stay small for any step change.

## Boundary

Supplied: the frustum carrier, kappa = 1 per rung cell, dust, the reference slicing, the threshold of 2 rung units, the k^3 junction hinge, the cube tick without copy mass at the listed steps and masses, the corner-map family, the half-tick midpoint (selected by test among three frames), sign projection by polar decomposition, the unit map, H_inf, relic stability and measured numbers. Negative results: no split keeps both record 4-volume and step; no split keeps every excitation's band and energy; every local map tried heats a half-filled sea by about 20% per split. Not shown: asynchronous or inhomogeneous splits, the copy mass, links and photons through a split, gravitational waves. The size of Lambda is not predicted.
