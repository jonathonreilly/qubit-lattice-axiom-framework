# A copy-blind vacuum condensate fixes no Koide phase

Claim type: bounded_theorem.
Primary runner: `scripts/a_copy_blind_vacuum_condensate_fixes_no_koide_phase_2026_10_21.py`.

The [candidate-law register](CANDIDATE_LAWS.md) has zero premise weight. Its supplied tick gives two copies with degenerate masses. Schur's lemma forbids a symmetric rule from splitting them, so this note tests the alternative of breaking a copy symmetry by the vacuum state rather than by the rule. The model supplies a third identical copy, the copy group S3 and a Hermitian condensate phi. The [minimal axioms](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md) choose none of these.

The vacuum is a stationary point of the sea phase plus a Hartree contact term. State-level breaking does avoid the Schur degeneracy and keeps isotropy. But no vacuum built this way lands on a derived r or delta, and every value it produces contradicts the measured lepton masses. Several PASS lines in the runner record such failures.

## Statement

1. Measured targets (estimates). A three-mass fit of sqrt(m_k) = a(1 + 2 sqrt r cos(delta + 2 pi k/3)) to pole masses gives r = 0.4999967(76) and delta = 0.2222248(63), each within 1 sigma of 1/2 and 2/9. The two values cannot both be exact: together they predict m_mu/m_e = 206.770316 against 206.7682830(45), about 452 sigma off. With r = 1/2 exact, delta = 0.2222220471 = 2/9 - 1.75e-7; with delta = 2/9 exact, r = 0.4999997868. No angle of the form p pi/q, arccos(p/q) or arctan(p/q) with q <= 30 lies within 2 sigma.
2. State-level breaking. An S3-invariant vacuum has commutant span{1, J} and masses {a+2d, a-d, a-d}, so it keeps a degenerate pair. A vacuum that keeps C3 is a circulant a + bP + b*P^2. The reflection maps delta to -delta, so an S3-invariant potential has the pair of vacua +delta and -delta, and the masses are distinct exactly when sin 3 delta is nonzero. The symmetric functions e1 = 3A and e2 = 3A^2(1-r) do not depend on delta, while e3 = A^3(1 - 3r + 2r^1.5 cos 3 delta). A copy-blind potential therefore sees delta only through e3. With a circulant copy mass, the 24 levels at any K are the single-copy levels at the eigen-masses, so the condensate adds no anisotropy.
3. The sea locks delta at degenerate points. dG/d delta = -6 A^3 r^1.5 sin(3 delta) f'[mu1, mu2, mu3], where f'[...] is a second divided difference of the derivative of the sea phase. That divided difference keeps one sign over the scan, so every stationary delta has sin 3 delta = 0. At r = 1/2 the minima have masses in the proportion (1.707, 1.707, 0.414) in the linear reading, or (5.83, 0.086, 0.086) in the square-root reading, against a measured m_mu/m_e = 206.77. A copy-blind (U(3)-invariant) Hartree term depends only on Tr phi and Tr phi^2, so it does not change the delta landscape. A cubic det(phi) term gives an interior stationary delta only within a 1.40 percent window of coefficients, and hitting delta = 0.2222220 then fixes that coefficient. It is tuned, not derived.
4. Copy-blind contact vacua cannot carry the lepton spectrum. With v = Omega + theta^2/(2 gamma) and Omega''' > 0, v' is unimodal on theta > 0. Hence at most two distinct positive eigenvalues occur, and three positive square roots of mass with the Koide signs are impossible. Three distinct masses put one copy on each root of v' = c, which bounds the heaviest/middle ratio by at most 2.54 in the linear reading, or 6.4 in the square-root reading, across the light band. The measured m_tau/m_mu is 16.82.

## Derivation

Item 2 is the commutant of S3 and C3 on three copies together with Vieta's formulas for the circulant eigenvalues. Item 3 differentiates the sea free energy G = sum_k f(mu_k) along delta at fixed (A, r): since only e3 depends on delta, dG/d delta factors through d e3 / d delta, which is proportional to sin 3 delta, times a divided difference of f'. Item 4 follows from Omega''' > 0: then v'' changes sign at most once, so v' = c has at most two positive roots.

## What is supplied and the boundary

Supplied:
- three identical copies (the rules give two) and the copy group S3;
- a Hermitian condensate, read either as the masses or as their square roots;
- the contact rule and its couplings, which are scanned rather than fixed;
- the step eps = 0.04;
- the Hartree treatment only, with no fluctuations and no conditioning on the actual configuration;
- the measured inputs, PDG and CODATA values.

The physical mass scale, mu/eps of about 1e-22, is not scanned. A theorem of Lindemann-Weierstrass type is cited, not rederived: phases built from algebraic matrix entries cannot equal 2/9 rad exactly.

Not established: any derived r, delta, copy count or mixing. The class "two copies of the register's tick, a copy-blind contact rule and spontaneous breaking" fails against m_tau/m_mu and the degenerate lock on delta. A copy-structured law would need at least two conditions on the copy potential, no fewer inputs than two supplied constants.
