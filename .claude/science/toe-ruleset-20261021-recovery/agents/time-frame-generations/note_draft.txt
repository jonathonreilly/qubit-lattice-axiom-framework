# Time locking and frame-drag winding do not force three families: the tick has no temporal Z3, and shift windings split

Claim type: bounded_theorem. Runner: 14 PASS, 0 FAIL, under 1 s. Pass criteria were fixed before the run, in criteria_pre_run.txt.

## Question

Two owner instincts for the three generations (2026-10-23):
- TIME: a composite whose internal phase locks to the tick (menu M5).
- FRAMES: a composite carrying circulation n of the adopted tick-offset / shift field (menu M9).

A route PASSes only if it gives all four of:
- (P1) a count of 3 forced by the rules;
- (P2) distinct masses;
- (P3) identical charges;
- (P4) allowed mixing with J != 0 possible.

## Rules used (rederived where used)

- CL-1 cube tick: two tilings offset by (1,1,1), U = U_odd U_even, the even cube with the copy-mass factor. Inlined from zoom3-fixed-families.
- tick-fraction-frame-dragging: the per-pair tick offset (shift) is a response variable, with stationary law curl curl beta = -16 pi G j.
  - On-shell energy of a stationary configuration: E = -L = (1/32 pi G) sum |curl beta|^2 = 8 pi G <j, (-lap)^-1 j>.
  - Rederived: L = (1/32 pi G)|curl beta|^2 + j.beta; on shell, j.beta = -(1/16 pi G)|curl beta|^2.

## Results

### Theorems (exact)

- T1a. The tick is one fixed unitary per tick, so exact time translations form Z. Their characters are a continuous quasi-energy mod 2 pi. A period-p locking sector theta = 2 pi k/p exists for every p equally, so three would be a chosen p, not a forced one.
- T5. The stationary Floquet sea C commutes with U. Hence <O_k> = 0 for the locking-character-k part of any operator (k != 0). No vacuum of the free tick connects locking sectors, so mixing is permutation-only and J = 0.
  - A vacuum carrying the character would be a period-3 discrete time crystal. That needs interactions and a chosen period (UNTESTED).
- F1. E is quadratic in the record current, so E(n) = n^2 E(1), and the n-winding composite stores n(n-1) E(1) more than n separated windings.
- F2 (Cauchy-Schwarz). |E_12(d)| < E(1) for d > 0, so coincidence is the maximum over two-winding configurations. Windings n = 2 and 3 split into n unit windings, i.e. heavy goes to several light, failing F6's one-lighter-family pattern.
- F3. The circulation (slip flux = 16 pi G x linked current) is a real number.
  - A compact offset (a whole-tick offset is invisible in a stationary state) gives winding group Z, with no 3.
  - E(-n) = E(n) exactly, so a supplied Z3 offset gives classes {0, +1, -1} with ONE nontrivial mass.

### Finite diagnostics (4^3; 24^3 FFT)

- T1b. No half-tick screw exists: the odd half-tick is not a (1,1,1)-translate of the even one, or of its inverse, conjugate or transpose, under any site gauge. Best residual 1.00 massive, 0.96 massless. Reason: the steps carry opposite signs, and the copy mass sits on even cubes only.
  - So the menu's Z6 (turn x two-tiling period) is NOT exact; the time group is Z.
- T1c. The body-diagonal turn is exact up to a site gauge (2e-14), with T^3 = 1. It is the only exact Z3, and it is spatial.
- T2. The spectrum mismatch under theta -> theta + 2 pi/3 is 2.09 rad, and under + pi it is 2.49 rad: no locking periodicity.
- T3. The turn sectors have dimensions 24, 20, 20. The two-body quasi-energies form one cluster, not three classes.
- T4. Lightest |theta| per turn sector: 0.05, 0.05, 0.212. Two sectors share the Dirac level; the third is a cell-scale partner. That is 2 + 1, not three light distinct masses. The particle-hole thresholds are all 0.1 = 2|mu|.
- F0. div j = 0, and the direct sum |curl beta|^2/(32 pi G) matches the FFT form (E(1) = 52.25 lattice units).
- F2 (pair interaction E_12/E(1) at d = 0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8):
  - coaxial: 1, 0.28, 0.09, 0.04, 0.009, 0.002 (repulsive, the gravitomagnetic sign);
  - coplanar: 1, 0.40, -0.13, -0.04, -0.007, -0.002 (weak far attraction, never a winding-2 bound state).

### Estimates against measurement

- T6. A locking offset of 2 pi k/3 per tick is a cell-scale mass. That fails F1 and F3 (1 : 207 : 3477).
- F4. The gravitomagnetic self-energy of a muon-mass composite of Compton size is about 8e-42 GeV, about 1e-40 of its mass, against m_mu - m_e = 0.105 GeV. Only a Planck-heavy composite gets an O(1) splitting.
