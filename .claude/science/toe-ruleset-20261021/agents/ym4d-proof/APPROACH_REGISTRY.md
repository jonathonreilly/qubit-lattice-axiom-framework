The final assembly must verify the common observable maps, physical
normalizations, trajectory, quantifier order, limits and all required inputs.

be read at their corrected scope: the partial distributional RP test algebra
is not an ordinary everywhere-defined positive Euclidean polynomial algebra.
Likewise, the [non-freeness proposal](NONFREE55.md) and
[its review](CODEX_REVIEW_NONFREE.md) distinguish canonical-free comparisons
from generalized-free-field exclusions. Neither distinction may be erased
when stating the completed theorem.

An unsuccessful construction will be preserved with its exact failed step.
It will not be promoted into a global no-go or route-exhaustion claim. New
findings are incorporated here only with source and evidence pointers.
