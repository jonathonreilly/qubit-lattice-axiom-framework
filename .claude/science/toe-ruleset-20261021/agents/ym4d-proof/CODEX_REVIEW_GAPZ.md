`(W_m H W_m^{-1})^{-1} = [I + H^{-1}(W_m H W_m^{-1}−H)]^{-1}H^{-1}`,

### F7 — The proposed HESS-rel Neumann argument omits the operator alignment and the actual smallness condition

**Verdict: REFUTED under the literal unframed difference; NOT-VERIFIED under an intended framed interpretation. Corrected implication: CONDITIONAL.** Attachments: `NBG_A.md:430-439`, `BFT_B.md:4328-4333`, and the "Lemma CONJ" definition immediately following the GAP-Z definitions.

First, gauge covariance is conjugacy, not equality. For a nonconstant pure gauge transform g of the trivial configuration, curvature f_k=0 but a Hessian in fixed link coordinates transforms as H_{1^g}=U_g H_1 U_g^{-1}. ### F8 — A load-bearing background identity in the proposed (N-bg) reduction omits the derivative of log

**Verdict: REFUTED for the displayed identity; NOT-VERIFIED for the corrected reduction.** Attachments: `NBG_A.md:275-277`, `NBG_A.md:290-291`, `NBG_A.md:360-367`.

Let W(t) be the plaquette product, W(0)=exp(X), and S=W′(0)W(0)^{-1} its left-trivialized variation. The transported signed sum of link variations is S. It is **not**, in general, the derivative of Y(t)=log W(t). Differentiating exp(Y(t)) gives

`S = ∫_0^1 exp(s ad_X) Y′(0) ds`,

so

`Y′(0) = [ad_X/(exp(ad_X)−1)] S = S − [X,S]/2 + [X,[X,S]]/12 + …`.

For noncommuting X and S, the first correction is nonzero. The file derives the variation of the **product** and identifies it with the variation of its **logarithm**. Plain-Python exact noncommutative polynomial expansion of log(exp(tS)exp(X)), keeping one S through total degree three, gives coefficients S:1, SX:1/2, XS:−1/2, SXX:1/12, XSX:−1/6, XXS:1/12, independently reproducing the correction.

This error does **not** by itself prove the vertex estimate false: the missing map is a bounded analytic factor I+O(|X|) on the plaquette output in a sufficiently small BCH ball, so it can preserve a covariant-derivative leg. That repair needs to be carried through the stated response and coefficient norms. The proof's exact identity and the claim that it was directly checked cannot be banked unchanged. The critical distinction is again between two meanings of "covariant coboundary": transported group variation versus derivative of the logarithmic plaquette coordinate.
