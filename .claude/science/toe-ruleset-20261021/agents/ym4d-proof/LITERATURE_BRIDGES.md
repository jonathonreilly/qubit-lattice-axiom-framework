# Literature bridges for the full Yang–Mills target

Checkpoint: 29 October 2026. The target is existence and a physical continuum mass gap; both remain **OPEN**. This register records the current bounded literature pass, not every historical citation or an exhaustive search. It distinguishes usable mathematical machinery from model-specific estimates that still have to be proved.

Detailed primary reading and version/hash evidence are in the [source-return ledger](checks/review_source_history_literature.md), [residual ledger](checks/review_residual_full_colour_literature.md), [physical-gap ledger](checks/review_massgap_block_literature.md), and [coordinator coverage record](checks/full_proof_literature_coverage.json). The [next coverage record](checks/full_proof_next_literature_coverage.json) adds the frozen sea/minimizer/crossover units and the pinned rotation proposal. The [import register](ASSUMPTIONS_AND_IMPORTS.md) fixes their status. Complete external papers are local research inputs, excluded from this PR packet.

The immediate reuse is concrete. B01 covers the successful pointwise gas after its actual activity estimate. B13–B15 supply established finite-transfer, strong-coupling and comparison machinery. B07–B08 remain comparisons: the [reviewed soft-minimizer unit](UNIFORM_SOFT_MINIMIZER_ASTRA.md) reconstructs the actual [LINEAR_PACKAGE](LINEAR_PACKAGE.md) argument, with the [tree-phase scope correction](checks/review_uniform_soft_scope_confirmation.md) explicit.
