**Independent derivations and adversarial checks.**

1. **Norm algebra, extraction and the physical distinction.** For an
   ordered derivative word with a fine, b coarse and c source positions,
   Leibniz distributes positions between factors without changing the
   order inside either subword. Its coefficients are the product of the
   three binomial coefficients. Division by a! b! c! converts them to
   reciprocal subword factorials. The allowed index set is downward
   closed, so the truncated convolution is bounded by T(F)T(G).
   Labelled sources instead partition their label set, with no source
   factorial. This verifies the norm-algebra input used throughout.

   The norm mismatch is real: for f(y)=sum_(i=1)^n y_i the first derivative
   has operator norm one on l1 directions and n on l-infinity directions.
   Fourth-order bounds also say nothing about a sixth field derivative
   or a mixed word with six field and four source derivatives. This
   checks lines 52–58 against `REALRGB.md:32–65` and
   `REALRGA.md:84–126,156–179`.

   Once beta is defined on the supplied bulk family and beta[W]=1,
   linearity gives u'=u+beta[F_bulk] and G=F-(u'-u)W. Splitting each G_X
   into P_2 G_X and its complement is then exact. P_2 is idempotent,
   commutes with source differentiation, and retains all source-only
   terms. A coefficient of fine/coarse bidegree (i,j) reconstructs with
   Taylor-norm cost at most

       sum_(a<=i,b<=j) binom(i,a) binom(j,b) = 2^(i+j).

   All coefficients of degrees zero through two are evaluated at the
   same (0,J). Their weighted sum is bounded by T_(0,J)(G), giving
   sup T(P_2 G)<=4 sup T(G) and sup T((1-P_2)G)<=5 sup T(G).
   No sum of separate domain suprema is substituted for RGA7.
   Group-coordinate conversion is a separate cost, exactly as stated.

   In the bulk curvature sector, invariance under conjugation gives a
   colour bilinear proportional to trace. This can be checked on matrix
   units: diagonal conjugations eliminate unmatched root pairs, and
   commutators relate the remaining root and diagonal coefficients.
   An invariant linear functional vanishes because commutators span
   su(N). Reflecting an axis in one of two distinct planes but not the
   other eliminates their cross coefficient; permutations equate the
   six diagonal coefficients. Reflection also excludes the orientation
   odd dual-curvature term. This uses the stated bulk symmetries, not
   symmetries of a fixed exterior field.

   Conversely, the quadratic form sum_(x,nu)|F(x+e_nu)-F(x)|^2 is nonzero
   on varying curvature and equals its own degree-two Taylor projection.
   Its potential-field Fourier symbol is the curvature quadratic symbol
   times sum_nu|exp(ip_nu)-1|^2. It therefore starts at order p^4 and has
   zero p^2 marginal coefficient. Thus beta[G]=0 does not imply P_2 G=0.
   No physical localization estimate is obtained by renaming that premise.

2. **The full Taylor contraction, including the supremum order.** Put
   s=L^-2. Since P_2 F=0 for every J, all included source derivatives
   also have zero field jets through degree two. Taylor integration of
   a field derivative of order r<3 along the common radial segment gives
   a factor s^(3-r)/(3-r)!. After the split factorials, a third-order
   coefficient of bidegree (i,j) contributes
   binom(i,a)binom(j,b), where i+j=3 and a+b=r. Summing those choices gives
   binom(3,r). Integration and the nonnegative source sum can be taken
   before the common bound along that segment. Hence

       A_r(sz,J;F) <= binom(3,r) s^(3-r)
                          sup_(0<=t<=1) A_3(tsz,J;F).

   Differentiating L^4 F(sz,J) r times adds L^4 s^r. The low orders
   together therefore cost (1+3+3)L^-2 sup_D T(F). For orders three
   through six, every derivative is evaluated at the same sz and its
   factor L^4 s^r is at most L^-2. Their whole sum costs only another
   L^-2 sup_D T(F). This proves E5, with no seventh field or fifth source
   derivative and with the supremum in its prescribed position. The
   identical calculation carries all labelled subsets.

   At L=3 this benchmark is 8/9. It does not control the actual map:
   replacing curvature scaling by link scaling already sends z^3 under
   L^4 F(L^-1 z) to L z^3. Likewise beta-zero need not give the Taylor
   premise just used. The triangle inequality proves E6 from E7 and the
   reference-operator bound; it does not establish E7. Lines 222–242
   correctly keep those missing identifications explicit.
