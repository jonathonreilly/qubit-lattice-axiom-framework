**Independent reconstruction of the load-bearing steps.**

For HP1, put `a_X=exp(mu|X|) sup T(K_X)`. Leibniz and the factorial or
labelled weights give `T(fg)<=T(f)T(g)`. Each saturated summand is at most
`A exp[-(tau-mu)S_X] a_X`. An active output root was already in X. For an
inactive root in a component H, every contributing connected X that meets
the active set contains an external face neighbour of H: follow a path
out of H if X intersects it. There are at most `8|H|` such neighbours,
and `S_X>=|H|`. Summing the input rooted bounds proves exactly HP1.
The maximum of `s exp(-delta s)` is `1/(e delta)`; for `delta=log 2`,
`m/2^m` has maximum `1/2` at m=1,2, giving `C_hole=4A`. Empty/full masks
and the unaltered inactive family have the dispositions stated in the note.

For HP2, every Omega-hole is contained in a Lambda-hole. Filling the former
cannot reach a new Lambda component through an already filled one, because
distinct complementary components have no edge between them. Monotonicity
gives the opposite inclusion. This proves the identity without any
probabilistic premise. HP3 then sums a fixed set of charges once. It supplies
no identity for a product of separately normalized or extracted activities.

For HP4–HP6, unitarity gives
`||I-U_p||_HS^2=2N(1-Re tr(U_p)/N)`. Each of the n distinct bad witnesses
therefore contributes at least `theta u epsilon(u)^2/(2N)=a u^d`.
The deterministic interior plaquette set has at most `6L^4 n` elements.
In an ordered field word, each derivative selects one of four unitary or
inverse-unitary occurrences; each occurrence contributes at most h_0.
Thus, writing `z=theta u C_P n` and `v=4h_0`,
`|D_I Q_F|<=z v^|I|` for every nonempty word.

A separate way to check the exponential derivative combinatorics is to
differentiate `D w=-w DQ` repeatedly. If `b_r` majorizes `|D_I w|/w` at
order r, Leibniz gives
`b_(r+1)<=z sum_(l=0)^r binom(r,l) v^(l+1) b_(r-l)`.
The exponential generating majorant solves
`b'(t)=z v exp(vt)b(t)`, `b(0)=1`, hence
`b(t)=exp[z(exp(vt)-1)]`. Its coefficients are `v^r M_r(z)`, agreeing
with the note's partition formula. This argument preserves the order of
every subword and does not commute Lie derivatives. Summing through six
with `1/r!`, and using `u^(k-r/2)<=u^3`, `n^k<=n^6` for `k<=r<=6`,
gives the stated A_6. Coarse and source derivatives of this factor vanish.
Finally, `k=max(6,3/d)` implies `u^3 n^6<=(u^d n)^k`. The unrestricted
maximum of `x^k exp(-a x/2)` is `(2k/(ea))^k`; the stated additional
maximum with 1 is harmless. HP6 and its constant follow.

For HP7, a singleton sup-distance collar has at most 81 blocks and is
face-connected. Assigning each birth block to the component containing its
collar covers each selected complete component and gives `S_X<=81|F_X|`.
Combining this with HP6 gives the denominator `2*2N*81=324N` in the
rate. Mergers unite assigned birth sets without repeating their plaquettes.
The strict inequality in HP8 is exactly the condition `tau_raw>mu`.
The example with `diag(-1,-1,1,...)` on interior links is admissible in
SU(N); an interior link can be chosen with all its incident plaquettes
inside its block for L>=3. A cut strictly below two and a component above
the size cutoff give an open positive-measure failed sector. None of this
normalizes the residual integral. In particular `log(p Z)=log p+log Z`
does not multiply each connected logarithmic coefficient by p.

For HP9, define `T_s(A)=N_1(pi_s A)`. Both image and collar distribute
over unions, so evolving singleton births separately reproduces H_j
exactly. Projection sends a face path to a face path with possible repeated
vertices, preserving cohort connectedness. If a cohort is inside radius
