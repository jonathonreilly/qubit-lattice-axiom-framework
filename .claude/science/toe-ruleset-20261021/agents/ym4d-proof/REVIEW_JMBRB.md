**Independent derivation checks.**

1. **Normalized action, threshold and exact transfer.** Expanding
   (I-U)* (I-U) gives 2N-2 Re tr U. Consequently
   s(U)>=||I-U||op^2/(2N), and the Wilson action charge is
   u epsilon^2/(2N). With epsilon=u^((delta-1)/2) this is
   u^delta/(2N); with epsilon=g p(g) it is p(g)^2/(2N).
   Taking the negative logarithm of exp(-S_j) times the normalized
   kernels gives exactly B2, including +sum log Z_t(M_c(U)).
   It does not give a bare Wilson action at every level.

   Summing the partition indicators in B6 gives K_j f_h. Integrating
   the output then gives the original mass, since each output kernel
   integrates to one. Nonnegativity permits Tonelli, and integrability
   makes the result finite. Integrating a variable can join all factors
   that read it; retaining the union of their other readers is exact.
   This rule proves dependence containment, not a small or uniformly
   local support. The note explicitly retains that distinction.

2. **Positive mass and the centered diagnostic.** On the joint law
   mu(dU)K(U,dV), the transported relative insertion is E[a|V].
   Integrating it proves B9; positivity converts the integral identity
   into equality of L1 norms. Conditional expectation gives the L-infinity
   bound and the signed L1 inequality. Thus a positive constant insertion
   cannot receive a fresh multiplicative loss while every descendant is
   retained. Also, the insertion 1_E/p has mean one under the original
   probability law, so its normalized norm is not its unnormalized price p.

   For the one-link SU(2) example, Haar invariance makes the normalizer
   independent of U and makes Haar stationary. Conjugation invariance
   forces E_t W to be scalar, and inversion makes that scalar real.
   Its trace identifies it as lambda_t. Substituting U=VW proves
   E[f(U)|V]=lambda_t f(V). For 0<eta<2, the probability of
   f<=1-eta is at most m_eta^(-1) exp(-t eta/2); hence lambda_t tends
   to one. This rules out the stated uniform centered contraction for
   this diagnostic kernel. It is not a gauge-RG counterexample, and
   lines 171–172 correctly say so.

3. **Gap price and repeated use.** A matrix-entry cell of side r/(2N)
   has Hilbert--Schmidt diameter at most r/sqrt(2). Choosing one group
   representative per occupied cell covers SU(N) with at most
   (1+4N/r)^(2N^2) equal-Haar-mass operator balls. This proves B10
   without a sharp small-ball formula. Trace duality and ||M||op<=1
   give the asserted Lipschitz constant one. On a 1/t ball about a
   maximizer, t psi<=1. Dividing exp(-t psi) by its resulting lower
   normalization bound proves both the pointwise and probability
   estimates B11.

   Conditional on the past, distinct output links are independent.
   For a fixed list of time steps, remove the last event by conditional
   expectation, then repeat. This proves B12 with deterministic uniform
   bounds on the conditional probabilities; adaptive choices must be
   past-measurable as stated. Conditioning instead on a future event
   introduces its conditional-probability density and its normalization.
   B12 does not estimate that tilt. At a=a_0 u^(delta-1), the available
   gap exponent is kappa a_0 u^delta-1-D log(1+4N kappa u), which
   diverges for fixed positive delta. This is a genuinely new event price.

4. **Ribbon geometry, constants and the unpriced branch.** Move the

   sweeps L plaquettes; parallel edges cancel. There are at most
   3(L-1)/2 transverse edges. The inequality
   ||AB-I||op<=||A-I||op+||B-I||op and unitary conjugation invariance
   give A_L=3L(L-1)/2. The same attachment argument on the L by L
   square gives B14. It uses no commutativity or deterministic
   averaged-log map.

   Comparing h_M to h_S at S and V costs at most 2d, proving B15.
   Under the smooth-input premise, B14 forces a witness with
   ||V_c-S_c||>=epsilon/8. Its gap is at least

       epsilon^2/(128N) - 2 A_L eta_1
       >= epsilon^2/(256N).

   A distinct geometric coarse link belongs to at most six plaquettes
   in four dimensions. Each assignment of witnesses therefore contains
   at least n/6 distinct links. The union over at most 4^n assignments
   proves B17, without pretending plaquettes are independent. Dividing
   the link exponent by six and subtracting log 4 gives precisely B18.
   Additional choices of interior block witnesses still cost entropy.

   For small epsilon, eta_1=epsilon^2/(512 N A_L). Its bare Wilson

7. **Exact overlap integration and the two convergence estimates.**
   Expand the finite product by selected atom sets. Each set has a
   unique decomposition into support-overlap components. Product
   integration and the stated coefficient factorization give fixed
   component weights w_C and an exact hard-core gas. Nonempty supports
   ensure two components sharing an atom cannot be compatible.
   The complete joint absolute-integral hypothesis gives
   |w_C|<=product_(i in C) alpha_i. Separate marginal estimates would
   not do this: the shared-y example gives the extra epsilon^2 s^2
   exactly. Unvisited cells must first integrate to one; otherwise
   unnecessary total variations of the entire volume would appear.

   For v_i and b_i in the note,

       sum_(j overlaps i) b_j <= Q |S_i|,
       v_i exp(sum_(j overlaps i) b_j)
           <= v_i exp(Q |S_i|) <= b_i.

   Iterating the positive tree recursion below b_i bounds connected
   selections containing i. Allowing repeated labels adds only positive
   terms. Marking an atom at the spatial root and using
   |union S_i|<=sum |S_i| proves

       sup_x sum_(C: x in S_C) exp((mu+1)|S_C|) |w_C| <= Q.

   For the logarithm, fix the incompatibility graph of a polymer tuple
   and order its edges. Kruskal's minimum-tree criterion says an extra
   edge is allowed precisely when it is later than every edge on its
   tree path. Thus graphs assigned to T form the claimed interval,
   and their alternating sum has modulus at most one. The resulting
   tree bound applies even to tuples with repeated polymer labels.
   Run the same recursion with polymer weights exp(mu|S_C|)|w_C|
   and supersolution exp((mu+1)|S_C|)|w_C|. The preceding rooted bound
   controls its child sum by Q|S_C|. Root marking overcounts by at most
   the tuple length, changing 1/m! to 1/(m-1)!. The log norm is at
   most Q, as claimed.

   On multiplying every atom by z, a component weight receives
   z^|C|. Replacing alpha_i by |z|alpha_i gives Q_z<=2Q<=1/2 on
   |z|<=2. Both supersolution arguments still work, since they require
   Q_z<=1. The convergent connected series agrees with the finite
   gas logarithm near zero; its exponential therefore equals the gas
   throughout the open disc. Evaluation at z=1 proves nonvanishing
   without assuming it. Holomorphy in the actual source/coarse
   parameters uses the separately stated holomorphic densities, atoms,
   coefficients and locally uniform integration envelopes. Analyticity
   in z alone would not prove that statement.

   These are overlap-connected expansions. Application to the consumer's

   Finally, grouping the integrated type bounds by n gives
