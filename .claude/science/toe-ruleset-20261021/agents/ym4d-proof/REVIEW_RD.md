An independent nonvanishing check avoids that logarithmic construction.
For the first-stage gas restricted to vertices A, write its partition
polynomial as Z_A and choose x in A. Then

```text
Z_A = Z_(A minus {x}) + sum_(P subset A, x in P) w(P) Z_(A minus P).
```

Induct on |A|. If all smaller one-vertex ratios differ from 1 by at most q,
each quotient in the sum is bounded by
`(1−q)^(−(|P|−1)) <= exp(|P|)` for `q<=1/4`. Equation (20), with mu_0>=0,
then bounds `|Z_A/Z_(A minus {x})−1|` by q. The base is Z_empty=1; hence
every ratio is nonzero. This confirms the conditional zero-free conclusion
by a different finite-polynomial recursion. It supplies no actual-model
smallness and no replacement for the locality proof.

Direct mixed-coefficient checks also distinguish the claimed expansion from
incorrect alternatives. For one interaction atom `f=x a(W)` and one

partition polynomial, and that coefficient is instead `−E(a)E(b)`.
For disjoint supports product independence gives zero mixed coefficient.
These exact written checks detect dropping the interaction/defect mixed
