**Independent mathematical checks.** These are written derivations and
adversarial controls, not claimed executions of the saved diagnostic script.

1. **Ordered jets and regulators.** For a fixed word of `a` fine, `b` coarse
   and `c` source derivatives, choose which positions act on each factor.
   Each resulting subword retains its order. The absolute derivative of a
   product is bounded by the convolution with coefficients
   `binom(a,i) binom(b,j) binom(c,k)`. Dividing by `a! b! c!` converts this to
   the product of reciprocal subword factorials. The allowed index set
   `a+b<=p, c<=q` is downward closed, so its sum is bounded by the unrestricted
   product of the two truncated sums. This proves RGA8 without commuting
   Lie derivatives. For labelled sources the same argument partitions the
   label set between factors; there is no division by the number of labels.
   Power-series multiplication then proves both stated exponential bounds.

   The energy in RGA5 is nonnegative: unitarity gives `s>=0`, and
   `|Re tr(V* M)|/N<=||M||op<=1`. Thus `G_m G_n=G_(m+n)` exactly. Already at
   order zero, using `F=H=G_(1_X)` defeats a putative product estimate with
   only the union regulator. The note retains this counterexample and does
   not presume regulator integrability. The coordinate chain rule is the
   set-partition formula RGA9. A source derivative of a source-dependent
   field map can hit F through that field, so a mixed `(p,q)` output can
   require `p+q` field derivatives. The restriction at 214–218 is necessary.

2. **Positivity, quotient derivatives and integration.** Probability Haar
   and `-1<=h_M<=1` give `e^-t<=Z_t<=e^t` and hence
   `e^-2t<=k_t<=e^2t`. A fixed positive-measure sector has a positive
   integral under a strictly positive real tilt. The all-cut identity cap
   supplies such a sector; an arbitrary history still needs its own witness.
   None of this gives a small inverse or a volume-uniform local logarithm.

   Writing `E_nu F=(int Fw)/(int w)` and differentiating the quotient gives
   `E_nu DF+E_nu(F D log w)-E_nu F E_nu D log w`, with derivatives of w used
   directly where a score is undefined. Repeated differentiation of `log Z`
   groups derivative positions into blocks. A partition with r blocks has
   coefficient `(-1)^(r-1)(r-1)!`; order within each block is inherited.
   Differentiating `Z Z^-1=1` instead gives RGA12 by isolating the term with
   no derivative on Z. These arguments require no exchange of ordered Lie
   derivatives and no complex logarithm. For the determinant assertion,
   diagonalize the symmetric A: integrating the scalar geometric series
   for each eigenvalue gives `log(1+lambda)`, and summing gives the trace
   series. A positive reference matrix and `||A||<1` are indispensable.

   Under the stated domination, bring each included derivative through the
   fixed integral. The triangle inequality for its direction supremum gives
   the first RGA13 inequality; the product estimate gives the second.
   Multiplying `T f_i<=a_i G_(m_i)` and using RGA14 then gives
   `sup T A_I<=c_I^# R_I product a_i<=product alpha_i`. Separate one-atom
   moments would not prove this. Integrating an unread normalized cell to
   one first makes all its external derivatives zero. A moving hard cut
   does not satisfy this smoothness argument; 304–312 correctly leaves its
   actual treatment OPEN.

3. **Both stages of the Mayer estimate.** Let C be a connected selection of
   distinct atoms, `S_C=union_(i in C) H_i`, and
   `beta_C=sup T(A_C)`. Exact factorization over overlap components turns
   the finite selection sum into a hard-core gas of these C before taking
   any logarithm. For a specified constituent root i, every connected
   selection admits a spanning tree. The exponential generating sum of
   rooted labelled trees, with repetitions allowed only in the positive
   majorant, is bounded by the monotone recursion
   `T_i=v_i exp(sum_(j overlaps i) T_j)`. With
   `v_i=e^((mu+1)|H_i|)alpha_i` and
   `b_i=e^((mu+2)|H_i|)alpha_i`,
   `sum_(j overlaps i)b_j<=Q|H_i|<=|H_i|`. Thus b is a supersolution.
   Marking any constituent through an arbitrary output vertex x and using
   `|S_C|<=sum |H_i|` proves
   `sup_x sum_(C:x in S_C)e^((mu+1)|S_C|)beta_C<=Q`.

   The hard-core logarithm has connected incompatibility-graph coefficients
   on tuples, including repeated polymer species. For any total edge order,
   Kruskal returns T exactly when the graph contains T and every extra edge
   is later than all edges of its path in T. The signed sum over those extra
   edges is therefore zero or `(-1)^|T|`. This proves the spanning-tree
   majorant directly. Apply the previous recursion to the integrated
   polymers, now with weights `e^(mu|S_C|)beta_C` and supersolution
   `e^((mu+1)|S_C|)beta_C`. Marking an output root bounds the connected log
   by Q at exponent mu. Jet submultiplicativity bounds every included
   derivative in this same calculation. On compact subsets of the open
   domain, uniform convergence of these derivatives gives the claimed
   regularity; a boundary extension is not needed.

   Multiplying each selected atom by z replaces Q by `|z|Q`; for `|z|<2`
   the supersolutions still work. The formal identity between the finite
   gas and the exponential of its connected series becomes an equality of
   absolutely convergent real power series. At z=1 it proves positivity
   and identifies the real logarithm. This is not continuation in physical
   sources. As a separate control, one polymer of activity a has partition
   function `1+a` and log `a-a^2/2+a^3/3-...`: deleting repeated species
   would already lose the quadratic term. An empty-support prefactor could
   defeat positivity while leaving Q unchanged, which is why its explicit
   exclusion at 370–371 is essential.

4. **Extraction, invariant budgets and windows.** On a ball, zero jets
   through degree two imply, for `r=0,1,2`,
   `sup ||D^r F||<=R^(3-r) sup ||D^3 F||/(3-r)!` by radial Taylor
   integration. Consequently the displayed higher-derivative seminorm is
   a norm on this subspace. Differentiating the ideal dilation r times
   multiplies its rth derivative by `L^(4-2r)` and evaluates F inside the
   smaller ball. For every `r>=3` this is at most `L^-2`. It proves RGA-E
   for that operator, not for an interacting RG transformation.

   For RGA17, nonnegativity and `x_j<=M_q` give
   `x_(j+1)<=theta_q x_j+B_q e_*q<=M_q`. This closes only the residual
   arithmetic. The separately assumed reproduction of the other clauses
   is the substantive induction premise. For an m-window product, split
   its length as `r=km+s`, `0<=s<m`; submultiplicativity gives
   `B0 theta^k`, independently of commutation or stationarity. Summing
   gives `mB0/(1-theta)`. A uniform lag envelope `a_r` controls both
   forward lifetime and fixed-output accumulation by reindexing; a bound
   on each birth's lifetime sum alone does not control the latter column
   sum. The note preserves that distinction.

5. **Source recursion and normalization control.** The actual Markov
   property gives
   `E[exp(sum z_i O_i)|U_(j+1)]=E[exp(Phi_j)|U_(j+1)]`.
   Expanding the outer logarithm and collecting distinct labels gives the
   partition cumulants in RGA19. At four labels these comprise one fourth
   jet, four triple/single covariances, three pair/pair covariances, six
   pair/single/single third cumulants, and one fourth cumulant of singleton
   jets. The terminal integration needs the same expansion.

   A direct probabilistic control uses a fair H and conditional Bernoulli
   probabilities `1/4,3/4`. The unconditional X is Bernoulli(1/2), with
   fourth cumulant `-1/8`. Conditional fourth cumulants average to `-3/128`;
   the four triple/single covariances sum to `-12/128`; the fourth cumulant
   of the conditional mean is `-1/128`; terms involving the constant
   conditional variance vanish. Omitting triples gives `-1/32`, not
   `-1/8`. Separately, differentiating
   `p(t)=p exp(t)/(1-p+p exp(t))` at zero gives `p(1-p)`, hence `2/9`
   at `p=1/3`; freezing the denominator gives the wrong `1/3`. These
   hand calculations challenge the two relevant omissions without using
   the author's nilpotent-ring implementation.
