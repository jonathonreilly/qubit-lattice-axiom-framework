## The reflection and reference argument

For an integer coordinate plane and its antipode on an even fine torus,
no elementary plaquette or closed unit cell reads links strictly in both
halves. Tangential links on the two planes are shared and fixed by reflection.
Direction-normal links are reflected with inversion. Conditional on the
shared links, the real density at every finite h is a positive boundary
factor times one half-density and its reflected copy. The reflection form
is therefore a positive integral of an absolute square. The nonsmooth cell
indicator causes no obstruction: it is bounded, local and reflection covariant.

The relevant primary result really is the **site-plane** chessboard theorem.
[FILS, Theorems 4.1 and 4.3, pp.19–21](https://math.caltech.edu/SimonPapers/93.pdf)
and [Biskup v2, Theorem 5.8, pp.44–46](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/0610025v2)
permit closed block algebras sharing boundary variables. Biskup assumes an
even number of blocks per direction and proves the arbitrary-even case by
a maximal-run argument. Taking the theorem's block side to be one gives the
present fine cells. Since n=L T with L odd and T even, n is even. Thus n=6,
10 and other non-dyadic side lengths are covered. The argument does not use
bond-plane positivity of the cell tilt and does not extend to odd n here.
Translation and reflection covariance identify every disseminated cell
event with the corresponding original G_x.

Every plaquette belongs to four closed fine cells. On the all-rough event,
at least v/4 distinct plaquettes therefore have
`s(U_p)>=delta^2/(2N)`. The all-rough numerator is at most
`exp[-u delta^2 v/(8N)-h v]`. Restricting all 4v links to the cap of radius
`r_delta/sqrt(u)` makes every G_x zero. Plaquette action is at most
`8 r_delta^2/u`, so the total action costs at most `48 r_delta^2 v`;
the normalized product Haar mass supplies the remaining factor in Sea5.
This denominator lower bound is independent of h. Applying the chessboard
inequality gives exactly Sea6, with its stated power `u^(2D)`.

Multiplying Sea6 by `exp(h|A|) Z(h)` leaves penalty only on cells outside A.
As h tends to infinity, that numerator converges to the **exact** pattern
weight Z_A, including absence of rough cells on its complement. The
denominator converges to the positive Z_delta. Bounded convergence on the
finite compact product proves Sea7 without a volume window. Summing exact
patterns proves both its partition bound and the hole identity Sea8. No
arbitrary frozen exterior has been conditioned upon in this argument.

The normalized coarse kernels integrate to one for each fine U, so their
full marginal is precisely the original Wilson density. The event defining
Z_delta implies the original cube-interior cuts, and hence
`0<Z_delta<=Z_cut,tot<=Z_W`. This comparison is between the actual integrated
references. It is not a fixed-V comparison inferred from the fine marginal.
