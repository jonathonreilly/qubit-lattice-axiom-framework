## 2. Reflection positivity across the equator for THIS prescription. PROVEN-HERE (re-derived; agrees with CODEX_REVIEW_P4 §1.1)

**Statement.** Let F be a bounded measurable function of the north links U_+ (links of theta(D_a)), invariant under
gauge transformations at north vertices. Put (Theta F)(U_-) := conj F(theta U_-), where theta U_- is the north
configuration obtained by reflecting the south configuration U_-. Then omega(Theta F . F) >= 0.

**Proof.**
1. *Gauge-fix the crossing links.* Convention U_xy -> g_x U_xy g_y^{-1}. Each north seam vertex theta v carries exactly
   one crossing link C_v (v -> theta v is a bijection of seam vertices: the crossing links form a matching). Take
   g_{theta v} := C_v, g = 1 elsewhere. Then every C_v -> 1. For fixed C the induced change of the north links is a
   product of left/right Haar translations, so dU_+ is unchanged; F and Theta F are unchanged (gauge invariance; g = 1
   on the south); S is gauge invariant. The C-integral then contributes 1. So
   Z omega(Theta F . F) = integral of conj F(theta U_-) F(U_+) exp(-S(U_-, U_+, C = 1)) dU_- dU_+.
2. *Split the action at C = 1.* Every plaquette of K_a is south (a 2-face of a cube of D_a, including the squares of
   dD_a = dD_a x {0}), north (its mirror), or a crossing plaquette e x [0,1] for an edge e = (v, w) of dD_a. Write
   A(U_-) := sum over south p of beta^_p s(U_p). Since beta_{theta Q} = beta_Q (same chart centre, same Omega) and each
   prism cell gives the same quarter-share to sigma x {0} and sigma x {1}, the north part equals A(theta^{-1} U_+). A is
   real. A crossing plaquette has holonomy U_vw C_w U_{theta w, theta v} C_v^{-1}, which at C = 1 equals W (W')^{-1},
   W := U_vw a south link and W' := U_{theta v, theta w} its mirror. So its Boltzmann factor is
   exp(-beta^_p) exp((beta^_p/N) Re tr(W W'^dag)), with beta^_p = (beta/4) k_e >= 0 (§1.3).
3. *Positive kernels.* For c >= 0 and A, B in SU(N), with Re tr(A B^dag) = (1/2) sum_ij (A_ij conj B_ij + conj A_ij B_ij):
   exp(c Re tr(A B^dag)) = sum over multi-indices (r, s) of [(c/2)^{|r|+|s|}/(r! s!)] A^r conj(A)^s conj(B^r conj(B)^s),
   absolutely convergent on the compact group. The product over crossing plaquettes is therefore
