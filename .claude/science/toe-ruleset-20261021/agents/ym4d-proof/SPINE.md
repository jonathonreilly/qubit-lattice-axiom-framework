## 2. Step 1 — exact finite model, without an observable substitution

Exact statement used from HRP §4.1, for its displayed complete zero-source
chain with finite real prescribed parameters:

> ∫W_ch Π_{j≥1}dU_j=exp(−S_0(U_0)),
> Z_ch=Z_Wilson,       E_ch[A(U_0)]=μ_Wilson(A).
>

> coarse marginal, or a conditional law at fixed U_K is needed or inferred.

Let Λ_(a,R)=(aZ/RZ)^4 with R/a an even integer, a=L^−K, and U_b∈SU(N),
U_−b=U_b^−1. For one orientation of each plaquette set

    s(U_p)=1−N^−1 Re tr U_p,
    W(U)=Σ_p s(U_p),
    dμ_(K,R)=Z_(K,R)^−1 exp[−u_0 W(U)] Π_b dU_b.

Because 0≤s≤2 and the integration space is compact with probability Haar,
0<Z_(K,R)≤1 and this is a probability measure at every finite regulator.
All continuum estimates below concern this normalized measure.

Decision 4, PROOF.md:2675–2684, gives the exact kernel:

    Mbar_c(U)=(4! L^4)^−1 Σ_σ Σ_(y∈B(X)) T_y^σ(U),
    k_j(V_c,U)=exp{−κu_j[1−Re tr(V_c^−1 Mbar_c(U))/N]}/N_j(Mbar_c(U)),
    N_j(M)=∫_SU(N) exp{−κu_j[1−Re tr(V^−1M)/N]} dV,
    K_j(V,U)=Π_c k_j(V_c,U).

The contour uses the **same** order σ at both ends; changing that average is
a different model. Each transporter is unitary, so ‖Mbar_c‖op≤1. The
denominator is positive, and integration in every output link gives
∫K_j(V,U)dV=1. Thus

    dP(U_0,…,U_K)=dμ_(K,R)(U_0) Π_(j<K)K_j(U_(j+1),U_j)dU_(j+1)

is normalized, and successive integration from K down to 1 recovers μ_(K,R)

approximation. Positivity is asserted on the real group; no complex log is

The designated field, verbatim in COMBO (1), is

## 7. Step 6 — one subsequence, for the whole hierarchy

and imaginary linear combinations, for every n. Their union is countable.
Every lattice pairing in this set is eventually defined and bounded by (G).

followed by the diagonal subsequence, gives convergence for the entire union.

## 8. Step 7 — OS axioms on the common separated algebra

Let A_e be finite sequences F_n∈E_n, with **partial** concatenation only
when the resulting tensor is in the corresponding E_(n+m). The positive
space A_+ uses tests zero off 0<t_1<…<t_n, including the constant component.
Let θ(t,x)=(-t,x), and let * conjugate and reverse the word. Then
ΘF_n*⊗G_m∈E_(n+m): strict positive and negative times separate the blocks,
and flatness handles their boundaries. It is not necessary, or correct,
to adjoin e(f)^2 on arbitrary overlapping supports to this Euclidean algebra.

Here OS0 means the E0′ regularity above, OS1 means full Euclidean covariance,
OS2 means the reflection-positive form, and OS3 means permutation symmetry;
reality and S_0=1 are stated separately. No OS4/clustering premise is inserted.

For RP, the Wilson plaquette kernel has a nonnegative character expansion;
splitting crossing links and integrating Haar yields a sum of squared
positive-half integrals. HRP treats both the plane at 0 and the periodic
wrap plane, with the corresponding site/link variants. Its valid support
condition is support on the actual positive-half link algebra, with the
repaired entire-half-arc condition when using arc arguments, not merely
positive endpoints. For fixed compact ordered tests choose a positive collar
ε from the reflection plane. For sufficiently small a the whole finite-range
clover footprint lies in that half, and for sufficiently large R it avoids
the wrap cut. Exact integration of the soft chain preserves this RP statement
for original-link insertions. It does not prove RP of an arbitrary weighted,
selected, truncated or auxiliary coarse observable law.

Finite-regulator RP gives

    Σ_(n,m) S_(n+m)^(i)(ΘF_n*⊗F_m)≥0.

This is a finite sum of entries of the common hierarchy. Pass to the limit,
then extend from compact interior tests using continuity and positive-space
density to obtain the same inequality for every F∈A_+. Reality and simultaneous
permutation symmetry pass identically from the original commuting fields.

Translations on the nested grids L^−K Z^4 are exact once supports fit;
their union is dense, and the action on Schwartz tests is continuous. Thus
translation invariance extends to all translations. The hypercubic rotations
and reflections are also exact. The missing actual-model symmetry input is

    S_n^(i)(g·F)−S_n^(i)(F)→0
       for every g∈O(4), n, F∈C_c^∞(R^(4n)\Δ_n),    (Euc)

on this *same* sequence and source family. An estimate on a dense set of g
with the uniform continuity needed for extension is an alternative.
Once (Euc) is supplied, (G) extends it to E_n, proving OS1. The finite cubic
group is not dense in O(4); exact gauge covariance, a classically isotropic
principal action, and a free covariance calculation do not establish (Euc)
for the interacting all-order hierarchy. Flat torque/pressure methods are
possible producers with their own original-law/limit hypotheses, not an
additional axiom silently used here.

**How the sphere route differs.** A sphere lattice needs its own action,
observable chart maps and finite coordinate-reflection RP hypotheses.
One first constructs a continuum functional S^R at fixed sphere radius R,
with the applicable symmetry/Ward theorem, then takes R→∞ in compatible
charts with uniform flat-test bounds. A common diagonal can be chosen by
approximating the first j tests to accuracy 1/j at a sufficiently small

common-family estimates; a diagonal is not a new dynamical obstruction.
Finite sphere RP supplies neither the full sphere covariance nor the flat
limit bounds. Its changed boundaries/weights and coarse-background symmetry
estimates cannot be imported from the flat calculation. We choose the flat
route here and do not combine the easier halves of two different laws.
