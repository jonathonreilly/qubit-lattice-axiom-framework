  - Yet the angular-momentum flux of c through every sphere of radius ≥ 1 is the non-zero constant (|S³|/4)·2 (tq55_a P3).
  - Quantitatively, for w_r(x) = W(x/r) one has ŵ_r(p) = r⁴Ŵ(rp). So a bound |ĉ| ≤ C/|p|² gives only
    |∫w_r c| ≤ C r² ∫|Ŵ(q)|q⁻²d⁴q/(2π)⁴ = O(r²), which grows.
  - A bounded ĉ gives only O(1). TQ is a **continuity** statement for the angular structure of ĉ at p = 0, and no upper
    bound on |ĉ| expresses it. ∎
- **(e) Direction.** The IR bound is an upper bound compatible with a δ(p) (long-range-order) component. Its classical use
  is to *prove* continuous symmetry breaking in d ≥ 3 [m]. **Verdict: no.**

### 2.3 Chessboard estimates: local bounds, no decay (ARGUED, standard [m: Fröhlich–Israel–Lieb–Simon])
- They bound |ω(Π_t A_t)| by geometric means of disseminated expectations. The bound for ω(BC) does not depend on the
  separation of B and C beyond its parity class, so it gives no decay.
- Uses: volume-uniform local large-field and moment bounds (PROOF "E8.1. Chessboard bound at the cutoff scale", CBE). This
  is the right tool for SRC-E-type inputs. Also Peierls arguments that *prove* phase coexistence.
- **Verdict for TQ: no.**

### 2.4 The spectral representation: positivity and monotonicity, no rate
- **(i) What it gives (standard OS/transfer-matrix construction on a translation-invariant limit point [m]; not
  load-bearing below).** Reflected autocorrelations take the form G_B(h) = ∫_{[0,1]}λ^{2h}dμ_B(λ) with μ_B ≥ 0 (link-RP
  gives a self-adjoint 0 ≤ T ≤ 1). So they are non-negative, non-increasing and completely monotone in h. Their limit
  μ_B({1}) is the long-range-order part.
- **(ii) It gives no rate. PROVEN as a statement about the constraint set.** For every α > 0,
  h^{−α} = Γ(α)⁻¹∫_0^∞ s^{α−1}e^{−sh}ds = ∫_{(0,1]}λ^h dμ_α(λ), with λ = e^{−s} and μ_α ≥ 0. So every power law, and every
  constant (long-range order), satisfies all constraints that positivity and the spectral representation impose.
- **Realizations in RP lattice models (standard; asymptotic constants [m]).**
  - The massless nearest-neighbour lattice free scalar in d = 4 is RP for site and link reflections. Its reflected
    two-point function decays like h⁻², and the Wick square :φ²: (a local composite) like h⁻⁴.
  - Generalized free fields realize any power.
  - Mixtures of RP states for one reflection are RP. Integrating the non-negative forms gives this; I agree with PANEL_P1
    §2 "Convexity with a fixed reflection". So non-decaying reflected autocorrelations are RP-admissible too.
- **Consequence.** Any rate in (RA) or (RA-1) must come from Yang–Mills dynamics beyond RP. This is the precise sense in which
  "RP gives averaged decay without a gap" fails here. RP gives positivity, monotonicity and the Cauchy–Schwarz reduction of
  §2.1, but no exponent.

### 2.5 What RP does exclude: reflection-odd order (Lemma 2.3, PROVEN)
- **Lemma 2.3.** Let ω be an infinite-volume Wilson limit point (lattice-translation invariant; site-RP for coordinate
  planes, inherited from L9a). Let B be a real bounded local observable with supp B ⊂ {|x_α| ≤ w} and B∘ρ_α = −B, where ρ_α
  is the site reflection x_α ↦ −x_α. Then **E_ω[B | 𝓘] = 0 a.s.**, with 𝓘 the σ-algebra of events invariant under all
  lattice translations. In particular ω_i(B) = 0 in every ergodic component.
  *Proof.*
  - For na > w, B_n := τ_{ne_α}B lies in the positive half for the site plane P = {x_α = 0}, and
    θ_P(B_n) = τ_{−ne_α}(B∘ρ_α) = −τ_{−ne_α}B.
  - RP and translation invariance give 0 ≤ ω(θ_P(B_n)B_n) = −ω(B·τ_{2ne_α}B). So ω(B·U^nB) ≤ 0 for all large n, where
    U f := f∘τ_{2e_α} is unitary on L²(ω).
  - Von Neumann's mean ergodic theorem: (1/N)Σ_{n≤N}U^nB → PB in L², with P = E[·|𝓘_{2α}] the projection onto
    U-invariant functions. Hence (1/N)Σ_nω(B·U^nB) → ω(B·PB) = ‖PB‖² ≥ 0.
  - With the sign above, ‖E[B|𝓘_{2α}]‖ = 0. Since 𝓘 ⊂ 𝓘_{2α}, E[B|𝓘] = E[E[B|𝓘_{2α}]|𝓘] = 0. ∎
- **Application.** Take SRC-W's 𝒯 site-centred and H(4)-covariant (for a link-centred 𝒯, use link reflections; the same
  proof works). For μ ≠ ν, ρ_μ maps 𝒯_{μν}(0) to −𝒯_{μν}(0).
  - So **in every ergodic component the stress expectation is diagonal in the lattice frame.** The same holds for any
    tensor order parameter component that is odd under some coordinate reflection.
  - Rotation-breaking order, if present, must be **axis-aligned** (invariant under all coordinate reflections). Polar
    order and order along non-coordinate axes are excluded.
  - This is the RP counterpart of the Vafa–Witten positivity argument [m] for Euclidean-odd order parameters.
- **What it does not exclude.** Axis-aligned anisotropy, ⟨𝒯⟩ = diag(τ₀,…,τ₃) not all equal. That is invariant under every
  coordinate reflection, so RP is silent about it.
  - Its Goldstone fluctuations δQ_{0i} are reflection-odd, so RP forces their reflected correlation along the axis to be
    ≤ 0 (Lemma 2.3's inequality).
  - That sign is realizable. Free Maxwell gives ⟨F_{0i}(0)F_{0i}(s,0⃗)⟩ = −(∂₀² + ∂_i²)(4π²|x|²)⁻¹ = −4/(4π²s⁴) < 0. So
    no contradiction follows. **OPEN** whether RP excludes axis-aligned rotation breaking with gapless modes.

## 3. T3 — dimension counting: what would have to go wrong

### 3.1 Canonical rates of the flux-relevant covariance (ARGUED; [m] where marked)
The flux sees only genuinely tensorial, non-improvement, anisotropic content (§1.2). Classify by the infrared behaviour of
the selected limit:
- **Gapped:** e^{−m|x|}. TQ holds (ROTDEF Prop. 8.3, trivial).
- **Free massless abelian comparator (Coulomb-type IR):** each crossing propagator of F is |x|⁻⁴, and a connected
  contraction of a centred quadratic pair with a quadratic stress has two crossings, giving |x|⁻⁸. Codex Finding 7
  confirms this count; I agree. (RA) holds with margin.
- **Unitary conformal IR with an O(4)-invariant vacuum:**
  - Scalar content enters 𝒯 only through improvement terms, which carry no flux (§1.2(b), PROVEN).
  - Spin-2 content obeys Δ ≥ ℓ + d − 2 = 4 [m: unitarity bound]. So the flux-relevant part is O(|x|⁻⁸).
  - (In this case TQ holds trivially anyway; the point is that the rate is canonical.)
- **Spontaneously broken continuous internal symmetry (Goldstones):** 𝒯 ⊃ (∂π)², giving |x|⁻⁸. Linear ∂∂π terms come only
  through improvements, so zero flux. Pure SU(N) YM has no continuous internal global symmetry; its centre symmetry is discrete.
- **Spontaneously broken rotations (Goldstones of the broken generators J_{0i} of an axis-aligned order):**
  - The order-parameter fluctuation π (Δ = 1) sits in F̃, and 𝒯 contains a genuinely spin-2 term linear in π (Δ = 3).
  - So Cov(F̃,𝒯) ∼ |x|⁻⁴ with a non-zero rotlet component: the Goldstone count |x|^{−(d−1)} for the angular-momentum
    current (§1.4).
  - **This is the failure of TQ.** The rotlet of tq55_a is its exact conserved profile. (RA) is saturated:
    Δ(F̃) + Δ(𝒯) = 1 + 3 = 4.

### 3.2 The mechanisms the brief names

- **Hence (ARGUED, given the classification above):** TQ fails **iff** the selected limit has an ergodic component that is

  invariance by the infrared dynamics. Lemma 2.3 forces such order to be axis-aligned.

### 3.3 Can RP or gauge invariance exclude it?
- **RP: partly.** Lemma 2.3 (PROVEN) excludes every reflection-odd order parameter, including all off-diagonal stress
  condensates. It does not exclude axis-aligned anisotropy, and its sign constraint on the odd Goldstone fluctuations is
  satisfiable (§2.5, Maxwell example). OPEN beyond this.
- **Elitzur-type argument: no, and the reason is exact.**
  - Elitzur's argument needs a *local* transformation that leaves the action invariant.
  - A local rotation (the field redefinition Ξ_{v_r}) changes the action by exactly 𝔚(v_r) = −𝔗_r + 𝔅(v_r).
  - ROTDEF's identity *is* the Elitzur-type computation for rotations, and what it leaves over is exactly TQ.
  - Gauge invariance makes every candidate order parameter (traceless ⟨𝒯⟩, nematic glueball condensates) gauge invariant,
    so Elitzur does not even apply to them.
- **"No continuous breaking for gauge-invariant order parameters": no such theorem in d = 4.**
  - Mermin–Wagner-type statements need d ≤ 2.
  - RP infrared bounds are the standard *proof* of continuous breaking in d ≥ 3 [m].
  - Gauge theories are expected to break continuous global symmetries with gauge-invariant order parameters [m: chiral
    symmetry breaking in QCD — an expectation, not a theorem].
  - The lattice has no continuous rotation symmetry at all; the would-be breaking is of an emergent symmetry.
- **Vafa–Witten-type positivity [m]:** excludes Euclidean-imaginary (P-odd) order parameters only. That matches Lemma 2.3.
  Diagonal stress anisotropies are Euclidean-real.
- **Nielsen–Olesen instability (NODEA_RES55 G5; not re-verified):** the uniform chromomagnetic background, the natural
  classical rotation-breaking condensate, is not a local minimum even on the small-field set. That is heuristic evidence
  against that one pattern, not an exclusion.

## 4. T4 — conclusion

### 4.1 Verdict: TQ is NOT proven. It is reduced; the obstruction is named exactly
**Proven here (implications; conditional only on SRC-O, SRC-W, SRC-E-normalization, the flow g_bare → 0, E6):**
1. **The IR input is a Wilson-measure statement** (Prop. 1.1). Λ = limsup_j sup_s|Cov_{ω_j}(F̃_s,𝔗_r)|/Z_e² is exactly
   r-independent. OS1 on the orbit ⟺ Λ = 0. CD and the coarse law are not needed for this.
2. **Exact sufficient condition** (Prop. 1.2, with Codex's sampling repair built in): inf_{r₁,H}A(r₁,H) = 0. In particular:
   o(|x|⁻⁴) on one annulus per scale (E-ann); a uniform envelope ε → 0 (E-unif, covering |x|⁻⁸ and |x|⁻⁴/log|x|); the
   discrete log-Cesàro form (E-Ces).
3. **The RP reduction** (Theorem 2.1 / Cor. 2.2), volume-uniform with constant 1. (E-unif) follows from (RA),
   G_{F̃}(h)G_{𝒯}(h) ≤ Z_e⁴η(h)²h⁻⁸, or from (RA-1), G_𝒯(h) ≤ η(h)²h⁻⁸, which covers all test pairs at once.
4. **What cannot help:**
   - RP supplies no rate (§2.4).
   - An infrared bound, even for the stress channel itself, does not imply TQ (§2.2(d), rotlet).
   - Chessboard estimates give no decay (§2.3).
   - Conservation plus symmetry permit a non-zero rotation charge (§1.2(d), CHECKED).
5. **What RP excludes:** reflection-odd order (Lemma 2.3).

**The exact obstruction.** TQ fails exactly when the selected limit carries a rotlet tail: an |x|⁻⁴ stress-channel tail of
non-zero rotation charge. Physically this is spontaneous breaking of rotation invariance by the infrared dynamics with
gapless angular modes, necessarily axis-aligned by Lemma 2.3.
- Nothing available excludes it: RP, gauge invariance (Elitzur), the H(4) selection rules (they are UV) and the lane's UV
  inputs all leave it open.
- **The minimal missing statement** is one of the following, uniformly along the sequence; each implies OS1 on the orbit
  given the UV inputs:
  - **(M1)** liminf_{r→∞} limsup_j sup_s sup_{r/2≤|x|≤6r} |x|⁴ Σ_{μν}|Cov_{ω_j}(F̃_s,𝒯_{μν}(x))|/Z_e² = 0.
  - **(M2) = (RA).** Decay of the reflected autocorrelations of F̃ and of the stress current, with exponents summing to more
    than 8 (envelope form).
  - **(M2′) = (RA-1).** The stress current's reflected two-point function is o(h⁻⁸); this works for every test pair at once.
- **Relative strength (ARGUED).**
  - A gap implies all three.
  - So does a free, or O(4)-symmetric unitary-conformal, infrared. Those satisfy (M1) and (M2) without a gap, so as
    hypotheses they are weaker than a gap.
  - Proving any of them for Yang–Mills is an infrared problem of the mass-gap family. I see no route to it from the
    lane's present inputs.
- **Not claimed:** that TQ is logically independent of uniqueness (Codex Finding 8; agree). Nor that some Yang–Mills-like
  model violates TQ — no such model is constructed here.
