# AUDIT verification criteria

No numerical experiment is planned for this mapping audit. Historical
numerical claims are attributed to their source reviews and are not rerun.
The sandbox rejects the mandatory nice-priority setting, so no scientific
numerical run may proceed here under a weakened guard.

Deterministic document checks will verify:

1. Every quoted status locator points to the recorded source text.
2. Source hashes remain unchanged between mapping and final verification.
3. Every DAG node has exactly one of LANDED, CONDITIONAL, OPEN, AT-RISK.
4. Route leaves reference defined nodes; no conditional hypothesis is
   silently treated as discharged.
5. New outputs remain within AUDIT.md, AUDIT_LOG.md and checks/audit_*;
   existing files remain unchanged.

These checks establish document consistency and provenance only. They do
not independently prove Yang–Mills estimates, confer retained status or
turn an author proposal into a reviewed result.
