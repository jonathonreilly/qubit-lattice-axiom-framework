# Prospective numerical criteria for the original-law boundary unit

Recorded before the new checker is written or executed. These are finite
falsification controls, not a proof of the uniform theorem. Single process,
nice 15, numerical library threads 1, estimated memory below 300 MiB; retain
the coordinator's lock and the >=50% free-memory / load gates. A resource
rejection is not a mathematical failure and is retained separately.

1. On an actual four-dimensional L=3 open block, use noncommuting SU(2)
   and SU(3) links with arbitrary vertex gauges. Recover the rooted complete
   axial tree exactly (matrix error <2e-11). Scale an actual nonflat field
   to the simultaneous interior-cut boundary. For the written radial
   contraction and product-Haar-cap perturbations, every actual plaquette
   must retain the predicted positive margin, allowing 2e-11 numerical
   error. Gauged and original-link plaquette defects must agree to 2e-11.
   A mutation contracting principal logs of the ungauged large links must
   violate the tiny cut on at least one fixture. This tests the gauge
   construction, not a commuting or linearized substitute.
2. On the actual periodic L=3,T=2 four-dimensional lattice, enumerate paired
   S4-prime words. A crossing edge is read by one coarse kernel, in exactly
   72 of its 1944 words, each once, so the averaged incidence is 1/27.
   Its six Wilson plaquettes are distinct and none is an interior cut.
   Direct noncommuting matrix products for the means and six plaquettes
   must match the specialized full-SU(2) conditional density to 2e-11.
   Omitting the paired root legs must change the tested incidence.
3. Keep the exact finite-u Haar normalizer H(z)=2 I_1(z)/z. In the crossing
   fixture use V0=exp(.55 i sigma1), V1=exp(.37 i sigma2), V2=V3=I,
   u=1,8,40, kappa=1.3, and one original curved-plaquette source epsilon=.1.
   Full SU(2) Haar radial/angular quadrature and analytic angular integration
   must agree relatively to 2e-9. The conditional shell probability for
   ||I-exp(A)||op in [.45-h,.45+h], h=.015,.0075,.00375, must have successive
   probability ratios in [1.85,2.15]. At least one fixture must detect
   omission of the field-dependent normalizer, omission of the noncommuting
   curved plaquette, and replacement of full Haar by a one-colour flat
   angle measure, with a probability discrepancy >1e-6 each.
4. At u=8, the original-source partition ratio must agree with the exact
   source-vacuum factor times the vacuum-subtracted expectation to 2e-10.
   Removing that factor must produce a discrepancy >1e-5. For complex
   epsilon=.1+.07i, the absolute raw shell integral must not exceed the
   raw shell integral at Re(epsilon), allowing 2e-10 relative error. This
   is an envelope check, not complex nonvanishing.
5. A hard selector at the actual SU(2) mismatch threshold must change by
   one under a sufficiently small actual link displacement, while its
   integrated shell mass tends to zero as in criterion 3. No uniform-norm
   localization or support-size cluster conclusion may be assigned to this.

Any failed execution and its exact source are preserved. Additional probes
after observing results are labelled post-hoc.
