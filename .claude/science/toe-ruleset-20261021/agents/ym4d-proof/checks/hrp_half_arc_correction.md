## Correction and direct check

the two boundary slices. A half defined only by those names therefore admits
F=Re Tr U0-Re Tr U1. Site reflection interchanges and reverses the edges;
real trace is unchanged by inversion, so F changes sign. Its reflected
quadratic form is -E(F²), strictly negative for every finite Wilson density
of full support. This verifies the reported consumer counterexample.

in the chosen closed geometric arc. Positive temporal bases run from zero

extent-two arc is excluded. With the shared boundary links held fixed,
the two remaining Haar families are distinct and exchanged by reflection.
