# JMBRB numerical criteria

No numerical experiment is planned. The intended evidence consists of
written proofs, explicit counterexamples to specified implications, and a
fresh hostile review. No numerical PASS is asserted.

Hash, source-stability, path, link and file-scope checks are bookkeeping,
not evidence for a mathematical claim. If a numerical experiment becomes
necessary, its statements, domain and acceptance thresholds must be added
here before creating its script. It must use a numerical lock, working
nice priority, one BLAS/OMP thread, less than 1 GB memory, load below 8 and
at least 30% free memory, with no pools. A failed guard is reported and is
never bypassed.
