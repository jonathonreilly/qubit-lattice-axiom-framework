# Predeclared injectivity-supplement controls

The additional analytic argument must establish the limiting kernel and
bounded tilt identity for every bottom field before using a common
full-measure set. Its finite controls cannot certify that infinite-space
construction.

1. On the actual seven-link, two-plaquette Z2 analogue, independently
   reconstruct the Perron vector and unprojected Doob kernel. Verify the
   complete probability tilt after two input links change, including the
   normalization; a dropped-normalizer implementation must fail.
2. Check the finite-volume conditional pi density domination on a block
   with its actual b and p_B, and use pi=psi^2 rather than A psi.
3. On exact rational compact finite-group kernels, check the nonzero
   character multipliers for every mode of blocks of one, two and three
   links. Solve convolution against a signed measure and recover that
   measure. Setting a nontrivial multiplier to zero must leave a nonzero
   signed measure undetected, demonstrating that positivity as a quadratic
   form alone is insufficient for the injectivity argument.
4. Explicitly keep beta=0 outside the asserted injectivity conclusion:
   the Haar kernel has a nonconstant zero mode. A positive local witness
   by itself is not a surrogate for the full injectivity proof.

The main analytic proof must separately justify: canonical half-space
kernels for all bottom fields; stationarity and positivity; passage of
local finite-energy bounds to conditional laws in infinite volume; the
countable intersection of Fubini full-measure sets; uniqueness of a finite
signed measure from its Peter-Weyl coefficients; and L1 martingale
convergence. No finite test substitutes for these steps.
