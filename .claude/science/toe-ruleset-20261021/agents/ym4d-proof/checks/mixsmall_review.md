## Independent reconstruction of the decisive calculations

For the finite jet bound, denote a relative word derivative of Z by
`z_I=D_I Z/Z`. Differentiating `Z*(1/Z)=1` and isolating the highest inverse
derivative gives the positive majorant recurrence

    I_empty=1,
    I_I <= sum_(empty!=B subset I) M_B I_(I minus B).

Expanding this recurrence gives an ordered collection of nonempty blocks;
forgetting their block order contributes `card(pi)!`. The exponential
derivative gives one term per set partition and the log derivative gives
`(card(pi)-1)!`. These are MS4. All words within blocks retain their original
order, so no commutation of Lie derivatives was used. Leibniz for `e^H/Z`
gives MS5's C. Division by the fine/coarse/source factorials turns the
binomial convolution into a product of factorial weights. The index set
`a+b<=p,c<=q` is downward closed; subset splitting proves the labelled
version without a source factorial. This checks MS6/MS7 independently of
the saved coefficient runner.

For MS14, write `theta=2pi/L^2` and `z=exp(2pi i/L)`. Translation of the
axial paths in direction one changes neither the original link field nor
the direction array. The contour is therefore just its length-L straight
leg. Differentiating the finite geometric sum before imposing `z^L=1`
gives `sum r z^r=-L/(1-z)`, hence

    m'(0)=-i L h_j/((L-1)(1-z)).

At the zero mean, `D log Z=t E_Haar D h=0` and

    D^2 log Z=t E_Haar D^2 h+t^2 Var_Haar(D h).

The first term vanishes. Haar symmetry of the SU(2) quaternion coordinates
makes the covariance matrix `I_4/4`; thus the variance is `|m'(0)|^2/4`.
Using `|1-z|=2 sin(pi/L)` and `h_j^2=2/u_j` gives exactly

    kappa^2 u_j L^2 / [8 (L-1)^2 sin^2(pi/L)].

This is an actual contour derivative, rather than free variation of an
abstract matrix M. Reversing or omitting the normalizer term in a derivative
of log k would change this nonzero second derivative. Rough-field charges
or cancellation with other factors remain outside this counterexample.

For MS17, the normalized density of all read outputs is `e^(H_C)/Z_C`.
Its fine-word scores are bounded by `card(C) t lambda^r`. Applying the
density bound before the fixed-event integration gives
`D_p(card(C),t,lambda) product s_i` times that event's probability. A Haar
cap of radius `1/t` loses at most one in its exponent and has mass at least
`(1+4Nt)^(-2N^2)`, proving the complete q_gap. For m distinct links that
probability is at most `exp(-m q_gap)` when q_gap is nonnegative. Every
monomial in C_l has degree at most l, so for `card(C)<=c m`,

    D_p(card(C),t,lambda) <= m^p D_p(c,t,lambda)
                            <= [2^p D_p(c,t,lambda)]^m.

The last step uses `m<=2^(m-1)` and `D_p>=1`. At fixed N,L,kappa,c and
u>=1, a degree-d monomial in `lambda^l C_l(c t)` scales as `u^(d-l/2)`
with `d<=l<=p`, so its maximal growth is `u^(p/2)`. The resulting logarithmic
bill is indeed O(log u). Near identity, `max h_M>=h_M(I)` gives
