## Routes that did not discharge the requested input

1. **An arbitrary conditional minimum.** CS21 supplies a correct excess-energy
   probability above the actual conditional minimum, but that minimum can
   be forced far from U0 by an arbitrary small conditional's exterior. It
   did not supply an original outside-chart price. The new construction
   never calls that minimum a small-chart reference.

2. **A fixed-width reset and a maximum exterior amplitude.** Substituting
   the maximum boundary field into CS27 leaves a cost proportional to that
   maximum squared times the boundary population. A component defined by
   one small threshold does not automatically make that cost smaller than
   its coercive charge. Merely increasing the width can increase the cap
   entropy and the uncharged region. This route was not declared proved.
   OC4 instead sums the actual exterior reader squares, and OC8--OC13 find
   a payable shell while charging every added block.

3. **An uncentered whole-torus reference.** Comparing the entire torus with
   U0 would pay a term of order u delta_V^2 T^4 for a local marked event.
   That does not supply a local probability bound. The new region always
   has at most V_K|A| blocks for its prescribed defect set A, even when
   that neighborhood wraps and becomes the whole torus. Its curvature cost
   is explicitly absorbed into q_*|A| under OC11.

4. **Using an uncollared lower energy.** A crossing free link's coercive
   filling uses a complete two-block brick and its root-path kernel.
   Assigning every link owned by R directly to a truncated local energy
   would be invalid. OC3 charges only owners B with N_1(B) contained in R.
   The construction uses I_j=N_(2j)(A) and R_j=N_(2j+1)(A), and retains
   the exact P_R,C_R. No exterior energy is subtracted without payment.

5. **Separated single-root prices.** A first possible organization used
   individual successful balls and a separated-root packing. It would
   lose a fourth power of the growing radius in simultaneous-event rates.
   This was not a failed theorem, but an unnecessary loss. The final proof
   grows the neighborhoods of ALL prescribed defect owners at once. Both
   the initial charge and the compact upper bound contain |A|, so the same
   finite K works for every A. No packing claim from the frozen GC packet
   was altered or relabelled; the new proof uses a stronger curvature window.

6. **Dyadic charge bands.** The initial shell proposal used dyadic bands
   Q<=q_inner<2Q, with shell<=2 alpha Q, so the shell condition would be
   exterior measurable. That valid possible route needs its factor two.
   The final argument avoids it: the pointwise inequality on H is
   E_R^e-2 A_ref^e(z)>=c q_*|A|/2. The subtracted A_ref^e is fixed in
   the actual conditional, and is exactly the one in its Haar-cap
   denominator. No random interior quantity is pulled through an integral.
   This simplification is not a post-hoc numerical acceptance change.

7. **Probabilities as analytic activities.** Integrated component probabilities
   and exact bad-pattern/Z_chart ratios depend on the full interacting
   exterior. The latter also contain a hard test at every owner outside
   the pattern. Their smallness does not assign them the finite support of
   a local indicator. The elementary mixture counterexample in the source
   verifies this logical limitation analytically. It is not this gauge
   model and does not prove a no-go theorem for the desired construction.

8. **Changing the selected or strong-good sector.** A smaller product group
   cap is used only as a literal subset of the existing zero-source affine
   white cube. The actual Omega and G are unchanged. Its real lower bound
   for Z_sec retains a factor (1-2b)^(T^4). It does not silently become a
   volume-independent complex normalization bound.
