# Periodization and quartic-tree checks

Saved before script/run. This is an independent rational scalar check of two algebraic mechanisms in RECOVERY_UIR_ASTRA §3.3, not a numerical proof for Yang–Mills.

P1. On a one-dimensional periodic lattice T=3,4,7, define H=(5/2)I−shift−shift*. Invert it by rational Gauss–Jordan elimination. Independently sum the infinite Green kernel G∞(x)=(2/3)(1/2)^|x| over all images using the geometric-series formula. The inverse matrices must agree entrywise exactly. Omitting all nonzero images must fail H G=I.

P2. For F(x,t)=x^T H x/2−t b^T x+(g/6)Σx³+(h/24)Σx⁴, solve its stationary equation through order t³, substitute into the polynomial energy, and compare its third and fourth derivatives with the vertex and exchange formulas. Use fixed nonzero rational data, g=3/5, h=7/4. The quartic exchange coefficient must be −3. Dropping the exchange must fail for every test case.

P3. All verdict arithmetic is exact Fraction arithmetic. The checks must not be described as verification of the group-action gauge slice, common local action, all momenta, or uniform n bounds. General validity of those claims rests on the written proof.
