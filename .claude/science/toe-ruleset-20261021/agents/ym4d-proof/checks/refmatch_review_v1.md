`q''(0)=2|a|^2`, `q''''(0)=6|b|^2+8 Re(conj(a)c)`, and
`(q^2)''''(0)=24|a|^4`. Substitution proves RM20.

The exact root sum gives `q=f^2/9`. At zero,
`f=0`, `f'=-sqrt(3) alpha`, `f''=alpha^2`,

    q''=2 alpha^2/3=3h_j^2/2,
    q''''=-2 alpha^4=-81h_j^4/8,
