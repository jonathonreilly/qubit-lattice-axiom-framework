# Pre-stated exact controls for the neutral finite-domain calculation

Written before the implementation and before any run. The source derivations FC1–FC9 are the claims to challenge; these finite controls do not prove a thermodynamic or depth-uniform theorem.

1. Construct the ordinary periodic cycle with nine vertices and three roots spaced by three, directly from incidence entries. Compute its harmonic lift, Schur complement, scalar pseudodeterminants and both constant kernels. Compare independent cofactor/eigenvalue-product or rank-one-lift definitions. Require the volume-ratio factor three and reject its omission.
2. Construct the root-mean completion explicitly. Require Q_per=P_int+J P_const J* and a nonzero far-root entry. With coarse covariance C=5/12 I, compute the square root R_c^(1/2) exactly and the completed scalar ghost. Verify both left and right constants, FC6 and the normalized gauge determinant identity. Deleting the rank-one term must fail the same-domain determinant formula.
3. Test FC7 with a background/residual-dependent positive stiffness and a fixed harmonic graph. In orthonormal fine/coarse harmonic coordinates M=1/ell, require the two Schur Gram factors 1+ell^-2 and 1+ell², and the remaining -log ell constant per harmonic coordinate. Its first and mixed derivatives must vanish while the individual determinant derivatives need not.
4. Enumerate the 3^4 periodic momentum mesh with lambda components in {0,3}. Verify the neutral Wilson tadpole including the missing zero fibre, and the Cartan completeness trace for N=2,3,4 by exact linear algebra. The background-independent-neutral answer must be rejected.
5. Expand the actual SU(2) normalized contour action from its complex contour average and modulus for phases a-s,a-s,a+2s, independently of FC2. Require the displayed mixed quartic coefficients, zero cubic tensor, and a nonzero polar correction. Directly substitute a stationary power series into a scalar quartic minimization to check that vanishing cubic energy removes the quartic exchange but not the quartic vertex.
6. Preserve every failed attempt, full stdout/stderr and the implementation. Arithmetic uses sympy/Fraction; no floating approximation determines a verdict.
