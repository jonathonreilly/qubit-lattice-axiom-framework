# Pre-stated exact controls for the periodic comparison

Written after FC14–FC16 and before implementation/run. No mathematical result is inferred from eventual finite samples beyond their stated scope.

1. Build the 3^4 ordinary periodic Laplacian directly in position space, compute its mean-zero Green function by a rank-one lift, and verify Delta G=I-P0. Use a periodic external cosine in one direction. Compare the trace of P0 V G^r V for r=1,2,3 with a separate Fourier calculation: 1/(2 lambda(P)^r), lambda(P)=3. The normalized value is divided by 3^4. These are explicit nonzero regulator-defect words; a control replacing Delta G by I must fail.
2. At fixed external P=pi/2 and T divisible by four, the normalized exceptional word is 1/(2 T^4 2^r), hence vanishes as T grows. For P=2pi/T and r=2, its limit is 1/(32pi^4), not zero. Establish the latter limit symbolically. This distinguishes the required order of limits from a generic finite-rank argument.
3. Compute the ordinary site-pinned Green function on the same 3^4 graph by deleting one vertex. Verify its exact relation to the periodic mean-zero Green kernel, including the constant term. Require that its normalized trace is twice the mean-zero trace; the difference is nonzero. This is a failed regulator substitution retained as a control, not a no-go theorem for all pins or all grouped ratios.
4. Check a fixed-kernel second log-determinant variation by direct determinant-polynomial differentiation and by the projected inverse formula, with a background-dependent kernel deliberately allowed in a separate wrong-formula control. The exact nonzero kernel-rotation Gram correction must be retained in that control. This does not assert that charged nonlinear pseudodeterminants define an analytic finite-torus Gaussian across stabilizer-rank changes.
5. No predecessor helper is imported; full stdout and stderr are preserved. All verdicts use exact arithmetic. The general Riemann-sum passage, actual Ward zero and scope of the consumer remain proof obligations in the source, not claims proved by these examples.
