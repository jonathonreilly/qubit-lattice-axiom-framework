   crosses the chosen coarse face once. This uses identical endpoint path

   deletion. The Wilson exponent is `sum W_p/sigma^2`, because `W_p=N s_p`
   and `sigma^2=N/u`; the normalized kernel and Haar Jacobian in IC2 then
   reproduce the exact original density.

2. **Real gap and its complex extension.** CB's flat-brick combing controls
   each free-link coordinate by the fine curvature and actual averaged
   contour derivative. The averaging must retain its Q term; curvature
   alone would miss harmonic data. The local isometric flattening remains
   valid for arbitrary coarse holonomy. Wilson nonflat plaquettes add a

   terms are positive. For the complex extension write `K=Kbar+E`.
   IC6 gives `||E||_2 <= sqrt(||E||_1 ||E||_infinity) <= gamma/4`.
   Consequently every principal compression has Hermitian part at least
   `3 gamma/4`. Conjugation by a distance weight adds a perturbation at most

   `1/(lambda+gamma/2) <= 2/(lambda+gamma)`. Multiplying the two diagonal
   weights gives IC8. The same proof applies to the determinant homotopy.
   No complex matrix has been called positive, and no local nonlinear
   collar Hessian was substituted for a principal compression.

5. **Auxiliary parameter and parity.** u, `F_u`, b, C, p, and the probability
