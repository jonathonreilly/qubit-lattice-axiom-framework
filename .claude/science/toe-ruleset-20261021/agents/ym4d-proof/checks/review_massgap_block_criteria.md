   factor; conflation of operator positivity and kernel positivity; or a

   support or input/output distinction invalidates the physical-time

5. Any cutoff-uniform continuum conclusion, MG-row closure, universal
   no-go/route-exhaustion assertion, or unsupported global novelty exceeds
