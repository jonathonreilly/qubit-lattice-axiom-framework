# Additional marked-incidence omission control

Before execution: construct three diagonal matrix atoms with singleton
left supports and right supports sharing a new marked star-center vertex.
The global incidence norm of their product must exceed B(A)M(B), while
satisfying B(A)M(B)+M(A)B(B). This isolates removal of the marked-right term
from the separate row-versus-column error. All supports must be connected
and contain their nonzero endpoints. Use exact rational arithmetic.
