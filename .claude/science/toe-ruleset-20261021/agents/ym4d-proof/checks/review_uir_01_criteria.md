# Independent recovery-unit checks: pre-execution criteria

These criteria follow a complete read of the target, so the expected identities are not blinded. No author implementation will be imported. Evidence is scoped; scalar tests and momentum samples do not prove Yang–Mills or all-momentum positivity.

1. Eliminate compatible general rational blocks in a different basis to verify the zero-residual Schur cancellation with a genuinely off-shell Wilson block. Compare full determinant products by SymPy Bareiss/LU elimination, not the author Leibniz-polynomial routine. Mutating the transverse covariance inverse to the full covariance inverse must spoil the identity. Also test the printed duplicate gauge coupling and corrected block for multidimensional noncommuting blocks; mutation restoring the duplicated coupling must fail.
2. Independently derive the scalar integral by spherical angular integration: the transverse angular integral is 3*pi/8 for r<1 and 3*pi/(8*r**4) for r>1; integrate the two radial pieces. Check the degree-five example by convolution symmetry (the change k -> -P-k) and the convergent scalar convolution integral; retain the separate-pole divergence condition as an analytic proof. A wrong factor and a pole exponent equal to 4 must be rejected. A Ward matrix whose leading log coefficient depends on direction (transverse projector) must demonstrate why direction independence is load-bearing.
3. Solve a two-internal/two-source polynomial stationarity system degree by degree and substitute into the energy. Include mixed source/internal cubic and quartic vertices. Compare fully polarized second, third, fourth jets with the stationary-value tree formulas. Removing any exchange pairing or changing its sign must fail on nondegenerate chosen data.
4. Construct the actual order-averaged rooted contour linearization in d=4, L=3, depth=1. Build curvature and mismatch matrices from oriented paths with Bloch phases, and a block-interior spanning-tree slice. At fixed preselected real Bloch momenta verify Qd=d_coarse*evaluation, annihilation of rooted gauge directions, and positive slice Hessians numerically. At p=0, omitting the mismatch must leave harmonic null vectors, and omitting the slice must leave gauge null vectors. These are mutation tests of the universal written proof, not a finite-sampling proof.
5. Independently test the scalar periodic Green identity by discrete Fourier diagonalization, with a different two-dimensional finite-range product operator and mixed torus sizes. Compare the geometric image sum. Omission of periodic images must fail; no loop-level periodization is inferred.

Store versions, commands, exact/numerical outcomes, stdout and stderr. Preserve any failed attempts. Do not rerun author checks, which have already recorded successful outputs; inspect their full source and outputs instead.

Pre-execution refinement: the scalar core will be derived by integrating the three perpendicular coordinates first. The degree-five vector integral uses the derivative of a spherically cut-off scalar bubble; that bubble itself has a logarithmic UV divergence, and only its derivative has a convergent limit. This corrects the wording “convergent scalar convolution” above before execution.
