# Sea construction: frozen pre-execution criteria

Created after the theorem draft and before executing its checker, 2026-10-29.
The checker uses independent finite geometries and finite Haar/gauge fixtures;
none proves the general RP, cap, limiting or SU(N) inequalities in the note.

1. Enumerate closed fine cells, actual oriented links and plaquettes on even
   4D tori; verify 32 links/24 plaquettes per cell and four cells per
   plaquette. Verify no cell/plaquette crosses an integer reflection plane
   into both open halves; include wraparound cells. The normal-edge inverse
   must preserve the Wilson energy of a noncommuting SU(2) field. Omitting
   that inverse must change it.
2. Derive K_c from actual endpoint-block vertices. Enumerate contamination
   and source supports for L=3,5, including a two-block periodic direction.
   Verify the stated upper bounds. A fine-cell-only source support must
   omit actual exterior plaquettes, demonstrating why the enlarged support
   is required. A plaquette assigned to one cell must fail the four-cell
   witness count.
3. Enumerate a finite Z2 periodic gauge field and its correlated plaquette
   histories directly from links. Check all history weights, all tilted
   events and all holes against the same good reference. Use several real
   penalty values and the exact infinite-penalty limit. The toy has its own
   explicit sharp bound; it is not a numerical test of SU(N) constants.
   A uniform single-defect distribution tests why an h=0 probability
   estimate cannot replace the penalty-family hypothesis.
4. With rational correlated weights and actual field-dependent normalized
   kernels in a finite two-interior fixture, verify direct integration
   against the full joint replacement identity, including a hard insertion
   reading both replaced interiors. Test failures from a uniform coarse
   sea weight, omitted kernel normalizer, omitted source compensation,
   discarded failed return and insertion evaluated on the unreplaced sea.
   This is an identity diagnostic, not a representation of the Wilson model.
5. Directly integrate the SU(2) radial Haar density for the unitary-mean
   input M=rI. Verify unit mass with the actual normalizer and non-unit mass
   without it. Report quadrature tolerance and parameters. A rough mean
   must defeat a small-mismatch tail imported without the clean-contour
   hypothesis. This is an actual group-kernel calculation, not proof that
   a particular contour field realizes that mean globally.
6. Evaluate the fixed source-radius inequalities and the lane's stated
   asymptotic-freedom slope in its Wilson-u convention. Record that the
   proposed vq<1 window misses N=2,3 fixed-physical-volume scaling. The
   general proof must not depend on this failed window.

Pass means every positive invariant holds and every advertised mutation is
detected with the specified check. Failed runs remain recorded. Runtime
dependencies, script/output hashes and exact predecessor hashes belong in
the manifest. No retained-grade, audit or independent-review status is
conferred by author execution.
