**Independent mathematical checks.**

For the actual arithmetic contour mean, each interior gauge matrix
telescopes. Thus M transforms as a M b^-1 and
Re tr[(a V b^-1)* (a M b^-1)] equals Re tr(V* M). Bi-invariance of
probability Haar gives the same identity for the normalizer. This is an
exact argument, including singular M, without polar decomposition.
For RD's H11 test, transforming the frozen exterior and coarse output
maps every admissible completion bijectively to a completion. Plaquette
norms are conjugation invariant, and the path/coarse difference has
common unitary endpoint factors. Its minimum and threshold predicate
are invariant, including equality cases. Induction then gives RD R3's
history invariance. A different chart/history convention still needs
its transformed label or complete complement, as the note states.

The relative transporter R=VT* changes by conjugation at one endpoint.
Its conjugacy class, and the norm of its small logarithm, can therefore
survive the full joint action. Freezing T and transforming V alone is a
different operation. At fine identity, the numerator of the actual
kernel gives t[1-Re tr exp(z t_a)/N]=t z²/2+O(z⁴); its normalizer has no
z dependence. The weighted output Hessian is consequently
t h_(j+1)²=kappa N u_j/u_(j+1), with no small-remainder inference.
For the Haar chart, log[(1-e^-x)/x]=-x/2+x²/24+O(x⁴), and
tr_ad((ad a)²)=2N tr(a²). Hence -log j=N²|a|²/12+O(a⁴), exactly the
coefficient claimed. These are determined terms in a representation.

For q independent relative one-forms, conjugation invariance makes each
colour bilinear a multiple of the trace form. Hessian symmetry leaves
(4q)(4q+1)/2 real coefficients. Individual coordinate reflections kill
off-diagonal spatial entries; axis permutations identify the diagonals,
leaving q(q+1)/2 under full B4. This count neither bounds q in the
retained history nor counts arbitrary boundary/source coefficient
functions. The distinction is explicit in lines 221–225.

For the SU(2) example, identify a group element with a unit quaternion
v in S³. The integrated exponent is v·(tT+sW), and unitary means have
constant denominators Z(t), Z(s). Rotation of the sphere therefore gives

    I(T,W)=Z(|tT+sW|)/(Z(t)Z(s)),
    |tT+sW|²=t²+s²+2ts Re tr(T*W)/2.

At Re tr(T*W)/2=cos z the radius has quadratic coefficient
-ts/[2(t+s)]. Differentiating -log Z proves exactly
m_rel²=ts Z'(t+s)/[(t+s)Z(t+s)]. Pairing q0 and -q0 makes Z'(r)>0
for r>0, and q0<1 almost everywhere gives Z'(r)/Z(r)<1. This establishes
the strict bounds independently of quadrature. Integrating W first
still gives one. Thus complete normalization does not annihilate an
intermediate relative interaction. The example's unitary-mean scope is
properly distinguished from a generic actual arithmetic mean.

For a complete smooth single-field marginal, constant conjugation fixes
the identity and kills every first derivative because su(N) is spanned
by commutators. Differentiating local gauge invariance consequently
gives H(d omega,B)=0. On a contractible container the tree primitive
removes exact forms, and the remaining fundamental-loop circulations

curls. Nonlinear single-connection loop logs start at engineering

Differentiation of a normalized positive integral gives
