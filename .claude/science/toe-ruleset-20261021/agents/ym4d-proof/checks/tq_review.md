## Normalization, Ward identity and the precise charge

variables, rather than importing a previous PASS. Let `Phi_epsilon` be the
flow of the smooth configuration-space vector field Xi. Differentiation of

`0 = omega(Xi F - F Xi S_W + F div Xi)`.

the covariance stated in the note, including the Haar Jacobian term. For
`F=(A-omega A)(B-omega B)`, the derivatives of the two centering constants
multiply zero-mean variables in the differentiated expectation. With
`B_r=W_r+T_r`, the identity is consequently

`S'_j + t_j = Z_j^-2 [Cov(F_tilde,B_r)-C_j]`.

The signs in (4) are correct. The state-independent positive `Z_j` must stay
outside both terms. The note correctly avoids normalization by a divergent
fine variance and does not adopt `Z_j=1` from an unproved producer.

and cutoff differences. The former cancels against symmetric T. The
remaining coefficients have size `O(||J|| ||h'||_infinity)` and are supported
on the enlarged shell. There is no exact forward-difference cancellation of
a variable trace contribution. The specified no-wrap margin is adequate.

For each fixed admissible radius, integrate the identity first:

`S_j(u)-S_j(0) = -integral_0^u t_j(s,r) ds + integral_0^u e_j(s,r) ds`.

contact also tends to zero when two fixed radii are compared. The note does
not repeat the older contact-free radius bound.

## Gapless comparators, flux and the sphere distinction

The massless scalar comparator is sound on real Schwartz tests. One can
realize its Gaussian family by applying an isonormal Gaussian process to
`hat f(p)/|p|` in momentum L2. The low-momentum squared norm is finite in
four dimensions because it has radial factor `r dr`; Schwartz decay
controls large momentum. Rotation is unitary on that L2 space, proving
invariance of every Gaussian moment.

For fixed spatial momentum k, the inverse of
`-d_t^2+|k|^2` is `exp(-|k||t|)/(2|k|)`: its derivative jump gives the delta
source directly. The reflected quadratic form is the positive integral of
the squares of the time Laplace transforms of the tests, with weight
`d^3k/(2|k|)`. Momentum sets with arbitrarily small positive |k| have
nonzero weight. This verifies the stated gapless spectral behavior without
borrowing a Yang–Mills spectral assumption.

In the separated stress insertion, Wick contractions leave exactly two
crossings to the distant insertion. Differentiating `1/(4 pi^2 |x|^2)`
gives one power `O(|x|^-3)` per crossing, so the displayed mixed tensor is
`O(|x|^-6)` and its absolute shell sum is `O(r^-2)`. This uses no coincident
stress variance or globally defined ordinary composite field. It establishes
compatibility of the sufficient power count with a gapless model, not an
interacting Wilson estimate.

The spectral example has the claimed reflected Gram representation
`integral exp(-u s) exp(-u t) dmu(u)` and Laplace transform
`(1+s+t)^(-alpha)`. Thus positivity alone does not fix a decay rate.
For the exterior tensor, differentiating its two summands gives respectively
`(Mx)_nu/r^6` and `(d+1-6)(Mx)_nu/r^6`; their sum vanishes in d=4.
Contracting with the outward normal on a radius-r sphere gives
`n_mu sigma_(mu nu)=r^-4 (Mn)_nu`. Multiplication by `Jx=r Jn` and surface
measure `r^3 dOmega` gives the radius-independent outward flux
`|S^3| tr(M^T J)/4`. A decreasing radial cutoff gives its negative in the
annular divergence integral; no inconsistent use of this sign occurs in
the note. Conservation plus a nonzero integrated flux is not a pointwise
rotlet classification, a Goldstone theorem or a Wilson/RP counterexample.

The sphere distinction is also correct conditionally. A global Killing
field contracts to zero with a symmetric continuum stress, without a radial

the sphere embedding,
`exp_N(x)=(R sin(|x|/R) x/|x|, R cos(|x|/R))`, so the stated SO(4)
