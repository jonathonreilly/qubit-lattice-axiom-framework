## Root causes

The 179 lane walls fall into 81 distinct walls. I read those as six roots.

1. **The axioms supply no dynamics, time or rate.** They say so
   themselves. This root covers T01, T02, T09, T11, T14, T15, T28, T29,
   T66, T79 and T80.
   - Reading: T01 (formation order and rate) and T11 (a clock with a rate)
     are one premise.
   - The minimal covariant clause is an independent unit-rate Poisson
     clock at each site. It is covariant and randomised, so it escapes
     L02-W1(d)'s theorem that no covariant acyclic order exists.
   - It supplies the unit of time and a definite formation law. That law
     is not the static law: L02-W1(b) proves the static law lies outside
     the hull.
2. **The axioms supply no readout or statistics.** This covers T03, T05,
   T06, T07, T08, T10, T41, T69 and T73.
3. **One qubit per site is too small for what each lane builds.** The
   lanes import larger spaces:
   - the walker (4 states);
   - the taste cube (C^8);
   - non-abelian links (2N states);
   - graviton slots (continuous);
   - chirality (4 components).

   This root covers T19, T21, T22, T24, T25, T27 and T64.
4. **A fixed regular Z^3 grid** brings anisotropy, an unprotected light
   cone, shape stiffness and an orientation-dependent entanglement
   coefficient. This covers T14, T61, T64 (partly) and T66.
5. **Every dimensionless number is supplied or matched.** This covers
   T28–T40 and T44–T57. Most of these lanes predate the 2026-06-29 reset
   and rest on the staggered-Dirac realization, which the axioms list as
   an open gate. That single bridge is load-bearing for about 30 of the
   81 walls.
6. **One orientation Z2 bit** is shared by T22, T23, T42 and T43, and
   possibly by the sign of T38's phase.

## Shared price I expect to recur

Several positive routes need Z^3 read as a typed cell complex: vertex,
edge, face and cube roles by coordinate parity. These routes are:
- the qubit-link photon (T78);
- exact bosonization for local fermions, which I expect for T18 and T19;
- the tensor graviton (T64).

T77 says this typing breaks the axioms' one-qubit-everywhere covariance.
If so, one owner decision ("sites have parity roles") prices three or
more walls at once.

## Per-wall expectations (pre-registered)

- **T03 (what a record is).** Reading B, soft locks with a rate and a
  length, is GRW/CSL collapse (Ghirardi–Rimini–Weber 1986; Pearle 1989).
  - It reproduces Born statistics by construction and is non-unitary, so
    probe 7's no-rise theorem does not apply.
  - The heat-budget bound on sharp one-site births is the known CSL
    heating constraint.
  - Viable CSL windows exist only at a mesoscopic length (r_C ~ 1e-7 m),
    not at one site.
  - Expected outcome: PRICED, at two constants (λ, r_C), with "one record
    per site" read as coarse-grained.
- **T05 / T06 (Born).** For a qubit, Gleason fails (dim 2), but Busch's
  POVM theorem gives the trace form. It needs non-contextual additivity
  over the whole effect algebra: T06's "menu independence plus
  abundance". Expected: PRICED at the Busch premise.
- **T18 / T19 (exchange sign, local fermions).** Levin–Wen 2003 and
  Chen–Kapustin exact bosonization (2018–2020) build emergent fermions
  from qubits under a local Z2 constraint, and the exchange sign follows
  from the constraint.
  - Admissibility, a nearest-neighbour rule, could in principle be that
    constraint.
  - The price is qubits on faces or links, i.e. the typed complex above.
  - Expected: PRICED, or PASSED for "a sign can emerge", with the
    covariance price stated.
- **T21 (mass).** M2(C) has no element anticommuting with all three
  Paulis (checked by the L02 probe). So mass needs either a staggered
  two-sublattice term (a 2-site cell) or M4(C). Expected: PRICED.
- **T30 (Planck to physical scales).** Suggested; I will check this.
  - With 16 light tastes the SU(3) one-loop b0 = 11 − (2/3)·16 = 1/3, so
    the coupling barely runs. α_LM = 0.0907 at M_Pl gives α(v) ≈ 0.11 at
    one loop, close to the chain's 0.1033.
  - The chain therefore works only if all 16 tastes stay light from M_Pl
    down to v. But lattice doublers are cutoff artefacts.
  - The premise "tastes are light down to v" is the real price. It
    conflicts with six observed flavours, unless tastes are relabelled as
    generations and flavours (T24, T25).
- **T38 (Koide).** Q = 2/3 ⇔ |b|²/a² = 1/2 ⇔ the trivial and doublet
  isotypes have equal Frobenius norm when each isotype, not each
  dimension, is counted once. Expected: PRICED at "uniform weight per
  isotype". The wall already says as much.
- **T01 / T11.** Expected: PRICED at one clause, a per-site Poisson
  clock. It also answers "what is t". Its law differs from the static
  law.
- **T02.** Expected: MISFRAMED as a derivation target. The axioms
  explicitly exclude dynamics. The cheaper question is which minimal
