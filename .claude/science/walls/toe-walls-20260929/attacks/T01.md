## Verdict in plain words
The axioms fix what a record says when it forms, not the order sites form in (`AX:66-69,181-184`); the whole gap is which neighbours are already written when a site is written. The cheapest closure is one clause, that the next site is chosen blind to the record state: no free numbers, one law on all of Z^3 (landed). It cannot be derived, since three covariant clauses differ in the bulk (my test). It also costs an ordered phase and the frozen-box picture.

## The wall (checked)
- Plain: The axioms say records form and each new record depends on its recorded neighbours, not in what order sites get records. Order matters: different orders give different overall laws, and the equilibrium law is none of them. No fixed order respects rotations, so it must be random or simultaneous, and nothing gives its odds or rate. The axioms yield a family of laws.
- Precise: Target: one finished-record law for the six-axis positive product rule (weights not all equal) from the four axioms alone. Proved on windows: the law depends on the order only through recorded-neighbour sets of size 2 or more; with a cycle the static law is outside the hull of all adapted schemes; on an acyclic predecessor structure every clause gives one law (09-20 note:23); no rotation-invariant order exists (09-13 note:26). Passing means deriving a unique law or clause, not adopting one.
- Real? Yes, correctly stated and unsolved: no open PR closes it (#9314/9316/9345 supply a birth law); archive R101/R112 name the same gap. L01-W1 and L02-W1 are one wall; L01-W2's rate/gate part is T01b, its sealed-box parts go to T76/T03.
