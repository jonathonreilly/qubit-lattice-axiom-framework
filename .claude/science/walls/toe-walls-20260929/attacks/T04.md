## Verdict in plain words
The wall says a moving record needs an identity rule, and the rule changes the answer. For the photon flip that is misframed: two identical records go round a square with no tracking rule. Only their class matters (bosons, fermions, or different locked contents); in my model the class changes the flip strength by 0.2 to 40 percent, never to zero. For particles, a lagging tagged record is what "particles are patterns" predicts. The real price is elsewhere: record hops move a field only if the ice rule is soft (an exact rule freezes it, landed), and hops leave readable records off their sites. No wall is passed; the question is replaced.
