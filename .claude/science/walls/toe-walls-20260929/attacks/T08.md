# Attack on T08: Records plus local odds cannot carry quantum (Bell) correlations

Attacker: Claude Sonnet 5.5 (same family as the supervisor; same-family checks, not independent referees).

Read: `docs/MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md` (all), `PRIMITIVE_REGISTRY_CHECK.md` (nothing registered bears on it); Bell-values note (09-24), records-only admissibility note, formation-versus-static note (09-06), probe 5, viability map (#9363). Only #9363 (probe 5) touches Bell among open PRs.

## Verdict in plain words
The wall is real for records that form one at a time from neighbours. But the axiom text only fixes each single site's odds; it says nothing about how sites forming together are correlated. I built an exact patch, forming in one draw, where every single site's odds are local yet CHSH reaches 4, at any range: a pair of middle records carries the far setting in their correlation. Forbid that (no readable cluster of records may depend on a non-adjacent setting) and the bound returns. So the wall is one premise, cluster locality. Passing costs a wave or non-local formation units.

## The wall (checked)
- Plain: Records form from what their neighbours show. Two distant experimenters pick settings freely. Results then act like classical coins agreed in advance: Bell's CHSH number is at most 2, while quantum mechanics and experiment give 2.83. Something extra is needed: a wave, or far-reaching odds.
- Precise: derive CHSH above 2 for outcome records a, b of two wings from the four axioms. Landed Thm 1 assumes fixed causal order, independent fresh noise per record, exogenous settings, one world. Passing needs a derived value above 2 or a decisive no-go for every exit; supplied waves do not pass.
- Real? Yes; unsolved in docs/, archive/, open PRs. Amendment: "records plus local odds" is over-broad (premise 3).

Thm 5/6 (suggested, numerically checked): in a Markov patch a cut G separating A from B gives P(a,b|s,t) = sum_g P(g|s,t) P(a|s,g) P(b|t,g). If P(g|s,t) is constant this is a local model, so CHSH is at most 2. Cluster locality makes it constant when no cut site touches a setting.
