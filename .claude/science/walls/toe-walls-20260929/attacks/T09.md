## Verdict in plain words
The wall is two walls glued together. A finite-speed cone is free once any local update exists; what is missing is an update carrying undamped waves. Non-negative odds cannot (a non-negative unitary matrix is a permutation, so only rigid sliding survives). The lane's tick evidence was one component per site. With a Hamiltonian instead of a tick the qubit works, and covariance leaves three free numbers. The wall is priced, not passed.

## The wall (checked)
- Plain: Records with odds that cannot be negative only spread out or slide rigidly. Waves need weights that cancel. A one-qubit rule ticking once, identically in every cube direction, gives nothing that both moves and curves. So the layer carrying light, and any speed limit, is not in the four axioms.
- Precise: Target: from the four axioms, a local update with finite-speed propagation and undamped dispersive (massless and massive) modes. Pass = derivation or decisive no-go; a supplied Hamiltonian, tick or coin does not pass.
