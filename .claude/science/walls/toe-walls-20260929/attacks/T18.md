## Verdict in plain words
The wall is real, and the lane's price ("one sign bit") is too low. The axioms give unlabelled records with nearest-neighbour rules. On such records in two or three dimensions, no rule of bounded range can make far-apart records exchange with a minus sign, in any gauge; the minus sign needs a string of unbounded reach. So locality tilts toward hard-core bosons, and fermions need a named non-local (graded) clause. This prices the wall; it does not pass it.
