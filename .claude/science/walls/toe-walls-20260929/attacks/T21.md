## Verdict in plain words
Not passed; the price is sharper and lower than the lane states. A walker's rest energy needs a background that breaks one-site translation, in practice a chessboard; no translation-invariant term gaps it (proved, bounded). The lane says records make that chessboard only at absurdly costly bonds (g ≤ 1.4e-4). That is a proof bound: the contentless record gas is exactly the 3D Ising antiferromagnet and orders below g ≈ 0.41, and the walker keeps a band-edge gap there (checked). But that needs records to equilibrate; random permanent formation orders leave no clean gap.
