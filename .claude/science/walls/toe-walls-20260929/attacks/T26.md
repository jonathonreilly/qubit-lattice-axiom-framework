## Test run
- Pre-registered (`T26_scratch/PREREGISTER.md`): R1 survives if su(3) commutes with the hopping or g8 is already translation-closed (dim 12); dies if the hopping commutant is abelian or the closure exceeds 12.
- Script: `T26_scratch/test_T26.py`, `test_T26b.py`, `test_T26c.py` (operators from `scripts/frontier_graph_first_su3_integration.py`).
- Result:
  - A: commutant of the shifts S_i = X_i is abelian (dim 8); of Cl(3) Gamma_i it is u(2)+u(2); su(3) meets them in dim 1 and 0. max ||[T_a, S_2+S_3]|| = 3.16.
  - B: closure of g8 under translations = su(4)+su(2), dim 18, exact match; same under Z_i. Only the four words in <X_1, X_2X_3> normalise g8. With axis permutations: 63. Control: weak su(2) alone closes to su(2)^3, dim 9.
  - C: on a bond, 3 of the 8 su(3)_Sym generators are single-site sums; 5 are two-site.
  - D: vertex vacuum H = X_1 has isotropy dim 32 (u(4)+u(4)); g8 meets it in dim 10.
  - L: on C^6 the commutant of su(3)+su(2)+u(1) is scalars, so a constant hopping commuting with it cannot separate it from u(6). One cross generator closes it to u(6) (dim 36).
- Reading: R1 is dead and adds a premise: either the gauge sector breaks translation covariance, or the algebra is at least su(4)+su(2) and colour is a vacuum choice (4 = 3+1).
