## Verdict in plain words
The wall is real and unsolved, but it is not quite what it says. With the repo's own 16 massless tastes the strong force has no scale at all, so the scale can only come from when the tastes switch off, and nothing supplies that. The repo's one-loop no-go survives at two and three loops (checked). The coupling half is easy: about 15 massless flavours carry alpha_LM to alpha_s(v) = 0.103. The scale half is an unsupplied threshold law plus one lattice computation.

## The wall (checked)
- Plain: The lattice coupling is order one at the Planck scale. Ordinary running from there confines near 10^15-10^18 GeV, not 0.2 GeV. The repo declares the lattice coupling to be the coupling at 246 GeV and uses a sixteen-rung formula. Neither is derived.
- Precise: From SU(3) Wilson at beta = 6, a^-1 = M_Pl and 16 staggered tastes, derive Lambda_QCD/M_Pl (1.6e-20), v/M_Pl (2e-17) and alpha_s at a stated scale. Passing is a proof or decisive runner, scheme conversion stated. Declaring alpha_bare/u0^2 to be alpha_s(v) (B4) does not pass.
- Real? Yes. L06-W7, L16-W2, L07-W7 are one wall; L06-W16 is its attempted route. Not solved in docs/, archive/ or the 36 open PRs.
