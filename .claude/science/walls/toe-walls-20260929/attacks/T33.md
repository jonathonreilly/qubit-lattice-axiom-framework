## Verdict in plain words
Nothing derives the weak or hypercharge coupling; 1/4 and 1/5 are supplied counts. The wall is misframed. "Unit charge" is a normalization convention, alpha_em and sin^2(theta_W) are combinations of two couplings, and their lattice values are not observables without a bridge to low energy, which the repo runs three incompatible ways. Under one bridge all three forces want about the same lattice coupling (1/4, spread 11.5%), not (1, 1/4, 1/5). The lane's triple fits only by giving SU(3) a no-running map and SU(2)/U(1) full running. Nothing here passes it.

## The wall (checked)
- Plain: The weak and hypercharge strengths at the smallest scale, and from them the charge unit and fine-structure constant, are set by hand ("1/4", "1/5", counting algebra generators). Nothing in the rules fixes them, and carrying them to ordinary energies needs a 17-decade bridge the repo treats differently for each force.
- Precise: derive g_2^2, g_Y^2 at the lattice scale (then e^2 = g_2^2 g_Y^2/(g_2^2+g_Y^2), sin^2 = g_Y^2/(g_2^2+g_Y^2)) from the axioms plus scale, isotropy and realized-state primitives, with the map to M_Z. Assigned counts, conditionals and fits do not pass.
- Real? Yes; unsolved in docs/, archive/, 36 open PRs. The lane omits a historic route (`archive_unlanded/historic_intake_originals/branch01/45_ALPHA_EM_DERIVATION_NOTE.md`). Member L06-W14 fits poorly: its ring-model alpha_G = 0.28-0.37 is about 35x the counting rule's alpha_em = 1/(36 pi).
