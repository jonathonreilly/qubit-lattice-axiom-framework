## Verdict in plain words
The atlas writes the four mixing numbers as alpha_s/2, 2/3, 1/6, 1/6, with 2, 3, 6 taken from the quark table. Nothing derives those four steps and no route I tried does: a coupling that treats up and down quarks alike cannot mix them (checked), so a derivation must first split the generations (T36). The pattern is not dismissible: small-integer formula families match all four numbers by chance with probability 1e-4 to 0.1, depending on the family. As a formula with no stated error it is off: lambda is 3.3 sigma high. The wall is one flavour rule with four identifications.
