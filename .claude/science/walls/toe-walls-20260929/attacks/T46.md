## Verdict in plain words
The wall says the strange/bottom mass ratio follows from the mixing number V_cb by a power 5/6, and a scale rule is needed to make it fit. It is misframed. A mass ratio at one energy does not change with energy; done that way the power law misses by 18.6% (13 sigma), not 0.2%. The 0.2% comes from quoting the strange mass at 2 GeV and the bottom mass at its own mass, a convention that lets any exponent from 4/5 to 8/9 fit. Running the Standard Model to the Planck scale does not rescue it. No scale rule is needed, and 5/6 has no supporting number left.

## The wall (checked)
- Plain: A bridge formula turns V_cb into a down-quark mass ratio by the power 5/6. It matches only if the two masses are quoted at different energies.
- Precise: derive |V_cb| = (m_s/m_b)^(C_F-T_F), unit prefactor, non-perturbatively at g=1, plus a rule fixing each mass's energy. Passing: a derivation on a scale-covariant surface. Not passing: +0.20% on m_s(2 GeV)/m_b(m_b).
- Real? Partly misstated: the common-scale miss is +18.6%, not +14%, because the lane's one-loop transport (1.14) understates the four-loop 1.184 (`t46_p1b_out.txt`). Otherwise unsolved.
