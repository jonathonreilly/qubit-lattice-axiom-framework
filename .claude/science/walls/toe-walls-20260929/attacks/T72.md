## Verdict in plain words
The wall is a list that mixes different questions. Where a clock scales a body's whole generator, "does it fall like a walker?" reduces to one number per kind of body, W = rest energy times inverse inertia, which must equal 1. Direct simulation confirms this. Free walkers and the eight doublers fall alike or fail for single named reasons; two listed items (diffusive drift, "minus its weight") are not free-fall failures. The composite part is real and not passed: a bound pair falls about five times its binding fraction too slowly unless the binding is tuned.
