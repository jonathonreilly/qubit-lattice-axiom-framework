The wall is real. Its support half (every vertex balanced) has a price: local formation avoids all defects only if the formation order is itself a sharp 3-earlier / 3-later front, so the balance law moves from the pattern into the order. That is the lane's own sweep, plus defect counts I ran. ## The wall (checked)
- Plain: A photon-like pattern needs each grid point to balance its six neighbours. Records form one at a time, each seeing only earlier neighbours, but a balance rule constrains both sides at once. The photon model also gives sites different jobs, against "every site alike".
- Precise: target is uniform ice (3 of 6 links at each vertex) as a directed nearest-neighbour formation outcome, one qubit per site, full covariance. Coordinators, plans, layer units and typed roles do not pass.
- Real? Yes. The members belong together, except L02-W10's freezing theorem (2-site generators), which is dynamics and sits with T79. No open PR or `archive/` note solves it.

## Test run
- Pre-registered (`T77_scratch/PREREGISTER.md`): A fails the route if the every-site rule has zero bulk entropy; B is a price if a sharp order gives zero defects and jitter, random and closed orders give defects.
- Scripts: `T77_scratch/testA_count.c`, `testA_prism.py`, `testB_orders.py`.
- Test A (exact balance at every site, values ±1): one sublattice has 140 patterns on the 4-torus and 0 on the 6-torus; the parity-typed control reproduces the landed 9600. A 4x4xn transfer matrix has spectral radius 1.0000 (140 for every n divisible by 4). Loosening to |sum| ≤ 2 gives 1.1e7 but allows charge.
- Test B (landed soldered model; an unrecorded vertex carries a Gauss charge): sweeps 0 defects; timing jitter 0 up to sigma 0.15, then 0.4%, 5.5%, 12.5% at sigma 0.3, 0.6, 1.2; iid random order 16.4% ± 0.3%, block charge variance per volume 0.43-0.46 for blocks 2-4 (no screening); lexicographic order on a closed torus: defects × L = 0.32-0.35 at L=6, 10, 14. Defect rate per vertex: 0 for ≤3 earlier neighbours, then 0.13, 0.34, 0.69 (landed 1/8, 3/8, 11/16).
- Reading: only the order relation between neighbours matters, not timing precision. Random and closed orders give defects: a price, not a free lunch.

## Outcome
PRICED, support half only. Price: **a sharp formation order with three earlier and three later neighbours at every vertex** (the arrow of formation obeys the same balance law), plus a supplied role pattern. Argument: each earlier neighbour hands a vertex a link value; four or more can conflict. In the declared model exact ice needs at most three, and averaging over a lattice forces exactly three. The uniform-measure half STANDS: sweep correlations live in forward cones only, the uniform law has none (DIRECTED_ICE_SWEEP Thms 3-4). Whether a cubic-invariant balanced order exists is open; sweeps break rotations to C3.
