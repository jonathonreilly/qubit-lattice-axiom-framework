## Verdict in plain words
The wall is real but smaller than listed. The axioms contain no time, so the class is a supplied clause. This attack shrinks the supply. "Energy is conserved" is the same statement as "no field runs away in either time direction". Keeping the ice rule invariant removes the vertex and cube extras. The "time rule" fork is one friction rate that must be exactly zero. What remains is exact reversibility, one real component per link with no coin, and one-neighbour reach. The class also cannot be an exact law on bounded qubit domains; it is the small-oscillation limit of a conservative law, so its content sits in T02 and T78.

## Outcome
PRICED. The price is not "the class". It is (N) exact reversibility of unrecorded field values with no runaway, equal to item 6 and to friction 0; (P) one real per edge and face, no coin, with the orientation law; (L) one-neighbour reach. With ice-rule invariance and covariance, N, P, L give the class. N is load-bearing and cannot be "small": friction above about 1e-61 per tick would show as photon attenuation, and the lane's slow-formation scaling (record-motion note, eq. 5) would need link spin near 1e10. The 9086 note supplies N conditionally, in the harmonic regime only. Siblings moved: T02, T78, T01, T14.
