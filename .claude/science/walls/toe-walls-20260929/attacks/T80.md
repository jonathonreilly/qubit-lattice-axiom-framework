## Verdict in plain words
T80 glues one real number to a list that belongs elsewhere. The real one is the binding scale c: how much more a record-beside-record contact weighs than a record-beside-empty one, which the axioms' odds cannot see. In a simulation of the ordered record gas, at equal record density order and stiffness do not depend on c or the fugacity (agreement 0.2%), and the neutral value c = c0 still orders at ordinary parameters. So the lane is not piling up infrared constants; what is open is what sets the density. K, alpha/K, bond-rate coefficients and formation rate belong to the gravity member and T01 (priced in T68, T70, T01).

## The wall (checked)
- Plain: The axioms say what a record contains given its neighbours, not whether crowding alone helps or hinders.
- Precise: Target: fix or make redundant every number beyond the rule's weights that record dynamics needs (c, z, K, alpha/K, bond-rate law, formation rate), from the axioms plus registered primitives. A proposal (c = c0) or a sufficient bound does not pass.
- Real? Partly. c is real and unsolved (proved: block 39 T2, block 40 T4); no PR or archive derives it.
