# T07 pre-registration (written before any script was run)

Attacker: Claude Sonnet 5.5 (same vendor family as the supervisor; same-family checks).
Wall: records start distributed as |psi|^2 (quantum equilibrium) in the records-as-beables reading
(probe 5, Bell's minimal jump law). Lane file: L03-W8.

Route R1 to test: "the jump law relaxes a non-equilibrium record ensemble by itself, with no
typicality measure and no coarse-graining" (the wall's own suggested test, made exact: I integrate the
ensemble master equation itself, not sampled histories).

## Objects (all exact, no Monte Carlo)
- Probe-5 protocol: two walkers, each on a 24-site ring, H = sin k sigma_z per ring, singlet coins,
  settings theta_A in {0, pi/2}, theta_B in {+-pi/4}, packet exp(-(x-12)^2/(2*1.5^2)), outcome = side
  of site 12 at t = 8, Bell minimal rates max(0,J_yx)/P(x) on the 576 joint configurations.
- rho_t: the record ensemble, dRho/dt = W_t rho (RK4, dt = 0.005, waves from the exact propagator).
- D_t = KL(rho_t || P_t), P_t = |psi_t|^2 summed over coins. S_t = signalling
  = max over theta_B, over pairs of theta_A settings, of |P(B=+ | theta_A, theta_B) - P(B=+ | theta_A', theta_B)|.

## Test A (lemma check; random waves, 1D/2D/3D)
Claims (elementary, to be checked): (A1) D_t is non-increasing for any rho_0 << P_0, with
dD/dt = - sum_{x,y} max(0,J_yx) phi(f_x, f_y), phi(a,b) = a ln(a/b) - a + b >= 0, f = rho/P;
(A2) df_y/dt = sum_x [max(0,J_yx)/P_y] (f_x - f_y) so max f falls and min f rises; (A3) D is exactly
constant for a real wave (all currents zero).
PASS: max increase of D over all steps <= 1e-10 in 20 random instances; identity for dD/dt within 1e-6
relative; max/min f monotone; real-wave drift <= 1e-12. FAIL of any: the lemma is wrong, R1's
"fine-grained relaxation without coarse-graining" claim is dead.

## Test B (the decisive one; the probe-5 protocol, non-equilibrium starts)
Starts: (0) equilibrium control; (1) product, each record width x2; (2) product, each record width x0.4;
(3) product shifted by 2 sites; (4) uniform on the ring; (5) point mass at site 12 for both;
(6) correlated: P_0 * (1 + 0.5 s_A s_B), s = sign(x - 11.5).
Control PASS: equilibrium start stays equilibrium (max |rho_8 - P_8| <= 1e-7) and reproduces the
exact CHSH 2.79 of probe 5.
R1 "relaxes by itself" PASSES if, for every start, D_8/D_0 <= 0.25 and S at t = 8 <= 0.02.
R1 FAILS (wall stands for this walker) if any start has D_8/D_0 >= 0.5 or S_8 >= 0.05.
In between: partial, reported as numbers.
Also recorded: max over t of S_t, CHSH from records, the minimum gamma below (Test E).
Reading of PASS: the wall would be a statement about the law, not an initial condition. Reading of
FAIL: quantum equilibrium is an initial-condition premise for this law (priced), with a measured size.

## Test C (scaling of memory with wavelength; single walker, ring L = 160)
rho_0 = P_0 (1 + 0.5 cos(2 pi m x / L)), m in {1, 2, 4, 8}, broad packet, evolve to t = 20.
Prediction (suggested, diffusive): -ln(D_T/D_0) grows like m^p with p between 1.5 and 2.5 and is
below 0.2 at m = 1. PASS = that scaling. FAIL = relaxation rate roughly independent of m
(p < 0.5) meaning the law mixes ballistically and R1 is stronger than I expect.

## Test D (reverse-past formula and Dobrushin coefficient, Bell protocol)
Kernel K_{0->8} (576x576) from the same integrator. Check f_8 = K diag(P_0) f_0 / P_8 (algebra) and
K P_0 = P_8 (equivariance, 1e-9). Compute delta = max_{x,x'} TV(K(x,.), K(x',.)) ("forgetting").
Prediction: delta >= 0.99 (records remember where they started). PASS of prediction = the law does
not forget by t = 8. If delta <= 0.5 the law forgets and R1 improves.

## Test E (price of forgetting: an added symmetric noise term, Vink freedom)
Add rates gamma * min(P_x, P_y)/P_x between joint configurations differing by one ring step
(detailed balance with P_t, so equivariance is kept). Find the smallest gamma (bisection over
0..4) that gives D_8/D_0 <= 0.25 and S_8 <= 0.02 for all starts of Test B. Also check equivariance
mismatch stays <= 1e-8 with gamma > 0. This gives a number for the "noise premise" price.

## Test F (route R2: what local formation can make)
Mutual information I(x_A; x_B) of P_t at t = 0 and t = 8, and the Bell-note kind of Markov deficit
D(P_t || P_A x P_B) = I. Prediction: I_0 = 0 (a product law is made by purely local odds) and
I_8 > 1e-3 (the correlation the Bell result needs is generated by the motion, not put in at
formation). PASS = both. FAIL (I_8 ~ 0) would mean the protocol never needed non-local structure.

## What would change the verdict
- Test A fail: drop R1 entirely.
- Test B pass: outcome moves toward PASSED-for-this-walker (R1 survives), though eigenstates (Test A3)
  and smooth starts (Test C) still limit it.
- Test B fail and Test E gamma of order one: PRICED with two named premises (registered initial data,
  or a noise clause of rate gamma).
