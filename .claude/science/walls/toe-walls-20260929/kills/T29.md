## Errors found
1. **Grade and band overclaimed.** The protocol's grade 4 needs (stat+FV) at z=2 below 5e-5. The attacker's sigma_tot is 4.3e-5, so 2 sigma is 8.5e-5 and the grade is not met. Protocol step 6 says a failed L^-4 gate means "certificate NOT issued, no ansatz-shopping". The attacker switched to direct points, disclosed in PREREG "as-run". So Band D is a reading of an uncertified estimate, not a formal band verdict. "Checked" is fine; "grade 4" and "fails its own pre-registered band" are not.
2. **Scope of "wrong".** 0.5937 lies inside the chain note's own +/-0.0006 (`docs/YT_ZERO_IMPORT_CHAIN_NOTE.md:192`), and the -0.19% residual is inside the repo's quoted +/-0.4%. The licence is broken only at 4-decimal grade.
3. **Dictionary does not discriminate.** The test passes for the bare coupling (n = 0, 0.0796) as well, so it cannot separate "n is a scale choice" from "n is a scheme statement at the cutoff". The plaquette also gives a V-scheme coupling. Using the Lepage-Mackenzie relation (coefficients from memory, a reading), alpha_V(3.4/a) is 0.125 (LO) or 0.152 (NLO). One-loop q for n = 4 is then 2.5 to 5.6 per a, against the attacker's 7.5 (`kills/T29_scratch/vscheme.py`). So the lattice does pick out n of about 4 near the cutoff. Only n = 1, 2 are far beyond it: 19 to 43 and 9 to 20 per a. The direction of the price holds; "n = 1, 2, 4 all beyond cutoff" and "fixed by no lattice observable" do not.
4. **"B-SPLIT + B4 are one choice" is a reading.** B-SPLIT was never tested (the attack says it needs fermions), so "proved/checked" cannot attach to it.
5. **"Owner policy" for P-NUM.** No such policy is in the repo. It is an unregistered proposed premise (PRIMITIVE_REGISTRY_CHECK item 6). L16-W4 lists it as an open ask.
