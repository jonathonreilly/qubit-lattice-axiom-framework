## Errors found
1. Wrong exit count. "Passing needs Pi" ignores exact spectral matching, which passes with no shear Ward identity (P6 N2). The price is "Pi, or matching that survives interactions".
2. Class not stated in the plain verdict. The proof needs hopping affine in the inverse vielbein (not "the metric"; P6:65-77), fermions only (boson zero-point energy is convex, P6 T5), an e-independent interaction, an isotropic gradient, and evenness (odd-range hops). K1: replacing V by V(1 + b t^2) along the shear path flips c_vp (V=0.5: -2.01 at b=0, +6.52 at b=1). "No interaction cancels it" holds only for e-independent interactions; a metric-dependent one cancels at a tuned number, which is P6 T10's picture.
3. Sign error in D5. c < 0 is growth, yet the headline "a >= 9e-10 m" and "1e-71" use the c > 0 mass bound. With the Hubble yardstick (in the attack's own table) an untuned pass needs a >= 8.4e-5 m, and tuning at a = 2e-27 m is about 3e-91 (K2). The numbers understate the wall.
4. Labels. "Linear in the metric", "one-line proof", "no interaction cancels it" read as proved; the report lists c <= K/12 as suggested. D3 is one 4x2 torus, near-degenerate at V >= 1 (gap 0.0265, 0.0003): it checks an inequality, not a prediction. "Three numbers tuned" (D4 counts allowed terms) and "at every order" are suggested.
5. Prior art: nothing on main or in open PRs (#9289, #9363) covers the interacting Jensen bound; the June diffeo-Ward notes are free-matter diagnostics. Novelty stands; citations are from memory.

## Corrected plain-language verdict
On a fixed grid, fermion matter with hopping linear in the vielbein and interactions that ignore the metric always makes flat space an energy maximum along shears, by at least a twelfth of its hopping energy per cell; the numbers reproduce. That is priced, not passed: passing needs an exact shear conservation law for interacting matter, or exact boson-fermion cancellation that survives interactions, or a tuned metric-dependent interaction at every order. The tuning is about 1e-91 at 2e-27 m spacing (growth sign), not 1e-71.
