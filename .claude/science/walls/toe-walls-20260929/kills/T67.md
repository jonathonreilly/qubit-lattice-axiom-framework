Nearest-neighbour bond lengths alone do not give Einstein gravity: independent diagonals make them pure relabelling or massive, and slaved diagonals give a healthy but non-invariant O(k²) form. The invariant form needs six shear numbers per vertex and a relabelling symmetry supplied, as the repository's 2026-09-21/23 notes already say. Blind formation clocks give no cone; a coupled clause gives macroscopic futures at a scale set by a supplied nucleation rate, and whether they form a Lorentz cone is open.
