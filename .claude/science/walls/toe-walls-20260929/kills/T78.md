## Errors found
1. **The "does not shrink" test is nearly tautological (checked/reading).** At fixed flux q the ensemble is a uniform background field B=q/L^2, so <N_f> = a - c' L^3 B^2 gives c_L constant for ANY translation-invariant local ensemble with a smooth n_f(B). A power-law decay of c_L would need a singularity. So the pre-registered FAIL branch (p>0.5) was practically unreachable, and "pre-registered pass" carries little weight. It confirms analyticity, not a photon. The exponent 0.012+/-0.020 is statistically fine (my L=8 rerun agrees to 0.03).
2. **Component selection missing from the premise table.** Main's note says E_Phi = eps*max over components of <N_f>_C, and the sampler only samples the seed's component. The attack only says "sampled component is the seed's". I tested: exact plaquette-tiling seeds (SAT, tiling.py, N_f = 0.328 per 3L^3 at L=4,6, far above typical) relax into the SAME component (0.2656 at L=4, 0.2616 at L=6; attack 0.2657, 0.2616). 300 random flux-0 ice states at L=4 and 60 at L=6 (loop moves, components2.py) give component means within noise of the big one (spread 0.264-0.268, error ~0.0014). No competing high-N_f component found (checked, generic states only; rare ordered components not excluded).
3. **Unreconciled V=0 comparison.** Attack says first-order U=0.80 eps vs pure-ring winding U_W = 1.46, 1.11, 0.67 (L=4,6,8) falling with L. At eps=K the first-order value is flat while the exact data fall, so O(eps^2) is L-dependent there; only E2's q=0 part was measured (second_order.py), not its flux dependence.
4. **Speed arithmetic.** sqrt(4uU) with u=K/4, U=0.8 eps gives 0.89 sqrt(eps), not 0.91; K=4u is a suggested premise (Gaussian note), not proved.
5. Prereg text calls 432/55 the q=0 check; script compares the 864-state component mean 8. Harmless. PRs #9372/#9391/#9392 (state CLOSED, not merged) concern V=0 curvature: 24^3 estimate 0.81 -> 1.05 -> 1.11, still rising, which is a projection-time bias on a DIFFERENT estimator; the attack's classical MC has no such bias (autocorrelation ~0.6-0.8 sweeps, first/last-quarter means agree). Extrapolated 0.91 sqrt(eps) at eps=1 sits under the V=0 moment upper bound ~1.01 s: consistent, not independent.

## Corrected plain-language verdict
No one has shown the lowest transverse wave of the ice-rule qubit model is light-like, and ground-state methods cannot. The 32^3 measurement is sound but weak: it shows the first-order flux stiffness is a smooth positive number (which any such ensemble gives), not that the lowest level is the photon or that the phase survives away from the RK point. Literature QMC supports a photon numerically, never as a bound.
