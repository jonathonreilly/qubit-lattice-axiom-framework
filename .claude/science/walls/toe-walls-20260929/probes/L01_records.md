# Lane L01: Records and record formation — walls

## Lane map (under 200 words, plain language first)

**Plain.** The Record axiom says four things: records form; a site holds at most one; records are permanent; only records can be read. This lane asks what a record is, how, where and how fast records form, how many exist, how they are read, and whether the arrow of time comes from them.

**Best current results** (all unaudited; "proved" below means a landed note or a referee-confirmed note in PR #9363):
- Given a formation clause, the formation law is exactly computable, but it changes with the clause (order, unit, who reads whom).
- Under a reversible wave with Born counts, records cannot appear from nothing (probe 7).
- A sharp one-site birth costs its bond energy and is excluded by heat budgets (probe 1).
- Records-only local formation cannot beat CHSH 2.
- The arrow's direction follows from record formation only given a low-record past.

**Where it stands.** The owner's picture (records hop, the count rises until the box freezes) is a working reading. No mechanism, rate or ontology for "records form" is chosen. Fifteen walls follow. File names are given by unique prefix; full paths are in the .jsonl.
