## Lane map (under 200 words, plain language first)

This lane asks whether the one rule the axioms give (a site's odds depend on its neighbours' records) can be turned into laws of motion: a joint law for records, a time evolution, an energy function or action, and a link from odds to frequencies. The axioms say plainly that they supply none of these (`AX:116-123`, `AX:173-190`).

Best result: about 330 landed notes (none audited; formal audit is deferred) map exactly what the rule does and does not fix. Proved on finite windows: the rule alone gives many different overall laws, depending on the order in which records form, and the textbook "equilibrium" law is not one of them. With extra supplied sentences the lane reproduces recognisable pieces: Bell's 2√2, a force equal to energy times clock gradient, a light-cone condition α = K/4.

Where it stands: the missing sentences are identified and priced, not chosen. Which one is wanted depends on what a "record" is, an owner question. Three parked owner decisions touch this lane (statistical bridge, composition clause, enlarging the qubit). About 45 further blocks are held or in open PRs (#9285, #9287, #9289); only some have an other-vendor referee.
