# Lane L03: Quantum structure (Born rule, measurement, Bell) — walls

## Lane map (under 200 words, plain language first)

This lane asks whether ordinary quantum behaviour comes out of the four axioms: Born-rule probabilities, entanglement and Bell-violating correlations, measurement as a permanent record forming, and a classical world of shared facts.

Best current results, all conditional:
- Given a menu-independent probability assignment and enough measurement menus, the Born trace rule is forced (Gleason-type theorems, notes from Jul to Sep 2026). The axioms supply neither premise.
- Records formed only from neighbouring records are a local causal model, so CHSH is at most 2. A supplied Heisenberg dynamics with the trace rule reaches 2 sqrt 2.
- Records as beables (#9363 probe 5) give CHSH 2.79 with nearest-neighbour moves and non-local odds.
- A sharp one-site record costs its bond energy. A unitary wave with Born count statistics cannot form records from nothing (probe 7).

Where it stands: the mathematics, given a state, menus and weights, is solid. Every physical input (the state, the weights, what a record is, the wave) is supplied. The statistical bridge is parked (`docs/repo/DEFERRED_DECISIONS.md` item 1). "What a record is" is the first owner decision on the viability map. Nothing here is adopted.

### L03-W8: Quantum equilibrium: why records start distributed as |psi|^2

### L03-W9: The odds are not yet linked to long-run record frequencies
- Plain: The framework's odds say what a record locks when it forms. Nothing yet shows that counts of records in a long history follow those odds. Counts alone fix no probability law, and repeated records are not forced to be independent. One direct measurement found bulk-record frequencies differing from the weights by 0.05 to 0.12 in probability, stable in lattice size. The theory currently makes no frequency prediction.
- Precise: Facts [proved]: finite counts do not determine a predictive law or IID structure; the history grammar supplies no IID trials; post-record counts are data, not a Born functional; one-copy Born marginals do not force outcome factorisation m(j,k) = p_j p_k (a correlated stack has the same marginals); the unravelled step-law results do not force it either. Under the supplied dynamics clause and trace rule the frequency F of +p outcomes has E F = (1/n) sum_i (1 + <p_i.s_i>)/2 and Var F = (1/4n^2) sum_ij C_ij, so it concentrates on the one-shot odds exactly when the average connected correlation vanishes; clustering is imported; a product state gives variance 2/(9n), a cat state 2/9 for every n; the ferromagnetic ring ground multiplet contains both (cat variance 1/4, product 1/24) with equal odds and "which ground state forms is not derived". The Bridge's Cesaro gate measured a different object in the bulk (see L03-W1), so the signing text disowns bulk-slot frequencies. Order dependence: with supplied sea preparation the 32 executed measure-reset laws are order-dependent, unlike the sea; only sequential commuting Lueders reproduces the sea law.
