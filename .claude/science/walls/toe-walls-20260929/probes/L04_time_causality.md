# Lane L04: Time, causality, Lorentz invariance and 3+1 dimensions — walls

## Lane map (under 200 words, plain language first)

This lane asks why the world has one time, one light cone shared by everything, Lorentz invariance and three space dimensions.

The axioms give a cubic grid, a qubit per site, a nearest-neighbour rule for odds, and permanent records. They give no clock, no update rule, no speed and no signature; the axioms memo lists "arrow ... time metric" as open gates. So the lane brings its own pieces: an approved primitive (one tick equals one edge in form, c_t = c_s), a declared premise for a single clock (B-AXIS), and comparator walkers and Hamiltonians.

Best results, all landed and unaudited (the whole ledger is unaudited): free-field boost covariance in the continuum limit; route no-gos for deriving c_t = c_s and for excluding a second clock; an exact count that Z^4 symmetry forces one cone for scalar, vector and spin-2 kinetic terms (I re-counted it); a measured speed split of 0.026 g^2 between two interacting fields on the campaign's continuous-time surface.

Where it stands: nothing derives time, the cone or the speed from the axioms. Each is a premise, and the premise that protects the cone lives on a different "tick" from the one the dynamics uses.
