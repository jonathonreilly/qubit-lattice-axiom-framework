# Lane L08: Charged leptons and Koide — walls

## Lane map (under 200 words, plain language first)

Plain: this lane tries to derive the electron, muon and tau masses from the four axioms. The lattice gives the shape exactly: the three generations are three cube-corner states, and their mass matrix has one overall scale `a`, one balance number `r` and one phase `δ`. Koide's rule Q = 2/3 is exactly `r` = 1/2 (proved). Putting `r` = 1/2 and `δ` = 2/9 rad into that shape reproduces the measured masses inside 1σ (checked, see W2), but none of the three numbers is derived. `r` hangs on counting a paired mode once instead of twice (twice gives Q = 1, i.e. two massless leptons). `δ` = 2/9 is an exact fraction whose reading as an angle is not proved. The scale is a free number. Work stopped 2026-08-24 by owner direction (parked decision 5). No open PR touches the lane. Every ledger row is unaudited, so statuses below come from the notes' own claim scopes. Read by one Sonnet 5.5 worker, no independent review.

Labels: proved = landed note at its own scope; checked = I ran it (`L08_scratch/`); suggested = not checked; reading = my interpretation.
