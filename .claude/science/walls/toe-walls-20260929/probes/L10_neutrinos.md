## Lane map (under 200 words, plain language first)

The lane asks three things. Why are neutrino masses so tiny? How do the three neutrino types mix (three angles and a CP phase, the PMNS matrix)? Is a neutrino its own antiparticle (Majorana) or not (Dirac)?

Best results, all bounded or conditional support:
1. Exact zero laws. On the current stack there is no Majorana mass and no PMNS mixing. The mass problem reduces to one Dirac Yukawa matrix.
2. A seesaw "benchmark" that lands the atmospheric mass gap within 3.5%, from several posited bridges. It fails on the solar gap by a factor of about 28.
3. A three-parameter "chamber chart" that reproduces the measured angles once its parameters are set from them. It forecasts one CP-phase band and an upper-octant θ23. Current global fits (as quoted in the repo) lean against both.
4. A trimaximal-column route (records) and a θ13 relation.

Where it stands: mixing values, the solar gap, the absolute scale and Dirac-versus-Majorana are all open. The lane's carriers (fermions, ν_R, three generations, Higgs) are not supplied by the four axioms, and the lane predates the 2026-06-29 foundation reset.
