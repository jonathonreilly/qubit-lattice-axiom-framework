## Lane map (under 200 words, plain language first)

This lane asks whether the theory explains the large-scale universe: why space expands, why dark energy is so small, what sets the Hubble rate and the matter fractions, whether inflation happened, and why matter beats antimatter.

Best result: an April to June 2026 stack of landed, unaudited notes. It has Λ = 3/R² written as a spectral gap on a 3-sphere, w = −1, Ω_Λ = (H_inf/H_0)², a Friedmann reduction, and a leptogenesis chain. The roughly 1% agreement with observation needs H_0, T_CMB, the baryon asymmetry η, a coupling α_GUT and flatness as inputs.

Where it stands: the lane's own no-go notes say the four axioms plus the scale-reference primitive derive none of R_Λ/a, H_0, Ω_Λ or η. The vacuum-energy audit is an honest negative. The newest in-axiom work (blocks 146 to 148) gives Friedmann-like equations only after a supplied length-growth law, and shows the lattice vacuum is not a cosmological constant. No open PR touches this lane. The lane's notes predate the 2026-06-29 axiom reset, so they rest on a dynamics the axioms do not supply.
