# Lane L15: Black holes, area law and entropy — walls

## Lane map (under 200 words, plain language first)

A theory of everything has to say what a black hole is and why its entropy is one quarter of its horizon area in Planck units. For this framework the question is also a test of gravity's strength: matching the quarter would fix Newton's constant in lattice units, so the lattice spacing would equal the Planck length.

Best current result, all conditional and with no current audit verdict (the premise-epoch guard cleared the ledger after the axiom revisions; every row reads "unaudited"). April to June packet: a four-slot cell whose first-order count is 4/16 = 1/4, plus textbook Wald, KMS, Hawking-temperature, first-law, second-law and Bekenstein theorems on an assumed smooth gravity surface. Every step that attaches the 1/4 to gravity rests on one named open premise, BP, and on Wald's formula taken as physics input. The simplest lattice fermion entanglement gives at most 1/6 unless a selector is added.

Newest work: PR #9363 probe 19 tests the owner's "frozen box = black hole" reading. The count of frozen record patterns is either one or grows with volume, never with area. The core packet dates from April to June, before the 2026-06-29 axiom reset.
