## Lane map (under 200 words, plain language first)

This lane asks where the theory's pure numbers come from: how big the lattice is next to the Planck length, how strong each force is, what the mass ratios are. The four axioms contain no such number. The memo says the admissibility rule's "form and values" are not specified, and the primitive registry says dimensionless physics "must derive from retained-grade framework content or remain conditional/open". The lane's supplied pieces are one approved ruler (lattice spacing = inverse Planck mass, units only), an approved tick = edge normalisation, and the "g_bare = 1" convention, which the memo lists as an open gate. The best result is an April to June 2026 chain, built on the older Cl(3) framework, that gets alpha_s(v) = 0.1033, v = 246.28 GeV and y_t from one lattice number, the plaquette 0.5934. Every link is a bounded or conditional note with declared inputs. September's record-dynamics work adds its own free numbers. Nothing dimensionless is derived unconditionally. Eight walls follow; two block.
