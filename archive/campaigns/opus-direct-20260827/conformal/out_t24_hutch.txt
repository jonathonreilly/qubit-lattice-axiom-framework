HONEST QUALIFICATION.  dW/ds_e is identical for all L^3 spatial translates of an
edge, so a single probe already delivers L^3 = 32768 independent samples of it;
that cuts the probe count by L^3 and the subsequent 15L-point fit by another
~n_fit/(1-corr^2).  With common random numbers across the background subtraction
(needed: the signal is only ~3%% of dW) B could be reached to ~1%% with O(10) probes.
So Hutchinson was not hopeless -- but the translation symmetry that rescues it is
exactly the symmetry that makes the problem EXACTLY solvable:
  32768 eigendecompositions of tridiagonal 32x32 blocks = 1.07e+09 flops,
  seconds of wall time, validated against a dense N x N reference to 2e-13 (T06).
  Stochastic estimation would have bought nothing and cost accuracy.
