R163 proposes the record field's topological defects as the route to fermions.
Prerequisite, never checked: are they PHYSICAL excitations or plaquette noise?

Tests and expectations:
 (a) COST.  A real defect costs action.  The local action (-log of the product of
     edge weights on the cube) should be measurably HIGHER on monopole cubes than
     on empty ones.  If equal, they are not excitations.
 (b) LOCALISATION.  The field disturbance should decay with distance from the
     defect and reach the bulk value within a few lattice spacings.  If flat,
     the "monopole" is a single-plaquette artefact with no structure around it.
 (c) NOT GAUSSIAN NOISE.  Treating plaquette phases as Gaussian with the measured
     rms (~0.32 rad), the chance that a six-face sum exceeds 2 pi is astronomically
     small (~1e-15), while the measured density is ~1e-3.  If the observed density
     is many orders above the Gaussian estimate, the defects are topological, not
     tail events.

If (a) and (b) fail, the fermion route of R163 is dead on arrival and should be
recorded as such.
