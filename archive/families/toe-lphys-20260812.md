# Family roll-up: campaign `toe-lphys-20260812`

Carrier note for the 1,019 pull requests of the `toe-lphys-20260812` sweep in
`jonathonreilly/qubit-lattice-axiom-framework`. Written so the pull requests
can be closed without the findings ceasing to be readable from `main`.

## 1. What the campaign was

A machine-generated sweep on the host `B_3(0)` (the radius-3 ball of the Z^3
lattice at the origin), one probe per pull request. Each member states a probe
cell -- a seed configuration, a probe axis, a readout defined on the recorded
state, and a timing (which tick or pair of ticks the readout is taken at) --
and reports the pair of verdicts `(reverse, face)`, each drawn from
`{HOLD, fails, UNDEFINED}`. Some members add a third verdict, `composition`,
for readouts compared across two ticks. `HOLD` means the relation held at every
probe site of the cell; `fails` means the readout was defined and the relation
did not hold; `UNDEFINED` means the readout was not determined at one or more
of the probe sites (typically `A`, `B`, `C`, `D`).
