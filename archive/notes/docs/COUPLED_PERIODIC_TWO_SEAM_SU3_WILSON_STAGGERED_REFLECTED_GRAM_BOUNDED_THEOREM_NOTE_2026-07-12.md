## 3. Full action decomposition

Fix `j` and work on `Sigma_j`. Removing the two seam edge layers disconnects
the remaining temporal edge graph into the positive and negative open
semicircles. Every action term belongs to exactly one of three classes:

1. all fields and links lie in the positive side;
2. all fields and links lie in the reflected negative side;
3. the term crosses one of the two seam layers `j` or `j+N`.

Define the sum of class 1 as `S_(j,+)`. The next lemmas show class 2 is
`theta_j(S_(j,+))` and class 3 is the explicitly positive crossing action.

### Lemma 3.1 -- Wilson part

Purely spatial plaquettes lie on one time slice and pair under `r_j`.
Temporal-spatial plaquettes not based at a seam lie wholly within one side and
pair under orientation reversal because

```text
Re Tr U_p = Re Tr U_p^dagger.                                         (3.1)
```

At a seam time `a in {j,j+N}`, both temporal links in the mixed plaquette are
identity, so

```text
U_p=U_k(a+1,x) U_k(a,x)^dagger.                                      (3.2)
```

Hence the gauge action has the exact three-way split.

### Lemma 3.2 -- staggered part

The mass term is fixed by `theta_j`. A spatial forward hop and its backward
partner are exchanged by the anti-automorphism. The spatial staggered phase
satisfies

```text
eta_k(r_j(t),x)=-eta_k(t,x),                                          (3.3)
```

because `2j+1` is odd; this is exactly the sign required when the forward and
backward bilinears are exchanged.

For temporal nonseam hops, the transported phases in (2.3), the reversal in
(2.2), and the AP wrap sign give the same entrywise covariance identity as in
the free repair, now with the parallel transporter carried along. At the two
seams `U_0=I`, so the crossing bilinears reduce exactly to the two positive
free seam polynomials.

Thus every noncrossing mass/spatial/temporal fermion term lies in
`S_(j,+)+theta_j(S_(j,+))`, and no residual temporal link crosses the split.
In particular, all unfixed links carrying the Polyakov product are ordinary
side variables paired by reflection.

Combining the two lemmas proves (0.1).
