# KCPT Dirac-even algebra reach: the H-invariant part of the lattice-Dirac word algebra ⟨D2, J_full⟩ equals C[M] and reaches four of the six commutant dimensions, and the two missing charges — the one separating 12⁺ ↔ 12⁻ and the one separating the two induced-12 — are character-level isotypic projectors

**Type:** bounded_theorem
**Date:** 2026-07-21
**Lane:** KCPT (periodic L = 4, N = 64 staggered qubit lattice on the 4³ torus)

## Abstract

On the finite L = 4, N = 64 staggered construction, the even-degree (H-invariant) part of the lattice-Dirac word algebra generated by the antisymmetric adjacency D2 and the total complex structure J_full equals the mass-square polynomial algebra C[M] = span{I, M, M², M³}, of dimension 4. The full commutant End_H(C⁶⁴) of the order-1536 group H = ⟨G_amb, S_eps⟩ is six-dimensional, so the even Dirac algebra reaches four of the six block-scalar directions and misses exactly two. This note locates those two missing directions and constructs the charges that supply them. The gap is not accidental: C[M] is constant on each M-shell, and M has exactly two intra-shell degeneracies — the CP-split pair {12⁺, 12⁻}, both sitting at M-shell −4, and the two induced-12 constituents, both sitting at M-shell −8. The two charges that resolve these degeneracies are character-level isotypic (Frobenius) projectors. The result is positive and constructive: the charge separating 12⁺ from 12⁻ is not any polynomial in the lattice-Dirac operators but a genuinely group-theoretic, χ_sgn-character-sensitive central projector in End_H, outside C[M]. This note records that location and the explicit construction, with every load-bearing quantity checked by the companion runner under live wrong-value and parity rejectors.

## Setup

The parent decomposition, carried over from the linked Unit-14 note, splits the complexified lattice space into six multiplicity-free H-constituents:

`C^64 = 8 + 8 + 12 + 12 + 12⁺ + 12⁻`,

with H = ⟨G_amb, S_eps⟩ of order 1536, the order-768 ambient group G_amb sitting as an index-2 subgroup and the chiral-parity involution S_eps = diag((−1)^{x₁+x₂+x₃}) generating the coset. Because the six constituents are pairwise inequivalent irreducibles, the commutant End_H(C⁶⁴) is six-dimensional and every H-invariant operator is block-scalar (Schur): it acts as one complex scalar on each constituent.

The mass square M = D2² is the diagonalizable object whose spectrum stratifies the lattice into four shells λ_m = −4m (m = 0, 1, 2, 3), with multiplicities {0: 8, −4: 24, −8: 24, −12: 8}. Its polynomial algebra C[M] = span{I, M, M², M³} is four-dimensional and, by construction, constant on each M-shell. The two induced-8 constituents fall on the extreme shells 0 and −12; the two induced-12 both fall on shell −8; and the split pair {12⁺, 12⁻} both fall on shell −4. From the linked Unit-16 note we also carry the χ_sgn-covariance of the symplectic leg and the twist relation between the split halves, `12⁻ ≅ 12⁺ ⊗ χ_sgn` — the fact that 12⁻ is 12⁺ tensored with the order-two quotient character χ_sgn of H/G_amb.

## T1 — the commutant is six-dimensional

Taking Hermitian-orthonormal bases Z_i of the six complexified constituents and forming the constituent characters χ_i(h) = tr(Z_iᵀ̄ ρ(h) Z_i) over all 1536 elements h ∈ H, the character Gram (1/|H|) Σ_h χ_i(h) χ_j(h)‾ equals the identity I₆. All six constituents are therefore irreducible and pairwise inequivalent, and dim End_H(C⁶⁴) = 6. This is the target dimension against which the Dirac-even reach is measured. [gate G-T1-CHARORTHO]

## T2 — the even lattice-Dirac algebra equals C[M]

Both generators are G_amb-invariant, and each is S_eps-odd: S_eps D2 S_eps = −D2 and S_eps J_full S_eps = −J_full. An even-length word in {D2, J_full} therefore carries an even number of S_eps-odd factors, so it is S_eps-even and, being also G_amb-invariant, is H-invariant — hence block-scalar. Conversely a nonzero odd-length word is S_eps-odd and cannot be H-invariant; the parity grading excludes it from End_H. These are the live rejectors that keep the grading honest. [gates G-REJ-D2ODD, G-REJ-JODD, G-REJ-PEVEN]

The block-scalar span of all 85 even words up to degree 6 is exactly the four-dimensional C[M]: the coordinate vectors of {I, M, M², M³} already have rank 4, and adjoining every enumerated even word leaves the rank at 4. Every enumerated even word equals its block-scalar reconstruction to machine precision. The finite scan is a discriminating check; the all-degree conclusion follows from the algebraic reduction in the next paragraph. [gates G-T2-CMRANK, G-T2-EVENREACH, G-T2-EVENBLOCK]

The radius operator makes this self-contained. Since [D2, J_full] = 0, the product P := D2·J_full is symmetric — Pᵀ = J_fullᵀ D2ᵀ = (−J_full)(−D2) = J_full·D2 = D2·J_full = P, using that D2 and J_full are antisymmetric and commute — and squares to P² = D2·J_full·D2·J_full = D2²·J_full² = M·(−I) = −M. Its sign is not inferred from the square: the rebuilt shell formula gives D2·J_ker = 0 and D2·[D2·Q_m/(2√m)] = −2√m·Q_m for m = 1, 2, 3, hence P = −Σ_m 2√m·Q_m = −√(−M). Therefore P is an element of C[M], not an independent direction. Because D2 and J_full commute, every word reduces to D2^a J_full^b. At even total degree, it is either ±M^k or ±M^kP, and thus lies in C[M]; conversely M = D2² is an even word, so the entire even word algebra equals C[M] at all degrees. This is the same radius operator studied in the Dirac-radius unit `KCPT_DIRAC_RADIUS_GRADING_OF_END_H_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-07-20.md`; here it is rebuilt from the shell/kernel formula rather than imported. [gate G-T2-PROOT]

## T3 — the reach gap is the two intra-shell degeneracies

The reach gap is dim End_H − dim C[M] = 6 − 4 = 2. Because C[M] is constant on each M-shell, it cannot tell apart two constituents that share a shell. M has exactly two such coincidences: the CP-split pair {12⁺, 12⁻} both sit at M-shell −4 (m = 1), and the two induced-12 both sit at M-shell −8 (m = 2). The two induced-8 constituents sit at the distinct shells 0 and −12 and are therefore already separated by M itself. The count of intra-shell degeneracies is 2, matching the reach gap exactly: the two directions C[M] misses are the two the shells cannot resolve. [gates G-T3-MSHELLS, G-T3-DEGENPAIRS, G-GAP]

## T4 — the two missing charges are character-level

The two missing block-scalar directions are supplied by character-level isotypic projectors. For constituent i the Frobenius projector

P_i = (d_i / |H|) Σ_h χ_i(h)‾ ρ(h)

is built purely from the character and the group action; on this multiplicity-free decomposition it equals the constituent projector exactly. Adjoining the CP-pair's isotypic projector to C[M] and the even words raises the reach from 4 to 5, and adjoining an induced-12 isotypic projector raises it from 5 to 6 = dim End_H. Each isotypic charge is block-scalar 1 on its own constituent and 0 on its M-degenerate partner, so it separates the pair that no polynomial in {D2, J_full} can. The CP-charge distinguishing 12⁺ from 12⁻ is thereby constructed explicitly, as an End_H element outside C[M]. [gates G-T4-ISOEQ, G-T4-RANK5, G-T4-RANK6, G-T4-SEPSPLIT, G-T4-SEPIND]

## T5 — why the CP-charge is group-theoretic

The reason the CP-charge cannot be a Dirac-even polynomial is structural. M is CP-even, S_eps M S_eps = M, and more decisively it has the same scalar −4 on both split constituents. Every f(M) is therefore constant on that pair and blind to the χ_sgn twist. The paired characters, on the other hand, obey χ_{12⁻}(h) = χ_sgn(h)·χ_{12⁺}(h) for every h ∈ H, carried over from the linked Unit-16 note. The Frobenius formula uses those different character coefficients to build the central projectors that distinguish the two irreducibles. The resulting isotypic projector is H-invariant — every element of End_H commutes with S_eps — not χ_sgn-odd as an operator. Its χ_sgn sensitivity lives in the character data that selects one twist-related constituent rather than the other. This is why the charge is genuinely group-theoretic rather than Dirac-polynomial. [gates G-T5-MCPEVEN, G-T5-TWIST]

## What this opens

Having located the CP-charge at the character level — as a central H-invariant projector outside C[M] whose coefficients distinguish χ_sgn-twisted characters — the path this hands forward is to ask which enlargement of the local operator content realizes that projector, or the difference of the paired projectors, in a geometrically natural closed form.
