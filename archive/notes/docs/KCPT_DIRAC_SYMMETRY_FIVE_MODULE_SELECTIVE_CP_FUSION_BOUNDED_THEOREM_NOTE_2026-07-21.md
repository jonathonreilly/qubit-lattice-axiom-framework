## T2 — selectivity is structurally forced

The fusion pattern is forced by the transformation law of the two generators, not put in by hand. D2 and J_full are each G_amb-invariant and χ_sgn-ODD under the parity coset, S_eps·X·S_eps = −X; i.e. each transforms in the χ_sgn representation of H. By the standard Schur fact for an operator that is invariant up to a character, such an operator can intertwine two irreducible constituents V_i → V_j only when V_j ≅ V_i ⊗ χ_sgn. Among the six constituents the ONLY off-diagonal χ_sgn-partner pair is {12⁺, 12⁻}, with 12⁻ ≅ 12⁺ ⊗ χ_sgn (Unit 16); the four induced constituents are each χ_sgn-self-conjugate. Hence exactly one pair can fuse, and it does.

In particular the two M-degenerate induced-12's, which share M-shell −8, are χ_sgn-self and are therefore NOT connected by D2 or J_full; they stay resolved as two separate modules.
