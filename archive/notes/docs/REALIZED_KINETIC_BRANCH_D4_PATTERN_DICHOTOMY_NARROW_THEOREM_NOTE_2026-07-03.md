## Statement

The parent representatives are used exactly. K0 is the scalar
tight-binding representative, whose one-coefficient-per-direction data on
the qubit factor is the scalar `C * I`. K1 is the Kawamoto-Smit `eta0`
sign system, whose first-order coefficient family
`{Gamma_1, Gamma_2, Gamma_3}` of mutually anticommuting self-adjoint
unitaries is COMPUTED from the parent absorbing-frame construction
`T(x) = sigma_1^{x1} sigma_2^{x2} sigma_3^{x3}` and the `eta0` phases,
then extracted by

```text
Gamma_mu = (1 / 2i) * (K_raw(+q_mu) - K_raw(-q_mu)).
```

A Hermitian nearest-neighbor symbol on the one-site `C^2` carrier has the
Fourier form

```text
K(p) = C + sum_mu ( A_mu cos p_mu + B_mu sin p_mu ),
```

with every coefficient matrix Hermitian by Fourier orthogonality. Write
`d_mu` for the dimension of the unital `*`-subalgebra of `M_2(C)`
generated by direction `mu`'s coefficient data.

**Pattern dichotomy.** On the parent surface's
one-coefficient-per-direction representatives, and for symbols covariant
under the proper cubic rotations:

1. Unital `*`-subalgebras of `M_2(C)` have dimension in `{1, 2, 4}`
   (`C * I`, a maximal abelian subalgebra, or the full matrix algebra).
2. A single Hermitian generator gives `d = 1` iff it is scalar, and `d = 2`
   otherwise, because Cayley-Hamilton makes `{I, B}` a spanning basis
   (`B^2` lies in that span).
3. Proper-cubic covariance maps direction coefficients by a (signed)
   unitary conjugation along the axis permutation. Conjugation preserves
   the generated-algebra dimension, the sign is irrelevant, and the
   24-element proper cubic group acts transitively on the three axes.
   Hence `d_1 = d_2 = d_3`: the per-direction dimension is constant.
4. On the surface's one-coefficient-per-direction representatives the
   constant value lies in `{1, 2}`, giving exactly the pattern `[1,1,1]`
   for K0 and `[2,2,2]` for K1.

**Both hypotheses are load-bearing.** Two Hermitian generators per
direction (off-surface) reach dimension 4 — e.g. `sigma_1` and
`sigma_3` — so the one-coefficient-per-direction scope is real.
Non-covariant symbols realize direction-mixed patterns — e.g.
`B_1 = sigma_3`, `B_2 = B_3 = 0` gives `[2,1,1]` — so the covariance
hypothesis is real. The map used in the constancy step (unitary
conjugation) preserves the dimension, which is why a covariant symbol
cannot host two directions of different dimension.

## Proof Sketch

Checks T1-T3 establish the machinery. T1 verifies the single-generator
dichotomy on 50 random Hermitian matrices (non-scalar -> 2, scalar -> 1)
and the Cayley-Hamilton identity `B^2 = tr(B) B - det(B) I` to `1e-12`.
T2 is the off-surface dimension-4 rejector: `alg_dim([sigma_1, sigma_3])`
is 4, and 20 random Hermitian pairs give the generic value 4. T3 is a
discriminating gate for the `alg_dim` routine itself: the non-Hermitian
nilpotent `[[0,1],[0,0]]` generates all of `M_2(C)` under adjoint
closure, so `alg_dim` returns 4 — a routine that branched on scalarness
would wrongly return 2.

T4 anchors the surface. The K1 `Gamma_mu` are computed from the parent
construction, verified Hermitian, unitary, and pairwise anticommuting,
with per-direction dimensions `[2,2,2]`; the K0 scalar coefficient gives
`[1,1,1]`.

T5-T6 supply the covariance-to-constancy content. T5 verifies the
explicit spinor-rep rotations on the COMPUTED `Gamma` family:
`U_z = exp(-i pi/4 sigma_3)` sends `Gamma_1 -> Gamma_2` and
`Gamma_2 -> -Gamma_1`, and `U_111 = exp(-i (2 pi/3) n.sigma / 2)` with
`n = (1,1,1)/sqrt(3)` cyclically permutes the family, each up to the
checked sign, with `alg_dim` preserved. T6 confirms `alg_dim` is
invariant under conjugation by 20 Haar-random `U(2)` matrices and that
the constancy filter over the eight patterns in `{1,2}^3` leaves exactly
`[[1,1,1],[2,2,2]]` while no filter leaves all eight.

T7-T8 make the two hypotheses load-bearing and pin the Fourier form.
T7 exhibits the non-covariant `[2,1,1]` symbol, then refutes covariance
directly by minimizing the covariance defect over 200 random unitaries
and reporting a positive floor. T8 extracts `A_mu, B_mu, C` from a
Hermitian symbol by discrete Fourier orthogonality on an `8^3` grid,
confirms each coefficient Hermitian, and confirms that inserting one
non-Hermitian coefficient breaks `K(p) = K(p)^dagger` on the grid.

One guard leg prevents a target-shaped check. T9 flips one `eta0` link
sign in the translation-cell blocking (as the discriminator runner's T8
does) and confirms the blocked first-order coefficient family loses the
clean anticommutation property, so the T4 pipeline is not built to
succeed regardless of its input.
