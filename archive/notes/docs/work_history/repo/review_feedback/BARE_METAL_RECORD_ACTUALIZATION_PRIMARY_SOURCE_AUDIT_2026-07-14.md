**Date:** 2026-07-14

**Type:** meta / bounded literature and exact-mechanism audit

**Scope:** primary sources only; record-actualization mechanisms that could

**Authority:** none. This is not an axiom proposal, retained theorem, audit

## Result In Plain Language

The literature does not provide a hidden one-line theorem saying that a
reversible local quantum process, once read twice or advanced by a clock,
automatically becomes one permanent fact with Born-rule frequency.

The closest executable bare-metal pattern is more explicit:

1. a causal condition says which local event is ready;
2. a local unitary carries coherent alternatives to that event;
3. a quantum instrument assigns one branch map and one norm-square weight to
   each possible write;
4. the law samples exactly one branch; and
5. later admissible operations preserve the written record sector.

That pattern is mathematically coherent. Lattice-collapse models, relativistic
flash models, Bell-type jump processes, and ordinary repeated instruments all
instantiate parts of it. But in each case the event rule, stochastic/Born
weights, and one-realized-history semantics enter the law as supplied physical
content. Causal-order independence and stable event archives can then be
theorems of the supplied law.

The strongest lesson for the present framework is therefore not “two witnesses
cause reality” or “the clock locks the record.” Unitary copying can make two,
twenty, or an environmental redundancy of mutually agreeing witnesses while
the global state remains a reversible superposition. A clock can order or
count commits after an event law exists; it does not by itself choose a branch.
Redundancy is a strong **record-stability and accessibility certificate**. It
is not an actualization operator.

Nor does a probability theorem finish the job. Gleason-type and operational
reconstruction theorems can force the Born *form* after a probability measure,
definite outcomes, or operational outcome functions are available. They do not
derive readiness or the existence of one actual append-only history.

### Bounded literature result: no joint theorem found

Within the named primary-source routes audited here, as of 2026-07-14, no joint
theorem found starts only from a local reversible qubit/QCA substrate and
derives all of:

- which event becomes ready;
- normalized Born weights;
- exactly one actual history;
- permanent local records;
- causal-order covariance; and
- generic predictive sufficiency of `state = records`.

This is not a universal no-go. It is a date-, corpus-, and premise-bounded
finding. A new constructive theorem could jointly retire several atoms. The
audit below states exactly where each examined route stops.

## Route Audit

### Repeated instruments and quantum trajectories

Davies and Lewis define an instrument as an outcome-indexed family of positive
operations whose sum is normalized; the instrument supplies both outcome
statistics and conditional state change. A schedule saying when the
instrument is applied and an interpretation saying one outcome is observed are
part of the operational setup, not outputs of the intervening unitary
dynamics. See [Davies and Lewis, *An operational approach to quantum
probability*](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01647093).

A single open-system master equation generally has multiple stochastic
unravellings. Wiseman and Diósi explicitly parameterize the class of diffusive
unravellings rather than deriving one unique trajectory record from the
nonselective dynamics. See [Wiseman and Diósi, *Complete parameterization, and
invariance, of diffusive quantum trajectories for Markovian open systems*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0012016).

The exact runner gives the smallest control. The same dephasing channel has:

- the projective instrument `{P_0, P_1}`, whose labels are candidate pointer
  records with state-dependent probabilities; and
- the random-unitary instrument `{I/sqrt(2), Z/sqrt(2)}`, whose labels are
  equally likely phase-operation tokens.

Forgetting the label makes both channels identical. Their possible record
meanings and label probabilities differ. Therefore a CPTP update or QCA
channel does not by itself identify the actual record instrument.

### GRW, CSL, and lattice collapse

The original GRW model adds random spontaneous localizations to Schrödinger
evolution, with localization rate, width, center distribution, and nonlinear
state update supplied by the model. Macroscopic localization then follows from
the many-constituent amplification mechanism. See [Ghirardi, Rimini, and
Weber, *Unified dynamics for microscopic and macroscopic systems*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.34.470).

CSL replaces discrete jumps with a universal nonlinear stochastic evolution.
The noise law and collapse parameters remain new dynamics; suppression of
macroscopic superpositions follows conditionally. See [Ghirardi, Pearle, and
Rimini, *Markov processes in Hilbert space and continuous spontaneous
localization of systems of identical particles*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.42.78).

Dowker and Henson provide the most directly relevant local finite-carrier
example: qubits live on links of a `1+1` null lattice, a supplied local
`R`-matrix propagates amplitudes, and realized field values are stochastically
written with supplied norm-square jump factors and a supplied collapse
parameter. Once written, those event values form an append-only realized
history. Commutation of spacelike update/jump factors proves that the history
probability is independent of the chosen linear extension. This is real causal
order covariance inside the lattice model. It is not derivation of the jump
law, its weights, or full Lorentz invariance. See [Dowker and Henson, *A
spontaneous collapse model on a lattice*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0209051).

That paper also exposes the exact `state = records` residual. The realized
field history is proposed as the ontology, but the wavefunction is still used
to calculate future probabilities. Treating field values alone as complete
would require a further result that the late record history determines the
conditional state. The paper treats that as a hypothesis to investigate, not
as a theorem.

### Relativistic flashes

Tumulka's `rGRWf` takes flashes—spacetime points—as the primitive ontology. A
supplied stochastic law, driven by the wavefunction and norm-square flash
rates, generates one flash history. Within the original noninteracting,
distinguishable-particle domain, the joint flash distribution is formulated
without a preferred slicing. Flash occurrence is permanent as event history,
but the wavefunction remains predictive input and the law is explicitly
nonlocal. See [Tumulka, *A Relativistic Version of the Ghirardi–Rimini–Weber
Model*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0406094).

This is evidence that one can have exact event ontology plus a useful causal
covariance theorem. It is not evidence that either follows from a reversible
local QCA alone.

### Bell-type jumps and Bohmian configurations

Bell-type QFTs define an actual configuration path with jump rates constructed
to preserve the `|Psi|^2` distribution. The equivariance calculation is a
theorem once the wavefunction, Hamiltonian, configuration observable, and rate
law are supplied. It does not derive the initial equilibrium distribution or
remove the wavefunction from the state. See [Dürr et al., *Bell-Type Quantum
Field Theories*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0407116) and [Bell, *Beables
for quantum field theory*](https://doi.org/10.1016/0370-1573(86)90072-8).

Bohm's original theory similarly supplies an actual particle configuration
and guiding equation. Quantum equilibrium/typicality explains Born statistics
conditional on the `|Psi|^2` equilibrium measure and its equivariance; it does
not derive that measure from an arbitrary actual configuration. See [Bohm, *A
Suggested Interpretation of the Quantum Theory in Terms of Hidden Variables,
I*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.85.166) and [Dürr, Goldstein, and Zanghì,
*Quantum Equilibrium and the Origin of Absolute Uncertainty*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0308039).

These routes solve “which world is actual?” by adding an actual configuration
or path. Ordinary apparatus records are stable patterns in that path, not an
axiomatically immutable archive. Standard forms also carry nonlocal guidance
and a time/foliation structure. Most importantly here, the state is not the
record configuration: it is at least wavefunction plus actual configuration.

### Consistent and decoherent histories

Consistent-histories formalisms supply an initial density operator, dynamics,
times or spacetime alternatives, and projective decompositions. A decoherence
condition then licenses additive probabilities for a selected history family.
See [Griffiths, *Consistent histories and the interpretation of quantum
mechanics*](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01015734) and [Gell-Mann and Hartle,
*Quantum Mechanics in the Light of Quantum Cosmology*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.04605).

The consistency condition does not dynamically choose one framework/realm.
If one actual history is asserted, that is interpretive content in addition to
the decoherence functional. Kent's exact contrary-inference construction shows
why realm choice cannot be silently identified with objective event selection:
different consistent sets can support contrary probability-one retrodictions.
See [Kent, *Consistent Sets Yield Contrary Inferences in Quantum Theory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9604012).

The runner checks the minimal algebraic point: for an initial `|+x>` state,
both a one-time `Z` family and an incompatible one-time `X` family decohere
exactly, but they assign different history weights. Consistency licenses both;
it does not select the physical question, much less one actual append-only
outcome.

### Quantum Darwinism and redundant witnesses

Quantum Darwinism derives a powerful conditional result. Given a supplied
system/environment split and unitary interaction, many disjoint environment
fragments can redundantly encode the same pointer observable. Multiple
observers can then infer that observable without directly disturbing the
system. See [Ollivier, Poulin, and Zurek, *Objective Properties from Subjective
Quantum States: Environment as a Witness*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0307229)
and [Ollivier, Poulin, and Zurek, *Environment as a Witness: Selective
Proliferation of Information and Emergence of Objectivity in a Quantum
Universe*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0408125).

Redundancy is therefore an excellent theorem target for readable, robust,
locally accessible records. It does not choose one term of the global
system-environment superposition. Its mutual information and branch weights
already use the quantum state and Born trace rule. The exact runner constructs
two redundant CNOT witnesses, verifies that each local marginal carries the
same outcome distribution, and then reverses both copies. The global branch
coherence never vanished. Thus “the second witness locks it” is not a theorem
of reversible copying.

### Everett, branch weights, and one-world actuality

Everett retains universal unitary evolution and relative records in all
branches. It deliberately does not select one actual history. See [Everett,
*Relative State Formulation of Quantum Mechanics*](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.29.454).

Deutsch's decision-theoretic route and Zurek's envariance route constrain
branch weights using additional rationality or symmetry/equivalence premises.
Those are weight theorems inside a universal quantum state; neither produces
one ontically selected branch or an append-only record substrate. See
[Deutsch, *Quantum Theory of Probability and Decisions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9906015)
and [Zurek, *Probabilities from Entanglement, Born's Rule from Envariance*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0405161).

Everett therefore cannot be counted as a derivation of “one fact forms,” even
if it is empirically adequate and even if an internal branch observer
experiences one stable record.

### Deterministic hidden-variable cellular routes

A deterministic cellular automaton supplies one ontic initial state and one
update rule, so one history follows. A reversible permutation can be
represented unitarily on a Hilbert basis. Neither fact derives complex quantum
interference, a Born equilibrium measure, or a permanent record ontology.

't Hooft's cellular-automaton interpretation explicitly distinguishes ontic
states from quantum templates and seeks equivalence classes that reproduce
quantum descriptions. The deterministic ontology and admissible initial
ontic state are supplied, while a general local construction reproducing the
target interacting quantum law and Born statistics is not derived. See
['t Hooft, *The Cellular Automaton Interpretation of Quantum Mechanics*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.1548).

This route is a live constructive alternative, not a negative result. It must
still show how a record-only state carries phase/contextual predictive content
and how a physically justified ensemble over ontic states yields quantum
frequencies.

### Causal-set events and quantum measure

Rideout and Sorkin's classical sequential growth models are unusually close to
append-only bare metal. New causal-set elements are born and never deleted.
Internal temporality and discrete general covariance constrain the allowed
transition probabilities and produce a family of growth laws. Coupling
constants still label that family; the principles do not select a unique law.
The process is classical stochastic rather than coherent quantum evolution or
a derivation of Born weights. See [Rideout and Sorkin, *A Classical Sequential
Growth Dynamics for Causal Sets*](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9904062).

Sorkin's quantum-measure program replaces an additive probability measure on
histories with a grade-two quantum measure/decoherence functional. This gives
a covariant histories-level home for interference, but the quantum measure is
supplied and a rule connecting it to one experienced actual history remains
additional interpretive content. See [Sorkin, *Quantum Measure Theory and Its
Interpretation*](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9507057).

The later coevent/anhomomorphic-logic route treats reality as a truth-value map
on the event algebra constrained by preclusion. That is a concrete
single-reality proposal, but the admissible coevent scheme and the link from a
quantum measure to observed probabilistic regularities remain interpretive law
content rather than consequences of reversible local dynamics. See [Sorkin,
*An exercise in “anhomomorphic logic”*](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0703276).

The important separation is exact: causal-set growth can derive permanent
event order and label covariance without coherent/Born quantum mechanics;
quantum measure can encode interference without by itself selecting one
ordinary stochastic history.

### Wolfram multiway observer equivalencing

In a multiway rewriting system, the supplied rewrite rule determines which
updates are available. Causal invariance can make different update orders
converge and yield an order-independent causal graph. Gorard develops the
quantum analogy in terms of multiway branching, branchial structure, and
observer equivalence classes. See [Gorard, *Some Quantum Mechanical Properties
of the Wolfram Model*](https://doi.org/10.25088/ComplexSystems.29.2.537).

That construction does not supply a normalized physical Born measure over
branches or select one actual path. Causal invariance quotients schedule/order
gauge; it does not choose between outcome sectors. Global confluence can also
conflict with the present Record axiom if branches carrying contradictory
permanent records are forced to reconverge. “Observer equivalencing” is thus
not yet a bare-metal actualization law.

### Gleason and operational reconstruction

Gleason proves that a nonnegative additive frame measure on projections in
Hilbert dimension at least three has trace form. This is a decisive derivation
of Born weight form from a supplied probability measure and consistency across
frames. It says nothing about event readiness, stochastic sampling, or record
formation. See [Gleason, *Measures on the Closed Subspaces of a Hilbert
Space*](https://doi.org/10.1512/iumj.1957.6.56050).

Masanes, Galley, and Müller go further: using unitary quantum state/dynamics,
composition constraints, operational outcome-probability functions, and a
finite state-estimation premise, they derive POVM Born probabilities and the
completely positive update structure. Their primary text is explicit that
measurement and outcome probability are operational primitives, that
experiments are assumed to yield definite outcomes, and that the work does not
seek to explain the emergence of probabilities. See [Masanes, Galley, and
Müller, *The measurement postulates of quantum mechanics are operationally
redundant*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.11060).

This theorem is highly relevant to shrinking a future law. It may retire a
separate Born formula after a sufficiently strong operational composition and
state-estimation structure is established. It cannot be credited with deriving
ontic actualization from a local reversible substrate.

### A 2026 claimed reversible/irreversible derivation

Axelsson's April 2026 preprint is close enough to the present vocabulary to
require a direct check. It assumes two regimes—reversible evolution before a
persistent record and irreversible evolution after a single realized outcome—
and seeks to derive Born weights from additive amplitude composition plus
multiplicative record refinement. See [Axelsson, *Born's Rule from Reversible
Evolution and Irreversible Outcomes*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07418).

It does not derive actualization: a single realized outcome and persistent
record regime are premises. More decisively, its displayed equation

```text
f(alpha_1 + alpha_2) = f(alpha_1) f(alpha_2)
```

is not satisfied by the proposed `f(alpha)=|alpha|^2`. With
`alpha_1=alpha_2=1`, the left side is `4` and the right side is `1`. For a
nonzero nonnegative phase-invariant solution, setting the second argument to
zero and then to the negative of the first forces the constant solution `1`,
not Born's rule. The paper later uses the different multiplicative-modulus
equation `f(rs)=f(r)f(s)`, which admits every power `r^q` and does not follow
from the failed additive-to-multiplicative equation. Finally, the identification
of weight with repeated record frequency is asserted rather than derived.

The classification `F` is limited to that displayed derivation. It is not a
claim that no corrected record-refinement theorem can exist, and the preprint
is not treated as peer-reviewed authority.

## Empirical Equivalence Is Not Ontology

Empirical equivalence is not ontology. Several routes can return the same
laboratory distribution while making incompatible claims about what exists:

- standard instruments, Bohmian mechanics in equilibrium, Everettian quantum
  mechanics, and consistent/decoherent histories can agree on Born
  probabilities while positing respectively observed outcomes, an actual
  configuration, all branches, or one history in a chosen realm;
- quantum Darwinism explains why branch-relative records become redundantly
  accessible but leaves the global superposition intact;
- objective-collapse theories generally predict small deviations from exact
  unitary quantum mechanics, so they are experimentally distinguishable in
  principle rather than merely ontological redescription; and
- two unravellings can give the same nonselective channel while assigning
  different meanings and probabilities to the discarded event labels.

Agreement after forgetting the record label proves channel equivalence. It
does not prove equality of event ontology. Conversely, a clear event ontology
does not prove the right empirical weights.

## Exact Controls And What They Establish

The companion runner checks six small implication boundaries.

1. **Channel versus instrument.** Projective dephasing and a random `I/Z`
   unitary mixture have the same CPTP map and different outcome-label laws.
2. **Witness redundancy versus actualization.** Two CNOT copies produce an
   exact GHZ-type redundant record state. Both branches and their global
   coherence survive, and the copies are exactly erasable by the inverse.
3. **Records versus predictive state.** `|+x>` and `|-x>` have identical
   `Z`-record statistics and opposite future `X` predictions. A
   phase/preparation/process record is needed to distinguish them.
4. **Consistency versus realm selection.** Incompatible one-time `X` and `Z`
   history families are both exactly decoherent; consistency does not choose
   one family.
5. **Spacelike covariance versus event law.** Disjoint local projectors
   commute, whereas overlapping `Z`/`X` operations have order-dependent branch
   probabilities. A causal net must still say which overlaps are ordered.
6. **Functional-equation audit.** The 2026 additive-to-multiplicative equation
   fails for the Born function exactly.

These are implication controls, not independent experiments and not a
simulation of an interpretation.

## What A Complete Bare-Metal Law Still Has To Say

The primary-source mechanisms support a compact but non-negotiable interface.
A final local law or stable law reference must determine:

1. **ready event:** which finite local context may commit next;
2. **coherent map:** how unresolved quantum information reaches that context;
3. **outcome instrument:** the physical branch maps, not only their summed
   channel;
4. **weight and actuality:** why the normalized weights have Born form and how
   exactly one branch becomes the actual continuation;
5. **record contract:** the identity and exhaustive future operation scope
   that makes a write append-only;
6. **causal composition:** why disjoint event order is gauge while overlaps are
   consistently ordered; and
7. **predictive sufficiency:** how the complete present records reconstruct all
   phase, preparation, process-memory, and boundary information needed for
   every future record probability.

One exact law can package several items; this list is not a demand for seven
new axioms. The local lattice-collapse and sampled-instrument routes show that
the package is executable. They also show which pieces are being supplied.

### Consequence for the current language/axiom gate

The literature does not force the proposed two-witness formation sentence.
It supports a weaker downstream theorem: redundant disjoint records can make a
selected outcome robust, objective, and locally readable after the selection
law exists.

It also does not support “the clock locks it” as formation content. A causal
front, flash process, or sequential growth law can derive an event order; a
clock can count that order and a formation frequency can later define a time
rate. None selects the instrument or actual outcome merely by ticking.

The literature instead reinforces the repository's constructive direction:
formation should be a consequence of one exact sampled/selected event law plus
an invariant-record scope. The constitutional problem is how to refer to that
law stably and minimally, not how to replace it with a phenomenological slogan.
This source audit does not authorize an axiom edit. It supplies acceptance
tests for the proposed referent.
