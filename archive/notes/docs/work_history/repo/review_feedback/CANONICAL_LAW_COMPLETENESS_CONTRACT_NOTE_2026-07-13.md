## Why An Exact Predictive Specification Is Unavoidable

The current Admissibility axiom says that one fixed covariant
nearest-neighbor rule exists. It does not identify the rule extensionally.
Many distinct rules satisfy that description and give different local
availability answers. Therefore a predictive framework must do one of three
things:

1. derive a unique rule from already supplied content; or
2. supply an exact rule as constitutional law content; or
3. prove that all remaining rule representatives are physically equivalent
   for every claimed observable, and specify that exact equivalence class.

This is a semantic minimum, not a preference for equations over prose. A
stable exact reference from the axiom memo to a canonical mathematical law
specification is sufficient if the reference makes that specification part of
the supplied foundation. An unofficial downstream assumption is not.

## Referent Identity And Constitutional Type Gate

The ten fields specify scientific completeness. A separate implementation
gate prevents a complete object from being referenced ambiguously.

A constitutional referent must provide:

1. a stable canonical claim identifier and one current source path;
2. an exact mathematical object or an exact physical-equivalence class, not a
   family or architecture name;
3. every dimensionless parameter and law-owned coefficient fixed, with every
   contingent boundary/history slot explicitly typed but not falsely derived;
4. an exact physical protocol/equivalence category, including which context,
   phase-reference, schedule, record recoding, and boundary transformations
   are gauge;
5. a theorem connecting the object's availability projection to the current
   nearest-neighbor Admissibility rule; and
6. a machine gate that rejects placeholders, missing fields, unresolved
   parameters, and a source/registry mismatch.

The object's type determines its eventual constitutional location:

```text
complete local continuation/instrument/QCA whose availability projection is
the current menu rule
    -> retype Admissibility as the complete local law and identify it there;

global-history action or constraint not identical to the local menu map
    -> retain Admissibility and identify the object in a separate Law axiom;

unique derivation from the present four axioms
    -> make no constitutional edit.
```

Putting only a formation trigger in Record cannot satisfy this gate. It does
not identify the remaining law fields. Nor can a complete law be silently
appended to the present Admissibility paragraph while the memo continues to
say that Admissibility is not dynamics.

## Candidate Record Contract

One exact, site-tagged extensionalization of the current word `permanent` is

```text
For every lawful continuation s' of s,
every record (site, content) in s is also in s'.
```

Together with transitive continuation and one-record-per-site uniqueness, this
proves that continuations containing different records at the same site never
reconnect at the record level. This is a conditional theorem, not yet the
unique meaning of permanence: a persistent fact might instead migrate or be
re-encoded while preserving its physical identity and content.

The site-tagged condition may be:

- proved from an append-only/invariant-algebra `ATOMIC_LAW`; or
- selected as an explicit semantics of Record permanence.

It does not tell the law when a record forms, which content becomes actual, or
with what statistics. Those remain `RECORD`, `ACTUALITY`, and `STATISTICS`
obligations of the law/history theory.

## State Retirement Test

Keep

```text
A state is a configuration of records.
```

only if the candidate proves:

1. operationally different preparations leave different complete persistent
   record configurations;
2. equal complete record configurations have equal lawful future support and
   statistics;
3. relational phase/reference records propagate locally to later contexts;
   and
4. the construction covers the full quantum repertoire, including entangled
   phases.

Failure of 1 or 2 for an exact physical counterexample forces a wider `STATE`
field. The finite phase-reference probe shows that failure is not already
forced by quantum phase sensitivity.

## Actuality And Statistics Retirement Tests

`ACTUALITY` is closed only by an exact uniqueness/selection result over the
candidate's complete histories. A branch support set, decohered mixture,
central decomposition, or normalized measure does not close it.

`STATISTICS` is closed only by an exact normalized context-compatible law or a
frequency theorem from the candidate dynamics and boundary class, tied to an
independently defined trial corpus and prepared record state. Determinism,
sector separation, or single-qubit frame normalization alone does not close
it.

## Completion Test

The predictive specification is complete enough for constitutional language
only when:

1. every foundational field is filled or retired by a cited proof;
2. one candidate, not a menu of inequivalent routes, is selected by science;
3. finite controls and full-lattice/limit claims are clearly separated;
4. every empirical parameter and boundary datum is identified as derived or
   contingent;
5. the repository's TOE interfaces arise from one coherent common substrate,
   with every dependence on approved primitives and contingent boundary data
   explicit; and
6. adversarial review finds no hidden state, context, measure, operation
   algebra, selector, clock, resource, or presentation quotient.

This checklist is not yet satisfied. It is a current candidate checklist, not
a proof of universal completeness; the predictive specification that must fill
it is not yet available.

## Boundary

This schema prevents the final axiom prose from hiding an unfinished theory
inside words such as `lawful`, `continuation`, `available`, `record`, or
`permanent`. It is deliberately more detailed than the final constitution
should be.
