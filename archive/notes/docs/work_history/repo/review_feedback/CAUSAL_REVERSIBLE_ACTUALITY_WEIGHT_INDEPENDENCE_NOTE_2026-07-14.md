## Bounded Red-Team Result

The conclusion survives, but in a narrower and more useful form.

No tested mechanism turns readiness plus reversible evolution alone into both
one actual append-only history and calibrated weights. Every successful route
places the missing information in one of two locations:

- the **exact law**: an outcome instrument, jump rate, transition kernel,
  guiding/decoder rule, invariant measure, record-preserving operation scope,
  or global solution rule; or
- the **boundary/state**: an actual initial configuration, flash/noise seed,
  ontic sector, two-boundary condition, preparation record, or measure over
  allowed initial states.

Deterministic routes can remove fundamental stochasticity. They do not remove
the selector information. A reversible law plus one actual initial state gives
one history, but it gives no calibrated ensemble. A uniquely ergodic law can
derive long-run weights once its physical record decoder and trial corpus are
fixed, but it still does not choose the actual orbit phase, and it does not
make current-state output an append-only archive. A global boundary can choose
the orbit; then the selector is boundary/state-owned.

Conversely, a normalized measure or Born representation gives weights but does
not name its actual member. Two different actual histories can have the same
frequencies, and one finite transcript is compatible with multiple weight
laws.

The irreducible item is therefore not “stochastic collapse” and not “two
witnesses.” It is **exact predictive information**: the complete rule or
boundary data that fixes both the record law and its realized member. Whether
that information is stochastic, deterministic, global, retrocausal, or
sector-valued is secondary.

This is not a universal no-go. A theorem could still uniquely derive a
deterministic law, physical decoder, boundary class, and actual solution from
the existing foundation. The finite controls show only that none of those
pieces follows from the generic words causal, reversible, decohered, redundant,
consistent, or multiway.

## Red-Team By Route

### Wolfram multiway and causal invariance

Causal invariance can quotient update schedules that differ only by commuting
or causally incomparable steps. It does not select an outcome sector. The
finite control has two syntactic paths ending in record `A` and one ending in
record `B`. Uniform path counting gives `2/3,1/3`; quotienting to terminal
records gives `1/2,1/2`. Duplicating a presentation path changes the first
weight while preserving physical endpoints.

A physical measure must therefore specify whether paths, causal histories,
terminal sectors, observer-equivalence classes, amplitudes, or another quotient
are elementary. Causal invariance may derive schedule gauge after that choice;
it does not make the choice or name one actual path. Global confluence is also
too strong for contradictory permanent records unless outcome labels are
forgotten.

### Decoherence and quantum Darwinism

Unitary system-environment interaction can suppress local interference and
copy one pointer value into many disjoint fragments. The runner constructs two
exact CNOT witnesses. Every local observer sees the same classical weight
distribution, while the global off-diagonal branch coherence remains nonzero
and the same local gates erase both witnesses exactly.

Decoherence therefore derives a stable effective basis and Darwinism derives
redundant accessibility conditional on the interaction/environment split.
Neither creates one global actual member. Born norms are already part of the
quantum state/probability reading. Append-only permanence requires an
exhaustive future-operation restriction or an asymptotic sector theorem.

### Consistent histories

For initial `|+x>`, both the one-time `Z` family and the incompatible one-time
`X` family decohere exactly. They carry different weights. Consistency licenses
each supplied realm; it does not select the realm or its one actual history.

A one-history consistent-histories interpretation can place actuality in the
realized history/boundary. The decoherence functional and realm repertoire stay
law-owned. This closes the ontology only by keeping the exact realm and member
inputs visible.

### Everett and branch weights

Everett preserves reversible evolution by retaining all branches, so it does
not satisfy the one-actual-history target. Decision-theoretic or envariance
arguments may derive norm weights under their rationality, symmetry,
equivalence, and additivity premises, but they do not select one branch.

Raw branch counting is not an alternative. Splitting one aggregate `B` branch
into three nonzero subbranches leaves its Hilbert weight `2/3` invariant while
changing the count weight from `1/2` to `3/4`. The physical quotient and measure
must be fixed before counting is meaningful.

### Objective collapse and sampled instruments

These are genuine positive mechanisms. A supplied collapse/jump or sampled
instrument law gives normalized event weights, selects one event, and can make
its event history permanent. But the law is exactly the additional content
this cycle is pricing.

The runner repeats the decisive unravelling control: projective dephasing and a
random `I/Z` mixture have the same nonselective channel and different event
labels/weights. Reversible or averaged evolution does not identify which
instrument is physical. Collapse succeeds by specifying that instrument and
its sample semantics, not by deriving it from the summed channel.

### Bohmian actual configuration and typicality

Bohmian mechanics gives one actual configuration once the guiding law,
wavefunction, and actual initial point are supplied. Equivariance preserves a
chosen equilibrium measure; it does not select the measure merely from the
fact that one trajectory exists.

The finite analog is a reversible permutation with two invariant cycles. It
has a continuum of stationary distributions distinguished by the total weight
assigned to each cycle. An actual basis point chooses one cycle, while the
ensemble weight between cycles remains arbitrary. This isolates the logical
role of quantum equilibrium/typicality without claiming the toy permutation
is Bohmian physics.

### Causal-set sequential growth

Sequential growth gives a clean append-only event ontology and derives label
independence after the transition law satisfies the required covariance
conditions. It does not select the transition couplings from causal order
alone. The exact finite analog holds append support and causal staging fixed
while changing a normalized first-extension kernel from `1/2,1/2` to
`2/3,1/3`.

Thus causal-set growth is another positive law-owned route: permanent events
and one stochastic history follow after the exact growth kernel is supplied.
It is not a derivation of quantum Born weights from reversible QCA dynamics.

### Deterministic superselection and hidden-variable encoding

A block-diagonal or superselected reversible law preserves sectors; it does not
choose which sector is actual. That is initial/boundary data. A distribution
over sectors is also not forced by preservation. The runner's two-cycle
permutation preserves both `1/4:3/4` and `3/4:1/4` sector ensembles exactly.

A hidden boundary string can encode every future outcome and thereby generate
one deterministic transcript. To predict calibrated frequencies, the theory
must also specify a measure/typicality rule over strings or prove an internal
frequency theorem for the actual one. Encoding the answer in a unique boundary
is a valid deterministic theory, but it is an equivalent selector, not a
derivation from readiness.

### Unique ergodicity: the strongest deterministic attack

A six-state single-cycle permutation is reversible and has one stationary
distribution, the uniform measure. This is the strongest exact counter-route:
long-run weights genuinely derive from dynamics once the observable partition
is fixed.

It still leaves three pieces:

1. the exact cycle law itself;
2. the physical record decoder—marking `m` of six states yields any rational
   weight `m/6` for the same dynamics; and
3. the actual orbit phase/initial point, which changes the transcript while
   preserving complete-cycle frequencies.

The current state also recurs after six steps, so the dynamics supplies no
append-only archive without memory allocation and preservation rules. Unique
ergodicity is a live route for deriving weights from a complete deterministic
law. It is not a free bridge from reversible kinematics to Record.

### Two-boundary and global-history selection

A final boundary, low-record sector, least-action global solution, or
retrocausal consistency condition could select one entire history without a
local stochastic collapse. This remains a serious live alternative. Its exact
global constraint and actual boundary instance are selector content. To recover
laboratory weights across preparation classes, it must also supply a measure,
typicality theorem, multiplicity quotient, or deterministic frequency theorem.

This route relocates actualization to `BOUNDARY_STATE_OWNED`; it does not make
it disappear.
