## 2. The Two-Ray Frame Theorem

Let `Z_f` be a traceless Hermitian involution dynamically selected by a local
interaction. Let `Q` be a transported rank-one record projector and

```text
R_f = 2Q-I.
```

Assume `[R_f,Z_f] != 0`. Every traceless Hermitian qubit involution is a unit
Bloch vector, so

```text
c = Tr(R_f Z_f)/2
```

is its component along `Z_f`. Removing and normalizing that component gives

```text
T_f = (R_f-cZ_f)/sqrt(1-c^2).
```

Direct multiplication gives

```text
T_f^dagger=T_f,
T_f^2=I,
Tr(T_f Z_f)=0.
```

The oriented third axis

```text
K_f=-iT_fZ_f
```

is also a Hermitian involution and is Hilbert--Schmidt orthogonal to both.
The ordered triple satisfies

```text
Tr(K_f T_f Z_f)/(2i)=+1.
```

Uniqueness is exact: after `Z_f`, the ray `R_f`, normalization, and orientation
are fixed, there is only one such `K_f`. Its sign is reversed if the
orientation is reversed.

Under any common one-site recoding `A -> VAV^dagger`, every line above is
conjugated by `V`. Therefore the construction is relational and presentation
covariant. It does not insert a constant `X` matrix into the law.

## 6. What Chirality Does And Does Not Do

Proper cubic rotations preserve the orientation of `(d,e,u)`. A mirror
reverses it. In the internal frame, complex-linear `M_2(C)` automorphisms act
by proper `SO(3)` rotations and preserve Pauli orientation. These facts make
chirality a real relational resource once the spatial/internal bridge and a
decoder exist.

However, the present residual is not a chirality pair.

- `X_f` and `Y_f` are related by a proper quarter-turn about `Z_f`.
- Both solderings in the runner have determinant `+1`.
- Both apparatus laws live in the same positively oriented full frame.
- Reversing the sign in `K_f=-iT_fZ_f` produces `-K_f`, not `T_f`.
- The unordered PVM `{(I+K_f)/2,(I-K_f)/2}` is identical after
  `K_f -> -K_f`; only the two outcome names are swapped.

So **chirality does not select X versus Y**. Chirality can select an oriented
outcome label, phase convention, or later parity decoder when those are held
fixed. It cannot replace the apparatus-role clause.

This also sharpens the separate Weyl/matter chirality lane. A proper/mirror
law-sign distinction may remain physically meaningful there, while the
present center-context fork is a proper azimuthal relation. They should not be
merged under one word.
