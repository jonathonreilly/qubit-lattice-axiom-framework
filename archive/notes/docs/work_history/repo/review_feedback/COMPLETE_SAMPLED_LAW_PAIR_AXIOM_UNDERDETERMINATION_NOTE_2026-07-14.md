## Question

The earlier probes separately showed that availability does not fix support,
support does not fix statistics, and a measure does not identify one actual
sample. The decisive constitutional control is stronger:

> Can two complete local sampled-record laws satisfy the same present
> foundation interface and still predict different readable records?

Yes. The pair below shares the carrier, state type, contexts, availability,
successor support, sampling convention, record identity, permanence, gluing,
projective extension, covariance, and unbounded fresh-site route. Only one
extensional kernel parameter differs. A one-record transcript separates the
two laws.

This is the exact reason that polishing `forms`, `witness`, `clock`, or
`permanent` cannot finish the framework while the value of the fixed rule is
absent.

The empty profile is menu-complete but need not be event-ready; readiness is

## The Two Exact Laws

For `lambda>0`, define on every two-label menu

```text
p_lambda(a | n_0,n_1)
    = lambda^(n_a) / (lambda^(n_0) + lambda^(n_1)).
```

On a singleton menu the available value has probability one. Compare

```text
L_1: lambda=1,
L_2: lambda=2.
```

Both laws are:

- nearest-neighbour local;
- covariant under every translation and all 24 proper cubic rotations;
- equivariant under global interchange of the two frame values;
- normalized and strictly positive on every available branch;
- identical in availability and successor support;
- append-only and exactly record-preserving;
- composable on ordered events and commuting on disjoint dependency regions;
- projectively consistent for every fixed context schedule; and
- equipped with the same one-uniform-seed sampling semantics.

The covariance claim in this list is a claim about the outcome distribution.
A numerical representation of the randomizer is itself coordinate data: under
the global label interchange, the convenient interval coordinate must change
as `u -> 1-u` (away from measure-zero threshold conventions), or the sampler
must be represented label-free. Holding the same number `u` fixed while
renaming the two intervals is not pathwise label covariance.

They nevertheless disagree at a target with two `0` neighbours and one `1`
neighbour:

```text
L_1: p(0)=1/2,
L_2: p(0)=2/3.
```

With one common coupling coordinate `u=3/5`, `L_1` writes `1` while `L_2`
writes `0`. This coupled-sample witness is illustrative; the invariant
operational discriminator is the `1/2` versus `2/3` transcript distribution.
The resulting readable one-record law therefore separates the two physical
laws. This is stronger than a difference in an unobservable availability
table.

## What The Pair Proves

Within this explicit sampled append-law class, the current four axioms and
their registered primitives do not determine the conditional law of future
record transcripts. The missing item is at least one exact extensional law
value—or an exact operational-equivalence theorem that removes every such
difference. Merely saying that one fixed rule exists does not tell a user of
the framework whether the observable answer is `1/2` or `2/3`.

The pair does **not** prove that `lambda` is a new free constant of nature,
that the physical law must be stochastic, or that no deeper exact rule can
derive one kernel. It supplies a lower bound on what the present prose fails
to identify.

It also clarifies the role of the Wolfram-style multiway picture. The common
branch graph is the multiway support. Attaching `L_1` or `L_2` gives different
physical measures on the same graph. Causal invariance or schedule quotienting
can remove update-order redundancy inside an outcome sector; it does not
choose between these two measures.

No Record-only sentence can distinguish the pair, because Record status,

No menu-only Admissibility sentence can distinguish it, because availability
and successor support are identical. A predictive constitution needs a stable

This result does not yet authorize that reference: the pair is a diagnostic,
not the candidate law of nature. It does establish the acceptance test for any
future axiom wording:

> a claimed complete law reference must determine every finite record
> transcript probability, or prove why all representatives left open by its

The causal-front and invariant-record language can still be the right
plain-language interface. It cannot replace this extensional requirement.
