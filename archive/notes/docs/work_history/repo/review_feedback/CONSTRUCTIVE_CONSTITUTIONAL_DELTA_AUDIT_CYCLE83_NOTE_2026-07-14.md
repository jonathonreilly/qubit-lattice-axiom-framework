carrier; continuation and context decoding; formation readiness and event
map; actuality/history semantics; normalized record predictions; record

record-transcript-equivalence class may replace one syntactic representative.

This sentence belongs immediately after the existing first Admissibility
sentence, or can replace that sentence once covariance is included in the
stable referent. It does **not** belong in Record and it does not create a fifth

verbatim live wording.

The tempting alternative

single TOE lane. The constitutional atom, if one is ultimately needed, is
the exact identity of `L*`, not the adjective `complete`.
