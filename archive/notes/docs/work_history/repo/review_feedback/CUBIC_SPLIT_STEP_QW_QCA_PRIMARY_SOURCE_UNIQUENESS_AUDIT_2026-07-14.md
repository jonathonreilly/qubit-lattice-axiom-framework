## Result Up Front

The bounded result is **no unique exact update selected**: no primary theorem
found in the audited sources selects one update satisfying the conjunction
presently needed:

1. the standard cubic lattice and its six primitive nearest-neighbour edges;
2. exact proper-cubic covariance under all 24 orientation-preserving cubic

3. a finite-range discrete-time update with a stated physical tick;
4. a genuine many-body QCA on primitive qubits or derived finite qubit blocks,
   rather than only a one-particle quantum walk; and
5. a relativistic low-energy sector with its carrier, species, mass, schedule,
   record instrument, and exact-versus-effective boundary exposed.

The strongest exact classification in the audited set is D'Ariano, Erba, and
Perinotti's complete classification at **coin dimension two**. In three
dimensions it selects the BCC Cayley graph and two Weyl walks under their
isotropy assumptions. Its exact isotropy representation is generated by
`{I,i sigma_x,i sigma_y,i sigma_z}`. It is smaller than the full proper cubic group.
It is not a classification of cardinal-edge walks on the live standard cubic
presentation, and it is a single-particle result.

The Cycle 7 factorization survives exact rechecking:

```text

```

It proves a useful **no lattice edit** compatibility statement: all BCC
body-diagonal support in that macro-step is generated by three moves along
edges already present in `Z^3`. It does not make the ordered product exactly
24-fold cubic covariant. Different axis orders agree at Weyl first order and
differ at second order. The exact update remains unselected.

An **external phase cycle is not unavoidable** in the broad law class. The
runner constructs exact one-step proper-cubic-covariant cardinal walks at

narrower package such as a two-component carrier, one cardinal axis per
radius-one microtick, one autonomous local rule, and no larger block. No
primary classification theorem establishing uniqueness in that combined

The **conditional no-Qubit-edit conclusion** also survives, but only with its

The audit therefore licenses neither a split-step sentence nor a clock

and the current qubit substrate do not yet select it.

## The Semantic Separation That Prevents A False Closure

Three mathematical objects are repeatedly called “QCA” in the literature but
do different jobs here.

### One-particle quantum walk

A translation-invariant walk has state space

```text
l2(Z^3) tensor C^s
```

and a finite-range unitary on position plus an internal coin. There is exactly
one walker. D'Ariano's Weyl walks, the Bialynicki-Birula two-component update,
Chandrashekar's split walk, and the Mlodinow-Brun walk begin in this semantic
class even where the historical source uses “cellular automaton.”

### Many-body QCA

In the algebraic definition of Schumacher and Werner, a QCA is a
translation-invariant finite-propagation automorphism of the quasi-local
algebra built from tensor-product cell algebras. It acts on arbitrary
many-cell states and arbitrary occupation sectors. A number-conserving QCA can
restrict to a one-particle sector and thereby induce a quantum walk, but the
reverse lift is not unique. In particular, the walk says nothing about what
happens when two excitations meet. The collision rule is additional data.

### Finite-block or sector realization

A block of primitive qubits can carry an effective coin, spinor, fermionic
mode set, or program phase. This can preserve the primitive axiom while
enlarging the effective carrier. It is nevertheless a theorem obligation, not
a change of notation: the local embeddings must compose, the physical sector
must be invariant, translations must act consistently across every block
origin, and the record interface must be defined on the many-body algebra.

This distinction matters constitutionally. A unique walk on a chosen
single-particle Hilbert space would still not be a unique exact many-body law.

## Exact Full-Cubic Cardinal-Walk Control

The six cardinal directions form a six-dimensional permutation
representation of the 24-element proper cubic rotation group. Its commutant
has dimension three. The representation decomposes as

```text
C^6 = A1 (rank 1) + E (rank 2) + T1 (rank 3).
```

Let the corresponding orthogonal projectors be `P_A`, `P_E`, and `P_T`. Every
coin of the form

```text
C(alpha,beta,gamma)
  = exp(i alpha) P_A + exp(i beta) P_E + exp(i gamma) P_T
```

is exactly unitary and commutes with all 24 proper cubic rotations. With

```text
D(k) = diag(exp(i k.h))  for h in {+/-e_x,+/-e_y,+/-e_z},
U(k) = D(k) C,
```

the transition matrices are exactly proper-cubic covariant and have precisely
the live six-neighbour support. The runner displays three inequivalent
members—identity, a Grover-type coin, and a relative-phase coin—with different
spectra. Modulo a global phase, the exact symmetry still leaves two continuous
relative phases.

This finite result establishes two narrow points:

1. a one-step exact full-cubic walk on standard cardinal edges does not need a
   physical three-phase clock merely to exist; and
2. exact full-cubic covariance, locality, and unitarity alone do not select a
   unique update.

It does **not** establish a Weyl continuum sector for this family. It is a
control against the broad clock/uniqueness claims, not the desired dynamics.
Embedding one hard-core walker needs at least six directional states plus a
vacuum, hence at least eight cell levels if implemented in qubits. Allowing six
independently occupiable fermionic directional modes instead needs local Fock
dimension `2^6=64`. Neither carrier is the primitive onsite `M_2` without a
block theorem.

## Cycle 7 Factorization: What Is Exact And What Is Effective

For

```text
S_i(q_i) = cos(q_i) I - i sin(q_i) sigma_i,
```

the runner symbolically confirms

```text
S_x(q_x) S_y(q_y) S_z(q_z) = A^-(q)
```

for the published BCC Weyl branch, with exact unitarity for every Brillouin
momentum. This is stronger than a numerical or continuum match. Each factor
uses one existing cardinal edge, so the resulting body-diagonal macro-support
does not require fundamental BCC adjacency.

The same calculation also fixes the boundary of that result:

```text
d^2/dq_x dq_y [S_x S_y S_z - S_z S_y S_x] at q=0
  = diag(-2 i, 2 i),
```

and a 90-degree rotation gives a nonzero exact covariance defect beginning at
quadratic order. Every axis order has the same first derivative
`sigma_x,sigma_y,sigma_z`; the Weyl equation therefore cannot distinguish the
orders at leading continuum resolution. The exact lattice updates can.

There are two legitimate tick conventions, with different residue:

- Treat `S_x S_y S_z` as one physical macro-tick. Then it is a finite-range
  radius-three update and needs no intermediate clock, but its exact ordered
  kernel remains supplied and is not full-cubic covariant.
- Require one primitive-edge radius-one update per physical tick. Then the
  factors are three distinct substeps. A fixed phase schedule, an internal
  phase register, or a different exactly symmetric construction is needed.

This refines the Cycle 7 language. “No lattice edit” is an exact reachability
claim, not a claim that a three-edge path is one primitive nearest-neighbour
tick. The qubit result is similarly conditional, as detailed below.

One further correction is important. A finite-range Wilson or staggered
Hamiltonian is not automatically a finite-range discrete-time QCA: in general
`exp(-i t H)` develops tails beyond every fixed radius. Those Hamiltonians
remain useful continuum/block probes, but a strict causal update requires an
exact circuit, partition, walk, or automorphism construction.

## Does A Phase-Cycled Walk Need A Physical Internal Clock?

Not in every formulation, but it cannot be erased by rhetoric.

The companion
`CAUSAL_SCHEDULE_EQUIVALENCE_WOLFRAM_INSPIRATION_PROBE_NOTE_2026-07-14.md`
proves an exact positive quotient for local sampled laws: execution orders
that differ only by swapping causally incomparable events give the same joint
record law when every event reads a fixed predecessor set. The total simulator
order is then bookkeeping. It also gives an exact counterexample when one
event reads another event's new record: opposite orders produce different
permanent transcripts, and randomizing the orders creates a third law.

The split factors `S_x`, `S_y`, and `S_z` are in the second category. They do
not commute. `XYZ` and `ZYX` are different exact kernels even though their
continuum first derivatives agree. Consequently:

- a causal-equivalence class may quotient disjoint or commuting circuit
  refinements without a clock;
- it does not identify these noncommuting split orders unless a further
  operational theorem proves equality of every finite readable-record
  distribution after the declared equivalence;
- stochastic twirling over orders supplies a probability measure and usually
  produces a channel, not the original unitary;
- coherent control over orders supplies a larger controlled unitary and a
  phase/program carrier; and
- cycling through orders over several ticks supplies an exact periodic law
  plus a question about start phase and which records may form within the
  period.

The primary classifications normally take one synchronous global step
operator as part of the model. They do not identify noncommuting update orders
as gauge. Mlodinow and Brun call the axis order arbitrary when defining their
product walk, but they also calculate direction-dependent corrections and
state that rotational invariance is recovered only in the continuum. That is
choice-of-model freedom, not a proof that the exact orders are physically
equivalent. Schumacher and Werner place the global automorphism in the QCA
definition; the schedule is distinct from the state unless it is encoded.
Shepherd, Franz, and Werner show constructively that external control can be
made an internal program, but the local program state is then physical
capacity and initial data.

Therefore a phase-cycled/twirled cubic walk does not always require an
external clock. It does require one of the following to be exact:

1. a fixed macro-update for which internal ordering is only a mathematical
   factorization;
2. an enlarged autonomous carrier containing an internal program/phase;
3. an exact commuting/causal-equivalence theorem removing the order; or
4. a supplied stochastic or coherent rule over orders.

Only option 3 removes the content. It has not been proved for the noncommuting
cubic split factors.

## Conditional No-Qubit-Edit Audit

The existing primitive-site algebra can remain `M_2(C)` if larger effective
carriers are built from finite regions. This is a compatibility route, not a
completed derivation.

### Capacity counts

- A six-direction hard-core walk plus vacuum has seven local states and fits
  in three qubits, not one.
- Six independently occupiable directional modes have Fock dimension `64`
  and need six qubits.
- A two-component fermion field has onsite Fock dimension four. Its
  one-particle coin has dimension two; those are not the same carrier.
- A fixed `2^3` cube has eight possible block origins. Primitive translations
  permute those origins rather than preserving one chosen tiling.

### Constructive bridges

Schumacher-Werner and Arrighi-Nesme-Werner establish many-body algebraic and
localizability machinery. Farrelly-Short prove that causal fermionic dynamics
can be represented in a qubit-QCA subsector; in dimensions above one, their
local construction introduces auxiliary fermions and hence auxiliary qubits,
with finite overhead. Mlodinow-Brun construct tensor-product particle QCAs and
then select antisymmetric or symmetric sectors. The coinless/coarse-graining
work shows how a finite cell can acquire an effective internal coin.

Together these results keep the block route open. None says that the live
primitive algebra, without composition and sector work, already is the
relativistic field carrier. A complete no-edit theorem must still show:

1. generated finite-region composition from the primitive local embeddings;
2. an autonomous finite-range QCA on those blocks;
3. covariance under primitive translations, including the block-origin
   orbit;
4. a local invariant fermionic/physical sector with any auxiliary modes
   controlled;
5. interactions and the multiparticle collision rule; and
6. compatibility with append-only record formation and readout.

The runner adds a useful many-body control. On a `3^3` torus, the product of
controlled-`Z` gates over every undirected cubic edge is a genuine
one-qubit-per-site radius-one QCA. Its binary symplectic map is invertible,
commutator-preserving, involutive, and exactly covariant under all 24 proper
cubic rotations. Identity and this cubic `CZ` QCA are two distinct exact
covariant primitive-qubit laws. This does not provide relativistic dynamics:
the `CZ` law selects a Pauli-`Z` frame and has no claimed Weyl sector. It only
shows that broad many-body QCA structure plus spatial symmetry is not a
uniqueness theorem.

## What A Genuine Selection Theorem Would Have To Say

The missing theorem is not “derive the Weyl equation again.” Several sources
already explain why Weyl/Dirac behavior is natural at long wavelength. The
needed statement must define one exact law class and one exact equivalence
relation. At minimum it would have to fix or derive:

- whether locality range is measured per microtick or macro-tick;
- whether the carrier is primitive `M_2`, a finite block, a Fock algebra, or a
  single-particle sector;
- the exact action of all 24 proper cubic rotations on space and carrier;
- the allowed translation action across block origins;
- unitarity/automorphism, reversibility, and any vacuum or number-sector
  conditions;
- an irreducibility or minimality criterion strong enough to exclude the
  continuous cardinal-coin family and trivial cubic QCAs;
- how opposite chirality, mass parameter, tastes, and auxiliary modes are
  treated;
- whether noncommuting schedules are distinct laws or operationally
  equivalent;
- the continuum domain and an error theorem; and
- the exact record instrument and actualization law, which a free reversible
  kernel alone does not supply.

A complete classification over that domain might select one operational class
or prove that parameters remain. The audited primary literature does not yet
contain that theorem. The exact update remains unselected, so the present
result is a research target and boundary, not an axiom candidate.
