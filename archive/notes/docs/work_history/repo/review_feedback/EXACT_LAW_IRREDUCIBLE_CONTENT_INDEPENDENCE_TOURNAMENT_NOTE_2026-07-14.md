## 7. Born Tournament: What The Three Words Really Buy

### 7.1 The loose reading fails exactly

For every qubit direction `n`, let

```text
f(n) = (1 + n_z^3)/2.
```

It is nonnegative and obeys `f(n)+f(-n)=1`. It is a noncontextual assignment
to each binary qubit projector and it composes as a normalized, no-signalling
product assignment on every product context. It is not Born-affine. Values on
the `X`, `Y`, and `Z` axes would force Bloch vector `(0,0,1)`, but on
`n=(X+Z)/sqrt(2)` the cubic assignment and that density matrix disagree.

Therefore

```text
normalization
+ qubit-projector noncontextuality
+ causal product composition
```

does **not** derive Born on the loose product-context reading.

### 7.2 The strengthened composite reading succeeds conditionally

There are two valid representation routes:

1. On a qubit, one effect-noncontextual assignment on the full effect interval,

   Busch/CFMR theorem and primary-source bridge recorded in
   `OPERATIONAL_QUOTIENT_BORN_AFFINITY_CYCLE20_NOTE_2026-07-14.md`.

   and the projective Born form. This is the composite-projective route of

The second result shows what “causal composition” must contain for the Born
claim: not merely independent product contexts, but the ordinary generated
