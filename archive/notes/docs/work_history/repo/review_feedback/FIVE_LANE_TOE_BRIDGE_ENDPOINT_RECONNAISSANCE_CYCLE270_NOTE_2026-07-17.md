## Result up front

A cross-lane bridge must have two independently reconstructed endpoints. A
compiler, conditional response theorem, or record decoder on one side is not a
bridge merely because the other side has a familiar physics name.

Reading and rerunning the strongest current science in all five TOE lanes gives
one common diagnosis:

1. The repository has substantially more conditional cross-lane science than
   the old integer maturity labels communicate.
2. The selected-substrate closure is much weaker because the microscopic
   update, occurrence law, clock normalization, source ledger, and numerical
   history law remain supplied in different places.
3. The Cycle-230 compiler is therefore important but cannot by itself complete
   the TOE. It can connect the intrinsic CAR matter endpoint to physical M2
   sites. It cannot select time, a Record-forming occurrence law, physical
   energy/source, or Born/history weights.
4. No lane reconnaissance produces a route-independent obstruction or axiom
   pressure. The live gaps are typed bridge interfaces and candidate-law
   selection problems.
