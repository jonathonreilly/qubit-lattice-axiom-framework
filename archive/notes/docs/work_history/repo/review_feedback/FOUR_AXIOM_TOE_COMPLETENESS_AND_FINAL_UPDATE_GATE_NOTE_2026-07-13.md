passes. The positive two-site bridge then passes all twelve algebra checks but
correctly fails its three premise-status checks because its required
authorities are currently `unaudited`.

Two further issues must be kept visible before any final update:

- the axioms name one fixed nearest-neighbor rule but do not state its actual
  extensional content; and
- `state = configuration of records` is not yet related to the density
  operators, preparations, effects, channels, and composite projection

Those two issues need not become new axiom sentences. A sufficiently explicit
physical rule and an operational reconstruction theorem could derive them.
They nevertheless prevent a claim that the two draft sentences alone make the
foundation complete.

### What the two proposed sentences close

The draft Admissibility sentence closes the `F` sub-atom
**availability-to-supported-formation-extension**, conditional on formation
being enabled. The draft Record sentence closes the `M/F` sub-atom
**site-and-content preservation in every lawful continuation**. Together with
one-record-per-site, they prove nonreconnection of futures containing
different records at the same site.

They do not close `Q`, `C`, `X`, `D`, `P`, `T`, `I`, or `G`. They also do not
specify the already-postulated fixed rule's predictive content, enable
formation, or choose a future.

### The additional composition seam

The exact finite countermodel is:

```text
B = M_4(C) direct-sum M_4(C),
A_x(a) = (a tensor I_2) direct-sum (a tensor I_2),
A_y(b) = (I_2 tensor b) direct-sum (I_2 tensor b).
```

Both local embeddings are faithful and commute. Their products span only the
diagonal `M_4(C)` copy, of dimension 16, while the full composite has
self-adjoint real dimension 32. The central observable
`I_4 direct-sum (-I_4)` is invisible to the local-product span. Therefore:

```text
one M_2(C) at each site + locality
    does not entail
the ordinary generated finite tensor product or local tomography.
```

The smallest direct composition content to test later is the statement that
finite joint possibility domains are generated by the one-site domains under
their ordinary complex tensor product, with no extra global sector. Exact
wording is deliberately deferred until the rule/typing probe has decided
whether this can instead be a theorem.

Tensor composition would authorize the kinematic `M_4(C)` carrier for two
sites. It would still not make every composite projector physically
available/readable, identify a prepared density operator, supply a frame
measure, or prove operational local tomography. Those remain `Q/P` work.

### State and possibility typing

The current Qualification says:

```text
A state is a configuration of records.
```

The current text supplies no map from an empty record configuration to the
density operators and phase-bearing representatives imported by later quantum
calculations. That absence does not make all such representatives physical
states of the framework; it identifies an unproved typing bridge. Conversely,
`M_2(C)` is an algebra, not by itself a declaration that a recordable
possibility is a ray, projector, effect, density matrix, or arbitrary matrix.

There are two live closure routes:

1. **records/modal reconstruction:** show that all physically relevant
   pre-record information is encoded in the full continuation structure and
   reconstruct preparations, effects, channels, and their equivalences from
   record statistics; or
2. **typed operational bridge:** explicitly relate record configurations to a
   larger operational state and event algebra.

No test yet selects between these routes. This is a required theorem/semantic
probe before final wording, not a fourth proposed Record clause.

The present evidence supports those four *candidate locations*. It does not
