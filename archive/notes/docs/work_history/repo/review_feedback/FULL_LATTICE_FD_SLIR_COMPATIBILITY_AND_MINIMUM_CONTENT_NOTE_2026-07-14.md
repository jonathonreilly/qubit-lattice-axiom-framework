## 1. Global Event And Carrier Data

### 1.1 A causal net is additional to the spatial lattice

Let

```text
E = (events, <=, S, x)
```

be a countable interval-finite event poset. Each event `e` has finite
nearest-neighbour support `S_e` in `Z^3` and a prospective outcome-record
address `x_e`. Interval-finite means every causal diamond

```text
D[p,q] = {e : p <= e <= q}
```

is finite. If two ready incomparable events have overlapping supports, the
family is ill typed; overlapping supports must be causally ordered. A ready
antichain therefore has pairwise-disjoint supports.

The current Lattice axiom supplies spatial adjacency, translations, and proper
cubic rotations. It does not supply this event order. A full FD-SLIR law must
either name one global event net or derive one. Choosing the auxiliary
finite-speed order on `Z x Z^3` is one exact mathematical completion, not a
derivation of physical time.

### 1.2 Generated finite carriers

For every finite spatial set `F`, set

```text
A_F = tensor_(x in F) M_2(C),
i_(F,G)(a) = a tensor I_(G\F),        F subset G.
```

## 2. Exact Compatible-Family Contract

For every finite diamond `D`, incoming boundary record `beta`, physically
recorded ready context `kappa`, and outcome tuple `r`, let

```text
I_D^(r | kappa,beta)
```

be a completely positive trace-nonincreasing branch map. The following are
the exact hypotheses of the extension theorem.

### H1 -- normalized local instruments

For each `(D,kappa,beta)`, the finite branch family is positive and

```text
sum_r I_D^(r | kappa,beta)
```

is trace preserving. Sharp rank-one repeatability may reduce each selected
branch to its Lüders form, but sharpness, the physical PVM menu, and
repeatability remain supplied content.

### H2 -- gluing

For adjacent causally composable diamonds,

```text
I_(D2 o D1)^(r2,r1) = I_D2^r2 o I_D1^r1,
```

with the outgoing record/boundary of `D1` used as the incoming boundary of
`D2`.

### H3 -- spacelike exchange

For incomparable ready events or antichains with disjoint supports,

```text
I_A^a o I_B^b = I_B^b o I_A^a.
```

For local Lüders projectors this follows from their distinct tensor factors.
It implies independence from the chosen linear extension and, after summing a
complete remote instrument, the usual no-signalling marginal identity.

### H4 -- projective cylinder compatibility

For finite causal ideals `F subset G` and a future extension of one boundary
and program,

```text
w_F(C_F) = sum_(r on G\F) w_G(C_F union r).
```

For a general subdiamond, the same equation is conditional on the complete
incoming boundary induced by the larger diamond. Marginalizing a larger law
without preserving that induced boundary is not a valid restriction.

Individual finite normalization is weaker than H4. The exact runner includes
two normalized laws for which a one-event probability is `1/2` while the
corresponding two-event marginal is `2/3`.

### H5 -- natural record decoder

A complete configuration `C_D` contains preparation, program, setting, and
outcome records sufficient to reconstruct the branch word and every future
kernel. Decoder restriction and diamond restriction commute. Operationally,

```text
C = C'  implies
P(future transcript | C, future protocol)
  = P(future transcript | C', future protocol)
```

for every finite adaptive future protocol. This is record-Markov predictive
sufficiency. It is stronger than merely declaring the name “state.”

### H6 -- append memory and sector preservation

Every extension restricts to its prior record configuration. Once outcome
`r` has formed, every later Kraus operator obeys at least the branch-relative
condition

```text
(I-Q_r) K_i Q_r = 0.
```

The stronger two-sided condition `Phi*(Q_r)=Q_r` may be used where no inflow
from an open sector is required. The two conditions must not be conflated.

### H7 -- physical sampling

The normalized trace weights define a finite outcome law, and exactly one
tuple is physically sampled. The nonselective map supplies normalization but
does not prove this sampling instruction.

### H8 -- renewal or export

For an indefinitely extendible process, fresh record carriers are supplied by
an injective causally reachable allocator into `Z^3`, or any reused carrier is
paired with an exact export dilation preserving its old information. No
bounded patch may silently accept infinitely many new permanent site-tethered
records.

## 3. Conditional Extension Theorem

Under H1-H8:

1. Any two linear extensions of one finite event ideal differ by adjacent
   swaps of incomparable events. H3 therefore makes the branch map and branch
   weight independent of update order.
2. H1 and H2 give normalization, while H4 gives a projectively compatible
   family of finite cylinder probabilities.
3. Because the event set is countable and each recorded outcome alphabet is
   finite, the Kolmogorov extension theorem gives a unique probability
   measure on infinite record histories for every fixed program/context
   schedule. Adaptive normalized kernels give the corresponding construction
   by the Ionescu-Tulcea theorem.
4. The carrier embeddings give `A_ql`. Finite-propagation local maps act
   consistently on every finite observable and hence define the quasilocal
   process at every finite causal depth.
5. H5 makes the reconstructed conditional branch functional a function of
   records alone. H6 makes formed records nonreconnecting. H8 prevents a
   hidden finite-capacity reuse in an indefinite history.

This theorem gives global **statistics**, not a realized global history. H7
is still the instruction that one history is actual. Nor does the theorem
guarantee that an infinite-history conditional state is represented by one
normal density matrix; the compatible local/cylinder state is the safe
claim.

## 5. Renewal And Memory Capacity

The radius-`R` Manhattan ball in `Z^3` has

```text
V_R = (4 R^3 + 6 R^2 + 8 R + 3)/3
```

sites. A shell-first allocator is injective and uses unbounded radius rather
than reusing an old site. If a repeated clean cycle may export `k` arbitrary
bits, then after `m` cycles its history support has size

```text
2^(k m)
```

and needs at least `k m` bits of distinguishable memory. A fixed `B`-bit
export store supports at most `floor(B/k)` arbitrary clean cycles.

This is not a claim that every microscopic update forms a record. Recyclable
working dynamics and sparse permanent record formation remain distinct. It is
only the memory condition for an indefinitely renewed permanent archive.

Before any typography is compressed into a stable reference, the exact

This is a checklist, not a claim that all thirteen rows are independent
premises. Under a strict normalized-instrument typing, support, one-shot trace
weights, writing, parts of continuation, and disjoint concurrency can be
theorems. Conversely, physical equivalence, exact propagation, record status,
and trial semantics can be hidden if the rows are read too loosely.

The actual boundary **instance** is different from its allowed class. The
allowed class belongs to the law domain; decoding a chosen
preparation/boundary record belongs to predictive state. Selection or a
distribution over the cosmological boundary is contingent input unless the
framework promises an unconditional prediction of this universe.

Metric clock calibration, continuum control, matter identification, resource
physics, gravity, and contingent cosmological data remain downstream TOE
interfaces even after the law jobs are filled. A single canonical law may
derive or jointly specify several jobs. Naming it once does not reduce the
semantic inventory to one premise.

The cylinder law predicts finite transcript probabilities. Convergence of
laboratory frequencies additionally needs an independently defined trial
corpus plus an IID, ergodic, martingale, or other applicable frequency
theorem. No generic frequency theorem is claimed here.

## 7. Constitutional Consequence

The present FD-SLIR construction starts by supplying
`A_F = tensor_F M_2(C)` and by defining `sigma_C` from a complete
preparation/program/setting/outcome decoder. It therefore does **not** derive
either generated Qubit composition or predictive completeness of records.

A one-line reference could place all of that content inside one mega-law, but
calling the resulting fields “theorems” would count typography rather than
premises. The clean minimum visible architecture is:

1. an explicit raw generated finite-site Qubit composition or a theorem
   deriving the same operational carrier;
2. predictive `state=records`, including record-status typing and complete
   preparation/program/context memory; and
3. one exact compatible full-law reference carrying the event/boundary class,
   atomic maps, availability, continuation, recorded contexts, concurrency,
   trace statistics, physical sampling, projective extension, record-sector
   preservation, and renewal/export.

This note does not propose that wording for the live axioms. A future law
formulated on a genuinely more primitive substrate could prove generated
composition or reconstruct states as separating future-record equivalence
classes. Either proof would retire the corresponding separate candidate.

## 8. Narrow No-Go Boundary

The bounded negative claim is only this:

> Within the current FD-SLIR typing and the explicit finite ablation class, a
> reference to the model cannot convert carrier composition or predictive
> record completeness from supplied input into derived physics.

Live alternatives remain:

1. adopt one fully expanded law as a deliberate mega-axiom;
2. use the modular three-clause architecture above;
3. derive generated composition from a more primitive extensional rule;
4. derive state operationally after proving a separating record repertoire;
5. use a low-record boundary/open environment instead of spatial export; or
6. replace the Lüders/trace realization with another compatible physical law
   that passes the same interface tests.

The composition and state walls are independent: the silent-sector witness
can leave all record predictions unchanged, whereas the hidden-phase witness
lives on the ordinary tensor carrier. Projective statistics does not imply
actuality, and actuality does not imply trace weights. No global derivation
route is declared closed.
