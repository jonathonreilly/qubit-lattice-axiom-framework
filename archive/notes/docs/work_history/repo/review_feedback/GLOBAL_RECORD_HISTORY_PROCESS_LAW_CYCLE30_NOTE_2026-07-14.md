## 2. The exact law object needed

### Closed, fixed protocols

For a finite history-event algebra E, a decoherence functional is a complex
bivariate map D(A,B). The finite controls use the standard structural
conditions:

- Hermiticity: D(A,B) is the conjugate of D(B,A);
- finite additivity in each event argument;
- normalization: D(Omega,Omega)=1; and
- strong positivity: every finite matrix D(A_i,A_j) is positive semidefinite.

The event weight is mu(A)=D(A,A). Unlike a classical probability measure, mu
obeys the grade-two sum rule and can contain pairwise interference. Ordinary
additive probabilities arise on a decoherent family of record events, where
the relevant off-diagonal terms vanish.

This gives a clean record-only reading. Physical facts are record events. A
fine path, branch index, or virtual alternative used to calculate D can be a
**law-side computational refinement**, not an unrecorded ontic state. If a
path becomes a record, the record-producing interaction must change the
functional so that the path alternatives decohere and their output weights
add normally.

Sorkin's primary quantum-measure proposal explicitly places a generalized
measure on sets of histories and discusses one real history, preclusion, and
the unfinished role of conditional coarse-graining
([Sorkin, 1995/1997](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9507057)). Isham and Linden
develop the decoherence-functional history framework as a generalized quantum
theory ([Isham and Linden, 1994](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9405029)). These
are type precedents, not derivations of this framework's law or ontology.

### Arbitrary interventions

A closed-history functional is not yet a complete rule for arbitrary future
experiments. The law must accept local record protocols—preparations,
transformations, instruments, and readouts—as composable inputs and return a
normalized joint answer for their record outcomes. A positive process
functional or quantum comb does exactly this. Chiribella, D'Ariano, and
Perinotti introduced the quantum comb as a circuit board into which variable
subcircuits can be inserted
([Chiribella, D'Ariano, and Perinotti, 2008](https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.1325)).
Pollock and collaborators give an operational process-tensor description of
arbitrary multi-time processes, including memory
([Pollock et al., 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00589)).

The important ontology point is an inference from how the object is used here:
although a comb has an operator representation mathematically analogous to a
many-body state, it can represent the fixed multilinear **law** that maps
recorded operations to record probabilities. It need not be a contingent
unrecorded physical state. If a proposed model instead makes the comb's value
change as an independent physical carrier not fixed by the records and the
one law, that is the enlarged-state route and Qualification must then be
revisited explicitly.

## 3. Exact Bell construction

Take the Bell state Phi-plus and the observables

~~~text
A0 = Z,                 A1 = X,
B0 = (Z+X)/sqrt(2),     B1 = (Z-X)/sqrt(2).
~~~

For settings x,y and record outcomes a,b in {-1,+1}, the exact table is

~~~text
p(a,b|x,y) = [1 + a b E_xy]/4,
E00=E01=E10= 1/sqrt(2),
E11=          -1/sqrt(2).
~~~

Every entry is positive, every context normalizes, and every local marginal
is one half independently of the remote setting. Its CHSH value is exactly 2
sqrt(2). The runner also enumerates all sixteen deterministic local response
vertices; every vertex has absolute CHSH value 2, so every positive convex
mixture obeys the Bell-local bound.

This reproduces the original finite separation tested by the CHSH proposal
([Clauser, Horne, Shimony, and Holt, 1969](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.23.880)).
The construction proves only that a context-labelled global record law can
carry the quantum transcript table. It does not prove that a local lattice
amplitude rule generates it, nor does operational no-signalling by itself
prove relativistic microcausality.

## 4. Exact interference and coarse-graining construction

Prepare a path qubit in plus, apply no intermediate operation, then recombine
with H and write the final Z record. The output record is bright with
probability one. Insert instead a Z-path instrument and forget its outcome: a
**measure-and-forget** protocol.
The nonselective operation removes the off-diagonal terms, and the two output
records each have probability one half.

~~~text
slot omitted / identity:       p(bright,dark) = (1,0)
path measured, outcome lost:   p(bright,dark) = (1/2,1/2)
~~~

This is the bare-metal distinction the formation language must preserve:
“nothing was recorded at the intermediate slot” is not the same event as “a
record was made and later ignored.” Forgetting a readout does not unmake its
physical disturbance.

The runner also constructs a four-history decoherence matrix. The fine
amplitudes are

~~~text
(upper-to-bright, lower-to-bright, upper-to-dark, lower-to-dark)
                 = (1/2, 1/2, 1/2, -1/2).
~~~

Its matrix D=alpha alpha-dagger is Hermitian, rank one, positive semidefinite,
and normalized. Each fine diagonal is one quarter. Classical diagonal
addition would therefore assign one half to the bright output. The
off-diagonal interference term contributes the other half, giving bright
weight one and dark weight zero. Every one of the 256 labelled disjoint-event
triples satisfies the grade-two sum rule exactly.

When the path is recorded, the off-diagonal path terms are removed. The same
output coarse-graining then gives one half to each detector. Thus
coarse-graining a coherent law and first creating a path record are distinct
physical maps.

### Why quantum measure alone is not the full intervention law

A scalar quantum measure mu(A)=D(A,A) can capture interference on a fixed
closed event algebra. It does not in general retain all composition data.
The runner gives an exact two-path separator: relative phases plus i and minus
i define distinct decoherence functionals, but their quantum measures agree
on every event of the **fixed coarse event algebra**. A later fixed phase gate
followed by recombination distinguishes them perfectly, producing opposite
detector records.

Therefore **quantum measure alone** on that fixed algebra is not enough to
predict arbitrary later instrument contexts unless supplemented by a rule for
extending/composing the event algebra. The narrow result does not say that no
complete quantum-measure theory can handle interventions; a measure on a
richer complete-history space plus an exact intervention-extension rule may
do so. It says the diagonal scalar data displayed here do not determine the
needed law, while the full complex D or an equivalent process functional does.

## 5. Instruments are physical context, not observer language

For the same plus preparation, compare two instruments with the same binary Z
outcome record:

1. a Lüders path instrument, which leaves each selected path state; and
2. a measure-and-reprepare instrument, which writes the same path outcome but
   prepares plus after either result.

Both write outcome 0 or 1 with probability one half. The current outcome
record statistics are identical. Their later detector records differ: the
Lüders instrument gives four joint path/detector transcripts of weight one
quarter; the reprepare instrument always gives the bright detector, with one
half on each prior path label.

So record content must include enough physical apparatus/protocol information
to distinguish instruments when they have different future consequences. An
outcome symbol alone is not a complete state label. This is instrument
context, not a claim of a new Kochen–Specker theorem.

The original operational instrument concept was introduced by Davies and
Lewis as a joint treatment of outcome statistics and state-changing
operations
([Davies and Lewis, 1970](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01647093)). The present
finite maps are standard completely positive instrument examples; the source
does not choose this framework's physical instrument.

## 6. Projective consistency is identity containment

Classical passive stochastic processes use ordinary marginalization to delete
an observation time. Active quantum interventions require a different
containment rule. In the finite process here:

~~~text
no intermediate slot
    = larger process evaluated on the identity operation;

sum over outcomes of an inserted path measurement
    = larger process evaluated on the measurement's nonselective channel;

identity operation != nonselective path measurement.
~~~

The runner checks all three equalities and inequalities exactly. It also
extends the Bell conditional table to a joint setting-and-outcome cylinder
family with explicit uniform setting-record weights and verifies every
displayed marginal.

Milz, Sakuldee, Pollock, and Modi prove the generalized extension theorem for
processes with active interventions. Their primary result explains why the
ordinary Kolmogorov theorem fails for arbitrary interventions and replaces it
with consistency under the appropriate containment operations
([Milz et al., 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.02589)). This is the correct
comparison class for a TOE law over all finite record protocols.

Projective consistency is indispensable but not sufficient. One must still
prove existence of an infinite-lattice/global extension, causal compatibility,
normalization for every legal adaptive protocol, and agreement between the
formal identity slot and the framework's physical “nothing happened” record
condition.

## 7. Record sufficiency and the hidden-state test

Take plus and minus path preparations. Under the same identity/recombination
future, one gives bright with certainty and the other dark with certainty. If
the current record decoder maps both preparations to the same “open” record
configuration, equal purported states have unequal future laws. The state
definition is then not predictively sufficient for this exact process.

There are three honest closures:

1. retain a preparation/phase/reference record, so the complete permanent
   record configurations differ;
2. prove the two descriptions are future-operationally equivalent under every
   legal record protocol; or
3. if neither works, enlarge the physical state type explicitly.

The condition connecting the first two is **record-fibre future-equivalence**:

~~~text
same complete record configuration
    implies
same conditional law for every legal future record protocol.
~~~

This is the same strong-lumpability obligation identified in earlier probes.
It is a theorem obligation on the selected law, not wording that can be
assumed from “state.” Because records are permanent, preparation and context
records can in principle remain in the configuration rather than requiring a
hidden memory carrier. The finite model therefore supplies a constructive
zero-state-edit route.

If an exact local model instead needs a contingent phase, environment, QCA
tape, or process tensor value that is neither a permanent record nor uniquely
recoverable from the records and fixed law, the route fails this test. At that
point Qualification, not Record, is the surface that must change.

## 9. Consequences for bare-metal axiom language

The science constraints on any future sentence are now sharper.

The weak phrase

> complete histories have weights

is unsafe. Positive classical weights do not encode interference, and a
context-labelled probability table does not tell the theory how protocols
compose. “Measure and forget” would also be liable to collapse into “nothing
happened.”

The exact mathematical phrase

> there is one fixed normalized positive process functional on admissible
> record protocols, consistent under identity containment

is sufficient in type for the finite physics, but it is not yet axiom-style
bare-metal language and does not state the local construction.

The shortest bare-metal target worth further stress-testing is:

> **There is one fixed composition law on admissible complete histories. Its
> restriction to every finite record context is normalized and consistent.**

This is a target string, not an edit. It needs a canonical definition saying
that “composition law” means the positive complex/process object above and
that “restriction” means identity containment, not erasing a real
intervention. Without that definition it is too vague to carry the physics.
It also chooses a global-law architecture and therefore cannot be called
forced while the local-amplitude derivation route remains open.

A more local target—“the fixed nearest-neighbor rule determines the relative
amplitudes of admissible continuations”—would be stronger and more TOE-like if
derived. Today it smuggles an amplitude codomain, composition rule,
normalization, continuation semantics, and perhaps a time ordering into an
axiom presently limited to availability. It is not licensed by these finite
constructions.

The placement gate is therefore:

~~~text
global D/process functional adopted as irreducible law
    -> separate Law placement; keep Qualification and Record unchanged;

local amplitude/process law uniquely derived from a completed Admissibility
    -> theorem or carefully retyped Admissibility; keep Qualification if
       record-fibre future-equivalence is proved;
~~~

No branch supports adding a second witness, read, or clock as the fundamental
