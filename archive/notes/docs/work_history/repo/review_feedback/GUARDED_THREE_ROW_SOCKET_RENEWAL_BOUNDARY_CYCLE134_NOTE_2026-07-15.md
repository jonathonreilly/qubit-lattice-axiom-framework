## Bare-metal meaning

The failure is informative at the mechanism level. A final snapshot can make
a local rule look unique even when an earlier partial history presents the
same neighbourhood at a different future record site. Append-only storage
turns that transient alias into a permanent error. A usable socket therefore
needs causal provenance in its enabling neighbourhood, not merely a label
that is fresh at the end.

Nothing in this result requires an observer, a global storage manager, or a
clock to lock a record. Ordinary local append history supplies the
counterexample. It also does not answer why an enabled append occurs, how
often it occurs, or how alternatives are weighted.
