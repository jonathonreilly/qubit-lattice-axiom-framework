## Question

be owned by an exact law or by boundary/state data, do the current four axioms
nevertheless force either of these matter-lane choices?

1. Are a conjugate pair one physical outcome cell or two physical elementary

## Bounded Result

No matter-specific axiom addition is justified by these probes.

The positive theorem is narrower and cleaner:

> Once an exact law identifies its elementary physical event/effect algebra,
> its operational equivalence relation, and a normalized finitely additive
> weight with equal weight on a law-symmetric atom orbit, the weight of a
> coarse event is its physical atom count divided by the orbit's total atom
> count.

That theorem does not identify the atoms. Two presentation-neutral exact local
laws fit the same current constitutional envelope:

- a **tied** law has physical atoms `{s,d}` and equal weight `1/2,1/2`;
- an **untied** law has physical atoms `{s,d_plus,d_minus}`, with conjugation
  exchanging the last two, and equal weight `1/3,1/3,1/3`.

The companion runner realizes both as normalized positive `M2(C)` instruments
on the same maximally mixed input. The tied law is a two-effect PVM. The untied
law is a genuine three-effect trine POVM; complex conjugation fixes the
singlet effect and swaps the two distinct conjugate effects. Both can be
installed identically at every lattice site, so neither refers to a spatial
direction and both are translation- and proper-cubic compatible. This is a
constructive counterexample to the claim that convention-freedom alone turns a
conjugate pair into one physical event.

For the one-actual-output check, the runner also gives each rational instrument
a deterministic finite seed dilation. The universal selector maps a supplied
seed residue to one outcome, then the same append-once interface writes that
outcome and rejects every overwrite. Complete seed periods reproduce the exact
`1/2` and `1/3` event frequencies, while the actual seed is visibly
boundary/state-owned. This is a compatibility witness, not a proposal that a
hidden residue counter is the universe's law.

The same split persists after finite additivity. Additivity determines weights
*on a supplied event algebra*; it does not select the algebra, the operational
quotient, or equal elementary weight. Nonuniform normalized additive measures
also exist on both finite algebras. The current Record additivity clause is
even farther away: it adds scalar readouts over disjoint records after they
exist, not formation propensities over candidate outcomes.

Under the explicitly supplied formation-energy bridge

```text
r = (1-w)/(2w),
```

and the separately supplied mass-map reading

```text
Q = (1+2r)/3,
```

the tied law gives `w=1/2`, `r=1/2`, and `Q=2/3`; the untied law gives
`w=1/3`, `r=1`, and `Q=1`. These are exact conditional consequences, not a
constitutional selection. Without the energy bridge, formation weights do not
define `r`; without the spectral-to-mass bridge and its positivity and
nondegeneracy conditions, `r` does not define the charged-lepton mass claim.

Proper-cubic covariance also does not choose a hand. If a handed local law is
represented by a frame `V` with `det(V)=+1`, multiplication by an improper
cubic mirror `P` gives a second law `PV` with `det(PV)=-1`. For every proper
cubic rotation `R`, `PRP^{-1}` remains proper, so the mirror law satisfies the
same covariance equation. No pair of proper rotations can relate the two
frames because determinant sign is invariant. The actual six-neighbor set is
unchanged by both.

The concrete constructions reinforce rather than evade that result:

- the naive six-neighbor `sin(k)` Weyl derivative has eight corners, four of
  each chirality;
- the Cycle 8 exact cubic repair carries all six axis orders, three even and
  three odd, while its symmetric clock mixer commutes with the parity-swapping
  mirror and does not choose one parity sector; and
- the domain-wall construction obtains a locally chiral light mode only after
  a mass profile is imposed, and the periodic construction also carries an
  opposite-handed anti-wall.

Thus a chiral exact law may own its mirror-odd sign, or an achiral law may have
a chiral realized domain owned by boundary/state data. Proper rotations alone
force neither.

### Site-net equivalence update

The later record-faithful classification makes the counting disposition more
exact. Cubic adjacency admits `48` signed-coordinate point maps, while the
supplied proper cubic action contains only the `24` determinant-`+1` maps.
Therefore the current supplied spatial symmetry has two handed presentation
orbits. Adding an improper map would merge them only if the physical-
equivalence definition admits that map and transports the complete law,
records, readout, and boundary. Complex-linear onsite recoding likewise does
not silently add antiunitary conjugation.

Consequently, “one ticket” means one orbit of the *complete record-faithful
physical equivalence*, not one coordinate, spelling, mirror drawing, or
algebraic conjugation symbol. Proper rotations alone leave mirror alternatives
distinct; a proved reflection/antiunitary equivalence can merge them; and a
chirality-sensitive record protocol separates them. This is a forced
downstream classification once the exact law and equivalence category exist,
not an owner ruling and not generic Record content.

This is not a universal no-go. A future uniquely derived exact law could
identify the conjugate outcomes operationally, supply the additive measure,
couple that measure to energy, and choose or dynamically generate a chiral
sector. The bounded finding is that none of those steps follows merely from
the present four axioms, presentation neutrality, or proper-cubic covariance.

### Why both branches are presentation-neutral

Presentation neutrality is invariance under changing names or redundant
coordinates while preserving the law's operational equivalence relation. It
is not a rule for choosing that equivalence relation.

For the tied law, presentations `d@plus` and `d@minus` map to the same physical
event `d`; duplicating either coordinate adds no atom. For the untied law, the
two effects are operationally distinct because they can write distinct record
contents, while K exchanges them. Relabeling all three events together with
their effects and record decoder changes no prediction. The unordered set is
K-stable even though its members remain distinct.

This is the same distinction as “one coordinate written twice” versus “two
mutually exclusive detector clicks.” Presentation neutrality removes the
first duplication. It cannot declare the second one unreal without physical
information about the instrument.

### The finite-additivity theorem and its missing premise

Let `A` be a finite set of physical elementary events supplied by the exact
law. Let `mu` be normalized and finitely additive on disjoint subsets of `A`.
If a declared physical symmetry acts transitively on `A`, then every atom has
one weight `c`. Induction gives

```text
mu(E) = |E| c,
mu(A) = |A| c = 1,
mu(E) = |E|/|A|.
```

The proof is exact. The hypotheses do the physical work. The present axioms do
not identify `A`, do not state a symmetry transitive between the singlet and
doublet cells, and do not extend post-formation readout additivity into a
formation measure. K symmetry only forces equality of `d_plus` and `d_minus`;
it leaves the singlet-to-doublet-total ratio free.

## Chirality: Law Sign Versus Realized Domain

Three different statements must stay separate:

1. **Law chirality:** the exact local rule contains a reflection-odd structure
   or chooses one member of a mirror pair. Its sign is law-owned.
2. **State chirality:** a reflection-even law admits mirror-related domains and
   the realized boundary/history occupies one. The actual domain is
   boundary/state-owned.
3. **Effective chiral mode:** a supplied domain wall or low-energy sector has a
   one-handed mode. This is conditionally derived from the law plus the domain
   and does not by itself establish a globally undoubled chiral theory.

The current Lattice axiom includes proper cubic rotations, not reflections.
That is enough to classify handed pairs but not to identify them. Adding full
reflection covariance would force an achiral law class; it still would not
select an observed hand. Adding one hand to the axiom would instead hard-code
matter content before the exact law has derived it. Neither move is licensed
by the present probes.

### N2 — Wall-independence audit

Four independent walls were kept separate:

1. physical atom identity/operational equivalence;
2. normalized formation measure and equal-atom premise;
3. formation-weight-to-energy and spectral-to-mass bridges; and
4. law chirality versus realized-domain chirality and global doubling.

Closing one does not close the others. In particular, tying the conjugate pair
does not supply an energy map, and a chiral domain does not prove an undoubled
chiral bulk law.

select the tied count or one hand. This note does not claim that no exact local
