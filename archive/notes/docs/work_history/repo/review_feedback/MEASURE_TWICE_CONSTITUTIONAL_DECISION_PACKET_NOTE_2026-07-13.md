## Recommendation

**Do not freeze axiom text yet.** First derive or supply an exact predictive
specification—or prove an exact physical-equivalence class—for the
already-postulated fixed rule. This is a conservative governance
recommendation, not a theorem that drafting cannot improve before a complete
TOE framework exists.

The campaign has run far enough to identify the next exact obligation and to stop
drafting extra foundation prose around it. The blocker is not witness count,
reading, a clock, probability, or a storage slogan. It is that the framework
names one fixed nearest-neighbor rule but does not yet state or derive the
predictive content of that rule.

Two distinct label-neutral, translation/cubic-covariant neighbor rules can
satisfy the present structural description and return different availability
answers. This proves structural underdetermination at the availability level;
it does not by itself prove different observable record statistics, because
formation, actuality, statistics, and operational equivalence remain open. A
predictive TOE must derive or supply an exact predictive specification, or
prove an exact physical-equivalence class of remaining representatives.

The final English cannot be locked before that object exists. Otherwise words
such as `lawful continuation`, `available`, `forms`, and `permanent` merely
hide the missing equation/table/constraint.

## What Is Now Required

The next unavoidable **obligation** is:

> An exact predictive specification—or a theorem deriving one—sufficient to
> determine every claimed observable up to a stated physical equivalence.

This is one constitutional identification, not a proof that carrier, state,
context, dynamics, actuality, statistics, and boundary data are one physical
atom. The completeness contract tracks those entries separately and records
which are supplied, derived, or contingent.

That object need not be printed inside the short axiom paragraphs. The clean
architecture is:

```text
short constitution
    -> exact stable reference
canonical mathematical law specification
    -> theorem packets
operational, time, matter, resource/gravity, and boundary interfaces.
```

If the law is supplied rather than derived, the stable reference must make it
part of the `minimal_axioms` foundation. Calling it a downstream assumption
would leave the TOE constitution incomplete.

One further **candidate semantic condition** may be needed if the word
`permanent` is intended to mean site-tagged immutable extension:

> Every lawful continuation preserves every existing record at the same site
> with the same content.

This is not already forced by the word alone: a permanent fact could migrate
or be re-encoded. If the predictive specification proves site-tagged
preservation, the condition belongs in a theorem. Together with transitive
continuation and one-record-per-site uniqueness it proves record-level
nonreconnection. Until site/carrier identity is derived or selected, a
separate continuation clause is not yet proved either necessary or redundant.

## The Smallest Eventual Constitutional Shape

This is a **logical skeleton**, deliberately not polished target prose.

### Admissibility / law reference

The eventual Admissibility section must do exactly two jobs:

1. identify an exact predictive specification, or exact physical-equivalence
   class, by a stable reference; and
2. state only the irreducible plain-language interface needed to read that
   law: complete state/context in, lawful local answer/continuations out,
   covariantly and locally.

The specification—not the prose—must carry the extensional table/equation,
generated carrier, context type, concurrency, formation support, and coherent
between-record behavior.

### Record / invariant meaning

The eventual Record section needs no witness, read, clock, rate, probability,
or branch-count clause on present evidence. One candidate change is to make a
site-tagged reading of `permanent` extensional:

```text
every lawful continuation preserves the record at its site with the same
content.
```

If that is proved from the predictive specification, the edit can disappear.
If permanent content is instead permitted to migrate or be re-encoded, the
public wording must track physical record identity rather than a fixed site.

### Qubit / composition fallback

If the canonical law is defined on the generated quasilocal qubit algebra, no
separate Qubit addition is needed. Otherwise the only surviving Qubit
candidate is the generatedness atom:

```text
finite joint possibility domains are generated by their one-site domains.
```

Its exact embedding language belongs in the law/specification, not necessarily
in the public sentence.

### Qualification

Keep `A state is a configuration of records.` while the four state-sufficiency
theorems are attempted. The phrase `exactly one answer` must ultimately be
typed so that one answer may be a complete successor set/kernel/history
constraint; it must not silently mean one selected branch unless the law is
single-valued.
