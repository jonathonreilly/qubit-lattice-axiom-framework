## 1. What The Campaign Has Removed From The Proposed Cut

### Continuation support

The earlier Admissibility draft said that the rule determines lawful
continuations and that every locally available possibility appears in some
record-forming continuation. Exact append and instrument models prove this can
be a theorem of a selected law. Other exact laws give different support or
weights. The sentence is a useful acceptance test, not the missing law value.

### Strong site-tagged permanence

The earlier Record draft expanded permanence into preservation at the same
site and with the same content under every lawful continuation. Append-only
laws derive that property. A moving logical apparatus repeats indefinitely
without moving any record. The current Record axiom already states permanence;
a selected law must prove compatibility with it. An expanded sentence is
needed only if the eventual law exposes a real identity/location ambiguity.

### Witnesses and readers

Copies can certify readability or robustness after an event, but reversible
correlations can be erased and do not select an outcome, weight, or occurrence
time. A read is another physical interaction unless the exact law proves it is
the commit. Neither is forced across the tested routes.

### Clock language

A law-generated commit chain has a schedule-independent relational clock given
by its count. The clock follows event identity; it does not cause a channel to
become one actual event. Rate and tensor response remain law fields.

Cycle 46 supplies a positive conditional reduction of the metric part. If the
selected law proves that its continuum record order is a distinguishing
Lorentzian causal order and that record number converges to a fixed uniform
spacetime-volume density, causal rigidity fixes the conformal class and the
density fixes the conformal factor. The selected law must additionally derive
how the density is calibrated to the existing scale reference; the primitive
does not choose that calibration. Thus neither clock-rate nor metric prose is
a separate axiom atom on that route. Causal faithfulness and volume
faithfulness are law-specific continuum theorems, and reconstructing a metric
still does not derive gravity dynamics.

### Probability and actuality prose

Operational equivalence, recorded randomization, coarse-graining, and a full
effect repertoire can derive the trace/Born representation after a normalized
record law exists. Certified-corpus frequencies reduce to a component-mean
condition. Record and the realized-state primitive supply actual records and
pointwise reference to one law-admissible realized state. They do not by
themselves supply a complete realized history.

For a law on complete record histories, the actual history must be uniquely
derived from the law and boundary, realized under law-owned one-outcome
dynamics, reconstructed from records by a proved state-to-history theorem, or
supplied explicitly as contingent world-history data. In the second route the
one-outcome mechanism is law content, while the realized outcomes remain world
data. A normalized measure alone selects no member. An algorithm that merely
implements the same pushforward measure is representational unless its steps
are themselves record-distinguishing physical events.

Because these routes are architecture-dependent, no separate generic Born,
prepared-state, sampler, or actuality axiom is forced within the declared
