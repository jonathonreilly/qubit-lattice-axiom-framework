## Purpose

If the exact-law gate eventually closes, the owner wants one synchronized
foundation cut rather than a sequence of provisional edits. The present axiom
text has many verbatim consumers. This map separates the files that must agree
constitutionally from historical evidence that must remain historical.

It is not a mechanical replacement list. An exact-string match can mean a
canonical premise, a live premise firewall, a theorem quoting its historical
input, or a generated audit result. Those have different treatments.

## Four Treatment Classes

### 1. Canonical synchronized sources

These define the live foundation and must agree exactly after the cut. A stale
registry summary or controlled-vocabulary definition would create two public
foundations even if the axiom memo itself were correct.

### 2. Live theorem and guard needles

Several runners deliberately assert that current Admissibility supplies
availability but no update law. If a canonical exact law becomes foundation
content, those guards must be reclassified one by one:

- a runner whose theorem is historical should pin the old premise and remain
  unchanged except for explicit historical labeling;
- a live theorem may consume the new law only after its dependency and claim
  scope are updated; and
- a no-laundering guard should continue to reject any content not actually
  present in the exact referent.

Blind replacement would turn old non-entailment tests into false derivations.

### 3. Current explanatory notes

Some current notes say Admissibility is not dynamics or list formation/update
as wholly outside the axioms. They need a semantic review, not an automatic
word edit. After an exact referent lands, the correct statement may be:

```text
the short Admissibility prose delegates exact dynamics to <referent>;
only the fields actually filled by that referent are foundation content;
all remaining bridges stay conditional/open.
```

### 4. Historical evidence and audit-owned outputs

Historical notes should retain the old words they analyzed. Add current versus
historical labels or supersession links where ambiguity matters; do not rewrite
their premise after the fact.

The audit ledger, premise-hash results, and independent audit verdicts are
audit-owned or generated surfaces. A foundation edit may invalidate them by
the established hash/reset mechanism. This local exercise must not hand-edit
them to manufacture a passing status or verdict.

## Outcome-Specific Mutation Plan

### Zero-update uniqueness theorem

If the existing four axioms uniquely derive the exact law, do not edit the
axiom prose. Land the theorem through the ordinary science/review process and
update only current explanatory surfaces that previously called the route
open. Audit status remains independent.

### Retyped Admissibility/local-law identification

If a supplied complete law is itself a local continuation object whose
availability projection is the current menu rule, retype Admissibility and
identify that object there. Synchronize all six canonical surfaces above.
Retire or rewrite the explicit no-dynamics firewall and reclassify every
availability-only guard according to whether it is historical or live.

### Separate Law identification

If the supplied object is a global-history action or constraint not identical
to the menu map, retain Admissibility and add a named Law axiom. This route must
also update every exact-four and exact-name-list consumer, including the 35-file
baseline above. The scientific addition is still one exact referent; the extra
name is a type-safe placement choice.

### Exact law plus Record clarification

Only if the selected law fails to prove the intended record identity/content
invariant, add the owner-polished Record clarification in the same cut. Then
update every exact Record needle and the policy entry together. Do not add
witness, reader, clock, counting, or gravity prose as collateral content.
