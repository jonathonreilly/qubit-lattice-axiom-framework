## Three interfaces that must not be conflated

### I1 — fixed selected output writer

One particular grown source/status history binds one designated physical
output.  This is the smallest interface.  Cycle 109 already supplies the
bounded positive: its lawful fixed-`H1` status writes one directed `R_B11`,
while its `H0` branch was originally only an injected reject control.  Cycle
114 then supplies a lawful `H0` history reaching that exact reference and
reject chain, although a schedule race selects between its `H1` and `H0`
terminals.

This is a real fixed writer and a real lawful alternate-value existence
result.  It is not yet a selectable address bus.

### I2 — relational-position-indexed fixed reference bank/stream

One fixed source grows several ordered bit records at stable relative
positions.  Cycle 112 proves this **layout half** exactly.  The integer
coordinates used by the runner are audit labels; the physical positions are
distinguished by the grown apparatus's local relational geometry, not by
privileged lattice coordinates.

Its writer and completion rows grow the full ordered word, complete the exact
status front, and exhaust to the pinned terminal under the tested asynchronous
append semantics.  The fixed word contains both `H0` and `H1`, so both values
are physically present at stable positions.

What is absent is the other half of a physical fixed-bank reader: no grown
address/pointer record selects one of those positions, and no decoder or fixed
wiring carries the selected record to a common output port.  Calling the bare
layout “addressable” is therefore legitimate only in the passive descriptive
sense that its relative positions are distinct.  The executable construction
does not yet establish operational positional readout.

### I3 — candidate/program-selectable addressable stream

A genuine candidate-selected port requires all of the following at the
claimed resolution:

1. at least two lawfully grown candidate/address histories, not injected
   faults or supplied word substitutions;
2. one common logical output port whose recorded value differs between those
   candidate histories under one output-single-valued covariant law;
3. a candidate/address record in the causal ancestry of that differing
   output;
4. schedule-independent exhaustion for each candidate history; and
5. for a 236-program claim, coverage of all 236 program identities together
   with the physical program/reference/output association source.

Cycle 112 has one fixed source word, not two candidate-selected sources.
Cycle 114 has two lawful terminal values at one reference, but the relative
append order chooses them and no address record does.  Combining those facts
does not manufacture I3.

The narrow open object is therefore
`CANDIDATE_SELECTED_COMMON_REFERENCE_PORT`, not “addressability” without a
qualifier and not a universal claim that selection cannot be built.
