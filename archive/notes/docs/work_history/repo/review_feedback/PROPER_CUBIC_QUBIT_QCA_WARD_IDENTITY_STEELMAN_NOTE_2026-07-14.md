## 1. Primitive Scalar Transport

Take the most direct homogeneous, number-preserving, radius-one scalar update
on a cubic lattice,

```text
(U psi)(x) = a psi(x) + b sum_{|e|=1} psi(x+e).
```

Proper-cubic symmetry gives one neighbor coefficient `b`. Its Fourier symbol
is

```text
u(k) = a + 2 b (cos k_x + cos k_y + cos k_z).
```

For unitary one-particle evolution, `|u(k)|=1` for every `k`. At the four cubic
momenta where the cosine sum is `3,1,-1,-3`, write

```text
A=|a|^2,  B=|b|^2,  C=Re(a conjugate(b)).
```

The four equations are

```text
A + 4 s^2 B + 4 s C = 1,    s in {3,1,-1,-3}.
```

Their unique solution is

```text
A=1, B=0, C=0.
```

The neighbor amplitude vanishes. The remaining law is an onsite unit phase.
This is an exact no-transport theorem only for the displayed scalar,
linear, number-preserving, radius-one convolution class. It does not cover
multi-component, interacting, partitioned, nonlinear, or larger-radius QCA.

Its value is diagnostic: a moving isotropic unit needs internal routing
structure or a less direct update. Transport is not obtained for free merely
from `Z^3`, one scalar amplitude, and cubic symmetry.

## 2. Six-Direction QCA

The minimal constructive repair labels six internal modes by

```text
+x, -x, +y, -y, +z, -z.
```

Each mode streams one macrocell in its labelled direction. The 24 proper cubic
rotations permute these modes and the lattice positions together, so the
streaming step is homogeneous and exactly proper-cubic covariant.

Let `J` reverse every direction and let `1` denote the six-component all-one
vector. The direction representation decomposes with projectors

```text
P_0 = (1 1^T)/6,                 rank 1,
P_E = (I+J)/2 - P_0,             rank 2,
P_V = (I-J)/2,                   rank 3.
```

The exact linear commutant of all 24 direction permutations has dimension
three. Every unitary collision in this diagonal family is

```text
C(theta_E,theta_V)
  = P_0 + exp(i theta_E) P_E + exp(i theta_V) P_V,
```

after fixing the overall phase on `P_0`. Two invariant phase ratios remain.
For example,

```text
C(0,0)       = I,
C(0,pi)      = J,
C(pi/3,pi/2) = complex cubic mixing.
```

All three are exactly unitary and commute with every proper-cubic rotation.
The first passes a `+x` mode forward; the second sends it backward. The third
mixes the invariant sectors. Homogeneity, locality, covariance, and the
number Ward identity do not select between them.

The one-particle collision lifts to a fermionic number-preserving unitary on
the six-mode Fock space. Under the direct occupation encoding that space has
dimension `2^6`, so a macrocell needs six primitive qubits. This is a valid
generated-block embedding candidate, not a derivation of the block from a
single primitive site. In particular, this probe does not establish:

- a finite-depth nearest-neighbor compilation on primitive cubic sites;
- absence of Jordan-Wigner or fermionic-parity locality costs;
- a canonical placement of six internal modes in a scalar lattice;

Those are live theorem targets, not defects hidden under the word “QCA.”

## 3. Exact Ward Identity And Event Occurrence

On a reservoir qubit and outgoing-rail qubit, use the occupation-preserving

```text

U(theta)|01> = cos(theta)|01> - sin(theta)|10>,
```

```text
U(theta)^dagger U(theta)=I,
U(theta)^dagger N U(theta)=N,
N=n_reservoir+n_rail.
```

```text

```


Only the last is full unit transfer. More strongly,

## 4. Orientation And Actual-Sector Semantics

The six-direction collision above is reflection-even, so its complex phase

Let `V` be the three-dimensional vector representation of the cubic group and

```text

```

```text

|p> = sum_abc epsilon_abc |abc>/sqrt(6).
```

Every proper cubic rotation fixes both vectors. A coordinate reflection fixes
`|s>` and reverses `|p>`. Therefore

```text

U_plus  = exp(-i kappa H_chi),
U_minus = exp(+i kappa H_chi)
```

gives two distinct unitary local collisions that commute with every proper
cubic rotation, while reflection exchanges them exactly. Either collision can
be direct-producted with the streaming law. Proper-cubic covariance therefore
permits the spatial-mirror pair and does not select it.

This orientation witness costs a `36`-state internal block, hence six qubits
with `28` unused basis states under direct binary embedding. It is evidence of

Independently, every six-direction collision with phases

the indispensable atom must concern occurrence/commit or actualization; its
conservation and downstream reuse should be derived where possible.

This is a statement about what the **exact law referent must contain**, not a

The generated-block QCA remains a useful candidate mechanism to test against
that atom. It should not be promoted merely because it can implement the
