five-record configurations. Calling those configurations one complete
32-codeword joint alphabet is nevertheless conditional on **generated finite
composition**, or on an operational theorem with the same force.

possibilities. It does not by itself prove that five disjoint local systems

1. all 32 host-specified five-bit record configurations have exact bounded

## Why five rails

A tempting compact layout places several payload lanes around one recurrent
rail and shares guide records. That reduces record cost, but a shared guide's
formation can inherit more than one bit lane. The resulting causal graph may
couple otherwise independent bits even when the guide's output label is
constant.

Cycle 178 instead chooses independence first:

- five finite Cycle-144 source boundaries;
- five recurrent rails and physical root sidecars;
- five guide heads and rear stops;
- five payload chains; and
- one common 288-row binary carrier law.

The cost is exact:

This is not claimed minimal. It is the first clean orthogonalization baseline
