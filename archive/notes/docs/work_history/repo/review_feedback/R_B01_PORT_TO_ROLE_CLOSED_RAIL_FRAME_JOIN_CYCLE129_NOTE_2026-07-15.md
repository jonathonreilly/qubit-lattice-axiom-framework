## Bare-metal meaning

The executable mechanism is smaller than the earlier verbal pictures:

1. a completed physical word exposes a new role port;
2. local phase records carry that role through an append-only causal path;
3. a provenance guard prevents an old context from impersonating the new
   path; and
4. one ordinary record joins that path to an independently renewable frame.

No later observer reads the old word into existence. No clock tick locks it.
No global storage counter approves the join. Completion, provenance, and
frame attachment are all represented by local records changing what may form
next.

This materially supports the “head/frame separation” bare-metal model: finite
word-specific work can terminate at a reusable structural rail instead of
rebuilding all scaffolding from scratch. It does **not** show why an available
append actually occurs, how often it occurs, how alternatives are weighted,
or why this candidate row union is Nature's law. It also does not yet show
that the 16-row bridge translates and repeats. Therefore this is **not a
recurrence theorem**, and the construction does not select the exact law.
**No axiom addition follows** from Cycle 129.
