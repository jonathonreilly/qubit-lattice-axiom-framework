## Result up front

Cycle 244 finds an exact finite-domain obstruction to the most direct local
decoder contract, but not a general measurement-preparation no-go.
The three explicit probes are **linear convolution**, a **retained local sign
frame**, and a **majority cellular decoder**.

Let `H` be the `11L^3 x 15L^3` binary matrix taking a face-`Z` correction `z`
to the measured modified-Gauss sign syndrome `s=Hz`.  A decoder on the lawful
syndrome quotient must satisfy

```text
H D H = H.
```

The runner searches translation-convolution kernels with `L1` radius `R`.
Exact sections first appear at

| Periodic size | First successful held radius | Kernel coefficients set to 1 | Disposition |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `L=3` | 2 | 186 | exact `H D H=H` |
| `L=4` | none | — | inconsistent even with all 64 periodic displacements |
| `L=5` | 4 | 794 | exact `H D H=H` |
| `L=7` | 6 | 1,902 | exact held-out section |

The selected `L=3` Gaussian kernel fails raw proper-cubic covariance in 23 of
24 frames.  That failure characterizes the selected solution, not all odd-size
solutions.  More decisively, `L=4` has no translation-convolution
linear solution even when the kernel contains every displacement: 10,560
variables become inconsistent after exact GF(2) elimination.

The linear miss reflects a stronger, representation-independent finite
witness.  At every even held size, the syndrome with the first coarse-edge
check bit set in every cell is lawful and translation invariant, but no
translation-invariant face correction maps to it.  The all-axis version, with
all three coarse-edge check bits set in every cell, is additionally invariant
under all 24 proper-cubic frames.  It is lawful and has a global correction,
but no correction fixed by translations.

Therefore, on the declared even periodic code and the domain of **every**
lawful syndrome:

> No deterministic translation-equivariant function `D(s)` can satisfy
> `H D(s)=s` for every lawful `s`.

The proof is one line once the exhaustive finite witness is established.  An
equivariant function must map a translation-fixed input to a translation-fixed
output; the witness has no such output.  This covers nonlinear functions and
unbounded support on that finite torus, not only convolution kernels.

The exact scope matters.  This does **not** exclude:

- odd-only periodic families, where exact translation-linear sections exist;
- a stochastic or measurement branch that spontaneously selects a
  non-translation-invariant correction;
- an autonomous coherent gauge field retaining a superposition of frames;
- a supplied face sign field;
- a marked origin or global decoder;
- an open boundary, puncture, or reservoir; or
- a decoder required only on a restricted syndrome basin.

Accordingly there is no route-independent obstruction and no axiom pressure.

## 2. Retained local sign-frame route

One might avoid a physical correction by retaining measured check signs as a
local frame.  That works only if the update can consume enough frame data.

For any supplied `z` with `Hz=s`, define

```text
E_z = Z(z) E_0,
G_z = Z(z) G_0 Z(z).
```

Then the branch-dependent intertwining is exact:

```text
E_z G = G_z E_z.
```

The required signs are local once `z` is stored on faces.  Contact is diagonal
in the occupation/flux generators and needs no sign.  The coin needs the
incident face signs, and an `A/B` FSWAP needs the sign of its hopping face.
Thus a **supplied face sign frame** is a constructive constant-overhead route.

The check syndrome `s` alone is insufficient.  A weight-five cell-flux mask
`k=B_v` satisfies `Hk=0`, so `z` and `z+k` have the same local syndrome.  Yet
`k` flips every incident hopping generator.  The executable consumer audit
finds:

| Consumer | Residual under one-mode gauge flip | Consequence |
|---|---:|---|
| Cycle-219 Fock coin | `10.6058660963` | sign frame required |
| two-mode FSWAP | `2.82842712475` | hopping sign required |
| Cycle-230 contact | `0` | syndrome/sign independent |

This is a logical-intertwining ambiguity, not code leakage.  Both sign choices
preserve the local constraints.  They implement different logical coin/stream
actions unless a face frame or equivalent dynamical gauge variable is fixed.

The route therefore separates two questions:

1. **consumer locality:** closed once local face signs `z` are supplied; and
2. **frame formation:** still the decoder/section problem `Hz=s`.

Calling the check bits themselves a complete sign frame would conflate these
questions.

## 9. Prior-art and novelty boundary

Tommaso Guaita, “On the locality of qubit encodings of local fermionic modes,”
*Quantum* **9**, 1644 (2025),
<https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2025-02-25-1644>, proves a local-unitary preparation
depth result under product-state/two-body-unitary hypotheses and explicitly
leaves measurements with adaptive sign reassignment open.  Cycle 244 does not
reuse that unitary theorem as a measurement-decoder theorem.

Ruby Wei, Aqua Chung, Luke Coffman, Su-Kuan Chu, and Xun Gao,
“High-Distance Error-Correcting Codes for Fermion-to-Qubit Mappings in 2D and
3D,” <https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.00147> (2025), concatenate the Chen-Kapustin
3D map with fermionic color-code blocks.  Their construction establishes
locality preservation at fixed code distance and constant stabilizer weights,
while its generators explicitly span the even fermionic algebra.  It also
keeps periodic nontrivial `Z` membranes as sector-changing logical operators.
The paper says mapping signs may be handled by classical syndrome-sign
redefinition, but does not provide the exact bounded equivariant sign section
tested here.
Their decoder is proposed for stochastic noise: the threshold discussion
leaves rigorous proof and numerics to future work, assumes perfect
measurements for that argument, and states that errors within the fermionic
algebra still require multiple stabilizer-measurement rounds.  It is therefore
direct evidence that high distance and local constant-weight checks coexist,
but it is not an exact bounded-radius all-syndrome decoder for the Cycle-235
fixture and is not used to prove the even-torus witness.

Cycle 235 supplies the exact square-pyramid even code.  Cycle 240 supplies the
30-subround local measurement layer, deterministic global Gaussian decoder,
and Wilson correction audit.  Cycle 244 adds the translation-convolution
quotient search, the even-torus fixed-syndrome section witness, the sign-frame
consumer split, the all-24 majority CA, and the exact separated-loop radius
