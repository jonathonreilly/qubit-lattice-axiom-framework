# Six-direction-qubit lane archive

The emergent-electron research route using six direction-labelled qubits per site is retired from the active campaign queue. This archive preserves its exact source, exploration and review evidence, including the later proposed supersession wording before correction. `MANIFEST.json` records the source commits, byte counts, SHA-256 hashes and recovery paths. Snapshot extensions are `.txt`; these are historical evidence, not live runners.

The canonical six-direction encoding note and paired runner remain in `docs/` and `scripts/`. Their bounded half-edge encoding, covariance and finite algebra results are not withdrawn. Historical conjectures and exploration reports do not establish spin identification, a universal exclusion of six-qubit models, or a new framework premise.

Retirement is a campaign choice. A fourth-root restriction in a specified realization is not a theorem excluding every direction-carried spinorial representation. In particular the binary lift of C4 admits one-dimensional eighth-root characters; triviality after U(1) rephasing does not exclude the Z2-valued spinorial restriction. Any obstruction needs its full localization, local-sector and phase hypotheses.

The current proposed successors are the corner-code and long-wavelength notes in the same dated campaign. They are supplied constructions with their own limitations, not a proof that qubit count is physically irrelevant. The block embedding uses frozen spectators, explicitly selected blocks, translations by five fine sites and interactions extending up to nine fine spacings; it does not derive a uniform one-qubit nearest-neighbour rule. Numerical band searches are not global-minimum certificates.

Historical C11/C13 directories remain in place for reproducibility and source links, with archive pointers. Canonical load-bearing citations remain intact. This archive is not a new backlog of PR snapshots and does not schedule work, apply an audit verdict or promote a claim.
