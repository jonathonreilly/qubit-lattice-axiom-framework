The supervising author supplied three pre-source targets, using the seven
labels 0,+/-e1,+/-e2,+/-e3 and all-label nearest-neighbor exchanges with a
fixed positive floor. For a symmetric k-label tensor S_i, the antisymmetric
edge drive is the sum over the k windows of k+1 consecutive sites containing
the edge of the value with the right endpoint omitted minus the value with
the left endpoint omitted. Rates are floor+max(h,0), or K+h/2 with fixed K
large enough. Footprint sites on a torus are distinct.

1. Derive product stationarity and exact species currents for arbitrary
   finite k, and check the hypotheses of my sealed Euler/fluctuation proofs.
2. For the symmetric degree-three tensor with product expectation
   alpha[rho(2rho-3q_i)g_i+3g_i^3], determine the full isotropic-product
   linear spectrum, nonlinear density/vector/quadrupole currents at
   q_i=rho/3, and their rotational scope.
3. Classify antisymmetric four-site h(l,a,b,r) with zero sum on every
   periodic string. Determine the decomposition of a coboundary
   G(l,a,b)-G(a,b,r), its product currents, the proper-cubic-covariant
   mean-potential space, and all-density directional isotropy in that space.
