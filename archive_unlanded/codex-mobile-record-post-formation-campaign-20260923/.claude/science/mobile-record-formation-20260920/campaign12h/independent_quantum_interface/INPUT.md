# Supplied mathematical interface

Input is one unknown physical qubit `rho_a=(I+v_a.sigma)/2`, where `v_a`
ranges over the six signed Cartesian unit vectors. There is no accessible
classical preparation label and no input-correlated ancilla. A stipulated
one-neighbor formation has classical outcome `b` with probability `p/D` for
the same content, `q/D` for the opposite content, and `r/D` for each of the
four other contents, with `D=p+q+4r` and `p,q,r>0`.

Determine exact POVM realizability, optimum worst-input-row total variation
over arbitrary six-effect qubit POVMs, and the maximal average parent
preservation fidelity over instruments and recovery where exactly realizable.
Distinguish classical outcomes from a newborn-qubit marginal when `b` is
unavailable. For two independently prepared parents, test whether the
observable supplied occurrence rate `epsilon sum_b W_ba W_bc`, with `W=6`
times the kernel, can be an ordinary quantum operation without preparation
labels.

The additional scope question allows the occurrence rate to depend on the
unknown inputs while keeping the conditional classical odds fixed. Determine
whether postselection can restore an otherwise unrealizable one-parent
kernel, and whether operational linearity restricts the rate for two or more
independent parents with product odds. Rates mean an instrument's observable
occurrence probability per unit time. A bounded necessary result suffices;
approximate multiparent optimization is not requested.
