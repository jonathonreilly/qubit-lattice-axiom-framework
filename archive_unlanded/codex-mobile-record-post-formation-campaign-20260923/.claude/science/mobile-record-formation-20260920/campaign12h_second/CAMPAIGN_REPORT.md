pursuing: reciprocal immutable record pairs can form on empty neighbors,
translate to reuse sites and exchange places through local moves. The explicit
geometric process reaches full matching density on the finite periodic models
under stated positive rates. A finite lattice has no further birth capacity
once it is full; these results do not establish unlimited new formation there.
At full density, permanent pair colors support
propagating transverse fluctuations, including while geometry moves. A conditional nonlinear limit now also controls finite-amplitude color
evolution while the matching geometry keeps moving, with arbitrary initial
matching laws and a supplied smooth interior profile.

These are results about specified mathematical models. The stochastic rates,
fourteen-color coding, clock scale and preparation assumptions are choices.

are nature's microscopic law.
