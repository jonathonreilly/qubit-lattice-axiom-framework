# Next decisions after finite formation and fast motion

Research planning, 2026-09-23. These are proposed routes and power counting,
not established continuum limits, source corrections or independent verdicts.
The exact source checkpoint is the parent CHECKPOINT.md. This memo does not
change any frozen author or independent packet.

The two completed formation descriptions answer different questions. Balanced
formation has a finite local Lindbladian and an ordinary fixed-volume rotor
limit, but its earlier fourth-order field coefficient vanishes. The higher
scale retains fourth-order field interactions and finite formation together
with an explicitly fast second-order matter generator. Its additional
pure-field identification is controlled before the first formation. The
six-site post-event calculation supplies a normalizable example of substantial
matter motion on tau=epsilon^2 u/delta, with an independently reviewable
singular-block proof. Its one-vacancy sector cannot form another pair.

The next substantive choice is the observable/state/time regime after births,
not whether to drop the fast Hamiltonian. Three routes remain distinct.

1. **Interaction-picture or averaged matter description.** For a fixed finite
   spin and graph, move to the interaction picture of delta H2/epsilon^2.
   Oscillatory terms can in principle be averaged with explicit nonzero
   frequency differences. This would yield a description in a specified
   frame or for specified slow observables. It would not show convergence of
   the original full density to the unchanged field-only law. Before reuse
   in the rotor or volume limit, the small denominators, degeneracies,
   resonances, spatial locality and preparation must be controlled. The
   six-site rotor fibers have a continuum of field-dependent fast energies,
   so a finite-matrix gap cannot simply be carried across that limit.

2. **Prepare a suitable matter band at formation.** An energy-filtered output
   f(H2) B_j is a mathematical candidate jump operator, but ordinarily changes
   the instrument and spreads its support. A physical implementation would
   need a reservoir/preparation construction, a quantified time/locality cost,
   and an energy account. It is not justified to insert the filtered jump and
   call the original local formation rule low energy. On the exact lifted
   one-vacancy path H2=-2I-(S+S^dagger), the localized output has mean -2 and
   variance 2, while the spectral infimum is -4. These follow immediately by
   applying H2 and H2^2 to the delta state. Thus that output is not concentrated
   at the fast-band bottom. A different reservoir, post-event cooling or
   controlled wave-packet preparation are constructive possibilities.

3. **A joint spatial and resource scaling.** This is only a dimensional
   diagnostic. Suppose an appropriate matter branch has a quadratic minimum
   with hopping scale h=delta/epsilon^2, lattice spacing a, and a prepared
   slowly varying envelope. Its putative kinetic coefficient scales as
   a^2 h. In the already derived weak-field lattice Hamiltonian, the field
   speed scales as a sqrt(KJ). If K/delta is fixed and J=2delta, keeping a
   nonzero field speed suggests delta proportional to 1/a. Simultaneously
   keeping a finite quadratic matter coefficient would then suggest
   a proportional to epsilon^2. With a fixed physical volume, site number
   would grow as epsilon^(-2d), and the rate per site would have to be chosen
   explicitly if a finite physical formation density is desired; a simple
   rate-density count suggests kappa proportional to a^d.

   This power counting does not establish that the actual many-record
   three-dimensional band has that dispersion or preparation, or that its
   interactions and creation outputs converge. It also leaves the original
   detuning Delta=delta epsilon^-4 divergent, here of order epsilon^-6;
   energy/fuel and number-sector offsets cannot be dismissed when births are
   live. The current fixed-graph theorem assumes fixed delta,kappa and has
   volume-dependent constants. None of its error estimates automatically
   survives this proposed scaling. A new local, energy-sensitive proof and
   control of higher virtual terms would be required. Even successful
   quadratic matter kinematics would be nonrelativistic, not a derivation of
   Lorentz-invariant particle dynamics or a TOE.

The immediate strongest test is to identify the fast-band structure and
actual formation-output spectral distribution on a larger cyclic or cubic
physical sector, then choose one of these routes based on the result.
Changing the Gauss background, Hamiltonian or reservoir is another supplied
model choice and must be named as such. Native selection of quantum dynamics,
record carriers, formation law and initial state remains a separate unresolved
premise problem. The conditional calculations do not establish that the
native axioms select this family of models.
