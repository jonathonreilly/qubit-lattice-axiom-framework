# Composition ⊥ Local Tomography for the Complex Qubit — Foundations Write-Up

**Date:** 2026-06-16
**Type:** write-up / synthesis (foundations-venue framing of two already-retained

**Status authority:** independent audit lane only. This note states no audit

## 0. Purpose and honest scope

This note packages two existing, independently-audited theorems into a single
foundations-paper-grade statement, and fixes a framing error that has circulated
in summaries. It is a **write-up**, not a derivation: both halves are exact
finite-dimensional linear algebra already verified by their own runners.

**What this is NOT (corrected framing).** It is *not* a claim that "tensor
composition collapses into / is derived from the Quantum axiom A1." No such
entailment is retained on `origin/main`. What is retained is a clean
**separation**: operational locality alone does not force the complex tensor
product, and the complex structure supplies local tomography *given* the ordinary
generated tensor carrier. The residual premise (local tomography / no-extra-global
generation) is named, not smuggled.

## 1. The result in one line

For the framework's per-site complex qubit `A_x ≅ M₂(ℂ)` (Quantum axiom):

> **Operational locality (faithful, commuting, separated local algebras /
> no-signalling) is strictly weaker than local tomography; the complex unit `i`
> supplies local tomography exactly once the ordinary generated two-site carrier
> `M₂(ℂ) ⊗_ℂ M₂(ℂ) ≅ M₄(ℂ)` is granted, while the real-rebit analogue fails.**

This instantiates, for the framework's specific site algebra, the
composition/local-tomography separation of the operational-reconstruction
literature (Hardy 2001; Barrett 2007; Masanes–Müller 2011; D'Ariano–Chiribella–
Perinotti 2017): local tomography is an independent postulate, satisfied by
complex quantum theory and violated by real/rebit theory.

## 2. The two halves (both retained on `origin/main`)

### 2a. No-go — locality does not force the tensor product `[retained_no_go]`
Source: `TENSOR_COMPOSITION_REQUIRES_LOCAL_TOMOGRAPHY_BEYOND_LOCALITY_NARROW_NO_GO_NOTE_2026-06-03.md`
(runner `scripts/audit_companion_tensor_composition_requires_local_tomography_exact.py`).

Keep the local algebra fixed at `M₂(ℂ)` and exhibit a duplicate-global-sector
composite:
```
B = M₄(ℂ) ⊕ M₄(ℂ),
A_x(a) = (a ⊗ I₂) ⊕ (a ⊗ I₂),
A_y(b) = (I₂ ⊗ b) ⊕ (I₂ ⊗ b).
```
The two local copies are faithful and commute (operational locality holds
exactly), yet their products span only the diagonal `M₄(ℂ)` (complex dim 16),
while `B_sa` has real dim 32. The central observable `Z = I₄ ⊕ (−I₄)` commutes
with all local products but is linearly independent of their span — a hidden
global sector invisible to local tomography. **Hence locality alone leaves an
exact `32 ≠ 16` gap; the ordinary tensor product is not forced.** The two
sufficient (and, in this finite commuting-matrix setting, equivalent) closing
premises are named exactly: (i) local tomography, or (ii) no-extra-global
generation (the composite is the C\*-algebra generated by the two commuting local
copies — and since `M₂(ℂ)` is nuclear and `M₄(ℂ)` simple, that generated algebra
*is* the ordinary tensor product).

### 2b. Bounded theorem — the complex structure supplies local tomography `[retained_bounded]`
Source: `LOCAL_TOMOGRAPHY_FROM_QUBIT_COMPLEX_STRUCTURE_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-06-03.md`
(runner `scripts/audit_companion_local_tomography_from_complex_structure_exact.py`),
via the carrier bridge `TWO_SITE_QUBIT_TENSOR_CARRIER_BRIDGE_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-06-06.md`
`[retained]`.

Given the ordinary generated carrier `C = A_x ⊗_ℂ A_y ≅ M₄(ℂ)`:
- For `Mₙ(ℂ)`, `dim_ℝ(Mₙ(ℂ)_sa) = n² = dim_ℂ(Mₙ(ℂ))` (Cartesian split
  `M = H₁ + iH₂`); complex dimension is multiplicative under `⊗_ℂ`, so
  `dim_ℝ((M₂(ℂ)⊗M₂(ℂ))_sa) = 16 = 4·4` — **the finite local-tomography count.**
- The two embeddings share one global unit: `(iI₂)⊗I₂ = I₂⊗(iI₂) = iI₄`; the 16
  Pauli products `σ_a⊗σ_b` form a basis — no extra finite degrees of freedom.
- **Contrast:** the real rebit gives `dim_ℝ(M₂(ℝ)_sa)² = 3·3 = 9 ≠ 10 =
  dim_ℝ(M₄(ℝ)_sa)` — the ordinary real tensor product is *not* locally tomographic.

## 3. Why the two fit together (the separation)

The no-go (2a) shows local tomography is **logically independent** of operational
locality for the complex qubit; the bounded theorem (2b) shows that **once the
generated complex carrier is granted, the global `i` makes local tomography
automatic** (and the rebit comparator shows the property is genuinely carried by
the *complex* scalar field, not by tensor products in general). Together they
isolate exactly one residual postulate and show complex quantum theory sits on the
local-tomography side of the standard reconstruction divide.

## 4. The named residual (what keeps 2b "bounded")

`local_tomography_from_qubit_complex_structure` is `retained_bounded`, not
`retained`, for a precise reason: it assumes the two-site composite **is** the
ordinary generated `M₄(ℂ)` (supplied by the carrier bridge). It does **not** derive
that carrier from {Lattice, Quantum, Record} + minimality alone — the no-go's
`Z = I₄ ⊕ (−I₄)` hidden-sector witness is exactly the obstruction that would have
to be excluded. Closing `retained_bounded → retained` (excluding the hidden global
sector from the axioms + minimality without importing the tensor surface) is a
genuine, narrow foundations sub-problem — and a prior attempt at that entailment
did not survive audit. **It is not a blocker to publishing the separation, which
is complete and exact as stated.**

## 5. What this does / does not claim

Claims: (i) a clean, exact composition⊥local-tomography separation for the complex
qubit; (ii) the rebit `9≠10` comparator; (iii) the `i`-supplies-local-tomography
half, conditional on the carrier; (iv) the residual named precisely.

Does NOT claim: composition derived from A1 alone; the hidden-sector witness
excluded by the axioms; anything about graded / fermion-parity-superselected /
interacting / continuum composites; `d = 3` (an admitted Z³ primitive, untouched
here); any numerical or empirical prediction.
