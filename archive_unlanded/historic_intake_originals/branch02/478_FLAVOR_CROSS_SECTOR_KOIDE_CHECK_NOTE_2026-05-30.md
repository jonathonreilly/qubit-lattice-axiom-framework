## Why the move-4 quark claim is wrong
1. **Direction.** A positive uniform VEV adds to the diagonal `a`, *diluting*
   `b/a = b_f/(a_VEV+a_f)` → `r` down → `Q` down toward **1/3**. So VEV-dilution can
   only put a sector *below* 2/3. The quarks are *above* 2/3 — the opposite.
2. **Category error.** "Heavy sector" was conflated with "VEV-dominated."
   VEV-dominated means *degenerate* (uniform mass); but the quarks are extremely
   *hierarchical* (one heavy + two light), the opposite of degenerate. Quark Q>2/3
   reflects that extreme hierarchy (toward the `b/a→1` RP edge), which needs
   *enhanced* off-diagonal structure, not VEV-dilution.

So **the cross-sector claim is retracted.**

- **The quarks are a separate, harder problem.** Q>2/3 requires `b/a>1/√2`
