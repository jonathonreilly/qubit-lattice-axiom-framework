## External comparator (context only — not a derivation input)

The STAR Collaboration, *"Measuring spin correlation between quarks during QCD
confinement,"* **Nature 650, 65–71 (2026)** / **arXiv:2506.05499**, reports a
**(18 ± 4)%** relative spin-polarization correlation between Λ and Λ̄ hyperons,
inherited from a spin-correlated strange quark–antiquark pair produced by the
QCD vacuum. The distinctive signature is that the correlation **decoheres with
angular separation** — it is largest when the pair emerges "together" and
vanishes once the two hyperons are well separated.

That result is used here **only as a named external comparator** to motivate the
structural questions below. No number, model, or claim from it is consumed as a
premise, matching rule, calibrator, or derivation input.

## Summary

Two of the four framework axioms make a sharp, comparator-relevant structural
commitment that has not previously been isolated in one note:

1. **Admissibility** couples sites *only* through the fixed nearest-neighbor
   rule. Two sites that are not nearest neighbors carry no supplied joint
   constraint, hence a **product** (uncorrelated) two-site state.
2. **Record** makes the *named* scalar readout **additive over disjoint
   records**. Its two-record connected cumulant therefore vanishes
   *identically*.

Together these give: **any readable two-body correlation is confined to
nearest-neighbor adjacency and is structurally absent between separated
records** — the framework's own, axiom-level "decoherence with separation,"
which is the qualitative STAR signature. The **bonded-pair arena** already
supplies the correlated substrate (the exchange singlet). But the framework's
*named* additive readout is **correlation-blind** (connected part ≡ 0), so
reproducing a *nonzero adjacent* correlation of STAR's kind is not expressible
without a **joint, non-additive** two-record functional on the adjacency arena —
which this note names as the minimal extension target, not as a result.

This is a bounded structural note. The spin labeling of the arena is stated
**conditional on a supplied spin-axis identification** exactly as the bonded-pair
arena note flags it; no dynamics, formation rate, Born weight, strangeness,

## Theorems (all exact, runner-verified)

- **T1 — the bonded-pair exchange substrate.** On the adjacent arena
  `C^2 ⊗ C^2`, `SWAP` is a symmetric involution; the exchange projectors
  `P_sym = (I+SWAP)/2`, `P_anti = (I−SWAP)/2` are complete orthogonal
  idempotents of ranks **3** and **1** (triplet ⊕ singlet). *(Re-established
  self-contained; consistent with the arena note.)*
- **T2 — spin content, conditional on a supplied spin-axis identification.**
  Under `S_i = σ_i/2` (a *downstream* identification, not axiom content), the
  two-body operator satisfies exactly `S_A·S_B = ½ SWAP − ¼ I`, with eigenvalue
  `+¼` on the symmetric (spin-1 triplet) block and `−¾` on the antisymmetric
  (spin-0 singlet) block; the total Casimir `S² = S_A² + S_B² + 2 S_A·S_B` is
  `2` (spin-1) and `0` (spin-0).
- **T3 — the singlet is a genuinely correlated pair.** On the singlet,
  `⟨S_A⟩ = 0` and the **connected** correlator
  `⟨S_A·S_B⟩ − ⟨S_A⟩·⟨S_B⟩ = −¾ ≠ 0` (maximal). This is the framework-internal
  substrate of a STAR-like spin-correlated created pair.
- **T4 — product states are uncorrelated (state-level decoherence with
  separation).** For every product two-site state, the connected correlator is
  **exactly 0** (49 tested; entangled-singlet control confirms the check is
  non-vacuous). Because the only supplied inter-site coupling is nearest-neighbor
  admissibility, **non-adjacent sites carry a product state** → zero connected
  correlator.
- **T5 — the named additive readout is correlation-blind (scoped no-go
  corner).** The Record scalar readout is additive over disjoint records, so its
  two-record connected cumulant `I(A ⊔ B) − I(A) − I(B) ≡ 0` (verified across
  disjoint families). A non-additive control functional (`I²`) has a nonzero
  cumulant, so the vanishing is a genuine consequence of additivity. Hence the
  *named* readout expresses **no** pair correlation — adjacent or separated —
  and a joint, non-additive functional is required to carry one.
- **T6 — assembly over `Z^3`.** The connected correlator built from the state
  the framework supplies at `{x,y}` is `−¾` **only** at adjacency (distance 1)
  and `0` at every larger lattice separation — the structural
  decoherence-with-separation profile (`d1→−¾, d2→0, d3→0, …`).

## The match, and the honest gap

- **Match (qualitative, structural):** STAR's *decoherence with angular
  separation* is, in the framework, **not an extra assumption** — it is forced
  by nearest-neighbor-only coupling (T4/T6) together with the correlation-blind
  additive readout (T5). Readable pair correlation cannot be a long-range
  readout phenomenon here; it is adjacency-confined by construction.
- **Gap (quantitative):** the framework does **not** produce STAR's *nonzero*
  adjacent magnitude. Three ingredients are missing and each is downstream/open:
  (i) a **spin-axis identification** (T2 is conditional on it); (ii) a **joint,
  non-additive** two-record correlation functional on the arena (T5 shows the
  named readout cannot be it); and (iii) **record-formation dynamics** — rate,
  weight, and the branch that would populate the singlet vs. triplet block
  (an explicit axiom-level open gate). No mapping of `−¾` to `(18 ± 4)%` is
  claimed; the two live in different normalizations and physical sectors.
