## The claim under attack

A scale-axis synthesis reported: the framework "derives the top mass
`m_t ~ 169.5 GeV` to `-1.84%`" via a derived Ward UV boundary
`y_t(M_Pl) = g_lattice/sqrt(6) ~ 0.436` (`g_lattice = sqrt(4 pi alpha_LM) ~ 1.067`,
`alpha_LM ~ 0.0907`), run down with the SM RGE, landing inside the part of the
quasi-fixed-point basin near the "special" UV value the QFP-alone no-go flags.

The owner does not believe the synthesis and asked for it to be broken. The probe
re-derives `m_t` from the chain (runner reproduces `169.516 GeV`, `-1.838%`) and
stress-tests it on four fronts plus a ledger census.

## Verdict

**GENUINE-PREDICTION-MODULO-NAMED-IMPORTS, but the headline `-1.84%` is over-stated
on two independent counts: (a) it is a scheme-naive comparison of a *running* mass
to the *pole* mass, and (b) the synthesis attached "derived" to the full numerical
chain, whereas only the algebraic subchain is `retained_bounded`; the number
inherits ~5 open/conditional links.**

It is **not** a fit of `alpha_LM` or `sqrt(6)` to `m_t`: both are forced (group
theory / `g_bare = 1`). It is **not** circular in the narrow sense (the observed
`m_t = 172.69` enters the runner only as a threshold for the separate `v -> M_Z`
cross-check; the `v`-scale `m_t` value is independent of it — verified by the
integration range). But the agreement is softer than `-1.84%` suggests.

## Front 1 — is `alpha_LM ~ 1/11` derived or fitted? (resolving the gate tension)

`alpha_LM = alpha_bare/u_0` with `alpha_bare = g_bare^2/(4 pi) = 1/(4 pi)` (since
`g_bare = 1`, `g_bare_derivation_note`, `retained_bounded`) and `u_0 = <P>^{1/4}`.
As a **symbol/identity** `alpha_LM` is derived (the algebra is `retained`:
`alpha_lm_geometric_mean_identity_theorem_note_2026-04-24`). The transmutation
agent's `HIERARCHY_ALPHA_LM_MAGNITUDE_DELTA0_OPEN_GATE` flags that the *magnitude*
of `alpha_LM` does not *transport* to give the 17-decade hierarchy through
`alpha_LM^16`.

**Resolution — and a new finding the synthesis missed:** there is no contradiction
in `alpha_LM` being "derived as algebra" yet "open as transport." BUT the top mass
does **not** escape the magnitude gate. `m_t = y_t(v) * v / sqrt(2)`, and the
hierarchy `v = M_Pl * (7/8)^{1/4} * alpha_LM^16` feeds `m_t` directly. The probe
shows a `+-2%` move in `alpha_bare` swings `m_t` to `[121, 235] GeV` (Axis C),
because `v ~ alpha_LM^16`. So the *same* `alpha_LM`-magnitude open gate that the
hierarchy lane flags is **load-bearing for `m_t`**. The two lanes are consistent:
the hierarchy gives `v = 246.28 GeV` (`+0.03%`), i.e. the `alpha_LM^16` magnitude
is empirically right *here*; the open gate is that this magnitude is not *derived*
to transport. The top mass rides on that same un-derived magnitude.

Conclusion: `alpha_LM` is **not tuned to `m_t`** (its value is fixed upstream by
`g_bare = 1` and `<P>`), but the `m_t` number is **not insulated** from the open
hierarchy-magnitude gate — it inherits it through `v`.

## Front 2 — is `y_t = g_lattice/sqrt(6)` derived or fitted?

The `sqrt(6)` is **derived group theory**: `sqrt(2 N_c) = sqrt(N_c * N_iso)` with
`N_c = 3` (color) and `N_iso = 2` (weak isospin), the unit-residue normalization of
the unique scalar singlet on the `Q_L = (2,3)` block
(`yt_ward_identity_derivation_theorem`, D8-D17). It is **not** chosen to land
`m_t`. Two honest caveats:

1. **Look-elsewhere is only modest.** Because the IR quasi-fixed point compresses a
   broad UV range, **8 of 11** tested O(1) denominators `C` give a "top-like"
   `m_t in [150, 200] GeV` (`C=5 -> 175.7`, `C=6 -> 169.5`, `C=7 -> 163.9`). The
   *value* `C=6` is principled; the *agreement* is not a sharp discriminator — many
   nearby O(1) Clebsch factors would also look "top-like."
2. **The load-bearing object is `g_lattice`, not `g_bare`.** The audited core is
   `y_t_bare = g_bare/sqrt(6) = 1/sqrt(6) = 0.408`. The chain instead uses
   `g_lattice = sqrt(4 pi alpha_LM) = g_bare/sqrt(u_0) = 1.067`, i.e. it applies a
   tadpole dressing `1/sqrt(u_0)` to lift `y_t(M_Pl)` from `0.408` to `0.436`. The
   Ward note explicitly marks that transport step `(4.2)/(4.3)` as **non-load-bearing
   conditional context requiring a separate retained tadpole-transport bridge that
   does not exist** (`yt_color_projection_correction_note`, `retained_no_go`, and the
   note's own §4). So the *exact* UV value `0.436` rests on an un-derived shared-tadpole
   premise; the audited value is `0.408`.

## Front 3 — is `-1.8%` robust or cherry-picked?

The probe varies every honest input. Two bands, kept separate by status:

| axis | variation | m_t band |
|---|---|---|
| **Local systematic** (alpha_bare fixed at derived `1/4pi`) | `<P>` over MC `sigma`, analytic `<P>`, 1-loop vs 2-loop | **`[165.5, 170.2] GeV`** |
| **Scheme axis** | is `169.5` the pole mass or a running mass? | **`[169.5, 179.1] GeV`** |

- **Local systematic:** `m_t = 169.5 (+0.7 / -4.0) GeV`. The dominant *local* mover
  is 1-loop vs 2-loop (`-4 GeV`), not the plaquette. So at fixed scheme the result is
  reasonably stable and the central `-1.8%` is not a knife-edge in the inputs.
- **Scheme is the real problem (front-2-of-3 killer).** `m_t = y_t(v)*v/sqrt(2)` is a
  tree relation at `mu = v`, i.e. a **running** (MSbar-like) mass. The headline
  compares it to the **pole** mass `172.69`. Converting the running value to a pole
  mass (`+5.7%` QCD shift) gives `179.1 GeV` (`+3.7%`); comparing to MSbar `m_t(m_t)
  = 162.5` gives `+4.3%`. **The sign and size of the deviation flip with the scheme
  choice.** The clean-looking `-1.84%` is the *scheme-naive* reading (treat a running
  mass as the pole mass). The honest, scheme-aware statement is `m_t` consistent with
  the top to within `~ +3.7% / -2.4%`, i.e. a few percent, **not** a pinned `-1.8%`.

**Honest headline band (local systematic + scheme): `m_t in [165.5, 179.1] GeV`,
which contains the observed `172.69 GeV`.** Quote this, not `-1.84%`.

(Axis C, the `alpha_bare`-hypersensitivity `[121, 235] GeV`, is a *structural*
sensitivity, not a free-input band — `alpha_bare` is fixed by `g_bare = 1`. It is
reported to show that `m_t` is not insulated from the hierarchy magnitude gate, per
Front 1, not to widen the honest band.)

## Front 4 — does the QFP wash out the UV boundary?

The chain advertises QFP insensitivity ("10% UV change -> <0.5% IR"). That is true
only for **large** UV `y_t (>~1)`, which lands `m_t ~ 218 GeV` — exactly the band the
QFP-alone no-go (`quark_top_qfp_attractor_route_no_go_note_2026-05-10`) rules out. At
the framework's actual UV value `y_t(M_Pl) ~ 0.44`, the probe finds the boundary **is
load-bearing**: a 23% UV change moves `m_t` by 9% (15 GeV). So the result genuinely
**rests on the Ward boundary being derived** — the QFP does not rescue an arbitrary
UV value here. This is a point *in favor* of the chain's logic: the `-1.8%` is not a
QFP coincidence; it requires `y_t(M_Pl) = g_lattice/sqrt(6)` specifically. The
no-go is fully consistent: it forecloses *generic* QFP closure and demands a derived
UV boundary near `0.5`, which is precisely what the framework supplies (`0.436`).
