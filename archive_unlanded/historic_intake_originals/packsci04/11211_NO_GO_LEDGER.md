## PMNS TM2 Endpoint

The maximal-CP conclusion cannot be stated on the full algebraic TM2 endpoint
surface. At `sin^2(theta_13)=2/3`, `c12=0`, so the phase coefficient
`2 c12 s12 s13` vanishes and the residual equation is true for every
`delta_CP`.

## Planck Daggered CAR

The fixed-background-dagger CAR equations are not invariant under arbitrary
nonunitary similarity transformations that preserve the Clifford relations.
Therefore P1 alone does not force daggered CAR unless a compatible Hermitian
representative is chosen.
