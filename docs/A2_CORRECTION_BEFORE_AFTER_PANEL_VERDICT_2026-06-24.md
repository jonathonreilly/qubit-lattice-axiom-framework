## SUBJECT

A panel chair synthesis of **10 blind physicists** who judged axiom **A2 (Quantum)** in two states:

- **BEFORE** — the canonical posit: *"the one-site operator algebra is `M_2(C)`, equivalently `Cl(3,0)` in its real-algebra reading,"* with the flat disclaimer list (`NOT a gauge group`, …).
- **AFTER** — the proposed minimal transparency correction (`docs/A2_MINIMAL_CORRECTION_PROPOSAL_2026-06-24.md`): bare posit demoted to `M_2(C)` as a `*`-algebra, plus four scoping clauses (i)-(iv) under the supplied-vs-derived discipline, addressing C1 (K-seed), C2 (`Spin(3)=SU(2)`), C3 (complex-`i`), C4 (grade-3 ↔ spatial-3).

Each panelist returned: BEFORE problems, whether the AFTER fixes each concern, over-reach, under-reach, new-smuggling, a `minimality_form` verdict, a `direction` verdict, residual concerns, and a one-line.

The improvement verdict is about that posit-level de-smuggling plus the C1/C2 supplied-vs-derived hygiene — **not** a blanket endorsement of the four-clause form (see §2) or of clause (iii) (see §4).

**Unanimous: 10/10 `scoping_belongs_in_companion_note`.** Every seat — including the bare-posit minimalists and the constructive-QFT/axiom-form specialist — judges that the four clauses (i)-(iv) **do not belong inside the axiom body**. The recurring framings:

- The bare posit ("one qubit per site; one-site `*`-algebra `M_2(C)`") is genuinely terse, self-contained, and should **stand alone with a pointer**.
- Loading four dense, cross-referencing clauses inline inflates A2 ~5× in length and, per the Bayesian-Occam seat, balloons its Kolmogorov/audit cost while the posited content is unchanged — an **anti-minimal / category error**: "an axiom posits; it does not cite theorems or state intentions."
- The clauses couple A2's text to A1, A3, and three external theorems, which is **the opposite of axiomatic isolation** and raises A2's own audit surface.
- The self-description "these clauses do NOT enlarge it / transparency only" is judged **slightly false** because (iii) and (iv) make positive identifications (i = ω; grade-3 INTENDED = `Z^3`) — so the clauses risk being read **as** axiomatic content despite the disclaimer, which is exactly the failure mode the correction set out to avoid.

This is the single most decisive panel signal: the *content* of the supplied-vs-derived discipline is welcomed; its *placement inside the axiom* is unanimously rejected.

## 3. PER-CONCERN CONSENSUS — DOES THE AFTER FIX IT?

| Concern | Fix consensus | Tally |
|---|---|---|
| **C1 — K/CPT seed** (Cl(3,0) real form pre-stages A3's `K`) | **FIXED** (in substance/form) | **10/10 FIXED** |
| **C2 — `Spin(3)=SU(2)`** (latent vs "NOT a gauge group") | **FIXED**, with 3 seats "partial" | **7 FIXED / 3 PARTIAL** |
| **C3 — complex-`i`** (i unscoped) | **ADDRESSED but OVER-COMMITTED** — scoped to a contested answer, not fixed cleanly | **0 clean / 10 over-committed** |
| **C4 — grade-3 ↔ spatial-3** | **HONESTLY FLAGGED, not fixed-by-derivation**; "INTENDED" criticized by ~all | **0 fully-fixed / ~10 partial-flag** |

### C1 (K-seed) — FIXED, 10/10
Unanimously the strongest part of the AFTER. Clause (i) demotes `Cl(3,0)` from primitive "equivalently" to a cited derived theorem; clause (ii) severs the one-site `*`-involution from A3's `K`, calling it **at most a candidate SEED** and requiring any antiunitary/CPT identification to be DERIVED while still permitting A2 as a premise. Every seat — lattice, foundations, operator-algebra, GR/arrow-of-time, QI, constructive-QFT, philosophy — calls this the correct supplied-vs-derived cut (the `*`-involution is linear/structural; `K`/CPT is antiunitary and must be earned). **Residuals named (do not retract the FIXED verdict):** (a) the *choice* of conjugate-transpose vs a symplectic/twisted involution is itself a reality-structure-seeding frame choice left implicit (skeptic, lattice, operator-algebra, sm-pheno, QI); (b) the linear-involution vs antilinear-`K` **category gap** is never stated explicitly, so the "must be derived" barrier could be crossed by quietly upgrading an automorphism to an antiunitary (operator-algebra, GR/arrow-of-time); (c) the discharge now leans on three cited theorems whose audit status is invisible at this tier — "derived not free-passed" is **asserted, not demonstrated** (all seats).

### C2 (`Spin(3)=SU(2)`) — FIXED (7) / PARTIAL (3)
Clause (iv) stops the incoherent denial-of-presence and instead admits the even-subalgebra `Spin(3)=SU(2)` generators ARE present, withholding only their PROMOTION to a gauged/dynamical/observable symmetry. Seven seats call this the correct distinction (algebra always carries the generators; gauging is a separate dynamical choice). **Three seats mark it PARTIAL:** GR/arrow-of-time (the `T²=±1` / Kramers spinor double-cover significance of the latent SU(2) is unflagged); QI and constructive-QFT/condmat (naming "THE" Spin(3) generators / `Spin(3)=SU(2)` as a flat group identity **adds** a specific group-theoretic reading and singles out one su(2) among many — which itself presupposes the Clifford embedding clause (i) demoted, so it is a small additive commitment, not pure scoping). Sm-pheno adds an under-reach: a residual **global/flavor-like** SU(2) imprint (neither gauged nor obviously withheld) is not scoped, so C2 reads "fully closed" only for the gauged case.

### C3 (complex-`i`) — ADDRESSED BUT OVER-COMMITTED, 10/10
**No seat calls C3 cleanly fixed.** All ten credit the genuinely useful move — separating the microscopic carrier-`i` from the **separate, audit-open** inter-site propagation complex structure (the single highest-value addition for the decoherence/condmat seats). But all ten flag that the AFTER **resolves C3 by fiat in the wrong direction**: it hard-pins `i = ω = γ₁γ₂γ₃ = MICROSCOPIC` rather than leaving the carrier-`i` agnostic. Philosophy states it bluntly: "C3 NOT fixed — OVER-corrected." This is simultaneously the #1 over-reach (§4) and the core under-reach (§5): the genuinely safe minimal fact — *that `M_2(C)` is a complex `*`-algebra and **that** is all the `i` A2 supplies* — is never stated, the ω-identification being a consequence of the very `Cl(3,0)` reading clause (i) demoted.

### C4 (grade-3 ↔ spatial-3) — FLAGGED HONESTLY, NOT FIXED-BY-DERIVATION
All seats credit the honesty upgrade over BEFORE's silence: clause (iv) surfaces the coupling as "a SHARED design coupling (NOT independent) … to be derived downstream, not free-passed off A2 alone." That is the appropriate axiom-tier posture (declared, deferred obligation). But ~every seat objects to the word **"INTENDED to be the same 3"**: it encodes the *conclusion* (carrier-dimension = spatial-dimension) as design teleology inside the axiom rather than logging an open coincidence. The disciplined wording is "**MAY** be identified; identification to be DERIVED, not assumed." Sm-pheno adds a sharper under-reach: the "same 3" secretly also aligns an **internal SO(3) frame** with the labeled lattice axes — a stronger coupling than "3 = 3" that is not separately scoped. Constructive-QFT adds: the AFTER never states a **non-derivability** disclaimer (that A2 does NOT derive `d=3` from the carrier), leaving INPUT-vs-EXPLAINED direction implicit.

## 5. UNDER-REACH CONSENSUS

- **C3 is under-reached precisely by being over-asserted (≥9 seats, the dominant under-reach).** The genuinely safe minimal statement is never offered: *`M_2(C)` is a complex `*`-algebra; that complex unit is the only `i` A2 supplies; the ω-identification and microscopic-vs-emergent status are contingent on the cited reading / audit-open.* The AFTER scopes C3 **to a position** instead of **to agnosticism**.
- **C4 withholding is under-scoped (≥8 seats).** The AFTER flags the coupling but does not cleanly withhold it: a fully-scoped version says A2 supplies **NO** grade-3 ↔ `Z^3` identification and any such identification is A1-downstream; "INTENDED" under-scopes that withholding. The internal-SO(3)-frame-vs-lattice-axes alignment is a second, stronger coupling left unscoped (sm-pheno), and a `d=3` **non-derivability** disclaimer is missing (constructive-QFT).
- **Involution-selection is left implicit (≥4 seats: lattice, operator-algebra, sm-pheno, QI/skeptic).** Conjugate-transpose is named as "THE" involution without noting that *which* involution (transpose, symplectic/quaternionic) is itself the choice that fixes the reality (real/complex/pseudoreal) class — load-bearing for any reality/CPT downstream and for C1's own quarantine.
- **The linear-`*`-involution vs antilinear-`K` category gap is unstated (operator-algebra, GR/arrow-of-time).** Naming it would make the "K must be derived" barrier sharper and unwaiveable.
- **One-site vs global/inter-site structure is under-scoped given (iii) opened the topic (decoherence, condmat).** The AFTER does not say the carrier supplies the one-site positive cone but **not** the inner product / trace / state selecting probabilities, nor how one-site involutions **compose** into the global `*`-structure that determines which superpositions decohere.

## 7. SHIP RECOMMENDATION

### `revise_substantially`

**Rationale.** The verdict is uniformly positive in *direction* (10/10 improvement) and the bare-posit de-smuggle is a real, creditable, minimal win that should be preserved. But two unanimous signals push past "minor revisions":

1. **Unanimous structural objection (10/10 `scoping_belongs_in_companion_note`).** Every seat says the four clauses must move **out** of the axiom body into a companion supplied-vs-derived note. This is a change to the *form of the artifact* (split the terse posit from the scoping ledger), not wording polish.
2. **Unanimous substantive over-commitment on C3 (10/10).** Clause (iii)'s `i = ω = microscopic` is judged a content over-reach that (a) re-imports the demoted `Cl(3,0)` reading, (b) partly undoes clause (ii)'s `K`-openness, and (c) makes the AFTER's own "bare posit unchanged / transparency only" self-description **false**. Two seats (philosophy, constructive-QFT) judge (iii) *worse* than a maximally-cautious rewrite. Fixing it **reverses a positive content claim** (microscopic ⟶ agnostic), which is substantive, not cosmetic.

No seat recommends `reject` (the math is sound; C1/C2 are genuinely fixed; the fixes are well-specified and bounded), and no seat rated a regression — so `reject` is unwarranted. But the combination of a form-level refactor **and** a content-level reversal of clause (iii), plus the `INTENDED`/C4 rewording and the cited-theorem audit dependency, is more than `ship_with_minor_revisions` honestly covers. Hence **`revise_substantially`** — with the explicit expectation that the revision is well-scoped and largely subtractive (move clauses out, weaken (iii)/(iv)), preserving every transparency gain.

## 8. REQUIRED REVISIONS (exact)

1. **Split the artifact (10/10, mandatory).** Keep the **axiom** to the terse bare posit only — *"each site carries one qubit; the one-site operator algebra is `M_2(C)` as a `*`-algebra,"* plus the preserved withholding parenthetical — followed by a **pointer**. Move clauses (i)-(iv) **verbatim** into a companion **supplied-vs-derived note** keyed to A2. No clause text need be lost; only its *tier/placement* changes.

2. **Weaken clause (iii) from a verdict to agnosticism (10/10, mandatory).** Replace "the complex unit `i` … is MICROSCOPIC … it is the central pseudoscalar ω = γ₁γ₂γ₃" with: *"`M_2(C)` is a complex `*`-algebra; its central scalar acts as `i·I`, and **that complex structure is all the `i` A2 supplies.** Whether this `i` is identified with the `Cl(3,0)` pseudoscalar ω (and hence whether it is 'microscopic' vs emergent) is **contingent on the cited real-form theorem and is audit-open**, NOT an axiom-level fact. The separate inter-site amplitude-propagation complex structure is likewise separate and audit-open."* This removes the circular re-import of the demoted reading and restores consistency with clause (i) and clause (ii).

3. **Reword clause (iv) — drop teleology (≥8, mandatory).** Replace "grade-3 (vector) subspace … is **INTENDED** to be the same 3 as the spatial `Z^3`" with a **declared, non-discharged identification debt**: *"`Cl(3,0)` has a 3-dim grade-1 subspace and A1 has a 3-dim lattice `Z^3`; **whether/how they are identified is OPEN and must be DERIVED downstream**, not posited here, and A2 alone does NOT derive `d=3`."* Split the deferred obligation into **two**: (4a) dim-3 internal grade vs dim-3 lattice, and (4b) internal SO(3) frame vs labeled lattice axes (sm-pheno).

4. **Neutralize the operator-algebra-only leaks (≥3, recommended-required).** Drop or rephrase **"amplitude field"** (use "the carrier's complex scalars") and **"in the Pauli irrep"** (state the ω = `i·I` fact, if retained at all, as part of the *cited* theorem, not as axiom-adjacent representation choice).

5. **Discharge — or explicitly defer to the framework auditor — the cited-theorem dependency (10/10, mandatory before any landing).** Confirm the three retained narrow theorems (`CL3_COMPLEXIFICATION_SPLIT` K2, `CL3_PAULI_IRREP_UNIQUENESS`, `AXIOM_FIRST_CL3_PER_SITE_UNIQUENESS` U1-U3) carry **ONLY** the bare real-algebra iso `M_2(C) ≅ Cl(3,0)` and **do not** silently import a fixed real structure, a chosen involution, an orientation/pseudoscalar sign, or a forced ω = `i·I` representation choice. If they do, those riders must be surfaced, not free-passed via citation.

6. **Add three small scoping lines to the companion note (≥2 each, recommended):**
   - In (ii): flag that the **choice of conjugate-transpose** (vs symplectic/transpose) is itself a reality-structure-seeding frame choice, and state the **linear-involution vs antilinear-`K` category gap** explicitly so the "derive `K`" barrier cannot be waved through.
   - If `i = ω` is retained anywhere, **declare the ω vs −ω orientation/sign** convention, since downstream `K`/CPT and parity depend on it.
   - In (iv)/C2: note that "present-but-not-promoted" still leaves a possible **global (flavor-like) SU(2) / selection-rule** imprint to be ruled in/out downstream (otherwise C2 reads closed only for the gauged case), and — for the GR/arrow-of-time seat — flag the `T²=±1` / Kramers significance of the latent `Spin(3)=SU(2)`.

**Preserved as-is:** the bare posit's qubit/`M_2(C)` content; the verbatim disclaimer parenthetical (with only "gauge group" scoped); the **C1 fix** (clauses (i)+(ii) substance) and the **C2 honesty** (acknowledge generators, withhold promotion); the "0 downstream reframe debt" triage in the source proposal is a separate claim and is **not** adjudicated by this panel.

But C3 is **not** cleanly fixed by any seat: clause (iii)'s `i = ω = γ₁γ₂γ₃ = MICROSCOPIC` is the load-bearing over-reach (10/10) — it re-imports the `Cl(3,0)` reading clause (i) just demoted (top new smuggle, 9/10), partly undoes (ii)'s `K`-openness, and makes the proposal's "bare posit unchanged" self-claim false; C4 is honestly flagged but its "INTENDED grade-3 = `Z^3`" injects soft `d=3` teleology (over-reach 8/10), and the whole discharge leans on three cited theorems whose audit status is invisible at this tier (over-reach 10/10). The chair recommendation is **`revise_substantially`** — not reject (the math is sound and C1/C2 are real fixes) but past minor-revision because the fix requires both a form refactor (terse axiom + companion note) and a content reversal (make `i` agnostic, drop "INTENDED"), plus discharging the cited-theorem dependency.
