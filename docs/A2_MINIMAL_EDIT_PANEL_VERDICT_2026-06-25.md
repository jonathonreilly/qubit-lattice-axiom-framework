## SUBJECT

A panel chair synthesis of **10 blind physicists** who judged the **MINIMAL A2 edit** — strictly subtractive, no companion note:

- **Drop** the clause ", equivalently `Cl(3,0)` in its real-algebra reading" from the A2 (Quantum) carrier posit.
- **State** the carrier as the **bare complex `*`-algebra `M_2(C)`**.
- **Nothing else.** No four scoping clauses (i)-(iv), no `i = ω` identification, no "INTENDED grade-3 = `Z^3`", **no companion supplied-vs-derived note**.

This is the maximally-subtractive realization of the prior BEFORE/AFTER panel's mandate (`docs/A2_CORRECTION_BEFORE_AFTER_PANEL_VERDICT_2026-06-24.md`: terse axiom, clauses out, weaken `i=ω`, drop teleology). The question this panel answers is narrower: **does clean removal of the `Cl(3,0)` clause, with no replacement scaffolding, neutralize the four original concerns (C1 K-seed, C2 `Spin(3)=SU(2)`, C3 complex-`i`, C4 grade-3 ↔ spatial-3) at the axiom level, and is that removal sufficient?**

Each panelist returned: direction, whether removal neutralizes each of C1-C4, an over-aggressiveness call (`clean_removal_correct` / `prefer_cited_pointer` / `must_keep_cl3`), any cited-pointer wording, new problems introduced by the edit, a sufficiency call, a cleanliness call, and a one-line.

## 3. PER-CONCERN CONSENSUS — DOES CLEAN REMOVAL NEUTRALIZE IT?

| Concern | Neutralized by clean removal (at the axiom level)? | Tally |
|---|---|---|
| **C1 — reality / K-seed** (Cl(3,0) real form pre-stages A3's `K`/CPT) | **NEUTRALIZED at axiom level** (real form no longer named); residue relocated to `complex` + which-involution, NOT resolved | **10/10 neutralized-at-axiom** |
| **C2 — `Spin(3)=SU(2)`** (latent even subalgebra vs "NOT a gauge group") | **NEUTRALIZED** — even subalgebra is a theorem about `M_2(C)`, no longer foregrounded; SU(2) is now a subgroup-of-units theorem | **10/10 neutralized** |
| **C3 — complex-`i`** (`i` read as pseudoscalar `ω`) | **NEUTRALIZED** — no grading ⇒ no pseudoscalar; `i` is just the field unit (operator-algebra: "indeed corrected") | **10/10 neutralized** |
| **C4 — grade-3 ↔ spatial-3** | **NEUTRALIZED** — `M_2(C)` carries no grading and no intrinsic "3"; the A1↔A2 dimension pun has no referent | **10/10 neutralized** |

All four neutralizations share a **single common source**: every seat identifies the dropped clause as the *one* place A2 named the structure each concern feared. Removing the common source removes all four as **posited** content while leaving them **true as theorems** — unanimously called the correct supplied-vs-derived demotion.

### C1 (reality / K-seed) — NEUTRALIZED at the axiom level, 10/10 (with a relocated residue)
The `Cl(3,0)` real-algebra reading **literally inscribes a real form** — a singled-out real subspace / reversion-conjugation, i.e. a latent K-like antilinear reality datum on the carrier. The GR/arrow-of-time seat states the decisive form: with the real form named, A3's K/CPT seed is **double-counted** (latent in A2, posited in A3) and the arrow-of-time seed looks *derived from kinematics rather than posited*. Dropping the clause leaves only the algebra's `*`-involution (a linear/inner datum), **not** a singled-out antilinear real form — so A3 regains a clean, independent K/CPT seed. **Unanimous caveat (does not retract the verdict):** removal **relocates rather than fully resolves** C1's substance. The replacement word `complex` re-installs a field commitment (fixes the scalar field to C; the complex unit `i` is now primitive), and `*-algebra` names *an* involution but **not which one** — and the *choice* of involution (Hermitian conjugate vs transpose vs symplectic/twisted) is itself the reality-structure-seeding choice that later carries CPT/K-reality. So the **named real-form pre-staging is gone** (the win), but reality is now **fixed-to-standard-complex-QM and deferred to A3**, not "left open." Five-plus seats stress the panel must not over-claim that "bare complex `M_2(C)`" leaves reality open.

### C2 (`Spin(3)=SU(2)`) — NEUTRALIZED, 10/10
`Cl(3,0)_even = H = SU(2)` double cover; naming `Cl(3,0)` foregrounds exactly those rotor generators next to the "NOT a gauge group" disclaimer. A bare `M_2(C)` foregrounds **no distinguished su(2)**: its unitary group is U(2), its automorphisms are inner (`PU(2)=SO(3)`), and SU(2) survives only as a **derivable subgroup-of-units theorem**, not axiom content — dissolving the verbal tension with the disclaimer. **One structural dissent worth logging (SM-pheno, single seat, folded into §7):** `complex M_2(C)` still ships 4 real Hermitian dimensions ⇒ `u(2)=u(1)⊕su(2)`; the *generator content* of that su(2) is structurally present, so removal **de-names** the latent SU(2) rather than structurally eliminating it. The majority treats "present as a derivable subgroup, not foregrounded" as the correct demotion, not a defect.

### C3 (complex-`i`) — NEUTRALIZED (and corrected), 10/10
With no Clifford grading there is **no pseudoscalar** to identify `i` with; the scalar `i` is simply the field unit of the complex `*`-algebra. The QI seat calls this the cleanest win (the resource scalar that makes superposition phases physical). The operator-algebra seat sharpens it to a **correction**: in `Cl(3,0)` the volume element `e₁e₂e₃` is central and squares to **+1**, so the "i = pseudoscalar" reading was in fact slightly **misleading**; calling the carrier `complex` makes `i` the honest field unit and kills the conflation. (The surviving piece — that `i` is now *primitive* axiom content via `complex` — is logged under C1's residue and new-problem #2, "C3 in a different guise.")

### C4 (grade-3 ↔ spatial-3) — NEUTRALIZED, 10/10 (the strongest gain)
Repeatedly named the **most important kill** (lattice, condmat/RG, philosopher) and a genuine **soundness** gain, not optics: a hidden axiom-to-axiom coupling. The grade-1/grade-3 vector triple was the *only* place A2 silently referenced the dimension 3, manufacturing an A1↔A2 coincidence ("two different 3's are the same 3"). A bare `M_2(C)` has **no grading and no intrinsic 3** (its dimensions — 4 over C, 8 over R, 2 for the Hilbert space — are decoupled from spatial `Z^3`), so the spurious coupling **has no referent in the new text**. The internal-SO(3)-frame-vs-lattice-axes alignment (a stronger coupling than "3=3") likewise loses its axiom-level anchor.

**8/10 `sufficient`; 2/10 `insufficient_needs_more`** (SM-pheno, constructive-QFT). The two dissents are **distinct in kind**:

If the intent is to leave reality fully open for A3, the cleaner carrier is the bare complex **associative** algebra `M_2(C)` (no `*`), or an explicit statement that the `*` is only the algebraic adjoint and carries no spacetime/CPT content.

Both dissents resolve to the **same one-clause fix the eight `minor_tweak` seats already want** (pin / clarify the involution); neither asks to restore `Cl(3,0)` or to add the four scoping clauses. So "insufficient" here means "needs the involution pinned," not "the removal was wrong."

## 7. SHIP RECOMMENDATION

### `tweak_then_ship`

**Rationale.** The signals point past `revise` but short of `clean_ship`:

1. **Direction is unanimous (10/10 improvement)** and **removal is uncontested (0/10 `must_keep_cl3`; 7/10 `clean_removal_correct`).** No seat wants `Cl(3,0)` retained as primitive, and no seat rates a regression. The math is sound: `M_2(C) ≅ Cl(3,0)` is a citable theorem, so nothing load-bearing is lost.
2. **All four original concerns are neutralized at the axiom level (10/10 each for C1-C4)** by removing their single common source — most decisively C4's accidental `3=3` soundness coupling. This is exactly the largely-subtractive fix the prior BEFORE/AFTER panel mandated, and it **introduces none of that proposal's self-inflicted new smuggles** (`i=ω`, "INTENDED", cited-theorem reliance) because it adds nothing.
3. **But it is not `clean_ship`:** only 2/10 mark it `clean`, and **10/10 flag the same residue** — `bare ... *-algebra` leaves the load-bearing involution unpinned (new-problem #1), which partially re-imports C1, and `complex` is a positive field commitment that must be acknowledged rather than sold as pure subtraction. Two seats rate it `insufficient` precisely on the unpinned involution. This is a **one-clause** fix, not a refactor and not a content reversal — hence `tweak_then_ship`, not `revise`.

`clean_ship` is unwarranted (10/10 name a real, if minor, residue). `revise` is unwarranted (no regression; the math is sound; C1-C4 are genuinely neutralized; the two `insufficient` seats ask for the same one-clause involution fix, not for restoring `Cl(3,0)` or adding the four clauses).

## 8. REQUIRED TWEAKS (before landing)

1. **Pin or explicitly defer the `*`-involution (10/10, mandatory).** Resolve the `bare`-vs-`*`-algebra tension by either (a) naming the involution — *"the bare complex `*`-algebra `M_2(C)` with its standard Hermitian (conjugate-transpose) involution"* (the unique `*` making it a C*-algebra; the qubit's physical adjoint) — **or** (b) if reality is meant to stay open for A3, stating that the `*` is only the algebraic adjoint and carries no CPT/K content (or using the bare complex **associative** algebra, no `*`). Without this, an unspecified involution quietly re-imports the very reality/conjugation choice (C1) the edit set out to remove.

2. **Acknowledge that `complex` is a field commitment, not a pure subtraction (10/10, governance note — does not change the axiom text).** The owner should record that `complex M_2(C)` **fixes the scalar field to C, makes `i` primitive, and defers reality to A3** — it does *not* "leave reality open." This is judged the **correct** call for a qubit, but the disposition note must not be read later as "reality unspecified." Flag explicitly only if the program intends to *derive* complexification from below.

3. **Ensure the `Cl(3,0) ≅ M_2(C)` isomorphism lives somewhere as a cited theorem (≥7, recommended-required given NO companion note).** Since the edit deletes the only in-axiom signpost to the geometric-algebra/spinor reading, confirm the **real-algebra** isomorphism (`Cl(3,0) ≅ M_2(C)` as 8-dim real `*`-algebras, pseudoscalar ↦ central element) remains citable downstream so the bridge does not become folklore. *Optional* (3 firm + 1 wanting, below the ≥5 bar): a single non-primitive pointer line appended **after** the disclaimer — wording in §4 — stated as a **REAL**-algebra iso, never complex.

4. **One-time cross-axiom regression check (≥2, owner/auditor).** Confirm **no** downstream axiom or canonical row currently DEPENDS on the *named* `Cl(3,0)` form as primitive (if one does, this becomes a regression, not an improvement). Consistent with — but not re-adjudicated from — the sibling triage's "0 free-pass / already-derived-or-cited" finding.

**Preserved as-is:** the qubit / `M_2(C)` carrier content (the Hilbert space `C²` is intact; no representation content lost); the disclaimer parenthetical (with "gauge group" now a clean withholding since SU(2) is no longer foregrounded); the **deletion** of `Cl(3,0)` from primitive content (10/10 endorse the direction). **Explicitly NOT required by this edit:** the four scoping clauses (i)-(iv), the `i=ω` identification, the "INTENDED grade-3 = `Z^3`" wording, and a companion supplied-vs-derived note — none is needed for the minimal edit, and adding them back would reintroduce the prior round's smuggles.

## 9. ONE-PARAGRAPH SUMMARY

A blind 10-physicist panel unanimously rates the **minimal** A2 edit — drop ", equivalently `Cl(3,0)` in its real-algebra reading", state the carrier as the **bare complex `*`-algebra `M_2(C)`**, add nothing, no companion note — an **improvement** (10/10) and judges clean **removal correct** (7/10 `clean_removal_correct`, 3/10 prefer an optional cited pointer, **0/10** want `Cl(3,0)` kept as primitive). Removing the `Cl(3,0)` clause deletes the **single common source** of all four original concerns at the axiom level (**10/10 neutralized** for each of C1 reality/K-seed, C2 `Spin(3)=SU(2)`, C3 complex-`i`, C4 grade-3 ↔ spatial-3 — most decisively C4's accidental `3=3` soundness coupling), while every fact survives as a citable isomorphism theorem about `M_2(C)`; crucially it introduces **none** of the prior four-clause proposal's self-inflicted smuggles (`i=ω`, "INTENDED", cited-theorem reliance) because it adds nothing. The one residue, flagged by **all ten** seats, is wording: `bare ... *-algebra` leaves the load-bearing involution unpinned (which partially re-imports C1 and is mildly self-contradictory, since a `*`-algebra ships an involution), and `complex` is a genuine field commitment — reality is now fixed-to-complex and **deferred** to A3, not "left open." The cited-pointer threshold (≥5) is **not** met, so a pointer is optional; if added it must be stated as a **real**-algebra isomorphism. The chair recommendation is **`tweak_then_ship`**: a one-clause fix to pin (or explicitly defer) the `*`-involution, a governance acknowledgment that `complex` is a field choice, assurance the `Cl(3,0) ≅ M_2(C)` theorem stays citable downstream, and a one-time check that no axiom depends on the named Clifford form — not `clean_ship` (10/10 name the involution residue) and not `revise` (no regression; the math is sound and C1-C4 are genuinely neutralized).
