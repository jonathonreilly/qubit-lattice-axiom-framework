## SUBJECT

A panel chair synthesis of **10 blind physicists** (Opus 4.8, max effort) who judged axiom **A3 (Record)** in two states:

- **BEFORE** — the canonical posit (`docs/MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-05.md`, the **Record** axiom): *"A record is the durable registration of the realized outcome. Given a readout context with a finite central-sector decomposition and a fixed `K`/CPT conjugation, the realized outcome is the `K`/CPT orbit of the realized central sector. For any finite pairwise-disjoint collection of records, the scalar readout `I` is finitely additive, with `I(empty)=0`. Durable means fixed once registered…"*
- **AFTER (proposed split)** — the same content **demoted into tiers**:
  1. a **lone CORE axiom** = the **finite-additivity VALUATION clause** only (`I` finitely additive over pairwise-disjoint records, `I(empty)=0`);
  2. a **tenseless idempotent record** definition (durable restated as an idempotent fixed point, `R∘R=R`, "re-registering a registered outcome returns the same value") demoted to accompanying, explicitly-**non-axiom-grade** material;
  3. a **CONDITIONAL orbit-identification** ("the realized outcome is the `K`/CPT orbit of the realized central sector") gated behind an antecedent: **GIVEN an externally-supplied finite central decomposition AND a bare antiunitary involution `K`** (with `K = CPT` **deferred**);
  4. **"realized" defined exactly once**, shared with **P3**.

The five BEFORE findings under test (the prior round's catalogued smuggles):
- **F1** — the orbit-identification presupposes a **finite central decomposition** the bare algebra does not possess;
- **F2** — `K`/CPT imports the **CPT theorem's conclusion** (Lorentz + locality + spin-statistics) absent its prerequisites;
- **F3** — "durable = fixed **once** registered" smuggles a **temporal arrow**;
- **F4** — **"realized"** double-posits an actualization/definiteness commitment;
- **F5** — **finite additivity** is the one honestly-scoped clause (must stay Born-free).

Each panelist returned: direction; whether the split addresses each of F1-F5; the F1 honest-vs-relabel call; the F3 arrow-free call; a well-posedness call; over-reach; under-reach; new problems; a `correct` verdict (`yes_correct` / `minor_tweak` / `no`); and a one-line.

### F1 (center) — ADDRESSED as a conditional, PARTIAL as physics (10/10)
The strongest substantive part of the split, and the one most seats spend their analysis on. Unanimously: moving the orbit-identification from a **premise** to a **properly quantified conditional with an explicit, named, currently-unsatisfied antecedent** is the correct structural move, and it is **honest, not a mere relabel** (§5a). It is independently corroborated by the repo's own `RECORD_FORMATION_NOT_UNCONDITIONALLY_FORCED_BY_MINIMAL_AXIOMS` no-go (the Lattice+Quantum+Record baseline does **not** force record/center formation: `H=0`, decoupled `H`, and energy eigenstates are no-center witnesses). Two universal caveats keep it PARTIAL: (i) the antecedent is **unsatisfied today**, so the clause is **derivationally inert / vacuously true** and must be marked pending, not leaned on; (ii) the demotion was lifted out of the axiom **but the axiom domain was not decoupled** — "record" is still defined via "realized outcome", so the lone axiom re-inherits the unsupplied-center premise **through the back door** (this is the §4 defect). Three operator/decoherence/RG seats add the **finite-ATOMICITY** sharpening: a single `M_2(C)` site is a factor (trivial center) and the `Z^3` infinite tensor product is generically a type-III factor (also trivial center), so there is **no finite central decomposition to "decompose"** — the supplier must **manufacture** an emergent/coarse-grained center (einselection / RG quotient), and finiteness/atomicity is a **separate extra burden** (the generic output is a *continuous* direct integral), so the clause should read "finite **atomic** center via a normal conditional expectation `E: M → Z`" and say "**emergent/coarse-grained center**", not "supplied **decomposition**".

### F2 (CPT) — ADDRESSED, the cleanest finding (10/10)
Unanimous clean demotion. CPT's prerequisites (Lorentz invariance + locality + spin-statistics) are absent from Lattice/Quantum, so the BEFORE imported the **conclusion**; demoting to a **bare antiunitary involution** (`K²=1`) with `K=CPT` **deferred** removes that import — an antiunitary involution is locally available (Tomita modular `J`, or a real/charge structure) with no Lorentz/locality input, and it matches the framework's own construction in which CPT is the **derived** `Θ_CPT = (E·Σ_PT·R_b)·K` with the bare `K` being mere complex conjugation (`i ↦ −i`). The residual (~6 seats, recorded not faulted): **"antiunitary" is not content-free** — antilinearity already fixes a reality / complex-conjugation structure (Wigner conjugation / time-reversal *flavor*), strictly weaker than CPT but not neutral. SM-pheno and the gravity-sign-aware seats add the decisive reassurance that this residual **cannot** smuggle an arrow: the framework proves the antiunitary involution hands back the *symmetric* orbit pair `{+,−}` and **cannot select a branch** (an involution defines the `Z_2`, it cannot break it). Recommendation: call `K` "a reality/conjugation structure (weaker than but not free of T-content)", and state *why* antiunitarity is needed (to make the orbit a conjugation/reality structure), so downstream proofs do not treat `K` as assumption-free and then "derive" reality content `K` already encoded.

### F3 (arrow) — ADDRESSED in substance, PARTIAL in prose, mis-labeled (10/10)
See §5b for the dedicated consensus. In short: the **equation** `R∘R=R` (equivalently `R(x)=x` on records) is genuinely tenseless/arrow-free — verified decisively by the cross-check that the theory's own irreversible relaxation `Φ_α(p)=(1−α)p+αu` is **idempotent only at `α=1`** (full reset), so idempotence is precisely the *non-arrow* residue and explicitly does **not** encode the relaxation/contraction arrow. Two residuals keep it short of fully clean: (i) the **prose** ("registration", "once registered", "re-registering") still reads directionally; (ii) idempotence is the **wrong/incomplete label** for "durable" — it is stability under **re-application of the same map**, which is *orthogonal to* (and strictly weaker than) durability-as-permanence (stability under the **dynamics / other maps**); the QI/Darwinism seat sharpens that idempotence **under-specifies** a record (no stability-under-perturbation, no redundancy/objectivity), while the skeptic/measure seats note it **over-specifies** by re-importing projection (`*`-algebra idempotent) structure F5 was careful to avoid.

### F4 (realized / actualization) — PARTIAL, with a dangling pointer (10/10)
The de-duplication answers the literal double-count complaint (one "realized", referenced rather than re-posited), and several seats credit that the AFTER **cross-references** rather than merely gestures. But two defects remain. (i) The **actualization/definiteness posit is named nowhere**: "realized" still rests on an unstated commitment (collapse-or-equivalent / einselection / a boundary / past-hypothesis condition); pointing at another axiom **relocates** the heaviest physical commitment without **stating what it is**. (ii) **The "P3" pointer is dangling.** The current canonical set is **Lattice, Quantum, Record** (`docs/MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-05.md`; legacy A1/A2 numbering is historical) — **there is no P3, and no "realized-state primitive"**. The constructive-QFT and condmat seats flag this directly: the F4 fix points at a **non-existent sharing partner**, so either P3 (a realized-state / actuality primitive — precisely the collapse-or-equivalent / boundary posit F4 flagged) must be **explicitly introduced and named as such**, or "realized" is an **undefined term** inside both the conditional clause and the idempotence definition. The document is **not closed under its own references**.

### F5 (finite additivity) — PRESERVED cleanly (10/10), the key non-finding
Unanimously preserved and **not** over-extended into a Born/measure skeleton — and several seats verified this rather than asserting it. Finite additivity + `I(empty)=0`, **lacking** non-negativity, normalization, σ-additivity, and any commitment to a Hilbert-space projection lattice, yields only a finitely-additive **signed content** = a free real per atom (`R^n`); panelists exhibited negative, unnormalized solutions. There is **no Gleason bridge** (Gleason needs non-negativity + normalization + a projection lattice of `dim ≥ 3`; and the single qubit `M_2(C)` is the type-`I_2` **Gleason-exclusion** case, so Born is not forced even one-site). The framework's own no-go independently confirms additivity-as-stated yields multiplicity 1, and any Born-like factor-of-2 weighting requires the disclaimed occupancy rule. **F5 honesty survives the split** — the only caveat (skeptic) is the honest one: finite additivity is itself a genuine **classical-valuation commitment** (it forbids sub/super-additive or interference-like valuation), so it should be **acknowledged as a choice**, not advertised as a tautology.

## 4. WELL-POSEDNESS CONSENSUS — can the CORE valuation axiom stand alone before "record" / "realized outcome" are defined?

### **NO — unanimous (10/10). `axiom_needs_record_defined_first`. This is the single decisive defect and the reason every seat says `minor_tweak`.**

The lone CORE axiom asserts *"`I` is finitely additive over any finite pairwise-disjoint collection of **records**, `I(empty)=0`"*, but **"record"** (and the **disjointness** relation, the **partial join** `⊔`, and the **empty/bottom** element) are defined **only in the demoted, explicitly-non-axiom-grade tier** — and part of that tier (the realized-outcome identification) is itself **conditional on the unsupplied center map**. An axiom **may not range over a primitive sort and a disjointness relation supplied by sub-axiomatic side-definitions**: the carrier set and its orthocomplement/Boolean structure are part of the axiom's **signature** and must be declared **in or before** the axiom (the standard discipline — a Wightman axiom naming its fields + test-function space; algebraic QFT naming its net + projection lattice; measure theory naming the Boolean/σ-algebra **before** positing additivity). As written, the **dependency runs the wrong way**: the lone axiom depends on demoted (and partly center-conditional) definitions — a **layering / definitional inversion**.

Three convergent framings of the same defect:
- **Quantifier domain undefined.** `I` cannot be "finitely additive over records" before "record", "disjoint", "empty", and "join" exist at axiom grade. "Pairwise-disjoint records" is **criterion-less** in the interim (the BEFORE could read "disjoint" as "orthogonal central sectors" inside the claimed decomposition; stripping the decomposition out removes the only thing that gave "disjoint" meaning).
- **Self-inflicted, NOT inherited.** This is a **new** defect the split **introduced** (§7). The monolithic BEFORE bundled record-structure and additivity in one block, so the additivity claim had its domain **locally** (however over-loaded); demoting the definitional material **exposes and worsens** the missing-signature problem. Honesty about presuppositions was **bought at the cost of local well-posedness of the lead axiom**.
- **Honest axiom count is TWO, not one.** A **STRUCTURE axiom** (records form an orthoalgebra / effect-algebra / generalized-Boolean / partial-commutative-monoid carrier with a disjointness relation `⊥`, a partial join `⊔` closed on disjoint families, and a bottom `0`) must **precede** the **VALUATION axiom** (`I` additive on it). The carrier is **not** derivable from valuation, so the "lone axiom" framing **undersells** what the theory needs. Two seats note additivity also presupposes an unstated **orthosummability / lattice-closure** axiom (the finite disjoint join exists and is again a record).

**Agreed fix (the one tweak):** give the CORE a **minimal axiom-grade record-carrier signature carrying NO center / `K` / arrow / outcome content** — e.g. *"records form a finite orthoalgebra (a join-semilattice / generalized-Boolean / partial-commutative-monoid with a least element `0=empty` and a disjointness relation `⊥`); `I` is a finitely-additive `R`-valued valuation on it with `I(0)=0`."* Then additivity has typed objects to range over, and **all** of {realized outcome, central sector, `K`-orbit identification, idempotent registration} stay in the **conditional / definition** layer where they belong, **dependency-ordered** so the axiom depends on nothing below it.

## 5. THE TWO HEADLINE CONSENSUS CALLS

### 5a. F1 — is "conditional-pending-external-center-map" HONEST or a relabel? → **HONEST (10/10), with one named residual seam; it is a promissory note, not yet a result.**
Unanimous: a conditional `P ⇒ Q` whose antecedent `P` is **never asserted** is genuinely **non-committal** about `P`, so the AFTER does **not** implicitly assume the center exists — unlike the BEFORE, which asserted the decomposition outright. The underlying algebra was independently verified across seats: `M_2(C)` is a type-`I_2` factor (`Z(M_2)=C·I`), the `Z^3` infinite tensor product is generically a type-III Powers factor (hyperfinite `II_1` at the trace, `III_λ`/`III_1` at generic KMS product states) — **all factors, all trivial center** — so no nontrivial finite central decomposition exists intrinsically and the "conditional-pending-external-supply" framing is the **mathematically correct status**. It **exceeds a relabel** because the missing input is **named**, **localized** to a separate future dynamics axiom, and **independently shown** to be a real omission (the repo's own no-go). **Why not *fully* honest (the residual seam, also unanimous):** the conditional was lifted out of the axiom but the **axiom domain was not decoupled** — "record" is still defined via "realized outcome", so the lone axiom re-inherits the unsupplied-center premise (the §4 defect). And the Occam/QFT seats stress the honesty holds **only as a deferral**: the antecedent is **not dischargeable within the stated axioms**, so the clause **does zero work today** and could remain **permanently vacuous** — at which point only the bare additive `I` survives over an undefined domain. It must be marked **"conditional + currently-without-witness (antecedent unrealized in the present axiom set)"** with an **explicit no-default clause**, so a reader cannot mistake **availability-in-principle** for an **existing decomposition**, and downstream lanes may invoke it **only by re-supplying its antecedent locally**.

### 5b. F3 — is the tenseless idempotent fixed point genuinely ARROW-FREE? → **YES in substance / in the equation (10/10); but the PROSE still leaks tense and idempotence is the wrong label for "durable".**
Unanimous at the formal level: idempotence `R∘R=R` (a projection / retraction / fixed-point condition) singles out **no direction** — a projection `P` satisfies `P²=P` **and** `P=P†`, i.e. it equals its own reverse. The **decisive cross-check** (operator-algebra, decoherence, GR seats): the surrounding theory's irreversible relaxation `Φ_α` is idempotent **only at `α=1`**; for `0<α<1` it has a fixed point but is **not** idempotent — so idempotence selects the already-settled value and **explicitly does not encode the relaxation/contraction arrow**. It is consistent with the framework's time-symmetric microdynamics (`Θ U Θ = U⁻¹`), the arrow living entirely in the low-entropy boundary / past hypothesis, with the maximally-symmetric `I/d` reference as the no-arrow fixed point. **Two residuals (unanimous), why it is not yet fully clean:**
- **Prose leaks orientation.** "registration", "once registered", "re-registering" are process/aspect verbs presupposing a *not-registered → registered* transition. The fully tenseless statement is **purely equational, with no "once" and no "re-"**: *"a record is an element of the image (fixed-point set) of an idempotent valuation/read map `e=e∘e`; no act, order, or arrow is asserted."*
- **Idempotence ≠ durability (mis-labeled, and over-/under-specified).** Idempotence is stability under **re-application of the same map**; durability-as-permanence is stability under the **dynamics / other maps** — these are **orthogonal**, and idempotence is **strictly weaker**. The retreat *from* permanence is correct (that **was** the F3 arrow), but framing idempotence as a *restatement* of durability **over-claims continuity**: it is a weaker **arrow-free substitute** and should be described as such. (QI/Darwinism: it **under**-specifies — a single idempotent map has no stability-under-perturbation and no redundancy/objectivity, so it is not yet a record in the einselection sense; skeptic/measure: it **over**-specifies — idempotents are projections in a `*`-algebra, re-importing the projection structure F5 avoided. Both point to: prefer a **bare value-stability / `V`-invariance / fixed-point** condition over an **idempotent operator**.)
