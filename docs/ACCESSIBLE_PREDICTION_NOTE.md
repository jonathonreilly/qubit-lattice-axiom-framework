## Named premises (used by the conditional scenarios below)

The conditional prediction scenarios named below all draw from the
following block of explicit premises. Each scenario lists which
premises it requires. None of the premises is claimed retained by this
note; they are stated as inputs to each conditional.

- **P1 (semiclassical Poisson source):** The Newtonian gravitational
  potential `phi` is sourced by the quantum mass density
  `rho = m |psi|^2` via `nabla^2 phi = 4*pi*G*rho`. The lattice
  scalar / Phi identification and the Poisson source normalization
  are imported from
  [`ACTION_NORMALIZATION_NOTE.md`](ACTION_NORMALIZATION_NOTE.md) as a
  named convention.
- **P2 (branch-level entangling dynamics):** A two-mass spatial
  superposition evolves under the mutual Newtonian potential of P1
  in such a way that the relative branch phases accumulate the
  pairwise gravitational interaction phase. This is the BMV / MAQRO
  semiclassical entangling assumption as used in the literature.
- **P3 (Born-gravity cross-link):** Both `I_3 = 0` (the Born-rule
  no-third-order-interference relation) and `beta = 1` (Newton's
  mass-scaling law) trace back to a common linear-superposition
  primitive in the framework, so deviations in one carry a
  controlled scaling to deviations in the other of the form
  `|beta - 1| ~ sqrt(|I_3 / I_1|)`.
- **P4 (extended-source potential ansatz):** For a single quantum
  wavepacket of width `sigma`, the Newtonian potential produced by
  P1 takes the closed form
  `phi(r) = -(G m / r) erf(r / (sqrt(2) sigma))`.
- **P5 (self-gravitating wavepacket ansatz):** A self-gravitating
  Gaussian wavepacket evolves under the Schrodinger-Newton equation
  derived from P1 with the wavepacket sourcing its own potential;
  the dimensionless self-gravity parameter is
  `alpha = G m^3 sigma / hbar^2`.
- **P6 (decoherence-rate Schwarzschild-correction ansatz):** A
  decoherence-rate correction induced by P1 sourcing scales with
  `r_S / sigma = 2*G*m / (c^2 sigma)` for a wavepacket of width
  `sigma` around a mass `m`.

The note does not derive P1..P6 from framework primitives. The
derivations of `rho = |psi|^2` sourcing and the Born-gravity cross-link
constraint are queued as future bridge-theorem work (the auditor's
alternative repair path).

## Five Conditional Prediction Scenarios

### #1: BMV Gravitational Entanglement [TESTABLE, 5-15 years]

**Required premises:** P1 (semiclassical Poisson source) and P2
(branch-level entangling dynamics).

**Conditional statement:** *If* P1 and P2 are granted, *then* two
masses in spatial superposition acquire a relative branch phase
`delta_phi = G m^2 T / (hbar d)` from the mutual Newtonian
potential, with the entanglement signature claimed in BMV-class
proposals. Classical GR with P1 replaced by a classical-mass-only
source does not predict this entanglement.

**Numbers (under P1+P2):**
- BMV proposal (m = 10 pg, d = 200 um): delta_phi ~ 10^5 rad -- large signal
- MAQRO (m = 1 fg, d = 500 um): delta_phi ~ 0.05 rad -- marginal
- Entanglement phase = G m^2 T / (hbar d)

**What this note does and does not claim about Scenario 1:** This note
records the conditional implication only. It does not derive P1 or
P2 from framework primitives. Whether a successful BMV-class
observation falsifies any specific quantum-gravity framework is a
literature claim, not a claim this conditional scenario makes.
**Lattice-spacing dependence:** the conclusion drawn under P1+P2
does not depend on the Planck lattice spacing within the
semiclassical regime of P1.

**Conditional statement:** *If* P3 is granted as a framework input,

deviation in one of `I_3` or `beta` carries through to a controlled

### #3: Extended Source Potential [TESTABLE, 10-20 years]

**Required premises:** P1 (semiclassical Poisson source) and P4
(extended-source potential ansatz).

**Conditional statement:** *If* P1 and P4 are granted, *then* a
single quantum wavepacket of width `sigma` has the gravitational
potential
`phi(r) = -(G m / r) erf(r / (sqrt(2) sigma))`,
and the fractional deviation from the point-mass Newtonian
`-G m / r` at `r = sigma`, `r = 3 sigma`, `r = 5 sigma` follows
from that closed form (≈ 80%, 3%, negligible respectively).

**Experimental access (under P1+P4):** Wavepacket widths for
massive objects are tiny:
- Microsphere (10^12 amu) in kHz trap: sigma ~ 10^{-13} m
- Nanoparticle in free fall (100s): sigma ~ 3 nm

Detecting this signature requires creating macroscopic quantum
superpositions (sigma > 1 um) and measuring gravity at that scale
simultaneously.

**What this note does and does not claim about Scenario 3:** This
note records the conditional implication only. It does not derive
the ansatz P4 from framework primitives; that is part of the
deferred bridge-theorem work for P1.
