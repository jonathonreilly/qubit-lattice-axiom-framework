## Exact Algebraic Boundary

The runner checks four finite facts.

First, determinant support and occupancy support are different. A determinant
or Berezin factor can be supported in an abstract Grassmann scope while the
doublet slot-count remains a separate binary:

```text
sector occupancy: Z_d = 2 -> r = 1
orbit occupancy:  Z_d = 1 -> r = 1/2
```

Second, a matter-blind or branch-independent factor cannot select the branch:

```text
sum_U w[U] rho^k f[U] = rho^k sum_U w[U] f[U].
```

The factorization preserves either branch; it does not choose the exponent
or the mode set.

Third, link-integration transposition changes variables edge by edge. At the
blind point it erases the kinetic bit from integrated polynomial observables;
at first order it registers the bit in a coupling-sign channel. Registration is
not selection.

Fourth, fixed-background positivity, fermion-sector OS equality, and local
density uniqueness are positive support theorems on their scoped surfaces. None
is a theorem that the physical AC_phi_lambda generation readout uses the
orbit-counting grain.
