## Stretch Attempt

The best h-class derivation would have to turn the following finite support
surface into a physical observable theorem:

```text
Record additivity + C3 covariance + finite supplied context
therefore the physical charged-lepton scalar readout is the C3 fixed-locus
density class.
```

The obstruction is already visible in the finite C3 frame. Let a finite orbit
record have three cells and let scalar readout be additive over disjoint
copies. A C3-covariant additive scalar on that orbit has the form

```text
I_alpha(x0, x1, x2) = alpha (x0 + x1 + x2).
```

The C3 covariance constraint forces equality of the three cell coefficients;
it does not force the coefficient `alpha`. On the single full C3 orbit
`(1,1,1)`, the fixed-locus density member requires

```text
I_alpha(1,1,1) = L3(1,2) = 2/9,
alpha = 2/27.
```

But `alpha = 0`, `alpha = 1/9`, `alpha = 1/3`, `alpha = 1`, and
`alpha = 2/27` all satisfy empty-record normalization, finite additivity,
and C3 covariance on the same supplied frame. They give different scalar
readouts. Only one of them is the fixed-locus-density member, and selecting it
is exactly h-class content.

This is not a claim that the other readouts are the right physics. It is the
opposite: current first principles do not yet contain the physical-observable
bridge that rules them out and selects the fixed-locus-density class.

## Theorem

On the current first-principles surface, h-class is not derived.

The finite C3 readout family witnesses the gap. Record additivity and C3
covariance can force an orbit-invariant additive form, but they do not select
the fixed-locus-density member of that family. The retained fixed-locus note
then computes the correct density once that class is chosen; it does not say
the physical charged-lepton readout must use that class. The supplied-context
and holonomy notes give useful typing and normal form support; they do not add
the missing physical-observable bridge.

Thus h-class remains a genuine part of `A_R-eta`. A future proof must derive a
physical readout/license theorem that selects the fixed-locus-density class, or
a retained theorem must derive the readout or an approved primitive must supply it explicitly.
