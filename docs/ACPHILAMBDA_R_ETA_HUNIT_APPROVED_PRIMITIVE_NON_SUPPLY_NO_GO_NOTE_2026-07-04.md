## Theorem

On the current approved-premise surface, the following implication is invalid:

```text
minimal axioms
+ scale reference
+ kinetic isotropy
+ realized-state primitive
+ carrier-class elimination context for `c != 1` conversion sources
therefore h-unit beta = 1 is supplied.
```

The approved premises have disjoint jobs:

1. `minimal_axioms` says records form and scalar readout is additive when
   records are present. It does not choose a physical readout bridge.
2. `scale_reference_primitive` supplies a dimensionful ruler. It cannot supply
   a dimensionless angle coefficient.
3. `kinetic_isotropy_primitive` supplies a kinetic-form graining ratio
   `c_t = c_s`. It is not a phase unit, selector, or readout bridge.
4. `realized_state_primitive` supplies only the fact of pointwise evaluation at
   a realized state. It does not supply a state-contingent value or
   normalization.

The conversion-factor carrier-class elimination context also does not derive
h-unit. It shows that several scanned carrier classes do not supply a rival
`c != 1` conversion factor; that is a compatibility/exclusion statement. It
does not turn `c = 1` into an approved primitive or a theorem. Eliminating
several alternatives is not the same as deriving the identity member unless the
surface is proven exhaustive and the identity rule itself is licensed.

Therefore the h-unit identity remains live. A future theorem must derive why
the fixed-locus density is identity-read as a bare holonomy angle, or owner
governance must register that narrow premise explicitly.
