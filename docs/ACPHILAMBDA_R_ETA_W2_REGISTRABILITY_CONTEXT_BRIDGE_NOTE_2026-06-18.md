`P_0, P_1, P_2` for the circulant carrier and checks idempotence,
orthogonality, partition of identity, and commutation with `H(delta)`. Inside
the commutative algebra generated by the supplied spectral resolution, these
are the central sectors required by Record.

**Record additivity.** For every pair of disjoint finite sector subsets, the
runner verifies that the scalar readout on the union is the sum of the scalar
readouts on the parts. This is exactly finite record additivity on the supplied
decomposition. It does not assert a probability rule, Born weights,
measurement dynamics, or a physical state-selection rule.

**Orbit and sign erasure.** Complex conjugation sends `H(delta)` to
`H(-delta)` and conjugates the Fourier sectors as `k -> -k`. The elementary
symmetric functions are even in `delta` and depend on the phase through
`cos(3 delta)`, while the unique degree-three orientation line is proportional
to `sin(3 delta)` and flips under `K`/CPT. The additive-plus-even theorem
therefore applies to the supplied context without importing a new
registrability assumption.

**Complement and species context.** The finite hw-complement note already
proved, and this runner rechecks, that the hw=1 and hw=2 complement readings
have the same unordered spectrum and symmetric functions on the supplied slot
model. The species-bridge decomposition already proved, and this runner
