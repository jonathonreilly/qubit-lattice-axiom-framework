## Three honest framings

Mirroring the radian precedent's structure-awareness, this note is
explicit that the reclassification is **partial** (sharpened, not full
closure). The honest framings are:

1. **CLOSURE framing** (strongest, but *conditional on current axioms*):
   the no-go portfolio + multi-attack failed-derivation panel record +
   the principle-grade analog of `P1` together establish that `AC_φλ`
   (charged-lepton sharpening) is best classified as a **framework
   principle** *under the current `A1+A2` axiom surface*. The "missing
   derivation" framing is misleading *on today's surface* because no
   derivation from current `A1+A2+retained` exists, by structural
   argument (cf. the retained no-go's). Admitted inputs of this kind
   are *principles* — until and unless the axiom baseline is updated, at
   which point the no-go's may no longer bite and derivation may become
   possible.

2. **STRUCTURAL OBSTRUCTION framing** (weakest): the choice of
   K-reality + det_C/equal-power-per-block is itself an input that
   demands derivation from a deeper substrate; the framework should
   keep this open as a "missing derivation" and continue attacking it.

3. **SHARPENED framing** (this note's verdict): the reclassification
   moves `AC_φλ` (charged-lepton sharpening) from "free admission /
   missing derivation" to "**framework principle**, admitted by stated
   principle text, under Tier-A admitted-input governance, parallel to
   how `P1` is principle-grade (mild)". The reclassification is
   admissible under the Tier-A registry's existing principle-grade
   pattern. The framework continues to value derivation attempts on
   any sharper structural carrier, but the *taxonomy* honestly names
   the current standing.

This note adopts framing (3): **SHARPENED partial closure /
principle-grade reclassification**. The audit lane has authority over
whether to retain this reclassification. If the audit lane prefers
framing (1) or (2), the note is straightforward to retag.
