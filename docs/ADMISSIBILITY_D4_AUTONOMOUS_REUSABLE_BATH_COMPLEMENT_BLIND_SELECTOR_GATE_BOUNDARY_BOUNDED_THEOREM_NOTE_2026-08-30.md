## Append channels are nonunital

Let the diagonal seven-state pointer have blank projector `P_0` and occupied
Record projectors `P_f`. An append-only channel has

```text
T(P_0)=h P_0+sum_f p_f P_f,
T(P_f)=P_f,
h+sum_f p_f=1.                                          (4)
```

Therefore

```text
T(I)=h P_0+sum_f (1+p_f)P_f.                            (5)
```

Equation (5) equals `I` only for the trivial no-write channel `p_f=0` for all
`f` and `h=1`. Every nontrivial append-only Record channel is thus nonunital.
For the maximally mixed pointer state `rho_*=I/7`, nonunitality implies

```text
Delta S = S(rho_*)-S(T(rho_*)) > 0.                     (6)
```

The symmetric primary fixture has `h=0`, `p_f=1/6`. It sends `rho_*` to the
uniform mixture of the six occupied marks and gives exactly

```text
Delta S=log(7)-log(6)=log(7/6).                         (7)
```

