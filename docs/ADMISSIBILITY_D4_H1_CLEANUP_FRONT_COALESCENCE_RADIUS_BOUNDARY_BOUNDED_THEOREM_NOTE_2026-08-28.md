## Exact Stage A2 counterexample

From `R-H-T^10-A` with contacts `(3,6,8,10)`, the legal trace
`CF_T@5, M@5, Q_T@0` reaches

```text

```

The enabled `CF_T@7` support `[7,10]` and `CF_A@9` support `[9,12]`
overlap; their union is the six-site interval `[7,12]`. Their successors are

```text

x_T = R-H-L-T-T_F-T-L-T-T-L-T-T_F-A.
```

Exhaustive reachability restricted to `[7,12]`, `[6,12]`, and `[5,12]`
finds four states from each successor and zero common states. These are every
contiguous neighborhood of length at most eight containing the overlap.
Unrestricted exploration has 72 and 120 states, 23 common states, and the
same sole normal `R-P^11-S`. This is a locality-certificate failure, not
nonconfluence.

This differs from a coordinate-hull artifact. Disjoint rows are tested by
exact commutation and may touch two separated supports without reading their
gap. Here the initiating supports overlap, and no join exists anywhere in the
whole contiguous eight-site environment.

## Length-dependent family and proof

For every `n>=10`, start with contacts `(3,n-4,n-2,n)` and execute
`CF_T@(n-5), M@(n-5), Q_T@0`. This produces the translated overlap
`CF_T@(n-3) / CF_A@(n-1)` by the seam. Exact searches for `n=10,...,16`
give minimum joining-window lengths `12,13,14,15,16,17,18`.

The growth has a symbolic cause. Outside a neighborhood containing `H`, only
`CF_A`, `CF_T`, `E_TL`, `M`, and `M_F` can act. The latter three preserve
visible `L` count; the `CF` rows increase it by one. The `CF_A` successor has
already quenched the seam participant; the `CF_T` successor has not. With
terminal `A` fixed, that participant cannot be consumed by `E_TL` or `M_F`;
it must be consumed by `CF_A`, creating a surplus `L`. No row removes or
coalesces that surplus without `H` in its source. A common successor must
therefore inspect the cleanup head, whose separation is unbounded.

This proves failure of the frozen fixed-radius target. It does not prove the
global rewrite nonterminating or nonconfluent and does not exclude another
finite table, associative component algebra, distributed incidence, serial
scanning, or coherent arbitration.
