## 4. The Reflection Blocker

This section is the headline. It concerns \(H_q\) alone, and it is
parameter-free.

**\(H_q\) carries no free symbols.** It is a real symmetric \(16\times16\)
matrix, and it is **identical** for symbolic \(s_x\) and symbolic \(s_t\).
Everything in this section is therefore a statement about a fixed integer-
and-rational matrix, not about a fixture slice.

**The fingerprint count.** Sort the absolute values of each row of \(H_q\).
The resulting 16 multisets fall into

\[
 \boxed{15\ \text{distinct absolute-row fingerprints},}        \tag{16}
\]

the sole repeat being the two sites \((1,1)\) and \((3,3)\). Fourteen sites
are thereby pinned outright by their own fingerprints.

**THE PERMUTATION THEOREM.** Exact backtracking over **all** 16-site
permutations, with the entrywise constraint
\(|H_q[\sigma(i),\sigma(j)]|=|H_q[i,j]|\), returns exactly one survivor:

\[
 \boxed{\sigma=\mathrm{id}\ \text{is the ONLY permutation preserving }
        |H_q|\ \text{entrywise}.}                              \tag{17}
\]

The one candidate that the fingerprint count leaves open — the
\((1,1)\leftrightarrow(3,3)\) swap — fails on the pinned neighbours: with
the other fourteen sites fixed, the swapped entries against those neighbours
do not match in absolute value. This is stronger than a fingerprint
argument, because it excludes the swap rather than merely bounding how many
sites could move.

**THE BLOCKER.** Every physical time reflection moves at least 8 of the 16
sites. Combined with (17), which permits zero moved sites:

\[
 \boxed{\text{NO signed lattice reflection preserves }H_q.}    \tag{18}
\]

Absolute values are the right invariant here because a diagonal sign twist
changes signs and nothing else; (17) therefore rules out the twisted maps at
the same time as the untwisted ones.

**The direct verification.** Independently of (16)--(18), the full family of
site maps

\[
 \boxed{(t,x)\longmapsto(b-t,\ a-x)\quad\text{with all sign twists},
        \qquad 256\ \text{maps},}                              \tag{19}
\]

was built and tested one by one. **Every** member of that family descends
through the antiperiodic quotient, and **zero** members preserve \(H_q\).
The blocker is therefore certified twice, once by the permutation theorem and
once by exhaustion over the displayed family.

**The canonical reflection and its defect.** The canonical link reflection is
\(t\mapsto7-t\), \(x\mapsto-x\), which descends to

\[
 \boxed{\theta:\ p\longmapsto3-p\ \text{with an overall minus sign},} \tag{20}
\]

a real symmetric orthogonal involution exchanging the halves \(\{p=0,1\}\)
and \(\{p=2,3\}\). Its defect on the form is

\[
 \boxed{\theta^{\mathsf T}H_q\theta-H_q\neq0,\qquad
        \operatorname{rank}=16,\qquad
        \max|\cdot|=\tfrac3{16},}                              \tag{21}
\]

and that defect is **identically free of \(m\), \(s_x\), and \(s_t\)**. The
failure is not a near miss on a low-rank subspace and not a fixture artifact:
it is full rank with an \(O(1)\) entry, at every point of the parameter
space.

Equation (18) is the exact scope of the blocker. It quantifies over signed
lattice reflections of the displayed carrier. It does not quantify over
metric-adapted involutions, over other carriers, or over completions, and
