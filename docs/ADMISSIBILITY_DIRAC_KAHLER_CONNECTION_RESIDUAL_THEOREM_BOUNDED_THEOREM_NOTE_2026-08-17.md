## 6. The Residual Operator

\[

(14) is part of the statement. In particular, \(P_x\) is not being renamed
as an arbitrary momentum or coordinate operator.

Three structural mechanisms determine the residual without an entrywise
accident.

**Grading.** Set

\[
 S_x:=T_t^{-1}P_{\rm even}P_x.
\]

The certified temporal shift commutes through the grading calculation, while
the spatial shift reverses the selected grade. The primitive identity is

\[
 \boxed{P_{\rm even}P_xP_{\rm even}=0,}
 \qquad
 S_x^2
 =T_t^{-2}P_{\rm even}P_xP_{\rm even}P_x=0.                \tag{15}
\]

Therefore

\[
 \boxed{\Omega^2=(2is_t)^2S_x^2=0.}                         \tag{16}
\]

The residual is itself nilpotent for a structural grading reason. That fact
does not make it a common differential: it is the non-removable overlap
disagreement between local differentials.

**Projector count.** The temporal and spatial shifts are invertible monomial
maps on their certified support. Hence the only rank loss in (14) is the
even-grade selection:

\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{rank}\Omega
       =\operatorname{rank}(P_{\rm even}P_x)
       =\operatorname{rank}P_{\rm even}=16}
 \quad(s_t\ne0).                                            \tag{17}
\]

Thus the rank is the exact projector count on the displayed carrier, not a
numerically estimated rank.

**Entry magnitude.** The matrix \(S_x\) is a partial monomial shift: each
selected source basis vector has one supported image with unit magnitude.

\[
 \boxed{\Omega_{uv}\in\left\{-\frac{8i}{5},+\frac{8i}{5}\right\}
 \quad\text{on its support},

and every other entry is exactly zero. Equations (17)--(18) explain the rank
and magnitude from the projector and shift primitives.
