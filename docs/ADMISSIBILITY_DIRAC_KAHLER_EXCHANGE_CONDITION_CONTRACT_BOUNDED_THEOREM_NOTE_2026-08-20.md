## The mechanism, in two channels

### The mass channel: a diagonality theorem, and it is irreparable

On every flat carrier the cover Hodge is diagonal, hence so is `H_q`. An
exchanging reflection `R` is a signed permutation whose involution `rho` carries
`H` onto `C`, so `[R D]_{++}[a,b] = eps_a D[rho(a), b]` with `rho(a)` in `C` and
`b` in `H` — and a **diagonal `D` has no `C x H` entry at all**. Measured, not
asserted: on the free-`nu` family the flat mass pairing is zero on **all 256**
exchange-compatible cells of the widened census (128 descending cover moves x 6
half carriers), the row-support mechanism holds in every one of them, and a
fully generic **diagonal** 16x16 operator gives zero on every one of them too.

Block 159's 8/8 flat census is a **corollary** of this theorem, and the channel
is **irreparable**: the witness pairing is identical at every mass on the flat
carrier precisely because this block of the seam is not there to move.

### The connection channel: a weight condition, not a support condition

Only the **8 boundary-crossing temporal hops** reach. On the committed probe
support, with the two antiperiodicity laws the committed differential is
*measured* to obey, the free entrywise reweighting has 24 free complex weights
(48 real parameters), keeps the residue anti-Hermitian throughout, and gives

```
P = i ( w(x') - conj(w(x)) ) / 2 ,      x' = the reflection's image of x
```

verified against the measured entries and verified to vanish identically when —
and only when — reflection-partner columns carry **conjugate weights**.

The committed connection's temporal weights are the **x-staggered** `+-2i/5`
(columns `0,1,2,3` carrying `+2i/5, -2i/5, +2i/5, -2i/5`), and
`conj(2i/5) = -2i/5`. Therefore:

- `theta-prime` (`x -> 1-x`, pairing `0<->1` and `2<->3`, **opposite**

operator: the `d-dagger H` term is antilinear. The family of all supports with

committed cell, **224** real-part generators and **256** imaginary-part
generators reach the pairing, 256 hops reach in all, and **32 hops reach through

**What linearity does and does not license.** Let `Phi` be the R-linear map from

"this class never reaches" holds **iff** every generator in it maps to zero.
Existence and never-reaches are decided generator by generator, and **nothing
else is**: the rank, inertia and definiteness of a superposition are read off
specific operators, which is where the 128-cell census and the certificate come
from.
