**Dead end two — proving the cone statement by sampling it.** Testing many
positive-definite points is not a theorem, and testing the *parameterization*

statement that both roots are positive needs three more facts than its sign. The
fix was to state all four tests explicitly — discriminant, product, sum, and
`tau - 2` — because `tau > 2` is what separates *positive and distinct* from
*positive and equal to one*, and it is the one a reader is most likely to assume.

**Dead end four — expecting the Block 197 cut to finish the job.** The round-2

**Dead end five — recording *no canonical ray* as an open question.** Saying the
cut left two dimensions invites the reading *so far*. Exhibiting an explicit
rational box inside the positive-definite cone turns it into a refutation: an
open two-dimensional set has no distinguished ray, so the route is closed rather

**Dead end six — trusting the adversarial record's displayed matrix.** The
