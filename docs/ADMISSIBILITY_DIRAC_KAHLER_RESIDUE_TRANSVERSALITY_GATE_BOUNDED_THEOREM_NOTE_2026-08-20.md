diagonal**: its DEAD-LIVE block is identically zero and its LIVE-LIVE block is
diagonal. Its inertia is therefore exactly the sum of three independent pieces,

* two uncoupled hyperbolic 2x2 DEAD blocks, `G'[1,3] = (b_31 + b_33)/8` and
