## 3. The Eigen-Relation

Fix one of the two rational shear fixtures and one displayed momentum
sector. Let \(y^{\mathsf H}\) be the nonzero row functional defining the
one-dimensional positive quotient coordinate. The fixed descending member
satisfies the exact relation

\[
 y^{\mathsf H}O^*=\lambda y^{\mathsf H},
 \qquad \lambda\ne0.                           \tag{5}
\]

This is an equality of rows, not a fitted matrix element or an expectation
value at one witness. For every carrier vector \(v\), equation (5) gives

\[
 y^{\mathsf H}(O^*v)=\lambda\,y^{\mathsf H}v.  \tag{6}
\]

It follows at once that \(O^*\) preserves the kernel of the quotient map:
if \(y^{\mathsf H}v=0\), then
\(y^{\mathsf H}(O^*v)=0\). The member therefore descends to a well-defined
endomorphism \(\widehat O^*\) of the positive quotient, and (6) identifies
that endomorphism exactly:

\[
 \boxed{\widehat O^*=\lambda\,\mathbf 1},
 \qquad \lambda\ne0.                           \tag{7}
\]

Thus \(O^*\) is not merely proportional to the identity on selected states.
It **is** \(\lambda\) times the identity on the entire quotient at that
fixture and momentum. The fixture- and momentum-specific eigenvalue may be
recorded as \(\lambda_{s,p}\); no equality among different
\(\lambda_{s,p}\) is needed for the conclusion.

The nonzero eigenvalue matters. The descending member is not killed by the
quotient. It survives, but only as a number multiplying the single quotient
direction. Accordingly, even a successful adjointness completion for this
member would not have supplied a nontrivial local observable algebra. It
would have supplied a scalar action already characterized by (7).

This conclusion is narrower than saying that the carrier operator \(O^*\)
is scalar before quotienting. Equation (5) controls its descended action.
The complement action can be non-scalar without becoming visible to the
one-dimensional positive quotient.

## 4. The Scalar-Quotient Theorem

The inertia certificate inherited from
[Block 119](ADMISSIBILITY_DIRAC_KAHLER_REFLECTION_INTERTWINER_COMPLETION_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-08-16.md)
and executed at the rational shear fixtures through
[Block 124](ADMISSIBILITY_DIRAC_KAHLER_SOURCED_QUOTIENT_EXECUTION_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-08-17.md)
is the exact count (1): one positive direction in every displayed momentum
sector. Write that positive quotient as \(\mathcal H^+_{s,p}\). Since every
one-dimensional complex vector space is isomorphic to \(\mathbb C\),

\[
 \operatorname{End}(\mathcal H^+_{s,p})
 \cong\operatorname{End}(\mathbb C)
 =\mathbb C.                                   \tag{8}
\]

Therefore any two descended observables have the form

\[
 A=a\mathbf1,
 \qquad B=b\mathbf1,
 \qquad [A,B]=0.                               \tag{9}
\]

The result includes composites built from quotient endomorphisms. Every
polynomial or finite product in such operators is again a scalar multiple
of \(\mathbf1\). Adding more names for operators inside one sector cannot
raise the dimension of its endomorphism algebra.

This is the scalar-quotient theorem: **a rank-one positive quotient admits
only scalar per-momentum observables.** It is a dimension theorem, not a
search failure. No different basis, normalization, lift through the
complement, or completion choice can make the algebra in (8)
noncommutative while its quotient dimension stays one.

Now apply the campaign's vacuity discipline prospectively. Suppose a
single descended operator \(A\) were used in a would-be Ward statement.
Any commutator expression made only from its quotient action and scalar
transfer data vanishes identically:

\[
 [A,f(A)]=0,
 \qquad
 \langle [A,f(A)]\rangle=0.                   \tag{10}
\]

Equation (10) is automatically true for every scalar \(A\). It carries no
information about a nontrivial local charge action, operator algebra, or
symmetry current. Calling it a Ward identity would not make it false; it
would make it vacuous as evidence for the hoped-for observable program.

The same discipline applies to (7). The exact eigen-relation is positive
information about descent, but it is not evidence for a non-scalar
observable. The walls of Blocks 122, 125, and 126 are thus retrospective
manifestations of (8)--(10), not three independent suggestions that a more
ingenious completion on the same rank-one quotient must succeed.

The theorem is per momentum. Whether a transfer-covariant algebra can mix
different momenta depends on transfer-rate degeneracy and is treated
honestly in N7. The theorem does not erase that separate question.
