## 3. Exact Joint-Action Theorem

Let finite sets `G` and `X` carry a strictly positive normalized law
`P:G x X -> (0,1)`. Fix `(g_0,x_0)`. Connectivity is automatic because all
joint probabilities are positive.

### Proposition 1 -- unique total representative

The unique action representative satisfying

    exp[-A(g,x)] / sum_(g',x') exp[-A(g',x')]=P(g,x),
    A(g_0,x_0)=0                                             (5)

is (1).

**Proof.** Every representative has

    A(g,x)=-log P(g,x)+C

for one configuration-independent constant `C`. The null condition fixes
`C=log P(g_0,x_0)`. `square`

This is the Block-26 anchor applied to the full geometry-bearing
configuration, rather than to matter configurations conditioned on an
external `g`.

### Proposition 2 -- exact anchored disintegration

Define

    A_geom,0(g)=-log[P(g,x_0)/P(g_0,x_0)],                  (6)

    A_cond,0(g,x)=-log[P(g,x)/P(g,x_0)].                    (7)

Then

    A(g,x)=A_geom,0(g)+A_cond,0(g,x),                       (8)

with both terms zero at their declared null. Equation (8) is exact because
the probability ratios telescope.

Another decomposition may use the marginal `P_G(g)` and normalized
conditional `P(x|g)`. The difference between decompositions is a function of
`g` alone. No experiment on `P(x|g)` chooses that split. The total (1), its
joint odds, and its total response are unchanged.

### Proposition 3 -- why endogenizing geometry matters

Multiply all joint weights at geometry `g` by `q(g)>0`. At fixed `g`,

    P_q(x|g)=P(x|g),                                        (9)

because `q(g)` cancels. Globally,

    P_q(g) proportional q(g)P(g),                           (10)

and

    A_q(g,x)-A(g,x)=-log[q(g)/q(g_0)].                      (11)

Therefore `q(g)` is the Block-25 common-shift freedom only when `g` is held
external. It is observable as a change of geometry-sector odds when `g` is
part of the joint state. For a fixed full joint law, only constant `q` is a
normalization symmetry.

The runner verifies (5)--(11) exactly on a positive `3 x 2` rational fixture.
