## Result in plain language

The 29-dimensional selected carrier from Block245 is not preserved by the
next supplied temporal crossing. At one disclosed rational sample, its exact
finite-field image together with the old carrier has rank 72 in each of three
prime fields. That is enough to disprove invariance of the rank-29 carrier.
It is not enough to prove that 72 is the exact generic rank: no universal
rank-72 upper bound is supplied here.

plaquette by one further defining-vector character and applying physical

direction. It is orthogonal to the old carrier before and after the supplied
crossing at the checked points. This is new Peter--Weyl content, whereas the
rank-72 temporal witness alone establishes multiplicity growth only.
