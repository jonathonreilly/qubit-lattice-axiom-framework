## Premises and declared objects
Use independent bond amplitudes 1+delta_j(-1)^x_j and coined hopping strength b>0. Write alpha,beta,gamma for the three coefficients of the separately supplied linear bond law, with matrix M_jj(k)=c0+2alpha cos k_j+2gamma sum_(l!=j)cos k_l, M_jl(k)=4beta cos(k_j/2)cos(k_l/2) and c0=-2alpha-8beta-4gamma. Set kappa=alpha+2beta. The energy balance below chooses b=1 and fixed arithmetic mean bond rate. It is not the log-rate completion.

## Theorem T1 — alternating symbol
The 16-component square is b^2 sum_j(sin^2 k_j+delta_j^2 cos^2 k_j) I: coordinate pair operators commute and distinct coin matrices anticommute. The filled negative branch per site is the negative zone average of this square root. At all delta_j=1 this is -b sqrt(3), allowing zero bonds.

## Theorem T2 — supplied quadratic cost
At k=pi e_1 the own-axis entry of M is -4alpha-8beta and its mixed entries vanish; the transverse block has diagonal -8beta-4gamma and off-diagonal 4beta. Thus the three independent alternations cost 2kappa sum_j delta_j^2 per site. The unnormalized vector v=(1,1,1) has v^T M(q,0,0)v=-(alpha+2beta+2gamma)q^2+O(q^4); the normalized acoustic coefficient is (alpha+2beta+2gamma)/3. Neither coefficient establishes stability of every mode.

## Theorem T3 — restricted continuum-zone balance
Let A=sum sin^2 k_j, B_j=cos^2 k_j and chi=<sum B_j/sqrt(A)>, with normalized integration over the three-dimensional continuous zone. The isolated A=0 points cause an integrable 1/|k| singularity. Differentiation at zero in the amplitudes, justified by this bound, gives
E(delta)-E(0)=-chi sum_j delta_j^2/6+o(|delta|^2).
Therefore the total quadratic coefficient is 2kappa-chi/6. Below kappa=chi/12 there is descent within this ansatz.
Sufficiency including equality follows without a fourth-order expansion: sqrt(A+X)<=sqrt(A)+X/(2sqrt(A)) for X>=0. Integrating gives E(delta)-E(0)>=-chi sum_j delta_j^2/6, so kappa>=chi/12 makes zero a minimum of this particular linear-rate ansatz. It does not establish a minimum against all bond fields, formation dynamics or which nonzero pattern is preferred.
A finite even momentum grid includes A=0 and generally has a |delta| cusp; the continuum susceptibility threshold must not be applied to that finite-grid energy. The original endpoint energy crossing and numerical minimizing-amplitude claims are deferred; an endpoint's energy relative to zero does not decide the global minimizer. Strict positive rates require |delta_j|<1.

## Theorem T4 — corner crossing and a literal wall ring
With a scalar hopping strength a through the same equal-axis alternating bonds, corner energies include +/-4a +/-sqrt(4a^2+3b^2delta^2). They vanish when b^2delta^2=4a^2; positive a,b,delta give delta=2a/b. This is a corner condition only.
On the 12-ring at delta=3/10, reversing the alternation on exactly half the ring gives coined nullity four, versus zero for uniform alternation. The check establishes this finite nullity, not two localized states per arbitrary wall.
