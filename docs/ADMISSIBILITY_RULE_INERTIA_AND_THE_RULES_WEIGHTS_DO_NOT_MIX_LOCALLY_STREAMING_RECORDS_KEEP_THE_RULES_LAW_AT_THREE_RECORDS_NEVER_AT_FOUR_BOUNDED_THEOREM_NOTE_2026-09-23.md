## Premises and declared objects
A record has one of six polar axis vectors. It streams one site in that direction, swapping contents if the target is occupied. The optional extra event redraws a head-on pair to a transverse opposite pair. The target weight is the product of c times(3,1,2) over occupied bonds, for c=1 or1/2.
A rate reads the union of the two endpoint radius-one neighborhoods:12 sites, or11 after aliasing on the3-torus. The data use all48 signed coordinate permutations, including reflections, with an8-element event stabilizer. This is an explicitly supplied symmetry class.
Moves into vacancies must have rate at least1; exchanges and redraws have rate at least0. Without the move lower bound the zero generator is an immediate counterexample to an unqualified impossibility statement. Strictly positive move rates over the finite set of local contexts can be rescaled to this lower bound.

The JSON listed among the runner inputs contains the exact rational rates and equation multipliers. It is part of this result, not deferred experimental data. Equation rows are reconstructed from configurations rather than accepted as a solver matrix. No numerical optimizer is needed to verify them.

## Theorem T1 — specified three-record clocks
On the3-torus, translation-anchored enumeration has70200 three-record configurations. Each has three streaming events and three predecessor events, counting self-events consistently; the reciprocal full-configuration weight gives balance. The local reciprocal incident-bond weight fails on3168 configurations, with largest defect3.
For vacancy moves fixed to this local clock or to1, the supplied signed combinations of stationarity equations exclude nonnegative exchange rates at both weight scales. Separate combinations exclude record-by-record balance with the stated lower bounds.

For a system Mr=b with r>=0, any y with M^T y>=0 and b.y<0 is an obstruction, because y.Mr cannot be negative. Equality-row multipliers y need not themselves be nonnegative.

## Theorem T2 — exact feasible rules on two tori
The data give274 local rate classes on L=3 and353 on L=4, at each weight scale. Every translation-anchored three-record balance row evaluates to zero, and all lower bounds hold. The respective row counts are70200 and421848. Translation covariance covers unanchored configurations. This establishes separate finite-volume rules, not a common rule for every volume or for three records on the infinite lattice.

## Theorem T3 — four-record obstruction in the supplied class
Let A be the reconstructed outgoing-minus-incoming balance matrix, and ell equal1 on vacancy moves and0 elsewhere. Writing r=ell+s, s>=0, balance would imply A s=-A ell. The stored multipliers obey A^T y>=0 and y.Aell>0, a contradiction. Each row used is checked to have exactly four records.
This excludes the stated rates on L=3, with and without redraws, at both weight scales.

The other data consist of finite four-record configurations embedded in a small region. Their rows are compared on L=6 and9; the balance stencil also admits direct reconstruction on the infinite sparse lattice. The resulting same coefficient inequalities obstruct FORMAL local balance for those finite configurations. A fixed-four-particle translation-invariant weight on the infinite lattice is not a normalized probability measure. Finite-row contradictions do not establish an equilibrium measure or exclude larger neighborhoods, different weights, different events or degenerate motion.
