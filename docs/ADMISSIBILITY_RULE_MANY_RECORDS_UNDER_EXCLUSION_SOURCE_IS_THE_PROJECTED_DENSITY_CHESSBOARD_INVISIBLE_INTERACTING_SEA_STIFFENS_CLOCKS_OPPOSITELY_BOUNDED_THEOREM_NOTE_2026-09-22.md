## Premises and declared objects

Let H_w=phi sigma3 S phi on a finite ring, phi_x>0, u_x=2 log phi_x, and H2=H_w tensor I+I tensor H_w. P is the fixed position projector onto distinct-site configurations. Dynamics generated by P H2 P and the exchange rule are supplied choices. Let Psi_P=P Psi be fixed while rates vary. The displayed expectations are unnormalized quadratic forms; for normalized expectations divide every term by the same fixed nonzero squared norm.

## Theorem T1 — fixed-state source identity

Since `dH_w/du_x={P_x,H_w}/2`, differentiation of the two slots gives
`d<Psi_P|H2|Psi_P>/du_x=e_x^(2)`,
where `e_x^(2)=Re<Psi_P|(P_x H_w tensor I+I tensor P_x H_w)|Psi_P>`.
The projector is rate-independent, so it adds no derivative. Summing over sites uses sum_x P_x=I and gives sum_x e_x^(2)=<Psi_P|H2|Psi_P>. This also follows by a common rate scaling.

For phi_x replaced by phi_x(1+t), the fixed-state quadratic form F(t) has degree at most two. Its coefficient of t is (F(1)-F(-1))/2, and the derivative in u_x is HALF that coefficient, hence (F(1)-F(-1))/4. Evaluation at t=-1 is a polynomial identity, not an admission of a zero clock to the positive-rate physical domain.

The six-site runner verifies the identity at every site on one exact projected pair and exhibits a difference from the unprojected pair. The latter difference is example-specific; some states agree. If the state depends on the rates, its derivatives also enter unless an appropriate stationary eigenstate identity is established. No claim about a sign of the source or a clock-slowing law follows merely from this derivative.

## Theorem T2 — diagonal and common-translation symmetries

P commutes with all site-diagonal operators, including phi tensor phi, and with T tensor T because equality of particle positions is invariant under a common shift. On an EVEN ring the reciprocal choice phi_x=r^((-1)^x) leaves every hopping entry unchanged, so it also leaves P H2 P unchanged. At uniform rates H2 commutes with common translation; together with [P,T tensor T]=0 this proves the same symmetry of the compressed generator. Varying rates need not conserve that translation label.

## Theorem T3 — failure to preserve the subspace

Let M=S tensor I+I tensor S use the coin-scalar one-step momentum. On a ring with at least four sites, the antisymmetric opposite-coin adjacent-site vector
`|0,up;1,down>-|1,down;0,up>`
is in range P, but M sends nonzero amplitudes into same-site opposite-coin configurations. These configurations are allowed by coin-state antisymmetry and excluded by P, so [P,M]!=0 also in the antisymmetric sector. The runner checks this exact witness on six sites.

This does not by itself imply `[P H2 P,P M P]!=0`. The latter commutator is the relevant test for conservation of the compressed observable and is not asserted here. Noncommutation with P can coexist with conservation under particular Hamiltonians; for example if H2=M then the compressed observable equals the compressed Hamiltonian. The earlier universal momentum-loss claim is withdrawn.
