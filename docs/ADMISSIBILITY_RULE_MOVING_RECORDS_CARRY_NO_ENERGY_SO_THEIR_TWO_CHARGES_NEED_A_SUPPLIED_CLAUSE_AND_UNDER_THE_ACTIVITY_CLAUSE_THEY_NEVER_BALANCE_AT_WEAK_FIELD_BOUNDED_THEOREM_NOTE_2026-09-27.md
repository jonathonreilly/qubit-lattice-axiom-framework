# Moving records carry no energy, so their two charges need a supplied clause, and under the activity clause they never balance at weak field

**Date:** 2026-09-27
**Type:** bounded_theorem
**Status:** bounded-support (exact within blocks 110 and 95 as landed, at prescribed fields; a harvest of probe #9122, confirmed by an other-family referee in #9336; nothing adopted or registered; unaudited)

This note works within blocks 110 and 95 as landed on main (the curvature member's two charges, and the rates at which records move) and asks what a record that moves contributes to the charges; nothing is adopted and no gravitational claim is made.
No bridge, Born-weight, plane-or-sum or gravity statement enters this note as a premise; this note does not fire wake condition 1 of the parked statistical-bridge decision.

## Result up front

Block 110 (landed) showed that a body's rays match the comparator's exactly when its two charges agree, `P = Q`. The charges agree only when the content's hop energy pays for its slowed clocks. The charges are built from derivatives of a content energy. This note, a harvest of a probe result another model family has confirmed, asks what happens when the content is records that move.

- **T1: crossing at the member's factor is blind to the fields.**
  - Records that cross a bond at the member's factor `√(w_x w_y)/(χ_x χ_y)`, with block 95's heat-bath factor, have the stationary law `π ∝ W` whatever the clocks and lengths are.
  - The same holds at first order when the fields follow the records with even kernels.
  - So such records do not gather in their own field. A clump needs timing by the site a record leaves (block 97), which gives `W/Π w`.
- **T2: a record's charges need a supplied clause.**
  - Records are a process, not an energy, so block 110's derivatives have nothing to act on until an energy is supplied for them.
  - Two clauses both have the form block 110 needs: rest only, and rest plus the activity at the crossing factor.
  - On the same three records and fields they give `P − Q = −117/3280` and `P − Q = +3893397/2555120`: opposite signs.
- **T3: under the activity clause, never balanced at weak field.**
  - At weak field, `P/Q = 1 + μ⟨B⟩/(mN + μ⟨B⟩/2)`, where `B` counts the bonds with exactly one end occupied. This exceeds 1 whenever a record can move.
  - Under the uniform law on `3³` it is `251/101` for two records and `121/49` for three. Under the clocked gas's own law it is about `2.43` and `2.34`.
  - `P = Q` at weak field needs a hop energy that vanishes with the field. The rest-only clause does that, but block 110 gives it `P < Q` at every finite field.

In plain terms: the member's two charges need to know how much energy a record carries. A record that just moves around does not say. If it carries only a rest energy, the body's clock charge falls below its length charge. If it also carries the energy of its hopping, the clock charge rises above. Neither choice makes them equal, which is what bending like the comparator requires.

## Theorem T1 — crossing at the member's factor is blind to the fields

*Statement.*
- Let records cross to an empty neighbour at rate `κ_xy h/6`, where `κ_xy = √(w_x w_y)/(χ_x χ_y)`. Then `π(C) ∝ W(C)` is stationary, and in detailed balance, for every fixed positive clock and length field.
- Timed by the site a record leaves (rate `w_x h/6`), the law is `W(C)/Π_{z∈C} w_z` instead.
- If the fields follow the records, `u = Σ_r U(z − r)` and `χ − 1 = Σ_r Φ(z − r)` with even `U` and `Φ`, then `π ∝ W` stays stationary at first order.

*Proof.*
- `κ_xy` is symmetric in `x ↔ y`, so `W(C)κ_xy h(C, C′) = W(C′)κ_yx h(C′, C)`.
- At first order, the forward and backward log-rates of a move `C = D ∪ {x} → C′ = D ∪ {y}` differ by `U(y − x) − U(x − y)` and the same with `Φ`. This is zero for even kernels.
- The runner checks all three statements exactly on `3³` with three records: random rational clocks `w = s²`, lengths and an even pair weight, over 600 configurations and every move (B1), and 300 configurations with the torus kernel (B2). ∎

## Theorem T2 — a record's charges need a supplied clause

*Statement.*
- Both clauses have the homogeneity block 110 T2(e) uses. The crossing factor has degree 1 in the rates and degree `−2` in the lengths.
- On the records `{0, 1, 3}` of `3³`, with clocks `(19/20)²` and lengths `41/40` at the records:
  - rest only gives `P − Q = −117/3280`;
  - the activity clause (`m = μ = 1`) gives `P − Q = +3893397/2555120`.
- So block 110's derivatives do not fix a record's `(e, τ)`.

*Proof.*
- For the activity clause, each occupied–empty bond term `b = (μ/2)κ_xy` gives `∂b/∂u = b/2` and `∂b/∂λ = −b/2` at each end. So each end gets `τ = b/2` and the same amount added to `e`, as in block 110's `e = r + τ`.
- The charges follow from block 110 T2(b) (C1). ∎

## Theorem T3 — under the activity clause, never balanced at weak field

*Statement.*
- At weak field (`κ = 1`), `Στ = μB/2` and `Σe = mN + μB/2`. So `P/Q = 1 + μ⟨B⟩/(mN + μ⟨B⟩/2)`, which exceeds 1 whenever `⟨B⟩ > 0`.
- On `3³` with `m = μ = 1`:
  - under the uniform law, `⟨B⟩ = 6N(V − N)/(V − 1)`, so `P/Q = 251/101` for `N = 2` and `121/49` for `N = 3`;
  - under the clocked gas's law `q^{486 Σ G}` at `q = 3/2`, `P/Q = 536512330153/220881143983 ≈ 2.429` for `N = 2`, and an exact rational `≈ 2.335` for `N = 3`. Both lie above 1 and below the uniform values.
- `P/Q = 1` at weak field exactly when `Στ/Σe → 0`: the rest-only clause, or a jammed gas with `⟨B⟩ = 0`. The rest-only clause has `P < Q` at every finite field (block 110 T2(c)).

*Proof.* By block 110 T2(c) with T2's charges at `κ = 1`. The averages are exact enumerations over all configurations. `G` has common denominator 486 on `3³`, so the clocked gas's weights are exact rationals (D1, D2). ∎
