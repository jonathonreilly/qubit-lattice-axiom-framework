## Theorem T1 — the one-neighbour operator and the lean's massless point

*Statement.*
- (a) `K₁` multiplies every degree-`ℓ` harmonic by `λ_ℓ = ½∫_{−1}^{1}e^{βt}P_ℓ(t)dt/Z`. In particular `λ₀ = 1`, `λ₁ = coth β − 1/β = β/3 − β³/45 + 2β⁵/945 − …`, and `λ_{ℓ+1} = λ_{ℓ−1} − (2ℓ+1)λ_ℓ/β`.
- (b) `λ_ℓ/λ_{ℓ−1}` is the mean of `t` under the weight `e^{βt}(1 − t²)^{ℓ−1}` on `[−1, 1]`. So `1 > λ₁ > λ₂ > … > 0` for every `β > 0`.
- (c) At the uniform field the map's derivative is `Σ_y (K₁ − P₀)` on departures of zero mean. A wave `k` in the channel `ℓ` has eigenvalue `λ_ℓ(6 − E(k))`.
- (d) `6λ₁ = 1` at exactly one `β₀`, and `5085/10000 < β₀ < 5086/10000`. For `β < β₀` every channel has a mass term; the lean's, `m² = (1 − 6λ₁)/λ₁`, is the smallest.

*Proof.*
- (a) `K₁` commutes with rotations, so it acts on each degree as a multiple of the identity, given by the zonal integral (imported at definition level). The recurrence is the three-term relation of those integrals (family B, symbolic to `ℓ = 4`).
- (b) `λ_ℓ Z = β^ℓ/(2^{ℓ+1}ℓ!) ∫(1−t²)^ℓ e^{βt}dt`. One integration by parts turns the ratio into the stated mean, which lies in `(0, 1)` for `β > 0` (family B, symbolic to `ℓ = 4`).
- (c) At `π_y = 1 + δ_y`, the product's first-order term is `Σ_y K₁δ_y`, and `K₁` keeps the mean.
- (d)
  - `dλ₁/dβ = 1/β² − 1/sinh²β > 0`, because `sinh β > β`.
  - Write `λ₁ = (β cosh β − sinh β)/(β sinh β)`. Numerator and denominator are series of positive rational multiples of powers of `β`, and their tails are bounded by geometric series.
  - At `β = 5085/10000` the upper bound on `6λ₁` is below `1`; at `5086/10000` the lower bound is above `1` (family B, exact rationals).
  - By (b), the higher channels have smaller eigenvalues. ∎

## Theorem T3 — the turn of the lean has no mass term, and only content off the lean feeds it

*Statement.*
- (a) At every `β > β₀` at which a leaning sea `F` exists, the turn `δ(s) = √(1 − t²)F′(t)cos φ` is an eigenvector of the per-neighbour linearization with eigenvalue exactly `1/6`. Its channel has no mass term: a wave `k` in it has eigenvalue `1 − E(k)/6` under the six-neighbour map, and its response to a source is `6/E(k)` times the source.
- (b) A record of content `s₀` replaces its neighbour's factor `g(t)` by `e^{βs·s₀}`, up to a constant.
  - The part of `log(e^{βs·s₀}/g)` that turns once around the lean is exactly `β√(1−t²)√(1−t₀²)cos(φ − φ₀) = β s_⊥·s₀⊥`.
  - It vanishes for every `s` if and only if the record is aligned with the lean (`s₀ = ±n`).
  - An aligned record leaves the problem unchanged by every rotation about the lean, so it feeds the turn nothing at any order. Correspondingly `P_ℓ¹(±1) = 0` for every `ℓ` (checked for `ℓ = 1..7`).
- (c) A source unchanged by the rotations about the lean has no part turning once around it, and feeds only the order `m = 0`, at every order. This covers:
  - a body's record count, with its contents averaged over those rotations;
  - a record aligned with the lean.

  A record whose content is drawn from the sea's own odds feeds the turn at random, with zero mean at first order.

*Proof.*
- (a) The map commutes with rotations of all contents, so every turned sea `F(R⁻¹s)` is again a solution. Differentiating along a one-parameter family of turns gives an eigenvector with eigenvalue `1` of the six-neighbour map.
  - Explicitly, `K₁` acts by `λ_ℓ` on degree `ℓ` for every azimuthal order, and `P_ℓ¹ = −√(1−t²)P_ℓ′`. So `K₁δ = √(1−t²)g′(t)cos φ`.
  - With `F = g⁶/⟨g⁶⟩`, `6F(K₁δ)/g = 6g⁵g′√(1−t²)cos φ/⟨g⁶⟩ = δ`.
  - The mean term vanishes because `cos φ` averages to zero around the lean (family D, symbolic).
- (b) `s·s₀ = tt₀ + √(1−t²)√(1−t₀²)cos(φ − φ₀)`, and `log g(t)` does not depend on `φ` (family D).
- (c) A rotation by `θ` about `n` multiplies the order-`m` part by `e^{imθ}`, so an invariant source has only `m = 0`. For a drawn content, `∫F(s₀)e^{βs·s₀}dσ(s₀)/Z = K₁F = g`: on average the sea's own odds give back its factor. ∎

## Theorem T5 — vacancies at the neutral scale: mass gets a first-order channel in the ordered sea, and the lean's birth turns abrupt

*Statement.* Count "no record" as a seventh possibility, at `g = 1`.
- (a) The content map at density `ρ` is the vacancy-free map with every `λ_ℓ` replaced by `ρλ_ℓ`. The sea leans when `6ρλ₁ > 1`.
- (b) In the ordered sea, a record changes each neighbour's odds of holding a record at first order, with strength `ρ(1 − ρ)⟨(K₁F − 1)/b⟩_F = (1 − ρ)(1 − ⟨b⁵⟩/⟨b⁶⟩)`.
  - This is positive whenever the sea leans; at small lean it is `(5/3)ρ²λ₁²a²(1 − ρ) + …`.
  - It is zero in the isotropic sea (block 42 T5).
  - It enters only the order `m = 0`, so the turn stays exactly at `1/6`.
- (c) At fixed formation odds `z`, the density rises with the lean by `5ρ³(1 − ρ)λ₁²a²`, and the cubic coefficient becomes `c₃(ρλ) + 30λ₁³ρ³(1 − ρ)`.
  - On the ordering line `6ρλ₁ = 1`, this coefficient equals `Φ/(216λ₁(λ₁ − λ₂))`, with `Φ = 12λ₁² + 8λ₁λ₂ − 5λ₁ + 5λ₂`.
  - `Φ` changes sign between `β = 9579/10000` and `9580/10000`. Below that point the lean is born gradually at fixed `z`. Above it the cubic term is positive and the birth is abrupt.
  - A scan of `(β₀, 30]` finds no other sign change.

*Proof.*
- (a) `K₁1 = 1`, so `b = 1 + ρK₁(ν − 1)`.
- (b) From `ρ_x/(1 − ρ_x) = z⟨Π_y b_y⟩` follows `δρ_x = ρ(1 − ρ)Σ_y⟨δb_y/b⟩_F`, with `δb_y = δρ_y(K₁F − 1) + ρK₁δν_y`.
  - `K₁F − 1 = (b − 1)/ρ` and `F = b⁶/⟨b⁶⟩` give the identity.
  - For positivity, `⟨b⟩ = 1` and the power means give `⟨b⁶⟩ ≥ ⟨b⁵⟩^{6/5} ≥ ⟨b⁵⟩`, strictly unless `b` is constant.
  - A change of density is unchanged by rotations about the lean, so by T3(c) it enters only `m = 0`.
- (c) The expansion is symbolic, with the density shift fixed by `z`. The sign change uses rational bounds on `λ₁` at the two points; `Φ` falls with `λ₁` when `β < 2` (family C). ∎

*Executed (control; a1's numbers reproduced by another method).*

- At `ρ = 1.001ρ_c`, the ordered branch needs `log z − log z_c = +2.9·10⁻⁵` at `β = 0.95` and `−6.3·10⁻⁶` at `β = 0.96`.
