## Result up front

Block 103, as landed, found a long-range channel in possibility's odds on the sphere menu: the turn of the lean, massless at every coupling past a point `β₀`, and charged by content, not by record count. Block 42, as landed, found the six-axis odds' massless surface `5p = 7q + 4r`. This note asks whether the turn is the only massless channel, how it moves when the odds are given a dynamics, and what a record's lean does far away on the massless surface.

- **T1: only the turn is massless.** For `β` past `β₀` and any leaning sea that does not decrease along the lean, every mode of azimuthal order `|m| ≥ 1` other than the turn is massive. As `β` grows the masses tend to `6ⁿ − 6`: the turn stays massless, the lean's size and the `m = ±2` modes go to `30`, and the next modes to `210`. Order `m = 0` is massive in every executed case, but it is not proved here.
- **T2: the turn diffuses and precesses; it does not ring.** Under a supplied relaxation `Γ` plus precession `Ω` about the lean:
  - The ordered sea's turns have exactly `ω(k) = (±Ω − iΓ) E(k)/6`. These are circular waves with `ω ∝ k²`, with the same damping ratio at every `k`, and they are not sound.
  - A staggered sea (`β < 0`) has a sound-like branch, `ω = ±Ω|k|/√3`, but only with no relaxation. With any `Γ > 0` its long waves are diffusive.
- **T3: the six-axis lean has a cubic term.** Block 42's map, expanded exactly to third order, gives for slowly varying fields `−Δv + m² v + u v³ = source`, with `u = 90 l₁²(1 − 36 l₂)/(1 − 6 l₂)`. On the massless surface `5p = 7q + 4r` this is `u = (5/2)(4r − 7q)/(r − q)`, which is `5/2` at `(3,1,2)`.
- **T4: a record's lean is 1/r only up to a logarithm.** On the massless surface `A = r v` obeys `A_ττ − A_τ = u A³` in `τ = log r`. Its decaying solutions satisfy `A⁻² = 2u log r − 3u log log r + C + o(1)`. So a weak record's `1/r` lean holds out to about `r₀ exp(1/(2u A₀²))` and then fades logarithmically.

In plain terms: the odds of the unformed sites have exactly one long-reach channel, the direction the local lean points. If that direction is given a dynamics of its own, turning and relaxing, it spreads like heat, with a twist. It rings like sound only in a special alternating pattern and only with no damping. On the six-axis menu, at the setting where the lean reaches far, the lean also pushes back on itself. A single record's influence then falls off like one over distance only for a while, and then a little faster, by a logarithm. None of these long-range pieces is a clean one-over-distance field with waves of its own.

## Theorem T1 — only the turn is massless

*Statement.*
- The kernel `e^{βs·b}` splits by azimuthal order `m` into the positive kernels `e^{βtt′} I_m(βrr′)`, with `t = s·n` and `r = √(1 − t²)`.
- For `β > β₀` and a leaning sea non-decreasing in `t`, every eigenvalue of `A` of order `|m| ≥ 1` other than the turn's `1/6` has modulus below `1/6`.
- As `β → ∞`, the sea tends in the tangent plane to a Gaussian of curvature `5β/2`. `A` tends to the chain `y | x` Gaussian with mean `x/6` and variance `1/(6β)`. Its eigenvalues are `6⁻ⁿ`, so the masses `(1 − 6μ)/μ` tend to `6ⁿ − 6`.

*Proof.*
- Order one: the turn's profile is positive, so by the imported positive-kernel theorem it is the simple top eigenvalue.
- Orders `|m| ≥ 2`: the kernels are dominated by order one's through the imported order comparison.
- Monotone seas stay monotone under `K₁`, by reflection through the plane normal to a meridian.
- The runner checks the rest:
  - the sector kernels, through `x¹²`;
  - the order comparison at three points, by exact rational bounds;
  - the Gaussian limit, its curvature map `a → aβ/(2a + β)` and fixed point `5β/2`;
  - the chain's polynomial eigenmodes for `n ≤ 3`, and the masses `0, 30, 210` (B1, B2). ∎

## Theorem T2 — the turn under a precession

*Statement.*
- Precession about the lean vanishes on every axial profile, so the ordered and staggered seas are fixed points for every `Ω`.
- The ordered sea's turns obey `τ̇ = −(Γ + Ω n×)(τ − τ̄)`, where `τ̄` is the mean over the six neighbours. So `ω(k) = (±Ω − iΓ) E(k)/6`, with `E = Σ(2 − 2 cos k_j) = 6(1 − γ)`.
- The staggered sea's two sublattices precess in opposite senses, which gives `λ² + 2Γλ + (Γ² + Ω²)(1 − γ²) = 0`.
  - At `Γ = 0`, `ω = ±Ω√(1 − γ²) = ±Ω|k|/√3 + O(k³)` along an axis.
  - For `Γ > 0` the slow root is `−(Γ² + Ω²)(1 − γ²)/(2Γ) + …`, which is diffusive.

*Proof.* The turn's per-neighbour weight is exactly `1/6` (block 103 T3), which gives the mean over neighbours. The rest is linear algebra, checked symbolically (C1). ∎

## Theorem T3 — the six-axis lean's cubic term

*Statement.*
- A site whose neighbours carry leans `v_y` and quadrupoles `D_y` along the record's axis gets
  - `v′ = l₁ S₁ + 3 l₁³(Σv³ − S₁Σv²) − 2 l₁ l₂(ΣvD − S₁ΣD) + O(t⁵)`;
  - `D′ = l₂ ΣD + (3/2) l₁²(S₁² − Σv²) + O(t⁴)`.
- For slow fields the quadrupole is slaved, `D = 45 l₁² v²/(1 − 6l₂)`. The lean then obeys `−Δv + m² v + u v³ = source`, with `u = 90 l₁²(1 − 36 l₂)/(1 − 6 l₂)`.
- On the massless surface, `l₁ = 1/6` and `u = (5/2)(4r − 7q)/(r − q)`, which is `5/2` at `(3,1,2)`. Where the quadrupole is massive (`r > q`), `u > 0` iff `4r > 7q`.

*Proof.* Expand the exact normalized product. The runner does this exactly for three weight triples and two neighbourhoods. It also checks the slaving, the coefficient and its value on the surface symbolically (D1). The gradient expansion itself is an assumption: the probes' torus control agrees with it to `4·10⁻⁴`, which supports it but does not prove it. ∎

## Theorem T4 — the logarithmic far field

*Statement.*
- For `v = A(r)/r`, `Δv = A″/r`, so away from the source `A″ = uA³/r²`. In `τ = log r` this is `A_ττ − A_τ = u A³`.
- Its decaying solutions lie on the centre manifold `A_τ = −uA³ + 3u²A⁵ − 24u³A⁷ + …`, so `(A⁻²)_τ = 2u − 6u²A² + …` and `A⁻² = 2u log r − 3u log log r + C + o(1)`.

*Proof.* The chain rule and a series solution, checked through order 8 (E1). The centre-manifold theorem is imported for the existence of the branch. ∎
