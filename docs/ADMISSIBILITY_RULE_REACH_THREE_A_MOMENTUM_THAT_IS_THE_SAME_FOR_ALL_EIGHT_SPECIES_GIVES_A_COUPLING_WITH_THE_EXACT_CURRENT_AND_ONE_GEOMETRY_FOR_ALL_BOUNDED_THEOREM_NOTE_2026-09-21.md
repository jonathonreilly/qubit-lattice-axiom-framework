# Reach three: a momentum that is the same for all eight species gives a coupling with the exact current and one geometry for all

**Date:** 2026-09-21
**Type:** bounded_theorem
**Status:** bounded-support (exact statements about block 54's supplied walk and a relabelling generated with a two-step momentum; nothing adopted or registered; unaudited)

This note works within supplied clauses for amplitudes on the lattice with the qubit as their coin and for the coupling of a bond strain as a relabelling's deformation; it reports a relabelling generated with a momentum that is the same for all of the walk's species, and what it costs; nothing is adopted and no gravitational claim is made.
No bridge, Born-weight, plane-or-sum or gravity statement enters this note as a premise; this note does not fire wake condition 1 of the parked statistical-bridge decision.

## Result up front

Block 68 (open PR #8599) put a weight on each side of block 63's fork. The nearest-neighbour frame shows all eight species of the walk the same lengths, but the walk's conserved current is not its response. The relabelling's second-neighbour coupling has that current as its response exactly, but shows the species different lengths — a stretch is a squeeze for a species reflected along that axis. The cause was named there: a relabelling was generated with the lattice momentum `sin k`, which has the sign `D_j` on species `n`. This note asks whether a relabelling generated with a momentum that has *no* such sign escapes.

1. **Not with reach one.** A momentum that is every species' own wave number must vanish at `k = 0` and at `k = π` with slope `+1` at both. No combination of `sin k`, `cos k` and a constant does (T1).
2. **With reach two, yes.** The symmetric difference over *two* steps, symbol `½ sin 2k`, does. It commutes with the walk and is conserved (T2).
3. **The relabelling it generates** deforms the walk by a term on the bonds with **reach three**, and the response to it is an exactly conserved current — block 63's, with the two-step momentum in place of the one-step one (T3).
4. **One geometry for all eight.** A strain coupled this way shows *every* species the same inverse metric, `(1 + B)ᵀ(1 + B)`: the same lengths and, unlike the frame, the same angles. The bending is `1 + β` times the fall for all (T4).

So the fork is a ladder. **Reach one:** one set of lengths, books slightly off. **Reach two:** exact books, a different geometry for each kind. **Reach three:** exact books and one geometry. Executed: two packets of different kinds deflect `−10.57` and `−10.57` under the reach-three coupling, where reach two gave `−12.64` and `+12.64`. The price, beyond reach, is larger lattice corrections: the stretch is seen through `cos² k`.

In plain terms: the trouble in block 68 was that the lattice's one-step notion of momentum points the wrong way for half the kinds of walker. A two-step notion of momentum points the right way for all of them. Let the sites be relabelled with *that* momentum, couple lengths to the walker the way such a relabelling would, and both good properties hold at once. The rule then looks three steps along the lattice instead of one.

## Theorem T1 — not with reach one

*Statement.* There are no real `a, b, c` with `f(k) = a sin k + b cos k + c` satisfying `f(0) = f(π) = 0` and `f'(0) = f'(π) = 1`.

*Proof.* `f(0) = b + c`, `f(π) = −b + c`, so `b = c = 0`; then `f'(0) = a` and `f'(π) = −a`. ∎

## Theorem T2 — a momentum that is the same for all species

*Statement.* `P_j = S_jC_j` has the symbol `½ sin 2k_j`, which vanishes at `k_j = 0, π` with slope `+1` at both; `[H, P_j] = 0`; `d⟨P_j⟩/dt = 0` for every state.

*Proof.* `S_j` and `C_j` are functions of the shifts, which commute with `H`. ∎

Near species `n`, `½ sin 2(πn_j + q_j) = q_j + …`: the species' own wave number, with no sign.

## Theorem T3 — the relabelling it generates

*Statement.* (a) `i[H, G] = Σ_a Σ_j σ_a ½{C_a[d_aξ_j], P_j}`. (b) It carries an amplitude on one site to sites three steps away. (c) `⟨ψ|i[H, G]|ψ⟩ = Σ_{a,j,x}(d_aξ_j)(x) K_a^j(x → x + e_a)` for every state; on stationary states the left side vanishes, so `K` is divergence-free there.

*Proof.* As block 63 T2: `[S_a, P_j] = 0` and `i[S_a, ξ_j] = C_a[d_aξ_j]`. `C_a` moves one step along `a`, `P_j` two along `j`. For (c), `⟨G⟩ = Σ_{j,x} ξ_j(x) Re ψ†(P_jψ)(x)` since `P_j` is Hermitian, and `d⟨G⟩/dt = ⟨i[H, G]⟩`: for a state in motion the statement is the local conservation law of the two-step momentum density `Re ψ†(P_jψ)` with `K` as its current. ∎

Block 64 T1's statement — the field equations of any function of the strains' curls are divergence-free identically — does not involve the content; with this coupling the strain is sourced by `K`, and the static equations are consistent at first order for every stationary state, as before.

## Theorem T4 — one geometry for all eight

*Statement.* For a uniform strain, `H(k) = Σ_a σ_a[s_a + c_a Σ_j B_a^j s_jc_j]` and `H² = Σ_a[s_a + c_a Σ_j B_a^j s_jc_j]²`. Near `k = πn + q`: `H² = qᵀ(1 + B)ᵀ(1 + B)q + O(q⁴)` for every `n`. With `ℓ = (w̄/w)^β`, `1 + b = 1/ℓ`, the ratio of bending to fall is `1 + β` for every species.

*Proof.* `s = Dq`, `c = D`, `s_jc_j = D_j²q_j = q_j`: `s_a + c_a(Bq)_a = D_a[q + Bq]_a`, and `D² = 1`. ∎

Compare block 68: the frame gives `DgD` (angles mirrored), reach two gives `(1 + BD)ᵀ(1 + BD)` (lengths species-dependent).
