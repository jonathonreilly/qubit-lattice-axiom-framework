## Theorem T1 — adding a bond
Global cubic symmetry makes each one-site marginal uniform and a two-site marginal constant on the three pair orbits. Write its normalized transition matrix as 6mu, with eigenvalues 1,t1,t2 of multiplicities 1,3,2. Adding a bond multiplies Q by the expectation of W/c0, namely 1+3 t1 lambda1+2 t2 lambda2, by the trace pairing of the common three invariant sectors. For sites in different components mu is the product of uniform marginals, so the factor is one, or g with the bond scale. Leaf elimination gives Q=1 for forests. Closing an odd-length path of length l gives 1+3 lambda1^(l+1)+2 lambda2^(l+1)>=1. This does not prove a sign for every multipath correlation.

## Theorem T4 — three background spectra
Let h=sigma dot s, s_j=sin(k_j) on the continuous zone. A uniform coin background c sigma_z has E^2=s_x^2+s_y^2+(s_z+c)^2: zeros exist iff |c|<=1. For |c|>1 its distance from zero is |c|-1.
The doubled staggered coin matrix [[h,c sigma_z],[c sigma_z,-h]] has characteristic polynomial (E^2-|s|^2-c^2)^2-4c^2(s_x^2+s_y^2). Its squared energies are s_z^2+(sqrt(s_x^2+s_y^2) +/- |c|)^2. A zero exists iff |c|<=sqrt(2); for larger |c| the distance is |c|-sqrt(2). Finite momentum grids need not realize a continuous-zone zero.
The occupancy matrix [[h+cI/2,cI/2],[cI/2,-h+cI/2]] obeys (H-cI/2)^2=(|s|^2+c^2/4)I. Its empty interval is between min(0,c) and max(0,c), of width |c|, collapsing at c=0. These examples do not classify every gapping mechanism.
