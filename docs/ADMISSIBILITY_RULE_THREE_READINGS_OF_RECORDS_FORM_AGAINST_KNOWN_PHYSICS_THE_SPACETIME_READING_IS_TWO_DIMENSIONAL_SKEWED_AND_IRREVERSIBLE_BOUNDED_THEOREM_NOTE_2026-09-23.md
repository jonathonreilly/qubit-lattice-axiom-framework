## Theorem T1 — finite layer identities
On the 4 by 4 level torus with opposite unit contents, the three-state cycle in the runner has forward/backward ratio exp(8 beta). Row normalizations cancel around the cycle. Thus the positive kernel is not reversible when beta is nonzero. At beta=0 it is reversible; small-period aliases can also remove the asymmetric stencil. The symmetric-past comparison has exponent zero, which alone is not a proof of reversibility for arbitrary rules.

The directed step has mean (1/3,1/3), covariance [[2/9,-1/9],[-1/9,2/9]] and centered third moments 2/27 and -1/27. Its level lattice has rank two and its m-step support has (m+1)(m+2)/2 points. The separate three-dimensional ball of radius m has (2m+1)(2m²+2m+3)/3 points. This ball describes at-most-m steps, or walks with holding; exact-m nearest-neighbor walks also have a parity restriction. These are geometrical counts, not a physical dimension or a continuum parity theorem.

## Theorem T2 — finite source differences
Define P_m(b,c)=m!/[b!c!(m-b-c)!3^m], zero outside its support. The runner computes D_N(y)=sum from n=1 to N of [P_3n(y+(n,n))-P_3n(-y+(n,n))]. At N=10,20,40 it is negative for y=(1,1), positive for (2,-1), and zero for (1,0),(0,1),(1,-1). The zero examples cancel termwise by permuting the three factorial arguments. These are finite differences. Neither a finite infinite-source potential nor a nonzero limiting odd part is established by these truncations.

## Theorem T3 — supplied motion and a correlation bound
For positive configuration weight W and symmetric proposals q(C,C'), rates q W(C')/[W(C)+W(C')] obey detailed balance with W: multiplying either directed rate by its initial weight gives the same expression. The ring fixture checks all allowed two-record moves. Formation is absent; a separately added positive one-way birth transition would violate detailed balance for any positive stationary law on both endpoints.

If P is self-adjoint in a stationary probability inner product, translations tau_r are unitary and commute with P, then
|<f,P^(2n)tau_r f>|=|<P^n f,tau_r P^n f>|<=||P^n f||².
Use centered f for connected correlations. This bounds stationary autocorrelations at even lag; it does not forbid secondary moving peaks, cross-correlations, trajectories or every wave mechanism. The exact five-site ring examples locate selected peaks at lags two and four only.
