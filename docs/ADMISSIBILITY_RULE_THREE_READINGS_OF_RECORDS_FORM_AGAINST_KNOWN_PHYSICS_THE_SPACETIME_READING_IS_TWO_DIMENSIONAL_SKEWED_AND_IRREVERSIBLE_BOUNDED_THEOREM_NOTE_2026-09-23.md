**Verdict.** Reading (i) is the furthest from known physics. By counting it is a two-dimensional world. Its law carries its own arrow, its propagation is skewed, and a resting source's field is logarithmic and not reciprocal. Reading (ii) matches the most benchmarks by proof, but only through a clause that contradicts the memo's text. Its "1/r" is the ordered state's massless transverse mode, whose charge is a direction, and detailed balance forbids it any waves. In plain terms: if the lattice is the whole of spacetime, the world it makes is flat in one dimension too few, has a built-in direction of time, and pulls unevenly. If the lattice is space and records wander through it, the world has the right number of dimensions and a natural arrow, from records being written and never erased, but the records alone do not yet make anything like gravity or light; that part still comes from the walker and its clocks, which are assumptions laid on top.

## Theorem T1 — reading (i) has an arrow, a skew and a triangular cone

*Statement.* In reading (i) the level chain is reversible with respect to no law: on the `4 × 4` plane torus the cycle `a → b → −a → a` has forward over backward exponent `8`. The level walk's centred step has covariance `[[2/9, −1/9], [−1/9, 2/9]]` and third cumulants `T₁₁₁ = 2/27`, `T₁₁₂ = −1/27`, so the propagation is skewed. After three levels the causal cone is a triangle of ten sites that point reflection does not preserve. The symmetric pasts of readings (ii) and (iii) have exponent `0`, zero third cumulant and centrally symmetric cones.

*Proof.* The cycle exponent is `2B(b, a)` with `B(b, a) = 4([d₀ ∈ N] − [−d₀ ∈ N])` (block 90 T1); for `N = {0, −e₁, −e₂}` take `d₀ = −e₁`. The cumulants are sums over three steps. Family B. ∎

## Theorem T2 — reading (i)'s resting source is two-dimensional and not reflection symmetric

*Statement.* In the linear theory of reading (i), the field of a source at rest (its worldline along `(1,1,1)`, at level-plane point `(n, n)` three `n` levels back) is `Φ(y) = Σ_n P_{3n}(y + (n, n))`, `P_m` the level walk's law. Its reflection-odd part `Φ(y) − Φ(−y)` is exactly zero on the three mirror lines and nonzero along the step directions: partial sums to `n = 10, 20, 40` at `y = (1, 1)` are about `−0.0665, −0.0679, −0.0683`, and the opposite at `(2, −1)`.

*Proof.* Termwise: the axis swaps fix the mirror lines. Exact partial sums in family C. ∎

*Executed* (control `specs/supervisor_control_block94_readings.py`, output in `.out.txt`). `Φ(0) − Φ(y)` grows like `log|y|` with slope `0.537 … 0.547` against the two-dimensional value `√3/π = 0.551` (sums to `n = 6000`), and the odd part falls like `1/|y|` (`−0.068, −0.032, −0.015` at `|y|` doubling). ## Theorem T3 — reading (iii): motion reversible, formation the only arrow

*Statement.* A record choosing between its place and an empty neighbouring place in proportion to the pair weights is in detailed balance with the static law's weight. Checked at every configuration and move of a ring of four with two records and two vacancies, `t` and `c` symbolic. Formation fills an empty site and is never undone, because records are permanent.

## Theorem T4 — the dimension of space in each reading

*Statement.* In reading (i) a level set of `Z³` is a rank-2 lattice, and the sites reachable in `m` levels number `(m + 1)(m + 2)/2`: two space dimensions. In readings (ii) and (iii) the reachable ball `|d|₁ ≤ m` holds `(2m + 1)(2m² + 2m + 3)/3` sites: three space dimensions.

*Proof.* Counting, exact for `m = 1 … 12` (family E). ∎

## Theorem T5 — no fronts under detailed balance

*Statement.* For a reversible translation-invariant chain `P` with stationary law `π` and any centred local observable `f`, `|⟨f, P^{2n} τ_r f⟩_π| ≤ ⟨f, P^{2n} f⟩_π` for every shift `r` and every `n`. A disturbance's imprint is largest at its origin at every even lag, so no front moves out: the record layers of readings (ii) and (iii) carry no waves. Exactly on a ring of five at `t = e^β = 2`, the lag-2 and lag-4 correlations of the reversible light-cone chain peak at shift `0`. The one-sided past's peak moves to shifts `−1` and `−2`.

*Proof.* `⟨f, P^{2n} τ_r f⟩_π = ⟨P^n f, τ_r P^n f⟩_π` by self-adjointness and commutation with translations. The inner-product inequality, with `τ_r` unitary on `L²(π)`, gives the bound. Family E (the exact stationary law of the 32-state chain and exact matrix powers). ∎
