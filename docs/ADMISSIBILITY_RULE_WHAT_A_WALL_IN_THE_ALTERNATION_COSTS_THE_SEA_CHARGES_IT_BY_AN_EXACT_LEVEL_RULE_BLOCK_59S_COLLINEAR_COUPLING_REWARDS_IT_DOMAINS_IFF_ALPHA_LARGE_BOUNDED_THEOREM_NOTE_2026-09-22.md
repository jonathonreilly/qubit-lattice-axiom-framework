## Premises and declared objects
Use the one-axis pure hop of PR8660, with uniform alternation or its equal-half reversal. Separately define f(d,z)=sqrt(z+d^2)+sqrt(z+1)-sqrt(z)-sqrt(z+1+d^2), z>=0. The law cost is the supplied quadratic F=-sum_b u_b(Mu)_b/2 in rate deviations u, with collinear, perpendicular and parallel coefficients alpha,beta,gamma.

## Theorem T1 — three finite spectral replacements
At d=3/10 on rings of length 4,8,12 the squared spectrum under equal-half reversal replaces two copies each of d^2 and 1 by two copies each of 0 and 1+d^2. The one-axis negative energy sum changes by 1+d-sqrt(1+d^2)>0 for each of these fixtures.
This is not a rule for every even ring. For L=6 the uniform squared spectrum does not contain 1, so the claimed replacement cannot even be performed. The runner checks this exception. General d or lengths are not proved by these three numerical algebraic fixtures.

## Theorem T2 — positive defined comparator
For d!=0, f(d,z)>0 because the increment sqrt(z+d^2)-sqrt(z) strictly decreases with z, and f is that increment minus its translate by one. At d=0 it vanishes. Thus sigma(d)>0 for nonzero d; at d=1, z=2 and sigma(1)=2sqrt(3)-sqrt(2)-2.
If a longitudinal spectrum satisfies the stated replacement, separability makes the coined sea's pair-wall difference divided by two wall areas equal the corresponding finite transverse average of f. This conditional relation does not certify a general longitudinal limit or arbitrary-wall tension. The endpoint d=1 allows zero amplitudes.

## Theorem T3 — specified quadratic law difference
On a compatible even cubic torus, change only the x-axis alternation to its equal-half reversal. A collinear sign slip changes the two adjacent bond products; summing F gives -2alpha d^2 per wall area. Parallel bonds keep their relative values, and perpendicular cross terms cancel on the even transverse patterns. The runner evaluates the full 4^3 real-space form at d=3/10 for mixed coefficients and each separately; the uniform cost is 6(alpha+2beta)d^2 per site.
This counting applies to the stated straight walls and supplied linear-rate quadratic form, not every profile or nonlinear completion.

## Theorem T4 — comparison of two prescribed configurations
For a model in which the sea contribution really is sigma(d), the defined cost sigma(d)-2alpha d^2 is negative iff alpha>sigma(d)/(2d^2), d!=0. At d=1 this threshold lies strictly between 2/100 and 3/100 by exact algebraic comparison. This is a conditional pair-energy comparison at fixed amplitude. It does not prove necessary or sufficient conditions for domains to form, full stability, wall density or a global minimizer.
