## Statement

Let `Λ = (Z/L)^4` with `L` even, let `D = D[U]` be the canonical
massless staggered Dirac operator on `Λ` coupled to U(1) link
phases `U_μ(x)`, and let `ε = diag(ε(x))_{x ∈ Λ}` with
`ε(x) = (-1)^{x_1+x_2+x_3+x_4}`.

**(W1) Heat-kernel Jacobian is linear in `α`.** Under the chiral
rotation
```text
    χ_x  ↦  exp(i α(x) ε(x)) χ_x ,
    χ̄_x  ↦  χ̄_x exp(i α(x) ε(x))                                      (1)
```
the Grassmann measure transforms as `Dχ̄ Dχ ↦ J[α, U] · Dχ̄ Dχ`
with heat-kernel-regularized log-Jacobian
```text
    log J[α, U]  =  -2 i  Σ_{x ∈ Λ}  α(x) · ε(x) · T_t[U]_x ,         (2)
    T_t[U]_x     :=  ⟨ x | exp(-t D†D[U]) | x ⟩  ,    t > 0 .          (3)
```
The map `α ↦ log J[α, U]` is `R`-linear in `α`, hence
`A[α_1 + α_2, U] = A[α_1, U] + A[α_2, U]` for all real `α_1, α_2`.

**(W3) `t`-independent integer-valued lattice index.** For every
`t > 0`,
```text
    A[1, U]  :=  Σ_{x ∈ Λ}  ε(x) · T_t[U]_x  =  n_+(D) - n_-(D)  ,    (4)
```
where `n_±(D)` are the multiplicities of `ε = ±1` zero modes of
`D†D[U]`. The expression (4) is independent of `t > 0`,
integer-valued, and **gauge-invariant**: under any U(1) gauge
rotation `U_μ(x) ↦ G(x)^* U_μ(x) G(x+μ̂)`, both `σ(D†D)` and
`A[1, U]` are unchanged.

**(C-int) Integer-cocycle corollary.** Let `L_ct[U; x]` be any local
lattice Lagrangian (depending on finitely many links touching site
`x`). Its gauge variation `δ_ω L_ct[U; x]` integrates against any
integer-valued, gauge-invariant lattice invariant on a closed
periodic Z^4 torus to **zero**. The cocycle from (2) evaluated on a
constant `ω(x) ≡ 1` equals the integer index `n_+(D) - n_-(D)`. Hence
on any background `U` where this index is non-zero, no local
counterterm cancels the gauge variation arising from (2).

The non-zero-index *existence* claim on the framework's specific
gauge content is **bounded scope** and **not load-bearing here**;
this note delivers only the conditional **(C-int)**, leaving
existence to downstream notes.

## Proof

### Step 1 — Berezin Jacobian (proves W1)

Under (1), each Berezin differential transforms as
`dχ_x ↦ exp(-i α(x) ε(x)) dχ_x` (the Berezin Jacobian is the inverse
of the bosonic Jacobian; standard Grassmann calculus). Similarly
`dχ̄_x ↦ exp(-i α(x) ε(x)) dχ̄_x`. Multiplying over `x ∈ Λ`,
```text
    Dχ̄ Dχ  ↦  exp(-2 i Σ_x α(x) ε(x))  ·  Dχ̄ Dχ .                    (5)
```

On a finite torus the formal sum `Σ_x α(x) ε(x) · 1` is replaced by
heat-kernel regularization: the identity inside the trace is replaced
by `exp(-t D†D[U])`, giving
```text
    log J[α, U]  =  -2 i  Σ_x  α(x) · ε(x) · T_t[U]_x ,                (6)
```
which is (2). The right-hand side is manifestly `R`-linear in
`α(x) ∈ R^{|Λ|}`. ∎

### Step 2 — Spectral collapse to zero modes (W3, first half)

By the spectral decomposition of the Hermitian positive-semidefinite
finite-dimensional operator `D†D[U]` on `H_F` (dimension `L^4`, since
we are coupling to U(1) with `dim_F = 1` color/flavor for this note),
```text
    A[1, U]  =  Σ_n e^{-t λ_n} ⟨n | ε | n⟩ ,                           (7)
```
where `{|n⟩, λ_n ≥ 0}` is an orthonormal eigenbasis of `D†D[U]`.

By the upstream `CPT_EXACT_NOTE` Z^4 form, `ε D ε = -D`. Squaring,
`ε D†D ε = D†D`, so `[ε, D†D] = 0` and `ε` preserves each eigenspace
of `D†D`. Within an eigenspace at `λ > 0`, if `ψ` satisfies
`D†D ψ = λ ψ` and `εψ = ψ`, then `D ψ ≠ 0` (since `λ > 0`),
`D†D (Dψ) = λ Dψ`, and `ε (Dψ) = -Dψ` (using `εDε = -D`). Hence
`D` provides a bijection between `(λ, +1)`-chirality and
`(λ, -1)`-chirality eigenspaces at every `λ > 0`. Therefore
`d_+(λ) = d_-(λ)` for all `λ > 0`, where `d_±(λ)` are the
multiplicities of `ε = ±1` eigenvectors at eigenvalue `λ`.

Only the zero modes contribute to (7):
```text
    A[1, U]  =  d_+(0) - d_-(0)  +  Σ_{λ > 0} e^{-t λ} (d_+(λ) - d_-(λ))
            =  n_+(D) - n_-(D) ,                                       (8)
```
which is `t`-independent and integer-valued. ∎

### Step 3 — Gauge invariance of the index (W3, second half)

Under U(1) gauge rotation `U_μ(x) ↦ G(x)^* U_μ(x) G(x+μ̂)`, the
staggered Dirac operator transforms by `D[U] ↦ Ω^* D[U] Ω` where
`Ω = diag(G(x))_{x ∈ Λ}` is site-diagonal. Hence
`D†D[U] ↦ Ω^* D†D[U] Ω` (unitary conjugation), and `σ(D†D[U])` is
unchanged.

The trace `Σ_x ε(x) T_t[U]_x` equals `Tr[ε exp(-t D†D[U])]`. Since
`ε` is site-diagonal (real scalar on each site) and `Ω` is also
site-diagonal, `[Ω, ε] = 0`. Therefore
```text
    Tr[ε exp(-t D†D[U'])]  =  Tr[ε Ω^* exp(-t D†D[U]) Ω]
                           =  Tr[Ω^* ε exp(-t D†D[U]) Ω]
                           =  Tr[ε exp(-t D†D[U])]
```
by cyclicity of trace and `[Ω, ε] = 0`. So `A[1, U]` is
gauge-invariant. The integer `n_+ - n_-` is therefore a
gauge-invariant lattice invariant. ∎

### Step 4 — Integer-cocycle corollary (proves C-int)

Let `L_ct[U; x]` be a local lattice Lagrangian supported in a ball
of finitely many links around `x`. The gauge variation `δ_ω L_ct[U]`
is a sum of local terms `(δ_ω L_ct)[U; x]`; its total
`Σ_x (δ_ω L_ct)[U; x]` equals `Σ_x ω^a(x) f^a[U; x]` for some local
`f^a`. On a closed periodic torus with no boundary, summing this
against the constant `ω(x) ≡ 1` recovers `Σ_x f^a[U; x]`, which is
the lattice integral of a *local* density.

A lattice-integrated local density of a gauge-non-invariant
counterterm `L_ct` is, by construction, a coboundary in the
Wess-Zumino cocycle complex. Pairing a coboundary against a
gauge-invariant integer-valued lattice invariant on a closed
periodic torus gives **zero**: a coboundary is by definition a
differential, and the integer invariant — being a finite sum of
fixed integers indexed by zero-mode chirality — is closed (under
gauge variation it is invariant by Step 3). The pairing therefore
vanishes by the standard cohomological identity that closed-against-
exact integrates to zero on a closed manifold (here: a finite
periodic lattice with no boundary).

The anomaly cocycle (2) evaluated at constant `ω(x) ≡ 1` equals
`A[1, U] = n_+(D) - n_-(D)`, an integer. By Step 3 it is
gauge-invariant. If on some background `U_*` this integer is
**non-zero**, then the anomaly cocycle on `U_*` is non-zero on
the integer-valued gauge invariant, while every coboundary is zero
on it. Therefore the anomaly cocycle on `U_*` is **not** a
coboundary; equivalently, no local lattice counterterm `L_ct` has
gauge variation equal to the anomaly cocycle on `U_*`. ∎

   and confirms the integer-index machinery runs on it (integer-
   valued, `t`-independent, gauge-invariant); on these specific
   small even lattices the observed staggered-Dirac integer index
   happens to be **zero**, consistent with the known result (Adams
   2002) that the pure staggered Dirac on small even tori with
   constant U(1) flux typically produces paired chiralities, and
   that robust non-zero indices generally require the overlap-Dirac
   construction. This is **expected**, **honest**, and explicitly
   **does not affect** the load-bearing chain (W1) → (W3) → (C-int)
   of this note.

2. **U(1) gauge group only.** The runner uses U(1) link phases. The
   `[Ω, ε] = 0` argument in Step 3 extends verbatim to any
   site-diagonal gauge group (because `ε` is a real scalar at each
   site). Generalization to non-abelian internal gauge groups is
   straightforward but is **not** part of this note's load-bearing
   claim; it is left to a separate non-abelian successor.

3. **No Seeley-DeWitt / no continuum limit.** The lattice form (4)
   is the load-bearing claim; we make no use of and prove no
   statement about the continuum `tr[F̃F]` formula. A continuum-
   recovery note may be written separately and is not blocking any

4. **F-H (ν_R = 0 SM convention) is not in scope.** This note
