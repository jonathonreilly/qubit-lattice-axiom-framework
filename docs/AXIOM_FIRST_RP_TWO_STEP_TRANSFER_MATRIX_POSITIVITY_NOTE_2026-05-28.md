## Why two steps (mechanism, not citation)

With the canonical staggered phases `eta_0 = 1` and `eta_1(t) = (-1)^t` the
temporal hop is clean but the spatial phase alternates with the time slice. The
one-step recurrence therefore alternates between two forms `T_even`
(slices with `(-1)^t = +1`) and `T_odd` (slices with `(-1)^t = -1`); the
two-step monodromy is `T_odd . T_even` over two lattice spacings. This is
the same alternation that makes the single-step spin-basis Lagrangian Gram
matrix non-PSD (reflection-positivity row, no-go runner, min eig `-0.80`).
The calculation below derives the two-step covariance support but keeps the
physical transfer-operator identification as an explicit open bridge.

## In-repo construction and proof (free case)

### Step 1 — single-step transfer from the action

For free staggered fermions in `1+1d` (one Grassmann component per site, `L_s`
spatial sites, periodic) at fixed spatial momentum `p`, the staggered action's
banded-in-time mode equation is

```text
    alpha_t psi_t + (1/2) psi_{t+1} - (1/2) psi_{t-1} = 0,
    alpha_t = m + i eta_1(t) sin(p) = m + i (-1)^t sin(p),
```

which rearranges to `psi_{t+1} = -2 alpha_t psi_t + psi_{t-1}`, i.e. the
one-step classical recurrence matrix on the amplitude 2-vector
`V_t = (psi_t, psi_{t-1})`:

```text
    V_{t+1} = T_s V_t,    T_s = [[ -2 alpha_s, 1 ], [ 1, 0 ]],
    alpha_even = m + i sin(p),   alpha_odd = m - i sin(p).
```

`T_even`, `T_odd` come straight from the action; no convention is admitted.

### Step 2 — one-step recurrence non-positivity

For `sin(p) != 0`, `spec(T_even)` and `spec(T_odd)` are genuinely complex (off
the positive real axis); the runner reports `min |Im eig(T_even/T_odd)| =
0.562` over the non-exceptional Brillouin-zone modes at `m = 0.5`. At the
real-spectrum exceptional modes `sin(p)=0` (`p=0`, and also `p=π` on even
lattices), the one-step matrix is real symmetric with eigenvalues
`-m-sqrt(m^2+1)<0` and `-m+sqrt(m^2+1)>0`; at `m=0.5` this is
`{-1.618, +0.618}`. Thus the specified recurrence carrier is non-positive.
This agrees with the direction of the separate single-step Lagrangian no-go,
but does not identify this classical amplitude recurrence with a physical
one-step Hilbert-space transfer operator.

### Step 3 — dispersion anchor (faithfulness)

The 2-step classical matrix `T2cl(p) = T_odd(p) . T_even(p)` has eigenvalues
`{ e^{+2 E(p)}, e^{-2 E(p)} }` with

```text
    E(p) = arcsinh( sqrt( m^2 + sin^2 p ) ),    sinh^2 E(p) = m^2 + sin^2 p,
```

the exact free staggered `1+1d` dispersion. The decaying eigenvalue is
`e^{-2 E(p)}`, real and positive. The runner verifies this match across the
Brillouin zone with maximum residual `2.9e-16`. Reproducing the known
dispersion is an action-faithfulness anchor for the recurrence, not by itself a
proof that either recurrence eigenchannel is the physical transfer kernel.

### Step 3b — decaying-channel bridge to the one-particle kernel

The candidate passage from the action-derived classical monodromy to a
one-particle positive-time transfer kernel begins with the following finite
spectral split. For `m>0`, the two
eigenvalues of `T2cl(p)` are reciprocal positive real numbers

```text
    lambda_-(p) = e^{-2E(p)} in (0,1],
    lambda_+(p) = e^{+2E(p)} >= 1.
```

Their spectral projectors are explicit:

```text
    P_-(p) = (T2cl(p) - lambda_+(p) I) / (lambda_-(p) - lambda_+(p)),
    P_+(p) = (T2cl(p) - lambda_-(p) I) / (lambda_+(p) - lambda_-(p)),
```

with `P_-^2=P_-`, `P_+^2=P_+`, `P_-P_+=0`, `P_-+P_+=I`, and
`T2cl P_- = lambda_- P_-`. This projector algebra is exact, but by itself it
does **not** decide which of the two reciprocal channels is the positive-time
transfer kernel: `T2cl` is a classical recurrence matrix, and reading
`K_2(p)=lambda_-(p)` off a growth or finite-norm argument is a selection, not a
derivation. Step 3c determines the half-line action-covariance branch but does
not complete the physical transfer identification.

For a one-mode coherent-state kernel
`<bar z'|T_2|z> = exp(bar z' lambda_- z)`, the induced operator on the
finite exterior algebra is exactly `diag(1,lambda_-)`; across momenta this is
the wedge product `Gamma(K_2)`. The runner verifies the projector identities and
residual, the split/orthogonality, the finite exterior construction, the
creation-operator intertwiner, and the `B^dag B` factorization at machine
precision (C6). Every one of those checks is
conditional on the branch: it verifies that *if* the kernel is `lambda_-`, the
finite exterior image is `Gamma(K_2) = B^dag B`. Step 3c derives the
corresponding half-line covariance decay ratio but not the still-needed
action-to-physical-CAR/OS transfer map.

### Step 3c — two-slice Berezin construction: covariance support

Steps 1-3b work with the classical recurrence. This step builds the action
integral from the exterior algebra alone --
`theta_i theta_j = -theta_j theta_i`, `int dtheta theta = 1`,
`int dtheta 1 = 0`, nothing further -- and computes the eliminated action and
its covariance (C7).

**Setup.** At spatial momentum `p` with `N_t` antiperiodic time slices the free
staggered action is quadratic, `S = chibar D chi`, with

```text
    D_{t,t}   = alpha_t,   alpha_t = m + i (-1)^t sin p,
    D_{t,t+1} = +1/2,      D_{t,t-1} = -1/2        (antiperiodic wrap)
```

so `eta_1(t)=(-1)^t` puts the alternation in the diagonal while the time hop is
**antisymmetric**. The engine reproduces
`int prod dchibar dchi e^{-chibar D chi} = det D` on all `n <= 4` test matrices
(`max|Berezin - det D| = 7.9e-15`), so it is the Berezin integral, not a
stand-in.

**Elimination.** Integrating out the ODD slices is an exact Berezin (Schur)
step. Splitting `D` into even/odd blocks `[[A, B],[C, E_odd]]`,

```text
    int (odd slices) e^{-chibar D chi}
        = det(E_odd) . e^{-chibar_e D_eff chi_e},
    D_eff = A - B E_odd^-1 C = a . 1 - b (S + S^-1),
    a = alpha_e + 1/(2 alpha_o),      b = 1/(4 alpha_o),
```

`S` the shift on the surviving even slices. Measured at `N_t = 6, 8`: residue
`|const - det(E_odd)| = 1.2e-16` and `max|D_eff - (A - B E_odd^-1 C)| =
2.2e-16`. This exposes a structural difference between the two carriers: the
one-step hop is ANTIsymmetric
(`|D[0,1] + D[1,0]| = 0.0`) while the eliminated two-step hop is SYMMETRIC
(`|D_eff[0,1] - D_eff[1,0]| = 0.0`). Symmetry of this coefficient alone does
not prove Hilbert-space transfer positivity.

**The chain roots are the monodromy pair.** Transfer roots of
`a . 1 - b (S + S^-1)` satisfy `z + 1/z = a/b`, and

```text
    a/b = 4 alpha_e alpha_o + 2 = tr(T_odd T_even)       (|diff| = 7.0e-16)
```

so `{z_+, z_-} = {lambda_+, lambda_-}`: the Grassmann chain and the classical
monodromy carry the *same* characteristic pair. Nothing so far chooses between
them; the choice is made below by measurement.

**What the integral selects.** Inverting `D_eff` on a growing antiperiodic
chain, the ratio of neighbouring covariance entries converges on the decaying
root:
`max|G_2/G_1 - lambda_-|` falls from `7.0e-3` (`M=8`) to `6.5e-13`
(`M=32`), while `min|G_2/G_1 - lambda_+| = 2.236`.  The contact term matches
the inverse-chain residue
`1/(b(lambda_+ - lambda_-))` to `7.6e-14`.  Thus the action-derived half-line
covariance has decay ratio `lambda_-`.

The finite determinant provides a consistency check, not a second branch
selector.  Across `M=2..6` the runner verifies

```text
det D = det(E_odd) b^M lambda_+^M (1 + lambda_-^M)^2
      = det(E_odd) b^M lambda_-^M (1 + lambda_+^M)^2 .
```

The equality of the two root forms is exact because
`lambda_+ lambda_-=1`; both reproduce `det D` to `9.1e-15`, and their mutual
relative difference is at most `5.1e-15`.  Replacing only
the trace factor while leaving the `lambda_+^M` prefactor fixed would be an
inconsistent root swap and is not used as evidence.

For the conditional Fock construction, the runner also checks the canonical
algebra of its independently constructed Jordan-Wigner operators and the PSD
of the unit-residue Toeplitz ansatz `kappa^{|i-j|}` at
`kappa=lambda_-`.  Those checks show that the proposed representation is
internally consistent.  They do **not** extract the CAR metric or reflection
map from `D_eff`, and the Toeplitz ansatz is not a derivation of the normalized
reflected action covariance.

**Result and remaining wall.** The Berezin calculation proves the odd-slice
Schur reduction, the equality of the action and monodromy characteristic
pairs, and selection of `lambda_-` as the half-line covariance decay ratio.
Over the sampled zone
`max|G_2/G_1-lambda_-|=6.5e-13`, with the ratio in
`[1.458980e-01,3.819660e-01]`.  To identify that ratio with the physical
coherent-state transfer kernel `K_2`, a further derivation must construct the
boundary terms, CAR metric, reflected inner product, and normalization from
the action.  Until then, Step 4 is the exact positive Fock image **conditional
on** `K_2=lambda_-`, not a proof of that target-equivalent identification.

### Step 4 — many-body 2-step positivity

Conditionally on the open identification `K_2=lambda_-`, the proposed
many-body transfer operator is the second quantization `Gamma(K_2)` of that
single-particle kernel.
This is the standard free-fermion second-quantization functor for a quasi-free
map (`Luscher 1977`; `Creutz 1977`; `Montvay-Munster Sec.4`; the underlying
functor is `Shale-Stinespring` / `Berezin`); it is used here as a functorial
relation, not as a positivity citation. For the diagonal free kernel of this
construction the functor is elementary finite-dimensional linear algebra,
recorded explicitly below and verified in-repo by the runner (C5), so the
relation `Gamma(t1) = B^dag B` is **derived/checked in-repo, not asserted**.

**The functor for a diagonal kernel (explicit, finite-dimensional).** The free
theory factorizes across spatial momenta `p`. Supplying the candidate
single-particle kernel `K_2=diag(lambda_p)` with
`lambda_p=e^{-2E(p)}`, the defining
property of the second-quantization functor `Gamma` is that it carries a
one-particle operator `K` (here diagonal, `K e_p = lambda_p e_p`) to the
many-body operator `Gamma(K)` on the Fock space `H = tensor_p {|0>, |1>}` that
fixes the vacuum and intertwines the creation operators,

```text
    Gamma(K) |vac> = |vac>,    Gamma(K) a_p^dag = lambda_p a_p^dag Gamma(K).
```

For a diagonal kernel these two requirements have a unique, explicit
finite-dimensional solution: the per-mode tensor product, because each occupied
mode `p` contributes a factor `lambda_p` and each empty mode contributes `1`,

```text
    Gamma(K_2) = tensor_p diag( 1, lambda_p )
               = exp( -2 a_tau H_hat ),    H_hat = sum_p E(p) a_p^dag a_p,
```

an exact identity of finite matrices (`lambda_p = e^{-2 E(p)}` gives the second
equality, so `Gamma` agrees with the number-operator exponential `exp(-2 a_tau
H_hat)`; this is the relation `Gamma(e^{-h}) = e^{-d Gamma(h)}` specialized to
the diagonal `h`). The runner builds `Gamma(K_2)` from its defining action on
occupation-basis states, confirms it satisfies the creation-operator
intertwiner `Gamma(K) a_p^dag = lambda_p a_p^dag Gamma(K)` mode-by-mode
(`intertwiner err ~ 1e-18`), and checks it equals the matrix exponential
`exp(-2 a_tau H_hat)` of the second-quantized `H_hat` assembled from
Jordan-Wigner number operators (C5, `||Gamma - exp(-2 a_tau H_hat)|| ~ 1e-15`).
So the functor relation `Gamma(t1) = B^dag B` is verified in-repo from the
functor's defining intertwiner, not imported as a theorem.

Under the open identification, set `K_2(p)=e^{-2E(p)}` on the Fock space
`H=tensor_p {|0>,|1>}` (dimension `2^{L_s}`):

```text
    T_hat^2 := Gamma( K_2 ) = tensor_p diag( 1, e^{-2 E(p)} )
            = exp( -2 a_tau H_hat ),     H_hat = sum_p E(p) a_p^dag a_p.
```

Since `E(p) >= 0` for all `p`, `H_hat >= 0`, hence `T_hat^2` is positive
Hermitian with `||T_hat^2|| = 1` (vacuum) and admits the explicit factorization

```text
    T_hat^2 = B^dag B,    B = exp( -a_tau H_hat ) = tensor_p diag( 1, e^{-E(p)} ).
```

This proves positivity of the conditional Fock image:
`H_hat=-log(T_hat^2)/(2a_tau)` is self-adjoint and bounded below by `0`.
The runner builds that image from the decaying classical two-step eigenvalue
and confirms the finite exterior algebra, positive Hermiticity
(`min eig > 0`), and the exact `B^dag B` reconstruction
(`||T_hat^2 - B^dag B|| ~ 6e-17`) for `L_s in {2, 3, 4, 6}`, and over a mass
range `m in [0.05, 5.0]`.
