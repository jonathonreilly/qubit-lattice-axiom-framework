## 1. Background: the normalization gap this note closes

The axiom-first spectrum-condition note
`AXIOM_FIRST_SPECTRUM_CONDITION_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-04-29.md` takes as its single
load-bearing input a strictly positive Hermitian transfer matrix on a finite
physical Hilbert space, and imports it as

```text

```

the **two-step blocked** staggered transfer matrix, from the retained-bounded
two-step positivity authority
[`AXIOM_FIRST_RP_TWO_STEP_TRANSFER_MATRIX_POSITIVITY_NOTE_2026-05-28.md`](AXIOM_FIRST_RP_TWO_STEP_TRANSFER_MATRIX_POSITIVITY_NOTE_2026-05-28.md).
That authority establishes, from the free staggered action,

```text
    T_hat^2 = exp(-2 a_τ H_hat),   H_hat = Σ_p E(p) a_p† a_p ≥ 0,
    H_hat = -log(T_hat^2) / (2 a_τ).                                   (2)
```

The factor `2` in the exponent is not cosmetic: `T_hat^2 = T_odd · T_even`
advances the physical Euclidean state by **two** lattice spacings (one
*block*), because the canonical staggered temporal/spatial phase
`η_1(t) = (-1)^t` alternates the single-step transfer operator between two forms
`T_even` and `T_odd`. The physical (time-translation-invariant, period-two)
object is therefore the **square** `T_hat^2`, and the physical time per block is
`2 a_τ` where `a_τ` is one lattice spacing.

The spectrum-condition note instead writes its reconstructed Hamiltonian and
gap with `1/a_τ`:

```text
    H_note  =  -(1/a_τ) log(T / M_T),
    m_gap_note  =  -(1/a_τ) log(λ_1 / M_T)     [as written in that note].  (3)
```

Substituting the imported `T = T_hat^2 = exp(-2 a_τ H_hat)` into (3) gives
`H_note = 2 H_hat`, i.e. exactly **twice** the physical Hamiltonian of the
cited authority (2). The mass gap is likewise doubled. The note's companion
runner side-steps this by constructing its *own* single-step object
`T = exp(-a_τ H_lat)` (for which `-(1/a_τ) log T = H_lat` is internally
consistent), rather than the cited two-step `T_hat^2`; so the exhibit is not
aligned with the object the note actually imports.

This narrow theorem closes that gap with the explicit blocked-time bridge
`a_τ → 2 a_τ` and records the corrected `1/(2 a_τ)` normalization.

## 6. Proof

### 6.1 (N1) The block spacing is `2 a_τ`

In the cited authority the staggered banded-in-time mode equation at fixed
spatial momentum `p` is

```text
    α_t ψ_t + (1/2) ψ_{t+1} - (1/2) ψ_{t-1} = 0,
    α_t = m + i η_1(t) sin p = m + i (-1)^t sin p,                     (6)
```

so the single-step classical transfer matrix `T_s = [[-2 α_s, 1], [1, 0]]`
alternates between `T_even` (slices with `(-1)^t = +1`, `α = m + i sin p`) and
`T_odd` (`α = m - i sin p`). For `sin p ≠ 0`, `T_even ≠ T_odd` (the runner
exhibits `max|T_even − T_odd| = 4|sin p|`, check B1), so the single-step
evolution is **not** invariant under a one-step time translation: it has period
two. The translation-invariant physical evolution operator over one period is
the product over two consecutive slices,

```text
    T_hat^2  =  T_odd · T_even,                                        (7)
```

which advances the state by two lattice spacings, i.e. by physical Euclidean
time `a_block = 2 a_τ`. This is the standard staggered two-step structure; the
cited authority derives it in-repo and shows the single-step object is
non-positive while `T_hat^2` is positive Hermitian. The reconstruction of a
Hamiltonian from a Euclidean transfer operator `e^{-(time) H}` therefore uses
the block time `2 a_τ`. ∎

### 6.2 (N2) Corrected reconstructed Hamiltonian `H = -(1/(2 a_τ)) log(T_hat^2/M_T) = H_hat`

By the cited authority (eq. (2)), `T_hat^2 = exp(-2 a_τ H_hat)` with
`H_hat ≥ 0` and vacuum eigenvalue `‖T_hat^2‖ = M_T` (here `M_T = 1`, the
empty-Fock vacuum). Taking the finite-dimensional functional calculus of the
strictly positive Hermitian `T_hat^2`,

```text
    -(1/(2 a_τ)) log(T_hat^2 / M_T)
        =  -(1/(2 a_τ)) [ log(exp(-2 a_τ H_hat)) - log M_T ]
        =  H_hat + (log M_T)/(2 a_τ)
        =  H_hat - E_0,                                                (8)
```

where `E_0 = -(log M_T)/(2 a_τ)` is the vacuum subtraction (`E_0 = 0` for the
empty-Fock vacuum `M_T = 1`). Hence `H := -(1/(2 a_τ)) log(T_hat^2/M_T) = H_hat`
(after vacuum subtraction), self-adjoint and `≥ 0`. In contrast the
spectrum-condition note's written form `-(1/a_τ) log(T_hat^2/M_T) = 2 H_hat`,
twice the physical Hamiltonian. The runner builds `H_hat` independently from
Jordan–Wigner number operators and confirms `‖H(c=2) − H_hat‖ ~ 1e-15` while
`‖H(c=1) − 2 H_hat‖ ~ 1e-15`, with the two normalizations differing by exactly
a factor of two on every excited mode (check B5), and confirms `H` is
self-adjoint with non-negative spectrum on the full Fock space (check B8). ∎

### 6.3 (N3) Corrected mass gap

With the corrected normalization, the gap between the vacuum (eigenvalue `M_T`)
and the next eigenvalue `λ_1 < M_T` of `T_hat^2` is

```text
    m_gap  =  E_1 - E_0  =  -(1/(2 a_τ)) log(λ_1 / M_T)  >  0,         (9)
```

half the value the `1/a_τ` form reports. The runner confirms, on the finite
free-staggered carrier, `m_gap(c=2) > 0`, `E_0 = 0`, that the reconstructed
first-excited energy equals (9), and that the old `1/a_τ` gap is exactly
`2 × m_gap` (check B7). Non-degeneracy of the top eigenvalue is exhibited on the
finite carrier, not asserted in closed form for every configuration — the same
conditional content as the spectrum-condition note's SC3. ∎

### 6.4 (N4) Single-particle dispersion consistency

The action-derived single-particle two-step kernel is the decaying eigenvalue
of `T_odd(p) · T_even(p)`,

```text
    t1^(2)(p)  =  e^{-2 E(p)},   E(p) = arcsinh(√(m² + sin²p)),       (10)
```

with the factor `2` the two-step block spacing. The runner verifies (10) over
the Brillouin zone to `~1e-16` (check B2). Under the corrected normalization the
single-particle energy is `-(1/(2 a_τ)) log t1^(2)(p) = E(p)/a_τ`, the physical
staggered dispersion; the `1/a_τ` form gives `2 E(p)/a_τ` (check B6). The
spectrum condition `H ≥ 0` and the placement of the spectrum in the forward
cone (`E_n ≥ 0`, `E_0 = 0`) hold for `H = H_hat` and are exhibited on the finite
carrier (checks B4, B7, B8). ∎
