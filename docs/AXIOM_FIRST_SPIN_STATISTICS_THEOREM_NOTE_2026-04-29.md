## 3. Proof

**(S1).** The relations (3) are the defining relations of the finite
Grassmann algebra `Λ_{2|Λ|}` on the `2|Λ|` independent generators
`χ_x, χ̄_x`. The operatorial realization is left exterior (wedge)
multiplication of the generators on `Λ_{2|Λ|}` itself
(`dim = 2^{2|Λ|}`): for any two generators `θ_i, θ_j`,
`L_{θ_i} L_{θ_j} + L_{θ_j} L_{θ_i} = L_{θ_i θ_j + θ_j θ_i} = 0`, so every
anticommutator vanishes — the `{χ̄_x, χ_y}` cross pairs included — and
each `L_θ` is nilpotent; the representation is faithful because
`L_a 1 = a` (left regular representation). The runner recomputes this at
`N = 4` modes (`8` generators, algebra dimension `2^8 = 256`) with exact
zeros and verifies faithfulness by the rank of the monomial images of the
unit (runner [S1]). The Jordan–Wigner construction (standard methodology,
recomputed by the runner: full pairwise CAR for `N = 4` modes at machine
precision) is the separate CAR operator realization on the `2^{|Λ|}`-dim
Fock space — `{c_x, c_y^†} = δ_xy I ≠ 0` — and is therefore not a
realization of (3); the runner's contrast line computes the difference
(cross anticommutators exactly `0` vs exactly `I`; runner [S1-CAR] and
the [S1] contrast line). ∎

**(S2′).** Let `a, a^†` act on a Hilbert space of finite dimension `d` and
suppose `[a, a^†] = I`. The trace is linear and cyclic, so
`tr([a, a^†]) = tr(a a^†) − tr(a^† a) = 0`, while `tr(I) = d > 0` —
contradiction. Hence (6) forces an infinite-dimensional per-site space.
The cited per-site readout (§4) gives a finite (dim-2) Cl(3) module per
staggered site — both chirality irreps `ρ_±(γ_i) = ±σ_i` have complex
dimension 2, and the dimensional conclusion is chirality-independent. The
substep-1 per-site **physical** space is this finite module by the Quantum
axiom's one-qubit-per-site content (the `k(x) = 1` single-module case in
the multiplicity vocabulary below; axiom premise nodes chain-satisfy
without bounding) composed with (C1) of the single-module row
(`k = 1 ⇒ dim_C H_x = 2`, retained_bounded) — the explicit one-hop
authority for the finite/dim-2 hypothesis this exclusion needs.
Therefore the CCR realization of (1) cannot live on the substep-1 surface:
the free boson is excluded. ∎

**Fact 2.3′ (correction of old Fact 2.3).** The finite-dimensional
alternatives at per-site dimension 2 are NOT exhausted by the Grassmann Fock
space. Per the 2026-05-25 no-forcing note, the candidate list on this surface

**(S3), (S4).** Standard finite Berezin calculus given (S1): the Gaussian
Berezin integral of `e^{−χ̄ M χ}` is `det(M)` (cross-checked against the
Pfaffian of the antisymmetrised form), and a single anticommutation gives the
exchange sign. ∎

**Load-bearing step.** The single decisive step is the (S2′) trace
