## 1. What this proposes, and the test it has to pass

A four-axiom replacement for Lattice, Qubit, Admissibility, and Record, drafted
against one hard constraint: **it must not smuggle in imports.**

That constraint is easy to state and easy to fail. The failure mode is adding a
clause because a lane needs it — "and the action is Wilson," "and the gauge group
is `SU(3) × SU(2) × U(1)`," "and there are three generations." Any of those closes
lanes and none of them is a foundation.

So this draft is held to a stricter rule than "don't name physics content":

> **Every change must be a net reduction in supplied structure.** A clause may
> be re-typed or deleted. A clause may be added only if it replaces two.

The reason this is achievable at all is that the current axioms are not
under-supplied — they are **over-supplied in the wrong places and under-supplied
in the right ones.** Three of the four carry clauses that constrain more than
they license: additivity of readout, strict nearest-neighbor coupling, and
one-permanent-record-per-site each *forbid* structure the theory needs while
supplying nothing that is used. Deleting them widens the theory. That is why a
reset can unlock lanes without importing anything.

Section 6 is the arithmetic showing the reduction is real, so the claim can be
checked rather than believed.

## 2. Design rules used

1. **Type changes over additions.** Prefer re-typing an existing clause to
   adding a new one.
2. **Weaker is better where the weaker form is what the work actually uses.**
3. **No clause may name an operator, basis, value, weight, selector, group,
   dimension, or coupling.**
4. **Every clause must pass the memo's own law test:** a supplied domain, and
   exactly one answer at every state where the domain condition holds.
5. **Nothing may be assumed that the axioms are supposed to explain.** In
   particular, probability is an *output* of a physical theory, so it may not
   appear as an axiom primitive.
6. **The axiom set must have a model, and the model must be exhibited.**

Rule 5 does most of the work below, and it is worth stating why it is a
minimality rule rather than a preference. The current Admissibility axiom
supplies a *probability distribution*. Born weights are simultaneously carried as
open. An axiom set cannot coherently assume the output of the rule it says it
cannot derive. Replacing the distribution with an amplitude is therefore not an
addition — it withdraws an assumption.

## 3. The drafted axiom text

This is the quotable surface. Everything after it is commentary.

---

### Substrate

Events form a set with a partial order. Every order interval is finite.

No event is privileged. Events are distinguished by the supplied order alone.

### Observables

To each finite region of events is assigned a unital algebra of observables.

The assignment is isotone: the algebra of a region contains the algebra of each
of its subregions. The algebras of two regions unrelated by the order commute.
The algebra of a union is generated by the algebras of its parts.

No observable is privileged. Observables are distinguished by the supplied
structure alone.

### Law

There is one fixed assignment of a complex amplitude to each finite
configuration, invariant under every automorphism of the supplied order.

The assignment is local: its dependence on an event decays with that event's
order separation.

The assignment is positive under order reversal.

The extensional form of the assignment is not fixed by these axioms. It is the
theory's single undetermined object.

### Actuality

Outcomes occur.

An outcome is exactly one of the mutually exclusive alternatives admitted by the
observable algebra of its region.

These axioms fix which alternatives are available, and with what amplitude. They
do not fix which one occurs.

### Qualification

These axioms state only their named primitive content. Further physical
structure requires a retained derivation or bridge, or explicit
approved-primitive registration, before use as a premise. A choice not fixed by
the supplied structure remains a named conditional or open dependency.

A state is an assignment of expectation values to observables.

A law privileges no states. Its domain is a supplied condition, and at every
state where the condition holds it gives exactly one answer.

## 4. Clause by clause: what it replaces, and why it is not an import

### Substrate replaces Lattice

**Deleted:** the specific set `Z^3`; nearest-neighbor adjacency; the translation
group; the proper cubic rotation group; the orientation that "proper"
presupposes.

**Added:** a partial order, and finiteness of order intervals.

**Why this is a reduction.** Five supplied structures become two. Dimension,
point group, and geometry stop being axiom content and become properties of
whichever substrate the Law selects — which is the correct home for them, since
the current axioms do not derive `d = 3` either.

**What the order buys, and why it is the load-bearing change.** A partial order
is causal precedence. Supplying it at the substrate resolves four separate
problems at once, which is why it earns its place under rule 1:

- **Time stops being smuggled.** The current axioms supply space and no time, so
  a temporal direction has to enter from outside as a record ordering. Here the
  order *is* the temporal structure, and it costs one relation.
- **"Records form" becomes statable.** Formation can be located, because the
  order supplies a notion of before and after that the current text lacks.
- **The Admissibility/Record fixed point becomes well-founded.** Finiteness of
  order intervals is exactly the condition that makes recursive definition along
  the order legitimate.
- **The preferred frame goes away.** This is the one repair no lattice can make.
  A Poisson sprinkling into Minkowski space is Lorentz invariant — the
  distribution picks out no direction — which is why an order-theoretic
  substrate can be discrete *and* frame-free, and a lattice cannot.

**Not an import** because it names no dimension, no symmetry group, no metric,
and no scale. It is strictly less specified than `Z^3`.

### Observables replaces Qubit, and absorbs Record's readout clause

**Deleted:** the possibility domain and its `M_2(C)` presentation; "only records
are readable"; "a readout value is determined by record content alone"; scalar
readout additivity.

**Added:** region-indexed algebras, isotony, commutation at order-unrelatedness,
generation of unions from parts.

**Why this is a reduction.** Four supplied clauses become four, but the new four
are structural conditions on an assignment while the old four *fixed a
dimension* and *restricted the observable class*. Dimension two is no longer
supplied, so the algebra is determined by the theory rather than posited — and
the three deleted readout clauses were the source of the foundation's most
damaging results:

- additivity plus content-determination, together with a substrate whose
  symmetry acts transitively, forces every readout to be invariant under
  rearranging records among sites, so no observable can see adjacency, distance,
  or shape;
- additivity makes every observable an extensive count, so no ratio — and every
  dimensionless target of the theory is a ratio — is a readout;
- additivity is the wrong shape for holonomies, which are multiplicative around
  a path, and for correlation observables, which are products.

Region-indexing repairs the first, dropping additivity repairs the second and
third. Extensive quantities remain expressible as sums; they simply stop being
the *only* thing expressible. **This is a strict widening, achieved by
deletion.**

**It also supplies the composition rule the current axioms lack.** Qubit is a
one-site statement and says nothing about how two sites combine, so every
multi-site statement in the theory rests on a composition convention the axioms
do not contain. "The algebra of a union is generated by the algebras of its
parts," together with commutation, is that rule, stated once.

**Not an import** because it names no dimension, no basis, no operator, and no
value.

**One honest structural commitment.** "Algebra" has to be typed for states and
spectra to be meaningful; the intended reading is a unital C\*-algebra. That is
the single place in this draft where a mathematical framework is chosen rather
than derived. The non-smuggling alternative is to derive it from operational
postulates — causality, local tomography, purification, in the manner of the
reconstruction programs — which costs more axiom text and buys the choice back
as a theorem. **This is flagged as an open decision, not resolved here.**

### Law replaces Admissibility

**Deleted:** "nearest-neighbor"; "probability distribution."

**Added:** complex amplitude; invariance; decay; positivity under order reversal;
an explicit statement that the assignment is undetermined.

**Amplitude rather than probability is a withdrawal, not an addition** (rule 5).
The current axiom supplies a probability distribution while the theory carries
Born weights as open. Amplitudes are the primitive from which probability is
derived; supplying the derived object and calling the derivation open is the
incoherence this fixes. It also supplies interference, which no real
non-negative local weight can produce: a non-negative nearest-neighbor
conditional specification is, by Hammersley–Clifford, a Gibbs measure with
nearest-neighbor potentials — classical lattice statistical mechanics.

**"Local" rather than "nearest-neighbor" is a weakening with a large payoff.**
Strict nearest-neighbor coupling, together with translation invariance, is
precisely the hypothesis of the Nielsen–Ninomiya theorem, which forces equal
numbers of left- and right-handed Weyl modes. Decay rather than strict
truncation removes that hypothesis, which is the standard route to lattice
chirality. **One word, and the chirality obstruction is no longer structural.**

**Positivity under order reversal is the one clause that adds capability, and it
is why it is worth its cost.** It supplies no operator and no value — it is a
positivity condition on the assignment. What it buys is the reconstruction
theorem: reflection positivity is what converts a Euclidean weight into a
Hilbert space with a self-adjoint, positive Hamiltonian and a spectrum
condition. So this single structural clause supplies, as consequences rather than
as axioms, the Hilbert space, unitary dynamics, and positive energy that the
current foundation lacks entirely.

**Naming the undetermined object is the anti-smuggling clause.** The current
Admissibility axiom asserts there is *one fixed* rule and never says which, so
the theory's entire physical content sits in a function quantified
existentially over an infinite-dimensional space, and the advertised premise
count understates the real one. The draft says this out loud. That converts a
hidden premise into a named research target, which is what the minimality
policy's section 1 requires of any unmade decision.

### Actuality replaces Record's remaining content

**Deleted:** "a site never carries more than one record"; "records are
permanent."

**Retained:** occurrence, and that what occurs is exactly one of a determined
menu.

**Why the deletions are reductions.** Permanence writes irreversibility into the
fundamental law, which no successful fundamental theory does, and it does not
even discharge what it appears to buy, since the low-entropy past is still
carried separately as a scope condition. One-record-per-site plus permanence
gives every bounded region a bounded total number of events for all time, so
persistent, re-interacting matter has no representation. Both clauses cost more
than they supply.

**Why the retained content now passes the law test.** "Records form" supplies no
domain and gives no answer at any state, so by the memo's own definition it is
not a law. The redraft gives a domain — a region with an observable algebra —
and exactly one answer: one alternative from the menu that algebra admits. What
is *not* determined is said explicitly rather than left to inference.

**Permanence becomes derivable rather than assumed**, as a thermodynamic
statement conditioned on the past hypothesis the framework already carries.

## 5. What happens to the three approved primitives

| Primitive | Status under the reset |
|---|---|
| `scale_reference_primitive` (`a^{-1}`) | **Retained.** Units conversion; nothing here supplies a scale. |
| `kinetic_isotropy_primitive` (`c_t = c_s`) | **Retired as unnecessary.** It exists to reconcile a temporal graining with a spatial one. An order-theoretic substrate has no such split, so the clause has nothing to say. This is the reset paying back a premise. |
| `realized_state_primitive` | **Absorbed into Actuality.** Its content — the axioms select no state, a history fixes one, evaluation is pointwise — is exactly what Actuality's third sentence states. |

Net: three approved primitives become one.

## 6. Supplied-structure arithmetic

The claim in §1 is that this is a reduction. Counted honestly:

**Current surface — 15 axiom clauses + 3 primitives + 1 unstated:**
the set `Z^3`; nearest-neighbor adjacency; translations; proper cubic rotations;
orientation; a per-site possibility domain; its `M_2(C)` presentation; a
nearest-neighbor rule; that the rule yields a probability distribution; record
occurrence; one record per site; permanence; readability restricted to records;
readout determined by content; scalar additivity — plus `a^{-1}`, `c_t = c_s`,
the realized-state interface — plus a temporal ordering that no axiom supplies
and every dynamical statement uses.

**Proposed surface — 12 clauses + 1 primitive:**
a set of events; a partial order; finiteness of order intervals; a
region-indexed algebra assignment; isotony; commutation at order-unrelatedness;
generation of unions from parts; a complex amplitude assignment; its invariance;
its decay; its positivity under order reversal; outcome occurrence — plus
`a^{-1}`.

**19 → 13, with the unstated temporal structure now supplied rather than
borrowed.** No named operator, group, dimension, coupling, or value appears in
either column.

## 7. What this unlocks — and what it does not

Stated plainly in both directions, because a reset that is oversold is worse than
no reset.

**Unlocked, structurally:**

| Lane | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Born form | Region algebras have dimension ≥ 3, so Gleason applies; Busch's effect form applies regardless. The current `M_2(C)` choice sits at the one dimension where Gleason gives nothing. |
| Hilbert space, unitarity, positive energy | Consequences of positivity under order reversal, via the reconstruction theorem. |
| Chirality | "Local" rather than "nearest-neighbor" removes the Nielsen–Ninomiya hypothesis. |
| Gauge observables | Region-indexed algebras admit holonomies. A gauge action stops being an import and becomes a *candidate for the amplitude assignment* — a well-posed question rather than a borrowed answer. |
| Spatial observables | Region-indexing restores position, distance, momentum, correlation functions. |
| Composition | Stated once, in Observables. |
| Time and dynamics | The order supplies causal structure; positivity supplies the generator. |
| Lorentz invariance | An order-theoretic substrate has no preferred frame. |
| Entanglement and area laws | Region algebras with commutation make entropies expressible; the current additivity clause forbids them. |
| Spin–statistics, CPT, superselection | Available as theorems on a local net, rather than as open gates. |

**Not unlocked, and no axiom reset will unlock them:**

- **the number of generations;**
- **the gauge group;**
- **any mass, coupling, mixing angle, or phase.**

All of these are downstream of the amplitude assignment, which this draft leaves
explicitly undetermined. That is the honest and slightly uncomfortable core of
the proposal: **a foundation fixes what kind of answers are possible, not which
answer is right.** Any axiom text that appears to deliver three generations is
importing three generations. The reset makes the numerical lanes *well-posed* —
they become questions about which amplitude assignment, asked in a language that
can express the answer — but it does not answer them.

**Also not addressed:** the volume-versus-area degree-of-freedom count is
improved but not resolved. Region algebras permit area-law entanglement rather
than forcing volume-law extensivity, but nothing here derives the
Bekenstein–Hawking coefficient.

## 8. What it costs

**Essentially every downstream derivation is invalidated.** Work that cites
`minimal_axioms` rests on `Z^3`, on `M_2(C)`, and on record additivity, and all
three are gone. This is a larger invalidation than the 2026-06-29 reset by a
wide margin, and the premise-hash guard will enforce it.

**The lattice is not lost, but it is demoted.** `Z^3 × Z` with the product order
is a model of the Substrate axiom, so existing lattice computations remain valid
*as computations on a particular model*, in the same relationship lattice QCD has
to continuum QCD: a regulator, not the ontology. Re-deriving results in that
relationship is real work, but it is recovery rather than loss.

**Permanence-dependent results need re-basing** on a derived rather than
axiomatic permanence.

**A cheaper partial version exists.** If the substrate change is too expensive
now, the other three redrafts are independent of it and can be adopted over
`Z^3` unchanged. That sub-reset unlocks Born, chirality, gauge observables,
spatial observables, composition, and dynamics, and invalidates far less. It does
**not** unlock Lorentz invariance, gravity, or the frame problem, because those
are properties of having a fixed lattice at all. It is the right move if the
goal is to make the current program work; it is not the right move if the goal
is a theory of everything.

## 9. Proof obligations before this could be adopted

The draft is not adoptable as it stands, and these are the reasons rather than a
formality. Listed in the order they should be discharged:

1. **Exhibit one model.** A foundation that has not been shown to have a
   solution has not been shown to be about anything. The current axiom set
   carries the same debt, unremarked.
2. **Show the model is non-trivial** — that the class of admissible amplitude
   assignments is non-empty and contains something other than the zero and the
   free assignment.
3. **Verify `Z^3 × Z` is a model**, so existing lattice work has a recovery
   path.
4. **State positivity under order reversal precisely** on an order-theoretic
   substrate. Reflection positivity is standard on a Euclidean lattice; its
   order-theoretic form is the least settled clause in this draft and should be
   treated as the main technical risk. **PROBED 2026-08-09 — negative so far.**
   Positive on a lattice and on a regular causal order; fails on sprinkled
   causal sets across a 625-action scan of the order-local quadratic family,
   with violations of the same order as the Gram matrix itself. The next probe
   is the Benincasa-Dowker causal-set d'Alembertian, which lies outside the
   scanned family.
5. **Derive the Born form** from Observables plus Law. This is the payoff check:
   if it does not go through, the reset has not bought what §7 claims.
6. **Decide the C\*-typing question** in §4 — posit it, or derive it from
   operational postulates.

Obligations 1 and 4 are the ones that could sink the draft. **Obligation 1 is
now discharged by probe; obligation 4 is probed and currently negative.**

## 10. Recommendation

Adopt nothing yet. Obligations 1 and 3 are now discharged by probe; obligation
4 is probed and currently negative, and it is the one that determines whether
the substrate change is real or merely tidier.

**The three non-substrate redrafts are separable, individually defensible, and
now have direct computational support**: amplitude for probability
(withdraws an assumption the theory says it cannot make), local for
nearest-neighbor (one word, removes a theorem-grade obstruction), and
region-indexed observables for additive readout (deletes the clause behind the
foundation's three most damaging results). None of the three requires the
substrate change, none imports physics content, and each is a reduction.

The substrate change is the one that decides whether this is a theory of
everything or a specific lattice model. That is an owner call, not a technical
one.
