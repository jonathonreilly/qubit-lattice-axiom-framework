## Correction

V1 claimed that every weight-preserving operator generated by the historical
A_min stack acts as a permutation on the four-dimensional block P_A H_cell.
That inference is invalid for a *-algebra, which is closed under linear
combinations, products and adjoints. Individual weight-changing generators
can have weight-preserving products. Compression is not multiplicative:
`P A B P` need not equal `(P A P)(P B P)`.

Consequently the old O1/O2 argument does not establish that the generated
algebra is the permutation algebra. Testing individual permutation matrices
for anticommutation also does not test arbitrary elements of their linear
span. O3 additionally confused a choice of commuting generators with the
absence of other Clifford generators in the same M_4(C) algebra. The finite
list of failed routes in O4/O5 is not an exhaustion theorem.

The previous source at commit
`f24d17d97d372df977476e4a69674577f31af575` preserves the original argument.
The filename is retained for stable references, not as an assertion of its
old title. No Axiom* adoption or downstream promotion follows from V1.

## Exact countermodel to the weight-preserving-permutation inference

Take a supplied four-mode CAR Fock space H_cell of dimension 16, with
creation/annihilation operators a_i^dagger,a_i and one-particle projector P_A.
On its ordered basis |i> = a_i^dagger|vac>,

```
E_ij = P_A a_i^dagger a_j P_A,
E_ij |k> = delta_jk |i>,
E_ij E_kl = delta_jk E_il.
```

The sixteen E_ij span M_4(C). In particular E_ii is a diagonal rank-one
projector, not a permutation matrix. Each odd a_i or a_i^dagger has zero
compression, yet their product can have nonzero compression. In M_4(C) the
four Hermitian matrices

```
X tensor I, Y tensor I, Z tensor X, Z tensor Y
```

square to I and anticommute pairwise. Thus the supplied bilinear algebra
has enough capacity to host Cl_4(C). The runner checks these equations with
exact integer/complex arithmetic, including rank sixteen and every matrix-unit
product. This is a constructive counterexample to V1's algebraic inference;
it is not a derivation of the supplied Fock realization from current axioms.

The existing
[bilinear active-block support note](HUBBLE_LANE5_C1_A6_BILINEAR_ACTIVE_BLOCK_SUPPORT_BOUNDARY_NOTE_2026-04-29.md)
already makes the same capacity/selection distinction.
