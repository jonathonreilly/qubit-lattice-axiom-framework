# A normal-frame wave shift fed by the per-tick volume rate takes three starting black-hole exterior slicings to one horizon-regular stationary slicing

Claim type: bounded_theorem.
Primary runner: `scripts/a_normal_frame_wave_shift_fed_by_the_per_tick_volume_rate_gives_black_hole_exteriors_an_attracting_horizon_regular_slicing_2026_10_22.py`.

Model status (2026-11-14). The update law's tied lapse and the response law's shift sentence (CL-1 and CL-3 in the three-law numbering of the originating register) are used here as supplied conditional model definitions with zero premise weight. The originating register text is not in this repository, and these clauses are not the entries of the same number in [docs/CANDIDATE_LAWS.md](CANDIDATE_LAWS.md). The revised shift law below is a candidate, not adopted.

The post-crunch note ([A_BLACK_HOLE_EXTERIOR_KEEPS_A_REGULAR_TIED_LAPSE_SLICING_ONLY_WHILE_ITS_SHIFT_CHARGE_LASTS_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-10-22.md](A_BLACK_HOLE_EXTERIOR_KEEPS_A_REGULAR_TIED_LAPSE_SLICING_ONLY_WHILE_ITS_SHIFT_CHARGE_LASTS_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-10-22.md), #9964, carried in this PR) found an open tension: under the wave shift as written, a black hole's exterior shift charge Q = r^2 beta^r is set by the core, is odd under time reversal, and leaks out of the horizon in the label frame. After a time-symmetric core bounce (argued there, not run) the exterior is left on the static slicing, whose edges reach the window floor at R of about 2.01-2.03 M: a visible bounce, in tension with black-hole observations. The cure tested there (trapped cells keep their shift) is not cell-local. This note gives a block-local revision of the shift law under which three different starting slicings settle onto one stationary, horizon-regular slicing (finite runs), which at linear order is causally sealed from the core. The [minimal axioms](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md) fix none of the inputs used here. Units are G = c = M = 1, edges are in far-edge units, r is the site label and R the areal radius.

## Candidate law text (revision of the CL-3 shift sentence; not adopted)

> "The shift obeys the undamped wave law read along the slice normals, (d_t - beta^j d_j)^2 beta_i = mu c_l^2 [Delta beta_i - 2 d_j(K_ij - delta_ij K) - kappa d_i(N K)], where c_l = N/l is the cube's light speed in labels (its tied lapse over its edge along i), N K is the cube's fractional volume change per tick along the slice normal, read from its edges' change between ticks, and kappa is a supplied constant with 0 < kappa <= 2(1 - mu)/mu, with mu as before."

Smuggling check, item by item:
- The normal-frame reading. In the current text the wave runs at sqrt(mu) labels per tick whatever the shift. This revision makes it run at sqrt(mu) times the cube's own light speed, relative to the cubes' normals. That is the same locality as the existing Laplacian: the block of cubes, one rung up. It uses only registers the update law already reads (edges, the tied lapse, the shift). No trapped test, horizon finder or outgoing-light expansion is used.
- The source term. -kappa d_i(N K) is the label difference of neighbouring cubes' per-tick volume rates, so it is block-local. In weak fields it equals the trace-weight change -2 d_j(K_ij - zeta delta_ij K) with kappa = 2(1 - zeta). Here zeta = 2/3 replaces the register's 1, which is what made the vacuum source vanish on shell.
- Per tick, not per proper time. A1 forces this. With K per proper time the gauge system is ill-posed wherever the lapse N is below kappa: in collapsing cores, and (with kappa ~ 0.5-0.67) in J0740-class neutron-star cores.
- kappa is supplied. Its upper end is forced by weak-field causality (A2). Placing it at that end ("the fastest longitudinal gauge mode is the light cone") is a rule choice, not a derivation. Delta = 1 needs kappa within about 10% of that end (B6). Delta = 2 holds at kappa = 0.5.
- mu now affects a stationary state: the black-hole slicing. The register line "mu affects no stationary state" would have to read "no static, homogeneous or stationary-rotating state".
- What is not used: no time-even source, no damping, no threshold, and no new register.

## Statement

A. Exact (sympy, on the constraint surface of a flat background with edges l_r, l_t and tied lapse).

1. For a radial plane wave the constraints force the tangential perturbations to zero. The longitudinal symbol is v^4 - (mu + q) v^2 + mu q (1 - kappa) = 0. Here v is in units of c_l, and q = l_t^2/(l_t^2 + 2 l_r^2) is the tied lapse speed squared, which is 1/3 for equal edges. With kappa = 0 the roots are mu and q, the register's shift and lapse speeds. If the source is K per proper time, the constant term becomes mu q (1 - kappa/N), with v^2 < 0 wherever N < kappa.
2. The discriminant is (mu - q)^2 + 4 mu q kappa >= 0, so the speeds are real. They are positive iff kappa < 1, at every edge ratio and lapse. Both are at most c iff (1 - mu)(1 - q) >= mu q kappa. In weak fields that means kappa <= 2(1 - mu)/mu, which is 2/3 at mu = 3/4, where the speeds are 1 and sqrt(1/12). At that kappa no gauge mode is faster than light wherever l_r >= l_t (q <= 1/3). Physical modes stay at c and the transverse shift modes at sqrt(mu).
3. The source -kappa d_i(N K) vanishes on static slices (K = 0) and on homogeneous slices (N K uniform). So beta = 0 is an exact solution around static stars, on FRW and on Bianchi I. For a stationary, axisymmetric, divergence-free rotation on a stationary slice, K = 0 and the source vanishes too, but the advection does not: in the flat-label Cartesian vector law, rigid rotation beta = Omega(-y, x, 0) gives (beta.grad)beta = -Omega^2 (x, y, 0) and (beta.grad)^2 beta = -Omega^2 beta, both nonzero and both second order in Omega. The rotating-body equation is therefore the register's up to the factor c_l^2 at first order in the rotation rate only. Negative result kept: the held statement that the advection vanishes exactly for stationary axisymmetric azimuthal shifts is withdrawn; its check computed only beta_phi d_phi beta_phi and dropped the vector-basis (connection) terms. A componentwise spherical law would be a different law and would not inherit Cartesian-vector covariance; it is not used here.
4. The law is invariant under time reversal: beta, K and d_t are odd and N is even, so the residual maps to minus itself (checked on explicit test fields; an added time-even source breaks it). No time-even source is needed. The time-reversed (white-hole, Q < 0) slicing is also stationary; that it repels in forward time is the time reverse of B1's finite runs, not a separate computation.
5. For the principal part linearised about a background, every gauge characteristic moves at -beta +- v c_l in labels. Where beta > v_max c_l all of them point inward. Since v_max <= 1 (A2, kappa <= kappa_max, l_r >= l_t) and outgoing light in labels moves at c_l - beta, the surface beta = v_max c_l lies at or outside the horizon wherever beta/c_l rises monotonically inward; on the attractor it sits at R of about 2.1-2.2 M (B2). So at linear order the core cannot signal the exterior through the gauge there. In the register's label frame the wave moves sqrt(mu) labels per tick at every beta, and does cross the horizon (#9964 A3).

B. Finite diagnostics. Vacuum ADM with the tied lapse, the revised law at mu = 3/4 and kappa = 2/3, labels excised inside the horizon, outer boundary at r = 70.

1. Common end state (called the attractor below; attraction is shown only from these three starts). Three different starting slicings are used: the label-family separatrices with Q0 = 4 and 8, and Q0 = 6 with a 20% shift bump. Their initial largest radial edges are 3.02, 1.24 and 1.76. After 120 M all three are on one stationary exterior. The spreads are 1.3% (largest l_r), 1.1% (smallest l_t) and 3.0% (lapse at 2M).

2. Window. The largest exterior radial edge is 1.81 (log2 0.85) and the smallest tangential edge 0.49. Every exterior edge is therefore inside [2^(-3-m), 2^(1+Delta-m)] at every far phase m with Delta = 1. The shift-wave sonic surface (beta = sqrt(mu) c_l) sits at R of about 2.1-2.2 M. The slow-gauge sonic point (beta/c_l = sqrt(1/12)) sits near 5 M.
3. Stationarity. Edges and lapse drift by 1.6% between t = 80 and 120 M. (A scratch run with r_out = 150 to 400 M, not in the runner, reportedly kept l_r max at 1.78-1.86 and l_t min at 0.49; unverified here.)

## Derivation

A1 linearises the free ADM equations in the normal frame on a flat background with constant edges (A, B). The tied lapse gives delta N = N^3 a/(6A) on the constraint surface b = k_y = 0, where a and b are the fractional radial and tangential edge-squared perturbations. With d_t a = -2 N k_x + 2 beta', d_t K_rr = -N'' and the revised shift law, the 3x3 symbol's determinant gives the quartic (N^2 = 3 q A is used). A2 is the quartic's discriminant and its value at v = 1. A3 computes, in sympy, the gradient of a homogeneous N K(t), and the Cartesian advection of rigid rotation and its first-order part in Omega. A4 applies t -> -t, beta -> -beta, K -> -K to the residual on explicit test fields. A5 solves (d_t - beta d_x)^2 u = c^2 v^2 d_x^2 u for plane waves. B evolves A, B, K^r_r, K^theta_theta, beta and (d_t - beta d_r) beta. It uses fourth-order differences, RK4, Kreiss-Oliger dissipation 0.8 and h = 0.1. The inner boundary is an outflow boundary inside the horizon, with labels removed below R = 0.5 and added by linear extrapolation when the inner edge rises above R = 1.7. The outer boundary is held.

## Literature reading (from memory, not re-checked in this run)

This section is context, not evidence: the cited results were not re-checked, and no conclusion of this note rests on them.

Moving-puncture evolutions (1+log lapse with Gamma-driver shift; Hannam, Husa, Pollney, Bruegmann and O Murchadha 2007-2008; Baumgarte and Naculich 2007 for maximal slices) settle on stationary "trumpet" slices. They are unique by regularity at a critical point where a gauge speed equals the shift. They are reached because data inside that point cannot escape, and they need the lapse condition. The coordinates are damped onto them by the Gamma-driver's eta. Here the lapse is tied to the edges, so the 1+log condition is not available. The trumpet mechanism (a gauge-sonic surface outside the horizon) is carried by the shift instead, and settling happens by gauge radiation, with no damping. The Gamma-driver's source is the divergence of the trace-free distortion rate (zeta = 1/3, kappa = 4/3). With the tied lapse that is ill-posed (A1), so the Gamma-driver itself cannot be copied. Its 3/4 is chosen so that its longitudinal speed (conformal Laplacian) equals c. The analogous rule here is kappa = 2(1 - mu)/mu. The puncture trumpet's radial edge in puncture labels diverges at the puncture, inside the horizon, so it says nothing about [L/8, 2^(1+Delta) L] outside the horizon. That was not computed.

- Sources from the outgoing-light expansion theta_+ of a cell face: these need a surface choice (the label surfaces), so they are not cell-local. Any source confined to trapped cells is, under the normal-frame law, inside the sealed region and cannot act on the exterior. Under the label-frame law it leaks the bounce as well. Not pursued.
- A time-even source: not needed, because the selection comes from causality (A5), not from a time arrow.

## Estimates against measurement

- Frame dragging. At first order in the rotation rate the extra terms vanish for stationary axisymmetric states (A3); the O(Omega^2) advection is not estimated. The remaining first-order change is the factor N^2 gamma^jj, a relative 7e-10 at the Earth, so the edge ring of about 4e-15 is unchanged. The extreme-Kerr ring (at most 0.5 in the register) was not recomputed. The runner prints this as an uncounted ESTIMATE line.
- GP-B, LAGEOS, Shapiro delay and bending are gauge-invariant at linear order and unchanged.
- Black holes. In the one linear core-seed test (B4) the exterior response stays at the numerical-leak level. Whether the exterior slicing is independent of the core's history in general (a finite Q flip, a matter bounce) is not computed, so prompt re-emergence after a bounce is neither excluded nor shown here (printed as NOT CHECKED).
