## Setup

Let `C` be the standard `3 × 3` cyclic permutation matrix with
`C^3 = I`. The `C_3`-doublet sub-rep of `Herm_circ(3) ⊂ M_3(C)` is
spanned (over `R`) by

```text
B_1  :=  C + C^2,      B_2  :=  i (C - C^2),                              (B)
```

both Hermitian (`B_1^† = B_1`, `B_2^† = B_2`) and `C_3`-doublet-equivariant.
A general doublet element is `b B_1 + b̄ ... ` parameterised by a single
complex `b ∈ C`, equivalently the two real coordinates
`(Re b, Im b) ∈ R^2`. The `U(1)_b` action is `b → e^{i θ} b`, equivalently
`(Re b, Im b) → R(θ) (Re b, Im b)` where `R(θ)` is the standard SO(2)
rotation matrix.

The F1-vs-F3 obstruction source note preserves the open question:
**does any retained-class authority supply a canonical direction on the
`(Re b, Im b)` plane** (equivalently a continuous-`SO(2)` quotient
collapsing the 2-plane to a single radial mode `|b|`)?

R3 (the `γ`-involution gives a `Z_2`-quotient `b ~ -b`) and the
six-ray fixed-point structure of `C_3` on the doublet plane: the `C_3`

direction) and `arg(b) ∈ {π, π + 2π/3, π + 4π/3}` (real-negative

discrete measure can place positive support, reducing the doublet plane
to a 1-real-dim ray axis. The question is whether a retained mechanism
**selects one of these six rays canonically**, equivalently whether
the C_3 fixed-ray structure plus a separate retained Z_2 (e.g. `γ` or
`K`) jointly forces a single canonical ray.
