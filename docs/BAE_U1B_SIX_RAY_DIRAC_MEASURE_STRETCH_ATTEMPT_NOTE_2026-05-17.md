## Setup

Let `C` be the standard `3 × 3` cyclic permutation matrix with
`C^3 = I`. From (X1), the `C_3`-doublet sub-rep of `Herm_circ(3)` is
spanned (over `R`) by

```text
B_1  :=  C + C^2,      B_2  :=  i (C - C^2),                              (B)
```

both Hermitian and `C_3`-doublet-equivariant. A general doublet
element is parameterised by `b ∈ C` via `b C + b̄ C^2`, equivalently
the two real coordinates `(Re b, Im b) ∈ R^2`. The `U(1)_b` labelling
freedom is `b → e^{i θ} b`, equivalently
`(Re b, Im b) → R(θ) (Re b, Im b)` where `R(θ)` is the standard `SO(2)`
rotation matrix.

The retained discrete symmetries of `Herm_circ(3)` available on the
doublet plane are (per Cycle 3 enumeration, verified in (T2) below):

```text
C_3  acting on the b-label as  b → ω b   (ω = e^{2 π i / 3}),             (D1)
K    acting as                 b → b̄,                                    (D2)
γ    acting as                 b → -b    (from (X3) vector-grade -id),    (D3)
```

The dihedral group generated by `<C_3, K>` is `D_3` (order `6`). The
three reflections of `D_3` are `K`, `C_3 K`, `C_3^2 K`, each of which
fixes a one-real-dim ray-pair in the `b`-plane. The full
`D_3`-reflection-fixed-ray locus on the `b`-plane is the union of
these three two-element ray sets:

```text
                      α = arg(b),
Six fixed rays:       α ∈ {0, π/3, 2π/3, π, 4π/3, 5π/3}                   (R)
                       = {k π/3 : k = 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5}.
```

This is the open contingency named in Cycle 3 (PR #1415): does any
retained mechanism select a **single** ray of `(R)` canonically,
equivalently does it place a Dirac measure on a one-element subset of
`(R)` from retained content alone?

## Conclusions

**Conclusion (T1) (sub-route-by-sub-route negative).** Under retained
authorities (X1)-(X4) and the tested sub-route set (S1)-(S3), no
sub-route supplies a single-ray Dirac measure on the six-ray
`D_3`-reflection-fixed locus `(R)` in the `U(1)_b` doublet plane.
Specifically, each sub-route fails at the explicit step recorded in
the right column of the table above.

**Conclusion (T2) (six-ray locus is the maximal retained
discrete-symmetry fix locus).** The maximal discrete-symmetry locus
of fixed rays on the `b`-plane reachable from retained operations is
the six-ray set `(R)`:

- `K` reflection fix locus: rays `α ∈ {0, π}` (the real axis).
- `C_3 K` reflection fix locus: rays `α ∈ {π/3, 4π/3}`.
- `C_3^2 K` reflection fix locus: rays `α ∈ {2π/3, 5π/3}`.

Symbolically the three reflection fix sets are pairwise disjoint, and
their union is `(R)`. No retained intersection of two or more
non-trivial group elements has fix locus a single ray. In particular:

- All three pairwise reflection-intersection fix loci are empty
  (reflections in `D_3` share only the origin).
- The triple-intersection fix locus is also the origin only.
- Joint fix with `γ` (point inversion `b → -b`) further collapses to
  the origin (since `γ` interchanges opposite rays of `(R)`).

Hence:

```text
Sharpened residue (Cycle 4):
  the smallest discrete subset of (R) supported by a retained
  intersection of two or more retained discrete symmetries is the
  empty set; no retained mechanism singles out one ray of (R) as a
  Dirac-measure support.
```

**Conclusion (T3) (structural-character distinction from Cycle 3).**
The Cycle 4 sharpened residue is structurally **distinct** from the
Cycle 3 residue:

- Cycle 3 / PR #1415 (continuous-`SO(2)` failure): no retained
  symmetry generates a continuous-`SO(2)` quotient. Six fixed rays
  exist as the `D_3`-reflection-fixed locus, but the question of
  selecting one was preserved as the named contingency.
- Cycle 4 (single-ray Dirac-measure failure): the six-ray locus is
  the maximal discrete fix locus; intersecting more retained
  symmetries does not collapse to a one-ray subset because the
  retained group structure `<C_3, K, γ>` does not produce a stabiliser
  that is exactly cardinality `1` on `(R)`.

These two residues map into each other (continuous-`SO(2)` failure
implies dimension-1 measure failure on the `R^2` plane in general;
six-ray finite-measure failure is the specific finite-symmetry
contingency named by Cycle 3) but the Cycle 4 framing names a sharper
load-bearing wall: **finite-Dirac single-ray selector is not retained**,
not merely "continuous-`SO(2)` quotient is not retained". A retained
mechanism would need either (a) a new retained symmetry breaking the
six-ray orbit further (e.g., an additional `Z_2` or `Z_3` distinct
from `<C_3, K, γ>`), or (b) a new retained non-symmetry mechanism
(e.g., a retained `D_3`-invariant function on `(R)` whose minimum
locus is a one-ray subset). Neither (a) nor (b) is in the current
retained authorities (X1)-(X4) nor in `A_min`.
