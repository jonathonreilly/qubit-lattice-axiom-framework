## 0. Object and result

The connected plaquette series is
`Delta(beta) = P_full(beta) - P_1plaq(beta) = sum_{n>=5} d_n beta^n`. Its exact
low-order coefficients are produced by the four elementary cube shells through
the marked plaquette (`d_5 = 4/18^5`, four shells each `1/18^5`). This note gives
those cube-shell contributions in **closed form to all orders**.

Let `J(beta) = sum_{N>=0} a_N beta^N` be the single-plaquette character
generating function defined by the on-main order-3 dominant-weight
recurrence (the Picard-Fuchs / character recurrence reproduced on main in
[`scripts/frontier_beta6_delta_analytic_class_2026_05_30.py`](../scripts/frontier_beta6_delta_analytic_class_2026_05_30.py)
and the V1 Picard-Fuchs notes
[`PLAQUETTE_V1_PICARD_FUCHS_ODE_ALL_ORDER_PROOF_NOTE_2026-05-09.md`](PLAQUETTE_V1_PICARD_FUCHS_ODE_ALL_ORDER_PROOF_NOTE_2026-05-09.md)):

```text
6 (N+1)(N+4)(N+5) a_{N+1} = N(N+1) a_N + 2(2N+3) a_{N-1} + a_{N-2},
a_0 = 1,  a_1 = 0,  a_2 = 1/36.
```

Put `K(beta) = log J(beta)` (so `K' = J'/J = P_1plaq`, the single-plaquette
character mean, leading coefficient `1/18`; `K''` its variance).

> **Theorem (cube-sector closed form).** The connected contribution of the four
> cube shells to `Delta(beta)` is, order by order, the Taylor series of
>
> ```text
> Delta_cube(beta) = 72 * K''(beta) * (K'(beta))^5,   K = log J.
> ```
>
> Its Taylor coefficients reproduce **exactly** `d_5 = 1/472392`,
> `d_6 = 7/5668704`, `d_7 = 5/17006112`, the order-`beta^8` coefficient
> `d_8 = 5/272097792`, and the cube-sector part of
> `d_9 = -235/29386561536`. The closed form is **import-free** (the on-main
> recurrence plus exact rational arithmetic only).

**Linked-cluster origin.** A cube shell has 6 faces: the marked plaquette `p0`
(carrying the observable, hence a connected 2-cumulant `K''`) and 5 action faces
(each a 1-cumulant `K'`). Resumming all multiplicity insertions on the faces
replaces each per-face factor by the full `beta`-dependent `K'',K'`; the constant
`72 = 4 shells * 18` fixes the leading term `72*(1/18)*(1/18)^5 = 4/18^5 = d_5`.

## 2. Cube-sector singularity diagnostic

`Delta_cube`'s only singularities are the zeros of `J` (poles of `K = log J`).
The SU(3) Haar-integral form makes `J(beta)` positive for real `beta`; the
runner also samples `J(beta) > 0` on `[0,8]`. Newton iteration on the
recurrence-generated `J` locates the complex-conjugate zero witness

```text
beta = 8.2052 * exp(+- i 66.152 deg)
```

The root witness is useful radius evidence, but this note does not supply an
argument-principle or interval proof that no zero of `J` lies inside `|beta|=6`.
The zero-free-disk statement is therefore not used as a theorem premise here.

## 3. Precise scope (what survives — honesty, non-negotiable)

- **Cube sector only.** By the GF(3) cycle-space certificate (the `d_8` note /
  PR #2408): the color-closable 2-cycle weights 7, 8, 9 are **empty**, so
  `Delta = (four-cube-shell sector)` **exactly through** `d_8`. But weight 10 =
  order `beta^9` is **nonempty** (60-80 new two-cube-overlap, non-`O_h`
  supports), so the **full** `Delta` reopens at `beta^9` with topologies this
  closed form does **not** organize.
- **Not a closure.** This note asserts no value of `Delta(6)` or `<P>(6)`,
  reuses no Monte-Carlo comparator, and closes neither the resummation route nor
  `beta=6`. It resums one sector to all orders as a formal expression and gives
  a numerical zero witness outside `|beta|=6`.
- **What it sharpens.** The exact coefficient identities move the finite-order
  combinatorics from the elementary cube shells to the new two-cube-overlap
  sector at `beta^9`. Any stronger statement about the full radius of
  convergence still needs the separate zero-free/growth control absent here.
