## 2. Coupled triangular graph hierarchy

Use indices0,...,n. Define nonnegative lower-triangular matrices

    L_(j,k)=binom(j,k) kappa_(j-k) if j>k; zero otherwise,
    D_(j,k)=binom(j,k) d_(j-k) if j>=k; zero otherwise.

Let 1 denote the vector of ones and set

    P_j(x)=(exp(xL)1)_j,
    Q_j(x)=(D exp(xL)1)_j
          =sum_(k=0)^j binom(j,k)d_k P_(j-k)(x).              (G3)

L is nilpotent, so these are finite polynomials with nonnegative coefficients,
of degree at most j. P_j(0)=1, P_0=1, Q_0=d_0. For example

    P_1=1+kappa_1 x,
    P_2=(1+kappa_1 x)^2+kappa_2 x,
    Q_1=d_0(1+kappa_1 x)+d_1.                                (G4)

The claimed new coupled bound is

    ||A^n(U_a(t)-U_ref(t))A^-n||
          <= epsilon_a B_a(t) Q_n(B_a(t)).                    (G5)

All displayed weighted operators mean bounded extensions with their domain
assertions. This is linear in epsilon_a, with polynomial control cost.

Proof: factor out exp(-itH), which commutes with A. For a unit vector x,
apply both interaction-picture propagators to A^-n x. Put y_j(t) equal to
the norm of A^j Y_ref(t)A^-n x and z_j(t) equal to the norm of
A^j(Y_a(t)-Y_ref(t))A^-n x. All initial y_j are <=1 and all z_j(0)=0.
Graph-space existence makes these vectors absolutely continuous. Commuting
A^j through the bounded generators gives the finite binomial identities.
Variation with the leading unitary removes its selfadjoint diagonal term.
Consequently

    y_j(t) <= 1+integral_0^t b_a(s)(L y(s))_j ds,
    z_j(t) <= integral_0^t b_a(s)
                       [(L z(s))_j+epsilon_a(D y(s))_j] ds.  (G6)

Here the k=0 difference term is retained in D. Omitting it would falsely
lose the ordinary bounded perturbation error. In the first inequality it
is only k>=1 commutators that increase a free graph norm. Triangular
induction gives y(t)<=exp(B_a(t)L)1 componentwise, and then

    z(t) <= epsilon_a integral_0^t b_a(s)
         exp((B_a(t)-B_a(s))L) D exp(B_a(s)L)1 ds.             (G7)

L and D commute. To check this, divide component j by j!. The conjugated
matrices are truncated lower-triangular Toeplitz matrices with entries
kappa_(j-k)/(j-k)! (positive order) and d_(j-k)/(j-k)! (including zero).
Their product is finite convolution, which is commutative. No commutation
between the physical interactions V is assumed. Thus G7 equals
epsilon_a B_a(t)D exp(B_a(t)L)1. Taking its nth component and the supremum
over x proves G5. This proof also retains the ordinary bound

    ||U_a-U_ref|| <= min{2,epsilon_a d_0 B_a(t)}.              (G8)

The same argument on an interval[s,t] uses its actual integrated budget.

## 2. G1-G2: norm-flow regularity gives the required domains and measurability

`K=lim_(t->0)(exp(itH)V exp(-itH)-V)/t` exists in operator norm and

    [exp(itH)V psi-V psi]/t

    A^j V A^-j

identity holds for the interaction-picture controls.

through order n. Equivalently, the usual C^n norm-flow definition
already includes these continuous derivatives.

    V_(a,I)(t)=sum_ell u_(ell,a)(t)
