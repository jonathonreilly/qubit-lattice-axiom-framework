# Candidate laws

Claim type: meta.

Candidate laws are the current working rules adopted to build on. They are not axioms. The [minimal axioms](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md), [policy section 6](audit/AXIOM_MINIMALITY_POLICY.md#6-explicit-owner-approval-for-axioms-and-primitives) and `docs/audit/data/axiom_premise_nodes.json` alone control premise admission, so this register has zero premise weight. A result that uses a candidate law is conditional on it.

The laws are judged against measured results, not against theoretical preference. Where a law restates an axiom clause, the note says so. The axioms are to be revisited once the laws account for the measured world.

## CL-1: update

> "Sites are the points of Z^3, tiled in two ways by 2x2x2 cubes offset by (1,1,1); each site's qubit is one fermion mode, and modes at different sites anticommute. The odds, one amplitude per joint occupation of the modes, change only by half-ticks that alternate between the tilings. In each half-tick every cube applies a unitary read from its own edges: the inverse spatial metric gamma^ij, fixed by its edge lengths, and the lapse N, the proper time per tick along the normal to its slice. The lapse is not free: every cube ticks at the same fraction of its own light crossing, sqrt(3) N times the rms of sqrt(gamma^ii) being one constant, sin(eps0), in every cube and every half-tick. Let W_i pass each site's odds to its edge neighbour along axis i with the phase of their spin-3/2 overlap along the body diagonals, each site keeping one overlap state shared by its two cubes. The cube's hop R is a sum of the W_i with positive weights, sqrt(gamma^ii) when gamma^ij is diagonal, scaled so that R^2 = 1 wherever none of the cube's registers is at an extreme value, and its step is eps0. Law-text notes (third review; see register-review3/changes.txt). The quote is regrouped into tick, links, edges and split paragraphs with no change of content except as flagged there: the closing sentence "A cube for which CL-3 has no stationary point keeps its edges and stops ticking" is deleted, since with edge registers there is no stationary point to fail, a collapsing cell's odds turn at the window floor, and stopping or skipping half-ticks is excluded by the heating each skip boundary makes (about 0.08 eps^2 excitations, about 1e125 W in a J0740+6620 core against about 1e27 W observed); "rms edge twice its flat length" is read as every edge at twice its flat length, since axis and diagonal edges have different flat lengths. Derived facts that stood in the quote are commentary: where no register is at an extreme value, R^2 = 1 makes the dressed tick exactly the free tick of the dressed modes; on equal edges the copy with s = -1 is the one the three-fold turns rotate by e^(2 pi i/3); and an edge register moves at all only if s exceeds about 0.85/sin eps0 (23 to 113 for the allowed eps0), since a slab changes ln l by less than sqrt 2 sin(eps0)/sqrt 3 per tick. Gaps still open in the text. (a) A cube's gamma^ij is fixed by eighteen edges (twelve axis edges and six face diagonals) through five tetrahedra that need not share one metric, so which reading of its edges the cube uses is unstated. (b) With lengths spaced as 2^(n/s), a whole number of steps is a flat displacement only at first order: two collinear axis edges shifted by plus and minus k steps sum to 2L cosh(2k ln 2/s), not 2L, so CL-3's displacement shift makes deficits of order (k ln 2/s)^2 on flat cells (reviewer's estimate, not run), far above the R l^2 breaking quoted under CL-3 for continuous lengths; equal steps within each octave (an octave count plus a linear mantissa) would make it exact inside an octave. (c) The window's walls are an edge-local term that also stiffens pure displacements, so the window itself breaks the displacement gauge CL-3 imposes; CL-3 now reads the floor in the minimal-strain frame, which fixes the gauge but does not remove the term. (d) "Children's odds outside the split image have no lowering below their window" needs a plain reading (presumably: only the split image is carried back, so no merge acts on other children's states). (e) CL-2's "a removed site keeps its records" gives records to a site absent from the actual pattern; it rests on the glued split and goes to the owner with CL-2's other record departure.

With equal edges R is the equal-weight hop W, and W is a unitary reflection exactly when every cube face carries phase -1. With one mode per site, a block unitary on sites and cube edges that hops and is covariant under the 24 rotations exists exactly when the site's rotation character is of spin-3/2 type, so the phase is forced rather than chosen; without the nearest-neighbour restriction it is not. The rotation action is then necessarily the double cover, but one mode cannot carry spin 3/2: the cube's eight corners form one spin-1/2 doublet times a two-dimensional copy factor, so the particles are spin 1/2.

Every species in a cube reads the same edges, so at tree level the shared step and the shared limiting speed follow from the geometry, and the cube's light cone is the null cone of its edges. After link dressing a step read from the edges alone would leave the electron slower than light by 0.17 to 1.0 alpha, which LHAASO excludes by about 1e16, and no symmetry of a finite-depth tick can forbid this, since every such symmetry fixes the time axis; so each species' step and mass angle are read from its dressed cone and dressed rest phase. That clause is adopted to meet measurement, not derived, and what is shared is the dressed cone. The mass-angle condition also absorbs, by rule, the additive mass the links induce. In a weak static field the reading makes all species fall alike up to (mu/eps)^2/4, below 2e-33 for every known particle against MICROSCOPE's 3e-15; clocks at rest redshift exactly; light bends by (1 + gamma) times the Newtonian value; and edges that grow redshift photons against atoms, with 1 + z the ratio of edge lengths. A single cube cannot carry a shift: det U(K) is fixed and reflections keep the spectrum symmetric, and on one cube the shift would come locked to helicity, which muon spin precession excludes. Read one rung up, moving whole cubes drags every species by exactly the shift, giving frame dragging at the general-relativity value (Gravity Probe B 39.1 mas/yr against a measured 37.2 +- 7.2), vanishing preferred-frame parameters (given that CL-3's field around a moving source is the general-relativity metric in the lattice frame, assumed rather than computed), light bending of 2 on every ray, and both gravitational-wave polarisations acting on matter. Given by every cube in every half-tick, the copy mass would split the light helicities at order K^2, far outside the Crab bound, which is why only the even cubes give it.

On flat edges the bands obey cos w = A S - (cos 2b - cos mu)/2, with b = pi/2 - eps, A = (cos 2b + cos mu)/2 and S the cubic structure factor. The light band is an isotropic massive Dirac band of mass mu, with speed sin(eps)/sqrt(3) block per tick, and its partner cone at K = pi(1,1,1) is gapped as long as the copy angle MN stays below 2 eps. eps is a time step: eps -> 0 is the continuous-time limit. Because eps is shared, every massless species has the same limiting speed; a massive one differs at order mu^2, which at a Planck-scale tick is far below measurement. A species-dependent step would make speeds differ; the absence of vacuum photon decay at 1.4 PeV bounds a faster photon to 3e-19 and SN1987A bounds the neutrino difference to 2e-9. Without the copy-mass sentence the light content is two degenerate Dirac copies, and no term covariant under all 24 rotations splits them at rest. The copy mass leaves exactly one light Dirac copy with no tuning. It keeps the 12 tetrahedral rotations, the translations, inversion and the combined CPT-type map, and it breaks the other 12 rotations, time reversal and particle-hole symmetry, but keeps both composed with a fourfold turn, so it carries no CP violation. At low energy it leaves the light band isotropic up to dimension-six terms. This narrows the rotation clause of the Admissibility axiom.

The split carries the sea exactly only because its map is not finite range: carrying the vacuum needs a kernel that falls as a power of distance, and a map of finite range r heats (see Start, splits and the departure law). So the split breaks the strict-cone premise as well as changing the Lattice axiom. It exists only while the parent's partner gap at K = pi(1,1,1) is open, that is while the copy angle MN stays below 2 eps0 at the split, or below about eps0 at a cube's birth; this is a condition on the supplied constant M. The children's site registers take parent values as shared boundary flux (weights 1, 1/2 and 1/4, forced by covariance and Gauss's law, since no covariant integer assignment exists); that the nearest unitary then carries the vacuum without heating and that a level-matched map carrying each sector's levels onto the children's lowest exists were shown for Z_3 registers on a cubic link lattice, and on the site bonds only at long wavelength (the coarse map leaks into the extra mode as q^2, 6e-4 at q = 0.1), so both need re-deriving for site registers.

**Why links are registers.** Links set to definite stationary values, the earlier reading, fail measurement on every count tested: they give classical Coulomb colour (no string tension, every Wilson loop of modulus 1), a QCD beta function of the wrong sign, zero topological susceptibility, a proton mass of about 9 MeV, free fractional charges, stationary levels that never decay, no antibunching, no gauge-mediated entanglement, a_e = 0 and a Lamb shift of -27 MHz, against 440 MeV, asymptotic freedom, (179 MeV)^4, 938 MeV, none, 1.60 ns for hydrogen 2p, antibunched light, Bell states from Coulomb gates, 1.1597e-3 and 1057.8 MHz. CL-2 forces the same conclusion: a link that responds as a definite value to the actual configuration makes the realised odds carry information about it, which breaks the Born distribution. So links carry odds, as CL-1's registers. The registers sit on the site bonds because any c-number dressing that keeps the cube hop a reflection is one phase per site, so a link's connection is the difference of a site's phases in its two cubes, a value on its bond from even-cube centre to odd-cube centre; with registers on cube edges the dressed hop is a reflection in no Gauss sector. Their site carrier costs states beyond one qubit (see Supplied pieces). The results on links below that were first derived for definite stationary links (Maxwell and Yang-Mills from a conserved current, the site-read link band) hold for the registers' equations of motion, but have not been re-derived for the register dynamics.

## CL-2: actuality

> "There is one actual configuration, distributed as its odds. Each half-tick it moves by the transport that keeps it so, uses only transitions (staying included) along nonzero elements of the half-tick, changes on average the fewest mode occupations, units of link electric value and units of edge-register value and, of those, has greatest entropy. The actual configuration lies in one refinement pattern; a transition between patterns counts changes through the passing, comparing inherited modes and edges with their passed values and not counting new modes. Each matter mode's occupation in it then gets a permanent record: a new site's first record comes at the half-tick after it appears, and a removed site keeps its records. Rishon and edge-register sites, whose occupations are the links' electric values and the edges' lengths, are part of the actual configuration but get no record, as the Record axiom allows. Only records are readable, and a record is read only by the half-tick that takes it as input; a past record enters a later reading only through a pointer, a mode whose occupation the update, acting on it as a control alone, leaves unchanged."

This replaces the Record axiom's "a site never carries more than one record" by one record per site per half-tick, the most one mode per site allows. Massive modes revisit sites, and a record locking a site's occupation outright would put Planck-scale noise into the gravitational source, which measurement excludes.

Such a transport always exists: the half-tick maps the odds of any set of configurations into the odds of the set it connects to, and a supply-demand argument then gives the transport. Counting changed occupations rather than changed configurations keeps unentangled relays independent, and the greatest-entropy choice makes the transport unique and independent of labels. The mean drift of relays equals the group velocity exactly. Records never condition the odds, because the joint update (CL-1 with CL-3's total phase) is the only change they undergo. The cost counts units of link electric value because with occupation-only cost every move of a link quantum is free, and greatest entropy then flips the actual electric values at order one per tick even in a stationary register state (0.85 jumps per tick on a 24-link chain, against 0 when electric units are counted); with them counted, an emitter's actual waiting times are exponential at the Wigner-Weisskopf rate (fitted to 1.01) with Born odds kept, as hydrogen 2p's 1.60 ns decay is measured. Edge-register units are counted for the same reason: without them greatest entropy moves the actual edge values at a Planck rate (0.977 changes per tick in a stationary register state), and with them stationary actual edges are frozen and moving ones move by exactly the forced amount. The pointer clause restricts what a reading is but no law defines a reader, so it is an interpretive rule, not a dynamical one: it is adopted because bare two-time record laws depend on distant settings (see Untested predictions).

The rule acts on the joint configuration of all modes. That is what makes two readers of one pointer agree and lets relays violate CHSH as measured. Number-changing transitions cover pair creation and annihilation.

## CL-3: response

> "Edges and links respond only to departures from the vacuum of the same edges, in the vacuum's own units.
>
> "Edge tick. Edges are CL-1's edge registers. Each half-tick applies to them the unitary generated by the edge hop together with the total phase read on the joint configuration of matter, links and edges, each cube's lapse following its edges. The edge hop is the Regge kinetic form of the edges, realised by one-register hops V_e + V_e^dagger and two-register hops V_e V_f + h.c. and V_e V_f^dagger + h.c. between registers sharing a hinge, each V normalised on its domain (the values at which every tetrahedron containing that edge keeps positive volume), with weights at which the vacuum's dressed kinetic form of each cell equals the hinge-sum second variation, the same in every cell, so that the dressed graviton cone is the edge cone. Where the edge registers of a block of many cubes hold coherent states, their mean lengths, apart from the lapse that CL-1 fixes, are those at which the total phase is stationary.
>
> "Momentum constraint and shift. The momentum constraint at a site is the generator of that site's displacement read on the flat lattice: the shift that changes each edge register at the site by whole steps, two on each axis edge and one on each diagonal per unit, a unit being the flat projection of a common displacement of 2 ln2/s times the local edge length, together with CL-1's block-pair rotation that carries the matter and link odds at that site back by the same displacement. The shift is its multiplier: stationarity in the shift gives the constraint, matter and link currents included, and does not fix the shift. The shift obeys the undamped wave law d_t^2 beta_i = mu c^2 [Delta beta_i - 2 d_j(K_ij - delta_ij K)] with 0 < mu < 1 and mu other than 1/3 (where the shift and lapse speeds coincide; supplied, affecting no stationary state), within CL-1's limit of one cube per tick: a wave inside the light cone whose only source is the divergence of the trace-reversed normal-frame distortion rate, equal on shell to twice 8 pi G times the matter momentum density, so static, stationary-rotating and homogeneous states give it no source and it keeps no memory. Edge values, the floor and the split trigger are read in that frame; only odds obeying the constraints in that frame occur, as only odds obeying Gauss's law occur for links, and each half-tick applies the unitary part of the tick restricted to those odds.
>
> "Hamiltonian constraint. It holds for the mean, not for each component of the odds: the dark constant, the tick's quasi-energy with the matter and link vacuum phases on the same edges subtracted (the edge terms, the constant per 4-volume included, are not subtracted), has zero mean in every cube, which the tick, splits included, keeps; its spread is not fixed.

Read through CL-3's site sum, the link action is the covariant Maxwell action: its light cone is the null cone of the edges at every order in the field, light bends by 2 on every ray, and the Shapiro delay and the photon's frame dragging take their general-relativity values. The plain one-to-one Yee pairing cannot read shear, which is why each site pairs the three links meeting at its even centre. The minimal shift reading drags orbital motion but not an elementary spin at rest, which needs a spin-connection term at the block rung; near Earth that effect is about 1e-29 eV.
- **Relay paths.** With CL-1's copy mass no tested plane wave splits into closed streams (0 of 72), since the even half-tick's two- and three-hop elements join the sublattices; without it about a third split. The same elements let relays jump two or three sites in one half-tick for 1e-2 to 0.3 of the odds, which plane waves never need. Counting each change by its squared hop length puts those jumps at the floor but brings closed streams back, while an entropic transport with weight exp(-h^2/T) has neither problem at the price of a supplied constant T. Pointer records persist and the joint configuration keeps the odds, so recorded tracks have the quantum statistics under every such rule; only a relay's own unread records differ.
