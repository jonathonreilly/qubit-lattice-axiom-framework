# Candidate laws

Claim type: meta.

Candidate laws are the current working rules adopted to build on. They are not axioms. The [minimal axioms](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md), [policy section 6](audit/AXIOM_MINIMALITY_POLICY.md#6-explicit-owner-approval-for-axioms-and-primitives) and `docs/audit/data/axiom_premise_nodes.json` alone control premise admission, so this register has zero premise weight. A result that uses a candidate law is conditional on it.

The laws are judged against measured results, not against theoretical preference. Where a law restates an axiom clause, the note says so. The axioms are to be revisited once the laws account for the measured world.

## CL-1: update

> "Sites are the points of Z^3, tiled in two ways by 2x2x2 cubes offset by (1,1,1); each site's qubit is one fermion mode, and modes at different sites anticommute. The odds, one amplitude per joint occupation of the modes, change only by half-ticks that alternate between the tilings. In each half-tick every cube applies a unitary read from its own edges: the inverse spatial metric gamma^ij, fixed by its edge lengths, and the lapse N, the proper time per tick along the normal to its slice, which is not free: every cube ticks at the same fraction of its own light crossing, sqrt(3) N times the rms of sqrt(gamma^ii) being one constant, sin(eps0), in every cube and every half-tick. Let W_i pass each site's odds to its edge neighbour along axis i with the phase of their spin-3/2 overlap along the body diagonals, each site keeping one overlap state shared by its two cubes. The cube's hop R is a sum of the W_i with positive weights, sqrt(gamma^ii) when gamma^ij is diagonal, scaled so that R^2 = 1 wherever none of the cube's registers is at an extreme value (there the dressed tick is exactly the free tick of the dressed modes), and its step is eps0. A species of mass m applies exp(-i(eps - mu/2)R) on every even cube and exp(+i(eps + mu/2)R) on every odd cube, with its step eps and mass angle mu the angles at which its dressed light cone, link dressing included, is the null cone of the cube's edges and its dressed rest phase is mN per tick; without links these are eps0 and mu = mN. Let s = i R_1 R_2 R_3 R, with R_i = sqrt(3) W_i read from the even cube's undressed hops, be the cube's copy sign (on equal edges its -1 copy is the one the three-fold turns rotate by e^(2 pi i/3)); each even cube applies exp(i (M N/4)(1 - s) R) before and after its half-tick, giving that copy an extra mass M. The shift and the shear are read one rung up: the even cubes, taken as sites, are tiled in two ways by 2x2x2 blocks offset by one cube; between the even and the odd half-tick every block of the first tiling rotates each pair of its cubes along axis i into each other by the angle theta_i with sin(2 theta_i) the shift per tick in cubes, plus a part proportional to the shear and to the odd half-tick's displacement along the other axes, and every block of the second tiling undoes the same rotation in the same site labels. Hop weights, angles and shear parts are those for which the cube's light cone is the null cone of its edges. A cube's split image is the state its eight children take from it by the passing below. No cube has a state with every edge register full: at the middle of an even half-tick, the raise of an edge register that would make every edge of a cube full (rms edge twice its flat length) carries the odds onto that cube's split image instead, and the matching lowering carries the split image back, so the update is unitary on the sum over refinement patterns, a tensor product over parent cubes of each cube's patterns, and children's odds outside the split image have no lowering below their window. The passing: it applies half of that half-tick; each corner mode passes, shared evenly, to that corner of the eight children, and the children's other modes start half filled as in the children's vacuum; each register's electric value passes as the flux of its even cube's children into the child centres on that cube's boundary, each centre's flux shared evenly among the registers whose odd ends are its nearest corners, and the children's other electric values start as in the children's vacuum with those totals and the children's charges; each edge register along a parent edge passes as the two children's edges along it at half its length, and the children's other edge registers start as in the children's vacuum of the same geometry; the odds then take the unitary nearest to this passing that carries the update's vacuum, link and edge registers included, onto the children's update's vacuum and each excitation of given charges, flux and momentum onto the children's lowest excitations of the same; and the children apply the other half, each read from its own edges. Each site carries a quantum link register on its bond from its even-cube centre to its odd-cube centre, valued in the update's symmetries (those the update commutes with when every register is transformed too, fixed coefficients staying fixed), made of 2S fermion-mode sites at each end (rishons, one set per factor of those symmetries, with a size S per factor that is supplied) role-assigned in a coarser block: even cubes hop undressed, and each odd-cube hop from x to y is dressed by V_y^dagger V_x, with V the register's raising operator normalised on its domain, so each cube's cluster fermion number is conserved, each half-tick also advances the odds by the links' plaquette phase of CL-3, and only odds obeying Gauss's law occur. Each edge of the spatial triangulation likewise carries a quantum edge register (the axis edges, which are the tilings' cube edges, and on every square face of Z^3 the diagonal joining its two even-sum corners, so that every unit cube is five tetrahedra alternating between neighbours: six registers per site, one per metric component) of 4s fermion-mode sites at each end, role-assigned in a coarser block; its length is its flat length (the rung length on an axis edge, sqrt 2 times it on a diagonal) times 2^(n/s - 3), with n the occupation at its first end, so lengths run from an eighth of the flat length to twice it, a split leaves each child edge at n = 3s, and a register moves only if s exceeds about 0.85/sin eps0, and only lengths at which every tetrahedron of the triangulation has positive volume occur; each cube's half-tick is the unitary above controlled on its edge registers' values. An update's vacuum is its state with every negative-quasi-energy mode filled. A cube for which CL-3 has no stationary point keeps its edges and stops ticking."

Phrases of the quoted text that differ from the current register are law-text changes for the lead's ruling (second review): the rishon parenthesis no longer fixes S = 3/2 for U(1) or one colour set per end for SU(3), since those sizes give cell-scale couplings far above the measured ones and the sizes are supplied (see Link-register carriers); and the split now passes the edge registers explicitly and carries the edge registers' vacuum as well as the links' (a relabelling split makes gravitons at about 4e6 times rho_Lambda). Four gaps in the quoted text are not repaired here. (a) "Each edge of a tiling's cubes" gives neither the off-diagonal gamma^ij nor any triangle, so which edges carry registers (face or body diagonals of a triangulation) is unstated, and "only lengths whose triangles are nondegenerate occur" is a projection that the update is not shown to keep (for mixed axis and face-diagonal edges it fails on about 7% of length triples). (b) An edge register spans only one factor of two, [l, 2l] in rung lengths, and CL-1 has no merge, the inverse of the split, so a cube whose edges shrink below the rung length has no state; every collapse result below assumes edges that shrink without bound. (c) With edge registers the split trigger, the lapse and the tick schedule are operators: the tiling, the number of half-ticks and so the number of records become branch-dependent, and CL-1 does not say how a split controlled on the edge registers acts, nor CL-2 how its per-half-tick records are counted across such branches. (d) The last sentence, "a cube for which CL-3 has no stationary point", has no definite meaning once edges are registers, since stationarity is only their coherent many-cell limit; it needs restating as a condition on edge-register values or dropping (homogeneous collapse never invokes it).

With equal edges R is the equal-weight hop W, and W is a unitary reflection exactly when every cube face carries phase -1. With one mode per site, a block unitary on sites and cube edges that hops and is covariant under the 24 rotations exists exactly when the site's rotation character is of spin-3/2 type, so the phase is forced rather than chosen; without the nearest-neighbour restriction it is not. The rotation action is then necessarily the double cover, but one mode cannot carry spin 3/2: the cube's eight corners form one spin-1/2 doublet times a two-dimensional copy factor, so the particles are spin 1/2.

Every species in a cube reads the same edges, so at tree level the shared step and the shared limiting speed follow from the geometry, and the cube's light cone is the null cone of its edges. After link dressing a step read from the edges alone would leave the electron slower than light by 0.17 to 1.0 alpha, which LHAASO excludes by about 1e16, and no symmetry of a finite-depth tick can forbid this, since every such symmetry fixes the time axis; so each species' step and mass angle are read from its dressed cone and dressed rest phase. That clause is adopted to meet measurement, not derived, and what is shared is the dressed cone. The mass-angle condition also absorbs, by rule, the additive mass the links induce. In a weak static field the reading makes all species fall alike up to (mu/eps)^2/4, below 2e-33 for every known particle against MICROSCOPE's 3e-15; clocks at rest redshift exactly; light bends by (1 + gamma) times the Newtonian value; and edges that grow redshift photons against atoms, with 1 + z the ratio of edge lengths. A single cube cannot carry a shift: det U(K) is fixed and reflections keep the spectrum symmetric, and on one cube the shift would come locked to helicity, which muon spin precession excludes. Read one rung up, moving whole cubes drags every species by exactly the shift, giving frame dragging at the general-relativity value (Gravity Probe B 39.1 mas/yr against a measured 37.2 +- 7.2), vanishing preferred-frame parameters (given that CL-3's field around a moving source is the general-relativity metric in the lattice frame, assumed rather than computed), light bending of 2 on every ray, and both gravitational-wave polarisations acting on matter. Given by every cube in every half-tick, the copy mass would split the light helicities at order K^2, far outside the Crab bound, which is why only the even cubes give it.

On flat edges the bands obey cos w = A S - (cos 2b - cos mu)/2, with b = pi/2 - eps, A = (cos 2b + cos mu)/2 and S the cubic structure factor. The light band is an isotropic massive Dirac band of mass mu, with speed sin(eps)/sqrt(3) block per tick, and its partner cone at K = pi(1,1,1) is gapped as long as the copy angle MN stays below 2 eps. eps is a time step: eps -> 0 is the continuous-time limit. Because eps is shared, every massless species has the same limiting speed; a massive one differs at order mu^2, which at a Planck-scale tick is far below measurement. A species-dependent step would make speeds differ; the absence of vacuum photon decay at 1.4 PeV bounds a faster photon to 3e-19 and SN1987A bounds the neutrino difference to 2e-9. Without the copy-mass sentence the light content is two degenerate Dirac copies, and no term covariant under all 24 rotations splits them at rest. The copy mass leaves exactly one light Dirac copy with no tuning. It keeps the 12 tetrahedral rotations, the translations, inversion and the combined CPT-type map, and it breaks the other 12 rotations, time reversal and particle-hole symmetry, but keeps both composed with a fourfold turn, so it carries no CP violation. At low energy it leaves the light band isotropic up to dimension-six terms. This narrows the rotation clause of the Admissibility axiom.

The split carries the sea exactly only because its map is not finite range: carrying the vacuum needs a kernel that falls as a power of distance, and a map of finite range r heats (see Start, splits and the departure law). So the split breaks the strict-cone premise as well as changing the Lattice axiom. It exists only while the parent's partner gap at K = pi(1,1,1) is open, that is while the copy angle MN stays below 2 eps0 at the split, or below about eps0 at a cube's birth; this is a condition on the supplied constant M. The children's site registers take parent values as shared boundary flux (weights 1, 1/2 and 1/4, forced by covariance and Gauss's law, since no covariant integer assignment exists); that the nearest unitary then carries the vacuum without heating and that a level-matched map carrying each sector's levels onto the children's lowest exists were shown for Z_3 registers on a cubic link lattice, and on the site bonds only at long wavelength (the coarse map leaks into the extra mode as q^2, 6e-4 at q = 0.1), so both need re-deriving for site registers.

**Why links are registers.** Links set to definite stationary values, the earlier reading, fail measurement on every count tested: they give classical Coulomb colour (no string tension, every Wilson loop of modulus 1), a QCD beta function of the wrong sign, zero topological susceptibility, a proton mass of about 9 MeV, free fractional charges, stationary levels that never decay, no antibunching, no gauge-mediated entanglement, a_e = 0 and a Lamb shift of -27 MHz, against 440 MeV, asymptotic freedom, (179 MeV)^4, 938 MeV, none, 1.60 ns for hydrogen 2p, antibunched light, Bell states from Coulomb gates, 1.1597e-3 and 1057.8 MHz. CL-2 forces the same conclusion: a link that responds as a definite value to the actual configuration makes the realised odds carry information about it, which breaks the Born distribution. So links carry odds, as CL-1's registers. The registers sit on the site bonds because any c-number dressing that keeps the cube hop a reflection is one phase per site, so a link's connection is the difference of a site's phases in its two cubes, a value on its bond from even-cube centre to odd-cube centre; with registers on cube edges the dressed hop is a reflection in no Gauss sector. Their site carrier costs states beyond one qubit (see Supplied pieces). The results on links below that were first derived for definite stationary links (Maxwell and Yang-Mills from a conserved current, the site-read link band) hold for the registers' equations of motion, but have not been re-derived for the register dynamics.

## CL-2: actuality

> "There is one actual configuration, distributed as its odds. Each half-tick it moves by the transport that keeps it so, uses only transitions (staying included) along nonzero elements of the half-tick, changes the fewest mode occupations, units of link electric value and units of edge length on average and, of those, has greatest entropy; each matter mode's occupation in it then gets a permanent record; rishon and edge-register sites, whose occupations are the links' electric values and the edges' lengths, are part of the actual configuration but get no record, as the Record axiom allows. The actual configuration lies in one refinement pattern; its records are those of that pattern's matter modes, a new site's first record at the half-tick after it appears and a removed site keeping its records, and a transition between patterns counts changes through the passing, inherited modes and edges compared to their passed values and new modes not counted. Only records are readable, and a record is read only by the half-tick that takes it as input; a past record enters a later reading only through a pointer, a mode whose occupation the update, acting on it as a control alone, leaves unchanged."

This replaces the Record axiom's "a site never carries more than one record" by one record per site per half-tick, the most one mode per site allows. Massive modes revisit sites, and a record locking a site's occupation outright would put Planck-scale noise into the gravitational source, which measurement excludes.

Such a transport always exists: the half-tick maps the odds of any set of configurations into the odds of the set it connects to, and a supply-demand argument then gives the transport. Counting changed occupations rather than changed configurations keeps unentangled relays independent, and the greatest-entropy choice makes the transport unique and independent of labels. The mean drift of relays equals the group velocity exactly. Records never condition the odds, because the joint update (CL-1 with CL-3's total phase) is the only change they undergo. The cost counts units of link electric value because with occupation-only cost every move of a link quantum is free, and greatest entropy then flips the actual electric values at order one per tick even in a stationary register state (0.85 jumps per tick on a 24-link chain, against 0 when electric units are counted); with them counted, an emitter's actual waiting times are exponential at the Wigner-Weisskopf rate (fitted to 1.01) with Born odds kept, as hydrogen 2p's 1.60 ns decay is measured. Edge-register units are not counted in the cost, though the same argument applies to them; that is a gap in the law text. The pointer clause restricts what a reading is but no law defines a reader, so it is an interpretive rule, not a dynamical one: it is adopted because bare two-time record laws depend on distant settings (see Untested predictions).

The rule acts on the joint configuration of all modes. That is what makes two readers of one pointer agree and lets relays violate CHSH as measured. Number-changing transitions cover pair creation and annihilation.

## CL-3: response

> "Edges respond only to departures from the vacuum of the same edges, in the vacuum's own units. The shift, like the edge lengths, is the one at which the total phase is stationary, and only odds obeying the edges' momentum constraint occur, as only odds obeying Gauss's law occur for links. The Hamiltonian constraint is a condition on the mean, not on the odds, since the dark constant (the tick's quasi-energy counted from the vacuum's on the same edges) generates the tick and odds with a sharp value of it never change: the dark constant has zero mean, which the tick keeps exactly together with its whole distribution, and with the momentum constraint the mean is zero in every cube; each split and crush carries odds of zero mean dark constant into odds of zero mean dark constant. Its spread is not fixed; it is what lets the odds change from tick to tick (a Planck-energy spread over a Hubble volume suffices, about 6e-53 of the dark radiation N_eff allows). The edges' momentum constraint at a site is the generator of that site's displacement, the edge-register shift that changes each edge at the site by the flat projection of one common displacement (two steps on each axis edge, one on each diagonal, per unit); only odds invariant under every such shift occur, and each half-tick applies the unitary part of the tick restricted to those odds, so the constraint holds exactly and the tick stays unitary. Edges are CL-1's edge registers: each half-tick applies to the edge registers the unitary generated by the edge hop (the Regge kinetic form of the edges, realised by one-register hops V_e + V_e^dagger and two-register hops V_e V_f + h.c. and V_e V_f^dagger + h.c. between registers sharing a hinge, each V normalised on its domain, the values at which every tetrahedron containing that edge keeps positive volume, with weights at which the vacuum's dressed kinetic form of each cell equals the hinge-sum second variation, the same in every cell, so that the dressed graviton cone is the edge cone) together with the total phase read on the joint configuration of matter, links and edges, with each cube's lapse following its edges, and where the edge registers of a block of many cubes hold coherent states their mean lengths, apart from the lapse that CL-1 fixes, are those at which the total phase is stationary. The total phase at each joint configuration is the matter phase: each site's share of the phase by which the update's terms advance the odds there, each interaction term counted once and shared equally between the sites it touches, minus the same share for the vacuum odds at the same configuration, on the same edges; minus the sum over hinges of area times deficit, with rotation deficits around hinges that contain a tick direction and rapidity deficits around the others, plus a constant per 4-volume, both in fixed proper units; plus the links' plaquette phase, likewise counted from the links' vacuum at the same configuration on the same edges. Each link's plaquette phase is a sum over sites: every site counts its own register's electric value together with the other three registers at each of its two centres, and the six smallest register loops through its bond whose two diagonals lie along axes (two even and two odd centres) as one magnetic vector, each loop's flux taken along its area vector, half the cross product of its diagonals; both are read with the weights of the covariant field in the lapse, shift and full spatial metric of the site's two cubes, (sqrt(gamma)/N) gamma^ij for the electric part taken along the slice normal and (N/sqrt(gamma)) gamma_ij for the magnetic part, which in a conformally flat cell is each loop read in proper area; and the electric and magnetic coefficients are those at which the vacuum's dressed coupling of that link, at a fixed proper wavelength and in a uniform field of every proper direction, is a constant of that link, the same for both and in every cell."

Edges are quantum registers too, for three reasons: a definite edge reading the actual configuration answers every one-cell hop, and each hop radiates exactly (1/6)(1 - 1/N^3) q^2/a into the edges, about 5e7 W for a thermal nucleon against a cosmic budget near 3e-31 W; conditioning on the actual configuration of the other sites is the same local reading, since number conservation fixes each site; and a coarser conditioning split would be a supplied scale that breaks CL-2's Born rule and lets a remote setting change a readable distribution. With edge registers the joint update never reads the actual configuration, so CL-2 stays exactly Born-preserving, nothing signals, a hop never reaches the edges, and Page-Geilker passes because pointer, edge and test-mass values agree. The vacuum standard still cancels the edges' zero-point force exactly, leaving an edge-motion term that falls as 1/N for collective modes. Results derived with definite edges (Friedmann, light bending, GP-B, binary pulsars, w = -1) change only by (l_P/L)^2; the crush and split results sit at Planck curvature, where the coherent many-cell limit is not controlled, and need re-deriving, the triangle constraint for mixed edges and the stability of the volume mode under the fixed lapse are open, and the register's inflation numbers, which assume quantised edges, now apply. The plaquette coefficients CL-3 defines are what CL-1's registers apply; they are fixed self-consistently, since the vacuum's dressed coupling depends on the dynamics they define. The edge hop (S+ + S- normalised on its domain, sign opposite to matter's, weight from the rung length) is adopted so that the edge tick is unitary and expands, not derived from the vacuum-standard principle; the earlier edge-register results (entanglement of masses, the 1/N residual, the tensor seed) used a supplied harmonic edge kinetic term and should be re-checked with this hop. With the opposite kinetic sign every edge mode is ghost-like relative to matter, so if inhomogeneous edge modes propagate and couple to matter the vacuum could decay into matter plus negative-energy edge quanta; that is the open item stated above, and it decides whether CL-3 as written has a stable vacuum.

All gravity results below were derived with definite stationary edges and hold for the coherent many-cell limit of the edge registers, up to (l_P/L)^2. Advancing the odds by the phase alone conserves every edge-value probability and expands nothing, and the bare kernel e^(i Phi) is not unitary on finite registers, so the edge tick is the unitary above. A one-register hop's sign is a relabelling ((-1)^n) with no content; the kinetic signature, five normal modes and one ghost (mostly the conformal combination), is carried by the two-register hop signs, and one-register hops alone cannot give the Regge kinetic form. Static responses (Newton's law, the adiabatic gravity-entanglement phase) do not depend on the kinetic form and reach their continuum values once each register's zero-point spread is at least about 4 values (S of at least about 10, from the graviton-mass bound) and well inside the window; with a bare rung-length weight the vacuum's momentum spread would move the graviton cone off the edge cone by about 1/(16 sigma_n^2), which GW170817 would push to S above 1e7, so the dressed weight clause is needed. Under the fixed lapse the homogeneous volume mode is then a ghost with no restoring force: its tick is unitary, coherent states follow the Friedmann orbit exactly in the mean and spread only linearly in ticks, and with matter it drives nothing beyond ordinary cosmological particle creation. Edges are classical only on blocks of many cubes, set by l_rung^2/(8 pi G hbar), about 4 on the unit map, not by the register size; a single cube's relative spread is about 0.2, while a Hubble patch at recombination spreads by 1e-84 to 3e-56. ## Candidate four-dimensional update (not adopted)

## Untested predictions

Each is conditional on the laws as written; the content-dependent ones are marked.

- **Photon decay with a cubic sky pattern.** CL-1's copy mass makes the light fermion band slower than the photon by a dimension-six term, so photons above roughly 80 to 110 PeV can decay to electron-positron pairs, except along the four body diagonals of the lattice. - **The lattice spacing is a few Planck lengths** (from G = a^2/(8 pi) per cell).

Inside CL-1 nothing yet does so usefully, and the edge registers seed only tensors (next item). With the step fixed at eps0 the tick reads the edges only through the mass and copy angles mN and MN, both proportional to the edge, so expansion never creates massless quanta. The copy-angle ramp across a split cycle creates pairs at a rate growing as (H N)^2 and saturating once a cycle lasts a few ticks, a fixed fraction of the critical density per split (at most about 0.09 per species at the split condition's edge); this is the marginal case, which either dies away or holds an endless Planck-scale steady state with lambda = 1, and it creates no number, no anisotropy at the mean and no CP-odd excess. A CL-1 term with a fixed per-tick angle gives m^2/H^2 >= angle^2/(Ha)^2, at least 1.5e5 for angles of eps0 size, so a fixed lambda needs a term clocked by the expansion; a definite field set by stationarity cannot carry it, since the vacuum seeds nothing. On present results the law needs a new term in CL-1 or a scalar register with its own clock.
  - With quantum edges the de Sitter phase seeds tensor modes from the edge registers' zero-point motion: P_t = 2H^2/(pi^2 M_P^2) up to O(H l_c), with n_t = 0 and a log-periodic ripple of period ln 2 from the split cascade. The vacuum standard does not remove them, since it subtracts only matter and link phases and frozen modes are departures with occupation (aH/2k)^2, and CL-2 makes each frozen amplitude one definite Born-distributed value. No linear scalar seed exists: tensors reach the lapse and the split trigger only at second order, and the fixed-lapse dark constant has no zero-point fluctuation; a propagating volume mode of tensor-like weight would give r of about 16 (conditional on the volume mode propagating, which is not yet computed), so a scalar seed needs a supplied clock with epsilon <= 2.3e-3. CL-3's single constant gives no early large Lambda (its only de Sitter phase is today's, P_t about 5e-122), and the split must carry the edges' vacuum onto the children's, since a relabelling split would make gravitons at about 4e6 times rho_Lambda.
  - Candidate, expansion-clocked angle (not adopted). The minimal such addition is an angle proportional to the edge change per tick on a bosonic mode carried by the odds (a further register beyond the one qubit): it gives m^2/H^2 = c in every cube whatever H is, a tilt of exactly 3 - 2 sqrt(9/4 - c) with no running, and lambda = g^-2 with g the mode's growth per split. The measured tilt needs c = -0.053 (Planck) or -0.039 (ACT), with r small for any allowed H; no law fixes c, and the exit, amplitude, reheating, B and CP stay supplied. Rule-number values of lambda land in the measured windows about as often as chance.
  - Candidate, curvature squared (not adopted). A curvature-squared term per proper 4-volume with coefficient about 5e8, fitted to the measured amplitude, would give n_s = 0.962 and r = 0.004, reheating through the colour anomaly at about 1e8 GeV, which is -0.8 sigma from Planck but -4.1 sigma from ACT DR6 combined with DESI; every exponential plateau gives 1 - 2/N, so meeting ACT needs a second supplied deformation, a power-law plateau or a curvature-cubed term, and the lattice's own higher terms are ten or more orders too small. With quantum edges the quoted numbers apply, but the rules still supply no curvature-squared term (edge loops give a coefficient of about 0.7 against 5e8 needed), and with every rule component at w <= 1/3 the ACT miss is at least 2.9 sigma (4.1 sigma with the rules' reheating).
  - Baryon excess and CP. The baryon excess needs a CP-odd relative phase between two four-mode terms of one class, which no law fixes, plus a hot start; order-one values overshoot the measured 6.1e-10 by 1e3 to 1e4. Quark-mixing CP violation needs the doublet register (without it no charged current is linked) and the family-sector translation breaking above (without it J = 0).
  - A departure-creating law must: change the odds through a CL-1 term or a register with its own clock (the edge registers give tensors only); carry its own clock (lambda of about 0.976 to 0.982 per split); keep r below 0.036; create at least 1e80 occupied modes into the half-filled sea; break B and CP to give 6.1e-10; and not heat the sea at the cell scale, nor below the Hubble radius during inflation.
- **Response gaps.**
  - Sea response. The sea's induced link term has the measured sign and running, but at one loop it would slow light relative to matter by about 7e-4 if the cones were fixed at tree level, far outside the 1e-11 bound; CL-3's link unit, the vacuum's dressed coupling taken equal for the electric and magnetic parts, is what removes this. Because the sea reads the same edges as matter, its induced gravity would have no Newtonian part, soften the flat lattice under shear and make the tensor and Newtonian couplings differ by about 20%, which binary-pulsar timing excludes; CL-3 therefore measures the matter phase from the vacuum phase on the same edges. That clause is adopted to meet measurement rather than derived, and on time-dependent edges it needs an adiabatic reference vacuum.
  - Vacuum standard off the vacuum's number sector. Because the vacuum is the sea, sea modes must not enter the source, or the sea adds Planck-scale noise to it. CL-3's per-configuration subtraction is defined only where the vacuum odds are nonzero: subtracting local vacuum phases at the same configuration keeps the mean and has no noise for every eigenstate, but an added electron has no vacuum amplitude at its own configurations, so the law text does not yet say what is subtracted there.
  - Source conservation. The earlier problem, an actual-configuration source that jumps whenever another part's configuration changes, is gone: with edge registers nothing reads the actual configuration. What remains is that the joint update must conserve the source operator so that the edge equations close; that is not shown.
  - Dressed cones. The dressed cones of charged fermions are set by CL-1 plus their link dressing, and no response law can fix them. One-loop shifts of order alpha/pi times an unforbidden lattice constant would exceed the LEP and LHAASO bounds by many orders; CL-1's dressed-cone clause fixes each species' cone by rule, adopted rather than derived, and a symmetry that forbids the shift would replace it.
  - Shift and shear. Read through CL-3's site sum, the link action is the covariant Maxwell action: its light cone is the null cone of the edges at every order in the field, light bends by 2 on every ray, and the Shapiro delay and the photon's frame dragging take their general-relativity values. The plain one-to-one Yee pairing cannot read shear, which is why each site pairs the three links meeting at its even centre. The minimal shift reading drags orbital motion but not an elementary spin at rest, which needs a spin-connection term at the block rung; near Earth that effect is about 1e-29 eV.
- **Relay paths.** With CL-1's copy mass no tested plane wave splits into closed streams (0 of 72), since the even half-tick's two- and three-hop elements join the sublattices; without it about a third split. The same elements let relays jump two or three sites in one half-tick for 1e-2 to 0.3 of the odds, which plane waves never need. Counting each change by its squared hop length puts those jumps at the floor but brings closed streams back, while an entropic transport with weight exp(-h^2/T) has neither problem at the price of a supplied constant T. Pointer records persist and the joint configuration keeps the odds, so recorded tracks have the quantum statistics under every such rule; only a relay's own unread records differ.
