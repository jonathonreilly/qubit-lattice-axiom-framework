# Candidate laws

Claim type: meta.

Candidate laws are the current working rules adopted to build on. They are not axioms. The [minimal axioms](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md), [policy section 6](audit/AXIOM_MINIMALITY_POLICY.md#6-explicit-owner-approval-for-axioms-and-primitives) and `docs/audit/data/axiom_premise_nodes.json` alone control premise admission, so this register has zero premise weight. A result that uses a candidate law is conditional on it.

The laws are judged against measured results, not against theoretical preference. Where a law restates an axiom clause, the note says so. The axioms are to be revisited once the laws account for the measured world.

## CL-1: update

> "Sites are the points of Z^3, tiled in two ways by 2x2x2 cubes offset by (1,1,1); each site's qubit is one fermion mode, and modes at different sites anticommute. The odds, one amplitude per joint occupation of the modes, change only by half-ticks that alternate between the tilings. In each half-tick every cube applies a unitary read from its own edges: the inverse spatial metric gamma^ij, fixed by its edge lengths, and the lapse N, the proper time per tick along the normal to its slice, which is not free: every cube ticks at the same fraction of its own light crossing, sqrt(3) N times the rms of sqrt(gamma^ii) being one constant, sin(eps0), in every cube and every half-tick. Let W_i pass each site's odds to its edge neighbour along axis i with the phase of their spin-3/2 overlap along the body diagonals, each site keeping one overlap state shared by its two cubes. The cube's hop R is a sum of the W_i with positive weights, sqrt(gamma^ii) when gamma^ij is diagonal, scaled so that R^2 = 1, and its step is eps0. A species of mass m applies exp(-i(eps - mu/2)R) on every even cube and exp(+i(eps + mu/2)R) on every odd cube, with its step eps and mass angle mu the angles at which its dressed light cone, link dressing included, is the null cone of the cube's edges and its dressed rest phase is mN per tick; without links these are eps0 and mu = mN. Let s = i R_1 R_2 R_3 R, with R_i = sqrt(3) W_i, be the cube's copy sign (on equal edges its -1 copy is the one the three-fold turns rotate by e^(2 pi i/3)); each even cube applies exp(i (M N/4)(1 - s) R) before and after its half-tick, giving that copy an extra mass M. The shift and the shear are read one rung up: the even cubes, taken as sites, are tiled in two ways by 2x2x2 blocks offset by one cube; between the even and the odd half-tick every block of the first tiling rotates each pair of its cubes along axis i into each other by the angle theta_i with sin(2 theta_i) the shift per tick in cubes, plus a part proportional to the shear and to the odd half-tick's displacement along the other axes, and every block of the second tiling undoes the same rotation in the same site labels. Hop weights, angles and shear parts are those for which the cube's light cone is the null cone of its edges. A cube whose rms edge reaches twice the rung length splits into its eight children at the middle of its next even half-tick: it applies half of that half-tick; each corner mode passes, shared evenly, to that corner of the eight children, and the children's other modes start half filled as in the children's vacuum; the odds then take the unitary nearest to this passing that carries the cube's vacuum onto the children's; and the children apply the other half, each read from its own edges. An update's vacuum is its state with every negative-quasi-energy mode filled. A cube for which CL-3 has no stationary point keeps its edges and stops ticking."

With equal edges R is the equal-weight hop W, and W is a unitary reflection exactly when every cube face carries phase -1. With one mode per site, a block unitary on sites and cube edges that hops and is covariant under the 24 rotations exists exactly when the site's rotation character is of spin-3/2 type, so the phase is forced rather than chosen; without the nearest-neighbour restriction it is not. The rotation action is then necessarily the double cover, but one mode cannot carry spin 3/2: the cube's eight corners form one spin-1/2 doublet times a two-dimensional copy factor, so the particles are spin 1/2.

Every species in a cube reads the same edges, so at tree level the shared step and the shared limiting speed follow from the geometry, and the cube's light cone is the null cone of its edges. After link dressing a step read from the edges alone would leave the electron slower than light by 0.17 to 1.0 alpha, which LHAASO excludes by about 1e16, and no symmetry of a finite-depth tick can forbid this, since every such symmetry fixes the time axis; so each species' step and mass angle are read from its dressed cone and dressed rest phase. That clause is adopted to meet measurement, not derived, and what is shared is the dressed cone. The mass angle condition also absorbs the additive mass the links induce. In a weak static field the reading makes all species fall alike up to (mu/eps)^2/4, below 2e-33 for every known particle against MICROSCOPE's 3e-15; clocks at rest redshift exactly; light bends by (1 + gamma) times the Newtonian value; and edges that grow redshift photons against atoms, with 1 + z the ratio of edge lengths. A single cube cannot carry a shift: det U(K) is fixed and reflections keep the spectrum symmetric, and on one cube the shift would come locked to helicity, which muon spin precession excludes. Read one rung up, moving whole cubes drags every species by exactly the shift, giving frame dragging at the general-relativity value (Gravity Probe B 39.1 mas/yr against a measured 37.2 +- 7.2), vanishing preferred-frame parameters (given that CL-3's field around a moving source is the general-relativity metric in the lattice frame, assumed rather than computed), light bending of 2 on every ray, and both gravitational-wave polarisations acting on matter. Given by every cube in every half-tick, the copy mass would split the light helicities at order K^2, far outside the Crab bound, which is why only the even cubes give it.

On flat edges the bands obey cos w = A S - (cos 2b - cos mu)/2, with b = pi/2 - eps, A = (cos 2b + cos mu)/2 and S the cubic structure factor. The light band is an isotropic massive Dirac band of mass mu, with speed sin(eps)/sqrt(3) block per tick, and its partner cone at K = pi(1,1,1) is gapped as long as the copy mass M stays below 2 eps. eps is a time step: eps -> 0 is the continuous-time limit. Because eps is shared, every massless species has the same limiting speed; a massive one differs at order mu^2, which at a Planck-scale tick is far below measurement. A species-dependent step would make speeds differ; the absence of vacuum photon decay at 1.4 PeV bounds a faster photon to 3e-19 and SN1987A bounds the neutrino difference to 2e-9. Without the copy-mass sentence the light content is two degenerate Dirac copies, and no term covariant under all 24 rotations splits them at rest. The copy mass leaves exactly one light Dirac copy with no tuning. It keeps the 12 tetrahedral rotations, the translations, inversion and the combined CPT-type map, and it breaks the other 12 rotations, time reversal and particle-hole symmetry, but keeps both composed with a fourfold turn, so it carries no CP violation. At low energy it leaves the light band isotropic up to dimension-six terms. This narrows the rotation clause of the Admissibility axiom; the axioms are to be revisited once the laws cover the measured world.

## CL-2: actuality

> "There is one actual configuration, distributed as its odds. Each half-tick it moves by the transport that keeps it so, uses only transitions (staying included) along nonzero elements of the half-tick, changes the fewest mode occupations on average and, of those, has greatest entropy; each mode's occupation in it then gets a permanent record. Only records are readable."


This replaces the Record axiom's "a site never carries more than one record" by one record per site per half-tick, the most one mode per site allows. Massive modes revisit sites, and a record locking a site's occupation outright would put Planck-scale noise into the gravitational source, which measurement excludes.



Such a transport always exists: the half-tick maps the odds of any set of configurations into the odds of the set it connects to, and a supply-demand argument then gives the transport. Counting changed occupations rather than changed configurations keeps unentangled relays independent, and the greatest-entropy choice makes the transport unique and independent of labels. The mean drift of relays equals the group velocity exactly. Records never condition the odds, because the update of CL-1 is the only change they undergo.

The rule acts on the joint configuration of all modes. That is what makes two readers of one pointer agree and lets relays violate CHSH as measured. Number-changing transitions cover pair creation and annihilation.

## CL-3: response

> "Edges and links respond only to departures from the vacuum of the same edges, in the vacuum's own units. Edge lengths, apart from the lapse that CL-1 fixes, and links valued in the update's exactly conserved symmetries, take the values at which the total phase, with each cube's lapse following its edges, is stationary. The total phase is the matter phase at the actual configuration: each site's share of the phase by which the update's terms advance the odds there, each interaction term counted once and shared equally between the sites it touches, minus the same share for the vacuum odds at the same configuration, on the same edges with flat links; minus the sum over hinges of area times deficit, with rotation deficits around hinges that contain a tick direction and rapidity deficits around the others, plus a constant per 4-volume, both in fixed proper units; plus the links' plaquette phase. Each link's plaquette phase is a sum over sites: every site counts the three bonds of its odd cube's centre cell that meet at its even centre and the three faces of that cell that contain it, read in proper area from the lapse, shift and full spatial metric of its two cubes, with the electric plaquettes taken along the slice normal, and with electric and magnetic coefficients at which the vacuum's dressed coupling of that link, at a fixed proper wavelength (for an abelian link, in a uniform field), is a constant of that link, the same for both and in every cell."

The matter phase depends on edge lengths only through CL-1's reading, and any dressing that keeps the half-ticks unitary acts as one phase per site, so a link's connection is a phase at each site and its holonomies sit on the bonds between neighbouring centres of each tiling's cubes.

**Gravity.**
- The second variation is the linearised Einstein operator, with G = a^2/(8 pi) per cell.
- The field gives attraction and gamma = 1, and CL-1's full reading of the metric, with the shift and shear one rung up, gives light bending of 2 on every ray in every gauge.
- Counting the matter phase from the vacuum on the same edges removes every vacuum term from the edge response, so the tensor and Newtonian couplings both equal the hinge-sum G, against 0.81 without it and the Hulse-Taylor 0.998(2), and gravitational waves are not dressed by the sea.
- Taking the vacuum's dressed coupling as the unit of every link keeps alpha's measured running and holds alpha and the hadron masses fixed in a potential (residual about 2e-34 against the Yb+ clock bound 1.4e-8); it also puts the dressed photon cone on the edge cone in every cell, with the remaining lattice Lorentz violation falling as (mL)^2.
- Gravitational waves carry positive energy and travel at the matter light speed.
- Every part couples with the same strength, including binding energy and light.
- For a system that is its own pointer, the source follows the actual outcome (Page and Geilker 1981).

**Links.**
- A conserved value current gives Maxwell, and a self-coupled multiplet gives Yang-Mills with the Jacobi identity.
- The site phase and the centre-bond holonomies are one photon at two levels, related by an exact gauge-covariant map. In a diagonal cell the site-read link band equals the copy-mass-free fermion band at every momentum; CL-1's copy mass moves the light fermion band by a dimension-six term (dv/c = c6 K^2, c6 from -0.083 on the axes to 0 on the body diagonals) and shear by at most 0.02 sigma K^2, both far below the LHAASO and LEP bounds. At tree level the site-read link needs no calibration; with the sea, CL-3's link unit sets the electric and magnetic coefficients so that the dressed photon cone is the edge cone.
- A magnetic term cannot act inside one cube, and a register hopped by W is fermionic, so the photon is not a mode of the update itself.
- Links attach only to currents the full update conserves exactly. If the content were chiral, which CL-1's is not, this would leave hypercharge and B - L. Fifth-force tests (MICROSCOPE: a B - L coupling below 1e-25) then require the update to hold a term that changes right-handed-neutrino number, whose form, (nu^c)^4, is forced as the only gauge-singlet four-fermion term that moves B - L. With flavour-generic Yukawa-type terms that term leaves exactly SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1)_Y, twelve generators, with hypercharges in the ratio 1 : -4 : 2 : -3 : 6 : 0 and the neutron exactly neutral, and in the Pati-Salam route it breaks SU(4) x SU(2) x SU(2) to the same group. So the Standard-Model gauge group follows from CL-3's rule once the content is chiral and those terms are present.

## What follows without a further law

- **Born reading.** CL-2 keeps the configuration distributed as its odds. For a mixing update, the squared magnitude is the only local, phase-blind reading that this preserves (`docs/RELAY_CONSERVATION_SELECTS_THE_SQUARED_MAGNITUDE_READING_OF_THE_ODDS_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-10-21.md`).
- **Read once.** The transport depends only on the current configuration, so each half-tick's records are the input of exactly one step.
  - A fact many readers share needs many records.
  - Readable trails cannot give which-way knowledge together with fringes, which matches complementarity (`docs/CANDIDATE_LAW_CL3_READ_ONCE_RECORDS_EVIDENCE_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-10-19.md`).
- **Proper time.** A clock's odds advance in phase at m/gamma, so moving clocks run slow.
- **Magnetic moment.** Without the copy mass, mu and eps enter every level the same way, so no Pauli term arises and g = 2 exactly at tree level. With the copy mass g = 2 still holds at linear order in the field; the lowest Landau level and the level pairing shift at second order, giving g - 2 of order (mu/M) eBa^2, about 1e-63 of the measured precision. Lattice corrections to g - 2 and to the hydrogen 1S-2S line are below 1e-44, far under the measured precision.
- **Photons.** A link quantum is registered only by the record of a site whose odds it changes. That gives photon-counting statistics, bunching and antibunching, complementarity and non-demolition counting.
- **Expansion law.** On cubic slices the stationary total phase gives exactly (8 pi G/3) rho l^2/4 = A(Hl/2), with A(u) = asin(u^2/(1-u^2))/sqrt(1-2u^2), which is the Friedmann law H^2 = (8 pi G/3) rho (1 - rho/rho* + ...) at low density, rho* = 3/(4 pi G l^2). It has no bounce, and growth never exceeds sqrt(2) edges per light crossing. Dust grows as t^(2/3) and radiation as t^(1/2).
- **The arrow.** The record set only grows, while the update is reversible.
- **Information.** Between records nothing is lost. Records never condition the odds, so the odds lose nothing at records either; a record carries on average exactly its outcome's entropy, which is what odds conditioned on it would lose (`docs/RECORD_INFORMATION_ACCOUNTING_LOSSLESS_ODDS_AND_READ_ONCE_RECORDS_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-10-21.md`).

## Candidate unification law (not adopted)

Two rules would replace CL-1's cube circuit and copy mass, with CL-2 and CL-3 unchanged:

> "Chiral update. The odds change by the flow of one generator: the commutator of the two tilings' edge reflections, read from the full proper metric of each site's two cubes, plus the mirror term, run for one proper tick per tick. The generator holds no mass term, and every coupling in it is a constant in proper units."
>
> "Mirror and vacuum. The mirror term is the Spin(10) pair square on the copy that the cube's three-fold turns rotate by e^(4 pi i/3), with the turn-invariant term that gaps the zone-corner partners across families. Masses are the vacuum values of fields charged under both SU(2)s, with scales that are constants in proper units."

A check of the light copy shows a decisive problem for this route: the light copy stays exactly massless at zero momentum, but gapping the mirror releases the light copy's zone-corner doublers, which the chiral generator gapped only through light-mirror mixing, so the light content becomes four left- and four right-handed tastes per family, vector-like, as Golterman and Shamir found for mirror-fermion schemes. A block-staggered light term gaps six of those doublers, leaving the partner at K = pi(1,1,1) still to be gapped. As it stands the route is not chiral, and the rung-ladder route of the chirality item is the surviving one. If that were repaired, the rules would give exact chirality at every step, exact energy conservation (removing the vacuum-stability bound), the link group SU(4) x SU(2) x SU(2) with no abelian factor, universal V - A weak decays with rho = 1, masses protected by SU(2), Z4 of mode number, and a family number divisible by 3. On one cell the mirror family's 32 modes under the Spin(10) pair square alone have a unique ground state that is a singlet of Spin(10), spin and the Z4, with a gap of 19.1 g and a fermion gap of 33.6 g for every nonzero coupling g, proved exactly by the multiplicity-free so(12) x SU(2) decomposition of the cell's Fock space; whether the gap survives inter-cell hopping is estimated but not computed. They supply the generator form, the mirror bit, the zone-corner term, the vacuum fields and their scales, and the family register; they cost the strict light cone and nearest-neighbour hops, and the shift part of the metric reading is not yet built. The spectral tilt, the size of the cosmological constant and dark matter are not addressed.

## Candidate four-dimensional update (not adopted)

If the odds advanced by U(t) = T^(it/a), with T the transfer matrix of a reflection-positive action local in all four directions and invariant under the hypercubic group of the sites and the tick, a quarter turn exchanging the tick with an edge would forbid every species-dependent speed in flat cells: one-loop speed shifts vanish exactly, while every tick without that turn leaves them at order alpha. Overlap fermions on such a lattice are exactly chiral in electric as well as magnetic backgrounds, and domain-wall fermions have a positive transfer matrix. The price is that the update is not a finite-depth circuit and its light cone becomes a Lieb-Robinson cone; one mode per site then forces at least two degenerate light tastes unless the site character is projective; domain-wall registers need 4 L_s modes per site; real-time gauging of a chiral charge is still open; and deformed cells still need the dressed-cone clause at order alpha Phi.

## Untested predictions

- **Photon decay with a cubic sky pattern.** CL-1's copy mass makes the light fermion band slower than the photon by a dimension-six term, so photons above roughly 80 to 110 PeV can decay to electron-positron pairs, except along the four body diagonals of the lattice. LHAASO photons reach about 1.4 PeV; ultra-high-energy photon searches can test this.
- **Dark energy is exactly constant.** Nothing in CL-1 to CL-3 moves w from -1 by more than about 1e-18 for the known content (1e-3 for the heaviest species allowed), and any log-periodic step in H(z) at the split epochs would be the only other signature. DESI DR2 prefers evolving dark energy at 2.8 to 4.2 sigma, so DESI's final data and Euclid can falsify CL-3 as written.
- **No gravity-mediated entanglement.** A definite geometry responding to the actual configuration cannot entangle two masses, so tabletop tests of gravitational entanglement would find none.
- **Dark matter has no non-gravitational signal,** since CL-3 links only exactly conserved currents and every linked slot is excluded.

- **Gravity's event-level remote dependence.** The source's Born mean carries no signal, but its event-level distribution depends on a distant measurement setting. For atom-scale systems this is more than 20 orders below any measured source.
- **Gravity follows where things actually are.** Under the local reading of CL-3's matter phase a single delocalised particle sources gravity from where it actually is, like a composite body, and only the source's Born mean follows the full odds; the reading is not yet defined for added charges, so whether any system gravitates through its full odds is open.
- **The lattice spacing is a few Planck lengths.**

## Where the rules are wrong or incomplete

- **Chirality.** A finite-range update gives no net chirality at any rung, and without the copy mass the tick has an exact mirror reflection, an odd rotation composed with inversion, that maps each family onto its mirror; with it the tick still keeps inversion, under which the one light copy is a vector-like Dirac fermion, so parity violation is not reproduced either way. Records, the transport and the responses do not help: every half-tick stays a block unitary of a fixed tiling whatever chooses it, so the net flow of every history is zero, and coherent chirality from records would need coherent fermionic registers, which are mirror fermions under another name. One exception is known: if every rung of the nested tilings is itself a lattice of cells with one mode per corner, running the same update at the same step and exchanging with the same corner of its eight children through W(1 - g5)/2, then exactly one handedness stays light on the sites and its mirror sits on no finite rung of an unbounded grid. It works only because coarse cells act across their own size in one tick, so there is no bounded light cone (the leak beyond the strict cone is about 1e-8 for a one-cell source, and the quadratic Lorentz-violation scale is 3e18 GeV). Its only exact charge sits on one-handed content and cannot carry a link. Carrying one generation's 16 in parallel on every rung, with the exchange W(1 + g5)/2, makes the light content on the sites exactly one left-handed family including the right-handed neutrino, with Standard-Model links attached covariantly; in background fields the light-sector currents of anomaly-free charges stay conserved while anomalous ones flow to coarser rungs, so the rule that links attach only to charges whose light current stays conserved selects exactly the anomaly-free charges. The neutral-pion decay to two photons comes out at 7.76 eV against 7.80 measured. With the orientation reversed on the weak singlets, mode number behaves like 3B + L and the largest such link group is SU(4) x SU(2) x SU(2). The handedness is one supplied bit and the 16 is supplied content. The route then fails measurement: the fine tick rate that hides the mirror also makes every coarse rung a charged sea moving at 2^j times the light speed, whose magnetic response grows by a factor 2 per rung, so the vacuum's magnetic stiffness would grow linearly with wavelength. That puts the photon's speed off by about 1e13 at 1 micrometre, against bounds near 3e-19, and is excluded by PVLAS, magnetar polarisation and the electron g - 2; only a fine-tuned counterterm on every rung at every order, or the zoom rate, which keeps the mirror light, would avoid it. Otherwise chirality needs interactions that give the mirror family a large mass. Every route tried so far fails, and the failures fix what a working route must avoid:
  - it must not freeze partners by a finite-range projector or cell-local mirror term, since the remaining free light update is then finite range and has zero net chirality in every on-site charge sector, and no light-band zero may be gapped only by mixing with a partner that is later frozen;
  - no charged mode may outrun its own edge cone, and any added link stiffness must be independent of wavelength above the cell;
  - no partner may sit at quasi-energy pi, since the vacuum then decays;
  - its content must be anomaly-free, so the protection must come from the update's structure rather than from anomalies;
  - its chirality must be exact in electric as well as magnetic backgrounds;
  - it must be interacting: in any free local update a one-body charge whose levels do not depend on momentum, which includes every quantised charge and every charge a link could attach to, on-site or not, splits the massless content into sectors that each obey Nielsen-Ninomiya, so left-handed charges come in pairs q and -q and one family's hypercharges are excluded.
  The hybrid, a rung ladder at the zoom rate with the mirror gapped on its top rung, passes the light-cone and magnetic constraints and keeps the light family exactly chiral, but freezing the far-end mirror creates seven doublers at the top rung's time-reversal-invariant corners, so it fails the first constraint by an exact index; only a finite-coupling entangled far-end phase, which has not been computed, could escape. The anomaly-allowed mirror term exists, but as half-ticks an exact chiral current exists only at step pi/2 with zero mass, where the band reaches quasi-energy pi and the vacuum decays. On 2^3 cubes the only generator found with an exact sublattice chirality and a cone is (i/2)[W_o, W_e]: used as a generator it keeps that chirality at every step and conserves energy exactly, but its hops reach next-nearest sites, the strict light cone becomes an approximate one, and it has light cones at both K = 0 and K = pi(1,1,1), four tastes, so the second pair must also be gapped. This candidate is not adopted. A family with a right-handed neutrino carries mixed anomalies between total mode number and the gauge charges, so no interaction that conserves total mode number can gap the mirror alone: total mode number must be broken, and Z4 is the largest part that can survive. The two copies are the two eigenspaces of the face-diagonal hop times the sublattice sign, conserved by every half-tick at zero momentum and broken at second order in momentum. The only four-mode term that acts on one copy alone takes one pair from each tetrahedron of that copy; it keeps Z4, breaks the same 12 rotations as the copy mass, gaps that copy as a whole and leaves the other free. It is a candidate, not adopted. Every such term conserves B - L, but one that moves B - L into the right-handed neutrino still leaves B = 28/79 per net event, because a Dirac right-handed neutrino (Majorana masses are forbidden by the Z4) never reaches equilibrium before the sphalerons freeze out. The charge rule of CL-3 needs chiral content. Interactions that gap mirror fermions without breaking the gauge symmetry are under study.
- **The two copies.** Resolved by CL-1's copy mass, at the cost of the 12 odd rotations. Without it the second copy conflicts with the measured electron g - 2 (a shift of 8.5e-8 at one loop, 6.5e5 times the precision) and the three light neutrinos. A fully covariant one-copy tick also exists on 4^3 blocks, with exact particle-antiparticle symmetry, but it supplies inversion symmetry, two heavy phases and a block-level projector; its massless point is not protected. At one loop the links shift its light level by about -1.8 alpha per tick (-0.33 alpha from the Coulomb part, fixed by Gauss's law), so the electron's mass must cancel the shift to about 1 part in 3e19. In a tick whose gauge charges are block occupations, any symmetry that protects one light copy and survives electric fields commutes with every block charge, and a second charged node then appears at quasi-energy 0 or pi; without particle-antiparticle symmetry the cone instead carries a CPT-odd term, which synchrotron and vacuum-Cherenkov bounds exclude. An overlap tick built from the 4^3 tick is exactly chiral only in magnetic backgrounds. So a light charged species is protected only by a gauge charge that forbids its mass, that is chiral content with gapped mirror partners: the additive-mass problem and the chirality problem are the same problem.
- **Gauge groups and generations.** No law selects SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1) or three generations. A conditional route to three exists: with the chiral generator (i/2)[W_o, W_e], if the mirror copy is gapped by its own four-mode term applied alike to every family, that term keeps the three-fold turn twisted so that the light copy carries charge, and the mixed anomaly of that turn with SU(2) is 4N mod 3 per window. Anomaly matching then lets the mirror and the zone-corner partners be gapped only if the family number N is a multiple of 3, so the smallest consistent number is exactly three; a solvable two-dimensional analogue confirms that 1, 2 and 4 families are obstructed and 3 and 6 are not. With the other available twist, or family-dependent mirror terms, any number works, so the count follows only from that choice of mirror term. A register that gives exactly three exists: read at the smallest zoom that maps the sites and both tilings to themselves (zoom 3), each coarse site carries its 3x3x3 block as an internal index, and the three-fold turn about the site's body diagonal fixes exactly three of the 27 positions, the site and the far corners of its two cubes. The other 24 form eight three-cycles that admit turn-invariant Dirac masses and so are vector-like and heavy, while the three fixed positions admit none and stay chiral; a factor-2 zoom fixes 2^n positions, never a multiple of three. This needs the register reading and the presence of every term the twisted turns allow, neither yet a law, and it predicts no mass hierarchy or mixing: those need a vacuum that depends on the block position. Read one rung up with each 2x2x2 cell's modes carried together, a cell holds one lepton and three colours, each as an up and a down state, with the multiplet pattern of one measured generation.
