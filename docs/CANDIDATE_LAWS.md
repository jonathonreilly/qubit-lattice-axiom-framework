# Candidate laws

Claim type: meta.

Candidate laws are the current working rules adopted to build on. They are not axioms. The [minimal axioms](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md), [policy section 6](audit/AXIOM_MINIMALITY_POLICY.md#6-explicit-owner-approval-for-axioms-and-primitives) and `docs/audit/data/axiom_premise_nodes.json` alone control premise admission, so this register has zero premise weight. A result that uses a candidate law is conditional on it.

The laws are judged against measured results, not against theoretical preference. Where a law restates an axiom clause, the note says so. The axioms are to be revisited once the laws account for the measured world.

## CL-1: update

> "Sites are the points of Z^3, tiled in two ways by 2x2x2 cubes offset by (1,1,1); each site's qubit is one fermion mode, and modes at different sites anticommute. The odds, one amplitude per joint occupation of the modes, change only by half-ticks that alternate between the tilings. In each half-tick every cube applies a unitary read from its own edges: the lapse N, the proper time per tick along the normal to its slice, and the inverse spatial metric gamma^ij, both fixed by its ten 4D edge lengths. Let W_i pass each site's odds to its edge neighbour along axis i with the phase of their spin-3/2 overlap along the body diagonals, each site keeping one overlap state shared by its two cubes. The cube's hop R is the sum of the W_i weighted by sqrt(gamma^ii) and scaled so that R^2 = 1, and its step eps is the angle whose sine is sqrt(3) N times the rms of sqrt(gamma^ii). A species of mass m applies exp(-i(eps - mu/2)R) on every even cube and exp(+i(eps + mu/2)R) on every odd cube, with mu = mN. Of the two copies, which a cube's three-fold turns rotate by e^(2 pi i/3) and e^(4 pi i/3), the first gets an extra mass M N per tick from the even cubes, half before and half after their half-tick. The shift and the shear are read one rung up: the even cubes, taken as sites, are tiled in two ways by 2x2x2 blocks offset by one cube; between the even and the odd half-tick every block of the first tiling rotates each pair of its cubes along axis i into each other by the angle theta_i with sin(2 theta_i) the shift per tick in cubes, plus a part proportional to the shear and to the odd half-tick's displacement along the other axes, and every block of the second tiling undoes the same rotation in the same site labels. Hop weights, angles and shear parts are those for which the cube's light cone is the null cone of its edges. A cube whose rms edge reaches twice the rung length splits into its eight children at the middle of its next even half-tick: it applies half of that half-tick; each corner mode passes, shared evenly, to that corner of the eight children, and the children's other modes start half filled as in the children's vacuum; the odds then take the unitary nearest to this passing that carries the cube's vacuum onto the children's; and the children apply the other half, each read from its own edges. An update's vacuum is its state with every negative-quasi-energy mode filled."

With equal edges R is the equal-weight hop W, and W is a unitary reflection exactly when every cube face carries phase -1. With one mode per site, a block unitary on sites and cube edges that hops and is covariant under the 24 rotations exists exactly when the site's rotation character is of spin-3/2 type, so the phase is forced rather than chosen; without the nearest-neighbour restriction it is not. The rotation action is then necessarily the double cover, but one mode cannot carry spin 3/2: the cube's eight corners form one spin-1/2 doublet times a two-dimensional copy factor, so the particles are spin 1/2.

Every species in a cube reads the same edges, so the shared step and the shared limiting speed follow from the geometry rather than being postulated, and the cube's light cone is the null cone of its edges. In a weak static field the reading makes all species fall alike up to (mu/eps)^2/4, below 2e-33 for every known particle against MICROSCOPE's 3e-15; clocks at rest redshift exactly; light bends by (1 + gamma) times the Newtonian value; and edges that grow redshift photons against atoms, with 1 + z the ratio of edge lengths. A single cube cannot carry a shift: det U(K) is fixed and reflections keep the spectrum symmetric, and on one cube the shift would come locked to helicity, which muon spin precession excludes. Read one rung up, moving whole cubes drags every species by exactly the shift, giving frame dragging at the general-relativity value (Gravity Probe B 39.1 mas/yr against a measured 37.2 +- 7.2), vanishing preferred-frame parameters, light bending of 2 on every ray, and both gravitational-wave polarisations acting on matter. Given by every cube in every half-tick, the copy mass would split the light helicities at order K^2, far outside the Crab bound, which is why only the even cubes give it.

On flat edges the bands obey cos w = A S - (cos 2b - cos mu)/2, with b = pi/2 - eps, A = (cos 2b + cos mu)/2 and S the cubic structure factor. The light band is an isotropic massive Dirac band of mass mu, with speed sin(eps)/sqrt(3) block per tick, and its partner cone at K = pi(1,1,1) is gapped. eps is a time step: eps -> 0 is the continuous-time limit. Because eps is shared, every massless species has the same limiting speed; a massive one differs at order mu^2, which at a Planck-scale tick is far below measurement. A species-dependent step would make speeds differ; the absence of vacuum photon decay at 1.4 PeV bounds a faster photon to 3e-19 and SN1987A bounds the neutrino difference to 2e-9. Without the last sentence the light content is two degenerate Dirac copies, and no term covariant under all 24 rotations splits them. The copy mass leaves exactly one light Dirac copy with no tuning. It keeps the 12 tetrahedral rotations, the translations, inversion and the combined CPT-type map, and it breaks the other 12 rotations, time reversal and particle-hole symmetry, but keeps both composed with a fourfold turn, so it carries no CP violation. At low energy it leaves the light band isotropic up to dimension-six terms. This narrows the rotation clause of the Admissibility axiom; the axioms are to be revisited once the laws cover the measured world.

## CL-2: actuality

> "There is one actual configuration, distributed as its odds. Each half-tick it moves by the transport that keeps it so, uses only transitions (staying included) along nonzero elements of the half-tick, changes the fewest mode occupations on average and, of those, has greatest entropy; each mode's occupation in it then gets a permanent record. Only records are readable."


This restates the Record axiom's "one per site" as one per site per half-tick; with one mode per site the bound follows. Massive modes revisit sites, and a record locking a site's occupation outright would put Planck-scale noise into the gravitational source, which measurement excludes.



Such a transport always exists: the half-tick maps the odds of any set of configurations into the odds of the set it connects to, and a supply-demand argument then gives the transport. Counting changed occupations rather than changed configurations keeps unentangled relays independent, and the greatest-entropy choice makes the transport unique and independent of labels. The mean drift of relays equals the group velocity exactly. Records never condition the odds, because the update of CL-1 is the only change they undergo.

The rule acts on the joint configuration of all modes. That is what makes two readers of one pointer agree and lets relays violate CHSH as measured. Number-changing transitions cover pair creation and annihilation.

## CL-3: response

> "Edges and links respond only to departures from the vacuum of the same edges, in the vacuum's own units. Edge lengths, and links valued in the update's exactly conserved symmetries, take the values at which the total phase is stationary. The total phase is the matter phase of each part's odds conditioned on the actual configuration of the rest, each interaction term counted once and shared equally, counted from the phase the vacuum odds have on the same edges with flat links; minus the sum over hinges of area times deficit, with rotation deficits around hinges that contain a tick direction and rapidity deficits around the others, plus a constant per 4-volume, both in fixed proper units; plus the links' plaquette phase. Each link's plaquette phase is a sum over sites: every site counts the three bonds of its odd cube's centre cell that meet at its even centre and the three faces of that cell that contain it, read in proper area from the lapse, shift and full spatial metric of its two cubes, with the electric plaquettes taken along the slice normal, and with electric and magnetic coefficients at which the vacuum's dressed coupling at one proper wavelength is one constant, the same for both and in every cell."

The matter phase depends on edge lengths only through CL-1's reading, and any dressing that keeps the half-ticks unitary acts as one phase per site, so a link's connection is a phase at each site and its holonomies sit on the bonds between neighbouring centres of each tiling's cubes.

**Gravity.**
- The second variation is the linearised Einstein operator, with G = a^2/(8 pi) per cell.
- The field gives attraction and gamma = 1, and CL-1's full reading of the metric, with the shift and shear one rung up, gives light bending of 2 on every ray in every gauge.
- Counting the matter phase from the vacuum on the same edges removes every vacuum term from the edge response, so the tensor and Newtonian couplings both equal the hinge-sum G, against 0.81 without it and the Hulse-Taylor 0.998(2), and gravitational waves are not dressed by the sea.
- Taking the vacuum's dressed coupling as the unit of every link keeps alpha's measured running and holds alpha and the hadron masses fixed in a potential (residual about 2e-34 against the Yb+ clock bound 1.4e-8); it also puts the dressed photon cone on the edge cone in every cell, with the remaining lattice Lorentz violation falling as (mL)^2.
- Gravitational waves carry positive energy and travel at the matter light speed.
- Every part couples with the same strength, including binding energy and light.
- For a system that is its own pointer, the source follows the actual outcome (Page and Geilker 1981).

**Links.**
- A conserved value current gives Maxwell, and a self-coupled multiplet gives Yang-Mills with the Jacobi identity.
- The site phase and the centre-bond holonomies are one photon at two levels, related by an exact gauge-covariant map. On the centre lattices the photon has the fermion band at every momentum, and the symmetric-stress clause fixes its speed equal to the fermion speed without a tuned coupling.
- A magnetic term cannot act inside one cube, and a register hopped by W is fermionic, so the photon is not a mode of the update itself.
- Links attach only to currents the full update conserves exactly. On chiral content this leaves hypercharge, fixes the charge pattern and gives baryon and lepton number no massless field.

## What follows without a further law

- **Born reading.** CL-2 keeps the configuration distributed as its odds. For a mixing update, the squared magnitude is the only local, phase-blind reading that this preserves (`docs/RELAY_CONSERVATION_SELECTS_THE_SQUARED_MAGNITUDE_READING_OF_THE_ODDS_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-10-21.md`).
- **Read once.** The transport depends only on the current configuration, so each half-tick's records are the input of exactly one step.
  - A fact many readers share needs many records.
  - Readable trails cannot give which-way knowledge together with fringes, which matches complementarity (`docs/CANDIDATE_LAW_CL3_READ_ONCE_RECORDS_EVIDENCE_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-10-19.md`).
- **Proper time.** A clock's odds advance in phase at m/gamma, so moving clocks run slow.
- **Magnetic moment.** In any coupling that keeps both half-tick hops reflections, mu and eps enter every level the same way, so no Pauli term arises and g = 2 at tree level. Lattice corrections to g - 2 and to the hydrogen 1S-2S line are below 1e-44, far under the measured precision.
- **Photons.** A link quantum is registered only by the record of a site whose odds it changes. That gives photon-counting statistics, bunching and antibunching, complementarity and non-demolition counting.
- **Expansion law.** On cubic slices the stationary total phase gives exactly (8 pi G/3) rho l^2/4 = A(Hl/2), with A(u) = asin(u^2/(1-u^2))/sqrt(1-2u^2), which is the Friedmann law H^2 = (8 pi G/3) rho (1 - rho/rho* + ...) at low density, rho* = 3/(4 pi G l^2). It has no bounce, and growth never exceeds sqrt(2) edges per light crossing. Dust grows as t^(2/3) and radiation as t^(1/2).
- **The arrow.** The record set only grows, while the update is reversible.
- **Information.** Between records nothing is lost. At a record the odds lose on average exactly the record's entropy (`docs/RECORD_INFORMATION_ACCOUNTING_LOSSLESS_ODDS_AND_READ_ONCE_RECORDS_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-10-21.md`).

## Candidate unification law (not adopted)

Two rules would replace CL-1's cube circuit and copy mass, with CL-2 and CL-3 unchanged:

> "Chiral update. The odds change by the flow of one generator: the commutator of the two tilings' edge reflections, read from the full proper metric of each site's two cubes, plus the mirror term, run for one proper tick per tick. The generator holds no mass term, and every coupling in it is a constant in proper units."
>
> "Mirror and vacuum. The mirror term is the Spin(10) pair square on the copy that the cube's three-fold turns rotate by e^(4 pi i/3), with the turn-invariant term that gaps the zone-corner partners across families. Masses are the vacuum values of fields charged under both SU(2)s, with scales that are constants in proper units."

Together with CL-2 and CL-3 these would give exact chirality at every step, exact energy conservation (removing the vacuum-stability bound), the link group SU(4) x SU(2) x SU(2) with no abelian factor, universal V - A weak decays with rho = 1, masses protected by SU(2), Z4 of mode number, and a family number divisible by 3. On one cell the mirror family's 32 modes under the Spin(10) pair square alone have a unique ground state that is a singlet of Spin(10), spin and the Z4, with a gap of 19.1 g and a fermion gap of 33.6 g for every nonzero coupling g, proved exactly by the multiplicity-free so(12) x SU(2) decomposition of the cell's Fock space; whether the gap survives inter-cell hopping is estimated but not computed. They supply the generator form, the mirror bit, the zone-corner term, the vacuum fields and their scales, and the family register; they cost the strict light cone and nearest-neighbour hops, and the shift part of the metric reading is not yet built. The spectral tilt, the size of the cosmological constant and dark matter are not addressed.

## Untested predictions

- **Gravity's event-level remote dependence.** The source's Born mean carries no signal, but its event-level distribution depends on a distant measurement setting. For atom-scale systems this is more than 20 orders below any measured source.
- **A single elementary system in superposition gravitates through its full odds.** Composite bodies follow their actual branch.
- **The lattice spacing is a few Planck lengths.**
- **Matter still relative to the lattice at nuclear density would exhaust its sites after about 1e8 years.** Moving matter does not.

## Where the rules are wrong or incomplete

- **Chirality.** A finite-range update gives no net chirality at any rung, and the tick has an exact mirror reflection, an odd rotation composed with inversion, that maps each family onto its mirror, so parity violation is not reproduced. Records, the transport and the responses do not help: every half-tick stays a block unitary of a fixed tiling whatever chooses it, so the net flow of every history is zero, and coherent chirality from records would need coherent fermionic registers, which are mirror fermions under another name. One exception is known: if every rung of the nested tilings is itself a lattice of cells with one mode per corner, running the same update at the same step and exchanging with the same corner of its eight children through W(1 - g5)/2, then exactly one handedness stays light on the sites and its mirror sits on no finite rung of an unbounded grid. It works only because coarse cells act across their own size in one tick, so there is no bounded light cone (the leak beyond the strict cone is about 1e-8 for a one-cell source, and the quadratic Lorentz-violation scale is 3e18 GeV). Its only exact charge sits on one-handed content and cannot carry a link. Carrying one generation's 16 in parallel on every rung, with the exchange W(1 + g5)/2, makes the light content on the sites exactly one left-handed family including the right-handed neutrino, with Standard-Model links attached covariantly; in background fields the light-sector currents of anomaly-free charges stay conserved while anomalous ones flow to coarser rungs, so the rule that links attach only to charges whose light current stays conserved selects exactly the anomaly-free charges. The neutral-pion decay to two photons comes out at 7.76 eV against 7.80 measured. With the orientation reversed on the weak singlets, mode number behaves like 3B + L and the largest such link group is SU(4) x SU(2) x SU(2). The handedness is one supplied bit, the 16 is supplied content, and in a field the compensating modes sit on the rung whose cells match the field's scale; this is a candidate, not adopted. Otherwise chirality needs interactions that give the mirror family a large mass. The anomaly-allowed mirror term exists, but as half-ticks an exact chiral current exists only at step pi/2 with zero mass, where the band reaches quasi-energy pi and the vacuum decays. On 2^3 cubes the only generator with an exact sublattice chirality and a cone is (i/2)[W_o, W_e]: used as a generator it keeps that chirality at every step and conserves energy exactly, but its hops reach next-nearest sites, the strict light cone becomes an approximate one, and it has light cones at both K = 0 and K = pi(1,1,1), four tastes, so the second pair must also be gapped. This candidate is not adopted. A family with a right-handed neutrino carries mixed anomalies between total mode number and the gauge charges, so no interaction that conserves total mode number can gap the mirror alone: total mode number must be broken, and Z4 is the largest part that can survive. The two copies are the two eigenspaces of the face-diagonal hop times the sublattice sign, conserved by every half-tick at zero momentum and broken at second order in momentum. The only four-mode term that acts on one copy alone takes one pair from each tetrahedron of that copy; it keeps Z4, breaks the same 12 rotations as the copy mass, gaps that copy as a whole and leaves the other free. It is a candidate, not adopted. Every such term conserves B - L, but one that moves B - L into the right-handed neutrino still leaves B = 28/79 per net event, because a Dirac right-handed neutrino (Majorana masses are forbidden by the Z4) never reaches equilibrium before the sphalerons freeze out. The charge rule of CL-3 needs chiral content. Interactions that gap mirror fermions without breaking the gauge symmetry are under study.
- **The two copies.** Resolved by CL-1's copy mass, at the cost of the 12 odd rotations. Without it the second copy conflicts with the measured electron g - 2 (a shift of 8.5e-8 at one loop, 6.5e5 times the precision) and the three light neutrinos. A fully covariant one-copy tick also exists on 4^3 blocks, with exact particle-antiparticle symmetry, but it supplies inversion symmetry, two heavy phases and a block-level projector; its massless point is not protected. At one loop the links shift its light level by about -1.8 alpha per tick (-0.33 alpha from the Coulomb part, fixed by Gauss's law), so the electron's mass must cancel the shift to about 1 part in 3e19. In a tick whose gauge charges are block occupations, any symmetry that protects one light copy and survives electric fields commutes with every block charge, and a second charged node then appears at quasi-energy 0 or pi; without particle-antiparticle symmetry the cone instead carries a CPT-odd term, which synchrotron and vacuum-Cherenkov bounds exclude. An overlap tick built from the 4^3 tick is exactly chiral only in magnetic backgrounds. So a light charged species is protected only by a gauge charge that forbids its mass, that is chiral content with gapped mirror partners: the additive-mass problem and the chirality problem are the same problem.
- **Gauge groups and generations.** No law selects SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1) or three generations. A conditional route to three exists: with the chiral generator (i/2)[W_o, W_e], if the mirror copy is gapped by its own four-mode term applied alike to every family, that term keeps the three-fold turn twisted so that the light copy carries charge, and the mixed anomaly of that turn with SU(2) is 4N mod 3 per window. Anomaly matching then lets the mirror and the zone-corner partners be gapped only if the family number N is a multiple of 3, so the smallest consistent number is exactly three; a solvable two-dimensional analogue confirms that 1, 2 and 4 families are obstructed and 3 and 6 are not. With the other available twist, or family-dependent mirror terms, any number works, so the count follows only from that choice of mirror term. A register that gives exactly three exists: read at the smallest zoom that maps the sites and both tilings to themselves (zoom 3), each coarse site carries its 3x3x3 block as an internal index, and the three-fold turn about the site's body diagonal fixes exactly three of the 27 positions, the site and the far corners of its two cubes. The other 24 form eight three-cycles that admit turn-invariant Dirac masses and so are vector-like and heavy, while the three fixed positions admit none and stay chiral; a factor-2 zoom fixes 2^n positions, never a multiple of three. This needs the register reading and the presence of every term the twisted turns allow, neither yet a law, and it predicts no mass hierarchy or mixing: those need a vacuum that depends on the block position. Read one rung up with each 2x2x2 cell's modes carried together, a cell holds one lepton and three colours, each as an up and a down state, with the multiplet pattern of one measured generation. The cell factors uniquely as one (4,2) of SU(4) x SU(2), and B - L and T3 are the only charges rotations leave alone. Rotations can be taken to leave the cell index fixed, so every cell state is a spin-1/2 Dirac fermion; but under that reading every internal generator is rotation-blind, so T3 and B - L are singled out only under the geometric rotation, and one reading does not yet give both. With the measured masses the tick keeps only colour-type U(3) x U(3) x U(1) x U(1), the second copy is a mirror family rather than a right-handed half, and no cell of 2^n sites per side holds three families.
- **Unobserved massless links.** On left-right symmetric content CL-3 allows massless links on the four species numbers of a cell (equivalently fermion number, T3, B - L and their product), which equivalence-principle tests exclude, so CL-3 needs a further selection rule.
- **Masses.** mu is given per species, and no law predicts a mass ratio or the step eps. Quasi-energy is conserved only mod 2pi, so any set of quanta whose quasi-energies add to 2pi can be made from the vacuum; keeping that below measured vacuum stability needs the band maximum below about 0.015 to 0.075, i.e. eps below about 0.008 to 0.04, for every tick.
- **Vacuum energy and dark matter.** Counting the matter phase from the vacuum on the same edges removes the sea's vacuum stress from the edge response entirely, so the Planck-scale vacuum energy does not gravitate. The cosmological constant is then CL-3's constant per proper 4-volume, with w = -1 exactly, but its size is supplied and no law predicts it. Holding each record's 4-volume fixed, an earlier working clause, is not needed for this: the lattice vacuum stress is radiation-like rather than proportional to 4-volume, and the clause also made every split kick the constant. No law gives a dark sector. The second copy, the cone partners at quasi-energy pi (charged, with inverted inertia) and records are each excluded as dark matter. The remaining slot is a species with no link, whose mass and abundance only the initial odds would fix, and no law gives the initial odds. Counting records as 4-volume fails: a half-filled sea's configuration is frozen under CL-2, so its record count does not fluctuate as Poisson, and an imported Poisson count matches the size of the constant but its measured equation of state excludes the resulting drift.
- **Initial odds and cosmology.** No law gives the initial odds. Any odds the tick leaves unchanged, including the empty state and the half-filled sea, freeze the configuration under CL-2. Number conservation and the strict light cone fix the large-scale spectrum by the start: starting correlations that fall off faster than a power give an index of 0 or at least 2, never the measured 0.965. Under CL-1's edge reading, edges that grow redshift photons against atoms, so expansion is observable on a fixed set of sites; splitting each cube into eight keeps the physical cell, and G per cell, fixed, and it must also halve the ticks to keep the step, since a split at fixed tick duration flips about 0.06 eps^2 of every light mode into the other band. Splitting breaks the strict-cone premise, changes the Lattice axiom and heats a half-filled sea. A fixed number of ticks per split gives an index of exactly 1, and the measured 0.965 needs the ticks per split to grow by about 1% per split, which no law supplies. The baryon excess needs a CP-odd relative phase between two four-mode terms of one class, which no law fixes, plus a hot start; order-one values overshoot the measured 6.1e-10 by 1e3 to 1e4. Measured CP violation in quark mixing is absent, since there is no charged current.
- **Sea response to the geometry.** The sea's induced link term has the measured sign and running, but at one loop it would slow light relative to matter by about 7e-4 if the cones were fixed at tree level, far outside the 1e-11 bound; the dressed form of the symmetric-stress clause is what removes this. Because the sea reads the same edges as matter, its induced gravity would have no Newtonian part, soften the flat lattice under shear and make the tensor and Newtonian couplings differ by about 20%, which binary-pulsar timing excludes; CL-3 therefore measures the matter phase from the vacuum phase on the same edges. That clause is adopted to meet measurement rather than derived, and on time-dependent edges it needs an adiabatic reference vacuum.
- **The weak interaction.** A left-handed SU(2) link needs three things the laws lack together: no per-species mass in the update, so that doublets stay degenerate; a mirror term that changes mode number in place of the bilinear copy mass; and an exact chirality grading at small step. The group CL-3 links is then exactly the symmetry of the mirror term, and the Spin(10) pair square on the mirror family gives SU(4) x SU(2) x SU(2) with no abelian factor, which also removes the unobserved massless links. Given those, the decay tests pass: the coupling is forced to be universal, decay rates go as the fifth power of the mass, the tau-to-muon ratio agrees to 0.8 sigma, the Michel parameters take their V - A values, and any electrically neutral pattern of dynamical masses gives rho = 1. A plaquette counted per mode over the cell's whole multiplet gives equal SU(4), SU(2)_L and SU(2)_R couplings and sin^2(theta_W) = 3/8 at the cell, but no law fixes the overall coupling. Only colour can bind a condensate that breaks the weak symmetry, and colour is measured to be weak at the weak scale; QCD-like content is excluded by the S parameter. So the weak scale needs an asymptotically free group beyond colour with new doublets, which no law supplies, and no 125 GeV scalar appears.
- **Weak decays.** CL-3 links a charge only if it commutes with the tick, and with the measured masses the charged current that turns a neutrino into an electron or an up quark into a down quark does not. So the muon, the tau, the charged pion and the neutron are exactly stable under the laws, against measured lifetimes of 2.2 us, 290 fs, 26 ns and 878 s. The measured decays follow one universal coupling with a rate proportional to the fifth power of the mass and V - A structure, so the gap is the same as the electroweak and chirality gaps: doublets that stay degenerate in the tick, with masses set by the vacuum.
- **Shift and shear, open parts.** Read through CL-3's site sum, the link action is the covariant Maxwell action: its light cone is the null cone of the edges at every order in the field, light bends by 2 on every ray, and the Shapiro delay and the photon's frame dragging take their general-relativity values. The plain one-to-one Yee pairing cannot read shear, which is why each site pairs the three links meeting at its even centre. The minimal shift reading drags orbital motion but not an elementary spin at rest, which needs a spin-connection term at the block rung; near Earth that effect is about 1e-29 eV.
- **Internal tensions and remaining gaps in the response.**
  - The vacuum must be the half-filled sea: the empty and full states have no vacuum polarisation, so alpha would not run and the muonic-hydrogen Uehling shift of 205 meV would vanish, and CL-2's records make the filling physical. A split carries the sea only if the children's new modes start half filled, which the exactly conserved charge also requires, and only by a map whose kernel falls as a power of distance; cut to a finite range r it leaves heating that energy-injection bounds allow only for r above about 1e14 to 1e15 cells, and a gradual handover lasting more than about 1e-21 s does the same with strictly local ticks. A split that heats the sea at the cell scale is excluded at every epoch. No split keeps every excitation's energy exactly, and a split must carry the coarse vacuum onto the fine one or the excitation it creates gravitates.
