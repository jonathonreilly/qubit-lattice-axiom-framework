# Candidate laws

Claim type: meta.

Candidate laws are the current working rules adopted to build on. They are not axioms. The [minimal axioms](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md), [policy section 6](audit/AXIOM_MINIMALITY_POLICY.md#6-explicit-owner-approval-for-axioms-and-primitives) and `docs/audit/data/axiom_premise_nodes.json` alone control premise admission, so this register has zero premise weight.

The laws are judged against measured results, not against theoretical preference. The axioms are to be revisited once the laws account for the measured world.

## CL-2: actuality

> "There is one actual configuration, distributed as its odds. Each half-tick it moves by the transport that keeps it so, uses only transitions (staying included) along nonzero elements of the half-tick, changes on average the fewest mode occupations, units of link electric value and units of edge-register value and, of those, has greatest entropy. The actual configuration lies in one refinement pattern; a transition between patterns counts changes through the passing, comparing inherited modes and edges with their passed values and not counting new modes. Each site that is free in the half-tick's odds then gets a permanent record of its occupation: a new site's first record comes at the half-tick after it appears, and a removed site keeps its records. A site is bound when some set containing it and that set's mirror across one of its bonds hold a definite total that the set alone does not; a bound site's occupation is part of the actual configuration but gets no record. Only records are readable, and a record is read only by the half-tick that takes it as input; a past record enters a later reading only through a pointer, a mode whose occupation the update, acting on it as a control alone, leaves unchanged."

Record exclusion read from the odds (neighbourhood-roles; working clause, adopted 2026-10-22 on owner direction). It replaces the fiat exemption of rishon and edge-register sites. The condition is covariant (the same functional of every site's neighbourhood odds), finds every rishon and no matter site in a U(1) quantum-link ground state with no labels, and catches several-site register ends by its set form. Bound sites cannot be recorded under the reading that a record's lock fixes no other site's possibility (locking one member of a definite-total mirror pair fixes its partner); that reading of the Record axiom is an OWNER RULING. Exclusion must be read from the odds, not from actual neighbours (actual-read exclusion biases recorded values, z 25). Roles themselves still come from the CL-1 layout: uniform odds (the half-filled sea) give every site the same role, and a uniform star-projector toy folds uniquely only in 1D (two pair coverings, gapped; misfolds are mobile spin-1/2 domain walls carrying matter; the period-3 layout holds ~0.9 of the low states at supplied wall density) while 2D/3D folds are massively degenerate (0.29 nats/site), so a fold-selection rule is open. Record rate: one record per free site per half-tick and record-on-change both keep Born (z 2.6) and the arrow; role-change-only and once-ever go stale (z 52).

This replaces the Record axiom's "a site never carries more than one record" by one record per site per half-tick, the most one mode per site allows: massive modes revisit sites, and a record locking an occupation outright would put Planck-scale noise into the gravitational source, which measurement excludes.

The transport always exists (each half-tick maps the odds of any configuration set into those of the set it connects to; supply-demand gives the transport). Counting changed occupations keeps unentangled relays independent; greatest entropy makes it unique and label-free; mean relay drift equals the group velocity exactly. Records never condition the odds, since the joint update (CL-1 with CL-3's total phase) is their only change. Link electric units are counted because otherwise greatest entropy flips actual electric values at order one per tick in a stationary register state (0.85 jumps per tick on a 24-link chain, against 0); counted, emitter waiting times are exponential at the Wigner-Weisskopf rate (fitted 1.01) with Born odds kept, as hydrogen 2p's 1.60 ns decay. Edge units likewise (0.977 Planck-rate changes per tick otherwise; counted, stationary edges freeze and moving ones move by exactly the forced amount). The pointer clause restricts what a reading is but no law defines a reader, so it is interpretive, adopted because bare two-time record laws depend on distant settings. Acting on the joint configuration makes two readers of one pointer agree and lets relays violate CHSH; number-changing transitions cover pair creation.
