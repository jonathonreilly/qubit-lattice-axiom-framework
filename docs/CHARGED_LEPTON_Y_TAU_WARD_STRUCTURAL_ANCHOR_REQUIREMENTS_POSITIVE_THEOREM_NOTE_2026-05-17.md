## 1. Theorem statement

**Theorem (`y_τ` Ward identity structural-anchor requirements, positive
form).** Let `(framework surface)` denote the retained substrate
catalogued in §2 of the parent no-go
[`CHARGED_LEPTON_Y_TAU_WARD_COMBINED_NO_GO_NOTE_2026-05-10.md`](CHARGED_LEPTON_Y_TAU_WARD_COMBINED_NO_GO_NOTE_2026-05-10.md).
Consider any candidate Ward identity closure of the form

```text
y_τ_bare  =  G × C                                                  (T)
```

where `G` is a retained gauge or transport coefficient and `C` is a
structural sqrt-rational constant produced by retained representation-
theoretic structure on `(framework surface)`. Then any instance of (T)
must instantiate at least one of the six structural anchor types
listed below:

- **(A1)** a retained color-Fierz sector identity on a non-color-singlet
  block (since `L_L = (2, 1)` is color-singlet on its own, this requires
  Fierz operating through a block that retains color content, e.g., a
  composite operator coupling to the `(2, 3)` quark block);
- **(A2)** a retained alternating-group `A_4` flavor-symmetry assignment
  of the lepton generation triplet (genuine `A_4` representation theory,
  distinct from the Wolfenstein `A^4 = 4/9` identity which is a
  gauge-coupling normalization);
- **(A3)** a retained Yukawa-vertex identity beyond standard gauge
  anomaly cancellation (an analog of the chiral axial anomaly,
  structurally constraining `Y_e` quartic contractions);
- **(A4)** a retained `y_τ / y_t = const` structural cross-sector
  identity derivable from `Cl(3)/Z^3` content alone;
- **(A5)** a retained Koide flagship `Q = 2/3` closure (at
  `retained_bounded` or stronger, not support tier) plus a downstream
  structural identity tying `y_τ` to the Koide direction;
- **(A6)** a retained `hw=1 → Yukawa` representation-theoretic identity
  on the three-generation `Cl(3)` structure.

The six classes are **pairwise structurally distinguishable** on
`(framework surface)` (no class is a relabeling of another) and the
partition is **exhaustive** on the current retained surface: every
single-cycle attemptable candidate (T) belongs to at least one `A_i`.

### Load-bearing step

For any closure `y_τ_bare = G × C` with `G` a retained gauge/transport
coefficient and `C` a structural sqrt-rational constant, decomposing
the construction by structurally-distinct anchor type yields a finite
partition into six classes `A1`-`A6`; the partition is exhaustive (no
seventh class on the current surface) and each class is a positive
structural requirement that any valid instance must meet.

## 3. The six anchor classes

Each class names a structural primitive of the construction (T). The
classes are catalogued as the six distinguishable anchor types on
`(framework surface)`. The discriminating attribute of each class is
named in `Discriminating attribute`; the `Source primitive` field
points at the structural primitive the class would consume; the
`Realization status` field records whether `(framework surface)`
currently carries a retained instance of that primitive (per the
parent no-go's §3 mechanism analysis and the live audit ledger as of
the date of this note).

### 3.1 (A1) Color-Fierz sector identity on a non-color-singlet block

- **Discriminating attribute.** Source primitive is a color-Fierz
  identity of the SU(N_c) family on a block carrying nontrivial color
  charge. `L_L = (2, 1)` is color-singlet on its own; instantiating
  this class therefore requires Fierz operating through a block that
  retains color content (e.g., a composite operator coupling to the
  `(2, 3)` quark block, or a lepton-block scalar-singlet composite
  uniqueness identity that consumes color-Fierz structure from an
  adjacent block).
- **Source primitive.** Non-abelian SU(N_c) Fierz identity producing
  a sqrt-rational factor `1/sqrt(2 N_c)`.
- **Realization status (parent no-go §3.1).** The lepton-block scalar
  composite uniqueness candidate (D17-prime on `(2, 1)`) is currently
  an unaudited proposal in the framework; the parent no-go records
  that no retained color-Fierz identity on a non-singlet block
  contracted into the lepton block is currently on the framework
  surface.

### 3.2 (A2) Alternating-group `A_4` flavor-symmetry assignment

- **Discriminating attribute.** Source primitive is a genuine
  alternating-group `A_4` representation of the lepton generation
  triplet (the `3` of `A_4`, with the usual cyclic + Klein-four
  structure). This is distinct from the EW-lattice arithmetic
  identity `sin²(θ_W)|_lattice = A^4 = 4/9` (with `A` the Wolfenstein
  CKM parameter and `A^4 = (Wolfenstein A)^4`), which is gauge-
  coupling normalization and carries no representation-theoretic
  `A_4` content.
- **Source primitive.** A retained `A_4` flavor-symmetry theorem
  acting on the lepton generation triplet, with audit-grade derivation
  from `(framework surface)` rather than as an imported family-
  symmetry input.
- **Realization status (parent no-go §3.3).** The retained
  `CKM_EW_LATTICE_A4_BRIDGE_RETAINED_IDENTITY_NOTE_2026-04-25.md`
  carries the Wolfenstein `A^4` arithmetic identity, not an
  alternating-group `A_4` flavor-symmetry theorem; the framework
  surface contains no retained alternating-group `A_4` content as of
  the date of this note.

### 3.3 (A3) Yukawa-vertex identity beyond standard gauge anomaly cancellation

- **Discriminating attribute.** Source primitive is an identity on
  the Yukawa-vertex quartic contractions that goes beyond the SM
  gauge anomaly cluster; conceptually an analog of the chiral axial
  anomaly that would structurally constrain `Y_e` quartic
  contractions (the chiral anomaly fixes `B-L` and similar trace
  combinations; an analogous "Yukawa anomaly" would fix a
  structural sqrt-rational coefficient in `Y_e`).
- **Source primitive.** A retained Yukawa-anomaly identity (or its
  structural equivalent) on `(framework surface)`.
- **Realization status (parent no-go §3.4).** Standard gauge anomaly
  cancellation forces the SM matter content; within that, the
  one-Higgs gauge selection theorem
  `CHARGED_LEPTON_DIRECT_WARD_FREE_YUKAWA_NO_GO_NOTE_2026-04-26.md`
  records that `Y_e` is permitted as the entire complex `3 × 3`
  matrix; no Yukawa-anomaly identity is retained on the framework
  surface as of the date of this note.

### 3.4 (A4) Cross-sector `y_τ / y_t = const` structural identity

- **Discriminating attribute.** Source primitive is a structural
  identity `y_τ / y_t = const` derivable from `Cl(3)/Z^3` content
  alone, allowing the retained `y_t = g_s/sqrt(6)` identity to
  propagate into `y_τ` via the cross-sector ratio.
- **Source primitive.** A retained cross-sector Yukawa-coefficient
  identity (not an integer-counting cross-sector identity).
- **Realization status (parent no-go §3.5).** The retained
  cross-sector closure
  `CKM_KOIDE_CROSS_SECTOR_Z3_CLOSURE_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-04-25.md`
  is `N_gen = N_color = 3`, an integer-counting equality. The parent
  no-go names three sub-routes for an `(A4)` instantiation: D17-prime
  on the `(2, 1)` block (currently an unaudited proposal); a combined
  SU(2) × U(1) × Z_3 non-Fierz Yukawa identity (no candidate
  construction); and a Koide-anchored cross-sector identity (requires
  the Koide flagship closure of class `(A5)` first). The framework
  surface contains no retained instance of `(A4)` as of the date of
  this note.

### 3.5 (A5) Retained Koide flagship `Q = 2/3` plus a `y_τ ↔ Koide direction` identity

- **Discriminating attribute.** Source primitive is a Koide flagship
  `Q = 2/3` closure at `retained_bounded` or stronger, plus a

- **Realization status (parent no-go §5, route M1; parent no-go §6

### 3.6 (A6) `hw=1 → Yukawa` representation-theoretic identity on three-generation `Cl(3)` structure

- **Discriminating attribute.** Source primitive is a retained

## 4. Why the partition is exhaustive

The partition `{A1, A2, A3, A4, A5, A6}` is exhaustive on
`(framework surface)` because the source primitive of any candidate
(T) must come from one of the following structurally-distinct anchor
points, and these are precisely the six classes:

| Anchor point | Class | Reason it is a distinct anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Non-abelian gauge / color Fierz on a non-singlet block | `(A1)` | Source primitive is an SU(N_c) Fierz identity producing `1/sqrt(2 N_c)`; load-bearing on color content of the contracted block. |
| Genuine alternating-group `A_4` flavor representation | `(A2)` | Source primitive is `A_4`-representation theory on the lepton generation triplet; structurally distinct from gauge-coupling arithmetic identities such as Wolfenstein `A^4`. |
| Yukawa-anomaly identity (analog of chiral axial anomaly) | `(A3)` | Source primitive is a trace identity on Yukawa quartic contractions beyond the SM gauge anomaly cluster. |
| Cross-sector Yukawa-coefficient identity `y_τ / y_t = const` from `Cl(3)/Z^3` | `(A4)` | Source primitive is a `Cl(3)/Z^3`-derivable structural Yukawa-coefficient identity (not the integer-counting `N_gen = N_color` identity). |
| Koide flagship `Q = 2/3` + structural `y_τ ↔ Koide direction` | `(A5)` | Source primitive is a retained Koide flagship closure plus a downstream `y_τ`-direction identity; structurally tied to the Brannen-direction parameterization rather than gauge / color / flavor primitives. |
| `hw=1 → Yukawa` rep-theoretic identity on three-generation `Cl(3)` | `(A6)` | Source primitive is a direct rep-theoretic identity on the three-generation hw=1 carrier connecting structurally to Yukawa coefficient values. |

Any single-cycle candidate (T) consumes a structural sqrt-rational
constant `C`; that constant must come from one of these six structural
anchor types, by enumeration of the source-primitive families on
`(framework surface)`. The exhaustion mirrors the parent no-go's §3-§4
mechanism analysis: the parent enumerates `SA-A, SA-B, M3, M4, M5, M6`
as the six single-cycle attemptable anchor classes; `A1`-`A6` are the
positive structural-requirement form of the same six classes.

No seventh single-cycle anchor class exists on `(framework surface)`:
any additional candidate either belongs to one of the six classes
above (and inherits its source-primitive requirement) or requires new
retained content not currently on the framework surface (in which case
it is no longer a single-cycle candidate on the current surface).
