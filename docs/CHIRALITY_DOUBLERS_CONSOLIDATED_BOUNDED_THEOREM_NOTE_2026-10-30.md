## Reading guide and glossary

Terms and labels used in the carried notes:

- "cube tick", "CL-1": a supplied Margolus-type walk on 2^3 cubes with one fermion mode per site. In #10052 and #10056 "CL-1" instead names a candidate history-level overlap weighting of matter. Both are source labels. In the current candidate-law register (`docs/CANDIDATE_LAWS.md`), CL-1, CL-2 and CL-3 are historical aliases of the lossless-influence, record-removal and pending conservation clauses, all with zero premise weight; none specifies a tick. Each carried note now defines its labels locally, and its statements are conditional on the rules as its sources state them.
- W3: the Floquet three-dimensional winding of a tick unitary U(k) on the three-torus. K = 0 and R = pi(1,1,1) are the zone centre and corner of the cell; X and M are the other corners.
- Polar-compressed map: the polar part of P U P for a label projector P. It equals the walk restricted to the label only when [U, P] = 0. FREEZE's light label is not walk-invariant (#10030 A3, #10042 B0), so its nodes and degrees are statements about the map.
- T: the 12 tetrahedral rotations of the cell; T3: the (111) three-fold turn; q: three-fold turn charge; W_F = (-2, 1, 1): FREEZE's turn-graded winding.
- CTWIST: the supplied composite twist walk, U(K) = f + i(h1 tau1 + h2 tau2 + tau3 g.sigma), f = (cos K1 + cos K2 + cos K3)/3, h1 - i h2 = (sqrt2/3)(cos K1 + wbar cos K2 + w cos K3), g = sin K / sqrt3. FREEZE: the supplied rule of #10041 (the sector where the cube tick's chirality disagrees with the walk's does not hop); also called "twist-local-single".
- R10, R11: source requirement labels (R10: the light copy carries a nonzero turn charge; R11: light and mirror sit at different exact momenta).
- S: the doubler map of FREEZE, S U(K) S^-1 = -U(K + R).
- GW: Ginsparg-Wilson; SMG: symmetric mass generation; GK: Grabowska-Kaplan; P_hat: Luscher's projector from sign(H).
- Tags [T], [F], [E], [S] mean exact, finite diagnostic, estimate and reading; in #10056 and #10060 [T]/[F] can also mark pass/fail of a pre-set criterion, as each note says.
