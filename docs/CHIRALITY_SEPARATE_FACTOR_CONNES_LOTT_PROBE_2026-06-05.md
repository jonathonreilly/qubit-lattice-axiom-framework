## 1. The hatch and the fresh construction

The four prior probes established that **no** Hermitian, off-block
(singlet↔doublet), `C_3`-orbit-splitting grading `Γ_χ` anticommutes with
the circulant generation operator

```text
   H = a I + b C + conj(b) C^2 ,        C = cyclic shift (C^3 = I)     (1.1)
```

at finite `a` on the **single** generation factor `C^3`; such a grading
exists only at `a = 0` (massless). Two **multi-factor** attempts already
failed:

- **PT-C / CPT-C3.** The spacetime grading `γ_CL = I_3 ⊗ σ_3` has the
  off-diagonal block `Y = I_3` (identity on generation), so its
  anticommutator reduces to the *trivial* generation anticommutator → zero
  generation residue (on-block collapse). (Cross-reference, non-load-bearing:
  `CPT_C3_CP_SQUARED_SCALAR_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-17`,
  `retained_bounded`.)
- **Native Cl(3) volume element.** `ω = i·I_3` is a *central scalar* on the
  generation factor → it commutes with everything and cannot grade.

The audit and the Record-axiom probe both explicitly left OPEN the
**separate-factor Connes-Lott** construction with an INDEPENDENT
`H_L ⊕ H_R` doubling. This note attacks exactly that hatch.

**The construction.** Take the finite spectral triple

```text
   Hilbert space   H = C^3_gen ⊗ C^2_LR ,   C^2_LR = H_L ⊕ H_R
   grading         γ = I_3 ⊗ σ_3 = diag(+I_3, −I_3)
   Dirac operator  D = [[0, Y], [Y^†, 0]]   (block in L/R)              (1.2)
```

with `Y` an **arbitrary** `3×3` generation operator (NOT forced to `I_3`).
By the Connes-Lott axiom, `{D, γ} = 0` holds **automatically on the full
`C^6`** for *any* `Y` (the off-diagonal Dirac anticommutes with the diagonal
grading; runner Part 1) — chirality is GENUINE on the doubled space. The
question is what happens after the **physical reduction** to the generation
factor.

## 2. Q1 — the L/R grading carries no generation content

`γ = I_3 ⊗ σ_3` acts as the **identity** on the generation factor. Its two
chiral projectors restrict to the generation factor as the identity
(runner Part 2):

```text
   P_L = (I + γ)/2 = diag(I_3, 0) ,   P_L |_gen = I_3 ,
   P_R = (I − γ)/2 = diag(0, I_3) ,   P_R |_gen = I_3 .                 (2.1)
```

So the generation-reduced image of `γ` is the **scalar `I_3`**. The
off-diagonal `Y` supplies generation **dynamics** — the fermion masses are
the singular values of `Y` — but `γ` supplies no generation **grading**.
The induced (scalar) generation operator `I_3` anticommutes with the
generation mass-squared `Y^†Y` only at zero mass (`{I_3, Y^†Y} = 2 Y^†Y`).

**Answer to Q1: NO.** The separate-factor grading does not induce a genuine
off-block `C_3`-orbit-splitting grading on the generation factor.

## 3. Q2 — the wrong-tensor-factor wall (airtight)

The discriminator vs PT-C is whether a *genuine* off-diagonal `Y` (acting on
generation, not `I_3`) leaves a non-trivial generation grading. The
**adversarial** version (runner Part 3b) settles it without any product-form
restriction.

Because `γ = diag(+I_3, −I_3)` is block-diagonal and scalar (`±1`) on each
L/R block, the **most general** Hermitian `O` on `C^6` with `{O, γ} = 0` is
forced to be purely **off-diagonal** in the L/R blocks:

```text
   {O, γ} = 0   ⟺   O = [[0, W], [W^†, 0]]   for some 3×3 W.           (3.1)
```

This is the **same structural form as the Dirac `D` itself**. Its
block-diagonal (within-sector) generation parts vanish identically:

```text
   O_LL = 0 ,    O_RR = 0 .                                            (3.2)
```

So `O` induces **no operator on the generation factor within a chiral
sector**; it only maps L-generation ↔ R-generation. Conversely, **any**
within-generation grading (a Hermitian involution on `C^3`, placed
block-diagonally in L/R) **commutes** with `γ` — it can never be the chiral
partner (runner Part 3b). The same conclusion holds in the alternative
`gen ⊗ chir` basis ordering: there `{O, γ} = 0` forces each chirality `2×2`
sub-block to anticommute with `σ_3`, killing its chir-diagonal (L→L, R→R)
entries — the within-sector generation operator again vanishes (runner /
note §6 cross-check).

**Answer to Q2: it COLLAPSES — structurally.** The L/R chirality grading and
a generation grading live in **distinct tensor factors**; the only operators
anticommuting with `γ` are L/R-off-diagonal (`D`-like) with zero
within-generation block, hence not generation gradings. This is the
**wrong-tensor-factor wall**, now shown to be structural and independent of
whether `Y = I_3` (PT-C) or a genuine generation operator.

## 4. Q3 — the doubling is imported; and even granted, the readout is
singular-value

**Provenance of the doubling (runner Part 5).** `A1 + A2 + A3` give the
`Z^3 mod L` qubit lattice and the emergent-time/unitary structure. The
generation factor `C^3` arises as the Hamming-weight-1 orbit
`{e_1, e_2, e_3}` of the `(Z_2)^3` corner cube `C^8 = (C^2)^{⊗3}` under the
cyclic `C_3`. The hw=1 slice is **3-dimensional** (`C(3,1) = 3`), with **no
leftover 2-fold**, and the native sublattice parity
`C = (−1)^{x+y+z} = (−1)^{hw}` is **constant** (`= −1`) on the entire hw=1
orbit — it does **not** split into L/R *within* generation. A separate
`H_L ⊕ H_R` doubling on the generation orbit is therefore an **extra 2-dim
space postulated** by the Connes-Lott construction, **not derived** from
`A1 + A2 + A3`. The doubling is **imported**.

**Even granting the import, the readout is the singular-value one
(runner Part 4).** With `Y` the circulant generation operator (genuine,
`≠ I_3`), the spectral-triple masses are the **singular values** `|L_k|` of
`Y`. At `r = |b|^2/a^2 = 1/2`:

- the **signed** (Brannen / `det_R`) Koide readout `Q = 2/3` holds for
  **every** phase `θ = arg(b)` (θ-independent; runner verifies
  `θ ∈ {0,60,120,180,240,300}°`);
- the **singular-value** (Yukawa) readout equals `2/3` **only** on the
  sign-homogeneous phases and **drops below `2/3`** off them — e.g. at
  `θ = 180°` (`b` real `< 0`) the spectrum has one negative eigenvalue and
  the singular Q is `54/49 − 24√2/49 ≈ 0.409`.

The grading `γ = I_3 ⊗ σ_3` supplies **no generation sign data**, so it
cannot convert the singular-value readout the triple delivers into the
signed `Q = 2/3`. (This is consistent with the established signed-vs-
singular-value readout distinction; non-load-bearing cross-reference below.)

**Answer to Q3: REQUIRES-IMPORTED-DOUBLING.** The `H_L ⊕ H_R` factor is an
import not in `A1 + A2 + A3`, and even granted it does not deliver the signed
`Q = 2/3` on the generation factor.

## 5. Verdict

**VERDICT: REQUIRES-IMPORTED-DOUBLING (and COLLAPSES-LIKE-PT-C on the
generation factor).**

The separate-factor Connes-Lott route with an independent `H_L ⊕ H_R`
doubling and a genuine off-diagonal `Y` acting on generation:

1. has genuine chirality `{D, γ} = 0` on the doubled `C^6` (for any `Y`);
2. but induces only the **scalar `I_3`** on the generation factor (Q1);
3. and **cannot** be the chiral partner of any within-generation grading —
   the only operators anticommuting with the separate-factor `γ` are
   L/R-off-diagonal (`D`-like) with **zero within-generation block** (Q2,
   the airtight wrong-tensor-factor wall);
4. and requires the doubling factor itself to be **imported** (not in
   `A1 + A2 + A3`), with the triple's mass readout being the **singular-value
   (Yukawa)** readout that diverges from the signed `Q = 2/3` off
   sign-homogeneity (Q3).

The route therefore **relocates** the chirality import to an imported L/R
doubling factor; because that factor sits on a **distinct tensor leg** from
the generation `R^3`, it cannot grade the generation factor. **No native
off-block `C_3`-orbit-splitting generation grading is produced.**
NATIVE-OFF-BLOCK-FROM-SEPARATE-FACTOR is **not** claimed.

## 8. Falsifiers

- A computational error in the off-diagonal-only characterization (3.1) or
  the vanishing within-generation blocks (3.2) (verified symbolically and by
  200 random numpy trials; runner Parts 3b and 6).
- A Hermitian involution `Γ` on `C^6` with `{Γ, D} = 0`, `Γ^2 = I`, whose
  **within-generation block is a non-scalar off-block (singlet↔doublet)
  `C_3`-orbit-splitting grading** anticommuting with a finite-mass generation
  operator and giving the **signed** `Q = 2/3` — this would falsify the
  collapse. The runner shows the only operators anticommuting with the
  separate-factor `γ` have zero within-generation block (3.2), so no such
  `Γ` exists in this product triple.
- A derivation of the `H_L ⊕ H_R` doubling from `A1 + A2 + A3` (the runner
  shows the native `(Z_2)^3` cube gives uniform parity on hw=1, no L/R
  2-fold; Part 5). Such a derivation would change the verdict from
  REQUIRES-IMPORTED-DOUBLING.
