## Claim scope

Let `Cl(3,0)` be the real Clifford algebra on three generators
`γ_1, γ_2, γ_3` satisfying `{γ_i, γ_j} = 2 δ_{ij} · 1`, and let
`ω := γ_1 γ_2 γ_3` be its central pseudoscalar. The retained sibling
narrow theorem
[`CL3_COMPLEXIFICATION_SPLIT_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md`](CL3_COMPLEXIFICATION_SPLIT_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md)
supplies the algebraic split
`Cl(3,0) ⊗_R C ≅ M_2(C) ⊕ M_2(C)` together with `ω² = -1` and
the central orthogonal idempotents `e_± := (1 ∓ i·ω)/2` (its (K1) and (K3)).
Then:

- **(S1) Schur scalar dichotomy.** For every faithful irreducible
  finite-dim complex representation `ρ : Cl(3,0) → End(V)`, the
  central pseudoscalar `ω` acts on `V` as a scalar
  `ρ(ω) = χ(ρ) · I_V` with `χ(ρ) ∈ {+i, -i}`. The map
  `χ : Irr_faithful(Cl(3,0)) → {+i, -i}` is well-defined and surjective.

- **(S2) Separator faithfulness.** Two faithful irreducible finite-dim
  complex `Cl(3,0)`-representations `ρ, ρ'` are unitarily equivalent
  **only if** `χ(ρ) = χ(ρ')`. Equivalently, the contrapositive
  *separator* property:

  ```text
  χ(ρ)  ≠  χ(ρ')   ⟹   ρ ≇ ρ'   as Cl(3,0)-modules.
  ```

  In particular, the positive-chirality Pauli realisation
  `ρ_+(γ_i) = σ_i` (with `χ(ρ_+) = +i`) and the negative-chirality
  realisation `ρ_-(γ_i) = -σ_i` (with `χ(ρ_-) = -i`) are
  **not** unitarily equivalent.

- **(S3) Idempotent factorisation.** Each faithful irreducible
  finite-dim complex `Cl(3,0)`-representation `ρ` factors through
  exactly one of the two simple summands `e_± · (Cl(3,0) ⊗_R C)`:
  `ρ` factors through `e_+ · (Cl(3,0) ⊗_R C)` iff `χ(ρ) = +i`, and
  through `e_- · (Cl(3,0) ⊗_R C)` iff `χ(ρ) = -i`. Concretely,
  `ρ(e_+) = I_V` and `ρ(e_-) = 0` when `χ(ρ) = +i`, while
  `ρ(e_+) = 0` and `ρ(e_-) = I_V` when `χ(ρ) = -i`.

(S1)-(S3) are standard finite-dim complex representation-theory
statements about the abstract real algebra `Cl(3,0)` and its

## Proof

The retained sibling K3 split gives
`Cl(3,0) ⊗_R C ≅ M_2(C) ⊕ M_2(C)` via the central orthogonal
idempotents `e_± = (1 ∓ i·ω)/2`. The retained K1 gives `ω² = -1` and
`[ω, γ_i] = 0`. We use these as cited inputs.

### (S1) Schur scalar dichotomy

Let `ρ : Cl(3,0) → End(V)` be a faithful irreducible finite-dim complex
representation. Extend `C`-linearly to
`ρ_C : Cl(3,0) ⊗_R C → End(V)`. By (K1), `ω` is central in `Cl(3,0)`
and therefore also central in `Cl(3,0) ⊗_R C`. Hence `ρ_C(ω) ∈ End(V)`
commutes with the entire image `ρ_C(Cl(3,0) ⊗_R C)`. By Schur's lemma
on the finite-dim complex irrep `V`, `ρ_C(ω) = c · I_V` for some
`c ∈ C`. From (K1), `ω² = -1`, so

```text
c²  = (ρ_C(ω))²  =  ρ_C(ω²)  =  ρ_C(-1)  =  -I_V,                       (S1.eq)
```

giving `c² = -1` in `C`, hence `c ∈ {+i, -i}`. Define
`χ(ρ) := c`. The map `χ : Irr_faithful(Cl(3,0)) → {+i, -i}` is
well-defined.

Surjectivity: the positive-chirality Pauli realisation
`ρ_+(γ_i) = σ_i` (which is faithful and irreducible on `C²`, as
established by the K3 sibling's exhibition of `M_2(C)`-action) has
`ρ_+(ω) = σ_1 σ_2 σ_3 = i · I_2`, so `χ(ρ_+) = +i`. The
parity-conjugate `ρ_-(γ_i) = -σ_i` has
`ρ_-(ω) = (-σ_1)(-σ_2)(-σ_3) = -σ_1σ_2σ_3 = -i · I_2`, so
`χ(ρ_-) = -i`. Both values are attained.

### (S2) Separator faithfulness

Suppose `ρ, ρ'` are faithful irreducible finite-dim complex
`Cl(3,0)`-representations and `U : V → V'` is a `Cl(3,0)`-module
isomorphism (in particular, unitary in the Hermitian-irrep setting).
Then for every `a ∈ Cl(3,0)`, `U · ρ(a) = ρ'(a) · U`. Applying this
to `a = ω`:

```text
U · ρ(ω)   =  ρ'(ω) · U,
U · χ(ρ) · I_V  =  χ(ρ') · I_{V'} · U,
χ(ρ) · U   =  χ(ρ') · U.
```

Since `U` is non-zero (it is an isomorphism), `χ(ρ) = χ(ρ')` in `C`.
Contrapositive: `χ(ρ) ≠ χ(ρ')` implies `ρ` and `ρ'` are not
isomorphic as `Cl(3,0)`-modules, so not unitarily equivalent.

In particular, `χ(ρ_+) = +i` and `χ(ρ_-) = -i` differ in `C`, so the
positive- and negative-chirality irreps are not unitarily equivalent.

### (S3) Idempotent factorisation

From (K3), the central orthogonal idempotents are
`e_± = (1 ∓ i·ω)/2` with `e_+ + e_- = 1`, `e_+ e_- = 0`, `e_±² = e_±`.
Apply `ρ_C` to these:

```text
ρ_C(e_+)  =  ρ_C((1 - i·ω)/2)  =  (I_V - i · ρ_C(ω))/2
          =  (I_V - i · χ(ρ) · I_V)/2
          =  (1 - i · χ(ρ))/2  ·  I_V.                                 (S3a)
ρ_C(e_-)  =  ρ_C((1 + i·ω)/2)  =  (I_V + i · ρ_C(ω))/2
          =  (1 + i · χ(ρ))/2  ·  I_V.                                 (S3b)
```

Case `χ(ρ) = +i`: the scalar coefficients become

```text
(1 - i · (+i))/2  =  (1 - i²)/2  =  (1 - (-1))/2  =  1,
(1 + i · (+i))/2  =  (1 + i²)/2  =  (1 + (-1))/2  =  0.
```

So `ρ_C(e_+) = I_V` and `ρ_C(e_-) = 0`, meaning `ρ_C` factors through
the `e_+` simple summand `e_+ · (Cl(3,0) ⊗_R C)`.

Case `χ(ρ) = -i`: the scalar coefficients become

```text
(1 - i · (-i))/2  =  (1 - (-i²))/2  =  (1 - 1)/2  =  0,
(1 + i · (-i))/2  =  (1 + (-i²))/2  =  (1 + 1)/2  =  1.
```

So `ρ_C(e_+) = 0` and `ρ_C(e_-) = I_V`, meaning `ρ_C` factors through
the `e_-` simple summand `e_- · (Cl(3,0) ⊗_R C)`.

The two cases are mutually exclusive (since `e_+ e_- = 0` and a
faithful irrep cannot have both `ρ_C(e_+) = 0` and `ρ_C(e_-) = 0`, as
those would force `ρ_C(1) = ρ_C(e_+ + e_-) = 0`, contradicting
`ρ_C(1) = I_V`). Hence each faithful irreducible complex `Cl(3,0)`-
representation factors through **exactly one** of the two simple
summands, with the chirality label `χ` indicating which. ∎

## Derivable corollaries

- **(C1) Chirality bijection.** The Schur-scalar map `χ` provides a
  bijection `χ : {ρ_+, ρ_-} → {+i, -i}` on the two non-isomorphic
  isomorphism classes of faithful irreducible finite-dim complex
  `Cl(3,0)`-representations.
- **(C2) Tensor-product chirality additivity.** For finite-dim complex
  reps `ρ_+^{n_+} ⊕ ρ_-^{n_-}`, `ω` acts as block-diagonal
  `diag(+i I_{2n_+}, -i I_{2n_-})`.
- **(C3) Convention independence of dim count.** `dim_C V = 2` holds
  in both chirality summands; the package convention `ρ_+` is a
  labeling choice, not a dimensional one.

All follow algebraically from (S1)-(S3).
