## Statement

Let `V = R³` carry the cyclic shift `R` (the generation C₃ generator,
`R³ = I`), let `J = I + R + R²` be the all-ones matrix, and let
`Γ_χ = (2/3)J − I` (eigenvalue `+1` on the singlet `(1,1,1)`, `−1` on the
orthogonal doublet plane). By the retained L4 theorem, every Hermitian `H` on
`V` with `{H, Γ_χ} = 0` and nonzero-eigenvalue eigenvector `v` gives Koide
`Q(v) = 2/3`, and such `H` are parametrized as `H = (1/3)(1 h^T + h 1^T)`,
`Σh = 0`.

**Conclusion (N1) — the singlet axis is forced to `(1,1,1)` and is native.**
The set of symmetric `H` with `{H, Γ_χ} = 0` is exactly 2-real-dimensional, and
every such `H` maps the singlet direction `(1,1,1)` entirely into the doublet
plane and annihilates nothing else of the singlet. Hence the "axis" `u` in
`H = (1/2)(u h^T + h u^T)` is **forced** to be `u = (1,1,1)` (the singlet); a
non-singlet axis (e.g. a coordinate axis `e_1`) does not anticommute with
`Γ_χ`. The vector `(1,1,1)` is the geometric **body-diagonal of the Z³ unit
cube** (the line `|000>→|111>`) restricted to the hw=1 generation triplet, and
it is the unique non-degenerate eigenvector of every native C₃-symmetric
operator on `V` (`J`, `J − I`, the native double-shift hop). So the axis is a
Lattice+Quantum-baseline forced object. *(runner Sections 2, 3)*

**Conclusion (N2) — the doublet `h` is NOT forced by any C₃-symmetric native
structure.** The doublet plane (orthogonal complement of `(1,1,1)`) carries the
2-dimensional irrep of C₃, on which C₃ acts irreducibly. Therefore:

```text
(i)  Every real C₃-equivariant native operator on V is circulant (the full
     real commutant is ⟨I, R, R²⟩). Inside the real self-adjoint/Hermitian
     source or mass-operator class, its restriction to the doublet plane is a
     SCALAR, so it supplies no preferred real line h.
(ii) The native non-scalar commutant element J_cs=(C-C²)/√3 is the
     real-antisymmetric skew/complex structure on the doublet. It commutes
     with Γ_χ, is block-diagonal against singlet ⊕ doublet, selects no real
     doublet line h, and lives on the separate block-count route rather than
     in the real self-adjoint anticommuting source class.
(iii) The Reynolds average over C₃ of ANY symmetric seed has a scalar doublet
     block. So the C₃-symmetric self-adjoint content of the lattice cannot
     prefer any direction h in the doublet plane.
```

A specific `h` direction would break the doublet plane's isotropy, i.e. break
C₃ to a proper subgroup. By the retained
`generation_degeneracy_minimal_symmetry_breaking` theorem, this is precisely the
minimal `S₃ → proper-subgroup` breaking the gate requires, and the theorem
explicitly does **not** derive that breaking. *(runner Sections 3a, 6)*

**Conclusion (N3) — the cube structures that DO pick an `h` each select one
element of a non-trivial C₃/S₃ orbit (the unsourced import).** Three native
candidate C₃-breaking structures were each tested:

```text
(a) A single OFF-body-diagonal of the cube. The cube has 4 body-diagonals; the
    (+,+,+) one is the singlet, and the other three sign-patterns (+,+,-),
    (+,-,+), (-,+,+) each project to a NONZERO doublet vector h, and
    H = (1/2)((1,1,1) h^T + h (1,1,1)^T) DOES anticommute with Γ_χ.  BUT the

(b) A single staggered / Jordan-Wigner AXIS ORDERING (a permutation of x,y,z).
```

In every case, the body-diagonal geometry supplies the **axis** but not the

## Proof

The proof is elementary linear algebra over `R³`, verified symbolically /
numerically in the runner.

**(N1).** In the C₃ Fourier basis, `Γ_χ = diag(+1, −1, −1)`. A symmetric `H`
with `{H, Γ_χ} = 0` must, block-wise against the `(+1) ⊕ (−1,−1)` split, have
zero singlet-singlet block and zero doublet-doublet block (the diagonal blocks
sit in the `+2`/`−2` eigenspaces of `ad_{Γ_χ}` and must vanish under
anticommutation), leaving only the off-diagonal singlet↔doublet block free. That
off-diagonal block is a single real 2-vector `h` (Σh=0 in the standard basis),
giving `dim = 2` and `H = (1/3)(1 h^T + h 1^T)`. Hence the only axis compatible
with `{H, Γ_χ}=0` is the singlet `(1,1,1)`. The runner confirms `dim = 2`,
confirms every nullspace element sends `(1,1,1)` into the doublet, and confirms a
non-singlet axis fails (`||{H,Γ_χ}|| = 1.633 ≠ 0`).

**(N2).** By Schur, the commutant of the irreducible C₃ action on the doublet is
`C`: over real matrices this is the two-real-dimensional algebra generated by
the identity and the native complex structure `J_cs`. The real self-adjoint
part of that commutant is only the scalar part, so any C₃-equivariant symmetric
operator restricts to a real scalar on the doublet — an isotropic 2-plane with
no preferred direction. The runner verifies the doublet block of `J`, `J−I`,
`I`, and the C₃-Reynolds average of a random symmetric seed are all scalar, and
then verifies separately that `J_cs` is the non-scalar real-antisymmetric
skew/complex-structure commuting route: it commutes with `Γ_χ`, is
block-diagonal, has no real doublet eigenline that could supply `h`, and is
not an anticommuting source operator.

**(N3).** (a) The three off-diagonal sign-patterns are `R·(+,+,−) = (−,+,+)` and
`R²·(+,+,−) = (+,−,+)`, a single transitive C₃ orbit (verified). (b) Direct
construction of the KS-staggered double-shift `Σ_{(a,b)} η_a η_b S_a S_b`
projected to the triplet yields, for the ordering `(x,y,z)`, the matrix
`[[0,1,−1],[−1,0,1],[1,−1,0]] = R² − R = R^T − R`, a circulant; its Hermitian
part is **zero** and its full form commutes with `R` and with `Γ_χ` (verified
for all 6 orderings). (c) `diag(1,1,λ)` has commutator `[·,Γ_χ] ≠ 0` but
anticommutator `{·,Γ_χ} ≠ 0` (norm `4.71`), and being real-diagonal it cannot
realize the off-diagonal class. ∎
