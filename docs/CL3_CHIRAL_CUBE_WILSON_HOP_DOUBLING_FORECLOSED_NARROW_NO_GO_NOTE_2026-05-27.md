```text

                   = 2 I - (g a)^2 (A_mu^a T^a) (A_mu^b T^b) + O(g^4).
                                                                   (1)
```

The order-`O(g)` terms cancel identically because the same matrix

where `C_F = (N_c^2 - 1)/(2 N_c) = 4/3` for `N_c = 3`. The result on

## Proof

**(N1).** The Taylor expansion of an exponential of a Hermitian matrix
`X = g a A_mu^a T^a` is

```text
exp(i X)         =  I + i X - (1/2) X^2 - (i/6) X^3 + (1/24) X^4 + ...
exp(-i X)        =  I - i X - (1/2) X^2 + (i/6) X^3 + (1/24) X^4 + ...
exp(i X) + exp(-i X)
                 =  2 I - X^2 + (1/12) X^4 + ...                  (4)
                 =  2 cos(X)                                       (5)
```

For `X = g a A_mu^a T^a` Hermitian (since A_mu^a are real and T^a are
Hermitian),

```text
X^2 = (g a)^2 (A_mu^a T^a)(A_mu^b T^b)
    = (g a)^2 A_mu^a A_mu^b T^a T^b                                (6)
```

is a single bilinear in the gauge field `A_mu^a` with one bilinear
factor `A_mu^a A_mu^b`, NOT two. Substituting (6) into (4):

```text
U_mu + U_mu^dagger
   =  2 I - (g a)^2 A_mu^a A_mu^b T^a T^b + O(g^4).                (7)
```

The O(g^4) terms (4/12 of X^4 and higher) are subleading and do not
contain bilinear `A A` structures relevant to the one-loop expansion.
Gauge-averaging at coincident points

```text
< A_mu^a A_mu^b >  =  delta^{ab}                                   (8)
```

is the standard textbook free-gauge propagator (cited
`DM_ETA_G1_COLEMAN_WEINBERG_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-06.md`
Section 7 honest residual: "standard one-loop CW gauge-averaging step"
as inherited bounded). Applying (8) to (7) yields

```text
<U_mu + U_mu^dagger>_(O(g^2))
   =  2 I - (g a)^2 delta^{ab} T^a T^b + O(g^4)
   =  2 I - (g a)^2 sum_a (T^a)^2 + O(g^4)
   =  2 I - (g a)^2 C_F I + O(g^4)                                  (9)
```

where the last equality uses the standard Casimir identity
`SU3_ADJOINT_CASIMIR_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-02.md`

```text
sum_a T^a T^a  =  C_F I,    C_F = (N_c^2 - 1)/(2 N_c) = 4/3.       (10)
```

The coefficient of the `O(g^2)` correction to the color identity is
**one** Casimir factor `C_F = 4/3`, not `2 C_F = 8/3`. The runner's
Section A verifies (10) by direct construction of the Gell-Mann
generators in the standard `Tr[T^a T^b] = (1/2) delta^{ab}`
normalization, and Section B verifies (1)-(3) by an explicit
symbolic Taylor expansion of `U_mu + U_mu^dagger` with sympy.

**(N2).** Suppose instead a derivation produces `2 C_F` from the
single-link expansion. Then either:

- (D1) The bilinear `A^a A^b` in (6) is replaced by two independent
  bilinears `A^{forward, a} A^{forward, b} + A^{backward, a}
  A^{backward, b}`, each contracting separately to `delta^{ab}` and
  contributing `C_F`. This requires `U_mu = exp(i g a A^{forward}_mu^a
  T^a)` and `U_mu^dagger = exp(-i g a A^{backward}_mu^a T^a)` to use
  two distinct gauge fields, but the Hermitian-conjugate relationship
  forces `A^{forward}_mu^a = A^{backward}_mu^a` (the conjugate of an

  `U_mu^dagger = (U_mu)^conjugate transpose` as a matrix identity for

demonstrating that the doubling argument's "independent SU(3) matrices
