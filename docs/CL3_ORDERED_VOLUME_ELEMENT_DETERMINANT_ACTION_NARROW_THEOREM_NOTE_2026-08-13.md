## Clifford identities

Reversing three symbols uses three adjacent transpositions:

```text
e3 e2 e1 = -e2 e3 e1 = +e2 e1 e3 = -e1 e2 e3.
```

Thus `omega_rev=-omega`. Both products are grade three. For any generator
`ek`, moving `ek` through the other two distinct generators contributes two
minus signs, hence

```text
ek omega = omega ek.
```

The generators span the algebra multiplicatively, so `omega` and
`omega_rev` are central. Finally,

```text
omega^2
 = e1 e2 e3 e1 e2 e3
 = (-1)^3 e1^2 e2^2 e3^2
 = -I.
```

Scalar multiplication by `-1` gives `omega_rev^2=(-omega)^2=-I`.

## Signed-permutation determinant action

For a permutation `p` and signs `a1,a2,a3 in {+1,-1}`, define the signed
permutation action by

```text
R(ej) = sum_k R_kj ek.
```

There are `3! * 2^3 = 48` such matrices. Their determinant is

```text
det(R) = sign(p) a1 a2 a3.
```

Because each image is a signed, distinctly permuted generator, their product
is the permutation sign times the product of the three coordinate signs:

```text
R(e1) R(e2) R(e3) = det(R) e1 e2 e3.
```

Half of the sign choices have determinant `+1` for each permutation and half
have determinant `-1`, giving the exact `24+24` split. The runner enumerates
all 48 matrices, verifies uniqueness and closure, and checks the displayed
identity element by element.
