## 1. Theorem (bounded, Gell-Mann embedding as fiber-SU(2) commutant)

**Setup.** Let `C^8 = (C^2)^⊗3` be the chiral cube with computational
basis `{|b_1 b_2 b_3⟩ : (b_1, b_2, b_3) ∈ {0,1}^3}`, indexed by
`n = 4 b_1 + 2 b_2 + b_3`. Choose the fiber direction `b_3`; this
selects the tensor decomposition

```text
C^8  =  C^4_base ⊗ C^2_fiber,            (Carrier)
```

with `C^4_base = span{|b_1 b_2⟩ : (b_1, b_2) ∈ {0,1}^2}` (basis
`{|00⟩, |01⟩, |10⟩, |11⟩}`) and `C^2_fiber = span{|b_3⟩ : b_3 ∈ {0,1}}`.

Let `s_1, s_2, s_3` be the standard Pauli matrices, `I_2` the `2 × 2`
identity, and `I_4 = I_2 ⊗ I_2` the `4 × 4` identity on the base.
Define the fiber `SU(2)` generators

```text
Jf_i  :=  I_4 ⊗ (s_i / 2),       i ∈ {1, 2, 3}.        (Fiber-SU(2))
```

Let `U_base` be the unitary `4 × 4` change-of-basis matrix that maps
`{|00⟩, |01⟩, |10⟩, |11⟩}` to the symmetry-sorted basis
`{|sym_0⟩, |sym_1⟩, |sym_2⟩, |antisym⟩}` with

```text
|sym_0⟩    =  |00⟩,
|sym_1⟩    =  |11⟩,
|sym_2⟩    =  (|01⟩ + |10⟩) / √2,
|antisym⟩  =  (|01⟩ − |10⟩) / √2.
```

Let `λ^1, …, λ^8` be the standard Gell-Mann matrices on `C^3` and set
`λ^a/2` as the canonical Gell-Mann generators with `Tr[λ^a λ^b/4] = (1/2) δ^{ab}`.
Define the `4 × 4` symmetric-base-embedding map

```text
M_{3,sym}(X)  :=  U_base^† · diag_{4×4}(X, 0_{1×1}) · U_base,
                                                     X ∈ M_3(C),     (M-sym)
```

i.e. embed `X ∈ M_3(C)` on the 3D symmetric block of `C^4_base` and
fill the 1D antisymmetric block with zero, then rotate back to the
ordered base. Define the 8D embedded Gell-Mann generators

```text
T^a_{8D}  :=  M_{3,sym}(λ^a / 2) ⊗ I_2,    a ∈ {1, …, 8}.    (T-8D)
```

**Hypotheses.**

(C1) The chiral cube `C^8 = (C^2)^⊗3` is the carrier inherited from
[`CL3_TASTE_GENERATION_THEOREM.md`](CL3_TASTE_GENERATION_THEOREM.md).
Its ledger row is `unaudited` as of 2026-05-27.

(C2) The `(base × fiber)` decomposition (Carrier) is the same

[`CL3_COLOR_AUTOMORPHISM_THEOREM.md`](CL3_COLOR_AUTOMORPHISM_THEOREM.md)
(ledger row `retained`). The selection of `b_3` as the fiber index is
a notational convention; the algebraic content of the embedding theorem
below is identical under any of the three `b_k`-fiber choices.

(C3) The Gell-Mann matrices `{λ^a}_{a=1}^8` are the standard
`su(3)` matrices of [Gell-Mann 1962]. With canonical Hermitian
generators `t^a = λ^a/2`, the structure constants are computed from
`f^{abc} = (2/i) · Tr([t^a, t^b] · t^c)`. Equivalently, in the
unhalved `λ` convention,
`f^{abc} = (1/(4i)) · Tr([λ^a, λ^b] · λ^c)`. The normalization is
`Tr[t^a t^b] = Tr[λ^a λ^b/4] = (1/2) δ^{ab}`.

**Conclusion (T1) (Embedding into the 4D base).** For each
`a ∈ {1, …, 8}`, the operator `T^a_{8D}` defined by (T-8D) is a
Hermitian `8 × 8` matrix with the tensor-product factorization
`T^a_{8D} = M^a_4 ⊗ I_2`, where
`M^a_4 = M_{3,sym}(λ^a/2) ∈ M_4(C)` annihilates the 1D antisymmetric
base subspace and equals `λ^a/2` on the 3D symmetric base subspace.
In particular `Tr[T^a_{8D}] = 0` (Gell-Mann generators are
traceless).

**Conclusion (T2) (Normalization, T_F = 1/2).** For all
`(a, b) ∈ {1, …, 8}^2`,

```text
Tr[ M_{3,sym}(λ^a/2) · M_{3,sym}(λ^b/2) ]  =  (1/2) δ^{ab}.        (N)
```

Including the `I_2` fiber trace gives
`Tr[T^a_{8D} T^b_{8D}] = (1/2 · 2) δ^{ab} = δ^{ab}`; the trace
normalization **on a single block** (the standard `T_F = 1/2`
Gell-Mann convention) is recovered on the 3D symmetric base factor.

**Conclusion (T3) (Lie algebra closure in 8D).** The structure
constants of (T-8D) computed in 8D match the cited Gell-Mann
`f^{abc}` in 3D: for all `(a, b) ∈ {1, …, 8}^2`,

```text
[ T^a_{8D}, T^b_{8D} ]  =  i · ∑_{c=1}^{8} f^{abc} · T^c_{8D}.     (L)
```

**Conclusion (T4) (Jacobi identity).** For all
`(a, b, c) ∈ {1, …, 8}^3`,

```text
[ [T^a_{8D}, T^b_{8D}], T^c_{8D} ]
+ [ [T^b_{8D}, T^c_{8D}], T^a_{8D} ]
+ [ [T^c_{8D}, T^a_{8D}], T^b_{8D} ]  =  0.                       (J)
```

**Conclusion (T5) (Commutant identification).** For all
`(a, i) ∈ {1, …, 8} × {1, 2, 3}`,

```text
[ T^a_{8D}, Jf_i ]  =  0,                                        (C)
```

i.e. the 8D embedded Gell-Mann generators commute with the fiber
`SU(2)` action. Moreover the 8D Lie algebra
`g_{sym} := span_R{T^a_{8D}}_{a=1}^{8}` is the fixed
symmetric-block `su(3)` subalgebra of the full commutant
`(End(C^4_base) ⊗ I_2)`: it is the traceless endomorphism algebra of
the chosen 3D symmetric base subspace and acts trivially on the 1D
antisymmetric base subspace.
(The full commutant `End(C^4_base) ⊗ I_2` is 16-dimensional as a
complex vector space and contains a `u(4) = su(4) ⊕ u(1)` Lie
algebra acting on `C^4_base`; the `su(3)` of `g_{sym}` is the
stabilizer-of-antisym `su(3) ⊂ su(4)`.)

**Conclusion (T6) (Tensor-product proof of commutativity).** The
commutativity (C) is the elementary tensor-product identity
`[A ⊗ I, I ⊗ B] = 0` applied to `A = M^a_4` on the base factor and
`B = s_i/2` on the fiber factor. No spectral or character-theoretic
machinery is used; the proof is direct multiplication.

## 2. Proof

**(T1).** Unitarity of `U_base` is direct:
`U_base · U_base^† = I_4` by inspection (each row of `U_base` has unit
norm and rows are pairwise orthogonal; verified at exact sympy
precision in the runner, Part 1). The `4 × 4` matrix
`diag_{4×4}(λ^a/2, 0_{1×1})` is Hermitian because `λ^a/2` is
Hermitian. Conjugation by the unitary `U_base` preserves
Hermiticity, so `M^a_4 = U_base^† · diag(λ^a/2, 0) · U_base` is
Hermitian. Tensoring with the Hermitian `I_2` preserves Hermiticity,
so `T^a_{8D}` is Hermitian on `C^8`. By construction `M^a_4`
restricted to the 3D symmetric block equals `λ^a/2` (in the
symmetry-sorted basis), and `M^a_4` restricted to the 1D antisymmetric
block is the zero `1 × 1` matrix. Tracelessness of `T^a_{8D}` follows
from `Tr[λ^a/2] = 0` (Gell-Mann) plus the zero antisymmetric block,
all multiplied by `Tr[I_2] = 2`. ∎(T1)

**(T2).** The trace `Tr[M_{3,sym}(λ^a/2) · M_{3,sym}(λ^b/2)]` reduces
to the block-diagonal computation in the symmetry-sorted basis:

```text
Tr_4[ diag(λ^a/2, 0) · diag(λ^b/2, 0) ]
    =  Tr_3[ (λ^a/2) (λ^b/2) ]  +  0  =  (1/2) δ^{ab}.
```

The unitary conjugation by `U_base` is trace-preserving (`Tr[UAU^†] = Tr[A]`).
Including the `Tr[I_2] = 2` from the fiber factor:
`Tr[T^a_{8D} T^b_{8D}] = (1/2) · 2 · δ^{ab} = δ^{ab}`. The Gell-Mann
`T_F = 1/2` is the trace on the single 3D symmetric block; this is
what is verified by the numerical runner Part 2. ∎(T2)

**(T3).** The Lie-bracket on 8D operators preserves the tensor
factorization:

```text
[T^a_{8D}, T^b_{8D}]  =  [M^a_4, M^b_4] ⊗ I_2.
```

The commutator `[M^a_4, M^b_4]` on the 4D base reduces to
`U_base^† · diag([λ^a/2, λ^b/2], 0) · U_base`, since the zero
antisymmetric block commutes trivially with itself. By the cited
Gell-Mann commutation relations
`[λ^a/2, λ^b/2] = i · ∑_c f^{abc} (λ^c/2)`, we get

```text
[M^a_4, M^b_4]
   =  U_base^† · diag( i · ∑_c f^{abc} (λ^c/2), 0 ) · U_base
   =  i · ∑_c f^{abc} · M^c_4.
```

Tensoring with `I_2`:
`[T^a_{8D}, T^b_{8D}] = i · ∑_c f^{abc} · T^c_{8D}`. ∎(T3)

**(T4).** The Jacobi identity is preserved by every associative
algebra; verified at exact precision on all `8^3 = 512` triples by
the runner Part 4. ∎(T4)

**(T5).** Compute directly. Let `A = M^a_4` (on the base) and
`B_i = s_i/2` (on the fiber). Then
`T^a_{8D} = A ⊗ I_2` and `Jf_i = I_4 ⊗ B_i`. By the elementary
tensor-product identity `[X ⊗ I, I ⊗ Y] = 0` for any
`X ∈ End(C^4)` and `Y ∈ End(C^2)`:

```text
(A ⊗ I_2)(I_4 ⊗ B_i)  =  A ⊗ B_i  =  (I_4 ⊗ B_i)(A ⊗ I_2),
```

so `[T^a_{8D}, Jf_i] = 0` for all `(a, i)`. ∎(T5-commutativity)

For the symmetric-block identification in (T5): the full commutant of
`{Jf_i = I_4 ⊗ s_i/2 : i = 1, 2, 3}` on `End(C^8)` is the von-Neumann
commutant `(I_4 ⊗ su(2))' = End(C^4_base) ⊗ I_2`: the fundamental
fiber `su(2)` representation is irreducible, so its commutant on
`C^2_fiber` is only the scalar multiples of `I_2`, leaving the full
multiplicity algebra `End(C^4_base)` on the base. The Lie algebra
`u(4) ⊕ {0}` embeds as `u(4) ⊗ I_2` inside this commutant. The
block-diagonal subalgebra preserving the symmetric-antisymmetric base split is
`u(3)_sym ⊕ u(1)_antisym`, and the traceless `su(3)_sym` subalgebra
acting faithfully on the symmetric block and trivially on the
antisymmetric block is `g_{sym} = span_R{T^a_{8D}}`. Once the 3D
symmetric block and the zero action on the 1D antisymmetric block are
fixed, this is exactly the traceless endomorphism algebra of that block.
∎(T5)

**(T6).** Direct multiplication, as in (T5-commutativity). ∎(T6)
