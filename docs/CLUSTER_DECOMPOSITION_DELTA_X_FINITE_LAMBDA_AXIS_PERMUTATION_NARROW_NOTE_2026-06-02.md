## §1. Setting (axis-permuted)

Fix a spatial axis `μ ∈ {1, 2, 3}` (one of the three Z³ generators).
Let `Λ ⊂ Z³` be a finite connected spatial sublattice with edge set
`E(Λ)`. Partition `Λ` into slabs orthogonal to `μ`, with slab edge
sets `E_⊥(s)` for slab `s`.

**Hilbert space.** The single-slab Hilbert space along `μ` is

```
H_Λ^{(μ)} := L²(SU(3)^{|E_⊥|}, dU_Haar)
```

(with `dU_Haar` the per-bond Haar measure). Structurally this is the
same `L²(SU(3)^E, dU_Haar)` as the temporal-axis parent's `H_Λ`, with
`E` instances of independent SU(3) Haar measure and the same product
structure.

**Spatial transfer operator.** The pure-Wilson spatial-axis transfer
operator is the integral operator with kernel

```
T_W^{(μ)}(U, V) = exp(-β · ΔS_W^{(μ)}[U, V]) · Π_{ℓ ∈ E_⊥} K_τ(U_ℓ V_ℓ^†)
```

where `K_τ` is the SU(3) heat kernel at lattice-spacing parameter `τ`
(identical to the parent's §1 definition) and `ΔS_W^{(μ)}` is the
spatial-Wilson action increment along axis `μ` (real-valued, by
`tr(plaquette) ∈ R` after the standard `Re tr` in the Wilson action).

**Fermion extension out of scope.** This row does not claim a full
staggered+Wilson `T_x · T_F` gap. The only theorem here is the
pure-Wilson finite-Λ axis-permutation statement.

## §2. Axis-permutation lemma (load-bearing)

### Lemma X (axis-permutation symmetry)

The four load-bearing properties used in the parent's proof of the
finite-Λ temporal-axis T_W gap are **axis-symmetric on the cubic Z³
substrate**:

- **(L-Heat)** SU(3) heat-kernel strict positivity (parent's
  Lemma A.2). Property of the SU(3) group and its bi-invariant
  metric; does not reference which lattice axis the kernel acts on.
- **(L-Kernel)** Strict positivity of `T_W` kernel on
  `Conf(Λ) × Conf(Λ)` (parent's Lemma B). Property of the Wilson
  action increment + heat-kernel product structure; same form on any
  axis with the standard `Re tr` Wilson action.
- **(L-Trace)** `T_W` trace-class on `L²(SU(3)^E)` (parent's
  Lemma C). Property of the smooth compact kernel on a compact Lie
  manifold; depends on `|E|` only via the product-measure factor
  count, not on which axis the slab is orthogonal to.
- **(L-PJ)** Perron-Jentzsch positivity-improving compact-operator
  theorem (parent's Lemma D). General operator theorem; entirely
  axis-independent.

**Proof.** Each of (L-Heat), (L-Kernel), (L-Trace), (L-PJ) is stated
in the parent without reference to the specific axis being
transferred along. The parent's proofs depend on:
- SU(3) group structure (axis-independent);
- the kernel/measure product structure on `L²(SU(3)^E, dU_Haar)`
  (axis-independent under relabeling of the finite Wilson
  transfer-kernel construction);
- standard real analysis on compact Lie groups (axis-independent);
- the Perron-Jentzsch theorem (axis-independent operator theory).

No step references "this is the temporal axis" or uses any property
that distinguishes the temporal direction from a spatial direction.
Relabeling the transfer coordinate as `x_μ` for any `μ ∈ {1, 2, 3}`
carries every load-bearing step through without modification on the
finite Wilson kernel surface. ∎

### Corollary X.1 (spatial-axis T_W gap)
