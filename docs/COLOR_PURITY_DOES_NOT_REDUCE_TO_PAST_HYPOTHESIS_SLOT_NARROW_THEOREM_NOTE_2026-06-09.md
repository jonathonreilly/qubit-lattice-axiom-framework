## What is claimed (narrow)

**The purity component of the global color-neutrality admission does not reduce
into the past-hypothesis slot.** The obstruction is on the orientation/symmetry
and sufficiency axes, not an entropy-orthogonality axis. Three exact facts
(runner groups: entropy boundary, sufficiency, and assembly):

1. **The past hypothesis fixes GLOBAL entropy; that does not fix the purity `P`.** The
   retained slot is a boundary selection on the *global* record-entropy axis. `P` is a
   property of the single carrier's *marginal*. The maximally-mixed marginal `rho_color =
   I3/3` (`P = 0`) is realized at **both** entropy poles: a globally pure state
   (`S_global = 0`, the `q`-`qbar` singlet, marginal `I3/3` by entanglement) **and** a
   globally maximally-mixed two-carrier state (`S_global = log 9`). So conditioning on the
   global-entropy boundary is uninformative about `P` — it neither supplies nor excludes
   `I3/3` (runner entropy-poles checks). The past hypothesis cannot *supply* the purity.

2. **"No unregistrable structure" is an orientation/symmetry selector, a different KIND of
   admission than the entropy-axis slot.** By the orientation-retirement result the
   orientation is vacuous; retiring
   it does **not** pin the registrable purity — a polarized `rho` with retired orientation
   still has `P > 0` (runner orientation-invariance and assembly checks). The uniqueness "`I3/3` is the only density with no
   orientation structure" is delivered by the **maximal-`SU(3)`-symmetry (invariance)**
   demand, i.e. a neutrality-type condition. The retained slot is purely an entropy-axis
   boundary state-selection; an orientation/symmetry selector is a distinct kind of
   admission. Folding the purity into the slot via this principle is a relabel of a
   different selector, not a unification with the existing slot.

3. **The purity condition is STRICTLY WEAKER than global neutrality.** A globally pure
   **non-singlet** two-fundamental state `|F> = (1/sqrt3) sum_i |i>_A |i>_B` has
   `rho_color = I3/3` (`P = 0`) yet is **not** invariant under the diagonal `SU(3)` action
   `g (x) g` — it is not a color singlet (runner non-singlet sufficiency checks). So `P = 0` does **not** entail
   neutrality; the forward direction (global neutrality `=> P = 0`, from
   [`COLOR_NEUTRALITY_ENTANGLEMENT_DEPOLARIZATION_IS_GLOBAL_INVARIANT_NOT_CONNECTION_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-06-09`](COLOR_NEUTRALITY_ENTANGLEMENT_DEPOLARIZATION_IS_GLOBAL_INVARIANT_NOT_CONNECTION_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-06-09.md)) does **not** invert. The
   named global-neutrality admission is one (stronger) realization of `P = 0`, not the
   residual itself.

**Conclusion.** No admission is discharged and the admission *count* is not reduced. The
purity is an independent `SU(3)`-invariant state-structure admission, on a different axis
than the entropy-boundary past hypothesis, and strictly weaker than the named
global-neutrality admission. The proposed purity reduction is refuted.

(orientation/symmetry axis) and 3 (sufficiency), which do not depend on any entropy
