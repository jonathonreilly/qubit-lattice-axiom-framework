## Claim (bounded)

Condition on the explicit chiral-cube carrier
`C^8 = C^4_base (x) C^2_fiber` of
[`CL3_SU3_SYMMETRIC_BASE_COMMUTANT_GELL_MANN_EMBEDDING_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-27.md`](CL3_SU3_SYMMETRIC_BASE_COMMUTANT_GELL_MANN_EMBEDDING_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-27.md)
and on the two candidate local symmetries it hosts: the base SU(3)
(Gell-Mann `T^a_8D` on the 3D symmetric base block) and the fiber SU(2)
(`Jf_i = I_4 (x) sigma_i/2`). On that carrier, the following are exact
algebraic facts (all verified by the primary runner, 23/23 PASS):

1. **Two distinct invariant (record) algebras.** The commutant of the
   base SU(3) inside `End(C^8)` has dimension `8`; the commutant of the
   fiber SU(2) has dimension `16`. They are different algebras. The base
   SU(3) singlets are the color singlets (the meson contraction
   `(1/sqrt 3) sum_i |i ibar>` of `3 (x) 3bar -> 1` and the baryon
   `eps_{abc} q^a q^b q^c` of `3 (x) 3 (x) 3 -> 1`, each of
   multiplicity one). The fiber SU(2) singlet is the antisymmetric
   doublet `(|01> - |10>)/sqrt 2`. These are structurally distinct
   records.

2. **The gauge-from-invariance-commutant half (a conditional identity).**
   The color-singlet projectors `P_meson` and `P_baryon` lie in the
   commutant of the base-SU(3) action. Hence, *given the antecedent that
   the physical records are the color singlets*, the
   "gauge group = the local symmetry whose invariants are the records"
   reading of
   [`TWO_ENDPOINT_GAUSS_LAW_INVARIANCE_PROFILE_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-06-05.md`](TWO_ENDPOINT_GAUSS_LAW_INVARIANCE_PROFILE_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-06-05.md)
   selects the base SU(3) (not the fiber SU(2)). This is an algebraic
   identity conditional on the antecedent; it is the "half" the
   record-invariance structure supplies.

3. **The crux: the antecedent is not forced.** On the same two-endpoint
   link carrier, a fiber-SU(2) Gauss law and a base-SU(3) Gauss law can
   both be written, and both exhibit the identical `0 -> 1 -> 2`
   endpoint-invariance profile of the two-endpoint note (bare transport
   variant at both ends; dressed Wilson-type observable invariant at
   both ends). Records, locality, and the link/Gauss-law structure do
   not discriminate which symmetry is gauged. The selector that fixes
   the antecedent — which subsystem the quarks occupy and which symmetry
   index the link connection carries — is a separate input.

4. **The color residual is specific, not a coin flip.** The framework's
   color triplet is `Sym^2(C^2) = C^3` of the `(b1, b2)` base pair, of
   dimension `3 = N_c`, which is not a single qubit. A bare qubit
   link-end carries SU(2) intrinsically (`Aut(M_2(C)) = SO(3)`; cf.
   [`QUBIT_LINK_U2_CONNECTION_ALGEBRA_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-06-04.md`](QUBIT_LINK_U2_CONNECTION_ALGEBRA_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-06-04.md)),
   whereas gauging the base SU(3) requires routing the base-pair
   symmetric color index onto the links — a structured matter-and-
   connection assignment.

This note is bounded because the chiral-cube carrier, the
`(base x fiber)` decomposition, the two-endpoint link model, and the
choice of which symmetry index sits on the link end are all model
conventions cited or stipulated here; they are not supplied by the
Lattice, Quantum, and Record axioms.

## Verdict (IS / IS-NOT)

- **IS.** The record-invariance structure supplies the
  gauge-from-invariance-commutant half of the bridge: on the explicit
  carrier, "the records are the color singlets" entails, by the
  commutant identity, that the gauged group is the base SU(3). This
  reduces the deferred bridge to a single, precisely named residual.

- **IS NOT.** The record-invariance structure does not supply the full
  bridge. It does not force the antecedent. Whether the base SU(3)
  (color) rather than the fiber SU(2) (weak isospin) is the gauged
  symmetry — equivalently, whether the physical records are the color
  singlets — depends on the matter realization, which is not delivered
  by Lattice + Quantum + Record.

The honest reading is **partial-pinning**: the deferred
"symmetric-base -> physical-color" bridge equals
`[gauge-from-invariance-commutant half] + [matter-realization residual]`,
and the record-invariance structure supplies only the first summand.
