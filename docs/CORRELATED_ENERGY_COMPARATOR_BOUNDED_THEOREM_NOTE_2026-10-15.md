To verify (3), differentiate `P(z)^2=P(z)` coefficientwise. Its diagonal blocks give `P P_j P=-P S_j P` and `Q P_j Q=Q S_j Q`. With `S=P-Q`, define the analytic direct rotation

```

```

```

```

