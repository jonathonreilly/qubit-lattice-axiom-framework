## The declared comparator

Open 3^3 cube, H = -J sum_<ij> Z_i Z_j - lambda J sum_i X_i, J = 1,
lambda in {0.05, 0.10, 0.20}; preparation = uniform product state with
Bloch vector (1,1,1)/sqrt(3) on all 27 sites (rotation-invariant, so
the whole evolution stays in the invariant sector and the orbit basis
is exact, not approximate); pointer = Z at the center site; registers =
the six axial single-qubit fragments. Certification per fragment:
H(Z_S) >= 0.05 bit, chi_Z(S:F) >= (1-delta) H(Z_S), excess over the
same-lambda ground state chi - chi_GS >= 0.02 bit, and pairwise
conditional independence I(F_a:F_b|Z_S) <= 0.02 bit; delta sweep
{0.05, 0.10, 0.20}, headline 0.10. Registration = first sampled t with
R_ind >= 2 (largest pairwise-independent certifying subset); grid
Jt = 0:0.1:10, headline window Jt <= 1;
theta(t) = mean over the six center bonds of ground-state-subtracted
(1 - purity). Copying, if it happens, must emerge from the ordinary
ZZ bond -- nothing is installed to copy.

## What was measured

1. **No certified registration anywhere.** Zero events at every
   lambda, every delta, over the entire grid Jt <= 10 -- not merely
   the headline window. No single fragment ever certifies (R_ind = 0
   at every sampled time; verified row-by-row in the committed
   streams), so R >= 2 never comes up. CHECK-03 and CHECK-04 fail;
   CHECK-05 (bar location) is unavailable. The BAR-BELOW-WINDOW flag
   is NOT raised -- that flag requires an onset with theta* below the
   sparse window, and there was no onset.
2. **The imprint exists, is lambda-independent at its peak, and is
   transient.** Per-fragment chi_Z rises from exactly zero (t = 0
   intersite Holevo 3.15e-12) to a peak of 0.0509 bits at Jt = 0.3 --
   the peak height agrees to three digits across all three lambda
   (0.05087 / 0.05081 / 0.05092), so the early-time imprint is set by
   the ZZ bond alone -- then RECEDES to under 0.01 bits. The content
   gate needs (1-delta) H(Z_S) ~ 0.66-0.67 bits: the peak is a factor
   of ~13 short. A single qubit in the uniform quench is a small,
   recohering antenna: its own five other neighbors scramble the
   imprint away faster than the bond deepens it.
3. **The pointer itself is stable the whole window (first predeclared
   risk signature, measured).** Z_S total-variation drift stays
   <= 0.056 over the full grid at every lambda (gate: <= 0.10), and
   H(Z_S) stays in [0.72, 0.84]. The demolition panel confirms the
   basis assignment: max centered-Frobenius C_F(H, Z_i) = 4.65e-6 vs
   min C_F(H, X_i) = 4.03e-5 across all site classes, and the
   X-declared-pointer control (same evolution data) certifies nothing.
   So the window is not empty of pointer stability -- it is empty of
   certified copying.
4. **No correlated-channel fake (second predeclared risk signature,
   measured).** Max pairwise conditional mutual information between
   fragments is 0.023 bits (lambda = 0.05, Jt = 9.4), at the
   independence gate's edge and irrelevant to the verdict since no
   singleton certifies. The six directions really are nearly
   independent channels -- the d = 1 Markov-blanket obstruction is
   absent here, as the Lattice axiom's geometry promises.
5. **Activity is abundant while registration is absent.** theta(t)
   reaches ~0.5 by Jt = 0.5 at every lambda -- well above the d = 1
   sparse-window candidate theta >= 0.2 -- with strong finite-volume
   recurrences at lambda = 0.05 (theta collapses to 0.007 at Jt = 1.5
   and revives to 0.46 by Jt = 2). Bond activity does not gate pointer
   information linearly; this repeats, in d = 3, what the
   activity-information block measured in d = 1.
6. **Ground-state baseline caveat.** At these lambda the ferromagnetic
   ground doublet is quasi-degenerate far below the eigensolver
   residual (4.63e-9), so the computed ground state is an arbitrary
   member of the doublet; the measured baselines chi_GS = 0.132 /
   0.080 / 2e-5 bits (lambda = 0.05 / 0.10 / 0.20) are
   convention-dependent within it. This touches only the excess-gate
   diagnostic at lambda <= 0.1. The verdict does not rest on it: the
   content gate fails by an order of magnitude regardless of baseline,
   and at lambda = 0.2 the baseline is ~0 and there is still no event.

Machinery is green and separate from the physics: ground residual

## What this negative localizes

The d = 1 obstruction was geometric: two outward directions, both
mediated, no independent second register possible. d = 3 removes
exactly that obstruction -- six near-independent channels, measured
conditional dependence <= 0.023 bits -- and the failure MOVES rather
than disappears: from register geometry to register capacity and
persistence. In the uniform symmetric quench no single-qubit register
ever accumulates certifiable content; the imprint saturates near 0.05
bits and recoheres. So the pilot does not pin the bar and does not
place it below the window; it measures that this comparator's
finest-grained registers are too small for the certification the
Record axiom's clauses demand, even where geometry no longer forbids
redundancy.

Named successor (design consequence, not a claim): keep Z^3 and
re-ask with (i) coarser registers -- multi-qubit fragment blocks
(faces, shells), since chi grows with fragment size while the
conditional-independence machinery is unchanged -- and/or (ii) a
pointer-contrast preparation (a local excitation on the uniform
background) so there is a distinguished local value for the bond to
copy, closer to the d = 1 block's kicked-charge protocol than to a
global quench. A d = 2 stand-in remains at most a method stepping
stone; the bar location can be dimension-dependent, and the target is
the framework's own Z^3.
