Item 1: interacting protection
Theorem (exact). W(F4) = Aut(D4) has 1, 3, 16 invariant tensors at rank 2, 4, 6 (character averages over the 1152
elements); O(4) has 1, 3, 15; the hypercubic group B4 has 1, 4, 31. Hence:
 - Any self-energy of a W(F4)-invariant lattice theory (any W(F4)-covariant propagators and vertices, any loop order) has
   a quadratic term c p^2 and a quartic term c' (p^2)^2 only: no dimension-four or dimension-six direction dependence,
   for scalars, and (via covariants of the reflection representation, generated by gradients of the basic invariants of
   degrees 2, 6, 8, 12) cubic gamma-terms of the form p^2 g.p only. The first allowed breaking is J6 (grad J6), dimension 8.
 - On Z^4 the extra rank-4 invariant (sum p_mu^4) is allowed and is generated.
 So the protection is the regulator's symmetry, not a tuning, and survives renormalisation as long as interactions keep
 W(F4). Supplied: the interaction terms are site-local or W(F4)-covariant.
Finite diagnostics (exact position-space products of torus propagators; D4 on the W(F4)-invariant torus with period
lattice L*D4, minimal images with ties averaged; moments at 40 random momenta, least-squares on isotropic bases):
 - dim 4: residual <= 2e-14 for all five self-energies on D4 and on Z^4. Every species has a single p^2 coefficient, so
   the speed of light is the same for all species and for time along any root (the time-blind slicing). The CPS
   dimension-four issue does not arise for a Euclidean point group containing the time direction (as on main for B4).
 - dim 6: D4 residual <= 8e-15 for all five; Z^4 control 3.2e-2 to 2.3e-1 (induced sum p^4 / p_mu^3). Discriminating.
 - dim 8: fitting the D4 sextic (quintic gamma) moment by (p^2)^3 alone leaves 8e-3 to 4.5e-2; adding J6 (grad J6)
   leaves <= 5e-15. J6/(p^2)^3 ratios (stable L = 16 -> 20 to 1e-5): bubble -0.23665, sunset -0.37995, Yukawa loop
   -0.14333, fermion mass part -0.09156, fermion gamma part (grad J6 / p^4 p) -0.03603 (masses: M^2 = 4, m_f = 1.5,
   lattice units; supplied, chosen for exponential locality). The dimension-8 coefficient is species-dependent at loop
   level, as allowed; its observable size stays suppressed by (E/E*)^4 (parent thresholds unchanged at this order,
   coefficients shift by O(g^2)).
Verdict item 1: PASS.

Item 2: fermion reflection positivity, root slicing
Method: exact transverse momentum (fiber p = k + phi u, u = r_t / (r_t.v)), time torus of Nt = 16 slices with period
(Nt/2) r_t so that the site reflection s (reflection perpendicular to the root r_t = (1,1,0,0), a W(F4) element) fixes
both planes pointwise; antiperiodic fermion time boundary. OS two-point form M = -(S(x' + j v - s x) G)^T with
Theta psi = psibar(sx) G, G = g.r_t/sqrt2 (the only choice among g_t, g5 g_t, g_0, 1 that gives a Hermitian form).
Calibration (Z^4, axis), all as known: Wilson r = 1 link RP and transfer T >= 0 (positive fraction 2e-14, 8e-14); naive
r = 0 link RP holds but T indefinite (1.00); scalar site RP holds; Wilson site RP with the plane fails (1.00). The
overall Grassmann sign was fixed by this calibration, as pre-registered.
Results:
 - D4 scalar, root site reflection: RP holds (1e-15), confirming the parent runner's C4a. (A first attempt on an L Z^4
   torus showed a spurious violation; that torus does not fix the second reflection plane pointwise. Artifact, removed.)
 - No link reflection exists: the isometries x -> A x + c (A in W(F4)) mapping slice n to 1 - n are all twisted
   (A = -1 on >= 3 dimensions; 7 found, 0 pure reflections). A twisted Theta pairs transverse momentum k with A k, so its
   form is off-diagonal between sectors and not an OS positivity in general (argument; not computed).
 - D4 fermions, site reflection, two-point form: indefinite (Hermitian to 1e-14) with the plane (as on Z^4) AND on the
   open half (n >= 1, which on Z^4 is the passing T >= 0 condition). Largest wrong-sign eigenvalue fraction: Wilson
   w = sqrt2 (projector hops along the root) 4.4e-2, w = 1 4.6e-3, overlap 8.9e-4; scan w in [0.5, 10]: minimum ~4e-3
   near w = 1; robust to m in {0.2, 0.5, 1.0} and Nt in {16, 24} (the w-scan and m/Nt robustness are scratch runs,
   not in the runner).
 Reading: the crossing hops at Delta n = 1 carry g.r with r = r_t/2 + r_perp, not a projector of g_t, so the
 Wilson-type positivity mechanism has no D4 root-sliced analogue. This is a necessary-condition failure for the natural
 Theta; it does not exclude RP with respect to some other antilinear involution.
Verdict item 2: FAIL (for Wilson-type and overlap D4 fermions under root slicing, natural Theta).

OVERALL (pre-registered rule): PARTIAL. The symmetry protection against dimension-six Lorentz violation holds at loop
level (it is a theorem of the regulator symmetry), the link sector has no extra light states, but the Euclidean D4 event
weight with time along a root does not give a reflection-positive fermion sector with the natural reflection, so the
fermion Hilbert space / positive transfer matrix is not established and appears absent for these operators.
