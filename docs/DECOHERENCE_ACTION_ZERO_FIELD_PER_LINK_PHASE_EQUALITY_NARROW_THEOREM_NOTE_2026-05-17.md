# Decoherence Action Zero-Field Per-Link Phase Equality Narrow Theorem

**Date:** 2026-05-17
**Type:** bounded_theorem
**Claim scope:** the standalone algebraic-substitution implication that,
given the two explicitly stated action-law definitions below, the per-link
action contribution `act(L, f_bar)` of the two action laws is

```text
spent_delay(L, f_bar)    :=  dl(L, f_bar) - ret(L, f_bar),                 (D1)
   with  dl  := L * (1 + f_bar),
         ret := sqrt(max(dl^2 - L^2, 0)),
valley_linear(L, f_bar)  :=  L * (1 - f_bar),                              (D2)
```

then in the zero-field restriction `f_bar = 0`, the two per-link action
values reduce to the same expression `L`,

```text
spent_delay(L, 0)    =  L,                                                 (P1)
valley_linear(L, 0)  =  L,                                                 (P2)
spent_delay(L, 0)  -  valley_linear(L, 0)  =  0  for every L > 0,          (P3)
```

and therefore the per-link complex phase factor that appears multiplicatively
in the propagation step (i.e. `exp(i k act)` with `k` and `L > 0` arbitrary
positive reals) coincides identically between the two action laws,

```text
exp(i k * spent_delay(L, 0))  =  exp(i k * valley_linear(L, 0))            (P4)
                              =  exp(i k L).
```

This is purely a statement about algebraic substitution of `f_bar = 0` into
the two action-law definitions; no propagation harness, no lattice
geometry, no slit configuration, no detector readout, no `<Lattice3D>`
import, and no numerical observable computation enters. The narrow theorem
isolates the load-bearing per-link algebraic step that, at zero field,
makes the two integrand factors identical edge-by-edge regardless of how
the propagation accumulation is implemented.

## Statement

Let `L > 0` and `f_bar` be free real symbols, and let `k` be a free
real symbol. Suppose the two action laws are explicit hypotheses,

```text
(I1) spent_delay(L, f_bar)    :=  dl(L, f_bar) - ret(L, f_bar),
        dl(L, f_bar)  := L * (1 + f_bar),
        ret(L, f_bar) := sqrt(max(dl(L, f_bar)^2 - L^2, 0)),
                                  (parent eqs. (D1)-(D3) of "Why" section)

(I2) valley_linear(L, f_bar)  :=  L * (1 - f_bar),
                                  (per-link form of S = L (1 - f) on the
                                   half-edge midpoint average).
```

Restrict to the zero-field substitution `f_bar -> 0`. Then

```text
(P1) spent_delay(L, 0)    =  L,
(P2) valley_linear(L, 0)  =  L,
(P3) spent_delay(L, 0)  -  valley_linear(L, 0)  =  0  for all L > 0,
(P4) exp(i k * spent_delay(L, 0))  =  exp(i k * valley_linear(L, 0))
                                   =  exp(i k L)         for all k real, L > 0.
```

`(P1)`-`(P4)` hold exactly as algebraic identities under the cited input
definitions `(I1)`, `(I2)`; no propagation accumulation, no slit
configuration, no detector readout, and no numerical observable enters.

## Proof

Pure algebraic substitution.

`(P1)`. From `(I1)`:

```text
dl(L, 0)  =  L * (1 + 0)  =  L,
ret(L, 0) =  sqrt(max(L^2 - L^2, 0))  =  sqrt(max(0, 0))  =  0,
spent_delay(L, 0)  =  dl(L, 0) - ret(L, 0)  =  L - 0  =  L.
```

`(P2)`. From `(I2)`:

```text
valley_linear(L, 0)  =  L * (1 - 0)  =  L.
```

`(P3)`. From `(P1)` and `(P2)`:

```text
spent_delay(L, 0) - valley_linear(L, 0)  =  L - L  =  0  for every L > 0.
```

`(P4)`. From `(P1)`, `(P2)`, and the equality of phase arguments:

```text
i k * spent_delay(L, 0)    =  i k L,
i k * valley_linear(L, 0)  =  i k L,
exp(i k * spent_delay(L, 0))  =  exp(i k L)  =  exp(i k * valley_linear(L, 0)).
```

∎

## Derivable corollaries

```text
(C1) The per-link kernel weight w * h^2 / L^2 (which depends only on
     geometry: L, the angular weight w(theta), and the lattice spacing h)
     is action-mode-independent by construction; the per-link complex
     factor entering the propagation step is then
        a_src * exp(i k * act(L, f_bar)) * w * h^2 / L^2,
     and at f_bar = 0 the only action-mode-dependent factor exp(i k * act)
     coincides between the two modes (consequence of (P4)).

(C2) (C1) holds for every edge (dy, dz, L) in the lattice's neighbor set,
     and in particular is independent of the specific L value, as long as
     L > 0, since (P3) holds parametrically in L.

(C3) The per-link phase difference between the two action modes at zero
     field is identically zero as a real (and as a complex) number:
        spent_delay(L, 0) - valley_linear(L, 0)  =  0,
        exp(i k * spent_delay(L, 0)) / exp(i k * valley_linear(L, 0))  =  1.
```

These corollaries follow directly from `(P1)`-`(P4)` and from the parent
runner's own per-link factorization
`c = a[nz] * np.exp(1j * k * act) * w * self._hm / (L * L)` (parent runner
[`scripts/decoherence_action_independence.py`](../scripts/decoherence_action_independence.py)
calling [`scripts/valley_linear_same_harness_compare.py::Lattice3D.propagate`](../scripts/valley_linear_same_harness_compare.py));
they do not claim anything about the accumulated post-propagation
amplitudes, only about the per-link algebraic factor.

  `VALLEY_LINEAR_ACTION_NOTE.md`.
- Does **not** claim equality of the accumulated propagated amplitudes

  numerical claim is the parent broad note's load-bearing content and
  requires the imported `Lattice3D.propagate` harness, which remains
  outside this narrow algebraic theorem.
- Does **not** claim equality of any downstream decoherence observable
  (`d_TV`, `MI`, `pur_cl`, `decoh`, `S_norm`) between the two action
  modes. Those observables aggregate per-link factors across many
  layers and edges; their equality at zero field follows informally
  from `(C1)`-`(C2)` PLUS the unchanged geometry, but the rigorous
  zero-field-equality-of-observables claim still rides on the broad
  parent's audit-conditional perimeter and is not asserted here.
- Does **not** claim anything about the nonzero-field case `f_bar != 0`.
  At `f_bar > 0` the two action laws differ:
  `spent_delay(L, f_bar) = L * ((1 + f_bar) - sqrt(2 f_bar + f_bar^2))`
  vs. `valley_linear(L, f_bar) = L * (1 - f_bar)`. The narrow theorem is
  scoped strictly to the zero-field substitution.
- Does **not** consume any decoherence observable, lattice geometry

  convention.
- Does **not** introduce any new vocabulary, new claim type, or new
