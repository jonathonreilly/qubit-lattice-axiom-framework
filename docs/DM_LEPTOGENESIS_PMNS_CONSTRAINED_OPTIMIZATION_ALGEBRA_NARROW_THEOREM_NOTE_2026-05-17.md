## Statement

Let `Omega subseteq R^n` be a non-empty open convex set, and let
```text
J : Omega -> R                                                           (J)
```
be a `C^2` function. Let
```text
C : Omega -> R                                                           (C)
```
be a `C^1` function. Assume the four supplied hypotheses:

```text
(H1)  J is strictly convex on Omega
(H2)  grad C(z) =/= 0  for all z in F := { z in Omega : C(z) = 0 }
(H3)  F is non-empty and bounded
(H4)  J extends continuously to the closure of F inside Omega and the
      minimum of J on F is attained at some interior point z_* in F
      (i.e., z_* in Omega, not on the boundary of Omega).
```

Then the constrained-minimization problem
```text
minimize    J(z)
subject to  z in F                                                       (P)
```
satisfies:

**Conclusion (T1) (uniqueness).** Any global minimizer of `(P)` is
unique. That is, if `z_a, z_b in F` both attain
`J(z_a) = J(z_b) = inf_{z in F} J(z)`, then `z_a = z_b`.

**Conclusion (T2) (Lagrange stationarity).** For any global minimizer
`z_* in F` of `(P)`, there exists a unique scalar `lambda_* in R` such
that
```text
grad J(z_*)  =  lambda_*  *  grad C(z_*).                                (L)
```

**Conclusion (T3) (Lagrange multiplier formula).** Under `(H2)`, the
unique multiplier `lambda_*` of `(T2)` is given by the projection
formula
```text
lambda_*  =  <grad J(z_*), grad C(z_*)>  /  || grad C(z_*) ||^2,         (L-formula)
```
where `<.,.>` is the standard Euclidean inner product on `R^n` and
`||.||` is the induced norm.

## Proof

We use one additional supplied hypothesis, stated explicitly:

**Hypothesis (H1') (global F-segment-feasibility).** Between any
two points `z_a, z_b in F`, the segment
`z_t := (1 - t) z_a + t z_b`, `t in [0, 1]`, stays in `F`. This is the
standard convex-feasible hypothesis (automatic for affine equality
constraints). For a nonlinear equality constraint it is a genuinely global
condition on `F`; componentwise segment-feasibility would prove only
componentwise uniqueness and is not enough for `(T1)`.

`(T1)`. Suppose `z_a, z_b in F` are two global minimizers with
`J(z_a) = J(z_b) = m`, and assume for contradiction `z_a =/= z_b`. By
`(H1')`, the midpoint `z_{1/2} := (z_a + z_b)/2 in F`. By `(H1)` strict
convexity of `J` on `Omega supseteq F`,
```text
J(z_{1/2})  <  (1/2) J(z_a) + (1/2) J(z_b)  =  m.                        (StrConv)
```
But `z_{1/2} in F` and `J(z_{1/2}) < m` contradicts that `m` is the
global minimum of `J` on `F`. Hence `z_a = z_b`.

`(T2)`. By `(H4)`, `z_* in Omega` is interior to `Omega`. By `(H2)`,
`grad C(z_*) =/= 0`. Hence the tangent space of the constraint surface
at `z_*` is
```text
T_{z_*} F  =  { v in R^n  :  <grad C(z_*), v>  =  0 }.                   (Tang)
```
By the standard Lagrange multiplier theorem for smooth equality
constraints with regular gradient (`(H2)`), any interior local
minimizer `z_*` of `J` restricted to `F` satisfies
```text
grad J(z_*)  perp  T_{z_*} F,                                            (Perp)
```
which combined with `(Tang)` is equivalent to the existence of a
scalar `lambda_*` such that `grad J(z_*) = lambda_* grad C(z_*)`. The
scalar `lambda_*` is uniquely determined because `grad C(z_*) =/= 0`
spans the one-dimensional complement of `T_{z_*} F` in `R^n` (with
`C` scalar-valued).

`(T3)`. Take the Euclidean inner product of both sides of `(L)` with
`grad C(z_*)`:
```text
<grad J(z_*), grad C(z_*)>  =  lambda_* * <grad C(z_*), grad C(z_*)>     (IP)
                            =  lambda_* * || grad C(z_*) ||^2.
```
By `(H2)`, `|| grad C(z_*) ||^2 =/= 0`, so
```text
lambda_*  =  <grad J(z_*), grad C(z_*)>  /  || grad C(z_*) ||^2.         (L-form)
```

QED

## Derivable corollaries

```text
(C1)  grad J(z_*)  -  lambda_* grad C(z_*)  =  0          (rearranged (L))
(C2)  At z_*, the projection of grad J(z_*) onto T_{z_*} F is zero
      (equivalent to (L) by (Perp)-(Tang) duality)
(C3)  If grad J(z_*) =/= 0 and grad C(z_*) =/= 0, then lambda_* =/= 0
      (otherwise (L) would force grad J(z_*) = 0, contradicting nonvanishing
      of grad J at z_*).
```

All follow algebraically from `(T1)-(T3)` and the supplied hypotheses
`(H1)-(H4)` plus `(H1')`.
