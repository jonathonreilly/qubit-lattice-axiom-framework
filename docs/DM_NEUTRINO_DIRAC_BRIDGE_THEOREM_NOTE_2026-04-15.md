## The Theorem

Let the `3+1` completed staggered lattice carry the exact chirality operator
`gamma_5 = G_0 G_1 G_2 G_3`, with left/right projectors
`P_L = (1 + gamma_5)/2`, `P_R = (1 - gamma_5)/2`.

Let the local spatial Higgs family be

`M(phi) = phi_1 Gamma_1 + phi_2 Gamma_2 + phi_3 Gamma_3`.

Then:

1. `M(phi)` is Hermitian.
2. `M(phi)^2 = |phi|^2 I`.
3. `{M(phi), gamma_5} = 0`.
4. Therefore `P_L M(phi) P_L = P_R M(phi) P_R = 0`, so `M(phi)` is purely
   chiral off-diagonal.
5. The exact selector `V_sel` has minima only at the axis vectors `e_1,e_2,e_3`.
6. At those minima, `M(e_i) = Gamma_i`.

Items 1-4 and 6 are certified symbolically over free real symbols
`phi_1, phi_2, phi_3` — not sampled — so they hold for arbitrary real
`phi`, and each is paired with a wrong-value rejector that fails for a
perturbed or rescaled operator. Item 1 in particular is the exhaustive
Hermiticity certificate: `Gamma_mu = Gamma_mu^dagger` and
`Gamma_mu^2 = I` for `mu = 0..3`, `{Gamma_mu, Gamma_nu} = 2 delta_munu I`
on all sixteen ordered pairs, and `M(phi) = M(phi)^dagger` identically in
`phi` with no reality condition imposed by hand. Item 5 is an
admitted-context input (see "Out of scope" below); the runner exhibits it
numerically rather than deriving it.

Therefore after EWSB axis selection the local post-EWSB Dirac operator is
uniquely `Gamma_i` up to the broken `S_3` choice and sign convention.

In the branch convention with weak axis 1:

`M_weak = Gamma_1`.

## Why `Xi_5` Is Excluded

The earlier operator-selection obstruction established that `Xi_5` and
`Gamma_1` are inequivalent framework-native operators.

The new bridge theorem sharpens that into an exact identity:

- `Gamma_1` anticommutes with `gamma_5`, so it connects `L <-> R`
- `Xi_5 = -gamma_5` exactly, so it is chiral diagonal

The second statement is strictly stronger than "commutes with `gamma_5`".
Write `Xi_5 = G_1 G_2 G_3 G_0` and move `G_0` leftward past three mutually
anticommuting generators: that costs `(-1)^3`, so
`Xi_5 = -G_0 G_1 G_2 G_3 = -gamma_5`. The step uses only
`{Gamma_mu, Gamma_nu} = 2 delta_munu I`, so it is a property of the
Clifford relations rather than of any particular matrix realization. The
runner confirms it entry-by-entry in the displayed `C^16` representation
and again in the standard 4x4 Cl(4) realization, and records that all 24
orderings of `G_0..G_3` give `+/- gamma_5` (twelve of each sign).

Stated as a single sentence for the record:

Xi_5 equals minus gamma_5 in the displayed representation, so it is chiral diagonal and cannot be a chiral off-diagonal Dirac Yukawa surface

That means `Xi_5` may still be the exact staggered mass / taste-chirality
surface in the old top-Yukawa lane, but it is **not** the direct local
post-EWSB neutrino Dirac Yukawa operator on the `3+1` completed lattice.

So the operator-selection ambiguity is resolved on the local chiral surface:

`Gamma_1` wins, `Xi_5` loses.
