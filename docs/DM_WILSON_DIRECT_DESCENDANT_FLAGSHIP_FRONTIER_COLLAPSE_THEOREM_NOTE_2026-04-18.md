## Diagnostic result: fixed-support ambient completions do not change the downstream data

Assume:

1. a supplied charged support block `E_e`;
2. the exact charged-source-response reduction
   `dW_e^H = Schur_{E_e}(D_-)`;
3. the exact projected-source-law derivation and exact transport selector;
4. the microscopic selector reduction theorem.

Then ambient completion freedom in `D_-` outside the fixed Schur block does not
change:

- the Hermitian response law on `E_e`;
- the reconstructed `H_e`;
- the `N_e` packet;
- the downstream selected-column diagnostic.

### Reason

With `E_e` fixed, any microscopic completion that yields the same
`L_e = Schur_{E_e}(D_-)` yields the same Hermitian response law on `E_e`, hence
the same `H_e`, the same `N_e` packet, the same selected transport column, and
the same downstream flavored DM value.

So the fixed-support diagnostic localizes downstream ambient-completion
ambiguity to the supplied charged Schur block. It does not derive the support
block and does not derive the right-sensitive microscopic law on that block.
