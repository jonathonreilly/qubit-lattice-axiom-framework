## 3. Operator classification (T1)

### 3.1 Hypercubic dispersion expansion: the leading anisotropy is dim-6

The free staggered fermion dispersion and the bosonic Laplacian
dispersion on `Z^3` are

```text
   E^2 = (1/a^2) sum_i sin^2(p_i a)        (staggered fermion)
   E^2 = (4/a^2) sum_i sin^2(p_i a / 2)    (bosonic Laplacian)
```

Taylor-expanding for `p a << 1`:

```text
   E^2 = p^2  +  c_4 a^2 sum_i p_i^4  +  O(a^4),
        c_4 = -1/3   (fermion),     c_4 = -1/12  (boson).
```

The leading term `p^2` is the SO(4)-invariant kinetic operator
(dimension-4 in the operator-dimension counting where a derivative
carries mass dimension 1 and the kinetic term sets the dimension-4
boundary). The **first anisotropic correction** `\sum_i p_i^4` carries
**two extra powers of momentum**, i.e. it is a **dimension-6** operator.
There is **no `O(a^1)` (dimension-5) anisotropy term**: every term in
the expansion is even in each `p_i`, so parity forbids an odd-power
(dimension-5) correction. (Runner PC1; this reproduces the
structural-dispersion core of
[`EMERGENT_LORENTZ_INVARIANCE_NOTE.md`](EMERGENT_LORENTZ_INVARIANCE_NOTE.md)

## 5. Power-counting irrelevance and the controlling fixed point (T2)

### 5.1 The arithmetic

In `d = 4`, a local operator `O` of mass dimension `\Delta_O` has a
coupling `g_O` of mass dimension `[g_O] = d - \Delta_O`. Its linearized
RG eigenvalue at the Gaussian fixed point is `y_O = d - \Delta_O =
[g_O]`. The operator is **relevant** iff `y_O > 0`, **marginal** iff
`y_O = 0`, **irrelevant** iff `y_O < 0` (standard textbook power
counting). For `d = 4`:

| `\Delta_O` | `y_O = 4 - \Delta_O` | class |
|---|---|---|
| 2 (a mass term) | `+2` | relevant |
| 4 (kinetic) | `0` | marginal (boundary) |
| 5 (parity-forbidden here) | `-1` | irrelevant |
| **6 (the `\ell = 4` artifact)** | **`-2`** | **irrelevant** |

So the dimension-6 SO(4)-breaking operator of (T1) has `y = -2 < 0`: it
is **RG-irrelevant**. Its dimensionful coupling carries mass dimension
`-2`; the lattice spacing supplies the inverse-mass scale, so the
physical coupling scales as `a^{+2}`, giving `O(a^2)` suppression that
vanishes as `a \to 0`. (Runner PC4.)
