physical-site compiler, but they fail for different exact reasons.  The
combined evidence therefore narrows the engineering target without supporting
a route-independent obstruction, minimum-content theorem, or axiom pressure.

The strongest new positive is Route B's finite staggered face-gauge tableau:
it executes the complete scheduled common-`E` coin/seam/contact action on the

constant individual Pauli support, and exact covariance under all 24

radius-one cell-parity span still misses `2`, `30`, and `104` seams.

tested support.  The construction uses constant overhead and has zero

`5.551115123125783e-17`.  Its maximum stream weight/diameter is nevertheless `23/32` on the
open fixtures and `28/32` on the periodic fixtures, compared with the retained
path construction's `17/28`.  This retires the frozen incidence, not direct
reference-edge gadgets in general.

Route C attacks the literal Cycle-269 carrier/role substrate.  The raw
