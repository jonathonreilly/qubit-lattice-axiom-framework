# Where Gravity Stands In This Framework -- What Is Now Derived, The One Missing Step, And The Decision This Opens

**Date:** 2026-07-08
**Type:** meta (campaign synthesis and owner decision surface; makes no
new technical claim; every technical statement below is carried by the

**Claim type:** meta
**Status authority:** none -- this is a conversation surface for the

## The question this campaign asked

Everything the mass campaigns established is about how matter RESPONDS
to a pull: one number per particle type governs rest energy and
inertia; bound objects weigh their energy in the right window; the pull
can only lawfully couple to energy differences, with one universal
constant. What was missing is the other half: does anything in the
framework GENERATE the pull? This campaign attempted that derivation
along the two live routes, with pre-registered outcomes either way.

## What came back (plain statements; details in the cited notes)

**1. You cannot build gravity here the way electricity was built.**
The electric sector exists because a counting law (charge) was upgraded
to a local bookkeeping rule at every site, and that upgrade is
mathematically permitted because charge counts at different places are
independent. The same upgrade for energy is mathematically forbidden:
energy amounts at neighboring places interfere with each other --
provably, exactly, and for a reason that no choice of interaction can
remove, because the interfering part is precisely the part of energy
that makes things move. Forcing the upgrade anyway drags in a new
object -- the flow of energy from place to place -- and a bookkeeping
system that must track both energy and its flow is no longer the simple
kind electricity uses. It has the structure of the bookkeeping system
of general relativity. That is not an analogy; it is what the
computation returned as the minimal consistent completion.

**2. The framework already contains a field-shaped hole with exactly
gravity's profile.** Take the local rate at which time runs -- the
"lapse": how much evolution happens per tick, place by place. Its
GLOBAL value is already registered in this program as a pure
convention (nothing physical changes if all clocks everywhere run
uniformly faster). The campaign computed what dynamics the matter
sector hands to a LOCAL version of that field, and three things came
back exact:

- Its uniform mode costs nothing -- exactly, at every particle mass,
  free or interacting -- because speeding up all clocks together moves
  nothing physical. This is the same *kind* of protection that keeps
  light long-ranged, and it is the property that the earlier
  classification proved is the unique route to a Newton-type law.
- Its stiffness against nonuniform variation is positive and
  computable. So the induced behavior is not just massless but stable,
  and it lands exactly in the one lawful class of field laws the
  classification note singled out: the Poisson class -- Newton's.
- The protection is enforced by energy conservation itself: the very
  object that obstructs the electric-style construction in point 1 is
  the object that guarantees the masslessness here. The two
  computations are one structure seen from two sides.

And this field would couple to energy density BY CONSTRUCTION -- a
local clock-rate variation couples to how much energy sits at that
place, universally, species-blind, with one overall constant. That is
the full list of properties the mass campaigns established gravity's
source must have.

**3. What is still missing is one step, and only one.** Everything
above treats the local clock rate as a fixed background that we
wiggled by hand to see what it costs. The framework's axioms do not
currently make it a VARIABLE -- nothing forces it to exist as a degree
of freedom, fluctuate, or respond. The computation says: IF it is a
variable, its dynamics is massless, stable, energy-sourced, and
Newton-class, with the strength of gravity as the single free
constant. WHETHER it is a variable is not derivable from the current
four axioms. That is the precise, and now very small, gap.

## The decision this opens

This is the axiom conversation the campaign was instructed to stop
for. The shape of the candidate step: promote the local time-rate from
convention to variable. Three honest framings of how that could enter,
in increasing order of ambition:

- **As a clarity extension of the existing time-convention registration:**
  the program already declares the global time unit a convention; the
  candidate says the convention is local, and what is conventional
  locally must be dynamical globally (the same move by which local
  phase convention becomes the electromagnetic field).
- **As the already-banked dynamics proposal:** the Dynamics/Evolution
  axiom candidate parked on main is the natural host -- this campaign
  gives it, for the first time, a specific job description and a
  computable consequence list.
- **As a new fifth axiom:** stated directly ("evolution rate is a
  local degree of freedom"), with the induced-kernel computation
  guaranteeing the resulting field theory is the classified one rather
  than something arbitrary.

What adoption would buy, immediately and testably: a derived
Newton-class field equation on the framework surface with computable
stiffness; the background-subtraction prediction (gravity couples to
energy contrasts) inherited automatically; and a closed derivation
chain from the axioms to universal free fall with exactly one supplied
constant. What it would NOT buy yet: dimensional lift beyond d = 1
comparators, the tensor (light-bending) structure of full general
relativity, and any statement about quantum backreaction -- all of
which become well-posed follow-up campaigns rather than speculation.

What declining would mean: the framework keeps a complete matter-side
story with the field side permanently supplied from outside -- a
consistent but weaker position, with the diagnosis notes recording
exactly why no smaller repair exists (the electric-style route is
closed by theorem; induced dynamics without the promotion has no
variable to carry it).

  triviality -- of course rescaling the Hamiltonian is free"): the
  response is that gauge protection is always a triviality of this
  kind -- that is what makes it protection. The nontrivial content is
  (a) the classification theorem saying shift symmetry is the unique
  long-range route, (b) the exact statement that ONLY the energy
  channel has it (bond and site channels are massive), and (c) the
  conservation identity showing it is structural, not accidental.

  and the classification note; nothing in this surface adds claims.
