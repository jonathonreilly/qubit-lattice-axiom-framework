# Synthesis: families, flavour and mass generation

Symmetric mirror gapping is cited, not established, and family-dependent copy signs allow every N >= 2. Later slab notes outside this theme report no count when the mirror carries the light copy's charge, as on static slabs (#10063, #10065); the sources do not reconcile this with #10022. What failed, in supplied models:
- Cube tastes are two doublets with no protecting symmetry (#10016, #10044); the cell slab wall holds at most two opposite-handed copies (#10059).
- Translation-invariant terms give family-diagonal mixing (#9922). Inversion-closed zoom charges give at most two spurion distances (#9958). The family copy form fits charged-lepton Koide Q but gives the same Q in every sector and one hierarchy step; quarks exclude it (#9960).
- Family-blind rules keep U(3), so a unique term gives no family label (#10071); no vacuum tested gives three hierarchical families with a T-odd phase (#10075).
- A kept family-fixing time-reversing antiunitary forces J = 0 (#10068). A turn-charged order parameter gives J and theta_bar together unless a Nelson-Barr block is supplied (#10047).

Conditional CP results, for supplied Yukawas or coefficients. With real coefficients, supplied heavy copies and singlet vacua, zoom-3 characters keep theta_bar real at renormalisable order with J nonzero; dimension five leaks, and cell-scale copies get no protection (#9948). Open: a family label, a hierarchy mechanism, a CP source that does not also give order-one theta_bar, symmetric mirror gapping dynamics. Logged Higgs, neutrino and weak-scale routes need supplied content, or fail against measured mixing or couplings (#10062, #10082).
