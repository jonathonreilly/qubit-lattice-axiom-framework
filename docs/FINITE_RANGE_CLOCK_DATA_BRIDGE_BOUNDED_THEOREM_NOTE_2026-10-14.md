## 2. Full-qubit mediator penalty and its exact inverse sector

Assume M>=1; if M=0 the program in (2) is already clock-local and no mediator is needed. Introduce M disjoint paths of N auxiliary qubits. Their labels are (alpha,j), with j=0,...,N-1. Write q_(alpha,j)=|1><1|, X_(alpha,j)=sigma+ + sigma-, and fix r=1/4. Define the dimensionless full-qubit operator

    L = sum_(alpha,j) q_(alpha,j)
        - r sum_(alpha,j<N-1)
             (sigma+_(alpha,j+1) sigma-_(alpha,j) + h.c.). (5)

This is a positive, gapped operator on the entire 2^(MN)-dimensional auxiliary Hilbert space, not just on one excitation. Indeed, for either endpoint degree d_j=1 or interior degree d_j=2 (degree zero when N=1),

    L = sum_(alpha,j) (1-r d_j) q_(alpha,j)
        + r sum_edges [q_u+q_v-(sigma+_u sigma-_v+h.c.)].  (6)

Each bracket has eigenvalues 0,0,2,2 and is positive. Thus, writing N_A=sum q,

    (1-2r) N_A <= L <= (1+2r) N_A,
    ker L = span{|Omega>},   Omega=|0>^(tensor MN),
    L|_(Omega perpendicular) >= mu I,   mu=1/2,
    ||L|| <= (3/2) MN.                                   (7)

The upper inequality also follows from -q_u-q_v<=hopping<=q_u+q_v. It is valid in every excitation sector. The actual full-space kernel and gap are therefore proved, not inferred from a one-particle matrix.

L preserves auxiliary excitation number. In the one-excitation sector of each separate path its matrix is

    D_N = I - r A_path,       G = D_N^(-1).               (8)

G is real symmetric, positive definite, with ||G||<=2 and G_jj<=2. Its entries connecting the left endpoint to j are strictly positive. The Neumann series sum_(k>=0) r^k A_path^k has nonnegative entries and contains the unique shortest walk, so

    G_(0j) >= r^j = 4^(-j).                              (9)

D_N has rational entries, so G can be obtained exactly by finite rational elimination; its positivity and the lower bound avoid any zero test or spectral-gap oracle. Although only this one-excitation inverse enters the leading compression, the dynamical proof uses the full inverse of L on the entire orthogonal complement in (7).

## 3. Couplings and the charged counterterms

Choose a design strength g>0, with units of the square root of energy in hbar=1 conventions. This is a freely supplied apparatus parameter; it does not rescale H. Define real coefficients

    f_(alpha j) = -v_(alpha j) / [2 g G_(0j)],
    S = M g^2 G_(00),
    s_j = sum_alpha f_(alpha j)^2 G_(jj).                (10)

The minus sign is important. Let

    V = sum_alpha g P_alpha tensor X_(alpha,0)
        + sum_(alpha,j) f_(alpha j) n_j tensor X_(alpha,j),
    A = H + K_C + sum_j [v_(0j)+s_j] n_j + S I,
    H_med(Delta) = Delta L + sqrt(Delta) V + A,
    Delta>0.                                            (11)

All factors omitted from a term are identities. These formulas specify actual full-qubit Hermitian operators. The on-site clock counterterms s_j and the scalar S are implemented and priced; they are not discarded as irrelevant phases. The same H, including its full scalar, is present unchanged in A. The apparatus is static throughout the original physical duration T.

Every term in (11) preserves the original clock number N_C=sum_j n_j. Restrict the logical clock only to the invariant one-excitation space C1. Data and passive references are unrestricted. With J_0 denoting the isometry that appends the bare auxiliary vacuum and Q=I-J_0 J_0*, set

    C = J_0* V Q (L|_Q)^(-1) Q V J_0.                   (12)

A single V creates one auxiliary excitation. The inverse in (12) stays in that sector. Different paths are orthogonal, so there is no second-order response between alpha and beta when alpha!=beta, even if P_alpha and P_beta do not commute. Within a path, the complete result on the full clock space is

    C = S I + sum_j s_j n_j
        + 2g sum_(alpha,j) f_(alpha j) G_(0j) n_j P_alpha
        + 2 sum_alpha sum_(j<k)
             f_(alpha j) f_(alpha k) G_(jk) n_j n_k.     (13)

Here P_alpha^2=I, n_j^2=n_j, and clock operators commute with all data Paulis. The last line is an actual cross-response. It is not zero on general clock inputs. On C1, however, n_j n_k=0 for j!=k, so (10) gives the exact identity

    A - C = H + K_C + sum_j n_j V_j = H_tar on C1.       (14)

Thus the unwanted scalar and on-site self-energies are exactly compensated, including their phases. No data operator proportional to H is added or cancelled. There is no assertion that higher-order cross-path interactions vanish: V can create multiple excitations in one or several paths and can apply noncommuting P_alpha. The next section bounds all of them on the full auxiliary space.

## 4. A finite-time bare-vacuum lemma, proved by a two-correction residual

Here is an elementary general lemma. Let a finite auxiliary L have unique vacuum, L>=mu Q for mu>0, and let A=A_logical tensor I_aux be Hermitian with ||A_logical||<=a0 on an invariant logical space. Let V be Hermitian with ||V||<=v there and J_0* V J_0=0. Define C by (12) and H_eff=A_logical-C. No commutation between A_logical and the logical factors of V is assumed. For H_Delta=Delta L+sqrt(Delta)V+A, Delta>0, the following estimate holds uniformly for 0<=t<=T:

    || exp(-it H_Delta) J_0 - J_0 exp(-it H_eff) ||
       <= E_med := A1/sqrt(Delta) + A2/Delta,             (15)

where the explicitly sufficient constants are

    A1 = 2v/mu + T[2a0 v/mu + 2v^3/mu^2],
    A2 = 2v^2/mu^2 + T[2a0 v^2/mu^2 + v^4/mu^3].        (16)

Proof. Extend the inverse of L by zero on its vacuum and put

    X = Q L^(-1) Q V J_0,
    Y = Q L^(-1) Q V X,
    C = J_0* V X,
    Z_Delta = J_0 - Delta^(-1/2) X + Delta^(-1) Y.        (17)

Then ||X||<=v/mu, ||Y||<=v^2/mu^2 and ||C||<=v^2/mu. Direct multiplication, with no series truncation, gives

    H_Delta Z_Delta - Z_Delta H_eff
      = Delta^(-1/2)[X H_eff - A X + V Y]
        + Delta^(-1)[A Y - Y H_eff].                    (18)

The identities L X=Q V J_0 and L Y=Q V X cancel the leading off-vacuum terms. The remaining vacuum piece is -J_0 C. Equation (18) includes Q V Q; it does not erase transitions between nonvacuum sectors. Since ||H_eff||<=a0+v^2/mu, the first bracket is bounded by 2a0 v/mu+2v^3/mu^2 and the second by 2a0 v^2/mu^2+v^4/mu^3.

Apply finite-dimensional unitary Duhamel to F(t)=Z_Delta exp(-itH_eff). Its initial value differs from J_0 by at most v/(mu sqrt(Delta))+v^2/(mu^2 Delta); the same amount separates F(t) from J_0 exp(-itH_eff) at the final time. These two endpoint allowances, plus T times the residual bound, prove (15)-(16). Z_Delta need not be an isometry. There is no assumed preparation of a dressed spectral subspace, no assumed gap for the full interacting H_Delta, and no removal of initial dressing error. The estimate is valid for every Delta>0, although useful small error needs a large Delta. All auxiliary excitation sectors and arbitrary noncommuting data factors were retained. QED.

For a desired e>0, a sufficient effective choice is

    Delta >= max{ (2 A1/e)^2, 2 A2/e }.                  (19)

The constants must be computed independently of Delta. Then E_med<=e. The entire elapsed T, including recurrence waits, enters (16); no gate-count time is substituted.

## 7. Scalars, physical generator energy and initialization

    mean(H_med) = Tr rho A,
    Var(H_med) = Var_rho(A) + Delta Tr rho Q0,             (29)

    Q0 = sum_alpha [g^2 I + sum_j f_(alpha j)^2 n_j
                         + 2g f_(alpha,0) n_0 P_alpha].  (30)

Proof: L annihilates the initial vacuum; V has zero vacuum expectation; the mixed expectations with A or L vanish. In V^2 only flips at the same auxiliary site return to the vacuum, yielding (30).
