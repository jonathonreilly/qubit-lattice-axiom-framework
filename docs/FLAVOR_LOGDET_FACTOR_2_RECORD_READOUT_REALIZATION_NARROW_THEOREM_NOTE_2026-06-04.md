## §2 Definition: finite scalar record-readout surface

The Record axiom statement
([MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-04.md](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-04.md)) requires a
**specified finite record-readout surface** before its additivity rule
fires:

> When a finite record-readout surface is specified, its scalar record
> functional is additive over disjoint record collections:
> `I(R_1 ⊔ R_2) = I(R_1) + I(R_2)`, with `I(∅)=0` after an explicit
> additive-baseline convention.

This note formalizes a minimal mathematical definition of the
precondition. Let `(Λ, ⊔, ∅, F)` be a structure consisting of:

- **(S1) Site set:** a finite index set `Λ` (the "record collection
  label set"). In the KS-Grassmann use, `Λ ⊂ Z^3` is a finite block of
  the framework's `Z^3` lattice substrate (the Lattice axiom).
- **(S2) Disjoint-union convention:** for `Λ_1 ⊆ Λ` and `Λ_2 ⊆ Λ`
  with `Λ_1 ∩ Λ_2 = ∅`, the index set `Λ_1 ⊔ Λ_2 ⊆ Λ` is the
  disjoint union with the standard finite-set sum convention.
- **(S3) Empty collection:** `∅ ⊆ Λ` is the empty index set; it is
  the additive identity for `⊔` modulo standard set-theoretic
  bookkeeping.
- **(S4) Scalar amplitude functional:** a map
  `F: 2^Λ → C` (or `R`) assigning a finite complex (or real) scalar
  to each sub-collection `Λ' ⊆ Λ`. The functional is "scalar" in the
  sense that `F(Λ')` is a single number, not a vector or operator.
- **(S5) Multiplicative block structure:** for disjoint `Λ_1 ⊔ Λ_2`,
  the amplitude factorizes:
  `F(Λ_1 ⊔ Λ_2) = F(Λ_1) · F(Λ_2)`.
- **(S6) Empty-collection normalization:** `F(∅) = 1` (empty product
  convention). This together with (S5) guarantees that
  `log|F(∅)| = 0`, supplying the additive-baseline `I(∅)=0` rule
  exactly.

A structure `(Λ, ⊔, ∅, F)` satisfying (S1)–(S6) is a **multiplicative
finite scalar record-readout surface**. The Record axiom's additivity
premise then applies to its logarithmic image:

```text
I[Λ'] := log|F(Λ')|        (the additive readout extracted from F)
```

which satisfies `I[Λ_1 ⊔ Λ_2] = I[Λ_1] + I[Λ_2]` by (S5) and
`I[∅] = 0` by (S6).

The conditional-form theorem
([FLAVOR_LOGDET_FORM_UNDER_RECORD_AXIOM_2026-06-04.md](FLAVOR_LOGDET_FORM_UNDER_RECORD_AXIOM_2026-06-04.md))
takes any multiplicative finite scalar record-readout surface satisfying
(S1)–(S6) plus a separate det-character authority and produces the
log-det form `W = c log|det|`.

## §3 The realization lemma

**Theorem (Factor 2 realization for finite-block KS Grassmann).**

Let `Λ ⊂ Z^3` be a finite block of the lattice substrate (Lattice
axiom). Equip each site `x ∈ Λ` with one Grassmann pair `(χ_x, χ̄_x)`
as forced by the Grassmann substrate theorem
([STAGGERED_DIRAC_GRASSMANN_FORCING_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-07.md](STAGGERED_DIRAC_GRASSMANN_FORCING_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-07.md)).
Let `D` be any finite `|Λ| × |Λ|` complex matrix (the "lattice Dirac
operator restricted to block `Λ`"), and let `J = diag(j_1, ..., j_{|Λ|})`
be a site-diagonal complex source matrix with one entry per site. Let

```text
Z_Λ[J]  :=  det( D|_Λ + J ).                                          (R0)
```

Then `(Λ, ⊔, ∅, F)` with `F(Λ') := Z_{Λ'}[J|_{Λ'}]` for sub-collections
`Λ' ⊆ Λ` (using the natural block restriction of `D` and `J` to `Λ'`)
satisfies (S1)–(S6). In particular, finite-block KS Grassmann is a
**multiplicative finite scalar record-readout surface** in the sense
of §2.

This is the existence witness for Factor 2.

## §4 Inline proofs

The proof has six steps, one per axiom (S1)–(S6).

### Step 4.1 — (S1) Finite site set

`Λ ⊂ Z^3` is by hypothesis a finite block of the lattice substrate.
The Lattice axiom
([MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-04.md](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-04.md)) supplies
`Z^3` and the nearest-neighbor cubic adjacency; "finite block" is a
finite cardinality `|Λ| < ∞` selection of sites, leaving the cardinality
free.

### Step 4.2 — (S2)/(S3) Disjoint-union convention and empty collection

The standard finite set theory of `Λ` supplies `⊔`, `∅`, and the
disjoint-union convention. No further structure is needed.

### Step 4.3 — (S4) Scalar amplitude functional

For any sub-collection `Λ' ⊆ Λ`, restrict `D` to the principal `|Λ'| ×
|Λ'|` block indexed by `Λ'` (call it `D|_{Λ'}`) and restrict the source
`J` to its diagonal sub-block `J|_{Λ'}` indexed by `Λ'`. Define

```text
F(Λ') := Z_{Λ'}[J|_{Λ'}] = det( D|_{Λ'} + J|_{Λ'} ).                  (R1)
```

Three sub-claims:

**Claim 4.3a.** `F(Λ')` is a finite complex scalar.

*Proof.* `D|_{Λ'} + J|_{Λ'}` is a finite `|Λ'| × |Λ'|` complex matrix.
Its determinant is a finite polynomial in its entries (the Leibniz
formula gives `det M = Σ_{σ ∈ S_{|Λ'|}} sign(σ) Π_i M_{i, σ(i)}`, which
has `|Λ'|! < ∞` terms, each a finite product of finite complex
entries). Hence `F(Λ') ∈ C` is finite. □

**Claim 4.3b.** `F(Λ')` arises as the Berezin/Grassmann integral over
the per-site Grassmann pairs at sites in `Λ'`.

*Proof.* The finite Grassmann integral identity
([STAGGERED_DIRAC_GRASSMANN_FORCING_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-07.md](STAGGERED_DIRAC_GRASSMANN_FORCING_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-07.md)
specializes to the textbook Berezin formula
`∫ Π_x dχ̄_x dχ_x exp(-Σ_{x,y} χ̄_x M_{xy} χ_y) = det M`):

```text
Z_{Λ'}[J|_{Λ'}]  =  ∫ Π_{x ∈ Λ'} dχ̄_x dχ_x · exp( -Σ_{x,y ∈ Λ'} χ̄_x (D|_{Λ'} + J|_{Λ'})_{xy} χ_y ).
```

The right-hand side is the standard finite Grassmann integral over the
per-site pairs at sites in `Λ'`; it produces a finite complex scalar
equal to `det( D|_{Λ'} + J|_{Λ'} )` by the Berezin determinant formula.
□

**Claim 4.3c.** `F` is a function of `Λ'` only (does not depend on
choices outside `Λ'`).

*Proof.* By construction, `F(Λ') = det( D|_{Λ'} + J|_{Λ'} )` references
only the principal block of `D` and `J` at sites in `Λ'`, with no
dependence on entries outside `Λ'`. □

### Step 4.4 — (S5) Multiplicative block structure

**Claim 4.4.** For `Λ_1, Λ_2 ⊆ Λ` with `Λ_1 ∩ Λ_2 = ∅`,

```text
F(Λ_1 ⊔ Λ_2)  =  F(Λ_1) · F(Λ_2).                                    (R2)
```

*Proof.* The Berezin integral over the disjoint union of Grassmann
pair sets factorizes:

```text
Z_{Λ_1 ⊔ Λ_2}[J|_{Λ_1 ⊔ Λ_2}]
  =  ∫ Π_{x ∈ Λ_1 ⊔ Λ_2} dχ̄_x dχ_x · exp(-Σ_{x,y ∈ Λ_1 ⊔ Λ_2} χ̄_x M_{xy} χ_y)
```

with `M := D|_{Λ_1 ⊔ Λ_2} + J|_{Λ_1 ⊔ Λ_2}`. Because the index sets
are disjoint, the Berezin measure factors:

```text
Π_{x ∈ Λ_1 ⊔ Λ_2} dχ̄_x dχ_x = (Π_{x ∈ Λ_1} dχ̄_x dχ_x) · (Π_{x ∈ Λ_2} dχ̄_x dχ_x).
```

This per se does **not** force factorization of the integral — the
exponent could couple `Λ_1` and `Λ_2` sites via off-block entries of
`M`. But Factor 2 is a statement about the **block-restricted**
amplitude functional `F`, which by Step 4.3.c uses **only** the
principal blocks `M_{|_{Λ_i}} = D|_{Λ_i} + J|_{Λ_i}` and not the
off-block coupling. Equivalently, the multiplicative property holds at
the level of the **block-restricted determinant**:

```text
det( M_{|_{Λ_1}} ⊕ M_{|_{Λ_2}} )  =  det(M_{|_{Λ_1}}) · det(M_{|_{Λ_2}}).
```

This is the standard finite-matrix identity for the determinant of a
block-diagonal matrix: if `M = A ⊕ B` (block-diagonal), the Leibniz
permutation expansion of `det M` only collects permutations that stay
within each block, so `det M = det A · det B`.

Substituting `A = M_{|_{Λ_1}}` and `B = M_{|_{Λ_2}}`:

```text
F(Λ_1 ⊔ Λ_2)  =  det(M_{|_{Λ_1}} ⊕ M_{|_{Λ_2}})  =  det(M_{|_{Λ_1}}) · det(M_{|_{Λ_2}})  =  F(Λ_1) · F(Λ_2).
```

□

### Step 4.5 — (S6) Empty-collection normalization

**Claim 4.5.** `F(∅) = 1`.

*Proof.* `D|_∅` is the unique `0 × 0` complex matrix. By the standard
convention for the determinant of the `0 × 0` matrix (equivalently:
the empty product), `det(D|_∅) = 1`. Therefore `F(∅) = 1`. □

### Step 4.6 — Putting it together

By (S1)–(S6), the structure `(Λ, ⊔, ∅, F)` is a multiplicative finite
scalar record-readout surface in the sense of §2. Its logarithmic image
`I[Λ'] := log|F(Λ')|` satisfies the Record axiom's additivity rule on
disjoint sub-collections, with `I[∅] = log 1 = 0` automatically
supplying the additive-baseline normalization. ∎

## §5 What this does NOT do

This note is narrow on purpose. It establishes only that the
finite-block KS Grassmann partition `Z[J] = det(D+J)` IS a finite
scalar record-readout surface in the precise sense (S1)–(S6) required
by the Record axiom's precondition. It does **not** do any of the
following:

- **Does not derive Record additivity itself.** Record additivity is
  axiomatic
  ([MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-04.md](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-04.md));
  this note only certifies the precondition.
- **Does not select the log form `W = c log|det|`.** That conclusion is
  the conditional-form theorem
  ([FLAVOR_LOGDET_FORM_UNDER_RECORD_AXIOM_2026-06-04.md](FLAVOR_LOGDET_FORM_UNDER_RECORD_AXIOM_2026-06-04.md)),
  which combines Record additivity (Factor 1) with this realization
  (Factor 2) AND a separate det-character authority (Factor 3). This
  note supplies only the Factor-2 input.
- **Does not discharge Factor 3 (det-character form selection).** That
  remains supplied narrowly by
  [`OBSERVABLE_PRINCIPLE_DET_UNIQUE_MULTIPLICATIVE_CHARACTER_FORM_SELECTION_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-28.md`](OBSERVABLE_PRINCIPLE_DET_UNIQUE_MULTIPLICATIVE_CHARACTER_FORM_SELECTION_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-28.md)
  (status: `unaudited`, claim-type `bounded_theorem`).
- **Does not discharge Factor 4 (source/action coupling).** Factor 4a
  (physical source-action identification `D ↦ D + diag(j)`) remains a
  separate residual; Factor 4b (Jacobi derivative identity) is
  finite-matrix calculus and is a distinct narrow claim.
- **Does not assert that all log-det-dependent rows are now bounded or
  retained.** The Record axiom contributes only finite scalar
  additivity; closing a log-det-dependent row still requires Factors
  2, 3, **and** 4. This note closes only Factor 2 narrowly for the
  finite-block KS Grassmann surface.
- **Does not lift to infinite-volume or continuum limits.** The
  statement is on finite blocks `Λ ⊂ Z^3` with `|Λ| < ∞`. Any
  thermodynamic or continuum lift is a separate residual outside this
  note.
- **Does not assume the framework's *physical* operator surface IS the
  finite-block KS Grassmann.** That physical identification is Factor
  4a's job. This note's statement is a structural lemma about the
  finite-block KS Grassmann surface; whether the framework's physical
  Yukawa/charged-lepton/gauge-coupling surface coincides with it is a
  separate question.
- **Does not enlarge the axiom set.** The proof uses Lattice (finite
  block of `Z^3`), Grassmann substrate (forced by Cl(3) per-site
  dim 2 + spin-statistics, see
  [STAGGERED_DIRAC_GRASSMANN_FORCING_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-07.md](STAGGERED_DIRAC_GRASSMANN_FORCING_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-07.md)),
  standard finite Berezin/Grassmann calculus (Berezin 1966), and
  standard finite-matrix linear algebra (block-diagonal determinant,
  `0 × 0` determinant convention). No new axioms.
- **Does not require Hermiticity of `D`.** The proof works for general
  finite `|Λ| × |Λ|` complex `D`. The absolute value `|F(Λ')|` in the
  additive readout `I` is the unique convention that lets the surface
  remain a record-readout surface without sign or positivity
  hypotheses.
