# Formation unit: block-chain equality, product-fill visibility, and the glued-unit hole dissolution

**Date:** 2026-09-22
**Type:** bounded_theorem
**Campaign block:** formation-unit, wave C of the TOE derivation campaign by
underdetermination witnesses (design note 2026-09-13). The block compares
specified sequential and joint formation constructions on the same finite
site sets, states every joint law rather than inferring it from the word
joint, and exhibits exactly where the choice of unit reaches the records.

## Result up front

1. **A joint law may equal a sequential law, exactly.** The cube's two
   site-plus-all-neighbour units U0 = {000, 100, 010, 001} and its antipodal
   partner partition the window and are covariant (the antipodal map swaps
   them, the diagonal C3 preserves each). Under the BLOCK CHAIN joint law —
   the forming unit draws its pattern from the pair measure mu conditioned
   on all formed sites, unformed sites marginalised — the finished law is
   exactly mu in both unit orders, on the cube and on the 2x3 window, just
   as every single-site order gives mu. For chain content the formation
   unit is invisible: the design note's caution is realised as a theorem.

2. **Product-fill joints are visible for the local rule.** Under PRODUCT
   FILL — each site of the forming unit draws from its conditional given
   the formed exterior alone, independently within the unit — the
   first-formed unit's three internal edges agree at exactly 1/2 each (the
   correlation a sequential process would build is erased), and the
   finished laws separate: the edge-agreement expectation is
   893200967/113400000 (sequential, about 7.877), 69/10 (product fill),
   1201/150 (grouped chain), against 128/15 for the static measure. The
   grouped chain — a fixed internal order conditioning on everything formed
   — is itself a sequential process, so the witness content is the product
   fill against every sequential construction.

3. **On the constrained window the unit is a dial that the hole mass
   reads, and the completed records cannot see it.** For the plane
   hard-support rule, with the joint law stated as uniform on the unit
   patterns consistent with every check the unit's formation completes:
   the aligned two-unit partition (the two top-corner checks, then the
   rest) and the whole-window unit complete with probability 1; single-site
   units under the uniform race complete with probability 5/8 (the
   support-rule and clock-and-rate value); the misaligned partition that
   leaves the shared link to form last and alone completes with probability
   exactly 1/2. In every case the completed-record law is the uniform law
   on the Gauss set. Formation units, like clocks, change how often full
   records exist and never what they say.

4. **Decision points recorded, nothing adopted.** (a) the single-site
   unit; (b) a covariant-set unit with product fill; (c) a covariant-set
   unit with block-chain fill, which chain content cannot distinguish from
   (a); (d) support-aligned glued units, which dissolve the hole channel.
   Together with the clock-and-rate block: for order-blind content both
   the clock and the unit are read exclusively by hole masses; a
   formation-model decision would pick a point on that dial, and it does
   not by itself amend the Admissibility sentence.

## Theorem 1 — Block-chain equality: the unit chain content cannot see

The covariant unit pair (U0, U1) partitions the cube, the antipodal map
swaps the units and the diagonal C3 preserves each. Under the block-chain
joint law the finished law equals mu exactly in both unit orders, on the
cube (256 atoms) and on the 2x3 window with the site-plus-neighbours unit
(64 atoms); the single-site chain rule gives mu under the uniform race
and under explicit orders. The chain rule over blocks is the chain rule:
for order-blind chain content the formation unit is invisible in finished
records.

## Theorem 2 — Product-fill visibility for the local rule

Under product fill the first-formed unit's three internal cube edges
agree at exactly 1/2 each: the intra-unit correlation that any sequential
process builds through conditioning is erased by simultaneous independent
draws. The finished laws are normalised, flip-covariant, and the two
product-fill unit orders are antipodal images of one another. Sequential
(uniform race), product fill (uniform over the two unit orders) and the
grouped chain give three distinct laws, separated by the edge-agreement
expectation 893200967/113400000, 69/10, 1201/150 against the static
measure's 128/15; none equals mu. The grouped chain is sequential by
construction, so the genuinely joint witness is product fill.

## Theorem 3 — The unit is a dial the hole mass reads

For the plane hard-support rule under the stated uniform-consistent
joint law: the aligned two-unit partition completes with probability 1 in
both unit orders; the whole window as one glued unit completes with
probability 1; single-site units under the uniform race complete with
probability 5/8, reproducing the support-rule note's value by the
absorbed-coin subset programme; the misaligned partition in which the
shared link forms last and alone completes with probability exactly 1/2,
because that unit completes two checks with one variable and is
over-determined on half the histories. In every listed case the
completed-record law is the uniform law on the Gauss set (exact 32
atoms), so the partition tunes 1 > 5/8 > 1/2 in completion mass while
conditional record content never moves. Units aligned with the glued
structure dissolve the hole channel; misalignment concentrates it.

## Falsifiers

- A block-chain unit order whose finished law differs from mu on any atom
  falsifies Theorem 1.
- Equality of the sequential, product-fill and grouped finished laws, or
  a first-formed-unit internal edge agreeing at other than 1/2 under
  product fill, falsifies Theorem 2.
- A listed partition whose completion mass differs from 1, 5/8, 1/2 as
  stated, or whose completed-record law is not uniform on the Gauss set,
  falsifies Theorem 3.
- A consistent-pattern count of zero for any unit of the aligned
  partition on any history falsifies the dissolution claim.
