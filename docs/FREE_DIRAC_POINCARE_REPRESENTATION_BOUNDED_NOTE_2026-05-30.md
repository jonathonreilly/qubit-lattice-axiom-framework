## 3. The Lorentz subalgebra: spin-1/2 generators and the boost-boost relation

The spin-1/2 carrier of the Lorentz algebra is built from the Minkowski Dirac
matrices `gamma^mu` (the mostly-minus continuation of the Euclidean `gamma` of the
mode-algebra fragment: `gamma^0 = gamma_4^E`, `gamma^k = i gamma_k^E`, giving
`{gamma^mu, gamma^nu} = 2 eta^{mu nu}`, `eta = diag(+,-,-,-)`). The generators are

```text
    Sigma^{mu nu} = (i/4) [gamma^mu, gamma^nu],
    J^i = (1/2) eps^{ijk} Sigma^{jk},     K^i = Sigma^{0i}.
```

These satisfy the Lorentz algebra `so(3,1)` exactly:

```text
    [J^i, J^j] = i eps^{ijk} J^k,
    [J^i, K^j] = i eps^{ijk} K^k,
    [K^i, K^j] = -i eps^{ijk} J^k.
```

The third relation is the **crux**: the boost-boost commutator is `-i eps J`
(non-compact `so(3,1)`), not `+i eps J` (compact `so(4)`). In the spin-1/2 rep
`J^i` is Hermitian and `K^i` is anti-Hermitian (the non-compact boost), as
required for a finite-dimensional non-unitary rep of the Lorentz group whose
exponentials act on spinors.

**Runner `P1`** verifies all three Lorentz brackets and the Hermiticity structure
on the explicit `4x4` spinor matrices, to `1e-9`. **Runner `P3` (non-triviality)**
verifies that the genuine boosts do **not** satisfy the Euclidean SO(4) sign
`[K^i, K^j] = +i eps J^k` -- so the `-i eps J` relation is the real Lorentz
content (the SO(4) -> SO(3,1) Wick-rotation distinction), not a tautology.

## 4. The full Poincare algebra: explicit closure of all ten generators

The ten generators (the four translations `P^mu = (H, P^i)` and the six Lorentz
generators `J^i, K^i`) close the Poincare algebra `iso(3,1)`. The mixed brackets
that make the rep genuinely Poincare (beyond the Lorentz subalgebra of Section 3)
are

```text
    [H, P^i] = 0,                  (energy commutes with momentum)
    [H, J^i] = 0,                  (energy is rotation-invariant)
    [H, K^i] = i P^i,              (boost of the Hamiltonian is momentum)
    [P^i, K^j] = i delta^{ij} H,   (boost of momentum is energy)
    [P^i, P^j] = 0,                (translations commute)
    [J^i, P^j] = i eps^{ijk} P^k.  (momentum is a 3-vector)
```

These are verified two independent ways:

- **Exact-matrix realization (runner `P2`).** The ten generators are realized in
  the `5x5` defining (vector + translation) representation acting on the affine
  coordinate `(x^0, x^1, x^2, x^3, 1)`. The Lorentz block uses the textbook vector
  generators `(M_{rho sigma})^mu{}_nu = i(delta^mu_rho eta_{sigma nu} - delta^mu_sigma
  eta_{rho nu})`; the translations are the standard nilpotent shifts fixed so that
  `P_mu` transforms as a four-vector. All ten brackets close to `1e-9`. The
  **non-triviality controls** (runner `P3`) confirm that a wrong-sign boost
  `K -> -K` breaks `[H,K^i]=iP^i`, and the Euclidean `[K,K]=+i eps J` sign fails --
  so the closure is a real statement about the Lorentz/Poincare structure.
- **One-particle mass-shell realization (runner `P4`).** On the positive-energy
  mass shell, the orbital generators

```text
    H psi = E(p) psi,    P^i psi = p^i psi,
    J^i psi = -i eps^{ijk} p^j d/dp^k psi,    K^i psi = -i E(p) d/dp^i psi,
```

  satisfy all four boost-containing brackets `[H,K^i]=iP^i`, `[P^i,K^j]=i
  delta^{ij} H`, `[J^i,K^j]=i eps^{ijk} K^k`, and the boost-boost
  `[K^i,K^j]=-i eps^{ijk} J^k`. The runner evaluates these **analytically** -- the
  generators are applied in closed form to a Gaussian test family using exact
  derivative identities (no finite-difference), so the brackets close to machine
  precision (`~2e-16`). The spin term `Sigma^i, W^i(p)` adds the same Lorentz
  subalgebra of Section 3 on the spin index; on the **full** spin-tensored space
  `J^i = J^i_orbital + Sigma^i` and `K^i = K^i_orbital + W^i` is the standard
  Foldy-Wouthuysen / Wigner construction, whose Lorentz subalgebra is Section 3 and
  whose mixed brackets are the orbital ones of `P4` (the spin term commutes with
  `H = E(p)` and `P^i = p^i` and contributes only to the Lorentz subalgebra). A
  **non-triviality control** inside `P4` confirms the wrong-sign orbital boost
  `+i E d/dp^i` misses `[H,K^i]=iP^i` by `O(p)`.

The two realizations are independent (one finite-dimensional non-unitary on
coordinates, one infinite-dimensional unitary on `L^2(H_m^+) tensor C^2`) and both
close the **same** algebra, including the load-bearing boost-boost relation.

## 5. Positive energy, mass shell, Wigner rotation, invariant measure

### 5.1 Positive-energy spectrum (rung C, made concrete) -- runner `P7`

The Minkowski Dirac Hamiltonian `H_D = gamma^0(gamma^k p_k + m)` has spectrum
`{+E, +E, -E, -E}`, `E(p) = sqrt(|p|^2 + m^2)`. After the CAR relabeling of the
`-E` branch into positive antiparticle energies (mode-algebra fragment), the
many-body Hamiltonian `H = sum_{p,s} E(p)(adag a + bdag b)` is bounded below by `0`
with vacuum energy `0`; every single-particle energy satisfies `E >= m > 0` and the
four-momentum `(E, p)` lies in the closed forward light cone (`E^2 - |p|^2 = m^2 >
0`, `E > 0`). This is rung C (`Hhat = -(1/a) log That >= 0`) made concrete with the
explicit positive single-particle energies. Runner `P7` verifies the spectrum to
`1e-9`, the forward-cone membership, and the bounded-below many-body spectrum
(exhaustive over `2^4 x 2^4` particle/antiparticle occupations: ground energy `0`,
all energies `>= 0`).

### 5.2 The boost preserves the mass shell; the Wigner rotation -- runner `P5`

Under a finite Lorentz boost `Lambda(w, n_hat)`, a positive-energy mass-shell point
`(E, p)` maps to another positive-energy mass-shell point (`E'^2 - |p'|^2 = m^2`,
`E' > 0`): the boost flow integrates `H_m^+` into itself. The induced little-group
element

```text
    W(Lambda, p) = L(Lambda p)^{-1} Lambda L(p)
```

(with `L(p)` the standard boost from rest `(m, 0)` to `(E, p)`) fixes the rest
momentum `(m, 0, 0, 0)` and is therefore a pure spatial rotation `block-diag(1, R)`
with `R in SO(3)` -- the **Wigner rotation** of a massive particle. Its spin-1/2
carrier is the corresponding `SU(2)` element `U = cos(theta/2) - i sin(theta/2)
n_hat . sigma`, which is unitary and whose adjoint action `U sigma^a Udag`
reproduces the `SO(3)` rotation `R`. Runner `P5` verifies mass-shell invariance to
`1e-8`, that `W` fixes the rest vector and is an `SO(3)` rotation (`R Rtranspose =
1`, `det R = +1`) to `1e-7`, and the `SU(2)` unitarity + adjoint-to-`SO(3)`
correspondence to `1e-9`.

### 5.3 The Lorentz-invariant measure is boost-preserved -- runner `P6`

The boost vector field on the mass shell in direction `i` is `V^j(p) = E(p)
delta^{ij}` (the velocity `dp^j/dw` of a mass-shell point under an infinitesimal
boost). A measure `rho(p) d^3p` is invariant under the flow of `V` iff
`div(rho V) = 0`. For the Lorentz-invariant measure `rho = 1/(2E)` and the boost
field, `sum_j d/dp^j((1/(2E)) E delta^{ij}) = d/dp^i(1/2) = 0` -- the invariant
measure is preserved (one-particle unitarity of the boost). Runner `P6` verifies
`div(rho V) = 0` to `1e-6`, with the **non-triviality control** that the *flat*
measure `rho = 1` is **not** boost-invariant (`div ~ 0.8`), so the invariance is a
real property of the `d^3p/(2E)` measure, not of any measure.

### 5.4 Boosted 2-point = SO(4) -> SO(3,1) continuation (rung A) -- runner `P8`

The Euclidean SO(4) denominator `p^2 + m^2` of rung A Wick-continues (`p_4 = i
p_0`) to the Minkowski mass shell `-p_0^2 + |p|^2 + m^2 = 0`, the Lorentz orbit. The
Minkowski Dirac 2-point numerator `pslash + m` transforms as a bispinor under a
boost: with the spinor rep `Lambda = exp(-i w n_hat . K)` (`K` from Section 3) and
the vector rep `L`,

```text
    S(L p) = Lambda S(p) Lambda^{-1},
```

equivalently `Lambda gamma^mu Lambda^{-1} = (L^{-1})^mu{}_nu gamma^nu` (the spinor
boost intertwines `gamma` as a four-vector). This is the SO(3,1) covariance
continued from rung A's continuum SO(4) covariance -- the standard Wick rotation,
cited as such, **not** an emergent-Lorentz claim. Runner `P8` verifies the
mass-shell continuation to `1e-9` and the bispinor covariance + the
`gamma`-intertwining to `1e-7` over random finite boosts.

## 7. Statistics-blindness of the Poincare-rep construction

The construction of the Poincare **representation** -- the one-particle mass shell,
the ten generators, the algebra closure, the Wigner rotation, the invariant measure
-- uses only the single-particle Dirac structure and the Lorentz/Poincare Lie
algebra. None of `P1`-`P8` invokes the anticommutator. Building the **many-body**
generators (Section 1) uses the CAR Fock space, but that is a downstream choice:
the same one-particle Poincare rep can be second-quantized on a CAR (fermionic) or
CCR (bosonic) Fock space, and the Poincare algebra closes identically on both. The
construction of the Poincare representation therefore does **not** itself select
statistics. Statistics selection is the separate LEVEL-1 spin-statistics mechanism
(named in plain text in the companion reconstruction note as gap G3); it is **not**
performed here. This is the precise sense in which this note is statistics-blind in
premise.
