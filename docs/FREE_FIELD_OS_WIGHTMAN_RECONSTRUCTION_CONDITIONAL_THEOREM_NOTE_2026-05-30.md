## 3. Sub-piece 1, the hard step: OS2 reflection positivity of the free Dirac Gaussian (DERIVED)

This is the step the rejected assembly (#2258) only *asserted* (it "inherited OS2
conditionally" from rung B without deriving RP of the Gaussian measure with
covariance `S`). Here it is derived directly.

### 3.1 The exact mixed (Euclidean-time, spatial-momentum) kernel

Fourier-transforming `S(p)` in the Euclidean-time component `p_4` at fixed spatial
momentum `k` (residue calculus on the poles `p_4 = +-iE`, `E = sqrt(|k|^2 + m^2)`,
using `int dp4/(2pi) e^{i p4 tau}/(p4^2 + E^2) = (1/2E) e^{-E|tau|}` and
`int dp4/(2pi) e^{i p4 tau} p4/(p4^2 + E^2) = (i/2) sgn(tau) e^{-E|tau|}`) gives
the closed form

```text
    S(tau, k) = (m 1 - i gamma.k_spatial) (1/2E) e^{-E|tau|}
                + (-i gamma_4) (i/2) sgn(tau) e^{-E|tau|}.
```

(Runner constructs this kernel and uses it throughout.)

### 3.2 The fermionic OS reflection-positivity condition for `S`

For the free fermionic Gaussian (Berezin) measure with covariance `S`, reflection
positivity in the Euclidean-time reflection `theta : tau -> -tau` reduces, by the
standard fermionic OS criterion (Osterwalder-Schrader 1973; Osterwalder-Seiler
1978; Montvay-Munster Sec. 4), to the positive-semidefiniteness of the Gram form
built from the reflected covariance restricted to positive-time test spinors. The
Euclidean-time reflection on Dirac spinors is implemented by the matrix
`R = gamma_4`. At fixed spatial momentum `k`, the reflected pair separation in
Euclidean time of two positive-time points `tau_i, tau_j > 0` is `tau_i + tau_j`,
and the OS Gram matrix is the `4n x 4n` block matrix

```text
    M_{(i,a),(j,b)} = [ gamma_4 . S(tau_i + tau_j, k) ]_{ab},   tau_i, tau_j > 0.
```

**Claim (OS2 positivity).** `M` is positive semidefinite.

This is the genuine reflection-positivity inequality of the free Dirac Gaussian
with covariance `S`. It is a finite, explicit matrix-positivity statement about the
closed-form kernel of Section 3.1.

### 3.3 Why it holds (mechanism) and the numerical certificate

The mechanism is the standard one: substituting Section 3.1,

```text
    gamma_4 S(tau_i + tau_j, k)
        = gamma_4 (m 1 - i gamma.k) (1/2E) e^{-E(tau_i + tau_j)}
          + (1/2) e^{-E(tau_i + tau_j)} 1,
```

(using `gamma_4 (-i gamma_4) (i/2) sgn(tau_i + tau_j) = (1/2) 1` for `tau_i + tau_j > 0`).
Because `e^{-E(tau_i + tau_j)} = e^{-E tau_i} e^{-E tau_j}`, every block factorizes
through the single-time vectors `e^{-E tau_i}`, so `M` is the Gram matrix of the
vectors `v_i = e^{-E tau_i} w` with the *fixed*, `tau`-independent, Hermitian
positive-semidefinite spinor weight
`W = gamma_4 (m 1 - i gamma.k)(1/2E) + (1/2) 1`. The exponential-separation
factorization is exactly the positivity-preserving structure (a Gram matrix of an
outer product of a positive-definite weight with the positive rank-1 kernel
`e^{-E tau_i} e^{-E tau_j}`). Positivity of `W` is the on-shell statement that
`2E W = gamma_4(m 1 - i gamma.k) + E 1` is positive semidefinite with eigenvalues
`{2E, 2E, 0, 0}`: it is `2E` times the rank-2 positive on-shell energy projector
`P_+ = (gamma_4(m 1 - i gamma.k)/E + 1)/2` (`P_+^2 = P_+`, `P_+ >= 0`), so
`W = P_+ >= 0`. This is the spinor-space content of
`sign(ubar u) = -sign(vbar v)` and the positive Hilbert norms `udag u = vdag v = 2E`
of the mode-algebra fragment.

**Numerical certificate (runner `R3`).** Over a 300-config random sweep
(`m in [0.2, 3.0]`, random spatial `k`, `2..5` positive Euclidean times), the
correct-reflection Gram (`R = gamma_4`) has worst minimum eigenvalue
`-7.1e-16` (machine zero, i.e. PSD). **Non-triviality is verified**: the SAME Gram
with the *wrong* reflection (`R = 1`, no spinor reflection) is strongly indefinite
(worst minimum eigenvalue `-0.65`). So OS2 positivity is a real theorem about the
kernel together with the correct fermionic reflection, **not** a tautology that
holds for any reflection.

## 6. Sub-piece 3: the Gaussian n-point / Wightman hierarchy (ESTABLISHED)

For a Gaussian (free) theory, **all** n-point functions are determined from the
2-point by the Gaussian recursion. This is the most routine piece and is
established cleanly and exactly (no Monte Carlo):

- **Bosonic (Isserlis).** The symmetric 2n-point is the unsigned sum over all
  perfect matchings of the 2-point, satisfying the Wick recursion
  `<x_{i0} ... > = sum_j C[i0, j] <rest \ {i0, j}>`.
- **Grassmann-Gaussian (Pfaffian).** The antisymmetric 2n-point is the **Pfaffian**
  of the antisymmetric 2-point matrix, satisfying the Pfaffian Laplace recursion
  `Pf(A) = sum_j (-1)^{j-1} A[i0, j] Pf(rest)`, with the fermionic identity

```text
    Pfaffian(A)^2 = det(A).
```

(`Pf^2 = det` to `1e-6`; both recursions to `1e-8..1e-9`; exact, deterministic).
The hierarchy is thereby fixed by the 2-point for *either* statistics -- which is
exactly why the **2-point alone is statistics-blind** and the statistics enters
only as the choice of recursion (symmetric Isserlis vs antisymmetric Pfaffian). The
note does not here select which recursion the framework's free sector obeys; that
selection is the spin-statistics question handled (conditionally, on a given field)
