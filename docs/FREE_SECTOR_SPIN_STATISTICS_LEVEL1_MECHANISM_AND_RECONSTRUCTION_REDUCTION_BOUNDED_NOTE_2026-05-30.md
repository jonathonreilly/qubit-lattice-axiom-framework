## 1. The two claims

*Let `psi` be a relativistic spin-1/2 field on Minkowski space whose
single-particle structure is the Dirac mode structure `{+E,+E,-E,-E}`,
`E(p) = sqrt(p^2 + m^2)`, with particle (`u`) and antiparticle (`v`) sectors,
positive-definite one-particle norms `udag u = vdag v = 2E`, and the
convention-independent relative sign `sign(ubar u) = - sign(vbar v)`. Then:*

1. *the commuting (Bose) quantization of `psi` has a Hamiltonian that is
   **unbounded below** (occupation of the `-E` modes is uncapped: `H = -E n -> -infinity`),
   equivalently it carries a negative-norm / ghost sector, and its spacelike
   field commutator does **not** vanish (**microcausality fails**); while*
2. *the CAR quantization is **healthy**: the `-E` modes are capped at occupation 1
   and relabeled to positive antiparticle energies `+E` (Hamiltonian bounded below,
   vacuum energy 0), and the spacelike field anticommutator vanishes
   (**microcausal**), governed by the Pauli-Jordan function.*

*Hence, for any field satisfying the hypothesis, CAR is selected and the
commuting quantization is excluded.*

T1 is a statement about a **given** field. It discharges the energy/microcausality
half of the spin-statistics connection for the free Dirac mode structure. It does
**not** assert that the framework supplies such a field.

## 2. The statistics-blind starting object (non-circularity anchor)

The free staggered Euclidean 2-point in momentum space is

```text
    S(p) = (m - i gamma.p) / (p^2 + m^2) = M^{-1}(p),   M(p) = m + i gamma.p,
```

with `{gamma_mu, gamma_nu} = 2 delta_{mu nu}` (Euclidean). This kernel is
**statistics-blind**: it is the inverse of the quadratic form `M`, and the same
matrix `M^{-1}` is the 2-point of *both* the Grassmann-Gaussian and the
bosonic-Gaussian theory built on the same quadratic action. The covariance does
not encode the field statistics. This is the non-circularity anchor: nothing in
the starting object presupposes CAR.

The scalar spectral function (continuum-scoped, from the SO(4) note) is
`Delta(p) = m^2 + (1/a^2) Sum_mu sin^2(p_mu a) -> m^2 + p^2` as `a -> 0`, with the
four tastes carried as a 4-fold spectral **multiplicity** (not a finite-`a`
taste-flat operator).

These are the established bounded facts (on main as the mode-algebra note),
re-verified here as the **hypothesis inputs** of T1. They are properties of the
kernel and of Dirac spinor algebra; they are **not** the reconstruction `R`.

- **`+-E` mode structure.** The scalar denominator `p4^2 + E^2` has poles at
  `p4 = +-iE`; continuing `p4 -> i p0` places the mass shell at `p0 = +-E`. In
  Minkowski signature the Dirac Hamiltonian `H_D = G^0 (G^k p_k + m)` has
  eigenvalues exactly `{+E,+E,-E,-E}`, two particle (`u`) and two antiparticle

  convention-independent `sign(ubar u) = - sign(vbar v)`; with positive-definite
  Fock norms `udag u = vdag v = 2E`.

## 4. T1 in detail: the LEVEL-1 energy/microcausality mechanism

Given the mode structure of Section 3, the two quantizations differ as follows.

**Energy.** Reorganizing the `-E` solutions:

```text
    H = Sum_{p,s} E(p) ( adag_{p,s} a_{p,s} + bdag_{p,s} b_{p,s} ),
```

`a` annihilates particles, `b` antiparticles. Under **CAR** (occupation in
`{0,1}`) `H >= 0` with vacuum energy 0 -- bounded below. Under **Bose** the raw
`-E` modes admit unbounded occupation, `H = -E n -> -infinity` -- a Dirac-sea
instability, unbounded below.

**Microcausality.** The field (anti)commutator is governed by the Pauli-Jordan
function `Delta(x)`, Lorentz invariant, odd under `x -> -x`, vanishing for
spacelike `x`. The convention-independent on-shell projector identity is the
discriminator:

```text
    (p_slash + m) + G^0 (p_slash - m) G^0 = 2E G^0     (CAR: canonical {psi,psidag}=delta^3, microcausal)
    (p_slash + m) - G^0 (p_slash - m) G^0 = 2(m - p_k G^k) != 2E G^0   (Bose: non-canonical, spacelike commutator nonzero)
```

using `Sum u ubar = p_slash + m`, `Sum v vbar = p_slash - m`, and
`sign(ubar u) = - sign(vbar v)`. CAR is microcausal; the commuting spin-1/2 field
is not.

Together: any field satisfying the Section 3 hypothesis is healthy under CAR and
pathological under Bose -- T1.

   established here** (this is the "Wightman hierarchy" that the #2258 review-loop

Established fragment of `R` (on main, mode-algebra note): the statistics-blind
