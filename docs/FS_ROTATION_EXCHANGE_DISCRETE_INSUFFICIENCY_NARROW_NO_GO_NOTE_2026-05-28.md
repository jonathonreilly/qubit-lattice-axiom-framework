## Supplied premises and exact claim

Take two ordinary tensor factors V=C², with Pauli matrices and the supplied spinor action U(θ)=exp(iθσ₃/2). This is a representation assumption, not a derivation that a microscopic qubit is physical spin. On V⊗V, the on-site 2π rotations both equal −I₄. They generate only the scalar algebra. They therefore neither produce the nonscalar factor swap nor select CAR generators over commuting bare ladders.

This finite counterexample is the entire negative claim. It does not prove a framework-wide absence of a spin-statistics derivation, that lattice systems cannot realize fermions, or that all exchange-rotation constructions fail. Hard-core ladders are not canonical bosonic CCR operators on-site: finite-dimensional matrices cannot satisfy [a,a†]=I. Here “hard-core boson” refers to on-site nilpotency and commuting distinct-site ladders.

## Proof and countermodels

1. U(2π)=−I₂ and U(4π)=I₂. Hence U(2π)⊗I=I⊗U(2π)=−I₄. Their unital complex *-algebra is C I₄.
2. The tensor swap P|i,j⟩=|j,i⟩ has eigenvalue +1 on a three-dimensional symmetric sector and −1 on the one-dimensional antisymmetric sector. Therefore P is not in C I₄. This concerns only the two 2π elements, not the algebra generated by arbitrary local operators or arbitrary rotations.
3. Let l=(σ₁+iσ₂)/2. The bare ladders a₀=l⊗I and a₁=I⊗l are nilpotent and commute, while {a₀,a₁}=2l⊗l≠0. They coexist with exactly the same on-site spinor sign. Thus that sign alone does not force cross-site CAR.
4. Instead choose c₀=l⊗I and c₁=σ₃⊗l. Their cross anticommutators, including adjoints, vanish; {cᵢ,cᵢ†}=I. This Jordan–Wigner choice supplies different generator localization. Each generator set and its adjoints generate M₄(C): one recovers the local Pauli matrices from occupation projectors and the ladders. Equality of the ungraded abstract matrix algebra does not identify the two physical locality assignments.

The tensor swap here is a kinematic factor permutation. Identification with an adiabatic exchange of physical particles, its allowed configuration paths, and a statistics sector would require additional data. No such identification follows from these four matrix statements.

A parity grading or parity superselection rule by itself is **not equivalent** to graded locality. For example Π=σ₃⊗σ₃ anticommutes with both bare ladders a₀,a₁, and states may be restricted to commute with Π, while [a₀,a₁]=0 and {a₀,a₁}≠0 still hold. A cross-site CAR or graded-locality premise would be additional structure, not a consequence of merely naming parity.
