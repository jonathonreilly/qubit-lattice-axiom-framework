## 1. Claim scope

The route under review grants a discrete spinor `2pi = -1` sign: a `2pi`
spatial rotation on a single matter site multiplies its state by `-1`, as in the
binary octahedral group `2O subset SU(2)` (the `SU(2)` double-cover of the
octahedral `O subset SO(3)`), where the central `2pi` element is `-I` and only
`4pi` returns to `+I`. The companion
`BINARY_OCTAHEDRAL_DISCRETE_SPINOR_SIGN_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-28.md` is
the proposed authority for that ingredient; this no-go does not depend on that
sibling being audited clean. It proves the route still does not force CAR even
when the sign is granted.

The naive closure of fermionic statistics (FS) from this sign is the
**rotation-exchange route**: "a `2pi` rotation gives `-1`, and by the
spin-statistics / Finkelstein-Rubinstein equivalence exchange equals a `2pi`
rotation, so exchange gives `-1`, hence the matter is fermionic." This note
proves that on the one-qubit operator algebra over the `Z^3` spatial substrate
this route does **not** close,
for a precise structural reason, and that the alternative bosonic reading (the
hard-core boson) survives every premise the route uses.

Let `V ~= C^2` be the one-qubit site Hilbert space from
[`MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-05-20.md`](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-05-20.md), and consider two
distinct sites with `H = V (x) V ~= C^4`. The runner verifies the following
exact finite-tensor-product facts (`PASS=30 FAIL=0`).

- **(F1) The `2pi = -1` sign is on-site only, decoupled from exchange.** The
  on-site `2pi` rotation is `U_2pi = exp(2 pi i S_3) = -I_2` with
  `S_3 = sigma_3/2` (and `U_4pi = +I_2`, the double-cover signature). Lifted to
  two sites, `U_2pi (x) I = I (x) U_2pi = -I_4` — the **same global scalar**.
  Two-site **exchange** is the tensor swap `P` (the element of `S_2 subset U(4)`
  that permutes the factors), acting on a **different** space than the on-site
  `SU(2)`. The on-site sign carries no two-site-exchange information.

- **(F2) The rotation-exchange (Finkelstein-Rubinstein) bridge is intrinsically
  continuous.** It identifies exchange with a `2pi` rotation via a homotopy in a
  **continuous** configuration space: for `N` identical particles in `R^d` the
  relevant space is `C_N(R^d)/S_N`, with `pi_1 = S_N` in `d >= 3`, and the sign
  comes from `pi_1(SO(3)) = Z_2` (the rotation by `2pi` is the non-trivial
  loop). The **bare discrete site set does not provide that continuous
  configuration-space `pi_1`** — there are no continuous exchange paths in the
  retained substrate data. The continuum engine that manufactures the
  exchange-rotation equivalence is therefore not supplied by the current lattice
  surface. A lattice-native graph-braid/discrete-homotopy construction would be
  a different, open route (§7), not the continuum Finkelstein-Rubinstein bridge.

- **(F3) Every continuous-rotation route dies identically on the lattice.**
  Finkelstein-Rubinstein, the non-relativistic Jabs/Peshkin rotation argument,
  and the relativistic Streater-Wightman spin-statistics theorem all require a
  **continuous** `SO(3)`/Poincaré symmetry to manufacture the exchange sign. The
  `Z^3` spatial substrate breaks continuous spatial rotation to the discrete
  `2O subset SU(2)`, so each route loses its load-bearing continuous-symmetry
  input simultaneously.

- **(F4) The retained locality is UNGRADED.** The retained Lieb-Robinson
  tensor-locality narrow
  `lieb_robinson_equal_time_tensor_locality_narrow_theorem_note_2026-05-10`
  (`retained_bounded`) proves that operators supported on disjoint sites
  **commute**. That is **bosonic-type (ungraded) locality** — the *opposite* of
  the CAR signal. **Graded** locality (odd operators **anticommute** at
  spacelike/disjoint separation) is the actual FS selector, and graded locality
  is **not** in the retained set.

- **(F5) The hard-core boson shares every premise yet is bosonic.** The
  hard-core boson is spin-`1/2`, per-site dimension `2`, carries the same
  on-site `2O` `2pi = -1` sign, and is nilpotent on-site (`(sigma_+)^2 = 0`) —
  yet its bare ladders **commute** at separation (`[sigma_+ (x) I, I (x) sigma_+]
  = 0`), so it is **bosonic**. Therefore any "spin-`1/2` + `2pi = -1` =>
  fermion" inference **proves too much**: it would make the hard-core boson
  fermionic. The **only** difference between the two readings is the cross-site
  (anti)commutator = graded locality. (This is exactly the parent
  `retained_no_go` restated, now confirmed from five disciplines — see §2.)

**Conclusion (the no-go).** On the one-qubit operator algebra over the `Z^3`
spatial substrate, the spinor `2pi = -1` sign (granted as a necessary route
ingredient) is **not sufficient** to force CAR over CCR / hard-core-boson
statistics **via the rotation-exchange route**. The bridge from
the on-site rotation sign to a two-site exchange sign requires either

- (a) a continuous configuration-space `pi_1` argument (not supplied by the
  bare discrete site set), or
- (b) a graded-locality / fermion-parity-superselection input (not in the
  retained set).

FS is therefore an **admission candidate** on the discrete substrate, not a
derivation gap closable from the retained inputs **via this route**. The route
does **not**, however, foreclose FS absolutely: a lattice-native
discrete-homotopy (graph-braid) construction or a graded-locality principle
could close it as future work (see §7). It is "unsolved / needs-new-structure",
**not** "impossible".

## 5. Proof

(F1)–(F5) are exact statements on finite tensor products of `2x2` complex
matrices, each verified by the runner
(`audit_companion_fs_rotation_exchange_discrete_insufficiency_2026_05_28.py`,
`PASS=30 FAIL=0`):

- **(F1)** `U_2pi = exp(2 pi i sigma_3/2) = -I_2` (and `U_4pi = +I_2`). Both
  two-site lifts `U_2pi (x) I` and `I (x) U_2pi` equal the **same** scalar
  `-I_4`. The exchange operator `P` (tensor swap) satisfies `P^2 = I`, has
  spectrum `{+1 (mult 3), -1 (mult 1)}`, acts as `+1` on `|00>`, `|11>`,
  `|01>+|10>` and `-1` on `|01>-|10>`. The on-site `2pi` rotations act as `-1`
  on **both** the symmetric and the antisymmetric sectors, so they cannot
  separate the two; `P` is non-scalar and so cannot equal any product/combination
  of the on-site rotations. *(`U_2pi` lives in the on-site `SU(2)`; `P` lives in
  `S_2 subset U(4)` — different groups, different action.)*

- **(F2)** The unital `*`-algebra generated by `{U_2pi (x) I, I (x) U_2pi}` has
  complex dimension **1** (scalars only). The exchange operator `P` is **not** in
  it (it is non-scalar). Hence the on-site `2pi` rotation **cannot generate** the
  exchange sign by any algebraic operation. The standard route that would couple
  them — the Finkelstein-Rubinstein homotopy `exchange ~ 2pi rotation` — is built
  on `pi_1` of a continuous configuration space and requires `pi_1(SO(3)) = Z_2`;
  on a discrete site set `pi_1` is trivial, so no such homotopy exists. *(This is
  a structural/topological statement; the runner verifies the finite-dim
  algebraic shadow — the on-site algebra is scalar and cannot reach the
  non-scalar `P`.)*

- **(F3)** Each continuous-rotation route (Finkelstein-Rubinstein,
  non-relativistic Jabs/Peshkin `2pi`-rotation argument, relativistic
  Streater-Wightman) requires continuous `SO(3)`/Poincaré symmetry. The
  `Z^3` spatial substrate retains only `2O subset SU(2)`, breaking all three inputs
  at once. *(Literature-context statement; not load-bearing on a numeric value.)*

- **(F4)** Disjoint-site bare ladders **commute**:
  `[sigma_+ (x) I, I (x) sigma_+] = 0` (and similarly with `sigma_-`). This
  ungraded (commuting) cross-site relation is the bosonic signal and matches the
  retained Lieb-Robinson tensor-locality narrow. The CAR signal is the
  **opposite** (cross-site anticommutation), which is **graded** locality and is
  not retained.

- **(F5)** The hard-core boson is spin-`1/2`, per-site dim `2`
  (`(sigma_+)^2 = 0`), carries the same on-site `U_2pi = -I_2`, and its bare
  ladders commute at separation (bosonic). The Jordan-Wigner dressing
  `c_0 = sigma_+ (x) I`, `c_1 = sigma_3 (x) sigma_+` makes the **same** ladders
  satisfy cross-site CAR: `{c_0, c_1} = 0`, `{c_0, c_1^dag} = 0`,
  `{c_0, c_0^dag} = I_4`, `c_0^2 = c_1^2 = 0`. Both the hard-core-boson
  generators `{sigma_+ (x) I, sigma_3 (x) I, I (x) sigma_+, I (x) sigma_3}` and
  the JW-fermion generators `{c_0, c_1}` close to the **same** full matrix
  algebra `M_4(C)` (complex dim `16 = dim End(C^4)`). The fermion/boson
  distinction is therefore **not** in the ungraded algebra or the on-site sign —
  it is exactly the cross-site (anti)commutator = graded locality.

The chain is: F1+F2 (the on-site sign cannot produce exchange) + F3 (no
continuous route survives) + F4 (retained locality is the wrong, ungraded, sign)
+ F5 (a bosonic object passes every premise the route uses) ⇒ the rotation-
exchange route does not close FS from the retained inputs.
