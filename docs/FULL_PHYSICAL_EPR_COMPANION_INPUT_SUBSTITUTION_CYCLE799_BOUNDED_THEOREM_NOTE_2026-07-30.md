## Exact substitution theorem

Let `A` be the signed stabilizer group required by Cycle 789. Its generators
have the form

\[
  P_O P_I^*,
\]

where `P` is a Hermitian physical Pauli word representing one companion
even-CAR character. Let `B` be the stabilizer group of ordinary corresponding-
site physical EPR pairs. Per physical site, `B` is generated by
`X_O X_I` and `Z_O Z_I`.

For the four local Pauli letters,

\[
 I\mapsto I I,\qquad
 X\mapsto X X,\qquad
 Z\mapsto Z Z,\qquad
 Y\mapsto -Y Y=(X X)(Z Z).
\]

Therefore every signed paired word `P_O P_I^*` is a product of the local EPR
stabilizers, and

\[
  A\subseteq B.
\]

The runner checks all four signed local identities at zero residual and
exhausts all `4^6 = 4096` six-site Pauli words. The ordinary preparation
`H(O); CNOT(O,I)` has state residual exactly zero.

Now let `U_prefix` be the fixed landed Cycle-789/794 Bell/correction prefix.
Conjugation is a group homomorphism, so subgroup inclusion is monotone:

\[
  U_{prefix} A U_{prefix}^{\dagger}
  \subseteq
  U_{prefix} B U_{prefix}^{\dagger}.
\]

Every signed target character already proven to lie in the left group lies in
the right group. Later linear postcomposition by the landed factorwise `G`
preserves the same equality. The runner stresses the binary inclusion lemma
over 4,096 deterministic invertible maps and separately checks signed matrix
conjugation, with maximum residual
`4.9076946705118025e-15`.

This is substitution by a stronger resource, not a refit and not a new
