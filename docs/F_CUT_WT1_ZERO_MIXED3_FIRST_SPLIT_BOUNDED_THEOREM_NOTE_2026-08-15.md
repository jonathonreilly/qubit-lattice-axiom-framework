## Result Up Front

The 24 proper cube rotations act on neighbor 6-tuples in `{0,1}^6` and
partition those 64 cells into 10 orbits. Cube-covariant predicates are the
`{0,1}`-assignments to those orbits. The three displayed cuts

1. vanish on empty: `f(empty)=0`,
2. vanish on full: `f(full)=0`,
3. complement-even: `f(c)=f(1-c)`

leave five free bits, so

```text
|F_cut| = 32.
```

The five remaining bits, in the displayed order
`(wt1, opp2, adj2, vertex3, mixed3)`, are the values on the three
complement-pairs and two complement-fixed orbits after empty and full are
forced to `0`. The two maps scored here are the `wt1=0` 3-site maximizer
pair

```text
f0 = (0, 1, 1, 1, 0)
f1 = (0, 1, 1, 1, 1)
```

They differ only on the complement-fixed orbit `mixed3`.

On the two-cube `{0,1,2}×{0,1}×{0,1}`, each unordered set of at most three
vertices is a bounded seed. There are `1+12+66+220=299` such seeds. They
are searched in lex order: increasing cardinality, and inside each
cardinality the combinations of the site order generated by
`x ∈ {0,1,2}`, `y ∈ {0,1}`, `z ∈ {0,1}`. Off-patch neighbors have
occupancy `0`. Each tick, every unlocked on-patch vertex evaluates `f` on
its six-neighbor occupancy tuple and locks if `f=1`. The process is
synchronous and stops at a fixed point in at most 12 ticks. Fill means
`|locks_halt|=12`. Coverage is

```text
cov3(f) = |{ S : |S|=3 and f fills from S }|.
```

`f_L1(c)=1` if and only if some axis is unbalanced: `c_{+μ} ≠ c_{-μ}` for
at least one `μ ∈ {x,y,z}`. Equivalently, some discrete neighbor contrast
`n_μ = c_{+μ} − c_{-μ}` is nonzero. This is **not** Hamming parity
`|c|_1 mod 2`. The unbalanced-axis map has remaining-bit tuple
`(1, 0, 1, 1, 1)`, so remaining bit `wt1=1`. It is outside this pair.

Not leftover of the opp2=0 versus opp2=1 pair. That leftover compared
`(0, 0, 1, 1, 1)` with `(0, 1, 1, 1, 1)` and asked a different remaining
bit. The present object is the first `|S|≤3` fill disagreement of the
mixed3 pair inside the already displayed `wt1=0` maximizer class.

**Theorem 1.** Both maps are members of `F_cut` with remaining bit
`wt1=0`. Exhaustive evaluation on all 220 three-site seeds gives

```text
cov3(f0) = 44
cov3(f1) = 44
```

so both attain the subclass 3-site maximum. They fill exactly the same
44 three-site seeds. Neither fills any seed of size `0`, `1`, or `2`.

**Theorem 2.** Lex search of all 299 seeds with `|S|≤3` finds no seed on
which `f0` and `f1` disagree on fill. There is no lex-first `|S|≤3` seed
`S` at which the pair disagrees on fill. On this bound, mixed3 is free
for fill of the `wt1=0` 3-site maximizer pair.

**Theorem 3.** Because the first-split seed is empty, neither map is a
unique filler of a first `|S|≤3` disagreement. On every such seed the
two fill bits agree. That emptiness is displayed. Do not adopt mixed3.

Do not write the ranking or the mixed3 bit into Admissibility.

Write a neighbor configuration as `c ∈ {0,1}^6`. A proper cube rotation `R`
acts by `(R·c)(d) = c(R^{-1}d)` on the six face directions `d`. A map
`f:{0,1}^6 → {0,1}` is cube-covariant when `f(R·c)=f(c)` for every such `R`.
Equivalently, `f` is constant on each orbit.

The axis type of `c` is the triple `(u,b,e)` with `u+b+e=3`, where `u` is
the number of axes with `c_{+} ≠ c_{-}`, `b` the number with
`(c_{+},c_{-})=(1,1)`, and `e` the number with `(c_{+},c_{-})=(0,0)`. These
ten types are exactly the ten orbits. Complement sends `(u,b,e)` to
`(u,e,b)`.

A locked set `S` determines occupancies: a lattice neighbor in `S` has
occupancy `1`, and every other neighbor — including every off-patch
