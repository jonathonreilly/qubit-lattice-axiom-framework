eigenvalues of `M_KS` are pure-imaginary `±iσ` pairs.

**(3)** Hence for real `m > 0`,
`det(M_KS + m I) = Π_i (m² + σ_i²) > 0` for every link configuration (verified
exactly, and over several random `SU(3)` configs). Positivity of the fermion
determinant is exactly what yields reflection/OS positivity of the staggered
