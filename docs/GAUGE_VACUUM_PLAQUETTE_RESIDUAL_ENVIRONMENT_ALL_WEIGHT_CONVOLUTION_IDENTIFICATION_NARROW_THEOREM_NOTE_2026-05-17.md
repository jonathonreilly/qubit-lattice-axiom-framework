## Statement

Let `J = (chi_(1,0) + chi_(0,1)) / 6` be the marked-plaquette source
operator acting on the marked-plaquette class-function sector
`H = closure of span{chi_(p,q) : (p,q) in P_+(SU(3))}`, where
`P_+(SU(3)) = { (p,q) : p, q in Z_{>= 0} }` is the full set of dominant
`SU(3)` weights.

Suppose the three retained input identities are supplied as cited inputs:

```text
(I1)  Source-sector matrix factorization (I1):
      T_src(beta) = exp[(beta/2) J] D_beta exp[(beta/2) J],
      D_beta chi_(p,q) = kappa_(p,q)(beta) chi_(p,q)
      for all (p,q) in P_+(SU(3)),
      with kappa_(p,q)(beta) >= 0, kappa_(p,q)(beta) = kappa_(q,p)(beta),
      kappa_(0,0)(beta) > 0.

(I2)  Temporal-gauge linkwise factorization (I2):
      the one-step mixed Wilson kernel in temporal gauge U_t = 1
      factorizes as
      K_mixed(U_{tau+1}, U_tau) = prod_{(x,mu)} w_beta(U_{tau+1}(x,mu) U_tau(x,mu)^{-1}),
      and on the marked-plaquette class-function sector the four-marked-link
      compression of K_mixed acts as
      D_beta^loc chi_(p,q) = a_(p,q)(beta)^4 chi_(p,q)
      with a_(p,q)(beta) = c_(p,q)(beta) / (d_(p,q) c_(0,0)(beta)), and
      every non-marked spatial link factor acts on the marked class-function
      sector through the trivial irrep only, contributing the trivial-channel
      scalar c_(0,0)(beta) before normalization and 1 after normalization.

(I3)  SU(3) character diagonal-convolution equivalence (I3):
      for any abstract positive conjugation-symmetric coefficient sequence
      (rho_(p,q))_{(p,q) in P_+} with rho_(p,q) >= 0,
      rho_(p,q) = rho_(q,p), and rho_(0,0) = 1, the diagonal operator
      R[rho] : chi_(p,q) -> rho_(p,q) chi_(p,q) is equal as an endomorphism
      of the character basis to the normalized convolution operator
      C_{Z/Z_(0,0)} by the central class function
      Z(W) = sum_(p,q) d_(p,q) rho_(p,q) chi_(p,q)(W).
      In particular R = C_Z/Z_(0,0) at all weights, with no truncation.
```

Define the **residual source-sector operator** at the marked-plaquette
class-function level by stripping the marked half-slice multipliers and the
marked four-link mixed-kernel factor from the source-sector kernel:

```text
R_beta^env  :=  D_beta^loc^{-1} * (exp[-(beta/2) J] * T_src(beta) * exp[-(beta/2) J])
            =  D_beta^loc^{-1} * D_beta
            chi_(p,q) -> (kappa_(p,q)(beta) / a_(p,q)(beta)^4) chi_(p,q).      (D1)
```

By (I1), `D_beta` is central and diagonal on the character basis. By (I2),
`D_beta^loc` is central and diagonal on the character basis with strictly
positive eigenvalues `a_(p,q)(beta)^4 > 0` for all `beta > 0`. So `(D1)` is
well-defined at all weights.

Define the **unmarked spatial Wilson environment boundary class function**
on the marked plaquette holonomy `W in SU(3)` as the (unique up to
overall positive normalization) real central class function on `SU(3)`
whose normalized convolution operator on the marked-plaquette
class-function sector equals the residual operator `R_beta^env` of (D1).
By (I1)+(I2)+(I3), `Z_beta^env(W)` has character coefficients

```text
z_(p,q)^env(beta)  =  (kappa_(p,q)(beta) / a_(p,q)(beta)^4) * z_(0,0)^env(beta),  (D2)
```

with `z_(0,0)^env(beta) > 0` a positive normalization. The physical
reading is the boundary class function of the unmarked spatial Wilson
environment compression: it captures the result of integrating all
unmarked spatial DOFs (non-marked spatial links and unmarked
spatial-plaquette action in both adjacent slices) with the marked
plaquette holonomy held fixed at `W`, after the marked four-link
mixed-kernel factor (by (I2)) and the two marked half-slice multipliers
(by (I1)) are factored out. This physical reading is forced by (I1)+(I2):
the only contribution to `D_beta` not in `D_beta^loc` and not in the
marked half-slice multipliers is the unmarked-DOF integration.

**Conclusion (T1) (structural diagonality of `R_beta^env`).** The operator
`R_beta^env` on the marked-plaquette class-function sector is:

- diagonal in the orthonormal `SU(3)` character basis at all weights,
- self-adjoint,
- positive (each eigenvalue `kappa_(p,q)(beta) / a_(p,q)(beta)^4 >= 0`,
  strictly positive whenever `kappa_(p,q)(beta) > 0`),
- conjugation-symmetric: `[S, R_beta^env] = 0` where `S` is the swap
  involution `S chi_(p,q) = chi_(q,p)`.

**Conclusion (T2) (structural central-convolution representation of
`Z_beta^env`).** The central class function `Z_beta^env(W)` admits a
unique Peter-Weyl expansion at all weights

```text
Z_beta^env(W) = z_(0,0)^env(beta) sum_{(p,q) in P_+(SU(3))}
                d_(p,q) rho_(p,q)^env(beta) chi_(p,q)(W)                     (T2)
```

with `z_(0,0)^env(beta) > 0`, `rho_(0,0)^env(beta) = 1`,
`rho_(p,q)^env(beta) >= 0`, and `rho_(p,q)^env(beta) = rho_(q,p)^env(beta)`.

**Conclusion (T3) (per-weight structural identification at all weights).**
Under (I1), (I2), (I3), at every weight `(p, q) in P_+(SU(3))` the
eigenvalue of `R_beta^env` on `chi_(p,q)` equals the eigenvalue of the
normalized convolution operator `C_(Z_beta^env / z_(0,0)^env(beta))` on
the same basis element:

```text
R_beta^env chi_(p,q)
   =  (kappa_(p,q)(beta) / a_(p,q)(beta)^4) chi_(p,q)
   =  rho_(p,q)^env(beta) chi_(p,q)
   =  C_(Z_beta^env / z_(0,0)^env(beta)) chi_(p,q)                           (T3)
```

i.e. the two operators agree on every basis element of the
marked-plaquette class-function sector at all weights. This is the
**load-bearing per-weight structural identification at all weights**
between the stripped residual factor and the compressed unmarked spatial
Wilson environment. (No L^2-closure or convergence claim is made about
the operator equality on the full infinite-dimensional Hilbert space `H`;
the conclusion is the per-weight diagonal action.)

**Conclusion (T4) (uniqueness of the per-weight eigenvalue sequence).**
The per-weight sequence `(rho_(p,q)^env(beta))_{(p,q) in P_+(SU(3))}` is
uniquely determined by the per-weight diagonal action of `R_beta^env`
(equivalently, by the Peter-Weyl character coefficients of `Z_beta^env`);
no other per-weight diagonal action on the marked-plaquette
class-function sector satisfies `(T3)`.

## Proof

`(T1)`. From (I1), `D_beta` is central and diagonal with eigenvalues
`kappa_(p,q)(beta)`. From (I2), `D_beta^loc` is central and diagonal with
eigenvalues `a_(p,q)(beta)^4`. The product of central diagonal operators
is central diagonal; the quotient `D_beta^loc^{-1} D_beta` is well-defined
because `a_(p,q)(beta)^4 > 0` for `beta > 0` (the trivial irrep gives
`a_(0,0)(beta) = 1 > 0`, and for any nontrivial irrep `a_(p,q)(beta) > 0`
by the convolution-eigenvalue formula `(8)` of (I2) applied to a real
positive class function `w_beta`). Hence `R_beta^env` is diagonal in the
character basis at all weights with eigenvalues

```text
rho_(p,q)^env(beta)  =  kappa_(p,q)(beta) / a_(p,q)(beta)^4.                 (T1')
```

Self-adjointness follows from each eigenvalue being real. Positivity
follows from `kappa_(p,q)(beta) >= 0` (I1) and `a_(p,q)(beta)^4 > 0` (I2).
Conjugation symmetry follows because both `kappa_(p,q)(beta) =
kappa_(q,p)(beta)` (I1) and `a_(p,q)(beta) = a_(q,p)(beta)` (I2)
[the latter because `c_(p,q)(beta) = c_(q,p)(beta)` is the Schur character
orthogonality property of `(p,q) <-> (q,p)` complex-conjugate irreps], so
their ratio is conjugation-symmetric.

`(T2)`. By `(D2)`, `Z_beta^env(W)` has character coefficients
`z_(p,q)^env(beta) = (kappa_(p,q)(beta) / a_(p,q)(beta)^4) *
z_(0,0)^env(beta)`. The Peter-Weyl theorem on `SU(3)` gives the expansion
at all weights stated in `(T2)`. Normalizing by `z_(0,0)^env(beta) > 0`,

```text
rho_(p,q)^env(beta)  :=  z_(p,q)^env(beta) / z_(0,0)^env(beta)
                       =  kappa_(p,q)(beta) / a_(p,q)(beta)^4.              (T2.1)
```

The structural properties follow term by term from those of
`kappa_(p,q)(beta)` (I1) and `a_(p,q)(beta)` (I2):

- `rho_(0,0)^env(beta) = 1` by construction;
- `rho_(p,q)^env(beta) in R` because `kappa, a` are real;
- `rho_(p,q)^env(beta) >= 0` because `kappa >= 0` and `a > 0`;
- `rho_(p,q)^env(beta) = rho_(q,p)^env(beta)` because
  `kappa_(p,q) = kappa_(q,p)` and `a_(p,q) = a_(q,p)`.

`(T3)`. The conclusion is a per-weight algebraic identity. Fix any weight
`(p, q) in P_+(SU(3))`.

By `(T1)`, the eigenvalue of `R_beta^env` on `chi_(p,q)` is
`rho_(p,q)^env(beta) = kappa_(p,q)(beta) / a_(p,q)(beta)^4`.

By `(T2)`, the character coefficient of `Z_beta^env(W) / z_(0,0)^env(beta)`
at weight `(p, q)` is `z_(p,q)^env(beta) / z_(0,0)^env(beta) =
kappa_(p,q)(beta) / a_(p,q)(beta)^4` (by `(D2)`).

By (I3) applied to the finite truncation `B_N` containing `(p, q)`,
normalized convolution by `Z_beta^env(W) / z_(0,0)^env(beta)` acts on
`chi_(p,q)` diagonally with eigenvalue `kappa_(p,q)(beta) /
a_(p,q)(beta)^4`. (The retained narrow theorem (I3) states the equivalence
on any finite `B_N`; per-weight, applying it at any `N >= max(p, q)`
gives the diagonal-action identity for `chi_(p,q)`.)

Hence the eigenvalue of `R_beta^env` on `chi_(p,q)` and the eigenvalue of
`C_(Z_beta^env / z_(0,0)^env(beta))` on `chi_(p,q)` are both
`kappa_(p,q)(beta) / a_(p,q)(beta)^4`. Since this holds at every weight
`(p, q) in P_+(SU(3))`, the two operators agree on every basis element
of the marked-plaquette class-function sector at all weights, which is
`(T3)`. ∎

**Remark on the physical reading of `(T3)`.** The structural identification
`(T3)` is independent of any specific analytic value of `kappa_(p,q)(beta)`
or `a_(p,q)(beta)`. It identifies, at all weights, the operator
`R_beta^env` defined by stripping `exp[(beta/2) J]` and `D_beta^loc` from
`T_src(beta)` with central convolution by the central class function
`Z_beta^env(W) / z_(0,0)^env(beta)`. By the construction in `(D2)`,
`Z_beta^env` is the boundary class function of the unmarked spatial
Wilson environment compression: it represents the contribution to
`D_beta` from the unmarked-DOF integration after the marked four-link
mixed-kernel factor (by (I2)) and the marked half-slice multipliers (by
(I1)) are factored out. The audit-named "compression of the unmarked
spatial Wilson environment on the marked source sector" is therefore
exactly `C_(Z_beta^env / z_(0,0)^env(beta))`, and `(T3)` is the structural
all-weight equality the parent gate names as the open derivation step.

`(T4)`. The diagonal eigenvalues of `R_beta^env` on the orthonormal
character basis uniquely determine the per-weight action of the operator
(linear extension on orthonormal basis). The character coefficients of
`Z_beta^env` are uniquely determined by Peter-Weyl orthogonality at every
weight (Schur orthogonality identity (T1) of (I3)). So both per-weight
sequences are uniquely determined by their operators, and by `(T3)` they
coincide at every weight. ∎
