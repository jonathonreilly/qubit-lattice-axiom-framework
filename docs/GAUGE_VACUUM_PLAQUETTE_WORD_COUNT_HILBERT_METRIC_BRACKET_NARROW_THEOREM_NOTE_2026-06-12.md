## Reduced Word-Count Formula

Let `B_4 = {(p,q): 0 <= p,q <= 4}`. Let

```text
D_mu       := c_mu(6) / (d_mu c_0(6))
M          := N_f + N_fbar
G          := M^T diag(D^2) M
C_w(mu)    := D_mu^w / d_mu^(w-1)
K_w        := C_w^(1/2) (G entrywise^w) C_w^(1/2).
```

The matrix-element adjacent bond forces the middle word labels onto the
all-equal diagonal. Thus the full `25^w` Perron problem reduces to the
`25 x 25` symmetric Perron problem for `K_w`.

If `z_w` is the top normalized eigenvector of `K_w` and
`q_w = C_w^(1/2) z_w`, then for a positive boundary vector `eta` on the
unmarked word slots:

```text
L_mu(eta)  := sum_a D_a eta_a M_(a,mu)

R_i(w,eta) := D_i sum_mu q_w,mu M_(i,mu) L_mu(eta)^(w-1)
rho_i      := R_i / R_(0,0)
P_w        := source_perron_readout(rho).
```

For the trivial-slice boundary, `L_mu` survives only on the fundamental
pair and the normalized slice is word-count stationary, matching the
companion lemma. For the deep-rim positive boundary `eta_inf`, the same
formula produces the non-monotone first rungs and the later growth of
the fundamental boundary coordinate.

## Hilbert-Metric Attempt

The direct positivity-improving route would require a finite projective
diameter for the positive matrix that advances word count, so that a
Birkhoff contraction coefficient can be computed from
`theta_H = tanh(diam/4)`.

On the actual reduced word-count matrices:

```text
G zero entries: 330 of 625
ordinary matrix-support primitivity index: 4
K_w zero entries for w=1,2,3,4: 330 of 625
Hilbert diameter(K_w): infinite for w=1,2,3,4
```

The ordinary support graph becoming positive after four ordinary matrix
powers does not repair the word-count bracket, because `K_w` contains
entrywise powers of `G`, not ordinary powers of a fixed positive matrix.
Entrywise powers preserve the zero pattern.

This blocks the requested finite Birkhoff coefficient on the full cone.
A restricted-cone theorem might still be possible, but this note does
not supply the invariant cone and denominator propagation needed for
that upgrade.

## Why The Bracket Is Not Certified Here

The obstruction has two parts.

First, the Hilbert-metric contraction coefficient requested for the
inserted word factor is not finite on the full positive cone:
`K_w` has persistent zero entries. The packet therefore does not supply
a direct `tanh(diam/4)` contraction for word count.

Second, the readout is a ratio. The numerator is driven by the dominant
`(0,0)->(0,0)` entry of the reduced Perron problem, while the denominator
for the marked trivial channel is carried by the fundamental pair. The
candidate `theta = 1/gamma` captures the measured scale of that ratio,
but the theorem still needs a uniform positive lower bound for the
denominator, or an equivalent source-perturbation estimate after scaling
by `rho10`.

## Obstruction Map

The following named targets remain open for a certified finite-packet
word-count bracket:

- **O1: invariant positive cone.** Find a cone on which the relevant
  inserted-word map is positivity-improving and has finite Hilbert
  diameter, or replace the Hilbert route with a finite symmetric
  perturbation theorem that directly controls `K_w`.
- **O2: denominator propagation.** Prove a lower bound for
  `R_(0,0)(w,eta_inf)` relative to the fundamental channel asymptotics.
- **O3: source-ratio perturbation.** Bound the source Perron readout
  after scaling `rho_w` by its fundamental coordinate and comparing to
  the fundamental-pair support solve.
- **O4: packet-to-physical residuals.** The finite box, Bessel cutoff,
  physical 3D unmarked spatial Wilson environment, all-weight limit,
  `L_perp` limit, and analytic `P(6)` remain separate targets.

## Negative-Gate Discipline

This section is included because the deliverable has a negative
component. The result is deliberately demoted to a partial-attempt
obstruction map, not a broad claim that no bracket route exists.

**N1: alternative route enumeration.**

1. Direct Birkhoff coefficient on `K_w`: attempted; fails because
   `K_w` has 330 persistent zero entries.
2. Ordinary support-power positivity: attempted; the support graph is
   positive by the fourth ordinary power, but `K_w` uses entrywise
   powers, so this does not give the word-count contraction.
3. Entrywise dominant asymptotics: attempted; gives `P_inf`, `gamma`,
   `theta`, and the measured envelope, but not the denominator theorem.
4. Source-sector spectral perturbation after scaling by `rho10`:
   identified; requires a uniform residual-over-`rho10` bound not proved
   here.
5. Monotonicity: checked against the first rungs; `P_2 < P_1` and
   `P_3 > P_2`, so monotonicity is not available as stated.
6. Rim-depth Perron gap: available for `eta_k -> eta_inf`, but it
   controls boundary-depth convergence, not the fixed-boundary word-count
   tail.

**N2: wall-independence audit.** O1, O2, and O3 are distinct. O1 would
give a contraction on a suitable cone but would still need ratio
propagation unless the cone includes the readout denominator. O2 would
not by itself control the source Perron functional. O3 would not by
itself produce the reduced Perron asymptotics.

**N3: hidden-wall scan.** Phrases equivalent to "by construction" are
kept only for runner-defined finite matrices. The physical environment,
untruncated limits, and source-ratio perturbation are explicit residuals,
not silent assumptions.

**N4: residual matching.** The witness used here is the runner-direct
zero-entry check on `K_w`; it attacks only the full-cone Hilbert-diameter
route for the finite packet. It is not cited against restricted cones,
source perturbation, or physical-environment convergence.

**N5: rhetoric audit.** The negative phrase is scoped to the full-cone
Hilbert coefficient for the actual reduced word-count matrices. It is
not stated for all possible cones, all possible comparison methods, or
all future packet refinements.

**N6: partial-closure path scan.** A partial closure path is named:
prove O2 and O3, or replace O1 by a symmetric finite perturbation bound.
No new axiom language is used.

**N7: steelman.** A reviewer could close this route by proving that the
entrywise dominant asymptotics of `K_w` give a lower bound for the
fundamental-pair contribution to the denominator, then applying a
finite-dimensional source Perron perturbation bound after scaling by
`rho10`. The runner already computes the constants that such a proof
would likely use.

**N8: cross-cycle echo.** The shape matches earlier plaquette packet
boundaries where a finite positive matrix fact landed but the physical
environment identification remained separate. This note keeps the same
separation: finite packet facts are recorded; physical and untruncated
residuals are named.

Gate outcome: the broad negative is not shipped. The shipped claim is
