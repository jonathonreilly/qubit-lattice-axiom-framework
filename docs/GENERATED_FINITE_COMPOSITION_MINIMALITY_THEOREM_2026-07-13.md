## Question

Within the stated finite-dimensional complex C-star category, what condition turns one physical
`M_2(C)` domain at each site into the ordinary joint complex-qubit algebra,
without silently admitting extra global sectors?

## Theorem

Let `F` be a finite set of `n` sites. Let `B_F` be a unital finite-dimensional
complex `C*`-algebra with faithful unital embeddings

```text
iota_x : M_2(C) -> B_F,       x in F,
```

whose images commute for distinct sites. If `B_F` is generated as a
`C*`-algebra by the union of the local images, then

```text
B_F ~= tensor_{x in F} M_2(C) ~= M_{2^n}(C).
```

### Proof

Commutation gives a unital `*`-homomorphism

```text
Phi : tensor_{x in F} M_2(C) -> B_F,
Phi(tensor_x a_x) = product_x iota_x(a_x).
```

The domain is the simple matrix algebra `M_{2^n}(C)`. A unital homomorphism is
nonzero, so its kernel is a proper closed two-sided ideal and must be zero.
Thus `Phi` is injective. Generatedness says its image is all of `B_F`, so it is
surjective. Hence it is an isomorphism.

No multiplicity-one Hilbert-space representation assumption is needed for the
abstract physical algebra. If the physical carrier is additionally identified
with all of `B(H_F)`, irreducibility/scalar commutant is the corresponding
carrier statement.

## Why Generatedness Is Load-Bearing In This Category

Faithful commuting local copies determine a canonical generated subalgebra,
but they do not say that this subalgebra is the whole physical joint domain.
Two exact extensions preserve every local product:

```text
M_{2^n}(C) direct-sum M_{2^n}(C),
M_{2^n}(C) tensor M_k(C),       k > 1,
```

with each local copy embedded diagonally or tensored with the identity. The
first has a silent central sector; the second has an extra spectator factor.
Local products span only `4^n` complex dimensions in either case while the
declared physical algebra is larger.

Therefore, inside the finite-dimensional complex `C*`-algebra language,

```text
physical joint algebra = algebra generated by the local copies
```

is necessary and sufficient for the ordinary abstract tensor product. This is
a category-relative algebraic equivalence, not universal physical minimality
and not a proof that it must be a new axiom.

## Derivation/Retirement Routes

1. **Canonical-law domain:** define the exact algebra-valued microscopic law
   on the generated quasilocal inductive limit. This supplies generatedness in
   the law's domain; it does not derive it from a record graph.
2. **Operational extensionality:** identify globally different algebraic
   objects whenever every possible record experiment identifies them. This
   retires extra silent sectors only after completeness/separation of the
   record repertoire is proved.
3. **Irreducibility/duality theorem:** derive that the local images have no
   nontrivial physical commutant or that the observable net satisfies an
   appropriate generation/duality property.
4. **Direct Qubit content:** state generated finite composition explicitly if
   none of the first three routes closes.

A rule whose complete input/output is only a record-configuration successor
graph cannot distinguish the ordinary algebra from either extension above.
An algebra-valued rule may distinguish them and remains a live retirement
route.

## Candidate Category-Relative Semantic Condition

The unpolished category-relative condition for later language work is:

> Finite joint possibility domains are generated by mutually commuting copies
> of their one-site domains.

`copies` must mean faithful unital embeddings. `Generated` excludes both
silent direct-sum sectors and spectator factors. The sentence does not supply
states, effects, Born weights, entangled preparations, or operational local
tomography; those require the operational reconstruction interface.
