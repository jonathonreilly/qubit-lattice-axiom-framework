## Question

The staggered-Dirac realization gate's substep-4 ("AC_φλ" species labeling)
needs the three hw=1 generation sectors `V = span(X_1, X_2, X_3)` to carry
three *distinct* masses. Two retained rows bracket the question:

- [`S3_MASS_MATRIX_NO_GO_NOTE.md`](S3_MASS_MATRIX_NO_GO_NOTE.md) (`retained_no_go`):
  unbroken `S_3`-invariant Hermitian operators on `V` have at most **two**
  distinct eigenvalues — a forced degeneracy;
- [`Z2_HW1_MASS_MATRIX_PARAMETRIZATION_NOTE.md`](Z2_HW1_MASS_MATRIX_PARAMETRIZATION_NOTE.md)
  (`retained`): a single preserved `Z_2` (swap `X_1, X_2`; fix `X_3`) gives a
  five-real-parameter family that is generically **nondegenerate**.

What is the *complete* picture across the `S_3` subgroup lattice — i.e. exactly
which preserved symmetry forces the degeneracy, and what is the **minimal**
symmetry-breaking input substep-4 actually requires?

## Answer

**The forced generation degeneracy holds for the full `S_3` and for no proper
subgroup.** It is carried entirely by the 2-dimensional `S_3` irrep `E`, which
is irreducible under the full `S_3` and reducible under every proper subgroup.

| Preserved group `G ≤ S_3` | mass-matrix params (commutant dim) | generic distinct masses | forces degeneracy |
|---|---|---|---|
| full `S_3` | 2 | **2** | **yes** |
| `C_3[111]` (3-cycle) | 3 | 3 | no |
| single `Z_2` (transposition) | 5 | 3 | no |
| trivial | 9 | 3 | no |

So the minimal symmetry-breaking input the gate's substep-4 requires is
**exactly `S_3 → (any proper subgroup)`**; the framework's own `C_3[111]` is
necessary-and-sufficient to lift the degeneracy. This characterizes the
residual precisely; it does **not** derive the breaking, the mass values, or the
generation labeling.

## Theorem (minimal-breaking characterization)

Let `V = C^3` carry the permutation representation `ρ` of `S_3` (so
`V ≅ A_1 ⊕ E`, the trivial plus the 2-dimensional standard irrep). For
`G ≤ S_3`, let `Comm(G)` be the algebra of operators commuting with `ρ(G)`.

**(1) Parameter count.** `dim_C Comm(G) = (1/|G|) Σ_{g∈G} |Tr ρ(g)|²`, equal to
the real dimension of the `G`-invariant Hermitian operators (the mass-matrix
parameter count). This gives `2, 3, 5, 9` for `G = S_3, C_3, Z_2, {e}`
respectively.

**(2) Generic spectrum.** A generic `G`-invariant Hermitian operator has a
number of distinct eigenvalues equal to the total multiplicity of irreducible
isotypic components of `ρ|_G`:
- `S_3`: `A_1 ⊕ E` → multiplicities `1 + 1` → **2 distinct** (the `E`
  eigenvalue is doubled);
- `C_3`: `1 ⊕ ω ⊕ ω²` → `1 + 1 + 1` → 3 distinct;
- `Z_2`: `(triv)² ⊕ (sign)` → `2 + 1` → 3 distinct;
- trivial: one irrep, multiplicity 3 → 3 distinct.

**(3) Obstruction carrier.** `dim_C Comm(G) = Σ m_i²` over irrep multiplicities
`m_i`. The value `2 = 1² + 1²` (full `S_3`) is the unique case in which `E`
remains irreducible; under every proper subgroup `E` splits, raising the
commutant dimension above `2` and lifting the forced degeneracy.

**Conclusion.** Forced generation degeneracy on `V` ⇔ the preserved symmetry is
the full `S_3`. Three distinct masses require breaking `S_3` to a proper
subgroup, and `S_3 → C_3[111]` is necessary-and-sufficient. ∎
