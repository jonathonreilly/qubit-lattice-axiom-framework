## Setting

Let `Lambda` be a finite site set with `N = |Lambda|`. Use two time slices
`t in {0,1}` and Grassmann generators `chi_{t,x}, chibar_{t,x}`. Let `Theta`
be the link reflection that swaps the two time slices and exchanges `chi`
with `chibar`, acting antilinearly and product-reversingly. For a symmetric
positive-definite kernel `K`, take the two-slice quadratic action

```text
S = sum_x (chibar_{0x} chi_{0x} + chibar_{1x} chi_{1x})
    - sum_{x,y} K_{xy} chibar_{0x} chi_{1y}.
```

The positive-time algebra `A_+` is generated by the slice-1 variables. The OS
form is

```text
<F,G> = <Theta(F) G>_S
      = Top(Theta(F) G exp(-S)) / Top(exp(-S)).
```

The reconstructed Hilbert space is the GNS quotient `H = A_+ / N_OS`, where
`N_OS` is the null space of this form. The reconstructed field is
`psi_x = [chi_{1x} .]`, left multiplication pushed to the quotient when that
pushdown is null-space invariant.

## Statement

For the finite surfaces certified by the runner (`N = 2,3` exactly over
rationals, plus the `L_s = 4` staggered-kernel leg in floating arithmetic):

1. `Theta(S) = S`, the OS Gram form on `A_+` is positive semidefinite, and
   `dim H = 2^N`.
2. The `chibar_1` occupation monomials have positive-definite Gram matrix and
   give a concrete `2^N`-dimensional occupation model of the quotient.
3. The null space is invariant under left multiplication by every field
   generator `chi_{1x}`, so each `psi_x` is a well-defined reconstructed
   operator on `H`.
4. The Grassmann identity gives `{psi_x, psi_y} = 0` for all `x,y`.
5. The OS adjoint satisfies `{psi_x, psi_y^dag} = (K^{-1})_{xy} I`. Since
   `K^{-1}` is positive definite, a field-multiplet normalization `B psi`
   with `B K^{-1} B^T = I` gives exact CAR.
6. The integrand grading descends to a parity involution on `H` and matches
   the tensor `sigma_3` parity in the occupation identification.

Therefore the OS-reconstructed, canonically normalized fields satisfy

```text
{psi_x, psi_y} = 0,
{psi_x, psi_y^dag} = 0          for x != y,
{psi_x, psi_x^dag} = I,
```

relative to the reconstructed parity `F`. This is the `GL(F)` predicate used
by the substep-1 statistics-selection boundary.

## Load-Bearing Step

The exchange sign of the reconstructed operators is the exchange parity of the
integration variables. In the Grassmann branch, the anticommutator vanishes
before any action, reflection, quotient, or normalization is chosen. The
quotient preserves that identity because the field multiplications are
null-space invariant, and the OS adjoint closes the `dag` half through the
central covariance `(K^{-1})_{xy} I`.
