## 4. Proof

**Lemma 1 (exchange identity; runner [C], frame-free).** In the Grassmann
algebra, `χ_{1x} χ_{1y} + χ_{1y} χ_{1x} = 0` for all `x, y` — an identity of
the exterior algebra. Left-multiplication operators on `A_+` therefore
anticommute identically; any quotient by an invariant subspace preserves the
relation. This is where the cross-site sign comes from: the integration
variables, not a postulate. ∎

**Lemma 2 (well-definedness; runner [B]).** The OS form is PSD (exact
rational LDL certificate on the full `4^N`-monomial basis), of rank exactly
`2^N`; the occupation (χ̄-monomial) sub-basis has positive-definite Gram and
spans the quotient. The null space is invariant under left multiplication by
every **field** generator `χ_{1x}` (verified on every exact kernel vector),
so `ψ_x` is canonical on `H`. Honesty record: `χ̄_{1x}`-multiplication does
**not** preserve the null space; the creator `φ_x` is therefore defined on
the distinguished boundary creation subalgebra `Λ[χ̄_1]` (PD Gram, isomorphic
onto the quotient) — the standard Fock/holomorphic transfer picture. `GL(F)`
concerns `ψ_x` and its Hilbert adjoint, both canonical. ∎

**Lemma 3 (central covariance and exact CAR; runner [C]).**
`{ψ_x, φ_y} = δ_xy I` exactly (the on-site ladder structure emerges from the
equal-slice Wick contraction), and `{ψ_x, ψ_y†} = (K^{−1})_{xy} I` exactly —
central and PD. Choosing rational `K = (AᵀA)^{−1}` exhibits an exact rational
normalizer `B = (Aᵀ)^{−1}` with `B K^{−1} Bᵀ = I`; the rotated multiplet
satisfies exact CAR. A field-multiplet rotation cannot change exchange signs
(`{(Bψ)_x, (Bψ)_y} = Σ B_xa B_yb {ψ_a, ψ_b} = 0`), so the normalization
smuggles nothing. ∎

**Lemma 4 (grading; runner [C]).** The integrand grading `χ ↦ −χ` preserves
`S` (even) and the Berezin top (even total generator count), hence descends
to a unitary involution `F̂` on `H` with `{F̂, ψ̃_x} = 0`; its diagonal in the
occupation identification is `(−1)^{|Y|}`, i.e. exactly the retained
`F = ⊗σ₃` spectrum pattern. ∎

**Lemma 5 (RP and the staggered kernel; runner [C], [D]).** The construction
requires `Θ(S) = S` and `K > 0`: a `Θ`-asymmetric kernel destroys the Gram
symmetry, and an indefinite symmetric kernel produces a negative exact LDL
pivot — the RP input is load-bearing for the existence of `H`. The kernel
class contains the retained 2-step staggered transfer kernel: the
action-derived classical 2-step matrix `T_odd T_even` reproduces
`e^{−2E(p)}`, `E = arcsinh √(m² + sin²p)` (the retained RP note's
load-bearing object, recomputed), and the `L_s = 4` reconstruction with that
kernel satisfies the same exchange relations (tol `1e-9`). ∎

**Load-bearing step.** *The exchange sign of the reconstructed operators is
the exchange parity of the integration variables — transmitted through the
GNS quotient because `{χ_{1x}·, χ_{1y}·} = 0` is an algebra identity (Lemma
1) and the quotient is invariant (Lemma 2), with the `†`-half closed by the
central covariance (Lemma 3).* Rubric classes: (A) for the identity and the
exact relations, (C) for the first-principles finite reconstruction
computations; the falsification legs keep the step from being a renaming —
