## Claim

Let `Gamma` be the framework's `Z^3` lattice site graph (the infinite
cubic lattice, equivalently any cube of side `L >= 3`). At the
**first-quantized** level — two indistinguishable particles moving on
`Gamma` — the relevant configuration space is the Abrams discretized
unordered 2-particle space `UD_2(Gamma)`, and its graph-braid group is
`B_2(Gamma) = pi_1(UD_2(Gamma))`. This note records the following exact,
verifiable facts.

- **(C1) Torsion classification of `H_1`.** The abelianization of
  `B_2(Gamma)` is `H_1(UD_2(Gamma)) = Z^{beta_1} (+) Z_2`, with the
  **two-particle exchange (Y-)class** generating the `Z_2` torsion
  summand, **whenever `Gamma` is non-planar**. For a *planar* graph
  `H_1` is torsion-free. (Ko-Park: planar `<=>` `H_1(UD_2)` torsion-free;
  non-planar `=>` a `Z_2` torsion summand.)

- **(C2) `Z^3` is non-planar and 3-connected.** Every cube of side
  `L >= 3` of `Z^3` is non-planar (it contains an explicit `K_{3,3}`
  subdivision; the `2x2x2` box `Q_3` is planar, which is why `L >= 3` is
  required) and is 3-connected (node-connectivity `= 3`).

- **(C3) Anyons excluded; `{boson, fermion}` dichotomy.** Abelian
  exchange statistics is `Hom(H_1(UD_2(Gamma)), U(1))`. A `Z_2` torsion
  class `t` (with `2t = 0`) maps under any homomorphism `phi` to
  `phi(t) in U(1)` with `phi(t)^2 = 1`, i.e. `phi(t) = +-1`. Because the
  exchange class generates that `Z_2` (C1) on the non-planar `Z^3` (C2),
  the exchange phase is **`+-1` only**: continuous anyon phases are
  **excluded**, and the first-quantized statistics is **Bose or Fermi**.
  (Harrison-Keating-Robbins-Sawicki: a non-planar 3-connected graph
  admits only Bose or Fermi statistics.)

- **(C4) Combination with the retained per-site dim-2 result.** The
  framework's retained per-site Hilbert dimension `dim_C H_x = 2`
  (`CL3_PER_SITE_HILBERT_DIM_TWO_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-02.md`) excludes
  the **free / infinite-tower** bosonic mode (a bosonic harmonic Fock
  space is `aleph_0`-dimensional per site). Combining the free-boson
  exclusion with the first-quantized `{boson, fermion}` dichotomy (C3),
  the surviving first-quantized matter statistics on `Z^3` is
  **`{hard-core boson, fermion}` — and nothing else**.

This is a **positive narrowing** (anyons removed; the remaining options
pinned to a 2-element set). It is **not** a derivation of fermions: the
sign (boson vs fermion) is a free 1D-representation choice and is **not**
selected here (see "Scope and what stays open").

## Construction (Abrams discretized configuration space)

For a graph `Gamma` with vertex set `V` and edge set `E`, the Abrams
discretized configuration space `D_2(Gamma)` is the cube subcomplex of
the square `Gamma x Gamma` whose product cells `sigma x tau` satisfy the
**disjoint-closure** condition `closure(sigma) ∩ closure(tau) = empty`
(`closure(v) = {v}`; `closure(e=\{a,b\}) = {a,b}`). The unordered space
`UD_2(Gamma) = D_2(Gamma) / Z_2` (particle swap) has cells:

| dim | cell | condition |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | unordered pair `{u, v}` of vertices | `u != v` |
| 1 | unordered `{w, e}`, `w` a vertex, `e` an edge | `w` not an endpoint of `e` |
| 2 | unordered `{e, f}` of edges | `e, f` vertex-disjoint |

Orienting each edge `e = (a, b)` with `a < b` (`d e = b - a`), the
cubical boundary maps on `UD_2(Gamma)` are

```text
  d1 {w, e=(a,b)} = {w, b} - {w, a}                       (1-cell -> 0-cells)
  d2 {e=(a,b), f=(c,d)} = {b, f} - {a, f} - {d, e} + {c, e}   (2-cell -> 1-cells)
```

`B_2(Gamma) = pi_1(UD_2(Gamma))` is the **graph-braid group**, and its
abelianization is `H_1(UD_2(Gamma)) = ker(d1) / im(d2)`. The free rank is
`beta_1 = (|C_1| - rank d1) - rank d2`; the **torsion** equals the
nontrivial (`>1`) invariant factors of `d2` in Smith normal form
(`im(d2)` lies inside the **pure** subgroup `ker(d1)` of the free group
`C_1` — the kernel of a map onto a free abelian group is saturated — so
`torsion(ker d1 / im d2) = torsion(C_1 / im d2)`, which the Smith normal
form of `d2` reads off directly).

## Scope and what stays open

This result is deliberately narrow. It is **first-quantized**
configuration-space statistics for indistinguishable particles on the
`Z^3` graph, and establishes the `{boson, fermion}` dichotomy (anyons
excluded) at **that** level only.

- **Does not select the sign.** Boson vs fermion remains a free
  1D-representation choice: `Hom(H_1, U(1))` admits **both** `+1` and
  `-1` on the exchange class. This note removes the *continuum* of anyon
  phases between them; it does **not** pick one of the two surviving
  endpoints.

- **Does not, by itself, govern the second-quantized matter sector.** The
  framework's actual matter is a **second-quantized, gauge-coupled**
  Kahler-Dirac / staggered field theory on the lattice, not two
  first-quantized particles. The bridge from first-quantized graph-braid
  exchange statistics to second-quantized field **graded locality** is a
  **separate, open** question. In particular,
  [`STAGGERED_DIRAC_SUBSTEP1_STATISTICS_AGNOSTIC_NO_FORCING_NOTE_2026-05-25.md`](STAGGERED_DIRAC_SUBSTEP1_STATISTICS_AGNOSTIC_NO_FORCING_NOTE_2026-05-25.md)
  (retained_no_go) shows that the operator-algebra / dimension baseline
  does **not** force the fermionic frame over the hard-core-boson frame at
  the field-algebra level. This note does **not** resolve that no-go: the
  anyon-exclusion argument lives on the configuration-space `Z^3`
  topology, a different axis from the field-algebra grading. Whether the
  first-quantized dichotomy lifts to the second-quantized sector requires
  an independent bridge that is not supplied here.

- **The honest combined statement is therefore:** *at the first-quantized
  configuration-space level, `Z^3` topology excludes anyons and forces
  `{boson, fermion}`; combined with the retained per-site dim-2 result
  (free / infinite-tower boson excluded), the surviving first-quantized
  matter statistics is `{hard-core boson, fermion}`.* Anything stronger —
  selecting the fermionic sign, or transporting the dichotomy to the
  gauge-coupled field theory — is not claimed.
