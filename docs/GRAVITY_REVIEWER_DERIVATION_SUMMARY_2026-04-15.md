# Gravity source index and open interpretation obligations

**Date:** historical ladder 2026-04-15; scope correction 2026-10-21.
**Claim type:** support/meta. This index establishes no theorem or audit grade.

The ladder collects supplied Poisson, finite-rank, bilinear-action and Gaussian models. In the [discrete assembly source](UNIVERSAL_GR_DISCRETE_GLOBAL_CLOSURE_NOTE.md), the declared fresh evidence is one Lorentzian background and one chart overlap. Its lift, slice generator and wider atlas structure are imported. Algebraic overlap compatibility does not identify the family with physical Einstein or Regge dynamics.

The historical overview asserted a complete sequence through refinement, Gaussian completion, Sobolev realization and smooth gravitational identification. Those are separate source claims with separate hypotheses and proof obligations. A Gaussian cylinder family is not automatically a countably additive field measure; a compatible finite family does not alone prove continuum convergence; an assigned smooth coordinate representation does not derive nonlinear Einstein dynamics. This summary supplies none of the missing hypotheses or bridges and does not promote the linked sources.

For any use, inspect the current source's explicit operator, sign and nondegeneracy conditions, compatible means/covariances, refinement maps, topological or measure extension assumptions, physical identification, and actual runner inputs. In particular a Lorentzian matrix does not make every associated Hessian negative definite; a Gaussian probability interpretation requires its own positive covariance condition. Historical assertions of exact physical closure must not substitute for these checks.
