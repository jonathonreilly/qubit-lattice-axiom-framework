## 1. Theorem

Let `V_3 = C^3` be the irreducible color carrier. Fix `N_c = 3` and
the canonical Gell-Mann basis `λ_a` on `V_3`. For each positive real
`N_F > 0`, define the L3b-rescaled generators

```text
T_a^{(N_F)}  :=  sqrt(2 N_F) · (λ_a / 2)  =  sqrt(N_F / 2) · λ_a.            (1)
```

The L3b orbit identity holds:

```text
Tr_{V_3}(T_a^{(N_F)} T_b^{(N_F)})  =  N_F · δ_{ab},      ∀ N_F ∈ R_{>0}.     (2)
```

Let `A_op^{(N_F)} = Σ_a A^a T_a^{(N_F)}` be the operator-valued
connection on V_3 with L3b-rescaled generators. Let `U_p^{(N_F)} =
expm(ia A_μ^{(N_F)}) expm(ia A_ν^{(N_F)}) expm(-ia A_μ^{(N_F)})
expm(-ia A_ν^{(N_F)})` be the elementary plaquette on V_3 with
L3b-rescaled connection. Let `F^{(N_F)} = i [A_μ^{(N_F)}, A_ν^{(N_F)}]`
be the field-strength operator (constant-A limit).

**Hypotheses.**

(L1) The lattice Wilson action on V_3 at L3b scalar `N_F` takes the
universal form

```text
S_W^{(N_F)}  =  β^{(N_F)} · Σ_p (dim(V_3) - Re Tr_{V_3}(U_p^{(N_F)})) / dim(V_3),  (3)
```

with `dim(V_3) = N_c = 3`.

(L2) The continuum kinetic term takes the convention-independent form

```text
S_cont  =  (1/(4 g²)) · Σ_a F^a_{μν} F^{a,μν},                              (4)
```

with `g > 0` the bare gauge coupling. This sum over color index `a`
makes no reference to any specific scalar normalization `N_F`.

(L3) The connection coefficients `A^a` are real-valued scalar fields,
independent of the L3b scalar (i.e., when we rescale generators
`T_a → T_a^{(N_F)}`, we do NOT simultaneously rescale `A^a`; the L3b
orbit acts on the generator basis, not on the connection coefficients).

**Conclusion (T1) (L3b scalar inflation).** Under (1) and (L3),

```text
Tr_{V_3}((F^{(N_F)})²)  =  2 N_F · Σ_a (F^a)²,                              (5)
```

i.e., the trace of the field-strength squared scales linearly with the
L3b scalar `N_F` along the orbit. (Equivalently, `Tr_{V_3}((F^{(N_F)})²)
= 2 N_F · Tr_{V_3}((F^{(1/2)})²) / 1`, since at `N_F = 1/2` the
canonical Gell-Mann gives `Tr_{V_3}((F^{(1/2)})²) = (1/2) Σ_a (F^a)²
= (1) · Σ_a (F^a)² · (1/2)`. The orbit-invariant form is (5).)

**Conclusion (T2) (lattice-side small-`a` coefficient).** The Wilson
plaquette functional admits the small-`a` expansion

```text
F^{(N_F)}(U_p^{(N_F)})  :=  (3 - Re Tr_{V_3}(U_p^{(N_F)})) / 3
                         =  (a^4 / (2 · 3)) · Tr_{V_3}((F^{(N_F)})²)  +  O(a^6)
                         =  (a^4 · N_F / 3) · Σ_a (F^a)²  +  O(a^6).         (6)
```

The lattice-side `a^4` coefficient is therefore **proportional to the
L3b scalar `N_F`** (after factoring out the convention-independent
`Σ_a (F^a)²`).

**Conclusion (T3) (continuum-side N_F dependence).** The continuum
kinetic (4), expressed as a trace on V_3 with L3b scalar `N_F`, takes
the form

```text
S_cont  =  (1/(2 g²)) · (1/N_F) · Tr_{V_3}((F^{(N_F)})²)
        =  (1/(2 g²)) · (1/N_F) · 2 N_F · Σ_a (F^a)²  =  (1/g²) · Σ_a (F^a)²  (7)
```

(absorbing the (1/2) by `F^2 = (1/2) F_{μν} F^{μν}` for antisymmetric
F; equivalently (1/(4g²)) Σ_a F^a_{μν} F^{a,μν}). The L3b scalar `N_F`
**cancels** between the prefactor `1/N_F` and the trace identity (5),
recovering (4).

**Conclusion (T4) (Wilson matching `g_bare` invariance).** Equating
the small-`a` lattice action coefficient (3) with (6) to the continuum
(4) per plaquette:

```text
β^{(N_F)} · (a^4 · N_F / 3) · Σ_a (F^a)²
         =  (a^4 / (4 g_bare²)) · Σ_a F^a_{μν} F^{a,μν}
         =  (a^4 / (2 g_bare²)) · Σ_a (F^a)²,                                (8a)
```

so dividing both sides by `(a^4 / 2) · Σ_a (F^a)²`:

```text
β^{(N_F)} · (2 N_F / 3)  =  (1 / g_bare²),                                   (8b)
```

equivalently

```text
β^{(N_F)}  =  3 / (2 N_F · g_bare²)  =  N_c / (2 N_F · g_bare²).             (8c)
```

[Note: the factor `2 N_F` on the right is the trace-form scalar in the
generator-basis orthonormality, i.e., `Tr(T_a^{(N_F)} T_b^{(N_F)})
= N_F δ_{ab}`; the factor of 2 inside the parenthesis emerges from

Equivalently, the **orbit identity**

```text
β^{(N_F)} · N_F · g_bare²  =  N_c / 2  =  3/2,      ∀ N_F ∈ R_{>0}.          (8d)
```

The right-hand side `N_c / 2 = 3/2` is the **L3b-orbit invariant**.
For each L3b scalar `N_F`, the canonical-normalization-forced Wilson
coefficient (the β value when `g_bare = 1`) is

```text
β_canonical(N_F)  =  N_c / (2 N_F)  =  3 / (2 N_F).                          (9a)
```

At `N_F = 1/2`:  `β_canonical(1/2) = 3` ??? — see note below.

convention has `Tr(T_a T_b) = (1/2) δ_{ab}` (so `N_F = 1/2`) and the

**Either convention.** The L3b orbit invariance of `g_bare = 1` holds

**Conclusion (T5) (L3b orbit inert for `g_bare`).** Combining (T1)-(T4),
under the L3b-rescaled lattice action (3) and the convention-independent

while `g_bare` remains constant at `g_bare = 1`. The L3b admission

**Conclusion (T6) (orbit differential).** Equivalently, along the

```text

```

since `g_bare² = N_c / (N_F · β_canonical(N_F)) = N_c / (N_F · (N_c/N_F))
