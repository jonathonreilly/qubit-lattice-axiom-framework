Its ascending order is `K6, K5, K4, K3`, while the landed Hankel-tail depth order
is `K6, K5, K3, K4`.

Thus the bounded result is qualified:

- Extra determinant-phase content is generated by the nonlinear harmonic ladder,
  with nonzero `k=2` in every state.
- The raw finite-window FFT has fixed off-grid leakage and is not an exact
  ladder display.
- The capture deficit remains the landed Hankel-SVD tail of the full sampled
  tone sum, not the simple Fourier coefficient mass beyond the fundamental.
