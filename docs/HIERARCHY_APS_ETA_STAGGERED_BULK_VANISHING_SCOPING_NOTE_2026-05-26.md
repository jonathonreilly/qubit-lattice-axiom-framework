framework's bulk staggered-Dirac surface and an explicit gap inventory
for any downstream Atiyah-Patodi-Singer (APS) η-invariant attack on
hierarchy primitive P3 (the `u_0^16 → α_LM^16` substitution) or on the
`(7/8)^(1/4)` factor.

The two facts:

- **(L1) Bulk-spectrum-symmetry bulk-vanishing lemma.** On the
  framework's `Z^3 × S^1_β` substrate at minimal block `L_s = 2`,
  `L_t ∈ {2, 4}`, with APBC fermion / PBC boson Matsubara modes,
  mean-field gauge factorization, and zero rest mass, the bare APS

  ```text
  η_APS(D_staggered, m=0, no boundary, mean-field, L_s=2, L_t∈{2,4})
    =  Σ_λ sign(λ)
    =  0                                                        (L1)
  ```

  This is **structurally the same fact** as the "η=0" entry in
  [`SIGNED_GRAVITY_STAGGERED_DIRAC_APS_BOUNDARY_REALIZATION_NOTE.md`](SIGNED_GRAVITY_STAGGERED_DIRAC_APS_BOUNDARY_REALIZATION_NOTE.md)'s
  "Retained-Compatible Boundary Readout" table, restated for the
  hierarchy substrate.

- **(L2) Identity-collapse observation at d=4.** Three independent
  algebraic expressions all yield `1/8` at d=4:
  - `2^(1 - d)` at `d = 4` (Riemann-Dirichlet odd-Matsubara complement)
  - `1/(2d)` at `d = 4` (canonical APS averaging fraction)
  - `1 - η(d)/ζ(d)` at `d = 4` (complement of the
    [`HIERARCHY_SEVEN_EIGHTHS_RIEMANN_DIRICHLET_DIMENSIONAL_ANCHOR`](HIERARCHY_SEVEN_EIGHTHS_RIEMANN_DIRICHLET_DIMENSIONAL_ANCHOR_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md)

  the existing retained integer-alignment equation `2^(d-2) = d` (cf.
  HIERARCHY_SEVEN_EIGHTHS_RIEMANN_DIRICHLET_DIMENSIONAL_ANCHOR
  witness (iii)). They are therefore **NOT three independent witnesses**
  for `1/8` at d=4 — they are one identity in three vocabularies.

The note is narrow: it computes a vanishing and an identity collapse on

## 2. The bulk-vanishing lemma (L1)

### 2.1 Statement

> **Lemma (L1).** Let `D_stag` be the Hermitian staggered Dirac
> operator on `Z^3 × S^1_β` at minimal spatial block `L_s = 2` with
> spatial APBC, temporal block `L_t ∈ {2, 4}` with temporal APBC
> (fermion Matsubara), mean-field gauge factorization `U_{ab} → u_0
> δ_{ab}`, and zero rest mass `m = 0`. Then the bare spectral
> asymmetry vanishes:
>
> ```text
> η_APS(D_stag)  :=  Σ_λ sign(λ)  =  0.                          (L1)
> ```

### 2.2 Proof

From the parent narrow theorem
[`HIERARCHY_MATSUBARA_DETERMINANT_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-02.md`](HIERARCHY_MATSUBARA_DETERMINANT_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-02.md),
the staggered Dirac eigenvalue-magnitudes-squared are

```text
λ²(k, ω)  =  u_0² · ( Σ_μ sin²(k_μ) + sin²(ω) ).                  (1)
```

At `L_s = 2` APBC, `k_μ ∈ {π/2 + n_μ π : n_μ ∈ {0, 1}}`, so
`sin(k_μ) = ±1` and `sin²(k_μ) = 1` for all spatial modes. The
spatial contribution to (1) is uniformly 3.

At `L_t ∈ {2, 4}` APBC, `ω_n = (2n + 1) π / L_t`. At `L_t = 2`:
`ω ∈ {π/2, 3π/2}` gives `sin²(ω) = 1`. At `L_t = 4`: `ω ∈ {π/4,
3π/4, 5π/4, 7π/4}` gives `sin²(ω) = 1/2` uniformly (Klein-four
orbit, cf.
[`HIERARCHY_LT4_KLEIN_FOUR_SIN_SQUARED_UNIFORMITY_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md`](HIERARCHY_LT4_KLEIN_FOUR_SIN_SQUARED_UNIFORMITY_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md)).

The Hermitian staggered operator `D_stag` is anticommuted by the
staggered phase operator `Γ_5 = (-1)^{n_x + n_y + n_z + n_t}`
(the staggered chirality), so for any eigenvalue `λ` of `D_stag`
there is a paired eigenvalue `-λ` with eigenvector `Γ_5 ψ`. Therefore
the multiset of signed eigenvalues is invariant under `λ → -λ`,
giving

```text
Σ_λ sign(λ)  =  -  Σ_λ sign(λ)  =  0.                            (2)
```

This is independent of `L_t ∈ {2, 4}`, of `L_s = 2`, of the value of
`u_0`, and of the spatial APBC mode placement. It uses only:

1. The Hermitian staggered Dirac dispersion (1) on minimal block.
2. The staggered chirality anticommutation `{Γ_5, D_stag} = 0` at
   `m = 0` (admitted standard staggered fermion algebra; same surface
   as the parent narrow theorem).

This is **class A** (algebraic identity on admitted-standard staggered
fermion algebra). ∎

### 2.3 What L1 means for hierarchy P3

The hierarchy primitive P3 (the `u_0^16 → α_LM^16` substitution, with
implicit `α_bare^16 ≈ (4π)^-16` content per
[`HIERARCHY_FORMULA_HONEST_STATUS_NOTE_2026-05-10.md`](HIERARCHY_FORMULA_HONEST_STATUS_NOTE_2026-05-10.md)
§3 (S)) requires a derivation of a 16-th power coupling suppression on
the framework's substrate. **An APS-η-driven mechanism would require
a non-zero η-invariant.** Lemma L1 establishes that the **bare** η on
the framework's substrate vanishes identically by spectrum symmetry, so
no bulk APS-η source for the `(4π)^-16` suppression is available
without **at least one** of the following non-bulk inputs:

- (NB1) Wilson / domain-wall mass with sign flip (breaks `Γ_5`
  anticommutation; canonical APS lattice realization per
  Fukaya-Onogi-Yamaguchi arXiv:1910.09675 — cited for context, not
  load-bearing).
- (NB2) Non-trivial gauge background (broken mean-field
  factorization; the framework currently rides mean-field for the
  hierarchy chain).
- (NB3) Manifold-with-boundary geometry (true APS context; the
  framework's substrate is a closed flat torus at the lattice
  matching surface).

None of (NB1)-(NB3) is present at the surface where hierarchy P3 is
admitted. Therefore L1 forecloses **the direct bulk APS-η lane** for
P3 on the existing hierarchy-formula surface.

This is not a foreclosure of all APS-η mechanisms; it is a foreclosure
of the **bulk-spectrum-asymmetry** route. The remaining mechanisms
(NB1)-(NB3) each require non-trivial extension of the substrate; each
is a distinct downstream derivation target outside this note's scope.

## 3. The identity-collapse observation (L2)

### 3.1 Statement

> **Observation (L2).** The three rational expressions
>
> ```text
> A(d)  :=  2^(1 - d)
> B(d)  :=  1 / (2d)
> C(d)  :=  1  -  η(d) / ζ(d)  =  2^(1 - d)
> ```
>
> all coincide at `d = 4` with value `1/8`. The simultaneous
> equality `A(d) = B(d) = C(d)` for integer `d ≥ 2` holds **uniquely
> at d = 4**, and is equivalent to the integer alignment equation
> `2^(d - 2) = d` (retained, per
> [`HIERARCHY_SEVEN_EIGHTHS_RIEMANN_DIRICHLET_DIMENSIONAL_ANCHOR_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md`](HIERARCHY_SEVEN_EIGHTHS_RIEMANN_DIRICHLET_DIMENSIONAL_ANCHOR_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md)
> witness (iii)).

### 3.2 Algebra

`A(d) = C(d)` is the Riemann-Dirichlet identity
`η(s) / ζ(s) = 1 - 2^(1-s)` (textbook). `A(d) = B(d)` is the
equation `2^(1-d) = 1/(2d)`, equivalent to `d · 2^{2-d} = 1`,
equivalent to `2^{d-2} = d` — exactly witness (iii) of the retained
seven-eighths anchor theorem.

### 3.3 What L2 means for "η_APS / 2 = 1/8" conjectures

Any conjecture of the form `η_APS / 2 = 1/8` at d=4 in the framework's
vocabulary is **NOT** an independent witness for `1/8`. It is the
**same** identity as:

- `(complement of η(d)/ζ(d))` at d=4 — i.e. the Bose-Einstein
  even-Matsubara fraction `2^(1-d)` (cf. partial-sum-split in
  [`AXIOM_FIRST_STEFAN_BOLTZMANN_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-01.md`](AXIOM_FIRST_STEFAN_BOLTZMANN_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-01.md))
- the canonical APS averaging fraction `1/(2d)` at d=4
- the `7/8 = 1 - 1/8` complement of the retained
  HIERARCHY_SEVEN_EIGHTHS_RIEMANN_DIRICHLET_DIMENSIONAL_ANCHOR

A new witness for `1/8` (and hence for `(7/8)^{1/4}` via the
existing
[`HIERARCHY_SEVEN_EIGHTHS_QUARTER_FERMION_BOSON_SCALE_CONVERSION_BRIDGE_BOUNDED_NOTE_2026-05-26.md`](HIERARCHY_SEVEN_EIGHTHS_QUARTER_FERMION_BOSON_SCALE_CONVERSION_BRIDGE_BOUNDED_NOTE_2026-05-26.md)
composition path) would require a derivation that **does not reduce**
to one of A, B, C at d=4 — i.e. that exhibits a structurally distinct
quantity equal to `1/8` only at d=4.

The bulk-vanishing lemma L1 forecloses the simplest such candidate
(bare staggered η on the framework's substrate is identically zero,
not `1/4`).
