## 2. Five structural obstructions

### 2.1 Failure (M1): SU(2) meron vs SU(3) framework Wilson surface

The de Alfaro-Fubini-Furlan meron is an explicit Euclidean **SU(2)**
gauge configuration:

```text
A_mu^a(x)  =  (1/g)  eta^a_{mu nu}  d_nu  ln(x^2 / rho^2),         (M1.1)
```

with `eta^a_{mu nu}` the 't Hooft symbol and the index `a` running
over the three SU(2) Lie-algebra directions. The configuration is
written explicitly on the SU(2) gauge bundle and its action evaluates
to `4 pi^2 / g^2` only because of the SU(2) structure constants and
the SU(2) Casimir normalization. The Callan-Dashen-Gross meron-pair
theory of confinement (PRD 17 (1978) 2717; PRD 19 (1979) 1826) is
formulated for SU(2) gauge theory; the meron sector has no
known closed-form direct extension to SU(3) gauge theory
(the SU(3) fractional-instanton structure on the 4D torus T⁴ uses
't Hooft twisted boundary conditions and gives action
`S_frac = (8 pi^2 / g^2) |k/N|` with charge `k/N`, not the SU(2)
meron's `4 pi^2 / g^2`).

The framework's canonical Wilson surface is **SU(3)**:

- the graph-first gauge sector fixes `N_c = 3`
  ([`PLAQUETTE_SELF_CONSISTENCY_NOTE.md`](PLAQUETTE_SELF_CONSISTENCY_NOTE.md));
- `beta = 2 N_c / g_bare^2 = 6` fixes `g_bare^2 = 1`;
- `<P> = 0.5934` is the same-surface SU(3) plaquette observable.

The framework also realizes an SU(2)_L weak gauge symmetry through the
Cl⁺(3) ≅ ℍ → SU(2)_L embedding
([`CL3_SM_EMBEDDING_THEOREM.md`](CL3_SM_EMBEDDING_THEOREM.md)), but
that SU(2)_L lives on the **fibre** (`b_3` coordinate), not on the
SU(3) Wilson lattice. Its gauge coupling at the EW scale is
`g_2^2(v) ~= 0.42`, not the SU(3) bare value `g_bare^2 = 1`.

The bridge would require either:

(M1a) extending the meron's `S = 4 pi^2 / g^2` formula to SU(3) at
fixed action value — for which no published SU(3) configuration with
classical action exactly `4 pi^2 / g^2` exists in the gauge-theory
literature
(SU(3) fractional instantons at `k = 1` have `S_frac = 8 pi^2 / (3 g^2) ~= 2.63`,
which is one-third the SU(2) meron action at the same `g`);

(M1b) identifying the framework's meron sector with the SU(2)_L fibre
operators — which contradicts (M3) below (the SU(2)_L coupling
`g_2^2(v)` is `~0.42`, giving `4 pi^2 / g_2^2 ~= 94`, off by factor
`~2.4` from the target).

No such substrate-level identification is supplied in the framework's
retained authority chain or in the external meron / fractional-instanton
scaffolds. QED.

### 2.3 Failure (M3): coupling identification and the `g^2 = 1` choice

The meron action `S_meron = 4 pi^2 / g^2` depends sharply on which
gauge coupling `g`. Three candidate identifications on the framework
surface:

(M3a) `g_bare^2 = 1`, framework SU(3) Wilson bare coupling at the
canonical surface: `S_meron = 4 pi^2 ~= 39.48`. Numerical match (M5).
But the meron lives on SU(2) not SU(3) (M1), and `g_bare` is the SU(3)
bare colour coupling, not an SU(2) coupling on which the meron is
defined.

(M3b) `g_2^2(v) ~= 0.42`, SU(2)_L weak coupling at the EW scale
(standard Particle Data Group value): `S_meron = 4 pi^2 / 0.42 ~= 93.99`.
The predicted scale is `M_Pl * exp(-94) ~= 1.8e-22 GeV`, off by
~24 orders of magnitude. The SU(2)_L coupling is moreover defined at
the EW scale `v`, not at the framework's UV anchor `M_Pl`; running
upward (asymptotic freedom for non-Abelian) further decreases
`g_2(mu)` and increases `S_meron / g^2`, worsening the mismatch.

(M3c) `g_s^2(v) ~= 1.55`, SU(3) strong coupling at the EW scale
(running the bare `g_bare^2 = 1 at M_Pl` down via beta-function):
`S_meron = 4 pi^2 / 1.55 ~= 25.47`. Predicted scale
`M_Pl * exp(-25.5) ~= 9.5e7 GeV`, off by ~6 orders of magnitude.

Only (M3a) gives the `~3 %` numerical fit, but it requires using
the SU(3) bare coupling on an SU(2) configuration — a category error
that has no derivation in the meron scaffold or the framework's
retained chain.

Equivalently: the substitution `g^2 -> g_bare^2 = 1` in the meron
action `S = 4 pi^2 / g^2` is an **identification by hand** at the
coupling level, not derived from a substrate-natural mechanism. The
framework's `g_bare^2 = 1` is the same-surface readout of `beta = 6,
N_c = 3` Wilson SU(3); the meron's `g` is the SU(2) gauge coupling on

### 2.4 Failure (M4): scale identification (condensate Lambda ≠ v_EW)

The fractional-instanton dilute-gas condensate mechanism
(Cox-Pisarski arXiv:2310.16289, 2023; Anber-Poppitz arXiv:1811.05882,

't Hooft PRD 14 (1976) 3432) produces a confinement-like dynamical
scale at which the topological condensate forms. In the standard
gauge-theory context, this is:

- the QCD `Lambda` scale at which the gluon condensate `<F^2>` becomes
  `O(1)` and chiral symmetry is broken (Shifman-Vainshtein-Zakharov
  sum rules, Diakonov instanton-liquid model);
- the center-vortex scale `Lambda_chi` at which the 't Hooft 1981 dual
  superconductor confines flux (Greensite arXiv:0810.4392);
- the `eta'` mass scale where U(1)_A is anomalously broken by
  instantons (Witten-Veneziano).

None of these is identified with the **SM electroweak VEV `v`**.
The electroweak VEV is generated by the Higgs potential's minimum at
tree level (Standard Model) or by composite-Higgs / technicolor
condensates of an *extended* gauge sector (Hill-Simmons,
arXiv:hep-ph/0203079); the meron dilute-gas mechanism on the
framework's SU(3) Wilson surface, if it produced a condensate scale at
all, would produce a colour-confinement scale near `Lambda_QCD ~ 200 MeV`,
not the EW VEV `v = 246 GeV`. The four-orders-of-magnitude difference
between `Lambda_QCD` and `v` reflects the difference between the
strong-coupling colour-confinement scale and the weak-scale Higgs VEV;
identifying them is itself an open derivation.

The framework's existing identification of the Higgs as a composite
**taste** condensate
([`HIGGS_MASS_FROM_AXIOM_NOTE.md`](HIGGS_MASS_FROM_AXIOM_NOTE.md);

is the standard composite-Higgs convention, but it does not derive

The same open primitive **P4** (EWSB observable identification) that

### 2.5 Failure (M5): residual `2.82x` numerical correction not closeable

The numerical fit is:

```text
ln(M_Pl / v_obs)  =  38.4425
4 pi^2            =  39.4784
discrepancy       =  39.4784 - 38.4425  =  1.0359                  (M5.1)
multiplicative correction needed  =  exp(1.0359)  ~=  2.82          (M5.2)
v_predicted = M_Pl * exp(-4 pi^2)  =  87.38 GeV
v_obs / v_predicted  =  2.8177                                      (M5.3)
```

For the bridge to close, the `Pi_i K_i` correction product in (M)
must give exactly `2.82`. Candidate substrate-natural correction
factors evaluated on the canonical surface:

- **`(7/8)^(1/4) ~= 0.9672`** (APBC fermion selector at `L_t = 4`,
  retained per
  [`HIERARCHY_BOSONIC_BILINEAR_SELECTOR_NOTE.md`](HIERARCHY_BOSONIC_BILINEAR_SELECTOR_NOTE.md)).
  Acts *multiplicatively* on `v`. Application: `v_predicted_apbc = 87.38 * 0.9672 ~= 84.51`. **Wrong direction:** moves `v_predicted` further from `v_obs`.

- **1-loop functional determinant prefactor** at the meron saddle.
  In standard instanton calculus (Shifman-Vainshtein-Zakharov; 't Hooft
  PRD 14 (1976) 3432), the one-loop determinant gives an `O(1)`
  numerical factor that depends on the gauge group, the regulator,
  and the size-modulus integration. For SU(2) BPST instantons, the
  Pauli-Villars regulated determinant gives
  `K_1loop ~ 2^10 pi^6 / 3 ~ O(1000)` (Bernard arXiv:hep-ph/9907499);
  for the meron-pair this is partially divergent at the meron core
  and requires explicit regularization (Callan-Dashen-Gross 1978).
  The framework has no retained-grade derivation of a meron one-loop
  determinant on its substrate. Even if such a derivation existed,
  the determinant would set the absolute normalization of the meron
  density, not the leading exponential, and the residual factor needed
  is `~2.82`, with no substrate-mechanism predicting exactly this value.

- **`exp(-(b_0 ln(M_Pl/v)) / 2)` one-loop coupling running.** The
  one-loop QCD `beta_0 = (11 N_c - 2 N_f) / (12 pi) = 7 / (12 pi)`
  at `N_c = 3, N_f = 6`. Running `g_s^2(M_Pl) = 1` down to `mu = v`
  via `1/g^2(v) = 1/g^2(M_Pl) + (b_0/4pi^2) ln(M_Pl/v)`:
  `1/g_s^2(v) = 1 + (7/(48 pi^3)) * 38.44 = 1 + 0.181 = 1.181`,
  so `g_s^2(v) = 0.847`. Substituting into `4 pi^2 / g_s^2(v) = 46.6`,
  predicted `v = M_Pl * exp(-46.6) = 5.7e-2 GeV`, **off by ~4 orders
  of magnitude** in the wrong direction.

- **Volume factor `V * Lambda^4` from dilute-gas integration**
  (Schaefer-Shuryak RMP 70 (1998) 323; Cox-Pisarski 2310.16289).
  The condensate-formation condition `exp(-S_frac) ~ O(1)` fixes the
  condensate density to one meron per unit-Lambda^4 volume, not the
  prefactor of the resulting `v`. The framework has no retained
  identification of its substrate volume with a meron-density unit.

- **Modulus-integral logarithm `~ ln(rho_max/rho_min)`.** Standard
  instanton-size moduli integration produces logarithmic factors
  proportional to the regulator ratio. For the meron at framework
  scale `~M_Pl`, a logarithmic factor `~ ln(M_Pl / v) ~ 38` would be
  enormous, not `~2.82`.

**Net assessment of M5:**

- `(7/8)^(1/4)` is the only substrate-canonical factor available
  with a retained authority, and it goes the *wrong way*
  (`87.38 * 0.9672 = 84.51 GeV < 87.38 GeV`).
- All other candidate factors (one-loop determinant, running coupling,
  volume factor, modulus integral) either lack framework derivation
  or have known QFT values that produce corrections of `O(1)` to
  `O(10^4)`, none of which lands at exactly `2.82` from a

- A correction of exactly `2.82` would either need to be derived
  from first principles (no such derivation exists in the framework
  or the external scaffolds), or imported numerically (which is
  primitive **P3** / **P4** / fit input by another name).

The `~3 %` numerical match is therefore a coincidence at the leading
exponential level — the kind of accidental near-equality of
`4 pi^2 ~ 39.5` and `ln(M_Pl/v) ~ 38.4` that the framework's own
counterfactual discipline classifies as a **bounded numerical

The same `bounded_numerical_match` discipline applied to
`alpha_LM^16` in
[`HIERARCHY_FORMULA_HONEST_STATUS_NOTE_2026-05-10.md`](HIERARCHY_FORMULA_HONEST_STATUS_NOTE_2026-05-10.md)

### 2.6 Joint sufficiency

Each of (M1)-(M5) is independently sufficient to block the bridge
identification at the published-theorem level:

- (M1) blocks because the meron's natural gauge group is SU(2),
  the framework's canonical Wilson surface is SU(3), and no
  closed-form SU(3) meron extension with action `4 pi^2 / g^2`
  exists in the gauge-theory literature.
- (M2) blocks because the meron's natural lattice realization
  (Itou-Iritani T⁴ with twisted BC) requires a 4D compact lattice
  that the framework's Z³ × derived-time substrate does not own
  at the retained-authority level (the same P2 open primitive).
- (M3) blocks because the coupling identification `g_canonical^2 = 1`
  pairs the framework's SU(3) bare coupling with the meron's SU(2)
  configuration — a category error not derived from substrate.
  The natural SU(2)_L coupling `g_2^2(v) ~= 0.42` gives `94`, off

- (M4) blocks because the meron condensate produces a
  confinement-like scale (`Lambda_QCD` or `Lambda_chi`), not the
  electroweak VEV `v`; the identification is the same P4 open

- (M5) blocks because the residual `2.82x` numerical factor has
  no substrate-natural derivation; the only retained factor

The five obstructions are jointly sufficient; even closing (M5)
alone (e.g. via a substrate one-loop determinant derivation) would
leave (M1)-(M4) unblocked. The bridge fails as a published-theorem-

## 5. Closure path (forward-looking; not part of this packet's authority)

Three classes of follow-on work *could* close the bridge but are not
supplied by this packet:

### C1. SU(3) closed-form meron-class configuration

A derivation of an SU(3) classical gauge configuration with action
exactly `4 pi^2 / g^2` at integer coupling `g^2 = 1` would close (M1).
The closest candidate is the `SU(3)` `k = 1` fractional instanton on
T⁴ with twisted boundary conditions
(González-Arroyo lineage; Anber-Poppitz arXiv:1811.05882), which
gives `S = 8 pi^2 / (3 g^2)`, **half** the SU(2) meron action. No
published SU(3) configuration with the exact SU(2) meron action
appears to exist; deriving one is a substantial gauge-theory
research project, not a single-PR scope.

### C2. Framework-internal SU(2)_L fibre meron at g_2(scale) = 1

If the framework derives that at some scale `mu_*` the SU(2)_L
weak coupling satisfies `g_2^2(mu_*) = 1`, then a meron on the
SU(2)_L fibre at that scale would give `S_meron = 4 pi^2`. The
SU(2)_L running from `g_2^2(v) ~= 0.42` upward gives
`g_2^2(M_Pl) ~ 0.30` (asymptotic freedom for non-Abelian SU(2)_L
above the EW scale, with `b_0 = 19/6`), monotonically decreasing.
There is no scale where SU(2)_L coupling reaches 1; the bridge
fails on (M3) for the SU(2)_L identification.

### C3. Substrate-natural derivation of the 2.82x correction

A first-principles derivation of the `~2.82` multiplicative
correction from substrate-natural primitives (Klein-four taste
structure, Cl(3) algebra, Z³ × derived-time, `u_0`, `alpha_bare`)
without consuming any retained-status authority by hand. This
would close (M5) and substantially weaken the no-go. The current
packet records that no such derivation exists in the framework's
retained authority chain.

None of C1, C2, C3 is supplied by this packet; the no-go therefore
stands as a narrow obstruction at the published-theorem level on
the current authority surface.
