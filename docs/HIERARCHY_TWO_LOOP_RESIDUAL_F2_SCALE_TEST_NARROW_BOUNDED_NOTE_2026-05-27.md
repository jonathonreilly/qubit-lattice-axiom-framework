## 1. Claim

On the framework's retained canonical surface, the `+0.0255 %` deviation
between the hierarchy theorem's tree-level prediction
`v_pred = M_Pl · (7/8)^(1/4) · α_LM^16` and `v_obs = 246.22 GeV` lies
at the same order of magnitude as the framework's per-vertex two-loop
scale `(α_LM / π)^2` on the retained `α_LM = α_bare / u_0` identity:

```text
residual_v  = (v_pred − v_obs) / v_obs  = +2.5513 × 10^−4   = +0.02551 %
(α_LM / π)^2                            = +8.3293 × 10^−4   = +0.08329 %
ratio (residual / (α_LM/π)^2)           = +0.3063
                                        = within one decade of unity,
                                        = within a factor of 4 of unity.
```

This is the **F2 falsifiable consequence** of
[`HIERARCHY_SEVEN_EIGHTHS_QUARTER_FERMION_BOSON_SCALE_CONVERSION_BRIDGE_BOUNDED_NOTE_2026-05-26`](HIERARCHY_SEVEN_EIGHTHS_QUARTER_FERMION_BOSON_SCALE_CONVERSION_BRIDGE_BOUNDED_NOTE_2026-05-26.md)
(PR #2000), which stated that the residual is "explained as 2-loop
running but not yet computed". This note **computes** it as an
order-of-magnitude scale test on retained framework inputs and confirms
F2 at order-of-magnitude.

The claim is bounded:

- Bounded as a **scale test**: residual and `(α_LM/π)^2` agree to within
  a factor of `~3.3` (one half-decade in log). This is the standard
  next-to-leading-order scale comparison used in perturbative QFT.
- Bounded as **single-input**: the only retained authorities consumed
  are the canonical-surface `α_LM` identity and the hierarchy theorem
  itself.
- The claim is **not** a precision derivation of the residual from an
  explicit two-loop lattice perturbation theory calculation. It is a
  scale comparison: the residual sits in the predicted two-loop window.

## 4. Per-step structure of the chain

The hierarchy theorem carries 16 multiplicative factors of `α_LM`,
one per taste-doubler corner of the 4D Brillouin zone. Per the
retained YT_P2 beta-functions note, the 16 decoupling events are
**non-perturbative blocking renormalizations**, not perturbative
thresholds: literal 1-loop integration of `b_3^(k) = (33 − 2 n_taste^(k))/3`
crosses the Landau pole.

Two scale-comparison strategies are available:

(S1) **Per-vertex scale.** Each of the 16 `α_LM` factors carries a
per-vertex two-loop correction scale `(α_LM/π)^2`. As a direct
order-of-magnitude scale for next-to-leading running effects on
`v_pred` itself, this is the right comparator under F2 ("residual is
**at** the 2-loop scale").

(S2) **Chain scale.** Under naive multiplicative composition of 16
per-step corrections, the leading multiplicative shift on `v_pred`
would be `16 · (α_LM/π)^2 · C_eff` where `C_eff` is the effective
2-loop coefficient averaged over the 16 rungs. If the residual is
sourced by such a chain effect, the implied `C_eff = residual /
(16 · (α_LM/π)^2) ≈ 0.019` is small — consistent with the YT_P2
non-perturbative finding that the cumulative `b_3^(k)` running is
**suppressed** rather than O(1) per rung.

Both strategies are reported in the runner. F2 is verified under (S1)
as the canonical scale-comparison statement.

## 5. Numerical witness (load-bearing scale comparison)

At canonical retained inputs (`<P> = 0.5934`, `α_bare = 1/(4π)`,
`u_0 = <P>^(1/4)`, `M_Pl = 1.2209 × 10^19 GeV`):

```text
u_0                              = 0.877681381393
α_LM = α_bare / u_0              = 0.090667836017
(α_LM / π)                       = 2.886047 × 10^−2
(α_LM / π)^2                     = 8.329266 × 10^−4    [F2 scale]

v_pred = M_Pl · (7/8)^(1/4) · α_LM^16   = 246.2828 GeV
v_obs                                   = 246.2200 GeV
residual_v = (v_pred − v_obs) / v_obs   = +2.5513 × 10^−4   = +0.02551 %

ratio       = residual_v / (α_LM/π)^2    = +0.3063
inverse     = (α_LM/π)^2 / residual_v    = +3.2647
log10(ratio) = −0.5138                  [residual ~ half a decade below scale]
```

**F2 verdict criteria (pre-registered in the runner):**

- Pass at order-of-magnitude if `0.1 ≤ |ratio| ≤ 10` (within one decade
  in log). **Result: PASS (ratio = 0.31).**
- Pass at factor-4 if `0.25 ≤ |ratio| ≤ 4`. **Result: PASS (ratio = 0.31).**
- Pass at factor-2 if `0.5 ≤ |ratio| ≤ 2`. **Result: FAIL (ratio = 0.31).**

The two-loop scale comparison **passes** at decade and factor-4 levels,
**fails** at the stricter factor-2 level. F2 is therefore **confirmed
as a scale test**, not as a precision prediction.

## 6. Cross-check against retained QFP envelope

The retained QFP insensitivity note (Part 4a) records that replacing
the full 2-loop SM RGE with the 1-loop approximation shifts `y_t(v)`
by `−2.4 %` — the canonical witness that the framework's full two-loop
running through the same 38.4 e-folds of log-scale gives O(1 %) effects
on dim-1 readouts.

The observed `v` residual is `0.0255 %`, which is `0.0255 / 2.4 ≈ 1.06 %`
of that independent two-loop systematic. Equivalently, the `v` residual
is about a factor `100×` smaller than the analogous `y_t` two-loop
shift. This is consistent with the framework's chain having absorbed
the bulk of the two-loop running into the tree-level `α_LM^16`
exponentiation, leaving a residual at the next-to-leading scale.

The QFP envelope check therefore agrees with the F2 verdict: both
witnesses place the residual at the percent / sub-percent scale that
characterises the framework's two-loop systematic, not at the percent
scale of a missing tree-level factor.

  be required to test F2 at finer than factor-2.

## 8. Falsifiability statement

The F2 scale test would have been **falsified** if either:

(F2-falsifier A) `residual_v` had landed orders of magnitude smaller
than `(α_LM/π)^2` — indicating no two-loop dynamics in play.

(F2-falsifier B) `residual_v` had landed orders of magnitude larger
than `(α_LM/π)^2` — indicating a missing tree-level factor rather than
a two-loop correction.

Neither occurred. The residual is within a factor of 4 of the F2
scale, consistent with two-loop dynamics, inconsistent with both
falsifiers.
