## 0. Scope and question

This probe attacks the hierarchy **VALUE**: the `~17` decades between the
Planck scale `M_Pl` and the electroweak scale `v`, and the `~22` decades
between `M_Pl` and the lightest charged lepton `m_e`. The companion question
— whether the framework even *has* the hierarchy **PROBLEM** — was settled
separately and negatively (the framework's Lattice (`Z^3`) + Quantum
(`M_2(C)`) + Record baseline carries no fundamental scalar mass term, so there is no
relevant `m_H^2` counterterm to fine-tune; see the concurrent sister-branch
scoping note `SCALE_AXIS_HIERARCHY_TRANSMUTATION_SCOPING_2026-06-05` (on
branch `codex/scale-axis-hierarchy-transmutation-2026-06-05`, not yet merged
to main at time of writing), §2). This note takes that **Q-mechanism: YES**
result as given and isolates
**Q-value**: which decades are mechanism-supplied and which remain unpinned.

The probe introduces **no** new axiom, selector, import, or fit. PDG masses,
the Cabibbo angle, and Froggatt-Nielsen charge ladders enter **only** as
post-hoc comparators. The Froggatt-Nielsen integer-charge readout in §4 is
reported as a **bounded numerical observation** (a coincidence-or-not flag),
explicitly **not** a derivation and explicitly **not** a framework mechanism
— positing FN charges would be an unauthorized import.

## 3. Angle 2 — the framework's actual EW mechanism is a 16th POWER

```text
v = M_Pl · (7/8)^{1/4} · alpha_LM^{16}  =  246.2828 GeV     (rel dev +0.0255%)
```

The exponent for `v` is **exactly the integer `16`** (runner §2,
`n = 16.0001`). Each factor of `alpha_LM` contributes `log10(alpha_LM)
≈ −1.04` decades; sixteen of them give `−16.68`, reproducing the EW depth.
**This is why the framework spans `~17` decades where a single transmutation
at `1/alpha ≈ 11` spans only `~4`: the suppression is a 16-fold *power*, not
one exponential pole.**

The transport origin of that power is the open gate. By the algebra
`alpha_LM^{16} = alpha_bare^{16} · u_0^{-16}`, the suppression is dominated by
`alpha_bare^{16} = (4 pi)^{-16} ≈ 2.59×10^{-18}`. The exact block determinant
/ condensate forms checked on the minimal lattice carry only `u_0`, not
`alpha_bare`, so the transport source of the `(4 pi)^{-16}` magnitude is the
named open gate
[`HIERARCHY_ALPHA_LM_MAGNITUDE_DELTA0_OPEN_GATE_NOTE_2026-05-30.md`](HIERARCHY_ALPHA_LM_MAGNITUDE_DELTA0_OPEN_GATE_NOTE_2026-05-30.md),
and the exponent `16` rides primitive P2 (4D taste counting through the open
staggered-Dirac realization gate). **The EW decade COUNT is supplied by a
real framework mechanism (the power-law); what is open is its substrate
transport derivation, not the magnitude of the answer.**

## 5. Angle 3 — is `1/alpha ≈ 11` the right input?

A single transmutation reaching `v` with `b0 = 7` would need
`1/alpha(M_Pl) ≈ 42.8` (runner §5); independently, running physical
`alpha_s(M_Z) = 0.118` **up** to `M_Pl` (1-loop, `b0 = 7`) gives
`1/alpha(M_Pl) ≈ 52.4`. The framework's bare `1/alpha ≈ 11` is `~4`–`5×` too
**strong** for a single-pole transmutation to span the hierarchy. The
framework resolves this not by a weaker derived coupling but by using the
16th **power** (§3), which *does* span `~17` decades at `1/alpha ≈ 11`. So
Angle 3's honest answer is: the EW decade count is not obtained from a
single-pole transmutation at any coupling the framework has; it is obtained
from the power-law, whose open gate is the `(4 pi)^{-16}` transport (DELTA0),
not the coupling magnitude.
