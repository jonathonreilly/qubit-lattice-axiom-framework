(||X||_2+||Y||_2)||X-Y||_2, rather than commuting rho through its weights.
It explains why the native c^2 rate in this full operator topology requires
p>=2u+4. Scalar moments can use less because they have no off-diagonal
column contribution. The resulting difference controls coherences as well
as diagonal energy weights.

There is an asymmetric extension. Put b=max(u,w)>0, b<p/2,
gamma=min(2,p/2-b), and define K_0=0 for this formula only. Interpolating
along the ray (u,w) to the endpoint max exponent p/2 gives a common-weight
trace bound 2^(2b/p)L_p^(u+w)alpha_c^(1-2b/p). Splitting the native product
once in each factor yields

    ||iota_c T_(H_c)(rho_c;u,w)iota_c^*
                      -T_(H0)(sigma_c;u,w) tensor P_H||_1
       <=[K_u^(gamma/2)+K_w^(gamma/2)]
                              b_c^gamma L_p^(u+w+gamma)
                  +2^(2b/p)L_p^(u+w)alpha_c^(1-2b/p).         (H27)

To verify the endpoint strip bound, its column exponents are pu/(2b) and
pw/(2b), each at most p/2. Holder bounds the product for each state by its
p-moment to power (u+w)/(2b). Taking the strip power 2b/p gives exactly
the displayed L_p exponent and factor of two. H24 supplies the same cutoff
limits. Thus a one-sided power and a balanced smaller power are distinct
trace ideals; their threshold depends on the largest side exponent.

For a bounded coefficient C, the balanced form
q_C(psi,phi)=<A_H^u psi,C A_H^u phi> on D(A_H^u) has an absolutely summable
normal-state expectation Tr C T_H(rho;u,u). H26 bounds the difference
uniformly on a fixed bounded coefficient ball. If the actual placed and
target coefficients differ in norm by e_C, add e_C L_p^(2u). Such a
coefficient comparison is an extra premise. These forms need not by
themselves specify a chosen selfadjoint detector or a microscopic current
with an additional spacing normalization.

## 7. Fixed, growing-time and growing-resource consequences

The preceding results are finite estimates. For given p,q,u the direct
sufficient conditions are especially transparent:

    b_c^gamma_q L_p^(1+gamma_q)->0,
    L_p alpha_c^(1/q-1/p)->0                                for W_q;

    b_c^gamma_u L_p^(2u+gamma_u)->0,
    L_p^(2u)alpha_c^(1-2u/p)->0                             for H26. (H28)

For scalar k-moments replace these by the two terms in H22. For asymmetric
weights use H27. A trace limit alone is insufficient when L_p grows.
These tests permit explicit growing preparation budgets from H6 and
variations from H10, using the actual finite alpha bound H12 or H13.
No duration or smoothness label silently makes those resources uniform.

For uniformly bounded preparation budgets and total variations, choose
p>=2d. Then L_p=O(1) and H16 gives alpha_c=O(epsilon_c), with
epsilon_c=(1+T_c)c^a tending to zero. For each fixed q>=1 and u>0, one
can choose a fixed p satisfying

    p>=2d, p>=3q, p>=2u+4.                                  (H29)

Smooth preparation from vacuum and the regular output family supply this
finite moment. H21 and H26 become

    W_q=O(c^2+epsilon_c^(1/q-1/p)),
    weighted trace at exponent u
                        =O(c^2+epsilon_c^(1-2u/p)).          (H30)

The weighted statement applies to both shifted and homogeneous operators.
For asymmetry replace u by max(u,w) in the trace exponent and require
p>=2max(u,w)+4. The scalar H22 gives
O(c^2+epsilon_c^(1-k/p)) whenever p>=k+2 and p>=2d.

Examples: at J=0, p=6 supplies W2=O(c^2+epsilon^(1/3)) and full H graph
trace O(c^2+epsilon^(2/3)). At J=1, p=10 supplies W2=O(c^2+epsilon^(2/5))
and full H graph trace O(c^2+epsilon^(4/5)). The latter preparation needs
its fifth graph budget, exactly the finite tenth-moment resource available
from P. These are bounds, not assertions of optimal rates for that protocol.

Every fixed finite q and u can therefore be treated. Increasing a chosen
fixed p makes the trace exponents arbitrarily close to 1/q and 1,
respectively. The constants and required derivative/moment budgets depend
on that choice. There is no limit p=infinity in this proof, no uniform
exponential tail and no asserted exact limiting trace power. All-moment
countercontrols below explain this distinction.

At bounded variations the sufficient density horizon is T_c c^a->0.
Constant controls incur no additional graph cost; freely extending a hold
still affects this density comparison through elapsed time unless a separate
commuting-readout argument applies. A free-energy law can remain unchanged
under free holds even when a density or coherence-sensitive weight changes.
The density horizon is not thereby proved necessary for every scalar law,
nor is arbitrary interacting holding time justified by free conservation.

For growing variation with bounded b_p, H10/H16 give L_p<=C zeta_c and
alpha_c<=C epsilon_c zeta_c^d. One explicit sufficient set for H21/H26 is

    c^gamma_q zeta_c^(1+gamma_q)->0,
    epsilon_c zeta_c^(d+pq/(p-q))->0,
    c^gamma_u zeta_c^(2u+gamma_u)->0,
    epsilon_c zeta_c^(d+2up/(p-2u))->0.                       (H31)

These follow by raising the common-trace terms in H28 to their positive
reciprocal exponents. For the saturated choice H29 the native powers are
c^2 zeta_c^3 and c^2 zeta_c^(2u+2). Retain these conditions rather than
assuming the density condition alone controls native readouts. Depending
on the orders some conditions may imply others; no optimality is claimed.
The scalar k-moment version is
c^eta_k zeta_c^(k+eta_k)->0 and
epsilon_c zeta_c^(d+kp/(p-k))->0.

With growing preparation budgets replace L_p by C zeta_c b_p and alpha by
H12/H15 in H28. For example, small c b_p and small
T_c c^a(zeta_c b_p)^d ensure the displayed high-moment density bound tends
to zero, but the extra H28 products must still be checked for readouts.
The direct resource formulation avoids pretending that a divergent moment
cap is uniform. Radius errors must be included in D_c if another radius,
a lower potential order or a moving-in-time cutoff is chosen.

The conditions are sufficient. A fixed degree-zero identity control of
arbitrarily large scalar variation changes the vacuum only by phase, so
all these comparisons can be exact while H31 fails. This is an actual even
Fock protocol within the stated class. Similarly long stationary preparation
may have a conserved interacting graph bound sharper than its integrated
commutator majorant. Neither variation nor a Bell-polynomial upper budget
is a necessary cost for every specific protocol.

### 7.1 A distinct corrected-free readout

There is also a stronger spacing comparison if the target readout is the
c-dependent corrected free F_c, rather than H0. This is a different measured
law and weight; it must not be silently substituted into H21 or H26.
Let L_J^read=F_c tensor I on the common carrier. The exact physical-band
remainder and high gap give

    0<=H_c-L_J^read<=H_c,
    H_c-L_J^read<=k_J,c H_c^(a+1),
    k_J,c=L_J c^a+Lambda_c^-a=O(c^a).                         (H31a)

Here L_J on the last line is the scalar remainder constant in H12, while
L_J^read denotes the operator. H10 was proved by first bounding the target
F_c moment, so its required moment cap is already available for this readout.
Repeat H19--H27 with the range 0<=gamma<=a: replace b_c^gamma by
k_J,c^(gamma/a), K_u^(gamma/2) by K_u^(gamma/a), and use

    gamma_q=min(a,p/q-1),
    gamma_u=min(a,p/2-u), eta_k=min(a,p-k).                   (H31b)

All common-weight trace and residual-measure terms are unchanged; only
the target weight and native bridge have changed. For example the saturated
native spacing order is c^a when p>=q(a+1) for W_q, when p>=2u+2a for a
balanced u-weight, and when p>=k+a for a scalar k-moment. The same column,
cutoff and joint-spectral proofs establish these statements. Full-low F_c
has its genuine maximal multiplication domain, including outside the band.
This supplies an optional corrected-energy theorem, not a higher-order
claim about actual H_c versus the uncorrected continuum H0 in H30.

## 8. Actual controls for sharpness and topology limits

All controls in this section use the supplied positive free-Fock carrier.
They are not invented finite-dimensional Hamiltonians. Unless stated
otherwise they are state families, possibly admitted as normal inputs to
zero preparation and zero output control. No claim is made that a single
fixed smooth protocol from the free vacuum realizes them.

### 8.1 Sharp common-law and weighted trace powers

On one fixed supplied free-Fock space choose normalized even 2n-particle
wedges chi_n with all modes in a small positive-energy momentum interval.
The interval has an infinite-dimensional one-particle L2 space, so Pauli
orthogonality does not obstruct choosing 2n modes. Taking its width to zero
makes their total energy lie in [E_n,(1+o(1))E_n], E_n->infinity. Each
individual vector belongs to every fixed energy-power domain.

Let w_n=E_n^-p, rho_n=(1-w_n)|Omega><Omega|+w_n|chi_n><chi_n|,
and sigma=|Omega><Omega|. Both are even normal densities with bounded
p-moment and trace distance delta_n=2w_n. Direct spectral integration gives

    W_q(rho_n energy law,delta_0) is of order E_n^(1-p/q),
                                    equivalently delta_n^(1/q-1/p),
    ||H^u(rho_n-sigma)H^u||_1 is of order E_n^(2u-p),
                                    equivalently delta_n^(1-2u/p).          (H32)

Shifted balanced weights have the same orders for u>0. Thus the powers
in the common terms of H21/H26 cannot be uniformly improved on this
bounded-p-moment state class. At q=p the transport remains nonzero; at
u=p/2 the balanced graph difference remains nonzero.

For asymmetric weights and a coherence test, choose a fixed normalized
even vector phi with spectral support in a compact strictly positive energy
interval and particle number disjoint from chi_n. Let

    psi_n^+/-=(phi +/- E_n^(-p/2)chi_n)/sqrt(1+E_n^-p).         (H33)

Their p-moments are bounded and their density distance is of order E_n^-p/2.
The weighted difference has two off-diagonal blocks with singular values
proportional to E_n^(u-p/2) and E_n^(w-p/2). Thus its trace norm is of
order delta_n^(1-2max(u,w)/p), and does not vanish at max(u,w)=p/2.
Using phi instead of the vacuum also tests homogeneous weights on both
sides. Their scalar energy laws are exactly identical, since spectral
functions preserve the disjoint particle/energy sectors. Scalar-law
agreement therefore cannot replace full weighted trace convergence.

For a domain control, choose even orthogonal wedges with energies comparable
to n and weights C n^(-p-1)/(log n)^2, n>=2, with their sum below one.
Add the remaining vacuum mass. This normal state has finite p-moment but
infinite k-moment for every k>p. A pure coherent square-root-weight version
has the same spectral moments. It is outside D(H^u) in mean square when
2u>p, so the higher balanced trace-class product is not even guaranteed.
It also prevents assuming W_q is finite when only a lower p<q cap is given.

### 8.2 Sharp native spacing powers

Choose two orthogonal positive high modes with fixed compact high-coordinate
momentum supports. For small c their normalized even pair chi_c is inside
the actual physical band and has total energy between two positive fixed
multiples of c^-1. Its low part is vacuum, so L chi_c=0. These are genuine
wave packets, not point eigenstates in continuous momentum space.

For rho_c=(1-c^p)|Omega><Omega|+c^p|chi_c><chi_c|, the shifted p-moment is
uniform. Its unconditional low marginal is vacuum and

    W_q(actual H_c law, its L law) is of order c^(p/q-1).       (H34)

This saturates gamma_q=p/q-1 when p<3q. It is also an actual-to-matched-
low-target comparison at initial time, with no control dynamics assumed.

For the full operator bridge choose instead a fixed low even pair phi,
with compact momentum support away from zero, and use
psi_c=sqrt(1-c^p)phi+c^(p/2)chi_c. The p-moment remains bounded.
The off-diagonal block of H_c^u rho_c H_c^u between the high and low pair
subspaces has trace norm of order c^(p/2-u). The corresponding block of
L^u rho_c L^u vanishes when u>0, since L chi_c=0. For shifted weights its
subtracted block is only order c^(p/2), leaving the same leading difference.
Thus the native weighted power gamma_u=p/2-u is attained when it is below
two. The low marginal is the stated low pair/vacuum mixture. Coherence,
not a large scalar p-moment, is responsible for this resource threshold.

The saturated power two has a separate actual lower control. Choose a
fixed low pair wave packet supported in compact nonzero momenta. The
checked J=0 scalar coefficient is

    e_(L,c)(xi)-E(xi)=c^2 v^2 xi^4/(8E(xi))+O(c^4)                (H35)

uniformly there. Its native energy mean difference is a positive constant
times c^2. W_q>=W1>=the absolute mean difference for q>=1. For u>0 the
native homogeneous or shifted weighted trace is bounded below by its
positive trace difference, also a positive constant times c^2 by the
same scalar expansion. Every fixed moment is uniformly bounded in this
example. Thus a generally faster native power cannot follow from arbitrarily
many moments while still comparing actual H_c with uncorrected H0.
Section 7.1 explicitly gives a different corrected-free readout theorem;
it does not remove this H0 comparison floor.

### 8.3 All finite moments do not supply an order-infinity modulus

In the fixed-carrier wedge construction of 8.1 take w_n=exp(-n), with energy
E_n comparable to n. For every fixed p the moments w_n E_n^p are uniformly
bounded and tend to zero, while delta_n=2exp(-n). Nevertheless

    W_q/delta_n^(1/q) is of order n,
    ||H^u(rho_n-sigma)H^u||_1/delta_n is of order n^(2u).       (H36)

Thus all fixed moment caps alone cannot upgrade the common-law modulus to
a uniform exact delta^(1/q), or a positive balanced weight to exact delta.
Constants for each separate moment may grow. This does not rule out a
better rate for a particular prepared protocol with additional tail control,
and is not a lower bound against the sum containing the separate c^2 term.

### 8.4 Energy-law total variation remains different

At each c take a normalized even low pair with the two momentum supports
of width o(c^2), centered at distinct fixed nonzero momenta. Its actual
H_c and continuum H0 energy laws have widths o(c^2), while H35 separates
their centers by a positive constant times c^2. For small c their compact
energy supports are disjoint. Their total variation is one, although all
fixed moments are uniformly bounded, the placed actual and matched low
density are identical at time zero, and every finite W_q tends to zero.
This is an actual c-dependent packet family, not a fixed spatial preparation.
It also prevents an unqualified uniform CDF conclusion at moving sharp
thresholds. No statement about a selected physical detector is inferred.

### 8.5 What these controls do not exclude

Under a bounded p-moment plus uniform integrability of the p-energy tails,
trace convergence can recover the resource endpoint W_p (p>=1) and weighted
max exponent p/2. At a fixed energy cutoff all weights are bounded and
the native difference tends to zero by H18/H20. The tails are controlled
by the assumed uniform integrability; mixed products use Cauchy--Schwarz
on their columns. Sending c to zero at fixed cutoff, then the cutoff to
infinity proves convergence. For moving targets this is a uniform tail
condition, not merely a numeric moment cap. A bounded higher moment is
one sufficient way to obtain it, exactly the route used above.

These positive alternatives remain open; no universal no-go is asserted.
The pure-state signs in H33 show why energy-law convergence alone cannot
recover quantum coherence. Conversely a free relative-phase density
counterexample does not show that scalar energy laws change under a free
hold. N's long-time coherent free examples restrict a universal density
extension beyond its kinetic horizon, while a moment-only argument does
not settle the optimal law or protocol-specific horizon.
