## Coupled residual packet

The open obligations are distinct:

1. **Active-block response `(G1a)`.** Derive or separately supply a compatible
   irreducible `Cl_4(C)`/two-mode-CAR response on `P_A H_cell`.
2. **Physical channel law `(G1b)`.** Derive or separately supply the normal and
   self-dual-tangent edge dispersions, tangent symbol, and Widom
   applicability/normalization conditions. CAR algebra does not select them.
3. **Gravitational boundary/action identification.** Separately justify or
   supply the row-local premise identifying the primitive coefficient with the
   gravitational/Wald carrier.
4. **Action-unit metrology `(G2)`.** Break the
   `(S,kappa) -> (lambda S,lambda kappa)` freedom and identify an absolute
   dimensional action unit; a dimensionless phase convention is insufficient.

These obligations are not proven equivalent. In particular, a supplied
Clifford/CAR response does not by itself give `c_Widom=1/4`, identify the
Wald carrier, fix `G_Newton,lat`, derive `a/l_P=1` from the four axioms, or
unlock a numerical `R_Lambda` chain.

## Research routes that remain open

- **Changed action or spinor packet:** find a representation on the active
  block whose spatial restriction is compatible with the substrate action.
- **Intrinsic active block:** derive a metric-compatible `M_4(C)` response law
  directly, without descending the canonical exterior odd generators.
- **Graph-first or taste route:** obtain the active response from a larger
  native graph/`C^8`/taste construction and prove its restriction.
- **Physical edge law:** derive the normal/tangent dispersions and half-zone
  selector from a response or locality principle rather than CAR dimension.
- **Gravitational carrier law:** derive the boundary/action identification
  required by the conditional source-unit algebra.
- **Action metrology:** identify an absolute action unit using a physical
  response law that removes common rescaling freedom.

The strongest positive route would combine an intrinsic or graph-first
active-block theorem with an equivariant coframe response, then independently
derive the edge-channel/Widom, gravitational-carrier, and action-metrology
legs. A successful result must state every additional condition explicitly.
