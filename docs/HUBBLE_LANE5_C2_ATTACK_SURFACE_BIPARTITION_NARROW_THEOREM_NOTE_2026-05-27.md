## 2. Why the bipartition holds

### 2.1 The bounded matter-cascade slice has exactly two `(C2)` pins

From `OMEGA_LAMBDA_DERIVATION_NOTE.md` / `COSMOLOGY_FROM_MASS_SPECTRUM_NOTE.md`:

```text
   -> Omega_Lambda = 1 - Omega_m - Omega_r
```

The only `(C2)`-counted observational pins inside this bounded
matter-cascade slice are `eta` (imported) and `alpha_GUT` (bounded in
`[0.03, 0.05]`). The radiation fraction
`Omega_r,0` is a separately-derived quantity carrying its own retained
structural support (`N_eff = 3.046` from three retained generations per
`N_EFF_FROM_THREE_GENERATIONS_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-04-24.md`) and is
*comparator-precision* observational only via `T_CMB` and `h`, which
themselves sit on Lane 5's `(C1)` side or on the deferred 5E inflation
target. `Omega_r,0` is therefore not counted as a `(C2)` pin by this
bounded matter-cascade slice.

The BBN factor `3.6515e-3 * eta_10` is a textbook standard-physics
arithmetic relation, not an extra cascade-internal pin: it is an
*identity* relating `Omega_b * h^2` to `eta_10`.

The flatness statement `F: Omega_total = 1` is cited separately on the
`S^3` spatial-topology / inflation surface; this note does not audit or

### 2.2 Retiring either pin alone narrows but does not close `(C2)`

`(C2)` per Cycle-3 no-go requires retiring at least one cosmic-history
ratio. On the cascade above, retiring `eta` while leaving `alpha_GUT`
bounded gives `Omega_b` framework-derived but `R` still bounded over
`[~4.8, ~5.3]` — `(C2)` advanced but not yet closed; `Omega_Lambda`
remains a bounded interval. Symmetrically, retiring `alpha_GUT` while
leaving `eta` imported gives `R` framework-derived but `Omega_b` still
imported — `(C2)` advanced but not yet closed.

Full single-number closure of this cascade (i.e., a fully retained
matter-cascade output for `Omega_Lambda`) therefore requires retiring **both**
`P1` and `P2`.

But for the purpose of the `(C2)`-class definition in the Cycle-3
no-go ("retire `eta`, `alpha_GUT`, or `T_CMB`-equivalent observational
pins from the bounded cascade"), retiring **either alone** discharges
the `(C2)`-class requirement: at least one cosmic-history ratio is
retained from framework rather than imported. The cascade still has a
residual bounded numerical output, but the `(C2)` premise discharge is
satisfied.
