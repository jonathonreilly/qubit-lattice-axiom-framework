## Theorem

**Theorem (structural ratio and normalized two-block pattern).** Let

    Y(alpha,beta) = alpha P_sym + beta P_anti

be a central block-scalar operator on the last-two-factor space and let its
embedding on V be I_2 tensor Y(alpha,beta). Then:

1. P_sym and P_anti are complementary orthogonal projectors with ranks 3 and
   1 on the last-two-factor space, hence ranks 6 and 2 on V.
2. Tracelessness on V gives

       6 alpha + 2 beta = 0,

   so beta=-3 alpha.
3. The traceless central block-scalar line is therefore one-dimensional and
   is generated by

       Y_0 = P_sym - 3 P_anti.

   Its two eigenvalues stand in the ratio +1:(-3) on (2,3)+(2,1).
4. On the explicitly supplied scale alpha=1/3,

       Y = (1/3) P_sym - P_anti,

   with eigenvalue +1/3 on the six-dimensional (2,3) block and -1 on the
   two-dimensional (2,1) block.

No label beyond these representation classes appears in the theorem.

## Proof

Because SWAP_23 is a self-adjoint involution, (I+SWAP_23)/2 and
(I-SWAP_23)/2 are complementary orthogonal projectors. The symmetric and
antisymmetric subspaces of C^2 tensor C^2 have dimensions 3 and 1. Tensoring
with the first C^2 factor doubles those multiplicities to 6 and 2.

A central block-scalar operator is determined by one scalar on each block.
Its trace on V is 6 alpha + 2 beta. The trace-zero kernel of this nonzero
linear functional has dimension one and is spanned by (alpha,beta)=(1,-3).
Substitution of alpha=1/3 gives the normalized eigenvalues (+1/3,-1).
