## 2. Exact balance with physical charge leakage

Let \(\sigma(t)\) be the actual state on \(S A R\), with arbitrary initial correlations. Its old marginals at \(0,t_a,t_b,T\) are \(\sigma_0,\sigma_a,\sigma_b,\sigma_f\). Define the signed dimensionless transfers

\quad
z_p=\frac{i}{\hbar}\int_{I_p}\operatorname{Tr}\sigma(t)[E(t),N]dt,
\quad
z_-=\frac{i}{\hbar}\int_{I_-}\operatorname{Tr}\sigma(t)[E(t),B]dt.
\tag{7}
\]

\[
\langle B\rangle_a-\langle B\rangle_0=z_+,

=\operatorname{Tr}K(\sigma_f-\sigma_0)
 -\operatorname{Tr}H_*(\sigma_b-\sigma_a)+\Xi.

\tag{8}

\tag{14}

### A separate global-norm certificate without a reservoir-width factor

Suppose a residual \(E(t)\) has charge bandwidth at most the integer \(r_Q\geq0\): its matrix elements between \(Q\)-eigenspaces vanish when their integer eigenvalues differ by more than \(r_Q\). \[
E_d=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}
     e^{i\theta Q}E e^{-i\theta Q}e^{-id\theta}d\theta.
\tag{15}
\]

For \(d\ne0\), scalar subtraction gives \(\|E_d\|\leq\inf_c\|E-cI\|\leq g\). Also \([Q,E_d]=dE_d\), and only \(-r_Q\leq d\leq r_Q\) occur. Therefore the elementary finite sum gives

\[
\boxed{\ \|[E,Q]\|\leq r_Q(r_Q+1)g.\ }
\tag{16}
\]

This constant is deliberately conservative; no optimal operator Bernstein inequality is imported. The proof uses only finite integer-charge decomposition and unitary norm invariance. It is valid for noncommuting residual terms and does not require simultaneous diagonalization of \(E\) or \(K\).

With a common bandwidth bound \(r\), and \(\eta_j=\hbar^{-1}\int_{I_j}g(t)dt\),

\[
|\Xi|\leq\Lambda_{\rm glob}:=
\Delta r(r+1)(\eta_++\eta_p+\eta_-).
\tag{17}
\]

The stronger available estimate among (14), (17), or their pointwise minimum can be used. A global norm is not the sum of the local residual norms: \(\inf_c\|E-cI\|\leq\sum_X b_X\), so choosing \(g=\sum_Xb_X\) is sufficient, potentially loose. A separately certified global bound may be tighter. Equation (16) is what permits a supplied small global norm to give a width-independent charge estimate under bounded bandwidth. Without that structural hypothesis, the trivial bound involves the full charge width instead.

\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
|W_{F_0}-W_V|\leq{}
 &\frac{\omega_K+q\Delta}{2}(\delta_0+\delta_f)\\
 &+q\Delta(\eta_++\eta_-)
  +\Delta r(r+1)(\eta_++\eta_p+\eta_-).
\end{aligned}}\tag{18}
\]

For genuinely onsite/nearest-neighbor residuals \(r=2\) suffices, so the last coefficient is \(6\Delta\), and the ramp coefficient is \((q+6)\Delta\). None depends on the reservoir length. The result is conditional on the residual norms and fixed support/bandwidth; it is not an estimate from final trace error alone.

\tag{25}
\]

The last bound follows from charge centering as in (13). This is a conditional algebraic observation for the supplied model, not a derivation or supplier of a dissipator. It can bound baseline energy transfer, but actual prescribed-drive work now also needs the heat term

## 8. Exact counterexamples to stronger replacements

### 8.1 Small full trace error alone does not control energy uniformly

On \(m\) resource qubits compare \(|0^m\rangle\) with
\(|\psi\rangle=\sqrt{1-p}|0^m\rangle+\sqrt p|1^m\rangle\). The unhalved trace distance of their pure-state density matrices is \(2\sqrt p\), while their \(\Delta B\) means differ by \(m\Delta p\). With fixed trace error \(\varepsilon=2\sqrt p\), the discrepancy is \(m\Delta\varepsilon^2/4\). Tensoring an unchanged old/reference state leaves all old errors zero. This refutes a universal work/energy replacement by trace error alone; it says nothing against (18), whose physical residual/bandwidth budget has not been supplied for these states.

### 8.2 Constant local fault strength can give extensive leakage on the actual sine packet

 \sin\frac{\pi\ell}{L+1}|1^\ell0^{M-\ell}\rangle.

\[
p_j=\frac2n\sum_{\ell=j}^L\sin^2(\pi\ell/n)
\leq\frac2n(L-j+1)\leq\frac14.
\tag{27}
\]

The packet is real in the number basis, so \(\langle Y_j\rangle=0\). During a nominal conserving idle plateau choose a smooth nonnegative envelope \(\dot\theta(t)\) of total area \(\theta\in(0,\pi/2)\), zero at both ends, and resource-only positive onsite residuals

\[

 e^{-i\Delta t n_j/\hbar}X_j e^{i\Delta t n_j/\hbar}\right],
\quad j\in J.
\tag{28}
\]

An absolute-time phase shift accounts for any preceding resource drift; equivalently begin this formula in the known interaction-picture coordinate of the packet. Place the envelope inside an idle interval and end it before the final number-phase slot. This is an explicit coherent fault example, not a claim that the required reference is native. \[

 =\sin^2\theta\sum_{j\in J}(1-2p_j)
 \geq\tfrac{s}{2}\sin^2\theta.
\tag{29}
