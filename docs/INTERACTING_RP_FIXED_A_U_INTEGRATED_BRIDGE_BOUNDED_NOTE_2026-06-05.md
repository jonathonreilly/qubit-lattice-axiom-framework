## 2. The crux handled honestly: entanglement survives `U`-integration

Because the fermion form (iii) is a reflection-square `||B[U] O Omega||^2 >= 0`
**pointwise** at each fixed `U`, and the determinant weight (ii) and the Haar
measure (v) are non-negative pointwise, the integrand at each `U` is `>= 0`
**before** the `U`-integral. The gauge-fermion entanglement means the
`U`-dependence cannot be pre-factored out — but it **need not** be: a
Haar-`U`-average (reflection-symmetric, positive measure `e^{-S_G} det(M)`) of a
pointwise-nonnegative function is nonnegative. The only place the cross-cut gauge
coupling enters beyond a positive measure is the **off-diagonal** link coupling
`K(U_a, U_b)` across the plane; that is exactly the H1 PSD kernel, itself a sum
of reflection-squares, so the assembled `G_ij = sum_{a,b} K[a,b] <psi_i(a)|
psi_j(b)>` is a manifest reflection-square. The genuine entanglement is verified
nonvacuous: the combined transfer has **operator-Schmidt rank `> 1`** (runner: 8
for `U(1)`, 5 for `SU(2)`, 18 for `SU(3)`), and the entangled Gram differs from
the naive `U`-diagonal factorized form by a large relative gap (`0.55`-`0.83`),
confirming the cross-cut coupling is real (consistent with the `#2399`
entanglement diagnostic).
