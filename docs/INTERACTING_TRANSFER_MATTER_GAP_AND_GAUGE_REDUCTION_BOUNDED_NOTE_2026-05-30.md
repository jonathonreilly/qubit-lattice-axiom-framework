## 2. The matter-sector uniform gap (T1)

At a fixed SU(3) background `U`, the canonical staggered spatial hop `H_hop[U]`
(staggered phases `η_k`, link variables `U`) is anti-Hermitian, so its spectrum is
`{iλ_j(U)}` with `λ_j(U) ∈ ℝ`. The cited rungs give the one-fermion sector of the
2-step transfer matrix as `e^{-2E_j[U]}` with

```text
    E_j[U] = arcsinh( √(m² + λ_j(U)²) ) .
```

Because `√(m² + λ_j²) ≥ √(m²) = m` for **every** real `λ_j` and **every** `U`, and
`arcsinh` is strictly increasing,

```text
    E_j[U] ≥ arcsinh(m) > 0    for all j, all U   (m > 0).
```

The matter-sector gap is the energy of the lowest one-fermion excitation above the
empty Fock vacuum (`E_0 = 0`): `Δ_T^matter = (1/a_τ)\,min_j E_j[U] ≥ arcsinh(m)/a_τ`.

**Uniformity.** The bound `m` is the `λ=0` floor of `√(m²+λ²)`; it is a lower bound
**independent of the actual hop spectrum**, hence independent of `U`. It is also
independent of spatial volume: it is an infimum of the single-particle dispersion,
not a Perron/finite-dimensional non-degeneracy statement, so it survives the
thermodynamic limit verbatim (adding any number of fermions costs `≥ arcsinh(m)`
for the first one). This **upgrades** the *sampled* per-config gap (the
gauge-extension note exhibits `min Ĥ[U]=0.481` over 200 random backgrounds) to an
*analytic, background-uniform, volume-uniform* lower bound `arcsinh(m)`.

**Runner checks:** `matter_dispersion_form`, `uniform_lower_bound_arcsinh_m`,

> The open content of `Δ_T>0` is the **pure-gauge SU(3) glueball gap

> which a full proof must also control).

`≈0.42`), and the thermodynamic Fisher zeros lie at `|β|≈5.5 < 6`. So `β=6` is at or
past the nearest singularity: **no convergent strong-coupling expansion reaches it**,
and `Δ_gauge(β=6)` is the open nonperturbative confinement gap. This is the spectral
twin of the open β=6 ⟨P⟩ singularity (the same undetermined `ρ_{p,q}(6)`).

**Therefore the full interacting thermodynamic `Δ_T>0` at `β=6` is a wall**, recorded
here, not claimed. What this note delivers is the matter-sector discharge (T1) and
