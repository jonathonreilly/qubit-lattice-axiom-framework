## Claim

Adopt the qubit-reframe of A1 as in
[`MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-05-20.md`](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-05-20.md): the
per-site operator algebra is `M_2(ℂ) ≅ Cl(3,0)` as real algebras,
with Cl(3,0) generated by three anticommuting self-adjoint
generators `γ_1, γ_2, γ_3` satisfying `γ_i² = +I` and `γ_i γ_j +
γ_j γ_i = 0` for `i ≠ j`. Let `H_x = ℂ²` be the per-site Hilbert
space carrying the canonical Pauli realization `γ_i = σ_i` (retained
via [`CL3_PAULI_IRREP_UNIQUENESS_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md`](CL3_PAULI_IRREP_UNIQUENESS_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md)).
Two natural Lie-group images act on H_x:

- **Internal SU(2):** the unique two-dimensional faithful irreducible
  representation of `su(2)`, generated on H_x by `S_i = σ_i / 2`
  (retained via [`PER_SITE_SU2_SPIN_HALF_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-02.md`](PER_SITE_SU2_SPIN_HALF_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-02.md)).
  The Casimir is `S² = (3/4) I`, identifying H_x with the `j = 1/2`
  irrep.
- **Spatial Spin(3):** the double cover of the spatial rotation group
  `SO(3)` acting on the 3-dim Euclidean space `V = span_ℝ(γ_1, γ_2,
  γ_3) ≅ ℝ³` underlying the (3,0) signature of Cl(3,0). The
  universal property of Clifford algebras lifts every `R ∈ O(3)`
  uniquely to an `ℝ`-algebra automorphism `φ_R: Cl(3,0) → Cl(3,0)`
  via `γ_i ↦ Σ_j R_{ij} γ_j`.

**Theorem (Internal-External Merger).** On the canonical Pauli
realization, the internal SU(2) and the spatial Spin(3) lift act on
H_x = ℂ² via **the same Lie-algebra generator data**:

1. **(M1) Internal su(2) is the bivector subalgebra.** The bivector
   triple `B_i := (1/2) γ_j γ_k` for `(i, j, k)` cyclic in `(1, 2, 3)`
   closes under commutator into a Lie algebra isomorphic to `su(2)`.
   On the Pauli realization, `B_i = (i/2) σ_i = i · S_i`, so the
   bivector triple and the spin triple span the **same complex Lie
   algebra** on `H_x = ℂ²` (with `B_i = i S_i`).

2. **(M2) Spatial SO(3) acts on bivectors as the vector
   representation.** For every `R ∈ O(3)`, the universal-property
   automorphism `φ_R` restricted to the bivector subspace satisfies

   ```text
   φ_R(B_i)  =  (cof R)_{ij} B_j  =  det(R) · R_{ij} B_j .
   ```

   For proper `R` (det = +1), this is `φ_R(B_i) = R_{ij} B_j`; for
   improper `R` (det = −1), the parity sign appears. On the cubic
   subgroup `O_h ⊂ O(3)` (48 elements), the action is by signed
   permutations.

3. **(M3) Infinitesimal coincidence.** Differentiating the spatial
   rotation flow `R(t) = exp(t J_i)` (with `[J_i]_{ab} = -ε_{iab}`)
   at the identity gives an infinitesimal action on H_x generated by
   `S_i = σ_i / 2`. The bivector flow `exp(t B_i)` on H_x produces
   the same SU(2) one-parameter subgroup, parametrized by `t ↔ t`
   under `B_i = i S_i`. Both flows are generated by the **same
   operators** on `H_x = ℂ²`; the rotation direction relation is
   `exp(t B_i) σ_a exp(−t B_i) = φ_{R(t)^{−1}}(σ_a)`.

4. **(M4) Cubic O_h lifts onto H_x.** Each of the 48 elements of
   `O_h` lifts to a sandwich action on H_x via the universal property:

   - 24 proper rotations: `U(R) σ_a U(R)^* = R_{ab} σ_b` with `U(R) ∈
     SU(2)` (kernel `{±I}` of the double cover);
   - 24 improper rotations: `−U(R') σ_a U(R')^* = R_{ab} σ_b` where
     `R = −R'` and `R' ∈ SO(3)` is the proper component.

   The image of the lift in `End(H_x)` is a finite subgroup of order
   48 (the full cubic Pin(3) ⊂ End(ℂ²) lift), so the cubic group
   acts on the qubit Hilbert space directly through A1's universal
   property — no separate "internal/external" identification step is
   required.

5. **(M5) Pauli equivariance is the merger statement.** For every
   proper `R ∈ O_h`, the spin lift `U(R) ∈ SU(2)` satisfies
   `U(R) σ_i U(R)^* = R_{ij} σ_j`. This **single equation** is
   simultaneously:

   - the standard `SO(3) → SU(2)` double cover (spatial side), and
   - the conjugation action of internal SU(2) on the qubit operator
     algebra `M_2(ℂ)` (internal side).

   The two sides are not isomorphic-but-distinct; they are the
   **same Lie-group map** read through the universal property.

## Honest residual

The merger collapses the "internal SU(2) ≅ spatial Spin(3)
identification" step into a corollary of A1's universal-property
content. The residual content that survives this collapse is:

- **Identification of the (3,0) signature with physical 3D
  Euclidean space.** The label "(3,0)" of `Cl(3,0)` names the
  algebra `M_2(ℂ)` as a real algebra of dimension 8 with a
  positive-definite quadratic form on a 3-dim real vector space.
  Whether this 3-dim real vector space IS physical 3D Euclidean
  space (or merely an abstract algebraic 3-dim ℝ-vector space with
  a positive-definite form) is a frame-identification statement,
  not an algebraic derivation.

  Under the **standard reading of Clifford-algebra signature** —
  i.e., the (p, q) labels are interpreted as physical
  (positive-norm, negative-norm) spatial dimensions — this
  identification is automatic. Without it, the merger is purely
  algebraic: the abstract 3-dim ℝ-vector space `V = span_ℝ(γ_i)`
  inside Cl(3,0) carries the SO(3) action.

- **Spatial-substrate scale.** This is the same scale parameter `a > 0`
  carried by `M_Pl`-pinning (separate lane) and by (P1) discrete
  translations of [`CUBIC_BRAVAIS_FORCING_FROM_CL3_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-27.md`](CUBIC_BRAVAIS_FORCING_FROM_CL3_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-27.md).

The merger does **not** eliminate (P1) or (P2) of the cubic-Bravais
companion: (P1) discrete translations is genuinely a separate
substrate primitive (Cl(3,0) is the algebra of *continuous* ℝ³);
(P2) generator-axis primitivity is a single-frame convention that
**(M2)+(M5) of this theorem render automatic** once the (3,0)
signature is read in its standard physical sense.
