## Exact Objects

Window `W={x,y}`. Menu `M={A,B}`. A configuration is a map

`J : W → {0} ∪ M`

with `0` the vacant token. This is the reconstructed C1 occupancy
arithmetic: one letter per site, vacancy written `0`, and permanence
forcing occupancy to be nondecreasing. A unit lock is one occupied site.
Scalar `I` of a configuration is the number of occupied sites, which is
additive over pairwise-disjoint unit locks and satisfies `I(empty)=0`.

A two-step addition is three snapshots `t=0,1,2`:

empty → one unit lock → two unit locks.

Two legal addition orders, both locking `A`:

- `H_xy`: `(0,0) → (A,0) → (A,A)`
- `H_yx`: `(0,0) → (0,A) → (A,A)`

Tuples are ordered as `(J(x), J(y))`. Histories are generated by
applying site-local additions to the empty map; `I` is then read off `J`,
not stored separately.

The **I-stack** of a history is the triple of scalar `I` at the three
snapshots. The **J-stack** is the triple of maps `J:W→{0}∪M`. The
site-blind content bag of a snapshot is the multiset of locked letters,
written `{∅}` when empty.

Identity gates call `I_stack(H_xy)`, `I_stack(H_yx)`, `J_stack(H_xy)`,
and `J_stack(H_yx)`.

## Theorems

### Theorem 1

`I(H_xy)=(0,1,2)=I(H_yx)`. Any site-blind content bag is
`({∅}, {A}, {A,A})` on both. The I-stack does not split addition order.

*Proof.* Each history begins empty, so `I=0` and the bag is `{∅}`. The
first addition locks one site to `A`, so `I=1` and the bag is `{A}`. The
second addition locks the remaining site to `A`, so `I=2` and the bag is
`{A,A}`. Site labels are discarded by both `I` and the bag. ∎

The mutation predicate "`I_stack(H_xy)` differs from `I_stack(H_yx)`"
fails.

### Theorem 2

`J(H_xy)=((0,0),(A,0),(A,A)) ≠ ((0,0),(0,A),(A,A))=J(H_yx)`.
The mid-step differs. The J-stack splits them.

*Proof.* `H_xy` occupies `x` first, so the mid-step is `(A,0)`. `H_yx`
occupies `y` first, so the mid-step is `(0,A)`. The maps are unequal as
functions `W→{0}∪M`. ∎

The mutation predicate "`J_stack(H_xy)=J_stack(H_yx)`" fails.

### Theorem 3

Quote current Record: “Records form,” permanence, content-only readout,
additive `I`. Those sentences allow both orders as finite growing
collections of pairwise-disjoint unit locks. They do not name a
site-indexed stack. “Records form” is existence, not a J-process.

*Proof.* Both `H_xy` and `H_yx` are finite collections of pairwise-disjoint
unit locks, occupancy is nondecreasing, each lock is one admissible
letter, and scalar `I` is the count of locks. Nothing in the quoted
sentences selects the mid-step site or names `J`. ∎
