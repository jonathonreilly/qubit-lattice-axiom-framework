# KCPT Dirac-even algebra reach: the H-invariant part of the lattice-Dirac word algebra ⟨D2, J_full⟩ equals C[M] and reaches four of the six commutant dimensions, and the two missing charges — the one separating 12⁺ ↔ 12⁻ and the one separating the two induced-12 — are character-level isotypic projectors

**Type:** bounded_theorem
**Date:** 2026-07-21
**Lane:** KCPT (periodic L = 4, N = 64 staggered qubit lattice on the 4³ torus)

## Abstract

On the finite L = 4, N = 64 staggered construction, the even-degree (H-invariant) part of the lattice-Dirac word algebra generated by the antisymmetric adjacency D2 and the total complex structure J_full equals the mass-square polynomial algebra C[M] = span{I, M, M², M³}, of dimension 4. The full commutant End_H(C⁶⁴) of the order-1536 group H = ⟨G_amb, S_eps⟩ is six-dimensional, so the even Dirac algebra reaches four of the six block-scalar directions and misses exactly two. This note locates those two missing directions and constructs the charges that supply them. The gap is not accidental: C[M] is constant on each M-shell, and M has exactly two intra-shell degeneracies — the CP-split pair {12⁺, 12⁻}, both sitting at M-shell −4, and the two induced-12 constituents, both sitting at M-shell −8. The two charges that resolve these degeneracies are character-level isotypic (Frobenius) projectors. The result is positive and constructive: the charge separating 12⁺ from 12⁻ is not any polynomial in the lattice-Dirac operators but a genuinely group-theoretic object — χ_sgn-odd, living in End_H but outside C[M]. This note records that location and the explicit construction, with every load-bearing quantity checked by the companion runner under live wrong-value and parity rejectors.

## Setup

The parent decomposition, carried over from the linked Unit-14 note, splits the complexified lattice space into six multiplicity-free H-constituents:

`C^64 = 8 + 8 + 12 + 12 + 12⁺ + 12⁻`,

with H = ⟨G_amb, S_eps⟩ of order 1536, the order-768 ambient group G_amb sitting as an index-2 subgroup and the chiral-parity involution S_eps = diag((−1)^{x₁+x₂+x₃}) generating the coset. Because the six constituents are pairwise inequivalent irreducibles, the commutant End_H(C⁶⁴) is six-dimensional and every H-invariant operator is block-scalar (Schur): it acts as one complex scalar on each constituent.

The mass square M = D2² is the diagonalizable object whose spectrum stratifies the lattice into four shells λ_m = −4m (m = 0, 1, 2, 3), with multiplicities {0: 8, −4: 24, −8: 24, −12: 8}. Its polynomial algebra C[M] = span{I, M, M², M³} is four-dimensional and, by construction, constant on each M-shell. The two induced-8 constituents fall on the extreme shells 0 and −12; the two induced-12 both fall on shell −8; and the split pair {12⁺, 12⁻} both fall on shell −4. From the linked Unit-16 note we also carry the χ_sgn-covariance of the symplectic leg and the twist relation between the split halves, `12⁻ ≅ 12⁺ ⊗ χ_sgn` — the fact that 12⁻ is 12⁺ tensored with the order-two quotient character χ_sgn of H/G_amb.

## T1 — the commutant is six-dimensional

Taking Hermitian-orthonormal bases Z_i of the six complexified constituents and forming the constituent characters χ_i(h) = tr(Z_iᵀ̄ ρ(h) Z_i) over all 1536 elements h ∈ H, the character Gram (1/|H|) Σ_h χ_i(h) χ_j(h)‾ equals the identity I₆. All six constituents are therefore irreducible and pairwise inequivalent, and dim End_H(C⁶⁴) = 6. This is the target dimension against which the Dirac-even reach is measured. [gate G-T1-CHARORTHO]

## T2 — the even lattice-Dirac algebra equals C[M]

Both generators are G_amb-invariant, and each is S_eps-odd: S_eps D2 S_eps = −D2 and S_eps J_full S_eps = −J_full. An even-length word in {D2, J_full} therefore carries an even number of S_eps-odd factors, so it is S_eps-even and, being also G_amb-invariant, is H-invariant — hence block-scalar. Conversely an odd-length word is S_eps-odd and cannot be H-invariant; the parity grading excludes it from End_H. These are the live rejectors that keep the grading honest. [gates G-REJ-D2ODD, G-REJ-JODD, G-REJ-PEVEN]

The block-scalar span of all 85 even words up to degree 6 is exactly the four-dimensional C[M]: the coordinate vectors of {I, M, M², M³} already have rank 4, and adjoining every even word leaves the rank at 4. Every even word equals its block-scalar reconstruction to machine precision. So the even part of the word algebra is C[M], no more and no less. [gates G-T2-CMRANK, G-T2-EVENREACH, G-T2-EVENBLOCK]

The radius operator makes this self-contained. Since [D2, J_full] = 0, the product P := D2·J_full is symmetric — Pᵀ = J_fullᵀ D2ᵀ = (−J_full)(−D2) = J_full·D2 = D2·J_full = P, using that D2 and J_full are antisymmetric and commute — and squares to P² = D2·J_full·D2·J_full = D2²·J_full² = M·(−I) = −M. Thus P = −√(−M) is a function of M, an element of C[M] and not an independent direction. This is the same radius operator studied in the Dirac-radius unit `KCPT_DIRAC_RADIUS_GRADING_OF_END_H_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-07-20.md`; here it is rebuilt from the commutation identity rather than imported. [gate G-T2-PROOT]

## T3 — the reach gap is the two intra-shell degeneracies

The reach gap is dim End_H − dim C[M] = 6 − 4 = 2. Because C[M] is constant on each M-shell, it cannot tell apart two constituents that share a shell. M has exactly two such coincidences: the CP-split pair {12⁺, 12⁻} both sit at M-shell −4 (m = 1), and the two induced-12 both sit at M-shell −8 (m = 2). The two induced-8 constituents sit at the distinct shells 0 and −12 and are therefore already separated by M itself. The count of intra-shell degeneracies is 2, matching the reach gap exactly: the two directions C[M] misses are the two the shells cannot resolve. [gates G-T3-MSHELLS, G-T3-DEGENPAIRS, G-GAP]

## T4 — the two missing charges are character-level

The two missing block-scalar directions are supplied by character-level isotypic projectors. For constituent i the Frobenius projector

P_i = (d_i / |H|) Σ_h χ_i(h)‾ ρ(h)

is built purely from the character and the group action; on this multiplicity-free decomposition it equals the constituent projector exactly. Adjoining the CP-pair's isotypic projector to C[M] and the even words raises the reach from 4 to 5, and adjoining an induced-12 isotypic projector raises it from 5 to 6 = dim End_H. Each isotypic charge is block-scalar 1 on its own constituent and 0 on its M-degenerate partner, so it separates the pair that no polynomial in {D2, J_full} can. The CP-charge distinguishing 12⁺ from 12⁻ is thereby constructed explicitly, as an End_H element outside C[M]. [gates G-T4-ISOEQ, G-T4-RANK5, G-T4-RANK6, G-T4-SEPSPLIT, G-T4-SEPIND]

## T5 — why the CP-charge is group-theoretic

The reason the CP-charge cannot be a Dirac-even polynomial is structural. M is CP-even: S_eps M S_eps = M, so every f(M) — the entire algebra C[M] — commutes past S_eps unchanged and is blind to the χ_sgn twist. The split pair, on the other hand, are χ_sgn-twists of one another: χ_{12⁻}(h) = χ_sgn(h)·χ_{12⁺}(h) for all h ∈ H, carried over from the linked Unit-16 note. Distinguishing 12⁺ from 12⁻ therefore requires an operator that is χ_sgn-odd — one that changes sign across the S_eps coset — and C[M] contains no such operator. The isotypic projector supplies exactly this χ_sgn-odd content, which is why the CP-charge is genuinely group-theoretic rather than Dirac-polynomial. [gates G-T5-MCPEVEN, G-T5-TWIST]

## Anti-fabrication discipline

J_full, M, S_eps, and G_amb are rebuilt only from the shell/kernel/lattice machinery, never from a parity or sign proxy. The commutant dimension is decided by the real six-character orthonormality Gram (multiplicity-free ⇒ dimension 6), never assumed. The Dirac-even reach is the real numeric rank of the block-scalar coordinate vectors of C[M] together with all 85 even words up to degree 6; the parity grading carries live anticommutation rejectors, so an odd word cannot masquerade as H-invariant. The radius identities P² = −M and Pᵀ = P are real max-abs norms, re-derived here from [D2, J_full] = 0 rather than imported. The isotypic separations are real block-scalar coordinates (1 versus 0), and the χ_sgn twist is a real per-element character comparison over all 1536 elements. No irrational value is gated: every claim is a rank, an exact spectrum multiplicity, a character identity, or an explicit wrong-value or parity rejector.

## Boundary

This is a bounded theorem about the even-degree lattice-Dirac operator algebra generated by {D2, J_full} on the specific L = 4, N = 64 staggered construction and the order-1536 group H = ⟨G_amb, S_eps⟩. The periodic boundary, the g = I counting metric, and the chosen J_full sign-family representative are inherited finite-surface inputs, not outputs of this note. It fixes no new free parameter, selects no bulk sign-family member, and chooses no dynamics; it is orientation-neutral and r-neutral (no dial is set). It makes no phenomenological identification: "12⁺/12⁻" and "CP" are geometric labels for the χ_sgn-graded constituents and the lattice-parity involution S_eps, not statements about physical charge conjugation. The character-level projector that resolves each degeneracy is an End_H element, not itself a lattice-Dirac polynomial — the note constructs the charge, and locates it outside C[M], without asserting it is realized by the Dirac operators.

## What this opens

Having located the CP-charge at the character level — χ_sgn-odd, outside C[M] — the path this hands forward is to ask which enlargement of the operator content realizes the isotypic projector as a word. Admitting the χ_sgn-odd generator S_eps itself, or an off-diagonal intertwiner between the χ_sgn-paired constituents, is the natural next lever; the question it opens is how the χ_sgn grading of End_H organizes the full six-constituent commutant, and which lattice object promotes the two character-level charges into the operator algebra. The two directions the Dirac-even algebra reaches and the two it does not together chart where that enlargement has to act.
