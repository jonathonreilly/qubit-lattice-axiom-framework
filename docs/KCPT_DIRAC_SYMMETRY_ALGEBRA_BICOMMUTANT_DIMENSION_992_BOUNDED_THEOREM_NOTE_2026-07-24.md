## T1 — the full five-block algebra, dim 992

Under 𝒜 the space C⁶⁴ carries the five pairwise-inequivalent irreducible modules of Unit 19, of dimensions [8, 8, 12, 12, 24], with block projectors Q₁, …, Q₅ (idempotent, mutually orthogonal, ΣQ_i = I₆₄). Both generators of the Dirac side preserve every block: D2 and J_full each commute with each Q_i (max_i ‖[D2, Q_i]‖ and max_i ‖[J_full, Q_i]‖ ≤ 1e-12), because D2 and J_full are G_amb-invariant and χ_sgn-odd and therefore preserve each χ_sgn-self constituent and preserve the SUM of the swap pair {12⁺, 12⁻}; ρ(H) commutes with all Q_i by construction. Hence every element of 𝒜 is block-diagonal on the five modules, so

  𝒜 ⊆ End(V₁) ⊕ End(V₂) ⊕ End(V₃) ⊕ End(V₄) ⊕ End(V₅),

which bounds dim_C 𝒜 ≤ 8² + 8² + 12² + 12² + 24² = 992. This is the block-preservation cap: the generated algebra can occupy at most 992 of the 4096 ambient dimensions — it is emphatically NOT the full matrix algebra M₆₄ on C⁶⁴.

Each inequality is then an equality. Every V_i is 𝒜-irreducible and the five are pairwise inequivalent (Unit 19), so by Burnside/Jacobson density the image of 𝒜 in End(V_i) is ALL of End(V_i), and by Artin–Wedderburn pairwise inequivalence makes the five blocks independent. The runner realizes the density argument per block by spanning End(V_i) from the represented group alone: with Z_i an orthonormal column basis of V_i, the 1536 matrices Z_iᵀ ρ(h) Z_i span End(V_i) of dimension d_i² on each of the four unfused blocks — span-ranks 64, 64, 144, 144, each SV-gap certified. Assembling the five blocks,

  `64 + 64 + 144 + 144 + 576 = 992`   and   `992 = 8^2 + 8^2 + 12^2 + 12^2 + 24^2`,

so dim_C 𝒜 = 992 exactly: 𝒜 = ⊕_i End(V_i) is the full five-block matrix algebra M₈ ⊕ M₈ ⊕ M₁₂ ⊕ M₁₂ ⊕ M₂₄.

The Burnside fill is localized to the single fused block, and this localization is the anti-noise control for the whole dimension count. On the fused 24-block the represented group ρ(H) alone is block-diagonal in the two H-constituents 12⁺, 12⁻ and so `stalls at 288 = 12^2 + 12^2 without D2 and fills to 576 = 24^2` only after the fusing Dirac generator D2 is adjoined and the closure iterated in the 24×24 restricted space (right-multiply the orthonormal span basis by the restricted generators until stable — reached within a bounded number of rounds). The identical span machinery that returns full rank d_i² on the four unfused blocks returns exactly 288 < 576 on the fused block when D2 is withheld, so the 576 is a genuine generated fill and not rank-saturation noise. (Historical trap being guarded: a naive incremental closure in the ambient 64×64 space noise-saturates to 4096; dimension counting here is strictly per-block, never ambient.)

## T2 — bicommutant identity

The generator set is closed under the adjoint: D2ᵀ = −D2 and J_fullᵀ = −J_full (both real antisymmetric), and each ρ(h) is a real orthogonal permutation-times-sign matrix with ρ(h)ᵀ = ρ(h⁻¹) ∈ ρ(H) (the runner checks ρ(h)ᵀ ρ(h) = I to ≤ 1e-12 across all 1536 elements). Hence 𝒜 is a *-subalgebra of End_C(C⁶⁴), and the finite-dimensional von Neumann bicommutant theorem gives 𝒜 = 𝒜″. The represented commutant is the center: 𝒜′ = Z(𝒜) ≅ C⁵, recomputed here by the Unit-19 null-space method — the constraint map c ↦ ([Σ c_k P_k, D2], [Σ c_k P_k, J_full]) on the six constituent projectors has rank 1 (single nonzero singular value ≈ 15.49, all others ≤ 1e-9), so the commutant has dimension 6 − 1 = 5. Because the five modules are inequivalent, the double commutant 𝒜″ = ⊕_i End(V_i) once more, of dimension 992: the generated algebra EQUALS its own bicommutant, and 992 is forced twice over — once by per-block density and once by 𝒜 = 𝒜″ with a five-dimensional center.

## T3 — the abelian Dirac pair and the commutative tower

Since [D2, J_full] = 0, the unital algebra ⟨D2, J_full⟩ of all words in the two Dirac operators is commutative. D2 has exactly 7 distinct eigenvalues {0, ±2i, ±2i√2, ±2i√3} with multiplicities [8, 12, 12, 12, 12, 4, 4], so C[D2] has dimension 7. On each bulk M-shell m ∈ {1, 2, 3} the complex structure is LOCKED to the adjacency, J_full·P_m = D2·P_m/(2√m) (‖·‖ ≤ 1e-12; the Unit-6/7 per-shell structure), so there J_full's ±i sign is fixed by the sign of D2's eigenvalue and adds no new operator; only on the m = 0 kernel, where D2 = 0, does J_full carry independent information, splitting the 8-dimensional kernel 4 ⊕ 4. Hence

  dim ⟨D2, J_full⟩ = 7 + 1 = 8,

with joint (M-shell, J-sign) eigenspace dimensions `[4, 4, 4, 4, 12, 12, 12, 12]` — a radius×chirality bigrading in which, per J-sign, the four shells carry [4, 12, 12, 4]. This assembles the strict commutative tower

  C[M] = C⁴  ⊊  C[D2] = C⁷  ⊊  ⟨D2, J_full⟩ = C⁸  ⊊  𝒜 = 992   (`dims 4 ⊊ 7 ⊊ 8 ⊊ 992`),

## T4 — the CP-idempotent is an algebra observable, not a central charge

The geometric CP-idempotent P₁₂₊ commutes with every module projector Q_i (max_i ‖[P₁₂₊, Q_i]‖ ≤ 1e-12): it is block-diagonal on the five modules, acting as the identity on 12⁺ inside the fused 24-block and as zero on every other block. By the per-block fullness of T1 every block-diagonal operator lies in 𝒜, so P₁₂₊ ∈ 𝒜 (rank 12, idempotency defect ≤ 1e-12). Yet P₁₂₊ is NOT central: ‖[P₁₂₊, D2]‖ ≈ 0.354 ≠ 0, in sharp contrast to the four unfused module projectors, which DO commute with both D2 and J_full (≤ 1e-12) and are therefore central. The geometric CP-separator that the center Z(𝒜) lacks (Unit 19 — Z(𝒜) has only five block charges, one fewer than End_H) is thus an element OF the algebra: within this finite model, the CP-charge is `an algebra observable, not a central charge`. As a corollary, every element of End_H is block-scalar on the six constituents, hence block-diagonal on the five modules, hence contained in 𝒜: End_H ⊂ 𝒜 (dimension 6 inside dimension 992). The generated algebra 𝒜 therefore contains both the represented group ρ(H) and its full commutant End_H.
