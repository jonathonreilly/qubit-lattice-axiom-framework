## claim_scope

- **Kind:** bounded_theorem. A finite linear-algebra census on the fixed L=6, N=216
  staggered lattice on the 6³ torus. Every statement is an exact statement about
  matrices, group orbits, and Frobenius projections of that finite object.
- **Object:** the reach observable
  `omega(g) = ||Pi_{C_g}(sep6)||_F^2 / ||sep6||_F^2`, where `sep6 = P8a - P8b` is the
  ind-8 separator (difference of the two multiplicity-1 rank-8 isotypic projectors on
  the top staggered shell m=3), `C_g = <D2, J_full, S_eps, U_g>` is the real matrix
  \*-algebra generated by the natural Dirac core together with one census element
  `U_g`, and `Pi` is Frobenius orthogonal projection onto `C_g`. The census ranges over
  the 75 H-conjugation orbits of the D2-commuting signed-permutation group `G_amb`.
- **Scope limits:** r-neutral. No physical, continuum, or thermodynamic claim is made;
  `omega`, `d(g)`, and `Dchi(g)` are numerical invariants of the finite L=6 construction.
  The comparison to the L=4 census reproduces the L=6 side by direct measurement and
  cites the L=4 side from the dependency note; no L=4 value is recomputed here.

## 1. Objects and setup

- Lattice L=6, N=216 on the 6³ torus; staggered integer adjacency `D2` with
  `eta_0 = 1`, `eta_1 = (-1)^{x0}`, `eta_2 = (-1)^{x0+x1}`. `M = D2^2` has spectrum
  `[0, -3, -6, -9]` with shell multiplicities `[8, 48, 96, 64]` (sum 216).
- `G_amb` = the group of D2-commuting signed permutations built from the affine linear
  bases {identity, `(-x1,-x0,-x2)`, `(x1,x2,x0)`}, the 216 torus translations, and the
  64 quadratic GF(2) sign fields; `|G_amb| = 2592 = 6^3 * 12`. Adjoining the Dirac sign
  operator `S_eps = diag((-1)^{x0+x1+x2})` gives `H`, `|H| = 5184`, `[H : G_amb] = 2`,
  and `S_eps` is not in `G_amb`. `G_amb` is the kernel of the Dirac sign character.
- Under H-conjugation, `H` splits into 137 classes; `G_amb` splits into **75 orbits**
  (sizes summing to 2592). These 75 orbits are the census partition.
- Natural Dirac core `A_nat = <D2, J_full, S_eps>`, real dimension 16, isomorphic to
  `M_2(C)^{\oplus 4}` with center `Z = C[M]` of dimension 4 (the four shell projectors);
  `J_full` is real antisymmetric with `J_full^2 = -I`.
- Ambient shell commutant `A'` has dimension 13 and is abelian; it contains `C[M]`
  (dimension 4) and a 9-dimensional separator complement. `sep6 = P8a - P8b` lies in
  that complement: it has rank sum 16, `||sep6||_F = 4`, commutes with `D2`, `J_full`,
  and every `rho(h)` (H-invariance licensed by the generating-set gate L1), and is
  blind to `A_nat` (Frobenius overlap^2 = 2.57e-33).

All objects and the census are recomputed from scratch by the runner
`scripts/kcpt_l6_ind8_separator_reach_census_surface_comparison_2026_07_25.py`

## 2. Theorem claims

**T1 (group and partition; gates S1, S2).** `|G_amb| = 2592 = 6^3 * 12`, `|H| = 5184`,
`[H : G_amb] = 2`, `S_eps` not in `G_amb`. Under H-conjugation `H` has 137 classes with
size multiset `{1:2, 2:5, 3:4, 4:3, 6:24, 12:57, 54:6, 72:6, 108:21, 144:9}`, and
`G_amb` has 75 orbits with size multiset
`{1:2, 2:3, 3:4, 4:1, 6:14, 12:27, 54:6, 72:6, 108:9, 144:3}`, sizes summing to 2592.

**T2 (Dirac core; gate S3).** `A_nat` has real dimension 16, center dimension 4 equal
to `C[M]` (each shell projector lies in the center), and Wedderburn type
`M_2(C)^{\oplus 4}` with block ranks `[8, 48, 64, 96]`; two independent random seeds
produce the same block data.

**T3 (separator; gate S4).** `sep6 = P8a - P8b` has rank sum 16, `||sep6||_F = 4`,
commutes with `D2`, `J_full`, and a 400-element random sample of `rho(h)` (max
commutator norm `<= 1e-10`); invariance under every `rho(h)` is licensed by gate L1's
generating-set check (T5). `sep6` is blind to `A_nat` (overlap^2 `<= 1e-10`).

**T4 (complement; gate S5).** `A'` has dimension 13 and is abelian; `C[M]` has
dimension 4; the separator complement of `C[M]` inside `A'` has dimension 9 and every
one of its 9 directions is blind to `A_nat`.

**T5 (licensing; gates L1, L2).** `sep6` is H-invariant: `max_h ||rho(h) sep6 rho(h)^{-1}
- sep6|| < 1e-10` over the H-generators and their inverses. The pair `(dim C_g,
omega(g))` is an H-class function: for six sampled orbits, one per measured `omega`
stratum, three random same-class conjugates each reproduce the orbit's
`dim C_g` exactly and `omega` to within `1e-10`.

**T6 (census value set; gates CN0, CN_total, CN_q[...], CN_nonq, CN_counts).** All 75

**T7 (reach depth; gate CN_dg).** `d(g)` (the number of the 9 complement directions with
reach `> 1e-6`) takes only the values `{0, 9}`; every orbit with `omega > 0` has
`d(g) = 9`. The split is pinned: 14 orbits have `d(g) = 0` and 61 have `d(g) = 9`.
Since only 14 orbits reach `sep6`, 47 of the 61 `omega = 0` orbits have `d(g) = 9`:
they reach all nine complement basis directions while leaving `sep6` unreached.

**T8 (surface comparison; gate CMP1).** The L=4 census value `1/9` does not appear at
L=6 (`min |omega - 1/9| > 1e-6`). The L=6 census carries three values absent from the
L=4 set `{0, 1/9, 1/3, 1}`: `1/4`, `4/15`, and `1/2`.

**T9 (character; gates CHR1, CHR2).** `Dchi(g) = Re(tr(P8a U_g) - tr(P8b U_g))` takes the
values `{-6, 0, +6}`: `+6` on 2 orbits (144 elements), `-6` on 2 orbits (144 elements),
`0` on the remaining 71 orbits; the four `Dchi != 0` orbits each have size 72 and
`dim C_g = 108`. The set `{Dchi != 0}` is a **proper** subset of `{omega = 1/3}`
(4 orbits inside 5): one orbit (size 12, `dim C_g = 68`) has `omega = 1/3` with
`Dchi = 0`. The L=4 equality `{Dchi != 0} = {omega = 1/3}` does not survive at L=6; at
L=6 it becomes proper containment.

**T10 (unlock; gates UNL1, UNL2, UNL3).** Among the 3 orbits at maximal reach
`omega = 1` (sizes `{4, 144, 144}`), the minimal size is 4. A concrete generator of the
size-4 orbit is `g1 = diag((-1)^{x1}) . T(1,1,1)` (linear part identity, translation
`(1,1,1)`, sign field `(-1)^{x1}`); `g1` is in `G_amb`, commutes with `D2`, and its
H-conjugation orbit has size 4. The closure `<A_nat, g1>` has real dimension 68 and
center dimension 17. Within it, `sep6` is central and is a difference of two minimal
central idempotents:

    sep6 = e_a - e_b,

where `e_a` (the `+1` spectral projector of `sep6`) and `e_b` (the `-1` spectral
projector) are each central in `<A_nat, g1>` (maximum commutator `1.9e-15`) with
reconstruction residual `6.5e-15`, and each is a *single* minimal central idempotent of
rank 8. Minimality is certified deterministically by the center dimension of the
orthonormalized compressed corner algebra: `e_a` and `e_b` each yield a 1-dimensional
corner center, while their rank-16 sum yields a 2-dimensional corner center — the
rejector that shows the method resolves two blocks when two are present.

**T11 (translation dial; gate DIAL1).** Fixing the sign field `(-1)^{x1}` and linear
part identity, varying only the translation moves `omega` across the reach spectrum:
`(1,1,1) -> 1`, `(1,1,3) -> 1/2`, `(1,1,5) -> 1/3`, `(3,3,3) -> 1/4`, `(1,1,0) -> 0`.
Each of these five elements is in `G_amb` and commutes with `D2`. Translation alone
sweeps `omega` over `{0, 1/4, 1/3, 1/2, 1}`.

**T12 (perturbation discriminator; gate PERT1).** Replacing `sep6` by the single rank-8
projector `P8a` (not a difference) breaks two gates: `P8a` is not blind to `A_nat`
(overlap^2 = 0.125, versus `2.57e-33` for `sep6`), and on the identity orbit it reads
`omega = 0.125` instead of `0`. Both the blindness gate (T3) and the `omega = 0`
stratum (T6) flip under this in-frame wrong separator.

dimension 7 -> 17) and the two idempotents are each of rank 8 at L=6.

## Honest-auditor read

- The dangerous move this census could make is to presuppose that L=6 reproduces the
  L=4 value set `{0, 1/9, 1/3, 1}`. It does not: `1/9` is absent, three new values
  appear, and one of them (`4/15`) breaks the `q <= 12` band. These are recorded as
  measured, with wrong-value rejectors on every quantized stratum (each pinned rational
  rejects the nearest different `q <= 12` rational by at least `1e-6`), and `4/15` is
  gated as non-`q<=12` with its exact denominator, not as a near-miss of a simpler
  fraction.
- The character relation is likewise recorded as found. At L=4 the coincidence is an
  equality; at L=6 the measured relation is proper containment, and the runner exhibits
  the explicit counterexample orbit (size 12, `omega = 1/3`, `Dchi = 0`). The weaker
  L=6 relation is stated as the measured fact, not strengthened to match L=4.
- The unlock is constructed directly from `g1 = diag((-1)^{x1}) . T(1,1,1)` rather than
  read off a comparison target: `g1` is gated to lie in `G_amb`, commute with `D2`, and
  have orbit size 4, and the central structure of `sep6` is certified deterministically —
  `e_a` and `e_b` each yield a 1-dimensional corner center (single minimal central
  idempotent) while their rank-16 sum yields a 2-dimensional corner center — so the
  `sep6 = e_a - e_b` form with both idempotents of rank 8 is measured, not assumed. The
  perturbation gate confirms the census discriminates: the in-frame wrong separator `P8a`
  flips both the blindness and `omega = 0` gates.
- Residual softness a reader should weigh: `omega` is a floating projection ratio, and
  the pinned rationals carry errors at the `1e-14` level; the gate tolerances (`1e-9`
  for quantization, `1e-6` for rejectors and reach) are chosen well inside those
  margins but are conventional choices, not derived bounds. The Wedderburn block data
  (T2) is cross-checked at two random seeds; the central structure of `sep6` (T10) is
  certified without any seed dependence, by corner-center dimensions carrying an explicit
  two-block rejector.
