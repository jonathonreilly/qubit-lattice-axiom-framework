### L-K1 — Joint K structure and the positive exhibit

automorphism of the joint supplied surface. The positive-list generator class

```text

```

is K-closed as a real generator class. Conjugation fixes all listed generators
except `sigma_2 tensor C^j`, which it sends to
`-sigma_2 tensor C^j`; the minus sign is a real scalar. It also fixes `C`,
`P_1`, and `C+C^2`, and swaps

```text

```

Set

```text

W = sigma_2 tensor A_odd.
```

The runner verifies exactly that `A_odd` and `sigma_2` are individually
K-odd, while `W` is Hermitian, invariant under `I_2 tensor C`, and jointly
K-even. Moreover,

```text

```

so the witness lies in the real algebra generated by the positive list. On a
`+sigma_2` carrier presentation,

```text

Tr(W (rho_+ tensor P_conjugate(w)))  = +sqrt(3).
```

Thus `W` is a positive doublet-resolving exhibit inside the jointly K-even
surface. K-closure of the admissible class does **not** by itself foreclose
doublet-resolving registration. This is the honest observable wall, stated as
a positive exhibit rather than an exclusion claim.


