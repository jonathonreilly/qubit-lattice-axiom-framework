## Statement

Let `sigma_1, sigma_2, sigma_3` denote the Pauli matrices on `C^2`
(selected Cl3Pauli), so the Cl(3) bivector basis on the dim-2
spinor irrep is

```text
e_1 e_2 = i sigma_3,    e_2 e_3 = i sigma_1,    e_3 e_1 = i sigma_2.    (Biv)
```

Let `C` be the standard `3 x 3` cyclic permutation matrix and let

```text
B_1 = C + C^2,    B_2 = i (C - C^2)                                      (B)
```

be the C_3-doublet basis on `Herm_circ(3) ⊂ M_3(C)` (retained: KKappa
Conclusion T1).

**Conclusion (Obs1) (algebraic mismatch).** The pair `(B_1, B_2)`
commutes pairwise:

```text
[B_1, B_2] = 0,                                                          (Comm1)
B_1^2 = 2 I_3 + B_1,                                                     (Comm2)
B_2^2 = 2 I_3 - B_1,                                                     (Comm3)
B_1 B_2 = -B_2.                                                          (Comm4)
```

The Cl(3) bivector pair `(e_1 e_2, e_2 e_3)` anticommutes:

```text
{e_1 e_2, e_2 e_3} = e_1 e_2 e_2 e_3 + e_2 e_3 e_1 e_2 = 0.              (Anti)
```

Hence no algebra homomorphism `phi: span_R{I_3, B_1, B_2}_alg → Cl(3)`
into the algebra generated by Cl(3) bivectors can carry `(B_1, B_2)`
to any anticommuting bivector pair. The proposed identification
`(B_1, B_2) ↔ (e_1 e_2 + e_2 e_3, e_1 e_3)` (or any linear variant) is
forbidden by `(Comm1)` and `(Anti)`.

**Conclusion (Obs2) (representation-level only embedding gives F3).**
At the Z_3 representation-theory level, `Lambda^2 V_3` decomposes as
trivial + doublet under the induced Z_3 cyclic-permutation action.
The unique Z_3-doublet sub-rep is 2-dim, orthogonal complement of the
Z_3-invariant bivector `e_1 e_2 + e_2 e_3 + e_3 e_1`. A Z_3-equivariant
linear injection `R^2 ↪ Lambda^2 V_3` exists at the rep level.

But the SO(3)-invariant Lebesgue measure on `Lambda^2 V_3 ≅ R^3`,
restricted to that 2-plane, is the 2-dim Lebesgue `d(Re b) d(Im b)`,
giving log-volume `2 log|b|` (two real coordinate factors). This
matches the F3 weighting `log E_+ + 2 log E_perp`. Verified by the
runner Part 5: F3 extremum at `E_+ = E_tot/3`, `E_perp = 2 E_tot/3`,
i.e. `kappa = 1`, NOT BAE.

**Conclusion (Obs3) (counterfactual probe).** Restricting to the 2-dim
doublet sub-plane and then applying a radial-only projection (treating
`|b|^2` as a single coordinate) would give one log factor in `|b|^2`,
matching F1. But this radial reduction is **not** a canonical retained
operation: it requires factoring out a U(1) angular direction on the
2-plane. This is exactly the U(1)_b angular convention named by Probes
13, 16 as the non-canonical residue. No new Cl(3) authority closes
this convention. Verified by the runner Part 6.

**Therefore:** the Cl(3) bivector route is one further attack vector
(AV8) closing as a return to the campaign's terminal F1-vs-F3 residue
(or to the U(1)_b convention sub-residue under selective reduction).
The BAE admission count is UNCHANGED.

## Proof

All steps are immediate-algebraic and are verified by the paired
runner (40 PASS / 0 FAIL).

**(Obs1).** The commutators and quadratic identities in `(Comm1)`-`(Comm4)`
are direct expansions of `(B_1, B_2)` in terms of `(C, C^2)` using
`C^3 = I`. For example,

```text
B_1 B_2 = (C + C^2) · i (C - C^2)
       = i (C^2 - C^3 + C^3 - C^4)
       = i (C^2 - C)  · (using C^3 = I and C^4 = C)
       = -i (C - C^2)
       = -B_2.
```

Hence `B_1 B_2 = -B_2`, so `[B_1, B_2] = B_1 B_2 - B_2 B_1 = -B_2 - (-B_2) = 0`
(since `B_2 B_1 = B_1 B_2` by direct computation = `-B_2`).

The anticommutation `{e_1 e_2, e_2 e_3} = 0` follows from
`e_2^2 = 1` (Pauli square identity) and the standard Clifford-algebra
relation `e_i e_j = -e_j e_i` for `i ≠ j`. Concretely,

```text
(e_1 e_2)(e_2 e_3) = e_1 (e_2^2) e_3 = e_1 e_3,
(e_2 e_3)(e_1 e_2) = e_2 e_3 e_1 e_2 = -e_2 e_1 e_3 e_2 = e_1 e_2 e_3 e_2
                  = -e_1 e_2^2 e_3 = -e_1 e_3.
```

Sum is zero. Hence the algebraic mismatch is exact. ∎

**(Obs2).** The Z_3 action on `V_3 = R^3` by cyclic permutation has
eigenvalues `{1, omega, omegabar}` (cube roots of unity). The induced
action on `Lambda^2 V_3` (the wedge-square representation) has the
same eigenvalues by general representation theory of wedge powers (or
by direct verification on the basis `e_1 e_2, e_2 e_3, e_3 e_1`,
runner Part 4).

The unique trivial sub-rep is the Z_3-invariant vector
`e_1 e_2 + e_2 e_3 + e_3 e_1`; the unique 2-dim doublet sub-rep is its
orthogonal complement.

The Lebesgue measure on `R^3` restricted to a 2-plane is two-dimensional
Lebesgue, giving a 2 log|b| volume factor as `|b|` (the radial coordinate
on the plane) scales. This factor is exactly the F3 vs F1 difference:

```text
F1 ~ log E_+ + 1 · log E_perp  (one log factor in |b|^2 = treats doublet as 1 mode),
F3 ~ log E_+ + 2 · log E_perp  (two log factors in |b|^2 = treats doublet as 2 modes).
```

The runner Part 5 verifies the F3 extremum at `kappa = 1` and the F1
extremum at `kappa = 2` separately. ∎

**(Obs3).** A radial-only projection on the 2-plane (mod U(1)) gives
one log factor in `|b|^2`. But this projection requires choosing a
U(1) angular direction. The Cl(3) bivector algebra does **not**
canonically supply such a direction: SO(3) acts transitively on
the unit sphere in `Lambda^2 V_3`, but the Z_3 doublet sub-plane is
not SO(3)-invariant (it's only Z_3-invariant). The residue is exactly
the same U(1) ambiguity as Probes 13, 16. ∎
