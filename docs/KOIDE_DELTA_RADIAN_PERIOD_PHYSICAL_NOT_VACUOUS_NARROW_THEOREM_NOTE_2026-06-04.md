## 1. The residual, and the vacuous-vs-physical criterion

The radian no-go's core fact (reproven in runner Part A): periodic (Type-A)
phase sources on the retained lattice surfaces give angles `q·π` with `q ∈ Q`;
combinatorial (Type-B) sources give pure rationals; and
`{q·π : q ∈ Q} ∩ Q = {0}` (π irrational). So the derived rational `2/9` is not
supplied as a literal radian by any periodic source, and the lane's natural
geometric angles are `(2/9)π ≈ 0.698` and `2π(2/9) ≈ 1.396` — **never** `2/9`
itself. Reading the dimensionless `2/9` *as* `2/9 rad` is the period-1 choice.

**Criterion.** A convention is *vacuous* iff every physical observable is
invariant under the choice (it cancels). The test for the period choice is then
direct: does any physical observable depend on whether `δ = 2/9 rad` (period-1)
or `δ = 2π·2/9` (period-2π)?

## 2. Load-bearing step — the period choice sets the charged-lepton masses

In the Brannen / Koide-Z₃ parameterization (a parameterization, not a proof
input) `√m_k ∝ 1 + √2·cos(δ + 2πk/3)`:

- **`Q = 2/3` for every `δ`** (runner Part B.1): the Koide ratio is the
  parameterization's invariant and is blind to the period choice.
- **The individual mass ratios depend on `cos δ`** (runner Part B.2–B.4):

| period reading | `δ` (rad) | `m_τ/m_e` | `m_μ/m_e` |
|---|---|---|---|
| **period-1** (`2/9`) | 0.22222 | **3477.47** | **206.770** |
| period-2π (`2π·2/9`) | 1.39626 | 40.12 | 14.340 |
| period-π (`(2/9)π`) | 0.69813 | 40.12 | 14.340 |
| **PDG (comparator)** | — | 3477.23 | 206.768 |

Period-1 matches the PDG ratios to relative error `≈ 8×10⁻⁵`; the other periods
are off by `~100×`. The mass-ratio observable is therefore **not invariant**
under the period choice: it is physical.

The `Q = 2/3` invariance and the period-1 (`δ = 2/9`) PDG match are the retained
S4/S5 facts of
[`CHARGED_LEPTON_BRANNEN_BAE_DELTA_TIER_A_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-30.md`](CHARGED_LEPTON_BRANNEN_BAE_DELTA_TIER_A_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-30.md)
(reproven here in runner Part B for self-containment). The **new** step is the
*alternative-period* evaluation (period-2π, period-π) and the rescaling-
cancellation contrast (§3), which together classify the residual as physical.

## 3. Contrast — a vacuous rescaling convention cancels

The `g_bare`/`Y₀` class acts as an overall rescaling. Under `m_k → λ m_k` a ratio
observable `m_i/m_j` is invariant (`d/dλ = 0`; runner Part C), which is exactly
why those are listed as conventions, not admissions
([`BETA_GBARE_RESCALING_ABSTRACT_IDENTITY_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md`](BETA_GBARE_RESCALING_ABSTRACT_IDENTITY_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md),
`retained`: `g_bare` is a rescaling-invariant convention). The radian period
enters as `cos δ` — a bounded, non-scale, non-cancelling argument
(`d cos δ/dδ ≠ 0`) — so it cannot be absorbed into any overall rescaling. The
period choice is structurally a *different kind of object* from `g_bare`/`Y₀`.

A subtle but important point: the period choice is **not** a global angular-unit
rescaling either. The `2π k/3` Z₃ offset in `cos(δ + 2πk/3)` is fixed in radians
by the circulant character structure; the residual rescales **only** the derived
rational `2/9` against that fixed offset. A genuine angular-unit convention would
rescale the offset too, under which the masses would be invariant (it would
cancel). Because the offset is pinned in true radians while only the `2/9` term
is read under a period choice, the choice is observable — which is exactly why it
is an admission, not a unit convention.
