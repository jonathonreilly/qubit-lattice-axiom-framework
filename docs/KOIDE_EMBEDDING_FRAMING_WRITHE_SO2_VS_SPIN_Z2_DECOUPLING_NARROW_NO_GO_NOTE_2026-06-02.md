## 2. The embedding-framing is SO(2)/Z-writhe data (the group/codimension fact)

A `Z^3` edge embedded in `R^3` is a `1`-cell of codimension `2`; its **normal
bundle** has rank `2` and structure group `SO(2)`. A **framing** of the edge is a
section of this `SO(2)` bundle; the self-linking / writhe of a framed strand
(Calugareanu-White-Fuller, `Lk = Tw + Wr`) is an **integer**, a class in
`pi_1(SO(2)) = Z`. This is the precise mathematical content of "the embedding
supplies a transverse plane": the transverse plane is the `SO(2)` normal `2`-plane,
and the framing datum it carries is `Z`-valued (a `U(1)` twist), **not** `SU(2)`
double-cover data.

The fermionic swap sign, by contrast, is the **nontrivial element of
`pi_1(SO(3)) = Z_2`**: the exchange of two particles in `R^3` is, in the
two-particle configuration space `C_2(R^3) = (R^3 x R^3 \ diag)/S_2`, the
generator of `pi_1 = Z_2`, whose square is a `2pi` rotation of the relative
frame; the sign `-1` is the value of that `2pi` rotation in the **spin-`1/2`**
(`SU(2)`) representation. The runner verifies:

```text
R^3 VECTOR/adjoint frame after 2pi exchange rotation = +I_3   (trivial; the
        embedding's O(3) frame, and Ad(-I) on su(2), see the swap as +1),
SPIN-1/2 frame after 2pi exchange rotation         = -I_2   (the swap sign lives
        ONLY in the spinor / matter-STATE representation).
```

`SO(2)`-writhe (`Z`) and `SO(3)`-spin (`Z_2`, non-abelian double cover) are
**different groups**; there is no canonical map carrying the integer writhe onto
the torsion spin sign on the exchange class. This is the first, group-theoretic
obstruction.

## 3. Every integral / abelian framing vanishes on the swap (the homological fact, airtight)

The group fact is reinforced by an exact, representative-independent homological
obstruction on the **actual** Abrams complex (the same `UD_2(Gamma)` construction

**The exchange class `t` is `2`-torsion** in `H_1(UD_2(Gamma); Z)`: the integral
Smith normal form gives `2t in im(d2)` and `t notin im(d2)`. Hence:

- **(I) Integer cocycles vanish on `t`.** For any integer-valued `1`-cocycle
  `phi_Z` (and the writhe self-linking **is** one),

  ```text
  2 phi_Z(t) = phi_Z(2t) = phi_Z(d2 x) = 0   in Z   =>   phi_Z(t) = 0.
  ```

  The runner exhibits a **basis** of the integral cocycle space (dimension
  `= b1`, the free rank of `H_1`) and verifies **every** basis vector pairs to
  `0` with `t`, on `K_{3,3}` (`dim = 18`), `K_5` (`dim = 15`), and a genuine
  non-planar cubic-lattice slab `3x3x2` of `Z^3` (`dim = 168`).

- **(II) Flat real (`U(1)`/`SO(2)`) framing connections have holonomy `+1` on
  `t`.** Because `2t in im(d2)`, `t = 0` in `H_1(UD_2; R)` (`t` is a rational
  boundary). For any flat real `1`-cochain `A` (a `U(1)`/`SO(2)` framing
  connection, holonomy `exp(i <A, .>)`), `<A, t> = 0`, so holonomy `= +1`. A
  **non-flat** `A` is not a homotopy invariant and so is not a well-defined
  statistics sign. Verified on all three graphs.

So **every abelian framing of the embedding** -- the flat `Z_2` connection (the
`P(t) = 0` fact of the prior `eta`-holonomy no-go), the integer writhe, **and**
the real-holonomy `U(1)` connection -- is **blind to the swap**. The swap is
detected only by a class in `H^1(UD_2; Z_2)` that is **not** the reduction of any
integral class (it is `2`-torsion / Bockstein data). The runner confirms a
non-fibered `GF(2)` cocycle `phi` with `phi(t) = 1` exists, is non-fibered

reduction of any integral writhe class.
