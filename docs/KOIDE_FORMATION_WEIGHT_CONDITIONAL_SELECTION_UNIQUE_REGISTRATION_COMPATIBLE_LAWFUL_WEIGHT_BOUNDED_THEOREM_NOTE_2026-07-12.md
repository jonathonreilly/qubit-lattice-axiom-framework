## T2 — branch-robust open pinned-`Q` selection under `B_abs`

Under the alternative branch

```text
B_abs: m_k=lambda_k^2,       sqrt(m_k)=|lambda_k|,
```

define

```text
N = sum_k lambda_k^2 = 3a^2+6|b|^2 > 0,
S_abs(theta) = sum_k |lambda_k| > 0,
Q_abs(theta) = N/S_abs(theta)^2.
```

For fixed `r`, `N` is phase-free. Thus `Q_abs` is constant on an open
interval exactly when `S_abs` is constant there.

### T2.1 Fixed-sign local-constancy lemma

On an open interval containing no zero of a `lambda_k`, let
`s_k=sign(lambda_k)`. Then

```text
S_s(theta) = sum_k s_k lambda_k
           = a(s_0+s_1+s_2)
             + 2|b|[A_s cos(theta)+B_s sin(theta)],

A_s = (2s_0-s_1-s_2)/2,
B_s = sqrt(3)(s_2-s_1)/2,

dS_s/dtheta = 2|b|[-A_s sin(theta)+B_s cos(theta)].
```

The derivative is identically zero exactly when `A_s=B_s=0`, which gives

```text
s_0=s_1=s_2.
```

There are algebraically two uniform vectors. The all-negative vector cannot
occur on any interval because `sum lambda_k=3a>0`. Consequently the only
physically realizable fixed-sign region on which `S_abs`, and hence `Q_abs`,
can be locally constant is an **all-positive** region.

This qualification matters: saying merely that the coefficient kernel
contains only the all-positive vector would be false. The runner enumerates
all eight exact sign vectors, finds both uniform algebraic solutions, and then
excludes the all-negative one with the positive signed trace.

### T2.2 Exact `r=1` sign-region computation

Normalize `a=1`, which leaves `Q_abs` unchanged. At `r=1`, the complete zero
set over `0<=theta<2pi` is

```text
lambda_0=0 at {2pi/3,4pi/3},
lambda_1=0 at {0,2pi/3},
lambda_2=0 at {0,4pi/3}.
```

Thus there are three open sign regions. The runner derives their signs,
absolute sums, symbolic derivatives, and exact critical sets:

| open region | sign pattern | `S_abs/a` | `d(S_abs/a)/dtheta` | critical set in the region |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| `(0,2pi/3)` | `(+,-,+)` | `1+4 sin(theta+pi/6)` | `4 cos(theta+pi/6)` | `{pi/3}` |
| `(2pi/3,4pi/3)` | `(-,+,+)` | `1-4 cos(theta)` | `4 sin(theta)` | `{pi}` |
| `(4pi/3,2pi)` | `(+,+,-)` | `1+4 cos(theta+pi/3)` | `-4 sin(theta+pi/3)` | `{5pi/3}` |

Each derivative is a nonzero trigonometric function, so it cannot vanish on
an open subinterval. The critical points are isolated; indeed their
registered squared patterns are permutations of `(4,4,1)a^2`, hence
degenerate. The sign boundaries are also isolated and have spectra that are
permutations of `(3a,0,0)`, again degenerate. These points do not constitute
open sectors.

It follows exactly—not from sampling—that `r=1` has no nonempty open phase
sector on which `Q_abs` is constant. Two exact non-degenerate witnesses also
make the phase dependence visible:

```text
Q_abs(pi/6)  = 9/(13+4sqrt(3)),
Q_abs(pi/12) = 9/(9+4sqrt(2)),
```

and the two values are unequal.

### T2.3 `ND_3` alone excludes neither endpoint

m      = (4+2sqrt(3), 4-2sqrt(3), 1)a^2,

### T2.4 The sharpened branch-robust selection

The all-positive region is the strict positivity window. It is empty at
`r=1`: only isolated nonnegative boundary points remain. It is open at
`r=1/2`, with

```text

```

```text

Q_abs = (3a^2+6|b|^2)/(3a)^2 = 2/3.
```
