the on-site Cl(3,0) chirality `γ₅` (the spin grade). This note is a **narrow
factor-bridge no-go**: the on-site `γ₅`, embedded the natural way as
`γ₅_spin ⊗ I_gen`, **commutes with `S` exactly** and is **K-even** on the
generation factor — so it supplies *neither* half of the requirement. It is

the left/right-coupling route hit.

The on-site `γ₅` is genuinely framework-native (Quantum axiom) and is

## 1. Setup

The carrier factorizes as **site/spin `C²` ⊗ generation `C³`**. On the
generation factor, `C` is the cyclic shift (`C³=I`), `S = C+C²`
(`eig {+2,-1,-1}`), `A = i(C-C²)` (`eig {0,±√3}`). The on-site chirality is
`γ₅ = diag(+1,-1)` on the spin `C²` (the operator floated in the
`KOIDE_DELTA_RANK2_SELECTOR_…CLIFFORD_CHIRALITY_DOMAIN_WALL` lead). Already a
**dimension mismatch**: `γ₅` is `2×2`, `S` is `3×3` — `γ₅` is not an operator
on the generation factor.

## 2. The no-go (computed)

Embed the on-site chirality the natural way, `γ₅ ⊗ I_gen`, and `S` as
`I_spin ⊗ S`:

```text
[ γ₅ ⊗ I_gen , I_spin ⊗ S ] = 0   exactly   (‖·‖ = 0)
```

So requirement **(ii) non-commuting-with-`S` FAILS**. Moreover `γ₅` is real
(`conj(γ₅)=γ₅`) → **K-even**, the wrong parity for the K-odd `δ` direction on
the generation factor; and `γ₅ ⊗ I_gen` also commutes with `A`. Hence
`γ₅ ⊗ I_gen` is **partition-blind** on C³: it cannot break C₃-equivariance.

**Structural reason (not an accident).** Any site-factor operator
`O_site ⊗ I_gen` commutes with any generation-factor operator
`I_spin ⊗ O_gen` (verified: `‖[·,·]‖ = 0` across 200 random Hermitian pairs).
So **no** site-factor operator can be non-commuting-with-`S` on the
generation factor; a selector that breaks C₃-equivariance on C³ must act
**on the generation factor**.

**Wrong rank-2 sector.** `γ₅`'s split is the chiral projector
`P± = (1±γ₅)/2` on the **spin** `C²` (a partition of the spin factor). The
"rank-2 zero-mode sector" of the eigenline construction is a **spin-lift
multiplicity-2** doublet at *fixed* generation Z₃ character
(`koide_delta_lattice_wilson_selected_eigenline_no_go`,
`koide_delta_marked_relative_cobordism_no_go`, both retained_no_go) — it is
**not** the generation C³ orbit `{singlet, doublet}` (the eigenspaces of
`S`). The conflation of these two rank-2 sectors is the error this note
isolates.
