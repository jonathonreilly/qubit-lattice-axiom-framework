The HS metric on `(a, Re b, Im b)` is `diag(3,6,6)` — **reading-neutral**
(`FLAVOR_DOUBLET_METRIC_DEFAULT_IS_DETR`, #2534/#2535). The decision `r=1` vs

modes per real-dimension (det_R, `3a² = 6(Re b)² = 6(Im b)²` → `|b|²=a²` → r=1) vs
