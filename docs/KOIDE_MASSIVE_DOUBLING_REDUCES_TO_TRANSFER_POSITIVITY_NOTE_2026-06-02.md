## 2. The doubled Dirac construction (matches the retained doubling algebra)

```text
    alpha_i = [[0, sigma_i],[sigma_i, 0]]   (Hermitian, alpha_i^2 = I),
    beta    = diag(I_2, -I_2)               (Hermitian, beta^2 = I),
    {alpha_i, alpha_j} = 2 delta_ij,  {alpha_i, beta} = 0.
```

The runner verifies the full `Cl(3,1)` signature `(+,+,+,-)`, mutual
anticommutation, and `e_4 = i gamma^0` (Block 1, matching the retained note's S7c).

**The mass needs both chiralities; the qubit carries one.** With

`[gamma5, alpha_i] = 0` (the kinetic term preserves chirality) but
`{gamma5, beta} = 0` — so the **mass term `beta` is purely off-diagonal in
chirality** (Block 2: diagonal chirality blocks of `beta` vanish, off-diagonal

## 3. The reduction: positive energy is a transfer choice, not a Clifford identity

**The Dirac Hamiltonian is sign-indefinite.** `H_D = alpha.p + beta m` has spectrum
`{+E, +E, -E, -E}` with `E = sqrt(p^2 + m^2)` (Block 3, machine precision over a
random `(p, m)` sweep). For every momentum and **every** `m`, `H_D` has
negative-energy modes: `min spec(H_D) = -E < 0`. **The doubling algebra does NOT
supply positive energy.** Mass `m>0` *gaps* the spectrum (`|E| >= m`) but never makes
`H_D` positive — `min spec = -m` at `p=0`.

**Positive energy is the quantization / transfer statement.** The physical
emergent-time field Hamiltonian is the Dirac-sea / normal-ordered
`H_field = |H_D| = sqrt(H_D^2) >= 0`, equivalently encoded in the **one-step

```text

                                (the -E modes blow up) -> NOT a contraction,
```

So "positive energy of the reconstructed massive field" is **precisely** the

Two independent facts (Block 5) show the mass term can only *strengthen* the
positivity gate:

1. **The fermion measure is floored by the mass.** The Kogut-Susskind Dirac operator

   conjugate pairs `+- i lambda` and
   `det(M_KS + m I) = prod (m^2 + lambda^2) >= m^{2k} > 0` — strictly positive
   *because* `m>0` (the retained case-A determinant positivity). At `m=0` the
   measure can hit zero modes. **Mass enables the positivity, it does not threaten
   it.**
2. **The one-particle energy floor is monotone in `m`.** At fixed momentum,
   `E(p,m)=sqrt(|p|^2+m^2)` is non-decreasing in `m` over the sweep. This is the
   mass floor used by the positive transfer construction; it is not a proof of
   the full interacting gap.

So the residual is **not** "does `m>0` break this finite transfer construction".

gate named by the free-field transfer-positivity reduction.
