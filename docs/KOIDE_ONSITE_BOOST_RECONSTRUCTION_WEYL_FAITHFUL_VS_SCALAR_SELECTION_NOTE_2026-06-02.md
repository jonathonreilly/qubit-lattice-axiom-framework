## 2. The Grassmann-free on-site so(3,1) carrier

`gamma_i = sigma_i` on the qubit `H_x = C^2`. The `Cl(3,0)` bivectors are
`B_i = (1/2) gamma_j gamma_k = (i/2) sigma_i` (cyclic). Define
`J_i := sigma_i/2` (Hermitian rotations) and `K_i := B_i = (i/2) sigma_i`
(anti-Hermitian boosts -- the bivector itself, no Grassmann field, no staggered
spinor). Then `{J, K}` close `so(3,1)` exactly:

```text
    [J_i, J_j] = i eps J_k,  [J_i, K_j] = i eps K_k,  [K_i, K_j] = -i eps J_k.
```

The anti-Hermiticity of `K_i` and the **load-bearing minus sign** are forced
together by the Lorentzian signature; the Euclidean alternative
`[K,K] = +i eps J` (needing Hermitian `K`) is a failing control (runner S2). This
is the same `so(3,1)` the companion 4-comp note carries, but realized on the qubit
`C^2` from the bivectors, **not** on the presupposed Dirac bispinor.

## 3. The carrier is a definite, faithful chiral Weyl irrep

Form `N_i = (J_i + i K_i)/2`, `Ntil_i = (J_i - i K_i)/2`; their Casimirs label the
`SL(2,C)` irrep `(jL, jR)` rep-invariantly. On `C^2`, `N^2 = 0` and

S3). The chirality label is a convention; what is invariant is that the qubit
