## 3. What the composition shows (step-by-step)

The composition is a four-step algebraic chain. Each step is labeled by
the upstream status it inherits per §2.

### 3.1 Step A — Dimensionless `2/3` algebraic identity (retained)

By the retained narrow theorem
`koide_circulant_q_two_thirds_algebraic_narrow_theorem_note_2026-05-10`
(T3), for any `v_0 > 0` and any real `δ`, the cosine ansatz

```text
x_k = v_0 (1 + √2 cos(δ + 2π k / 3)),    k = 0, 1, 2
```

satisfies

```text
(Σ_k x_k^2) / (Σ_k x_k)^2 = 2/3                                       (A)
```

independent of `(v_0, δ)`. This is the dimensionless Koide ratio on the
Brannen / Rivero positive cosine ansatz, treated as an abstract algebraic
identity (no physical-mass identification of `x_k`).

**This step is retained-clean on the live ledger.**

By the V8 note
`koide_q_op_locality_source_domain_closure_theorem_note_2026-04-29`

with PHYSICAL_LATTICE_NECESSITY_NOTE §9, the ONSITE no-go, canonical-
descent Theorem 1, and the background-zero `Z`-erasure criterion CRIT
yields the structural support claim that, on the admitted normalized

observables on a background `K = sI + zZ ∈ A` are computed via the

```text
Y_phys = I_2,    ⟨Z⟩ = 0,    Q_l = 2/3.                                (B)
```

The V8 cross-sector consequence

**This step is conditional. (B) itself is unaudited; its cross-sector

## 4. The named residual: descent normalization

The four pieces (A) – (D) line up. (A) gives the dimensionless ratio
`2/3` exact and retained. (B) records `Q_l = 2/3` on the V8 chain (still
conditional). (C) puts `arg(b) = δ` inside the Brannen parameterization
and notes the empirical witness `δ ≈ 2/9 rad` from PDG. (D) records an
ambient anomaly coefficient `2/9` exact on a candidate descent geometry.

What is still NOT shown by the composition is the identification of (D)
with (C) at the dynamics-selected charged-lepton mass point. Concretely,
define the **descent-normalization factor**

```text
N(m_*) := Φ_Berry(m_*) / η_descent[Tr[Y^3]_{q_L}](m_*)                (E)
```

where:

- `Φ_Berry(m_*)` is the selected-line Berry phase on `H_sel(m)` at the
  dynamics-selected mass point `m_*` (cf.
  `koide_berry_phase_theorem_note_2026-04-19`, unaudited);
- `η_descent[Tr[Y^3]_{q_L}](m_*)` is the descent of the ambient anomaly
  coefficient (D) onto the same charged-lepton `CP^1` carrier at `m_*`,
  using the candidate Callan-Harvey inflow geometry; the
  Callan-Harvey-candidate note records this descended quantity as
  carrying value `2/9` along the candidate inflow route, with the
  normalization left implicit.

The framework conjecture is

```text
N(m_*) = 1.                                                            (F)
```

If (F) were a retained theorem, then by (E) `Φ_Berry(m_*) = 2/9 rad`,
which by Step C identifies with `δ`, closing `δ = 2/9 rad` on the live
authority surface and discharging the `P_A1` residual.

**The current state** (per the live ledger on 2026-05-25):

- Step A (dimensionless `2/3`) is **retained**.
- Step B (V8 `Q_l = 2/3` source-domain structural chain) is
  **unaudited** (cross-sector composition is `audited_conditional`).
- Step C (`arg(b) = δ` inside the Brannen parameterization plus the
  empirical PDG witness `≈ 2/9 rad`) is **unaudited**.
- Step D (ambient anomaly coefficient `2/9` on the candidate descent
  geometry) is **unaudited**.
- (E) and (F) are **open**. The descent-normalization factor `N(m_*)`
  is the irreducible gap. Without (F), the composition shows only that
  `Φ_Berry(m_*)` and the ambient `2/9` are both candidate values for
  `δ`, not that they coincide as the same observable on the live
  authority surface.

This precisely identifies what is missing: not the algebra of `2/3`
(retained), not the algebra of `arg(b) = δ` inside the parameterization
(unaudited but not the obstruction), not the algebra of the ambient
anomaly coefficient `2/9` (unaudited but exact on its candidate
geometry). The obstruction is the bridge equation (F): the descent
normalization at the dynamics-selected mass point.

Two earlier formulations are already in the repository:

   `koide_a1_radian_bridge_irreducibility_audit_note_2026-04-24`
   (retained_no_go) isolates `P_A1` as the "Type-B-to-radian observable
   law on the live authority surface". (F) is a sharper version: it
   reduces `P_A1` to a single bridge equation between two already-named
   objects (the Berry holonomy and the descended ambient anomaly).
2. The Callan-Harvey candidate note §3 already names two load-bearing
   open steps: (i) "Berry/inflow map" and (ii) "unit descent
   normalization". (F) is the joint statement: `N(m_*) = 1` simultaneously
   demands (i) the identification of the descended anomaly object with
   the Berry holonomy at `m_*`, AND (ii) the normalization equals unity
   at that point.

(F) is therefore the precise composite of the two open steps the
candidate route already names. It is not new content; it is a sharper
naming of the joint obstruction.
