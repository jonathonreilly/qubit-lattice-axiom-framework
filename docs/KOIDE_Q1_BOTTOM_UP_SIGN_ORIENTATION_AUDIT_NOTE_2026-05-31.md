## Question

Redo the sign problem from first principles.  Does the `Q = 1` packet secretly
contain the sign needed for

```text
delta = +2/9
```

once the C3 group algebra, APS value, and selected-line phase are reconstructed
bottom up?

## Answer

No.  The bottom-up audit reproduces the sign no-go without using the previous
no-go as an input.

The first-principles split is:

```text
Q1 source data:      S_Q1 = 10/9 e - 2/9(g + g^2)
signed phase line:  J = i(g - g^2)
```

A transposition sends

```text
g <-> g^2,
S_Q1 -> S_Q1,
J -> -J.
```

So `S_Q1` lives entirely in the transposition-even subalgebra

```text
span{e, g + g^2},
```

while the sign of the selected-line phase lives on the transposition-odd line

```text
span{i(g - g^2)}.
```

The projection is exactly zero:

```text
<S_Q1, i(g-g^2)> = 0.
```

## What The Audit Adds

### 1. Q1 cannot generate the odd line

The even generator closes internally:

```text
(g + g^2)^2 = 2e + (g + g^2).
```

Therefore every polynomial or functional readout from `S_Q1` remains in
`span{e, g + g^2}`.  It cannot produce an odd selected-line sign.

### 2. APS gives magnitude without orientation

The APS arithmetic still gives

```text
eta_abs = 2/9.
```

But a signed comparison target has the form

```text
eta_signed = epsilon * eta_abs,
epsilon in {+1, -1}.
```

The Q1 coefficient average recovers the magnitude:

```text
-(coeff_g + coeff_g2)/2 = 2/9.
```

It does not recover `epsilon`.

### 3. Selected-line mirrors are spectrally degenerate

The selected-line cosine spectrum at `+delta` and `-delta` is the same unordered
triple.  The sign becomes visible only after choosing an oriented Fourier/slot
frame or a based endpoint/source law.

### 4. Existing candidate sources do not supply the sign

- Retained parity/chiral violation acts on the `3 <-> 3bar` axis, not within
  the generation triplet.
- Existing C3 orbit maps leave a cyclic-shift labeling convention.
- The preserved-C3 interpretation treats species labels as downstream readout,
  not derivation input.
- The APS parity route still records the physical identification
  `delta = eta_APS` as the remaining gap.

## What This Claims

- `Q = 1` alone has no hidden signed selected-line primitive.
- The Q1-generated algebra has zero odd component.
- Q1 supplies the APS/Brannen magnitude `2/9`, not the sign.
- A positive sign theorem must derive an oriented C3 generator, selected-line
  endpoint, or equivalent odd primitive.

## What This Does Not Claim

- Does not kill future sign routes that add a genuine orientation primitive.
- Does not negate the Q1 coefficient bridge.
- Does not prove `delta = eta_APS`.
- Does not prove or disprove the physical source-domain law for charged
  leptons.
- Does not claim `Q = 1` is dark matter.
