## Question

After the Q1 bottom-up audit proved that `Q=1` itself is
transposition-even, the next natural candidate sign source was the fixed
imaginary slot in the selected-line carrier:

```text
Im H[0,2] = -gamma = -1/2.
```

Can the source-oriented `gamma` sheet supply the missing sign of

```text
delta = +2/9
```

and turn the Q1 APS/Brannen magnitude into a signed selected-line input?

## Answer

No.  The gamma sheet does not supply the selected-line sign in the current
readout.

This does **not** say the selected-line sign is wrong.  In the already-admitted
oriented selected-line frame, the target remains

```text
delta = +2/9
```

and the bridge still finds the same first-branch point

```text
m(+2/9) = -1.160443440065.
```

The result is only an underivation statement: `Q=1` and the source-oriented
`gamma` sheet do not force the orientation.  If a later theorem derives the
physical slot/Fourier orientation or based endpoint, the current `+2/9` branch
is still the compatible branch.

The reason is exact at the carrier/readout layer:

```text
H(m, -gamma) = conjugate(H(m, +gamma)).
```

The selected-line bridge reads only the real diagonal slots of `exp(H)`:

```text
v = Re exp(H)[2,2],
w = Re exp(H)[1,1].
```

Since

```text
exp(H(m, -gamma)) = conjugate(exp(H(m, +gamma)))
```

and the diagonal entries of a Hermitian exponential are real, the selected-line
slots are unchanged by `gamma -> -gamma`.  Therefore the small-branch
amplitude, Fourier phase offset, branch endpoints, and `delta=+2/9` root are
all identical on the two gamma sheets.

Numerically, both sheets give the same first-branch target:

```text
m(+2/9) = -1.160443440065
```

and neither sheet creates a `delta=-2/9` first-branch point.

## What This Solves

This prunes the tempting bridge:

```text
source-oriented gamma sign -> signed selected-line delta
```

The fixed imaginary slot is real structure, and the affine theorem remains
useful, but that sign is invisible to the selected-line diagonal-slot readout.
It cannot be the missing Q1 sign theorem.

## Where The Sign Actually Lives

The runner rechecks the mirror:

```text
{cos(delta + 2 pi k/3)}_{k=0,1,2}
```

has the same unordered spectrum at `+delta` and `-delta`.  The ordered mirror
is a transposition of the last two slots.  Thus the sign lives in an oriented
slot/Fourier frame, a based endpoint, or an equivalent odd selected-line
primitive.

Q1 is fixed by the same transposition:

```text
coeff_g(S_Q1) = coeff_g2(S_Q1) = -2/9,
coeff_g - coeff_g2 = 0.
```

So neither Q1 nor the gamma sheet supplies the odd datum by itself.
