## What was overstated
Those notes escalated to "A1+A2+retained natively PREDICT Q=1 (F3)" on the
basis that any *consistent* dimension count gives F3 and the equal-block
(1,1) weight is "inconsistent." **That criterion is wrong.**

## The correction (self-audit)
The equal-block weight `(w_+, w_⊥) = (1,1)` that gives F1 (Q=2/3) is the
**counting measure on the two minimal central idempotents** of
`ℝ[Z₃] = ℝ ⊕ ℂ` — i.e. weight each *real-irreducible block* once. That is a
fully canonical, internally-consistent measure (the uniform measure on the
spectrum of the algebra's center). It is **not** "inconsistent."

Two canonical measures therefore exist, and they disagree:
- **dimension / trace (Plancherel)** weighting `(1,2)` → `r=1` → **Q=1** (F3);
- **block / central-idempotent** counting `(1,1)` → `r=1/2` → **Q=2/3** (F1).

Pure representation theory ranks **neither** above the other (this is what
the earlier 14-lens panel and the kinematic-no-go's own rep-theory angle
found; the "predicts Q=1" notes silently contradicted that).

## Scope of what the campaign DID establish (still valid)
The trace/dimension-type mechanisms — modular flow, canonical/Plancherel
trace, Euclidean-Jordan trace, Gaussian functional determinant,
Kähler/Bargmann measure, positivity saturation, perturbative +
nonperturbative dynamics — **all realize the dimension measure → F3**.
That is real: *if* the physical weighting is trace-type, Q=1. What is NOT
established is that trace-type is forced; the block-counting measure
(→Q=2/3) is equally canonical and is untouched by those mechanisms (it is
not a trace).
