## Result — `sign_is_free_import`
1. **Additivity is sign-agnostic.** The signed trace `Tr(H)=Σλ_k`, the unsigned `Σ|λ_k|`, the magnitude
   `log|det|=Σlog|λ_k|`, **and** the η-count `η=Σsign(λ_k)` are **all** additive over disjoint/direct-sum
   records (verified). So `I(R₁⊔R₂)=I(R₁)+I(R₂)` selects **none** of them — additivity cannot fix the sign.
   `MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-04` independently disclaims Born weights / modulus / phase-blindness, putting the
   signed-vs-modulus choice *outside* the axiom by its own text.
2. **Born kills the sign.** `|+x|² = |−x|²` — a post-Born record is sign-blind. The only wired-up record
   generator on `main` (`FLAVOR_LOGDET`, `I=log|det|=Σlog|λ_k|`) is modulus-first → the **unsigned** side →
   Q≠2/3 generically. The signed Q=2/3 needs the **pre-Born** operator trace `Tr(H)=Σλ_k`, a *different*
   functional the axioms do not select.
3. **η is refuted as a forcer.** `η=Σsign(λ_k)` is a **ℤ-valued sign-count** — too coarse: two spectra with
   identical `η=3` give **Q=1/3 vs Q=1/2** (verified counterexample). It cannot reconstruct the real-valued
   Brannen denominator `Tr(H)=3a`. (And the framework's genuine `2/9=η_APS` lives on the **phase** axis δ,
   orthogonal to the Q-value; "H=iD" is itself unaudited.) The Dirac structure **favors** the signed reading
   — a real spectrum is available from self-adjointness — but does **not force** it.
4. **Empirical deflation.** The physical charged leptons are **sign-homogeneous** (all `√m_k > 0`), so
   **signed = unsigned = Q_PDG = 0.666661 ≈ 2/3** there (verified). The signed-vs-unsigned dichotomy is **not
   load-bearing on the observed spectrum** — both readings coincide at the physical point.
