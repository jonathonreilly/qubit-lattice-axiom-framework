## Theorem (narrow)

### T1 — The complex unit is a convention, by the Qubit axiom's own clause

The live axiom memo
[`MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md`](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md) states,
verbatim:

> A `Cl(3,0)`-compatible real-algebra presentation may be used equivalently and
> adds no further primitive structure.

and:

> No possibility is privileged. Possibilities are distinguished by the supplied
> algebraic structure alone.

In the real presentation, the complex unit corresponds to the central
pseudoscalar `omega_ps = e_1 e_2 e_3`, with `omega_ps^2 = -1`. The Clifford
grade involution `alpha`, defined by `e_i -> -e_i` and extended
multiplicatively, is a canonical automorphism of the supplied real algebra.
It sends `omega_ps -> -omega_ps`. Thus the two central square roots of `-1`
are exchanged by an automorphism of the supplied structure, and the choice
between them (`i` versus `-i`) is not fixed by that structure.

In the `M_2(C)` presentation, entrywise complex conjugation is a real-algebra
automorphism realizing the same swap. The conjugated Pauli generators still
satisfy the Clifford relations, while their pseudoscalar is `-iI` rather than
`+iI`.

The memo's Qualification clause states, verbatim:

> a law may not depend on a choice not fixed by the supplied structure, unless
> that choice is admitted.

Therefore no A_min-derivable law or selector may depend on the complex-unit
orientation.

### T2 — Conjugation fixes the derivable carrier surface and swaps the doublet sectors

On the `C_3` sector algebra of the corner-triplet carrier, let `C` be the real
cyclic generator and let

`P_chi = (1/3)(I + conjugate(chi) C + conjugate(chi^2) C^2)`

for `chi` in `{1, w, conjugate(w)}`, with the algebraic value
`w = -1/2 + (sqrt(3)/2)i`. These character projectors resolve the identity.
Entrywise conjugation fixes `C` and `P_1` and swaps
`P_w <-> P_conjugate(w)`. The translation eigenvalue structure is likewise
real. Hence the automorphism fixes the supplied and derived real carrier
operators pointwise while exchanging the two doublet character sectors. The
`w`-versus-`conjugate(w)` distinction is exactly a complex-unit-orientation
distinction.

### T3 — Finest derivable sector partition is the K/CPT orbit partition

By T1 and T2, every A_min-derivable sector selector must be invariant under
the swap. A swap-invariant function on the three sectors is constant on the
doublet pair. The exhaustive finite check confirms that exactly 9 of the 27
functions from three sectors to three labels are invariant, and every one
satisfies `f(w-sector) = f(conjugate(w)-sector)`. The mechanically induced
partition restriction has only the one-block partition and
`{singlet, doublet-orbit}`; its finest member has exactly two blocks.

Therefore the finest derivable partition of the three character sectors is
the K/CPT orbit partition

`{singlet, doublet-orbit}`,

with the doublet as **one** block. Its projector
`P_w + P_conjugate(w)` is conjugation-fixed and has rank 2.

The Record additivity clause states, verbatim:

> For any finite collection of pairwise-disjoint records, scalar readout `I` is
> additive, with `I(empty)=0`.

Accordingly, the registrable sector-weight simplex at this surface is the
2-simplex `(p_s, p_d)`, with `p_d` the **one** orbit weight.
