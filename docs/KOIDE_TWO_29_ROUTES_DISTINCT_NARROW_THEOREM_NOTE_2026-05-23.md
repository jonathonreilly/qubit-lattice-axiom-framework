## Question

Two index/anomaly objects both evaluate to `2/9` at the framework's `d=3` and
have been proposed as the geometric source of the Koide phase `δ`:

- the **APS equivariant η-defect** of the `C_d` rotation with transverse weights
  `(1, d−1)` (the APS-η route, `KOIDE_PHASE_APS_ETA_PARITY_ROUTE...`);
- the **Callan-Harvey hypercharge-cubed anomaly** `Tr[Y³]_{Q_L}` per generation
  (the anomaly-inflow route, `KOIDE_OPLOCALITY_BRANNEN_PLANCHEREL_CALLAN_HARVEY...`).

Are these the *same* `2/9` (two sides of one index theorem, hence one shared
normalization residual), or two distinct objects that merely coincide?

## Answer

**Distinct.** As functions of `d` they are

```text
APS η-defect(1, d−1; d)  =  (d² − 1) / (12 d)        [geometric, rotation order d]
Tr[Y³]_{Q_L}             =  2 N_c (1/N_c)³ = 2/d²     [anomaly, color rank d]
```

These differ for all `d ≠ 3`. They are equal iff `(d²−1)/(12d) = 2/d²`, i.e.
`d³ − d = 24`, whose **unique real root is `d = 3`**. At `d = 3` both equal
`2/9`. So the agreement is a consistency at the framework's independently
retained `d = 3`, **not** a single shared object.

## Theorem

**(1) Closed form.** Using `(ζ^k−1)(ζ^{−k}−1) = 4 sin²(πk/d)` and
`Σ_{k=1}^{d−1} 1/sin²(πk/d) = (d²−1)/3`, the APS η-defect with weights
`(1, d−1)` is `(1/d)·(1/4)·(d²−1)/3 = (d²−1)/(12d)` (verified `d=2..7`).

**(2) Anomaly.** `Q_L` has `Y = 1/N_c` and multiplicity `2N_c`, so
`Tr[Y³]_{Q_L} = 2N_c·(1/N_c)³ = 2/N_c² = 2/d²`.

**(3) Coincidence locus.** `(d²−1)/(12d) = 2/d² ⇔ d(d²−1) = 24 ⇔ d³−d−24 = 0`.
Factoring: `(d−3)(d²+3d+8) = 0`; the quadratic has negative discriminant, so
`d = 3` is the unique real root. Both routes equal `2/9` there. ∎

## What this claims

- The two `2/9` objects are different functions of `d`: `(d²−1)/(12d)` and
  `2/d²`.
- They coincide exactly at `d=3` (`=2/9`), uniquely (`d³−d=24`).

## What this does NOT claim

- Does **not** derive `d=3` (color rank `N_c=3` is independently retained); the
  coincidence is a consistency *at* `d=3`, not a selection of it.
- Does **not** prove `δ = 2/9` on either route; each route still needs its own
  normalization coefficient.
- Does **not** identify the rotation order `d` (of `C_3[111]`) with the color
  rank `N_c` as a derivation; the framework ties both to `3`, but they enter the
  two formulas as a-priori different parameters.
