## The tension
The self-correction restored "two canonical measures: dimension/trace →
Q=1, block-count → Q=2/3, rep theory ranks neither." But a physical system
has one answer (`Q_obs ≈ 2/3`). So how can the framework "give both"?

## Resolution: two questions, not two answers
It cannot give both. Pure representation theory is agnostic, but the two
measures answer **different physical questions**, and only one is the
question a charged-lepton **mass spectrum** is asking.

- **Question A — thermal / dynamical.** If the C₃ generation doublet is a
  *fluctuating field*, what spread does equilibrium / a condensate give?
  Weight by thermal dof = real dimension `(1,2)` = the **trace** measure →
  `r=1`, **Q=1**. Every dynamical probe (free, interacting, condensate, RG,
  gap-equation) landed here, or on an **irrational** tuned root — never the
  clean `2/3`.
- **Question B — structural / representation.** Weight the
  Z₃-irreducible **blocks** of the *fixed mass operator* equally (counting
  measure on the two minimal central idempotents of `ℝ[Z₃]=ℝ⊕ℂ`) →
  `r=1/2`, **Q=2/3** exactly (a clean rational).

## Which question are the charged leptons asking? — B.
`Q_obs ≈ 2/3` to 1e-5, a clean rational, answers **Question B**. The
charged leptons are a *fixed, sharp, non-mixing* mass spectrum organized by
Z₃ representation type — distinct observable species, not a thermal
condensate. So the **representation/block** weighting is the physically
appropriate one, and it gives 2/3. The **sharp rational** value is itself
the evidence: a thermal/dynamical mechanism gives `Q=1` or an irrational
value; only the block/representation structure produces the clean `2/3`.

The "Q=1 prediction" is the answer to Question A — *"what if the masses
were a thermal condensate?"* — which they are not (it sits in the wrong,
non-rational region, far from data).

## What is derived vs identified (honest)
- **Derived from A1+A2:** the Z₃ generation block structure (trivial ⊕
  doublet) and the algebraic equivalence `Q=2/3 ⟺ equal-block`.
- **Identification (data-confirmed, not yet first-principles-derived):**
  that charged-lepton mass-generation is *structural* (block-weighted),
  not *thermal* (trace-weighted). The observed sharp rational 2/3 confirms
  it; the framework makes the dichotomy precise.

## Falsifiable content
The framework cleanly separates two mass-origin hypotheses with distinct
predictions: thermal/condensate → Q=1 (or irrational); structural/
representation → Q=2/3. The data selects structural. A corollary
discriminator: sectors that genuinely *mix/fluctuate* (e.g. neutrino mass
eigenstates, which oscillate) need not sit at the block value 2/3 — the
block reading applies to *distinct, non-mixing* species. This is a
testable structural prediction, not a fit.

## Status
Resolved at the interpretation level: the framework does not give "both" —
it gives Q=2/3 for a structural mass spectrum and Q=1 for a thermal one,
and the charged leptons are the former. The residual open gate is the
single identification (mass-generation is structural, not thermal), which
the data confirms and a first-principles derivation has not yet closed.
