## Result Up Front

Six occupancy bits. `n_μ = c_{+μ} − c_{-μ}` and
`k = |n|^2 ∈ {1,2,3}` on the 56 nonzero cells. Proper cube
rotations act by `c'(g) = c(R^{-1} g)`.

The group generated by `90°` about `z` and about `x` decomposes
those 56 cells into more orbits than the three `k`-bins. In
particular `k = 1` (24 cells) is more than one orbit, while
`k = 3` is a single orbit of 8.

`k` is a union-of-orbits label, not a cubic type.

This is still a comparator fact, not a TOE.
