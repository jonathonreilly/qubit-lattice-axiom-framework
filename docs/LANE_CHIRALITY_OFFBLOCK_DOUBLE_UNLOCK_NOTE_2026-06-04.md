## 0. The question

Two sector-specific open gates may share one structure (a possible **double-unlock**):

- **Gate 1 — lane assignment.** Charged leptons sit at the symmetric dial point `r = |b|²/a² = 1/2`
  (Q = 2/3). Per `FLAVOR_LANE_PANEL_REDUCES_TO_DOUBLET_MODE_COUNT_2026-05-31`, the assignment
  reduces to ONE primitive: the C₃ doublet counts as **one complex mode** (det_C → `3a²=6|b|²` →
  `r=1/2` → Q=2/3) vs **two real modes** (det_R → `3a²=3|b|²` → `r=1` → Q=1). A candidate
  discriminator: charged ∧ colorless (color breaks toward hierarchy, neutrality toward degenerate).

- **Gate 2 — chirality (off-block).** Per `KOIDE_ANTICOMMUTING_OPERATOR_DERIVATION_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10`,
  any Hermitian `H` on the generation `R³` with `{H, Γ_χ}=0` (where `Γ_χ=(2/3)J−I` is the Z₃
  character grading) forces Koide Q=2/3 on its nonzero eigenvectors. Per
  `KOIDE_Z3_EQUIVARIANT_ANTICOMMUTING_NO_GO_NOTE_2026-05-16`, `comm(R) ∩ anticomm(Γ_χ) = {0}`:
  every **on-block** (block-diagonal / circulant / C₃-equivariant) operator COMMUTES with `Γ_χ`.
  Anticommutation REQUIRES an **off-block** (singlet↔doublet mixing) generator on the generation
  factor. The records/decoherence flow (`FLAVOR_R_HALF_IS_THE_RECORDS_FLOW_SEPARATRIX_2026-06-02`)
  is intrinsically on-block — it preserves the isotype blocks — so it cannot supply chirality.

**Hypothesis to test.** The gauge content distinguishing charged leptons is their CHIRAL coupling:
LH doublet `(1,2)_{−1/2}` + RH singlet `(1,1)_{−1}` — the chiral Dirac mass structure, which
couples LH↔RH (distinct chirality sectors). On the generation factor the Yukawa `Y_e` couples the
generations. Could the chiral (LH↔RH) coupling be the off-block (singlet↔doublet) generator
chirality needs — present for charged leptons (chiral Dirac) but absent/different for neutrinos
(Majorana), thereby tying Gate 2 to Gate 1?

## 1. Verdict: OFF-BLOCK-BUT-WRONG-TENSOR-FACTOR — no double-unlock

The chiral Dirac mass structure `D = [[0, Y],[Y†, 0]]` **is** a genuine off-block generator and
**does** anticommute with a chirality grading — but off-block in the **chirality (σ₃) factor**,
NOT the **generation factor** that `Γ_χ` grades. The textbook anticommutation `{D, γ_CL}=0` does
**not transport** to `{D, Γ_χ⊗I₂}=0`. The Koide value the chiral structure actually yields (with
the framework-primitive C₃-equivariant Yukawa) is **Q = 1** (dimension / det_R lane), not 2/3.

This is precisely the "wrong tensor factor" wall flagged as **escape-hatch (II)** in the
`KOIDE_Z3_EQUIVARIANT_ANTICOMMUTING_NO_GO` note. Gate 1 and Gate 2 remain **two distinct
unsupplied pins**.

## 2. The decisive algebra (the transport law)

Build the standard NCG/Connes-Lott fermion Hilbert space
`H_st = R³ ⊗ (H_L ⊕ H_R)`, generation factor `R³` tensored with the L/R chirality doubling
(`H_L ⊕ H_R ≅ C²`). Order basis states by `index = 3·χ + g` (χ∈{0=L,1=R}, g∈{0,1,2}). The chiral
Dirac and the two candidate gradings are

```text
   D        =  [[0, Y],[Y†, 0]]            (chirality-OFF-diagonal; Hermitian)
   γ_CL     =  σ₃ ⊗ I₃   = diag(+I₃, −I₃)  (grading on the CHIRALITY factor)
   Γ_χ⊗I₂   =  I₂ ⊗ Γ_χ                    (grading on the GENERATION factor; identity on chirality)
```

**(a) The chiral Dirac is off-block in the CHIRALITY factor.** `{D, γ_CL}=0` holds *identically*,
for any Yukawa `Y` (runner Part B; verified for 25 random `Y`). This is the finite-dimensional
analog of "`D` anticommutes with `γ₅`". The off-block-ness lives entirely between L and R: the
chirality-diagonal blocks `D_LL = D_RR = 0`.

**(b) The transport law.** Because `D` is chirality-off-block and `Γ_χ⊗I₂` is chirality-identity,

```text
   {D, Γ_χ⊗I₂}  =  [[0, {Y, Γ_χ}],[{Y, Γ_χ}†, 0]]          (runner Part C1)
```

i.e. the 6×6 anticommutator collapses **exactly** to the 3×3 generation-factor anticommutator
`{Y, Γ_χ}`. The chiral LH↔RH off-block structure contributes **nothing new** — `{D, Γ_χ⊗I₂}=0`
holds **iff** `{Y, Γ_χ}=0` on the generation factor. The hypothesis reduces, with no residue, to
the **same on-block requirement** Gate 2 already names.

**(c) The framework-primitive Yukawa is C₃-equivariant ⇒ it COMMUTES with Γ_χ.** A circulant
Yukawa `Y = aI + bR + cR²` lies in the commutant of the cyclic shift `R`. Since `Γ_χ` is *itself*
circulant (`Γ_χ = −⅓I + ⅔R + ⅔R²`), `[Y, Γ_χ]=0` ⇒ `{Y, Γ_χ} = 2YΓ_χ ≠ 0` (runner C2–C5). And
symbolically (runner C6, reproduced in Part G as `comm(R) ∩ anticomm(Γ_χ) = {0}`), **no** nonzero
circulant `Y` satisfies `{Y, Γ_χ}=0`. So a chiral Dirac built on the framework-primitive Yukawa
does **not** anticommute with the generation grading.

## 3. Projection to the generation factor

Restricting/projecting the chiral Dirac to `R³` does not yield an off-block operator (runner Part D):

- Partial trace over chirality: `Tr_χ(D) = D_LL + D_RR = 0` — the chirality-off-block Dirac leaves
  **no** generation operator under the trace.
- The physically meaningful induced generation operator is the mass²/`|D|²` block `Y†Y` (and
  `YY†`), which acts *within* a fixed chirality. For a C₃-equivariant `Y` this is **circulant
  (on-block)**: `[Y†Y, R]=0`, hence `[Y†Y, Γ_χ]=0`. The projection lands squarely in the
  on-block (no-go) class.

## 4. The Koide value the chiral structure gives, and the genuine off-block pin

With the framework-primitive C₃-equivariant Yukawa, the induced generation operator is circulant,
so it sits on the **dimension/det_R lane `r=1` → Q=1** (runner Part E), in agreement with the
records-flow analysis (the 3-real-DOF endpoint) and the dimension-count reading of the lane panel.
The singlet Fourier mode gives the degenerate `r=0` → Q=1/3 endpoint. Neither is `r=1/2`.

To realise Q=2/3 one STILL needs the **genuine off-block family**
`H = ⅓(1⊗h + h⊗1)`, `Σh=0` (the 2-dim anti-commuting family of the L4 theorem): this `H` *does*
anticommute with `Γ_χ`, its nonzero eigenvectors give Q=2/3, and it is **not circulant**
(`[H,R]≠0` — it genuinely mixes singlet↔doublet). The chiral Dirac with a C₃-equivariant Yukawa
does **not** produce this `H` on the generation factor.

> **The Q the chiral-Dirac sector-structure gives is `Q = 1`** (det_R / dimension lane, `r=1`) —
> not 2/3.

## 5. Lane tie-in: Dirac vs Majorana lives in the chirality factor, not the generation factor

The hypothesis's appeal was that the chiral/Majorana distinction (charged vs neutral) might *tie*
chirality to the lane assignment. It does not (runner Part F):

- Dirac (charged lepton): chirality-**off-diagonal** Yukawa `Y`. Majorana (neutrino): mass on the
  **same** chirality → chirality-**diagonal** block. The distinction is a **chirality-factor**
  block-pattern, with *identical* (circulant) generation content.
- Both induce the **same** circulant (on-block) generation operator `Y†Y = M†M`. So the
  charged/neutral distinction produces **no** generation-factor sector split.
- The lane primitive (det_C: doublet = 1 complex mode → `r=1/2`; det_R: doublet = 2 real modes →
  `r=1`) is a **generation-factor mode-count**, untouched by the Dirac/Majorana chirality-factor
  distinction. So the chiral structure supplies **neither** the generation-factor chirality grading
  **nor** the det_C-vs-det_R lane discriminator.

## 6. Why this was worth testing (the no-coincidence intuition) and why it fails cleanly

The intuition is sound at the level of *category*: chirality genuinely is "an off-block /
LH↔RH-coupling phenomenon", and Koide Q=2/3 genuinely needs "an off-block / singlet↔doublet
generator". The hypothesis bets these two off-block structures are the same. The computation shows
they are off-block in **orthogonal tensor factors** of `R³ ⊗ (H_L⊕H_R)`:

| structure | off-block in | grading it anticommutes with |
|---|---|---|
| chiral Dirac `D` | chirality factor (σ₃) | `γ_CL = σ₃⊗I₃` ✓ |
| Koide-needed `H` | generation factor (R³) | `Γ_χ⊗I₂` (needs `[H,R]≠0`) |

The transport law (§2b) is the precise statement that these do not couple: anticommutation with the
chirality grading says nothing about anticommutation with the generation grading. This is the same
**wrong-tensor-factor obstruction** the `KOIDE_Z3_EQUIVARIANT_ANTICOMMUTING_NO_GO` note isolated as
escape-hatch (II): `γ_CL` and `Γ_χ` live in distinct factors, and a separate bridge theorem — one
that puts a C₃-orbit-splitting (off-circulant) operator **on the generation factor** — would be
needed. The chiral Dirac is not that bridge.
