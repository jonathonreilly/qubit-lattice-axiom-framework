## The framework-local statement

Let `(-Delta_lat)` be the nearest-neighbor graph Laplacian on the
cubic lattice `Z^3` with stencil

```text
(-Delta_lat f)(x) = 6 f(x) - sum_{|y - x| = 1} f(y).
```

Its Green's function `G(r) = (-Delta_lat)^{-1}(r)` (the lattice
potential generated by a unit point source, with the usual zero-mode
regularization on finite tori before the infinite-volume limit) satisfies the
framework-local large-separation normalization:

```text
G(r)  ->  1 / (4 pi |r|)        as |r| -> infinity.
```

The leading `1 / (4 pi |r|)` continuum-limit form is therefore the
framework's own nearest-neighbor `Z^3` Poisson kernel normalization with unit
lattice spacing. Subleading corrections decay faster than `1 / |r|` and are
not load-bearing for the downstream inverse-square coefficient.

## Proof on the framework operator

Fourier transform the `Z^3` graph Laplacian. Its symbol is

```text
lambda(k) = 6 - 2(cos k_x + cos k_y + cos k_z),    k in [-pi, pi]^3.
```

The runner checks the exact framework convention

```text
lambda(k) = |k|^2 + O(|k|^4)
```

near `k = 0`, and `lambda(k) > 0` away from the zero mode. Hence the
infinite-volume Green kernel is the oscillatory integral

```text
G(x) = (2 pi)^(-3) int_{[-pi,pi]^3} exp(i k.x) / lambda(k) dk
```

with its only non-smooth contribution at `k = 0`. Split the Brillouin-zone
integral with a smooth cutoff `chi(k)` equal to one near zero:

```text
1/lambda(k) = chi(k)/|k|^2 + R(k).
```

The remainder `R(k)` is less singular than `|k|^-2`; after subtracting the
continuum singularity its Fourier transform is lower order at large `|x|`.
The load-bearing leading term is therefore the inverse Fourier transform of
`1/|k|^2` in three dimensions, whose unit-flux normalization is
`1/(4 pi |x|)`. The runner separately checks this flux convention and checks
that applying the actual lattice stencil to `1/(4 pi r)` away from the source
leaves an `O(r^-5)` residual, confirming that the continuum-leading kernel is
asymptotically lattice-harmonic for the exact stencil used here.
