## Exact Arithmetic Check

The runner verifies (B1)-(B4) by a combination of exact symbolic `sympy`
arithmetic and finite numerical lattice-arithmetic checks:

- (B1) `sympy` verifies that the second-order Taylor expansion of
  `lambda(k) = 6 - 2(cos k_x + cos k_y + cos k_z)` is
  `k_x^2 + k_y^2 + k_z^2`, that all cubic terms vanish by cosine parity, and
  that the first correction is quartic.
- (B2) `sympy` verifies that for `phi(r) = 1/(4 pi r)`,
  `-d phi / dr = 1/(4 pi r^2)`, so integration over any sphere gives unit
  flux.
- (B3) the runner evaluates the exact nearest-neighbor stencil on
  `1/(4 pi |r|)` at axis points `r = (n, 0, 0)` for
  `n in {16, 32, 64, 128}`, confirming bounded/stable
  `|(-Delta_lat phi)(r)| |r|^5`.
- (B4) the runner pins the unique coefficient compatible with the unit
  point-source convention and the local `|k|^2` singular mode:
  `c = 1/(4 pi)`.

The runner does not compute a fitted continuum limit and does not consume a
physical observed value. It certifies the coefficient for the explicit
framework stencil.
