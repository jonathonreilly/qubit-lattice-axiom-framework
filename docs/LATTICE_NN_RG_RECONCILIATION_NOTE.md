## What Would Be Needed To Promote RG Beyond Suggestive

The raw NN refinement trend itself has now extended one step beyond the
previously closed `h = 0.25`: the 2026-05-16 high-precision raw-kernel
certificate adds a Born-clean `h = 0.125` row directly on the raw kernel. That
closes the narrow "does the raw kernel extend to `h = 0.125`" question for the
no-rescale case. It does not by itself close the RG/continuum question.

To promote the RG story beyond "suggestive," we would still need artifact-backed
evidence of all of the following:

- a successful continuation at `h = 0.125` under the chosen RG scaling law
  (the float64 RG-schedule probe still fails at `h = 0.125` for the three
  tested schedules; the high-precision certificate covers only the raw,
  no-schedule kernel)
- a scaling law whose gravity trend stays stable across more than one refinement
  step, not just a single `h = 0.5` to `h = 0.25` pair
- either a verified `alpha = 2.0` sweep or another law that beats it on a clear
  criterion, with the result frozen in logs
- practical runtime or exact-arithmetic continuation under the schedule so the
  next spacing is not blocked by overflow or timeout in the RG runner

Until that exists, RG is best described as a narrow fixed-point-style probe, not
as a promoted renormalization result.
