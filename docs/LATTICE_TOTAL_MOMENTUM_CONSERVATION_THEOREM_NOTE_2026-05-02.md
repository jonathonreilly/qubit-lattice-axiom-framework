## Statement

Let `Lambda` be a finite periodic staggered carrier with even spatial
side length `L_s`. For each spatial axis `rho`, let `T_{2 rho}` denote
the unitary shift by two lattice sites in that axis. Its finite cyclic
order is

```text
    N_2 = L_s / gcd(L_s, 2) = L_s / 2                     (1)
```

for even `L_s`. Let `H` be any finite Hamiltonian or transfer generator
on this same carrier that respects the retained two-step translation
symmetry:

```text
    [H, T_{2 rho}] = 0.                                   (2)
```

Then:

**(M1) Two-step translation sectors are conserved.** The spectral
projectors

```text
    Pi_{rho,k} = (1 / N_2) sum_{m=0}^{N_2-1}
                 exp(-2 pi i k m / N_2) T_{2 rho}^m       (3)
```

commute with `H`. Therefore time evolution by `H` preserves the sector
weight `||Pi_{rho,k} psi||^2` for every state `psi`.

**(M2) The conserved quantum number is the two-step character.** The
eigenvalues of `T_{2 rho}` are

```text
    exp(2 pi i k / N_2),    k = 0, ..., N_2 - 1.           (4)
```

Equivalently, one-site momenta are identified only modulo `pi`: the
one-step modes `n` and `n + L_s/2` have the same two-step character.

**(M3) No canonical density theorem is claimed.** Equations (1)-(4) do
not derive a local density `P^mu_x`, do not prove
`partial^L_mu P^mu_x = 0`, and do not establish a full one-site
momentum operator on `H_phys`. Those stronger statements require a
separate retained bridge deriving the canonical density from the
two-step Ward identity.

## Proof

### Step 1 - retained two-step symmetry

The cited Noether theorem supplies the exact `(2Z)^3` central two-step
Ward identity for the admitted staggered carrier. On a finite periodic
carrier this symmetry is represented by the finite unitary shift
`T_{2 rho}`.

### Step 2 - finite symmetry projectors

Since `T_{2 rho}^{N_2} = I`, the projectors (3) are the standard
orthogonal idempotents of the finite cyclic group generated by
`T_{2 rho}`. They resolve the identity and satisfy

```text
    T_{2 rho} Pi_{rho,k} = exp(2 pi i k / N_2) Pi_{rho,k}.
```

This is finite-dimensional group algebra.

### Step 3 - sector conservation

If `H` respects the same retained two-step translation symmetry, then
`[H, T_{2 rho}] = 0`. Since each `Pi_{rho,k}` is a polynomial in
`T_{2 rho}`, `[H, Pi_{rho,k}] = 0`. Thus for
`psi(t) = exp(-i t H) psi(0)`,

```text
    ||Pi_{rho,k} psi(t)||^2 = ||Pi_{rho,k} psi(0)||^2.
```

This proves (M1). The phase spectrum of the finite cyclic group proves
(M2), and the explicit scope boundary proves (M3).
