## Verdict

one more block learning that the list is complete. Every trick for
smuggling freedom into a law — feed it a tape, let it peek at its

it, and nothing in this block or this window earns the asking of
it. The Born lane's successor line ends here, one sentence wide,
with the sentence's price tag attached and its adoption nobody's to
make but the owner's — on a day the evidence earns it. Independent
