## Proof

Direct verification on the cited retained inputs plus standard
finite-dimensional Lie-algebra representation theory.

### (B1) Sym² carries a non-trivial 3-dim SU(3) irrep.

By (I2), su(3) acts on the 3-dim Sym² block via the embedded Gell-Mann
generators per Step 4 of the integration note. The action is
non-trivial: the joint commutant has dimension 10, with 9 dimensions
coming from gl(3) on Sym²; a trivial su(3) action on Sym² would leave
only the 1-dim gl(1) factor in the commutant, contradicting (I2).

By the standard su(3) representation classification (Cartan-Weyl
highest weights `(p, q)` give `dim(p, q) = (p+1)(q+1)(p+q+2)/2`), the
non-trivial irreps of su(3) of dimension `≤ 3` are exactly the
fundamental `3 = (1, 0)` and its conjugate `3̄ = (0, 1)`; no irrep
has dim 1 (non-trivially) or dim 2. Hence the su(3) action on the
3-dim Sym² block is either `3` or `3̄`. The Gell-Mann embedding of
(I2) fixes the realised rep as `3` (the conjugate `3̄` is a
parity-related choice that produces the same block-content claim
modulo complex conjugation).

### (B2) Anti² carries the trivial SU(3) representation.

The Anti² block is 1-dimensional. Any 1-dim complex representation
of SU(3) is trivial because SU(3) is its own commutator subgroup
(`[SU(3), SU(3)] = SU(3)`, so the abelianisation is trivial, so
the only continuous homomorphism `SU(3) → U(1)` is constant).
Equivalently, by (I2) the `gl(3) ⊕ gl(1)` commutant has a single
abelian direction `gl(1)`, so the su(3) traceless part of `gl(3)`
acts as zero on Anti² — that is the structural meaning of the
Sym²/Anti² block split under (I2).

### (B3) LH-doublet block decomposition.

By tensor-product distributivity of direct sums,

```text
C² ⊗ (Sym² ⊕ Anti²)  =  (C² ⊗ Sym²) ⊕ (C² ⊗ Anti²)
                     =  (2, 3)        ⊕ (2, 1),
```

with dimensions `2·3 = 6` and `2·1 = 2`, summing to 8 consistent with
the (6+2) tracelessness count of (I3). The SU(2)-isospin label `2`
is the SU(2)_weak doublet on the first factor; the SU(3)-color label
`3` is the fundamental rep by (B1); the SU(3)-color label `1` is the
trivial rep by (B2).

∎
