## Theorem (template from retained substrate, I3 not load-bearing)

first factor and the residual transposition `τ` on the 4-point base:

**(B1) Sym² carries the fundamental 3.** `τ` splits `C⁴ = Sym²(3) ⊕ Anti²(1)`
(I1). The su(3) of the `gl(3)⊕gl(1)` commutant (I2) acts non-trivially and
irreducibly on the 3-dim Sym² block; by the su(3) dimension formula

are `3 = (1,0)` and `3̄ = (0,1)`, and the Gell-Mann embedding fixes `3`.

**(B2) Anti² carries the singlet 1.** Every embedded su(3) generator annihilates

and `[SU(2)_weak, su(3)_color] = 0` (product structure).

representation dimensions `2·3` and `2·1`. No eigenvalue-ratio input is used in
