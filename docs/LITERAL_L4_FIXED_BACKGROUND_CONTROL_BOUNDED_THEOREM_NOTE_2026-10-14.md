## 4. Positive-time full-unitary recurrence with a finite certificate

Let `D=2^1344` and `s=√D=2^672`. The background on the entire box is `H_A⊗I`; hence a full active-space operator bound has the same operator norm on the box and after any passive reference. No dimension for the reference enters the argument.

**Recurrence lemma.** For any positive step a and integer Q≥1, there is an integer `1≤q≤Q^D` such that

\[
 \|e^{-iqaH_A}-I\|\le2\pi/Q,
 \quad \|e^{-iqaH_A}-I\|_F\le 2\pi s/Q.               \tag{9}
\]

Proof: diagonalize the finite Hermitian H_A only for the argument. Place the D spectral phases of `exp(-ijaH_A)`, divided by 2π and taken modulo 1, into Q^D equal half-open boxes for `j=0,...,Q^D`. Two points are in the same box. Their difference q has every phase within 2π/Q of an integer multiple of 2π. The scalar eigenvalue offset is one of these phases, not factored out. The spectral operator estimate and the Frobenius estimate follow. Degeneracies, zero eigenvalues, irrational or incommensurate coefficients and either sign of an energy cause no difficulty. This is a finite full-U(D) recurrence argument, not exact periodicity.

For the effective certificate take a **rational** step a>0 and rational `0<η≤1`, and choose

\[
 Q=\lceil32s/\eta\rceil,\quad N=Q^D,\quad T_{\max}=aN.
 \tag{10}
\]

At some q≤N, the true Frobenius residual is below η/4, since `2πs/Q≤πη/16<η/4`. The following deliberately expensive deterministic search supplies a finite certificate, rather than selecting an unknown returning time by continuity:

1. Obtain a complex-rational Hermitian matrix Hhat with

2. Put `X=T_max(h+1)` and choose an integer n≥0 so that

3. For `q=1,...,N`, calculate the finite matrix polynomial
