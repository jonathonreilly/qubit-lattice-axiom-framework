## 2. Fixed coordinates and literal static operator

Supply a lattice translation/symmetry mapping the following unit cube to the actual placement, with its six new vertices disjoint from **all** original occupied sites:

    y=(0,0,0),  w=(1,0,0),
    a=(0,1,0),  b=(1,1,0),  a'=(0,0,1),  b'=(1,0,1),

The four a,b,a',b' sites are mediator qubits and l,r are head qubits. The initial new state is one excitation at l, with r and all four mediators zero. These are six supplied pure bits, not an assumption about existing dirty transit. The original data edge y-w remains at its original coordinates. A fully occupied adjacent cube does not satisfy this premise; no packing or vacancy theorem is supplied.

For distinct new sites u,v define

    n_u=|1><1|_u,   sigma_u^+=|1><0|_u,
    S_uv=sigma_v^+ sigma_u^-+sigma_u^+ sigma_v^-
        =(X_u X_v+Y_u Y_v)/2,
    D_uv=i(sigma_v^+ sigma_u^- - sigma_u^+ sigma_v^-)
        =(X_u Y_v-Y_u X_v)/2.

Both S and D have full two-qubit norm one, vanish on 00 and 11, and preserve excitation number. In the ordered one-excitation basis (u,v), D has matrix `[[0,-i],[i,0]]`; this orientation is part of the gate specification.

Fix a real requested theta and a positive frequency j. Set

    g=sqrt(21 j |sin(theta)|),
    q=sign(sin(theta))g, with q=0 when sin(theta)=0,
    c=j+(97/42)g^2,       d=g^2/42,
    R_theta=sqrt(cos(theta)^2+169 sin(theta)^2)<=13.

No exact sign/zero oracle is required for an effective approximation: q is the continuous signed-root function. Define the full-qubit dimensionless penalty and port operator

    L=N_A+(1/4)B(n_a-n_a')+(1/4)C(n_b-n_b')
                  -(1/4)(S_ab+S_a'b'),
    N_A=n_a+n_b+n_a'+n_b',

The two right ports have the displayed orientation r-to-b and r-to-b'. Define

All terms are static. The scalar c and data-edge counterterm dP are actually installed. They are independent of the entries of K and do not implement an inverse or attenuation of K. ## 3. Whole-space gap and the actual invariant sector

The exchange obeys `-n_u-n_v<=S_uv<=n_u+n_v` on its full four-dimensional space, including 11. Since B,C commute with mediator occupations and have norm one, summing the two rails gives

    L >= (1-1/4-1/4)N_A=N_A/2.                       (3)

Every mediator vacuum is annihilated. Therefore on the complete old/head/mediator space, the kernel is D tensor the entire two-head-qubit space tensor mediator vacuum, and the gap above it is at least 1/2. No original-data sector or one-excitation approximation establishes this inequality.

In fact `||L||=4`, and the gap 1/2 is attained. Resolve the commuting B,C only for this one-cell spectral calculation. For signs b0,c0 in {+1,-1}, one rail's one-excitation matrix is

    D_+(b0,c0)=[[1+b0/4,-1/4],[-1/4,1+c0/4]],

and the other is D_-(b0,c0)=D_+(-b0,-c0). Their zero-excitation energy is zero and their double-excitation energies are `2+(b0+c0)/4` and `2-(b0+c0)/4`. For equal signs the one-excitation upper eigenvalue is below the corresponding double energy; for opposite signs it is `1+sqrt(2)/4<2`. Consequently each rail's top energy is its double energy and their sum is four. Full mediator occupation attains four for every old state. At b0=c0=-1 the first rail has an eigenvalue 1/2. K is not part of L and need not share these eigenspaces.

Let `N_new=n_l+n_r+N_A`. Every term of H_Delta commutes with N_new, including K and all counterterms. The prepared apparatus is in the exactly invariant sector N_new=1. Within that sector define J0 to append mediator vacuum to the logical space D tensor span{|l>,|r>}, and P0=J0J0*, Q0=I-P0. The entire Q0 sector has one mediator excitation and no head excitation. The inverse

    R_A=Q0(L|_Q0)^-1 Q0,       ||R_A||<=2              (4)

is the restriction of the full reduced inverse. It has the two actual D_+^{-1},D_-^{-1} rails. In this invariant sector V maps P0 to Q0 and Q0 to P0, so

    P0 V P0=0,         Q0 V Q0=0.                     (5)

Equation (5) is a sector fact, not a claim that the full six-qubit port operator has no higher-excitation transitions. The global gap in (3) and the one-head input have distinct roles. General preparation/calibration leakage is handled later with full-space norms, not by assuming it obeys (5).

## 4. Exact response, scalar and old-data counterterm

The determinant of D_+ is `(1+b0/4)(1+c0/4)-1/16`. Direct inversion of the two rails gives the following exact sums:

| b0 c0 | Sum of either diagonal entry | Sum of off-diagonal entries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| +1 | 7/3 | 2/3 |
| -1 | 16/7 | 4/7 |

For example, with equal signs the determinants are 3/2 and 1/2. With opposite signs both determinants are 7/8. Hence the diagonal and off-diagonal functions are respectively

    d_rail(P)=97/42+P/42,
    f_rail(P)=13/21+P/21.                              (6)

This uses the actual inverses, not a common reciprocal gap. At a left port `Q0 V|l>=g(|a>+|a'>)`. At a right port `Q0 V|r>=iq(|b>+|b'>)`. Therefore the complete logical response is

    C_eff=J0* V R_A V J0
          =g^2 d_rail(P) I_head - gq f_rail(P) D_lr.   (7)

In particular its r,l matrix entry is `-i gq f_rail(P)`. The equality of the two diagonal sums is essential: on one head their unwanted BC-dependent part is a head-independent dP, which is a legal two-qubit interaction on the original data edge. No three-body counterterm is hidden.

Since `gq=21j sin(theta)`, the exact second-order logical operator is

    H_eff=J0*AJ0-C_eff
         =K+jI-j cos(theta)S_lr+j sin(theta)P D_lr
         =K+j(I-T_U),                                (8)

where

    U=exp(-i theta P),
    T_U=|r><l| tensor U + |l><r| tensor U*,
    T_U^2=I on the one-head logical space.             (9)

The physical terms cI, dP and -13j sin(theta)D_lr cancel exactly the corresponding unwanted self-energy and head hopping. Dropping any of them changes the full phase or the data operation. H_eff is not asserted to be the exact finite-Delta Schur complement at every energy.

## 5. A bare-input estimate with arbitrary active K

This short proof is given explicitly rather than importing an auxiliary-only lemma. Set `X=R_A V J0`. Then LX=VJ0 and, by (5), `VX=J0 C_eff`. A preserves P0 and Q0. For `Z=J0-Delta^(-1/2)X`, direct multiplication gives

    H_Delta Z-Z H_eff
       =Delta^(-1/2)[X H_eff-A X].                    (10)

This identity retains all commutators of K with B,C and the response. No simultaneous K eigenbasis, dressed input or adiabatic preparation is assumed. A second correction vanishes here because R_A V R_A V J0=0 in N_new=1; this is precisely the number-sector reason, not a deletion of an allowed virtual return.

In this sector `||V||=sqrt(2)g`, since the two logical head columns enter disjoint mediator vertices with squared norm 2g^2 each. Thus

    x=2sqrt(2)g >= ||X||,
    b=h+2j >= ||H_eff||,
    a_Q=h+c+d=h+j+49j|sin(theta)| >= ||A|_Q0||.

The head bond vanishes on Q0. For any finite T>=0, both bare endpoint differences and finite-dimensional unitary Duhamel applied to Z give

    sup_(0<=t<=T)
      ||exp(-itH_Delta)J0-J0 exp(-itH_eff)||
       <= E_med:= [x/sqrt(Delta)] [2+T(a_Q+b)].        (11)

One endpoint costs x/sqrt(Delta), and the other costs the same; the integrated residual costs `T x(a_Q+b)/sqrt(Delta)`. Z is not required to be an isometry. This establishes the same physical-time full prepared-map comparison, with the four mediator outputs retained. Tensoring any passive reference does not alter the norm bound. For arbitrary mixed logical/reference states the full trace distance is at most min(2,2E_med).

## 6. Gate endpoint, phase and sufficient parameters

Let `tau=pi/(2j)`. For K=0 the exact logical history is

    exp[-it j(I-T_U)]|l>psi

    ||exp(-itH_eff)-exp[-it j(I-T_U)]||<=h t.          (13)

## 9. Scope failures and selected check fixed before execution

The one-head hypothesis cannot be dropped. At N_new=0 all mediator/head hoppings vanish, so the exact old generator is `K+cI+dP`, not the natural zero-head extension `K+jI`. The difference is the nonzero `g^2 d_rail(P)`. Empty cells therefore are not silent. A chain with one occupied cell and many empty copies would accumulate their uncancelled counterterms; no full-word composition follows from this one-cell response. This is a precise remaining interface, not a no-go for compensating or redesigning a larger apparatus.

A gap reciprocal is not the rail inverse. Replacing R_A by 2Q0 erases its off-diagonal rail response and cannot produce (6)–(8). Reversing a right current flips the corresponding response phase. Omitting dP leaves an old-data rotation error; omitting the +jI in c leaves a phase error even if the density output happens to agree. Positivity through an added reserve does not authorize removal of its phase. These are rejected shortcuts, not changes to the valid model.
