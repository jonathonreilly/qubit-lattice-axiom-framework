of the supplied continuum local even CAR algebra. The maps are explicit unital
completely positive maps into actual local even spin algebras. They become
multiplicative in the limit and identify a constructed algebra of sections,
modulo sections vanishing in norm, with the continuum even algebra. Actual

## 2. A canonical conditional expectation for any real projection

Let Q be a real orthogonal projection on a separable real Hilbert space H_R.
Choose an orthonormal basis (e_j) of (1-Q)H_R. Let R_j=1-2|e_j><e_j|.
The CAR relations give a Bogoliubov *-automorphism beta_j with
beta_j(c(f))=c(R_j f): R_j is an invertible real isometry, so the inverse
map obeys the same universal relations. These automorphisms commute because
the reflections commute. They also commute with parity. Put

    E_N = product_(j=1..N) (id+beta_j)/2.                      (A1)

Every E_N is a unital completely positive contraction and preserves parity.
For a polynomial in finitely many vectors chosen from QH_R and a finite
subset of the e_j, reorder its monomials using CAR and cancel repeated
orthonormal generators. Averaging eliminates every remaining monomial that
contains an e_j. Once N includes that finite subset the result is constant
in N and is in A(QH_R). Polynomials of this form are norm dense: approximate
each vector's complement by finite basis sums, use field norm continuity,
then the product telescoping inequality.

Consequently E_N has a point-norm limit E_Q on the entire algebra. Indeed,
for A approximated by such a polynomial P and N,M beyond its finite set,
||E_N A-E_M A||<=2||A-P||. E_Q is unital, completely positive (matrix
positivity is norm closed), contractive and parity preserving. Its range
is A(QH_R), it is the identity there, and it is bimodular there because
every beta_j fixes that subalgebra. Thus it is a conditional expectation.

This limit is independent of the complement basis. To see this without
assuming a uniqueness theorem, expand f_i=Qf_i+(1-Q)f_i in any field
monomial. Complement fields anticommute past Q-fields. The average of an
odd complement monomial is zero. The average of an even complement
monomial is its scalar Clifford trace: reorder an orthonormal expansion,
keep only the identity coefficient. That scalar is basis independent,
because the canonical Clifford trace has covariance (f,g) and its ordered
even moments are the signed pairing sum of those covariances. The latter
formula follows by CAR induction on the monomial length. Therefore the
result depends only on Q and the inner product, and density gives uniqueness.
In particular,

    E_Q c(f) = c(Qf),
    E_Q(c(f)c(g)) = c(Qf)c(Qg)+((1-Q)f,(1-Q)g)1.            (A2)

Omitting the second term is not a unital map on CAR: for unit f in kerQ,
c(f)^2=1 whereas replacing every field by its projection gives zero.

The construction also proves locality in the form needed here. If each
f_i is supported in I and Q=Q_a, every surviving Q-field is supported on
the cells intersecting I. Complement contractions are scalars. Hence
E_Qa maps A^+(I) into A^+(Q_a L2(I_a,R2)); norm closure extends this from
polynomials to the whole local algebra. There is no infinite JW string
or enlargement beyond those cells in this conclusion.

## 3. Point-norm approximation and asymptotic multiplicativity

For A=product_(i=1..r)c(f_i), put A_Q=product c(Qf_i), and let

    d_Q(A)=sum_i ||(1-Q)f_i|| product_(k!=i)||f_k||.

The usual product telescoping gives ||A-A_Q||<=d_Q(A). Since E_Q fixes
A_Q and is contractive,

    ||E_Q A-A_Q||<=d_Q(A),
    ||E_Q A-A||<=2d_Q(A).                                (A3)

The same finite sum estimate applies to polynomials. With Q=Q_a and
smooth compact fields it is O(a), with the explicit derivative seminorms
above. Polynomial density and ||E_Qa||<=1 prove

    ||E_Qa A-A|| ->0   for every fixed A in A.             (A4)

The extension has no uniform rate for general A. Contractivity also gives
point-norm convergence uniform over every norm-compact set of A's, by a
finite epsilon-net. The exact map into the spin algebra is

    Phi_a = iota_a^(-1) E_Qa | A^+.                       (A5)

It is unital completely positive, contractive, parity compatible and local
as in section2. It satisfies lim ||Phi_a A||=||A||. For A,B in A^+,

 ||iota_a(Phi_a(AB)-Phi_a(A)Phi_a(B))||
 <= ||E_Qa(AB)-AB|| + ||E_Qa A-A|| ||B||
                         + ||A|| ||E_Qa B-B|| ->0.        (A6)

At fixed a these maps are generally not multiplicative or injective. For
orthogonal real unit f in kerQ_a and g in ranQ_a, i c(f)c(g) is a
norm-one selfadjoint even element killed by E_Qa. If f is a smooth
zero-cell-average function and g is in another cell, both can lie in any
fixed nonempty I for all sufficiently small a. This gives a norm-one
counterexample to uniform convergence on the local operator unit ball;
it depends on a and does not contradict A4.

## 4. The actual chosen states and the complement covariance

Let omega_a be the supplied actual spin vacuum. On the mathematical cell
CAR algebra its even restriction is the quasi-free state with positive
projection P_a. The accepted parent embeds that projection as
S_a=j_a P_a C_a on all K, zero on Q_a-perp. It has

    S_a+Gamma S_a Gamma=Q_a,      S_a -> P_+ strongly.     (A7)

The actual pullback state on A^+ is psi_a=omega_a Phi_a, a state by
complete positivity. For computing it one may extend to the full
mathematical CAR algebra using the cell quasi-free state composed with
E_Qa. This is a mathematical extension; it does not identify odd spin
operators. Expanding into cell and complement fields as in section2,
the complement trace has covariance one-half times the complement
projection, while cross covariances vanish. The ordered Wick pairing
expansion therefore proves that this extended state is quasi-free with

    K_a=S_a+(1-Q_a)/2,
    0<=K_a<=1,     K_a+Gamma K_a Gamma=1.                 (A8)

The two inequalities follow separately on the orthogonal range and
complement of Q_a. The self-dual identity follows from A7 and reality
of Q_a. This explicitly repairs the invalid inference that S_a alone
is a state covariance on all K. The complement term is forced by this
specified trace expectation; it is not extra microscopic physics.

Since Q_a->1 and S_a->P_+ strongly, K_a->P_+ strongly. Every finite
ordered covariance matrix thus converges. Wick's finite pairing formula
gives psi_a(A)->omega_infinity(A) for polynomials. The states have norm
one, so density extends this to every A in A^+. This is weak-star state
convergence; no norm convergence of all states or common-vacuum Hilbert
identification has been asserted. At finite a the extended state need
not be pure.

## 5. Compact mixed actions on every fixed algebra element

Let beta_a^w(z) denote the actual supplied spin actions for a fixed finite
word w of time translations, rescaled original boosts, and lattice shifts
floor(ell/a). Its parameters z range over a fixed compact set. Let
alpha^w(z) be the corresponding continuum Bogoliubov action, with the
parent's convention tau=-t and boost rapidity vs. The provisional mixed
parent proves, on compactly supported smooth real fields and their fixed
even products, convergence of the embedded actual transformed naive
cell polynomial to alpha^w(z)A, uniformly in z.

Replace the naive polynomial A_Qa by E_Qa A. A3 bounds their difference
by d_Qa(A); every actual automorphism is isometric. Thus the same
convergence holds for Phi_a(A). Smooth finite polynomials are dense in
A^+. For a fixed general A approximate by a polynomial P: both terms in
the comparison have norm at most ||A-P||, uniformly over z. Therefore

 sup_z ||iota_a beta_a^w(z) Phi_a(A)-alpha^w(z)A|| ->0.   (A9)

The continuum orbit of a fixed A over a compact parameter set is norm
compact. Norm continuity first holds on fields because the one-body
groups are strongly continuous, then on polynomials and their closure.
A4 is uniform on this orbit. Combining it with A9 yields

 sup_z ||beta_a^w(z)Phi_a(A)-Phi_a(alpha^w(z)A)|| ->0.     (A10)

No field-domain differentiability is used to extend A9/A10 from the
parent polynomial theorem. There is no common O(a) rate on the algebra
unit ball. Actual rounded translations still fail the group law at a>0.

## 6. A constructed section algebra and its quotient

Fix a0>0. Let B be the C*-algebra of all bounded families (b_a) with
b_a in B_a^+, 0<a<=a0, with sup norm and pointwise operations. No
continuity in a is imposed. Let J be its closed ideal of families with
||b_a||->0 as a->0. Define the section s(A)=(Phi_a A), and define

    D=C*(s(A):A in A^+, J) contained in B.                (A11)

This inclusion of J is explicit; D is a constructed subalgebra of the
full bounded product. Let pi denote the quotient by J. A6, adjoint
preservation, linearity and unitality imply that

    Xi:A^+ -> D/J,       Xi(A)=pi(s(A))                  (A12)

is a unital *-homomorphism. For every bounded family b,
||pi(b)||=limsup_(a->0)||b_a||: subtracting a family vanishing near0
gives the upper bound, and adding any member of J leaves that limsup
unchanged. A4 and isometry of iota_a give

    ||Xi(A)||=lim_(a->0)||Phi_a A||=||A||.               (A13)

Thus Xi is injective and isometric. Its image is a closed C*-algebra.
The quotient is generated by that image because J vanishes; Xi is
therefore onto. This proves D/J is isomorphic to A^+, without claiming
that the entire bounded spin product or a standard scaling algebra is D.

There is an equally precise local assertion. Let B(I) be the product of
the finite local even spin algebras B_a^+(I_a), let J(I)=B(I) intersect J,
and set D(I)=C*(s(A):A in A^+(I), J(I)). Then D(I) is a subalgebra of
B(I) and D. Its kernel under pi is J(I); A13 and the generation argument
give

    D(I)/J(I) is isometrically *-isomorphic to A^+(I).     (A14)

I subset I' implies all three local inclusions. Disjoint intervals give
commuting algebras in the quotient. At positive separation their cell
supports are disjoint for sufficiently small a, so even spin locality
already gives eventual commutation. At touching boundaries use A4:
the limiting CAR local algebras commute, so the commutator section tends
to zero in norm. No endpoint cell assignment changes the quotient.

Each actual time or boost action acts componentwise by automorphisms
of B and preserves J. A10 shows it preserves D and induces exactly the
corresponding continuum action under Xi. The same holds for a fixed
rounded translation: its inverse differs from the opposite rounding
by a one-cell shift, but A10 makes the difference vanish in the quotient.
The induced spatial maps therefore form the exact continuum group.
Compact A10 also implies strong continuity of the induced action.

The accepted continuum source net A_cont(O) on spacetime can consequently
be carried to Xi(A_cont(O)), inside D/J. Its local covariance and
spacelike even locality follow by the isomorphism. For elements of a
transformed time-slice algebra, the corresponding actual transformed
spin sections are representatives by A10. This is the link to actual
microscopic dynamics. It supplies no claim of strict finite-a Lorentz
locality or a uniform microscopic support bound for boosts.

Finally lim omega_a(b_a) exists on D and equals omega_infinity(Xi^-1 pi b).
First this holds on sections by section4 and on J by the state norm
bound. Products of sections differ from the section of their product
by J, by A6. The result extends by uniform approximation to D. This
limit is unique for this constructed algebra, not a classification of
states or subsequential limits on larger algebras. Its GNS is the
accepted continuum even-vacuum representation and hence has even-sector
threshold2m. It does not produce a one-particle observable of mass m.
