The conjugates in the first line are required: V returns from b to r. The two real diagonal contractions are s_l and s_r in(7); their P-dependent pieces cancel because the two arm magnitudes coincide. Thus, on the complete logical head/old space,

## 5. The added apparatus is positive on its full space

For fixed p, write the six-site coefficient matrix with head sites first as

    diag(d_l,d_r)-B^dagger D_p^-1 B
                         =kappa(I+W_theta(p)) >=0.        (15)

Hence M_p>=0. Factoring a positive coefficient matrix into sums of squared linear combinations of the commuting-site annihilators proves H_app>=0 on the entire old/six-qubit Hilbert space. No canonical fermionic anticommutation relation is used. The same conclusion holds for superpositions of p; K may mix those sectors after installation.

The six-site exchange graph is bipartite, with classes{l,b,b'} and{r,a,a'}. A particle-hole flip on all six sites, followed by the sign gauge on one class, transforms the quadratic form to Tr(M_p) I minus the form for M_p transposed in site indices. The latter matrix is positive as well. The trace is independent of p:

    B_app=Tr(M_p)=4Delta+d_l+d_r.

Thus0<=H_app<=B_app I. The all-six-occupied state attains B_app, and

The bipartite hypothesis is essential to this particular upper-bound proof; positivity of a general quadratic coefficient matrix would not suffice (section12). ## 6. A full-space apparatus gap, with no claim of a gap for arbitrary K

For completeness the positive *added* apparatus has an explicit gap above its nontrivial kernel. Put

    delta_* = min(2kappa,Delta/2)/(1+2b_v/sqrt(Delta))^2.   (17)

The matrix(14) is congruent to diag(kappa(I+W_theta(p)),Delta D_p) through the triangular map with lower-left block Delta^-1/2 D_p^-1 B. The inverse map has norm at most1+2b_v/sqrt(Delta). Since the diagonal matrix has rank5 and smallest positive eigenvalue at least min(2kappa,Delta/2), the smallest positive eigenvalue of M_p is at least delta_*. One way to justify the semidefinite congruence step is to compare the nonzero eigenvalues of A^(1/2) T T^dagger A^(1/2), using T T^dagger>=||T^-1||^-2 I. There is exactly one normalized one-particle null vector q_p.

Here is a hard-core argument covering all higher excitation sectors. For any normalized six-component q, lift I-|q><q| to

    H_q=N-a_q^dagger a_q,    a_q=sum_i conj(q_i) a_i.

On a normalized n-excitation vector psi, n>=2, set E=<H_q> and w=sum_i |q_i|^2 ||a_i psi||^2. The exact pair identity gives

    E=sum_(i<j)||q_j a_i psi-q_i a_j psi||^2.

Apply a_i to each ordered pair difference and use a_i^2=0, [a_i,a_j]=0 for i!=j, and ||a_i||=1. Summing yields

    2E >= sum_i |q_i|^2 sum_(j!=i)||a_i a_j psi||^2
        =(n-1)w.

Also ||a_q psi||^2<=6w by Cauchy-Schwarz. Consequently

    E >= n(n-1)/(n+11) >=2/13.                         (18)

Since M_p>=delta_* (I-|q_p><q_p|), positivity of the same quadratic lift proves an apparatus energy at least(2/13)delta_* in every n>=2 sector. In n=1 the nonzero gap is at least delta_*, and n=0 is vacuum. Therefore H_app has full-space nonzero gap at least

Its kernel consists of the added vacuum and the one-particle null mode at each old P sector, with all remaining old degeneracies. The prepared entry head is not claimed to be a ground state, dressed state or adiabatically prepared state. Original arbitrary K can alter the interacting spectrum and need not preserve that kernel. ## 8. Endpoint rotation and its separately paid drift error

Take

    T_gate=pi/(2kappa).

If K were absent, exp[-iT_gate kappa(I+W_theta)]=-W_theta, so a left head reaches r and applies R_theta with common phase-1. With K present, the unitary product-formula integral gives

    ||exp[-iT(K+kappa(I+W_theta))]
             -exp(-iTK) exp[-iT kappa(I+W_theta)]||
       <=(T^2 kappa/2)||[K,W_theta]||
       <=(T^2 kappa/2)|sin(theta)| k.

The first inequality follows by two unitary integral comparisons and has no exponential norm factor. The block off-diagonal commutator has norm ||[K,R_theta]||=|sin(theta)| ||[K,P]||. Therefore at the stated endpoint,

    delta_K=pi^2 |sin(theta)| k/(8kappa),

                 +J_r exp(-iT_gate K)R_theta||
       <= E_gate:=E_med(T_gate)+delta_K.                  (23)

J_l and J_r append respectively the left or right head with all four mediators vacuum. The plus sign preserves the known common phase-1.
