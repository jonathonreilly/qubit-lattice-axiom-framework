## Statement

Let each site carry the complex qubit algebra `M_2(C)` (axiom A1), with a
shared unit `1`. Compose two sites under Locality (commuting subalgebras)
and minimality (the composite `C` is generated by the two site algebras,
with no extra global observables; equivalently the joint action is
irreducible). Then:

1. (**CX — complex ⟹ observable dim = complex dim.**) For any complex
   `C*`-algebra `D`, the imaginary unit splits it,
   `D = D_sa ⊕ i·D_sa`, so

   ```text
   dim_R(D_sa) = dim_C(D).                                          (CX)
   ```

   For `D = M_n(C)`: `dim_sa = n^2 = dim_C`.

2. (**MUL — multiplicativity ⟹ local tomography.**) Complex dimension is
   multiplicative under the complex tensor product, so by (CX) the
   observable dimension is too:

   ```text
   dim_sa(A ⊗_C B) = dim_C(A ⊗_C B) = dim_C(A)·dim_C(B)
                   = dim_sa(A)·dim_sa(B),                           (MUL)
   ```

   which is exactly **local tomography** (the joint observable dimension is
   the product of the local ones). For two qubits: `dim_sa(M_4(C)) = 16 =
   4·4`.

3. (**RE — the real alternative lacks `i` and fails.**) The rebit algebra
   `M_2(R)` has no imaginary unit; its observable (symmetric) dimension is
   `n(n+1)/2`, which is **not** multiplicative:
   `3·3 = 9 != 10 = dim_sa(M_4(R))`. Real QM is not locally tomographic
   (the companion no-go's witness).

4. (**SH — the sites force one shared `i`.**) Each site algebra `M_2(C)`
   **contains** the element `i·1` (the imaginary unit is `i` times the
   shared identity). It is a single global element, the **same** in every
   site algebra. Hence the composite generated by the sites contains
   `i·1`; both factors' imaginary units coincide with it
   (`(i·1)⊗1 = 1⊗(i·1) = i·1` on the composite), so `C` is a **complex**
   algebra with one global `i`.

5. (**RED — the reduction.**) By (SH) the qubit composite is complex; by
   minimality it is the algebra generated by two commuting complex factors,
   i.e. `M_2(C) ⊗_C M_2(C) = M_4(C)` (the `16` products `σ_a ⊗ σ_b` are
   independent, no extra observables); by (MUL) it is locally tomographic.
   Therefore **local tomography is entailed by A1** (the complex qubit) and
   minimality — it is not a separate admission. The companion no-go's
   residual is absorbed: the precise content the tensor rule needed beyond
   Locality is supplied by the qubit's complex unit `i`.

## Proof

Pure finite-dimensional linear algebra; each step is reproduced at exact
precision by the runner.

### Step 1. (CX) The imaginary unit splits the algebra.

Any `M in M_n(C)` decomposes uniquely as `M = H_1 + i H_2` with
`H_1 = (M + M^†)/2` and `H_2 = (M - M^†)/(2i)` both Hermitian (the runner
exhibits this for `n = 2`). So `M_n(C) = sa ⊕ i·sa` and
`dim_R(sa) = dim_C(M_n(C)) = n^2`. The complex unit `i` is precisely what
makes the observable space carry the **full** complex dimension.

### Step 2. (MUL) Multiplicativity gives local tomography.

`dim_C(M_m(C) ⊗_C M_n(C)) = dim_C(M_{mn}(C)) = (mn)^2 = m^2 · n^2`. By (CX)
this equals `dim_sa(M_m(C)) · dim_sa(M_n(C))`. For two qubits
`16 = 4 · 4` — local tomography. The bipartite observable space has no
dimension beyond the product of the local ones.

### Step 3. (RE) The real alternative.

`M_n(R)` (rebit) has observable (symmetric) dimension `n(n+1)/2`, strictly
less than `n^2`, because it has no `i` to promote the antisymmetric part to
observables. `3 · 3 = 9 != 10 = dim_sa(M_4(R))`: the two extra dimensions
are the antisymmetric⊗antisymmetric global observables not fixed by local
data. Real QM fails local tomography — the difference from the complex case
is exactly the presence of `i`.

### Step 4. (SH) The sites carry one shared `i`.

`i·1 ∈ M_2(C)` at every site, and `1` is the shared global unit, so `i·1`
is one global element common to all sites. On the composite,
`(i·1)⊗1 = i·1 = 1⊗(i·1)` (runner: `(iI_2)⊗I_2 = I_2⊗(iI_2) = iI_4`), so
the two factors' imaginary units coincide; the composite is a complex
algebra with this single `i`. (A `C`-linear `*`-embedding sends the scalar
`i` to `i·1`; both site embeddings do, so they agree.)

### Step 5. (RED) Assemble.

By (SH) the composite is complex; by minimality it is generated by two
commuting complex `M_2(C)` factors, hence `M_2(C) ⊗_C M_2(C) = M_4(C)` (the
`16` products span, runner M6); by (MUL) `dim_sa = 16 = 4·4`, local
tomography. So A1 + minimality ⟹ local tomography. QED.
