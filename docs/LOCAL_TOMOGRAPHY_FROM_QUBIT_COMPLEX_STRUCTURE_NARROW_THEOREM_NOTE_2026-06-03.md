# Local Tomography From Complex Qubit Tensor Product - Conditional Narrow Theorem

**Date:** 2026-06-03
**Claim type:** bounded_theorem
**Claim boundary:** conditional finite-dimensional algebra check. If two

## Statement

Let each site carry the complex one-qubit operator algebra named by the
Quantum axiom in
[`MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-04.md`](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-04.md):
`A_x ~= M_2(C)`. Now add the following explicit composition premise:

```text
C = A_x tensor_C A_y ~= M_4(C),
```

with the two embedded site factors sharing the same scalar field and with the
composite generated by the products of the two site factors. Under this
premise:

1. For `M_n(C)`, the real dimension of the self-adjoint part is `n^2`, equal
   to the complex dimension of `M_n(C)`.
2. Complex dimension is multiplicative under the ordinary complex tensor
   product, so

   ```text
   dim_R((M_2(C) tensor_C M_2(C))_sa)
     = dim_C(M_4(C))
     = 16
     = 4 * 4
     = dim_R(M_2(C)_sa)^2.
   ```

3. Therefore the ordinary complex two-qubit tensor product is locally
   tomographic in this finite dimension-count sense.

The same check shows the real rebit comparison fails local tomography for the
ordinary real tensor product: `dim_R(M_2(R)_sa)^2 = 3 * 3 = 9`, while
`dim_R(M_4(R)_sa) = 10`.

## Proof

Every matrix `M in M_n(C)` decomposes uniquely as

```text
M = H_1 + i H_2,
H_1 = (M + M^dagger) / 2,
H_2 = (M - M^dagger) / (2 i),
```

with `H_1` and `H_2` self-adjoint. Thus `M_n(C) = A_sa + i A_sa` as a real
vector-space split, and `dim_R(A_sa) = dim_C(M_n(C)) = n^2`.

For ordinary complex matrix algebras,
`M_m(C) tensor_C M_n(C) ~= M_{mn}(C)`, so complex dimensions multiply. For two
qubits, this gives `dim_R(M_4(C)_sa) = dim_C(M_4(C)) = 16`, while each one-site
self-adjoint space has dimension `4`. The equality `16 = 4 * 4` is exactly the
finite local-tomography dimension count.

The shared scalar field matters. In the ordinary complex tensor product, the
two site embeddings agree on the scalar imaginary unit:

```text
(i I_2) tensor I_2 = I_2 tensor (i I_2) = i I_4.
```

The Pauli products `sigma_a tensor sigma_b`, with `a,b in {0,1,2,3}`, form a
rank-16 basis of `M_4(C)`. Hence there are no extra finite matrix degrees of
freedom beyond the products of local ones under the stated composition
premise.

For contrast, `M_n(R)_sa` is the real symmetric subspace, of dimension
`n(n+1)/2`. Thus the rebit comparison gives `3 * 3 = 9`, but
`dim_R(M_4(R)_sa) = 10`; the ordinary real tensor product is not locally
tomographic.

- A direct finite-dimensional contrast with the ordinary real rebit tensor

- A shared-scalar check that the ordinary complex tensor product has one
  global `i`, not two independent local imaginary units.
