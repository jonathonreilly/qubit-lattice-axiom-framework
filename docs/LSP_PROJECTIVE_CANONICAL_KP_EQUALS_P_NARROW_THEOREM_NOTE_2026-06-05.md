`LSP_PROJECTIVE_DERIVATION_FROM_NAIMARK_FRAME_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-22.md`
states the canonical result that the outcome Kraus operator of a projective

## 1. Canonical dilation and `K_r = P_r` (reproven from primitives)

A projective measurement is a finite family of orthogonal projections `{P_r}`
on the finite-dimensional region carrier `H_sys = H_Λ` with

```text
P_r P_{r'} = δ_{rr'} P_r,    P_r† = P_r,    Σ_r P_r = 𝟙_sys.            (proj)
```

Take an apparatus space `H_A` with orthonormal basis `{|r⟩_A}` indexed by the
outcomes and a fixed unit vector `|0⟩_A` (one of the basis vectors). The
canonical interaction isometry is defined on the prepared subspace
`H_sys ⊗ |0⟩_A` by

```text
U_int (|ψ⟩_sys ⊗ |0⟩_A)  =  Σ_r (P_r |ψ⟩)_sys ⊗ |r⟩_A.                 (dil)
```

**(dil) is an isometry on the prepared subspace.** For any `|ψ⟩`,

```text
‖U_int(|ψ⟩⊗|0⟩)‖²  =  Σ_{r,r'} ⟨P_r ψ | P_{r'} ψ⟩ ⟨r|r'⟩_A
                   =  Σ_r ⟨ψ| P_r† P_r |ψ⟩          (⟨r|r'⟩_A = δ_{rr'})
                   =  Σ_r ⟨ψ| P_r |ψ⟩               (P_r† P_r = P_r² = P_r)
                   =  ⟨ψ| (Σ_r P_r) |ψ⟩  =  ⟨ψ|ψ⟩.  (Σ_r P_r = 𝟙)      (iso)
```

A linear isometry on a subspace of a finite-dimensional Hilbert space extends to
a unitary on the whole space (the orthogonal complements of its domain and range
have equal dimension `dim H_sys · (dim H_A − 1)`, and any unitary between them
completes the extension). That extension is the canonical dilation `U_int`.

**Kraus extraction.** The outcome-`r` Kraus operator of the system–apparatus
instrument with apparatus prepared in `|0⟩_A` and read out in `{|r⟩_A}` is the
contraction

```text
K_r  =  ⟨r|_A · U_int · (𝟙_sys ⊗ |0⟩_A).                               (kr)
```

Substituting (dil) into (kr):

```text
K_r  =  ⟨r|_A · Σ_{r'} (P_{r'} |·⟩)_sys ⊗ |r'⟩_A
     =  Σ_{r'} P_{r'} · ⟨r|r'⟩_A
     =  Σ_{r'} P_{r'} · δ_{rr'}
     =  P_r.                                                           (KP)
```

So in the canonical frame the outcome Kraus operator is exactly the projector,
`K_r = P_r` — the **Lüders selection**. The Kraus extraction only inspects the
isometry on the prepared subspace, so it is independent of how the isometry is
completed to a unitary on the orthogonal complement. (This independence is
verified numerically in the runner, including against a sign/phase-perturbed
completion that does *not* change `K_r`.)

**Sequential composition.** With `K_r = P_r` and `P_r† = P_r`, the sequential
composition of "outcome `r`, then later effect `E`" is the Lüders sandwich

```text
M_{r,E}  :=  K_r† E K_r  =  P_r E P_r.                                  (PEP)
```

References for the general measurement-theory background (comparator only; the
projective case above is constructed inline): M. A. Naimark, *Izv. Akad. Nauk
SSSR Ser. Mat.* **4** (1940) 277; W. F. Stinespring, *Proc. Amer. Math. Soc.*
**6** (1955) 211; A. S. Holevo, *Probabilistic and Statistical Aspects of
Quantum Theory*, Ch. III; J. Watrous, *The Theory of Quantum Information*, §2.2,
§2.4; G. Lüders, *Ann. Phys. (Leipzig)* **8** (1951) 322 (Lüders state update /
projective instrument).

## 2. The restricted Step-3 scope (sufficiency)

Replace `U_int` by `U_int^{tw} := (U_sys ⊗ V_A) · U_int` for `U_sys ∈ U(H_sys)`
and `V_A ∈ U(H_A)`. The twisted Kraus operators are

```text
K_r^{tw}  =  ⟨r|_A · (U_sys ⊗ V_A) · U_int · (𝟙_sys ⊗ |0⟩_A)
          =  U_sys · Σ_s ⟨r|V_A|s⟩ · P_s.                              (tw)
```

The canonical theorem is preserved exactly on the following restricted scope.

**(a) `V_A = 𝟙_A`, `U_sys` arbitrary.** Then `⟨r|V_A|s⟩ = δ_{rs}` and (tw)
gives `K_r^{tw} = U_sys · P_r`, hence `K_r^{tw}† K_r^{tw} = P_r† U_sys† U_sys P_r
= P_r`. The first-outcome POVM element is preserved. With the canonical
convention `U_sys = 𝟙_sys` this is exactly `K_r = P_r` again. (A nontrivial
`U_sys` is a *system*-side post-rotation, not the apparatus-frame issue the
repair note is about; it changes later sequential statistics unless the later
effect is co-rotated `E ↦ U_sys† E U_sys`, and is excluded by the canonical
"no extra post-measurement system unitary" convention. This note's scope
restriction is on the apparatus unitary `V_A`.)

**(b) Outcome-label phase `V_A = diag(e^{iφ_0}, …, e^{iφ_{m-1}})`, `U_sys =
𝟙_sys`.** Then `⟨r|V_A|s⟩ = e^{iφ_r} δ_{rs}` and (tw) gives `K_r^{tw} = e^{iφ_r}
P_r`, so `K_r^{tw}† K_r^{tw} = P_r` exactly. The instrument is unchanged up to a
per-outcome global phase, which does not affect any probability or
post-measurement state. The canonical `K_P = P` holds up to that irrelevant
phase.

**(c) Outcome-label permutation `V_A |s⟩_A = |π(s)⟩_A`, `U_sys = 𝟙_sys`.** Then
`⟨r|V_A|s⟩ = δ_{r,π(s)} = δ_{π^{-1}(r),s}` and (tw) gives `K_r^{tw} =
P_{π^{-1}(r)}`, so `K_r^{tw}† K_r^{tw} = P_{π^{-1}(r)}`. The POVM family is
preserved, merely with the outcomes relabeled `r ↦ π(r)`. This is a relabeling
of the readout register, not a different physical measurement; the canonical
`K_P = P` holds outcome-by-outcome after the relabeling.

In all three sub-cases the apparatus unitary acts as a pure readout-register
relabeling/phase (equivalently, "an explicit rotated readout-frame convention"),
and the canonical Kraus selection is preserved (exactly, or up to outcome
relabeling and global phase). This is the sufficiency direction of the scope
restriction.

## 3. General `V_A` breaks `K_P = P` on nonzero sectors (necessity)

If `V_A` is a general apparatus unitary that **mixes** outcome labels — i.e.
for a displayed nonzero outcome `r`, the row `⟨r|V_A|·⟩` has nonzero weight on
some distinct nonzero outcome `s ≠ r` — then with `U_sys = 𝟙_sys`, (tw) gives

```text
K_r  =  Σ_s ⟨r|V_A|s⟩ · P_s,
K_r† K_r  =  Σ_{s,s'} \overline{⟨r|V_A|s⟩} ⟨r|V_A|s'⟩ · P_s P_{s'}
          =  Σ_s |⟨r|V_A|s⟩|² · P_s          (P_s P_{s'} = δ_{ss'} P_s).  (mix)
```

This is a **convex combination** of the projectors `{P_s}` with weights
`w_{rs} := |⟨r|V_A|s⟩|² ≥ 0`, `Σ_s w_{rs} = 1` (the `r`-th row of the doubly
stochastic matrix `|V_A|²` is a probability vector). On the convention that
displayed outcome labels have `P_s ≠ 0`, the mutually orthogonal nonzero
projectors are linearly independent: applying the equality to a vector in
`ran(P_s)` forces the coefficient of each distinct `P_s` to vanish, and applying
it to a vector in `ran(P_r)` forces the coefficient of `P_r` to be one.
Therefore the mixture equals `P_r` only in the degenerate case
`w_{rs} = δ_{rs}`, i.e. `V_A` does **not** mix nonzero outcome labels. Whenever
`V_A` genuinely mixes a nonzero outcome row into a distinct nonzero sector,
`K_r† K_r ≠ P_r`: the first-outcome POVM element is a nontrivial mixture, so
`(U_sys ⊗ V_A)·U_int` is **not** a dilation of the projective measurement
`{P_r}` on those sectors.

If a bookkeeping list keeps formal zero projectors, the exact statement is the
weakened one above: a row is forced to break precisely when its weight vector has
positive weight on a nonzero projector different from the target nonzero
projector, or when the target is nonzero and its own weight is less than one.
Mixing solely among labels whose projectors are all zero does not change the
POVM and is not claimed to break `K_P=P`.

This is exactly the over-broad point in the source note's original Step 3 ("this
is still a valid dilation of the same first-outcome POVM" for arbitrary `V_A`):
the assertion is false for label-mixing `V_A`. The general-`V_A` twist is a
distinct measurement (a smeared/coarse-grained POVM), not the canonical
projective one, and is therefore scoped out of the `K_P = P` theorem and named
as an open convention item (§5). The runner exhibits an explicit Haar-random
mixing `V_A` for which `K_r† K_r ≠ P_r` on nonzero sectors and verifies it
equals the mixture `Σ_s |⟨r|V_A|s⟩|² P_s` of (mix), contrasts it with the
phase/permutation cases of §2 where equality (up to relabeling) is restored,
and includes a zero-projector edge-case check showing why the displayed theorem
uses the nonzero-outcome convention.

## 4. Why this is a tightening, not a new claim

The canonical identity `K_r = P_r` (KP) and the sandwich `P_r E P_r` (PEP) are
unchanged from the source note's Steps 1–2 and from the standard Lüders
construction. The only correction is to the source note's Step 3: the previously
unrestricted statement that *any* apparatus-and-system twist preserves the
first-outcome POVM is replaced by the precise dichotomy

- **preserved** (exactly, or up to outcome relabeling/global phase) under
  `V_A = 𝟙_A` / outcome-label phase / outcome-label permutation, and
- **broken** under any label-mixing `V_A` on nonzero outcome sectors,

with the latter named as a non-canonical convention choice. The canonical
`K_P = P` theorem is thereby stated on a scope on which it is true, which is
what the `scope_too_broad` repair note requested.
