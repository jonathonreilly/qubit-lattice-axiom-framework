## 2. Microscopic flux-creating probes: a spatial test

An observable can change flux even when H does not. Such an observable may have nonzero time-dependent response; conservation alone does not make every microscopic correlator static.

Define a Pauli probe's syndrome by W_a O_i W_a = s_ai O_i, s_ai=+-1. Suppose [rho,W_a]=0 and [H,W_a]=0. Then O_i(t) has the same syndrome as O_i and

    Tr(rho O_i(t) O_j) = s_ai s_aj Tr(rho O_i(t) O_j).

If any syndrome differs, that two-point function vanishes for all times. The reversed ordering and retarded commutator obey the same selection rule. This proof allows sector-diagonal mixtures and does not require a Gaussian state. Coherent off-sector preparations are outside its hypothesis.

For all3072 single-qubit Z probes in the actual L4 corner encoding, every flux syndrome is nonempty. There are2304 distinct syndromes; classes have size at most2 and coarse-cell Manhattan diameter at most1. The two-element classes are the repetition halves of one link; the singleton classes are on-site hop qubits. Thus these probes cannot transport their two-point response between separated links/cells in a sector-diagonal state. The fine-site embedding moves the support within its fixed blocks but does not change these operator identities. This is a concrete restriction on this proposed microscopic field channel, not on all composite operators or all states. Flux-neutral composite operators have empty syndrome and are not excluded by this selection rule; in particular it does not by itself exclude a neutral collective scalar. For products of two distinct Z probes, neutrality requires the same syndrome, so this finite enumeration leaves only repetition-pair products, which are conserved R operators. Higher-degree neutral products require separate analysis.

## 3. High bands and charge fluctuations

Charge/hopping dynamics remains nontrivial. But the exact identity

    [c† A c, c† B c] = c† [A,B] c

keeps it inside the quadratic algebra. In any invariant fermion parity sector, the free many-body energies remain sums of occupied one-particle energies (with that parity restriction). No Wick assumption about an arbitrary prepared state is required for these operator statements.

Split the one-particle matrix into low/high blocks. Exact elimination gives the low resolvent

    G_LL(z) = [z-h_LL-h_LH(z-h_HH)^(-1)h_HL]^(-1).

This includes virtual high-band effects but remains quadratic and energy dependent. It cannot supply a newly generated independent four-fermion vertex. Treating its frequency dependence as an auxiliary field is a representation change, not a derivation of an additional interacting scalar.

A separate four-mode numerical fixture checks the algebra, additive two-particle spectrum and Schur formula: respective errors0,2.7e-15 and5.9e-16. It is a diagnostic of the general identities, not a replacement corner-model spectrum. Adding a density-density term of strength.37 changes the two-particle spectrum while preserving the one-particle matrix. This is a positive control illustrating what information the supplied one-particle h does not determine; that term is not adopted.

Free particle-hole observables can have discrete, even isolated, spectral poles. Nothing here rules those out. Their frequencies and vertices are already free many-body transitions. Establishing a scalar field with a physical local action, normalization and cosmological/source-detector coupling would need further evidence. No bound-state or continuum-limit conclusion follows solely from these finite checks.

## 4. Why the successful one-particle tests cannot fix the missing interaction

There is an explicit alternative family, used only as a diagnostic:

    H_V = H + V sum_{corner graph edges ij} n_i n_j,
    n_i n_j = (1-B_i)(1-B_j)/4.

Every added term vanishes on the vacuum and one-fermion sector. Thus all choices of V leave that sector's dispersion, node velocities and applied one-particle mass vertex exactly unchanged. For real V these are Hermitian finite-range encoded qubit terms, commuting with the loop/repetition constraints. Summing uniformly over the same corner graph also preserves its charge-permuting rotations and translations; the number twist commutes with these densities. This refers to the supplied block geometry, not to full fine-site translation symmetry.

The two-particle sector does change: its added operator is positive semidefinite and nonzero for V>0, with positive trace there, so the two-particle spectra cannot all stay identical. This is a direct witness that the successful one-particle sector does not determine the many-body interaction. H_V is **not** inserted into the working theory. V=0 remains the tested quadratic class. No value of V, including zero, follows from matching the one-particle tests. A microscopic law or additional many-body observations must select it before we derive a scalar from it.

## 5. Other routes actually examined

- **Retain all flux sectors.** Static sector mixtures or tracing a conserved label may generate non-Gaussian statistics. They do not create PHQ or virtual-flux exchange. Fixed-number/parity ensembles need not satisfy unrestricted Wick factorization either.
- **Unfreeze spectators.** Decoupled spectators factorize from matter. Changing their preparation does not couple them; an actual coupling would be a new Hamiltonian term.
- **Increase feature support.** Relabelling tensor factors onto more fine sites preserves commutators and spectra. A broad wavepacket in the same quadratic Hamiltonian does not change the interaction class. A self-organized large object remains a separate question.
- **Use the native compass model.** The repository's September24 carving construction is a different supplied qubit Hamiltonian. Its record fields can break loop conservation. It is a plausible alternative candidate, but no source derives it as the completion of this corner model. Switching to it would change the physical premises, not uncover a hidden term here.

The probe test is the most concrete additional discriminator beyond the prior response calculation: the natural single-qubit flux-creating channel has no separated-link two-point response under the stated conditions.
