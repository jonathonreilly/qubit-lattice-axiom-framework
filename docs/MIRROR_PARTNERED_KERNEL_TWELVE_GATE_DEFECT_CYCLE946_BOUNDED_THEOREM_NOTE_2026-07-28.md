## Verdict

The owner adopted an axiom in the morning, and by evening the
machine had used it to derive its first probability — one half,
twice over, at three sites of a kernel built to be fair. The route
is the right kind of narrow: no principle of indifference, no
borrowed symmetry argument, just the axiom's own determination
clause, the qubit's refusal to privilege a label, and a measured
fact about which neighborhoods actually match. The price is
printed on the same page: the kernel is partnered, not physical;
the coverage is three menus of five; and the whole construction
hangs from one imported sentence — that a symmetry of the compiled
circuit is a symmetry of the axiom's rule — which no block in this
stack has derived. The lane that once proved nothing forces the
dice has now, conditionally, forced two of them to be fair; what
remains is to prove the table belongs to the casino. Independent
audit still required.
