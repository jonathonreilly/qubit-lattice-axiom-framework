```text
   <Theta(F) F>_Berezin-path-integral   =   <Omega| O_hat^dag T_full O_hat |Omega>_operator
```

for a basis of **mixed** gauge+fermion observables `F` on the 2-step blocked
staggered surface, in temporal gauge (`U_0 = 1`), under the temporal-link
reflection `theta(t, x_vec) = (-1 - t, x_vec)`. The left side is the
Berezin/Grassmann path integral with the staggered propagator `M[U]^{-1}`,
det-weighted and Haar `U`-averaged; the right side is the operator inner product

`T_full = (T_gauge^{1/2} (x) I)(oplus_U B[U]^dag B[U])(T_gauge^{1/2} (x) I)`.

## Why two steps, and why the single-step version breaks the equality

Under `theta(t, x) = (-1 - t, x)` the staggered phase flips,
`eta_1(theta t) = -eta_1(t)`. This is precisely the place the single-step
construction fails, so it is where scrutiny is hardest; the runner handles it
explicitly.

- **Single-step (negative control).** The naive single-slice reflection
  `Theta(chi_0) = + bar(chi)_{theta 0}` (the Sharatchandra map without the staggered
  phase compensator) yields a reflected positive-cone metric that is **indefinite**
  (per-mode min eig `−0.5528`; full naive Lagrangian Gram min eig `−0.8000`,
  reproducing the documented Caracciolo–Palumbo 2013 no-go). An indefinite metric
  cannot equal any positive operator sandwich, so the single-step equality is
  violated. The runner asserts this violation (so the positive result is not an
  artifact of a trivially-true construction).
- **Two-step (the fix).** The 2-step blocking is exactly the modification that
  restores `Theta`-covariance. On the block (slices `0, 1`) the Osterwalder–Seiler
  fermion reflection carries the `gamma_0`-type sign, `Theta(chi) = - bar(chi)_{theta}`;
  this is the convention determined by reflection = adjoint, not a free parameter
  (the opposite sign is the single-step naive map above). The reflected block metric
  is then positive-semidefinite, with one physical positive eigenvalue per spatial
  mode equal to `c_block · e^{-2E_j}`, `c_block = 2` (two Grassmann pairs per 2-step
  block; a mode-independent normalization, verified numerically against `e^{-2E_j}`).
  The single positive eigenvalue per mode is the reconstructed-state norm, and its
  value `e^{-2E_j}` is the operator two-step transfer eigenvalue — the equality.

The runner additionally verifies that the Berezin forward two-point function decays
as `e^{-2E(p)}` per 2-step block (the transfer eigenvalue appearing **inside** the
path integral), confirming the construction is faithful to the staggered action and
not an artifact of the reflection convention.

## The mixed (U-integrated) equality — the entangled object

For a **mixed** observable `F` (gauge links and fermion fields together) the fermion
factor `<Theta(F) F>^ferm_U` is contracted with the propagator `M[U]^{-1}` and so
**depends on `U`**; it does not factor out of the gauge observable. The reflected
inner product is therefore *not* a product of a separately-positive gauge factor, a
separately-positive fermion factor, and a determinant — the gauge–fermion
entanglement is genuine (operator-Schmidt rank `> 1`, verified). The representation
equality holds with this entanglement carried **identically** on both sides:

- on the operator side, `T_full` is built from the action-derived per-config fermion
  blocks `B[U]^dag B[U]` (single-particle kernel `e^{-2E_j(U)}` second-quantized)
  conjugated by the gauge transfer `T_gauge^{1/2}`;
- on the Berezin side, the same mixed Gram is assembled from the **independently
  computed** Grassmann per-config block metrics integrated against the positive
  determinant weight `det(M[U]) > 0` and the same gauge transfer.

The two Gram matrices agree to `1.5e-14` (`U(1)`) and `5.7e-15` (`SU(3)`) across a
mixed-observable basis (10 and 54 genuinely mixed observables respectively). A
shared finite gauge sample feeds both sides, so the comparison is an exact
finite-carrier equality, not a statistical one.
