## Exact aligned-product diagnostic

For a uniform pure product of Bloch vector n, sum n_a²=1, <H>/N=K. Direction independence of this variational energy is not a continuous symmetry of the Hamiltonian. Direct connected bond counting gives

Var(H)/(NK²)=3+2 sum n_a²−3 sum n_a⁴−8 sum_(a<b)n_a²n_b² = 1+sum n_a⁴.

It lies between4/3 and2, so no member of this uniform pure-product manifold is an exact energy eigenstate when K!=0. The enumeration compass_product_state.py checks the full symbolic expression on L=3,4,5 periodic cubic lattices; the infinite-lattice density follows from the same finite overlap classification. Equal-bond, adjacent-collinear and adjacent-cross-axis pairs exhaust connected terms. This is not a claim that relative extensive energy fluctuations fail to shrink; it is the exact nonzero residual that invalidates treating the product itself as a stationary quantum vacuum.

A uniform onsite infinitesimal generator G=sum_x g dot sigma_x commutes with H only for g=0: on each a-directed bond, the two-site words sigma^b sigma^a and sigma^a sigma^b generated by g perpendicular to a have independent supports/components. Each nonzero g component produces such words on the other two bond orientations. Distinct bond supports cannot cancel on nonaliasing tori. This excludes the proposed global onsite SU(2) rotation symmetry, not all possible emergent/entangled symmetries or criticality.

## A correlated variational improvement without new terms

For K=-J<0, tile the lattice with disjoint x-directed dimers in cos(theta)|00>+sin(theta)|11>, using the z basis. Take even Lx>=4 and Ly,Lz>=3. This is a supplied diagnostic family, not a derived physical preparation. Put s=sin2theta, m=cos2theta. Intra-dimer XX=s, one-site Z=m and one-site X,Y=0. Each z bond joins different dimers and has ZZ=m²; inter-dimer x and all y bond means vanish. Thus

<H>/N = -J(m²+s/2) = -J(1-s²+s/2).

Within this family s=1/4 minimizes energy and yields E/N=-17J/16, strictly below the entire aligned-product family at -J. The variational principle therefore proves the latter cannot be an exact ferromagnetic ground-state family. It does not prove the dimer family is the ground state or that its angle is a universal scale. For C_x=sum_a sigma_(x-e_a)^a sigma_(x+e_a)^a, nonaliasing distance-two x/y pairs have vanishing expectations, while z pairs have m², giving <C>/N=15/16 at this variational optimum. This is a one-point expectation, not a propagator or source coupling.

The local sign automorphism already proved in the energy-feature note maps H to -H on infinite/even-periodic compatible lattices. Thus the sign choice is not an added interaction. Applying it maps this variational upper bound to the positive-K Hamiltonian on compatible even tori; the transformed state is not a uniform product.

## Separable-state bound (focused checker contribution)

The focused checker supplied a stronger exact boundary. Every fully site-separable state obeys E>=-JN for K=-J: for each product component n_x^a n_y^a <= ((n_x^a)^2+(n_y^a)^2)/2, and summing the two incident a bonds per site gives sum_bonds n_x^a n_y^a <= sum_x |n_x|² <= N. Convex mixtures preserve the bound. The dimer trial at -17JN/16 therefore proves any ground state achieving the true minimum is not fully site-separable. It does not prove a specific entangled phase, fermion, scalar or actual preparation. The trial itself breaks one-step x translation and cubic symmetry and has z magnetization. ## The exact projected Hamiltonian and its leakage

Let P project every x dimer onto span{|00>,|11>}. This defines one effective Pauli tau per TWO original one-qubit sites; it adds no physical Hilbert-space factor. In this subspace XX on the intra-dimer x bond is tau_x, and either original Z is tau_z. Every inter-dimer x bond and every y bond flips a single qubit in each of two dimers and leaves P. The two physical z bonds between neighboring dimers give the exact compression

P H P = -J sum_d tau_x(d) - 2J sum_d tau_z(d)tau_z(d+e_z).

There is no transverse coupling in this compressed operator. It consists of transverse-field Ising chains, not an isotropic three-dimensional scalar. More importantly, compression is not autonomous dynamics: QHP is nonzero. No energy penalty protecting P has been derived, and adding one would change the model.

At the optimized dimer trial, the local tangent condition cancels one-pseudospin deviations inside P. The residual two-pseudospin flip on each z link has amplitude -2J s²=-J/8. Distinct link flips are orthogonal in the trial's local spin basis away from short-period aliases, giving Var(PHP)=NJ²/128. Direct exact Pauli calculation independently separates this from leakage:

||(H-<H>)psi||²/N = 201J²/128,
||P(H-<H>)psi||²/N = J²/128,
||QHpsi||²/N = 25J²/16.

The script checks L6 and L8 and preserves the L4 alias value. Thus200/201 of this trial's energy residual lies OUTSIDE the proposed effective subspace. The number is a residual fraction in this state, not a transition probability at arbitrary time. At finite volume the initial leakage probability is t²||QHpsi||²+O(t³); it is not legitimate to extrapolate this Taylor estimate uniformly in N.

This is a useful failed route: projecting onto an appealing encoded Ising theory supplies an unjustified invariant-subspace assumption. The correct next step must retain the parity-changing channels or establish actual low-energy separation, rather than add the projector to the microscopic law. Any projected collective dispersion alone would fail this check.

Focused independent checker derived the local bulk leakage formula J²(3/2+s²) per original site, matching25J²/16 at s=1/4. The Lx4 correction is J²s²/2 from repeated odd-dimer pair sectors, exactly accounting for the preserved wrap artifact. This supplies the local bulk justification beyond finite-size agreement. Independent physical-dimer and logical-matrix report is INDEPENDENT_DIMER_PROJECTION_CHECK.md.

## Retain parity: exact coordinates and a four-site improvement

Use |t,r> -> |z1=t,z2=t xor r> on each x dimer, so the two effective bits are merely coordinates for TWO original sites. The exact maps are X1=tx rx,Y1=ty rx,Z1=tz,X2=rx,Y2=tz ry,Z2=tz rz. Explicit4x4 matrices and16x16 inter-dimer bonds reproduce them. No projection or extra physical qubit is used.

In these coordinates H/(-J) is the sum over dimers d of

 tx_d + rx_d tx_(d+x) rx_(d+x)
 + ty_d ty_(d+y) rx_d rx_(d+y)
 + tz_d tz_(d+y) ry_d ry_(d+y)
 + tz_d tz_(d+z) (1+rz_d rz_(d+z)).

Here x steps by one dimer (two original x sites), while y,z retain their original spacing. This keeps the formerly discarded parity-changing channels. Holding tau in a product Bloch state (s,0,m), m²=1-s², gives an exact partial expectation with parity-compass couplings (s,m²,m²) and constant -J N_d(s+m²). This is a variational energy functional; it does not make rho evolution autonomous or derive a scale-invariant renormalization flow.

Choose disjoint y dimers of rho in cos(eta)|00>-sin(eta)|11>, with t=sin2eta. Their YY expectation is+t. This produces an actual four-original-site block-product state after inverse coordinate change. Its energy per original site is

E/N= -J/2 [s+(1-s²)(2-t²+t/2)].

Optimization within this explicitly supplied two-parameter family gives t=1/4,s=8/33 and E/N=-1153J/1056=-1.091856060606...J. The direct physical16-component block-vector calculation reproduces the energy, evaluating internal bonds jointly and inter-block bonds by factorization. Thus retaining some parity correlations improves on the two-site value -17J/16. No new microscopic interaction, coupling, ground-state selection or mass is inserted.

Full-H variance is still nonzero. Floating local16x16 Pauli-moment contraction gives variance/(NJ²)=1.32972495967796 on L6 and1.32972495967798 on L8, versus1.37478868283068 on L4 (short wrap artifacts retained). These numbers are numerical controls, not certified rational bounds. The residual reduction from201/128 is useful variational progress, not convergence to a stationary state or proof of a propagating scalar. Both this state and the previous dimer state break spatial symmetries. A larger correlated patch calculation must keep this residual alongside energy and composite spectra.

## Larger correlated blocks and full-H residual

Root cluster variational script tiles the infinite physical lattice by identical pure blocks. The original nearest-neighbor bonds are split into internal bonds and actual inter-block face bonds. A self-consistent boundary field is used ONLY to optimize a trial energy; it is not added to the microscopic Hamiltonian. Three seeds are retained, including a nonconverged random seed and a symmetric zero-moment fixed point. Finite numerical precision and local optimization do not certify a global variational minimum.

For arbitrary normalized block psi, define actual means m and centered face operators delta A. Expand H about those means. The one-block part is Heff-<Heff>, with Heff=Hint-sum_face(m_lower A_upper+m_upper A_lower). The two-block part is -sum_face delta A_upper delta A_lower. In the block-product state, terms with different sets of nontrivially centered blocks are orthogonal. Consequently the infinite-volume variance density is exactly

Var(H)/N = [Var_psi(Heff)+sum_a sum_ij G_upper^(a)(i,j) G_lower^(a)(i,j)]/n_block,

where G(i,j)=<delta sigma_i^a delta sigma_j^a>. Each positive-oriented neighbor pair is counted once; opposite faces to distinct neighbors are distinct pairs. This is not the short periodic supercell formula when neighbors alias. It remains valid before self-consistency, provided the actual means are used. The code rebuilds Heff from actual moments at the final iterate.

At J=1, seed0 gives2x2x2 energy/site -1.1262428846864 and full variance/site1.07803756538;3x2x2 gives -1.1495195302896 and variance .915834204756. Local Heff variance is about1e-18 in both, while the physical full-lattice variance is order one and comes from interfaces. This demonstrates directly why self-consistent patch stationarity is not full-H stationarity. The3x2x2 random-seed run did not meet its convergence tolerance after120 iterations and is retained as such; the zero-moment seed has substantially higher energy. No thermodynamic extrapolation from thin blocks is justified.

This operational failure carries no scientific conclusion. Larger-block results and the independent variance-identity check are recorded below. No neutral excitation pole or isotropic propagation follows from these energy improvements alone.

The4x2x2 seed0 result is E/N=-1.161430623404632, full variance/N=.83516007479105. Both other seeds are retained (random seed still unconverged at120 iterations). ## Exact energy bracket and its limit

For the supplied ferromagnetic Hamiltonian on a nonaliasing periodic cubic lattice, write H=sum_x h_x, h_x=-J sum_a sigma_x^a sigma_(x+e_a)^a. The three positive-directed bond operators in h_x anticommute pairwise and square to I. Hence h_x²=3J²I and h_x>=-sqrt(3) J I. Summing gives the rigorous lower bound E0/N>=-sqrt(3)J. The explicit four-site tiled trial gives the rigorous upper bound E0/N<=-1153J/1056 on compatible tilings (and in the thermodynamic energy density). This bracket is wide. The better floating cluster energies are numerical trial controls; they are not a certified small-error ground-state approximation. A low trial energy by itself does not establish overlap with a ground eigenspace without a gap or additional spectral information.
