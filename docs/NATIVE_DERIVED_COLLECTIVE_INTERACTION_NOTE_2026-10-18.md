## Exact feature coordinates

Restrict each four-site cross-section to positive Z parity. Three binary coordinates describe its eight states exactly; this compression adds no physical qubits. The transverse rung Hamiltonian h has eigenvalues -2sqrt(2) twice, zero four times, and +2sqrt(2) twice. The longitudinal bond is -J_z sum_a D_a tensor D_a, where D=(Z0,Z1,Z2,Z0Z1Z2) in the compressed coordinates. Direct physical-to-coordinate intertwining is checked independently through three rungs.

The two lowest rung states form a collective two-state feature. Projected physical bilinears define anticommuting Pauli operators tau1 and tau2; they are not supplied degrees of freedom. In the diagnostic regime lambda=J_z/J_perp small, the isolated finite low band has

H_eff=-2sqrt(2)J_perp L - [J_z²/(sqrt(2)J_perp)] sum_bonds [I+(tau1_i tau1_j+tau2_i tau2_j)/2] + O(J_perp lambda^4).

The coefficient follows from the existing longitudinal bonds and their -4sqrt(2)J_perp intermediate-state denominator. The original neutral composite across three rungs, O=sum_a D_a,0 D_a,2, has zero zeroth/first-order low-band matrix elements. Its numerically reconstructed and independently checked second-order readout is

O_eff=lambda²[(3/4)I+(3/8)sum_(i<j,a=1,2) tau_a,i tau_a,j]+O(lambda^4).

Independent finite-matrix calculations check the readout coefficients and comparisons with the exact low band. No volume-uniform perturbation estimate is asserted. Small lambda is an explicit diagnostic condition, not a derived property of the original equal-coupling model.

## The interaction is richer than a free XY mode

Fourth order generates nearest-neighbor tau3 tau3 and next-neighbor XY terms, with linked support and independently checked coefficients recorded in [the derivation](../.claude/science/physics-loops/neutral-composite-24h-20261018/native/DERIVED_RUNG_COLLECTIVE.md).

At sixth order a simultaneous four-feature transition appears. For four rungs, exact arithmetic gives the all-up/all-down matrix-element magnitude 63 J_z^6/(128sqrt(2) J_perp^5). A wave-operator recursion, exact Gram normalization, and the Bloch-to-canonical conversion are documented in [the sixth-order proof](../.claude/science/physics-loops/neutral-composite-24h-20261018/native/SIXTH_ORDER_COLLECTIVE_PROCESS.md). This is a genuine process generated by the original interaction.

Because these two states share both second- and fourth-order energies but have opposite total tau3, the sixth-order element obstructs any analytic conserved charge whose zero-coupling limit is sum_i tau3_i in this finite low band. This does not exclude other charges or emergent infrared symmetry. It prevents silently upgrading the leading free XY approximation into an exact number-conserving microscopic theory.

## Existing bonds also determine a dressed source vertex

For two adjacent two-rung regions, the actual boundary bonds connect even/even and odd/odd regional parity sectors. Exact elimination gives Sigma(E)=V_PQ(E-H_QQ)^(-1)V_QP. For a block-diagonal existing observable O, its transition operator in the P components is

O_eff(E_f,E_i)=O_PP+V_PQ(E_f-H_QQ)^(-1)O_QQ(E_i-H_QQ)^(-1)V_QP.

This is the [standard projection method](https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-4916(58)90007-1) applied to the supplied model, not a new interaction. Actual P components include their normalization; normalized P vectors require the corresponding residue factors. At full boundary coupling, the ground-state eliminated weight is .3019345654, so a naive weak-boundary approximation is inaccurate. For the checked left-region energy transition, the bare P amplitude .7956097556 and induced contribution -.4664996601 combine to .3291100955, matching the full finite matrix. Overall state phase is conventional; the interference is not. Off-diagonal observables require additional cross terms.

## What this advances and what remains

The feature interaction, leading composite readout and finite source dressing are calculated rather than postulated. Their detailed forms also expose the limits of a simple free scalar description. The missing steps remain substantial: control at equal coupling and growing cross-section, a three-dimensional long-distance mode, a selected cosmological state, physical scales, and the identification of actual source/detector matter. Standard effective-Hamiltonian and projection methods are prior art; no novelty claim is made for the methods. The campaign continues toward those gaps without treating this conditional chain result as a TOE.
