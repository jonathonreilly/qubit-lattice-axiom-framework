# A growing environment around an actual native defect

Status: bounded-support. The one-qubit model now gives more than a large
trial feature: every near-minimal state in its specified two-wall cluster
has a growing region of nearly flat flux relative to the defect's position.

The [proof](../.claude/science/physics-loops/neutral-composite-24h-20261018/native/EXTENDED_ENVIRONMENT_PROOF.md)
uses the native kinetic-energy bound to transport the local environment
constraint outward. At distances L proportional to lambda^-q, each bad-flux
probability tends to zero for q<3/4. The probability of any bad plaquette in
the entire relative square tends to zero for the stricter q<3/8.

This is a particle/environment correlation, not just a spread-out center of
mass. A symmetrized actual vacuum retains bad-flux probability at least
0.1-O(lambda), so the conditional environment differs from the vacuum over
a growing set of relative positions without assuming clustering.

The observables are dressed by the controlled microscopic reduction. The
result neither selects a sharp radius nor fixes classical column signs.
It remains conditional on the supplied Hamiltonian, weak anisotropy and
four-layer slab; it does not identify a fermion, a measured scalar or gravity.

[Replay](../scripts/native_extended_environment.py) checks transport and
joint-event inequalities for background-valued states. These are exposed
model-development controls, not empirical tests.
