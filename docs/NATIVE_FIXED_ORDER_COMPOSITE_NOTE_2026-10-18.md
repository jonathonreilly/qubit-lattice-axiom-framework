**Status:** bounded-support.

The previous [range-two result](NATIVE_RANGE_TWO_AIRY_NOTE_2026-10-18.md)

whether the other coefficients generated by the same one-qubit Hamiltonian

The conclusion remains conditional on the supplied Hamiltonian, ordered

outgoing pole of the complete fixed-order coefficient operator, not yet a
pole of the untruncated microscopic dynamics or a cosmological scalar.

## What is derived

For each fixed m>=4, use the order-m local normal form with every generator
zero inside each unperturbed energy block. In the physical square-scalar
channel, with even coordinate reflections, the vacuum-subtracted pole is

    E_p/Jz = 8-6lambda^2
      +alpha1 lambda^2 (lambda^2/8)^(2/3)
      +lambda^4[109/96+(2+a0)/8]+O_m(lambda^(14/3)),
    Gamma = (-Im a0)/4 Jz lambda^4+O_m(Jz lambda^(14/3)).

Here alpha1 is the first positive Airy zero,
a0=Sigma0/(1+Sigma0), and Sigma0 is the derived punctured-square-host
Green function at energy -2. Its imaginary part is strictly negative by an
analytic spectral-density argument. Numerical evaluation gives
Sigma0=-0.30605214391-0.24643562127i, a real fourth-order energy coefficient
1.35046015003 and width coefficient0.11360791220. These are model outputs,
not fitted measurements.

## Checks and limits

The runner compares the local fourth-order construction with the canonical
kernel on every first-order nullspace of a physical eight-site grid. Five
energy blocks agree to better than4.1e-16. It also checks the punctured-host
Schur spectral identity and the strict-density witness. These finite checks
supplement the proof; they do not establish the thermodynamic pole alone.

The feature extent grows as lambda^(-2/3), but its mass starts at8Jz. Thus
large extent alone has not produced a light mediator. Constants depend on
fixed order m, so taking m to infinity is not justified. The earlier actual
local remainder controls finite-time observables; outgoing microscopic
resonance transfer still needs a separate argument. Original-source residue
accuracy, state selection, physical units, isotropy, detector coupling and
cosmological identification remain open. No observed value is fitted and no
independent observation test has been performed.
