swap of the two complementary axes. On `C^8`, define

```text
Pi_+ = (I + tau) / 2,
Pi_- = (I - tau) / 2,
Y_like = (1/3) Pi_+ - Pi_-.
```

```text

rank(Pi_-) = 2,
```

## Proof

The residual swap `tau` is an involution. Therefore `(I + tau)/2` and
`(I - tau)/2` are complementary projectors. On the doubled weak-fiber
bookkeeping surface, the symmetric sector has dimension `6` and the
antisymmetric sector has dimension `2`. The displayed linear combination
is traceless because

```text
6 * (1/3) + 2 * (-1) = 0.
```

projector ranks, Hermiticity, trace, and eigenvalue multiplicities directly.
