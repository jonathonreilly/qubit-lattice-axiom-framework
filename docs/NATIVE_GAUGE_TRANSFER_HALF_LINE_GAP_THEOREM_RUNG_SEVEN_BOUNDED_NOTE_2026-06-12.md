## Route A: Eventual Monotonicity Attempt

Let

```text
bar T_beta = E_beta bar D_beta E_beta,
E_beta = exp((beta/2)(J-I)),
```

where `bar D_beta` is the positive diagonal `D_beta` divided by any positive
scalar factor. This scalar normalization does not change
`lambda_1/lambda_0`. On a finite packet block with a simple eigenvalue
`lambda_i` and normalized eigenvector `u_i`, the formal derivative identity is

```text
d/d beta log(lambda_i)
  = <u_i, (J-I) u_i>
    + <E_beta u_i, (d/d beta bar D_beta) E_beta u_i> / lambda_i.
```

Thus monotone decrease of the ratio would follow from the sharper inequality

```text
d/d beta log(lambda_1) <= d/d beta log(lambda_0)
```

on the native block after the finite-window certificate range. The attempted
shortcut was to seek a Loewner sign for `d bar T_beta / d beta`. The runner
recomputes a finite-block diagnostic at shell `8`: the normalized derivative
matrix has both negative and positive Rayleigh directions at beta `12`. That
does not disprove monotonicity of the ratio, but it blocks the direct
Loewner-order proof. The remaining route A target is the explicit
block-Hellmann derivative inequality above, including shell and tail control.
