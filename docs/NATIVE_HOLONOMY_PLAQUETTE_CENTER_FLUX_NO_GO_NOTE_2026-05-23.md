## Question

The retained `native_gauge_closure_note` surface supplies `su(3)` as a
*static symmetry* of the 8-dimensional taste space
`V = C^2 ⊗ C^2 ⊗ C^2`. The lattice-gauge holonomy form
`U = exp(i a A^a_μ T_a)` instead requires a *gauged connection* on edges. Does
the native staggered/taste structure (A1+A2 plus the staggered nearest-neighbor
hopping) **force** a continuous `su(3)` parallel transport around a plaquette,
or is the native flux purely center-valued — so that the continuous connection
`A^a_μ` is added structure rather than derived?

## Answer

**No** — the native structure does not force a continuous `su(3)` connection.

Under both natural identifications of the native "hop in direction μ" that the
framework actually supplies, the single-plaquette holonomy is **exactly a
center element** of the gauge group, with **zero traceless `su(3)`
component**. The continuous color connection `A^a_μ` is therefore not generated
by the native hops.

The remaining open object is narrower and clean:

> the gauge **group** `su(3)` and its canonical generator normalization are
> native; the gauge **field** `A^a_μ` enters by the standard minimal-coupling
> principle as added structure. The native lattice carries only a center
> (`Z_2`) flux.

This is a boundary, not a wall: it delimits exactly what staggered hopping
alone supplies, and leaves the complementary positive programs untouched
(see *Remaining open* below).

## Theorem (native plaquette holonomy is center-valued)

Let the native taste-space directional hops `L_μ` (μ = 1,2,3) be involutory
unitaries on `V` (`L_μ† L_μ = I`, `L_μ² = I`), and let the plaquette holonomy
in the (μ,ν) plane be the path-ordered group commutator

```text
W_{μν} = L_μ L_ν L_μ^{-1} L_ν^{-1}.
```

**(1) Clifford-folded hops.** With the staggered-phase folded hops equal to the
cubic Clifford generators `Γ_μ` of `native_gauge_closure_note`
(`{Γ_μ, Γ_ν} = 2 δ_{μν} I`), anticommutation gives, for μ ≠ ν,

```text
W_{μν} = Γ_μ Γ_ν Γ_μ Γ_ν = − Γ_μ Γ_μ Γ_ν Γ_ν = − I.
```

So every Clifford-folded plaquette holonomy is the `Z_2` center element `−I`.

**(2) Cube-shift hops.** With the graph-first one-step axis shifts `S_μ` (the
shifts whose commutant yields `su(3)`), which mutually commute, for μ ≠ ν

```text
W_{μν} = S_μ S_ν S_μ^{-1} S_ν^{-1} = I.
```

So every cube-shift plaquette holonomy is the trivial center element `+I`.

**(3) Center membership and vanishing traceless part.** In both cases
`W_{μν} = c·I` with `c ∈ {+1, −1}`, hence the traceless component
`W_{μν} − (Tr W_{μν}/8) I = 0` exactly. The flux element `−I` commutes with all
native generators (it is central). No `W_{μν}` has a nonzero projection onto the
traceless `su(3)` sector. ∎

## Constructive corollary (`g_bare = 1` is unaffected)

This no-go does **not** weaken `g_bare = 1`. That statement is the canonical
*normalization of the generators* `T_a` fixed by the taste Hilbert-space trace
form (`g_bare_rigidity_theorem_note`, `Tr(T_a T_b) = δ_{ab}/2`, no scalar
dilation freedom). It is logically independent of whether the field `A^a_μ` is
native. When the connection is added by minimal coupling in the canonical
basis, the holonomy is `U = exp(i a A^a_μ T_a)` with no independent
multiplicative coupling, i.e. `g_bare = 1`. The present no-go in fact *closes*
the cite-or-derive admission left open in `g_bare_rigidity_theorem_note`: the
holonomy form is imported because the native flux is center-valued, and this
note supplies the proof of why.

## What this claims

- The single-plaquette native holonomy is exactly center-valued under both
  natural hop identifications: `−I` (Clifford-folded) and `+I` (cube shifts).
- Its traceless `su(3)` component is exactly zero; the continuous connection
  `A^a_μ` is not generated by the native staggered hopping.
- Therefore the gauge field enters as added structure (minimal coupling), while
  the gauge group and its canonical normalization remain native.
- Constructive corollary: `g_bare = 1` holds for the minimally-coupled
  connection in the canonical basis, independent of this no-go.

## What this does NOT claim

- Does **not** claim the gauge group `su(3)` is non-native; it is native and
  retained as a symmetry (`native_gauge_closure_note`).
- Does **not** rule out a *separate* derivation of the connection from some
  other lane (variational/dynamical action principle, or a gauge principle
  forced by locality together with an explicitly stated additional input).
  It rules out only that staggered nearest-neighbor hopping *by itself*
  generates the continuous `su(3)` transport.
- Does **not** address the absolute scale, the lattice spacing `a`, the
  physical coupling magnitude, or any dimensionful quantity.
- Does **not** consume any PDG value, fitted selector, literature comparator,
  or admitted unit convention.
