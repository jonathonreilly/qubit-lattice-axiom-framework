# Actual local remainder and finite-time native dynamics

Claim type: bounded_theorem.

Primary runner: `scripts/native_local_normalform.py`

**Status:** bounded-support.

The [earlier native coefficient bound](NATIVE_SLOW_COMPOSITE_CONTROL_NOTE_2026-10-18.md)
controlled a finite sum of formal coefficients but left the actual microscopic
remainder open. Here an exact finite product of local unitaries supplies a
volume-independent local remainder at every fixed order. This closes that
specific existence/bound gap. It does not validate the truncated scalar
channel, prove a microscopic resonance, or select a physical state.

All work is conditional on the supplied compass Hamiltonian. No added
interaction or empirical input is used. Strong-field normal forms and
prethermal dynamics are established prior art: [Abanin, De Roeck, Ho and
Huveneers](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.05386) prove quasi-exponential results
for their stated hypotheses. Their static setup uses on-site integer number
operators. The present finite-order commuting-bond adaptation is derived
below; no optimized-order theorem is imported unchanged.

## Local inverse of the longitudinal energy commutator

In units Jz, dropping a constant, write

    H=2N+lambda V, N=sum_(z bonds)(1-Z_i Z_j)/2.

N has integer spectrum although its bond terms overlap. They commute.
For alpha_theta(A)=exp(i theta N) A exp(-i theta N), define

    P A=(1/2pi) integral_0^(2pi) alpha_theta(A) dtheta,
    S(A)=i/(4pi) integral_0^(2pi)(theta-pi) alpha_theta(A) dtheta.

If [N,A_m]=m A_m, then S(A)=sum_(m!=0)A_m/(2m). Hence
[S(A),2N]=-(A-P A). For Hermitian A, S(A) is anti-Hermitian and
||S(A)||<=pi||A||/4. No state, nondegenerate vacuum, or inverse of an
extensive perturbation norm is required.

Only longitudinal bonds meeting A's original support can affect its
conjugation: all other commuting bond rotations cancel. Thus P and S enlarge
support only by its immediate z neighbors. In the interaction norm

    a0(A)=sup_i sum_(X containing i)||A_X||,

this gives a0(P A)<=3a0(A), a0(S A)<=3pi a0(A)/4. The bounds are uniform
in finite volume, with open or periodic boundaries.

## Exact fixed-order construction

At order k, the current Taylor coefficient A_k is finite range. Choose
S_k=S(A_k) and conjugate by exp(lambda^k S_k). This removes its off-N part;
its remaining part D_k commutes with N. After any fixed m steps,

    U_m H U_m^dagger = 2N + sum_(k=1..m)lambda^k D_k + R_m(lambda).

This is an exact equality with an actual remainder, not just formal notation.
To justify it locally, for finite-support interaction terms use

    a0([A,B]) <= 2(s_A+s_B)a0(A)a0(B),
    a0(ad_A^n B) <= (2a0(A))^n
                    product_(j=0..n-1)[s_B+(j+1)s_A] a0(B),

where s denotes maximum term cardinality. For the full product of
conjugations let s=max_k s_(S_k), A(lambda)=sum_k |lambda|^k a0(S_k).
The joint mixed-word expansion is bounded by

    a0(B) sum_(n>=0) [2A(lambda)]^n/n!
           product_(j=0..n-1)[s_B+(j+1)s].

It converges whenever2s A(lambda)<1. Bounding the full product is important:
after the first exact exponential, the interaction is no longer finite range.
One cannot repeatedly pretend it remains finite range. The joint expansion
starts from the original finite-range Hamiltonian and avoids that mistake.

The same majorant works with exponential diameter weights on overlapping
supports, after decreasing the lambda radius. Analyticity and a Cauchy
remainder estimate then give, for each fixed m and sufficiently small lambda,

    a_mu(R_m(lambda)) <= C_m |lambda|^(m+1),

with mu>0 and C_m independent of system volume. Constants and the allowed
radius can deteriorate rapidly with m. No convergence as m tends to infinity,
useful numerical threshold at m20, or optimized exponential remainder is
claimed. In finite boxes U_m is unitary; the local interaction bounds define
the corresponding infinite-volume local dynamics.

## Why the linewidth timescale is accessible to this bound

Set B_m=2N+D_m(lambda), retaining ALL terms through m. Since [N,D_m]=0,
the fast evolution generated by2N only enlarges a local observable's support
by one longitudinal layer, uniformly in time. The remaining D_m interaction
has strength O(lambda), so its Lieb-Robinson velocity is O(lambda).

For O supported on X, Duhamel's formula, the pair-sum locality estimate and
the trivial commutator bound give

    ||tau_H'(t)O-tau_Bm(t)O||
       <= C_m ||O|| |X| lambda^(m+1) |t| (1+lambda|t|)^3.

Here time is in Jz^(-1) units and H'=U_m H U_m^dagger. To see why there is
neither a second |X| factor nor exp(lambda t), apply
min(1,sum a_j)<=sum min(1,a_j) before summing interaction supports. Summing
min(1,exp[-mu distance+v lambda t]) over the three-dimensional lattice gives
the polynomial cone volume. The complete proof is in the
independent dynamics check (`outputs/neutral_continuation_review_2026_10_18/pr9747/native/INDEPENDENT_LOCAL_DYNAMICS.md`).

For fixed m20, times O(lambda^-4), feature support O(lambda^(-2/3)), and
uniform OPERATOR norm ||O||=O(1), the bound is O(lambda^(22/3)), smaller
than lambda^6. Variance normalization is not sufficient: a commuting sum
divided by sqrt(|X|) can have operator norm O(sqrt(|X|)), giving lambda^7
instead. The explicit norm factor must be retained for any actual readout.
This is an asymptotic bound, not an error bar at the numerical lambda values
used in earlier model calculations.

## What this does and does not retire

The actual finite-order normal-form remainder now has a local bound. But
B_m contains all D5 through D20 in the example above. Dropping them or keeping
only the preferred scalar channel introduces a different error. The earlier
finite-coefficient estimate on length L gives O(lambda^5 L); at L~lambda^(-2/3)
and t~lambda^-4 this alone costs O(lambda^(1/3)). It is not a lambda^6
readout-precision bound. Therefore this theorem does not certify the earlier
small spectral residue or microscopic pole.

Effective Hamiltonians can also differ by block-preserving unitary gauge.
Matching this recursive construction to earlier canonical coefficient matrices
requires transforming both the state and source; their entries cannot simply
be identified. An original physical local observable becomes quasi-local
under U_m. Its tails must be summed with the locality bound, or kept explicitly;
one cannot drop the dressing as O(lambda) when resolving a lambda^6 signal.
The displayed finite-support estimate directly concerns local O in the dressed
frame. It does not supply cosmological preparation, a detector map, or an
infinite-time Fourier/resonance estimate.

## Exact dressing of the original readout packet

For C_w=sum_j w_j Z_(j,z)Z_(j,z+2), the same mixed-word expansion proves
that each exactly dressed local density has a decomposition with
sum_Y |Y| ||c_(j,Y)||<=C_m, uniform in j and volume. Multiplying the
convergent majorant by support size only adds a polynomial in its word
length. Thus the source-tail summability needed above is derived, not assumed.
Applying the local-time estimate term by term gives

    ||tau_H'(t) Ctilde_w-tau_Bm(t) Ctilde_w||
       <= C_m ||w||_1 lambda^(m+1)|t|(1+lambda|t|)^3,

where Ctilde_w=U_m C_w U_m^dagger is retained exactly. For any initial state,
use its SAME transformed state under both evolutions. Since
||Ctilde_w||<=||w||_1, two-time correlation and commutator differences are
bounded by the same expression with ||w||_1 squared (and fixed constants).
No stationarity assumption is needed for this comparison.

At m20 and the time/length scales above, weights1/sqrt(L) give correlation
error O(lambda^(22/3)); unweighted sums give O(lambda^(20/3)). Both are
smaller than lambda^6 asymptotically. This discharges the exact-source-tail
summation issue for finite packets, while retaining all D20 terms. It does
not derive the initial state, detector identification or a spectral pole.
See the source proof (`outputs/neutral_continuation_review_2026_10_18/pr9747/native/DRESSED_SOURCE_BOUND.md`)
and independent check (`outputs/neutral_continuation_review_2026_10_18/pr9747/native/INDEPENDENT_DRESSED_READOUT.md`).

## Computational controls

An eight-physical-qubit open cube checks the recursive construction with
exact dense unitary conjugations at orders1 through4 and lambda=.02,.04,.08.
The transformed Taylor coefficients commute with N. Actual remainder norms
scale as lambda^(m+1); unitarity errors are below3e-15. This is a finite-model
control of signs, recursion and power counting, not a numerical proof of
volume independence or the high-order theorem. The latter follows from the
local estimates above and their independent check. A separate six-site2x1x3 open grid checks the original distance-two source
with exact dressing and the same transformed initial state. At lambda=.08,
its dressing norm is .15999947, so dropping it would be a much larger error
than the checked correlation differences. Finite global Duhamel bounds hold
at times .5,2,8. Source and outputs are bound by the runner; all tests are
exposed model development.
