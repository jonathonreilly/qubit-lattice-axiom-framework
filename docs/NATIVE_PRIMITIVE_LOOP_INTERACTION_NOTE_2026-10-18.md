# Native loop law and directional interactions

Status: bounded-support.

Exact local spin correlations give a closed coefficient for each primitive simple transverse loop, depending on its perimeter and number of turns. Rectangles are attractive in this coefficient convention; six-turn loops have the opposite sign. No potential is fitted or added.

Including overlapping two-square processes and their folds gives a complete local order-eight test: two columns at separation (2,2) repel, while separation (3,0) attracts. These are derived diagonal interaction coefficients, not a scattering result or a new isolated-band theorem.
