# What the remaining sectors cost in supplied structure: a synthesis of the next-needs blocks

**Date:** 2026-09-24
**Type:** bounded_theorem
**Status:** synthesis of open blocks with consolidated certificates; unaudited.

## Result

The morning campaign (open PR 9061) supplied one local dynamics clause and
found what it generates. It left five sectors open: the composition law,
when records form, charged matter, a linear graviton, and the photon's phase.
Eight further blocks attacked them. Each supplies a structure and certifies
its price. This note collects the prices.

| Sector | What was supplied | What it buys | What it costs | Blocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Composition | two readings of axiom text: readout locality as local tomography (D-tomo), and the generated algebra (D-alg) | the ordinary tensor product `M_2(C) ⊗ M_2(C)`; the graded composite is excluded, so fermions emerge rather than compose | nothing beyond the readings; whether Record's text forces them stays open | 9140 |
| Formation | a memoryless clock whose rate is a function of the site's conditional state (D-form) | the recorded odds are clock-independent exactly on the field-aligned menu of an isolated site, where the clause conserves `r·ĥ` | everywhere else "when" changes "which"; the rate function is supplied | 9142 |
| Charge | composite sites of two qubits (D-comp, D-roles) with the Yao–Lee bond | an exact SU(2) generated by the matter spin, a charge-one fermion, chiral charged edge modes on a layer | plaquette-corner terms; one qubit per site gives neutral Majoranas | 9144 |
| Gauged charge | the U(1) link field with a staggered background (D-background), the bond dressed by one link operator | exact gauge invariance, SU(2) broken to the gauged U(1), the Z2 partner's flux conserved, chirality kept | a third site class, a `4 × 2 × 2` block, the half-filled sea, exact solvability lost in the charged sector | 9149 |
| Gravity | oscillator (Fock) slots for the tensor field (D-osc) | two isotropic linear modes on the doubly constrained surface | both canonical variables non-compact: no rotor or clock realization; qubits emulate only up to a top-level defect | 9145 |
| Photon | the exact Gauss law, the covariant ring at `V = 0` (D-gauss, D-ring) | finite diagnostics: no plaquette order to 8³, a flux-quantum cost compatible with `1/L`, energies converged to 0.4% | no phase determination; the gap and dispersion need another estimator | 9146, 9148, 9153 |

Three lessons run through the blocks.
- **The cost of each sector is a structure, not a neighbourhood size.** The
  morning ladder measured how far a generator must reach. Here the prices are
  a second reading of axiom text, a clock, a second qubit per site, a site
  class, a slot type.
- **Records keep the bookkeeping.** In the gauged model the matter record at
  a vertex is fixed by the surrounding link records; on the field-aligned
  menu the record's odds do not depend on when it forms; the graded
  composite fails because its records cannot tell two states apart.
- **The photon question narrows without being settled.** Three independent
  diagnostics lean the same way at the pure-ring point; none is the photon.

## Blocks

- **9140 — composition.** Local record menus along four axes rebuild every
  two-site state exactly; `(|01⟩ ± |10⟩)/√2` agree on every parity-even local
  product; half the swap operator is positive on product states with
  smallest eigenvalue −1/2, so D-tomo alone leaves the state set open and
  D-alg is the second price.
- **9142 — formation clock.** Covariance leaves the rate functions
  `{1, |r|²}`, plus `r·ĥ, (r·ĥ)²` with a field axis, plus `(r·n)²` with an
  unordered menu. On the field menu of an isolated site all clocks record
  `(1 + r·ĥ)/2`; on a tilted menu, or with an unrecorded neighbour, the
  clock enters. The record law is a trace rule of one formation-weighted
  state.
- **9144 — composite charge.** A Majorana bilinear between two carving
  components is odd under the gauge constraint, so one qubit per site carries
  no charge. Four dimers in a star have the three-flavour free spectrum
  `−3e, −e, e, 3e` (32, 96, 96, 32); the matter spin is exact; a
  time-reversal-odd term keeps it; a honeycomb layer of dimers has a charged
  chiral edge mode.
- **9145 — linear graviton.** Both terms of the linear comparator are only
  weakly invariant, so neither canonical variable can be compact; on
  oscillator slots the lattice symbol has `λ/|k|² = 1.000` isotropically and
  is indefinite off the constraint surface; a truncated oscillator has
  `[x_N, p_N] = i(1 − N|N−1⟩⟨N−1|)`.
- **9146 — ring model diagnostics.** The fine torus has 9600 ice states in
  937 classes (largest 864); the pure-ring ground state has fidelity 0.807
  with the uniform one; the Jastrow optimum is `α = 0.14` there and `0.2` on
  the coarse tori, with no plaquette order. Its loop sampler was corrected
  the same day.
- **9148 — projector Monte Carlo.** Controlled against exact diagonalization;
  energies 2.7% below the variational ones on 6³ and 8³;
  `S(π,π,π)/N_p ≈ 0.29` on every size, stable in imaginary time.
- **9149 — gauged composite charge.** One role per site class; the dressed
  bond and ring commute with every Gauss operator; the Z2 flux survives; the
  gauge sector of one plaquette has 112 states with charge 2; the dressed odd
  term is gauge invariant and odd under record-basis conjugation, the gauged
  model's anti-unitary symmetry, while the spin-flip time reversal of the
  ungauged model is broken by the gauging.
- **9153 — winding sectors.** Fixed-flux sectors are exact and constructible;
  the one-quantum splitting is 0.811 ± 0.095 on 4³ and 0.348 ± 0.133 on 6³,
  `dE·L` consistent between them; the relaxation from uniform ice is
  multi-exponential and yields no gap.

## What stays open

- Whether Record's text forces D-tomo, and where D-alg lives in the axioms.
- Which rate function, if any, the axioms favour.
- A three-dimensional network of composite sites realized by records; the
  phase of the gauged model; the Chern number of the dressed odd term on a
  layer with a fluctuating link field.
- A finite model on truncated-oscillator slots with its constraints handled.
- The photon: its gap and dispersion at the pure-ring point, and the extent
  of the Coulomb phase in `V/g`.
- Independent checks: every block here is self-checked only.
