# The tick fixes the U(5) frame; the half-link 2 + 3 split is orbital, not internal

Type: bounded theorem (exact commutants and representation checks) plus finite diagnostics. Gate verdict at the end.
Runner: runner.py (30 PASS / 0 FAIL, about 1 s, about 107 MB peak). Tested through scripts/runner_cache.execute_runner
under a unique filename.
Inputs:
- agents/spin10-breaking: C2 says 13 is the structural floor. C3 gives C(Theta, J) = u(2)+u(3). C4 says the hypercube
  supplies no J. Repair (b) asks whether J comes from time.
- agents/minimal-toe-merged: Y, X = 2N - 5 = U(1)_chi, and the open question of whether "the dynamics orients a 45 along
  aX + bY".
- agents/why-three-dims: CONE gives d = 3 and complex amplitudes.
- agents/commutant-c: the cube tick's commutant; corner labels carry no su(4).
- CL-1 text: "each site's qubit is one fermion mode"; W_i "pass each site's odds to its edge neighbour". The Links
  clause gauges "the update's symmetries of matter modes".

## Question

Spin(10) is generated by the 45 bilinears of 10 Majorana generators. U(5) is the stabiliser of an orthogonal complex
structure J on R^10. Does the update's own time structure supply J? Candidates:
- the tick U = exp(-iH);
- the amplitude i;
- particle-number conservation;
- the half-tick.
Given J, can a COMPOSITE of sites, built from d = 3 and the qubit and covariant, supply the second split
U(5) -> S(U(3) x U(2))?

## T. Time and the complex structure

**T1 (theorem, positive frequency).**
- For any nondegenerate real antisymmetric one-body tick generator A on R^10, J_A = A(-A^2)^(-1/2) is an orthogonal
  complex structure commuting with A.
- H_A = iF(A) conserves N_{J_A} = iF(J_A) + 5/2.
- The ground state of H_A is the N_{J_A} = 0 state, with E0 = -sum(omega)/2.
- C_so10(J_A) = u(5), of dimension 25. Choosing a J by hand is a choice of 20 numbers (SO(10)/U(5)); the tick makes that
  choice with no extra input.
This is the Segal/Kay-Wald one-particle structure, rederived here on the 10.

**T1e (theorem: arrow <-> 16).**
- (-1)^{N_J} = -Pf(J) Gamma_11 for every J tested.
- Time reversal (A -> -A, so J -> -J) flips Pf, because 5 is odd. It therefore swaps which Weyl half has even
  J-number, i.e. 16 <-> 16bar.
- So the time orientation, not a separate clause, decides which chiral half is "particles".

**T2 (theorem).** C_so10(A) = sum of u(m_k) over the multiplicities m_k of the frequencies |omega|. The exact dimensions
are:

| Multiplicities | (5) | (3,2) | (4,1) | (2,2,1) | (1^5) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dim C(A) | 25 | 13 | 17 | 9 | 5 |

So time gives the U(5) frame, and the tick's actual internal symmetry is set by its frequency degeneracies. The 2+3 split
is a statement about which frequencies are degenerate.

**T3.** The i of complex amplitudes is central on Fock space and commutes with all 45 generators. On its own it selects
nothing. It enters only through iH, i.e. through T1. CONE's "complex amplitudes" is necessary but not sufficient.

**T4.** The Z^3 x tick point group W(B3) x {time reversal} acting on R^4 has commutant R (+) R (W(B4): R). Both blocks
are real and odd-dimensional, so they contain no J. Breaking 4D isotropy by time does NOT rescue spin10-breaking's
repair (b) in the geometric sense. Time supplies J through its generator (frequency), not through the point group.

**T5 (the decisive refinement).**
- (a) With pairing (a generic Majorana tick on a chain), the positive-frequency J(k) depends on momentum
  (|J(.3) - J(1.9)| = 2.25). The on-site commutant is 0, so there is no internal U(1).
- (b) A number-conserving tick (hops that move occupations, as CL-1's W_i do) has the k-independent J0. Its generic
  commutant is exactly 1, which is U(1)_X.
- (c) The pi/2 half-tick exp(-i pi/2 R) = -iR does square to -1, but it is the hop itself: site-nonlocal. At generic eps
  it is not a J.

Answer to the first question:
- The internal J is fermion-number conservation, and CL-1 already has it: one fermion mode per site, and odds passed
  between neighbours.
- It is the time structure's J (T1) exactly when the tick has no pairing.
- Fermion-number conservation is therefore not an extra rule in CL-1, but it is a property of CL-1's hop form, not of
  "time" in general (T5a).
- S4c: its twisted generator X = 2N - 5 has charges -5, -1, 3 on 1, 10, 5b. That is U(1)_chi, the merged ruleset's Z4
  carrier.

## S. The second split from d = 3 as a composite

**S1 (theorem).**
- The 2d half-links (link ends) of a site carry the permutation representation of the hyperoctahedral group. It
  decomposes as A1 + (d-1) + d: even traceless patterns plus dipole patterns, irreducible and inequivalent, checked for
  d = 2, 3, 4.
- At d = 3 this is 1 + 2 + 3 = A1 + E + T1.
- E appears once in the 3 unoriented axes, never in the 3 oriented axes (vector) or the 2^3 cube corners
  (A1 + A2 + T1 + T2), and once in the 6 half-links (S1b).
- So the 2+3 split is SU(d-1) x SU(d), with d - 1 = 2 for d = 3. No number is chosen. The 2 is the inversion-even
  quadrupole patterns and the 3 the inversion-odd dipole patterns.

**S2 (theorem).**
- Site + 6 half-links, with the only covariant site-link hop, has spectrum +-sqrt6 (site with A1) and 0^5. The zero
  space is exactly E + T1, so it is degenerate, with u(5).
- Any covariant half-link-half-link term splits it:
  - antipode swap: E +1, T1 -1;
  - face adjacency: A1 4, E -2, T1 0.
- This is a two-stage chain (U(5) at leading order, 2 + 3 at next order) from the cluster's own structure.

**S3 (theorem).** A cubic-invariant on-site tick on E + T1 has exactly two parameters (omega_E, omega_T).
- Generically omega_E != omega_T, and the so(10) commutant is u(2)+u(3) = 13: the SM x U(1)_chi floor of
  spin10-breaking C2.
- S3c: the tick generator itself equals aX + bY + const with residual 0. This answers the merged ruleset's orientation
  question at the free-tick level: the on-site part of a cubic-invariant tick IS an aX + bY orientation, with no
  condensate.

**S4 (theorem).**
- The even-N_J 16 is (3,2)_{1/6} + (3b,1)_{-2/3} + (3b,1)_{1/3} + (1,2)_{-1/2} + 1_{1} + 1_{0}, with Y = N_E/2 - N_T/3
  the su(5)-traceless combination.
- Y, Y^3, SU3^2 Y, SU2^2 Y, X, X^3, X^2 Y, X Y^2, SU3^2 X and SU2^2 X all vanish. There are 4 doublets, so the Witten
  anomaly is absent.
- This is standard SU(5) in SO(10) once 2+3 is given. It is not a new prediction.

**S5 (the failure, theorem).** The labels E, T1 turn with space.
- Cubic-covariant k-linear terms on (E+T1) x spin (with SU(2) lifts) form a 13-dimensional space. The colour- and
  weak-blind cones (P_E x sigma.k, P_T x sigma.k) are 2 of these.
- A generic covariant cone mixes E with T1 (block norm 1.18), and its internal commutant is 1: only U(1)_X survives. It
  breaks SU(3), SU(2) and even Y.
- Keeping SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1)_Y needs 11 marginal (same order as the cone) parameters set to zero. That is tuning,
  and it fails R4/M4 "absent unless protected" in reverse: nothing protects the blindness.
- CL-1's own Links rule gauges "the update's symmetries of matter modes", which is the commutant of the whole update.
  For the generic covariant update that is U(1) only.
- This is the same lock that commutant-c found for corner labels and spin10-breaking B2 found for Gamma_mu: a label set
  on which the lattice acts (needed for Schur to force degeneracies) is an orbital label, and covariant hops do not
  respect any internal SU on it.

**S6.** The "3 axes + 2 qubit levels" reading fails differently. If the 2 is the cone's two-component spinor (CONE p2),
sigma.k leaves commutant 1 on it, so it is spin, not weak isospin. This is B3 again.

**S7.** Keeping the A1 half-link mode gives Spin(12). Its half-spinor 32 is 16 + 16bar of the 5-mode Spin(10): a mirror
family. That fits CONE p3 (mirrors forced) but needs the A1 gapped against the site (S2's +-sqrt6 pair).

**S8 (finite diagnostic, composite cells).**
- 7-site stars (site + 6 neighbours) tile Z^3 exactly. This is the Golomb-Welch perfect Lee code, with centres at
  x + 2y + 3z = 0 mod 7, checked on 7^3.
- But the tiling keeps only a C3 of the 24 rotations. The generic C3-invariant tick on the 5 is non-degenerate
  (9 invariants, minimum gap 0.156).
- So a real one-mode-per-site supercell that realises the star loses the cubic protection of 2+3.
- Within the axioms (one mode per site), a 5-fold internal multiplet needs a cell of at least 5 sites. The cubically
  symmetric cells tested (2^3 cube, 3 links) contain no E + T1 alone. The half-link reading needs modes on link ends,
  which bends the Qubit axiom.

## Against measurement

- The structural floor reproduces the measured one-family charges and anomaly freedom (S4), but that is group theory
  once 2+3 is given.
- Measured exact colour SU(3) (8 massless gluons, colour-blind quark propagation) and an internal weak SU(2) separate
  from spin falsify the orbital readings of S5 and S6 unless 11 marginal couplings are tuned to zero.
- The chain therefore does not reach measured physics.
