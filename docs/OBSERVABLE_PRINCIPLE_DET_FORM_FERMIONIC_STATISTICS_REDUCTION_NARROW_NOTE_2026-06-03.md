## 1. The three propositions and the question

- **(Ber)** *the fermion weight is a determinant.* For Grassmann generators

  the antisymmetric (Grassmann) one, not the symmetric (bosonic) one.
- **(M)** *the readout is a product character.* `chi(A·S) = chi(A)·chi(S)` (the
  #2503 FORM premise that selects `det` over `tr`).

The dissolution hypothesis is the implication chain **(Fer) ⟹ (Ber) ⟹ "(M)
is vacuous, `det` is forced as the weight."** The question this note settles is

### 2.2 The exact hypothesis Berezin needs — the antisymmetric measure

The minus signs in `det = ∑_π sign(π) ∏_x M_{x,π(x)}` come from the

### 2.3 `det` multiplicative ⟹ `−log det` additive (the records→information shape)

`det(A·B) = det(A)·det(B)` (composition) and `det(A ⊕ B) = det(A)·det(B)`

## 3. The reframe DOES defuse #2503's abstract non-sequitur

**In the info-of-fermion-weight framing this witness is no longer available.**

## 4. …but the choice RELOCATES to the statistics, which is admitted

The dissolution claim would require that **no** choice remains once `tr` is
removed. It does not hold, because removing `tr` does not remove the
**fermion-vs-boson** choice.

### 4.1 `det`-vs-`tr` is replaced by `det`-vs-permanent (a genuine binary)

The symmetric (bosonic) Gaussian weight is the **permanent**
`perm(M) = ∑_π ∏_x M_{x,π(x)}` (all signs `+1`), and for generic `M` the
permanent differs from the determinant: `perm(M) ≠ det(M)` at `N = 2, 3`
(runner R3a, e.g. `perm − det = 2 w_{12} w_{21}` at `N = 2`). The convergent
real bosonic Gaussian is `(det M)^{−1/2}`, the reciprocal-square-root of the
Grassmann answer — the sign of the `det`-exponent flips `+1 → −1/2` (runner
R3b; cf. the cited Berezin narrow's (D4) and the
[`STAGGERED_DIRAC_SUBSTEP1_GRASSMANN_FORCING_BRIDGE_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-16.md`](STAGGERED_DIRAC_SUBSTEP1_GRASSMANN_FORCING_BRIDGE_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-16.md)
(D4)). So `det ≠ permanent` is a **real binary** that must be fixed (runner
R4d): the two statistics give **different** observables. The choice the reframe
removed (`det`-vs-`tr`) is replaced by the choice it exposes
(`det`-vs-permanent = antisymmetric-vs-symmetric measure = the statistics).

### 4.2 The statistics is NOT forced by A1/A2 (`retained_no_go`)
