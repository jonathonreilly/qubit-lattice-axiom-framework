## 1. The wall, restated precisely

`F_p[J] := |Z[J]|^p`, `Z[J] = det(D+J) = Tr e^{-(H+J)}`, `p ∈ ℝ∖{0}`. It is

## 2. Stage 1 (FORM): `F_p` collapses to one real scale `c` — proved

Worked from the current minimal axiom surface
`MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-05-20.md`: the one-qubit operator algebra
`M₂(ℂ) ≅ Cl(3,0)` at each site on the `Z^3` spatial substrate, with no
additivity premise.

1. **Two invariants only.** Conjugation-invariant polynomial functionals on
   `M₂(ℂ)` are generated by `tr` and `det`; Newton's identity
   `tr(M²) = (tr M)² − 2 det M` shows there is no third independent invariant.
   *(runner S1.1, 2000 random conjugations.)*
2. **det is canonical.** `det` is the action of `M` on the top exterior power
   `Λ²ℂ² ≅ ℂ`: `(Me₁)∧(Me₂) = det(M)·(e₁∧e₂)`. This singles out `det` from the
   one-qubit operator algebra alone — no composition law, multiplicativity, or
   additivity invoked.
   *(runner S1.2, exact.)*
3. **The character theorem.** The commutator subgroup of `GL₂(ℂ)` is
   `SL₂(ℂ)`, so the abelianization is `GL₂(ℂ)/SL₂(ℂ) ≅ ℂˣ` via `det`. The
   continuous conjugation-invariant multiplicative characters
   `GL₂(ℂ) → (ℝ_{>0}, ×)` are therefore exactly `|det|^c`, `c ∈ ℝ`; their
   logarithm `c·log|det|` is the unique additive (operator-product) generator.
   The orthogonal characters `z ↦ (z/|z|)^k` give `arg(det)` — the compact
   `U(1)`/phase sector, which admits no nontrivial map to `ℝ`. *(runner
   S1.3: `|det|^c` is a character for all real `c`; `|tr|` is not.)*
4. **The collapse.** `F_p = |det|^p` **is** the multiplicative-character family
   of step 3: `log F_p = p·log|det| = c·log|det|` with `c = p`. So the entire
   `F_p` *function space* — the campaign's "family no principle excludes" — is
   precisely the **one-parameter scale freedom `c`** left open by the character
   theorem. *(runner S1.5.)*
5. **Product/tensor log-det additivity is internal to the P-form scope.**
   `det` is multiplicative under product (`det(AB)=det A·det B`) and,
   per-dimension-normalized, under tensor
   (`det(A⊗B)=(det A)^{dim B}(det B)^{dim A}`); taking `log|·|` gives additivity
   for the chosen product-compatible generator. This does not, by itself,
   close the parent scalar-additivity premise for the physical observable
   principle. *(runner S1.4.)* Trace is **not** a product character
   (`tr(AB)≠tr A·tr B`); it is the *infinitesimal generator*,
   `log|det(exp X)| = Re tr X`. *(runner S1.6.)*

**Status of Stage 1: proved (classical character theory + runner).** Its
single substantive premise is **composition-compatibility (P-form):** the
generator's logarithm is a homomorphism of operator-*product* composition —
the defining property of an action-like generator. P-form is distinct from the
parent tensor-extensivity/additivity premise (trace satisfies the ⊕/ensemble
law but is not a product character; `log|det|` is a product character and
⊗-additive).
**Stage 1 does not, by itself, fix `c` — it does not exclude `p ≠ 1`.** That is
Stage 2's job.

## 3. Stage 2 (SCALE): the Gibbs-expectation identity fixes `c = 1`

On the qubit algebra `Z[J] = Tr e^{-(H+J)} > 0` (self-adjoint `H, J`), define
the equilibrium expectation of `O_i = -∂(H+J)/∂J_i` with the normalized trace
state `ρ_J = e^{-(H+J)}/Z[J]`, `Tr ρ_J = 1` (no logarithm: `ρ` is an
exponential):

```
⟨O_i⟩_J := Tr(ρ_J O_i),     ∂ log Z[J] / ∂J_i = ⟨O_i⟩_J.        (★)
```

**Selector.** Require the generator to be the **potential of the expectation
field**, `∇_J W = ⟨O⟩_J`. Then:

- as a bare potential, the multiplicative scalar `Z^p` fails:
  `∂(Z^p)/∂j = p Z^p (Z'/Z) = ⟨O⟩` would force `p Z^p ≡ 1`, impossible for
  non-constant `Z`. In the additive-generator family `W=c log Z`, the same
  criterion leaves only the scale `c`, which the identity-source response
  fixes below. *(runner S2.1, symbolic; S2.2, numeric on 1- and 2-qubit Gibbs
  blocks, `max |∂ log Z/∂j − ⟨O⟩| < 1e-5`.)*
- **the scale is fixed by a single-system fact, not additivity.** Under the
  identity-coupled source `J → J + s·𝟙`, `e^{-(H+J+s𝟙)} = e^{-s}e^{-(H+J)}`, so
  `log Z(s) = log Z − s` and `∂ log Z/∂s = −1 = ⟨−𝟙⟩ = −Tr ρ = −1`. For
  `W = c·log Z`, `∂W/∂s = −c`; demanding `∂W/∂s = ⟨O⟩ = −1` gives **`c = 1`**.
  *(runner S2.3.)* The input is `⟨𝟙⟩ = Tr ρ = 1`, made canonical (not a free
  convention soaking up the scale) by the canonical normalized finite-block
  trace; the infinite-UHF uniqueness theorem is listed in §9 only as external
  mathematical context.

**Status of Stage 2: `c = 1` is fixed *given* the selector `∇W = ⟨O⟩`** (the
calibration premise; see P-cal, §5). The identity-source arithmetic itself is
a one-line calculation with **zero subsystem-additivity content**.

**Honest caveat (carried from the assumption stress-test lane, §B-7, and the

reflection-compatible (mass-like) source sector**. For `J = jI` (the hierarchy

antisymmetric). For a **generic non-self-adjoint source** that breaks
reflection symmetry, `det(D+J)` can leave the positive real axis and the `|·|`
does real work again. *(runner P2.3 exhibits a complex `det(D+J)`.)* The
general P2-vacuity claim is therefore scoped to the mass-like sector; the

## 6. The honest disagreement (the live frontier)

The lanes **agree** on §2 (Stage 1 proved), §4 (P2 dissolves on the mass-like
sector), and that the residual is the single premise P-cal. They **disagree**
on whether P-cal is a genuine escape or the parent additivity premise relocated.
This note records both readings with equal weight and adopts neither as settled.

**Skeptical reading (review panel §2 + assumption stress-test §5).** The decisive
counterexample: the *normalized* logarithmic derivative
`(1/p)·W⁻¹·∂W/∂j` of `W = Z^p` recovers `⟨O⟩` for **every** `p` — so
probability normalization `Tr ρ = 1` selects **nothing** among `{F_p}`
*(runner R.1)*. What singles out `log` is the **bare-gradient** demand
`∂W/∂j = ⟨O⟩`, and that demand is **additivity in differential form**: it is
equivalent to cross-block second-derivative vanishing
`∂²W/∂j_A∂j_B = 0`, the locality-of-source-derivatives lemma already proven
equivalent to the parent additivity premise *(runner R.2: cross-block
`∂²(log Z)=0` on independent blocks; `∂²(Z^p)≠0`)*. On this reading,
**P-cal is the parent additivity premise relocated to differential form**; the
synthesis is a strict reduction and sharper statement of the wall, not a
derivation. The same lens flags that the qubit-trace note's standalone
additivity step (log of the product `Z = Z_A Z_B`) is **circular**, because
`Z = Z_A Z_B` is the `F_p` multiplicative property for every `p` *(runner
R.3)*.

**Constructive reading (first-principles + lit/math lanes).** P-cal is
**strictly stronger than the parent additivity premise** (it also fixes the
scale `c`, which additivity leaves free), and its two ingredients are
individually log-free and independently motivated (Born-rule expectations of
the normalized trace; "the generator is an action/potential"). A requirement
stronger than the parent additivity premise is precisely what the eleven-route
campaign did not supply, since every prior route left the scale ambiguity. On
this reading the move is a better-motivated, log-free relocation and a
candidate primitive requiring explicit approval before any repo-wide adoption.

**Both readings agree on the bottom line:** the parent scalar-additivity
admission's remaining open content is exactly *"does the framework retain that
the physical scalar generator is the potential of the canonical expectation
field (i.e. `W = log Z`) rather than a multiplicative function `Z^p` of the
same forced `Z`?"* — and whether P-cal discharges that or merely names it is
not settled by this note. The variational (Klein / Gibbs–Bogoliubov) route is
**not** usable
as a selector (it embeds `log` inside `S(ρ) = −Tr ρ log ρ`, Pattern-L
circular); Tomita–Takesaki is *consistent with* `c=1` but does not *derive*
it. The single clean non-circular anchor is the exponential-forcing of the
reference state (finite-system KMS ⟺ Gibbs), which supports P-cal's premise
but not the last-mile form selection.
