## 1. The wall, restated precisely

`F_p[J] := |Z[J]|^p`, `Z[J] = det(D+J) = Tr e^{-(H+J)}`, `p ∈ ℝ∖{0}`. It is
continuous, CPT-even, positive, and **multiplicatively factorizing**
(`F_p[J_A⊕J_B] = F_p[J_A]·F_p[J_B]`) for every `p`; it is **not additive**
except in the `p→0` limit. Selecting `log` over `(·)^p` is the Cauchy
classifier `f(xy)=f(x)+f(y)+`cty`→ c·log`, which *is* additivity, which *is*
P1 (Pattern-L). Eleven prior routes all reduced to this single step.

The synthesis observation is that the wall was always attacked on the
**form/additivity** axis, where it is genuinely insurmountable, and that the
problem in fact splits into **form** (which functional class) and **scale**
(which member), each independent of the cross-subsystem Cauchy step.

## 2. Stage 1 (FORM): `F_p` collapses to one real scale `c` — proved

Worked from the axiom base `MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-05-20.md` (A1: per-site
`A_x ≅ M₂(ℂ) ≅ Cl(3,0)`; A2: sites form `Z³`), with no additivity premise.

1. **Two invariants only.** Conjugation-invariant polynomial functionals on
   `M₂(ℂ)` are generated by `tr` and `det`; Newton's identity
   `tr(M²) = (tr M)² − 2 det M` shows there is no third independent invariant.
   *(runner S1.1, 2000 random conjugations.)*
2. **det is canonical.** `det` is the action of `M` on the top exterior power
   `Λ²ℂ² ≅ ℂ`: `(Me₁)∧(Me₂) = det(M)·(e₁∧e₂)`. This singles out `det` from A1
   alone — no composition law, multiplicativity, or additivity invoked.
   *(runner S1.2, exact.)*
3. **The character theorem.** The commutator subgroup of `GL₂(ℂ)` is
   `SL₂(ℂ)`, so the abelianization is `GL₂(ℂ)/SL₂(ℂ) ≅ ℂˣ` via `det`. The
   continuous conjugation-invariant multiplicative characters
   `GL₂(ℂ) → (ℝ_{>0}, ×)` are therefore exactly `|det|^c`, `c ∈ ℝ`; their
   logarithm `c·log|det|` is the unique additive (operator-product) generator.
   The orthogonal characters `z ↦ (z/|z|)^k` give `arg(det)` — the compact
   `U(1)`/phase sector, which admits no nontrivial map to `ℝ`. *(runner
   S1.3: `|det|^c` is a character for all real `c`; `|tr|` is not.)*
4. **The collapse.** `F_p = |det|^p` **is** the multiplicative-character family
   of step 3: `log F_p = p·log|det| = c·log|det|` with `c = p`. So the entire
   `F_p` *function space* — the campaign's "family no principle excludes" — is
   precisely the **one-parameter scale freedom `c`** left open by the character
   theorem. *(runner S1.5.)*
5. **Additivity is a theorem, not a premise.** `det` is multiplicative under
   product (`det(AB)=det A·det B`) and, per-dimension-normalized, under tensor
   (`det(A⊗B)=(det A)^{dim B}(det B)^{dim A}`); taking `log|·|` gives subsystem
   additivity (P1) for free. *(runner S1.4.)* Trace is **not** a product
   character (`tr(AB)≠tr A·tr B`); it is the *infinitesimal generator*,
   `log|det(exp X)| = Re tr X`. *(runner S1.6.)*

**Status of Stage 1: proved (classical character theory + runner).** Its
single substantive premise is **composition-compatibility (P-form):** the
generator's logarithm is a homomorphism of operator-*product* composition —
the defining property of an action-like generator. P-form is *distinct from
and weaker than* tensor-extensivity/P1 (trace satisfies the ⊕/ensemble law but
is not a product character; `log|det|` is a product character and ⊗-additive).
**Stage 1 does not, by itself, fix `c` — it does not exclude `p ≠ 1`.** That is
Stage 2's job.

## 3. Stage 2 (SCALE): the Gibbs-expectation identity fixes `c = 1`

On the qubit algebra `Z[J] = Tr e^{-(H+J)} > 0` (self-adjoint `H, J`), define
the equilibrium expectation of `O_i = -∂(H+J)/∂J_i` with the **unique** trace
state `ρ_J = e^{-(H+J)}/Z[J]`, `Tr ρ_J = 1` (no logarithm: `ρ` is an
exponential):

```
⟨O_i⟩_J := Tr(ρ_J O_i),     ∂ log Z[J] / ∂J_i = ⟨O_i⟩_J.        (★)
```

**Selector.** Require the generator to be the **potential of the expectation
field**, `∇_J W = ⟨O⟩_J`. Then:

- only `W = log Z` satisfies it: `∂(Z^p)/∂j = p Z^p (Z'/Z) = ⟨O⟩` would force
  `p Z^p ≡ 1`, impossible for non-constant `Z`; every `F_{p≠1}` is excluded.
  *(runner S2.1, symbolic; S2.2, numeric on 1- and 2-qubit Gibbs blocks,
  `max |∂ log Z/∂j − ⟨O⟩| < 1e-5`.)*
- **the scale is fixed by a single-system fact, not additivity.** Under the
  identity-coupled source `J → J + s·𝟙`, `e^{-(H+J+s𝟙)} = e^{-s}e^{-(H+J)}`, so
  `log Z(s) = log Z − s` and `∂ log Z/∂s = −1 = ⟨−𝟙⟩ = −Tr ρ = −1`. For
  `W = c·log Z`, `∂W/∂s = −c`; demanding `∂W/∂s = ⟨O⟩ = −1` gives **`c = 1`**.
  *(runner S2.3.)* The input is `⟨𝟙⟩ = Tr ρ = 1`, made canonical (not a free
  convention soaking up the scale) by the **unique trace** on the UHF algebra
  `⊗_x M₂(ℂ)` (Murray–von Neumann; standard UHF theory).

**Status of Stage 2: `c = 1` is fixed *given* the selector `∇W = ⟨O⟩`** (the
calibration premise; see P-cal, §5). The identity-source arithmetic itself is
a one-line calculation with **zero subsystem-additivity content**.

## 4. P2 (phase-blindness) dissolves — with an honest source-class caveat

In the positive-trace formulation `Z = Tr e^{-(H+J)}` (self-adjoint `H, J`),
`Z` is **manifestly real-positive** (trace of a positive operator), so there
is **no phase to be blind to**: P2 becomes *vacuous*, not relocated. *(runner
P2.1: `Z>0` on 200 Hermitian samples; P2.2: `det(D+jI)=det(D−jI)` for real
anti-Hermitian `D`, so `|det|` is even.)* Equivalently, the whole `F_p` family
is already phase-blind, so P2 never selected anything among `{F_p}` — all the
selection work was always in the form/scale (P1/P4), never P2.

**Honest caveat (carried verbatim from the assumptions audit, §B-7, and the
panel, §1).** The dissolution is verified **only on the self-adjoint /
reflection-compatible (mass-like) source sector**. For `J = jI` (the hierarchy
application) and real-symmetric mass-like sources, positivity provably
survives (`det(S+A) > 0` for `S` symmetric-positive-definite, `A`
antisymmetric). For a **generic non-self-adjoint source** that breaks
reflection symmetry, `det(D+J)` can leave the positive real axis and the `|·|`
does real work again. *(runner P2.3 exhibits a complex `det(D+J)`.)* The
general P2-vacuity claim is therefore scoped to the mass-like sector; the
even-`L_t`, antiperiodic transfer-matrix slice that the parent's `L_t=4`
selector uses is inside that sector.

## 6. The honest disagreement (the live frontier)

The lanes **agree** on §2 (Stage 1 proved), §4 (P2 dissolves on the mass-like
sector), and that the residual is the single premise P-cal. They **disagree**
on whether P-cal is a genuine escape or P1 relocated. This note records both
readings with equal weight and adopts neither as settled.

**Skeptical reading (panel §2 + assumptions audit §5).** The decisive
counterexample: the *normalized* logarithmic derivative
`(1/p)·W⁻¹·∂W/∂j` of `W = Z^p` recovers `⟨O⟩` for **every** `p` — so
probability normalization `Tr ρ = 1` selects **nothing** among `{F_p}`
*(runner R.1)*. What singles out `log` is the **bare-gradient** demand
`∂W/∂j = ⟨O⟩`, and that demand is **additivity in differential form**: it is
equivalent to cross-block second-derivative vanishing
`∂²W/∂j_A∂j_B = 0`, the locality-of-source-derivatives lemma already proven
equivalent to P1 *(runner R.2: cross-block `∂²(log Z)=0` on independent
blocks; `∂²(Z^p)≠0`)*. On this reading, **P-cal is P1 relocated to differential
form**; the synthesis is a *strict reduction and sharper statement* of the
wall, not a derivation. The same lens flags that the qubit-trace note's
standalone P1 step (log of the product `Z = Z_A Z_B`) is **circular**, because
`Z = Z_A Z_B` is the `F_p` multiplicative property for every `p` *(runner
R.3)*.

**Constructive reading (first-principles + lit/math lanes).** P-cal is
**strictly stronger than P1** (it also fixes the scale `c`, which P1 leaves
free — exactly the "`c=1`" the Tomita-Gibbs no-go bracketed out and never
derived), and its two ingredients are *individually log-free and independently
motivated* (Born-rule expectations of the unique trace; "the generator is an
action/potential"). A requirement *stronger* than P1 is precisely what the
eleven-route campaign never found, since every prior route was *no stronger
than* P1 and so left the scale ambiguity. On this reading the move is a
**better-motivated, log-free relocation** — arguably a Path-(a) primitive.

**Both readings agree on the bottom line:** P1's remaining open content is
exactly *"does the framework retain that the physical scalar generator is the
potential of the canonical expectation field (i.e. `W = log Z`) rather than a
multiplicative function `Z^p` of the same forced `Z`?"* — and whether P-cal
discharges that or merely names it is **the audit lane's call, not this
note's**. The variational (Klein / Gibbs–Bogoliubov) route is **not** usable
as a selector (it embeds `log` inside `S(ρ) = −Tr ρ log ρ`, Pattern-L
circular); Tomita–Takesaki is *consistent with* `c=1` but does not *derive*
it. The single clean non-circular anchor is the exponential-forcing of the
reference state (finite-system KMS ⟺ Gibbs), which supports P-cal's premise
but not the last-mile form selection.
