### Exercise 2: Elon first-principles

Strip to first principles: WHY would free cumulants matter for a Z³
lattice framework where disjoint regions are physically commuting?

**First principle 1: Voiculescu's freeness is built on the FREE PRODUCT
of algebras.** If `A_1`, `A_2` are unital \*-algebras, the free product
`A_1 * A_2` is the algebra of formal words in elements of `A_1` and
`A_2` with no commutation relations imposed between elements of `A_1`
and elements of `A_2`. The defining example is the free-Gaussian / Wigner
semicircular: two free-Gaussians `s_1, s_2` satisfy `[s_1, s_2] ≠ 0`
generically (they are non-commuting), and their joint distribution is
NOT the product distribution — it is the free-product distribution. The
free convolution `μ_A ⊞ μ_B` is the spectral measure of `a + b` for
freely-independent `a, b`; it is NOT the classical convolution `μ_A * μ_B`,
and it is NOT the convex combination `(μ_A + μ_B) / 2`.

**First principle 2: The direct-sum construction `D = D_A ⊕ D_B` lives
on a direct-sum Hilbert space `H_A ⊕ H_B`, NOT on a free-product algebra.**
The C\* algebra of operators on `H_A ⊕ H_B` factorizes as
`B(H_A) ⊕ B(H_B)` (block-diagonal). The two embedded sub-algebras
`B(H_A) ⊕ 0` and `0 ⊕ B(H_B)` are MUTUALLY COMMUTING (their product
is zero from both sides), so they are NOT a free product. The spectral
measure of `D = D_A ⊕ D_B` is the convex combination
`(d_A / d) · μ_A + (d_B / d) · μ_B` where `d_A = dim H_A`, `d_B = dim H_B`,
`d = d_A + d_B` — NOT the free convolution.

**First principle 3: Classical cumulants are additive on commuting
independent variables.** If `X, Y` are commuting independent random
variables on a probability space, the cumulant generating function
`K_{X+Y}(t) = log E[e^{t(X+Y)}] = log(E[e^{tX}] E[e^{tY}]) = K_X(t) + K_Y(t)`,
so all classical cumulants are additive: `κ_n(X+Y) = κ_n(X) + κ_n(Y)`.
This is the COMMUTING analog of Voiculescu's free-cumulant additivity.

**Structural conclusion (Exercise 2 finding):** for the framework's
direct-sum (= commuting) independence, the RIGHT cumulant tool is
classical cumulants of a log-MGF, NOT Voiculescu free cumulants. The
free-cumulant route is solving the wrong problem.

The relevant question becomes: **can `log|det(D + J)|` be identified
with a log-MGF `K(J) = log E[e^{J · X}]` on some probabilistically
interpretable spectral measure?** Answer (Exercise 4 below): there is a
natural identification via the spectral measure of `D`, but the
additivity step on direct-sum spectral measures is again the
elementary block-diagonal determinant identity `det(A ⊕ B) = det(A) det(B)`
— no probabilistic machinery is load-bearing.

### Exercise 3: Literature search

Three+ real published references on the free-probability cumulant route
and adjacent classical-cumulant theory:

1. **D. V. Voiculescu, "Addition of certain non-commuting random
   variables", J. Funct. Anal. 66 (1986), 323-346.** Original free
   convolution paper; introduces the additivity of free cumulants on
   freely-independent variables. Defines the `R`-transform
   `R_a(z) = sum_n κ_n^free(a) z^{n-1}` such that
   `R_{a+b}(z) = R_a(z) + R_b(z)` on free `a, b`.

2. **R. Speicher, "Multiplicative functions on the lattice of
   non-crossing partitions and free convolution", Math. Ann. 298 (1994),
   611-628.** Combinatorial moment-cumulant formula for free cumulants
   in terms of non-crossing partitions
   `κ_n^free(a_1, ..., a_n) = sum_{π ∈ NC(n)} μ(π, 1_n) · M_π(a_1, ..., a_n)`,
   where `NC(n)` is the lattice of non-crossing partitions of `{1, ..., n}`
   and `μ` is the Mobius function of this lattice. This is the non-
   commutative analog of the classical cumulant-moment formula
   `κ_n(X) = sum_{π ∈ P(n)} μ(π, 1_n) · M_π(X)` over ALL partitions
   `P(n)`. The difference (free uses `NC`, classical uses `P`) IS the
   structural difference between freeness and classical independence.

3. **D. V. Voiculescu, K. J. Dykema, A. Nica, "Free Random Variables",
   CRM Monograph Series 1, AMS (1992).** Textbook treatment. §1.6
   distinguishes the four "universal products" (classical / Boolean /
   monotone / free) for non-commutative independence; classical and
   free are the two extremal commuting / non-commuting cases.

4. **N. Muraki, "The five independences as natural products", Infin.
   Dimens. Anal. Quantum Probab. Relat. Top. 6 (2003), 337-371.**
   Classification theorem: there are exactly FIVE universal products
   (classical / Boolean / monotone / anti-monotone / free) on the
   category of non-commutative probability spaces. These correspond
   to different sub-lattices of partitions (`P`, interval `I`, monotone
   `M`, etc.). For the framework's direct-sum commuting independence,
   the relevant universal product is CLASSICAL, not free.

5. **R. Speicher, "On universal products", Fields Inst. Commun. 12
   (1997), 257-266.** Classification proof of the five universal
   products. Confirms that classical (commuting) independence and free
   (non-commuting) independence are structurally distinct universal
   products with different cumulant theories.

6. **P. Billingsley, "Probability and Measure", 3rd ed., Wiley (1995),
   §27 (Cumulants and the CGF).** Standard reference for classical
   cumulants: for commuting independent `X, Y`,
   `κ_n(X + Y) = κ_n(X) + κ_n(Y)` follows from `K_{X+Y}(t) = K_X(t) + K_Y(t)`.

7. **W. Feller, "An Introduction to Probability Theory and Its
   Applications", Vol. II, Wiley (1971), §V.3 (Moment generating
   functions).** Classical MGF: `E[e^{t(X+Y)}] = E[e^{tX}] E[e^{tY}]`
   for independent `X, Y` is the multiplicative property; taking `log`
   gives the additive cumulant generating function.

Collectively these citations establish:

- Voiculescu's free cumulants are the right additivity tool for
  free-product (non-commutative) independence (Voiculescu 1986, Speicher
  1994);
- The five-universal-products classification (Muraki 2003, Speicher 1997)
  proves that classical and free independence are structurally distinct
  notions with different cumulant theories;
- Classical cumulants of the log-MGF are the right additivity tool for
  commuting independence (Billingsley §27, Feller Vol. II §V.3);
- For the framework's direct-sum (= commuting) independence on disjoint
  lattice patches, the relevant universal product is CLASSICAL, not
  free.

### Exercise 4: Math search (Tao-style)

**Pose.** Let `A` be a self-adjoint matrix on a finite-dimensional
Hilbert space `H_A`, `B` self-adjoint on `H_B`, and let
`D = A ⊕ B` on `H_A ⊕ H_B`. Is `log|det(D)| = log|det(A)| + log|det(B)|`
immediate, and does this rely on any non-trivial probability theory or
just elementary linear algebra?

**Answer (compact).** It is elementary linear algebra. Choose
orthonormal bases `e_1, ..., e_{n_A}` for `H_A` and `f_1, ..., f_{n_B}`
for `H_B`; the matrix of `D = A ⊕ B` in the concatenated basis
`(e_1, ..., e_{n_A}, f_1, ..., f_{n_B})` is the block-diagonal matrix
`[[A, 0], [0, B]]`. The determinant of a block-diagonal matrix equals
the product of the block determinants by Laplace expansion (or by
upper-triangularizing each block via row operations within its own block,
both of which leave the other block's rows untouched):

```text
det([[A, 0], [0, B]]) = det(A) · det(B).                                  (D)
```

This identity is exact over any field; it does not depend on the
matrices being self-adjoint, real, complex, or anything else. Taking
absolute values:

```text
|det(D)| = |det(A) · det(B)| = |det(A)| · |det(B)|.                       (M)
```

Taking real logarithms (assuming both determinants are non-zero so the
logs are defined):

```text
log|det(D)| = log|det(A)| + log|det(B)|.                                  (L)
```

No probability theory is invoked. No measure. No expectation. No
cumulant. This is literally what every first-year linear algebra
textbook proves about block-diagonal determinants.

**Conclusion (Exercise 4 finding).** The P1 multiplicative factorization
step on the framework's direct-sum substrate is elementary linear
algebra. Free-probability cumulants are NOT load-bearing for it.
Classical cumulants are NOT load-bearing for it. The honest landing
class is therefore a `bounded_theorem` that records this elementary
identity as the Class-A load-bearing step, plus the honest external
admission that free probability is the wrong tool for direct-sum
independence and classical cumulants are the right tool but do not
themselves derive what makes the physical scalar generator "additive"
rather than "multiplicatively factorizing on its modulus".

The honest finding is that Voiculescu's free-cumulant additivity is
the right additivity tool for a DIFFERENT notion of independence
(free-product, non-commutative) than the framework's notion
(direct-sum, commuting). Selecting the right tool for the framework's
notion gives elementary block-diagonal determinant factorization, not
free probability.

## 3. Load-bearing step (Class A)

The note's load-bearing step is the elementary block-diagonal
determinant factorization, recorded as Class A (symbolic-Fraction
verified via `sympy`):

> **Lemma (block-diagonal determinant factorization, elementary).**
> Let `R` be a commutative ring, `A` an `n_A × n_A` matrix and `B`
> an `n_B × n_B` matrix with entries in `R`. Then
> `det(A ⊕ B) = det(A) · det(B)` in `R`, where `A ⊕ B` denotes the
> block-diagonal `(n_A + n_B) × (n_A + n_B)` matrix
> `[[A, 0], [0, B]]`.

Proof sketch: Laplace expansion along the first `n_A` columns gives
`det(A ⊕ B) = sum_{σ} sgn(σ) prod_i A_{i, σ(i)} · det(B)` where the
sum is over permutations `σ` of `{1, ..., n_A}` (because any column
beyond the first `n_A` has zero entries in the first `n_A` rows). The
inner sum is `det(A)` by definition. QED.

Apply to `D = D_A ⊕ D_B`, `J = J_A ⊕ J_B`, so that
`D + J = (D_A + J_A) ⊕ (D_B + J_B)`. The lemma gives

```text
det(D + J) = det(D_A + J_A) · det(D_B + J_B).                             (Z)
```

Taking absolute values (working over `R` or `C`):

```text
|det(D + J)| = |det(D_A + J_A)| · |det(D_B + J_B)|.                       (R)
```

Taking real logarithms (assuming `D_A + J_A` and `D_B + J_B` are
invertible, which is the regularity condition of the parent note's P3
checked by its runner-local Part 7):

```text
log|det(D + J)| = log|det(D_A + J_A)| + log|det(D_B + J_B)|.              (W)
```

The runner verifies this on a symbolic 4×4 block-diagonal `D` plus
several numerical rational instances at exact `Fraction` precision.

**Status of free-cumulant content in this note.** Voiculescu / Speicher
free-cumulant additivity is recorded as Layer-1 external content (cited
classification theorem in §1 Exercise 3 references) and exhibited
symbolically by the runner on a minimal example, but it is NOT
load-bearing on the framework's substrate. The runner checks (a) that
free-cumulant additivity on free-product variables is consistent with
the Voiculescu-Speicher statement, (b) that the framework's
direct-sum spectral measure is the convex combination, NOT the free
convolution, and (c) that the elementary block-diagonal determinant
identity (the load-bearing step) holds at exact `Fraction` precision.

## 9. Honest verdict

**Outcome class: `bounded_theorem`. P1 is NOT closed positively by the
free-probability cumulant route.**

The honest structural finding is that Voiculescu's free-probability
cumulant theory is the right additivity tool for FREE-PRODUCT
(non-commutative) independence, which is structurally DIFFERENT from
the framework's DIRECT-SUM (commuting) independence on `D = D_A ⊕ D_B`.
Free convolution `μ_A ⊞ μ_B` is not the spectral measure of a
direct sum (which is the convex combination), and free-cumulant
additivity therefore does not apply to the framework's substrate at
all.

The correctly-applicable tool on the framework substrate is the
elementary block-diagonal determinant factorization
`det(A ⊕ B) = det(A) · det(B)`, which is Class-A linear algebra. This
identity closes the multiplicative factorization step
`|Z[J_A ⊕ J_B]| = |Z_A[J_A]| · |Z_B[J_B]|` cleanly with no probability
theory required, but it does NOT close P1 in its strong sense
(additivity of `W`, not merely multiplicative factorization of `|Z|`):
the `F_p[J] = r(J)^p` counterexample family continues to
satisfy multiplicative factorization on the framework substrate, and
selecting `p = 0` (`log r`) from this family is the SAME classification
choice as P1 in different vocabulary.

(consolidated Route D + Tempesta composability) to 7 scaffold classes

open route on the no-go side, not on the closure side.
