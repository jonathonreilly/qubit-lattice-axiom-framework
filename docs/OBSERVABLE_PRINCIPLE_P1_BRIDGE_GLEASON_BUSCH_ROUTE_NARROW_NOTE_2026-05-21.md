### 1.2 Exercise 2 — Elon Musk first-principles

Strip the problem to first principles. What is "scalar additivity on
independent subsystems" really asking?

**The asking.** P1 is the assertion that the physical scalar bosonic
observable generator `W: U_0 → R` for the framework's source-deformed
Dirac operator `D[J] = D + J` satisfies

```text
W[J_A ⊕ J_B] = W[J_A] + W[J_B]
```

on independent direct-sum decompositions `D = D_A ⊕ D_B`,
`J = J_A ⊕ J_B` with no hopping bonds across the partition. The
"additivity" here is **real addition in (R, +)** applied pointwise to
a real-valued scalar functional, NOT lattice join in `vN(H)` or
direct-sum-of-vector-spaces additivity. The "independence" is
**Grassmann block direct sum** of the lattice Dirac operator, NOT
spatial separation in a continuum spacetime.

**Why Gleason might force it.** The Gleason-Busch route's hope: the
joint qubit register `H_Λ = ⊗_x C^2` carries a Gleason-type
classification of all state functionals as `p(E) = Tr(σ E)` for a
unique density matrix `σ`. On product effects `E = E_A ⊗ E_B` and the
product state `σ = σ_A ⊗ σ_B`, the trace factorizes:

```text
Tr((σ_A ⊗ σ_B)(E_A ⊗ E_B)) = Tr(σ_A E_A) · Tr(σ_B E_B).
```

So Gleason **does force multiplicative factorization of `p`** on
product effects under product states. The hope is that this
multiplicative factorization on `p` translates to **additive
factorization on `W = log Z`** when `Z` is identified with the
Gleason-Busch state-functional value on the source-deformed Dirac
operator's projector spectrum.

**Why this hope fails — the deep structural reason.** Three distinct
failures:

1. **(G1)** The Gleason-Busch theorem **classifies** the additive

   non-additive primitives. Every published Gleason-type proof

   takes σ-additivity of the state functional on POVM effects as
   an explicit hypothesis input. This is structurally analogous to

2. **(G2)** Even granting additivity, the framework's qubit-per-site

   unentangled-Gleason theorem (Wallach 2000) requires **each factor

   `⊗_x C^2` form a **strictly larger** class than density operators,
   so even the multiplicative factorization on product effects does
   not uniquely pin `σ` — Wallach's theorem fails in dim 2.

3. **(G3)** Even if `σ` were uniquely pinned, going from

### 1.3 Exercise 3 — Literature search

Searched for prior work on:

- **Gleason's theorem on tensor product Hilbert spaces.** Wallach
  2000 "An Unentangled Gleason's Theorem" (arXiv:quant-ph/0002058,
  Contemp. Math. 305, AMS 2002) is the canonical reference. It
  generalizes Gleason to unentangled frame functions on multipartite
  Hilbert spaces. **Critical finding**: for multipartite systems
  where each subsystem dim ≥ 3, unentangled frame functions are
  uniquely density operators; **if any subsystem has dim 2, this is
  not necessarily the case**. Pitowsky 2002 (LMP) extends this in
  *On unentangled Gleason theorems for quantum information theory*
  (arXiv:quant-ph/0004036).

- **Additivity of state functionals on quantum tensor products.**
  Wright PhD thesis 2019 (Univ. of York, *Gleason-type Theorems and
  General Probabilistic Theories*) systematically surveys
  Gleason-type theorems including the tensor-product extensions and
  the Cauchy-functional-equation reformulation. The Cauchy form is
  Wright-Weigert 2019, Found. Phys. 49, 594-606 (arXiv:1905.12751):
  *"Gleason-Type Theorems from Cauchy's Functional Equation"*.
  **Critical finding**: the Cauchy reformulation makes explicit that
  the Gleason classification is **structurally the same** as the
  Cauchy log functional equation classifier — i.e., additivity is
  the load-bearing hypothesis input, not the derived conclusion.

- **Whether Gleason implies additivity on disjoint POVM effects.**
  Busch 2003 PRL 91, 120403 (*Quantum States and Generalized
  Observables: A Simple Proof of Gleason's Theorem*,
  arXiv:quant-ph/9909073) extends Gleason to POVMs (dim ≥ 2). The
  Busch additivity hypothesis is on **POVM effects**, which is
  **stronger** than the projector additivity of Gleason 1957. The
  CFMR paper (Caves, Fuchs, Manne, Renes 2004, *Gleason-Type
  Derivations of the Quantum Probability Rule for Generalized
  Measurements*, arXiv:quant-ph/0306179, Found. Phys. 34, 193-209)
  provides a simpler proof. **Critical finding**: Busch's stronger
  additivity hypothesis is necessary for the dim-2 case; without it,
  Gleason 1957 is silent on dim-2 systems.

- **Counter-examples or sharpenings.** Fiorentino-Weigert 2025
  (arXiv:2511.15607, *Gleason's Theorem for a Qubit as Part of a
  Composite System*) explicitly studies the qubit-in-composite case
  and finds that the tensor-product structure plus additional
  no-signaling conditions can rescue dim-2 Gleason, but the
  additivity hypothesis is still required as input. The 2026 paper
  *"Summing to Uncertainty: On the Necessity of Additivity in
  Deriving the Born Rule"* (arXiv:2603.06211) is the decisive
  reference: it formally surveys five major Born-rule derivations
  (Gleason, Busch, Deutsch, Zurek, Hartle) and concludes
  *"additivity cannot be derived from non-contextuality or
  normalization. There is no equivalent assumption that can
  substitute for it"*. This is the structural confirmation of G1.

**Three real citations with publication-quality details:**

1. N. Wallach (2000), *An Unentangled Gleason's Theorem*, Contemporary
   Mathematics 305 (Amer. Math. Soc., 2002), 291-298. arXiv:
   [quant-ph/0002058](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0002058). Key
   theorem: unentangled frame functions on `H_1 ⊗ ... ⊗ H_n` are
   uniquely density operators iff each `dim(H_i) ≥ 3`. Fails for
   any factor at dim 2.

2. P. Busch (2003), *Quantum States and Generalized Observables: A
   Simple Proof of Gleason's Theorem*, Phys. Rev. Lett. 91, 120403.
   arXiv: [quant-ph/9909073](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9909073).
   Key theorem: POVM σ-additivity (stronger than Gleason 1957
   projector additivity) plus normalization implies `p(E) = Tr(σ E)`
   for all dim ≥ 2.

3. V. J. Wright, S. Weigert (2019), *Gleason-Type Theorems from
   Cauchy's Functional Equation*, Found. Phys. 49, 594-606. arXiv:
   [1905.12751](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.12751). Key reformulation:
   the Gleason classification is structurally the multiplicative-to-
   additive Cauchy classifier; additivity over POVM effects is the
   load-bearing hypothesis input.

The published math results:

- **Affirmative for dim ≥ 3 each factor** (Wallach 2000): if
  `μ` is an unentangled frame function on `H_1 ⊗ ... ⊗ H_n` with
  each `dim(H_i) ≥ 3`, then `μ` extends uniquely to a Gleason
  measure on `H_1 ⊗ ... ⊗ H_n`, hence has the form
  `μ(P) = Tr(ρ P)` for a unique density matrix `ρ`. For product
  projectors `P_A ⊗ P_B` under product state `ρ = ρ_A ⊗ ρ_B`,
  factorization holds:
  `μ(P_A ⊗ P_B) = Tr((ρ_A ⊗ ρ_B)(P_A ⊗ P_B)) = Tr(ρ_A P_A) ·
  Tr(ρ_B P_B) = μ_A(P_A) · μ_B(P_B)`.

- **Negative for any factor at dim 2** (Wallach 2000 sharpened):
  the unentangled frame functions on `⊗_i H_i` with some
  `dim(H_i) = 2` form a strictly larger class than density operators.
  Concrete witness: see Fiorentino-Weigert 2025 (arXiv:2511.15607)
  Section 4 for explicit qubit examples; Pitowsky 2002 Section 3 for

  geometry admits frame functions that are not affine on projectors,
  hence not density matrix traces).

  the Gleason classification is reformulated as: any continuous

  `f(P_1 + P_2) = f(P_1) + f(P_2)` on orthogonal projectors (the
  additivity hypothesis) is forced to be `f(P) = Tr(ρ P)`. The
  proof goes through the Cauchy log functional equation. The
  additivity hypothesis is the load-bearing input.
