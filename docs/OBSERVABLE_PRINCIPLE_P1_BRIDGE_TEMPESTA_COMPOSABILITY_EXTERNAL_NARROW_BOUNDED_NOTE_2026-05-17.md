## 1. External classification theorems

### 1.1 Tempesta composability axiom (arXiv:1407.3807)

Tempesta (arXiv:1407.3807, 2014; "Beyond Shannon-Khinchin: Composability
Axiom and Universal Group Entropy") replaces the Shannon-Khinchin K3
chain rule with a **composability axiom**: for any two statistically
independent systems `A` and `B`, the scalar generator `W` of the
composed system satisfies

```text
W(A ⊔ B)  =  G(W(A), W(B); λ),                                          (C)
```

where `G: R × R → R` is a (formal) group law with composition parameter
`λ` (which may itself be a sequence of coefficients), and `G` satisfies
the formal-group-law axioms:

(G1) **Left/right identity:** `G(x, 0) = x = G(0, x)`.

(G2) **Associativity:** `G(G(x, y), z) = G(x, G(y, z))`.

(G3) **Commutativity:** `G(x, y) = G(y, x)` (for the Abelian case
relevant to independent statistical systems).

The Boltzmann-Gibbs entropy corresponds to the additive formal group
`G_a(x, y) = x + y` and recovers Shannon-Khinchin K3 (chain rule).
Tsallis q-entropy corresponds to the multiplicative-shifted formal group
`G_q(x, y) = x + y + (1 - q) x y` and recovers non-extensive
statistical mechanics for `q ≠ 1`. Rényi α-entropy is additive
(corresponds to `G_a` after an internal logarithm). Sharma-Mittal is a
two-parameter family. **Every Lazard-admissible formal group law gives
rise to a composable scalar generator (a Z-entropy in Tempesta's
classification).**

### 1.2 Tempesta Z-entropy construction (arXiv:1507.07436)

Tempesta (arXiv:1507.07436, 2015; "Formal Groups and Z-Entropies")
constructs explicitly, for each commutative one-dimensional formal group
law `G` over a coefficient ring containing `Q`, a Z-entropy
`Z_G(p) = (a function of the distribution `p` constructed from `G`)`
satisfying the composability axiom (C) with composition law `G`. The
classification produces:

- **G_a (additive)**: Boltzmann-Gibbs `H_BG = -k_B sum_i p_i log p_i`.
- **G_q (multiplicative-shifted)**: Tsallis `S_q = (1 - sum_i p_i^q) / (q - 1)`.
- **Sharma-Mittal**: two-parameter generalization of Rényi and Tsallis.
- **Z-entropy continuum**: infinitely many additional admissible
  scalars indexed by the Lazard ring `L = Z[a_1, a_2, ...]`.

Each is composable on independent subsystems with its respective
formal group law. None is uniquely selected by composability alone;
selecting one over the others requires an additional principle
(extensivity, thermodynamic limit, or a specific identification of the
physical scalar generator with the additive subclass).

### 1.3 Lazard's theorem on commutative one-dimensional formal group laws

Lazard 1955 ("Sur les groupes de Lie formels à un paramètre", Bull.
Soc. Math. France 83, 251-274) and Cartier (*Geometry of Formal
Groups*) classify commutative one-dimensional formal group laws:

(L1) **Universal formal group:** Over the Lazard ring
`L = Z[a_1, a_2, ...]` (free polynomial ring on countably many
generators with explicit relations enforcing G1-G3), there exists a
universal formal group law `G_univ(x, y) ∈ L[[x, y]]` such that every
commutative one-dimensional formal group law over any ring `R` is the
image of a unique ring homomorphism `L → R`.

(L2) **Q-isomorphism to additive:** Over a `Q`-algebra `R`, every
commutative one-dimensional formal group law `G` over `R` is isomorphic
to the additive formal group `G_a(x, y) = x + y` via a **unique**
formal change of variables `log_G: G → G_a` (the **logarithm of the
formal group law**), satisfying

```text
log_G(G(x, y))  =  log_G(x) + log_G(y).                                  (L)
```

(L3) **Multiplicative formal group:**
`G_m(x, y) = x + y + x y = (1 + x)(1 + y) - 1`.
Its logarithm is `log_{G_m}(x) = log(1 + x) = x - x^2/2 + x^3/3 - ...`.

(L4) **Tsallis formal group:**
`G_q(x, y) = x + y + (1 - q) x y`.
For `q ≠ 1`, this is isomorphic to `G_m` via the rescaling
`x → (1 - q) x`. Its logarithm is the **Tsallis q-logarithm**
`log_q(1 + (1-q)x) / (1 - q)`.

### 1.4 What these classification theorems collectively give

Composability (C) plus a chosen formal group law `G` together specify
a unique Z-entropy class. **No single Lazard-admissible formal group
law is uniquely selected by composability alone.** Selecting `G_a`
(additive, Boltzmann-Gibbs) over `G_m` (multiplicative, `F_p = r^p`)
over `G_q` (Tsallis) over any other Lazard ring image is **the same
classification choice as P1**, expressed in formal-group-law
vocabulary. Composability is a strictly **weaker** scaffold than
Shannon-Khinchin K3: K3 forces `G_a` by the chain-rule additivity
hypothesis; composability admits the entire Lazard ring image.
