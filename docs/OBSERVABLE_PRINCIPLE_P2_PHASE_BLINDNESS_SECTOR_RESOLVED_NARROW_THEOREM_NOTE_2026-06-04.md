## 2. Load-bearing step (Class A) — the compact-phase lemma

The standard structural fact, stated for completeness and reproven in the runner
(Part A):

> **Lemma (the phase carries no continuous single-valued real readout).** Under
> the polar isomorphism of topological groups `C* ≅ R_{>0} × U(1)`, every
> continuous homomorphism `φ : (C*, ×) → (R, +)` satisfies `φ|_{U(1)} ≡ 0`, and
> hence `φ(r e^{iθ}) = c · log r = c · log|z|`. Equivalently: a continuous
> additive `g(θ) = c·θ` on the phase, required to be single-valued on `U(1)`
> (so `g(θ) = g(θ + 2π)`), forces `c = 0`; and `arg` is only `R/2πZ`-valued, so
> it is not a single-valued real homomorphism.

This is textbook Pontryagin/character theory: the dual of the compact circle
`U(1)` is the **discrete** group `Z` (characters land in `U(1)`, not in the
non-compact `(R, +)`), and a compact group has no nontrivial continuous
homomorphism to `(R, +)` because its image would be a compact subgroup of
`(R, +)`, i.e. `{0}`. References:

- E. Hewitt & K. A. Ross, *Abstract Harmonic Analysis*, Vol. I, Springer
  Grundlehren 115 (2nd ed. 1979), §23–§24 (LCA structure/duality: `\hat{T} = Z`,
  `\hat{R} = R`);
- W. Rudin, *Fourier Analysis on Groups*, Wiley (1962), Ch. 1–2 (the dual group;
  compact ⟹ discrete dual);
- the elementary self-contained fact: a continuous homomorphism from a compact
  group into `(R, +)` is trivial (compact image in `(R,+)` is `{0}`).

The last-mile `R_{>0} → R` step (after the phase is killed) is the Cauchy
classifier `f(xy) = f(x) + f(y) ⟹ f = c·log` — **this is the Record-supplied
additivity/exponent content, not re-derived here** (J. Aczél, *Lectures on
Functional Equations and Their Applications*, 1966, §2.1 Thm 1; the same
comparator the form-selection note cites). The runner reproves the additive
solution, the single-valuedness `⟹ c = 0` step, the `R/2πZ`-valuedness of `arg`,
and the phase-blindness of `c·log|z|`.

**Prior statement.** The compact-`U(1)`-admits-no-map-to-`R` fact is already in
`OBSERVABLE_PRINCIPLE_P1P2_TWO_STAGE_SYNTHESIS_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-28.md`
§2 step 3. It is reproduced here only as one leg of the residual analysis and is
not claimed as new content.

## 3. Load-bearing Step (Class A) — Determinant Positivity on the Mass-Like Sector

On the self-adjoint / mass-like source sector the amplitude has **no phase to
begin with**:

> **Fact.** For the free staggered operator, `M_KS` is real and
> antisymmetric (real `η` phases, antisymmetric forward–backward difference), so
> its eigenvalues come in conjugate pairs `± iλ`, and for a real mass `m > 0`
> `det(M_KS + mI) = ∏ (m² + λ²) ∈ R_{>0}`.

This is the determinant-positivity theorem
[`STAGGERED_ONLY_DET_POSITIVITY_CASE_A_NOTE_2026-05-17.md`](STAGGERED_ONLY_DET_POSITIVITY_CASE_A_NOTE_2026-05-17.md)
(`det(M_KS + mI) ≥ m^n > 0` config-by-config). The runner reproves the reality
mechanism symbolically (`n = 2, 4` antisymmetric: `det = ∏(m²+λ²)`) and
numerically (200 real-antisymmetric `6×6` seeds: `det` real to `1e-9`, positive),
plus an `ε`-grading cross-check (`{ε, A} = 0` inherits the `λ → −λ` pairing so the
real-positive-det-plus-mass property survives complex entries with the grading).
On this sector `arg det = 0`, so the readout depends on `|det| = det` trivially:
**P2 is automatic, and the compactness lemma of §2 is not needed.**

A generic complex *anti-Hermitian* matrix has **unpaired** imaginary eigenvalues
and would **not** give a real determinant; the `± iλ` pairing requires the real /
`ε`-graded structure that `M_KS` has. The runner records this so the reality
claim is not over-stated.
