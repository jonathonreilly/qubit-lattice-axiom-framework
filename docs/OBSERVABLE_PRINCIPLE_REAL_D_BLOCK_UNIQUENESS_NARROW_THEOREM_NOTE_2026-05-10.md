## Statement

Let `D` be an invertible real anti-Hermitian operator on a finite-dimensional complex
Hilbert space `V = C^N` (with `N` even), so that `D^T = -D` and `D` has
real entries. Equivalently, in the language of
[`CPT_EXACT_REAL_ANTI_HERMITIAN_D_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md`](CPT_EXACT_REAL_ANTI_HERMITIAN_D_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md),
this is the abstract operator type satisfying premise identity (3)
`T D T = D` with `T = K` complex conjugation. Let the source `J` range
over the real-symmetric matrices `Sym_N(R) ⊂ Mat_N(R)`. Define the
candidate generator

```text
W[J]  :=  log |det(D + J)|  -  log |det D|,                            (W)
```

restricted to the open neighborhood `U_0 ⊂ Sym_N(R)` of `J = 0` on which
`det(D + J) ≠ 0`. (Such a `U_0` exists because `det(D + λ I)` is a
degree-`N` polynomial in `λ` and `det D ≠ 0` by the explicit
invertible-block hypothesis, checked in the runner on the selected
test subregion.)

Fix three pieces of input:

**(X1) Real-D structural fact plus invertible domain.** `D` is an
invertible real anti-Hermitian operator on `V`, with the discrete-symmetry
algebra of
[`CPT_EXACT_REAL_ANTI_HERMITIAN_D_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md`](CPT_EXACT_REAL_ANTI_HERMITIAN_D_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md)
holding on the block; in particular premise identity (3) `T D T = D`
holds. The cited authority supplies the real-D structural fact; the
nonzero-determinant condition is an additional local-domain hypothesis
of this bounded theorem, not a conclusion of the cited CPT row. (Live
ledger status of the cited authority: `retained_bounded`.)

**(X2) Strengthened admissibility class (over a direct-sum-closed
block family).** Fix a *direct-sum-closed block family* `B` of invertible
real anti-Hermitian blocks: that is, a collection of invertible real
anti-Hermitian operators that is closed under the no-bond direct sum
`D_A ⊕ D_B` whenever `D_A, D_B ∈ B`, and that contains at least one
pair `(D_A, D_B) ∈ B × B` whose realized determinant-ratio pairs
`{ (r_A(J_A), r_B(J_B)) : J_A ∈ U_0(D_A), J_B ∈ U_0(D_B) }` have image
dense in `R_+ × R_+`. (Concretely, a 2x2 real-skew block
`D_a = a σ_y`, `a ∈ R_+`, already realizes every positive ratio:
with source `J = j I`, `r(j I; a) = |a^2 + j^2|/a^2 = 1 + (j/a)^2`
sweeps `(1, ∞)`, while with source `J = j σ_x`,
`r(j σ_x; a) = |a^2 - j^2|/a^2` sweeps `(0, 1)` for `|j| < a`
and hits `1` at `j = 0`. Two independent factors therefore realize
`R_+ × R_+`; the runner verifies the determinant identities and the
explicit inverse choices at exact symbolic precision.)

A *universal* scalar generator on `B` is a family
`{ F_D : U_0(D) → R }_{D ∈ B}` of scalar functions, one per block, such
that the family is jointly admissible iff it satisfies all four of:

  - (A) **block-additive on every realized no-bond direct sum within `B`.**
    For every `D_A, D_B ∈ B` and every `D = D_A ⊕ D_B ∈ B`, and every
    `J = J_A ⊕ J_B` with `J_A ∈ U_0(D_A)`, `J_B ∈ U_0(D_B)`,
    `F_D[J_A ⊕ J_B] = F_{D_A}[J_A] + F_{D_B}[J_B]`. (Non-vacuous by the
    direct-sum closure and density requirement above.)
  - (C) **universal CPT-even determinant-ratio dependence.** There exists
    a single continuous scalar response function `f : R_+ → R`,
    independent of `D ∈ B`, such that
    `F_D[J] = f(r(J))` for every `D ∈ B`, every `J ∈ U_0(D)`, where
    `r(J) = |det(D + J)| / |det D|`. (Equivalently, by (X1) and the
    Hermitian-lift invariance of
    [`CPT_EXACT_REAL_ANTI_HERMITIAN_D_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md`](CPT_EXACT_REAL_ANTI_HERMITIAN_D_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md),
    each `F_D` is invariant under the antiunitary `Θ_H = P K` action that
    transports `det(D + J) → det(D + J)^*` on real-D blocks; the
    fermionic phase is in the antiunitary-fixed eigenspace and is
    therefore not a free degree of freedom. Universality of `f` across
    `B` is the strengthened content beyond per-block determinant-ratio
    dependence.)
  - (R) **continuous on `U_0(D)` for every `D ∈ B`**, with `f` continuous
    on `R_+`.
  - (N) **normalized to** `F_D[0] = 0` (equivalently, `f(1) = 0`).

Why this is a *strengthening* of the prior per-block admissibility
criteria: the universality of `f` across `B` (criterion (C)) and the
non-vacuous block-additivity on dense-image direct sums (criterion (A))
together force the multiplicative-to-additive functional equation
`f(r_A r_B) = f(r_A) + f(r_B)` to hold on a subset of `R_+ × R_+`
whose image, by the density hypothesis on `B`, is dense in `R_+ × R_+`.
Continuity (R) then extends the equation to all of `R_+ × R_+` by
density, not by the unsupported continuity-only argument of the original
Step 6.

**Counterexample without strengthening (recorded for the audit lane).**
Without the universality and direct-sum-closure clauses, the candidate
`F_D[J] = (log r(J))^2` satisfies *vacuously* the weaker per-block (A)
on a single indecomposable block (no no-bond direct-sum decomposition
of that block exists, so the additivity condition has no instances to
check), satisfies the per-block (C), is continuous, and is normalized.
On any direct-sum-closed family `B` with dense realized ratios, this
candidate fails (A): for any `(r_A, r_B)` in the realized image with
`r_A, r_B ≠ 1` and `r_A r_B ≠ 1`,
`(log(r_A r_B))^2 = (log r_A + log r_B)^2 ≠ (log r_A)^2 + (log r_B)^2`
(the cross term `2 log r_A log r_B` is generically nonzero on the dense
realized image). Hence `(log r)^2` is ruled out by the strengthened
class but would have been admissible under the original per-block
criteria — this is the precise gap the audit lane flagged.

**(X3) Cauchy–Erdős functional-equation closure.** The only continuous
function `g : R_+ → R` satisfying `g(r_1 r_2) = g(r_1) + g(r_2)` for
all `r_1, r_2 ∈ R_+` is `g(r) = c log r` for some real constant `c`.
(Standard one-variable Cauchy multiplicative-to-additive theorem; see
e.g. Erdős, *On the distribution of additive functions*, 1946.)

### Conclusion

Under (X1), (X2), (X3), there exists a real constant `c` such that, for
every admissible universal family `{ F_D }_{D ∈ B}` in the strengthened
class (X2), and for every block `D ∈ B`,

```text
F_D[J]  =  c · W_D[J]    on   U_0(D),                                   (U1)
```

where `W_D[J] = log|det(D + J)| - log|det D|`. Consequently the local
source-derivative content of `F_D` at `J = 0` matches that of `W_D` up
to the overall scale `c`:

```text
∂F_D / ∂J_x    = c · ∂W_D / ∂J_x      at J = 0,                         (U2)
∂²F_D / ∂J_x ∂J_y  =  c · ∂²W_D / ∂J_x ∂J_y    at J = 0.                (U3)
```

In particular, on every block in the direct-sum-closed family `B`, the
**direction** of the source-response is fixed (one real degree of
freedom `c` is left, which is removed by the overall-normalization
convention; the standard choice is `c = 1`, the canonical natural log
baseline). The single constant `c` is shared across all blocks `D ∈ B`,
because (C) requires the response function `f` to be universal on `B`.

This is the **block-family local uniqueness statement** on `B`. Outside
scope: removing universality of `f` (criterion (C)), removing the
direct-sum-closure + dense-realized-ratio requirement on `B` (which
makes (A) non-vacuous), or removing continuity (R) — each of these
admits a strictly larger class of candidates (e.g. `(log r)^2`
indecomposably on a single block, or arbitrary post-composition with a
non-log function on a single-block family). Those remain selection
premises on the class of scalar generators that count as "physical
scalar bosonic observables".

## Proof

Pure algebraic substitution closed by Cauchy–Erdős.

**Step 1 — `det(D + J)` is real for real `D` and real `J`.** By (X1),
`D` has real entries. The source `J` is real-symmetric, in particular
real. So `D + J` is a real matrix on `R^N`, hence
`det(D + J) ∈ R`. (The determinant of a real matrix is real because it
is a polynomial in the real entries with integer coefficients.)

**Step 2 — `det(D + J) ≠ 0` on `U_0`.** By (X1), `det D != 0`.
Continuity of the determinant gives an open neighborhood `U_0` of
`J = 0` on which `det(D + J)` remains nonzero. In particular
`det(D + J) ∈ R \ {0}` is a continuous nonvanishing real-valued function
on `U_0`, so it has a well-defined locally constant sign
`σ(J) = sign(det(D + J)) ∈ {+1, -1}`. On any connected component of
`U_0`, `σ(J)` is constant.

**Step 3 — `W[J] = log |det(D + J)| - log |det D|` is well-defined and
real-analytic on `U_0`.** Because `det(D + J)` is a polynomial in the
entries of `J`, and `|det(D + J)| > 0` on `U_0`, the composite
`J ↦ log|det(D + J)|` is real-analytic on `U_0`. The normalization
constant `log|det D|` is finite because `D` is invertible (premise of
`U_0` containing `J = 0`; runner-checked).

**Step 4 — block-additivity for `W`.** If `D = D_A ⊕ D_B` and
`J = J_A ⊕ J_B` is a no-bond direct-sum decomposition, then
`det(D + J) = det(D_A + J_A) det(D_B + J_B)`, so

```text
log|det(D + J)|  =  log|det(D_A + J_A)|  +  log|det(D_B + J_B)|,
```

hence `W[J] = W_A[J_A] + W_B[J_B]` after subtracting baselines on each
block. The determinant factorization is elementary linear algebra
(block-diagonal determinants multiply); this note uses it as such and
re-verifies it in the runner without citing any external authority for
it. A sibling source note
`observable_generator_additivity_from_cluster_decomposition_theorem_note_2026-05-10`
records the same elementary fact but is not cited as a dependency here
(see "Cited dependencies" below).

**Step 5 — application of the strengthened admissibility class.** Let
`{ F_D }_{D ∈ B}` be any admissible universal family in the strengthened
class (X2). By (C), there is a single continuous response function
`f : R_+ → R` such that, for every `D ∈ B` and every `J ∈ U_0(D)`,
`F_D[J] = f(r(J))`, where

```text
r(J)  :=  |det(D + J)| / |det D|  >  0    for J ∈ U_0(D).               (r)
```

The function `f` is *the same function on `R_+`* across the whole
family `B` — this is the universality content of strengthened (C).
Continuity of `f` on `R_+` is criterion (R) directly (no patching
needed).

**Step 6 — Cauchy–Erdős multiplicative-to-additive closure under the
strengthened class.** Apply (A) on every realized no-bond direct sum
`D = D_A ⊕ D_B ∈ B`, `J = J_A ⊕ J_B`. By Step 4 (determinant
factorization) plus universality of `f`,

```text
f(r_A · r_B)  =  f(r_A)  +  f(r_B)                                       (CE_real)
```

holds for every pair `(r_A, r_B)` in the *realized image* of the
joint map `(J_A, J_B) ↦ (r(J_A; D_A), r(J_B; D_B))` over
`(D_A, D_B) ∈ B × B` and `(J_A, J_B) ∈ U_0(D_A) × U_0(D_B)`. By the
direct-sum-closure-with-dense-realized-ratios clause of strengthened
(X2), this realized image is dense in `R_+ × R_+`. Continuity of `f`
on `R_+` (criterion (R)) extends (CE_real) by joint continuity in
`(r_A, r_B)` from the dense subset to all of `R_+ × R_+`:

```text
f(r_A · r_B)  =  f(r_A)  +  f(r_B)    for all (r_A, r_B) ∈ R_+ × R_+.    (CE)
```

This continuity extension is sound because both sides of (CE_real) are
continuous functions of `(r_A, r_B)` (the left side because `f` is
continuous and multiplication is continuous on `R_+ × R_+`; the right
side because `f` is continuous and addition is continuous on `R × R`),
agreeing on a dense subset of a Hausdorff space `R_+ × R_+`, so they
agree everywhere. Cauchy's multiplicative-to-additive theorem (X3) then
forces `f(r) = c log r` for some real constant `c`. (Without the
strengthened class, only (CE_real) on the realized image is available,
and the auditor correctly noted that continuity alone does not promote
that to (CE) on `R_+ × R_+` — density of the realized image is the
needed additional input, supplied by strengthened (X2)(A).)

**Step 7 — collecting.** For every `D ∈ B` and every `J ∈ U_0(D)`,
substituting back,

```text
F_D[J]  =  f(r(J))  =  c log r(J)  =  c (log|det(D + J)| - log|det D|)
        =  c · W_D[J]                                                    (U1)
```

on `U_0(D)`. (Normalization (N) — `F_D[0] = 0` — is automatic because
`r(0) = 1` and `c log 1 = 0`.) Source derivatives (U2), (U3) follow by
differentiating (U1) on `U_0(D)`. The same `c` works for every
`D ∈ B` because `f` is universal across `B` by strengthened (C). ∎

## Derivable corollaries

**(R1) Imaginary part of `Tr log(D + J)` has zero source derivatives.**
On the real-D block, `det(D + J) ∈ R` (Step 1). Therefore
`Im Tr log(D + J) = arg(det(D + J)) ∈ π Z` is locally constant on
each connected component of `U_0`, so its source derivatives vanish
identically:

```text
∂ Im Tr log(D + J) / ∂J_x  =  0    on each component of U_0.            (R1)
```

This is consistent with (U1): the unique CPT-even continuous additive
scalar generator on the real-D block is real-valued, and any imaginary
contribution from `Tr log(D + J)` is in the kernel of the
source-derivative map.

**(R2) `Re Tr log(D + J) = log|det(D + J)|` identically on `U_0`.**
This is a direct consequence of Step 1 — for real `det(D + J) ≠ 0`,
`log(det(D + J)) = log|det(D + J)| + i π · χ_<0(det(D + J))` with the
imaginary part purely a sign indicator. Taking real parts gives the
identity. Equivalently, the candidate generator `W = log|det(D + J)|`
is the real part of `Tr log(D + J)` on the real-D block, and the
ambiguity in the complex logarithm (winding number) drops out of all
source derivatives.

Both corollaries follow algebraically from the proof and are checked
exactly in the runner.

## What this claims

- Block-family local uniqueness `(U1)`: any admissible universal family
  `{ F_D }_{D ∈ B}` in the strengthened class (X2) equals
  `c · W_D[J] = c (log|det(D + J)| - log|det D|)` on `U_0(D)` for every
  `D ∈ B`, up to a single real overall-scale constant `c` shared
  across the entire family.
- Source-derivative uniqueness `(U2)`, `(U3)`: on every block `D ∈ B`,
  the local source-response direction at `J = 0` is fixed; only the
  overall scale `c` (shared across the family) is free, removed by the
  standard normalization convention `c = 1`.
- The two derived corollaries `(R1)`, `(R2)`.

## What this does NOT claim

- Does **not** derive the strengthened admissibility class (X2). The
  four criteria (A), (C), (R), (N) — including direct-sum closure with
  realized-ratio density of `B`, universal response function `f` across
  `B`, continuity, and normalization — are admitted as the selection of
  "physical scalar bosonic universal observable generator on a
  direct-sum-closed real-D block family". Removing any of these
  strengthening clauses is outside scope; all remain selection premises
  on the broad parent `observable_principle_from_axiom_note` (see its
  §"Audit-named conditional scope" and §"Out of scope").
- Does **not** claim uniqueness for a *single* indecomposable real-D
  block in isolation. On a single indecomposable block (e.g. a single
  invertible 2x2 real-skew block) there is no nontrivial no-bond
  direct-sum decomposition, so per-block (A) is vacuous and continuous
  determinant-ratio functions such as `F[J] = (log r(J))^2` satisfy
  the per-block versions of (A), (C), (R), (N) without being a scaled
  copy of `W`. The strengthened class explicitly avoids this gap by
  requiring the universal-family + direct-sum-closure + dense-realized-
  ratio structure on `B`; the runner records `(log r)^2` failing
  strengthened (A) across realized two-block direct sums as an
  explicit counterexample probe.
- Does **not** promote (CE) on `R_+ × R_+` from continuity alone on
  an arbitrary block. Without the realized-ratio density of `B`, the
  functional equation (CE_real) is only known on the realized image,
  and continuity alone cannot extend it to `R_+ × R_+`. The density
  clause of strengthened (X2) is what makes the continuity-extension
  step in Step 6 sound.
- Does **not** derive invertibility or absence of zero modes for every
  real anti-Hermitian block. If `det D = 0`, this theorem applies only
  after a separately justified projection, regulator, or block choice
  supplies an invertible source neighborhood; such a block is excluded
  from `B`.
- Does **not** promote the audit status of
  `observable_principle_from_axiom_note`. That row's
  `effective_status` remains whatever the live ledger records and
  updates only on next audit pass; this narrow theorem is a separate,
  narrower row.
- Does **not** extend to non-real-D blocks. The real-D structural fact
  (X1) is load-bearing for Step 1. On a generic complex anti-Hermitian
  `D`, `det(D + J)` is not real, and the uniqueness chain may fail
  (this is a counterfactual probe in the runner).
- Does **not** derive (X1) itself. The real-D structural fact is
  imported from the retained-bounded narrow theorem
  [`CPT_EXACT_REAL_ANTI_HERMITIAN_D_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md`](CPT_EXACT_REAL_ANTI_HERMITIAN_D_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md).
- Does **not** identify `D` with any specific physical Hamiltonian or
  consume any numerical comparator. No PDG values, no fitted constants,
  no admitted unit conventions enter the load-bearing claim.
- Does **not** claim a continuum limit, an effective-action statement,
  or any QFT-language identification. The statement is finite-block
  algebraic only.
