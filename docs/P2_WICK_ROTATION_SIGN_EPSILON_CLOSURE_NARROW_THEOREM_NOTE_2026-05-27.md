### 5.1 (W1) Lattice-Euclidean signature on Z^4

By (C-Aft) Step 3 + Step 4 (chirality + single-clock), the total
spacetime dimension is `d_s + d_t = 4` with `d_s = 3` (Z^3 spatial


signature `Cl(4, 0)` on the lattice action. This is the lattice-

### 5.2 (W2) Reconstructed real-time signature is Cl(3, 1)

By (C-RP), the staggered-Dirac + Wilson-plaquette lattice action on
the staggered-only fermion sector is reflection positive: there
exists a positive Hermitian transfer matrix `T : H_phys → H_phys`
with `0 < ‖T‖_op ≤ 1`. The discrete-time iteration
`T^n = exp(-n a_τ H)` is the lattice-Euclidean semigroup.

By the finite-dimensional spectral bridge recorded in (C-Sc)/(R-STONE),
the discrete-time semigroup `T^n` is the imaginary-time sample
`U(-inτ)` of the unique strongly-continuous one-parameter group
`U(t) = exp(-itH)` on the *same* finite-dimensional Hilbert space
`H_phys`, with real parameter `t ∈ R`, via the spectral expansion

```text

    U(t) = Σ_k exp(-i t E_k) |k⟩⟨k|,   E_k = -(1/a_τ) log(λ_k).        (W2.a)
```

The group `U(t)` is **unitary** on `H_phys`:
`U(t)^† U(t) = U(-t) U(t) = U(0) = I`. This is the current (C-Sc)
transfer-relative output; its own bounded premises and parent statuses

This is precisely the defining relation of `Cl(3, 1)` from (C-Ext)
§5.3 with `e_4^2 = -I`, i.e., `ε = -1`.

The Lorentzian signature of the reconstructed real-time Dirac operator
follows from the unitarity of `U(t)`: a contraction semigroup

unitary group `U(t) = exp(-itH)` corresponds to the Lorentzian (3, 1)
cell. The factor of `i` in `exp(-itH)` is precisely the algebraic
