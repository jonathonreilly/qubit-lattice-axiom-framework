## 5. Proof

### 5.1 (W1) Lattice-Euclidean signature on Z^4

By (C-Aft) Step 3 + Step 4 (chirality + single-clock), the total
spacetime dimension is `d_s + d_t = 4` with `d_s = 3` (Z^3 spatial
substrate) and `d_t = 1` (single-clock). The Wick-rotated lattice
is `Z^4 = Z^3 × Z` with Euclidean signature: each lattice generator
direction carries a positive-square Clifford generator on the lattice
action. Following the staggered-Dirac convention (Kawamoto-Smit, as
in (C-Aft) Step 4), the four lattice phases satisfy

```text
η_μ(x)^2 = +1,  for μ = 1, 2, 3, 4    (all positive on Z^4 lattice)
```

and produce the Clifford-algebra relations of the **Euclidean**
signature `Cl(4, 0)` on the lattice action. This is the lattice-
level appearance of the `(p, q) = (4, 0)` Cartan-Bott classification
cell.

### 5.2 (W2) Reconstructed-Wightman signature is Cl(3, 1)

By (C-RP), the staggered-Dirac + Wilson-plaquette lattice action on
the staggered-only fermion sector is reflection positive: there
exists a positive Hermitian transfer matrix `T : H_phys → H_phys`
with `0 < ‖T‖_op ≤ 1`. The discrete-time iteration
`T^n = exp(-n a_τ H)` is the lattice-Euclidean semigroup.

By (P-OS) (Osterwalder-Schrader Wick rotation, identical to the
admitted infrastructure used by (C-Sc) Step 1 lines 261-266), the
discrete-time semigroup `T^n` analytically continues to a strongly-
continuous one-parameter group `U(t) = exp(-itH)` on the *same*
finite-dimensional Hilbert space `H_phys`, with real parameter
`t ∈ R`, via the spectral expansion

```text
    T = Σ_k λ_k |k⟩⟨k|,   λ_k > 0,
    U(t) = Σ_k exp(-i t E_k) |k⟩⟨k|,   E_k = -(1/a_τ) log(λ_k).        (W2.a)
```

The group `U(t)` is **unitary** on `H_phys`:
`U(t)^† U(t) = U(-t) U(t) = U(0) = I`. This is the (C-Sc) Step 1
output already retained on the framework's surface.

In the OS-reconstructed Wightman theory, the spacetime Clifford
algebra acting on `H_phys` is determined by the structure of the
real-time evolution `U(t)`. The Dirac operator on the reconstructed
Wightman theory satisfies the anticommutation relation

```text
    {γ_μ, γ_ν} = 2 η_μν I                                              (W2.b)
```

with `η_μν` the **Lorentzian** metric `η = diag(+1, +1, +1, -1)`.
This is precisely the defining relation of `Cl(3, 1)` from (C-Ext)
§5.3 with `e_4^2 = -I`, i.e., `ε = -1`.

The Lorentzian signature of the reconstructed-Wightman Dirac operator
follows from the unitarity of `U(t)`: a contraction semigroup
`exp(-tH)` corresponds to the Euclidean (4, 0) cell, whereas the
unitary group `U(t) = exp(-itH)` corresponds to the Lorentzian (3, 1)
cell. The factor of `i` in `exp(-itH)` is precisely the algebraic
content of the time generator squaring to `-1` (Lorentzian signature
sign in (W2.b)).

### 5.3 (W3) Closure of (C-Ext) sign-`ε` binary

Combining (W1) and (W2):

- The lattice action lives in the `(4, 0)` Cartan-Bott cell (W1).
- The reconstructed Wightman Hilbert-space theory lives in the
  `(3, 1)` Cartan-Bott cell (W2).
- The two are related by the OS Wick-rotation correspondence (P-OS),

- The (C-Ext) binary sign-`ε` question asks which Cartan-Bott cell

```text
    ε = e_4^2 = -1.                                                    (W3.a)
```

This closes the binary sign-`ε` question of P2 as a **bounded
theorem** consequence of (C-Ext) + (C-Sc) + (C-Aft) + (C-RP) +
