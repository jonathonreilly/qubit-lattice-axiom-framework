# Retained Parity/Chiral Violation Does Not Reach the Generation Triplet

## Question

Could the generation `S_3 → C_3` breaking be supplied by the framework's
*already-retained* parity violation (`CPT_EXACT_NOTE.md`: C and P individually
send `H → -H`; "lattice origin of parity violation"), rather than by a separate
flavor input? I.e. does that violation act on the hw=1 generation triplet so as
to lift its forced `S_3` mass degeneracy?

## Answer

**No.** The retained parity/chiral violation acts on the `3 ↔ 3̄`
(particle/antiparticle-like) axis, not within the generation triplet. The
operation whose breaking would lift the generation degeneracy — the
axis-permutation `S_3` — is a distinct operation. So the generation breaking is
a separate input.

## Theorem (momentum-space corner combinatorics)

The eight Brillouin-zone corners `{0,π}^3` group by Hamming weight as
`1 + 3 + 3 + 1`. The hw=1 triplet `{(π,0,0),(0,π,0),(0,0,π)}` carries the three
generations; the hw=2 triplet is the `3̄`.

**(A) Chiral `ε`.** `ε(n) = (-1)^{Σ n}` acts in momentum space as a shift by
`(π,π,π)`, i.e. flips every coordinate, sending Hamming weight `w → 3-w`. Hence
`ε` maps the hw=1 triplet **entirely onto the hw=2 triplet** (image disjoint
from hw=1). `ε` is the operation behind `H → -H`; it relates generations to
anti-generations, **not one generation to another**, so it is not an operator on
the triplet and cannot lift its degeneracy.

**(B) Spatial inversion.** `p → -p (mod 2π)` fixes every corner (`-π ≡ π`), so it
acts as the **identity** on the triplet and commutes with every mass matrix.

**(C) Axis permutation `S_3`.** Coordinate permutations map the hw=1 triplet
onto itself; a transposition genuinely swaps two generations (moves 2, fixes 1).
This is the degeneracy-forcing group (`s3_mass_matrix_no_go`), and it is
distinct from both `ε` (which leaves the triplet) and inversion (trivial). ∎

## What this claims

- `ε` (the `H → -H` operation) maps the hw=1 triplet onto the hw=2 triplet
  (`3 ↔ 3̄`), disjoint from hw=1.
- Spatial inversion is the identity on the hw=1 triplet.
- The axis-permutation `S_3` acts within the triplet and is the distinct
  degeneracy-forcing group.
- Therefore the retained parity/chiral violation cannot lift the generation
  `S_3` mass degeneracy; the generation `S_3 → C_3` breaking is a separate input.
