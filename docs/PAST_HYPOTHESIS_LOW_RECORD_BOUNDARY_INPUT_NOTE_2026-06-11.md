## The Input

The realized history has a low-record (low-entropy) boundary.

That is the entire content. It supplies the **existence** of the boundary
only: the arrow's **direction** (away from the low-record boundary) is derived
from record formation, on an explicit time-symmetric system, in
`docs/ARROW_FROM_RECORD_FORMATION_PAST_HYPOTHESIS_RESIDUAL_NOTE_2026-06-05.md`
— the same note that exhibits, with the dynamics held fixed, that the
candidate arrow reverses from a record-full state, is exactly flat at the
`I/d` equilibrium, and fluctuates from a generic high-entropy state. The sign
of the arrow is an output; the existence of somewhere low to point away from
is this input.

## Why Tier-A, Not Axiom Or Primitive

- **Not an axiom:** it is not a law. The microdynamics is time-symmetric
  (proven in the arrow note); this is a fact about the realized history, not
  about the rules.
- **Not a primitive:** the primitive registry holds content-free structure
  (a units reference, a graining symmetry, the realized-state interface). This
  input is **content-bearing**: it asserts the realized state is *special* —
  a specialness claim of exactly the class the `realized_state_primitive`
  declares it does not supply ("no typicality claim ... no boundary
  condition"). The section-6 precedent (the Born-reading classification)
  already records that content-selecting statements are not
  primitive-eligible.
- **Tier-A is the honest semantics:** rows that need the arrow's existence
  are genuinely conditional on this input, and Tier-A's chain-satisfy-at-
  `retained_bounded` records that conditionality instead of hiding it. The
  drawer also keeps the **retirement route open**: Tier-A admissions are
  derivation *targets* — a future retained derivation of the low-record
  boundary (for example, from record structure itself) retires the admission
  and upgrades every bounded dependent automatically.

## Scope (existence ONLY)

- It supplies no measure or typicality machinery beyond the boundary fact
  itself, no specific initial state, no within-history data, no entropy
  functional choice, no rate, no time metric, and no second boundary claim.
- It does not orient any quantity other than through the derived
  away-from-the-boundary direction; sharpening results record what it does
  NOT determine (it is provably uninformative about the color-purity slot,
  and the time arrow alone is insufficient for flavor handedness — see the
  sharpening sources in the registry entry).
- It is a **universal-floor** input: every theory with time-symmetric
  microdynamics (classical mechanics, quantum mechanics, quantum field
  theory, general relativity) requires the same boundary input for a
  thermodynamic arrow. Registering it is bookkeeping honesty, not a
  framework-specific concession.

## Relation To The Realized-State Primitive

The `realized_state_primitive` supplies the neutral slot — *a* law-admissible
state is realized, with no specialness claims. This input is the one named
**stronger** statement the framework makes about that realized state: its
history has a low-record end. The primitive's clause names it ("the past
hypothesis is a separate, stronger input"); this registration is that
separate input's formal home. The two are independent axes: the support and
sharpening runners exhibit that the boundary fact does not pin sector
patterns, and that the neutral slot does not orient history.

## Retirement Condition

A retained derivation of the low-record boundary from the three axioms plus
retained structure (no new inputs) retires this admission. Until then it
stands as a named, bounded, attackable input with a standing bounty.
