## Claim

Let `Λ ⊂ Z³` be a finite qubit-lattice region with
`H_sys = ⊗_{x ∈ Λ} ℂ²` of dimension `d = 2^|Λ|`. Let
`{K_r}_{r=1}^R ⊂ M_d(ℂ)` be a Kraus family satisfying
`Σ_r K_r† K_r = 𝟙_sys` (the operator-sum representation of a CPTP map
on the qubit-lattice, per the cited Kraus-Choi narrow theorem).

Let `H_record = ℂ^R` carry the orthonormal basis `{|r⟩}_{r=1}^R`.

Define the **block-column matrix**

```text
V :=  [ K_1 ]
      [ K_2 ]   ∈ M_{R·d × d}(ℂ)                                         (1)
      [  ⋮  ]
      [ K_R ]
```

This block-column codomain is the direct sum `⊕_{r=1}^R H_sys`, canonically
identified with `H_record ⊗ H_sys` in the ordered basis `{|r⟩ ⊗ |i⟩}`. Equivalently,
the linear map `V: H_sys → H_record ⊗ H_sys` is

```text
V |ψ⟩ = Σ_r |r⟩ ⊗ (K_r |ψ⟩).                                            (2)
```

If a downstream argument uses the system-first convention `H_sys ⊗ H_record`, it
uses the fixed tensor swap `S(|r⟩ ⊗ |i⟩) = |i⟩ ⊗ |r⟩`; since `S†S=1`, `(SV)†(SV)=V†V`.

**Theorem (narrow).**

- (V1) `V` is well-defined as a linear map (matrix entries inherited
  from the K_r matrix entries; no other input).
- (V2) `V† V = Σ_r K_r† K_r = 𝟙_sys` as a direct block-matrix algebra
  computation: the (i,j) entry of `V†V` is
  `Σ_{r=1}^R Σ_{k=1}^d (K_r†)_{ik} (K_r)_{kj} = Σ_r (K_r† K_r)_{ij}`,
  which sums to `δ_{ij}` by the Kraus resolution-of-identity hypothesis.
- (V3) Equivalently, for any `|ψ⟩ ∈ H_sys`:
  `⟨V ψ | V ψ⟩ = ⟨ψ | (Σ_r K_r† K_r) | ψ⟩ = ⟨ψ | ψ⟩`.

The conclusion `V† V = 𝟙_sys` is a **direct algebraic consequence of
the block-matrix construction**, not a property inherited from
standard QM. The runner verifies this explicitly for multiple
instrument families.

## Proof

### Step 1 — (V1) well-definedness of V

`V` is a matrix; its entries are inherited from the entries of the
`K_r` matrices via the block-column stacking. Specifically, the
`(α, j)` entry of `V` is:

```text
V_{α, j} = (K_r)_{i, j}    where  α = (r-1)d + i,  1 ≤ i ≤ d,  1 ≤ r ≤ R. (3)
```

There is no additional input or admission. ∎

### Step 2 — (V2) V† V = 1_sys by block-matrix algebra

Compute `V† V` entry by entry. The (i, j) entry of `V† V` is:

```text
(V† V)_{i,j}  =  Σ_{α=1}^{R·d} (V†)_{i, α} V_{α, j}
              =  Σ_{α=1}^{R·d} \overline{V_{α, i}} · V_{α, j}.            (4)
```

Re-index `α = (r-1)d + k` for `r ∈ {1, ..., R}`, `k ∈ {1, ..., d}`:

```text
(V† V)_{i,j}  =  Σ_{r=1}^R Σ_{k=1}^d \overline{(K_r)_{k, i}} (K_r)_{k, j}
              =  Σ_{r=1}^R [K_r† K_r]_{i, j}
              =  [Σ_r K_r† K_r]_{i, j}
              =  [𝟙_sys]_{i, j}    (by the Kraus hypothesis Σ_r K_r†K_r = 𝟙)
              =  δ_{i, j}.                                                (5)
```

So `V† V = 𝟙_sys`. ∎

### Step 3 — (V3) total probability preservation as a bilinear-form statement

For any `|ψ⟩ ∈ H_sys`:

```text
⟨V ψ | V ψ⟩ = (V|ψ⟩)† (V|ψ⟩) = ⟨ψ| V† V |ψ⟩ = ⟨ψ| 𝟙_sys |ψ⟩ = ⟨ψ|ψ⟩.    (6)
```

This holds for **arbitrary input states** `|ψ⟩` — exactly the property
the reviewer flagged as missing in PR #1634's standard-QM inheritance.
Here it follows by direct algebra from (V2). ∎

## Runner exhibits V on six instrument families

The companion runner `scripts/frontier_persistent_record_instrument_construction.py`
exhibits V explicitly for six concrete instrument families and verifies
(V2) symbolically:

| Test | Instrument | H_sys | R | V shape |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Projective σ_z on single qubit | ℂ² | 2 | (4, 2) |
| T2 | Projective in rotated basis (symbolic angle t) | ℂ² | 2 | (4, 2) |
| T3 | 3-outcome planar POVM (K_r = √E_r) | ℂ² | 3 | (6, 2) |
| T4 | Bell-basis on two qubits | ℂ⁴ | 4 | (16, 4) |
| T5 | Biased 2-outcome POVM, p = 1/3 | ℂ² | 2 | (4, 2) |
| T6 | Continuous parametric family in λ | ℂ² | 2 | (4, 2) |

For each, the runner:

1. Constructs `K_r` as explicit sympy matrices
2. Builds `V` via `build_stinespring_isometry({K_r})` (block-column stack)
3. Computes `V† V` symbolically and checks it equals `𝟙_sys`
4. Independently checks `Σ_r K_r† K_r = 𝟙_sys`
5. Checks the block structure: row-block `r` of `V` equals `K_r`

The row-block check uses the same convention as the theorem: rows are ordered first by
record label and then by system basis, i.e. `⊕_r H_sys ≅ H_record ⊗ H_sys`. Any
system-first downstream use is obtained by the fixed tensor swap described above.

T7 adds a numerical sanity check: across three instrument families and
five random complex `|ψ⟩` per family (15 trials total), verifies
`‖V|ψ⟩‖² = ‖|ψ⟩‖²` to float64 precision.
