## Independent graph and arithmetic checks

The primary recognizes the two graph types by direct backtracking graph
isomorphism. The helper instead constructs binary cube coordinates from one
root:

1. breadth-first layers must have binomial sizes;
2. the root's neighbors receive distinct basis bits;
3. each later vertex receives the union of its lower-layer neighbor bits;
4. every adjacency is finally checked to be exactly one-bit difference.

That independent recognizer gives the same `60/72` split and the same family
assignment. Its pair counts are accumulated by streaming every cutting row
and incrementing each unordered pair in that row, rather than by the primary's
dense Gram multiplication. The exact distance sets above agree.

As declared controls, four cyclic spacings applied at each of the 192 starting
pieces give 768 sixteen-piece sets. Neither recognizer finds `Q_4` or
`Q_3 disjoint-union Q_3` on any of them. This is a control result only; it is
not a rarity theorem and does not classify all sixteen-piece subsets.

## Ambient-corner statistic boundary

Each carrier piece is a five-corner cell in the supplied ambient coordinate
four-cube. On every `Q_4` carrier, the sixteen cells cover all sixteen ambient
corners, and every join connects two cells sharing exactly two ambient
corners. The converse is false for this statistic: 2,016 non-joined pairs at
graph distance two, three, or four also share exactly two ambient corners.

Therefore the obvious shared-cell-corner count does not reconstruct the join
relation. This is the narrow result supported by the calculation. The broader
submitted phrase that the graph was “not the ambient four-cube relabelled” is
withdrawn: as an abstract graph it is explicitly isomorphic to `Q_4`, and no
claim is made against every possible ambient-coordinate interpretation.
