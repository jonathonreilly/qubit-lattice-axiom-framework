# Physical cell cutting: the pair action is free and every orbit table has rank 144 (cycle 771)

Status: a bounded theorem about the cell object of this lane, verified end to end by
[the runner](../scripts/physical_cell_cutting_cell_orbit_cycles_cycle771_2026_08_11.py), which
rebuilds the object from nothing and reports 24 gates, all passing. The group of 384 signed

exactly 96 orbits, each of the full size 384. Every one of those 96 orbit tables has rank exactly
144 and nullity exactly 48 over every field, because all 48 products of a piece-stabiliser

on the object itself and adds no axiom to [the minimal axiom set](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md).

## 1. The object and the group

The cell is the unit four-cube on its sixteen corners. Of its five-corner subsets, 2672 have unit
determinant, and 400 of those sit at the adjacency cost floor 6. Those 400 cut the cell in exactly
15800 ways, every cutting using 24 pieces; 192 distinct pieces occur, each in 1975 of the cuttings,
and the double count agrees both ways, 24 x 15800 = 379200 = 1975 x 192 (`C0`). There are 192 covers, each of
8 pieces, each meeting every cutting exactly once, with 8 x 1975 = 15800 (`C1`). The 384 signed
coordinate maps are distinct, shut under composition, and act by 384 distinct bijections on the
pieces and on the covers, each action a single orbit of 192 (`C2`). Both point stabilisers have
order 2, and the non-identity element of each squares to the identity (`C3`).

## 2. The action on pairs is free

Exactly 12 non-identity maps fix at least one piece and exactly 4 fix at least one cover, and those
two sets are disjoint (`C4`). Each of the 12 fixes 16 pieces and each of the 4 fixes 48 covers, so
12 x 16 = 192 and 4 x 48 = 192: every point carries exactly one non-identity fixer, and all 16 of
these maps are involutions (`C5`). A direct scan over all 383 non-identity maps finds none fixing
both a piece and a cover (`C6`). The stabiliser of a pair is therefore trivial and the action on
the 36864 pairs is free. Taking each pair through all 384 maps gives exactly 96 orbits, each of the
full size 384, with 96 x 384 = 36864 (`C7`).

## 3. Two-regular tables and cycles of length 8

Read as a zero and one table with the 192 covers as rows and the 192 pieces as columns, an orbit
carries exactly 2 ones in every row and 2 in every column (`C8`). The reason is structural: the two
pieces paired with a fixed cover are swapped by that cover's stabiliser generator, and the two
covers paired with a fixed piece are swapped by that piece's (`C9`). A table with 2 ones per row
and per column is the bipartite adjacency of a disjoint union of even cycles, and the cycle through
a pair is the orbit of that pair under the subgroup generated by the two involutions. Two
involutions generate a dihedral group of order twice the order of their product, and the action is
free, so the cycle length is exactly twice that order. There are only 12 distinct piece-stabiliser
generators and 4 distinct cover-stabiliser generators, hence only 48 products to inspect, and every
one of the 48 has order exactly 4 (`C10`). Walking the components of every table confirms the
consequence directly: each component is a cycle of length 8, there are 48 per table and 4608 in
all (`C12`), and each cycle's length is twice the order of the product of the two generators at its
base pair (`C13`).

That order 4 is a real condition and not an automatic one. Multiplying the same 12 piece-stabiliser
generators by every one of the 75 involutions of the group gives 900 products whose orders are 1,
2, 4 and 8 (`C11`). Orders 1, 2 and 8 all occur among them, so landing on 4 for all 48 of the
relevant products is a fact about which involutions the two kinds of stabiliser supply, not a
consequence of multiplying two involutions.

## 4. The rank in every characteristic

Ordering rows and columns cycle by cycle makes each table block diagonal with 48 copies of the
4 by 4 zero and one matrix carrying ones on the diagonal and on the cyclic superdiagonal. On every
cycle the submatrix on its 4 rows and 4 columns is exactly that block, and those rows and columns
carry no ones outside it (`C15`). The block has determinant 0, so its rank is at most 3 over every
field, and its leading 3 by 3 minor has determinant 1, so its rank is at least 3 over every field:
rank exactly 3 and nullity exactly 1, in every characteristic (`C14`). Every one of the 96 tables
therefore has rank exactly 48 x 3 = 144 and nullity exactly 192 - 144 = 48 over every field (`C16`).
The route to that value runs through the two small determinants of `C14` and through an exhibited
kernel, not through elimination on a 192 by 192 matrix. The kernel is exhibited rather than
inferred: the integer vector alternating in sign along a cycle is annihilated by its table
exactly over the integers, for all 4608 cycles, and the 48 such vectors belonging to one table have
pairwise disjoint supports and are therefore independent (`C17`). Real elimination confirms the
value independently, returning 144 for all 96 tables at each of two fixed primes, 192 measurements
in all (`C18`). Moving a single one along one row of one table into a column that row did not
occupy breaks two-regularity and moves the measured rank from 144 to 145 (`C19`).

## 5. The sum and the incidence

The 96 orbit tables sum entrywise to the all ones 192 by 192 matrix (`C20`). The cover by piece
incidence, whose entry in row j and column i is 1 exactly when piece i lies in cover j, is the
entrywise sum of exactly 4 of the 96 (`C21`). That incidence has rank 105 at each of the two
primes, so its kernel has dimension 192 - 105 = 87 and the excess over a single orbit table is
144 - 105 = 39 (`C22`). The drop is born on the sum and not on any single orbit: each of the four
summands alone has rank 144, while the six two-element sums of the four measure 72, 93, 117, 129,
144 and 144 (`C23`).

## 7. Boundary and honest auditor read

- The theorem says nothing about which four of the 96 orbits the geometry selects, and nothing
  about why their sum falls to 105. The excess 39 is not explained here. It is localised, to the
  sum rather than to any single orbit, and that is all.
- The rank 144 is uniform across all 96 orbits. A single orbit's rank therefore cannot be the
  feature that distinguishes the geometric four from the rest of the 96. That is a negative result
  and it is stated as one.
- No axiom, no import and no literature comparator is used. The object and the group are rebuilt
  from the sixteen corners inside the runner.
- Every measured number in this note is a number the runner printed.
- `C16` is arithmetic bookkeeping downstream of `C12`, `C14` and `C15`, not an independent
  measurement: once the cycle count and the block are fixed, its equation cannot fail. The
  independent confirmations of the value are `C17` and `C18`, and the rejector is `C19`.
