## The correspondence

An automorphism of the incidence structure is a pair of permutations — one of the 15800
cuttings, one of the 192 pieces — carrying the incidence table onto itself. Two gated
facts collapse that pair to its piece part: all 15800 rows are distinct as bit vectors,
so a piece permutation admits at most one compatible cutting permutation; and distinct
pieces have distinct columns, since a piece overlaps itself in 1975 cuttings while two
distinct pieces overlap in at most 1266, so a pair whose cutting part is the identity has
identity piece part too. The automorphism group is therefore exactly the set of piece
permutations carrying the family of 15800 cutting supports onto itself, and membership is
one comparison of sorted row bytes, column-permuted table against original.

## Method — iterated color refinement

Let G be the overlap table of the pieces, the integer matrix whose entry at (i, j) counts
the cuttings using both piece i and piece j. Its diagonal is uniformly 1975, its largest
off-diagonal entry is 1266, and off the diagonal it takes 47 distinct values.

Given a list of individualized pieces, refine() starts from the coloring giving every
piece the color zero except the individualized ones, which receive distinct negative
tags. It then repeats one step: the key of a piece i is its current color together with
the sorted multiset of pairs (G[i, j], color of j) over all pieces j, the piece i
included; keys are sorted and renumbered densely to give the next coloring. The step
repeats until the number of distinct colors stops growing, and the runner records both
the stable coloring and the rounds taken.

Soundness lemma. If pi is an automorphism of the incidence structure keeping each
individualized piece, then pi preserves every color class of every round. Proof: the
initial coloring is pi-invariant by construction, and G[pi(i), pi(j)] = G[i, j] because
pi permutes the cutting supports, so the key of pi(i) at round k+1 equals the key of i
whenever the round-k coloring is pi-invariant; by induction on rounds the stable coloring
is pi-invariant. The two-run form of the same induction underlies the branching below,
and the runner gates the resulting correspondence directly rather than resting on it.

## The certified search

With nothing individualized the refinement stabilizes after 1 round at a single cell
holding all 192 pieces, consistent with the transitivity of E.

Individualizing one piece, it stabilizes after 3 rounds at 104 cells: 16 singletons and
88 cells of size two. The runner checks that this partition is exactly the suborbit
partition of the point stabilizer — the stable coloring is kept by s0, the 16 singleton
cells are exactly the 16 pieces s0 keeps, and every cell of size two is exactly an orbit
of s0. The rank-104 partition of the previous cycle is recovered by refinement alone,
with no group input.

Take the cell of size two of least color index and call its members, in index order, a
and b. An automorphism keeping the individualized piece preserves the stable coloring by
the lemma, so it sends a to a or to b; those two branches cover every case.

Individualizing that piece together with a refines to a discrete coloring — 192 cells —
in 3 rounds, so an automorphism in the first branch preserves a discrete coloring and is
the identity. Individualizing it together with b also refines to a discrete coloring in 3
rounds, and the two discrete colorings correspond pointwise along s0, so an automorphism
in the second branch is the unique color-matching bijection between them; the runner
computes that bijection, gates that it equals s0, and verifies it against the incidence
table by the row-byte test. The identity is verified by the same test through the same
code path. The point stabilizer of the automorphism group is therefore exactly the
identity together with s0, of order 2, both elements verified rather than inferred.

Three rejector gates check that these tests discriminate rather than accept whatever is
put to them: swapping one piece s0 keeps with one it moves is rejected by the row-byte
test; one edited entry in a copy of the incidence table makes the identity fail that same
test; and color-matching a discrete branch coloring against the un-individualized one is
rejected outright on incompatible histograms. All three pass by rejecting.

## The count

The 50 generators of E — the 48 cutting symmetries and the two extra automorphisms — each
pass the direct row-byte test, so E is contained in the automorphism group. E is
transitive, so the automorphism orbit of the individualized piece is already all 192
pieces, and orbit-stabilizer gives the order of the automorphism group as 192 times 2,
which is 384 — the point stabilizer produced by the search coinciding elementwise with
that of E. Since E is contained in a group of the same order 384, the two are equal: the
group of the previous cycle is the full automorphism group of the incidence structure.
