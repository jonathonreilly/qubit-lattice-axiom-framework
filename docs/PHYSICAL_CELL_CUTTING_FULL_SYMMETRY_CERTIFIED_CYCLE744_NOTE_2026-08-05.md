An automorphism here is a pair of permutations, one on cutting rows and one
on support columns, preserving incidence and preserving the two roles. All
15,800 cutting rows are distinct, so a support-column permutation has at most
one compatible row permutation. The primary and checker therefore test a
candidate support permutation by applying it to every row and requiring the
same row family exactly.

Let

\[
G = I^{\mathsf T} I,

where I is the binary incidence matrix. Every incidence automorphism induces

## Exhaustive point-stabilizer certificate

With no support column individualized, refinement leaves one cell. With
column 0 individualized, it stabilizes at 104 cells: 16 singletons and 88
two-element cells. The nonidentity element s0 of E's point stabilizer fixes
exactly those 16 singleton columns and swaps the members of every two-element
cell.

Choose any one two-element cell `{a,b}`. The primary takes its first canonical
cell; the checker deliberately takes a lexicographically last pair. An
automorphism fixing column 0 must preserve the stable partition and therefore
sends a to either a or b. These are the complete branches.

- Individualizing `(0,a)` gives 192 singleton cells. Its branch stabilizer is
  therefore the identity.
- Individualizing `(0,b)` also gives 192 singleton cells. Matching the two
  discrete partitions gives one candidate permutation. It is exactly s0 and
  directly preserves all 15,800 incidence rows.

Thus the full point stabilizer has exactly two elements, `{id,s0}`. No graph
automorphism package or heuristic search cutoff is used: the invariant
partition covers all candidate images, both branches are discrete, and both
unique candidates are checked against the original row family.

Every one of the 50 declared generators directly preserves the row family.
Their generated group E has order 384 and is transitive on all 192 support
columns. Therefore E is contained in the full incidence automorphism group,
while orbit-stabilizer gives the upper bound

\[
|\operatorname{Aut}(I)| = 192\,|\operatorname{Aut}(I)_0|
                         = 192\cdot 2 = 384.
\]

Containment and equal finite order prove equality.
