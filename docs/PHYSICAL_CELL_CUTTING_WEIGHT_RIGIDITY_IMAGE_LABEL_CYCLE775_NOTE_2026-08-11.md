# Physical cell cutting: the top weights are rigid, and an image label separates the twin (cycle 775)

Date: 2026-08-11
Authority: none
Audit: unset.
Claim type: bounded_theorem
Constitutional effect: none.

**The top two weights of the flip grading are rigid.** Every one of the 96 orbit tables dies on the all-signs block, and on each of the 4 weight-3 blocks every one of them has rank 6, one and the same image, and one of exactly two kernels of dimension 6 that meet in 0. The cover side forces both facts: a cover is held by a single axis flip, so the cover-side block of a weight-w pattern has dimension six times four less the weight, which is 0 at weight 4 and 6 at weight 3.

**A lone minority colour forces the rank.** If exactly one of the four parts of a member carries the minority value of the `cycle 772` colour, that member has weight-3 rank exactly 6 and its kernel meets the majority kernel in 0. This is a theorem, proved below and checked on a deterministic sweep of 1056 members. The cover incidence and the `cycle 773` twin are both lone-minority members, which is why the top of the grading cannot tell them apart, and why the whole excess of one table over the incidence, 39 dimensions, has to sit at weights 0, 1 and 2.

**An image label does tell them apart.** The image of a table on a weight-1 block is a label with 6 values; the incidence carries the label multiset 0, 0, 1, 2 and the twin carries 3, 4, 5, 5, disjoint from it, and 30720 of the 3321960 members carry the incidence multiset.

## 1. What is measured

The object is the unit four-cube cell and the ways of cutting it. Its 16 corners carry 2672 five-corner subsets of unit determinant; 400 of those sit at the adjacency cost floor 6; the 400 pieces admit 15800 cuttings of the cell into 24 pieces; 192 distinct pieces occur, each in exactly 1975 cuttings, filling 379200 slots; and the pieces are grouped by 192 covers of 8.

The paired runner is [`../scripts/physical_cell_cutting_weight_rigidity_image_label_cycle775_2026_08_11.py`](../scripts/physical_cell_cutting_weight_rigidity_image_label_cycle775_2026_08_11.py), it builds everything in-run from the corners upward, reads no cached data and no input file, works exactly over the integers and two fixed primes, uses no floating point in any gate, fits no constant, and prints one line per gate; all 35 gates pass, three are rejectors, six are honest negatives, and one gate is both at once. The object is built from the lattice and admissibility content of the four axioms in [MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md) and imports nothing else.

The group of 384 signed coordinate maps — the 4 coordinate permutations composed with the 16 sign flips — acts freely on the 36864 pairs of a piece and a cover, giving 96 orbits of size 384, each read as a zero-one table over covers by pieces. The 96 tables are pairwise disjoint and sum to the all-ones table, so every 4-subset of them is an 8-regular zero-one member of a family of 3321960; the cover incidence is one member and the `cycle 773` twin is another. The 16 pure flips act freely on the 192 pieces with 12 orbits, so each of the 16 sign patterns of the four axes carries a piece-side block of dimension 12, and the 16 blocks fill all 192 piece coordinates.

## 2. The top weight is empty

At the all-signs pattern every one of the 96 tables has rank 0 on its block and a kernel of the full dimension 12, at both primes. The cover-side block is 0-dimensional there, and a table carries its piece-side block into the cover-side block — over the 1536 pairs of a table and a pattern there is not one image outside — so the death at weight 4 is forced, not a coincidence of this object. Every member of the family dies at the top for the same reason, so nothing at weight 4 separates any two members.

## 3. The next weight is rigid: one image, two complementary kernels, and the colour

At each of the 4 weight-3 patterns all 96 tables have rank 6, at both primes. The 96 images collapse to a single common image, and stacking all 96 of them still gives rank 6: the tables do not merely have equal rank there, they have the same image. The kernels are not all equal: there are exactly 2 of them, each of dimension 6, with class sizes 48 and 48, they meet in 0, and stacked they give the full block dimension 12. The 2 kernel classes are the `cycle 772` colour: the kernel class agrees with the colour on all 96 orbits, while against the colour shifted by one orbit the pairing breaks into 4 classes, so the agreement is a fact about the colour and not about the labelling of orbits.

## 4. A lone minority part forces the rank

The cover side gives the ambient space in which all of this happens, and it is what makes the two facts above forced rather than observed. Each of the 192 covers is held by a subgroup of order 2 generated by a single axis flip, so the covers fall into 24 orbits of 8 under the 16 flips, and the stabiliser masks are the 4 single-axis masks, 6 orbits each. A weight-w sign pattern therefore has a cover-side block of dimension six times four less the weight: 24, 18, 12, 6 and 0 by weight from 0 up, equal at both primes to its own rank and to the span of the images of all 96 tables. A table carries the piece-side block of a pattern into the cover-side block of the same pattern, with 0 misses over all 1536 table-and-pattern pairs. At weight 4 the cover-side block has dimension 0, so every table dies there with nothing further measured. At weight 3 it has dimension 6, so no table can have rank above 6; and since the 96 images coincide and together span that whole 6-dimensional block, the single common image is the block, and the rank is exactly 6. Bounding the rank of a table on each block by the smaller of the piece-side 12 and the cover-side dimension gives a ceiling 12, 12, 12, 6, 0 against the measured 12, 12, 10, 6, 0; the shortfall is 0, 0, 2, 0, 0, weighting to 156 against 144, a deficit of 12 that sits wholly at weight 2. So the cover-side count fixes the top two weights and leaves the rest open, which is exactly where the excess turns out to live.

> Fix a weight-3 sign pattern and write W for the common image, K_a and K_b for the two kernels. Every table sends the 12-dimensional block into W, which has dimension 6, so any entrywise sum of tables does too and its rank is at most 6. Suppose exactly one of the four parts has colour b and the other three have colour a. Take a vector v in K_a. The three majority tables kill it, so the sum sends v to what the minority table sends it to; if the sum kills v then v lies in K_b as well. The two kernels meet in 0, so the sum's kernel meets K_a in 0. A subspace meeting a 6-dimensional subspace in 0 inside a 12-dimensional space has dimension at most 6, and the rank bound forces the kernel to have dimension at least 6. So the kernel has dimension exactly 6 and the rank is exactly 6.

The scope of that argument is narrow and worth stating plainly: it delivers dimension 6 for each lone-minority member separately, and it does not deliver that two such members have transverse kernels. Where two members are compared below, the transversality of their kernels is measured, not derived. The sweep of 1056 members agrees with the theorem on every one of the 538 lone-minority members at every weight-3 pattern, with 0 misses, and the rank never exceeds 6 on any member.

## 5. Why the incidence and the twin had to agree at the top

The incidence's four parts carry colours 0, 0, 0, 1 and the twin's carry 0, 1, 1, 1. Both are lone-minority members, with different majorities. So the theorem applies to each of them, and both have weight-3 rank 6 and a 6-dimensional kernel transverse to their own majority kernel, at all 4 weight-3 patterns. Their agreement at the top of the grading is therefore not evidence that they are alike: it is what the theorem forces for any member of that shape, and the lone-minority shape is not rare. A rejector confirms the theorem is doing work rather than restating the sweep: the 369 even-split members give weight-3 ranks spread over 0, 3, 4, 5 and 6, so an even split does not force rank 6.

## 6. Where the excess actually lives

Per weight from 0 up, the pair of ranks of the incidence and the twin, together with the dimension in which their kernels meet, is class-constant: 9, 9 and 3 at weight 0; 9, 9 and 0 at weight 1; 6, 6 and 3 at weight 2; 6, 6 and 0 at weight 3; 0, 0 and 12 at weight 4. Recomposing over the 16 patterns gives rank 105 for each of the two and a total kernel meet of 33, at both primes.

A single orbit table profiles 12, 12, 10, 6, 0, so the drop from one table to the incidence is 3, 3, 4, 0, 0, weighting to an excess of 39 dimensions. Every dimension of that excess sits at weights 0, 1 and 2, because the top two weights are pinned by the previous two sections. Of the 33 shared kernel dimensions, 12 sit at weight 4 where everything dies, 21 sit at weights 0 and 2, and 0 sit anywhere else, so the shared structure is also a low-weight phenomenon.

## 7. The image label

The image of a table on a block is a subspace of the 192-dimensional cover space, and its exact canonical form is a label of the orbit. By weight from 0 up, the label takes 3, 6, 3, 1 and 1 values. At weight 0 the classes have sizes 32, 32 and 32; at weight 1 they are 6 classes of 16; at weight 2 again 32, 32 and 32; at weights 3 and 4 the label is constant, which is the rigidity of the top two weights seen a second way.

The label is not the colour and is not implied by it. Joint classes of label and colour number 6 of size 16 at weight 0, 12 of size 8 at weight 1, and 6 of size 16 at weight 2, so the two are independent in the exact sense that every combination occurs equally often. The weight-0 label and the weight-2 label agree on all 96 orbits, so they are one map with 3 values; the weight-1 label refines it, each of the 3 fibres holding exactly 2 of the 6 values, and no value of the weight-1 label is fixed by the pairing inside its fibre.

## 8. The label separates the incidence from the twin

All 4 weight-1 patterns give one and the same label, so this is one fact and not four. Under it the incidence's parts carry the multiset 0, 0, 1, 2 and the twin's parts carry 3, 4, 5, 5; the two multisets are disjoint. Swapping each label for its partner in its fibre is an involution of the 6 values, and applying it to the incidence multiset gives exactly the twin multiset. The twin is the fibre-swap of the incidence at this label, which is the sharpest statement available for a pair the top two weights cannot separate at all.

The narrowing is real but partial. The members carrying the incidence label multiset number 30720, by binomials over the class sizes and by enumerating all 3321960 members alike: 9 in a thousand. A rejector shows the label moves under perturbation: of the 368 members obtained from the incidence by replacing one part, only 58 keep the label multiset, whether the keeping is counted directly or by class sizes.

## 9. Boundary and honest auditor read

(a) The lone-minority shape is common, not special. Colour classes of 48 and 48 give 389160 same-colour, 1660416 lone-minority and 1272384 even-split members, totalling 3321960. The theorem of section 4 therefore covers about half the family and is in no way a characterisation of the cover incidence.

(b) At all 4 weight-3 patterns the two kernels are 6 and 6 and meet in 0. The theorem gives dimension 6 for each member separately; it does not give the meet. The transversality of two members' kernels is measured, not derived.

(c) The label multiset is not a rank invariant. Grouping members by label multiset, the multiset fixes the sum rank in 0 of 15 groups at weight 0, 28 of 123 at weight 1, and 0 of 15 at weight 2. The group of 13 members holding the incidence multiset spreads over ranks 9, 11 and 12, with 1, 4 and 8 members.

(d) Overlaps do not separate the incidence from the twin. Over the 18336 pairs the row overlaps spread as 0 for 15072, 1 for 1920, 2 for 960 and 4 for 384, and the column overlaps as 0 for 15168, 1 for 1728, 2 for 960, 3 for 192 and 4 for 288 — identically for the incidence and for the twin.

(e) The weight-3 rigidity is one pattern at a time. The 4 common images, each of dimension 6, stack to rank 24, not 6, so there is no single 6-dimensional statement covering all four weight-3 blocks at once.

(f) The label is a separation, not an identification. It puts the twin outside the 30720 members sharing the incidence multiset, and 30720 is far from 1.

(g) The cover-side ambient is not itself a witness for the four parts. One table repeated spans 12, 12, 10, 6, 0 against the ambient 24, 18, 12, 6, 0, short at weights 0, 1 and 2; taking tables in orbit order, 4, 3, 3, 1 and 0 of them saturate the ambient by weight, while the dimension bound alone already allows 2, 2, 2, 1 and 0. Saturation is cheap, and the count 4 at weight 0 is a small number rather than evidence for the four parts of the incidence.

**What this note does not do.** It does not pin the cover incidence inside its family, and it does not claim that the image label is complete: 30720 members still share the incidence label multiset, and nothing here orders or separates those. The next thing to measure is the label carried by the weight-2 and weight-1 kernels rather than the images, and the joint label of a member's four parts as an ordered structure rather than a multiset, which is where the remaining 30720 can be cut down.
