## Result

The tested mechanisms do not supply such a derivation. On the declared surface
`V = C^2 (x) (C^2 (x) C^2)` with `Y(alpha,beta) = alpha*P_sym + beta*P_anti`
(multiplicities 6 and 2 on `V`):

**1. Tracelessness fixes the ratio and nothing else.** `6*alpha + 2*beta = 0`
gives the full solution line `(alpha,beta)=t(1,-3)`, including `t=0`. The ratio
`+1:(-3)` is exact on the nonzero part of the line; the scale parameter is
untouched.

**2. The generated-subgroup algebra fixes a group up to scale, never the
scale.** The formal additive subgroup generated by the two block values
`alpha` and `-3*alpha` is exactly `alpha*Z`, since `gcd(1,3)=1`. This is pure
algebra. The Record axiom supplies finite scalar additivity, but the parent row
does not derive a physical readout identification or arbitrary populations of
the two record types. See the exact scope of
[Record](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md). This cycle therefore does **not** call
`alpha*Z` an achievable physical readout set.

**3. Exactly one tested condition yields positive `1/3`, and it is the target
choice of unit.**

| tested condition | computed consequence |
|---|---|
| tracelessness alone | alpha free |
| generated-subgroup primitive generator magnitude = 1 | `alpha^2 = 1` |
| symmetric-block charge = +1 | alpha = 1 |

| `Tr(Y^2) = 1` | `alpha^2 = 1/24`; no rational alpha |
| **trivial-block charge = −1** | **alpha = 1/3** |

The only condition selecting 1/3 is the stipulation that the one-dimensional
trivial block reads unit charge — a choice of which block carries the unit, not
a framework-derived fact. The **unit** quadratic condition is incompatible:

the approved units authority is closed by that authority's own scope.
