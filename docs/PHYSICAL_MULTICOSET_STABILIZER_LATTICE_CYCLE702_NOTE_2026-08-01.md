## What this cycle establishes

Let `P` be the Cycle-696 single-complex coframe pipeline — source domain, then
response `eps`, then metric/coframe field `h` — and let `O` be the group of the
24 proper cubic rotations. For a subset `S` of `O` define the partial average

    X_S(rho) := (1/|S|) sum_{g in S} g^{-1} . P(g . rho),

where `g . rho` rotates the source domain and `g^{-1} . ( )` is the matrix-field
pullback. The weight `1/|S|` is not a supplied constant: it is the reciprocal of
the number of terms, so every `X_S` here is the uniform average with
group-determined weight over `S`. Re-measured by this cycle's runner from the
landed compiler, the exact equivariance group of `P` is the body-diagonal
stabilizer `D3` on frames `[1, 4, 9, 15, 18, 23]`, with element orders
`{1: 2, 4: 2, 9: 2, 15: 3, 18: 3, 23: 1}`.

**Theorem 1 (coset collapse, and containment exact for every source).** For `h`
in `D3` and any frame `g`,

    (h g)^{-1} . P(h g . rho) = g^{-1} . h^{-1} . P(h . (g . rho))
                              = g^{-1} . h^{-1} . h . P(g . rho)
                              = g^{-1} . P(g . rho),

the middle line being exactly `D3`-covariance of `P` applied to the source
`g . rho`. The summand therefore depends only on the right coset `D3 g`. There
are exactly four such cosets; write `t_[c](rho)` for the common value on coset
`[c]`. A partial average over a union `C` of right cosets equals its transversal
form `(1/|C|) sum_{[c] in C} t_[c]`, one term per coset.

Next, for `u` in `O`, substituting `k = g u`,

    X_S(u . rho) = (1/|S|) sum_{g in S} g^{-1} . P(g u . rho)
                 = u . (1/|S|) sum_{k in S u} k^{-1} . P(k . rho)
                 = u . X_{S u}(rho).

Right multiplication permutes the four right cosets, so `C u` is again a union
of cosets, and `X_C(u . rho) = u . X_C(rho)` whenever `C u = C` setwise. Writing
`Stab(C) := {u in O : C u = C}`, this gives `Stab(C)` contained in the
equivariance group of `X_C`, for **every** source and for any `D3`-covariant
pipeline. There is no tolerance in this direction: it is change of variables in
a finite group sum.

**Theorem 2 (the lattice).** Send the coset `[D3 g]` to the unsigned line
`phi([D3 g]) := axis(g^{-1} . d0)` through the image of the body diagonal
`d0 = (1,1,1)`. This is well defined because `D3` fixes `d0` up to sign, and it
is a bijection from the four cosets onto the four body diagonals; right
multiplication by `u` on cosets corresponds to `u^{-1}` acting on axes. Let
`pi(u)` be the permutation sending `[c]` to `[c u]`; then
`pi(u v) = pi(v) . pi(u)` under standard function composition, and the 24
permutations `pi(u)` are distinct, so the induced action is the full permutation
action on four letters. Under this dictionary the subset stabilizers are:

- `|C| = 1`. `Stab([D3 g0]) = g0^{-1} D3 g0`, of order six, since
  `D3 g0 u = D3 g0` holds exactly when `g0 u g0^{-1}` lies in `D3`. A conjugate
  of `D3`, hence isomorphic to it.
- `|C| = 2`. The setwise pair stabilizer has order four: the identity, the two
  elements that swap within each pair, and the element that swaps both pairs at
  once. All three nontrivial elements have rotation trace `-1`, so each is a
  half-turn; their axes are one coordinate axis and two face diagonals.
- `|C| = 3`. `Stab(C) = Stab(C^complement)`, again a conjugate of `D3` of order
  six.
- `|C| = 4`. All of `O`, order 24.

The profile by union size is therefore 6, 4, 6, 24, and complement duality
`Stab(C) = Stab(C^complement)` holds for every `C`.

**Theorem 3 (order bound).** No proper nonempty union of cosets has a stabilizer
of order above six. Enumerate all subgroups of the 24-frame group: start from
the cyclic subgroups generated by single elements, and repeatedly adjoin, for
every subgroup `H` already found and every element `g`, the subgroup generated
by `H` together with `g`, until nothing new appears. The fixpoint family is
complete. Certificate: were some subgroup missing, take a minimal missing `K`;
every proper subgroup of `K` is then in the family, so take a maximal proper
subgroup `H < K` and any `g` in `K` outside `H`. The subgroup generated by `H`
and `g` strictly contains `H` and lies in `K`, so by maximality of `H` it is `K`
itself — and the closure step applied to `H` and `g` would already have produced
it. Contradiction. The family has 30 members with order histogram
`{1:1, 2:9, 3:4, 4:7, 6:4, 8:3, 12:1, 24:1}`, so exactly five subgroups have
order above six: three of order 8, one of order 12, and the whole group. Each of
those five acts transitively on the four cosets, and a transitive group
stabilizes no proper nonempty subset. Hence no proper nonempty union of cosets
has `|Stab|` above six, and — given the equality statement below — the full
average is the one coset-union average that is `O`-equivariant.

The `|C| = 1` row reproduces, from this cycle's own machinery and nothing else,
the statement that a one-coset average is equivariant exactly under a conjugate
subgroup of the same order six: relocation of `D3`, not enlargement. This cycle
derives that row as the smallest case of a lattice rather than as a separate
result.

## Construction

`X_C` is assembled entirely from landed machinery. For each of the 24 frames the
source domain is rotated by the compiler's own decorated action, the landed
response and metric/coframe steps produce `h`, and the resulting matrix field is
pulled back under the pinned convention

    out[x] = R.T @ h_rotated[rotate_site(x, frame, L)] @ R,

with `R` the frame matrix and `rotate_site` the rotation of lattice sites about
the lattice centre `(L-1)//2`. Rotation about a centre is geometric only when
the centre is itself a lattice site, so the gates run at odd `L`, namely `L = 3`
and `L = 7`. The 24 per-frame results are cached once per source, and every
average is a linear combination of cached fields; `X_C` uses the cache entries
of the frames belonging to the cosets in `C`.

The source is the physical domain carrying a single link relabel adjacent to the
anchor, the same edited source as the reference equivariance battery, at
amplitude `0.20`. The edited source is deliberately not `O`-invariant, which is
what makes the equivariance gates discriminating. The defect of an average `X`
under a frame `u` is the maximum over lattice sites of the entrywise absolute
difference between `X` evaluated on the `u`-rotated source and the pushforward
under `u` of `X` evaluated on the base source. The rotated side is computed by
running the pipeline on the rotated source, never by substituting the
translation identity of Theorem 1, so the two sides of every comparison are
independent pipeline evaluations.

Equality of the measured equivariance set with `Stab(C)` needs a genericity
input, because the measured set could exceed `Stab(C)` only if the four coset
terms satisfied an accidental linear relation. When `C u` differs from `C`, the
two differ by one coset per side or by two cosets per side, so it is enough that
the six pairwise term distances and the three two-against-two split distances
stay away from zero. Both families are measured and printed, and both sit above
`3.6e-01`, more than eight orders of magnitude above the classification
threshold `1e-9`.

## The lattice in numbers

| union size `k` | number of unions | order of `Stab(C)` | structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 | 6 | conjugate of `D3`; conjugators `[0, 1, 2, 3]` |
| 2 | 6 | 4 | identity, two face-diagonal half-turns, one coordinate-axis half-turn |
| 3 | 4 | 6 | conjugate of `D3`, equal to the omitted singleton's stabilizer |
| 4 | 1 | 24 | all of `O` |

The profile is not monotone in the number of terms averaged. A two-coset average
is strictly less symmetric than a one-coset average: order six drops to order
four when a second coset is added. Adding the third coset restores order six,
and a three-coset average is exactly as symmetric as the one coset it omits —
that is complement duality, measured at `15/15` unions and proved for all `C`.
Only the fourth coset takes the group to all of `O`.

The order-four groups have a clean geometric reading. Each pair of body
diagonals is stabilized by the identity, by two half-turns about face diagonals,
and by one half-turn about a coordinate axis; all three nontrivial elements have
rotation trace `-1`, and the axis kinds measured over all six pairs are one
coordinate axis and two face diagonals every time. The `+1` eigenline of each
element is read off the column space of `R + I` and verified to be fixed by `R`.

The order ceiling is where the lattice bites. Five subgroups of the 24-frame
group have order above six — three of order 8, one of order 12, and the group
itself — and every one of them moves each body diagonal onto every other, so
none of them can stabilize a proper nonempty union. For every source and every
`D3`-covariant pipeline, the equivariance group that the averaging structure
itself guarantees is the stabilizer, and the stabilizer of a proper nonempty
union is therefore capped at order six. Anything beyond the stabilizer would
have to come from the pipeline, not from the average — from covariance beyond
`D3`, or from an accidental linear relation among the coset terms — and for the
landed compiler at the pinned source the measured battery finds nothing beyond
it, at `15/15` unions.

## What this does not establish

(a) Cycle 690's no-go quantifies over constructions that repeat one fixed
triangulation, and is untouched here. The one-coset case is such a
single-complex construction, and its order-six ceiling is consistent with that
no-go; the multi-coset averages repeat two to four complexes and sit outside
that quantification rather than contradicting it.

(b) Cycle 696's `achievable_covariance_scope = 6` rows are untouched and remain
correct for the single-complex pipeline. Nothing here re-scopes them.

(c) Improper rotations, of determinant `-1`, are out of scope. `O` here means
the 24 proper cubic rotations and nothing larger.

(d) Odd `L` only. The pullback convention is site-centred, so even sizes are not
covered by these gates.

(e) The containment direction is exact for every source; the equality direction,
that the measured equivariance set is exactly `Stab(C)`, is established at the
pinned source, amplitude `0.20`, and sizes `L = 3` and `L = 7`, justified by the
printed genericity witnesses `3.670333e-01` and `7.340666e-01`. A source with
extra symmetry would make coset terms coincide and would enlarge the measured
set; such a source is not covered.

(f) This cycle does not reunify Cycle 700's two carriers, and it does not
re-derive any Cycle-696 prediction row through an averaged carrier. That remains
open; what the lattice supplies is the map of which averages could carry which
group, and the order ceiling on any partial one's stabilizer.
