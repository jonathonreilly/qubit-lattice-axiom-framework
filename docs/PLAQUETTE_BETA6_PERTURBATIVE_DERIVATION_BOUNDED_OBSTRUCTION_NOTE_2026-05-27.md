## Statement (explicit runner-local-input diagnostic)

only as a residual comparator, never as a derivation input or downstream
value). Let `{w_n}_{n=1..16}` be the explicit runner-local coefficient packet of
the Wilson-plaquette perturbative expansion
```text
<P>(beta) = 1 - sum_{n>=1} w_n / beta^n .                              (1)
```
The runner consumes `w_1 = 4/9` and the decimal values listed in the
explicit-input license above. This note does not derive those entries and
does not license them outside this diagnostic.

**Conclusion (T1; truncated series saturation).** The truncated series
in (1) at `beta = 6`, evaluated at every finite truncation order
`N in {1, 2, ..., 16}` using the explicit runner-local coefficients listed in the runner,
saturates at
```text
<P>_PT(N=16; beta=6)  =  0.919331 ,                                    (2)
```
with the differences `|<P>_PT(N) - <P>_PT(N=16)|` already below `1e-4`
for `N >= 6` (a runner-pinned finite-range stabilization over the 16-term


**Conclusion (T2; tadpole-substitution fixed-point saturation).** Iterating
the runner-local mean-plaquette self-consistent substitution fixed point

plain 1-loop value `0.925926` to the `N = 1` fixed point `0.919435` (a

shift of `0.01538` (`1.66%`) against the plain 1-loop value at `beta = 6`.

single-rooted; the diagnostic scopes its statement to the high

the runner asserts convergence, the fixed-point equation residual, and the
