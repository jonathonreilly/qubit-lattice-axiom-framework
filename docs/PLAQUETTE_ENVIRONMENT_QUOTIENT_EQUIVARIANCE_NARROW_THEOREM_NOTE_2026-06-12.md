## Stabilizer Group

Work on the periodic spatial torus V = (Z/3Z)^3. The cost-verification note's
group is the affine signed-permutation group acting on sites,

```text
x -> A x + t mod 3,
```

where A is a 3 by 3 signed permutation matrix and t is a torus translation.
The marked plaquette is the (0,1)-plane plaquette based at (0,0,0). The
stabilizer G_stab is the subgroup whose affine action preserves both:

- the four marked plaquette vertices as a set;
- the four marked boundary links as an unoriented set.

## Equivariance Statement

At finite NMAX, let B_N be the admitted dominant-weight box
`0 <= p,q <= NMAX`. A complete network basis cell contains:

- one link label lambda_e in B_N for every spatial link e, with dualization
  lambda -> lambda* = (q,p) when the stabilizer action reverses the link;
- all internal intertwiner/multiplicity basis data kappa used by the
  plaquette coefficient tensors, link Haar projectors, and site/intertwiner
  contractions;
- the marked boundary sector data, when the environment is read as a boundary
  tensor rather than a scalar coefficient.

The Wilson character-expansion tensor network is G_stab-equivariant in this
finite-box sense. For every g in G_stab:

1. g permutes sites, links, and plaquettes of the L_s = 3 torus while
   preserving the marked plaquette setwise;
2. link labels are permuted and dualized exactly when the link orientation is
   reversed;
3. plaquette coefficient tensors are central/class-function tensors, so the
   cyclic order and reversed orientation are absorbed by the dual map;
4. Haar/projector and site/intertwiner contractions use the induced maps on
   intertwiner spaces.

Thus the scalar summand for a complete cell x = (lambda, kappa) is constant
on the complete G_stab orbit of x.

The exact quotient identity is therefore the finite orbit-sum identity:

```text
Z_N = sum_{x in X_N} a(x)
    = sum_{[x] in X_N/G_stab} |G_stab| / |Stab_G_stab(x)| * a(x_rep).
```

Equivalently, first choose a link-label orbit representative lambda. Let
H_lambda <= G_stab be its stabilizer, including dual-label action. The
internal tensor space K_lambda then carries an H_lambda representation. A
flattened exact quotient cell is an H_lambda-orbit representative kappa in
that internal basis, with weight

```text
|G_stab| / |Stab_G_stab(lambda, kappa)|.
```

In a symmetry-adapted tensor contractor, this same rule is implemented by
H_lambda-isotypic blocks on the relevant cuts. Terminal scalar data must land
in the trivial sector, but intermediate cuts can carry nontrivial sectors that
pair to a trivial final scalar. Dropping those blocks is not licensed by
equivariance alone.

## Obstruction To The Link-Orbit Diagnostic Quotient

The prior link-orbit diagnostic quotient assigns one variable to each link
orbit and one factor to each active plaquette orbit. That is a tied/fixed
carrier. It does not
enumerate complete G_stab orbits of independent link-label configurations,
and it does not carry the H_lambda action on intertwiner data.

The obstruction is therefore exact and local:

- orbit-tying keeps cells fixed by the stabilizer action rather than orbit
  representatives of the full independent-link state space;
- orientation-reversing stabilizer elements force `(p,q) <-> (q,p)` dual
  bookkeeping;
- internal intertwiner indices have their own stabilizer representation, so a
  one-label-per-link-orbit quotient is missing required isotypic data.

This verifies equivariance but does not license the link-orbit diagnostic
quotient as an exact L_s = 3 environment contraction.
