## Normalization Untangling

The landed source packet is a marked class-function compression:

```text

M_beta = exp[(beta/2) J].
```

Its three Hellmann-Feynman pieces are:

```text
d_beta log lambda_src = H_mark + H_Dloc + H_env.
```

Their current source-side meaning is:

- `H_mark`: derivative of the two marked half-slice spatial plaquette multipliers.
- `H_Dloc`: derivative of the local four marked-link mixed-kernel packet `a_(p,q)(beta)^4`.
- `H_env`: derivative of the compressed residual environment packet, when that packet is beta-dependent.

The important point is state dependence. `H_mark` is evaluated in the Perron state of the whole source packet being tested. Once `D_beta^loc` or `C_rho` is present, `H_mark` is not automatically the isolated one-plaquette derivative `P_1plaq`. That is exactly where the raw candidate identity fails below.
