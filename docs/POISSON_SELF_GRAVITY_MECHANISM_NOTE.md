## Hard Bar

A self-gravity mechanism on this exact family would need all of:

1. exact identity reduction at `epsilon = 0`;
2. matched-null stability under the same update machinery;
3. step-local and end-to-end Born checks that are both clean and converged;
4. a detector observable stronger than raw escape that remains nontrivial;
5. a stable converged nonzero-coupling loop with a non-tiny effect.

## What The Included Runs Support

The cached rows support a bounded control statement:

- the `epsilon = 0` reduction is exact in the main loop cache;
- frozen-field / step-local Born values stay at the cancellation floor;
- nonzero-coupling rows keep the weak-field TOWARD sign;
- the loop/instantaneous ratio and source-strength scaling remain near-linear;
- the V3 matched-null observables move in the expected signed direction.

These are useful finite controls.

## Why The Mechanism Bar Fails

The same cached rows also show the mechanism does not close:

- nonzero-coupling loop rows are not converged under the strict tolerance;
- end-to-end Born is diagnostic only and not machine-clean at nonzero coupling;
- the V3 matched-null effect remains tiny on the tested family;
- no stable converged nonzero-coupling regime with a non-tiny effect is present.

Therefore the exact-lattice family remains a bounded control/no-go for the
mechanism claim, not a positive self-gravity mechanism.
