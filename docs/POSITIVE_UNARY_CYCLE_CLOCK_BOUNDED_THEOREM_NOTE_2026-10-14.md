## 2. Positive smoothing and the exact sampled program

Periodically extend V0 on the circle of length S. For integer R>=1 define

    F_R(x)=|sum_(l=0)^R exp(i l omega x)|^2/[S(R+1)],
    V_R(x)=integral_circle F_R(y)V0(x-y)dy.                (3)

The kernel is nonnegative, normalized and of degree R. Thus V_R is Hermitian with norm at most c. Signed pulse logarithms, including their scalar parts, remain signed; no positivity of V_R is assumed. The finite jump measure gives

    integral_circle ||V0(x+y)-V0(x)||dx <= B_var |y|_S.     (4)

Each jump contributes its norm times the short-arc length. Positive convolution preserves (4). The first cosine moment of F_R is R/(R+1); combining `sin(pi|y|/S)>=2|y|/S` for |y|<=S/2 with Cauchy–Schwarz gives

    integral |y|_S F_R(y)dy <= S/sqrt[8(R+1)],
    integral_0^T ||V_R(t)-V0(t)||dt
          <= d_R=B_var S/sqrt[8(R+1)].                   (5)

    Vhat_(0,X)(0)=S^(-1)sum_(p:X_p=X)(b_p-a_p)K_p,

where the second line is for q!=0. Write A_q=sum_X A_(X,q); outside the band they are zero. Then `A_(-q)=A_q*` and `V_R(x)=sum_q exp(iq omega x)A_q`. These finite sums specify all actual sample matrices. No millions-of-pulses fixture is claimed emitted here.

## 3. The actual qubit clock and its full-space positivity

Use L additional qubits, with n_j=|1><1|, `sigma_j^+=|1><0|` and cyclic indices. Define

    b_j=[n_j+n_(j+1)+i(sigma_(j+1)^+sigma_j^-
                           -sigma_j^+sigma_(j+1)^-)]/(2a),
    K_C=sum_(j=0)^(L-1) b_j,       N_C=sum_j n_j,
    V=sum_j n_j tensor V_j,
    H_aut=I_C tensor H+K_C tensor I+V.                    (7)

In the vacuum and two-excitation blocks of a pair, b_j is respectively 0 and 1/a. In the ordered one-excitation pair basis (|10>,|01>) it is `[[1,-i],[i,1]]/(2a)`, with eigenvalues 0 and 1/a. Hence each b_j>=0 and `||b_j||=1/a`. It follows that

    K_C>=0 on all 2^L clock states,    ||K_C||<=L/a,
    [K_C,N_C]=[V,N_C]=0.                                  (8)

The vacuum has clock energy zero. This proves positivity by actual two-site terms rather than by a one-excitation spectral guess. It does not prove positivity of H_aut; for example negative H persists on the clock vacuum.

On the one-excitation space identify |j> with a head at qubit j, and let `T_L|j>=|j+1 mod L>`. Use

    |k>_L=L^(-1/2)sum_j exp(+2pi i k j/L)|j>,
    D_a=i(T_L-T_L*)/(2a),
    K_C|_(N_C=1)=I/a+D_a,
    D_a|k>_L=[sin(omega k a)/a]|k>_L.                     (9)

The sign follows from `T_L|k>_L=exp(-2pi i k/L)|k>_L`. Thus `0<=K_C|_1<=2I/a`. Reversing the hopping reverses the slope at k=0. A real-hopping phase gauge is possible when L is divisible by four, but its carrier phase must be applied to the state as well; the theorem uses (7) directly.

The sampled interaction restricts to `V_L=sum_j |j><j| tensor V_j`, with `||V_L||<=c`. On the full qubit space only the generally larger bound `||V||<=Lc` follows. All approximation estimates below use the exactly invariant one-excitation sector and its specified preparation. Arbitrary clock inputs are not covered.

For integer K>=1, d=2K+1 and L>2K, prepare independently

    chi_K=d^(-1/2)sum_(k=-K)^K |k>_L,
    chi_K(j)=[L d]^(-1/2)sum_(k=-K)^K exp(2pi i k j/L).    (10)

Orthogonality of the L Fourier modes proves exact normalization. This coherent one-head state needs L clean clock factors and a supplied state-preparation operation; it is not a single localized head and its synthesis is not supplied by the old neutral-ray preparation theorem. It is independent of the unknown data/reference input.

One explicit preparation cost can be charged under a separate addressable-control interface. Start from the supplied product vacuum on these L qubits and apply X to site 0. Write z_j=chi_K(j), and r_j=(sum_(l=j)^(L-1)|z_l|^2)^(1/2). For j=0,...,L-2, if r_j>0, apply the two-site unitary which fixes |00>,|11> and has one-head block

    G_j=[[alpha_j,-beta_j],[beta_j,alpha_j*]],
    alpha_j=z_j/r_j,       beta_j=r_(j+1)/r_j>=0.

It maps r_j|j> to z_j|j>+r_(j+1)|j+1>. The two columns are orthonormal because |alpha_j|^2+beta_j^2=1. If r_j=0, all remaining target amplitudes vanish and the identity is used. A final one-site phase z_(L-1)/r_(L-1), or identity when that residual is zero, completes exactly chi_K. Earlier amplitudes are unchanged because their tokens see |00> on the operated pair. Induction proves the state rather than merely its populations. Every pair is an ordinary perimeter neighbor, with the closing edge unused. The z_j and their squared magnitudes are algebraic root-of-unity expressions, so these zero branches and coefficients are effective for finite integer L.

This is at most L+1 sequential one-/two-qubit gates. A specified Hermitian logarithm of each unitary has norm at most pi. Under supplied arbitrary addressed one-/two-qubit generators of norm J_p>0, external timing, and removal or exact compensation of the clock/preparation drift, each gate can therefore be implemented in time pi/J_p, for total T_p<=(L+1)pi/J_p and depth L+1. ## 4. Strict ordinary compression and the two finite buffers

Choose integer r>=1, put `B=K+Rr`, and require

    L>2B+R.                                               (11)

Represent the band -B,...,B by its distinct grid Fourier modes; denote this isometry by J_B. In the continuum Fourier space let P=-i d/dx and Pi_B be ordinary projection onto that band. The matrix entry of the sampled potential is

    <k|V_L|l>=sum_(q=-R)^R A_q
          [L^(-1)sum_j exp(2pi i(q+l-k)j/L)].              (12)

The bracket is one when q+l-k is a multiple of L and zero otherwise. For |k|,|l|<=B the magnitude is at most R+2B<L. The only multiple is zero, so

    J_B* V_L J_B=Pi_B V_R(X) Pi_B=:V_B.                   (13)

This is ordinary Toeplitz compression, with no cyclic boundary replacement. The strict inequality is material: B=3,R=2,L=8 and V_R(x)=cos(2 omega x) give entry 1/2 between +3 and -3 on the ring, but zero in ordinary compression.

On the finite band, define analysis generators

    G_B^lin=I tensor H+diag_(|k|<=B)(omega k) tensor I+V_B,
    G_B^sin=I tensor H+diag_(|k|<=B)(sin(omega k a)/a) tensor I+V_B.

Their norm difference is at most

    delta_B=a^2(omega B)^3/6,                              (14)

since `|sin z-z|<=|z|^3/6` for every real z. Finite-dimensional unitary Duhamel therefore gives `||exp(-itG_B^sin)-exp(-itG_B^lin)||<=t delta_B`. No exponential in hT appears; H cancels in the difference and need not commute with V_B.

There are two distinct outside-band comparisons. First compare the continuum generator `G_infinity=H+P+V_R(X)` to G_B^lin, starting in modes [-K,K]. In the interaction picture of H+P, each insertion changes momentum by at most R and has norm at most c. Every prefix of an order-j word for j<=r stays in [-K-jR,K+jR] inside [-B,B]. The free propagators preserve these modes. Thus the infinite and ordinary-band word coefficients agree through order r. Both norm-convergent interaction series have order-j norm at most `(ct)^j/j!`, so their prepared-isometry difference is at most

    2 E_r(T),     E_r(T)=sum_(j>=r+1)(cT)^j/j!.            (15)

Second compare the actual ring generator `H+D_a+V_L` to G_B^sin. A sampled Fourier insertion changes k by q modulo L. The same prefix bound, together with B<L/2 from (11), ensures that no word of order at most r wraps around the ring. Its free sine phases and data phases are identical to those in the band. The two series again have norm bound c and give another `2 E_r(T)`. This comparison uses the actual cyclic clock, but its verified prefixes are ordinary unwrapped ones. The large full-qubit norm Lc is irrelevant inside the exactly invariant prepared sector; it cannot be used to certify arbitrary leakage errors later.

All statements are operator bounds from the data space into the respective clock/data spaces. Embeddings of the common finite band are isometries. It is unnecessary, and would be incorrect, to treat evaluation of an arbitrary infinite Fourier vector on L points as an isometry.
