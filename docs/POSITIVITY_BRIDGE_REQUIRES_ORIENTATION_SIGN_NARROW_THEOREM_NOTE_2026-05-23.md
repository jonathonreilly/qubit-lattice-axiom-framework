## Question

The positivity/orientation program needs a "reality cannot be negative"
constraint to break the (physically unbroken) `S_3` axis-symmetry on the hw=1
generation triplet down to `C_3`. The natural candidate is the retained
staggered determinant positivity `staggered_only_det_positivity_case_a_note`
(`det(M_KS + mI) > 0`). Does that positivity actually do the breaking?

## Answer

**No — the determinant-magnitude positivity route does not close.** The
staggered determinant is `S_3`-invariant (reflection-even), so it is a
trivial-representation constraint and breaks nothing. Breaking `S_3 → C_3 = A_3`
requires coupling to the **sign (orientation) representation** of `S_3`; the only
"reality cannot be negative" constraint that achieves it is a **handedness /
orientation-sign positivity** — the sign of the volume form / Cl(3) pseudoscalar
— which is a different object from the determinant magnitude.

## Theorem (representation-theoretic obstruction + selection)

`S_3` has exactly two one-dimensional representations: the trivial rep and the
sign rep `sgn`. A binary ("positive/negative") constraint that is a class
function on `S_3` can preserve only a union of `sgn`-level sets.

**(A) Determinant-magnitude positivity is the trivial rep.** The massless
staggered Dirac operator `D` is anti-Hermitian, so `det(D + mI) = ∏_i(m² + σ_i²)
> 0` for every configuration. Axis permutations act by gauge-equivalent
relabelings (see `STAGGERED_AXIS_SYMMETRY_IS_S3_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-23.md`),
so `det(D + mI)` is identical under the identity, a transposition, and a 3-cycle
(verified: `Δlog det = 0` exactly, sign `+1` throughout). Hence
`det`-positivity is constant on `S_3` (trivial rep) and **selects all of `S_3`**
— it cannot break `S_3 → C_3`.

**(B) Orientation sign is the sign rep, and selects exactly `C_3`.**
`sgn(σ) = det(ρ_perm(σ))` equals `+1` on the identity and the two 3-cycles and
`−1` on the three transpositions. The subset `{σ : sgn(σ) = +1}` is exactly
`A_3 = C_3`. Hence a constraint requiring the orientation sign to be positive —
a handedness selection — breaks `S_3 → C_3` and does so uniquely among 1-dim
constraints.

**Conclusion.** The `S_3 → C_3` breaker must couple to the orientation (sign)
representation. Determinant-magnitude positivity (trivial rep) cannot supply it;
an orientation-sign / handedness positivity can and must. ∎
