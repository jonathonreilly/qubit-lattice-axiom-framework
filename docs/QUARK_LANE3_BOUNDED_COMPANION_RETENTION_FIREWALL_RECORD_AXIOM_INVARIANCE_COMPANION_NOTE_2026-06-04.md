## The Record axiom is not used by the load-bearing firewall structure

The Record axiom (`MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-04.md` §"Record") says:

> When a finite record-readout surface is specified, its scalar record
> functional is additive over disjoint record collections:
>
>     I(R_1 sqcup R_2) = I(R_1) + I(R_2)
>
> with `I(empty) = 0` after an explicit additive-baseline convention.

The parent's load-bearing structural content does the following:

1. **Negative-boundary accounting.** "Absent down-type bridge/scale,
   up-type partition/amplitude, and species-differentiated Yukawa
   premises, the existing packet remains bounded companion support."
   This is a logical-implication firewall: it specifies what premises
   would *unblock* retention and asserts none of them is currently
   supplied. No record functional `I(.)` appears in the statement or
   its proof; the implication is closed by the absence of the named
   premises in the cited support notes.

2. **Ratio invariance under bottom-anchor rescaling.** The down-type
   ratios are written as functions of `alpha_s(v)`. Re-scaling the
   bottom mass `m_b -> lambda * m_b` for any `lambda > 0` rescales
   `m_s, m_d` by the same `lambda` (by the ratio chain), preserving
   all three down-type ratios but moving the absolute masses. This
   is a one-line algebraic invariance, with no record functional or
   measurement-collection structure entering.

3. **Comparator no-go on species-uniform Ward reuse.** Applying the
   top-channel Ward `y_t = 1/sqrt(6) * g_s` to the bottom species
   with the same prefactor predicts a Yukawa that, after the
   canonical-surface bridge to the SM Yukawa parameter, would land
   roughly `34.7x` above the PDG `m_b = 4.180 GeV`. This is a
   single multiplicative-comparator check on observed-vs-predicted
   ratios; no record functional `I(.)` is invoked, and no
   record-readout collection structure is needed.

4. **CKM-closure vs mass-closure separation.** The CKM atlas supplies
   structural mixing magnitudes but does not pin any individual mass
   anchor. This is a pure-logical type-distinction statement: a
   theorem about a 3-by-3 unitary's structure does not entail any
   absolute scale on the diagonal of the mass matrix. No record
   functional appears.

Items 1-4 are fixed by:

- counting index structure on the bilinear surface (Lattice + Quantum
  content);
- standard ratio / homogeneous-degree arithmetic (mathematical
  infrastructure);
- multiplicative comparator factors with PDG inputs as comparator-only;
- logical-implication firewall structure on the open derivation-target
  premise (staggered-Dirac realization gate).

The Record axiom adds an additive scalar record functional. It does
not modify (and is not modified by) the index structure, the ratio
algebra, the comparator no-go arithmetic, or the logical-firewall
implication. So the firewall conclusion is invariant under the
axiom-set change.

This invariance is what the companion runner verifies block-by-block:
every load-bearing arithmetic / logical-firewall check passes using
only Lattice + Quantum content, standard ratio algebra, PDG
comparator constants, and the upstream open-gate premise; and a
"Record-axiom counterfactual" block confirms that the firewall
conclusion is unchanged whether or not a Record-axiom statement is
appended.
