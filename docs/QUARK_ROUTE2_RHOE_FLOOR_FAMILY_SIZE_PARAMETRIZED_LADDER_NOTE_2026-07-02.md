## 6. Honest Read And Boundary

The size-parametrized floor family preserves the size-15 anchor and passes
T-isotropy on every computed row, but it does not produce an admissible
convergence tail. Sizes 17, 19, and 21 are excluded before tail comparison
because the floor element is not smooth on the exposed E-shell basis component;
they also fail the `q_T` admission window.

This note does not derive `rho_E`, does not prove an infinite-size limit, and
does not change any audited row. It records a finite ladder and a named
functional obstruction in the extremal floor readout. The next path this opens
is a non-extremal tensor-response primitive, or an independent derivation of why
the size-15 smooth floor value is the physically selected one.
