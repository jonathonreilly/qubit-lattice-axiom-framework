## 10. Preserved narrow failure routes

These examples test specific tempting relaxations. They do not refute the
stated conditional theorem or imply a universal no-go.

* **Omitting full D drift.** Let K=omega|1><1| on an untouched D qubit, all
  other static terms zero, and input D=|+>. It commutes with every control.
  The actual D output is exp(-iKT)|+>, whereas the unaged comparator is |+>;
  their trace distance is 2|sin(omega T/2)|. An old F-only bound B=0 would
  miss this. The new complete B>=omega correctly pays it.
* **Dropping baseline scalar phase.** For K=aI, the state channel is unchanged
  by drift but U_actual=e^-iaT U_word. The operator difference is
  2|sin(aT/2)|. This separates phase-sensitive isometry and channel claims;
  it does not license deleting a physical reserve from resource accounting.
* **Inferring exact switch-off from small timing error.** The explicitly
  translated final-pulse calculation in section 7 leaves a nonzero field at
  the nominal endpoint. Repricing time or guards is necessary.
* **Using a one-excitation exchange as full SWAP.** The exchange unitary that
  maps |10> to -i|01> and fixes |00> maps (|00>+|10>)/sqrt(2) to
  (|00>-i|01>)/sqrt(2), rather than the full-SWAP output. Their trace distance
  is sqrt(2); the relative phase cannot be removed by one common scalar.
  The supplied full-SWAP/ZZ dictionary avoids this failure.
* **Inferring fault slew/work allowance from L1 calibration.** For a fixed
  nonnegative smooth compact envelope f and positive projector P, faults
  N^-1/2 f(t)(1+sin Nt)P have vanishing integrated norm but unbounded total
  variation as N grows. They can vanish at both endpoints and remain positive.
  This defeats a fault-variation inference, not the finite endpoint work bound.
* **Dropping raw retained marginal terms.** The corrected source's positive
  SWAP-overrotation witness can be embedded in the first root-to-E_1 swap.

  although beta=||K-aI||=0 and every centered moment is zero. Later complete
  endpoint words restore their transit factors; the retained-record defect
  is not erased by refusing to look at E. The corrected identities (G) and
