## Result up front

The Record axiom makes readout content-determined and additive over
disjoint records. This cycle asks whether the two candidate formation
readings are readout-DISTINGUISHABLE on the landed histories — and
they are, generically:

- **the stamp/content census** (replaying the certified 852 census,
  748 keys, horizon 51,115; snapshots captured at every stamp):
  stamped 182 (E1) / 114 (E2) with E2 ⊆ E1; of the 114 both-stamped
  universes, 34 stamp at the same moment, 49 at different moments on
  IDENTICAL content, and **31 on genuinely different content** — the
  same universe locks a different record under the two readings;
  68 universes stamp under E1 only; 76 distinct content classes in
  all (independently reproduced by the checker, digest-exact);
- **the separation theorem**: readings disagree in readout on some
  landed history for every content-determined value function outside
  an exact agreement kernel — the constraint closure (v = 0 on all 68
  E1-only contents; v equal across the 31 split pairs) forces 6 of
  the 48 E2 record contents to zero and leaves 42 free: a nontrivial
  agreeing readout EXISTS, but only as a constraint-satisfying
  exception; witness: key (2, 0, (0, 2)) — any v nonzero on its
  record content gives I_E1 != 0 = I_E2 on that single history;
- **the naturalness probe (checker, constructive)**: all four natural
  candidates — record counting (v = 1), content Hamming weight,
  payload-register weight, and the single-source sector's
  direction-derived convention extended content-determinedly — lie
  OUTSIDE the agreement kernel. No natural readout makes the readings
  agree;
- **the corpus gap made explicit**: no multi-source scalar value
  function is implemented anywhere on the pinned surfaces — the
  landed corpus evaluates I only on the single-source sector, so this
  analysis legitimately ranges over ALL admissible value functions.

**What this changes**: the Cycle-828 audit's "the difference is
