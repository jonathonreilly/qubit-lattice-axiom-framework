## Claim

A form generated by a linear functional on the observable algebra,
`<X,Y> = phi(X^dagger Y)`, satisfies the associativity (Frobenius) condition

```text
<u v, t> = <v, u^dagger t>     for all u, v, t.
```

**T1 (circulant cone).** On Hermitian circulants `u = a_u I + b_u C + conj(b_u) C^2`
with the general `C_3`-invariant cone form `<u,v> = g0 a_u a_v + g1 (b_u . b_v)`,
the associativity residual is not identically zero, every nontrivial constraint
it produces is the *same* line, and the solution set is exactly the ray

```text
g1 = 2 g0        i.e.   (g0, g1) proportional to (3, 6) = the Hilbert-Schmidt ray.
```

**T2 (ambient restatement).** On `Herm(3)` with the canonical Jordan product
`A o B = (AB+BA)/2`, the `alpha`-part `Tr(AB)` is associative on every tested
Hermitian triple including genuinely complex ones, and the `beta`-part
`tr(A)tr(B)` is not, with an explicit exact witness. Hence `B_{alpha,beta}` is
associative **iff `beta = 0`**. This is the same statement as T1.

**T3 (group-free strengthening).** In eigenvalue coordinates a Hermitian
circulant is a real triple with a *pointwise* product, and a form is a Gram
matrix. Associativity forces that Gram to be diagonal with **no invariance
assumption used at all**; adding only scalar/traceless orthogonality — one of the
three conditions the standing no-go already grants — forces the diagonal entries
equal, giving the counting measure on eigenvalue slots, `sum_j lam_j lam'_j =
Tr(H H')`. The Hilbert-Schmidt ray is reached twice, by independent routes.

## Premise classification — this is a readout bridge, not free structure

Two propositions must be kept apart:

1. **`Herm(3)` carries a distinguished associative form, unique up to positive
   scale.** This is a theorem with zero premise cost, and it is what T1-T3
   establish.
2. **The physical readout bilinear form *is* that form.** This is **not**
   derivable from Lattice, Qubit, Admissibility, or Record.

This note claims (1) and **supplies** (2) as a named condition. Record's readout
clause gives a *scalar* `I`, additive over finite pairwise-disjoint records with
`I(empty)=0`; there is a typing gap between that and a bilinear form on an
observable algebra, and no axiom sentence closes it. Associativity is strictly
stronger than the Qubit axiom's no-privilege/naturality test — every member of
the Ad-invariant family passes that test — and its entire marginal content is one
dimensionless ratio. Per
[`docs/ai_methodology/skills/PRIMITIVE_REGISTRY_CHECK.md`](ai_methodology/skills/PRIMITIVE_REGISTRY_CHECK.md)
item 5, a normalization rule or readout bridge remains separate unless
independently derived.

Accordingly (2) is recorded here as an explicit **supplied readout bridge**
awaiting owner input, not as use of supplied structure, and not as a primitive
proposal.
