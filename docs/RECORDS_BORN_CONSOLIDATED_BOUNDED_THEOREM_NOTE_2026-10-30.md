## What this theme found

# Records and Born probabilities: synthesis

Question: can an actuality and record rule preserve Born statistics and yield consistent readable histories?

All statements concern supplied finite models and candidate laws as each note states them (zero premise weight). No law is adopted. Kept results: #9873, #9893, #9930, #9995, #10054.

**What held.** One actual configuration moved by a transport that uses only nonzero elements of each half-tick, changes the fewest modes and, among those, has maximal entropy, with records that never condition the odds, keeps the Born law at every half-tick in the supplied models. It gives no Zeno effect, full fringes, agreeing readers, no signalling of single-time marginals and CHSH 2 sqrt 2 (#9893, #9930; #9873 reports the same kinds of result for minimal switching on supplied 1D walks and few-qubit models). Bare which-path records coexist with full fringes, so they cannot be read as path knowledge; a coupled pointer gives V = cos(alpha). Bare two-time records depend on the distant setting (total variation 0.2183 in the supplied model; the two sides share half-ticks of one joint update, with no space-like separation claimed); after a pointer copy the two-time law equals the Born law of (pointer, outcome). Recording every site leaves the tested odds-level predictions unchanged in the supplied toys (#9995), under the fewest-change LP plan without the entropy tie-break. A history-based version reproduces the readable statistics in toys (#10054).

**What failed.** Proportional shortfall switching does not keep Born on one Margolus block (#9893). The stated reason for excluding bound sites from records is contradicted in the supplied ring (#9995). History actuality has no decoherence functional for the chiral weighting, which has no known tick form (#10054).

**Open.** Which sites are recorded is untested by any readable distribution (#9995). Set selection is not settled, only narrowed: the selector works for a three-site redundant record (finite optimisation, Elby-Bub uniqueness supplied) and fails for a bare two-site copy (#10054).

**Not resting on kept results.** Logged PRs also report that turn deposits are not fixed by a relay's own odds (#9866) and that burn-and-turn deposits miss the stress on the Margolus tick (#9885); these concern the gravity source. All results are toy-scale.

### #9873: Formation law diagnostics, records never condition the odds

**Statement (scope, verbatim):** "Finite diagnostics on supplied lossless 1D walks and few-qubit couplings of the candidate formation law that records never condition the odds, with relays following the joint odds by minimal switching: Born statistics, kept drift, agreeing pointer readings, no signalling of outcome marginals, CHSH above 2, kept interference and undisturbed free crossing, against four conditioning alternatives, plus one failure of the turn deposit under a remote basis choice; no physical identification, measurement theory or adoption of the law is established."

Numbers from the note:
- One walker. The flow image of the odds equals the odds after 100 ticks to 4.3e-16, and sampled relays match the Born histogram with chi2/dof 1.412 over 76 bins. The Born spread is 54.1 sites, ballistic, against about 10 for a classical walk. The unconditioned odds keep the drift (shift over v T = 0.9845 at v = 0.8926), and relays carry it (351.79 against 351.52 sites). Conditioning on the record every tick keeps a drift ratio of only 0.111, and conditioning at a rate twice the energy (0.447 per tick) keeps 0.466 by T = 400.
- Pointers, singlets, interference. With an ideal pointer coupling, the reading frequency is the Born weight (0.3024 against 0.3), and joint switching gives zero disagreement between two readers, while marginal-only switching gives 0.4188 against 2p(1-p) = 0.42. For a singlet the exact odds give CHSH 2 sqrt 2 = 2.828427 and relay readings give 2.841; B's outcome odds are independent of A's setting to 5.6e-17, and sampled B frequencies are 0.5060 and 0.5006. In an interferometer the law keeps full fringe visibility for both odds and relays, while conditioning on branches of disjoint support gives 1/2 at every phase. Two free walkers crossing leave walker 1's momentum odds unchanged (8.9e-17), while conditioning when relays meet disturbs them (total variation 0.303 at met fraction 0.42).
- Failure of the turn deposit. B's position and direction odds are independent of A's basis at every tick, to 5.6e-17, so the burn density is unchanged. But B's expected turn count at its coupling tick depends on A's basis: 0 with no measurement, 0.3536 in z, 0 in x. The z value equals the sum over A branches of the change in B's odds, sin(pi/4)/2 = 0.353553. ### #9893: Support-confined relay transport on the Margolus tick

**Statement (scope, verbatim):** "In supplied models on the spin-3/2 Margolus tick (one relay on 12^3 and 8^3 tori, two entangled fermion relays on 4^3, a three-block ring with relay number free, and two-block CHSH and reader models), a transport of the configuration distribution that uses only nonzero elements of each half-tick, moves the fewest modes and is of maximal entropy among those keeps Born statistics exactly, gives agreeing readers and CHSH 2 sqrt 2, and is unique, label-equivariant and separable; it also splits some plane waves into sublattice streams of different velocity and revisits burnt sites. Content of the note:
- The register's switching rule (proportional shortfall switching among connected targets) does not keep Born statistics on one Margolus block: the worst inflow error is 0.317. A transport that uses only nonzero elements of the half-tick always exists. For any target set S, P_S U = P_S U P_{N^-1(S)}, so with U unitary ||P_S U psi||^2 <= ||P_{N^-1(S)} psi||^2. That is the supply-demand (Hall) condition, and max-flow/min-cut then gives a plan. Its largest excess over all 254 sets is 5.6e-16. The earlier "minimal switching" of the straight-pairing picture also keeps Born, but only by sending 32 to 100 per cent (mean 68 per cent) of each half-tick's switched mass along zero elements of W.
- The law tested: the configuration moves by the plan that (i) uses only nonzero elements, staying included, (ii) has row sums equal to the old odds and column sums equal to the new odds, (iii) changes the fewest mode occupations on average, and (iv) has the largest entropy among such plans.
- Clause (iii) alone never gives a single plan. The optimal face has dimension 10 for every state at mu = 0, where no stays are allowed, and dimension at least 1 at mu = 0.3. An LP vertex depends on the labels (95 per cent of cases). Clause (iv) gives one plan. It is equivariant under relabelling (to 1.7e-16) and under translation, and it is continuous (|dJ|/|dpsi| <= 0.72). Least squares is also unique and equivariant, but it couples unentangled relays (5.4e-3), while max entropy does not (1.9e-15). Counting the whole configuration as staying, rather than counting modes, also couples unentangled relays (0.077).

- Relays jump on 53 to 100 per cent of half-ticks and zigzag. In 5 of 8 (K along x) and 4 of 8 (K along xy) mu = 0.3 plane waves, the per-tick chain splits into two sublattice streams that never mix, for example at 1.507 and 0.513 site/tick against a group velocity of 0.839. ### #9930: Records that never condition the odds give no Zeno effect, full fringes and CHSH 2 sqrt 2, while bare two-time records need a pointer

**Statement (scope, verbatim):** "For the actuality law's records (one actual configuration moved by the fewest-change, greatest-entropy transport; every matter mode's occupation recorded each half-tick; records never condition the odds), in supplied few-register models with one fermion per disjoint mode set: the record law is the Born law at every half-tick, so a record every half-tick neither freezes a slow transfer (no Zeno effect) nor removes interferometer fringes; each actual track carries a definite bare which-path record together with full fringes, so bare records cannot be readable without violating complementarity, while a pointer the update couples to the path gives V = cos(alpha); a register the half-tick acts on only as a control keeps its occupation, so a coin record fixes the setting; CHSH from records with locally recorded settings reaches 2 sqrt 2 and single-time record marginals carry no signal; the joint law of one side's bare records at two half-ticks depends on the distant setting (total variation 0.2183 in the supplied model, generic over random settings), while after a pointer copy the two-time law equals the Born law of (pointer, outcome) at one half-tick, the quantum sequential law. Hence past records can enter a later reading only through pointers. Finite supplied models; no physical identification of the registers."

Statement of the note (in supplied models with one fermion per disjoint mode set, a register):
1. Records never condition the odds, so the law of the records at every half-tick is the Born law of the odds at that half-tick. A record every half-tick does not freeze a slow transfer: there is no Zeno effect.
2. In a two-configuration transfer whose Born probability rises monotonically, the fewest-change transport moves each actual track exactly once; the odds keep the superposition.
3. A two-path interferometer recorded at its outputs gives visibility 1 with P(D1) = cos^2(phi/2) up to the splitter phase.
4. Every actual track also passes through a definite arm, so it carries a definite bare which-path record, and the fringes conditioned on that record are still full. If bare records were readable, path knowledge K = 1 with V = 1 would give V^2 + K^2 = 2, above the measured complementarity bound 1. Bare records are therefore not readable as which-path knowledge.
5. A pointer register that the update couples to the path by an angle alpha removes fringes in the odds and in the records: V = cos(alpha), V^2 + D^2 = 1. Sorting by a rotated pointer record (an eraser) restores fringes and anti-fringes with V = 1.
6. A register that a half-tick acts on only as a control keeps its occupation: a coin record taken before the setting-controlled rotations equals the coin's later occupation, so the coin record is the setting.
7. CHSH from detector-mode records, with settings chosen by local coin records and the two sides' steps in the same half-ticks, reaches the quantum value 2 sqrt 2. Single-time record marginals at one side do not depend on the other side's setting.
8. The joint law of one side's own bare records at two half-ticks (a half-rotated detector and the final outcome) depends on the distant side's setting. This is generic over random settings, and it disappears when the rotations are completed in one half-tick, so that A has nothing to move at the final half-tick.
9. After a pointer copy of the detector occupation in the half-tick right after the first of the two records, the two-time records equal the Born law of (pointer, outcome) at one half-tick: the quantum sequential law, with no dependence on the distant setting.

### #9995: Recording every site changes no odds-level prediction, and no record of one half-tick empties a support

**Statement (scope, verbatim):** "Under the actuality law (one actual configuration, distributed as its odds and moved by the exact fewest-change transport; records never condition the odds), three candidate record rules are compared: (i) bound sites excluded, as now; (ii) every site recorded each half-tick, rishons and edge registers included; (iii) as (ii), but a record that would leave some site with an empty admissible support cannot form. Theorems: the actual configuration always lies in the odds' support, so no set of records of one half-tick leaves any site without a possibility, and the clause in (iii) never blocks a record (0/12000 formations, every order); a value forced by records equals the actual value; in a gauge-invariant state the record law of colour-component occupations is the same in every gauge-rotated basis. Finite diagnostics: on a U(1) quantum-link ring (4 matter + 8 rishon modes, Gauss sector dimension 7, K = 4000 histories, 10 half-ticks), matter records are bit-identical under (i), (ii) and (iii), joint records are Born (max z 2.5) and every recorded pattern satisfies Gauss's law (0/44000 violations); one rishon record fixes its link partner (4000/4000); one free matter record already fixes another, otherwise open, site through Gauss's law (1777/4000), so the justification of (i) fails on matter; latest records mixing half-ticks give empty supports (6751/10000), current ones never do; with every link site recorded an emitter decays at the Wigner-Weisskopf rate whatever the half-tick (0.189 at dt 0.05 and 0.02, 2g^2/J = 0.180); a single photon through a link-site Mach-Zehnder keeps V = 1.000, arm records never coincide (g2(0) = 0); repeated link-number records agree (1.000) with |<a>| unchanged, and a dispersive pointer in the odds gives the decoherence (0.805 to 0.569); a recorded oscillator mediator gives the gravity-mediated concurrence 0.1563 (inner phase 0.791 rad, dphi 0.314). Contrast: if records conditioned the odds, matter frequencies would move 0.45 off Born, decay would be Zeno-frozen (2p lifetime ~3e16 s at a Planck half-tick, estimate), fringes would vanish (V 0.005) and the mediated concurrence would fall to 0.0075. Toy models and couplings are supplied; no 3D lattice, no CL-1 cube tick, no non-abelian dynamics."

Theorems of the note (exact):
- Support. The transport uses only transitions along nonzero elements of the half-tick, and it carries the odds to the odds. So every actual configuration it reaches has nonzero odds. The records of one half-tick copy that configuration. Locking any subset of them therefore leaves every site at least one possibility: the actual value. "No possibility" never arises. Read as a formation rule, the clause in (iii) never blocks a record; the two readings of "or no possibility" (forbidden, or allowed but inconsistent) cannot be told apart because neither ever applies.
- Forced values. If the records leave some site only one possibility, that value is the site's actual value. It is a definite value fixed by records and the rule, but it is not a record: "Records form", whereas this value is inferred. Its readout law is the Born law of the union of recorded and forced sites. Whether it counts as a record has no effect on any readable distribution.
- Record-set independence. The odds change only by the update (CL-1 together with CL-3's phase), and the transport reads odds alone. So the actual histories, and with them the values that every rule records, do not depend on which sites are recorded. All three candidates give the same matter record law, bit for bit. The only difference between them is the readable set.
- Gauge safety. If a state is invariant under a gauge rotation g, the Born law of the occupations in the g-rotated basis equals the law in the original basis (singlet 2e-16; non-singlet control 0.58).

Finite diagnostics (U(1) quantum-link ring with 4 matter sites and 8 rishons, Gauss sector (2,1,2,1) of dimension 7, w = 1, m = 0.5, dt = 0.35; 10 half-ticks; 4000 histories; exact LP transport): matter records max z 1.71 and joint records 2.54; recorded patterns violate Gauss's law 0 times in 44000; one rishon record fixes its link partner (4000/4000); a matter record plus one rishon record fixes the other rishon (4000/4000); matter records alone fix 25896 of 32000 rishon values, and every forced value equals the actual value; a single record of a free matter site leaves some other, otherwise open, site one possibility in 1777/4000 samples through Gauss's law, so candidate (i)'s reason ("a lock fixes no other site") would exclude matter as well; latest records mixing half-ticks give empty supports in 6751/10000 samples, current records none in 10000; if records conditioned the odds, matter frequencies would move off Born by 0.47 with every site recorded and by 0.45 with matter only recorded.

Photons (every link site recorded at every step): an emitter on a chain (g = 0.3, J = 1) decays at the fitted rate 0.189 against 2g^2/J = 0.180, for dt = 0.05 and dt = 0.02 alike (if records conditioned the odds, 0.0045 and 0.0018); a single photon in a Mach-Zehnder of link sites gives V = 1.000 (conditioning gives V = 0.005); arm records never coincide (0/24000) so g2(0) = 0; repeated link-number records agree at every half-tick (1.000) and |<a>| is unchanged (0.805); a dispersive pointer coupled in the odds takes 0.805 to 0.569 at a phase of pi/2 per photon.

Mediated entanglement: an oscillator mediator recorded at each of 200 half-ticks per period stays indefinite mid-run (P(n>0) = 0.0475), returns (overlap 1.000000) and leaves the two branch qubits with concurrence 0.1563 (target |sin(dphi/2)| = 0.1563, inner phase 0.791 rad, dphi = 0.314 for 1e-14 kg, 200 um, 250 um and 2.5 s). With conditioning records the concurrence is 0.0075.

Estimate against measurement: with records conditioning the odds at a Planck half-tick, the hydrogen 2p decay rate would be suppressed by about t_P (c/a0)/(2 pi), giving a lifetime of about 3e16 s, against the measured 1.60 ns. The note states that the measured lifetime selects "records never condition the odds", not a record set.

Assessment in the note: all three candidates tie on Born statistics, photon counting, Gauss's law in the actual configuration, and the gravity-entanglement phase, because no law reads records. Candidate (i) fails its own reason (it excludes matter too), needs a supplied set form (|A| <= 2) and a tolerance, and in a compact universe binds every site (from #9989's covering artefact; #9989 does not claim a closed-universe version). Candidate (iii) ties with (ii) and adds an idle clause. The note recommends (ii) in the form "records copy the actual configuration". Proposed CL-2 text (a proposal): "Each site then gets a permanent record of its occupation in the actual configuration: a new site's first record comes at the half-tick after it appears, and a removed site keeps its records." Proposed replacement of the Record axiom's wording is also given in the note. Caveat in the note: these results follow from "records never condition the odds" and hold under (i) too; "the only content that distinguishes (ii) from (i) is the readable set, which no measurement tests. That part is UNTESTED." The axiom bent: Record ("a site never carries more than one record"), inherited from CL-2's one record per site per half-tick. Not derived: a 3D cube-tick version; non-abelian dynamics.

The recommendation of rule (ii) is a candidate proposal.

### #10054: History actuality reproduces the readable Born odds and which-way complementarity, but needs a tick form the chiral weighting lacks

Results of the note:
- Born (exact). A single-time partition always decoheres (Tr(P_a rho P_b) = 0, a != b) with D(a,a) = Born, for any state; so CL-2's "distributed as its odds" is the single-time content of history actuality.
