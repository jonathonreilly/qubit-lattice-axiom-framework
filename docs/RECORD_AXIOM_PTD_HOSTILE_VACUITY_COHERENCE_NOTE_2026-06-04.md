## The candidate under test (the WEAKER, multi-lane form)

> "A record is an irreversible registration of which **real (CPT-even) superselection sector** is realized," with consequences: (time) time = formation order; (cut) classical cut = real Wedderburn center; (dial) sector-weight is a free dial whose **symmetric** stationary point is equipartition (r=1/2); (lane) sectors occupy stationary points.

The prior **FORCING** version died of **overreach** — it forced *all* sectors to Q=2/3 (falsified by quarks/neutrinos). This test checks the **opposite** failure mode of the weaker version: **vacuity**. If "sectors occupy stationary points" says nothing, the axiom is a tautology dressed as physics. The hostile question: does the weaker form **thread the needle** — strong enough to predict charged-lepton 2/3, weak enough not to force the other sectors — or does it tip into vacuity, or smuggle the value back in via an unstated premise?

All algebra runs on the **retained** line `Q = 1/3 + (2/3)r` and the **retained** biconditional `Q=2/3 ⇔ r=1/2` ([`charged_lepton_koide_cone_algebraic_equivalence`](CHARGED_LEPTON_KOIDE_CONE_ALGEBRAIC_EQUIVALENCE_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md), retained). ## Per-front verdict

### F1 — NON-VACUITY: **CONTENTFUL, but the load-bearing word is "SYMMETRIC."**
The central hostile worry is correct as stated: *every* smooth functional has stationary points, so "sectors sit at stationary points" is — by itself — nearly vacuous, and the einselection note makes it concrete (2-sector entropy → r=1/2; Born/per-DOF → r=1; both are "stationary points"). The axiom escapes vacuity **only** because it names the **symmetric** one, and the symmetry — the sector swap `r ↦ 1−r` (singlet↔doublet exchange) — has a **unique** fixed point r=1/2 (runner 1.1), **functional-independent**. That unique point maps to **Q=2/3 exactly** (1.2), a definite value with **no residual freedom** (`dQ/dr = 2/3 ≠ 0`, 1.7). It is **falsifiable and passes with teeth**: a candidate symmetric value must equal 1/2 to ~3×10⁻⁵, and the nearest simple fraction (4/9) is **~10⁹×10³ ≈ 10⁴σ** away (`r(4/9)=1/6`, 10929σ; runner 1.6); counterfactual symmetric values r=0 (Q=1/3) or r=1 (Q=1) **would be falsified** by data (1.4). **Verdict: CONTENTFUL** — but the note must be ruthless that the content lives entirely in "symmetric," not in "stationary point." Drop "symmetric" and the axiom goes vacuous (1.5, 1.5b).

### F2 — COHERENCE: **COHERENT and general (not R[Z₃]-only).**
"Real superselection sector" = a **minimal real-central projection** of the relevant finite-dim real *-algebra (a block of the real Wedderburn decomposition, type ℝ/ℂ/ℍ). For the generation case the real commutant of the C₃ action is ℝ[Z₃] ≅ ℝ⊕ℂ → **exactly two** real sectors of ℝ-dims {1,2} (2.1), confirmed **numerically** on M₃(ℂ) by diagonalizing the real generator `C+Cᵀ` (eigenspaces {2; −1,−1} → 2 distinct real sectors; runner 2.5). The recipe is **general**: on any finite-dim real *-algebra the dial is a probability weight over the **list** of real sectors (well-defined whenever ≥2 sectors; degenerate/no-dial for a single block — ℝ, ℂ-as-real, ℍ all give 1; ℝ⊕ℝ and ℝ⊕ℂ give 2; runner 2.2), and it is well-defined **across heterogeneous block algebras** since weights live on the sector index, never on a cross-algebra dimension comparison (2.4). The **symmetric** point generalizes to the unique Sₙ-invariant (uniform) weight 1/n for n sectors — for n=2 that is (½,½) = r=1/2 (2.3, 2.6). **Verdict: COHERENT** — the axiom is not parochial to the one case it was built for.

### F3 — MINIMALITY: **MINIMAL CORE = {irreversible registration of WHICH sector} + the single adjective {real}.**
Of the four advertised consequences, **time and cut are THEOREMS** of the one-clause core, not separate axiom clauses: "registration of which" presupposes a sector decomposition, and "frozen/irreversible" forces the recorded observable into the **center** (superselection) — the proven *frozen⇒central* content (3.1); irreversible accumulation induces a reflexive-transitive **formation order** = time (3.2). **The "dial" is not a fourth physical clause** but a *meta*-restatement that the core fixes which-in-a-run, not the ensemble weight — so the weight is free **by omission** (3.3). The **one** genuinely separable adjective is **real/CPT-even** (a K-odd self-adjoint observable is also registrable; 3.4). **Verdict: the four-consequence statement is BLOATED;** the minimal statement is the 3-word core plus the single qualifier "real," with {time, cut} as theorems and {dial} as a meta-observation.

### F4 — CONSTITUTIVE vs ASSUMED: **three constitutive, one assumed.**
- **irreversible** — CONSTITUTIVE (a reversible "record" retrieves nothing; a unitary is invertible, a projection is not; 4.1).
- **registration-of-which** — CONSTITUTIVE (a record must distinguish ≥2 alternatives, else it records nothing; 4.2).
- **superselection (frozen⇒central)** — CONSTITUTIVE-VIA-THEOREM (proven, not posited; 4.3).
- **real / CPT-even** — **ASSUMED** (4.4). This is the honest crux: a K-odd (T-violating) self-adjoint observable `i(C−C²)` is logically registrable, so "classical = real" is an **added physical premise** — and it is precisely the **K-reality import already named on origin/main** (the same δ=0 / det_C / signed-readout object; [`flavor_einselection_2sector_modulo_kreality`](FLAVOR_EINSELECTION_2SECTOR_MODULO_KREALITY_2026-06-02.md)). **Verdict:** the axiom does not hide its import — reality is the single non-constitutive clause, and it is the gate the campaign already knows about.

### F5 — NO-OVERREACH RE-CHECK + the F1↔F5 needle: **avoids overreach; threads the needle as a CONDITIONAL, one lane-assignment bit short of full determination.**
The multi-lane form genuinely **avoids the forcing version's fatal flaw**: it is satisfiable with charged leptons at r=1/2 *and* another sector at r=1 (no value forced; 5.1); it asserts a value only for sectors carrying the ℝ[Z₃] 2-sector cut, so it makes **no quark/neutrino claim to falsify** (the cross-sector 2/9 is a *count* ratio `n_pair/n_color`, a different observable; 5.2); and it does **not** inherit the falsified "all sectors → 2/3" universal claim (5.6). The needle's two ends hold **simultaneously**: the symmetric lane is a **sharp** prediction (strong end) while the broken lanes are **free** (weak end) — so the axiom is contentful for leptons *and* non-overreaching for the rest (5.3).

**The honest hole (be ruthless).** The axiom by itself permits charged leptons at r=0, r=1/2, *or* r=1 — the no-overreach property (5.1) cuts **both** ways. So predicting charged-lepton 2/3 **specifically** requires **one extra, unstated premise**: *"the charged-lepton sector occupies the symmetric stationary point."* The core permits all three lanes (5.4), so this lane-assignment is genuinely **extra information** — exactly the "which extremum/lane does each sector occupy" residual the stationary-point note already isolated ([`flavor_r_half_is_a_stationary_point_not_forced`](FLAVOR_R_HALF_IS_A_STATIONARY_POINT_NOT_FORCED_2026-06-02.md), retained_bounded). **Verdict:** the needle is threaded at the level of the **conditional** "symmetric lane ⇒ Q=2/3" (5.5) — contentful, coherent, minimal, non-overreaching — but full charged-lepton determination costs exactly **one lane-assignment bit** on top. Crucially, that bit is **not** a disguised re-import of the value 2/3 (the conditional is value-rigid on its own); it is a separate selection of *which sector is the symmetric one*.

## The two crucial findings

1. **VACUOUS or CONTENTFUL → CONTENTFUL.** The axiom makes the **sharp, falsifiable** prediction that the **symmetric lane = Q=2/3** (unique swap fixed point r=1/2; matches charged leptons to ~10⁻⁵; nearest simple fraction ~10⁴σ off). The vacuity trap is **real** but **dodged** — and dodged **only** by the word **"symmetric"** (functional-independent), never by the phrase "a stationary point" (which is satisfiable at both r=1/2 and r=1 and is therefore vacuous). The non-vacuity is genuine but **fragile**: it rests entirely on the symmetry-pinned uniqueness.

2. **THREADS THE NEEDLE or NOT → THREADS IT, as a CONDITIONAL, one bit short.** The axiom is strong enough (symmetric ⇒ 2/3, sharp and rigid) **and** weak enough (forces no other sector, asserts nothing for quarks/neutrinos) **at the same time** — so it neither overreaches nor goes vacuous. But pinning charged leptons (rather than some other sector) to the symmetric lane needs **one unstated lane-assignment premise**. **Net:** the needle *is* threaded at the conditional level; turning the conditional into the unconditional charged-lepton prediction Q=2/3 costs exactly one lane-assignment bit — which is **the same open "which-vacuum / lane-assignment" residual already on the ledger**, now seen to be neither overreach nor vacuity but a single, named, missing selection.

## Honest synthesis (the design verdict)

The weaker stable-dial/multi-lane axiom **survives the hostile check far better than the forcing version**: it is **contentful** (F1), **coherent and general** (F2), can be stated **minimally** as one core clause + one adjective (F3), is **mostly constitutive** with a single honestly-named assumed clause (reality, F4), and **genuinely avoids overreach** (F5). It does **not** collapse into vacuity. The price of avoiding overreach is **not** vacuity — it is a single **lane-assignment bit** ("the charged-lepton sector is the symmetric one"), and that bit is exactly the residual the campaign already isolated, not a new hole. So the honest win is real but **bounded**: a contentful, coherent, minimal, non-overreaching axiom that **predicts charged-lepton 2/3 as the symmetric lane — conditional on one lane-assignment premise** — rather than an axiom that derives it outright.

**What would close the remaining bit (next paths, not a wall):**

- Equivalently, the named **K-reality** assumed clause (F4) and the **lane-assignment** bit (F5) may be the *same* selection viewed from the partition vs. the value side; if a single emergent T-odd / reality structure supplies both, the axiom's charged-lepton prediction becomes unconditional. This is the standing open object, here sharpened to "one bit," not widened.
