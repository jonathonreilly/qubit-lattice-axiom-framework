## The original decision surface (historical; superseded by the resolution)

Whether to append one sentence to the Record axiom:

> There is one configuration of records.

## Why this is in front of you

It is the last missing piece of the arrow. With "Records form." landed, any
realized succession is already monotone (permanence plus one record per site)
and directed (formation adds, never removes). What nothing landed supplies is
**comparability**: that any two realized configurations are nested — one
world-line of records, rather than incomparable realized alternatives.

Comparability was assessed here (2026-07-04) as not derivable from the
current sentences; the 2026-07-07 note narrows that assessment by arguing an
admission-discipline inadmissibility route, with one explicit audit judgment
point (see Resolution above).
Original text: Comparability is not derivable from the current sentences. Permanence is a
per-configuration statement; it cannot see across realized alternatives. If a
record at site x and a record at site y each arise from a common stage,
nothing landed says the realized world contains the chain rather than two
incomparable alternatives.

## The physical warrant

Outcomes are single. Experiments end with one result; realized records never
occur as co-realized incomparable alternatives. This is the same fact you
already filed at pair level as the recorded-branch principle: one statistical
slot per realized branch. The sentence states that fact at the level of the
whole record configuration — the definite article the current text lacks.

## What the sentence buys

With it, the realized stages are stages of one growing configuration, so any
two are nested and the realized history is a chain. Combined with occurrence
and permanence, the arrow — ordering, direction, irreversibility — becomes an
axiom consequence end to end. Simultaneous multi-record formation stays a
chain (a stage jumps by more than one record; stages are still nested). These
would be certified in a bounded note with runner in the same PR, exactly the
pattern the formation append used.

## What it does not do

No formation rule, no rate, no time metric, no clock. It says nothing about
the possibility side — superposition before records is untouched. It does not
conflict with any landed sentence, and the per-site "When present" reading is
unchanged. Saturation stays a valid terminal boundary; the arrow does not
need eternal succession.
