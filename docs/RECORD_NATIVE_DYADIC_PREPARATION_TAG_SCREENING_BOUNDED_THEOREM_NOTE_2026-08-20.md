## Result Up Front

This block locally instantiates the recorded-randomizer and compiler objects
that the July operational-affinity analysis left abstract, on one exact and
narrow apparatus family. It does not derive the randomizer's fair weight.

Take any rank-one projector `P` in one `M_2(C)` copy and its orthogonal
complement `Pbar=I-P`. A finite permanent Record scaffold makes one blank site
locally recognizable by five occupied nearest neighbours and one blank
forward neighbour. Four transverse binary guards select one of three roles by
relations only:

```text
all four equal                 accumulator
three equal, one complement    preparation copy
two opposite pairs, complementary to each other    tester copy
```

The accumulator uses one fixed mark kernel. If its predecessor equals the
four guards, it writes the matching projector or its complement with weights
`1/2,1/2`. If the predecessor is already the complement, it copies that
absorbing failure. After `n` accumulator sites, a preparation site copies the
accumulator. Therefore the preparation Record equals the reference projector
with exact probability

\[
 p_n=2^{-n},                                                     \tag{1}
\]

and equals its complement with probability `1-p_n`. No auxiliary randomizer
coordinate enters the causal state: the fair weight is explicit law content,
and its sampled branch is immediately a permanent Record. No simulator stage
survives outside the Record map; selection of the actual branch remains open.

The future tester rail begins one edge beyond the preparation Record. Each
tester copies only its immediate predecessor. The old controller Records are
permanent but lie outside the test site's nearest-neighbour condition. For
every pair of lawful microhistories with the same preparation Record, every
finite declared tester continuation has the same transcript measure. This is
an exact record-fibre strong-lumpability theorem for the declared rail, not a
universal theorem over every possible future protocol.

Coarse-graining over the controller history therefore gives the Record-label
endpoint statistic `p_n`. A separate supplied bridge `B` identifies endpoint
Record values with density representatives `P,I-P`, represents the forgotten
mixture by their barycenter, and identifies the copy/equality event with effect
`P`. Under `B`, the density representative is

\[
 \rho_n=p_nP+(1-p_n)(I-P),                                    \tag{2}
\]

and barycenter evaluation on the supplied endpoint effect `P` gives
`Tr(rho_n P)=p_n`. At the first nontrivial point `n=2`, the physical Record
transcript is `1/4`, while the two named nonlinear maps return

```text
normalized square:  p^2/[p^2+(1-p)^2] = 1/10
cubic power:        p^3/[p^3+(1-p)^3] = 1/28.
```

They disagree for every executed `n=2,...,6`. Thus the exact Record
coarse-graining, conditional on `B`, excludes those two formulas on that
family. This is not an affine-map theorem on a convex preparation domain. It
does **not** select all preparation maps, derive a noncommuting effect algebra,
or derive the Born rule.

The normalized cubic power displayed here is an illustrative preparation-map
control. It is not the repository's distinct smooth directional grading
`g_c(n)=(1+n_z^3)/2`, and this block does not retire that survivor.

The construction is conditional. The fair branch weight is supplied law
content. The binary scaffold, its initial reference, and its commuting-pointer
interpretation are supplied apparatus boundary content. A local Markov
generator can execute the appends, but its abstract rate is not seconds;
physical time remains open. No microscopic reversible action is derived that
creates the fair write from pre-Record dynamics. Those are the next physics
obligations rather than concealed achievements.

## 5. Dyadic Preparation Theorem

Let `S_j` be the event that accumulator `j` still matches the reference. From
(6),

\[
 \Pr(S_{j+1}\mid S_j)=\frac12,\qquad
 \Pr(S_{j+1}\mid S_j^c)=0.                                  \tag{7}
\]

The seed matches the reference with certainty. Induction gives

\[
 \Pr(S_n)=2^{-n}.                                             \tag{8}
\]

The preparation site copies accumulator `n-1`, so (8) is its exact Record
law. The alternative histories are explicit: either all `n` fair branches
succeed, or the first failure occurs at one of the `n` accumulator sites and
the complement then propagates deterministically. Their probabilities sum to
one. The runner executes `n=1,...,6` in exact rational arithmetic.

This is a Record mixture protocol on its declared apparatus. The randomizer
outcomes are Records, the conditional preparation is a local append, and
forgetting the controller means summing mutually exclusive transcript atoms;
it does not erase permanent Records.

The result does not derive the number `1/2` from the four axioms: the fair branch weight is supplied law content in (6). What is derived from that one
datum is the entire `2^-n` family, its conditional preparation histories, and
their normalized coarse-graining.

## 6. Exact Tag Screening And Strong Lumpability

After the preparation site, the first tester lies one step forward. Its local
condition contains the preparation Record and four tester guards. It does not
contain an earlier accumulator Record. Later testers contain only the previous
tester and their guards.

Let `h,h'` be two lawful apparatus histories at preparation completion, and
let `R_p(h)` be the preparation Record. For every finite tester length `m`,
the kernel therefore gives

\[
 R_p(h)=R_p(h')
 \Longrightarrow
 \Pr(\tau\mid h)=\Pr(\tau\mid h')
 \quad\text{for every tester transcript }\tau.                \tag{9}
\]

Indeed the unique transcript is `m` copies of `R_p`. At `n=4`, four distinct
failure histories occupy the complement fibre; the runner checks that all
four have the same three-test future. A hostile mutation that makes the first
tester XOR its output with the first controller Record splits that fibre into
two future laws and is rejected.

Equation (9) is strong lumpability for the declared tester category. It is not
a claim that arbitrary future apparatuses cannot walk back to and inspect the
old controller Records. The legal tester category is part of this candidate
law surface. A universal operational quotient still requires a complete
physical intervention/effect category.

## 7. Record Coarse-Graining And Named-Map Discriminator

Call equality to `P` success and equality to `I-P` failure. The two endpoint
Record labels have deterministic copy/equality outcomes:

\[
 p(P\mid P)=1,\qquad p(P\mid I-P)=0.                          \tag{10}
\]

Coarse-graining the dyadic controller histories gives the Record-label law

\[
 p(P\mid \mu_n)=2^{-n}.                                      \tag{11}
\]

Under the separately supplied bridge `B`, the barycenter of the endpoint
preparation measure is (2), so `Tr(rho_n P)=2^-n` as well. `B` contains three
claims that the Record process itself does not prove: the two endpoint labels
represent `P,I-P`, forgetting is represented by their density barycenter, and
the equality-copy event represents quantum effect `P`. The identification is
therefore conditional even on the displayed commuting preparation hull.

The August 13 normalized-square map instead represents the same barycenter by

\[
 \sigma_n={\rho_n^2\over\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_n^2)},
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Tr}(\sigma_nP)
 ={p_n^2\over p_n^2+(1-p_n)^2}.                              \tag{12}
\]

The illustrative normalized-cubic-power comparator is

\[
 c(p_n)={p_n^3\over p_n^3+(1-p_n)^3}.                        \tag{13}
\]

For `n=2`, (11)--(13) are respectively `1/4`, `1/10`, and `1/28`.
They remain pairwise unequal in the required comparison for every executed
`n=2,...,6`. Equations (12) and (13) therefore do not represent the Record
endpoint transcript law of this apparatus under bridge `B`.

The scope cannot be widened. Another nonlinear function can be engineered to
agree on the dyadic set, and the representing state could be redefined as
`sigma_n`; both escape the broad claim by changing the preparation map. No
classification of every nonlinear map, no arbitrary convex weight, and no
noncommuting effect theorem is proved.
