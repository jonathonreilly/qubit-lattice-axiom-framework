# Record readout locality, read as local tomography, admits the ordinary composite and excludes the graded one

**Date:** 2026-09-24
**Type:** bounded_theorem
**Status:** exact finite certificates under supplied readings; unaudited.

## Result

The landed no-go
`TENSOR_COMPOSITION_REQUIRES_LOCAL_TOMOGRAPHY_BEYOND_LOCALITY_NARROW_NO_GO_NOTE_2026-06-03.md`
names the residual premise for the ordinary two-site composite: a local
tomography or no-extra-global generation premise. The landed
`GENERATED_FINITE_COMPOSITION_MINIMALITY_THEOREM_2026-07-13.md` shows that
generation by commuting local copies gives `M_{2^n}(C)`. This note asks where
the tomography premise could come from inside the axioms, and what it costs.
- **A reading of Record supplies it.** The Record axiom says only records are
  readable and a readout is determined by record content alone. Read for
  reconstructions, that is local tomography: what a joint state is, is what
  its local record menus and their joint frequencies say. This is a reading,
  named `D-tomo`, not an adopted axiom.
- **The ordinary composite passes.** Joint frequencies of local record menus
  along four axes rebuild every two-site state exactly.
- **The graded composite fails.** Two fermionic modes with parity
  superselection have states, `(|01⟩ ± |10⟩)/√2`, that no local readout
  distinguishes. So the graded matter composition of the landed 2026-09-01
  notes is not locally tomographic. Under `D-tomo`, fermions are not composed
  from sites; they emerge, as in open PRs 9048, 9054 and 9112 and the landed
  superlattice fermion notes.
- **The reading alone does not fix the state set.** Half the swap operator has
  every local-product coordinate a state has and is positive on every product
  effect, but it is not a state. The second reading, `D-alg`, positivity on
  the algebra generated by the commuting local copies, excludes it.

So the ordinary tensor product costs two readings of axiom text, `D-tomo` and
`D-alg`, and the graded product is what one gets under the alternative reading
in which odd local elements anticommute.

## Setting and decision points

- **The one-site algebra.** The Qubit axiom of `MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md`:
  "The full one-site possibility domain has algebraic presentation `M_2(C)`."
- **The Record text.** "Only records are readable. A readout value is
  determined by record content alone. A site with no record cannot be read."
  and "When present, a record locks exactly one admissible local possibility."
- **`D-tomo` (supplied reading).** A local record menu at a site is a set of
  admissible possibilities, one of which a forming record locks; with the
  odds of open PR 9083, its frequencies are `Tr(P_q ρ)` for rank-one
  projectors `P_q`. `D-tomo` says: the reconstruction `ρ` of several sites is
  determined by the joint frequencies of local menus. Since no non-local
  readout exists under Record, a joint state carrying content that no such
  frequencies reflect would carry unregisterable content; `D-tomo` says there
  is none. It is a reading of the text, not an entailment.
- **`D-alg` (supplied reading).** The composite's observables form the
  algebra generated by the commuting local copies of `M_2(C)`. This is the
  hypothesis of the landed generated-composition theorem.
- **The alternative reading (supplied, not used here).** Odd local elements at
  distinct sites anticommute: the graded composition of
  `MATTER_RECORD_GRADING_SAME_CARRIER_COMPATIBILITY_TWO_MODE_EVEN_REPAIR_AXIOM_DECISION_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-09-01.md`.

None is adopted.

## Theorem 1 — the ordinary composite passes `D-tomo`

Take two sites with `M_2(C) ⊗ M_2(C) = M_4(C)`, and local record menus along
the four tetrahedral axes `n_k`, with locked-possibility projectors
`P_k = (1 + n_k·σ)/2`.
- **Reconstruction.** For 20 random two-site states, the 16 joint frequencies
  `Tr(ρ P_j ⊗ P_k)` determine `ρ` exactly (largest deviation 1e-16), as do
  the 16 local Pauli products (6e-17).
- **Counting.** The traceless dimension of `M_4(C)` is 15 = 3 + 3 + 9:
  one-site content twice plus two-site correlations. For three sites,
  63 = 9 + 27 + 27.

So every two-site reconstruction is fixed by local record statistics. This
recomputes the landed
`LOCAL_TOMOGRAPHY_FROM_QUBIT_COMPLEX_STRUCTURE_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-06-03.md`
in menu language. ∎

## Theorem 2 — the generated algebra is the ordinary composite

The local copies `a ⊗ 1` and `1 ⊗ b` commute exactly. The two-letter words in
them span 16 complex dimensions, all of `M_4(C)`; for three sites the
three-letter words span 64, all of `M_8(C)`. This is the finite content of the
landed generated-composition theorem, rechecked here so that Theorem 4 can
use it. ∎

## Theorem 3 — the graded composite fails `D-tomo`

Take two fermionic modes with parity superselection, in the Fock basis
`|n_1 n_2⟩`, realized on two qubits by `c_1 = σ^- ⊗ 1`, `c_2 = Z ⊗ σ^-`.
- **Local readable content.** Under superselection a mode's observables are
  parity-even, so its readable algebra is `span{1, n_i}`, the readable
  one-site content found in the landed 2026-09-01 note. The local products
  `{1, n_1, n_2, n_1 n_2}` fix 3 real parameters.
- **Joint content.** A parity-block-diagonal two-mode state has 7 real
  parameters. So 4 are invisible to local readouts.
- **A witness pair.** `(|01⟩ + |10⟩)/√2` and `(|01⟩ − |10⟩)/√2` agree on every
  local product (deviation 0) and have trace distance 1.
- **What separates them, and why it is not local.** Read as two qubits,
  `σ^x ⊗ σ^x` separates the pair by 2. But `σ^x_1` anticommutes with the
  mode-1 parity `1 − 2n_1`, so under superselection it is not a mode-1
  observable; and the mode operators `c_1`, `c_2` anticommute (deviation 0),
  so the local algebras do not commute. The separating observable is a joint
  one.

So the graded composite is not locally tomographic. This is the finite form
of the fermionic-theory result of D'Ariano, Manessi, Perinotti and Tosini
(2014), cited as prior art. ∎

## Theorem 4 — `D-tomo` alone leaves the state set open

Let `S = XX + YY + ZZ` and `W = (1 + S)/4`.
- `W` has both marginals `1/2` and 4 nonzero local-product coordinates, so it
  has every coordinate a locally tomographic state has.
- On product effects it is positive: `⟨ab|W|ab⟩ = (1 + a·b)/4` for Bloch
  vectors `a`, `b` (checked on 4000 random pairs to 1e-16), with minimum 0 at
  antiparallel axes; positivity on mixed product effects follows by
  linearity.
- `W` is half the swap operator, and its smallest eigenvalue is −1/2. So it is
  not a state of `M_4(C)`.

Hence `D-tomo` fixes the coordinates of a joint state but not the set of
allowed joint states, which can lie anywhere between the separable set and
the set positive on product effects (prior art: the minimal and maximal
tensor products of Namioka and Phelps 1969; Barnum, Barrett, Leifer and
Wilce 2007). `D-alg` fixes it: a state is a positive functional on the
generated algebra `M_4(C)`, and `W` is not positive there. ∎

## What this means for the lanes

- **Composition.** The landed no-go's residual premise has a home in the
  axiom text, as a reading. The price of the ordinary tensor product is two
  readings, `D-tomo` and `D-alg`; nothing here says the text forces them.
- **Matter.** Under `D-tomo` the graded composition of sites is excluded, and
  fermions are emergent objects of the ordinary composite: the Kitaev
  carvings of open PRs 9048, 9054 and 9112, and the landed superlattice
  fermion notes such as
  `FINITE_BKSF_SIGN_AND_SUPERLATTICE_MARKER_CENSUS_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-09-02.md`
  and
  `CHARGE_CONJUGATION_AND_THE_CONSERVED_U1_CURRENT_OF_THE_EMERGENT_FERMION_BOUNDED_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-09-03.md`.
  Under the alternative reading the graded composite stands.
- **Quantum rules.** Open PRs 9083–9086 take Hilbert-space kinematics as
  supplied. `D-tomo` and `D-alg` are the readings under which that kinematics
  is the ordinary tensor product of the sites.

## What stays open

- Whether the Record text forces `D-tomo`. It is consistent with it, and
  `D-tomo` is the natural reading of "register, not read", but a reading is
  recorded here, not derived.
- Whether `D-alg` can be replaced by something in the axiom text. The landed
  no-go shows locality alone does not supply it.
- The many-site state set under `D-tomo` without `D-alg`, and the graded
  composite's own consistency conditions, are not treated.
