Record locking and record readability:

> When present, a record locks exactly one admissible local possibility. A site
> never carries more than one record; records are permanent.

> Only records are readable. A readout value is determined by record content
> alone. For any finite collection of pairwise-disjoint records, scalar readout
> `I` is additive, with `I(empty)=0`.

State and law single-valuedness:

> A state is a configuration of records.

> A law privileges no states. Its domain is a supplied condition, and at every
> state where the condition holds it gives exactly one answer.

Supplied-structure discipline:

> These axioms state only their named primitive content. Further physical
> structure requires derivation, bridge, explicit admission, or approved
> primitive registration before use as a premise.

> No site is privileged. Sites are distinguished by the supplied lattice
> structure alone.

> No possibility is privileged. Possibilities are distinguished by the supplied

For this target, a permitted readout must read only records, be fixed by record
content, be a real scalar additive over finite disjoint record collections, give

oriented readout such as `Im(b)` also needs an orientation supplier and a

## Inventory Sweep

### 1. Lattice

The lattice axiom says:

> Physical sites are the points of the cubic lattice `Z^3`, with nearest-neighbor
> adjacency, standard translations, and proper cubic rotations about each site.

> No site is privileged. Sites are distinguished by the supplied lattice
> structure alone.

The phrase "proper cubic rotations" is spatial and orientation-preserving. It
does not itself transport a spatial handedness onto the internal doublet phase
plane.

### 2. Qubit

The Qubit axiom in full is:

> Each site has a domain of local possibilities.
>
> The full one-site possibility domain has algebraic presentation `M_2(C)`.
>
> A `Cl(3,0)`-compatible real-algebra presentation may be used equivalently and
> adds no further primitive structure.
>
> No possibility is privileged. Possibilities are distinguished by the supplied
> algebraic structure alone.

This is the site-level presentation fork. On the complex-presentation reading,
the `M_2(C)` sentence is primary and can be read as naming a complex algebra
with its scalar `i`. On the real-algebra reading, the following sentence controls
the presentation:

> A `Cl(3,0)`-compatible real-algebra presentation may be used equivalently and
> adds no further primitive structure.

The real-algebra exhibit is mathematical and does not depend on which reading
one adopts. As real algebras, `Cl(3,0) ~= M_2(C)`. The only central square roots
of `-1` are `+iI` and `-iI`. Entrywise conjugation is an `R`-algebra
automorphism of `M_2(C)` that sends `iI` to `-iI`. Therefore the real algebra
alone cannot distinguish the two orientations.

This note does not decide the fork. The complex reading remains textually
possible because the `M_2(C)` sentence is the primary sentence and the
real-algebra sentence can be read as subordinate notation. The doublet-grade
verdict does not require deciding that textual question, because an oriented
doublet readout also needs the missing transport law.

The site-level presentation fork ("does naming `M_2(C)` supply a preferred `i`?")
is a textual-clarity question about the Qubit axiom wording; if it ever becomes
load-bearing on its own, it is a candidate for an axiom-clarity sentence.

## Computed Exhibits

In the real-algebra branch, entrywise conjugation on sample `2 x 2` complex
matrices preserves products and real-linear combinations and sends `iI` to
`-iI`. This is the no-supplied-orientation exhibit for the real-algebra
reading.

The same branch has the single-valuedness failure for `Im(b)`. Take
`b = 2 + 3i`. Conjugation sends `b` to `conj(b) = 2 - 3i`, so `Im(b) = 3` and
`Im(conj(b)) = -3`. Without an orientation supplier on the doublet phase plane,
the sign convention is not single-valued.

In the orientation-supplied branch only, the negative control passes the
mechanical screen: `I({r_1, ..., r_n}) = sum_j Im(b_{r_j})` is real,
finite-additive over disjoint record collections, has `I(empty)=0`, and
separates `b` from `conj(b)`. It passes only if an orientation supplier and a
doublet transport are both quoted. Neither is quoted in the current inventory.

The K-even factorization exhibit covers additive radial scalar functionals

```text
I_F(b) = F(b * conj(b)) = F(|b|^2)
```

and finite sums of such one-record terms. For this class,

```text
I_F(conj(b)) = F(conj(b) * b) = F(|b|^2) = I_F(b).
```

It is also invariant under the `C3` phase action `b -> omega b` because
`|omega b|^2 = |b|^2`. The runner checks polynomial, rational, and exponential
samples of `F` on several values of `b`.

The boundary witness `Re(b^3)` is `C3`-invariant and K-even:

```text
Re((omega b)^3) = Re(b^3)
Re(conj(b)^3) = Re(b^3)
```

It is not radial, since equal values of `|b|^2` can give different values of
`Re(b^3)`. It would need its own supplied phase-zero or cubic tensor reference.
The bounded radial factorization claim does not cover that witness, and the
current quoted inventory does not supply it as a readout.

sentences quoted above.

The covered functional class is additive radial scalar readouts `F(|b|^2)` and

Two defeat routes remain open. One can adopt the complex-presentation reading

On the covered class the result is close to definitional, and that should be
said plainly. In physics a relative phase is measured by one method only:
interference, which appears operationally as a failure of additivity between
alternatives. The record interface requires readouts additive over disjoint
records, which is exactly the property interference violates. So at this
functional class the interface itself legislates phase-blindness. The
substantive finding is narrower and structural: the four axioms supply no
machinery with which an interference readout could even be posed, and both
open defeat routes are demands for exactly such machinery.

Cross-check against the admitted inputs: the program carries a conjugation-odd
phase downstream (the theta admission), and such a phase is measurable in
principle only because a dynamics couples it to rates. That is consistent with
this note only because theta arrives by explicit admission in a downstream
readout context, not from the four axioms. This note therefore does not say
conjugation-odd phases are unphysical; it says the axioms alone cannot read
one.
