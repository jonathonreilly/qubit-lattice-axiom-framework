## Theorem

**(T1) `D` registers only the C₃-equivariant (circulant) content.** For any
Hermitian `M`, `D(M)` is circulant (`[D(M), C]=0`), hence `[D(M), S]=0`.

**(T2) Every T-odd operator registers as the S₃-symmetric A-line.** For any
T-odd Hermitian `M` (`conj(M)=−M`, i.e. `M ∈ i·so(3)`), `D(M) ∝ A`. In
particular `[D(M), S]=0`: the registered part of every T-odd operator is the
unique C₃-invariant K-odd direction, which is partition-blind.

**(T3) The S₃-breaking direction is exactly what `D` annihilates.** The
S₃-breaking content `M − D(M)` (non-circulant, `[·,S]≠0`, in the doublet
plane) is the inter-sector coherence `P₁ M P₂ + P₂ M P₁`, and `D(M − D(M)) = 0`.
The registration map is identically blind to it.

**(T4) The S₃-breaking direction is within a single K/CPT orbit.** Complex
conjugation `K` swaps the doublet characters (`conj(P₁) = P₂`, `conj(P₀)=P₀`):
this is the S₃ reflection on the characters, and `⟨C-cycle, K-reflection⟩`
generates the full S₃ (order 6). Hence `{χ₁, χ₂}` is **one K/CPT orbit**. The
RECORD axiom's K/CPT-orbit clause registers the realized outcome as the *orbit*
of the realized central sector; the S₃-breaking direction distinguishes two
members of a single orbit — exactly the within-orbit data the axiom does not
supply. The realized coarsening is `{χ₀} | {χ₁, χ₂}` = the `(1,1)`
singlet|doublet partition on which the Koide weight `r=|b|²/a²` lives.

**Conclusion.** The S₃-breaking direction on the generation orbit is
**unregistrable** (filtered by `D` in T1–T3; within one K/CPT orbit in T4). It
is not a pre-record operator awaiting derivation, and not an import; what is
realized is a registered pattern.
