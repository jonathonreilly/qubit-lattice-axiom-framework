# Cycle 715 recurrent directional packet bank — bounded theorem note

Status: bounded constructive theorem, authority none, audit unset. This note
makes no constitutional or no-go claim.

## Question

Can the repaired Cycle-713 physical-M2 seam opportunity and the Cycle-714
fixed packet be promoted to more than a one-shot fixed-address write, without
copying a branch ray or supplying freshness and orientation?

The bounded answer is positive on a two-cell append code. It is not yet a
many-bank or unbounded-history answer.

## Construction

The literal repaired Cycle-713 seam circuit is left unchanged through its
contact block. Two exact Toffolis refine its retained pointer into the
three-way endpoint word

```text
no_change = pointer 0
u_to_v    = pointer AND post-right-occupation
v_to_u    = pointer AND post-left-occupation.
```

On the physical seam grammar these outputs are one-hot. The packet word reads
them, then applies the same Toffolis again and XORs the two post-seam endpoint
occupations into the pointer. Pointer and directional instruments therefore
return to zero without undoing the matter update. This is what makes another
application of the same instrument possible; the instrument output is not a
selected occurrence, Record, or Born variable.

The packet bank has two structurally addressed cells. Each has the unchanged
34-bit Cycle-704/Cycle-714 packet payload plus one retained freshness witness.
A local two-rail token selects a cell. Before an append, a literal reversible
zero test reads all 34 payload bits and writes the selected cell's freshness
witness. The test is controlled by the one-hot-token witness, endpoint word,
BINDER, ACTUAL, ADMISS, and LAW. Freshness and orientation are therefore not
supplied. ACTUAL and ADMISS deliberately remain supplied.

The fixed word copies the old head to predecessor, copies the rotor-before,
increments the global K16 rotor, writes rotor-after/carry, writes the seam
delta mask and direction sign, moves the head to the structural cell address,
and swaps the local allocation token. It processes both structural branches
before moving the token, preventing a within-word cascade. Reversing the same
gate list unappends exactly.

The declared append domain requires a clean packet/work genesis, exactly one
token, a clean endpoint instrument at word entry, and a blank selected cell.
After two successful appends the finite bank is locally diagnosed as
exhausted and is outside that domain. The raw reversible permutation is not
claimed to fix an exhausted state.
