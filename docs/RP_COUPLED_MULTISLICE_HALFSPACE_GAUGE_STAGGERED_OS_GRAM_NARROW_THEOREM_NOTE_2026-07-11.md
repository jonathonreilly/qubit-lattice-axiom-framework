## 3. Proof of L2': multi-slice and gauge-dressed side covariance

Fermion side-covariance is set-agnostic. The Berezin functional tensors over

supplier's `I_-[theta(A)]=conj(I_+[A])` holds verbatim for multi-slice
plus-side `A`.

The new content is the gauge dressing. A gauge-dressed plus-side element

reflection-symmetric background `U_k(x,1-t)=U_k(x,t)`, so the minus-side
integral sees the conjugate of the plus-side coefficient. Combined with the

