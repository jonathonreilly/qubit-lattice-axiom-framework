## Claim


1. the Wilson temporal-gauge transfer kernel `T_gauge` is positive;
2. each fixed-gauge fermion two-step block has the form
   `B[U]^dag B[U] >= 0`.

Then every represented positive-time observable `F` on that finite carrier,
including observables that mix gauge-link multiplication with fermion Fock
operators, satisfies

```text

                 = || W (O_hat Omega) ||^2  >=  0,
```

where the **single** two-step transfer operator on `H_gauge (x) H_ferm`
factorizes exactly as

```text
    T_full = ( T_gauge^{1/2} (x) I ) . ( oplus_U  B[U]^dag B[U] ) . ( T_gauge^{1/2} (x) I )
           = W^dag W,     W = ( oplus_U B[U] ) . ( T_gauge^{1/2} (x) I ),
```

with

- `T_gauge` the actual Wilson temporal-gauge transfer kernel
  `K(U, U') = exp(-beta (1 - Re Tr(U' U^dag)/N_c))`; the repaired note treats
  Wilson-boundary positivity as a supplied PSD-transfer premise and checks
  finite carriers below only as diagnostics (R1);
- `B[U]^dag B[U] = T_hat^2[U] >= 0` the per-configuration fermion two-step
  transfer, block-diagonal in the gauge background `U`.

In particular the combined transfer form covers represented observables that
**mix** gauge links and staggered fermion fields; the gauge-background
dependence of the fermion sector is carried by the `U`-block-diagonal structure,
and the genuine gauge-fermion entanglement (`T_full` is not a tensor product)

## Why a single transfer operator, not a product of factors

The reflected expectation, after Berezin integration at fixed gauge
background `U`, is

```text
    <Theta(F) F> = (1/Z) int dU e^{-S_G[U]} det(M[U]) . <Theta(F) F>^ferm_U,
```

and the fermion factor `<Theta(F) F>^ferm_U` is contracted with the propagator
`M[U]^{-1}`, hence depends on `U`. For a **mixed** observable `F` (gauge links
and fermion fields together) this fermion factor does **not** factor out of the
gauge observable, so the reflected inner product is **not** a product of a
separately-positive "gauge factor" times a separately-positive "fermion factor"
times a determinant. The assembly lemma recorded here is the corresponding
two-step transfer statement: if the Osterwalder-Seiler representation has the
single combined transfer operator
`<Theta(F) F> = <Omega| O_hat^dag T_full O_hat |Omega>`, then
`T_full = W^dag W` makes positivity a norm-square of one vector
`W(O_hat Omega)`.

The three previously-separate positivity facts are exactly the ingredients of
this single operator, assembled (not multiplied):

- the per-configuration fermion two-step transfer positivity is the
  `oplus_U B[U]^dag B[U]` block-diagonal core;
- the positive determinant weight `det(M_KS + m I) > 0` is the consistency
  condition making `e^{-S_G} det(M[U])` a positive measure (the `U`-average
  weight), not an independent multiplicative positivity factor;
- the gauge-half norm-square is the positivity of the gauge transfer
  `T_gauge`, instantiated on the actual Wilson boundary (R1).

### Per-configuration fermion two-step positivity (restated, self-contained)

In temporal gauge the staggered spatial hop on the spatial Hilbert space is

```text
    h[U]_{x, y} = (1/2) ( U_1(x) delta_{y, x+1} - U_1(x-1)^dag delta_{y, x-1} ),
```

which is **anti-Hermitian** (`h[U]^dag = -h[U]`) on every fixed `SU(3)`/`U(1)`
background, so it is unitarily diagonalizable with purely imaginary eigenvalues
`i lambda_j`, `lambda_j` real. With the alternating staggered phase
`eta_1(t) = (-1)^t`, the per-slice couplings are `A_even = m I + h[U]`,
`A_odd = m I - h[U]`, and the two-step single-particle transfer
`T2cl[U] = T_odd[U] . T_even[U]` splits into independent `2 x 2` blocks with
eigenvalues `exp(+/- 2 E_j)`, `E_j = asinh( sqrt(m^2 + lambda_j^2) ) >= 0`, both
**real and positive** for every real `lambda_j`. The decaying single-particle
kernel `t1^(2)[U]` is therefore a positive-semidefinite contraction, and the
many-body two-step transfer is its second quantization
`T_hat^2[U] = Gamma(t1^(2)[U]) = B[U]^dag B[U] >= 0` with
`B[U] = exp(-a_tau H_hat[U])`, `H_hat[U] = -log(t1^(2)[U])/(2 a_tau) >= 0`.
This is the same anti-Hermitian-hop modal reduction recorded for the
fixed-background case in `RP_P2_GAUGE_EXTENSION_AND_REALIZATION_RESIDUAL_NOTE_2026-05-28.md`;
it is restated here so this note's fermion-block positivity is self-contained
and does not assume that row's current audit status.

the Wilson action is exactly the reflection-plane coupling between the spatial


## Single-step vs two-step (honest framing)

Under `theta(t, x) = (-1 - t, x)` the staggered phase flips,
`eta_1(theta t) = -eta_1(t)`. An exhaustive search confirms that **no**
site-dependent `+/- 1` reflection makes the single-step Dirac operator
covariant (for any of `M^T`, `M^dag`, `M`, `M^*` and any reflection plane), so
single-step spin-basis Lagrangian RP is a genuine no-go (Caracciolo-Palumbo
2013), reproduced here as the `-0.80` negative control. The positive object
tested here is **intrinsically** the two-step transfer matrix. This note's
honest surface is the combined inequality for the represented two-step
transfer-matrix correlator under the stated inputs, not for the single-step
Lagrangian Gram and not for the full continuum Schwinger-function problem.
