## Gauge Extension of Reflection Positivity

### Temporal Gauge and Position-Space Transfer

The free-case two-step positivity in the reflection-positivity row uses
translation invariance to diagonalize over spatial momentum `p`. A fixed
nontrivial gauge background breaks translation invariance, so momentum is no
longer a good quantum number; the construction must be done in **position
space**.

Temporal-gauge fixing sets the temporal links to the identity: `U_0(x) = 1`
can be reached by a gauge transformation, leaving nontrivial spatial links
`U_i(x)`. The temporal hop (`eta_0 = 1`, `U_0 = 1`) is then clean, and the
spatial hop carries `eta_i(t) . U_i`. The reflection
`theta(t, x_vec) = (-1 - t, x_vec)` crosses **only** temporal links, which in
temporal gauge are `1`; the spatial structure is reflection-symmetric, so the
`B^dag B` form is expected to survive at arbitrary spatial `U`. This is the
standard lattice-gauge Osterwalder-Seiler picture: positivity is a temporal-gauge,
config-by-config statement. We verify this rather than assume it.

Define the staggered spatial hop matrix on the spatial Hilbert space
`C^{L_s} (x) C^{N_c}` (periodic spatial direction):

```text
    h[U]_{x,y} = (1/2)( U_1(x) delta_{y, x+1} - U_1(x-1)^dag delta_{y, x-1} ).
```

`h[U]` is **anti-Hermitian** (`h[U]^dag = -h[U]`): the forward-hop block
`+(1/2) U_1(x)` and the backward-hop block `-(1/2) U_1(x-1)^dag` are
minus-conjugate-transposes of each other. The runner confirms
`||h[U] + h[U]^dag|| = 0` exactly on random SU(3) backgrounds. This is the
gauge-background generalization of the free `i sin(p)` term: at `U = 1` and one
color, the position-space `h` is the discrete derivative whose eigenvalues are
the `i sin(p)`.

### Per-Config Two-Step Classical Transfer

With the alternating staggered phase `eta_1(t) = (-1)^t`, the per-slice
coupling matrix is

```text
    A_even = m I + h[U],     A_odd = m I - h[U].
```

The banded-in-time staggered mode equation
`A_t psi_t + (1/2) psi_{t+1} - (1/2) psi_{t-1} = 0` gives the single-step
classical transfer matrix on the amplitude vector `V_t = (psi_t, psi_{t-1})`
(now `2 L_s N_c`-dimensional, in position space):

```text
    T_s(t) = [[ -2 A_t,   I ],
              [  I,        0 ]],     T_even, T_odd from A_even, A_odd.
```

These come straight from the action; no convention is admitted. The physical
(translation-free) two-step single-particle transfer is the block

```text
    T2cl[U] = T_odd[U] . T_even[U]                 (2 L_s N_c x 2 L_s N_c).
```

Because `h[U]` is anti-Hermitian on the finite spatial Hilbert space, it is
unitarily diagonalizable with eigenvalues `i lambda_j`, `lambda_j in R`.
In that eigenbasis the two-step transfer splits into independent `2 x 2`
blocks

```text
    T2(lambda) =
      [[ 4 (m^2 + lambda^2) + 1,   -2 (m - i lambda) ],
       [ -2 (m + i lambda),         1                 ]],
```

with determinant `1` and trace `2 + 4 (m^2 + lambda^2)`. Writing
`q_j = sqrt(m^2 + lambda_j^2)` and `E_j = asinh(q_j)`, the two eigenvalues are

```text
    exp(+2 E_j),  exp(-2 E_j).
```

Both are real and positive for every real `lambda_j`; the decaying branch
`mu_j = exp(-2 E_j)` lies in `(0, 1]`. This is the load-bearing finite
linear-algebra reason the fixed-background per-config transfer is positive.
The runner checks this modal formula directly, verifies the free `U = 1`
specialization against the momentum-space dispersion, and stress-tests random
fixed `SU(3)` / `U(1)` backgrounds as exhibits of the same anti-Hermitian-hop
reduction.

### Per-Config Positivity

The `L_s N_c` decaying eigenvalues are the single-particle two-step kernel
`t1^(2)[U]`; the many-body two-step transfer is the second quantization
`T_hat^2[U] = Gamma(t1^(2)[U])`. Then

```text
    T_hat^2[U] >= 0   <=>   every decaying eigenvalue mu of T2cl[U] is REAL and
                            POSITIVE (0 < mu <= 1),  i.e.  spec(T2cl[U]) subset R_{>0}.
```

When this holds, `t1^(2)[U]` is a positive-semidefinite contraction,
`H_hat[U] = -log(t1^(2)[U]) / (2 a_tau)` is self-adjoint with non-negative
spectrum, and the many-body transfer factorizes as
`T_hat^2[U] = exp(-2 a_tau H_hat[U]) = B[U]^dag B[U]` with
`B[U] = exp(-a_tau H_hat[U])`. This is exactly the two-step
reflection-positivity statement at fixed gauge background.

**This is the property we derive from anti-Hermitian spatial hopping and then
verify on finite carriers.** The analytic modal reduction above supplies the
arbitrary fixed-background argument. The runner builds `T2cl[U]` from the
action at many random fixed `SU(3)` (and `U(1)`) spatial backgrounds and
checks that every decaying eigenvalue is real-positive:

| free-case bridge | `U = 1`, `L_s in {3,4,6}` | position-space decaying eigenvalues reproduce the momentum-space free spectrum `{e^{-2E(p)}}`; max residual `4e-16` (faithfulness) |

**Outcome.** Per-config two-step positivity **holds** at fixed, arbitrary

case is the translation-invariant specialization (free-case bridge reproduces it). This is
the genuine extension of the reflection-positivity row's free-case

## Standalone Relabeling Invariance

### Invariance Theorem (Positive, Not a No-Go)

> **Theorem (det/spec/Z relabeling invariance).** Let `P` be any permutation
> unitary relabeling the three selected `hw = 1` corner-triplet staggered modes
> (extended by the identity on the rest of the mode space). Then
> `det(M_KS + m I)`, `spec(H_hat)`, and `Z = Tr(e^{-beta H_hat})` are invariant
> under conjugation `X -> P X P^dag`.

This is a **positive invariance statement**, framed as permutation-invariance
of `det`/`spec`/trace, not a no-go.

**Proof.** `det` and the spectrum (hence any symmetric function of eigenvalues,
including the trace `Z = Tr(e^{-beta H_hat}) = sum_k e^{-beta E_k}`) are
invariant under conjugation by any unitary, in particular by a permutation
unitary `P`: `det(P X P^dag) = det(P) det(X) det(P^dag) = det(X)` since
`det(P) det(P^dag) = |det(P)|^2 = 1`, and `spec(P X P^dag) = spec(X)` because
conjugation is a similarity transform. A relabeling of the selected
`hw = 1` triplet is exactly such a conjugation. Therefore these three readouts
are relabeling invariants. QED.
