## 4. Inline lemmas

### 4.1 Positivity preservation under finite relabeling

**Lemma (PFR).** Let `X` be a finite-dimensional Hermitian operator with
non-negative spectrum, and let `P` be a unitary (in particular a
permutation unitary). Then `P X P^dag` is also Hermitian with non-negative
spectrum.

*Proof.* `(P X P^dag)^dag = P X^dag P^dag = P X P^dag` is Hermitian.
Conjugation by a unitary is a similarity transform, so
`spec(P X P^dag) = spec(X) subset R_{>= 0}`. QED.

In particular, at fixed `U`, `T_hat^2[U]`'s non-negativity (parent result
(A)) is preserved under any permutation-unitary relabeling of the
underlying fermionic mode basis. The runner exhibits this for the
relabelings of result (B).

### 4.2 Fixed-background scope: explicit exclusion of dynamical-gauge integration

**Lemma (FBS).** Let `phi : U -> R_{>= 0}` be a non-negative real-valued
function on a finite gauge background configuration space, and let `dmu(U)`
be any non-negative measure. Then the integrated quantity `int dmu(U) phi(U)`
is well-defined and non-negative *as a separate statement*, with the
non-negativity following from the integrand and measure both being
non-negative. The integration step is **not** itself the per-`U`
non-negativity statement and is **not** covered by the per-`U` proof.

*Proof.* Standard measure-theoretic non-negativity of integrals of
non-negative integrands against non-negative measures. QED.

This lemma is invoked here only to make explicit the scope boundary: the
parent's load-bearing per-config proof (result (A)) supplies the
non-negativity of one factor of the integrand at each fixed `U`. The
integrated RP inequality additionally requires the dynamical-gauge factors
(positive Wilson plaquette weight, positive Haar measure, positive
determinant weight, positive gauge-half norm-square contribution after
`U`-integration). The companion's narrowed scope (N1)-(N3) excludes the
*dynamical-gauge integration* step and therefore excludes the contested
abstract-to-Wilson application step the auditor flagged.

### 4.3 Finite relabeling at fixed `U`: half-action correspondence

**Lemma (HAC).** At fixed `U`, the Wilson plaquette half-action
`S_+^{Wilson}[U] := S_G^{positive-time half}[U]` and the abstract
half-action `S_+^{abstract}(x)` evaluated at the configuration
`x := U` correspond by `S_+^{abstract}(U) = S_+^{Wilson}[U]` whenever the
abstract formulation's underlying measured space is taken to be the
configuration space of `U` with the appropriate reflection-symmetric
restriction. At this fixed-`U` level the correspondence is a relabeling of
variables; it does **not** establish that the dynamical Wilson plaquette
weight after `dU`-integration satisfies the abstract formulation's
`Theta`-invariance and reflection-Hermiticity hypotheses with the
specific Wilson plaquette boundary structure. That is the missing
bridge theorem of alternative (i).

*Proof sketch.* At fixed `U`, both expressions are real-valued evaluations
of the half-action at a single configuration, so equality is a trivial
relabeling once the underlying configuration space is taken to be the same.
The non-trivial claim — that the dynamical Wilson plaquette setup
automatically discharges the abstract formulation's hypotheses after
`dU`-integration with the Wilson plaquette boundary structure — is the
content of the missing bridge theorem and is not asserted here. QED.

This lemma states only the trivial fixed-`U` relabeling correspondence; it
is the largest fixed-background statement we can make that preserves
honest scope.
