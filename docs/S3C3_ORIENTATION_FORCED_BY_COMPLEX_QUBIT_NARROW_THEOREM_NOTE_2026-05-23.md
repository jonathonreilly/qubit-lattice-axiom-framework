## Answer

**It is forced by A1.** In `Cl(3,0) = M_2(C)` the spatial volume form
`ω = e_1 e_2 e_3` equals `i·I` — the orientation of the three spatial axes *is*
the qubit's complex unit. An axis permutation `σ` conjugates `ω` by `sgn(σ)`.
Unitary (`C`-linear) symmetries preserve `i·I`, so they realize only the
`sgn = +1` permutations, `A_3 = C_3`; the orientation-reversing transpositions
(`sgn = −1`) send `i → −i` and can be realized **only antiunitarily** (complex
conjugation).

If physical symmetries must preserve the complex / Born structure — i.e. be
unitary — then only `C_3` acts physically. The transpositions are antiunitary
and are not symmetries of the complex reality. So `S_3 → C_3` is forced by A1's
complex structure, not chosen by convention.

This is the precise form of "the lattice is physical and there is no
not-physical": there is no external real, un-oriented substrate relative to
which `i` would be a free convention; A1 commits to the complex qubit `C^2`, and
`i = ω` is therefore intrinsic and physical.

## Theorem

Let `e_1, e_2, e_3` be the `Cl(3,0)` generators on the qubit (`e_i^2 = I`,
mutually anticommuting), realized as the Pauli matrices on `C^2`.

**(1) Volume form is the complex unit.** `ω := e_1 e_2 e_3 = i·I`, with
`ω^2 = −I`, and `ω` is central.

**(2) Permutations conjugate `ω` by sign.** For `σ ∈ S_3`,
`e_{σ(1)} e_{σ(2)} e_{σ(3)} = sgn(σ)·ω`. The three transpositions give `−ω`; the
identity and two 3-cycles give `+ω`.

**(3) C_3 is unitary; transpositions are antiunitary.** There is an explicit
unitary `U` (the `120°` rotation about `(1,1,1)/√3`) with `U e_i U† = e_{i+1}`,
and `U ω U† = ω` (it preserves `i·I`). Conversely, a unitary `V` with
`V e_i V† = e_{τ(i)}` for a transposition `τ` would give
`V ω V† = sgn(τ) ω = −ω`, while `V (i·I) V† = i·I`; hence `i·I = −i·I`, a
contradiction. So no unitary realizes a transposition; its realization is
necessarily antiunitary (complex conjugation, `i → −i`).

**Conclusion.** Requiring physical symmetries to preserve the complex structure
(unitary) selects exactly the orientation-preserving `C_3`. The orientation is
fixed by A1's complex unit `ω = i·I`. ∎
