## Theorem

**S1 [in-gap integer, anchored].** At `(r, m0) = (0.5, 1.5)` and `L_h = 64`, the
in-gap signed mode count is integer and edge-antisymmetric:
`D = eta_omega - eta_omega2 = +2` at the bottom edge and `-2` at the top edge.
This anchors the construction to the landed W19 edge-engine number.

**S2 [global vanishing].** Every total full-spectrum boundary quantity is zero
to machine precision on the supplied slab:
`eta_0`, `eta_omega`, `eta_omega2`,
`A2 = (eta_omega + eta_omega2)/2 - eta_0`, `S`, and `D`. The runner checks
`eps in {0.2, 0.1, 0.05, 0.02}`, `L_h in {16, 32, 64, 128}`,
`r in {0.5, 1}`, `m0 in {0.5, 1.5}`, and both boundary-condition labels. The
global boundary spectral asymmetry of the slab vanishes because the two edges
cancel.

**S3 [per-edge nonzero but non-universal].** The per-edge boundary spectral
asymmetry, support-weighted to one half of the slab, is nonzero, so the
single-edge FOY structure is alive. It is not a universal or topological number:
the runner gates the verified bottom-edge values
`eta_omega = 0.1223`, `0.2007`, and `0.0499` at
`(r,m0) = (0.5,0.5)`, `(0.5,1.5)`, and `(1,0.5)`, respectively. The values are
distinct and parameter-dependent. None equals the fixed-locus density
`2/9 = 0.2222`; the closest tested point is `0.2007`, about `0.0215` away.

**S4 [misread guard].** Since the total boundary value is zero, the distance of
the total from `2/9` is exactly `2/9` by arithmetic. That is not a hit. The
nonzero objects in this probe are the per-edge values of S3, and those are
non-universal.

**S5 [wall and next paths].** The C_3-reducible Wilson slab supplies no
universal fractional boundary eta carrier: globally zero, per-edge
non-universal, and in-gap integer. Donnelly fixed-point localization, if
realized in the framework, requires the genuinely 3d equivariant geometry in
which the C_3[111] fixed point is isolated rather than the reducible slab's
factorized ring momentum. The direct multiset-to-geometry equation is the other
named next path. Neither path is probed here.
