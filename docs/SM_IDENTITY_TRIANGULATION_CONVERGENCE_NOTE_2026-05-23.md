`Q = T_3 + Y/2`), as a function of color rank `N_c`:

```text

Y(u_R^c) = -(1 + 1/N_c),   Y(d_R^c) = (1 - 1/N_c),
```

with the anomaly-family row. (The Burnside three-generation row's

`## Demoted to future work` block above; it is not load-bearing on the
narrowed claim.)

**(3) Hypercharge convergence (narrowed).** Substituting `N_c = 3` into the
family yields the right-handed singlet particle hypercharges
`(u_R, d_R, e_R, nu_R) = (+4/3, -2/3, -2, 0)`, identical to the unique
tuple of the hypercharge-uniqueness enumeration row, with `Y(Q_L) = +1/3`
and `Y(L_L) = -1`. The left-Weyl carrier electric-charge spectrum is
`{0, ±1/3, ±2/3, ±1}`.

normalization residual is genuinely free; the template residual is an explicit
hypothesis of the hypercharge-enumeration row. ∎
