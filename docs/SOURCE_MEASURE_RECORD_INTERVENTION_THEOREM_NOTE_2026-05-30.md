## Operational equivalence

Let `Omega = {omega_1,...,omega_N}` be the finite set of sharp-record histories.
The indicator functions `1_{omega_i}` form a basis of the record algebra.
If two source settings `a` and `b` produce probability vectors `P_a` and `P_b`,
then

```text
E_a[1_{omega_i}] = P_a(omega_i),
E_b[1_{omega_i}] = P_b(omega_i).
```

Therefore equality of all sharp-record probabilities is equality of all record
observable expectations.  Conversely, any difference in probability on one
history is detected by the corresponding sharp indicator.  The probability law
is the complete operational state on the record algebra.
