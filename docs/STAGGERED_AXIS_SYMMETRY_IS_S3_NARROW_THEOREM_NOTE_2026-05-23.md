three planes — permutation-symmetric.

**Conclusion.** Two `Z_2` gauge fields with equal plaquette field strength and
equal Polyakov holonomies are gauge-equivalent on the torus; by (1)+(2) the

Hence every `S_3` axis permutation is a physical symmetry of the staggered
