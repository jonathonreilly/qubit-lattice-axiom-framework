## Derivation

### Step 1: BZ-corner enumeration

On the supplied staggered Z³ BZ-corner surface, each corner is labeled by a
binary vector `n = (n_1, n_2, n_3) ∈ {0,1}³` with `k_μ = n_μ · π`. There are
`2³ = 8` corners total.

This is the standard staggered-fermion doubling phenomenon. The
8 corners constitute the "taste-cube" `V = {0,1}³`.

### Step 2: Hamming-weight grading

Each BZ corner has a Hamming weight `hw(n) = n_1 + n_2 + n_3 ∈ {0,1,2,3}`.

The 8 corners distribute as:
- hw = 0: (0,0,0) — **1 corner**
- hw = 1: (1,0,0), (0,1,0), (0,0,1) — **3 corners**
- hw = 2: (1,1,0), (1,0,1), (0,1,1) — **3 corners**
- hw = 3: (1,1,1) — **1 corner**

This is a structural counting fact on `{0,1}³`. The total `1+3+3+1 = 8`
matches the finite corner-count surface. No position-space sublattice or
chirality interpretation is used in this narrowed row.

### Step 3: M_3(C) algebra on hw=1 (retained)

Per [`THREE_GENERATION_OBSERVABLE_THEOREM_NOTE.md`](THREE_GENERATION_OBSERVABLE_THEOREM_NOTE.md):

The three lattice translations `T_x, T_y, T_z` act on the hw=1 triplet
as:

```
T_x: diag(−1, +1, +1)
T_y: diag(+1, −1, +1)
T_z: diag(+1, +1, −1)
```

(distinct joint characters separating the three corners). Combined
with the `C_3[111]` cyclic generator that maps `(1,0,0) → (0,1,0) →
(0,0,1) → (1,0,0)`, these operators generate the full M_3(C) algebra
on `H_hw=1`.

This is the retained "exact M_3(C) algebra" structure.

### Step 4: No proper quotient (retained)

Per [`THREE_GENERATION_OBSERVABLE_NO_PROPER_QUOTIENT_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-02.md`](THREE_GENERATION_OBSERVABLE_NO_PROPER_QUOTIENT_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-02.md):

If a quotient claims to preserve any exact retained operator, the
observable-descent lemma forces its kernel to be invariant under that

exact-preserving quotient must have a kernel invariant under all three

the trivial subspace.

Therefore **no proper exact quotient** of the M_3(C) algebra on hw=1
exists.
