## 2. Realization Definition — What "The Staggered-Dirac Realization From Quantum + Lattice" Means

Throughout, Quantum is the one-qubit / `Cl(3,0)` local operator algebra
per site and Lattice is the `Z^3` lattice, per
[`MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-05.md`](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-05.md).

> **Definition.** "The staggered-Dirac realization from Quantum +
> Lattice" is the conjunction of:
>
> - **Matter-statistics clause.** The matter measure on the Quantum + Lattice
>   baseline is the finite single-mode Grassmann partition, one pair
>   `(χ_x, χ̄_x)` per site, on the dim-2 per-site Cl(3) module;
>   bosonic second quantization is excluded.
> - **Kinetic-form clause.** Within the declared kinetic class (the
>   naive-Dirac kinetic form on nearest-neighbor `Z^3` links, made
>   compatible with the matter-statistics clause by site-local spin diagonalization), the
>   kinetic operator is the staggered operator
>   `D = (1/2) Σ_{x,μ} η_μ(x) (χ̄_{x+μ̂} χ_x − χ̄_x χ_{x+μ̂})` with the
>   Kawamoto-Smit phases `η_1 = 1, η_2(x) = (−1)^{x_1},
>   η_3(x) = (−1)^{x_1+x_2}`, unique as a local Z2 gauge class.
> - **Corner-structure clause.** The free staggered operator has the
>   8-element BZ-corner (taste-cube) doubler set, decomposing uniquely
>   by Hamming weight as `1 + 3 + 3 + 1`; the hw=1 triplet carries an
>   exact irreducible `M_3(C)` algebra (translations + `C_3[111]`)
>   with no proper exact quotient.
> - **Species-surface clause.**
>   *Algebraic part, closes:* the hw=1 triplet is three pairwise
>   orthogonal, translation-character-distinct states in one physical
>   Hilbert space, connected by the `C_3[111]` lattice unitary, with
>   the simultaneous-diagonalization corollary (any operator commuting
>   with all three translations on hw=1 is diagonal).
>   *Labeling part, no-go:* no canonical bijection from the hw=1
>   triplet to a labeled SM-generation 3-set (e.g. `{e, μ, τ}`) is
>   derivable from the minimal axiom baseline; the bijection enters only via the
>   labeling-convention external premise.

The realization definition deliberately separates what is derivable on
declared premises from what is provably not derivable — so the synthesis
cannot silently promote a convention into a derivation.

### 3.3 Composition theorem

> **Theorem (bounded synthesis closure of the staggered-Dirac
> realization gate).** On the Quantum + Lattice baseline plus the
> premise set of §3.2 and the one-hop cited authorities of §3.1: the
> matter-statistics, kinetic-form, corner-structure, and algebraic
> species-surface clauses of the realization definition hold, and the
> species-labeling clause holds as a no-go with the labeling convention
> as the compatible closure route. Hence the staggered-Dirac
> realization in the sense of the realization definition is realized at the bounded tier,
> with the generation labeling carried as convention, not derivation.

**Proof.** The four substep theorems are cited; what this synthesis
must itself establish — its load-bearing content — is that they
*compose*: that the object each substep delivers is the object the
next substep consumes, and that the chained conclusion is reproduced
end-to-end by direct computation from the axiom instantiation. This is
done by three interface lemmas, one invariance lemma, and the runner's
end-to-end computation.

**Matter-to-kinetic interface.** Substep 2 consumes "a single
Grassmann mode per site on the dim-2 Cl(3) module" as its input
premise. Substep 1 delivers exactly that object: Theorem 1's
conclusion is the single-pair finite Grassmann partition on the dim-2
module, and no other surface. Runner checks 1–4 instantiate the
interface: the Pauli Cl(3) relations, the dim-2 module, the single
fermion mode `a = (σ_1 + iσ_2)/2` with `a² = 0`, `{a, a†} = I`, and
the finite-dimensional CCR trace obstruction
(`Tr[b, b†] = 0 ≠ Tr I = n` for every finite dimension n) that
excludes the bosonic alternative on this module. ∎

**Kinetic-to-corner interface.** Substep 3 consumes "the staggered
kinetic operator on `Z^3` with the Kawamoto-Smit phase gauge class"
as its input premise. Substep 2 delivers the gauge *class*, not a
single representative — so the interface is sound only if substep-3's
outputs are gauge-class invariants. That is Lemma L-G below. Runner
checks 5–10 re-derive the delivered object from first principles
(scalarization solved site by site, phases extracted then compared to
the closed form, cocycle, gauge recovery, all-plus rejection); checks
11–16 compute the consumed object's outputs directly. ∎

**Gauge-class invariance lemma.** Every output of the corner-structure and
species-surface algebraic clauses —
kernel dimension, joint translation-character multiset, Hamming
multiset, the `M_3(C)` algebra up to unitary equivalence, and the
commutant dimensions — is invariant under the local Z2 gauge freedom
of the substep-2 gauge class. *Proof:* a gauge transform replaces
`(D, T_μ, C_3)` by `(G D G†, G T_μ G†, G C_3 G†)` with `G = diag(g)`
unitary; all listed outputs are functions of the operator tuple up to
simultaneous unitary conjugation, hence unchanged (class-A argument).
Runner check 17 verifies it concretely (gauged kernel dim 8, identical
character multiset). Axis permutations are coordinate relabels
(substep-2 note) and permute the multisets within themselves. ∎

**Corner-to-species interface.** Substep 4 (both the direct
three-state theorem and the retained AC_λ bridge) consumes "three
basis states with pairwise distinct joint translation-eigenvalue
triples (2a)-(2c) and a regular `C_3` action." Substep 3 delivers
exactly those triples on the hw=1 corner triplet. Runner check 23
computes the hw=1 joint characters from the kernel of D and finds
exactly `(−1,+1,+1), (+1,−1,+1), (+1,+1,−1)` — the AC_λ bridge's
(2a)-(2c) — and checks 20–22, 24 construct the `C_3` unitary
*from the lattice* (the cyclic axis rotation `U_R`, shown to preserve
ker D and 3-cycle the hw=1 triplet) rather than postulating it. ∎

**End-to-end computation (closure).** With the interfaces verified,
the chain composes: matter-statistics -> kinetic form -> corner
structure -> algebraic species surface. The
runner then reproduces the entire composed conclusion from the axiom
instantiation on the 4³ torus, producing numbers present in no single
input note: dim ker D = 8 by SVD; the 8 joint characters = the full
taste cube `{±1}³`; Hamming multiset 1+3+3+1; the algebra generated by
`(T_1, T_2, T_3, C_3)|_{hw=1}` has computed dimension 9 = `M_3(C)`
with scalar commutant (irreducible ⇒ simple ⇒ no proper exact
quotient); the translations-only commutant has computed dimension 3
and is exactly the diagonal algebra (the AC_λ corollary). For the species-labeling no-go clause, the
runner computes the no-go witness on the same surface: conjugation by
the constructed `C_3` cyclically permutes the translation generators,
the three hw=1 states form a single `C_3` orbit, every
`C_3`-equivariant Hermitian equidistributes over the orbit
(`diag(H) = Tr(H)/3`), and the π_A/π_B counter-model evaluations
agree — so no structure-derived functional separates the corners, and
the labeling can only enter by the labeling convention (whose distinguishing power is also
verified: a generic `C_3`-equivariant Hermitian has three distinct
eigenvalues, so a sorted-order convention fixes a unique bijection).
∎ (Theorem)
