## 1. Claim scope

The gate's substep 1 asks whether the matter sector is **forced** to be
Grassmann (fermionic), vs. merely **compatible** with it. Two retained narrows
bracket the question, and a third candidate they do not compare settles it.

Let `V ≅ C²` be the one-qubit site Hilbert space from
[`MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-05-20.md`](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-05-20.md), let `Λ` be a
finite block of `Z^3` with a total ordering, and `H_Λ = V^{⊗|Λ|}`.

- **(Retained dimensional half.)**
  `staggered_dirac_substep1_grassmann_forcing_bridge_narrow_theorem_note_2026-05-16`
  (retained_bounded) proves: the **free** bosonic Fock tower has per-site
  dimension `ℵ₀` and is excluded by `dim_C V = 2`; the Grassmann per-site Fock
  has dimension `2` and matches.
- **(Retained cross-site half.)**
  `staggered_dirac_substep1_jw_bridge_narrow_theorem_note_2026-05-17`
  (retained_pending_chain) proves: a Jordan-Wigner (JW) string
  `c_x = (∏_{y<x} σ₃^(y)) σ₊^(x)` on the qubit tensor product produces operators
  satisfying cross-site CAR exactly.

This note adds the **third candidate the dimensional dichotomy omits** — the
**hard-core boson** (the literal qubit: one two-level system per site, operators
**commuting** across distinct sites) — and proves the following exact,
finite-tensor-product facts.

- **(A) The natural qubit operators are hard-core bosonic, not fermionic.** The
  bare per-site ladders `σ₊^(x)`, `σ₊^(y)` at distinct sites `x ≠ y`
  **commute** (disjoint tensor factors); `{σ₊^(x), σ₊^(y)} ≠ 0`. On-site they
  are nilpotent (`(σ₊^(x))² = 0`), so the per-site Fock dimension is `2`.

- **(B) JW dresses them into fermions.** The string-dressed `c_x` satisfy
  `{c_x, c_y} = 0`, `{c_x, c_y^†} = δ_{xy} I`, `c_x² = 0` (cross-site CAR).

- **(C) Same ungraded operator algebra — statistics is a frame choice.** The qubit-ladder
  algebra `⟨σ₊^(x), σ₃^(x)⟩` and the JW-fermion algebra `⟨c_x, c_x^†⟩` each
  generate the **same** full matrix algebra `M_{2^|Λ|}(C)` of complex dimension
  `4^|Λ| = dim_C End(H_Λ)`. JW is an invertible change of generators on **one**
  ungraded algebra. The tested operator-algebra and dimension data therefore
  do not prefer the fermionic frame over the qubit/hard-core-boson frame.

- **(D) Dimension is blind to the choice.** Per-site Fock dimension is `2` for
  the fermion **and** for the hard-core boson; only the **free** boson is
  infinite. So the retained dim-`2` readout excludes the free boson but **cannot
  distinguish** fermion from hard-core boson.

- **(E) Locality horn.** Under the lexicographic order, a nearest-neighbour pair
  in the slow coordinate is separated by a full `L²−1`-site slab, and brute
  force gives grid-graph bandwidth `4` already on the `2×2×2` block (so no
  ordering makes every neighbour adjacent). The fermionic frame is therefore
  not selected as a strictly local nearest-neighbour consequence of the qubit
  lattice either.

**Conclusion (the no-go).** Substep 1 is a **compatibility, not a forcing**.
The Axiom 1 / Axiom 2 baseline admits the Grassmann/fermionic reading (via a
JW frame choice) but also admits the hard-core-boson reading; the two are the
same ungraded operator algebra, and the fermionic frame is not selected by the
checked local-lattice ordering tests. The Grassmann content remains an
**admission candidate** (a statistics selection), not a theorem derived here
from the baseline alone.

## 2. Why this note exists

The in-flight, **unaudited** broad theorem
`STAGGERED_DIRAC_GRASSMANN_FORCING_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-07.md` claims, using
legacy shorthand for Axiom 1 / Axiom 2, that the matter measure is uniquely
Grassmann and that bosonic 2nd quantization is ruled out. Its argument is a
dimensional dichotomy: **free** bosonic Fock is
`∞`-dimensional, Grassmann is `2`, so "bosonic is excluded." That dichotomy is
a **false binary**: the physically-relevant alternative on a qubit lattice is
not the free boson but the **hard-core boson** — which is the *literal* qubit,
dimension `2` per site, and is therefore **not** excluded by the dimensional
argument.

The two retained narrows are each correct on their stated scope: the dimensional
narrow excludes only the *free* boson; the JW narrow *constructs* a fermionic
frame but explicitly disclaims uniqueness ("Does **not** claim the JW
representation is the **unique** cross-site CAR realization", JW note §6) and
records that the bare ladders commute (JW note §10, hopping-bilinear pointer).
What was missing was the explicit statement that, taken together, these facts
**defeat the forcing claim**: the fermionic and the hard-core-boson readings are
the *same* algebra in different frames, so the Axiom 1 / Axiom 2 baseline cannot
select between them by these tests. This note supplies that statement and the
verifying computation, and records the registry consequence as a candidate only.

## 5. Proof

(A)–(E) are exact statements on finite tensor products of `2×2` complex
matrices, each verified by the runner (`PASS=34 FAIL=0`):

- **(A)** disjoint-tensor-factor commutativity `[σ_a^(x), σ_b^(y)] = 0` for
  `x ≠ y`, with `{σ₊^(x), σ₊^(y)} = 2 σ₊^(x) σ₊^(y) ≠ 0`; on-site
  `(σ₊^(x))² = 0`.
- **(B)** the JW computation of the parent JW-bridge narrow, re-verified here:
  `{c_x, c_y} = 0`, `{c_x, c_y^†} = δ_{xy} I`, `c_x² = 0`.
- **(C)** the unital *-algebra generated by `{σ₊^(x), σ₃^(x)}` and the one
  generated by `{c_x, c_x^†}` are each closed (under products and adjoints) to
  full rank `4^N = dim_C M_{2^N}(C)`. Identical span ⇒ identical algebra; the JW
  map is a change of generators. This is the decisive fact for the scoped
  algebraic claim: a forcing argument from operator-algebra and dimension data
  would need a baseline-derived functional that is **non-constant** across the
  two frames, but those data see the same ungraded algebra.
- **(D)** the hard-core-boson per-site space `{|0⟩, a^†|0⟩}` with `(a^†)² = 0`
  has dimension `2`, identical to the Grassmann per-site dimension and to
  `dim_C V`; the free-boson truncations `{2,3,6,11,101,…}` diverge.
- **(E)** lexicographic string lengths `L²−1` for slow-axis nearest neighbours
  at `L ∈ {3,4,5}`; brute-forced `2×2×2` grid-graph bandwidth `= 4 > 1`.
