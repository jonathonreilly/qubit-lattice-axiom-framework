## 1. Setting and the one open predicate

Let `Λ` be a finite block of `Z^3` with `N = |Λ|` sites, `H_Λ = (C²)^{⊗N}`
the qubit tensor product of the Quantum axiom, and let

```text
    F  =  ⊗_{x ∈ Λ} σ₃^(x)  =  (-1)^{Q̂_total}
```

be the **retained** fermion-parity involution
(`fermion_parity_z2_grading_theorem_note_2026-05-02`, retained). Both tied
realizations of the substep-1 matter generators are families of `F`-odd site
fields on `H_Λ`:

- **hard-core boson:** `ψ_x = σ₊^(x)` (bare per-site ladder);
- **Grassmann/CAR:** `ψ_x = c_x = (∏_{y<x} σ₃^(y)) σ₊^(x)` (Jordan-Wigner,
  standard methodology, recomputed by the runner).

**Definition (the predicate `GL(F)` — cross-site graded locality relative to
the retained grading).** A realization `(ψ_x)_{x∈Λ}` of `F`-odd site fields
satisfies `GL(F)` iff for all `x ≠ y`:

```text
    {ψ_x, ψ_y} = 0   and   {ψ_x, ψ_y^†} = 0 .
```

This is exactly the "graded local net / Z₂-graded locality" condition the
no-go's N7 steelman names: odd sectors anticommute across disjoint sites.

## 2. Statement (what is proved)

On `H_Λ` (runner verifies at `N = 3`, hopping surface at `L = 4`, tolerance
`1e-12`, all constructions exact-integer-entry):

**(T1) Frame-blindness of every retained input.** Per-site Fock dimension
(`2 = 2`), the generated ungraded operator algebra (both generator families
span the full `M_{2^N}(C)`, dim `4^N`), the retained `Z₂` grading data (both
families are `F`-odd, bilinears `F`-even, in **both** frames), and the finite
transfer-positivity surface (the open-chain nearest-neighbour hopping
Hamiltonians are **identical matrices** in the two frames) are each constant
across the two tied candidates. Hence no retained row on this surface — alone
or in composition — discriminates between them.

**(T2) Conditional discriminator.** `GL(F)` is **non-constant** across the
candidates: the Grassmann/JW realization passes (`{c_x, c_y} = 0`,
`{c_x, c_y^†} = 0` for `x ≠ y`; on-site CAR `{c_x, c_x^†} = I`, `c_x² = 0`),
while the hard-core realization fails (`{σ₊^(x), σ₊^(y)} ≠ 0`; the fields
commute cross-site instead). Combined with the retained per-site `dim = 2`
readout (which excludes the free boson — no finite truncation satisfies the
CCR), **exactly one** of the three candidates `{free boson, hard-core boson,
Grassmann/CAR}` passes `{dim = 2} AND GL(F)`: the Grassmann/CAR realization.
**Therefore: given the two-candidate collapse plus the hard-core third
candidate, `GL(F)` forces the Grassmann/CAR statistics class.** This selects
the statistics class only; it does not claim the JW frame is the unique CAR
realization (the 05-17 JW note's uniqueness disclaimer is respected).

**(T3) Falsification leg — the no-go is reproduced.** With `GL(F)` removed
from the predicate list, every remaining tested predicate is constant across
the two frames: the tie returns, reproducing facts (C)/(D) of the retained
2026-05-25 no-go. Moreover `GL(F)` is strictly **additional input, not
ungraded-algebra data**: the same `M_{2^N}(C)` contains both a
`GL(F)`-passing generating family (`c_x`) and a `GL(F)`-failing one
(`σ₊^(x)`), so no functional of the ungraded algebra alone can decide it.

**(T4) Supplier audit — `GL(F)` is currently supplied by nothing.** See §4;
the load-bearing computed fact is that the hard-core ladders violate the
canonical CCR **hypothesis** of the 04-29 spin-statistics note's Step 2
(on-site `[a, a^†] = 1 - 2n ≠ I`, defect norm `2`), so that note's bosonic
exclusion is scoped to the free (CCR) boson only and never reaches the tied
candidate.

**Sharpened boundary (the result).** The substep-1 statistics-selection
residual (B-stat) is exactly the single predicate `GL(F)` — equivalently the
cross-site exchange sign / FS input. Given `GL(F)`, the selection closes
(T2). Without it, the retained no-go stands unchanged (T3). `GL(F)` is not
retained, not Tier-A admitted, and not derivable from the rows audited on
this surface (T1, T4).

## 5. Proof

**(T1).** Exact finite tensor-product computations, runner [A]: per-site Fock
dimensions (`σ₊² = 0` ⇒ dim 2, both frames); span-closure of the unital
*-algebras generated by `{σ₊^(x), σ₃^(x)}` and by `{c_x, c_x^†}` each reach
rank `4³ = 64 = dim_C M_8(C)` at `N = 3` (identical span ⇒ identical
ungraded algebra, re-verifying the no-go's (C)); `F² = I`,
`Spec(F) = {±1}`, `{F, σ₊^(x)} = 0` **and** `{F, c_x} = 0`, all bilinears
`F`-even in both frames; `H_hardcore = H_JW` exactly on the open `L = 4`
chain (JW strings cancel on nearest-neighbour bilinears), so
`T = e^{-τH}` and every spectral/positivity readout coincide.

**(T2).** Runner [B]: cross-site CAR for `c_x` (passes `GL(F)`);
`{σ₊^(x), σ₊^(y)} = 2 σ₊^(x) σ₊^(y) ≠ 0` with cross-site commutation
(fails `GL(F)`); finite-cutoff CCR defect for the free boson at cutoffs
`{2, 3, 5, 8}` (`Tr[a, a^†] = 0 ≠ Tr I`), excluding it on the `dim = 2`
readout; the explicit three-candidate table then has exactly one survivor of
`{dim = 2} ∧ GL(F)`. ∎(T2)

**(T3).** Runner [C]: the predicate vector with `GL(F)` deleted —
(per-site dim, ungraded-algebra dim, `F`-oddness, hopping surface) — is
verified equal across frames, restoring the tie; and the same algebra
containing both a passing and a failing generating family shows `GL(F)` is
not a function of the ungraded algebra. ∎(T3)

**(T4).** Runner [D]: on-site `[a, a^†] = 1 - 2n`, defect norm exactly `2`;
hence the hard-core candidate is outside the 04-29 note's eq.-(6) hypothesis
class. The remaining bullets of §4 are licensed by the cited rows' own
audited scopes. ∎(T4)

**Load-bearing step.** The single decisive step is (T2)'s non-constancy
