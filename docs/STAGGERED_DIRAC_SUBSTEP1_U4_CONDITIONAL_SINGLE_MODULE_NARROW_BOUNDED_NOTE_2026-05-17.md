## 1. Claim scope

Let `Cl(3,0)` be the real Clifford algebra on three generators
`γ_1, γ_2, γ_3` satisfying `{γ_i, γ_j} = 2 δ_{ij} · 1`. Let `Λ` be a
finite block of `Z^3` lattice sites. For each site `x ∈ Λ`, let
`H_x` be a finite-dim complex vector space carrying a representation
`ρ_x : Cl(3,0) → End(H_x)` of the physical Cl(3) local algebra.

**Multiplicity classification (M1).** By the cited retained narrow
theorem on the Cl(3) faithful complex-irrep dim-two readout, every
finite-dim complex representation of `Cl(3,0)` decomposes as

```text
H_x  ≅  ρ_+^{n_+(x)}  ⊕  ρ_-^{n_-(x)}                                  (M1)
```

with `dim_C H_x = 2 (n_+(x) + n_-(x))`. The total multiplicity
`k(x) := n_+(x) + n_-(x) ∈ Z_{≥0}` is the *multiplicity index* of the
per-site Cl(3) module.

**Faithfulness classification (M2).** `ρ_x` is faithful if and only if
`k(x) ≥ 1` (otherwise the trivial Cl(3) action `γ_i ↦ 0` fails the
Clifford relation `γ_i² = 1 ≠ 0`, so `k(x) = 0` is the unfaithful
"zero-rep" class). Specifically:

- `k(x) = 1`: minimal faithful, `dim_C H_x = 2`; this is the canonical
  Pauli realization (positive- or negative-chirality summand alone).
- `k(x) ≥ 2`: faithful with multiplicity, `dim_C H_x = 2 k(x) ≥ 4`. The
  Cl(3) action is still faithful (each summand carries a faithful
  irrep), but contains a non-trivial isotypic component (multiple
  copies of the same irrep).

**Conditional sub-claim (C1).** *If* the per-site Hilbert space `H_x`
carries a single faithful Cl(3) module — i.e., `(n_+(x), n_-(x))` is
either `(1, 0)` or `(0, 1)`, equivalently `k(x) = 1` — *then*
`dim_C H_x = 2` exactly.

**Conditional sub-claim (C2).** *If* additionally the canonical
positive-chirality convention is adopted (the framework-baseline
chirality choice recorded in `AXIOM_FIRST_CL3_PER_SITE_UNIQUENESS_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-04-29.md`
as the package convention), *then* the unique chirality summand is `ρ_+`
and `ρ_x(γ_i) = σ_i` on `H_x ≅ ℂ²` up to unitary conjugation.

**Honest scope (Non-closure of U4).** Sub-claims (C1) and (C2) are
*conditional* on the single-module-per-site selection. the physical Cl(3)/Z^3 framework baseline + the
retained Cl(3) algebraic classification **do not** force `k(x) = 1` on
their own; both `k(x) = 1` and `k(x) ≥ 2` are admissible Cl(3) modules
on the abstract surface. The single-module selection is the explicit
additional admitted-context input — what the open
`STAGGERED_DIRAC_REALIZATION_GATE_NOTE_2026-05-03.md` gate (substep 1)
delivers via the staggered-Dirac/Grassmann one-particle-per-site
realization. This narrow theorem therefore **does not close U4**; it
isolates the conditional algebraic content of U4 from the open
gate-content and supplies the per-multiplicity enumeration that the
gate's substep 1 needs as one input.

## 5. Proof

### 5.1 Multiplicity classification (M1)

By the cited retained narrow theorem
[`CL3_FAITHFUL_IRREP_DIM_TWO_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md`](CL3_FAITHFUL_IRREP_DIM_TWO_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10.md)
(its (D2)), the complexification `Cl(3,0) ⊗_R C ≅ M_2(C) ⊕ M_2(C)` is
semisimple, and every finite-dim complex representation decomposes as
`ρ_+^{n_+} ⊕ ρ_-^{n_-}` with `dim_C = 2(n_+ + n_-)`. The total
multiplicity index `k = n_+ + n_- ∈ Z_{≥0}` is well-defined. ∎

### 5.2 Faithfulness classification (M2)

If `k = 0`, the representation is the trivial zero-rep `ρ_x ≡ 0`, which
maps `γ_i ↦ 0`. But the Clifford relation `γ_i² = 1` requires
`ρ_x(γ_i)² = 1 ≠ 0`, contradiction. Hence the zero-rep is unfaithful
(in fact non-Clifford). Conversely, if `k ≥ 1`, at least one of `n_+`
or `n_-` is ≥ 1, so the representation contains a copy of `ρ_+` or
`ρ_-` (each faithful by (M1)). Since `ρ_+` and `ρ_-` are both faithful
2-dim irreps, their direct sums (of any multiplicity) are faithful
representations of `Cl(3,0)`. Hence `ρ_x` is faithful iff `k ≥ 1`. ∎

### 5.3 Conditional sub-claim (C1)

Assume `k(x) = 1`, i.e., `(n_+(x), n_-(x)) ∈ {(1, 0), (0, 1)}`. Then
`H_x = ρ_+` or `H_x = ρ_-`, in either case `dim_C H_x = 2`. ∎

### 5.4 Conditional sub-claim (C2)

Assume additionally the canonical positive-chirality convention. Then
the chirality choice is `(n_+, n_-) = (1, 0)`, so `H_x = ρ_+` and
`ρ_x(γ_i) = σ_i` on `H_x ≅ ℂ²` up to unitary conjugation. The
intertwiner uniqueness up to nonzero scalar within a chirality summand
follows from Schur's lemma applied to the simple module `ρ_+`. ∎

### 5.5 Counter-example surface: `k ≥ 2`

For each `k ≥ 2`, the representation `ρ_x = ρ_+^k` (`k` copies of the
positive-chirality irrep) gives a faithful Cl(3) representation on
`H_x ≅ ℂ^{2k}` of complex dimension `2k`. Similarly `ρ_-^k`,
`ρ_+^{k-1} ⊕ ρ_-`, etc., give other faithful representations of
dimension `2k`. These are not unitarily equivalent to the minimal
`k = 1` case (different complex dimensions, different isotypic
multiplicities), and none of them violate any retained framework
primitive on the algebraic surface (M1)-(M2). Hence `k = 1` is **not**
forced by the physical Cl(3)/Z^3 framework baseline plus the retained Cl(3) classification alone.

A separate forcing argument is needed; the relevant admitted-context
input is the staggered-Dirac/Grassmann one-particle-per-site
realization carried by the open
`STAGGERED_DIRAC_REALIZATION_GATE_NOTE_2026-05-03.md` gate (substep 1).
Specifically, the Grassmann per-site Fock construction
`H_x^G = span(|0⟩_x, |1⟩_x)` from a single
`(χ_x, χ̄_x)` Grassmann pair gives `dim_C H_x^G = 2`, matching `k = 1`.
Selecting more than one Grassmann pair per site would give
`dim_C H_x^G = 2^p` for `p` pairs, hence `k = 2^{p-1}` — a different
multiplicity-index selection. Both are admissible Grassmann
realizations of the per-site algebra; the choice of "one pair per site"
is a substep-1 substantive content that this note does **not** close.
