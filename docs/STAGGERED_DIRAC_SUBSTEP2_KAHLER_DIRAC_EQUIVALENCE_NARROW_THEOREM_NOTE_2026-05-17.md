## 5. Proof

### 5.1 (E1) Per-hypercube Z₂^d-indexed component count

A hypercube `H_n` of `Z^d` contains `2^d` sites indexed by
`b ∈ {0, 1}^d`. The abstract per-hypercube Z₂^d-indexed complex
vector space `V_{H_n} := C^{2^d}` with basis `{ε^b : b ∈ {0, 1}^d}`
has complex dimension `dim_C V_{H_n} = 2^d` by direct enumeration of
`{0, 1}^d`. By the cited substep-3 narrow theorem (R1), the BZ-corner
cardinality on `{0, π}^d` is also `2^d`. At `d = 4`,
`dim_C V_{H_n} = 2^4 = 16` matches the cited substep-3 BZ-corner
count `16`. The substep-1 narrow theorem's per-site Grassmann
nilpotency `χ_x² = 0` is the algebraic ingredient that makes the
per-site Grassmann generator pattern algebraically compatible with
the exterior-algebra generator pattern (`dx^μ ∧ dx^μ = 0`); this
compatibility is the algebraic basis for the (E3) graded bijection
below. The narrow theorem does **not** identify `V_{H_n}` with the
tensor product `⊗_b H_b^G` of per-site Grassmann Fock spaces (that
would give `2^{2^d}` not `2^d`); rather, `V_{H_n}` is the Z₂^d-indexed
component space of a per-hypercube field, with dimensional readout
`2^d` independently fixed by the BZ-corner enumeration. ∎

### 5.2 (E2) Form complex dim = 2^d

The exterior algebra `Λ^*(C^d) = ⊕_{p=0}^d Λ^p(C^d)` over `C^d` has
the standard graded decomposition with
`dim_C Λ^p(C^d) = binom(d, p)`. By the binomial identity

```text
∑_{p=0}^d binom(d, p) = (1 + 1)^d = 2^d,
```

the total complex dim is `dim_C Λ^*(C^d) = 2^d`. At `d = 4`,
`2^4 = 16` matches the cited substep-3 narrow theorem's
naive-lattice species count. ∎

### 5.3 (E3) Z₂^d-graded bijection: Hamming-weight ↔ form-degree

Define the Hamming weight `hw : {0, 1}^d → {0, 1, ..., d}` by
`hw(b) = ∑_{i=1}^d b_i`. The fibers of `hw` partition `{0, 1}^d`
by weight, and by the cited substep-3 narrow theorem (R4 and its
verification),

```text
card({b ∈ {0,1}^d : hw(b) = p}) = binom(d, p).
```

By (E2), `dim_C Λ^p(C^d) = binom(d, p)`. Hence

```text
card({b ∈ {0,1}^d : hw(b) = p}) = dim_C Λ^p(C^d)
```

for every `p ∈ {0, 1, ..., d}`. Pick an ordered basis of `Λ^p(C^d)`
indexed by ordered subsets `S ⊂ {1, ..., d}` with `|S| = p`; the
canonical bijection between `{b ∈ {0,1}^d : hw(b) = p}` and ordered
subsets `S = {i : b_i = 1}` of size `p` yields a graded
linear-algebra bijection

```text
V_{H_n}  :=  C^{2^d}  =  span(ε^b : b ∈ {0,1}^d)
         ≅  Λ^*(C^d)  =  ⊕_{p=0}^d Λ^p(C^d),
```

matching the Hamming-weight grading on the left to the form-degree
grading on the right. At `d = 4`, the per-hypercube graded
decomposition is `1 + 4 + 6 + 4 + 1 = 16` on both sides. ∎

### 5.4 (E4) Kähler-Dirac operator definition on the form complex

On `Λ^*(C^d)`, define the exterior derivative `d : Λ^p → Λ^{p+1}` and
co-derivative `δ : Λ^p → Λ^{p-1}` by the standard exterior-algebra
operations. For a basis form `dx^S := dx^{i_1} ∧ ... ∧ dx^{i_p}`
with `S = {i_1 < i_2 < ... < i_p}`:

```text
d (dx^S)  =  ∑_{j ∉ S}  σ(j, S) · dx^{S ∪ {j}},
δ (dx^S)  =  ∑_{j ∈ S}  σ̃(j, S) · dx^{S \ {j}},
```

where `σ(j, S), σ̃(j, S) ∈ {+1, -1}` are the standard insertion /
deletion signs (Koszul sign convention).

The Kähler-Dirac operator on `Λ^*(C^d)` is `D_KD := d - δ`. Three
abstract-algebraic properties hold:

(a) **Form-parity reversal.** `D_KD` maps `Λ^p → Λ^{p-1} ⊕ Λ^{p+1}`,
    i.e., it reverses form-degree parity (sends even-graded to
    odd-graded and vice versa). This matches the Z₂-graded parity
    of a Dirac operator on a fermionic Hilbert space.

(b) **Nilpotency identities.** Standard finite-dim exterior-algebra:
    `d² = 0` (cocycle identity for the exterior derivative) and
    `δ² = 0` (dual cocycle identity for the co-derivative). Hence
    `D_KD² = (d - δ)² = d² - dδ - δd + δ² = -(dδ + δd)`,
    a Hodge-Laplacian-type operator on `Λ^*(C^d)`.

(c) **Hodge-Laplacian decomposition.** The operator
    `Δ := dδ + δd` preserves each form-degree subspace `Λ^p`, so
    `-D_KD²` decomposes blockwise as
    `-D_KD² = ⊕_{p=0}^d Δ|_{Λ^p}`.

Properties (a)-(c) are standard finite-dim exterior-algebra
identities. They define `D_KD` as a linear operator on `Λ^*(C^d)`
with the expected Z₂-graded / Hodge-Laplacian structure. ∎

### 5.5 (E5) Spinor-count factor match at even d (cont.)

At `d = 4`, the form-complex graded dim count

### 5.6 (E6) Hermiticity of i · D_KD on Λ^*(C^d)

On the orthonormal form basis of `Λ^*(C^d)` (basis subsets ordered
within each grade by lex, normalized to unit norm under the standard
Hodge inner product), the runner verifies `δ = d^T` exactly at
`d ∈ {2, 3, 4}` (Part 6). Since the matrix representations of `d`
and `δ` have integer ±1 entries (Koszul sign convention), the
adjoint coincides with the transpose: `δ = d†`. Therefore

```text
D_KD†  =  (d - δ)†  =  d† - δ†  =  δ - d  =  -D_KD.
```

The operator `i · D_KD` satisfies `(i · D_KD)† = -i · D_KD† =
-i · (-D_KD) = i · D_KD`, i.e., `i · D_KD` is Hermitian on
`Λ^*(C^d)`. This matches the standard physics-convention "kinetic
Dirac operator" (the antihermitian `D` appearing in first-order
equations of motion; the Hermitian `iD` appearing as the kinetic
bilinear in the action). ∎

### 5.7 (E7) Wilson r-term breaks form-parity reversal

Define the Wilson lattice Dirac operator on `Λ^*(C^d)` as
`D_W := D_KD + r · M`, where the Wilson mass-like term `M` is any
operator preserving each `Λ^p → Λ^p` subspace (the simplest case is
`M = I` the identity, used in the runner). The exterior derivative
`d` and co-derivative `δ` strictly shift form-degree by `±1`
(`d : Λ^p → Λ^{p+1}`, `δ : Λ^p → Λ^{p-1}`), so `D_KD = d - δ` has
exactly zero diagonal blocks on each `Λ^p → Λ^p` subspace (E4 part
(a), Part 7 of the runner).

The Wilson r-term `r · M` adds nonzero diagonal contributions on
each `Λ^p → Λ^p` block (for `r ≠ 0`). The runner verifies at
`d ∈ {2, 3, 4}` that the `Λ^0 → Λ^0` block of `D_W = D_KD + r · I`
is `r · I_{1×1}`, a nonzero `1 × 1` block. Hence `D_W` does NOT
reverse form-degree parity: it has a nontrivial parity-preserving
component.

Consequently, the Wilson operator on the abstract form complex
`Λ^*(C^d)` is **not** of the `d - δ` Kähler-Dirac shape; it sits
outside the narrow uniqueness frame "Hermitian, Z₂-graded-parity-
reversing, range-1 in Hamming-weight" set by (E4) + (E6). This is
a narrow algebraic statement about operators on `Λ^*(C^d)`; it
makes no statement about chiral-symmetry breaking, Wilson
fine-tuning, or doubler-removal cost on the framework's physical
lattice substrate (those are downstream substrate-level issues,
out of scope here). ∎
