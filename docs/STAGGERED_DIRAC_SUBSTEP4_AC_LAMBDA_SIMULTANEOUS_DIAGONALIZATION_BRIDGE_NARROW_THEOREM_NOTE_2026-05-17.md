## 1. Claim scope

Let `V_3` be a 3-dimensional complex vector space with a fixed ordered
basis `(e_1, e_2, e_3)`. Let `(T_1, T_2, T_3)` be a triple of pairwise
commuting unitary operators on `V_3` satisfying

```text
[T_μ, T_ν]  =  0   for all μ, ν ∈ {1, 2, 3},                       (1a)
T_μ |e_α⟩   =  τ_μ^(α) |e_α⟩,    α = 1, 2, 3, μ = 1, 2, 3,         (1b)
```

with each `T_μ` diagonal in the basis `(e_1, e_2, e_3)` and joint
eigenvalue triples

```text
(τ_1^(1), τ_2^(1), τ_3^(1))  =  (-1, +1, +1),                       (2a)
(τ_1^(2), τ_2^(2), τ_3^(2))  =  (+1, -1, +1),                       (2b)
(τ_1^(3), τ_2^(3), τ_3^(3))  =  (+1, +1, -1).                       (2c)
```

That is, the three basis vectors are simultaneous eigenvectors of all
three `T_μ`, with the three joint-eigenvalue triples (2a)-(2c) pairwise
distinct.

The following identities hold:

- **(L1) Pairwise distinctness.** Any two of the joint eigenvalue
  triples in (2a)-(2c) differ in at least one component:

  ```text
  ∀α ≠ β, ∃μ ∈ {1,2,3} :  τ_μ^(α) ≠ τ_μ^(β).                       (3)
  ```

- **(L2) Simultaneous-diagonalization corollary.** Let `K : V_3 → V_3`
  be a linear operator commuting with all three `T_μ`:

  ```text
  [K, T_μ]  =  0    for μ = 1, 2, 3.                                (4)
  ```

  Then `K` is diagonal in the basis `(e_1, e_2, e_3)`:

  ```text
  ⟨e_α | K | e_β⟩  =  0    for all α ≠ β.                          (5)
  ```

- **(L3) Off-diagonal vanishing follows from any single distinguishing
  `T_μ`.** For each ordered pair `(α, β)` with `α ≠ β`, there exists
  at least one `μ ∈ {1, 2, 3}` with `τ_μ^(α) ≠ τ_μ^(β)`, and the
  commutator identity `[K, T_μ] = 0` together with `T_μ`-eigenvalue
  distinctness on `(e_α, e_β)` alone forces `⟨e_α | K | e_β⟩ = 0`.

- **(L4) Diagonal class.** The set of operators `K` on `V_3`
  satisfying (4) is exactly the algebra of diagonal matrices

  ```text
  K  =  diag(k_1, k_2, k_3),    k_α ∈ C,                            (6)
  ```

  i.e., the 3-dimensional complex commutative algebra `C^3` (one
  complex degree of freedom per basis vector).

Facts (L1)-(L4) are exact algebraic identities on the abstract 3-dim
complex carrier with the explicit commuting-unitary triple
`(T_1, T_2, T_3)` having joint eigenvalue triples (2a)-(2c). They make
**no** claim that the framework's hw=1 carrier on the physical lattice
substrate IS this 3-dim complex space with these specific lattice
translations; that bridge is carried by the open staggered-Dirac
realization gate (specifically, substep 3 plus the BZ-corner forcing
content) and remains out of scope here.

## 5. Proof

### 5.1 (L1) Pairwise distinctness of joint eigenvalue triples

By inspection of (2a)-(2c):

```text
(τ^(1), τ^(2))  differ at μ = 1, 2     (e.g., τ_1^(1) = -1 ≠ +1 = τ_1^(2));
(τ^(1), τ^(3))  differ at μ = 1, 3     (e.g., τ_1^(1) = -1 ≠ +1 = τ_1^(3));
(τ^(2), τ^(3))  differ at μ = 2, 3     (e.g., τ_2^(2) = -1 ≠ +1 = τ_2^(3)).
```

For every ordered pair `(α, β)` with `α ≠ β`, there is at least one
`μ ∈ {1, 2, 3}` with `τ_μ^(α) ≠ τ_μ^(β)`. ∎

### 5.2 (L3) Single-`T_μ` distinguishability implies off-diagonal vanishing

Fix `(α, β)` with `α ≠ β`, and pick a `μ` such that
`τ_μ^(α) ≠ τ_μ^(β)` (which exists by (L1)). Consider the matrix
element `⟨e_α | [K, T_μ] | e_β⟩`:

```text
⟨e_α | [K, T_μ] | e_β⟩  =  ⟨e_α | K T_μ | e_β⟩ - ⟨e_α | T_μ K | e_β⟩.    (7)
```

Since `T_μ` is diagonal in `(e_1, e_2, e_3)` with eigenvalue `τ_μ^(β)`
on `e_β`, the first term is

```text
⟨e_α | K T_μ | e_β⟩  =  τ_μ^(β) ⟨e_α | K | e_β⟩.                         (8)
```

For the second term, `T_μ |e_α⟩ = τ_μ^(α) |e_α⟩` and `T_μ` is
self-adjoint at the corner-basis level (since it acts diagonally with
real eigenvalues `±1`), so

```text
⟨e_α | T_μ K | e_β⟩  =  (T_μ^† | e_α⟩)^† K | e_β⟩  =  τ_μ^(α) ⟨e_α | K | e_β⟩,   (9)
```

using `T_μ^† |e_α⟩ = τ_μ^(α) |e_α⟩` (the eigenvalues are real, so
`T_μ^† = T_μ` on these eigenvectors). Subtracting (9) from (8):

```text
⟨e_α | [K, T_μ] | e_β⟩  =  (τ_μ^(β) - τ_μ^(α)) ⟨e_α | K | e_β⟩.          (10)
```

By the hypothesis `[K, T_μ] = 0`, the left side vanishes. Since
`τ_μ^(α) ≠ τ_μ^(β)`, the prefactor `(τ_μ^(β) - τ_μ^(α))` is nonzero,
forcing

```text
⟨e_α | K | e_β⟩  =  0.                                                   (11)
```

∎

### 5.3 (L2) Simultaneous-diagonalization corollary

By (L1), for every ordered pair `(α, β)` with `α ≠ β`, there is at
least one `μ` with `τ_μ^(α) ≠ τ_μ^(β)`. Apply (L3) at that `μ` to get
`⟨e_α | K | e_β⟩ = 0`. Since this holds for every off-diagonal
`(α, β)`, the operator `K` is diagonal in the basis
`(e_1, e_2, e_3)`. ∎

### 5.4 (L4) Diagonal class

By (L2), any `K` commuting with all three `T_μ` is diagonal in
`(e_1, e_2, e_3)`. Conversely, any diagonal operator
`K = diag(k_1, k_2, k_3)` commutes with each diagonal `T_μ` (diagonal
matrices commute pairwise). Hence the set of `K` satisfying (4) is
exactly the algebra of diagonal `3 × 3` complex matrices, of complex
dimension `3`. ∎
