## Atomic decomposition

The prior single-clause AC

```
AC_narrow := "physical-species reading of joint-translation-character-
              distinct hw=1 corners as SM matter generations"
```

asserts (i) the three states are physically distinguished, (ii) they
carry a species-type label, and (iii) the species-label IS the SM
generation label. These three sub-assertions decompose into three
independent atoms:

### Atom AC_φ (physical observable)

> The three orthogonal eigenstates `|c_α⟩` of the joint translation
> triple `(T_x, T_y, T_z)` on `H_{hw=1}` are distinguished by **at
> least one physical observable** beyond translation eigenvalues.
> In this note the check is deliberately bounded: it tests whether a
> C3-symmetric self-adjoint observable on `H_{hw=1}` can distinguish
> the three corner-basis states by expectation value (or by any
> moment of the observable's distribution).

### Atom AC_λ (species label / corner-block no-mixing)

> The triplet `{|c_α⟩}` carries a label-type that is the same kind
> as SM flavor — i.e., the free kinetic operator and the regulated
> free propagator have **no inter-corner matrix elements** on the
> hw=1 corner basis (corner-block no-mixing; the finite, exactly
> computable form of "free propagator block-diagonal on the species
> index").

### Atom AC_φλ (SM-flavor-generation identification)

> The species-label of atom AC_λ **is** the SM "generation" label
> (the structural-replication label e/μ/τ, u/c/t, d/s/b, ν_e/ν_μ/ν_τ),
> not some other kind of flavor (hidden sector, internal symmetry
> unrelated to e/μ/τ, multiplicity-only label).

### Validity of decomposition

The conjunction `AC_φ ∧ AC_λ ∧ AC_φλ` is logically equivalent to the
prior synthesis single clause. The atoms are independent: each can vary
without forcing the others. The independence countermodels are now
**computed**, not asserted, in the runner (Section 3):

- CM1 (AC_φ fails, AC_λ holds): the framework's actual free sector —
  zero corner block of `D` together with equal corner expectations

- CM3 (AC_φ holds with AC_λ untouched): the C3-breaking observable

AC_φλ's independence is semantic (a labeling claim, not an operator

## Derivation

### Step 1: AC_λ corner-block no-mixing, computed from upstream substep 2

By substep 2 (Kawamoto-Smit forcing, recorded in
[`STAGGERED_DIRAC_KAWAMOTO_SMIT_FORCING_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-07.md`](STAGGERED_DIRAC_KAWAMOTO_SMIT_FORCING_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-07.md)),
the framework's lattice fermion kinetic operator on Z³ is the
staggered form

```
D = (1/2) Σ_{x,μ} η_μ(x) (χ̄_{x+μ̂} χ_x − χ̄_x χ_{x+μ̂}),
    η_1 = 1,  η_2(x) = (−1)^{x_1},  η_3(x) = (−1)^{x_1+x_2}
```

unique up to local Z2 gauge, boundary holonomy, and axis permutation.
Acting on a plane wave `e^{ip·x}`, each phase factor shifts the
momentum by π times its support vector:

```
D e^{ip·x} = −Σ_μ i·sin(p_μ) e^{i(p + π m_μ)·x},
    m_1 = 0,  m_2 = e_1,  m_3 = e_1 + e_2   (mod 2)
```

so `D` is **not** diagonal in momentum — it acts block-wise on each
momentum orbit `p + π{0,1}³`. (The 2026-05-07 original of this note
wrote a single-momentum formula `K(k) = Σ_μ i η_μ sin(k_μ) γ_μ` with
position-dependent `η_μ` inside a momentum-space expression and
claimed `[K, T_μ] = 0`; both are incorrect for the one-component
staggered field and are repaired here.)

At every BZ-corner momentum `p ∈ {0, π}³` — in particular at the
three hw=1 corners — **every** amplitude `sin(p_μ)` vanishes, so

```
D |c_n⟩ = 0   exactly, for all 8 corner plane waves |c_n⟩.
```

This is a local identity, independent of the boundary wrap. Two exact
consequences, both computed in the primary runner (Section 1, in
exact integer arithmetic at L=4 and L=6 on the even-L periodic-wrap
representative — the upstream note classifies finite-boundary wrap
phases as holonomy convention data, and the even-L periodic wrap is
the representative on which the corner momenta are exactly
representable at finite L):

1. The hw=1 corner block of `D` is the **zero 3×3 matrix** — in
   particular there are no inter-corner matrix elements
   (`⟨c_α|D|c_β⟩ = 0` for all α, β).
2. The corner states are exact eigenvectors of the regulated operator
   `D + m`, so the regulated free propagator satisfies

   ```
   ⟨c_α | (D + m)^{-1} | c_β⟩  =  δ_{αβ} / m      (exactly, any m > 0)
   ```

   — the free 2-point function has no inter-corner mixing at the
   corner momenta. This is the corner-block form of the standard
   species decomposition of free fermion fields (cf. Streater-Wightman

### Step 3: AC_φ is blocked by C_3[111] symmetry unless C3 is broken

Under the Z³ point-group setup, the cyclic axis permutation C_3[111]
acts as `(x, y, z) → (y, z, x)`. This permutes the hw=1 corners
cyclically: `(1,0,0) → (0,1,0) → (0,0,1) → (1,0,0)`. Let `U_{C_3}`
be the corresponding 3-cycle unitary on the corner basis of
`H_{hw=1} ≅ C³`.

**Lemma (equal corner expectations).** If `A` is any operator on
`H_{hw=1}` with `[A, U_{C_3}] = 0`, then `A` has the **same
expectation value** on each corner-basis state:

```
⟨c_α | A | c_α⟩  =  ⟨c_β | A | c_β⟩    for all α, β = 1, 2, 3.
```

**Proof (orbit transport).** `U_{C_3} |c_α⟩ = |c_{σ(α)}⟩` where σ is
the 3-cycle. Then

```
⟨c_{σ(α)} | A | c_{σ(α)}⟩ = ⟨U c_α | A | U c_α⟩
                          = ⟨c_α | U† A U | c_α⟩ = ⟨c_α | A | c_α⟩,
```

so the expectation value is constant along the orbit. ∎

Three sharpenings, all runner-verified (Section 2):

1. **Full family, not an instance.** The set of self-adjoint
   operators commuting with `U_{C_3}` is exactly the real span of
   `{I, U + U², i(U − U²)}` (3-dimensional commutant — `U_{C_3}` has
   the non-degenerate spectrum `{1, ω, ω²}`, so its commutant is the
   polynomial algebra in `U`); the runner verifies the dimension by
   SVD nullspace and verifies equal diagonals on every basis element,
   which proves the lemma for the whole family by linearity. The
   general element is `H = aI + bU + b̄U²` with `a` real.
2. **All moments, not just expectations.** If `[H, U] = 0` then
   `[H^n, U] = 0`, so all moments `⟨c_α|H^n|c_α⟩` are equal across
   the three corners: a C3-symmetric observable cannot distinguish
   the corner states by *any* statistic of its outcome distribution.
3. **Non-triviality and sharpness.** The family is not spectrally
   trivial (the canonical instance `a = 1.5`, `b = 0.7` has
   eigenvalues `{0.8, 0.8, 2.9}` in the C_3-phase basis yet
   expectation `1.5` on each corner state), and the obstruction is
   exactly the symmetry restriction: the C3-breaking observable
   `diag(1, 2, 3)` distinguishes all three corners.

**Consequence:** a C3-symmetric observable cannot distinguish the
three corner-basis states by expectation value (or by any moment).
AC_φ therefore requires C3-breaking dynamics or a non-C3-symmetric
observable input; it is not closed by the current upstream stack.
