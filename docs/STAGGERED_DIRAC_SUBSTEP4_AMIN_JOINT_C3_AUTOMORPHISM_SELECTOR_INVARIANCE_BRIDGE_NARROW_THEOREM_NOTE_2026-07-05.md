## Theorem (narrow bridge)

Let `sigma` be the cyclic coordinate permutation `x -> y -> z -> x` of `Z^3`,
realized by the proper cubic rotation `C_3[111]`, acting **jointly**:

- on lattice axes/translations: `T_mu -> T_sigma(mu)`;
- on the hw=1 BZ corners: `c_alpha -> c_sigma(alpha)` (momentum components
  permute with the axes).

Then:

**(T1) — the joint action is axiom-supplied symmetry.** The live axiom memo
[MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md) supplies, verbatim:
the Lattice axiom includes "standard translations, and proper cubic rotations
about each site" with "No site is privileged. Sites are distinguished by the
supplied lattice structure alone."; the Admissibility axiom is "covariant
under lattice translations and proper cubic rotations"; the Qubit axiom is
site-uniform with "No possibility is privileged."; the Record axiom is stated
site-uniformly with no axis reference. `C_3[111]` is a proper cubic rotation,
so `sigma` maps the axiom-supplied presentation to itself: it is an
automorphism of the A_min presentation, not additional structure.

**(T2) — the derived corner structure is jointly equivariant.** On the
carrier representative (the retained corner-triplet surface of
[STAGGERED_DIRAC_SUBSTEP3_BZ_CORNER_HAMMING_ORBIT_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-17.md](STAGGERED_DIRAC_SUBSTEP3_BZ_CORNER_HAMMING_ORBIT_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-17.md)
and
[STAGGERED_DIRAC_SUBSTEP4_AC_LAMBDA_SIMULTANEOUS_DIAGONALIZATION_BRIDGE_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-17.md](STAGGERED_DIRAC_SUBSTEP4_AC_LAMBDA_SIMULTANEOUS_DIAGONALIZATION_BRIDGE_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-17.md)):
exactly, `C^{-1} T_mu C = T_{sigma^{-1}(mu)}` (conjugation by the carrier
`C_3` permutes the translation set to itself), the joint eigenvalue table
satisfies `tau^{sigma(alpha)}_{sigma(mu)} = tau^{alpha}_{mu}` for all
`alpha, mu`, and `sigma` maps the hw=1 corner momentum set
`{(pi,0,0), (0,pi,0), (0,0,pi)}` to itself cyclically. Consequently the
relabeled readout of any operator word in the supplied generators equals the

**(T3) — no definable corner selector.** The memo's Qualification clause
states, verbatim: "a law may not depend on a choice not fixed by the supplied
structure, unless that choice is admitted." By (T1) the supplied structure is
`sigma`-symmetric and by (T2) every derived readout is `sigma`-covariant, so
any A_min-derivable (non-admitted) selector value on the corner triplet is
constant on the `sigma`-orbit; since `sigma` is transitive on `{c_1, c_2,
c_3}`, no such selector fixes a bijection to a labeled 3-set. In particular
axis-naming selectors (e.g. "the corner with `T_1`-eigenvalue −1") do not
escape: the axis name itself is moved by the automorphism, and privileging an
axis is a choice not fixed by the supplied structure — available only as an
admitted premise, which is exactly the labeling no-go's P1/P2/P3 surface.

**Corollary (what this wires).** `pi_A` and `pi_B = pi_A ∘ sigma` are
indistinguishable for **every** A_min proposition, not only the sampled ones;
any distinguishing premise must add non-A_min content. This is the missing
