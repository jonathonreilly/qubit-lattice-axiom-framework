## Statement

Let `Λ = (Z mod L)^3` with even `L`. Let `V_lat := C^{|Λ|} = C^{L^3}`
with the lattice-basis vectors `|x⟩` indexed by `x = (x_1, x_2, x_3) ∈ Λ`.
Let `H := i D` for `D` the staggered hopping operator used by
`HERMITIAN_LIFT_THETA_H_PK_BOUNDED_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-17.md`
and explicitly defined here, i.e.

```text
    D |x⟩  =  Σ_{μ=1}^{3}  (1/2) · η_μ(x) · (|x + e_μ⟩ − |x − e_μ⟩)     (D)
```

with the staggered phases

```text
    η_1(x) = 1,    η_2(x) = (−1)^{x_1},    η_3(x) = (−1)^{x_1 + x_2}.   (η)
```

Define the **direction-`μ` hopping summand** of `D` by

```text
    D_μ |x⟩  :=  (1/2) · η_μ(x) · (|x + e_μ⟩ − |x − e_μ⟩),               (D_μ)
```

and let `H_μ := i D_μ` (each `H_μ` Hermitian by the same argument as
`H = i D` Hermitian, since `D_μ` is real anti-Hermitian). The
Hilbert-Schmidt inner product on `End_C(V_lat)` is
`⟨A, B⟩_HS := Tr(A^† B)`.

Then:

**(S1) Direction-decomposition.** The staggered Hamiltonian decomposes
exactly:

```text
    H  =  H_1 + H_2 + H_3.                                                (S1)
```

This is the matrix identity `D = D_1 + D_2 + D_3` (immediate from (D),
(D_μ)) multiplied by `i`.

**(S2) On-site (diagonal) projection vanishes.** Let
`P_diag: End_C(V_lat) → End_C(V_lat)` be the linear projector onto
matrices supported on the diagonal `{(x, x) : x ∈ Λ}` (formally
`P_diag(A)_{x, y} := δ_{x, y} · A_{x, x}`). Then

```text
    P_diag(H)  =  0_{V_lat}.                                              (S2)
```

Equivalently, every diagonal matrix entry of `H` vanishes:
`⟨x | H | x⟩ = 0` for every `x ∈ Λ`. Equivalently the Hilbert-Schmidt
inner product of `H` with every on-site diagonal operator is zero:
`⟨ |x⟩⟨x|, H ⟩_HS = 0` for every `x`.

**(S3) Longer-range hopping projection vanishes.** Let
`P_{r > 1}: End_C(V_lat) → End_C(V_lat)` be the linear projector onto
matrices supported on pairs `(x, y) ∈ Λ × Λ` with periodic Manhattan
distance `d_per(x, y) > 1`. (Periodic Manhattan distance:
`d_per(x, y) := Σ_{μ=1}^{3} min(|x_μ − y_μ|, L − |x_μ − y_μ|)`.) Then

```text
    P_{r > 1}(H)  =  0_{V_lat}.                                           (S3)
```

Equivalently, `⟨x | H | y⟩ = 0` for every `x, y ∈ Λ` with
`d_per(x, y) > 1`.

**(S4) Cross-direction hopping projection vanishes.** Let
`P_{cross}: End_C(V_lat) → End_C(V_lat)` be the linear projector onto
matrices supported on pairs `(x, y)` with `d_per(x, y) = 1` but with
the difference `x − y mod L` not aligned with any single coordinate
axis. (For nearest-neighbor pairs in `Λ` with even `L ≥ 4`, the
displacement `x − y mod L` is always axis-aligned, but the projector
is defined for completeness.) Then

```text
    P_{cross}(H)  =  0_{V_lat}.                                           (S4)
```

**(S5) Pairwise Hilbert-Schmidt orthogonality of direction summands.**
For every `μ ≠ ν ∈ {1, 2, 3}`:

```text
    ⟨ H_μ, H_ν ⟩_HS  =  Tr(H_μ^† H_ν)  =  0.                              (S5)
```

**(S6) Direction-completeness corollary.** Define
`H_NN^single := span_C{H_1, H_2, H_3} ⊂ End_C(V_lat)`. Then for every
`x, y ∈ Λ`, the matrix entry `⟨x | H | y⟩` lies in the span of the
direction-resolved entries of the `H_μ`. Equivalently, the
Hilbert-Schmidt projection of `H` onto the orthogonal complement
`(H_NN^single)^⊥` of the single-direction-NN span (within the Hermitian
subspace) is zero:

```text
    Proj_{(H_NN^single)^⊥}(H)  =  0_{V_lat}.                              (S6)
```

(S1)–(S6) are the load-bearing content of this narrow theorem. They
are pure class-A algebraic facts about the matrix entries of `D`
derivable from (D), (D_μ), (η). They are sympy- and numpy-verified
exact at audit-companion precision on the framework instances
`L ∈ {4, 6}`. They are exact on every even `L ≥ 4`; the runner's
finite-`L` verification is a sanity check, not the load-bearing step
(the load-bearing step is the structural argument in the proof, which
is `L`-independent).

## Proof

Pure structural algebra at the matrix level. All six items follow from
the explicit row-by-row matrix entries of `D` per (D), (η).

### (S1) Direction-decomposition

By definition (D),

```text
    D |x⟩  =  Σ_{μ=1}^{3} (1/2) η_μ(x) (|x + e_μ⟩ − |x − e_μ⟩)
            =  Σ_{μ=1}^{3} D_μ |x⟩.
```

Therefore `D = D_1 + D_2 + D_3` as a matrix-valued linear identity on
the basis `{|x⟩ : x ∈ Λ}`. Multiplying by `i` yields
`H = i D = i (D_1 + D_2 + D_3) = H_1 + H_2 + H_3`. ∎

### (S2) On-site (diagonal) projection vanishes

By (D), `D |x⟩` has only contributions on basis states `|x ± e_μ⟩`
with `μ ∈ {1, 2, 3}` and the displacements `± e_μ ≠ 0` (note
`e_μ ≠ 0 mod L` for `L ≥ 2`). Therefore `⟨x | D | x⟩ = 0` for every
`x ∈ Λ`. Multiplying by `i` gives `⟨x | H | x⟩ = i · 0 = 0`. Hence
`P_diag(H) = 0`. ∎

### (S3) Longer-range hopping projection vanishes

By (D), the only basis states with nonzero overlap to `D |x⟩` are
`|x ± e_μ⟩` for `μ ∈ {1, 2, 3}`. Each `|x ± e_μ⟩` lies at periodic
Manhattan distance exactly `d_per(x, x ± e_μ) = 1` from `|x⟩`
(individual axis distance `min(1, L − 1) = 1` for `L ≥ 2`; all other
axes contribute zero). Hence `⟨y | D | x⟩ = 0` for every `y` with
`d_per(x, y) ≠ 1`. In particular `⟨y | D | x⟩ = 0` for
`d_per(x, y) > 1`. Multiplying by `i` gives `⟨y | H | x⟩ = 0` for
`d_per(x, y) > 1`. Combined with (S2)'s `d_per = 0` vanishing, the
support of `H` lies on `d_per(x, y) = 1` pairs only, and
`P_{r > 1}(H) = 0`. ∎

### (S4) Cross-direction hopping projection vanishes

By (D), every nonzero entry `⟨y | D | x⟩` corresponds to a
displacement `y − x ∈ {± e_μ : μ ∈ {1, 2, 3}}`, hence is axis-aligned
along exactly one axis. No cross-direction (diagonal-NN) entries
appear. Multiplying by `i` preserves this. Hence `P_{cross}(H) = 0`. ∎

### (S5) Pairwise Hilbert-Schmidt orthogonality

For `μ ≠ ν`,
`⟨H_μ, H_ν⟩_HS = Tr(H_μ^† H_ν) = Tr((-i D_μ)^† (i D_ν))
= Tr((i D_μ^†) (i D_ν)) = i^2 Tr(D_μ^† D_ν) = − Tr((-D_μ)(D_ν))
= Tr(D_μ D_ν)`. We show `Tr(D_μ D_ν) = 0` for `μ ≠ ν`.

By (D_μ), `D_μ |x⟩` is supported on `|x ± e_μ⟩`. Then
`D_μ D_ν |x⟩ = D_μ ((1/2) η_ν(x) (|x + e_ν⟩ − |x − e_ν⟩))`. This is
supported on basis states `|x + e_ν ± e_μ⟩` and `|x − e_ν ± e_μ⟩`,
all of which have nonzero displacement from `|x⟩` (since `e_μ ± e_ν`
is never zero for `μ ≠ ν` in `Λ` with `L ≥ 2`). Hence
`⟨x | D_μ D_ν | x⟩ = 0` for every `x ∈ Λ` and every `μ ≠ ν`, giving
`Tr(D_μ D_ν) = Σ_x ⟨x | D_μ D_ν | x⟩ = 0`. Therefore
`⟨H_μ, H_ν⟩_HS = 0`. ∎

### (S6) Direction-completeness corollary

By (S1), `H = H_1 + H_2 + H_3 ∈ H_NN^single = span_C{H_1, H_2, H_3}`.
Therefore `Proj_{(H_NN^single)^⊥}(H) = 0` trivially. The Hilbert-
Schmidt orthogonal projection of any vector onto the orthogonal
complement of a subspace containing it is zero. ∎
