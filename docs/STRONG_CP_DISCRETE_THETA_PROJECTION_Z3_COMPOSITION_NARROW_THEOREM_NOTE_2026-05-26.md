## Claim

Let `Z₃ ⊂ O_h` be the body-diagonal subgroup generated by the C₃
rotation `R_{(1,1,1)}` cyclically permuting the three spatial axes:

```text
R_{(1,1,1)} : (x_1, x_2, x_3) ↦ (x_2, x_3, x_1)
```

**Theorem (Discrete θ-Projection via Z₃ ⊂ O_h).**

1. **(D1) Substrate-natural Z_N choice.** The `Z₃` body-diagonal
   subgroup of O_h is a substrate-natural realization of the
   discrete subgroup of the (−1)-form θ-shift symmetry per
   Aharony-Razamat-Tachikawa (2026-03, arXiv:2603.05195).

2. **(D2) Z₃ gauging shrinks θ-Brillouin zone.** Gauging this `Z₃`
   subgroup of the θ-shift symmetry orbifolds the θ-Brillouin zone
   from width `2π` to width `2π/3`, so admissible θ values lie in
   `θ ∈ (−π/3, +π/3]`.

3. **(D3) O_h-improper Z₂ orbifold pinches further.** Step 2's
   action-class O_h-invariance imposes `θ ∼ −θ` (the Z₂ orbifold
   from improper rotations in O_h, which transform `Q^{(r)} → −Q^{(r)}`).
   Combined with D2, the residual θ-Brillouin zone reduces to
   `θ ∈ [0, π/3]`.

4. **(D4) Retained RP-positivity selects θ = 0.** The retained
   reflection-positivity chain (PRs #1971 + #1973) gives `Z_Q ≥ 0`
   on the framework's selected action surface. By the standard
   triangle-inequality / Vafa-Witten argument:
   ```text
   |Z(θ)|  =  | Σ_Q Z_Q · exp(iθ Q) |  ≤  Σ_Q Z_Q  =  Z(0)
   ```
   with equality if and only if `θ = 0` (mod the gauged Z₃). The
   free energy `F(θ) = −log|Z(θ)|` is therefore **strictly minimized**
   at `θ = 0` on the reduced Brillouin zone `[0, π/3]`.

5. **(D5) Unique CP-symmetric point on the reduced Brillouin zone.**
   On the reduced range `[0, π/3]`, the **only** CP-symmetric point
   is `θ = 0` (no `θ = π` in this range, in contrast to the unreduced
   Z₂-only orbifold). Hence θ = 0 is both the variational minimum
   (D4) and the unique CP-fixed point.

6. **(D6 — UNIFIED COROLLARY) θ = 0 is the unique vacuum on the
   framework's substrate under (S1) + (S2) + RP-positivity (PRs
   #1971 + #1973) + the named premise "admissible action class is
   O_h-invariant".**

(D1)–(D5) compose existing retained and shipped results with the
substrate-natural Z₃ choice. (D6) is a **conditional retirement
result for θ**: removes θ from the admitted set conditional on the
named premise of Step 2 + the discrete-θ-projection mechanism
specialized to the framework's Z₃.
