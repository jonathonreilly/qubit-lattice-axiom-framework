## The candidate axiom

> A record registers which real classical alternative is realized; the real
> classical alternatives are the real superselection sectors (real Wedderburn
> blocks); each is one alternative; record readout counts alternatives
> additively, dimension-blind.

Operationally on a finite group/algebra carrier this is the **counting measure
on the real-block lattice**, `COUNT(A) = #{real Wedderburn blocks} = rank
K0_real(A)`: each minimal real central idempotent weighted once, regardless of
its real dimension. It is the `(1,1)` block-count weighting already on the
framework's table, now stated as a record-reading principle. Its two rivals are
`DIM_R` (real dimension per block) and `TRACE`/Plancherel (`rank K0_complex`).

### 1. Born rule / probability — TOUCHES-CONSTRAINS

The axiom cleanly separates **which alternative** (the block partition) from
the **Born weight**. The counting functional is invariant under any reweighting
*within* a block — it only sees how many blocks carry support (runner: same
block support, 50 draws with generically different Born weights, COUNT
identical). So the axiom fixes the *partition into classical alternatives* and
that they are counted additively, but it supplies **no** quadratic amplitude
law. The Born weight remains independent data (Gleason/Busch territory,
`BORN_RULE_FROM_GLEASON_BUSCH_DERIVATION_NOTE_2026-05-20`). It constrains the
*event algebra* the Born rule is a measure on; it does not derive the measure.

### 2. Arrow of time / (3,1) signature — TOUCHES-CONSTRAINS

"Records register an alternative" + record persistence gives an arrow: record
sharpening (`p -> p^2/Z`, the `r -> 2r^2` Lüders map) is entropy-decreasing and
non-invertible (runner). But the COUNT itself is a **static, time-symmetric**
invariant of the algebra (block number unchanged forward/back). The axiom
supplies the arrow's *carrier* (the block partition that gets recorded) and,
via reality (item 4), favors CPT-even dynamics — but it reaches only **discrete**
data (a count), never the **continuous** Lorentzian signature. Same category
mismatch the AFT time-emergence panel found: discrete invariants don't pin a
continuous modulus. So it strengthens the *carrier* side of the
anomaly-forces-time lane without supplying signature.

### 3. Color SU(3) vs generation — ORTHOGONAL (the heaviest check)

This is the most interesting negative. The prior diagonal/record attacks found
the 3-way structure gives a *generation* su(3) with a **mismatched Z₃ character**
vs color. The real-vs-complex distinction does **not** repair it. Runner
computes both characters explicitly:

- **generation** Z₃ acts by **permutation** (regular rep, `χ = (3,0,0)`,
  decomposes `1·triv + 1·ω + 1·ω̄`);
- **color** Z₃ = center of SU(3) acts by **central scalar** `ω·I₃`
  (`χ = (3, 3ω, 3ω̄)`, = 3 copies of one character).

Real fusion (ω,ω̄ → one real block) is a move on the *generation* side; it
leaves `χ = (3,0,0)` a permutation character and **cannot** turn it into color's
central-scalar character. Algebraic witness: color-center elements are scalar
(commute with all of M₃); the generation `C` is not. The real reading changes
how the doublet is **counted**, not the **group-action type** that separates
generation-Z₃ from color-Z₃. **No color bridge.**

### 4. CPT exactness — TOUCHES-CONSTRAINS, does not derive

"Records are real/CPT-even" is the records analog of the retained
`CPT_EXACT_REAL_ANTI_HERMITIAN_D_NARROW_THEOREM` (real anti-Hermitian `D`,
`H = iD` Hermitian). On the record/generator factor it **forces the forward
implication** C1/C2 (the reality involution commutes: runner builds real
anti-Hermitian `D` on an even ring, `Θ = PK`, `Θ H Θ⁻¹ = H`). But it does **not**
yield the full spacetime CPT theorem: `Θ² = (CP)²` is scalar only with extra
(C,P) algebra — exactly the 2026-05-17 audit narrowing (C1,C2 hold, C3 needs
more). So the axiom **restates / rides on** CPT-realness on the record factor;
it does not derive spacetime CPT. It constrains the dynamics to the real/CPT-even
side it already shares with the retained row.

### 5. Three generations / why 3 — ORTHOGONAL (measure, not count)

Honest: the COUNT of generations is the hw=1 orbit size = `dim Z³ = 3`, supplied
by the **lattice**, not the axiom. The axiom supplies the **measure** on those 3
(block-count → Q=2/3 vs trace → Q=1). Decisive tell (runner): the axiom's own
real-block count for C₃ is **2**, not 3 — a *different number* from the
generation count. So the axiom cannot be the source of "3"; it weights the
generations it is handed. (Runner table: across N=3,5,7 the real-block count
tracks the group's FS structure while the orbit size = N tracks the lattice
dimension independently.)

### 6. det_C / signed-vs-singular readout — TOUCHES-AND-UNLOCKS (genuine bonus)

A real Wedderburn block is *one classical alternative carrying its own (signed)
determinant* — that is precisely the **signed / det_C** side of the readout
fork, not the singular-value (modulus) side. Runner: the signed readout gives
`Q = 2/3` θ-independently at `r=1/2`; the singular-value readout needs an extra
modulus step and is θ-dependent (`Q ∈ [0.409, 0.667]`); the two diverge exactly
where the signed spectrum has a sign flip (θ=0.9 witness, smallest eigenvalue
`-0.399`). Combined with reality (item 4) placing the native operator on the
signed side (`KOIDE_REALITY_FAVORS_SIGNED_READOUT_SHARED_MECHANISM`), the axiom's
"real-block-with-sign" reading **picks the signed/det_C readout** — the one
giving the empirical Q=2/3. This is the one place the axiom moves a live fork.

## Bonus unlock (flagged)

**Item 6 is a genuine bonus.** The axiom does double duty: it is *both* the
measure selector that lands `r=1/2`/Q=2/3 (block-count) *and* the readout-class
selector that lands the signed/det_C reading. Those were two separate pins
(`KOIDE_HERMITIAN_RECORDS_IMPORT_REQUIRED` measure pin; the signed-`√m` value
pin). One statement — "a record registers which *real* block, counted once" —
touches both, because "real block, counted once, with its own sign" is
simultaneously the `(1,1)` weight and the det_C readout. Color, Born, and the
(3,1) signature do **not** move: the axiom reaches discrete/partition data
there, never the continuous moduli those need.

## Honest net

Most of the stack is **ORTHOGONAL** or **TOUCHES-CONSTRAINS**: the axiom is a
*measure/readout principle on a partition the lattice already supplies*, not a
source of counts, characters, the Born weight, or the signature. Its real reach
is the **charged-lepton readout fork** (item 6) plus reinforcing the *carrier*
side of Born/time/CPT. It does not, by itself, force `r=1/2` (the separatrix
remains an occupancy needing a stabilizer per
`FLAVOR_R_HALF_IS_THE_RECORDS_FLOW_SEPARATRIX`); it makes block-count the
*natural* reading and proves block-count and signed-det_C are the **same** move.
This sharpens, but does not close, the open charged-lepton measure/readout slot,
and it opens a precise next path: whether record persistence / einselection
forces the 2-sector (isotype) partition as the physical record basis
non-circularly.
