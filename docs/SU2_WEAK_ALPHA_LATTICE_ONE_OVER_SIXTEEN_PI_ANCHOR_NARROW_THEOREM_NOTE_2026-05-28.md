## Proof

`(fine-structure-convention)` plus the explicit retained_bounded assumed coupling input `(coupling-input)` and
the retained native SU(2) group-dimension input `(native-su2-dimension)`.

**Step 1 (apply QFT convention `(fine-structure-convention)` to lattice-scale SU(2)_L).**

```text

              =  (1/4) / (4 π)
```

```text

```

**Step 2 (apply Wilson convention `(wilson-convention)` at the SU(2)_L lattice scale).**
On the canonical Wilson plaquette action, the bare gauge coupling
`g_bare²` at the lattice scale equals the framework's lattice-scale
bare coupling `g_2² |_lattice`. Substituting `(coupling-input)` and `(native-su2-dimension)` into `(wilson-convention)`:

```text

                =  2 · 2 / (1/4)
                =  4 / (1/4)
                =  16.                                                     (wilson-beta substitution step)
```

This is `(wilson-beta identity)`.
