## Proof

Pure substitution into the two admitted textbook conventions `(X1)` and
`(X3)` plus the retained / retained_bounded identifications `(X2)` and
`(X4)`.

**Step 1 (apply QFT convention `(X3)` to lattice-scale SU(2)_L).**
Substitute `(X2)` `g_2² |_lattice = 1/4` into `(X3)` at `g = g_2`:

```text
α_2 |_lattice  =  g_2² |_lattice / (4 π)
              =  (1/4) / (4 π)
              =  1 / (16 π).                                               (S1)
```

This is `(A1)`. Taking the reciprocal gives `(A2)`:

```text
1 / α_2 |_lattice  =  16 π.                                                (S2)
```

**Step 2 (apply Wilson convention `(X1)` at the SU(2)_L lattice scale).**
On the canonical Wilson plaquette action, the bare gauge coupling
`g_bare²` at the lattice scale equals the framework's lattice-scale
bare coupling `g_2² |_lattice`. Substituting `(X2)` and `(X4)` into `(X1)`:

```text
β_W |_lattice  =  2 · N_c |_{SU(2)_L} / g_2² |_lattice
                =  2 · 2 / (1/4)
                =  4 / (1/4)
                =  16.                                                     (S3)
```

This is `(A3)`.

**Step 3 (numeric readout).** From `(S1)`, with `π ≈ 3.14159 26535 89793`:

```text
α_2 |_lattice  =  1 / (16 π)
              =  1 / 50.26548 24574 36692 8...
              ≈  0.01989 43678 86 ... (14 sig figs).                       (S4)
```

This is `(A4)`. ∎
