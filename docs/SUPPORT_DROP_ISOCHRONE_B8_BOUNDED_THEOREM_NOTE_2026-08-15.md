# Isochrones Of The Named Support-Drop Hop-Cost On B_8(0)

**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Type:** bounded_theorem
**Scope:** one Dijkstra arrival field for a named six-neighbor hop-cost on the
closed ℓ¹ ball of radius 8 in `Z^3`. The cost, the isochrones, and the ℓ¹
comparator are displayed, not adopted.
**Audit-status authority:** independent audit lane only. This note authors no
audit verdict and predicts none.
**Primary runner:**

## Result Up Front

On `B_8(0)={v in Z^3 : |v|_1 <= 8}` (833 sites), let `σ_v` be the set of
nonzero coordinates of `v`, and write `|σ_v|` for its cardinality. On the
six-neighbor graph restricted to this ball, the named support-drop hop-cost is

```text
ν(v → w) = 3  if |σ_v|=0 or (|σ_v|=|σ_w|=1) or |σ_w| < |σ_v|,
         = 1  otherwise.
```

Let `t` be first-arrival time from the origin under one Dijkstra with this
cost. Then `t(4,0,0)=10`, `t(6,0,0)=12`, `t(8,0,0)=16`, and `t(2,2,2)=8`.
The same run assigns an arrival to every other site of the ball; those values
are not copied from the four named points.

The 832 nonzero sites form 44 site-types for the proper cubic group `G+`
(order 24). Arrival time is constant on each type. The `t`-constant shells
are **not** all single Euclidean radii: six shells are single-radius and six
mix several radii. Population variance of `|v|_2/t` on the 832 nonzero sites
is smaller for this named cost than for the displayed ℓ¹ comparator
`t_ℓ¹(v)=|v|_1`. Displayed, not adopted.

On B_8(0), the isochrones of the named support-drop hop-cost are reported.
Do not write `ν` into Admissibility. The ℓ¹ arrival is a displayed comparator
only and is not attached as a hop-cost.

## Named Objects

`B_8(0)` is the closed ℓ¹ ball of radius 8. Lattice supplies nearest-neighbor
adjacency and the proper cubic rotations about each site. The hop-cost `ν` is
not Admissibility content: Admissibility supplies one fixed nearest-neighbor
probability rule and does not name a path metric, a seed-exit penalty, an
axis-skeleton penalty, or a support-drop penalty.

The three clauses of `ν` are:

1. seed-exit: `|σ_v|=0`, the unique hop leaving the origin;
2. both-weights-1: `|σ_v|=|σ_w|=1`, a hop along the coordinate-axis 1-skeleton;
3. support drop: `|σ_w| < |σ_v|`, a hop that strictly decreases the number of
   occupied axes.

Each expensive clause costs 3; every other six-neighbor hop that stays in the
ball costs 1. Uniqueness of this cost among all integer hop-costs is not
claimed.

`G+` is the proper cubic group generated by the 90-degree rotations about the
three coordinate axes. A site-type is a `G+`-orbit. For each orbit the
representative used below is the lexicographically greatest nonnegative site
in that orbit. When all three absolute coordinates are distinct and nonzero,
`G+` splits the 48 signed permutations into two 24-point types (for example
`(3,2,1)` and `(3,1,2)`). Those two types share a Euclidean radius and, under
`ν`, share an arrival time.

The displayed ℓ¹ comparator is ordinary unit-cost nearest-neighbor arrival.
On this ball that arrival equals `|v|_1`. It is reported only as a variance
baseline.

## Theorem 2 — Isochrones Versus Euclidean Radii, And Variance Versus ℓ¹

A `t`-constant shell is a single Euclidean radius when every nonzero site with
that arrival has the same `|v|_2^2`. On this ball the shells are:

| `t` | sites | `|v|_2^2` values | single radius |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 3 | 6 | `{1}` | yes |
| 4 | 12 | `{2}` | yes |
| 5 | 32 | `{3,5}` | no |
| 6 | 66 | `{4,6,8,10}` | no |
| 7 | 96 | `{9,11,13,17}` | no |
| 8 | 140 | `{12,14,18,20,26}` | no |
| 9 | 198 | `{9,17,19,21,25,27,29,37}` | no |
| 10 | 258 | `{16,22,24,26,30,32,34,38,40,50}` | no |
| 11 | 6 | `{25}` | yes |
| 12 | 6 | `{36}` | yes |
| 13 | 6 | `{49}` | yes |
| 16 | 6 | `{64}` | yes |

Six shells are single-radius. Six shells mix Euclidean radii. Therefore a
`t`-constant shell is **not a single Euclidean radius** in general. The first
mixed shell is already `t=5`, which joins the body diagonal `(1,1,1)` to the
type `(2,1,0)`. The large shells `t=8,9,10` mix five, eight, and ten radii
respectively. The late single-radius shells `t=11,12,13,16` are exactly the
remaining axis sites `(5,0,0)` through `(8,0,0)`.

Let `r(v)=|v|_2/t(v)` on the 832 nonzero sites, and let the population
variance be

```text
var(r) = (1/832) sum_v (r(v) - mean(r))^2.
```

The second moments are exact:

```text
E_ν[r^2] = 1255910253469/4501790092800,
E_ℓ¹[r^2] = 55/104.
```

The means involve square roots. To twelve displayed digits the population
variances are

```text
var_ν(|v|_2/t)  = 0.006349675165,
var_ℓ¹(|v|_2/t) = 0.011339404957.
```

So `var_ν < var_ℓ¹` on `B_8(0)\{0}`. The comparison is displayed, not
adopted: a smaller variance is a score of this named cost against the
displayed ℓ¹ comparator, not a derivation that `ν` is a physical time metric.

arrival times, isochrones, or a preferred path metric. This note does not
amend the axiom memo.
