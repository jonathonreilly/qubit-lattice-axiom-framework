# Tensor Composition Requires Local Tomography Beyond Locality Narrow No-Go

**Date:** 2026-06-03
**Type:** no_go
**Claim scope:** the standalone reconstruction-theoretic fact that Locality
(axiom A2 — "separated parts are independent", equivalently no-signalling /

fermionic statistics, no superselection / graded composite, no dynamics,

## Statement

Let each site carry the qubit algebra `M_2(C)` (axiom A1), and let
"Locality" denote A2 read at its operational strength: separated regions
are independent, i.e. their local algebras commute and no local operation
signals (no-signalling). Consider how two such parties compose.

1. (**NG — no-go core.**) Locality does **not** entail the
   `C*`-tensor-product composition `M_2(C) ⊗ M_2(C) = M_4(C)`. There is a
   composition rule — real-vector-space QM (rebits) — that satisfies
   Locality (commuting local algebras, no-signalling) yet is **not** the
   tensor product, because it fails **local tomography**:

   ```text
   dim_sa(rebit composite) = 10  !=  9 = dim_sa(rebit) · dim_sa(rebit).   (NG)
   ```

   So local tomography is a postulate strictly stronger than Locality.

2. (**LT — the precise residual.**) The exact additional content that
   selects the tensor product is **local tomography**: the joint state is
   determined by the statistics of local measurements and their
   correlations. Complex qubits satisfy it
   (`dim_sa = 4`, `4·4 = 16 = dim_sa(M_4(C))`); rebits do not
   (`dim_sa = 3`, `3·3 = 9 != 10`).

3. (**COR — conditional corollary.**) **Given** local tomography, and using
   that `M_2(C)` is nuclear (a finite-dimensional matrix algebra has a
   unique `C*`-tensor norm), the composite of two qubits is the **unique**
   `M_2(C) ⊗ M_2(C) = M_4(C)`. The composition rule is therefore a theorem
   on A1 **plus local tomography**, not on A1 plus Locality.

4. (**MECH — the distinguishing content is the complex unit.**) The rebit
   fails (NG) precisely because it lacks the central imaginary unit:
   `dim_sa(M_2(C)) - dim_sa(M_2(R)) = 4 - 3 = 1`, the single missing
   observable being the `i` that promotes the real two-state algebra to the
   complex qubit. Local tomography is the composition-level shadow of the
   qubit's complex structure.

## Proof

Pure finite-dimensional linear algebra; each step is reproduced at exact
precision by the runner.

### Step 1. Observable dimensions.

The self-adjoint (observable) subspace of a matrix algebra has real
dimension: `dim_sa(M_n(R)) = n(n+1)/2` (symmetric matrices) and
`dim_sa(M_n(C)) = n^2` (Hermitian matrices). Hence
`dim_sa(M_2(R)) = 3`, `dim_sa(M_2(C)) = 4`, `dim_sa(M_4(R)) = 10`,
`dim_sa(M_4(C)) = 16`.

### Step 2. (LT) Local tomography distinguishes complex from real.

Local tomography requires the bipartite observable dimension to equal the
product of the local observable dimensions. Complex qubits:
`4 · 4 = 16 = dim_sa(M_4(C))` — satisfied. Rebits:
`3 · 3 = 9 != 10 = dim_sa(M_4(R))` — violated. The two extra real
dimensions in the rebit composite are global (entangled) observables not
fixed by local data: real QM is not locally tomographic (a standard fact;
cf. Wootters; Hardy; Chiribella–D'Ariano–Perinotti).

### Step 3. (NG) Rebits satisfy Locality.

The rebit composite is `M_2(R) ⊗ M_2(R) = M_4(R)` with the two factors
`{X ⊗ I}` and `{I ⊗ Y}` commuting — separated parts are independent and no
local operation signals, exactly axiom A2. (The runner exhibits the
commuting factor subalgebras; they are built from the real-valued `s_1,
s_3`, so the same structure holds in the real case.) So Locality is
satisfied by a composition rule that is **not** locally tomographic. Local
tomography is therefore not entailed by Locality.

### Step 4. (COR) Uniqueness given local tomography.

Local tomography forces the joint state space to be the tensor product of
the local ones; on the algebraic side `M_2(C) ⊗ M_2(C) = M_4(C)` (the 16
products `σ_a ⊗ σ_b` are linearly independent, runner check N5), and `M_2`
is nuclear so the `C*`-completion is the unique `M_4(C)`. Hence the tensor
rule is determined once local tomography is granted.

### Step 5. (MECH) The complex unit is the gap.

`dim_sa(M_2(C)) - dim_sa(M_2(R)) = 4 - 3 = 1`. The single missing
observable is the central `i` (the pseudoscalar `ω = s_1 s_2 s_3 = i·I`).
Real QM lacks it, fails local tomography, and admits the non-tensor
composite; the complex qubit has it and is locally tomographic. The same
`i` is what the companion spatial-dimension note reads as the third spatial
direction — so the qubit's complex structure simultaneously fixes `d_s = 3`
and the composition rule. QED.
