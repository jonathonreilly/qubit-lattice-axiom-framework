## Statement

Use the Quantum axiom's one-site carrier `A_x ~= M_2(C)` from
[`MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-04.md`](MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-04.md). Add only the
operational locality condition that separated local copies are faithful,
commute inside the composite, and hence do not signal through local
operations. Do not add a generation condition saying that the composite is
generated by products of local factors, and do not add local tomography.

Then the ordinary tensor product is not forced. The duplicate-sector
composite `B = M_4(C) oplus M_4(C)` above satisfies operational locality with
the same local `M_2(C)` factors, but it contains the central sector observable

```text
Z = I_4 oplus (-I_4)
```

which is not in the span of local products. Consequently the local-product
span has dimension 16, while `B_sa` has real dimension 32. A joint state on
`B` is not determined by the statistics of local effects and their products.

Thus any derivation of the ordinary two-qubit composite needs one more
premise. Two sufficient, and in this finite commuting-matrix setup equivalent,
ways to state the missing premise are:

1. **Local tomography:** the joint state is fixed by local effects and their
   products, excluding hidden global sectors like `Z`.
2. **No-extra-global generation:** the composite is the C-star algebra
   generated by the two commuting local copies. Since `M_2(C)` is nuclear and
   `M_2(C) tensor_C M_2(C) ~= M_4(C)` is simple, that generated C-star algebra
   is the ordinary `M_4(C)` tensor product.

The real-rebit comparison remains a useful external sanity check:
`dim_R(M_2(R)_sa)^2 = 3 * 3 = 9`, while `dim_R(M_4(R)_sa) = 10`. It shows the
standard no-signalling-versus-local-tomography separation when the scalar
field is changed. It is not the load-bearing framework witness, because the
framework's Quantum axiom names complex qubits, not rebits.

## Proof

For a complex matrix algebra `M_n(C)`, the self-adjoint real dimension is
`n^2`. Hence a single complex qubit has four real observable coordinates and
the ordinary two-qubit algebra `M_4(C)` has 16.

In the duplicate-sector witness `B = M_4(C) oplus M_4(C)`, define the local
embeddings as diagonal copies of the ordinary tensor-factor embeddings:

```text
a |-> (a tensor I_2) oplus (a tensor I_2),
b |-> (I_2 tensor b) oplus (I_2 tensor b).
```

Each embedding is faithful because the first summand is the usual faithful
embedding. The two images commute because `(a tensor I_2)(I_2 tensor b) =
a tensor b = (I_2 tensor b)(a tensor I_2)` in each summand. Thus operational
locality holds exactly.

However, products of the two local images have the form

```text
(a tensor b) oplus (a tensor b).
```

Their complex span is the diagonal copy of `M_4(C)`, dimension 16. The full
algebra `B` has complex dimension 32, and therefore `B_sa` has real dimension
32. The central observable `Z = I_4 oplus (-I_4)` commutes with all local
products but is linearly independent from their span. Local data cannot see
which sector a state occupies, so local tomography fails.

This proves the no-go: operational locality by itself does not force the
ordinary generated tensor product or exclude extra global sectors. A
tomography/generation premise is required.
