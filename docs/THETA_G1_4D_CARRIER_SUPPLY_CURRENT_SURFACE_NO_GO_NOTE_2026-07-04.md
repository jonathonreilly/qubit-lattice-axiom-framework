## Route Fan-Out

### Frame 1: spatial `T^3` has 2-fluxes but no theta scalar

The spatial lattice can support closed non-exact 2-flux labels on a periodic
spatial witness:

```text
H^2(T^3,Z) = Z^3.
```

That is not enough for the theta gauge carrier. The theta charge in the July
4D carrier is a 2-cup-2 scalar, hence a 4-cochain summed over 4-cells. On a
purely spatial 3D cubical complex,

```text
C^4(T^3) = 0,    H^4(T^3,Z) = 0.
```

There are no 4-cells on which `F cup F` can be evaluated as the 4D theta
charge. The obstruction is not absence of spatial 2-fluxes. It is absence of
the 4D intersection slot.

### Frame 2: pure spatial flux embeds with zero 4D theta charge

Embedding the three spatial flux coordinates into the 4D intersection formula
with no time-involving fluxes gives

```text
Q(m) = m01 m23 - m02 m13 + m03 m12 = 0.
```

Odd support requires complementary 2-planes such as `01` and `23`; at least
one member of each complementary pair uses the fourth direction. Therefore a
spatial-only carrier cannot be the Block36 carrier by relabeling. The physical
route must supply a 4D multi-plaquette/gauge carrier, not merely spatial
`H^2(T^3)` data.

### Frame 3: Record occurrence is not a 4D carrier supply rule

Record now says records form, and scalar readout is additive over finite
pairwise-disjoint records. That can discipline an already licensed record
context. It does not add a fourth cubical direction, create 4-cells, define
branch 2-cochains, impose `dn=0`, identify the gauge sector, or select the
topological-sector observable.

### Frame 4: kinetic/time support does not supply theta topology

The kinetic-isotropy primitive and anomaly-forces-time theorem are useful
downstream support surfaces. They are not the 4D carrier theorem.

The kinetic primitive supplies a ratio in the kinetic form. It does not build
the gauge cochain complex or supply compact Euclidean 4-cells.

The anomaly theorem is conditional on declared premises and derives a dimension
count under those premises. It does not derive the physical time-axis premise,
does not equip the resulting `3+1` count with branch 2-cochains, does not
construct closed non-exact gauge sectors, and does not register the
intersection pairing as physical theta readout.

### Frame 5: Block36 stays conditional

Block36 proves the right shape of a supplied interface. This block identifies
one of the load-bearing supplied words in that shape:

```text
4D multi-plaquette carrier
```

That carrier is not in the current axiom/primitives/time-support surface. It
must be derived by a physical 4D gauge-carrier theorem, supplied by a narrow
approved premise, or kept conditional.
