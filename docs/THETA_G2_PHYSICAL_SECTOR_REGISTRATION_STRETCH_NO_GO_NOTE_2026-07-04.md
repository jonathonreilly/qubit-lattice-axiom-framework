a supplied central-sector surface. Record then disciplines a readout only

## Independence Witness

Use the finite SU(3) clock/shift surface from Blocks31-33. For a closed triple
of noncentral Heisenberg staples, let

```text
q_c(A,B,C) = k_ABC - k_ACB mod 3
```

be the central cocycle recorded by the supplied central-sector projection.
For a finite set of disjoint supplied triple-record atoms, all of the following
scalar readout maps are additive and are determined by the supplied record
content:

```text
I_0(record set)       = 0
I_+(record set)       = sum q_signed(atom)
I_-(record set)       = - sum q_signed(atom)
I_even(record set)    = sum |q_signed(atom)|
```

where `q_signed` is the representative in `{-1, 0, 1}`. These readouts disagree
on the same supplied central-sector carrier while satisfying the finite
additivity form required by Record once record atoms have been supplied.

This is not a new physical model. It is an independence witness: Record
additivity by itself does not select the zero map, the oriented cocycle map,
the sign of the oriented map, or the even absolute-value map. Selection of the
physical G2 channel must come from a sector/readout theorem or an explicit
approved primitive/governance premise.
