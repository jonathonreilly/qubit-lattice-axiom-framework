site-local gauge group is a finite product of connected groups and is
connected. A would-be continuum winding label on a discrete lattice can change
along a site-local homotopy because there is no continuity constraint between
neighboring sites. The runner exhibits this in `U(1)^L`: the standard winding
phase pattern contracts to the identity through sitewise phases while the
discrete winding counter is not locally constant.

per-site connected gauge surface. It does not supply the Euclidean route: an
integer sector functional `Q`, a fixed log branch/section choice, or a
nonvacuous weighting law can only enter through additional gauge-action and
