## Stretch Analysis

### Frame 1: the determinant algebra is not the weak point

Inside the supplied determinant-channel interface, the phase-erasure algebra is
sharp. Additivity over multiplicative determinant union forces a homomorphism;
K/CPT orbit constancy makes its phase part even; additivity makes it odd; odd
and even force zero phase. K-even nonmultiplicative probes such as
`cos(arg z)` are excluded by additivity only after the determinant datum is
identified as the Record scalar channel.

### Frame 2: Record does not choose the determinant channel

Record says that, once a record is present, a scalar readout is determined by
record content and is finitely additive over finite pairwise-disjoint record
collections. It does not state that every physical action observable is a
record scalar. It does not pick the determinant datum, the central-sector
decomposition, the K/CPT predicate, the mass-sector readout interface, or the
physical observable bridge.

Therefore the sentence

```text
the mass determinant datum is the physical Record scalar readout
```

is not in the axioms, the realized-state primitive, or the existing
determinant algebra. It is exactly the W2 bridge.

### Frame 3: finite two-extension witness

Take two disjoint records `e1,e2` and a supplied determinant datum with

```text
z(e1 union e2) = z(e1) z(e2).
```

There are two current-surface extensions with the same record skeleton and the
same determinant multiplication:

1. **W2-supplied extension.** Declare the determinant datum to be the scalar
   channel. Then `F(z1 z2) = F(z1) + F(z2)` is required, and the determinant
   phase is erased by the existing algebra.
2. **W2-withheld extension.** Keep a perfectly additive Record scalar that is
   independent of the determinant datum, and let a K-even action-level witness
   such as `cos(arg z)` remain a non-record action probe. The Record axioms are
   still satisfied, because they do not require that probe to be the record
   scalar. The determinant algebra is still available if W2 is later supplied,
   but W2 has not been forced.

The second extension is not a proposed physical model. It is a counter-witness
to the logical implication that the current axioms and primitives already
select the determinant readout channel.

### Frame 4: realized-state registration cannot repair W2

The realized-state primitive lets a derivation evaluate an already-defined
functional at the supplied realized state. W2 is prior to that evaluation: it
decides whether the physical mass-orientation datum is an already-defined
Record-registrable determinant scalar. The primitive supplies no readout
context, measure, weighting, probability, preferred state, or value, so it
cannot choose W2.


registrability bridge remains live.
