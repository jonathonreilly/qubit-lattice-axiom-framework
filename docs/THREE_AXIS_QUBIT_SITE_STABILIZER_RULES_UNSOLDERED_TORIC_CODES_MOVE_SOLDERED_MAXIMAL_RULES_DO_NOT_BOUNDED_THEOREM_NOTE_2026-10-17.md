# Three axis qubits per site: unsoldered toric codes have mobile charges; soldered maximal rules do not

**Date:** 2026-10-17
**Type:** bounded_theorem
**Status:** bounded support, unaudited. The statements are exact within the
supplied stabilizer model, and Part B also within its stated symmetry
restriction. Nothing is adopted.

## Question

The two companion notes concern one and two qubits per site with soldered
spin. In those classes, no rule without free local variables has a
three-dimensionally mobile charge. Is that a question of storage (too few
qubits per site) or of soldering (labels turning with space)? This note
answers it in the simplest three-qubit setting.

## Result

**Part A: unsoldered labels allow coherent three-dimensional motion (exact).**

The sites of Z^3 with exactly one odd coordinate are the edge midpoints of
the coarse lattice 2Z^3. Each site of Z^3 is an edge midpoint, along each
axis, of exactly one of the eight translates of 2Z^3. So three axis qubits
per site carry eight interleaved 3D toric codes:
- the vertex term at v is X on qubit a at v +- e_a;
- the plaquette terms at v are Z on qubit b at v +- e_a and on qubit a at
  v +- e_b, one for each normal c.

The runner checks:
- the set is closed under O when the labels are unsoldered;
- the generators commute;
- no single-site Pauli commutes with every generator;
- log2 code dimension is 24 on the L = 4 and L = 6 tori;
- every single-site Pauli violates an even number of vertex terms in each
  copy, so a vertex charge cannot be created locally;
- a single Z on qubit a moves a vertex charge by 2 e_a.

The charges are bosons, those of the 3D toric code.

**Part B: soldered labels, restricted class (exact within the restriction).**

The class is star strings fixed (labels up to sign) by the C4 rotation about
z or the C3 rotation about (1, 1, 1). Up to conjugation, these include every
string whose stabilizer in O contains an element of order 3 or 4, such as the
vertex and plaquette shapes above.
- **Count.** 719 strings commute with all translates of all their rotated
  images. They form 227 sign-consistent orbits and 11 maximal commuting
  sets: eight of 3 orbits, and one each of 23, 31 and 187.
- **Rank bounds (3L^3 - rank of each set's full generator union).**

  | maximal set | 3L^3 - rank | valid rule? |
  |---|---|---|
  | each 3-orbit set | 48, 72 and 96 at L = 4, 6, 8 (even L; odd L differ) | yes, with all signs + |
  | 23- and 31-orbit sets | 32 and 48 at L = 4, 6 | no: contains -1 for every sign choice |
  | 187-orbit set | 0 at L = 4 | no: contains -1 for every sign choice |

  Every valid sub-rule has rank at most that of its set. So every valid rule
  inside the first ten sets has log2 code dimension at least these
  growing values.
- **Mobility.** The eight 3-orbit rules have no three-dimensionally mobile
  charge. Their charge modules are supported on lines along the axes, as in
  fracton models: Ext^3(C, R) has empty support off the coordinate planes.
  The mobility of valid sub-rules of the 23- and 31-orbit sets is not
  classified, although their code dimension is unbounded.
- **The 187 family.** It uses only the abelian alphabet generated by X on
  qubit x, Y on qubit y and Z on qubit z. That alphabet is its own
  single-site commutant, and O permutes its three generators transitively.
  So either all three lie in the group, giving a product state, or none
  does. In the second case they are free single-site variables, and every
  operator commuting with them commutes with every generator. Some members
  do have three-dimensionally mobile charges, but only through operators
  that flip a free variable. An example is the single orbit X_x(+-e_x)
  Y_y(+-e_y) Z_z(+-e_z), found by the independent check; that is the C2
  pattern.

**Conclusion within the stated classes.** With three axis qubits:
- unsoldered labels permit a covariant star rule with bounded code
  dimension and coherently three-dimensionally mobile charges;
- soldered labels, restricted to C4 or C3 symmetric strings, do not. Every
  valid rule either has unbounded code dimension or belongs to the
  187 family, where three-dimensional motion needs a free variable as in
  the two-qubit note.

Within these classes the obstruction tracks soldering, not storage.

## Independent check

A fresh-context agent (same model family) wrote its own code, including
SU(2) sign checks and phase-tracked torus ranks able to detect -1 in the
group.
- **Part A.** It confirmed Part A, including Ext^3 not equal to 0, and
  ran a 2D-stack control with Ext^3 = 0.
- **Counts.** It reproduced every count in Part B.
- **Correction.** It found that the 23-, 31- and 187-orbit unions contain -1
  for every sign choice. Its witness is four mutually commuting strings from
  orbits 11 and 158 whose product is -I. This note has been corrected
  accordingly.
- **Logic.** It confirmed the rank-bound logic and the 187-family
  dichotomy, and supplied the orbit-66 example of motion that flips a free
  variable.
- **Odd tori.** The 12L growth holds for even L; odd tori give different
  values.

## Premises

- **Axioms used.** Lattice; Qubit read as three qubits per site, a declared
  enlargement (M_8(C)), not the axiom's M_2(C).
- **Supplied model.** The stabilizer reading of nearest-neighbour
  conditions, as in the companion notes.
- **Declared choices.** Axis-indexed qubits permuted by O; trivial or full
  label action; Pauli conditions; star support.

## Relation to prior work

- The 3D toric code (Hamma, Zanardi and Wen, PRB 72, 035307 (2005); Castelnovo
  and Chamon, PRB 78, 155120 (2008)) and its bosonic point charges are
  standard.
- Interleaving eight copies on the edge-midpoint sublattices of Z^3 is an
  elementary construction. Its use here is to give a site-covariant star
  rule in the framework's setting.
- Fracton models with axis foliations, such as X-cube (Vijay, Haah and Fu,
  PRB 94, 235157 (2016)), are standard.
- **New here.**
  - The restricted soldered classification for three axis qubits.
  - The contrast with the unsoldered exhibit.

## No-Go Discipline Gate

- **N1 — routes around the claim.**
  - Part B covers only strings with C4 or C3 symmetry; strings with smaller
    stabilizers are not searched.
  - Valid sub-rules inside the ten sets may have three-dimensionally mobile
    charges. They have unbounded code dimension, and their mobility is not
    classified.
  - Also outside: other site structures (for example spin x axis triplet),
    non-Pauli rules and dynamics.
- **N2/N3 — scope.** One restricted class and one contrast.
- **N4 — inputs.**
  - The enumeration is exhaustive within the restriction.
  - Code dimensions use exact GF(2) ranks on tori.
  - The mobility of maximal rules uses Singular over GF(2), exact in the
    natural field; the runner calls it when available.
- **N5 — wording.**
  - "Obstruction tracks soldering" is a summary of these two classes, not
    a general theorem.
  - The exhibited charges are bosons. Fermionic charges are not addressed.
- **N6/N7 — strongest objection.** The restriction may exclude the
  interesting soldered rules, so a generic-stabilizer search is still open.
  It is infeasible by brute force: 64^7 strings.
- **N8 — earlier claims.** None changed.
