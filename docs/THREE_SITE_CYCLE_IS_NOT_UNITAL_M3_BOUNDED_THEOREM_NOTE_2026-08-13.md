## Result Up Front

Lattice plus Qubit supply three sites and therefore the composite Hilbert
space `H = (C^2)^{⊗3} ≅ C^8`. On that space the site-cycle map

`U |q1 q2 q3⟩ = |q2 q3 q1⟩`

is a displayed permutation unitary. The unital `*`-algebra it generates is

`C*(U) = span{I_8, U, U^2}`,

dimension 3 and commutative. That algebra is not `M_3(C)` (`dim M_3 = 9`,
and `M_3` is non-commutative). Independently, there is still no unital
`*`-homomorphism `M_3(C) → B(H) ≅ M_8(C)`, because `3` does not divide `8`.

This `U` is a different type from the one-site Bloch `Ad_R` orbit (that
orbit lives in `M_2`, not `M_8`) and from the adjoint of `su(3)` (a
non-commutative 8-dimensional Lie algebra). Do not identify them.

Record, when present, locks one local possibility per site. It does not lock
`U`. The four axioms do not name QCD, SU(3), or a color axiom. The cycle is
a leftover displayed map and is not adopted.

## Exact Objects

Let

`H = (C^2)^{⊗3} ≅ C^8`

with product basis `|q1 q2 q3⟩`, `qi ∈ {0,1}`, ordered as the integers

`n = 4 q1 + 2 q2 + q3 ∈ {0,...,7}`.

Define the site-cycle unitary by

`U |q1 q2 q3⟩ = |q2 q3 q1⟩`.

In the ordered basis this is the 8×8 permutation matrix of `0/1` entries
with a single `1` in column `n` at row

`n' = 4 q2 + 2 q3 + q1`.

Explicitly, `U` fixes `|000⟩` and `|111⟩` and acts by the two 3-cycles

`(1 2 4) = (|001⟩ |010⟩ |100⟩)`,
`(3 6 5) = (|011⟩ |110⟩ |101⟩)`.

The unital `*`-algebra generated by `U` inside `B(H) ≅ M_8(C)` is written
`C*(U)`. Because `U` is unitary of finite order, that algebra equals the
polynomial span `span{I_8, U, U^2}`.

A unital `C`-linear `*`-homomorphism `φ : M_k(C) → M_m(C)` exists if and
only if `k` divides `m`: the standard module `C^m` must be a multiple of
`C^k`.

## Theorem 1 — Site-Cycle Permutation Unitary Of Order 3

The matrix `U` has only entries `0` and `1`. Each column and each row
contains exactly one `1`, so `U` is a permutation matrix. Real permutation
matrices are orthogonal:

`U^* = U^{T} = U^{-1}`.

The cycle decomposition above has order 3, so `U^3 = I_8`. The 3-cycles are
nontrivial, so `U ≠ I_8`. Equivalently `U^2 ≠ I_8` and `U^2 ≠ U`.

The same identities follow from the index rule without listing cycles: three
applications send `(q1,q2,q3)` to itself, and the basis vector `|001⟩` is
moved.

## Theorem 2 — Generated Unital `*`-Algebra Is Commutative Of Dimension 3

Because `U^3 = I_8` and `U^* = U^2`, every word in `{U, U^*}` reduces to one
of `I_8`, `U`, `U^2`. Thus

`C*(U) = span{I_8, U, U^2}`.

These three matrices are linearly independent over `C` (equivalently over
`Q`). One exact witness is the Gram matrix of the Frobenius product
`⟨A,B⟩ = Tr(A^* B)`:

```
⟨I,I⟩ = ⟨U,U⟩ = ⟨U^2,U^2⟩ = 8,
⟨I,U⟩ = ⟨I,U^2⟩ = ⟨U,U^2⟩ = Tr(U) = 2,
```

so the Gram matrix is

```
[[8, 2, 2],
 [2, 8, 2],
 [2, 2, 8]]
```

with determinant `432 ≠ 0`. Hence `dim C*(U) = 3`.

The span is commutative: it is the group algebra of a cyclic group of order
3, or directly `U U^2 = U^2 U = I_8`.

`M_3(C)` has dimension `9` and is non-commutative (already
`E_{12} E_{21} ≠ E_{21} E_{12}` for the standard matrix units). Therefore
`C*(U)` is not `M_3(C)`.

Predicate `dim C*(U) == 9` fails.

## Theorem 3 — Still No Unital `*`-Homomorphism `M_3(C) → M_8(C)`

Independently of how `U` sits inside `M_8(C)`, a unital `*`-homomorphism

`φ : M_3(C) → B(H) ≅ M_8(C)`

would make `C^8` a unital left `M_3`-module, hence a direct sum of copies of
the unique irreducible `C^3`. That requires `3 | 8`. But `8 = 2^3` is a
power of two, so `3 ∤ 8`.

Predicate `3 | 8` fails.

The three-site Hilbert space therefore hosts the cycle unitary and does not
host a unital `M_3` factor. Existence of some other, non-unital, or
non-`*`, linear map is not claimed and is not used.

## Theorem 4 — Different Type From The Bloch Orbit And From The Adjoint

This `U` is a different type from

- the one-site Bloch `Ad_R` orbit (lives in `M_2`, not `M_8`);
- the adjoint of `su(3)` (non-commutative 8-dimensional Lie algebra).

Do not identify them.

The Bloch conjugation orbit of an equatorial projector is a three-ray menu
inside one copy of `M_2(C)`. Its generated associative algebra, if taken,
still sits in `M_2` (`dim 4`). The present `U` is an operator on the
three-site tensor `C^8` and generates a commutative associative algebra of
dimension 3. Those are not the same object and are not identified.

The adjoint representation of `su(3)` is an 8-dimensional non-commutative
Lie algebra of 8×8 matrices. The present `C*(U)` is a 3-dimensional
commutative associative `*`-algebra. Dimension, commutativity, and category
(Lie versus unital associative) all differ. They are not identified.

In particular the three-cycle on sites is not the Bloch triad on one site,
and it is not `SU(3)`.

## Theorem 5 — Axioms Supply The Map; Leftover Not Adopted

Lattice plus Qubit supply three sites and therefore `H` and this
permutation as a displayed map. Lattice supplies sites of `Z^3` and
therefore any three distinct sites. Qubit supplies a local possibility
domain with algebraic presentation `M_2(C)` at each site, and therefore
the composite Hilbert space `H = (C^2)^{⊗3}` together with its product
basis. The site-cycle permutation is then a displayed linear map on that
basis.

They do not name QCD, SU(3), or a color axiom. They do not
rewrite the one-site algebra from `M_2(C)` to `M_3(C)`. Admissibility
determines a nearest-neighbor distribution on local possibilities; it does
not promote `U` to a color symmetry. Record locks exactly one admissible
local possibility at a site that carries a record. Record does not lock `U`.

The construction is a leftover: three `M_2` sites may be cycled, and that
fact is displayed. It is not adopted as QCD.
