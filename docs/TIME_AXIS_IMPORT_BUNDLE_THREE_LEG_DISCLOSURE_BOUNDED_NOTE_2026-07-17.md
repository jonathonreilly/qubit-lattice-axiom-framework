"the `Z^4` operator block with OS time" (equivalently: "`Z^3` + tick"). That
phrase silently consumes a bundle of supplied inputs. This note makes the bundle
explicit so that downstream work — in particular Target-3 constructions that
consume the `Z^4` surface — can cite it rather than consume it silently.

Concretely, this note:

1. identifies **at least three supplied legs** inside "OS time" (axis
   existence/compactification, axis label, axis rate/spacing), each of which is
   already named open or supplied by an existing on-main source note;
2. quotes each source clause **verbatim** and gates selected load-bearing

   the source file AND in this note, whitespace-normalized); the gates
   establish provenance, not necessity or completeness of the decomposition;

4. records the **quantifier-scoped record-layer facts that consume none of
   legs A–C** — conditional on source (1)'s declared history, event,
   realized-sector, and comparability conventions — so the boundary between
   record-layer facts and supplied operator-surface content is explicit.

Nothing here is a new import: the note names an EXISTING implicit consumption.

## The three identified legs (decomposition; list not claimed complete)

**Leg A — axis existence / compactification.** That the operator-layer stack is
built from a realized history with transfer direction = history index, and that
the periodic operator block (cyclic fourth coordinate) is the compactification
of the linearly ordered history index. This is the *representation-faithfulness
bridge*; both sub-legs are named OPEN by the time-axis bounded note.

**Leg B — axis label.** Which lattice direction carries the transfer/OS
structure. The axis-label clause of `B-AXIS.2` is, per the single-clock narrow
no-go note, not derivable from the current retained surface (that note's own
scope: on the staggered kinetic surface, among its enumerated retained
candidate structures); the supplier shape source (2) computes as sufficient is
one per-axis `Z_2` boundary-condition asymmetry datum (antiperiodic on the
axis, periodic on the others), or a declared registration-direction bridge as
the record-shaped equivalent — one sufficient marker witnessed on the tested
block, with no minimality or uniqueness claim for the shape.

**Leg C — axis rate / spacing.** Any clock unit, time metric, blocked step, or
spacing content (`B-AXIS.1a`/`B-AXIS.1b`). Supplied, walled by the
count-not-rate firewalls.

What holds at the record layer without consuming legs A–C (quantifier-scoped;
conditional on source (1)'s declared history, event, realized-sector, and
comparability conventions; replayed on explicit finite fixtures in the paired
runner):

- **(i) universal (source (1)'s proposition)** — the history index nests
  (record-monotone) for every realized history, grounded by source (1) on the
  landed Record-axiom permanence sentence; the runner replays this on an
  explicit eight-history witness family (finite replay, not a universal
  recomputation);
- **(ii) existential (witnessed)** — for each spatial axis there exist
  event-bearing histories whose translation-identified opposite slices are
  incomparable (spatial nesting fails);
- **(iii) bounded uniqueness (witnessed)** — on the witness family, the
  history index is the unique record-monotone stack direction on both generic
  witnesses; non-uniqueness is exhibited on named degenerate fixtures
  (full-window static, single-record, uniform-burst, translation-invariant
  growth, face-confined) and does NOT extend to all of `D0`: a spatially
  translation-inequivalent static fixture retains uniqueness. Membership in
  `D0`/`D1` is not claimed to force non-uniqueness.

These three facts give the index a distinguished record-theoretic role; they
neither supply nor evidence leg A, leg B, or leg C.
