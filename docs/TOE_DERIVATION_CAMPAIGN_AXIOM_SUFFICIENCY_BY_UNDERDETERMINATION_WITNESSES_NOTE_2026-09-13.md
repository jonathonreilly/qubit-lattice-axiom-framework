## Common method and outcome contract

For each target, declare the mathematical model class, the exact axiom and
primitive interpretation, the target observable, and any added structure.
The one-site algebraic presentation M_2(C) does not automatically identify
the full possibility domain with the Bloch ball. Binary, ternary, pure-state,
and Bloch-vector families below are explicit restricted test models. Record
any missing full-domain or global extension instead of claiming it exists.

A proposed model-pair witness needs two actual models satisfying every
premise claimed for the witness and differing on the same named target.
A local conditional-law example alone is not a completed all-axiom world:
normalization and positivity, locality/covariance, nearest-neighbour variation,
formation, Record, readout, global consistency, and relevant primitive
obligations must also be checked when claimed. Finite computations can
establish finite witnesses or controlled tests; universal conclusions need
proof over the declared domain. An assertion count is not such a proof.

Every block may finish with one of these outcomes:

- A proved result within its explicit premises and quantified domain.
- An explicit model pair within that same declared domain, with the target
  difference and every claimed premise check recorded.
- An unresolved or inconclusive result, with the missing proof, extension,
  computation, or resource requirement named.

Agreement in a tested class does not itself supply a derivation from the
informal axiom text. Failure in a family does not prove global impossibility.
A separating clause is a possible owner decision, not a physically compelled
axiom change. Calling one clause minimal requires a declared language and
comparison criterion; uniqueness must not be assumed. No clause is adopted
by this plan, by a finite search, or by landing a campaign result.

## Formation-order covariance and order-blind rules

Study finite-window laws mu_sigma constructed from a specified normalized
local formation kernel and a supplied order sigma. Compare deterministic
order invariance, covariance when order/initial data transform, covariant
order distributions, and order-independent observable laws separately.
A finite-orbit obstruction for an invariant deterministic order must not be
promoted to a no-go theorem for every covariant formation process.

Test the adjacent-exchange identity
r(a|S) r(b|S+a) = r(b|S) r(a|S+b) in a declared finite model.
For necessity claims, address reachable prefixes, zero probabilities, and
null histories explicitly. Compare exact binary or ternary laws on 2x3,
3x3, and 2x2x2 windows when feasible. A uniform order mixture is a supplied
comparison measure. Order blindness alone does not identify an arbitrary
static Gibbs law or force a commuting-projector quantum reconstruction.

## Possibility covariance and the soldering fork

Classify explicitly specified symmetry actions on restricted possibility
models, carrying independent internal rotations and simultaneous spatial/
internal rotations as separate conditional hypotheses. Specify complex-linear
*-automorphisms if using their SO(3) action on Bloch vectors; do not substitute
the larger class of arbitrary algebra automorphisms. Any real or antilinear
extension needs its own precise action and covariance condition.

Entrywise complex conjugation in a chosen Pauli basis must not be called an
SO(3)-commuting automorphism. A proposed soldering witness needs actual
normalized rules invariant under the stated complete group; dependence on a
fixed component such as p.e_z requires an explicit transforming selector.
An invariant measure on a continuous sphere does not automatically assign
weights to a separately supplied finite outcome menu. Test that menu/readout
bridge separately. Any interpretation change remains an owner decision.

## Handed-rule census (triple-product chirality)

In a specified Bloch-vector family, test candidate pseudoscalar invariants,
including T = det[d_x,d_y,d_z] with d_a = q_(+a)-q_(-a), under every declared
spatial and internal action. Construct an actual outcome-dependent conditional
law and verify positivity, normalization, covariance, and any asserted exchange
property. A factor depending only on neighbours can cancel on normalization;
its presence in an unnormalized formula does not establish a handed law.

Compare a candidate and its mirror through a named record statistic on feasible
finite windows. Distinguish handedness of a law, order-induced effects, and a
physical chirality claim. A vanishing statistic or failed search in a selected
family leaves other laws and observables open; it does not force a symmetry
clause or prove the absence of chirality.

## Menus from neighbours and the Born overlap function

Study covariant supports for one, two, and three supplied neighbours under a
specified action, including stabilizer-degenerate cases. For nonzero q, the
latitude circles are distinct SO(2)_q orbits; the equator is not a uniquely
smallest continuous orbit. At q=0 the stabilizer is larger.

Test whether covariance and a precisely formulated exchange condition restrict
normalized antipodal-menu weights f(p.q). Affinity alone leaves a coefficient
in f(t)=(1+c t)/2 and does not select the Born value. Any endpoint,
repeat-certainty, additivity, menu, or readout condition used to select a value
must be explicit. A non-affine example or an unresolved classification is a
valid scoped outcome; a new axiom is not automatically necessary.

## Continuum-alphabet readability

For a declared parameterized law, formation process, and record observation
map, compute finished-window distributions and investigate identifiability.
Symbolic Fisher information on feasible 2x3 or 2x2x2 models can be a local
regularity diagnostic. Distinguish local parameter identifiability from global
law identifiability. Rank loss may reflect a singular or redundant
parameterization; a claimed indistinguishable law pair requires actual equal
observable distributions and genuinely different laws within the stated class.

## Clock-and-rate clause witness

Supply and compare concrete covariant rate laws, for example independent
exponential clocks with constant or neighbour-dependent rates, on specified
finite windows. Bind the induced order laws and finished-record laws separately.
Different rate or order laws need not yield different record statistics even
when some orders do. Exhibit the proposed difference rather than infer it.
Order-independent statistics do not settle every physical time or rate target.
Any candidate rate clause remains a possible owner decision after a scoped
witness; the realized-state primitive supplies no comparison measure.

## Formation-unit clause witness

Compare specified sequential and joint formation constructions on the same
finite site set, including the proposed site-plus-six-neighbours example.
State the joint law rather than infer it from the word joint. A joint law may
equal a sequential law even when other sequential orders differ. If a
commuting-projector chain rule is used, its operators, probabilities and
premises must be explicit; it is not implied by classical order blindness.
A proven difference may motivate an owner decision about a formation model,
but it does not by itself amend the Admissibility sentence.

## Record-process dynamics and unrecorded sites

The original proposal's claim that unrecorded sites carry no law-level state
is a hypothesis for investigation, not an adopted reading or a theorem. Ask which dynamical conclusions follow under
the actual axiom text and which require a separately specified law or bridge.
Do not infer that all propagating modes must be finished-record statistics.

For an explicitly supplied admissible state, formation law and observable,
compare finite or asymptotically controlled record correlators with transverse
or lattice-Laplacian kernels. A supplied staggered sea, clock, Hamiltonian or
comparison kernel remains an input. Finite short-range examples cannot exclude
all long-range laws; a matching correlator alone does not identify a physical
photon or gravitational field.

## Composition and law selection

Compare explicitly defined tensor and graded models, their observable maps,
and local/global premise checks. Matching a finite graded zero pattern is a
scoped property of a rule. It does not alone derive physical fermions or settle
cross-site composition from the axioms. Test candidate order-blind families on
feasible 2x2x2 examples; report family-specific counterexamples or missing
extensions honestly. Existing encoding or composition notes must be used at
their actual supplied-premise scope, not as completed all-axiom witnesses.

## Glued groups as support rules (Gauss law)

Classify a declared finite family of covariant binary or ternary support rules
and test whether its satisfying configurations carry the specified local group
operation. A Gauss-type interpretation needs an explicit map from site records
to edge variables, the constraint, and the claimed observable/dynamical bridge.
Use feasible 2x2x2 or 3x3x3 cases with stated completeness and resource limits.
Failure in a nearest-neighbour family does not prove gauge structure requires
a larger window or a new premise. Any operational extension remains explicit.
