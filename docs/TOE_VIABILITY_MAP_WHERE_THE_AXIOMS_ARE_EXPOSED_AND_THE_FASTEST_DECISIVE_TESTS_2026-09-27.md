## The answer in plain words

The core of the axioms is ordinary lattice physics: a cubic lattice, a qubit
at each site, one local rule. Nothing found so far says that core is wrong.
Everything found so far says the two places where the framework departs from
ordinary lattice quantum physics are where it gets into trouble.

1. **Records as primitive events.**
   - Records alone cannot produce the correlations experiments measure
     (Bell; landed).
   - Records that collapse the lattice's quantum state one site at a time
     cost the lattice's own energy scale per record: about five billion
     joules if the lattice is Planck-sized.
   - So a record must be either a soft lock on something large, or the
     stable product of ordinary quantum dynamics.
2. **Time handled differently from space.** When time is not a lattice
   direction on the same footing as space, different fields drift to
   different speeds of light once they interact. The drift is about a
   quarter of a percent at electromagnetic strength, and it does not shrink
   as the lattice gets finer. The framework already approved the fix in June
   (`kinetic_isotropy_primitive`: a tick is grained like an edge). The
   current gravity campaign does not run on that surface.

The two hardest problems of any lattice theory of everything come out as
constraints, not as failures of these axioms in particular:
- **Handedness.** Free lattice particles always come with mirror twins,
  whether time flows or ticks. Handedness has to come from interactions that
  make the twins heavy, which constrains how much matter a site must carry.
- **Gravity.** The campaign's gravity results hold at first order in the
  fields, and their key identity (`α = K/4`, gravitational waves at the
  walker's speed) is a tuning off the approved surface.

So the axioms are not pointed the wrong way at their core. Two readings the
campaign has been working under are exposed:
- records as fundamental single-site events;
- a level time separate from space.

Both have cheap fixes, and the fixes are standard physics.

## What looks right

- **The lattice–qubit–local-rule core** is the setting of lattice quantum
  field theory and of emergent-gauge-field constructions from qubits. It has
  a mature toolkit: reflection positivity, transfer matrices, emergent
  Lorentz invariance on hypercubic regulators, string-net gauge fields.
- **The June `kinetic_isotropy_primitive`** already puts time on the same
  footing as space. The B4 note proves it protects one light cone at one
  loop.
- **The gravity campaign's first-order structure** reproduces linearised GR's
  constraint algebra on the lattice: closure at `β = −α`; exact books for
  free walkers; lapse, shift and two polarisations. That is real, and it
  carries over to any formulation that keeps the same continuum limit.

## The highest-leverage work, ranked

Each item names its decisive test and what each outcome would mean. The
first three are owner decisions that cost no computation. The rest are
probes.

1. **Put quantum dynamics in the axioms** (owner decision; the evidence is
   landed).
   - Layman sentence to test against the axiom-update criterion: "Between
     records, what can happen at neighbouring sites changes together, as
     waves do; a record is where a wave becomes a fact."
   - Without it the framework cannot reach measured Bell correlations.
   - The dynamics-clause lane already built the minimal version:
     nearest-neighbour two-qubit generators (Heisenberg under possibility
     covariance) and the trace rule from locality of marginals.

2. **Decide what a record is** (owner decision).
   - Option A: **emergent.** A record is a fact the dynamics has copied
     redundantly into its surroundings. "Records form" becomes a theorem;
     the formation site, rate and unit become computable.
   - Option B: **soft and collective.** A record locks a large-scale
     variable weakly. This is collapse-model physics, testable against
     heating and X-ray bounds, but its relativistic form is known to excite
     the vacuum.
   - What the record-cost note excludes, at a Planck-sized lattice, is the
     reading the formation lane has mostly used: sharp locks of single
     sites. Option A is the cheaper and more standard of the two.

3. **Run all dynamics on the approved surface where time is a lattice
   direction** (owner decision, with an existing approval).
   - The primitive is approved; the current campaign's continuous-time
     walker and level-time clocks do not use it.
   - The block-105 tension must be faced: in discrete ticks a local clock is
     exact only for walks that do not move.
   - Pre-registered test (next item): if the member lives on the same
     hypercubic surface, `α = K/4` should become a symmetry consequence.

4. **Gravity-lane probe: `α = K/4` on the hypercubic surface** (one to two
   blocks).
   - Write the most general `Z^4`-hypercubic-invariant, nearest-neighbour
     quadratic action for a symmetric tensor `h_μν` (lattice linearised
     gravity, Euclidean). Reduce it to block 101's 3+1 form
     `L = [α tr(ḣ²) + β(tr ḣ)²]/w̄ + K w̄(u R_1 + R_2) − e u` and read off
     `α, β, K`.
   - Prediction: the transverse-traceless speed is forced to the walker's,
     `K = 4α`, and the closure ratio `β = −α` comes out or is shown
     independent.
   - If both come out, the campaign's two central tunings become one
     symmetry. If `β = −α` does not, the lattice constraint algebra and the
     hypercubic surface are in tension, which the owner should know before
     going to nonlinear order.

5. **Handedness probe: can the walker's mirror partners be gapped by a
   symmetric local interaction?**
   - Start from the one-dimensional analogue (8 Majoranas, the known Z_8
     classification) in-framework.
   - Then take the three-dimensional content count: 16 per generation.
   - Outcome: either a concrete route to a chiral spectrum inside the
     axioms, or a precise statement of what the Qubit axiom must supply.

6. **Matter-count decision** (owner). Fermions as elementary walkers need
   more than one qubit per site, about 12 per cell for three generations
   with mirrors. Fermions as composites of qubits (emergent Z_2 gauge
   structure; the dynamics-clause lane's Kitaev carvings are the first
   in-framework instance) keep one qubit per site.

## What not to spend on until 1–3 are settled

- Higher-order refinements of the gravity campaign's continuous-time books
  and couplings (the current third-column work). Their central identities are
  tunings on the surface they use (item 3).
- Formation-law mathematics whose physical reading is a sharp single-site
  collapse (item 2).

Neither is wasted as mathematics. Both risk being built on a reading that the
cheap decisions above would change.
