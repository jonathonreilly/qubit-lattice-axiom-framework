## Proof

Take the finite block `Lambda = {x,y}`. By the retained per-site dimension
row, each site contributes a two-dimensional complex factor `C^2_x`, and a
finite block has tensor-product carrier `tensor_{z in Lambda} C^2_z`. Hence
`H_{xy} = C^2_x tensor_C C^2_y` and `dim_C H_{xy} = 2 * 2 = 4`.

For finite-dimensional complex Hilbert spaces, `End(U tensor_C V)` is
canonically isomorphic to `End(U) tensor_C End(V)`. Applying this to
`U = C^2_x`, `V = C^2_y` gives

```text
End(H_{xy}) ~= End(C^2_x) tensor_C End(C^2_y)
            ~= M_2(C) tensor_C M_2(C)
            ~= M_4(C).
```

The embeddings `a |-> a tensor I_2` and `b |-> I_2 tensor b` are faithful
because tensoring with the identity preserves nonzero matrix action. They
commute because

```text
(a tensor I_2)(I_2 tensor b) = a tensor b = (I_2 tensor b)(a tensor I_2).
```

The products `a tensor b`, with `a,b` ranging over a basis of `M_2(C)`,
span `M_2(C) tensor_C M_2(C)` and therefore span `M_4(C)`. Equivalently,
because the per-site dimension row supplies the Pauli realization of each
`M_2(C)` factor, the sixteen explicitly constructed Pauli products
`sigma_mu tensor sigma_nu`, `mu,nu=0,1,2,3`, form a basis.

The Bell vector `|Phi+> = (|00> + |11>) / sqrt(2)` is a unit vector in
`C^2 tensor_C C^2`; no dynamical preparation claim is involved.

The self-adjoint part of `M_n(C)` has real dimension `n^2`. Therefore the
two-site generated algebra has self-adjoint real dimension `16`, equal to the
product of the two one-site self-adjoint dimensions `4 * 4`. This is exactly
the finite local-tomography dimension count on the ordinary generated complex
two-qubit surface.
