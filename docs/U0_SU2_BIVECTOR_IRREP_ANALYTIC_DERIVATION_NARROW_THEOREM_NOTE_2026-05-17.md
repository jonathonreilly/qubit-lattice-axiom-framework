`Cl(3,0) ⊗_R C ≅ M_2(C) ⊕ M_2(C)` denote its complexification split (from
the cited retained sibling theorem), and let `U_plaquette^SU(2)` denote
the Wilson plaquette holonomy variable in the SU(2) factor's fundamental
representation. Assume the three named inputs:

**(X1) Cl(3) complexification split.** From the retained sibling theorem,

```text
Cl(3,0) ⊗_R C  ≅  M_2(C) ⊕ M_2(C),                                       (X1)
```

with central-pseudoscalar chirality `ω ↦ ±i` distinguishing the two
simple summands `e_± Cl(3,0) ⊗_R C`. The SU(2) bivector subgroup of
`Cl(3,0)` (generated by `{γ_i γ_j : i < j}` ∪ `{1}`) acts irreducibly on
each simple summand via the dim-2 fundamental representation.

```text
u_0(SU(2))  =  ⟨ (1/2) · Re Tr U_plaquette^SU(2) ⟩^(1/4),                  (U1)
```

## Proof

Pure substitution.

**Step 1 (Cl(3) bivector subgroup is SU(2)).** From `(X1)`, the
even subalgebra `Cl(3,0)^+ ≅ C^4` of `Cl(3,0)` (real-dim 4) is spanned
by `{1, γ_1γ_2, γ_1γ_3, γ_2γ_3}`. The unit-norm subgroup of `Cl(3,0)^+`
is isomorphic to `Spin(3) = SU(2)`, with `{γ_i γ_j : i < j}` generating
the bivector subalgebra `so(3)` (the Lie algebra of `SU(2)`). The
identification `Cl(3,0)^+ ≅ H` (quaternions, real-dim 4) makes
`SU(2) ≅ Spin(3) ⊂ Cl(3,0)^+` explicit. This SU(2) is the framework's
native bivector SU(2) gauge structure.

**Step 2 (irrep dimension forces N_SU(2) = 2).** From `(X2)`, every
faithful irreducible finite-dim complex representation of `Cl(3,0)` has
complex dimension `dim_C V = 2`. The SU(2) bivector subgroup of step 1
embeds into `Cl(3,0)^+ ⊂ Cl(3,0)` and inherits this dimensional readout:
its restriction to the simple summand `e_± Cl(3,0) ⊗_R C ≅ M_2(C)` is the
standard `2 × 2` matrix representation, which is precisely the
fundamental representation of `SU(2)` on `V ≅ C^2`. Hence

```text
N_SU(2)  :=  dim_C ( fundamental rep of SU(2) )  =  2.                    (S1)
```

This is the load-bearing structural identification: the framework's
native SU(2) bivector gauge group is not assigned an abstract fundamental
dimension; the dimension is **forced** by the Cl(3) faithful-irrep
theorem to equal `2`.

**Step 3 (substitute N_G = N_SU(2) = 2 into Lüscher convention).** Apply
`(X3)` with `G = SU(2)` and `N_G = N_SU(2) = 2` from `(S1)`:

```text
u_0(SU(2))  =  ⟨ (1/N_SU(2)) · Re Tr U_plaquette^SU(2) ⟩^(1/4)
             =  ⟨ (1/2) · Re Tr U_plaquette^SU(2) ⟩^(1/4).                 (U1)
```

This is `(U1)`.

**Step 4 (recover fourth-root form (T6)).** Defining
`P_SU(2) := ⟨(1/2) Re Tr U_p^SU(2)⟩` (an abstract positive real once the
plaquette expectation is taken), `(U1)` reads

```text
u_0(SU(2))  =  P_SU(2)^(1/4),                                              (U2)
```

which is the `G = SU(2)` specialization of the repo's fourth-root form
`(T6)` in `ALPHA_S_TADPOLE_IMPROVEMENT_VERTEX_POWER_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-10`. ∎
