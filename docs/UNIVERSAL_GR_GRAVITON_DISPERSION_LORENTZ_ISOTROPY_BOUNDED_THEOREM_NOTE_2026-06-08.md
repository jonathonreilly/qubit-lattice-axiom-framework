## Summary

This note records a finite-grid diagnostic for the W-native induced-gravity
program. In the supplied staggered Kahler-Dirac stress two-point calculation,
with fixed `yz` transverse-traceless polarization and mass `m=0.7`, the sampled
TT stiffness

```text
C(Khat) = (Pi_TT(k0 Khat) - Pi_TT(0)) / k0^2
```

has two runner-verified features:

- the axis samples `Khat=e0` and `Khat=e1` agree within the numerical tolerance
  on the sampled grids;
- the `t-x` diagonal sample is larger than the axis sample, and the excess
  decreases across `N=6,8,10` with a stable finite-grid coefficient
  `(C_diag/C_axis - 1)/k0^2 ~= 0.043`.

That is useful finite-grid support for locating the earlier few-percent
staggered Kahler-Dirac Zener anisotropy as a diagonal finite-momentum lattice
effect in this channel. It is not, by itself, a continuum Lorentz theorem,
physical gravitational-wave dispersion relation, General Relativity recovery
claim, or observational speed-bound comparison.

The checked statements are:

- **T1 axis equality:** for `N in {6,8,10}`, `C(e0)` and `C(e1)` agree to
  relative error below `1e-3` in this finite-grid setup.
- **T2 sampled diagonal excess:** the diagonal excess
  `C_diag/C_axis - 1` is positive and decreases over the sampled grids as `k0`
  decreases.
- **T3 finite-grid coefficient stability:** the three sampled values of
  `(C_diag/C_axis - 1)/k0^2` have spread below `10%`, giving the diagnostic
  coefficient `~0.043` for this channel and sampled range.
