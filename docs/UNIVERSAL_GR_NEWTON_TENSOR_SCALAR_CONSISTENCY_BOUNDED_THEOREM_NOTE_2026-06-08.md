## Summary

This note records a bounded structural diagnostic comparing two weak-field
surfaces:

- the scalar `Z^3` graph Green function, whose tail approaches `1/(4 pi r)`;
- a finite tensor-channel stress response whose small-momentum difference is
  positive and proportional, on the sampled grid, to the one-direction lattice

The durable statement is shape-level: both sampled surfaces are compatible with
an inverted `Z^3` graph-Laplacian propagator form. The runner does not derive
the absolute Newton constant, does not prove the tensor and scalar routes
register the same observed `G`, and does not supply the GR tensor/source
normalization factor.
