## Result

For the non-collinear finite lattice momenta
`k_1 || x`, `k_2 || y`, and `k_3 = -(k_1 + k_2)`, the proper staggered
Kähler-Dirac fermion has nonzero paramagnetic stress three-point channels after
all three external tensor legs are projected to transverse-traceless (TT)
polarizations for their own momenta.

The runner verifies:

- each tested external tensor is genuinely TT (`trace(E)=0`, `k.E=0`) on its
  own leg;
- selected staggered TT-projected mixed channels are nonzero at `N=6`:
  `plus,cross,cross = +0.03344`, `cross,plus,cross = +0.03344`,
  `cross,cross,plus = +0.01623`;
- the same nonzero support persists under one Brillouin-zone refinement at
  `N=8`: `+0.05344`, `+0.05344`, `+0.03603`;
- the same selected TT-projected channels vanish in the two-component `Cl(3)`
  control to numerical precision (`~10^-17`) while the staggered values remain
  finite; and
- the runner explicitly does **not** prove all-same-helicity vanishing: the
  `right,right,right` TT-projected amplitude has finite magnitude at `N=6`.

`TOTAL: PASS=5 FAIL=0`.

## Bounded Meaning

This establishes a bounded support result:

> The proper staggered Kähler-Dirac fermion supplies nonzero finite-momentum,
> TT-projected paramagnetic stress-triangle channels that are absent in selected
> two-component control channels on the same TT kinematics.

This is evidence for a W-native pure-TT cubic ingredient in the induced-gravity
chain. It is **not** a quantitative Einstein-Hilbert cubic vertex match.
