## 3. Direct all-degree cross-Wick locality

Normalized correlated Wick expectation can be written on the coefficient
algebra as

```text
G_C[F]=[exp(D_C)F]_0,
D_C=sum_(x,y,a,b)C_(yb,xa)
      partial_(zeta_y^b)partial_(bar zeta_x^a).                    (3.1)
```

Split `D_C` into onsite and unordered offsite-pair operators. These even
differential operators commute. The final constant-extracted product-onsite
expectation `[product_x exp(D_xx) dot]_0` is contractive because every site
covariance block has operator norm at most `1/m`; the polynomial operator
`exp(D_xx)` by itself is not claimed contractive. Thus

```text
exp(D_C)=product_x exp(D_xx) product_(x<y)exp(D_xy).                (3.2)
```

The Neumann path expansion and the eight-neighbor, half-hop grammar give

```text
sum_y exp[alpha d_I(x,y)]||C_xy||_2
 <=1/(m-4exp(alpha)),                                              (3.3)

sum_(y!=x)exp[alpha d_I(x,y)]||C_xy||_2
 <=4exp(alpha)/[m(m-4exp(alpha))].                                 (3.4)
```

For a `3 by 3` block, entrywise `l1` norm is at most `3sqrt(3)` times operator
norm. Paying both orientations of an unordered pair and the derivative cost
`eta^(-2)=m` gives `kappa_alpha` in (0.13). If
`R_xy=exp(D_xy)-1`, then

```text
sup_x sum_(y!=x)exp[alpha d_I(x,y)]||R_xy||
 <=K_C(alpha),                                                     (3.5)
```

because `exp(delta)-1<=delta exp(kappa_0)`.

For disjoint local blocks `X_i` and even balanced polynomials `F_i`, put

```text
delta_ij=m sum_(x in X_i,y in X_j)
 [|C_xy|_(entry,1)+|C_yx|_(entry,1)],
z_ij=exp(delta_ij)-1.                                              (3.6)
```

For the block estimate, regroup all covariance operators internal to `X_i`
into `D_i`. The principal covariance block `C_(X_i X_i)` has operator norm at
most `1/m`, so its full constant-extracted Gaussian expectation is contractive
by the covariance-minor bound. Only the inter-block operators `D_ij` remain to
be expanded. The joint cumulant is therefore a sum of cross-edge graphs
connected on the block labels. Bounding every connected graph by a chosen
spanning tree and then summing remaining edges gives the finite, all-degree
estimate

```text
||cum_C(F_1,...,F_n)||
 <=exp[sum_(i<j)z_ij]
   sum_(T tree on [n]) product_((i,j) in T)z_ij
   product_i ||F_i||_eta,                                         (3.7)
```

Equation (3.7) is regulator-uniform and decays exponentially when the block
separations grow for fixed finite block sizes or fixed local shapes, by
(3.4)--(3.6). The complete block-size dependence remains explicit in
`delta_ij`; no block-size-independent cross-edge constant is claimed.

For `n=2` and `G_A O=0`, the left side is `||G_A[OF]||`. It is linear in the
norm of `F`, so it vanishes when the external attached factor is removed. A
full exponential family of physical residual factors requires a separate
colored two-species resummation; (3.7) is its locality supplier, not that
resummation.
