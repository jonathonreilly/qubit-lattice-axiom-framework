# World / setup / bookkeeping terminology — meta convention note

Date: 2026-08-08 (created by review loop iteration 1 per the labeling
split ruling on the Cycle-911 package)

Authority: none

Audit: not applicable (non-propositional)

Claim type: meta (naming convention only; asserts no physical fact)

## What this note is

The Cycle-911 package originally bundled a terminology recommendation
into its theorem verdict: call the recorded census keys "setups"
(alternative initial conditions) rather than "worlds" (branches of one
configuration), call the historical census weightings "apportionment
bookkeeping" rather than "occurrence weights", and describe the
historical interface results as "bookkeeping between lanes". The
review found that recommendation useful as VOCABULARY and unprovable
as PHYSICS: it is a typing/interpretation choice, not a consequence of
any zero-coupling calculation, and bundling it into a theorem made it
an unprovable pseudo-premise for downstream cycles.

This note records the convention as a convention.

## The convention

When discussing the recorded Cycle-878-lineage census (currently
unlanded; provenance-scoped):

- **setup** — one recorded census key (an initial condition plus its
  schedule), preferred over "world" where branching is not
  established;
- **apportionment bookkeeping** — a normalized fraction over recorded
  setups, preferred over "occurrence weight" or "probability" where no
  occurrence law has been derived or imported;
- **bookkeeping identity** — a cross-lane numerical identity among
  recorded aggregates, carrying no occurrence-law force.

## What this convention does NOT do

- It does not assert that the recorded census keys ARE alternative
  initial conditions of one law, nor that they are not branches; that
  typing question is open and substrate-scoped.
- It does not assert that census weightings can never be occurrence
  weights: a separately derived or explicitly imported ensemble/setup
  measure remains a legitimate route to be costed. The realized-state
  primitive
  ([REALIZED_STATE_PRIMITIVE_NOTE_2026-06-11.md](REALIZED_STATE_PRIMITIVE_NOTE_2026-06-11.md))
  supplies no such measure and forbids none.
- It licenses no inference in any theorem, runner, or audit row.
  Downstream artifacts may adopt the vocabulary; they may not cite
  this note as evidence for a typing claim.
