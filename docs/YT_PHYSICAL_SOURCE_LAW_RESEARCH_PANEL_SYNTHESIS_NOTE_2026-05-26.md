## First-Principles Reframe

The outcome we need is not "make Y_T look natural."  The outcome is:

```text
fix the physical source unit and the physical top source direction without
importing a target mass, old Ward definition, H_unit matrix element, or hidden
normalization convention.
```

The first-principles drivers are:

1. A physical intervention must be expressed in terms of records the substrate
   can actually produce.
2. Ideal sharp projective readout supplies finite signed records.
3. A primitive intervention should change only the named record statistic.
4. Adding an unobserved scale or channel is extra information unless the
   substrate supplies it.
5. Source units must be invariant under relabeling, blocking, and sufficient
   record coarse-graining.
6. These requirements point to KL projection for the source curve and Fisher
   arclength for its unit.

This is why the most promising route is not another flavor-model analogy.  The
first primitive to attack is the physical intervention law.
