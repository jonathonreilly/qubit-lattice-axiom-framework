## 1. Theorem

Fix the retained `Cl(3) x Z^3` Wilson-plaquette + staggered-Dirac bare
action of `MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-05-03.md`:32-43, conditional on the named
open gates (A) and (B). Let `g_bare > 0` be arbitrary; do **not** preselect
the canonical surface. On the retained `Q_L = (2, 3)` block, project the
amputated tree-level four-fermion 1PI Green's function
`Gamma_S^(4)(q^2; g_bare)` onto the color-singlet x iso-singlet x
Dirac-scalar channel `O_S = (psibar psi)_(1,1) (psibar psi)_(1,1)`.

**Hypothesis (H1) — Lagrangian operator content.** The retained bare action
contains exactly:

- the Wilson plaquette gauge operator
  `S_W = -(beta / N_c) sum_p Re Tr U_p`, with `beta = 2 N_c / g_bare^2`;
- the staggered Dirac fermion kinetic operator
  `S_F = sum_x sum_mu eta_mu(x) (1/2) [psibar(x) U_mu(x) psi(x+mu)
  - psibar(x+mu) U_mu^dag(x) psi(x)]`;
- and no other operators. In particular, the bare action contains:
  - no fundamental scalar field of any representation,
  - no contact two-fermion mass term `m psibar psi`,
  - no contact four-fermion operator
    `(psibar Gamma psi)(psibar Gamma' psi)` of any Clifford structure
    `(Gamma, Gamma')` and any color/iso projection.

**Hypothesis (H2) — Tree-level power-counting decomposition.** The
amputated four-fermion 1PI Green's function from any local Lagrangian
field theory at tree order is the sum of contributions from each
admissible tree diagram, classified by the field content of each
internal line:

```text
Gamma^(4)_tree = Gamma^(4)_OGE + Gamma^(4)_contact + Gamma^(4)_higher
```

where

- `Gamma^(4)_OGE` is the sum over single-gauge-boson-exchange tree
  diagrams (one internal gauge propagator, two trilinear gauge-fermion
  vertices);
- `Gamma^(4)_contact` is the contribution from a bare contact
  four-fermion operator in the Lagrangian, if any (zero internal
  propagators, one four-fermion vertex);
- `Gamma^(4)_higher` is the contribution from multi-gauge-boson exchange
  tree topologies (two or more internal gauge propagators, three or more
  trilinear vertices).

This decomposition is textbook (Peskin–Schroeder §10.1, Itzykson–Zuber
§5-3): it follows from Wick's theorem applied to the tree-level
T-product expansion of `Z[J]` in the bare action.

**Hypothesis (H3) — D9 composite-Higgs structural fact.** The framework's
unique scalar bilinear on Q_L is the composite
`phi = (1/N_c) psibar_a psi_a` (`YUKAWA_COLOR_PROJECTION_THEOREM.md`:33-40).
There is no independent fundamental scalar field. This is a retained
structural fact about the operator content of the framework.

**Hypothesis (H4) — Higher-topology power suppression.** Each
two-gauge-boson exchange tree diagram contributes
`O(g_bare^4 / q^4) * O_S` to `Gamma_S^(4)`, which is sub-leading in
`g_bare^2 / q^2` relative to the OGE contribution `O(g_bare^2 / q^2)`.
This is standard Feynman-rule power counting (each trilinear vertex
carries one `g_bare`; each gauge propagator carries one `1/q^2`).

**Conclusion (T1) — contact-4-fermion vanishing.** Under (H1)–(H3), every
admissible bare-action contact four-fermion coefficient `c_contact` is
identically zero on the retained block:

```text
c_contact[(psibar Gamma psi)(psibar Gamma' psi)]  =  0
                                  for all (Gamma, Gamma').        (T1)
```

In particular, projected onto the scalar-singlet channel `O_S`, the
bare-action contact contribution vanishes:

```text
Gamma_S^(4)_contact  =  0.                                       (T1a)
```

**Conclusion (T2) — OGE-only tree completeness at leading order.** Under
(T1) and (H4), at leading order in `g_bare^2 / q^2` the OGE diagram is
the **complete** tree-level contribution to `Gamma_S^(4)`:

```text
Gamma_S^(4)(q^2; g_bare)  =  Gamma_S^(4)_OGE(q^2; g_bare)
                          +  O(g_bare^4 / q^4) * O_S.            (T2)
```

The OGE coefficient is the standard exact tree-level expression
(D12 + S2):

```text
Gamma_S^(4)_OGE(q^2; g_bare)  =  - c_S g_bare^2 / (2 N_c q^2) * O_S
                              =  - g_bare^2 / (2 N_c q^2) * O_S
                                  for c_S = +1.                  (T2a)
```

**Conclusion (T3) — same-1PI-bridge reduction.** The Rep A vs Rep B
comparison reduces, at leading order in `g_bare^2 / q^2`, to the
two-line statement

```text
Gamma_S^(4)_Rep A(q^2; g_bare)  =  Gamma_S^(4)_OGE(q^2; g_bare)
                                  + 0 + O(g_bare^4 / q^4),       (T3a)
Gamma_S^(4)_Rep B(q^2; g_bare)  =  - F_Htt^(0)(g_bare)^2 / q^2 * O_S
                                  + 0 + 0.                       (T3b)
```

Eq. (T3a) is Rep A = OGE + (vanishing contact) + (sub-leading higher);
Eq. (T3b) is Rep B with the retained narrow form-factor identity
`F_Htt^(0)(g_bare) = 1/sqrt(6)` for all `g_bare`
([`UNIT_SINGLET_OVERLAP_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-02.md`](UNIT_SINGLET_OVERLAP_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-02.md);

Hence the same-1PI-bridge gate equation

## 2. Proof

### 2.1 Operator enumeration of the bare action

Direct reading of `MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-05-03.md`:32-43 enumerates the
bare action's operators exhaustively. The Wilson plaquette term is
purely gauge-field-dependent (no `psi` factor); the staggered Dirac
term is bilinear in `(psibar, psi)` with one gauge-link insertion per
hop. Neither term contains a Lagrangian-level
`(psibar Gamma psi)(psibar Gamma' psi)` four-fermion operator with any
Clifford structure `(Gamma, Gamma')` and any color/iso projection.

The D9 composite-Higgs structural fact forbids the addition of a
fundamental scalar field that could couple to such a contact operator
via Yukawa-mediation followed by integrating out. Per D9, the
framework's only scalar bilinear is the **composite** `phi` built from
`psibar psi`; this is not an independent field that can be added to
the Lagrangian as a separate operator.

By the standard rule that bare-action coefficients are read off the
operator-by-operator decomposition of `S = sum_O c_O O`, the absence
of a `(psibar Gamma psi)(psibar Gamma' psi)` operator in the
enumerated action means `c_contact[O] = 0` for every such operator
`O`. This proves `(T1)`.

In particular, the scalar-singlet projection
`O_S = (psibar psi)_(1,1) (psibar psi)_(1,1)` is one specific element
of the Clifford × color × iso decomposition of four-fermion operators,
so `c_contact[O_S] = 0` and `Gamma_S^(4)_contact = 0`, proving `(T1a)`.

### 2.2 OGE-only tree completeness

By `(H2)` the tree-level four-fermion Green's function decomposes into
OGE + contact + higher topologies. By `(T1a)` the contact contribution
on the scalar-singlet channel vanishes identically. Among higher
topologies, the lowest is two-gluon exchange, which by Feynman-rule
power counting contributes `O(g_bare^4 / q^4)`. Therefore at leading
order in `g_bare^2 / q^2`,

```text
Gamma_S^(4)(q^2; g_bare)
  =  Gamma_S^(4)_OGE(q^2; g_bare)  +  O(g_bare^4 / q^4) * O_S,
```

which is `(T2)`.

The OGE coefficient is computed from the bare action's gauge-fermion
trilinear vertex and one internal gluon propagator. Color projection
onto the singlet channel uses the exact SU(`N_c`) color-singlet Fierz
coefficient `-1/(2 N_c)` (D12, retained, machine-precision verified by
the existing yt-ward runner Block 4). Dirac projection onto the
scalar-scalar channel uses the exact Clifford-algebra coefficient
`c_S = +1` (S2, retained, machine-precision verified by the existing
yt-ward runner Block 8). Combining these gives the standard tree
coefficient

```text
Gamma_S^(4)_OGE(q^2; g_bare)
  =  - c_S g_bare^2 / (2 N_c q^2) * O_S
  =  - g_bare^2 / (2 N_c q^2) * O_S,
```

which is `(T2a)`.

### 2.3 Same-1PI bridge reduction

The H_unit-representation tree coefficient on the scalar-singlet
channel is `- F_Htt^(0)(g_bare)^2 / q^2 * O_S`, with
`F_Htt^(0)(g_bare) = 1/sqrt(6)` for all `g_bare` by the retained
narrow Rep-B independence theorem. There is no contact or higher
topology contribution in Rep B at tree order on this channel — Rep B
is the H_unit-mediated tree diagram, computed by two operator
insertions of `H_unit`, with no internal gauge boson. This gives
`(T3b)`.

`(T3a)` is `(T2)` with the contact term made explicit as `0` per
`(T1a)`.

Equating `(T3a)` and `(T3b)` at leading order in `g_bare^2 / q^2`
gives the coefficient identity

```text
F_Htt^(0)(g_bare)^2  =  g_bare^2 / (2 N_c)
                           + O(g_bare^4 / q^2 leading-corr).    (R)
```

This is **not** a separate matching axiom or auxiliary input — it is
the structural identity that two representations of the same projected
Green's function must agree. The (T1)+(T2)+(H4) chain establishes
exactly that:

- two representations exist (Rep A from gauge dynamics; Rep B from the
  H_unit-residue);
- Rep A is contact-free per `(T1a)` and OGE-only at leading order per
  `(T2)`;
- Rep B is tree-only (the H_unit-mediated diagram is the unique
  tree-level Rep B contribution on this channel);
- the projected channel `O_S` admits a unique scalar-singlet
  coefficient per the D17 composite-Higgs uniqueness theorem
  (retained).

The two representations therefore agree at leading order, yielding
`(R)`. This proves `(T3)`. ∎
