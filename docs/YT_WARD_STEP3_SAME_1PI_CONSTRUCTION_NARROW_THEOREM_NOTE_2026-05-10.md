## Exact identities

These identities are unconditional coefficient reductions from the declared
inputs. They do not assert that Rep A and Rep B are the same 1PI object.

Rep A, the OGE projection, reduces to

```text
C_A(g_bare) = c_S * g_bare^2 / (2 * N_c).
```

Rep B, the `H_unit` projection, reduces to

```text
F_Htt0(g_bare) = 1 / sqrt(N_c * N_iso),
C_B = F_Htt0(g_bare)^2 = 1 / (N_c * N_iso).
```

Their exact residual is

```text
C_A - C_B
  = c_S * g_bare^2 / (2 * N_c) - 1 / (N_c * N_iso)
  = (N_iso * c_S * g_bare^2 - 2) / (2 * N_c * N_iso).
```

At the framework surface `N_c = 3`, `N_iso = 2`, `c_S = +1`, this becomes

```text
canonical residual = (g_bare^2 - 1) / 6.
```

The exact off-surface counterexample is

```text
g_bare = 2 gives residual 1/2.
```

The D12 SU(3) Fierz identity input is sourced to
[`YT_WARD_IDENTITY_DERIVATION_THEOREM.md`](YT_WARD_IDENTITY_DERIVATION_THEOREM.md).
In ASCII paraphrase, the consumed singlet-channel content is

```text

  = 1/2 (delta_il delta_jk - (1/N_c) delta_ij delta_kl),
```

with singlet coefficient `-1 / (2 * N_c)`, hence `-1/6` at `N_c = 3`.

The `H_unit` normalization input is sourced to the
[unit-singlet note](UNIT_SINGLET_OVERLAP_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-02.md).
In paraphrase, the consumed framework-surface content is

```text

diagonal Wick-contraction count = N_iso * N_c = 6.
```

## Conditional chain

Under SAME-1PI, Rep A and Rep B are supplied as two complete representations
of the same projected amputated 1PI Green's function. Only under that named
premise may the coefficients be equated:

```text
C_A = C_B
c_S * g_bare^2 / (2 * N_c) = 1 / (N_c * N_iso)
c_S * g_bare^2 = 2 / N_iso.
```

At the `Q_L = (N_iso, N_c) = (2, 3)` surface with `c_S = +1`, this is

```text
g_bare^2 = 1.
```

Without SAME-1PI, the equality is not available. The residual above is the
computed no-equality branch, and `g_bare = 2` is an explicit off-surface
counterexample to symbolic equality.
