The contract means:

1. **K2_EXACTNESS_TEXT_LOCK:** K2R.1-K2R.6 above is the complete object being
   decided.
2. **REGISTERED_PHI_VALUE_FACE_ACCEPTED:** the registered value-face standing
   from `#5020` or a landed successor is accepted as a lawful K2 value-face
   input.
3. **DISTINGUISHED_TWO_NINTHS_THEOREM_RETAINED:** a retained theorem forces
   the distinguished registered value to exact pure `2/9` without observed
   lepton data or fitted phase data.
4. **RADIAN_READOUT_LICENSE_RETAINED:** the pure number is licensed as the
   charged-lepton `delta` phase.
5. **FOLD_AND_BRANCH_DOMAIN_LOCK:** the `Phi = (1/3) arccos(cos 3delta)`
   fold, branch, sign/chamber, and nondegenerate-domain conventions are fixed
   without empirical selection.
6. **NO_K1_K3_K4_OR_MASS_INPUT:** the K2 decision excludes counting,
   physical species, absolute scale, native bridge, branch mass-map, and
   physical electron mass inputs.
7. **NO_COMPARATOR_PROOF_INPUT:** observed or fitted lepton/hydrogen data is
   excluded as proof input.
8. **NO_NEW_PRIMITIVE_OR_AXIOM:** the decision retires an import; it does not
   add an axiom, approved primitive, or new Tier-A numerical admission.
9. **OWNER_RATIFICATION:** the owner accepts this exact K2 R-eta exactness
   object.
10. **AUDIT_ACCEPTANCE:** the normal independent review/audit path accepts the
    decision and dependency consequences.

No proper subset of those ten contract inputs is a retained K2 R-eta
exactness handoff.
