therefore retains a source-ledger fork, not a failed gravity prediction.

not license that vertex for a `beta=0` field packet or for a moving body. The

## Exact conditional statement

Let `U` be the Cycle-215 six-direction massless walk and let `|s>` be its
uniform scalar coin state. Let

```text
Gamma = one-sixth times the sum over the six nearest-neighbor shifts,
L     = 6(I - Gamma),
Psi_0 = f tensor |s>.
```

Cycle 215 proves that the scalar projection follows the centered wave law at
`dt^2 = 1/3`. For real `f`, the Cycle-213 conserved energy on the first pair of
slices reduces to

```text
E_wave = (3/2) <f, (I - Gamma^2) f> >= 0.
```

The Cycle-216 stiffness is

```text
K = 2I - U - U^dagger,

<Psi_0, K Psi_0>
    = 2 <f, (I - Gamma) f>
    = (1/3) <f, L f> >= 0.
```

Because `K` is a polynomial in `U` and `U^dagger`, its expectation is exactly
conserved by `U`. The runner reports `K/2` as its stiffness coordinate. For a
point packet `f=delta_a`, the two exact values are

```text

K/2    = 1.
```

For an acoustic plane wave of nonzero momentum `k`, Cycle 215 gives

```text
cos(omega(k)) = 1 - L(k)/6,

K(k) = 2 - 2 cos(omega(k)) = L(k)/3 > 0.
```

At the same time, the Cycle-219 vacuum-relative rest scalar is

```text

```

up to floating-point evaluation of the exact zero phase. Cycle 219 does not
declare this field value to be a gravitational source charge. Rather, the

## Logical redundancy is not physical duplication

Cycle 219 correctly proves that tensoring a body with one or more normalized
spectator record factors does not multiply the body's rest mass. That is
logical redundancy invariance, and Cycle 227 preserves it.

Physical carriers are a different question. A bit can be represented by one
packet in either of two translated rails; the two logical values have the same
carrier coordinates. If two independent physical rails are supplied, their
quadratic ledger adds while their two zero rest scalars still sum to zero. The
last phrase is not a claim that those scalars are licensed source charges.

The runner also contains the essential normalization trap:

```text
Psi_A + Psi_B                 has twice the quadratic coordinate,
(Psi_A + Psi_B) / sqrt(2)     has the one-carrier coordinate.
```

The normalized superposition is still one carrier. Genuine two-carrier
additivity requires a tensor, direct-sum, or Fock-sector construction with an
additive lifted generator. Cycles 215 and 219 do not yet supply that sector.
Accordingly, the two-rail calculation is a conditional physical-carrier
fixture, not a derivation of record cost.

## Prior art and attribution boundary

That the full stress-energy tensor rather than rest mass alone appears on the
source side of general relativity is standard prior physics, beginning with
Einstein's field equations. Energy density, momentum flux, pressure, stress,
and the rest of the closed physical system matter to that account:

<https://doi.org/10.1002/andp.19163540702>

Tolman's classic analysis of the weight of heat is a direct early treatment
of non-rest energy in gravitating systems:

<https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.35.904>

Tolman, Ehrenfest, and Podolsky treat the gravitational field produced by
light directly:

<https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.37.602>

These sources do not license the slogan “energy alone gravitates” with the
carrier stresses and containing system deleted. A closed composite containing
radiation may itself have nonzero invariant mass. The exact contrast tested
here is the repository's restricted rest coordinate versus a complete
physical carrier ledger.

The distinction between logical irreversibility and physical dissipation is
also prior work. Landauer treats the physical cost of irreversible logical
operations, and Bennett gives a reversible-computation construction:

<https://doi.org/10.1147/rd.53.0183>

<https://doi.org/10.1147/rd.176.0525>

Those results do not say that every logical copy carries a fixed energy.
Cycle 227 deliberately avoids that inference. A settled classical or
orthogonal outcome can be copied reversibly into a blank register; an
arbitrary unknown quantum state cannot be cloned:

<https://doi.org/10.1038/299802a0>

Quasienergy for periodic dynamics is defined only modulo the drive frequency;
the general Floquet warning predates this framework:

<https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.138.B979>

For causal discrete-time dynamics, taking a logarithm of the one-step unitary
also need not yield a local generator; locality requires its own proof:

<https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2022-06-29-748>

Within this repository, Cycles 213, 216, and 219 already state that the source
map, energy interpretation, and stress-energy completion remain supplied or
open. Cycle 226 already distinguishes retained export from recyclable
