## 4. Five exact repeated-process extensions

For a fixed protocol, write its pointer distribution as `p`.

### Product route

```text
mu_product(o_0,...,o_(N-1)) = product_n p(o_n).
```

This is the strong predictive-reset/IID route. Every finite restriction is
normalized and projectively consistent.

### Sticky Markov route

On the positive support of `p`, use

```text
P(i,j) = (1/2) delta(i,j) + (1/2) p(j).
```

The chain is stationary with distribution `p`, irreducible and aperiodic on
every nontrivial support, and correlated. It therefore has the same long-run
frequency vector without independent trials.

### Frozen route

Choose one outcome `o` with law `p`, then repeat it forever:

```text
o,o,o,o,...
```

Every block position has marginal `p`. The process is stationary and
projectively consistent. Yet each invariant component has empirical
frequency `delta_o`, not `p`, whenever `p` is nontrivial.

This is the exact residual-matching control: same one-shot quantum weights,
same visible block skeleton, different long-run frequencies.

### Deterministic balanced route

Because every Cycle-189 weight is a multiple of `1/4`, construct a period of
length at most four containing each outcome with its required multiplicity.
For example:

```text
p=(1/2,1/2)       -> period (a,b)
p=(1/4,...,1/4)   -> period (a,b,c,d).
```

Every cyclic phase has exactly the target empirical distribution over one
period and converges pointwise to it. A uniform phase distribution makes the
process stationary, but the frequency result itself holds for every phase.

This is the deterministic balanced route. It proves that exact rational
frequencies alone do not force stochastic sampling or IID.

It does not reproduce the product law's higher-order correlations. Repeated
blocks expose its period immediately.

### Equal-mean nonergodic mixture

Mix the product process and the balanced process with equal weight. The
result is nonergodic because the component label is permanent, but every
component has the same frequency vector `p`.

This is the control that proves global ergodicity is stronger than necessary.
