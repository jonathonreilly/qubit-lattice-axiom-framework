## Result in Plain Language

The strongest deterministic escape partly works.

If the exact law and boundary have one global continuation, no random draw is
needed to decide what happens. If the resulting trial process is uniquely
ergodic and the record decoder is fixed, long-run frequencies can be theorems
of that deterministic process. A future TOE is allowed to close actuality and
probability this way. Nothing in this probe rules it out.

But the words *deterministic*, *unique*, and *ergodic* do not provide that
content for free:

1. Permanent distinguishable records split the dynamical state space into
   noncommunicating history sectors. Whenever two stable record archives are
   lawful, each supports an invariant measure, so the full state space is not
   uniquely ergodic.
2. An exact deterministic translation-covariant law starting from the fully
   homogeneous all-open state inherits that symmetry at every step. It may
   write every site or no site, but it cannot create one sparse nonhomogeneous
   classical archive without a boundary, perturbation, nonunique choice,
   coherent-to-record step, or global asymmetry.
3. A unique continuation can remove sampling semantics, but its exact map
   still says which outcome occurs. Two equally single-valued permanent-record
   laws can choose opposite outcomes.
4. A deterministic local Bell completion with one setting-independent hidden
   distribution obeys `|CHSH| <= 2`. Exact Tsirelson frequencies can be
   reproduced deterministically only by locating the missing content in
   boundary correlation/measurement dependence, a nonlocal or
   joint-context response, or a global consistency rule.

The deterministic route can therefore compress the constitutional burden. It
can eliminate a separate sampling sentence and can derive a frequency measure
inside one selected component. It does not reduce below an exact-law reference
unless a theorem also derives the exact map, its lawful component, physical
decoder, trial corpus, Bell causal semantics, and boundary contract from the
existing framework.

The simplest summary is:

> Unique extension is not unique ergodicity. Unique ergodicity on one
> irreducible boundary component is not selection of that component. And
> neither adjective tells us which deterministic law nature uses.

## Definitions Kept Separate

**Unique extension** means that one supplied current state or boundary has one
lawful continuation under the supplied law.

**Temporal unique ergodicity** means that the declared dynamical state space
has one invariant probability measure under time evolution.

**Spatial unique ergodicity** means that a declared translation-invariant
configuration class has one shift-invariant measure. This can derive spatial
frequencies inside that class even when no physical time dynamics has been
given.

**Actuality** means the one complete record history that is realized, not only
its limiting frequencies.

These notions can coincide in a special construction. They are not synonyms.

## Theorem 1: Permanent-Record Sectors

Let `X` be a compact lawful state space of site-tagged records and open sites,
and let `F:X->X` be a continuous deterministic update. Suppose a record of
content `b` at site `x` is permanent:

```text
C(x)=b  implies  F(C)(x)=b.
```

Then the cylinder sector

```text
X_(x,b) = {C in X : C(x)=b}
```

is closed and forward invariant. If two distinct contents `b != c` give
nonempty sectors, each sector supports at least one temporally invariant
probability measure: take time averages of any point in the compact sector and
pass to a weak-* accumulation point. The two measures are distinct because
their supports lie in disjoint record cylinders.

The stable-archive version is even simpler. If `C_b` and `C_c` are distinct
fixed record configurations, then `delta_(C_b)` and `delta_(C_c)` are distinct
invariant measures, as is every convex mixture. If both archives are
translation invariant, these measures are also spatially invariant.

The finite runner uses alphabet `{open,0,1}` on two sites. An append-only law
fills every open site with `0` and preserves every existing record. All four
fully recorded configurations are fixed, and the left-invariant eigenspace of
the nine-state transition matrix has dimension four. The all-zero and all-one
Dirac measures and five exact convex mixtures are checked directly.

This theorem does not say every permanent-record law has multiple invariant
measures. Its conditions can be defeated by declaring only one sector lawful,
quotienting sectors that are operationally identical, or using a noncompact or
noncontinuous global dynamics. Those are substantive domain/physics moves,
not consequences of the word permanent.

## Theorem 2: Deterministic Symmetry Inheritance

Let a group `G` act on configurations, let `F` be deterministic and
equivariant,

```text
F(g C) = g F(C),
```

and let the initial state `C_0` be fixed by every `g` in `G`. Then every
iterate is fixed:

```text
g F^n(C_0) = F^n(g C_0) = F^n(C_0).
```

For translations on a transitive lattice with only one site value at each
site, a fixed classical configuration is spatially uniform. Starting from the
all-open record configuration, a deterministic homogeneous law can therefore
produce an all-site burst or remain open, but it cannot select a sparse or
oriented archive.

Nearest-neighbor locality is stronger than needed for this obstruction. The
runner nevertheless checks all 27 possible responses of a three-symbol
radius-one rule on the three uniform neighborhoods. Every eight-step orbit on
a seven-site torus remains uniform. A coordinate-parity writer makes a sparse
checkerboard, but shifting the output exposes its failure of translation
covariance.

This is deterministic symmetry inheritance, not a no-go for spontaneous
symmetry breaking in sampled histories, coherent states followed by a record
rule, asymmetric physical boundaries, or global consistency solutions.

## Theorem 3: Unique Continuation Still Contains the Choice

Consider the same append-only architecture on an all-open finite region.

```text
F_0: every open site writes 0; preserve records.
F_1: every open site writes 1; preserve records.
```

Both laws are deterministic, translation covariant, site-local, permanent,
and uniquely extend the all-open state. Their unique records are opposite.
Single-valuedness removes a separate sample coordinate, but the subscript on
`F` is the outcome-selection content.

The boundary version uses one homogeneous nearest-neighbor copy rule. Each new
oriented layer copies the records in its predecessor layer. Boundary pattern
`001` has permanent frequency `1/3`; boundary pattern `011` has frequency
`2/3`. Every continuation is unique and the local rule is identical. The
frequency is in the boundary.

This is the exact place where a deterministic TOE can legitimately put the
answer: in a simple law, a simple boundary, or a theorem selecting both. The
point is not that this is illicit. The point is that it must be named as
physical content rather than credited to determinism itself.

## Unique Ergodicity: The Strongest Positive Route

The finite controls separate the positive theorem from its placement cost.

- Restrict `F_0` to the component reachable from all-open: `{all-open,
  all-zero}`. That two-state component has one invariant measure,
  `delta_(all-zero)`.
- Restrict `F_1` to its corresponding component. It too has one invariant
  measure, now `delta_(all-one)`.
- The union of both lawful archive sectors is not uniquely ergodic.

Spatially, the three translates of periodic boundary `001` form one
irreducible shift cycle with unique measure and frequency `1/3`. The three
translates of `011` form another with frequency `2/3`. Each component is
uniquely ergodic. Their union has two invariant measures. Selecting one
irreducible boundary component selects the frequency.

A six-state deterministic cycle gives the cleanest positive control. It has
one invariant measure, uniform on six phases. But a decoder that calls two
phases “outcome 1” gives frequency `1/3`, while a decoder that calls four
phases “outcome 1” gives `2/3` for the same dynamics. The actual phase changes
short transcripts while leaving complete-cycle frequencies unchanged.

The infinite steelman is stronger. An irrational circle rotation is uniquely
ergodic, and an interval decoder has limiting visit frequency equal to its
interval length. Such a law can derive irrational frequencies without a
stochastic draw. The rotation angle, observable partition, reset/trial
identification, and map from visits to permanent records are still exact law
content. A recurrent circle state is not itself an append-only archive.

Thus unique ergodicity can retire a separately postulated frequency measure
after the physical component and decoder are fixed. It cannot by itself select
the component, decoder, or actual finite record history.

## Bell-Capable Determinism: Exact Location of the Price

For a local deterministic hidden state `lambda`, write

```text
A_x(lambda), B_y(lambda) in {-1,+1}.
```

If the same distribution `rho(lambda)` is used independently of settings
`x,y`, every deterministic assignment obeys

```text
A_0 B_0 + A_0 B_1 + A_1 B_0 - A_1 B_1 = +/- 2.
```

Convex averaging gives `|CHSH|<=2`. The runner enumerates all sixteen
assignments. The exact relational Bell table carried by CFSI-QB16 has
`CHSH=2 sqrt(2)` and is no-signalling, so a local deterministic continuation
must relax some premise.

The runner constructs the three relevant placements exactly:

1. **Measurement independence retained, locality retained.** The CHSH ceiling
   holds; the target is not reproduced.
2. **Local responses retained, measurement independence relaxed.** For each
   joint setting, a context-correlated boundary distribution over local
   deterministic assignments reproduces every target probability. The hidden
   state distribution now depends on `(x,y)`. This is boundary correlation,
   sometimes called a measurement-dependent or superdeterministic placement.
3. **One setting-independent distribution retained, joint-context response
   allowed.** A global deterministic table carries a separate outcome pair for
   each joint context. Its fixed product distribution reproduces every target
   probability, but Alice's response can depend on Bob's context. The content
   is nonlocal/global consistency rather than a local hidden-variable model.

A block-universe or two-boundary model may describe the third placement
without superluminal causal influence. That interpretive move does not change
the mathematical location of the content: counterfactual local responses are
not one setting-independent joint assignment, or the settings are correlated
with the boundary.

This probe does not assume measurement independence is constitutionally
mandatory. It requires the final exact law to state which causal placement it
uses. Hiding boundary correlation under “one unique history” would make the
Bell frequencies true by boundary construction rather than derive them from
local record formation.

## Consequence for Axiom Language

Deterministic uniqueness gives no reason to add “sample,” “reader,” “second
witness,” “clock,” or “unique ergodicity” directly to Record. Those are
candidate mechanisms or theorems of an exact law.

The bare-metal sentence that survives all tested deterministic and stochastic
routes is a reference-shaped one: records are written by the one physical
formation law. The sentence should not pretend to state that law's extensional
content. A complete law dossier must expose its eligibility domain,
outcome/update map or instrument, concurrency rule, record-preserving scope,
decoder, and boundary type.

If a future theorem derives one deterministic law, one component, one decoder,
and one global history from the four axioms, even that exact-law reference may
be redundant. Nothing here proves such a derivation impossible. On the current
surface, however, adding only “the continuation is unique” would not advance
the science: the law qualification already requires one answer on a supplied
domain, while the answer and domain remain unknown.

The deterministic route therefore reduces constitutional content only this
far:

- no separate sampling or stochastic-actuality clause is needed if global
  continuation is unique;
- no separately imposed frequency measure is needed if the physical record
  process is proved uniquely ergodic with a fixed decoder and corpus; but
- the exact-law reference and actual state/boundary contract remain unless a
  uniqueness theorem derives them.
