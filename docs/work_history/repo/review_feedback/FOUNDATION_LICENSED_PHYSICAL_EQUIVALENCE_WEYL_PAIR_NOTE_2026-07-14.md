## Result Up Front

The current foundation does **not** supply one universal physical-equivalence
group. It supplies one exact spatial covariance group,

```text
G_found = Z^3 semidirect O_cubic^+,
```

where `O_cubic^+` is the 24-element proper cubic rotation group. It also makes
the presentation of a one-site `M_2(C)` algebra nonprivileged. The narrow safe
re-presentation is a common complex-linear star-automorphism—an inner
`PU(2)` conjugation—applied to the complete rule, record content, and decoder.
Neither statement licenses arbitrary site-dependent quantum frames,
reflections, antiunitary conjugations, boundary changes, or a quotient over
experimental protocols.

Equivalently: the foundation does not supply a universal physical equivalence
group beyond those scoped covariance and presentation statements.

Physical equivalence of two completed laws is therefore a **groupoid-level
conditional**, not another hidden axiom: an arrow must transport the law,
state/boundary, preparation, intervention, record content, and readout decoder,
and the admitted protocol category must remain closed under that transport.
Moving only the law or only the labels is not enough.

Applied to the classified Weyl pair:

1. The staggered character

   ```text
   chi(x)=(-i)^(x+y+z)
   ```

   still gives the exact one-particle conjugacy `U_+=G_chi U_- G_chi^dagger`.
   Its adjoint action is **origin-independent but frame-dependent**. Changing
   the origin multiplies `G_chi` by one global scalar, which cancels in
   conjugation. But the proper cubic group moves `chi` through eight distinct
   fourth-root characters on the full cardinal lattice. The bare lattice
   canonically supplies checkerboard parity up to its irrelevant overall sign;
   `chi^2=parity`, but it does not select one of parity's eight fourth-root
   frames.

2. `G_chi` is not a nontrivial onsite `M_2(C)` automorphism. At each site it is
   a central scalar, so its adjoint action on every onsite matrix and every
   local record content is the identity. It becomes nontrivial only on
   coherent intersite transport. A one-particle line bundle, hopping law, and
   relational phase-reference category are therefore required before `chi`
   can be a physical gauge arrow.

3. Reflections are graph automorphisms but are deliberately absent from the
   named spatial covariance group. Common complex-linear `M_2(C)`
   automorphisms also preserve the Pauli orientation. Consequently the two
   Weyl chirality signs lie in different orbits of the safely licensed
   spatial/common-internal presentation transformations. Proper-only
   covariance makes chirality **not gauge-identified**; it does not by itself
   make chirality a readable record observable, because the kinetic law,
   coherent context, formation rule, and decoder remain downstream.

4. A fixed coherent two-endpoint protocol still separates the walks with
   exact probabilities `1/4` and `0`. If its effect and phase reference are
   co-transformed by `G_chi`, the probabilities agree exactly. Thus `chi`
   yields a valid conditional protocol equivalence and an invalid unconditional
   same-protocol equivalence.

The smallest stable Weyl referent that survives the safe foundation quotient
is therefore not one class. It is **two proper-chiral presentation orbits**,
conditional on the coherent one-particle extension and including their proper-
cubic presentation orbit. Even that pair is not yet a complete admissible law:
a single fixed-order two-component walk fails exact full proper-cubic
covariance, and neither orbit supplies records. The exact residual is a
generated full-cubic law plus a derived physical protocol/boundary category.

This is a `partial-attempt-with-named-untested-routes`. A universal claim that
the pair must remain physically distinct is not shipped; a future relational
record theorem could make every legal observable commute with `G_chi` and
collapse the pair. The result does not establish that a new axiom is required.

### Covariance is not quotienting the realized world

The Admissibility law is required to be covariant under the named spatial
group. That says a co-transformed local question receives the co-transformed
answer. It does not say a realized record configuration is equal to its
translate or rotation. A realized state may contain asymmetric records; the
realized-state primitive permits pointwise evaluation there without declaring

Similarly, physical equivalence of presentations does not permit holding a
detector address, record decoder, or boundary fixed while transforming only

## 2. Exact Spatial Covariance Group

The proper automorphisms of the cardinal cubic neighbor set are the signed
permutation matrices with determinant `+1`. There are 24. With translations,
they form

```text
G_found = Z^3 semidirect O_cubic^+.
```

The other 24 signed permutations have determinant `-1`. They preserve the
undirected cubic graph but are not named in the axiom. “Graph automorphism” is
therefore broader than “foundation-supplied covariance.” This matters for
chirality: a mirror can exchange handed laws, while every supplied spatial
rotation preserves orientation.

Rotations “about each site” do not select a preferred origin. They generate
the same orientation-preserving cubic space-group action when combined with
standard translations.

The foundation names this action on sites but does not select a homomorphism
from `O_cubic^+` into onsite `PU(2)`. The usual Pauli/Spin lift is an exact
downstream algebraic construction. In the safe comparisons below, “co-
transform the internal presentation” means transport whichever representation
a candidate law explicitly supplies; it does not smuggle a spin law into the
axioms.

## 3. The One-Site Algebra: What Is A Safe Recoding?

Every complex-linear star-automorphism of `M_2(C)` is inner:

```text
A -> U A U^dagger,
```

with `U` unique up to a central scalar, so the effective presentation group is
`PU(2)`. Landsman's operator-algebraic Wigner analysis gives the general
automorphism/anti-automorphism distinction
([arXiv:1911.06635](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.06635)); Molnár gives an
operator-algebraic unitary/antiunitary treatment
([math/9808033](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9808033)). The companion runner also
checks the two-dimensional Pauli case directly.

Conjugation by `U` rotates the Pauli triple by an `SO(3)` matrix. It preserves

```text
Tr(sigma_x sigma_y sigma_z)/(2i)=+1.
```

Complex conjugation maps `(sigma_x,sigma_y,sigma_z)` to
`(sigma_x,-sigma_y,sigma_z)` and reverses that sign. It is conjugate-linear on
the Hilbert carrier or, at algebra level, belongs to the anti-automorphism/
real-structure side. The supplied complex `M_2(C)` presentation does not name
it as a gauge transformation. The optional `Cl(3,0)`-compatible real
presentation “adds no further primitive structure,” so it cannot silently add
improper transformations excluded by the complex presentation.

### Common versus site-dependent frames

A single common `PU(2)` conjugation is the narrow safe re-presentation of the
unprivileged one-site algebra: transform every occurrence in the rule, record
content, and decoder. It changes notation, not the represented relations.

An arbitrary family `U_x` is different. It changes neighbor comparisons by
the relative frame `U_x^dagger U_y`. A particular gauge/connection law may
make those changes redundant, but the foundation does not supply that law.
The runner's two-site control shows the distinction exactly: common conjugation
preserves the isotropic Pauli relation

```text
sigma_x tensor sigma_x + sigma_y tensor sigma_y + sigma_z tensor sigma_z,
```

whereas conjugating only one endpoint changes it. This is a counterexample to
automatic local-frame gauge status, not a claim that local gauge theories
cannot be derived.

Schumacher and Werner formulate reversible QCA as translation-covariant local
automorphisms of the quasilocal algebra
([quant-ph/0405174](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0405174)). Their structure
theorem reinforces the distinction: a local algebra presentation and a
specific translation-invariant global automorphism are separate data.

## 4. Records And Recodings

The Record axiom says that readout is determined by record content alone. It
does not say every bijection of contents is a physical gauge symmetry.

Let `f` recode record contents. A pure presentation change transports all of

```text
availability, law, locked content, and decoder,
```

so the new decoder satisfies `I'(f(c))=I(c)`. Every scalar transcript is then
unchanged. If content is recoded while the decoder `I` is held fixed, a record
can change its readable value. That is a different protocol, not equivalence.

The same applies to outcome-name permutations. Renaming detector clicks and
renaming the transcript together is harmless. Permuting physical effects while
leaving record names or numeric values attached to the old effects can change
data. The existing matter-counting probe makes the adjacent distinction: a
presentation-neutral quotient cannot decide whether two operationally distinct
effects are one event or two.

Thus record recoding is best represented as an arrow between complete
presentations, not as an unconditional subgroup acting on fixed records.

## 5. Why Physical Equivalence Is A Groupoid Here

A completed predictive object would contain at least

```text
(domain, state/boundary, law, contexts, record decoder, actuality/statistics).
```

A transformation is a physical-equivalence arrow only if it maps every field
to another admitted object and preserves every finite readable transcript.
Composition of compatible arrows is defined, but not every transformation acts
on every boundary or context category. This is a groupoid rather than one
universal group.

For a quantum protocol, the familiar sufficient condition is simultaneous
unitary transport:

```text
rho' = G rho G^dagger,
M'_a(X)=G M_a(G^dagger X G)G^dagger,
E' = G E G^dagger.
```

Cyclicity of trace gives the same probability for every finite outcome string.
If `E`, a phase standard, or the boundary is held fixed, the proof no longer
applies. Reference-frame work makes this physical rather than semantic:
Bartlett, Rudolph, and Spekkens analyze how lacking shared phase or Cartesian
frames restricts operations and promotes relational encodings
([quant-ph/0610030](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0610030)). The foundation
does not state which such restriction Nature's records obey. Primary
resource/reference-system constructions make the same conditional point:
Kitaev, Mayers, and Preskill show how a suitable shared reference system can
simulate operations otherwise restricted by a compact-group superselection
rule ([quant-ph/0310088](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0310088)), while Gour
and Spekkens formulate lack of phase, chirality, or spatial-orientation frames
as distinct resource theories
([arXiv:0711.0043](https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.0043)). None selects which
restriction applies to this framework.

## 6. The Staggered Character Without Coordinates

Write characters of the cardinal translation group as

```text
chi_q(x)=i^(q dot x),  q in (Z/4Z)^3.
```

The Weyl-pair character is `q=(-1,-1,-1)=(3,3,3) mod 4`:

```text
chi(x)=(-i)^(x+y+z).
```

### Origin

Changing the origin from `0` to `o` gives

```text
chi_o(x)=chi(x-o)=chi(-o) chi(x).
```

Therefore `G_chi,o=chi(-o)G_chi,0`. The two operators differ by one
global phase, and their adjoint actions are identical. No privileged origin is
needed to define `Ad_G_chi` once the character itself is chosen.

### Frame

The character itself is not selected by the unframed proper cubic lattice.
Acting with the 24 proper rotations sends `q=(3,3,3)` through all eight
vectors with entries in `{1,3}`. Its stabilizer has order three. Exhausting all
64 fourth-root characters shows that the only characters fixed by the entire
proper cubic group are

```text
q=(0,0,0)       trivial,
q=(2,2,2)       (-1)^(x+y+z), checkerboard parity.
```

Hence

```text
chi^2=parity,
```

but the lattice does not select a fourth root of parity. The missing datum is
not origin; it is one cube-corner/body-diagonal phase frame, equivalently a
tetrahedral coloring plus the orientation of the four-phase cycle.

An ordered cardinal split-step program can carry such a frame. A chiral
boundary or phase-reference record can also carry it. An exact law could
derive it dynamically. None is currently supplied, so it is inaccurate both
to call `chi` constitutionally canonical and to say it can arise only from a
boundary. The precise classification is: **additional law, program, or
boundary/frame data are needed**.

### BCC component quotient

The body-diagonal generators span an index-four sublattice of the cardinal
lattice. Four cardinal characters are trivial on that sublattice:

```text
(0,0,0), (0,2,2), (2,0,2), (2,2,0).
```

Modulo this annihilator, the eight cardinal roots reduce to two character
classes on one BCC component. On different BCC components the discarded
characters are component-dependent constants. They remain invisible if
components are superselected, but a cardinal substep or coherent cross-
component protocol can resolve them. Thus “two BCC roots” is a conditional
sector statement, not a foundation-wide quotient.

### Boundary

On a periodic cardinal torus of side `L`, `chi` descends only if `4` divides
`L`; otherwise conjugacy maps periodic to twisted boundary conditions.
Checkerboard parity needs only even `L`. Infinite and open lattices avoid this
finite-period obstruction, but a fixed external phase reference can still
distinguish the transformed protocol.

## 7. Applying The Safe Transformations To The Weyl Pair

D'Ariano, Erba, and Perinotti's complete `s=2` isotropic-walk classification
finds two three-dimensional Weyl walks on the BCC Cayley graph
([arXiv:1708.00826](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.00826)). D'Ariano and Perinotti
give their exact matrices, momentum translations, parity relationships,
dispersion, and Dirac coupling
([arXiv:1306.1934](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.1934)). Bisio, D'Ariano, and
Perinotti characterize nonlinear inertial-frame symmetries of a supplied Weyl
walk ([arXiv:1707.08455](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.08455)). These are
symmetries of the completed walk equations under their definitions, not an
automatic enlargement of this framework's foundation group.

For the published transition matrices,

```text
A^+_h= i A^-_h,      A^+_-h=-i A^-_-h,
U_+=G_chi U_- G_chi^dagger.
```

At each site `G_chi` is a scalar, so

```text
Ad_(chi(x) I_2)(A_x)=A_x
```

for every onsite matrix. On a hopping/link operator from `x` to `y`, however,
the phase is `chi(y)chi(x)^*`. The map is therefore absent from the bare
onsite-record ontology and meaningful on the extra relational transport
surface.

### Fixed protocol versus transformed protocol

For a localized `|+x>` coin input, retain endpoints `h1` and `-h1`. The exact
endpoint states obey

```text
psi_+=G_chi psi_-.
```

If a coherent effect is also mapped as `E_+=G_chi E_- G_chi^dagger`, both
branches give the same probability. A fixed effect

```text
|E>=(|h1,+x>+i|-h1,-x>)/sqrt(2)
```

instead gives `1/4` on one walk and `0` on the other. Both calculations are
exact. The first proves conditional protocol equivalence; the second proves
that the foundation cannot infer the necessary context quotient merely from
unitary conjugacy.

## 8. Proper-Only Covariance And Chirality

At a selected Weyl node let

```text
D_i=i partial_i W(k)|_(k=0),
C=Tr(D_x D_y D_z)/(2i).
```

The two ordered walks give `C=+1` and `C=-1`. Under a proper spatial rotation,
the spatial derivative frame has determinant `+1`. Under a common
complex-linear `M_2(C)` automorphism, the Pauli frame also has determinant
`+1`. Therefore `C` is invariant under every safe spatial/common-internal
presentation transformation. A reflection or complex conjugation reverses it,
but neither is foundation supplied.

This warrants the narrow statement:

> Chirality is a stable label of the two proper-covariance presentation orbits.

It does **not** warrant “the axioms measure chirality.” The minimal ontology has
no `W(k)`, momentum node, derivative frame, intersite phase reference, or
chirality decoder. Once such a kinetic/context extension is supplied, the
fixed coherent protocol makes the sign operational. Before that, chirality is
candidate law content rather than a formed record value.

There is one more exact obstacle. A fixed ordered two-component split-step
walk has only the source's smaller exact isotropy and fails a generic exact
90-degree cubic covariance test. The safe proper-cubic presentation orbit is
larger than one representative; treating that orbit as a set-valued law would
conflict with the axiom that one fixed rule gives one answer. A generated
schedule, enlarged carrier, or another exact full-cubic construction is still
required.

## 12. Constitutional Readout

The result supports no immediate axiom sentence. In particular:

- “up to unitary equivalence” is too broad unless it identifies which
  unitaries act on which physical carrier and transports records/boundaries;
- “up to lattice symmetry” must mean the supplied proper group, not all graph
  automorphisms;
- “no possibility is privileged” does not turn an arbitrary site-dependent

- “readout depends only on content” does not make all content bijections

- “the Weyl pair is one law” imports a frame/context/boundary quotient that has

A future exact-law axiom referent could be an equivalence class, but its dossier
must state the complete tuple and transcript-preserving arrows. If a relational
record theorem proves that all legal finite protocols are `G_chi`-covariant,
the pair can collapse without adding a chirality ruling. If a generated
coherent record protocol realizes the fixed two-endpoint separator, the two
proper-chiral orbits remain physically distinct. Either outcome is science;
the current foundation chooses neither.
