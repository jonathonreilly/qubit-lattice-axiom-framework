## Result Up Front

The continuous random priority from Cycle 18 is removable. The actual
compilation problem is harder and more specific.

At each possible `B0` site, draw one Bernoulli candidate bit. Accept that site
only if its bit is one and every other bit in its radius-nine Chebyshev cube is
zero. This **isolated-Bernoulli factor** is translation/proper-cubic covariant,
schedule-independent as a static function, positive density, collision safe,
and needs no continuous priority. Therefore **continuous priority is not
necessary** for a homogeneous collision-safe seed field.

But this factor is still a **static global-history kernel**, not a fully
**compiled event law** on the current record substrate. Its decision at the
origin depends on the candidate bit at `(9,9,9)`, which is 27 nearest-neighbor
edges away. Consequently the exact **nearest-neighbor causal-depth floor is
27** for any radius-one causal implementation of this same decision. A
proper-cubic monotone message DAG can reach that bound and gives a **typed
causal schedule quotient**: all topological event orders converge to the same
Boolean answer.

That is a conditional causal compile, not yet the requested bare-metal one.
It needs a **closed candidate layer** whose absences are final, transient
mutable messages or extra tracks, and a way to dispose of computation debris.
Those resources are not permanent records. If candidate and message states are
made records, permanence prevents a provisional candidate from becoming a
loser or a `B0`, and any losing record on one of the future role sites blocks
the exact Cycle 17 continuation. This is the **record-only clean-output
obstruction**.

The local state bill is visible. The displayed flood compiler must distinguish
at least candidate/not-candidate and rival-seen/not-seen, four exact classical
control cases. **One `M2` per site** provides at most two mutually orthogonal
pure labels. Spatial encodings, nonunitary environments, or nonorthogonal
control states may evade that particular count, but each adds a physical
carrier or instrument that must be stated and tested. One permanent record per
site cannot be reused for 27 causal layers.

Finite priorities, random sequential adsorption, and quantum random bits do
not erase the boundary; they move it:

- finite labels with strict-tie rejection are safe and priority-oracle-free,
  but fail maximality whenever equal labels occur;
- lazy infinite random bit strings recover unique priorities almost surely,
  but are a continuous priority in binary clothing and have no uniform finite
  stopping depth;
- random sequential adsorption is safe and maximal after a causal order is
  supplied, but distinct orders give different seed sets;
- a measured qubit supplies one random bit only after preparation and an
  instrument are supplied; a closed unitary produces coherence, not an actual
  classical sample; and
- finite-speed NN exclusion signals lose with positive probability to a still-
  enabled competing birth at every finite signal/birth-rate ratio.

The exact outcome is therefore a **partial compile with named residuals**:

1. the continuous-priority oracle is retired;
2. the minimum NN causal depth for the exact cube decision is derived;
3. a schedule-independent typed causal DAG is explicit;
4. append-only record-only realization on one local qubit is not compiled;
5. a schedule-finalization or mutable-workspace mechanism remains; and
6. rate and actuality remain separate.

This is not a universal QCA or dissipative no-go. A translation-invariant
dissipative blockade, a reversible QCA that exports garbage, or a spatially
encoded asynchronous MIS protocol may still close the gap. No such exact
one-qubit, all-open, clean-output construction was produced here.

For the executable classification contract: **the static continuous-priority
oracle is retired**; **the exact radius-nine factor is not yet a

## Static Finite-Bit Replacement

Let `C_x` be IID Bernoulli with activity `p`. Define

```text
S_infinity(C)
  = {x : C_x=1 and C_y=0 for every 0<|y-x|_infinity<=9}.
```

No two accepted sites conflict, because each would see the other's one bit.
The map is a translation/proper-cubic-equivariant finite-radius factor. It has
no tie and no priority. Its exact intensity is

```text
rho_infinity(p) = p (1-p)^6858,
```

because the radius-nine cube has `19^3=6859` sites. Differentiation gives

```text
rho_infinity'(p)
  = (1-p)^6857 (1-6859 p),
```

so the maximum occurs at `p=1/6859`. The density is lower than the continuous-
priority Matérn value `1/6859`, but it is strictly positive. On infinite `Z3`,
events centered on `19 Z3` have disjoint input cubes, so positive density again
implies infinitely many accepted seeds almost surely.

This construction shows exactly what continuous priority bought in Cycle 18:
higher retention and local maximality, not the existence of a safe stationary
field.

### A directly NN-floodable conservative version

```text
S_1(C)
```

The radius-27 Manhattan ball contains

```text
M_1(27) = (4*27^3 + 6*27^2 + 8*27 + 3)/3 = 27775
```

## Exact NN Depth Lower Bound

Consider two candidate configurations:

```text
C^(0): C_0=1 and every other bit in B_infinity(9) is zero;
C^(1): same, except C_(9,9,9)=1.
```

The origin is accepted under `C^(0)` and rejected under `C^(1)`. A radius-one
causal circuit of depth `d` can depend only on the Manhattan ball `B_1(d)`.
For `d<27`, `(9,9,9)` is outside that lightcone, so the two inputs give the same
origin output. Contradiction.

Therefore:

> Any radius-one causal implementation of the same exact radius-nine isolated-
> candidate decision has depth at least 27.

This is a dependency/lightcone theorem. It does not say every possible hard-
core seed law needs 27 steps. A different law may impose stronger local
structure or use a supplied correlated environment.

## Conditional Typed-DAG Compile

There is an exact causal circuit at the dependency level.

1. Treat candidate bits as an immutable input antichain.
2. Give every message a displacement or remaining-budget type that transforms
   under proper cubic rotations.
3. Propagate each candidate fact along NN edges through causal depth 27.
4. At each potential source, compute whether any distinct source reaches its
   radius-nine decision domain.
5. Emit `B0` only for an isolated source; choose its frame from a covariant
   random branch.

The monotone propagation rule is a max/OR fixed point. Message deliveries may
occur in any topological order: idempotence and commutativity give the same
final reachability and isolated-source decision. The runner executes first,
last, and random asynchronous relaxation schedules and obtains one fixed
point. This is the **typed causal schedule quotient** promised by the target.

It uses causal depth as a certificate, not as elapsed physical time. There is
no external scalar clock in the abstract DAG. But two pieces remain supplied:

- the candidate antichain must be closed, so “no candidate arrived” is final;
- source-tagged mutable messages and their disposal must exist somewhere.

Without the first, an accepted source cannot know that a new candidate will
not appear later in its exclusion region. Without the second, the DAG is a
mathematical dependency graph rather than a one-`M2` event law.

## Why Absence Is the Hard Part

Presence can propagate: an existing record sends a causal descendant. Absence
cannot propagate unless a domain has closed. In the static factor, the entire
candidate field is one supplied probability-law sample, so the zeros are
already meaningful. In an ongoing event law, an open site can remain a future
candidate indefinitely.

Three escapes are distinct:

1. close a candidate epoch and run the 27-depth decision afterward;
2. use an RSA-style causal order in which earlier accepted seeds exclude later
   attempts; or
3. impose a timeless global history kernel whose whole seed set is sampled at
   once.

The first supplies an epoch boundary. The second supplies an order/schedule
and needs finite-speed reservation. The third is exactly the static history
law Cycle 19 is trying to compile. Renaming any of them “local read” does not
remove its physical content.

## Finite-State Priorities

Give every candidate one of `L=2^k` priority labels and accept only a strict
local minimum. With all sites candidates in an `M`-site comparison domain, the
exact winner density is

```text
rho_(L,M) = L^(-M) sum_(r=0)^(L-1) r^(M-1).
```

It approaches `1/M` as `L` grows. For every finite `k`, two conflicting sites
tie with probability `2^-k`. In a symmetric two-site tie configuration, an
equivariant deterministic rule cannot choose just one without more asymmetry.
Strict tie rejection is therefore the safe neutral branch: both lose. It
preserves hard core and positive density, but the output need not be maximal.

Lazy fair bit strings compare priorities lexicographically. Two candidates
remain tied after `k` bits with probability `2^-k`, so each finite conflict
neighborhood resolves almost surely. There is no deterministic finite bit
bound, and the infinite binary string is measure-isomorphic to a continuous
uniform priority. It retires a prewritten continuous oracle but not unbounded
randomness or causal work.

## Random Sequential Adsorption

The **random sequential adsorption** route orders attempts and greedily accepts
a candidate exactly when no earlier accepted candidate conflicts with it. It
is always hard-core and maximal on the attempted-candidate graph.

It is not schedule independent. On three collinear candidates at `0,9,18`,
accepting the middle first gives `{9}`; accepting the ends first gives
`{0,18}`. Both are maximal and safe. The schedule can be treated as typed
causal history, so the **RSA route** is a viable schedule quotient rather than
an inconsistency. Its probabilities and rate remain law data.

The NN compilation still faces a race. A seed at cube-corner displacement
needs a 27-hop exclusion signal. If a competing birth remains enabled with
finite rate `lambda` and each signal hop has finite rate `mu`, even the ideal
race in which every hop competes only against that birth succeeds with
probability

```text
[mu/(mu+lambda)]^27 < 1.
```

Thus a finite-rate signal does not guarantee exact exclusion. Exact safety
requires a handshake that prevents commit until conflict resolution, a closed
candidate phase, zero competing-birth rate during reservation, or a nonlocal
blockade. Each is real law content.

## One-`M2` Transient Capacity

The displayed direct compiler needs the local control cases

```text

(not candidate, rival signal),
(candidate, no rival),
```

Four perfectly distinguishable classical states require a carrier of Hilbert

pure states. Encoding four roles as nonorthogonal rank-one projectors does not

This is not a theorem that every distributed algorithm needs local dimension
four. Spatial encoding can spread control over several qubits; stochastic

So **one `M2` per site** is enough for the final `B0` label and every later
Cycle 17 site separately. It is not enough for the four perfectly
distinguishable transient states of this direct dense flooding compiler.

## Quantum Random-Bit Route

A prepared `|+>` qubit measured in a supplied binary instrument yields a fair
bit. Repeating gives finite or lazy-infinite priority strings. This is a valid
**quantum random-bit route** to replace a prewritten classical priority field.

It does not by itself close the event law:

- all-open is not a supplied `|+>` product preparation;
- unitary evolution preserves purity and yields a coherent superposition, not
  one actual classical bit field;
- measurement/instrument choice and branch weights must be supplied or
  derived;
- recording every candidate bit fills sites that the diamond later needs;
- leaving bits unrecorded makes them transient hidden/workspace state rather
  than framework records; and
- lazy priorities have no uniform finite causal depth.

The **QCA garbage-export route** remains live. A translation-invariant
reversible automaton might compute winners while exporting input and tie data
to degrees of freedom that never intersect a winning diamond. No exact
one-qubit construction from all-open is known here, and unitarity prevents
simply deleting the discarded candidate field.

The **dissipative blockade route** also remains live. A uniform local open-
system law could continuously suppress nearby births and relax toward a hard-
core stationary state. It supplies an environment, dissipative rates, and an
asymptotic or stopping semantics; exact radius-nine safety from only NN
couplings must still be proved.
