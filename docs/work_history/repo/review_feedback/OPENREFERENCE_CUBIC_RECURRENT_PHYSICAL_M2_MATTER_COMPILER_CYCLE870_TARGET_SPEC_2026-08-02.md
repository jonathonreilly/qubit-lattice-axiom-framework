# Cycle 870 target — OpenReference cubic recurrent physical-M2 matter compiler

**Frozen:** 2026-08-02

**Claim class:** bounded constructive theorem

**Authority:** none

**Audit:** unset

## Exact target

On finite open cubic boxes, construct one physical carrier/register map and:

1. an executable clean-domain encoder `E` from arbitrary `6N` logical matter
   input into the local OpenReference even-CAR code; and
2. a fixed, recurrent physical update `G_physical` for the complete supplied
   coin/mass, onsite reverse FSWAP, directed seam FSWAP, and onsite contact
   word,

such that

```text
G_physical E = E G_native
```

as an exact vector equation after the global scalar is included.  The same
literal carrier addresses must be used by `E` and `G_physical`.

## Required acceptance surface

- primary open `L=2` cube and held open `L=3` cube on the same placement law;
- arbitrary logical input, proved by exact signed-generator relations rather
  than a basis-only state sample;
- bounded carrier, persistent auxiliary, and transit-substrate censuses;
- returned nearest-neighbour routing with arbitrary transit state restored;
- local OpenReference constraints preserved by every update generator;
- one-particle Cycle-219 mass fixture and the Cycle-230 contact fixture;
- every onsite reverse and directed seam FSWAP;
- proper-cubic transport through 24 frames and all 576 ordered products;
- a volume-independent schedule for recurrent `G`, without host-volume
  enumeration;
- hostile layer-order, contact/seam deletion, route deletion, unlawful-domain,
  and spent-replay controls; and
- an independent reconstruction of `E`, of the update action, and of a fixed
  schedule.

## Allowed supplied structure

- finite open cubic boundary, spacing-16 origin, transported proper-cubic
  coframe, and incident-edge gauge;
- one clean encoder invocation with raw input, syndrome/work registers, and
  root epoch bits in `fresh=1, token=spent=0`;
- Cycle-703 check/decoder/loader and reversible echo-forest semantics;
- Cycle-219 `beta=-0.3` coin and Cycle-230 `g=0.37` contact; and
- fixed serial factor order for the logical update.

All supplied structure must be enumerated in the theorem note and receipts.

## Forbidden promotions

This target does not permit a global Jordan--Wigner string, global parity
service, host-selected parity answer, or host-volume scheduling pass.  Circuit
ordinals and schedule layers are not physical time.  The result must not be
promoted to autonomous genesis, physical occurrence, Record formation, Born
weighting, source/gravity response, a minimum-resource theorem, a no-go, or
axiom pressure.

The `18N+3E` encoded-carrier count is not the total physical resource.  Every
auxiliary and every substrate M2 touched by a returned route must be counted.

## Honest bounded outcome

The encoder may remain a single supplied clean-domain invocation.  If a local
spent-sector admission/reset law is not compiled, `G_physical` may still be
called recurrent on the encoded code space, but `E` may not be called an
autonomous recurrent preparation law.  Hostile spent replay must then be an
active control and the missing guard must remain explicit.

An independent checker may discover redundant listed gates without defeating
exactness.  In that case the package must distinguish exact execution,
deletion-class witnesses, and per-occurrence deletion completeness.
