## 4. Route A — explicit local stochastic seed/hazard law

```text

sum_j p_A(j | Psi) = 1.
```

When `q_j=0`, branch `j` has law weight zero and no normalized conditional
state is evaluated; the runner must test this domain branch explicitly.

The Born-matching hypothesis is `p_A(j | Psi)=q_j`. This is new candidate-law
content, not a derivation from the Cycle-478 grade identities. A local seed
word realizes the kernel and is retained with a spent receipt. Seed genesis
and seed distribution are deliberately supplied. The physical schedule may
not compute thresholds by a host grade/norm query; the candidate stochastic

calculation may compute `q` only outside the physical schedule to test the
hypothesis.

After the stochastic transition produces one provenance-bearing member, a
separate local occurrence gate raises `OccurrenceReceipt`. Only then may the
Cycle-505 singleton predicate write one admitted atom. Fresh independently
distributed seeds plus exact re-preparation would give a product repeated
law; both freshness and re-preparation are explicit premises, not consequences
of locality.

### Repeated-law layer

empirical confirmation. Exact transition matrices/cylinder weights, rather

must reuse the frozen decoder and likelihood/discrepancy statistics.

```text
D1 = max_psi ||p_r(.|psi)-q(.|psi)||_1
D2 = max_psi ||P_r(J_0...J_3)-product_n q(J_n|psi)||_TV
D3 = maximum invariant-component mean spread
D4 = lag-1...lag-16 admitted-outcome covariance / periodic spectrum

D6 = actual-member minus lawful-occurrence minus admitted-atom count residual
D7 = E/G, inverse-where-claimed, deletion, leakage, all24, mass, and resources
```

`D2` is a product-law discriminator, not a universal Born requirement: a
non-IID law can pass the Born frequency target through component-mean equality.
