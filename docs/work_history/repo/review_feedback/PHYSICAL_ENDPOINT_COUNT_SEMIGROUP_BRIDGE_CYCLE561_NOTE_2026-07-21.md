## Why the raw ratio fails composition

The raw chain ratio is

```text

```

It is the reference-extent-weighted mean of `r(A)` and `r(B)`, not their sum.
For a delay cell followed by an equal cell,

```text

r(A+B)=7/8 != 7/4=r(A)+r(B).
```

Thus Cycle 469's earlier `t=r` works on the original one-cell fixture but does
not define a concatenation homomorphism. This is a route-specific failure of
the raw-ratio parameterization, not an obstruction to causal time. The

## Pair-valued and signed routes

