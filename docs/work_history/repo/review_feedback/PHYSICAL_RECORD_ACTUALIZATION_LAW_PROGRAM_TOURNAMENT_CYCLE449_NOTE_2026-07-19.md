## Three explicit law-relative precommit hypotheses

Let `A_i` mean that all twelve Cycle-443 admission bits for bank `i` are one,
let `P_i` be its protected 79-bit word, let `L_0` be the first physical
predecessor-link triple, and let `m` be the local migration token.

### I — immediate site-tethered append

The reversible ready predicate is

`p_I AND A_0`.

When true, the block XOR-writes `P_0` into the immediate output bank and raises
its precommit-ready bit.  Under the separately supplied semantic law, the
candidate site remains the Record identity.  The fixed reversible write is not
itself that semantic transition.

### M — one-step migrating append

The reversible ready predicate is

`p_M AND A_0 AND L_0[0] AND L_0[1] AND L_0[2] AND m`.

When true, the block XOR-writes `P_0` into the migrating endpoint bank and
raises both a precommit-ready bit and a predecessor-history marker.  Under the
separately supplied semantic law, active identity moves one adjacent endpoint
while the predecessor link remains part of the immutable finite history.  The
token, endpoint interpretation, and law are explicit supplied structure.  This
is one bounded recoding step, not autonomous unlimited migration or renewal.

### T3 — threshold-three agreement append

The reversible ready predicate is

`p_T AND A_0 AND A_1 AND A_2 AND (P_0=P_1) AND (P_1=P_2)`.

When true, the block XOR-writes the common word into one threshold output bank
and raises one precommit-ready bit.  Under the separately supplied semantic
law, the three candidate banks identify one convergence-site occurrence.  The
number three and that identification remain candidate-law content.
