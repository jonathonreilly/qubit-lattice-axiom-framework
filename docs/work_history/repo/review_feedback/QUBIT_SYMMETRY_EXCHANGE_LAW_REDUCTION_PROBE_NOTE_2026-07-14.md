# Qubit Symmetry Exchange-Law Reduction Probe

**Date:** 2026-07-14

**Type:** meta

**Authority:** none. This is a conditional classification and candidate-law
reduction probe. It is not an axiom proposal, an audit verdict, or a claim that
the current phrase “no possibility is privileged” already supplies continuous
Hamiltonian dynamics or a particular covariance reading.

## Live Reduction Route

Suppose, in addition to the present one-site `M_2(C)` domain and cubic
nearest-neighbor graph, that microscopic between-record evolution is:

1. an autonomous, strongly continuous reversible generator;
2. a sum of identical nearest-neighbor two-site terms; and
3. covariant under a common change of qubit basis on the two sites.

Then Schur-Weyl duality becomes an exact simplifier. The commutant of the
diagonal `SU(2)` action `U tensor U` on two qubits is the span of identity and
SWAP. Hence every Hermitian pair generator has the form

```text
h_xy = a I + b SWAP
     = (a+b/2) I + (b/2)(X X + Y Y + Z Z).
```

The scalar identity changes only an overall phase on a fixed active graph and
sector. If record formation changes the active-edge count, it can become a
relative sector phase and must be handled in the full law. Conditional on the three
premises, the nontrivial interaction is therefore Heisenberg exchange, up to
sign and scale. This is a genuine reduction of an arbitrary pair Hamiltonian
to one physical coefficient.

## The Covariance Reading Is Load-Bearing

“No possibility is privileged” does not itself say whether local basis changes
act together or independently.

- Under **common-basis covariance**, the invariant algebra is the two-dimensional
  span of identity and SWAP, so relational alignment is physical.
- Under **independent onsite covariance**, the commutant is only the identity,
  so no nontrivial pair interaction survives without a connection, shared
  frame, link variable, or symmetry reduction.

The current Qubit prose supplies neither interpretation as a dynamical law.
Promoting the exchange result without choosing and defending the covariance
meaning would hide a new physical premise in a symmetry slogan.

## What The Reduction Does Not Select

Both `+SWAP` and `-SWAP` have the same locality and covariance. Their triplet
and singlet ground sectors are reversed: `+SWAP` has a one-dimensional
singlet ground space, while `-SWAP` has a three-dimensional triplet ground
space. An overall coefficient also fixes a
rate only after a time unit is supplied. Therefore sign and scale do not
follow from the classification.

“Generator” must mean autonomous/time-independent for the one-coefficient
reduction. A covariant family `h(t)=J(t) SWAP` retains an arbitrary coefficient
function. Strong continuity alone does not remove it.

On overlapping edges, pair SWAP terms do not commute. A Hamiltonian sum is
well-defined and graph-covariant, but a discrete product update still needs an
ordering/layering rule or a causal-invariance theorem. The assumption of a
continuous reversible generator is doing real work.

The continuous Hamiltonian and the finite-diamond event net also have
different exact causal semantics. Nested commutators on a three-site chain
reach the third site at second order in time. A finite-range Hamiltonian has a
Lieb-Robinson quasilocal cone with small tails, not the strict finite light cone
of a layered circuit/QCA. The exact law must choose one and later prove its
physical-speed/continuum interpretation.

At exchange angle `pi/4`, the unitary generated by SWAP maps `|01>` to a
maximally entangled state. With later local measurement contexts it is Bell
capable; the runner now computes four correlators and `CHSH=2 sqrt(2)` after
an explicit local basis change. Entanglement capability does not supply the contexts, the outcome
instrument, Born sampling, or a single actual result.

## Exact Record Boundary

A reversible automorphism on a fixed finite carrier cannot make a record flag
strictly grow for every input. If a projection `R` satisfied

```text
R <= U^dagger R U,
```

both sides have the same trace and rank, so equality is forced. The
infinite-qubit UHF analogue uses its faithful invariant trace and gives the
same conclusion for an automorphism.

Exchange may transport or entangle information, and it may preserve a pointer
sector already selected. Record formation still requires at least one of:

- a noninvertible sampled instrument;
- a larger unitary carrier with fresh pointer/environment factors;
- a special blank-future boundary sector that excludes the inverse histories;
  or
- a non-surjective/open evolution.

Thus the exchange reduction does not close record formation or actuality.
It also needs an exact record-status carrier: one `M_2` cannot autonomously
present three orthogonal readable values `OPEN/R0/R1`. The open/locked bit may
live in causal-front/history metadata or a separate pointer, but that typing
must be part of the sampled-record law.

## TOE-Lane Result

| lane | exact gain | still missing |
|---|---|---|
| operational/quantum | parameter-reduced entangling unitary | context, instrument, sampling, preparation link |
| record | can preserve declared invariant sectors | formation trigger, physical pointer/export, one actual branch |
| clock | a generator gives an order parameter | sign/scale, physical duration, universal lapse response |
| matter | Heisenberg excitations are available | relativistic continuum, chirality, fermion/gauge/species selection |
| resource | unitary dynamics conserves norm | energy identification, record-capacity renewal, thermodynamics |
| gravity | no direct gain | active stress, WEP, nonlinear geometry response |

In particular, chirality and gravity are not consequences of the two-site
classification. A cubic spin system may have interesting emergent phases, but
those phases and their continuum limits must be demonstrated rather than read
off from `M_2` and symmetry.

## Constitutional Consequence

This is a live reduction route worth retaining. If a deeper theorem derives
the continuous pair-generator premises and the common-basis covariance from
the exact admissibility law, much of the between-record dynamics could become
theorem content. Today those premises are extra physics, the sign and scale
remain open, and a reversible exchange law cannot create permanent records on
its fixed carrier.

It therefore does not replace the need for an exact full-law specification.
It does show how such a specification might be made substantially smaller:
use symmetry to derive the reversible working part, then state only the
irreducible sampled-record and boundary/export part.
