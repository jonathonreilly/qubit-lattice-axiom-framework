## Result

The second route remains the owner's current research priority, not a result
selected by the finite probes. Its correct bare-metal
object is not a fixed superselection rule and not a witness count. It is a
**record-generated refinement of physical continuation**:

```text
before formation: alternatives can still interfere or reconnect;
at formation:      their physical continuations separate;
after formation:   the separated continuations do not reconnect;
actual record:     one continuation contains the locked local possibility.
```

That architecture can make permanence exact without dephasing all unresolved
matter. It gives probability, time, arrow, causality, and gravity lanes a clean
event boundary. It does not itself choose a sector, weight sectors, set a
formation rate, define metric time, renew storage, or identify a gravitational
source.

The exact route-two sentence worth constitutional testing is:

> **When a record forms at a site, the site's admissible local possibilities
> separate into law-admissible continuations that do not reconnect.**

Its current candidate placement is immediately after `Records form.` inside
Record:

```text
Records form. When a record forms at a site, the site's admissible local
possibilities separate into law-admissible continuations that do not reconnect.

When present, a record locks exactly one admissible local possibility. A
site never carries more than one record; records are permanent.
```

This is a **target string, not an edit**. It is deliberately conditional on an
actual formation event. It therefore does not say that every eligible state
fires, does not supply a maximal rate, and preserves the 2026-07-04
occurrence-strength ruling.

The sentence is composite: it bundles a continuation relation, support for the
available menu, formation-indexed separation, and—on the strong physical
reading—a restricted post-record operation algebra. Once continuation and
support are separately supplied, formation-indexed nonreconnection is the
residual under test. The exercise does not prove that residual axiom-minimal.
If it derives from the predictive specification and its physical operation
algebra, it belongs in a theorem and no axiom addition is needed.

## Why This Is Not Yet Ready To Land

Four obligations remain before constitutional promotion:

1. **Continuation:** define a law-admissible continuation without importing an
   unmentioned global wavefunction, universal clock, or preferred foliation.
2. **Local generation:** derive the nonreconnecting sectors from the fixed
   nearest-neighbor Admissibility rule, or identify the operation-algebra
   restriction as new physics.
3. **Finite causal certificate:** show how a finite local formation event can
   establish the separation. Infinite-volume disjointness only at infinite
   time is not yet a laboratory record mechanism.
4. **Contextual scope:** continuations must be conditioned on the actual
   measurement context. A single joint completion assigning all incompatible
   settings would fail the Bell/CHSH discriminator.

The modal recognition sentence

> `A record forms when every admissible continuation of the current state fixes
> the same available local possibility at a site.`

is an excellent theorem target, but a riskier axiom. Unless continuation,
nestedness, locality, and contextual scope are independently derived, it hides
an arrow, branch entry, and possibly a global block-universe selector. Its
`when` can also be read as a firing law. The proposed constitutional target
above states what an actual formation does while leaving trigger and rate
downstream.

## Exercise Two — First-Principles Reduction

### Make the requirement less wrong

The requirement is not “find what causes collapse.” It is:

> Identify the boundary after which alternatives distinguished by record
> content cannot be connected by any law-admissible continuation, while
> unresolved alternatives remain coherent beforehand.

That separates four questions which must not be collapsed:

1. activation of nonreconnecting sectors;
2. which sector is realized;
3. how sectors are weighted across trials;
4. when activation occurs and at what rate.

### Delete nonessential content

The working formation sentence contains no witness count, read event, clock,
Born weight, possibility-counting rule, rate, thermodynamic cost,
compute/storage slogan, gravity law, fixed pointer basis, or universal
microscopic commit.

### Smallest exact algebraic model

For a record-content projector `P`, the full pre-record algebra `B(H)` contains
cross terms. The smallest post-record algebra is

```text
A_P = P B(H) P  direct-sum  (1-P) B(H) (1-P).
```

The conditional expectation

```text
E_P(X) = P X P + (1-P) X (1-P)
```

is idempotent, content-covariant, and compositional on disjoint records. It
does not say why it activates, which block is realized, how blocks are weighted,
or at what rate activation occurs. If imposed from the beginning, it destroys
pre-record interference. The load-bearing object is therefore a
**record-generated algebra refinement**, not a fixed superselection rule.

### Smallest combinatorial model

Make admissible configurations vertices and admissibility-preserving local
changes edges. Components are candidate sectors. An equality constraint gives
locally readable sectors; a pair-flip parity constraint gives global sectors
with no local decoder; unrestricted flips give no sector. The graph topology
and the physically allowed change class are independent ingredients because
the present Admissibility axiom is explicitly not dynamics.
