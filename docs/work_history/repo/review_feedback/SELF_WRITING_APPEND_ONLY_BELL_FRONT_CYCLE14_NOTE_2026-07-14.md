

It maps arbitrary pure, mixed, and entangled inputs on the data triple to


### Phase L/R — endpoint reads

independently ready. Append `Z0` or `Z1`. The reads commute. The right read at
`c` makes the next self-write ready even if the left read is delayed.

At every stage, an exact already-written assignment counts as done, compatible

## Dynamic Collision Rule and Countermodel

For each ready builder stage, compute its six NN target assignments. The local
collision semantics is:

1. exact already-written assignment: done;
2. every target open and no currently ready incompatible assignment: write;
3. an incompatible occupied target: blocked;
4. two simultaneously ready incompatible assignments: both wait/freeze.

Every status is reconstructible from nearby records and ready parent fronts.
No hidden collision bit is carried.

Take

```text

g2=((-2,-1,2), +y, +x).
```

Their first-builder assignments overlap at `(1,0,2)`. The first demands `B0`;
the second demands `B1`. The conflict and its transform under every lattice
symmetry are exact. The same rule applies at `D` and final-`H` stages.

If both are ready together, the rule freezes them. If `g1` writes before `g2`
becomes ready, `g2` later sees an incompatible permanent record and blocks.
If `g2` writes first, the other final record remains. Those histories differ.

No finite local front can, merely from current nearby records, know that an
incompatible front will arrive arbitrarily later from outside its causal
neighborhood. This example is not a general impossibility theorem, because a
braid/merge rule, global solution, conserved priority field, or same-content
monotone code might restore confluence. It shows only that the present
blocked-on-contact law is exact but not multi-front confluent. Multi-front
confluence remains open.
