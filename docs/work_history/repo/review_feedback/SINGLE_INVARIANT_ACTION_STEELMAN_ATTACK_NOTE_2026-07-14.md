## Result In Plain Language

No complete target-free winner was found. The reason is sharper than “there
are many possible actions.” Two exact positive constructions isolate the
problem.

First, a positive frustration-free history Hamiltonian can have **one unique
zero-energy history** for any supplied one-step unitary. Identity, bit flip,
phase flip, and Hadamard laws all have exactly the same complete action
spectrum and each has a unique minimizer after the same input boundary is
applied. Their final states differ. The update `U` appears inside the
propagation term. Unique least action therefore does not select `U`; it is a
universal wrapper capable of encoding whichever law was supplied.

Second, there is a formally perfect one-functional selector. On the quotient
of laws by every admitted operational protocol, define squared distance to the
target transcript law. If the protocol family separates physical laws and has
positive weights, the target equivalence class is the unique minimizer. This
functional is representation independent and complete. It also contains the
target transcript distribution, context repertoire, and protocol weights.
The runner deletes each target coordinate in turn; exactly one two-way physical
ambiguity returns. Replacing the target by its opposite makes the opposite law
equally uniquely selected. The law moved into the reference functional.

The most economical target-free actions do achieve real compression:

- common-`SU(2)` pair invariance reduces quadratic interaction to identity and
  SWAP;
- least disagreement fixes copy rather than oppose after a write is required;
- a Dirichlet extremum gives an exact finite cubic Green response after a
  source and zero-mode prescription are supplied;
- positive history constraints prove causal composition once their update
  table is supplied; and
- Gibbs or real-time weighting turns an exact action into a normalized measure
  or amplitude after its measure/temperature/quantization rule is supplied.

But the actual invariant action space already contains independent even
exchange, multi-site spectral, chiral, occurrence, source, nonlinear, and
species-coupling terms. Overall action/clock normalization removes at most one
scale. Their signs and dimensionless ratios change observable physics.

The bounded conclusion is not that a one-action TOE is impossible. It is:

> Writing all answers in one scalar expression is syntactic unity. A genuine
> one-principle derivation requires a theorem that fixes the functional,
> quotient, domain, coefficients, boundary, and history-to-record measure
> without using the target predictions as inputs. None of the tested extremal
> principles supplies that theorem.

The strongest surviving route is still one exact invariant action. It must be
discovered and shown unique; “least action,” “frustration free,” “maximal

## 3. Least Disagreement

The pure relational disagreement score on an equal-record trigger gives

```text
score(no write) = 0,
score(copy)     = 0,
score(oppose)   = 4J.
```

It resolves copy versus oppose for `J>0` but not write versus no-write. Add a
missed-trigger coefficient:

```text
score(no write) = 2 mu,
score(copy)     = 0,
score(oppose)   = 4J.
```

Now `mu>0` selects copy, `mu=0` ties copy and no-write, and `mu<0` selects no
write. The occurrence answer is the sign of an independent invariant term.

Calling `mu` a Lagrange multiplier does not derive it. A multiplier is fixed
by a supplied constraint, such as total record number, endpoint, or resource
budget. That constraint is then the occurrence content.

Least disagreement also favors static smooth fields and identical neighbors.
Without a nontrivial boundary, topological sector, negative chemical
potential, or required event count, the homogeneous all-open/no-event history
is cheaper than a universe that forms records and matter.

**Result:** least disagreement is the clean content selector found in Cycle
13. It is not the complete action.

## 4. Frustration-Free Projector And History Actions

This is the strongest exact attack because it grants a global history action
and a unique minimum.

For a supplied one-step unitary `U`, use a two-state clock and define

```text
H_prop(U)
 = (1/2) [ I    -U^dagger
           -U    I       ].
```

It is positive semidefinite. Its zero space consists of history vectors

```text
(|0_clock>|psi> + |1_clock>U|psi>)/sqrt(2).
```

Add the same positive input penalty at clock zero to require `|psi>=|0>`.
The total action has one unique zero-energy history. The companion checks this
for `U=I,X,Z,H`.

All four total Hamiltonians have the same complete spectrum. In fact

```text
H(U) = diag(I,U) H(I) diag(I,U)^dagger.
```

Yet the final states differ. The action is frustration free, positive,
gapped, and uniquely minimized in every case. The update is not derived; it
is the matrix `U` printed inside `H_prop`.

The same construction encodes either overlapping-gate schedule, either Bell
phase, and either record-output table. A classical diagonal constraint can
assign zero to copy and one to oppose, or reverse those assignments. Both
constraints have one zero, one unit gap, and the same symmetry once extended
over all label-related contexts.

This does not make history Hamiltonians empty. Given an exact `U`, they can
derive composition, propagation, and a ground-history representation. The
result is a precise placement theorem:

> Frustration freedom and unique ground history validate an exact supplied
> transition law; they do not identify that law.

The finite clock superposition is also not by itself one actual append-only
record history. A measurement/instrument, boundary-conditioned history, or
record-sector theorem remains required.

A path integral over every stationary/nonstationary history supplies amplitudes

## 6. Gibbs/Free Energy, Maximum Entropy, And Maximum Caliber

For two histories with action gap `Delta`, a Gibbs rule gives

```text
p_low = 1/(1+exp(-beta Delta)).
```

With `Delta=log(2)`, `beta=1` gives `2/3` and `beta=2` gives `4/5`.
Normalization fixes their sum, not the dimensionless product `beta Delta`.

At zero temperature, unique minimization can select one history, but then it
does not supply nontrivial trial statistics. At finite temperature, the
measure supplies weights but still does not name the realized sample. A
stochastic action such as an Onsager-Machlup or negative-log-likelihood
functional can include sample probabilities exactly; then its kernel and
reference measure are the stochastic law.

Maximum entropy is unique only on a supplied physical event algebra with
supplied constraints. Refining one zero-action event into two syntactic
branches changes a naive equal-path coarse weight from `1/2` to `2/3`.
Maximum caliber likewise needs the path quotient, allowed transitions, and
constraint expectations.

Free energy is therefore a coherent one-functional law architecture, not a
target-free selector of its own energy, temperature, event quotient, or
constraints.

## 7. Concurrency And Schedule

Two neighboring CNOT gates on three qubits are an exact control. From `|100>`:

```text
CNOT(1->2) then CNOT(2->3)  -> |111>,
CNOT(2->3) then CNOT(1->2)  -> |110>.
```

Both histories use the same two local unitary gates and have the same operation
count, locality, and gate-wise action. Their schedules are physically
different because the gates overlap and do not commute.

A whole-history action can select one order by printing the ordered product
into its propagation term. It can sum both orders and produce a multiway/path
amplitude. It can add a commutator/confluence penalty and exclude the pair. Or
it can restrict the gate repertoire to commuting layers. Each is an exact
concurrency law.

The positive control is two diagonal controlled-phase edge gates: they
commute, so schedule really is gauge. The theorem must be proved for the
actual gate/event family. “Least action” and equal operation count do not
provide it.

## 8. Persistence And Renewal

```text
Z_N(z) = (1+z)^N,
E[K]   = Nz/(1+z).
```

The normalized laws `z=1` and `z=2` give mean occupancies `N/2` and `2N/3`.
The fugacity/chemical potential is not fixed by normalization.
