## Result

No tested family closes all six atoms without supplying at least one of them.
This is not a universal no-go. It is a constructive tournament result: the
families expose different pieces of a viable hybrid, but no exact predictive
specification—or proved physical-equivalence class—for the postulated fixed
rule has yet been produced.

The tournament also removes three tempting additions from the current axiom
drafting queue:

1. a deterministic selector is not universally required, because a boundary

2. determinism does not logically preclude a derived visit-frequency theorem,

3. a widened state sentence is not presently required, because persistent

The live foundation burden is narrower:

## Decisive Probe 1 -- Symmetric Self-Activation

Take a blank local condition invariant under interchange of two otherwise
indistinguishable possibilities. A deterministic covariant selector would
have to choose a value `c` satisfying

```text
c = swap(c).
```

Neither binary value satisfies that equation. The runner exhausts both
deterministic choices. By contrast:

- the set-valued answer `{0,1}` is covariant but does not actualize;
- a normalized invariant kernel is forced to `(1/2,1/2)` in this finite
  transitive control, but probability normalization and stochastic semantics
  have then been supplied; and
- a boundary record permits deterministic covariant propagation because the
  asymmetry is now physical input rather than a privileged label.

Therefore an exactly symmetric blank local neighborhood cannot both
self-activate and select one label under a deterministic equivariant local
rule. This does **not** exclude a global-history boundary, spontaneous
stochasticity, dynamically inherited asymmetry, or a richer local condition.

The append propagation control is monotone, finite-speed, content-covariant,
and has a finite certificate at every newly written site. It does not
self-seed. A seed or a prior record is load-bearing.

## Decisive Probe 2 -- Sector Permanence

Finite full matrix algebras have no nontrivial central record sector. The
runner verifies this for the full one- and two-qubit Pauli-generated algebras.
A block restriction or conditional expectation creates invariant blocks, but:

- the restriction is an extra physical operation rule unless derived;
- the nonselective block map does not choose one block; and
- applying it before formation destroys the coherence formation is meant to
  distinguish from a record.

Redundant encoding gives a genuine partial closure. For an `N`-site repetition
pair, every Pauli word supported on fewer than `N` sites has zero matrix
element between the two code states, whereas the `N`-site flip connects them.
The thermodynamic sequence makes every fixed finite-support connector vanish.
But a finite-speed event has finite support at every finite time. Infinite
sector disjointness alone therefore cannot be the finite causal certificate
for laboratory formation.

Exact permanence can instead be immediate if the physical law itself is
append-only or preserves a dynamically increasing record algebra. That makes
permanence a theorem of the law, not a consequence of witness count alone.

## Decisive Probe 3 -- Actuality And Global History

A support relation with two lawful successors does not choose one. A
normalized measure over both does not choose one actual history unless sampled
history semantics is additionally supplied.

Global consistency can close actuality in a bounded model: two constant
histories are allowed without a boundary record, and exactly one survives when
the initial boundary value is fixed. The selector then resides in the full
constraint plus boundary data. This keeps the global-history route live. It
also identifies its required controls: intervention freedom, contextual
scope, locality of observable response, and absence of a disguised preferred
measurement setting.

## Decisive Probe 4 -- Determinism And Statistics

Determinism alone neither forbids nor determines frequencies:

- identity evolution admits every probability distribution as invariant;
- a supplied finite cycle has a unique invariant distribution and exact
  uniform visit frequencies over complete periods; and
- changing the supplied cycle changes the frequencies without changing the
  word `deterministic`.

Thus determinism does not logically preclude a frequency theorem, but a claim
that deterministic evolution already supplies quantum/Born statistics is
false. The unresolved obligation is a theorem from the predictive specification
showing a unique, context-compatible physical frequency measure and connecting
it to prepared record corpora.

## Decisive Probe 5 -- Qubit Frame Weights

For a projective qubit context `{n,-n}`, the finite function

```text
f(n) = (1 + n_z^3)/2
```

is positive and obeys `f(n)+f(-n)=1`. It is not affine in `n` and cannot be
represented as `Tr(rho P_n)` by one density matrix. This exact control shows
why single-qubit orthogonal-frame normalization alone does not derive the
ordinary density/Born form.

Ordinary composite quantum mechanics supplies the stronger one-spectator
marginal condition. The runner verifies the density-matrix marginal identity
for entangled states and the independent complete-positivity warning: matrix
transpose is positive on one qubit but its extension to half of a Bell state
has a negative eigenvalue. Those facts motivate the composite route, but they
do not derive physical composition, normalized probabilities, or prepared
state identification from the four axioms.

The relevant recent mathematical route proves a qubit Gleason-type result
after ordinary composite structure and marginal consistency are assumed
([Fiorentino and Weigert, arXiv:2511.15607](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15607)).
Accordingly, generated composition is a high-leverage bridge, not a hidden
Born theorem.

## Decisive Probe 6 -- Clock, Rate, And Storage

An append-only event order supplies a partial order and an integer event
count. Inserting deterministic idle updates preserves the same ordered record
corpus while changing events per update by an arbitrary integer factor.
Therefore record order, record count, and a clock read do not alone define
metric duration or a physical formation rate.

A finite local archive also saturates after finitely many append events.
Long-lived formation requires one of:

- unbounded fresh support reached at finite speed;
- reversible compression plus an exact preservation theorem;
- export to a larger record region; or
- a law under which old records cease to consume the active resource being
  counted.

This is a law/resource obligation, not a reason to insert “compute” or
“storage” into an axiom. Storage-limited simulation language is presently a
useful engineering analogy only. To become physics it must yield an invariant
resource, a conservation or monotonicity law, and measurable response.
