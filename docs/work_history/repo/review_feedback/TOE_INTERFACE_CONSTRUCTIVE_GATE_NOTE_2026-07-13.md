## Finish Line Tested

The foundation campaign does not require a quantitative TOE before axiom
drafting. It uses an **interface-complete common substrate** as a repository
checklist: one coherent framework—predictive specification, approved
primitives, and explicit boundary data—must exhibit a dependency-tracked route
to all five downstream interfaces, without using mutually incompatible toy
models or silently supplying each answer independently. This packet does not
prove that the checklist is universally minimal or exhaustive.

The five interfaces are:

```text
O  operational quantum closure
T  time and causal continuum
I  matter and physical individuation
G  resource, thermodynamics, and gravity
B  boundary and realized-history data
```

## Result

The current program is **not interface-complete by this declared checklist**.
Each interface has a finite conditional witness showing that it is compatible
with the common comparison language. No coherent candidate substrate produces
all five witnesses, and the load-bearing maps in the witnesses are supplied
separately.

This is a useful narrowing, not a generic demand for five more axioms:

- `O` can be reconstructed once generated composition, complete contextual
  record statistics, and sequential/ancilla consistency are theorems;
- `T`, `I`, and `G` must be outputs of the exact law and its controlled
  continuum/resource limits;
- `B` is partly contingent history data and should not be universalized into
  the local constitution; and
- none of these results requires new Record prose before the exact law is
  known.

## O -- Operational Quantum Closure

The finite ordinary-quantum witness supplies density operators, projectors,
the trace pairing, ordinary tensor composition, and a Lüders instrument. It
then verifies:

1. separating tests distinguish different preparations;
2. operationally equivalent mixtures give identical tested statistics;
3. adding an untouched spectator preserves local event probabilities;
4. a selective repeat test is stable; and
5. the transpose map passes one-site positivity but fails the Bell-ancilla
   test, demonstrating why ancilla stability is a real channel condition.

This is a finite **interface witness**, not a derivation. In addition to the
abstract `C/S/L/W` slots it explicitly supplies density operators, effects,
the trace rule, tensor composition, mixing, Lüders update, and ancilla
structure. The operational reconstruction route can turn preparations,
effects, and channels into representations rather than new ontology only once
complete record statistics and the sequential/ancilla conditions are proved.

**Open O obligations for the canonical law:** separating record repertoire,
cross-context event identity, physical mixing where invoked, spectator
marginality, sequential consistency, ancilla stability, and a proof that the
represented prepared state is the record-conditioned state.

## T -- Time And Causal Continuum

A nearest-neighbor append process supplies a finite signal cone and a causal
event partial order. Neither yields metric duration. Inserting idle updates
preserves the record-event order while rescaling events per microscopic update.

The standard cubic nearest-neighbor Laplacian has symbol

```text
4 sum_i sin^2(k_i/2),
```

which approaches `|k|^2` at small momentum. The runner verifies that the error
shrinks under scale reduction, while equal-norm axis and diagonal momenta have
different finite-lattice values. A relativistic continuum can therefore be a
controlled limit, but it is not already present in nearest-neighbor locality
or the kinetic-isotropy primitive.

**Open T obligations:** a physical rate/duration observable, clock
normalization, lapse response, overlap/concurrency rule, interacting continuum
limit, signal cone, and Lorentz/CPT recovery.

## I -- Matter And Physical Individuation

A supplied translation-invariant hopping Hamiltonian on a finite ring gives a
stable one-particle sector, norm conservation, and momentum eigenmodes. That
is enough to demonstrate a matter interface shape.

The hard-core/CAR control remains decisive. Two-site lowering operators have
the same local occupation projectors in commuting hard-core and
Jordan-Wigner/CAR presentations. Their cross-site exchange algebras differ.
Local qubit occupancy therefore does not select physical statistics.

Unitary conjugate presentations also preserve spectra and trace
fingerprints. A physical quotient must be defined from operational records or
the law before coordinate, basis, mirror, or conjugate presentations can be
counted as distinct possibilities.

**Open I obligations:** stable interacting excitations, exchange statistics,
chirality, species/generations, gauge carriers and dynamics, presentation
quotient, masses, and couplings.

## G -- Resource, Thermodynamics, And Gravity

A finite accounting model can conserve a declared capacity while moving
tokens from free support to active records and then to exported/archive
support. That proves bookkeeping consistency only. The same append history is
compatible with multiple active-source maps, lapse responses, and force laws.

Record count also does not determine thermodynamic entropy. The runner pairs
the same fixed record length with a pure history distribution and a uniform
history ensemble, which have different Shannon entropies. Conversely, an
append-only archive can grow while the active source remains constant or zero.

**Open G obligations:** identify a physical conserved resource, show how
support renews, derive entropy and temperature in an appropriate limit,
separate active stress from permanent archive content, prove universal clock
response/WEP, and derive the gravitational field/response equation.

The compute/storage-limited-universe picture earns physical status only if it
supplies these invariant and measurable outputs. At present it is an analogy
that correctly flags capacity questions.

## B -- Boundary And Realized History

One deterministic append law accepts distinct seed configurations and
therefore distinct complete histories. The law does not select its initial
record configuration. A low-record boundary permits a long monotone growth
history; a saturated boundary permits none. The arrow visible in the two
histories is consequently a joint property of law and boundary.

A global boundary may also make one otherwise branching history unique, as
the substrate tournament showed. This keeps a derived-actuality route live,
but it does not turn the boundary into a local axiom. The realized-state
primitive permits pointwise reference to the history actually fixed by
physics; it supplies no selection rule or typicality measure.

**Open B obligations:** state the admissible boundary/history class, identify
which part is contingent data, recover the observed low-entropy/low-record
past, and establish compatibility with interventions and the continuum limit.

## Consequence For The Constitutional Cut

The interface gate does not justify adding `O/T/I/G/B` sentences to the four
axioms. It does justify one structural requirement on the final architecture:

> The exact predictive specification, or a proved physical-equivalence class
> of specifications, must be derived or be part of the constitution's supplied
> foundation by an exact stable reference.

Without that content, “there is one fixed rule” leaves every interface free.
With it, candidate composition, state, and permanence sentences can be tested
as theorems and removed wherever they are redundant.

This statement is a design result, not yet final wording. The next gate must
classify the remaining atoms as `derived`, `definition/clarification`,
`law-specification content`, `boundary data`, or `unresolved conditional`, and
must refuse the live edit unless every proposed deletion/addition has passed
that classification.

single coherent substrate may correlate them, which is the target rather than
a contradiction.
