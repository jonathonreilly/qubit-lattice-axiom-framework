## Objective

The objective is not to defend or minimize the present four axioms. The
objective is a new predictive physics framework that can join quantum
measurement, causal time, information, matter, and gravity under one physical
law with explicit conditions and empirical failure modes.

The current axioms and the current local rule are therefore treated as
falsifiable substrate hypotheses. A construction that works only because the
compiler uses unphysical labels is not a physics closure. A failure that
identifies a wrong ontology or law type is progress.

## The important reclassification

The campaign must distinguish three grades:

1. **Extensional compiler closure** — an exact symbolic local table maps
   named record contents to named record contents.
2. **Orthogonal record implementation** — every classically read control is
   encoded by locally distinguishable record bits, with finite composition
   stated or derived.
3. **Quantum physical implementation** — a CP/unitary/process law, preparation
   interface, and record instrument reproduce the same protocol without
   nondestructively discriminating nonorthogonal states or importing the
   answer.

Most of Cycles 149–177 are grade 1. Cycle 178 begins grade 2 for the recurrent
payload. Cycle 182 supplies a narrow conditional grade-3 dilation for the
orthogonal copy map, but it assumes CNOT dynamics and fresh targets rather
than deriving them from the selected law. Cycle 189 supplies a complete
finite conditional grade-3 process for two system qubits, a two-bit pointer,

repertoire. Cycle 191 reduces all six context instruments and both
preparations to one real-Clifford H/CNOT interpreter with short program
records. The process and interpreter remain conditional and are not yet
implemented by the nearest-neighbour record law.

This is not a cosmetic downgrade. It is the difference between demonstrating
a cellular computer and demonstrating a quantum physical law.

## Missing framework content

### 1. Exact microscopic law identity

The current law is a large hand-assembled compiler. Cycle 185 shows that its
101,996 raw rows contain 4,414 proper-cubic cases and compress strongly under
one representation, so no incompressibility claim is licensed. It is still
not a compact natural rule, a unique consequence of the foundation, or an
empirically selected equivalence class.

A TOE needs either:

- a derivation of one complete exact law or record-equivalent class;
- a scientific identification of that law with independent predictions; or
- an explicit admission that law identity remains open.

Compiler universality alone does not select nature.

One concrete architecture is now sharply posed: move the canonical table into
physical binary program records and derive a small autonomous interpreter law.
Success would make the present compiler software rather than microscopic
physics. It would still leave program/boundary selection and the quantum
process open.

### 2. Actuality and occurrence

Local signatures say which writes are enabled. The runners explore lawful
schedules and prove confluence on bounded graphs. They do not say:

- which enabled event occurs next;
- whether every enabled event eventually occurs;
- whether one history or all histories are physical;
- what sets event density or rate; or
- which complete history is actual.

This is the largest remaining bare-metal gap for record formation.

Cycle 186 turns that general gap into an actual-table discriminator. On an
otherwise recorded lattice with two adjacent open sites, both exact writes can
be enabled while either first write permanently blocks the other. Sequential
asynchronous, synchronous pre-state, and weighted-history readings therefore
produce different permanent facts from the same availability table.

The positive deterministic route remains live only as a theorem:

```text
physical boundary
+ invariant lawful-state domain
+ unique-extension/confluence on that domain
-> one deterministic actual history.
```

That theorem is not supplied by the current apparatus-local confluence runs.

Cycle 187 removes the easiest dismissal—that the problem appears only in an
arbitrary saturated background. Two named recurrent sources have a reachable
contact state with two adjacent enabled writes and two distinct permanent
terminals. The result is conditional on the supplied two-tube boundary, but
any framework claiming matter must either admit such multi-carrier states and
resolve them or derive a physical rule excluding them.

Cycle 188 proves that explicit local tolerance rows can restore confluence on
that whole aligned contact class without a clock, priority, sampler, or new
role. It also separates a genuine alternative: synchronous pre-state batching
writes both competitors into a third terminal that no old asynchronous
history reaches. Batching is therefore a distinct foliation/update law, while
the Cycle-188 repair is ordinary local asynchronous continuation.

The actuality lesson is now narrower and better: nonconfluence in a candidate
table is not automatically constitutional pressure. First search for local
law content that closes the physical domain. Occurrence semantics remains
independent where no such confluence theorem or process law exists.

Thus the six-context process does not itself force positivity across

“Born rule”: a normalized, effect-complete operational process with physical

### 8. Thermodynamics, interactions, and empirical closure

- forces or gauge-field dynamics;
- unitary or probabilistic scattering amplitudes;
- bound-state spectra;
- renormalization or scale flow;
- dimensionless masses and couplings; and
- a map from lattice/record observables to laboratory protocols.

A TOE claim ultimately requires novel numerical or qualitative predictions
that survive independent empirical tests. Internal compiler universality and
post hoc fitting are not empirical closure.

## Minimum predictive substrate interface

The current evidence compresses the missing framework content into five
interfaces. They need not become five axioms. One better exact law may derive
several or all of them.

1. **Physical state and composition domain.** Which finite and global record
   configurations are physical, how disjoint local systems compose, and which
   apparent compiler states are excluded by a proved invariant.
2. **Occurrence/process semantics.** Whether enabled events form
   asynchronously, synchronously, by priority, by a normalized instrument, as
   a weighted set of histories, or through a unique-extension theorem.
3. **Intervention and interaction composition.** How preparations, omitted
   operations, measurements, memory, carrier contact, scattering, and
   coarse-graining compose without a host-selected answer.
4. **Boundary/preparation content.** Which initial or global state is physical,
   how first records and repeated preparations arise, and how multiple
   interacting carriers are generated from one boundary.
5. **Continuum and empirical map.** How record order, density, currents, and
   instruments become Lorentzian spacetime, quantum laboratory statistics,
   matter observables, gravity, and falsifiable dimensionless predictions.

This is the minimum current framework interface, not a proof that five new
constitutional sentences are required.
