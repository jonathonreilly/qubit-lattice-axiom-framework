# Universal Rule-Space Multiway Law Steelman

**Date:** 2026-07-14

**Type:** authority-free research note and bounded exact probe

**Status:** partial attempt

**Authority:** none. This note does not amend an axiom, register a primitive,
select a law, choose a boundary or actuality semantics, or issue an audit
verdict.

## Question

The strongest Wolfram-inspired escape from the missing-law problem is not to
choose one update rule at all:

> What if all exact local rules are retained, their updates form one universal
> multiway object, and only presentation or scheduling redundancy is
> quotiented away?

This would turn “there is one law” into “the one law is the complete rule
space.” It deserves a direct steelman because it could make the law canonical
without privileging one microscopic rule.

## Result Up Front

The route is mathematically coherent only after an **exact grammar** says what
counts as a rule and an exact construction says how rule branches compose. It
then gives one set-valued successor or one universal history object. That is a
real candidate architecture, and the current policy can in principle regard a
set or distribution as one answer.

It is not a free law selector. In the bounded Boolean analogue, the union of
all radius-one rules is maximally permissive: on a cyclic seed displaying each
local neighborhood once, its next-state set is every possible eight-bit
configuration. Bit-flip and reflection covariance still leave multiple rules;
the proper-cubic scalar count analogue leaves `128` complement-covariant rule
tables. Causal-graph equivalence can identify update order while two branches
retain different readable record transcripts.

The universal object therefore still needs one of two completions:

1. prove that all retained branches are exactly record-transcript equivalent;
   or
2. specify a record-faithful equivalence, a measure or deterministic selection
   over the surviving branches, and—if the ontology is single-history—one
   actuality semantics.

Uniform rule counting is compiler-relative. Adding a harmless alias changes a
two-class weight from `1/2` to `2/3`; swapping finite prefix-code lengths swaps
the normalized prior. Quotienting aliases cures that example only after the
physical equivalence relation has been supplied. Causal invariance does not
supply the relation when record contents differ.

Thus “all rules happen” does not remove the exact-law residue. It moves the
residue from a selected rule to the exact rule grammar, universal history
construction, record-faithful quotient, and selection/actuality semantics.
No axiom sentence follows from this bounded result.

## 1. What The Route Gets Right

Wolfram-style multiway evolution usefully separates four notions that the bare
metal discussion must not blur:

- a local rewrite grammar;
- the graph of possible updates;
- the causal dependency graph of events; and
- a quotient that treats some update orders as presentation.

Causal invariance can make scheduling nonphysical without demanding that
different permanent outcome records reconverge. This was the useful lesson
from the earlier Wolfram probe. A universal rule-space proposal goes further:
rule choice itself becomes another branch coordinate of one mathematical
object.

That construction can be exact. For a finite alphabet, radius, rule grammar,
initial state, and branch convention, “take every rule” names a definite set.
The positive content should not be dismissed merely because it is broad.

The relevant primary technical background is Wolfram's discussion of
[multiway systems](https://www.wolframphysics.org/technical-introduction/the-updating-process-for-string-substitution-systems/multiway-systems/),
[causal invariance](https://www.wolframphysics.org/technical-introduction/the-updating-process-for-string-substitution-systems/causal-invariance/),
and [weighted multiway graphs](https://www.wolframphysics.org/technical-introduction/the-updating-process-for-string-substitution-systems/weighted-multiway-graphs/).
The present conclusion is narrower: those mechanisms are acceptance and
quotient tools unless the grammar, equivalence, and predictive reading are
also exact.

### Primary-source boundary of the stronger ruliad claim

The strongest official formulation is the ruliad: the limiting object obtained
by following all possible rules in all possible ways. The source itself draws
the same predictive boundary needed here. It says that merely pointing to the
ruliad does not yield specific predictions about our perceived universe; one
must additionally identify the sampled slice
([Wolfram, 2021](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2021/11/the-concept-of-the-ruliad./)).
It also makes merging an observer-equivalence judgment and locates the
observer by its sensory and descriptive organization. That is not yet the
record-faithful quotient required by this framework.

The technical rule-space treatment constructs finite all-rule multiway
examples, but describes unbounded causal invariance as an expectation and says
new measures of rule distance/translation effort are needed
([technical background](https://wolframphysics.org/technical-introduction/potential-relation-to-physics/multiway-systems-in-the-space-of-all-possible-rules/)).
The observer-theory account explicitly assumes computational boundedness,
persistence, a single thread of experience, locality, and experimental
independence when deriving the perceived description
([Wolfram, 2023](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2023/12/observer-theory/)).
Those are observer-interface conditions, not consequences of the present four
axioms.

The formal causal-set comparison likewise recovers classical or quantum
sequential-growth dynamics only with an appropriate discrete measure
([Gorard, 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.12174)). Causal invariance supplies
important covariance structure; it does not eliminate the measure input in
that result.

So the exact finite control below is not dismissing a theorem that already
closes the gap. It tests the still-explicit primary-source seams: sampled
slice, observer equivalence, measure, and single-thread semantics.

## 2. Exact Universal-Support Control

The runner uses elementary radius-one binary rules only as a bounded Boolean
analogue. There are eight three-bit neighborhoods and `2^8=256` deterministic
local maps. For every neighborhood, exactly `128` rules return `0` and `128`
return `1`.

The cyclic word

```text
00010111
```

contains every three-bit neighborhood exactly once. Each local truth-table bit
therefore controls one independent output site. Across all `256` rules, every
eight-bit successor occurs. The universal set-valued successor is exact but it
predicts no bit of that next state.

This does not prove every universal `Z^3` qubit rule space is trivial. It proves
the precise warning needed here: an all-member construction may replace an
unidentified member with a maximally permissive union. Predictive content then
has to come from the grammar, quotient, selection principle, boundary, or a
theorem showing the apparent branches are physically identical.

## 3. Symmetry Is A Filter, Not The Missing Member

Requiring covariance under global bit complement leaves `16` elementary
rules. Adding left-right reflection still leaves both the identity and
complement rules, which give different next states on the same input.

The closer cubic counting analogue is also explicit. A proper-cubic-invariant
Boolean scalar rule can depend on the center bit and the number `0..6` of live
nearest neighbors: fourteen input orbits. Global complement pairs them into
seven pairs, so `2^7=128` complement-covariant truth tables remain.

The exact counts are not claimed as a classification of complex one-qubit
instruments. They demonstrate that locality plus the named presentation
symmetries can leave a large universal rule family whose members disagree on
records.

## 4. Causal Invariance Is Not Record Equivalence

Two one-event laws can have the same causal DAG

```text
input -> event -> record
```

while writing different readable labels. A quotient by the unlabeled DAG
would erase a physical record difference. A record-faithful equivalence keeps
the two transcripts separate.

This is exactly where the Wolfram mechanism helps: it gives a sharp test for
schedule gauge. It does not establish that different rules or different
outcome sectors are the same physics. Causal invariance and Church–Rosser
confluence are also distinct properties; neither should be used as shorthand
for complete observer equivalence.

## 5. Measure And Actuality Do Not Emerge From Enumeration

With two physical rule classes `A,B`, uniform counting gives

```text
P(A),P(B) = 1/2,1/2.
```

If the syntax contains two aliases for `A` and one for `B`, raw uniform
counting gives `2/3,1/3`. A physical quotient can restore the two-class count,
but then the quotient—not enumeration alone—does the work.

An algorithmic-looking prior has the same exact finite control. Giving `A`
code length one and `B` length two yields normalized weights `2/3,1/3`;
swapping the compiler assignments swaps those weights. No universal
impossibility theorem is asserted. The demonstrated point is that a compiler
and invariance theorem are load-bearing if description length supplies
physics.

Finally, one normalized `1/2,1/2` measure is compatible with actual member `0`,
actual member `1`, or an all-branches/no-single-member reading. A branch graph
and its weights do not alone choose which ontology is intended.

## 6. Computational Boundedness Is Not Yet An Exact Observer Quotient

Observer boundedness can justify practical coarse-graining, but it does not by
itself define which record histories are physically identical. For every
finite observation horizon `H`, the runner constructs two histories that write
the same first `H` bits and differ at the next permanent record. An `H`-horizon
observer conflates them; an `H+1`-horizon observer separates them. The family of
quotients depends on `H`.

More importantly, a computationally bounded persistent observer need not store
or simulate the entire prefix to read the later one-bit separator. It can wait
for and read that permanent record. A fixed computational budget is therefore
not automatically a fixed future horizon. To make the quotient exact, the
theory must specify the observer protocol/resource bound or require agreement
under every admitted finite record experiment. The latter is the full
record-transcript equivalence already demanded by the zero-edit route.

This does not refute observer theory. It identifies what must be translated
into framework language: exact observer state, allowed experiments, resource
bound, persistence relation, and sampling/weight. Without those, “bounded
observer” is a motivation for a quotient rather than the quotient itself.

## 7. TOE-Lane Impact

| lane | what universal rule space supplies | what remains |
|---|---|---|
| law identity | one enumerated set after a grammar is fixed | grammar/radius/alphabet, branch construction, and physical quotient |
| records | branches containing permanent writes | which writes are legal/actual and whether contradictory transcripts coexist |
| probability | possible path or class counts | invariant physical measure, preparation/corpus link, and frequency theorem |
| time | a causal partial order inside each branch | event occurrence, duration/rate, and cross-branch clock semantics |
| matter/chirality | branches realizing many carriers and hands | selection or exact equivalence of their observable records |
| resource/gravity | branches with many transport/response laws | tensor response, universal coupling, scale bridge, and selected branch/history |
| boundary/cosmology | many possible seeds and global histories | boundary class, actual instance, or a unique-boundary theorem |

The architecture does not close these lanes merely by containing a successful
member. It must predict why that member's records, or one equivalence class of
records, describe our world.

## 8. Minimum Exact Universal Package

The strongest honest universal version has at least these fields:

```text
rule grammar
rule-space member set
history construction
record-faithful equivalence
measure or deterministic selection
actuality semantics, if one history is factual
```

Some fields can collapse. A theorem may make every branch transcript-
equivalent; a unique continuation may retire measure and actuality; an
all-branches ontology may reject single-member actuality. But none collapses
from the phrase “all rules.”

If this object were ultimately the exact law, its constitutional location
would depend on its type. A local set-valued continuation law whose
availability projection is the present menu could retype Admissibility. A
global rule-space/history construction would require its own Law
identification while leaving Admissibility as availability. The present probe
does not provide either stable referent, so it licenses no edit.

## No-Go Discipline Gate

The scoped negative is only:

> In the displayed finite rule grammars, retaining all symmetric rules and
> quotienting causal order does not derive a unique record transcript, measure,
> or actual member.

It is not a claim that no universal multiway law can be predictive.

### N1 — Alternative-route enumeration

| route | status |
|---|---|
| exact all-rule set-valued continuation | positive architecture, weak prediction in the control |
| all branches physically equivalent | live if full record-transcript equivalence is proved |
| weighted rule-space measure | live after exact invariant measure and preparation link |
| algorithmic/Solomonoff-style weighting | live after machine/compiler invariance is made physically exact |
| unique observer sector or anthropic conditioning | live but requires exact observer/event and conditional measure |
| unique deterministic fixed point of the universal object | live if existence and uniqueness are proved |
| one intrinsically universal rule instead of all rules | live but returns to exact member selection/equivalence |

### N2 — Wall-independence audit

The exact grammar does not determine a probability measure: the same two rule
classes admit `1/2,1/2` and `2/3,1/3`. The measure does not determine an actual
member: the same normalized law admits either selected outcome or none. The
causal DAG does not determine record content: two rules share the DAG and
differ in readable transcript.

### N3 — Hidden-wall scan

“All,” “rule,” “same causal history,” “uniform,” “simple,” “observer,” and
“branch” are not free words. They require an alphabet/carrier, radius and rule
grammar, update-event convention, equivalence relation, reference machine or
counting measure, observer/record protocol, and state/boundary domain.

### N4 — Exact residual matching

| finite witness | exact residual |
|---|---|
| de Bruijn cyclic seed over all elementary rules | universal support can equal every successor |
| identity/complement symmetric pair | named symmetries do not identify the rule |
| same causal DAG, different record label | causal quotient is weaker than record equivalence |
| syntax alias | raw uniform rule count is presentation-dependent |
| swapped prefix lengths | description weight is compiler-relative in the control |
| one measure, three actuality annotations | measure is not actuality |

### N5 — Rhetoric and resolution audit

The enumeration is a one-dimensional binary finite control, not a theorem
about every hypergraph, QCA, amplitude-valued multiway system, or continuum
limit. “Maximally permissive” describes the displayed de Bruijn fixture, not
every universal rule grammar. Runner PASS totals are checks, not independent
evidence counts.

### N6 — Partial-closure paths

Causal invariance can retire update order. Full abstraction can retire compiler
and representation choices. A uniquely extendible universal constraint can
retire measure and actuality. A normalized invariant quantum measure can close
one-shot weights. Each is a real route; each consumes an exact object not
contained in the phrase “all rules.”

### N7 — Strongest surviving steelman

The best survivor is a canonical algebraic grammar whose complete multiway
object is causally invariant, whose record-aware quotient has only one
operational class for our boundary, and whose invariant quantum measure is
uniquely fixed by refinement and composition. If built, that would be one
exact law without selecting a syntactic rule. This probe does not rule it out.

### N8 — Cross-cycle echo

The result matches the intrinsic-simulation and Wolfram schedule probes:
logical reachability and causal-graph equality are below complete record
equivalence, while path refinement changes naive counts. Its new contribution
is the direct all-rule steelman and the exact de Bruijn control showing how the
universal support can erase every next-state prediction.

## Bottom Line

Wolfram mechanisms are relevant. They give powerful language for local
rewriting, schedule gauge, causal structure, rule-space exploration, and
observer quotients. They do not by themselves make the framework a TOE.

“All rules” is one possible exact architecture only after the rule grammar and
predictive reading are fixed. In the tested form it replaces the missing rule
with a maximally permissive set and then needs the same record-equivalence,
measure/selection, and actuality work. It therefore sharpens the exact-law
contract but adds no new minimum axiom content.
