U2  (b) the Dobrushin coefficient, exactly, at a fixed fugacity
     Dobrushin: if sup_x sum_y C_xy < 1 the Gibbs state is unique.  Here every site has
     six neighbours and the model is translation invariant, so the condition is 6C < 1
     with C the single-site total-variation sensitivity.  Computed exactly (the five
     unchanged neighbours enter only through their multiset, 462 cases):
     There are TWO channels, and they pull in opposite directions in z:
       (3, 1, 2) at c = 1/2: z=0.1 -> 6C=1.4133, z=1.0 -> 6C=1.6171, z=10.0 -> 6C=1.6321
         rises with z (the CONTENT channel)
ok            and the direction is the predicted one for (3, 1, 2): rising in z
       (1, 1, 1) at c = 2: z=0.1 -> 6C=1.0227, z=1.0 -> 6C=0.3956, z=10.0 -> 6C=0.0488
         falls with z (the OCCUPANCY channel)
ok            and the direction is the predicted one for (1, 1, 1): falling in z
     At uniform weights the content carries no information, so only occupancy couples,
     and C falls with z; away from uniform the content channel dominates and C rises.
     A sup over a GRID of z is therefore not a proof either way - hence U3.

U4  (c) content-less records
     With p = q = r = w the content is irrelevant and the weight of a configuration is
     z^N (c w)^B with N the number of records and B the number of occupied adjacent
     pairs.  That is EXACTLY the lattice gas with bond activity c w, i.e. the Ising
     model in a field under the standard map with K_Ising = (1/4) log(c w).
ok          at p=q=r=1 the neutral scale is c_0 = 1 and c_0 w = 1 exactly
ok          and there C = 0 identically: at c = c_0 with uniform weights the sites are
         INDEPENDENT, so there is no transition at the neutral scale at any fugacity.
     For c > c_0 the bond activity exceeds one and the lattice gas is ferromagnetic.
     LITERATURE, NOT PROVED HERE: the Z^3 Ising model has a transition at
     K_c = 0.2216544... , so clumping of content-less records sets in at
       c/c_0 = exp(4 K_c) = 2.4269... ,
     whereas U3's rigorous bound only certifies uniqueness up to c/c_0 = 5/4.  The
     bound is therefore about a factor two from the truth in the one case where the
     truth is known - which is the honest measure of how lossy it is.

U5  (a) above: what is available and what is not
     The chessboard estimate needs reflection positivity through bond planes.  For this
     law that holds exactly when the 7x7 pair-weight matrix is positive semidefinite,
     which (issue 8534) is
       c >= c_0   AND   p >= q   AND   p + q >= 2r,
     the last two being SCALE-FREE.  The unit's statement of block 39's condition omits
     them, and at (5,2,4) the third fails (7 < 8), so no scale makes the tool available
     there at all.  That is the region question answered.
     NOT DONE HERE: the contour argument itself.  A chessboard estimate bounds the
     weight of a contour by a product of one-site quantities, and turning that into two
     translation-invariant states needs a Peierls count whose constant I did not
     establish.  So part (a) yields the exact region where the METHOD applies, and no
     theorem.  A precise no-go it is not; an unfinished route it is.

SUMMARY: PARTIAL on brackets for the clumping onset of the six-axis gas with vacancies. BELOW: the seven-state single-site conditional has two channels that move oppositely in the fugacity - at uniform weights only occupancy couples and the Dobrushin coefficient FALLS with z, away from uniform the content channel dominates and it RISES - so a sup over a grid of z proves nothing; instead, because the five unchanged neighbours contribute the same factor to both weights and cancel, the single-neighbour weight ratio is independent of them AND of z, giving the z-free bound (M-m)/(M+m) and hence uniqueness AT EVERY FUGACITY wherever 6(M-m)/(M+m) < 1 - certified here for (1,1,1) and (9,8,8) up to c/c_0 = 5/4.
