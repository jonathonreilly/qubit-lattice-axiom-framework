     OCCUPIED SET alone:   mu(A)  proportional to  z^|A| Z_A,   where Z_A is the
     content partition function on the subgraph induced by A (for the two-valued menu,
     an Ising partition function).  Every question about rho and rho_2 is a question
     about mu.  Checked on a ring of 4 at w = e^beta = 2, z = 1:

ok        Z_{two adjacent} = 5 = 2(w + 1/w) - the bond is counted once

V2  the FKG lattice condition for the occupation marginal
     If mu(A u B) mu(A n B) >= mu(A) mu(B) for all subsets A, B, then the occupation
     variables are positively associated, and rho_2 >= rho^2 follows at once.  The
     condition is checked here EXACTLY over every ordered pair of subsets:
ok        ring of 6, w = 2, z = 1: all 2080 pairs satisfy it (0 violations)
ok        ring of 6, w = 3, z = 1/2: all 2080 pairs satisfy it (0 violations)
ok        ring of 8, w = 2, z = 2: all 32896 pairs satisfy it (0 violations)
ok        3x3 torus, w = 2, z = 1: all 131328 pairs satisfy it (0 violations)
     So on every instance tested the marginal is log-supermodular, which is the
     hypothesis of the FKG inequality.  Given FKG (ASSUMED as a theorem, not re-proved
     here), 1[x occupied] and 1[y occupied] are increasing functions of the occupied
     set, hence positively correlated, hence
       rho_2  >=  rho^2    for every bond.
