A1  candidate (a): 'no state is privileged', applied to the empty state
ok   for any fixed neighbour content, sum over the six a of omega(a,.) = p + q + 4r
ok   so 'an empty neighbour weighs what a record of uniformly random content weighs' is
     c * (p + q + 4r)/6 = 1, i.e. c = c_0 = 6/(p+q+4r): the ARITHMETIC mean reading.
     But 'privileged' does not say which average.  Four readings, each a sentence one
     could write with the same words:
       arithmetic (c_0)     c = 6/(p + q + 4*r)   at (3,1,2): 1/2
       geometric            c = 1/(p**(1/6)*q**(1/6)*r**(2/3))   at (3,1,2): 2**(1/3)*3**(5/6)/6
       harmonic             c = 2/(3*r) + 1/(6*q) + 1/(6*p)   at (3,1,2): 5/9
       same content         c = 1/p   at (3,1,2): 1/3
ok   at (3,1,2) the four give [1/3, 1/2, 2**(1/3)*3**(5/6)/6, 5/9]: all different, so the sentence alone
     fixes a RELATION (the empty bond weighs some mean of the record bonds), not a
     number.  Something else has to say which mean, and (A4) is the candidate that does.

A2  the pendant identity at c_0
     Integrated reading: an unrecorded site is given a content and averaged over the
     six, uniformly.  With ONE occupied neighbour of content s, it contributes
     (1/6) sum_a c omega(a,s) = c (p+q+4r)/6.
ok   at c = c_0 that is exactly 1, the vacancy weight: for a PENDANT unrecorded site the
     two readings agree, and c_0 is the unique scale for which they do.
ok   (solving c(p+q+4r)/6 = 1 gives c = c_0 and nothing else)

A3  candidate (b): can any scale identify the two readings?
     With TWO occupied neighbours of contents s1, s2 the integrated reading gives
     (1/6) sum_a c^2 omega(a,s1) omega(a,s2), while the vacancy gives 1.
       s2 = s1          (1/6) sum_a omega omega = (p**2 + q**2 + 4*r**2)/6
       s2 = -s1         (1/6) sum_a omega omega = (p*q + 2*r**2)/3
       s2 orthogonal    (1/6) sum_a omega omega = r*(p + q + r)/3
ok   at (3,1,2) the three are [13/3, 11/3, 4]: distinct
     So the integrated contribution is c^2 times a function of (s1, s2) that is NOT
     constant, while the vacancy contributes the constant 1.  A constant cannot equal
     a non-constant function of the neighbours' contents, so:
ok   NO scale c identifies the two readings once an unrecorded site has two occupied
     neighbours - the identification in candidate (b) fails for every c, and c_0 is
     special only in the pendant case (A2).  That is block 24's condition: the two
     readings agree exactly when every unrecorded component is pendant.

A4  candidate (c): the normalizer, on average
     Z_x = sum_a prod over neighbours (c omega(a, s_y)), empty neighbours weighing 1.
     With k occupied neighbours, averaging each neighbour's content uniformly:
       <Z_k> = 6 (c (p+q+4r)/6)^k = 6**(1 - k)*(c*(p + q + 4*r))**k
ok   at c = c_0 this is 6 for EVERY k - the empty-neighbourhood value - so the formation
     rate is independent of the neighbourhood on average, at every neighbourhood size.
ok   and c_0 is the only such scale: at c = 2 c_0 the k = 2 average is 4 times the k = 0 one
     (in general <Z_k>/<Z_0> = (c/c_0)^k, which is 1 for all k iff c = c_0).
     THIS is the sentence that picks the arithmetic mean in A1: 'the rate a record
     forms at does not depend on how many neighbours it has, on average' forces c_0.

A5  and not pointwise
       Z_2 with s2 = s1          = 13/2 at (3,1,2)
       Z_2 with s2 = -s1         = 11/2 at (3,1,2)
       Z_2 with s2 orthogonal    = 6 at (3,1,2)
ok   at c_0 the two-neighbour normalizer still takes three distinct values, so no scale

SUMMARY: PARTIAL what c_0 fixes, exactly: (a) 'no state is privileged' applied to the empty state fixes only a RELATION - the arithmetic, geometric, harmonic and same-content readings give four different scales, all distinct at (3,1,2) - so the sentence alone does not pick c_0; (b) the identification with block 24's integrated reading holds for a PENDANT unrecorded site at c_0 and at no other scale, but fails for EVERY c as soon as an unrecorded site has two occupied neighbours, because the vacancy contributes a constant while the integral contributes a non-constant function of the neighbours' contents; (c) c_0 is the unique scale at which the average normalizer 6 (c(p+q+4r)/6)^k is independent of the neighbourhood size k, and no scale achieves that pointwise
