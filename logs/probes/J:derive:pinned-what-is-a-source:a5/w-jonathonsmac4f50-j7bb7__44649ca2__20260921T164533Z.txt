     c_0 is fixed by 'an empty site acts on each neighbour as a record of unknown
     content', i.e. c_0 times the average pair weight is 1.
ok        (sphere) the antiderivative e^{beta t}/(2 beta) checks by differentiation, so
ok          <e^{beta s.s'}> = sinh(beta)/beta, hence c_0 = beta/sinh(beta)
ok          and c_0 * <e^{beta s.s'}> = 1 identically, which is the defining property

N2  (a) what a pinned record is
     In the ORDERED medium the transverse (Goldstone) field is massless, with quadratic
     action (rho_s/2) sum_<xy> (theta_x - theta_y)^2.  A record whose content is HELD
     does not add a term to that action: it fixes theta at its site.  That is a
     DIRICHLET condition, not a source term.  Solving it,
       theta(x) = theta_0 G(x)/G(0),      G the lattice Green function, ~ 1/r,
     so a pinned record does produce a one-over-distance field - but its strength is a
     CAPACITY, 1/G(0), the reciprocal of an on-site Green function, and capacities do
     not add.  This is the same structure as the persistent-source result of issue
     8543 and it is reused here rather than re-derived.
ok        L = 5: G(0) = 0.198260661, a single pinned record has capacity 1/G(0) = 5.043864956
ok        L = 6: G(0) = 0.195520895, a single pinned record has capacity 1/G(0) = 5.114542874
ok        L = 7: G(0) = 0.194529726, a single pinned record has capacity 1/G(0) = 5.140602521

     Either way a jammed cluster is not a mass.

N4  (c) does a density excess source the transverse field at all?
     The transverse field IS the content direction, and the content is carried by the
     records.  Extra records that are ALIGNED with the medium carry no transverse
     component, so they add nothing to theta.  What they change is the local stiffness:
       action = (1/2) sum_<xy> rho_s(x) (theta_x - theta_y)^2,   rho_s proportional to rho
     so a density excess multiplies the COEFFICIENT, giving
       div( (rho + delta_rho(x)) grad theta ) = 0,
     which is a modulation of the medium, not a source term on the right-hand side.
ok        substituting theta == 0: the modulated equation gives 0 (satisfied), the
       sourced equation gives -S(x) (not satisfied unless S == 0).  So a density
       excess admits theta == 0 as an exact solution and a real source does not:
       nothing: a density excess in an aligned medium does not source the massless
       transverse mode at linear order.  It refracts an existing field; it does not
       create one.  (Second order, it scatters - not a far field proportional to N.)

N5  (d) a region where formation is faster
     This one IS additive, and the unit's own setting says how: block 41 gives
       d<n_x>/dt = kappa (Lap <n>)_x + j_x,
     so a steady production excess carries the stationary halo G*(j - <j>)/kappa, a
     genuine one-over-distance field whose strength is the TOTAL excess production
     Q = sum_x (j_x - <j>) - additive in the sites of the region, hence proportional to
     the number of records it makes per unit time.
ok          n(R) = Q/(4 pi kappa R) with gradient -Q/(4 pi kappa R^2): additive in Q
     BUT it is in the DENSITY channel, not the transverse one.  The massless mode of
     the ordered medium is the content direction; the halo is an excess of records.
     They are different fields, and block 41's own result (4) is that the content field
     around a record is a number times its content vector - the mass carries no charge
     in the long-range tilt channel.

N6  the verdict
     candidate                    far field   additive in N?   channel
     (a) one pinned record        1/r         -                transverse (massless)
     (b) N aligned pinned records 1/r         NO: capacity ~ N^(1/3)   transverse
     (c) a density excess         none at linear order          -
     (d) faster formation         1/r         YES, in Q         DENSITY, not transverse
     So NO candidate sources the massless transverse mode additively.  The one additive
     source is in the wrong channel.  That is the clean negative the unit anticipates,
     and it says the strength of the long-range potential in the lattice's own units
     cannot yet be computed from any of these four definitions - so the memo's open
     gate on the natural unit stays untestable by this route.

SUMMARY: PARTIAL and NEGATIVE on what a source is when records move: at the pinned scale (c_0 = beta/sinh(beta) on the sphere menu and 6/(p+q+4r) on the six axes, both verified as c_0 times the average pair weight = 1), none of the unit's four candidates sources the massless transverse mode additively. A pinned record fixes the content at its site, which is a DIRICHLET condition on the Goldstone field rather than a source term, so its far field is theta_0 G(r)/G(0) with strength the capacity 1/G(0); a jammed aligned cluster of N records is then a conductor, whose capacity is strictly sub-additive - checked exactly on tori of side 5, 6 and 7, with the capacity-to-N*C_1 ratio falling monotonically in N and depending on the cluster's shape, a 2x2x2 cube being more screened than a line of the same N; the N^(1/3) growth of a compact cluster's capacity is argued from the conductor analogy and NOT computed here; a density excess of ALIGNED records carries no transverse component and enters the quadratic action as a coefficient multiplying grad theta, so it modulates the medium and does not source the massless mode at linear order; and a region of faster formation IS additive, carrying block 41's halo G*(j-<j>)/kappa with strength the total excess production Q, but in the DENSITY channel rather than the transverse one
