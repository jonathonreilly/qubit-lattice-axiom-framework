C5  Thomson's principle: the minimum twist energy is bounded BELOW, uniformly in T
     The order-beta cost of a site-wise twist is the quadratic Dirichlet form on the
     backward cone with theta = theta_0 at the apex and 0 on level 0.  Its minimum is
     theta_0^2 times the effective conductance C_T, and C_T = 1/R_T with R_T the
     effective resistance, which by Thomson's principle is the minimum energy over
     unit flows - so ANY unit flow bounds it above:
       R_T <= E_T < 3/4   ==>   C_T > 4/3   for every T.
