R1  the relative entropy of one site's kernel
ok   KL(vMF(k u) || vMF(k u')) = E[log(e^{k u.s}/e^{k u'.s})] = k (u - u').E[s]
     and E[s] = A(k) u, so KL = k A(k)(1 - u.u') - the task's GIVEN, in one line.
ok   k A(k) = k^2/3 + O(k^4), so the per-site cost of a small twist is quadratic in the angle
     and, at fixed angle, grows linearly in k at large coupling.

R4  what route A needed, and what it was confused with
     Route A needed the cost to vanish like 1/sum_{k<T} P_k.  The plane walk's return
     sum diverges - that is the recurrence the route rests on - so 1/sum -> 0 like
     1/log T.  But that sum is a property of the PLANE walk, while the twist's cost is
     the conductance of the SPACE-TIME cone, and R2/R3 show the latter does not
     vanish.  The two are different objects; conflating them is what made the route
     look open.  Three regimes, now separated:
       twist static in level time:        cost Theta(T)      (the earlier attempts)
       twist varying in space and time:   cost Theta(1)      (R2, R3)
       what route A needs:                cost o(1)          (unreachable this way)
ok   the space-time twist buys a factor T over the static one and no more
