## 2. Exact norm, compression and physical energy contract

Write \(D=\sum_v\delta_v n_{B_v}\) with real static coefficients satisfying \(\sum_v\delta_v=0\). In the computational occupation basis, each eigenvalue is a subset sum. The maximum is the sum of the positive coefficients; the minimum is the sum of the negative coefficients. Centering makes them opposite, so exactly
\[
 \|D\|=\frac12\sum_v|\delta_v|\leq\frac{N d}{2},
 \qquad d=\max_v|\delta_v|.
\]
This also covers the all-zero vector. For odd \(N\), the last interval bound need not be attained; the exact half-\(\ell^1\) identity still holds. Duhamel on the actual full Hilbert space consequently gives \(t\|D\|\) for any nominal input isometry and horizon. The sufficient additional vector budget \(b\) follows from \(d\leq2b/(NT)\). The channel addition is at most twice the vector addition. Nothing in this baseline is a lower bound on actual error.

For the fully symmetric reference projector \(\Pi\),
\[
 \Pi n_{B_v}\Pi=\frac1N\Pi N_B\Pi,\qquad
 N_B=\sum_u n_{B_u}.
\]
It follows that \(\Pi D\Pi=0\) exactly. Thus the comparison generator is still \(\Pi W\Pi\), not a fitted new generator. With a nonzero coefficient mean \(\bar\delta\), the compression would instead be \(\bar\delta\,\Pi N_B\Pi\). That collective occupation is not a scalar on the full symmetric space or on the coherent product reference. Exact centering is a load-bearing hypothesis.

Every actual term commutes with \(H_E=\omega(n_P+N_B)\): the grid swaps and corner exchange conserve the corresponding excitation sum; the data fields act on freely degenerate Q or are diagonal on P; \(D\) is diagonal. Therefore the ordered factorization
\[
 e^{-itH_{{\rm loc},\delta}}
 =e^{-ist}e^{-itH_E}e^{-it(H_F+W+D)}
\]
is exact. In the full loader the installed suffix is likewise diagonal on P in its data-dependent blocks and conserves this same \(H_E\).

In contrast,
\[
 [H_{\rm tot},H_E+D]=[H_F+V_{\rm loc},D]
\]
in this construction. On an edge \(u,v\),
\[
 [\kappa_{uv}(I-S_{uv}),D]
 =\kappa_{uv}(\delta_u-\delta_v)(n_u-n_v)S_{uv}.
\]
For example its action on the reference occupation \(|1_u0_v\rangle\) contains
\(\kappa_{uv}(\delta_v-\delta_u)|0_u1_v\rangle\). Unequal coefficients therefore give a nonzero edge commutator. The corner exchange also has a nonzero commutator when \(\delta_e\ne0\). These are different physical transition supports; they do not acquire a general cancellation from centering. A centered pair \(\delta_u=d,\delta_v=-d\), with all other offsets zero, already displays the distinction. Total static energy remains conserved, as does nominal \(H_E\); actual unequal free energy is not thereby conserved. The source's qualifications are necessary and correct.

## 3. Full-sector occupation Gram and exact inverse range

For zero-sum complex \(f\), define \(B_n(f)=\sum_v f_v n_{B_v}\Pi\). On a normalized Dicke vector \(|D_r\rangle\), the diagonal occupation correlation is \(r/N\), and the off-diagonal correlation is \(r(r-1)/[N(N-1)]\). Using
\(\sum_{u\ne v}\overline f_u f_v=-\|f\|_2^2\) gives
\[
 \|B_n(f)|D_r\rangle\|^2
 =\left[\frac rN-\frac{r(r-1)}{N(N-1)}\right]\|f\|_2^2
 =\frac{r(N-r)}{N(N-1)}\|f\|_2^2.
\]
This verifies source (6), including both fully empty and fully occupied endpoints, where the answer vanishes. Distinct \(r\) remain in different reference-number sectors, so their outputs are orthogonal. Arbitrary coherent data/passive coefficients do not change that orthogonality. \[
 \|B_n(f)\|\leq a_N\|f\|_2,\qquad
 a_N=\sqrt{\frac{N}{4(N-1)}}\leq\frac1{\sqrt2}.
\]
For even \(N\) the middle Dicke sector attains the coefficient; for odd \(N\) this is a valid slight overestimate. Also \(\Pi B_n(f)=0\).
