# A support-restricted boundary-row Wilson mode

Use the stated two-dimensional open-time Wilson strip, Wilson parameter r=1 and mass parameter M=1, with antiperiodic spatial boundary condition. The boundary has one missing time hop. A spinor chi in the appropriate eigenspace gamma_t chi=+chi or -chi has its surviving hop into the bulk killed by the complementary Wilson projector. This is the specified termination; no assertion about arbitrary boundary terms is made.

After gauge-transforming the spatial links on that row to constant holonomy, a mode wholly supported on the row obeys

(D_W-1)psi=[1-cos k'+i gamma_x sin k']chi.

Hermitian Euclidean gamma_x obeys gamma_x²=I. Multiplying by the adjoint gives

||(D_W-1)psi||²/||psi||²=(1-cos k')²+sin² k'=4sin²(k'/2).

Consequently this explicitly supported ansatz is a zero mode exactly at k'=0 mod2pi. The antiperiodic spatial momenta are k'=[2pi(n+1/2)+e W_edge]/L_x up to the charge-sign convention; a zero occurs exactly when e W_edge=pi mod2pi. Interior plaquettes do not enter because the into-bulk hopping projector annihilates the mode.

This iff is restricted to wholly boundary-row support with the specified spin projection and termination. Other bulk or boundary zeros are not classified. Finite numerical locality fits, sampled kernel gaps and Wilson-line scans do not establish a universal asymptotic obstruction to every local chiral measure. Those original claims remain deferred in recovery with their correction boundary.
