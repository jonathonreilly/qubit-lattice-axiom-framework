# What an autonomous program loader would actually require

Bounded assessment and conditional author lemma, not a licensed replacement of the complete-loader premise. The checked router/writer inventory does not currently provide a physical all-output loader from an independently prepared blank apparatus and arbitrary external logical input. A concrete enlarged-block construction does remove the PROGRAM relocation step during the operation itself: two SWAP-conditioned entrance bonds admit an exact one-head gauge comparison to the existing finite router. It preserves old program/environment information and requires no timed switch. Those four-qubit bonds are not among the displayed nearest-neighbor one-/two-qubit primitives, and coherent endpoint head/path injection remains supplied. Neither qualification is hidden in the construction.

## 1. The actual contract that needs replacing

In preparation9979, the fixed apparatus/environment state omega_AE is independent of arbitrary logical/reference input rho_LZ. Its normalized ground comparator is

    omega_0=|vac_heads,0_R><vac_heads,0_R| tensor sigma_PE.

The old physical program P may be unknown, mixed and entangled with inaccessible E. The assumed complete loader acts only on accessible input/apparatus/workspaces, identity on E,Z, and must produce

    E_seed rho E_seed^* tensor sigma_S

up to the stated diamond error eta_load. The spectator sigma_S retains old P/E, consumed input remnants and EVERY loader/environment output by a lossless input-independent relocation. During the separately modeled operating interval S must be decoupled. The general preparation discrepancy is 2sqrt(q)+eta_load. Correct apparatus marginals do not replace this joint contract.

The earlier head-only energy source e6df has sigma_PRE instead. It does not certify blank R; any loader for that version must retain old R and supply a blank operating record by another declared resource. The present conditional lemma uses the stronger comparator with R=0. It does not silently solve the old unknown-record case.

The complete interface has at least three distinct physical tasks: place the external program coherently into operating P while retaining old P/E; create/inject the one operating head with the arbitrary path amplitude at the two emitters; and sequence subsequent evolution while retaining or isolating all remnants. A mathematical SWAP addresses only the first information transfer. A supplied initial one-head path state already performs a substantive part of the second task.

## 3. A conditional enlarged-block bond

Let Q be a new ordinary program-input qubit, and W=SWAP_QP, acting only on Q,P. For two head sites i,j define the full operator

    Q_a^W(i,j)=a[n_i+n_j-a_j^dagger a_i tensor W
                                   -a_i^dagger a_j tensor W^*]. (1)

W is real unitary and self-adjoint. On head00 the block is zero; on11 it is2aI; on the one-head pair it is a[[I,-W^*],[-W,I]]. Its eigenvalues there are0 and2a, since it is unitarily conjugate to Q_a tensor I. Thus (1) is positive on its ENTIRE four-qubit support and has norm2a. Double occupation is retained rather than removed from the norm/load count. It conserves total head number but need not conserve n_P or n_Q separately.

This is a proposed supplied FOUR-qubit gate-conditioned hopping interaction. Positivity and finite support do not identify it with the previously realized two-qubit primitives. There is no asserted nearest-neighbor two-body decomposition, effective gadget, coupling supplier or native derivation. Everything below that uses (1) is explicitly conditional on enlarging the supplied block class to include it.

## 5. Exact one-head gauge identity, including the runtime generator

Work on the finite one-head space, with all Q,P,R and arbitrary reference factors retained. Let A_pre consist of BOTH I ray prefixes n=1,...,M. Let A_post contain every remaining head coordinate: both I origins n=0, core, cables, writers and all F sites. Write their orthogonal head projections Pi_pre,Pi_post. Define

    G=Pi_pre tensor I_QP + Pi_post tensor W.             (2)

G is a unitary, G^*=G. It is a mathematical gauge comparison, not a physical operation to be applied at a selected time.

Let H_ref,Q be the frozen finite operating Hamiltonian with its program factor mathematically relabeled to Q and the OLD P retained as a decoupled spectator. Its program-dependent mediator coefficients therefore depend on Q in this comparison operator. Its physical implementation at Q is not asserted. Let H_new denote the actual conditional construction of section4, whose core continues to depend on physical P. On the one-head space,

    H_new=G H_ref,Q G^*.                                (3)

To check (3), the only head bonds crossing the partition are the two entrance bonds. Conjugating their -j hopping by (2) inserts W and gives precisely (1), leaving their occupation diagonals unchanged. Every other head hopping stays within one side; W commutes with its head operators and with the writer's R operators. Program-dependent core occupations are supported on mediator heads in A_post, where W f(n_Q) W=f(n_P). On A_pre those occupations vanish. All vacancy scalars, head occupations, source blocks and caps commute with (2). The shared record bias commutes with W and is unchanged globally. A uniform head penalty equals mu I on this one-head space, also unchanged. These checks include the large microscopic mediator energies; no effective Hamiltonian approximation or extra time-accumulating error is introduced.

Hence for every real t,

    exp(-itH_new)=G exp(-itH_ref,Q)G^*.                  (4)

This identity is asserted only in the one-head sector. The positive full-support definition (1) is valid on all occupations, but no analogous many-head gauge identity is inferred from it. That distinction does not weaken the stated nominal one-head operation transfer.

The nominal initial head is at one of the two emitters, hence in A_pre. Let rho_Q,path,Z be arbitrary logical input, independent of sigma_PE, with physical R=0 and all remaining heads vacant. G acts identically on this entire initial subspace. The reference evolution operates on Q/path/R/heads while P,E stay spectators. On the ideal final F target every head position is in A_post, so G is exactly W there. It follows that the ideal complete output has

    logical program in physical P,
    the parent's controlled logical path output and radial profile on F,

    every other head vacant and physical R=1.

This factorization holds for mixed sigma_PE and arbitrary program/path/Z entanglement: swap is a fixed unitary that relocates tensor factors, and the original input and apparatus are independent. No operation is performed on E or Z, and no old-P information is discarded. Q is a retained physical output, not a traced garbage register.

More explicitly, if the parent's complete isometry error is d(t), conjugating both actual and comparison vectors by G gives EXACTLY the same operator-norm error d(t) in this construction, uniformly over the old P/E spectator as well. ## 7. Exactly what remains missing for the complete loader

Within the old supplied primitive set, the first decisive calculation is a positive local realization or a full finite-time simulation bound for the two conditional entrance bonds (1), including the load cap, actual local geometry and complete Q/P/reference output. A claim that a sum of old two-body gates implements their unitary is insufficient without autonomous sequencing and a bound on leakage/clock remnants over the operating horizon. The exact gauge lemma identifies a concrete target for such a calculation; it does not provide it.

Even if those bonds were supplied, the original blank-apparatus loading contract still requires the arbitrary external path input to become one head across the two physical emitters. The current lemma starts with that encoded head state. It does not prepare it from the head-vacuum comparator, convert an ordinary path qubit into that encoding, inject energy, or dispose of/retain the corresponding consumed-input register by an actual construction. Treating the initially excited emitters as both blank apparatus and supplied logical input would be inconsistent.

If a separate complete path-injection channel, identity on P/E, were supplied with error eta_inj, preparation contraction would give 2sqrt(q)+eta_inj before applying the integrated unitary (4). Old P would then relocate autonomously in this enlarged model, with no additional program-loading error. But eta_inj is still a physical interface assumption, not a result of the present proof. For the head-only e6df comparator, retaining old R and supplying blank R is an additional unsolved task. A timed swap, timed injection/quench, physical reset with discarded output, or an undeclared fresh blank register cannot be used to set these errors to zero.

There is therefore a concrete conditional simplification of program relocation, but no demonstrated removal of the complete black-box loader in the original licensed architecture. This is not a universal obstruction: alternative redesigned geometries, positive gadgets or autonomous input encodings remain possible. The next decisive target is the explicit bond-realization or endpoint-injection interface, not a claim of broad impossibility or an optimization of the old runtime exponent.
