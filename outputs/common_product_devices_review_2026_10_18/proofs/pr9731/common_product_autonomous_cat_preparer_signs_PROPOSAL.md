# Static signed splitter for either cat sign

ROOT-authored short extension of the checked stationary cat-preparerd4e708/review1f606. It changes only supplied signs in the splitter, not preparation dynamics or the unproved finite-source interface. Let sigma=+1 or-1, D_sigma=diag(1,sigma) on its output paths, and H_sigma=D_sigma H with H the real Hadamard from that source. Use hopping -j(H_sigma)_ba in the four signed splitter bonds. Their magnitudes are still j/sqrt2; each positive signed exchange and its vacancy correction therefore has the same norm, occupation diagonal and true scalar. The four endpoints, cubic edges and all other components are unchanged.

The internal matrix is now V_sigma=[[0,H_sigma^T],[H_sigma,0]], which is real symmetric and squares toI because H_sigma is orthogonal. Thus the exact endpoint calculation still gives S_split=zV_sigma. It would be incorrect to use H_sigma in both off-diagonal blocks for sigma=-1; its transpose is required for the reverse direction.

The forward ideal word is U_sigma=C_A1 W4 W3 W2 W1 H_sigma, with reverse word U_sigma^*. Applied to path0 and0000 it gives path1 tensor (0000+sigma1111)/sqrt2. The common phase remains z^(90+sum L), since only a branch coefficient sign changes. The full backward word and network unitarity remain intact; no pulse, postselection or erased output is introduced.

The ideal stages still have zero reflection, so the same noncommuting internal matrix is nilpotent by monotone travel. The exact splitter has zero approximation error, and every writer/core error and norm is unchanged. The stationary full-channel error remains49delta/(1-6delta), on the same domains. The exact geometry0bc688 and ROOT geometry reviewcac4a are unchanged as coordinate/positive-block interfaces; coefficients are inherited with this explicitly declared sign replacement. Counts, range, incidence load and scalar8b+36(sqrt2-1)j are unchanged.

This only provides both desired signs at the stationary-column level. It does not assert a complete finite endpoint-seed theorem, simultaneous preparation of twelve operating cats, or attachment to permanently present receiving ZZ/data couplings. Any future finite theorem must check its actual source/packet obligations for these full operators rather than use the desired sign as evidence of preparation. A separate checker should inspect the transpose, exact scalar and unchanged feedback/error interface.
