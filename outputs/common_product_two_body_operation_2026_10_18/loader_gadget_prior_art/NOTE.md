# Prior-art context, not an imported proof

Consulted 2026-10-18: Stephen P. Jordan and Edward Farhi, *Perturbative Gadgets at Arbitrary Orders*, arXiv:0802.1874v4 (31 January 2012), https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.1874 ; PDF https://arxiv.org/pdf/0802.1874 . Read the abstract and construction on pages1–3; not a full-paper verification.

The paper supplies established context for reducing many-body interactions to two-body ones perturbatively. Its construction uses Pauli couplings to ancillary registers, complete-graph ferromagnetic penalties, conserved ancillary parity sectors, and supplied cat states. The basic cat-mediator paradigm is not new to this campaign.

The campaign's square penalties, exact mixed-path scalar, explicit literal-input/output norm remainder, local-background residual and proposed finite-family integration are separately derived and require their named scoped checks. No theorem, error estimate or physical preparation claim is imported from the paper. This brief comparison does not establish novelty or exhaust the literature. Primitive supply, selected ancillary state, interaction range, complete outputs and runtime matching remain explicit obligations.
