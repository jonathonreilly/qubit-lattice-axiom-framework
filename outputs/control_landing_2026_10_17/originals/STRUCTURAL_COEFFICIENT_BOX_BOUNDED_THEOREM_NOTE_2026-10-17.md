# A structural coefficient box for the two-body reflection ladder

This proposed extension replaces ten exact gate coefficients by an explicit
nonzero tolerance box, while retaining the supplied block forms, exact dark
source vacuum, uniform clock hopping and old Hamiltonian. It does not supply
the incoming clock packet, native interactions or a physical precision budget.
The proof uses a closed-tail resolvent identity instead of a bound proportional
to the total observation time. Its all-future comparison must use the actual
common physical scalar; a fixed nominal scalar has a separate phase cost.

## 1. Precisely which coefficients may change

Keep

    J = 0.030021107107032,
    K = 2I + Z1 Z2,
    W = ((9+12i)I + (-16+12i)X1X2)/25

exactly as in the source. Let a=10^-8. Independently perturb each of

    DeltaA, DeltaB, h, u, gA, gB, lambdaA, lambdaB, mu0, nu

by a real number of absolute value at most a about the source's exact values.
In particular, nu is positive and the signed matching coefficient is always
mu1=-nu. A tilde denotes the resulting actual coefficient or operator.
Use exactly the following full-qubit block structure:

    C = DeltaA nA + DeltaB nB + h(nA+nB-L_AB) + u nA nB,
    G = gA nA(I+X1) + gB nB(I+X2),
    B = K+C+G,
    VA = lambdaA(I+XA)+mu0 I,
    VB = lambdaB(I+XB)-nu I.

The physical vacancy term is nu(I-n_c1), not a negative local block. In the
one-head sector the actual physical Hamiltonian is

    Htilde_phys = h0 tensor I + I tensor Btilde
                 + P0 tensor VAtilde + P1 tensor VBtilde + nutilde I,
    h0 = 2J I-J(S+S*).                                      (1)

All listed positive coefficients remain strictly positive throughout the box.
The nearest-neighbor geometry, positive-block decomposition, head-number
conservation, source-number reduction of B and parity p=X1X2 are unchanged.
For the same vacuum embedding iota x=x tensor |00>_AB,

    Btilde iota = iota K.                                   (2)

This exact structural identity is essential. The box does not include extra
vacuum-leakage terms, changes to K, arbitrary missing bonds, clock disorder,
or a separately perturbed source-vacuum energy. No result about those errors
is inferred. Likewise, varying J or the ready packet is outside this argument.

The elementary operator bounds are

    ||Btilde-B|| <= b := 9a,
    ||VAtilde-VA|| <= c := 3a,
    ||VBtilde-VB|| <= c.                                    (3)

The weights for B are 1,1,2,1,2,2 in the coefficient order
DeltaA,DeltaB,h,u,gA,gB. Here ||nA+nB-L_AB||=2 and
||nA(I+X1)||=||nB(I+X2)||=2 on the full qubit space. Both contact weights
are 2+1. These bounds require no commutation between K and G.

## 2. The closed subspace stays fixed

Work within either eight-dimensional parity sector. For either old energy
b_s=2+s, s in {-1,+1}, the open vacuum vector u=u_(s,p) is the same exact
vector for every coefficient choice, by (2). Set P=|u><u| and Q=I-P.
Since Btilde is selfadjoint and Btilde u=b_s u, both P and Q reduce it.
The incident total energy with its common scalar removed is still

    lambda_s(k)=b_s+epsilon(k), epsilon(k)=2J(1-cos k),
    |k-k0|<=10^-3, k0=2 arctan(1/4.510351185997098).         (4)

Define the decoupled closed half-line tail

    H_Q = h0 tensor I_Q + I tensor (Q B Q)|_Q,

and its tilded version. The source certificate gives
dist(lambda_s(k),spec(H_Q))>=g=9/50 on the whole interval. The perturbation
has norm at most b, so its resolvent Neumann series gives

    ||(H_Q-lambda)^-1|| <= 1/g,
    ||(Htilde_Q-lambda)^-1|| <= 1/(g-b).                    (5)

Equivalently the new closed gap is at least g-b>17/100. This includes the
other old-vacuum channel; it does not rely on a numerical eigenbasis or only
on the six source-excited directions. In fact the other old-vacuum channel
does not change, but the displayed bound safely includes it.

Let E embed the internal closed space at the first tail site. Define the
closed surface matrices

    G_Q(lambda)=E* (H_Q-lambda)^-1 E,
    Gtilde_Q(lambda)=E* (Htilde_Q-lambda)^-1 E.

The resolvent identity yields the finite-matrix norm estimate

    ||Gtilde_Q-G_Q|| <= b/[g(g-b)].                        (6)

The signs in this definition use H_Q-lambda. For a scalar closed channel
with d=2J+b_channel-lambda and decaying root q+q^-1=d/J, solving the
delta-source recurrence at the tail endpoint gives G_Q=q/J. The formula
holds for either real sign of q. Thus the actual outgoing matching function
on the full parity space is

    F(lambda)=lambda-2J-B + J exp(ik) P + J^2 G_Q(lambda),  (7)

where the surface term is extended by zero on P. Its open entry is
-J exp(-ik), and its closed entry is -J/q, exactly the source convention.
Because P,lambda and J are fixed, (3),(6),(7) imply

    ||Ftilde-F|| <= b + J^2 b/[g(g-b)].                    (8)

No differentiability of eigenvectors with respect to coefficient errors is
assumed. Each fixed actual Btilde may subsequently be diagonalized once for
the momentum derivative estimates; (6) compares the physical bases directly.

## 3. Uniform physical matching and reflection perturbation

The source's physical matching matrix and its actual version are

    M(k) = [[lambda-2J-B-VA, J I], [J I, F(lambda)-VB]].

Only the two diagonal blocks change. Hence throughout the full interval,

    ||Mtilde-M|| <= eta := b+c+J^2 b/[g(g-b)].              (9)

The already qualified source gives ||M^-1||<A=166 uniformly. Exact rational
comparisons with a=10^-8 give

    eta < 1.226*10^-7,
    A eta < 1,
    ||Mtilde^-1|| <= A/(1-A eta) < 167.                   (10)

This is a non-grid interval certificate. It remains valid even when the
closed eigenvalues cross one another, because the fixed open subspace stays
separated and the physical-basis resolvents were used.

For site-1 incident normalization, both systems have the same right side

    M x = [0; 2iJ sin(k)u],   ||rhs||<=2J,
    r_site1=<u,x1>-1.

The inverse identity therefore gives, for every old-energy/parity column,

    |rtilde_abs(k)-r_abs(k)|
      <= ||xtilde-x||
      <= e_R := 2J A^2 eta/(1-A eta) < 0.000203.          (11)

    max_(s,p)|rtilde_abs-r0 zeta w_p|

The fixed central calibration residual is retained. The extra term is a
