## 2. Position moment and Sobolev domain: separate derivation

Let S on the integer chain satisfy `(S psi)_n = psi_(n-1)`, and let

```text
h = 2J I - J(S+S*),       X psi(n)=n psi(n),
V = iJ(S-S*).
```

On finitely supported sequences, direct multiplication gives `i[h,X]=V` and `[h,V]=0`. Since the commutator is bounded, h preserves the graph domain of X. One can prove this by the finite-support core and closure, or by the periodic Fourier representation where h is multiplication by the smooth E(k). Differentiating on that domain and using the second commutator gives the exact Heisenberg identity `exp(ith) X exp(-ith)=X+tV`. No position/velocity independence or affine dispersion is used.

Under the candidate's F_plus convention, S has multiplier exp(-ik), hence V has multiplier `2J sin(k)=E'(k)`. Under F_minus its multiplier is `-E'(k)`. Integration by parts gives `X F_plus g=F_plus(i g')` and `X F_minus g=F_minus(-i g')`. A function in H1_0(I) has zero endpoint trace, so its zero extension lies in periodic H1. Multiplication by exp(-itE) preserves that domain for each finite t. These facts justify the identities as equalities of Hilbert-space norms, not merely formal differentiation.

For normalized g and v0=E'(k0)>v, this yields

```text
||(X-v0 t) F_plus(exp(-itE)g)|| = ||i g' + t(E'-v0)g||,
||(X+v0 t) F_minus(exp(-itE)g)|| = ||-i g' - t(E'-v0)g||.
```

On n<0, `|n-v0t| >= v0t`; on n>=0, `|n+v0t| >= v0t`. The two projections are exactly the unwanted outgoing and incoming half-lines. Taking norms, using the Hilbert-space triangle inequality and `|E'-v0| <= 2J sigma`, proves the candidate's equation (10):

```text
wrong-half-line norm <= ||g'||/(v t) + 2J sigma/v.
```

The floor is the cost of one constant center velocity, not a physical nonzero limiting leakage. It cannot establish exact past-limit normalization. The candidate correctly retains the old decay-to-zero integration-by-parts argument and its exact scattering isometry for that purpose. The new inequality is used only for the stated finite-error late-time estimate.

The new finite-support discriminator takes `psi_0=3/5, psi_1=4i/5`. Exact real-space arithmetic gives

```text
<V> = 24J/25 > 0,
||X psi||^2 = 16/25,
||V psi||^2 = 2J^2,
Re <X psi,V psi> = 12J/25,
||(X+tV)psi||^2 = 16/25 + 24Jt/25 + 2J^2 t^2.
```

A reversed propagation sign fails the positive velocity check. Replacing the Hilbert-space triangle step by a Pythagorean sum would omit the nonzero cross term.
