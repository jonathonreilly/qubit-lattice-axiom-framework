## 2. Geometry, positivity and exact local partition

Use the same literal old sites \(1=(0,0,0),2=(1,0,0)\), neighbors \(L=(-1,0,0),R=(2,0,0)\), and \(S=\{L,1,2,R\}\). The finite open box contains the \(6\times3\times3\) box in the accepted argument, so all these sites and their nearest neighbors are present. Every directional edge e carries
\[
 Q_e=j_e(I-P_e),\qquad
 P_e=\sigma_u^{a(e)}\sigma_v^{a(e)},\qquad j_e>0.
 \tag{R2}
\]
Each \(P_e\) is a Hermitian involution. Thus \(Q_e\) is positive, real, two-body, with eigenvalues \(0,0,2j_e,2j_e\) and full-support norm \(2j_e\). Different coefficients do not change its support or Pauli direction. The additional given old \(K=2I+Z_1Z_2\) remains positive with norm three and is installed exactly once.

There are three x edges meeting the old pair, carrying \(J_L,J_0,J_R\), respectively. There are eight old/E transverse edges, four y and four z, whose coefficient sum is b. Separately, there are eight E/E transverse edges at L/R, again four y and four z, whose coefficient sum is c. These sets are disjoint. They are exactly the sixteen transverse edges incident to S. None joins two members of S.

Write
\[
 a_x=J_L+J_0+J_R,\qquad
 B_O=\sum_{\text{8 old/E transverse}}j_eP_e,\qquad
 B_E=\sum_{\text{8 E/E transverse at L/R}}j_eP_e .
 \tag{R3}
\]
The full-support estimates \(\|B_O\|\le b,\|B_E\|\le c\) follow from triangle inequality, without assuming commutation or a preferred input state.

Let \(H_E\) be the actual sum of every E-only block, and \(H_{E,\mathrm{ref}}\) the same sum with only the latter eight E/E blocks omitted. Then
\[
 H_E=H_{E,\mathrm{ref}}+cI_E-B_E.
 \tag{R4}
\]
All terms retained in \(H_{E,\mathrm{ref}}\) that touch L or R are x terms, and hence commute with \(X_L,X_R\). Every other retained term acts away from those endpoints. This is unaffected by arbitrary nonuniform coefficient values. Internal environment terms need not commute with each other.

Define the actual four-site part
\[
 H_4=K+a_xI-J_LX_LX_1-J_0X_1X_2-J_RX_2X_R .
 \tag{R5}
\]
The installed system obeys both exact identities
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 H&=H_4+H_E+bI-B_O\\
  &=H_4+H_{E,\mathrm{ref}}+(b+c)I+V,\qquad
    V=-B_O-B_E,\quad \|V\|\le b+c .
 \end{aligned}                                                   \tag{R6}
\]
The omission is only in the comparison Hamiltonian. No actual edge is removed. \(H_4\) commutes with \(H_{E,\mathrm{ref}}\) because the latter is disjoint from old sites and commutes with the two endpoint controls.

## 3. Unequal exterior x bonds and the complete four-site bound

Decompose by \(X_L=\alpha\), \(X_R=\beta\), \(X_1X_2=p\), with \(\alpha,\beta,p=\pm1\). In the old two-dimensional p sector, set \(Z=Z_1Z_2\), \(X'=X_1\), so \(X_2=pX'\). The operators Z and \(X'\) anticommute and square to I. Therefore
\[
 H_{4,\alpha\beta p}=(2+a_x-J_0p)I+Z-qX',
 \qquad q=J_L\alpha+J_R\beta p,\qquad |q|\le g:=J_L+J_R .
 \tag{R7}
\]
For unequal \(J_L,J_R\), both p sectors can be detuned: their possible magnitudes are \(J_L+J_R\) and \(|J_L-J_R|\). The homogeneous argument's exactly unaffected sector is not assumed. Its all-real-q inequality is the part that survives.

For \(\Omega_q=\sqrt{1+q^2}\), \(N_q=(Z-qX')/\Omega_q\), direct Pauli algebra gives
\[
 \|N_q-Z\|^2=2-2/\Omega_q
      =\frac{2q^2}{\Omega_q(\Omega_q+1)}\le q^2 .
 \tag{R8}
\]
Comparing first at equal frequency and then comparing frequencies,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \|e^{-it(Z-qX')}-e^{-it\Omega_qZ}\|
   &=|\sin(\Omega_qt)|\,\|N_q-Z\|\le|q|,\\
 \|e^{-it\Omega_qZ}-e^{-itZ}\|
   &=2|\sin[(\Omega_q-1)t/2]|
     \le(\Omega_q-1)t\le q^2t/2 .
 \end{aligned}                                                   \tag{R9}
\]
These are exact inequalities for every real q and \(t\ge0\), not a truncated small-q expansion. Taking the maximum over all \(\alpha,\beta,p\) gives detuning cost at most \(g+g^2t/2\).

The literal \(W=e^{i\theta}e^{i\theta X_1X_2}\), with the inherited principal \(\theta\in(\pi/4,\pi/2)\), \(\cos\theta=3/5,\sin\theta=4/5\), includes its scalar. All endpoint signs and their coherent superpositions are covered; neither endpoint is prepared in an X eigenstate. The \(2I\) in K remains inside \(e^{-itK}\).

 2\sum_{e\ni v}j_e\le\Lambda .
