# Native discrete symmetry and a conditional infrared scalar route

Status: conditional-support. This develops the same positive-parity tube and its derived weak-longitudinal effective theory. It does not establish the bulk phase of the isotropic three-dimensional model, select its state, or identify physical matter.

## A discrete symmetry survives exactly

Label the four physical rung sites (0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1). Let S rotate the square by (x,y)->(y,1-x) and rotate each physical spin about Z by pi/2. Let P_x=X_(0,0)X_(0,1), and T=P_x S. Both factors preserve the transverse Hamiltonian. T permutes the four longitudinal D operators with signs; using the same T on every rung therefore preserves every existing interrung bond. This is an exact microscopic global symmetry, not an extra condition imposed on an effective action.

In the low doublet, conjugation by T sends tau1->-tau2, tau2->tau1, tau3->tau3. T_low^4=-I, so its adjoint action has order four. Its tensor product on the low band preserves charge modulo four collective flips (Delta Q0 a multiple of8 for Q0=sumtau3). Canonical polar reduction inherits this symmetry because the low projector and its identification are equivariant. The sixth-order four-feature process is allowed, while two-feature number-changing terms are forbidden. The exact continuous-charge obstruction and this discrete symmetry are consistent.

## Leading long-distance theory is a candidate, not a supplied scalar

The derived leading Hamiltonian is -t/2 sum(XY)_j,j+1 plus a constant, with t=J_z²/(sqrt(2)J_perp). Jordan-Wigner transformation gives hopping -t and dispersion epsilon(k)=-2t cos(k). At half filling its Fermi velocity is 2t a_rung and its standard free continuum limit is a compact boson with K=1. This is the known one-dimensional bosonization limit of the derived leading interaction, not a new proof of bosonization or a full native-model phase theorem.

In conventions H_LL=(v/2pi) integral[K(dtheta)^2+K^(-1)(dphi)^2], translation forbids the half-filled single umklapp vertex, and the native quarter rotation forbids cos(2theta). The first allowed charge-changing vertex is cos(4theta), dimension4/K; the usual double umklapp cos(4phi) has dimension4K. Both have dimension4 at K=1. Thus the standard perturbative infrared analysis permits an emergent continuous symmetry even though no exact analytic microscopic charge exists. This is a conditional route to investigate, not a claim that the finite sixth-order theorem proves gaplessness. Higher terms, locality/uniform control, possible spontaneous ordering and the equal-coupling phase still need checks.

## An exact full-rung state constraint

P_y=Y_(0,0)Y_(1,0) also commutes with the transverse rung H; its signed action on D preserves the longitudinal bond when applied on both rungs. P_x and P_y square to identity and anticommute because the physical supports intersect at one qubit with different Pauli axes. Their adjoint actions realize Z2 x Z2 with a nontrivial projective representation on every eight-state positive-parity rung. This statement does not require the low-doublet approximation.

Consequently the translation-invariant infinite positive-parity tube satisfies the premises of the discrete on-site Lieb-Schultz-Mattis theorem of [Ogata, Tachikawa and Tasaki, Theorem4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.06458): finite-dimensional rung Hilbert space, uniformly bounded finite-range interactions, on-site projective symmetry and one-rung translation. It cannot have a unique gapped ground state. This is an application of an established external theorem, not a new proof. It permits symmetry breaking/degeneracy instead of gaplessness and does not assert a neutral scalar pole, choose the parity sector in the full model, or generalize a fixed-width tube to3D. Finite odd-length global anticommutation yields a separate exact spectral doublet; finite even-length behavior must not be confused with the infinite-volume theorem.
