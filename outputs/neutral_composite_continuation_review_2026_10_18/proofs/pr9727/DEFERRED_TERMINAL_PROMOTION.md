# Deferred terminal promotion

Status: inspectable supporting science; terminal negative promotion is packet-pending and is not an active claim of the canonical constructive note. This is a procedural readiness boundary, not uncertainty about the exact identities under their stated hypotheses. No route coverage is invented. The complete original proof extracts in this directory remain live; they are not premises for a stronger physical exclusion.

The centered Oπ is reflection even. In a reflection-positive ground state it
cannot excite the even-edge/D branch, at any order that preserves this symmetry.
Different states or sources need not obey this restriction.

Exact root counting gives
two admissible roots, corresponding to κ1=.162943892684775 and
κ2=1.045214358255971. The conditional operator has two modes strictly above its
continuum for |κ|<κ1, one for κ1≤|κ|<κ2, and none for |κ|≥κ2.

This count follows beyond a finite truncation. M(κ)−2κI decreases as a quadratic
form: its derivative on the host is T+T†−2I and on D2 is−2. At zero there are two
upper modes. For κ>sqrt3, eliminating D2 and using |f1|²≤2|f0|²+2|f1−f0|² bounds
the quadratic form by

    [1−κ+2/(1+κ)]|f0|² + [−κ+2/(1+κ)]|f1−f0|²
        −κ sum_(n≥1)|f_(n+1)−f_n|² < 0.

Thus both modes must disappear; the two simple boundary determinant zeros are
the only thresholds. At either threshold the constant tail is non-normalizable.
Negative κ is unitarily equivalent. This is an exact count for M, not for native H.
