# Complete plane-neutral operator algebra

Status: conditional-support (exact finite algebra for the supplied compass candidate). Root derivation, checked independently. No additional Hamiltonian interaction.

On an open Lx by Ly by Lz rectangular grid with each dimension at least2, represent a Pauli string, ignoring its phase, by binary fields u(x,y,z),v(x,y,z), where X=(1,0), Z=(0,1), Y=(1,1). A string commuting with every native x bond must have v constant along x. Commutation with every z bond makes u constant along z. Commutation with each y bond then makes u+v constant along y. Write u=u(x,y),v=v(y,z). Comparing any y with a fixed y0 gives

u(x,y)+u(x,y0)=v(y,z)+v(y0,z).

The left side is independent of z and the right independent of x, so both equal a function B(y). Hence u=A(x)+B(y), v=C(z)+B(y). Conversely these fields commute with every native bond. The sole parameter redundancy is adding the same binary constant to A,B,C. Therefore the Pauli commutant of the native bond span has dimension Lx+Ly+Lz-1 and is generated exactly by the X-normal, Y-normal and Z-normal plane operators.

The binary Pauli space has a nondegenerate symplectic commutator form. Taking the double orthogonal gives: the span of native bonds is exactly the commutant of all plane operators. Every fully plane-neutral Pauli string is therefore a finite product of native bond operators, up to phase. Its binary dimension is 2N-(Lx+Ly+Lz-1). This is a complete finite operator-algebra classification, not a truncation chosen to obtain a scalar.

It does NOT show that a polynomial of bonds is a term in H, that its nested commutators are generated by H from a specified source, or that it forms a stable propagating mode. Associative algebra generation must not be confused with Lie/Krylov reachability. Cubic-scalar classification and the actual spectrum remain separate tests.
