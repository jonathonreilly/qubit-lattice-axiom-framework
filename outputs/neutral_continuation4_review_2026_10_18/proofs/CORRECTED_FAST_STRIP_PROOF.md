# Root completion of corrected-arm strip route

Work in the prescribed strip eta/f bounded above/below, f=lambda^3/16, x=-6lambda+O(lambda^(7/3)). Let epsilon=lambda/4, T=S+S*, v=f(n+1)-2lambda, A=(sqrt(1+epsilon(v-x))-1)/epsilon, D=2+epsilon A, L=T-A, C=D+epsilon T. All operators below first defined on finite support; uniform finite-section bounds give domain-safe closure. D,C>=c>0 for small lambda.

Exact H0-x=-(CL+LC)/2, H=H0-epsilon P1. Since increments A_(n+1)-A_n=O(f) globally, [C,L]=-2epsilon[T,A] boundedO(epsilon f), and B=[C,[C,L]]=-2epsilon²([T,[T,A]]+[A,[T,A]]) boundedO(epsilon² f). The half-line endpoint in the first nested commutator is included.

For positive C, sqrt functional calculus gives [sqrt C,L]=(1/pi) integral_0^infty t^(1/2)(C+t)^-1[C,L](C+t)^-1 dt. Apply the same formula to [sqrt C,[C,L]] and commute resolvents with sqrt C. The double integral bounds ||[sqrt C,[sqrt C,L]]|| <= ||B||/(4c), using integral t^(1/2)/(c+t)^2=pi/(2sqrt c). Hence H0-x=-sqrt C L sqrt C+R, ||R||=O(epsilon²f). This is an actual bounded remainder, unlike a formal O(lambda) perturbation.

Set J=sqrt D L sqrt D, U=sqrt C D^(-1/2). J is a self-adjoint Jacobi operator (diagonal grows linearly, offdiagonal O(sqrt n), Carleman sum diverges). U and U^-1 are bounded I+O(epsilon), using ||sqrt C-sqrt D||=O(epsilon). Then H0-x=-U J U*+R. Congruence by U^-1 gives the resolvent of J-i eta M-E, with M=U^-1 U^-*=sqrt D C^-1 sqrt D=I+O(epsilon), E=U^-1 R U^-*=O(epsilon² f). Relative to J-i eta its bounded perturbation is O(epsilon eta). Its Neumann resolvent series therefore converges uniformly in global norm after multiplication by the base resolvent, since eta~f.

Crucial LOCAL estimate: base NN strip proof on first M0~c0/f sites applies to J. There D bounded above/below, all coefficients varyO(f), and the local energy stays a fixed distance inside the NN band. It yields |g11|<=C and Im g11>=c. Fixed finite endpoint Green matrices are uniformly bounded by the finite Jacobi recurrence. For any fixed-supported source v, ||(J-i eta)^-1v||²=Im<v,(J-i eta)^-1v>/eta=O(1/eta). The adjoint norm is the same. The resolvent identity with perturbation O(epsilon eta) then gives LOCAL matrix correctionO(epsilon), not O(epsilon/eta).

Transformed source U^-1 delta_j=sqrt D C^-1/2 delta_j is not finitely supported. However expand (C+t)^-1 around diagonal D+t in the integral for C^-1/2. Terms of order k move at most k sites. For any fixed K>=1, tail beyond j+K has norm <=C_K epsilon^K. The weighted factor sqrt D is controlled by ||sqrt D(D+t)^-1||<=(c+t)^-1/2; the remaining k resolvents yield integrable t^-1/2(c+t)^(-k-1/2). Thus truncating at a fixed K>=8 loses O(epsilon^K/eta)=o(1) even with the crude global resolvent bound. Its finite part differs from delta_j byO(epsilon). This closes the previously exposed nonlocal-source gap without supposing lambda<<eta.

For j=1,2 the transformed boundary Green matrix is consequently uniformly bounded. For w=(-2,-epsilon), the limiting source is -2delta1, so its dissipative quadratic form stays bounded below by a positive constant. Finally -epsilon P1 is handled by the finite-rank resolvent formula: 1+epsilon g11 stays uniformly away from zero, all local correctionsO(epsilon). The corrected physical arm therefore has bounded2x2 boundary Green matrix and Im(w*g w)>=c>0 uniformly on the stated strip. No positive-definite2x2 dissipative lower bound is claimed.

## Explicit variable-Jacobi strip step
Write J with diagonal b_n=-D_n A_n and positive offdiagonal a_n=sqrt(D_n D_(n+1)). On 1<=n<=M=floor(c0/f), choose fixed small c0 so |b_n|<=1 and 3/2<=a_n<=5/2. Coefficients change by O(f). Introduce a_0=a_1 for the boundary recurrence and u_0=-1/a_0 for the resolvent column of J-i eta (a sign convention changes neither bounds). The transfer matrices have determinant a_(n-1)/a_n, telescoping over any prefix; their real elliptic eigenvalues have moduli sqrt(a_(n-1)/a_n)=1+O(f). Uniform band margin bounds diagonalizers and their inverses, with consecutive changes O(f). The complex perturbation is O(eta). Hence prefix and inverse products are bounded by C exp(C M(f+eta)) uniformly. Summing |u_n|² over this segment yields Im g11 >= c eta M (|g11|²+1/a_0²), up to the resolvent sign convention. As eta/f is bounded above and below, this bounds |g11| and bounds its positive imaginary part away from zero. Resolvent recurrences express every fixed endpoint matrix entry g_ij through g11 and finitely many bounded coefficients; therefore those entries remain bounded. This proves the base variable-Jacobi step used above, without invoking an adiabatic approximation or a statement about the remote tail. Infinite J is self-adjoint since its offdiagonal grows at most sqrt(n), so the Carleman reciprocal sum diverges. The finite-section proof is uniform once the section contains M+1 sites.
