# Independent 4 by 6, odd-height-five coefficient check

The requested coefficients agree with an independent exact rational subset recursion and explicit boundary counting. Control script: `/private/tmp/neutral_rectangle_coefficient_check.py`. This check certifies the specified coefficient calculation; the physical effective-source connection is a separate premise under review.

## Height-five tunneling derivative

For two aligned open columns, use the exact virtual-subset energy cost

Delta(S;s)=4 Jz n1(S)-4 s n2(S),

where n1 and n2 count nearest- and distance-two longitudinal edges crossing the flipped subset. This follows from -2 Jz sum ZZ +2s sum Z_j Z_(j+2). The source parameter is only a differentiation device.

Starting A(empty)=1, sum A(S minus j)/Delta(S;s) over j in S for proper subsets; at the full set omit the final denominator. Differentiate this same recursion, including derivative of every denominator. Exact Fraction arithmetic at Jz=1 gives t/g^5=1/16 and t'/t=9/2. Restoring units, t=Jz lambda^5/16 and t'/t=9/(2Jz), at fixed transverse microscopic coupling and Jz.

## Open-boundary plaquette coefficient

For a same-layer square, K_j=Jx^2 Jy^2/(2 d_j^3), d_j=2n1(j)Jz-2s n2(j). Thus K_j'=6 n2(j) K_j/d_j, with positive sign. The energy term is -K_j P, so its source operator contribution is -K_j' P.

At height five, (n1,n2) by layer are (1,1),(2,1),(2,2),(2,1),(1,1). In Jz=1 units the K_j coefficients are 1/16,1/128,1/128,1/128,1/16. Their derivatives are 3/16,3/256,3/128,3/256,3/16. Consequently K=19 Jz lambda^4/128, K'=27 lambda^4/64, K'/K=54/(19Jz). This is boundary counting, not a fit in column length.

## Rectangular neutral block

With four rows of circumference six and six columns of circumference four, the matching formula gives

tau_row=12t^3/(8K)^2=3 Jz lambda^7/1444,
tau_col=t^2/(2K)=Jz lambda^6/76.

The maximal neutral block is H=-aX-bZ, a=4tau_row, b=6tau_col. Its gap is 2sqrt(a^2+b^2), whose leading coefficient is 3 Jz lambda^6/19.

Let r3=3t'/t-2K'/K and r2=2t'/t-K'/K. Exact values are r3=297/(38Jz), r2=117/(19Jz), and r3-r2=63/(38Jz). For the derivative operator -a'X-b'Z, the off-diagonal amplitude magnitude is

ab |r3-r2|/sqrt(a^2+b^2).

Its leading value is 189 lambda^7/13718, and its squared weight is (35721/188183524)lambda^14. Signs of an individual transition matrix element depend on eigenvector phases; the magnitude and weight do not.

Unlike the square, the rectangle has no exact spatial interchange symmetry exchanging these two tunneling processes. Their unequal logarithmic source derivatives therefore yield a nonzero transition in the coefficient-source model. This is a neutral transition, not a global-gap assertion. It is also not by itself a proof that the full original native readout has this coefficient: a parameter-dependent block transformation can add a connection commutator to the physical effective operator. That issue must be controlled separately, especially because the retained line is very small. The calculations here neither assume away that connection nor certify higher-order microscopic corrections relative to lambda^7.
