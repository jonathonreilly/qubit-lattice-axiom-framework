# Root structural derivation: fixed-order native long arms

Take a fixed order m>=4 local normal form of H=2N+lambda V, with N commuting longitudinal wall number and V the existing XX/YY bonds. Work in an infinite bulk coefficient limit (or finite boxes with both ends far from walls), an all-up background, compact E8 configurations: two adjacent x columns flipped over the same longitudinal interval of length ell. These are basis configurations, not asserted ground states. Compress the effective D<=m to their span and take longitudinal center-of-mass momentum zero. Subtract the background scalar energy coefficientwise.

Each coefficient is a sum of translates of connected bounded-support terms. Justification: starting bonds are connected; a commutator vanishes unless supports overlap; the local P/S inverse only adds adjacent z bonds. At fixed order there is a uniform support diameter rho_m and finite cardinality. Recursive subtraction/projection preserves connected-cluster decompositions. This statement concerns finite Taylor coefficients, not the untruncated exponential interactions.

For ell>2 rho_m, a connected term cannot touch both longitudinal ends. A nonzero offdiagonal matrix element to another compact adjacent-column interval must change spins at an endpoint. Changing its transverse separation/orientation requires changing at least 2ell spins and is impossible beyond the fixed support bound. Thus all surviving transitions on this long arm move one common endpoint by finitely many steps. Their amplitudes are independent of ell by bulk translation invariance; at q=0 the two-end contributions add. Reality of H and the real homological inverse makes these amplitudes real in this fixed x-oriented basis. No physical x/y scalar symmetry claim is needed for this restricted statement.

A diagonal connected term wholly inside the flipped region has a fixed difference from its background value; wholly outside has zero difference. Counting its allowed longitudinal translates yields a term linear in ell plus an endpoint constant. Terms meeting either endpoint give only constants once ell>2rho_m. Hence the compressed long-arm matrix is exactly affine diagonal plus a finite-range real symmetric Toeplitz hopping, at every fixed order. All exceptional short-ell entries form a matrix supported within a fixed endpoint segment (the maximum hop range adds a fixed amount).

Every real symmetric finite-range Toeplitz halfline operator is a polynomial p(T), T=S+S*, modulo such a finite endpoint matrix: S^r+S*^r=2 Chebyshev_r(T/2) plus endpoint corrections. Therefore, in fast units lambda Jz, the native finite-order compressed arm has
  H_arm=p_lambda(T)+f_lambda(n+1)+a_lambda+F_lambda.
The already derived K1 gives p_lambda=-2T+O(lambda); K4 bulk tension gives f_lambda=lambda^3/16+O(lambda^4)>0 for sufficiently small positive lambda at fixed m. At lambda=0 the native endpoint is the ordinary halfline adjacency, so F_lambda=O(lambda). Additive scalar shifts are absorbed in a_lambda. The general polynomial-strip lemma therefore covers ALL finite-order corrections to this compressed long arm, at x-a_lambda=O(lambda), eta/f_lambda in a fixed positive compact interval.
