# Root: exact plane-charge mobility boundary and remaining constructive channel

Supplied 3D compass H. Even periodic cubes. Plane generators Gx(x)=prod_yz X, Gy(y)=prod_xz Y, Gz(z)=prod_xy Z commute pairwise (even intersection lengths). Actual ground band from previous theorem has Gz=+1 and one state per allowed Gx/Gy character. A chosen ground character or symmetry-invariant mixture is invariant under all G. General coherent ground superposition need not be; do not silently extend.

Any local Pauli string P has charge vectors cx(x)=parity of Y/Z sites in xplane, cy(y)=parity of X/Z sites in yplane, cz(z)=parity of X/Y sites in zplane. H preserves each charge. For invariant rho, <P(t)Q> and retarded cross response vanish unless all three charge vectors match. This is exact for any size/coupling/state commuting symmetries, not a weak-coupling expansion. Decompose arbitrary local A into character components by finite local conjugation averages; only matching components contribute.

Translate same local template by displacement d. Each charge pattern shifts only in its corresponding axis. A nonempty finite charge pattern cannot equal a distinct small translation of itself on an infinite line; on the finite torus, if its support width w<L/2, translations preserving it are only0 modL. Proof a nonzero cyclic translation orbit spreads across at least half the circle; no orbit fits in proper interval width<L/2. Thus if cx nonzero, translated-template response requires dx=0; similarlycy,cz. Neutral templates escape selection rule but have the preceding gapped short-range ground response. Charged template can propagate along at most two axes. LocalZ charges one xplane andone yplane, hence onlyz; X onlyx; Y onlyy. ZZ transverse dipole separatedx atsamey,z has two xplanecharges, otherchargevectorszero, hence yz-plane response allowed. Larger bounded features do not remove these exact charges by size alone; neutralization changes the sector.

This is known subsystem symmetry/dimensional-reduction structure, not a novel Hamiltonian theorem. It does not prove lack of all3D physics: composites can be neutral, states can break symmetry, feature shape can change, nonlocal dressing can carry charge, and excited backgrounds differ. It does constrain using a single bounded charged template as an isotropically propagating low-energy scalar in invariant ground states.
