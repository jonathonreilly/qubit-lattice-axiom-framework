# All fixed orders: bulk rectangular cost lives near corners

Fix order m. Every local coefficient has finite transverse range R_m and finite longitudinal range. The chosen construction is plane-equivariant: H0 and V commute with each physical plane symmetry; homological projection/inversion is a function or finite integral of ad_N, with N=(H0-Evac)/(2Jz), so it commutes with those symmetry actions. Commutators and the iterative unitary conjugations preserve this property by induction. The usual diagonal-function symmetry is therefore derived in this construction, not imposed on a chosen effective model.

In a locally longitudinally constant Z reference, each diagonal coefficient is a finite-range polynomial in column signs s_xy. Plane X and Y operations flip entire x or y rows; any basis phases cancel in diagonal expectations. Collecting its unique Walsh coefficients therefore leaves only monomials with even incidence in every x and every y slice. Collection/symmetry averaging does not enlarge spatial support.

For a rectangular flipped set I_x times I_y, an allowed monomial changes sign only if it intersects both an x boundary and a y boundary. If it crosses no y boundary, its overlap parity is a sum of even x-slice incidences or zero; the analogous statement holds for x. Consequently every contributing monomial lies within range R_m of a corner. When all four corners are separated beyond2R_m, including across periodic seams, no area/perimeter term survives at that fixed order. On a homogeneous background and translation-invariant lattice, each separated corner contribution is independent of the side lengths. On arbitrary backgrounds only the corner-support statement holds, since moving corners samples different signs.

This is a separate coefficient theorem for each finite m. It does not sum the series, provide uniformity as m grows, or include endpoint layers. The number/norm of cluster types depends on m; O(R_m²) counts possible anchors per corner, not a universal coefficient bound.

At m4, exactly four bad plaquettes give tension8kappa=Jz lambda^4/16 for any nonwrapping rectangle. Meanwhile its finite longitudinal segment has two broken z bonds per flipped column, hence H0 endpoint energy4Jz A for area A. A rectangle with both transverse widths even can be created by a plane-neutral product of physical X operators: Y-plane intersections have an even number of flips and each Z plane has even area. This class is symmetry-allowed, not thereby an eigenmode or a low-order readout excitation.

The different area scaling does not test the equivalence principle: neither diagonal tension nor this selected pattern has been identified with gravitational scalar charge or a physical particle mass.
