# Focused bulk four-wall source-channel check

The proposed leading source-connected operator is correct for a specified uniformly polarized Z reference, zero total momentum in all three directions, and the appropriate square/reflection scalar symmetry. It is not the complete scalar E0+8Jz band. The background-state and frozen-channel qualifications below are essential. This is a mathematical projection check, not a bulk native mode theorem.

## Four longitudinal walls and plane parity

Assume finite excitations above a fixed column-aligned reference, with the same reference at both z infinities (or use sufficiently large periodic z first). Each broken longitudinal Ising bond costs2Jz. In the relative even-parity sector of every z plane, an E0+8Jz configuration has exactly four broken bonds.

Every nonempty finitely flipped column has at least two walls. A single column with two disjoint flipped intervals would have four walls but violates plane parity wherever it is flipped. Thus there must be exactly two flipped columns, each with one interval. Even parity on each plane forces their occupied z sets to coincide. They are therefore two distinct transverse columns flipped on one common finite z interval; their transverse separation is unrestricted. This statement does not include noncompact changes of background or different asymptotic vacua.

An infinite product of physical plane-Z operators needs interpretation. The relative parity of finite excitations is well-defined, or one can impose exact parity on a finite periodic transverse grid before taking its limit. Do not use an undefined literal infinite product without this qualification.

## First-order transitions

For interval length1, transverse XX/YY exchange moves either of the two flipped spins to a vacant neighboring site in its plane. On the uniformly polarized reference, an occupied/vacant pair has opposite physical Z spins, and both XX and YY exchange have matrix element+1; the Hamiltonian -Jperp bond therefore gives hopping-Jperp. Moving onto the other occupied site annihilates the pair and leaves this energy band, so the projected particles are hard core.

For interval length>=2, moving just one flipped spin to a different column creates additional longitudinal walls and leaves the band. The only nontrivial first-order moves retain both original columns and extend/contract their common interval by one layer. They exist only when the two columns are nearest neighbors so that a native transverse edge flips both spins. An X edge has creation/annihilation amplitude-Jperp; a Y edge on two parallel physical spins has amplitude+Jperp. Internal deletions split intervals and leave the band.

All non-nearest-neighbor column pairs with interval length>=2 remain in E0+8Jz but are frozen at K1. They can form the same square/reflection symmetry as the source; no symmetry removes them. They are disconnected from this particular source component only at first order. Higher-order terms may couple them back. Consequently the proposed host-plus-half-line is a source-connected K1 block, not an exhaustive full-band closure.

## Relative-coordinate normalization

At zero transverse center-of-mass momentum, the two equivalent single-spin moves produce relative-coordinate hopping-2 in Jperp units on Z² excluding the origin. Identical spin flips require wavefunctions even under r→-r. One may use the ordinary l2 inner product on even relative functions by embedding the normalized physical unordered-pair orbit into the normalized vector (|r>+|-r>)/sqrt(2).

The normalized endpoint vector is therefore

d=(|e_x>+|-e_x>-|e_y>-|-e_y>)/2,

with norm one. Equivalently it is the difference of normalized x- and y-oriented nearest-neighbor pair orbits, divided by sqrt(2). No further sqrt(2) should be inserted in its coupling.

The d_x²-y² sector is odd under the pure spatial quarter rotation and even under transverse coordinate reflections. Every function in this sector vanishes at the origin, and the sum of its nearest-neighbor values is zero. Thus its relative tight-binding Hamiltonian neither populates nor couples through the origin. Within this sector, removing the hard-core site has no additional effect. A quarter-rotation character alone would also allow the d_xy reflection character; the scalar source/reflection assumptions specify the former.

## Physical scalar symmetry and interval chain

A physical square rotation accompanied by the native spin-Z rotation contributes phase(-1)^ell to a configuration with2ell reversed spins relative to the uniform polarized vacuum. For undirected x/y adjacent-column interval orbits, the scalar combination is

E_ell=(|x,ell>+(-1)^ell |y,ell>)/sqrt(2).

At ell1 this is precisely d. At zero longitudinal center-of-mass momentum, adding at either interval end gives total amplitude-2 for x and+2 for y. Hence

<E_(ell+1)|K1|E_ell>=(-2+2*(-1)^(ell+1)*(-1)^ell)/2=-2.

This also gives the coupling between E2 and d as-2. The resulting source-connected operator consists of the two-dimensional relative hopping-2 host in the specified d-wave symmetry plus sites E2,E3,... with hopping-2 and endpoint coupling-2 to d. Nonzero longitudinal total momentum would introduce end-move cosine factors and must be treated separately.

## Background state is a genuine premise

Strong longitudinal coupling alone does not select the uniform transverse reference: every assignment of column signs is an unperturbed aligned vacuum. Fixed even z-plane parity does not eliminate that degeneracy.

For a nonuniform background with column signs sigma_c, a relative-flip hop on a Y edge acquires the factor sigma_a sigma_b relative to the uniform hopping convention; X-edge hopping is unchanged. Around a transverse plaquette the resulting hopping-sign flux is the four-column background parity. Generic backgrounds therefore need not yield the uniform zero-flux two-dimensional host or its simple scalar source. Some backgrounds can be gauge equivalent, but that equivalence and the observable transformation must be shown. The calculation must explicitly retain a prepared uniform/gauge-equivalent background rather than presenting the host as a consequence of plane parity alone.

No new interaction is added by this projection, but a background state, source symmetry and first-order source component have been specified. The calculation does not establish isolation of this component under higher orders, a stable bulk excitation, global ground-state selection, a physical scalar or a cosmological connection. The parent's planned independent finite native-band control can test these first-order matrix elements, not replace those further obligations.
