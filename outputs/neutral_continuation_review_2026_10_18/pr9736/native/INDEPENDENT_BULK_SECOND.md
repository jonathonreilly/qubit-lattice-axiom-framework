# Independent local derivation of the bulk second-order interval row

Checked `/private/tmp/neutral_bulk_second.py` and its ell=3,4,5,6 output. The following all-length argument independently establishes the reported row for every ell>=3 in the stated uniform strong-Z reference and even relative parity per longitudinal plane. No repository files changed.

## Perturbative convention

With Jz set to one in denominators, the canonical second-order block is PVQ(8-H0)^-1QVP. Thus a first flip with positive energy cost delta contributes minus the product of its two matrix elements divided by delta. Intermediate states in P (delta=0) are excluded: their repeated first-order motion is already generated by K1, not an extra K2 matrix element. A nonzero PVP does not introduce an additional second-order folded term in this canonical convention.

## Diagonal: exact local counting

For an occupied interval z=0,...,ell-1 on two neighboring columns, an individual spin flip has longitudinal cost zero on each of the four layers z=-1,0,ell-1,ell, and cost four elsewhere. These four layers are distinct for ell>=3. On each layer:

* The transverse edge joining the two occupied columns has cost zero, so its diagonal Q contribution vanishes; relative to the vacuum contribution -1/8 this gives +1/8.
* Six transverse edges each touch exactly one occupied column and have cost four. Each contributes -1/4 instead of the vacuum -1/8, a difference -1/8.
* Every other edge retains vacuum cost eight.

Therefore the entire vacuum-subtracted diagonal is 4*(1-6)/8=-5/2. XX and YY repeated-edge matrix-element products are both +1. There is no ell-dependent interior term at this order.

## Complete off-diagonal classification for this row

Four longitudinal walls in the finite-excitation, even-plane-parity sector mean precisely two distinct columns carrying the same nonempty finite interval. Two transverse bond flips change at most four physical spins.

For an initial interval of length at least three, any final allowed configuration on a different column pair differs in more than four spins. For example, if exactly one column is shared, even a final one-layer interval differs in at least 2*ell spins. Thus all accessible distinct final E8 configurations retain the same column pair.

For that pair, at most two layers can change. A one-layer change cannot be achieved by two distinct nearest-neighbor transverse edges: their symmetric difference is a distance-two pair if they share a vertex, or four sites if disjoint; it cannot equal the original nearest-neighbor pair. The remaining possibilities are two changes at one endpoint or one change at each endpoint.

Two changes at opposite endpoints have only P intermediate states (each one-layer endpoint motion costs zero), hence no K2 contribution. For extension or contraction by two layers at the same endpoint, one ordering has a P intermediate and is excluded. The other first flips the farther layer, costs eight, and gives -1/8. Both XX amplitudes are -1, or both YY amplitudes are +1 for the initially parallel pair, so the product is +1. This remains true at ell=3: contraction by two leaves a legitimate single-layer state, and the first interior-layer flip still costs eight.

Consequently the only off-diagonal entries are extension/contraction by two at either endpoint, each -1/8. No cancellation among additional remote channels needs to be presumed: the E8 classification and support bound exclude them. Remote repeated-edge diagonal contributions cancel exactly against the vacuum subtraction.

## Box and longitudinal momentum

The 6x5 transverse box puts the initial pair two lattice steps from the relevant exterior, and the longitudinal margin of three contains every two-layer extension plus its nearest longitudinal neighbors. The retained external up spins in `de` implement the uniform reference. All diagonal changes occur on incident edges; every possible two-step off-diagonal support lies within these margins. Thus these particular rows are unaffected by omitted exterior transverse edges, whose diagonal contribution is the identical vacuum constant.

At total longitudinal momentum zero, the two endpoint motions add to -1/4 for ell->ell+/-2. The long-interval K2 symbol is -5/2-(1/2)cos(2p), with bottom -3 at p=0. Accordingly the long-arm edge receives -3 Jperp^2/Jz, in addition to the first-order edge -4 Jperp. This is a bulk-arm coefficient, not a claim that the short-interval host or boundary has the same K2 structure.

## Scope for the resonance calculation

This is an all-ell row statement for ell>=3 on an adjacent pair in the specified reference. It does not establish every other row of the full E8 block. In particular the ell=1 host, ell=2 boundary, other column separations, reference-state selection, and higher-order mixing still require their own treatment. K2 also has a dispersive correction around its edge; even when the constant -3 shift is removed, that correction must be included at whichever higher precision is claimed. Absolute native resonance energies cannot be read from K1 plus the proposed string tension alone.
