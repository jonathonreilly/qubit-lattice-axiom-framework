For the periodic3×3×4 physical lattice, the proposed nine commuting generators have binary rank8, not9. Their dependence is

    (product of three X planes)(product of three Y planes)
      = i^36 Z_all = Z_all

The full plane-Pauli span has rank9 and symplectic rank2, so this rank8 commuting subgroup is maximal within the plane algebra; maximal does not make all nine listed generators independent.

Physical translations preserve this sector. Transverse translations permute the X or Y plane generators. Longitudinal translation permutes the even-Z-plane-product subgroup; the wrapping pair Z3Z0 is the product of the three listed adjacent pairs. All positive signs are preserved. Consequently physical translations act unitarily within the sector and commute with the uniformly coupled periodic native H. Exact signed logical-coordinate maps must still implement this physical action and its group law; sector preservation alone does not imply their signs may be discarded.
