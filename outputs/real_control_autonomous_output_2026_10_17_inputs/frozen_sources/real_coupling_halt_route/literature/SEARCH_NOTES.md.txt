## Matched sources and limits

**Cubitt, Montanaro, Piddock, Universal Quantum Hamiltonians.**
[arXiv:1701.05182v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1701.05182v4), dated 2019-05-31.
The complete relevant state-encoding discussion in section 7.1 and
complex-to-real lemma in section 7.2, equations (25)-(35), were read.
Their conjugate-sector construction requires appropriate state encoding for
dynamics; a simple extra flag can cost locality. This is the matching
architecture. The later general local-encoding theorem was not fully read
and is not imported. Version v4 identifies a time-evolution correction
relative to the published version.

**Childs, Universal computation by quantum walk.**
[arXiv:0806.1972v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0806.1972v1), dated 2008-06-12.
The introduction, scattering setup and complete section VII universal-computer
protocol were read. The construction uses real unweighted adjacency and can
start from one vertex. Encoded wires, filters, measurement timing and its
success/restart protocol are part of the construction. It is therefore a live
counterexample to a broad inference that real couplings or a local initial
vertex cannot compute. It does not directly prove a near-unit complete-output
halt on the two literal old qubits with their fixed K. No gate/network theorem
from the paper is applied to this apparatus.

**Nagaj and Wocjan, Hamiltonian Quantum Cellular Automata in 1D.**
[arXiv:0802.0886v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0802.0886v4), dated 2008-08-14;
[publisher record](https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.78.032311).
The abstract/introduction and the selected random-time output/readout span
were read, not the whole proof. This is context for autonomous computation
from a basis-program state. Its ten-dimensional local system and readout
protocol do not directly establish this qubit, literal-old full-isometry
contract. No assertion about all its interaction coefficients being real is
needed here or inferred from the inspected spans.

**Watson, The Effect of Final State Interactions on Reaction Cross Sections.**
[Physical Review 88, 1163 (1952)](https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.88.1163).
Only the publisher abstract was read. It motivates credit for the familiar
connection between production phases and scattering phases. Equation (27)
in ROUTE.md is instead derived directly under explicitly stated realness,
one-channel and uniqueness hypotheses; this abstract supplies no theorem
used in the proof.
