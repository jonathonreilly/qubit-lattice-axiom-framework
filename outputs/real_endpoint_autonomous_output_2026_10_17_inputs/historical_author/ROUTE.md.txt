positive local Hamiltonian model. Two copies of the **clock arm** share one
literal old pair and one source pair. A real two-site phase filter on one arm
and a real Hadamard junction turn the qualified boundary gate into an

real endpoint emitter then supplies the input from a delivered real product
seed, with no timed preparation or runtime controls.

The new mathematical conclusion proposed here is:

* With the exact coefficients, kappa=10^-12, sigma=10^-6, and the finite core
  specified below, infinite complete isometry error is below 0.0013 for every
  elapsed t>=T=10^136.
* Choose the **same literal finite apparatus before delivery** with M=50T
  output-clock sites. Complete isometry error is below 0.0023 only on
  t in [T,2T]. It uses M+116=5*10^137+116 qubits, including old/source sites.
  The corresponding complete unhalved trace distances are below 0.0026 and
  0.0046. No apparatus of this size was allocated or simulated.

vacancy scalar, all elapsed old aging, all clock/emitter/source outputs, and
an arbitrary passive reference. The seed is |1> on one emitter, blank clock

amplitude is bounded, not discarded. The positive central calibration ceiling
