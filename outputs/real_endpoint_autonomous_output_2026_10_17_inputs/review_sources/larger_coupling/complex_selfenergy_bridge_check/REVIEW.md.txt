## 1. Premises actually consumed

For every real x in the unchanged I, the exact pre-emitter endpoint matrix G is holomorphic on a neighborhood of the closed disk |w-x|<=rho, rho=10^-12. It has the exact decomposition

    G(w)=g(w)P-Qc(w)/J+DeltaG(w),
    g(w)=-(exp(iw)/J)
           [1+R_bg(w)exp(40iw)]/[1+R_bg(w)exp(42iw)],

with fixed orthogonal open/complementary projections, .03<J<.031, |R_bg|<2e-5, ||Qc||<.15 and ||DeltaG||<2e-21 on each disk. The chosen analytic branches and fixed real internal basis are part of these premises. The real complementary bare emitter gap exceeds .18 and |E'(w)|<.062 there.

There is no need to differentiate the real port cancellation or prove a real energy derivative bound to establish this bridge. Conversely, such a real derivative bound alone would not replace the disk hypotheses.

## 2. Uniform matrix bound

Reverse triangle inequality gives

    |g(w)| <= exp(rho)/J
       *[1+2e-5 exp(40rho)]/[1-2e-5 exp(42rho)] <34.

The denominator is positive and very far from zero; the same displayed bound remains <34 using exp(42rho)<1.000000001 and 1/J<100/3. Also ||Qc/J||<.15/.03=5. Since gP and -Qc/J act on orthogonal fixed subspaces, the norm of their direct sum is their maximum. Consequently ||G||<34+2e-21<35, and the proposed loose bound 36 is valid. Summing 34 and 5 would unnecessarily lose this useful constant; no channel-count factor is needed.

## 3. Operator Cauchy and the whole narrow rectangle

Expand G(x+z)=sum_(n>=0) A_n z^n. The operator-valued Cauchy integral, or scalar matrix elements with independent unit dual vectors followed by the supremum, gives ||A_n||<=36/rho^n. For |z|<=h=10^-18,

    ||G(x+z)-G(x)||
      <=sum_(n>=1)36(h/rho)^n
       =36*10^-6/(1-10^-6)

Holomorphy on a neighborhood of the closed disk permits the circle radius rho directly; if only an open-disk formulation were used, taking inner radii to rho yields the same bound. The hypotheses provide a disk centered at every x in I, including both endpoints. Hence (1) covers the bottom and both vertical sides of the desired lower rectangle without extending the real certified band. Fixed orthogonal projection onto the jj entry is contractive, so the same estimate bounds |Gjj(w)-Gjj(x)|.

## 4. All complementary emitter coordinates

At the real center the bare complementary emitter block is self-adjoint and its distance from zero exceeds .18. Its complex energy shift has norm <.062rho, and its endpoint correction has norm <36kappa^2. At the single proposed kappa=10^-8,

    sigma_min(Dcc(w))>.18-.062*10^-12-36*10^-16,
    ||Dcc(w)^-1||<6.                              (2)

This is a singular-value perturbation/Neumann statement; it does not assume Dcc(w) stays self-adjoint or normal. It also establishes its analytic inverse, so the exact scalar Schur correction is analytic. All seven complementary coordinates remain in Dcc.

Contractivity of block projections gives ||Gjc||,||Gcj||<36, with no assumption of small off-real cross blocks. Thus at both w and x,

    |s_j-Gjj|
      =|kappa^2 Gjc Dcc^-1 Gcj|

       =7.776*10^-13<8*10^-13.                    (3)

Using a triangle inequality for the two correction values is valid; a derivative of Dcc^-1 is unnecessary here. The bridge does not omit that derivative from a claimed product derivative—it never takes one.
