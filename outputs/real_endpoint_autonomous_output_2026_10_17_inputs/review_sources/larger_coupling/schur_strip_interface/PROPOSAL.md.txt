## 1. Exact separation

Work in the fixed real physical basis with one open and seven closed output channels. Write z=x-iy, x in the original I, and let

    A(z) = background matching matrix on 896 coordinates,
    R(z)=A(z)^-1,
    E = real endpoint inclusion C^8 -> C^896,
    G(z)=E^T R(z) E,
    De(z)=(b_j+Eenergy(z)-E0)I-B-kappa^2 G(z).

The background here has the emitter deleted, with endpoint diagonal exactly 2J. The physical compensation in the source makes it independent of the chosen endpoint kappa. It includes the original terminated stub and the single output boundary self-energy. The complete 904-coordinate inverse obeys exactly

    M(z)^-1=diag(R(z),0)+V(z) De(z)^-1 V(z)^T,
    V(z)=[-kappa R(z)E; I8].                         (1)

Transpose is not adjoint. Complex symmetry nevertheless gives equal norms of a matrix and its transpose. The positive identity for V*V gives ||V||<=sqrt(1+kappa^2 ||R E||^2), also off the real axis. No off-real scattering unitarity is used.

Split the emitter into its resonant coordinate and all seven complementary coordinates. In this fixed basis define

    Dcc=(De)_cc,
    s_j(z)=Gjj(z)+kappa^2 Gjc(z) Dcc(z)^-1 Gcj(z),
    Dj(z)=Eenergy(z)-E0-kappa^2 s_j(z).

The second Schur inverse has the exact bound

    ||De^-1|| <= d_c + (1+kappa^4 d_c^2 G_*^2)/|Dj|, (2)

provided ||Dcc^-1||<=d_c and ||G||<=G_*. This follows from its rank-one factor w=(1,kappa^2 Dcc^-1 Gcj), with ||w||^2<=1+kappa^4 d_c^2 G_*^2. The rank-one factorization uses transpose and keeps the complementary inverse term. Omitting the seven coordinates would invalidate the interface.

## 3. Exact sufficient complex self-energy hypotheses

The first condition supplies d_c=1/(g-.062h-kappa^2 G_*) from the bare complementary gap g=.18. It establishes analyticity of Dcc^-1 before s_j is defined on the whole rectangle.                   >=6kappa^2,
