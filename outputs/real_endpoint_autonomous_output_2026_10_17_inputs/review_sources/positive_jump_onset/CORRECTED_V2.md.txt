    ||x||^2 = -2 E' Im tr(M^-1)
             <= 2 E' sum_{113 sites} 8 ||(M^-1)_{ss}||
             < .122 * 904 * 5e31 < 1e34.

the raw inverse bound times the incoming source needlessly gives 4e32.
No cancellation or unproved small matrix spectrum is used. The diagonal-block
bound, not a trace bound on the infinite resolvent, is the premise.

    ||x'|| <= ||M^-1|| (|E''| + ||M'|| ||x||) < 7e49.

## 2. Reciprocity avoids squaring the full inverse again

M is complex symmetric (not Hermitian). Consequently u^T M^-1=x^T/(iE').
Since R=u^T x-1, exact differentiation gives

    R' = (E''/E')(1+R) - x^T M' x/(iE').

The transpose is essential. Cauchy-Schwarz bounds |x^T M'x| by
||M'|| ||x||^2, even for complex x. Therefore

    |R'| < 2*.061/.025 + .1*1e34/.025 < 5e34.

This is a reciprocal scattering identity in the real fixed basis, not an
assumption about a delay or the commutation of K with the apparatus.

For the exact comparison packet a=omega f/R, |R|=|omega|=1 gives

    Var_edge(a) <= Var_edge(f)+||f||_1 sup|R'| < 2e32.

No substitution of a by conjugate(omega f), and no new uniform profile error,
is made. This preserves the original preparation-to-packet bound.
