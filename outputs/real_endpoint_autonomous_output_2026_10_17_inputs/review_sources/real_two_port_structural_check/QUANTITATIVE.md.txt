## Details making the bounds independent of coordinate shortcuts

All internal norms are Euclidean operator norms in orthonormal physical parity coordinates. P/Pc are orthogonal spectral projections of the real B. V acts on the two filter positions and is identity internally, so it commutes with those projections. The stacked Q powers, rather than entrywise counts over seven closed eigenvectors, supply the factors sqrt(1+r²) and sqrt(2). This avoids an unearned dimension-free bound while also avoiding an unnecessary factor seven.

For the scalar free filter, with v=(1,z), the reflected coefficient is obtained from the actual outgoing free Green row Aopen v^T: a=Aopen v^T V D0^-1 v. The bare gate image is Aopen r_g z^(2F) vv^T. The Sherman–Morrison scalar is therefore exactly 1-r_g z^(2F)a, not a guessed determinant lower bound. The transpose is appropriate to the complex-symmetric retarded kernel. The row norms used above are unchanged by transpose because the Euclidean spectral norm is transpose invariant.

Writing the filter equation in P/Pc blocks gives xC=Cc(yC+Gcp V xP) and S xP=yP+Gpc V Cc yC, with S=K0-Gpc V Cc Gcp V. The difference equation is S(xP-xP0)=(K0-S)xP0+Gpc V Cc yC. The complete inverse factorization has lower and upper triangular factors each bounded by 1+(45/2)r^19 and diagonal block norm below 1000. No self-adjointness of this retarded inverse is assumed.
