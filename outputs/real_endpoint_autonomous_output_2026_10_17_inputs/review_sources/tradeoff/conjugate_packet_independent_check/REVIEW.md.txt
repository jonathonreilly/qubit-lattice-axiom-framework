## 1. Seed-to-packet comparison and the outgoing discrepancy

Use the canonical unit-incoming synthesis convention

    S_j a = (2pi)^(-1/2) integral_I a(k) Phi_j(k) dk,
    Phi_j(m)=u_j[e^(-imk)+R_j(k)e^(imk)]+C_j(m,k).

S_j is isometric on L2(I). The actual real basis seed has incoming coefficient conjugate(F_j), with its outside-band component retained. Define g_j=omega_p f and a0,j=conjugate(g_j). Both have L2 norm one. Then

    ||conjugate(F_j)-a0,j||_2 = ||F_j-g_j||_2
      <=epsilon_F sqrt(p0)+1-sqrt(p0).

Since p0>=1-1.6e-9, 1-sqrt(p0)<=1.6e-9. Adding the actual off-band amplitude 4e-5 gives <.000792002 per column. The two old-energy residual vectors inside each parity may overlap, so their aggregate costs sqrt(2); different parities are orthogonal. The preparation operator ceiling <.001121 is therefore unchanged. Complex old/reference inputs are obtained by linear extension of real basis-column comparisons, not by conjugating the arbitrary input amplitudes.

Let e_j=F_j/sqrt(p0)-g_j. The pointwise canonical estimate gives |e_j|<epsilon_F|f|. The real-seed relation F_j=R_j conjugate(F_j), with |R_j|=1, implies exactly

    R_j conjugate(g_j)-g_j=e_j-R_j conjugate(e_j).

Thus the new packet's outgoing coefficient differs from the original target by <2epsilon_F|f|, and its L2 error is <.001504. Fourier evolution and restriction to m>=0 do not increase this norm. Every such error is supported in its corresponding fixed orthogonal open internal fiber u_j, so its full operator norm is the maximum column bound, not its sqrt(2) aggregate. This discrepancy is paid once, after comparing the seed directly to the new packet. Comparing through the former packet first would unnecessarily double-count an error.

## 2. Holomorphic continuation and contour signs

From the canonical exact profile,

    f(k)=zeta0 A_f E'(k) exp(i113k)/Dref(k),
    Dref(k)=E(k)-E0+alpha exp(ik), alpha=kappa^2/J>0,
    A_f=kappa/[sqrt(2pi)J sqrt(p0)]<17kappa,

the continuation of a0,j from the real interval is

    a0,j(z)=conjugate(omega_p zeta0) A_f E'(z)
                 exp(-i113z)/Dbar(z),
    Dbar(z)=2J(1-cos z)-E0+alpha exp(-iz).

This is a holomorphic function, not the non-holomorphic operation of conjugating f at arbitrary complex z. On z=x-iy, 0<=y<=h=sigma/2,

    Im Dbar(z)=-sin(x)[2J sinh(y)+alpha exp(-y)]<0.

I independently obtain exp(-y) by multiplying -i(x-iy)=-ix-y. The cosh alternative is false. The exact formula is strictly negative on the closed rectangle, including its real upper boundary, because sin(x)>.4 and alpha>0. It proves no zeros without an argument-principle computation or an assumed resonance-pole location.

The incoming propagation and numerator phase have modulus

    |exp[-imz-itE(z)-i113z]|
      =exp[-(m+113)y-2Jt sin(x)sinh(y)]
      <=exp[-(m+113+vt)y]

for t>=0, using E'(x)>v. The direction of contour deformation is therefore correct. The corresponding statement for the original outgoing denominator would not follow; the proposal does not use it.

## 3. Both vertical edges and the bottom

The strip derivative obeys |E'(x-iy)|<=2J cosh(y)<.062. At x=k0+/-sigma the real-axis distance is |E(x)-E0|>=v sigma. The continued real part has corrections bounded by .061h^2+alpha, since cosh(y)-1<=y^2 and exp(-y)<=1. Hence at both sides

    |Dbar(x-iy)|>=|Re Dbar(x-iy)|
      >=v sigma-.061h^2-alpha>.024sigma.

This covers either sign of E(x)-E0: reverse triangle inequality is sufficient, with no assumed reinforcing sign. Thus A_f|E'|/|Dbar|<44 kappa/sigma. Integrating each side vertically and keeping both gives, before Fourier normalization,

    |vertical contribution at m|<=88 kappa/[sigma(m+113+vt)].

For s=vt>=1, sum_(m>=0)(m+s)^(-2)<=2/s; multiplying by 1/sqrt(2pi) gives vertical norm <=88 kappa/[sigma sqrt(pi vt)]. This confirms the factor 88 already includes both hard-cutoff endpoints. Neither endpoint value is artificially zero.

On the bottom, |Dbar(x-ih)|>=2J sin(x)sinh(h)>vh. The loose coefficient ceiling 44 kappa/h is valid. Bounding the integral by its length 2sigma and summing exp(-2mh) yields

    bottom norm <=88 kappa sigma/h * exp(-vth)
                   /sqrt[2pi(1-exp(-2h))].

The inequalities 1-exp(-2h)>=h and sqrt(2pi)>2 imply a ceiling 88 kappa/sqrt(h)*exp(-vth)<1.3e-7*exp(-vth). The final comparison follows by squaring positive quantities: 1.5488e-14<1.69e-14. It does not need evaluating the huge exponential.

At t>=10^30, sqrt(pi vt)>2e14. The vertical bound is <4.4e-19 and the bottom is <1.3e-7 even after discarding all its time decay. Their sum is <2e-7. These incoming waves again live in orthogonal u_j fibers. No cross-column sqrt(2) is needed for this term. Triangle inequalities between the bottom and sides are conservative; no cancellation is assumed.
