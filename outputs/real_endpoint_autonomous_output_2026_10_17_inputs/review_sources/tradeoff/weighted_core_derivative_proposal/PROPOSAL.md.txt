## 1. Endpoint column before and after deletion

The source kernel gives the exact full internal block

    G(b,end)=A Q+Q^(ell+1)T_aa Q^ell
             =Q(A+X).

The order is important: T_aa need not commute with Q, whereas A does. Hence ||G(b,end)||<41. For every retained background head site s,

    Gtr(s,end)=G(s,end)-G(s,b)Gbb^{-1}G(b,end),
    ||Gtr(s,end)||<3e7+3e7*22*41
                     =2.709e10<3e10.                 (1)

This is the improvement. The canonical generic 2e16 bound uses a 3e7 bound for both factors next to Gbb^{-1}; for this particular endpoint column, one factor is instead <41. That identity is valid before replacing removed site b by the emitter, so it does not confuse the background Dirichlet problem with the completed apparatus.

Let R_bg be the 896-by-896 finite-coordinate background matching inverse, and E:C^8 -> C^896 the isometric inclusion at end. Equation (1) and the generic terminated-background block ceiling imply

    ||R_bg||<112*2e16=2.24e18,
    ||R_bg E||^2<112*(3e10)^2=1.008e23.                (2)

The second estimate uses squared physical block norms and the Euclidean sum over head positions. No factor of eight is missing: each block bound is already its norm as an operator on C^8. R_bg is complex symmetric because it is the inverse of a complex-symmetric finite-core matching matrix, so E^T R_bg=(R_bg E)^T. In the Euclidean complex norm ||V^T||=||V|| even though V^T generally differs from V*.

## 2. Exact emitter update of the complete core inverse

Order the physical coordinates as background followed by emitter. The full matching matrix has block form

    Mcore = [[R_bg^{-1}, kappa E],
             [kappa E^T, Dbare]],
    De=Dbare-kappa^2 E^T R_bg E.

The sign +kappa in matching follows from the physical hopping -kappa. The source's endpoint compensation leaves the background diagonal unchanged. The output self-energy lies in R_bg^{-1}, so eliminating the output tail commutes with this finite emitter Schur step.

The exact inverse is

    Mcore^{-1}=diag(R_bg,0)+V De^{-1} V^T,
    V=[-kappa R_bg E; I8].                              (3)

This formula retains the complementary emitter directions as well as the resonant one. As maps between the physical Euclidean spaces,

    ||V||^2=||I8+kappa^2 (R_bg E)* (R_bg E)||
            =1+kappa^2||R_bg E||^2<1.1008.

Using ||V^T||=||V||, (2), (3) and the source's full De inverse bound gives

    ||Mcore^{-1}||<2.24e18+1.1008*1e23
                   =1.1008224e23<1.2e23.            (4)

No bound on the spectrum of a huge finite clock, derivative of De^{-1}, or approximation to a resonance shape is used. This estimate does not assert that R_bg is Hermitian; its transpose symmetry and finite-coordinate interpretation are sufficient.
