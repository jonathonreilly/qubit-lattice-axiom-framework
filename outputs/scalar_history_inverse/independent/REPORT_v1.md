## Mathematical findings

Starting only from the neutral action and input contract, the frozen proof
derives dust exchange, the first-order velocity flow, reconstruction of A and
V, and the fixed-bare-density curvature constraint. Substitution verifies the
original scalar equation before differentiation; affine potential constants
cannot be silently added. The flow and algebraic constraint must both hold
throughout the interval. An instantaneous admissible root need not be a
history. ODE uniqueness bounds the number of full histories by the roots at
one time; it does not guarantee existence or unconditional uniqueness.

The independent proof also establishes the exceptional infinite-history
class. With Q=-2M^2 H_dot>0, infinitely many full histories require and are
supplied by alpha=0, Q_dot+3HQ=0 and
mu=(9H^2-6H_dot)/4 throughout the interval. Then v^2/Q may be any constant in
(0,1). Outside that class there are at most six histories, before accounting
for field-origin convention and unconstrained extensions beyond the sampled
field interval. These alternatives can reconstruct different A,V; they are
not claimed to be trajectories of one already fixed common action.

On a connected chart with positive squared coupling, its global sign is a
canonical field reflection. Simultaneously reversing v, alpha and alpha_dot
makes the velocity flow odd and leaves the constraint polynomial invariant.
This does not settle sign registration across coupling zeros. A registered
Einstein dust history r of class C2 fixes K=-2M^2 H_dot-r>0 and gives the
stated alpha and mu expressions, with compatibility still required if those
functions are independently prescribed. Bare particle density alone does not
supply r=A rho. An initial calibrated r fixes speed magnitude only.

The small-coupling sensitivity formula is correct for an instantaneous
algebraic candidate with other local input jets fixed. It is not a
full-history, statistical, or observational stability result. The fixed-rho
mass derivative, Einstein/Jordan density and clock factors, canonical field
normalization, and reference-unit qualifications were checked explicitly.
