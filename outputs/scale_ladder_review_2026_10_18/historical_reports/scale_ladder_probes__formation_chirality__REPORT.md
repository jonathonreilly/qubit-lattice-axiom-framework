## Formation

- **F1, which mechanism supplies formation. Reframed sharply, not resolved.**
  - The priority note's ancestor bound, P(decreasing path ≥ n) ≤ 6·5^(n−1)/(n+1)!, does not depend on the rule.
  - So every iid-priority, finite-range formation field is a finitary factor of iid noise whose coding radius has a super-exponential tail.
  - Coupling then gives |Cov(f(0),g(x))| ≤ C·P(radius ≥ |x|/2).
  - That places it at the trivial fixed point: no symmetry breaking, no critical correlations and no Coulomb (ice) phase, from an empty start.
  - Universal infrared content must therefore come from the start slice, from correlated clocks, from infinite ancestry, or from the Hamiltonian rung.
  - The proof still has to be written. Prior art: van den Berg–Steif 1999; Spinka.
- **F2, static versus formation reading. Reframed.** At the native triple both readings are trivial and differ only in non-universal data. At strong coupling they can sit in different phases (probe F1).
- **F3, formation-time versus ongoing support. No help.**
- **F4, uniform ice. Largely explained and extended.** Pinch-point correlations cannot come from a super-exponential ffiid. Caveat: heavy-tailed finitary codings are not excluded.
- **F5, rule selection. Marginal.** Under cubic covariance the chiral-pair orbit is the only allowed pseudoscalar coupling.

## Handedness

- **H1, four notions. Reframed as four different cross-rung invariants.**
  - Texture: a non-universal pseudoscalar coupling.
  - Motion direction: a polar, time-reversal-odd current.
  - Chiral mode: a quantized Weyl monopole charge.
  - Selected representation: 't Hooft anomaly data.
  - In equilibrium, a pseudoscalar texture cannot produce a current (probe C1).
- **H2, physical chirality. Partly resolved.**
  - Nielsen–Ninomiya forces net chirality to zero for number-conserving local Z³ Hamiltonians, so the corner Weyl pair is forced and minimal.
  - Physical chirality needs either an anomaly-free set with symmetric mass generation of the mirrors (the Spin(10) route, including Z16), or a non-on-site chiral symmetry (w1-odd rotations as γ5×taste).
- **H3, the corner Dirac pair. Partly resolved.**
  - m_D is technically natural.
  - Whether a symmetric mass is possible at all is an LSM/crystalline-anomaly question.
  - Whether the Dirac point is the global low-energy feature is untouched.
- **H4, removing the mirror sector. Reframed** as anomaly bookkeeping.

## Probe C1 (ran)

Two-band Weyl model with nodes at kz = ±π/2. A parity-odd term λ sin kz splits the node energies by ±0.3. Landau field with q = 8 to 24.

- The total lattice current is about 0 (within kz-grid error).
- The near-node window current approaches the continuum CME magnitude, with ratios 0.43, 0.64, 0.74 and 0.83 at q = 8, 12, 16 and 24.
- The deeper-band states cancel it.
- The sign convention against the continuum formula is unresolved.
- So the infrared theory alone mispredicts a current; the lattice rung enforces cancellation.
