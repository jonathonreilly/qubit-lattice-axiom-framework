**C3. No hard 3-colouring.** Holroyd–Schramm–Wilson show that every translation-invariant finitary iid proper 3-colouring of Z^d, d ≥ 2, has infinite second coding-radius moment (Holroyd–Schramm–Wilson, Theorem 2(ii), imported rather than re-proved here). Theorem 1 gives a factorial tail. So no rule in the class, with B = ∅, produces a proper 3-colouring of Z^d for d ≥ 2, provided the total alphabet has exactly three colours and every neighbour pair, including any symbol called a hole, obeys proper colouring.

Proper 3-colourings of Z² map locally onto square-ice (six-vertex) arrow configurations, but the inverse map is not local. So this does **not** prove that hard 3D ice-rule fields are impossible in the class; only the pinch-point exclusion (C2) is proved for ice. *Open:* is there any ffiid hard ice-rule field on Z³ with factorial tail? It would need an entire S(q) annihilated by the lattice divergence.

**C4. Consistency with known coding obstructions.**

- van den Berg–Steif (1999): the ferromagnetic Ising plus state on Z^d, d ≥ 2, has a finitary coding below β_c and none above it. A phase transition in a Markov random field obstructs ffiid. Consistently, no iid-priority formation produces a low-temperature Ising phase.
- At criticality, finitary codings exist (Spinka 2020), but the coding radius has an infinite d-th moment (attributed to van den Berg–Steif with Peres). Critical correlations therefore need heavy-tailed codings, which the class excludes.
