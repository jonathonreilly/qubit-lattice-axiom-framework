## 3. Full trace composition, chronological old aging and phase

Let U_s=U_setup have actual complete duration s, and put
V_s=e^(-i Phi_setup) E e^(-isK). The premise
||U_s E-V_s||<=delta_release and the runtime isometry estimate imply

```

  -zeta e^[-i(Phi_setup+c_phys t)]
       B_(kappa,t) e^(-itK)W e^(-isK)||
 <=delta_release+F_res.
```

Only unitarity on the first error and right multiplication by the input
unitary on the second are used. Because X1X2 commutes with Z1Z2,
[W,K]=0, so the old factor is e^[-i(s+t)K]W. No claim that K commutes
with H_M or any partially released Hamiltonian is needed.

For the actual full delivered state rho, first apply
||rho-E rho_OR E*||_1<=d_prep, where rho_OR is its exact **unconditioned**
old/reference marginal at delivery. Unitary evolution and the output
embedding preserve this error. For two isometries A,B and a density
matrix sigma, expansion as
(A-B)sigma A*+B sigma(A*-B*) gives

```
||A sigma A*-B sigma B*||_1 <=2||A-B||.
```

norm. The resulting complete trace error is therefore less than
d_prep+2delta_release+2F_res, exactly as stated. The thermal error is not
multiplied by a huge evolution time, counted twice, or converted into an
isometry error without justification.

The auxiliary marginal may be Gibbs while the full input is correlated
with old/reference. The source pure-factor estimate is the appropriate

block; replacing that block's old/reference marginal by the unconditioned

```
g(epsilon)=epsilon+sqrt[epsilon(4-3epsilon)] <=3 sqrt(epsilon).
```

The smaller 2epsilon bound would require a product *joint* input and is
not used. No postselection on the good seed or redefinition of rho_OR

The trace comparison is applied **before** the single-head supported
isometry theorem. All bad thermal sectors then undergo their actual
