## 1. The clause

> Between records, the unrecorded sites hold one joint possibility on the complex algebra generated by their site domains, each domain entering complex-linearly as M_2(C) and nothing else entering. It changes continuously, reversibly and the same way at every moment and every site, keeps every locked possibility, and at each moment the change in a site's part is set by the part held jointly by that site and its nearest neighbors. A forming record takes its odds from its own site's part, with some odds for every possibility that part does not exclude, and on forming cuts the joint possibility down to the part in which its site holds the locked possibility. Taken over its own odds, a forming record changes no other site's odds except through the change between records.

**Sentence 1** supplies S1 and generation.
- "On the algebra" excludes the classical Liouville spin flow (CHSH ≤ 2), which satisfied the earlier wording.
- Complex-linearity makes each site's i central and even. That excludes the Θ-graded Cl(3n,0) composite (EXACT).

**Sentence 2** supplies S2–S4, translation covariance, lock permanence, and A-diff on the closed neighbourhood N[x].
- The open-neighbourhood reading forces H = const (EXACT).
- A finite-time reading would force J = 0 (ARGUED), hence "at each moment".

**Sentence 3** supplies three premises:
- C3. It is supplied, not read from the text. Given C1, it is equivalent to the law being unchanged by the partner's local unitaries (EXACT).
- Positive odds.
- C2. It excludes the replacement rule and noisy locks (EXACT). The word "instrument" is avoided on purpose: compression branches plus a linear non-selective map force Born (Lemma U1, EXACT).

**Sentence 4** is C4 at every pair, at equal time. It splits in two:
- C4a, no-signalling between menus. It is vacuous when the conditions are records.
- C4b, the null option: a site's odds are the same whether or not a partner forms. This half carries calibration.
