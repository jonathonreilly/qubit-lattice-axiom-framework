# Lane G: composition law and dynamics between records

## 1. The problem in framework terms

The axioms (origin/main `MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29`) supply:
- a one-site domain M_2(C), equivalently Cl(3,0), with "No possibility is privileged";
- one fixed nearest-neighbour covariant rule that fixes each site's law-level distribution;
- records: each locks one admissible local possibility, at most one per site, permanently, and is readable by content.

They name no cross-site product, no evolution and no time.

The sharp questions:
- **(i)** Suppose the site domains sit as faithful unital copies A_x ≅ M_2(C) inside some algebra B. Do Record and Admissibility fix how the A_x relate across sites (commuting, i.e. the ordinary tensor product, or a Z_2-graded sign), or must that relation be registered?

- **(iii)** If it is supplied, what is the smallest clause, written in axiom register?

## 4. Derivation for R1

### A. Composition

**Setting (supplied).**
- **S1:** a finite window W, and faithful unital *-embeddings ι_x into a finite-dimensional real or complex C*-algebra B generated by their images A_x.
- **Lock reading (L):** a record locking p at x means ρ = PρP with P = ι_x((1+p·σ)/2), and permanence means this holds at every later stage.
- **Local formation (F):** a formation at y is an instrument whose Kraus operators lie in A_y; outcome q leaves y locked at Q.

**A1. Gradings of the site domain (EXACT).**
- A complex-linear order-2 automorphism is Ad(u) with u² ∝ 1. So u ∝ 1 (trivial) or u ∝ n·σ, which picks an axis ±n.
- An antilinear automorphism commuting with every internal rotation must satisfy UV̄U† = cV for all V in SU(2), which gives Uσ_y ∝ 1. So it is Θ(A) = σ_y Ā σ_y, the Cl(3,0) grade involution, with odd part spanning σ_k and i·1.
- The axis-graded (Jordan–Wigner) composite is M_{2ⁿ}(C). The Θ-graded composite is Cl(3n,0); for two sites that is M_4(H).
- No axis is fixed by SO(3) or by the octahedral group O, which acts irreducibly on R³. Under the trivial and sign-twist soldering actions an axis grading is lattice-covariant, so it falls under A3 alone.

**A2. Mutual exclusion (EXACT).** Let u and v be anticommuting self-adjoint involutions, with P = (1+u)/2 and Q = (1+v)/2.
- (a) ran P ∩ ran Q = {0}: if uψ = vψ = ψ then 2ψ = (uv+vu)ψ = 0.
- (b) P(1+v)P = P, so PQP = P/2.
- (c) Σ± P±QP± = ½·1.
- In the Θ-graded composite every pair of site projectors (1+n·e^x)/2 and (1+m·e^y)/2 at distinct sites is of this type. By (a), at most one site can ever carry a record, which is excluded if "Records form" means several records (ARGUED reading).
- Without the lock reading, (c) says an unread record anywhere moves y's odds to 1/2, at any distance.

**A3. Axis-graded composite (EXACT, with Reach ARGUED).**
- Use the Jordan–Wigner frame: site x carries σ⊗1; at site y the even Z maps to 1⊗Z and the odd σ_⊥ maps to Z⊗σ_⊥.
- For ψ = |n⟩⊗φ in ran P: vψ = |n⟩⊗(m_∥Z + n_∥ m_⊥·σ_⊥)φ + c|n^⊥⟩⊗(m_⊥·σ_⊥)φ, with |c| = |n_⊥|.
- So vψ = ψ forces φ = 0 when both n_⊥ ≠ 0 and m_⊥ ≠ 0. Two off-axis records never coexist, at any distance. On-axis records commute with everything.
- Now let x be the first site ever to lock an off-axis value, under nearest-neighbour condition C (built from on-axis records and unrecorded sites).
- Reach (ARGUED): a translation-covariant formation process realizes C again at a distant y.
- Admissibility then gives y the same off-axis support it gave x, while exclusion gives that support zero mass. Contradiction.
- Hence every record lies on ±n. That support confinement is EXACT; reading it as the privileging that the Qubit sentence forbids is ARGUED, though literal.
- R3 variant: for records in the odd plane, (c) is explicit signalling to a non-neighbour.

**A4. General B (EXACT).**
- Any faithful prior followed by Lüders formation at x gives a state with support ran P. A later Lüders formation at y must preserve the lock, so Q(ran P) ⊆ ran P, i.e. QP = PQP. Taking adjoints, PQ = PQP. Hence [P,Q] = 0.
- General local instrument: non-selective permanence gives PΦ*(P)P = P with Φ*(P) ≤ 1. That gives (1−P)KP = 0 for every Kraus K. If the Kraus operators generate A_y, which is *-closed, then [A_y, P] = 0.

**A5. Spanning (EXACT).**

**Steps.**

**B4. Clique lemma (EXACT).**
- Write H = Σ_A h_A, where h_A is the canonical component supported exactly on A. Then L_x(ρ) = Tr_{W∖x}[H,ρ] = Σ_{A∋x} Tr_{A∖x}[h_A, ρ_A].
- Suppose h_A ≠ 0 with x ∈ A, and some z ∈ A lies outside N[x].
- Pick a Pauli string S in h_A. Set M = Σ_a c_{(σ^a, S_rest)} σ^a, which is nonzero and traceless.
- Set T = T_x ⊗ S_rest, where T_x is a Pauli that does not commute with M. T has zero N[x]-marginal, since T_z ≠ 1.
- Every string that contributes to L_x(T) agrees with S away from x, so L_x(T) = 2^{|W|−1}[M, T_x] ≠ 0.
- The states (1 ± εT)/2^{|W|} then have equal N[x]-marginals but different dρ_x/dτ, which contradicts A-diff.
- So every term's support A is a clique of the lattice graph. Z³ is bipartite, so |A| ≤ 2 and H = Σ h_x + Σ_⟨xy⟩ h_xy. The converse also holds.
