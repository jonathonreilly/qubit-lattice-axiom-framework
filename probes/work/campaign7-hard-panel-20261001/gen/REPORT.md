# Lane B: the generation count and the chirality gate

Labels: **EXACT** (a proof given here), **CHECKED** (a finite exact evaluation, done by hand), **ARGUED**, **LANDED** (a bounded note on `origin/main`, no audit grade claimed).

## (1) The problem, sharpened

**What the axioms supply.** Z^3 with translations and the proper rotations O (order 24, no inversion), M_2(C) per site, a covariant nearest-neighbour (NN) rule, and records.

**What they do not supply.** No Hamiltonian, no statistics, no copy count.

**Four separate targets.** The program memory treats these as one; they are not.
- **G1, the grading.** A grading γ on a low-energy carrier whose value is the handedness of each band touching (sign of the determinant of its 3×3 velocity matrix). γ must not be the hybrid γ = Γ_χ on one R^3.
- **G2, the count.** Under the full symmetry group (translations ⋊ rotations), the light carrier contains an irreducible multiplet C^3_copy ⊗ (one Weyl or Dirac multiplet). The 3 must be fixed by representation theory, not by a parameter.
- **G3, the rest.** The singlets and mirror copies are gapped, or moved off in energy, by named supplied terms.
- **G4, charged chirality.** Net chirality under an exact on-site U(1) or gauge group.

**D1. EXACT: the NN coin Hamiltonian is forced.**
- The general NN, translation-invariant, O-covariant coin Hamiltonian is H = a0 + 2aΣ cos k_j + vΣ σ_j sin k_j.
- Proof by O representations:

  - T_1⊗T_1 contains exactly one A_1;
  - (A_1+E)⊗T_1 = 2T_1+T_2 contains none;

- So the landed 09-22 family is the general NN one.

**D2. EXACT: the eight corner nodes.**
- Near node πn: H_n(q) = vΣ(−1)^{n_j} q_j σ_j + a0 + 2a(3−2|n|).
- Handedness is (−1)^{|n|}: L = {e_a, 111}, R = {000, ē_a}.

**D3. EXACT (Mackey): three protected copies.**
- Translations act on node n by the characters (−1)^{n_a}. Rotations permute corners through O → S_3 and rotate spin.
- The stabiliser of πe_1 is D_4. Spin-½ of its double cover is irreducible, because the lifts of C_4x and C_2y do not commute.
- Hence V_L = V_6 ⊕ V_2 (weight-1 ⊗ spin, and singlet ⊗ spin). Both are irreducible and inequivalent; same for V_R.

**D5. EXACT (G3): masses.**
