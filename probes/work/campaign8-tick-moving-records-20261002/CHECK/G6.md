## 3. Top 3 issues for the owner's plain-language summary

**1. The "three internal parts" cost belongs to your gluing (Q3), not to charge: the repo already has an exact one-part hop through spin-½ links that does not keep the gluing.**

- Where: one-page item 6 ("and charged matter that has three internal parts and one sign of charge") and section 14 ("matter with a single internal state cannot hop through light's links at all").
- Repo side: [E6] (`THE_FERMIONS_U1_COUPLED_TO_QUANTUM_LINKS_...2026-09-03.md`, `unaudited`) gives a one-component fermion on the coarse lattice coupled to one spin-½ link per coarse edge, with an exact gauge-invariant hop and exact Gauss conservation on three finite blocks, using a fixed edge order and Kawamoto-Smit signs; covariance is listed there as an open obligation. The repo's coupled model carries its ring coefficient as a supplied real number (computed at 0 and 1), so its family contains, up to that relabelling, the "twist from light" model with the simple one-part matter that section 14 treats as excluded. The exclusion holds only for hops that respect your gluing.
