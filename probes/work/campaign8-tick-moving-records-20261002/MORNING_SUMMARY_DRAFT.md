# The night in one page (draft: to be finalized after A33 and the fourth review)

**What we did.** We took your instincts and worked out what each would do:
- records form at set ticks;
- they move at most one site per tick;
- clashes are settled by relative odds;
- the possibilities flow;
- a full region may act like a black hole.

The tools were exact derivations and small computer toys. Nothing here is adopted: these are explorations of your instincts, not positions. Four hostile reviews attacked the claims, and their corrections are folded in.

**The biggest finding: a hard limit on motion (exact).** Suppose a change is all of these:
- reversible;
- reaching only next-door sites in one tick;
- built on one qubit per site;
- treating every site and every turn of the grid exactly alike on every tick.

Then nothing can move at all: not records, not light, not matter. Something has to give.

**The most coherent way through: "ticks for records, smooth change for possibilities".**
- **How it works.** Records step at most one site per tick by trading places with an empty neighbour. The shared possibilities change smoothly in between.
- **It uses two of the limit's ways out at once.** The smooth change reaches faintly beyond next door, and records' steps cannot be undone. The two fit together, so records still never move more than one site per tick.
- **The price.** Unrecorded influence has no exact speed limit, only an exponentially faint leak.

**What it gives**
1. **Your tick.**
   - The possibilities never notice the ticks, so the earlier troubles (mirror walls, freezing) disappear.
   - A tick written into the law must be the same everywhere. Ticks that differ by place need each place to remember its phase.
   - The tick can be influenced by gravity, but what must follow gravity exactly is how often records form.
   - The tick never touches how light travels.
   - "Minimum distance implies minimum tick" comes out as an upper limit: shorter than light's time to cross one grid step.
2. **Empty space.** If records form only next to records, empty space is exactly quiet, whatever it is made of, and distant light crosses it untouched. The world must start with some records, and next to matter the emptiness must be the tidy, lined-up kind.
3. **Black holes.** A fully recorded region is a place where nothing can happen again, and it can be sealed. But it is grey, not black:
   - it catches fast things and bounces slow ones;
   - it keeps what it caught as records on its skin, in plain view.

   Whether gravity's clocks stop inside depends on your choice about room per site.

**What it does not yet give**
4. **Light and matter.**
   - Over the calm empty background this shape needs, a rule that treats every spot and turn alike can only make slow, heavy ripples, never light (exact).
   - Light-like matter needs a painted-on pattern of plus and minus signs, and that pattern can never be the one gravity needs (proved).
   - Last search tonight (A33): [result].
5. **Gravity.**
   - A field of the shared possibilities, kept exactly balanced, is pinned by the grid's turns to Einstein's linear gravity.
   - With matter tied in, light bends by the full amount and things fall alike, so far in a ticked toy.
   - The costs:
     - a fixed 2×2×2 pattern of jobs, 1 of 8 layouts;
     - more settings per place than one qubit;
     - the places carrying the field never recorded;
     - records formed gently ("catch first, record later").
6. **Handedness.** Neither ticks nor smooth change prefer a handedness (exact for simple particles).
