## 17. Matter and light: where things stand (4 October)

Your aim is to show that the axioms can be the basis of matter and light. Here is where things stand with one qubit per place and your exact turns (Q3, kept for now), after lanes A42–A55. Grades are as in the lanes; every rule is a supplied exploration, not adopted.

- **Light: fits, not yet shown to form.** Light lives on the in-between (edge) places of the 2×2×2 pattern. It moves by a loop rule that respects every turn, and its charge bookkeeping holds exactly (A45). Whether this rule really carries light on the cubic grid is the repo's open photon question.
- **Electron-like matter, route 1: light's own charges.**
  - With plain hops the charges are bosons under every construction tried: simple flips, any single-place turning, and a broad controlled family (A45, A48, A50, my scan).
  - With a record at every corner place, locked along one of the cube's 8 diagonals, they become fermions. The law still respects every exact turn once the records turn too (A52; checked).
  - In the half-turn flux sector they move like massless electrons, doubled as usual on a grid. A mass needs the corners to come in two kinds; a twisting record pattern supplies it while the law still respects every turn (A55).
  - Light's loop rule then carries extra factors. In empty space light behaves exactly as before, but with charges present this is a real change, not a relabeling (A54).
- **Electron-like matter, route 2: the half-particle background.**
  - At mean field it gives fermions and a light-like field together. With short rules, ordered magnets beat it (A44, A46, A47, A49).
  - Under its best farther-reaching rule it beats every rival computed on grids of 216 to 1,000 places (A51, A53). The far-lower states on small exactly solvable grids turned out to be quirks of how those grids wrap (A53). A near-tie with loosely linked cubes is unsettled.
- **Decisions this leaves with you:**
  - 29: records at every corner, or a two-place relabeling reading;
  - 4: records only next to records, which here would be counted two steps apart;
  - 23: matter and light from one shared empty space.
- **Not reached yet:** handedness (the grid's doubling leaves no net hand), several kinds of charge, and generations.
