## 5. Top findings

1. **The "smallest tick" argument is new; the repo's "causal locality" note does not contain it.** The note titled "tied to the lattice edge by causal locality" is an open gate. It computes how far influence can spread in one step on a chosen nearest-neighbour update, and its own text says the equation "one record tick = one edge" is a naming convention. That kind of result can cap speed; it cannot stop a tick from being short. No note uses "records must not outrun light" to bound the tick from below.

2. **In 3D the two halves of the argument fight.** If records may step one site per tick along an axis and must never outrun light, the tick cannot be shorter than a/c. If one site per tick is also supposed to limit light, which spreads in a round cone, the tick must be shorter than a/(1.73 c). Only in one dimension do they meet. The repo already says the one-site-per-tick reach is a diamond, not a round cone, and its own photon-tick model is stable only for steps below about 0.58 of the primitive's tick.

3. **The "minimum time step is the Planck time" note is stale.** It leans on a companion it calls retained; that companion is an open gate whose own boundary calls the tie a naming convention and which was re-graded "renaming" three times in July, and the Planck note also treats the lattice spacing as the Planck length, which the scale-reference note says it does not assert. What it really shows is units arithmetic once the tick is declared equal to the edge.

4. **What the repo did derive about ticks is a conditional speed rule, never a clock.** If a tick is a strict nearest-neighbour unitary step on one component per site, it either does nothing or moves things exactly one edge per tick, with no slower movers and no mass. Every premise is supplied ("the four axioms do not choose a tick"), the 3D version loses the cone, the link to the Euclidean statement `c_t = c_s` is unfixed, and the July selector rests on an Admissibility sentence that was rewritten on 2026-08-05. The primitive is therefore still supplied, not derived.

5. **"Ticks are a basic unit, all alike" goes beyond what the primitive grants.** The approved wording fixes only the ratio of tick to edge ("One tick is one edge in form") and states it does not make time basic. Uniformity across places is an added hypothesis in the September notes. The closest repo statement is its own sharpest open residual: that the tick is the unit at which the normalization lives.
