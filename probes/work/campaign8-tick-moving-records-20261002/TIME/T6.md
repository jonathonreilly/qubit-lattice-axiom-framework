### 2.2 What the 2026-06-29 reset removed or demoted, and why

The policy entry (`AXIOM_MINIMALITY_POLICY.md:374-406`) gives the reasons:

- "**Why it is admissible.** The 2026-06-05 Record wording already depended on realized-outcome registration, but it left arbitrary record mosaics underconstrained." (`:378-380`) The reset adds Admissibility to constrain records; it is not a time change.
- "**No laundering.** Admissibility does not choose the readout context, ..., define time metric or arrow, choose a Hamiltonian or transfer operator, select a kinetic branch, ..." (`:388-398`). The posture on time did not change: S3a and S3b already said "time arrow" and "time metric" were not supplied. What is new is that "Hamiltonian or transfer operator" is named.
- "**Scope.** ... This reset invalidates prior direct `minimal_axioms` audits through the axiom-premise hash guard" (`:404-406`).

The reset-impact note says what that cost (`docs/audit/AXIOM_RESET_IMPACT_2026-06-29.md`): "The pipeline reset 251 previously settled rows to `unaudited`." (`:35`) and "The effect is therefore a foundation reset with a large audit blast radius, not a wording-only change." (`:44`). It also states where time sits: "3. **Temporal dynamics layer.** This concerns Hamiltonians, transfer matrices, record preservation, clock rates, and physical persistence." (`:128`) and "The right next direction is therefore not to add a broad 'Dynamics' axiom to the foundation set." (`:136`).

So the reset removed no time, Hamiltonian or evolution premise, because no axiom set after 05-03 contained one. The only tensed content in S3b and S4 is Record's durability or permanence. What the reset and the edits after it did to the time work is the following list.

1. **Durability was dropped for four days.** Policy `:116-121`: the 07-03 entry "restores the durability content of the 2026-06-05 lineage ('durable realized-outcome registration') that was dropped in the reset rewording." Every record-based time derivation needs durability ("durable containment poset").
2. **Occurrence was never in the reset text.** The reset says "a site need not carry a record". "Records form." was added on 2026-07-04 (policy `:300-327`; the owner's stated reason is "the axiom's own subject presupposing occurrence - with no records, no Record axiom would be needed"). This is close in spirit to the branch-only 06-06 "realist slip" correction (an empty record stack is "no reality"), now made axiom text (my reading of the two texts).
3. **The additive count `I` was removed on 2026-08-13** (policy `:795-818`): "named scalar `I` and finite additivity are no longer axiom content (counting occupied sites is arithmetic if later supplied)"; "prior judgments that consumed the removed structure must return to fresh review and audit". This is the premise the 06-06 "I-gradient" time-axis derivation was built on.
4. **2026-08-05 (Admissibility: availability becomes a probability distribution)** changed the premise of the 07-09 tick-cell selection theorem. `docs/AXIOM_EDIT_REPAIR_MAP_CYCLE973_NOTE_2026-08-09.md` row 21 lists `TICK_CELL_SELECTION_BY_TRANSLATION_AND_VARIATION_CLAUSES_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-07-09.md` as `STRICTLY_STRONGER` ("the same conclusion is demanded from the weaker distribution premise"), witness `BEARS`, outstanding obligation `O973-TICK-DISTRIBUTION-BRIDGE`.
5. **Everything is `unaudited` now.** Of 5336 rows none is audited; the history rows show the time lane had many `audited_conditional`/`audited_failed` verdicts and a handful of clean ones (section 2.3).

The window in which all three premises of the 06-06 time-axis derivation (occurrence, durability, additive count) were simultaneously axiom text is **2026-07-04 to 2026-08-12**. The derivation itself was branch-only (`archive_unlanded`, "branch_only_never_mainlined") and has never been audited.
