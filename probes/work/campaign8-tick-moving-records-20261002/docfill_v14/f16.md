## 16. What the repo already says about time (review of 3 October; six read-only reviewers)
- **Earlier axiom sets did "force" time, but only through ingredients that carried time.** April's axioms included a microscopic dynamics (the staggered-Dirac action). Under it the repo built a unique evolution and its Hamiltonian, the positivity and thermal results, and "anomaly forces time" (an odd number of time directions, so at least one). That dynamics became an open gate on 3 May. June's Record axiom had an additive record count; branch notes used it to force a unique time axis, the arrow and the 3+1 signature. They were never merged, and the count was removed on 13 August. "Exactly one time direction", "one step" and "one clock" were always put in by hand, and the repo withdrew its own "unique axis" step on 11 June.
- **Under the current four axioms nothing forces time** (the axiom memo lists the time metric and the arrow as open). What survives:
        - order: records only accumulate, so along any actual history later states contain earlier ones (before and after, not durations or simultaneity);
        - mathematics: given a direction, a step and one clock, the evolution is unique;
        - walls: the time axis is not picked out by positivity, records, anomalies or thermal arguments; a second clock is not excluded; the Lorentz sign is a separate input; records give order, not rate.
- **Relativity** appears only as a low-energy limit of a supplied Hamiltonian. The grid is a preferred frame (its 24 turns contain no boost), hidden at low energy by your approved primitive "one tick is one edge in form".
- **Your ticks.** "A basic unit" is that primitive (supplied). "Alike everywhere" is what the repo calls the uniform-clock clause: declared, never adopted. About 30 repo clock notes assume ticks that differ by place; uniform ticks make them trivial, so gravity must act on what happens per tick (section 5). Identical but independent clocks at every site set off records everywhere and cannot build extended structures, so uniform ticks also need to be shared. The lower bound "records must never outrun light, so a tick is at least one edge's light-crossing time" is not in the repo. It needs a light speed that does not come from the tick, which only the record-tick shape supplies.
- **Credit.** June notes on main already have the hard limit's shape for one fermion mode per site: a strict one-step reversible tick either does nothing or moves exactly one edge per tick, and never hosts mass; covariant 3D polynomial ticks are flat (all unaudited; section 3).
- **Cleanup.** Stale time wording on main (time called "derived" where the cited theorem now takes the axis as a premise; an open gate treated as settled; removed axiom wording quoted) is being corrected in a separate PR.
