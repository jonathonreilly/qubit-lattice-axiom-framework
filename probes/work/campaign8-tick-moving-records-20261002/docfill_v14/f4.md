## 4. An exact speed limit, and relativity at low speed, for ticked change (A5)
- Nothing gets further in one tick than the change reaches. That is an exact limit (one site per tick if the change is held to one site), where smooth change only gives an approximate one.
- In a one-dimensional toy, moving clocks slow by Einstein's formula with each kind of matter's own top speed in place of light's, so the amount is Einstein's only at low speed.
- The record-tick shape's smooth change gives up this exact limit for unrecorded influence, and these exact one-dimensional identities with it.
- In 3D this is not secured. Rules giving equal speeds in every direction either split speeds in direct proportion to energy, which gamma-ray burst timing rules out for light if the tick is anywhere near the Planck time, or need a pattern of plus and minus signs painted onto the rule (section 10).
