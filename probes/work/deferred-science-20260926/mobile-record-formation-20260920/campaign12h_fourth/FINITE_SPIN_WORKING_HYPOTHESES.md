# Finite-spin post-formation hypotheses, before controls

Primary-author working hypotheses during the fourth campaign on 2026-09-23,
recorded before the finite-spin controls. No claim established.
In the joint electric-field scaling C=S(S+1)=delta/(K epsilon^2), write the
normalized spin shift on an integer link as sqrt(1-E(E+k)/C) for k=+/-1.
For finite-support field states H2_S=H2_rotor+D2/C+O(C^-2), where a two-hop
path contributes +(a+b)/2 to D2 and a=E1(E1+k1), b=E2(E2+k2), each evaluated
at its actual hop input. Thus eta H2_S formally contains K D2. This is an
operator-core expansion, not an ordinary-time convergence theorem.

The full rotor H2 has a physical flat eigenspace on the eight-site ring.
In the word/two-B-particle coordinates, its two flat vectors in each word
sector alpha can be chosen as (|01>-exp(i alpha)|23>)/sqrt(2) and
(|12>-|03>)/sqrt(2). Consequently the physical projector is a finite shift
in the integer circulation, smooth in theta despite extra crossings.
Test this expression directly against H2 before reusing it. Then test whether
D2 preserves it. Exact invariance would offer a route to post-formation electric
field dynamics. If it fails, preserve the coupling and do not project it away
without an averaging theorem. H2 dispersive branches can drive large field
excursions; a finite-support expansion does not control their laboratory-time
motion. Possible semiclassical, embedded-band and preparation routes remain
open, not established conclusions.
