The continuum steps that would turn the virial into `P = Q` for a self-bound body are the attempt's assumptions. This Hamiltonian puts content on interior bonds only.

## Steps

1. **Bond derivatives.** On a 3-site line and a `2×2` box, `e − τ` is the rest energy `ρ w`. The field `F = −8K Σ (N_y − N_x)(χ_y − χ_x)` has `∂F/∂u = 8K N Δχ` and `∂F/∂λ = 4K(N Δχ + χ ΔN)`. Stationarity is `Δχ = −e/(8KN)` and `ΔN = (e + 2τ)/(8Kχ)`.

2. **Charges.** `P − Q = (1/8K)[2τ − e(1/w − 1)]/χ`. A pinned body (`τ = 0`, `e = mw`) has `Qχ = m/(8K)` and `P = Qw`. With no rest term, `e = τ` and `P − 3Q = (3/8K) e(w−1)/(wχ)`. At weak field the gap starts at `ε(2τ + eu)`.

3. **Box virial.** On a `3×2` rectangle, `{C, S}/2` is the truncated two-step hop `(T² − T†²)/(4i)`, and `i[φHφ, G_x] = φ H₂ φ − (ΛHφ + φHΛ)`. The staggered sign anticommutes with `H`, gives `[ε, G_x] = 2ε G_x`, and commutes with `G_P = ½ Σ {x_j, S_j C_j}`.

## Verdict

The bond equations, the charge identities, and the box virial hold. They do not by themselves prove `P = Q` for a self-bound body.
