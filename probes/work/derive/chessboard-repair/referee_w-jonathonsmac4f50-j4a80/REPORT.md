## The claim

The attempt claims a partial and a no-go for route (iii) of the task, cells of side two.
1. **Counting.** On the even torus, an even 2-cell `c + {0,1}^d` holds one direction-`i` bond per transverse parity class, all with even
   longitudinal start. The tiling's union is the `N/2` even-start bonds.
2. **The no-go.** "a Peierls event attached to 2-cells of one tiling … disseminates at most the even-longitudinal half of `D_all`". The
   statement adds: "Overlapping 2-windows of every parity would cover `D_all` but are not a chessboard product."
3. **Its reasons (step 3).**
   - Combining with site reflections "does not move `x_i mod 2`".
   - "Bond-plane reflections *would* move longitudinal parity; that is route (ii), not (iii)."
4. **The spectrum.** The spectrum of `W` and its PSD condition.

## Step by step

**Step 1 (`W` spectrum): holds.** S1:
- the eigenvalues are `p+q+4r` (×1), `p−q` (×3) and `p+q−2r` (×2);
- for `p,q,r ≥ 0`, `W` is PSD exactly when `p ≥ q` and `p+q ≥ 2r`, checked on all 343 integer triples in `[0,6]³`.

**Step 2 (2-cell inventory): holds.** S2, on `(Z/4)²`, `(Z/6)²`, `(Z/4)³` and `(Z/6)³`: each cell holds `2^{d−1}` direction-0 bonds, one per
transverse class, all with even start. The union is exactly the `N/2` even-start bonds.

**Step 3 (no-go): fails.**
- **The narrow statement holds** (S3b). The reflections that map the even tiling to itself are the bond planes, and they carry a cell's
  bonds onto exactly the `N/2` even-start bonds.
- **Both parity sentences are reversed** (S3a, checked on every bond and every plane of the four tori).
  - A site plane along the bond sends a start `s` to `2k−1−s`, so it flips the longitudinal parity.
  - A bond plane along the bond sends `s` to `2k−s`, so it preserves it.
  - Bond planes move the *transverse* parity, which is what the task's lead for route (ii) says.
- **The overlapping 2-windows are a chessboard** (S3c). Take the group generated by site planes along the bond and bond planes across it.
  - It carries the 2-cell onto the windows at every corner along `i` and at even corners across.
  - Distinct windows meet only in shared faces `x_i = const`, which is the block structure of a site-plane chessboard.
  - The orbit of the cell's direction-`i` bonds, and even of a single bond, is all `N` bonds.
- **That chessboard is reflection positive** (S3d) whenever `W` is PSD:
  - site planes for any weights, by block 17's T2 as read from the note on PR #8151's branch;
  - bond planes because `W` is PSD, which means `p ≥ 3` on `(p,1,2)`.

  So "not a chessboard product" is false, and so is section (3)'s "neither of which is a single chessboard product". This group is the
  repair route of attempt a1.

## Classic failure modes

The narrow result is true, but the attempt uses reversed parity facts to widen it into a no-go for combinations with site reflections
and for overlapping windows. Those combinations are exactly what repairs the dissemination.
