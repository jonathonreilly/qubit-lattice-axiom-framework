## The claim

For the 7-stencil light-cone automaton on `Z³`, the attempt claims:
- **(a)** reversibility with respect to `π ∝ ∏ Z(β S_x)`, and `π` as the `s`-marginal of a Heisenberg ferromagnet on the doubled graph `Γ`;
- **(b)** reflection positivity on `Γ` for the maps `θ_Γ` (a spatial bond plane composed with a layer swap), Gaussian domination, and LRO for
  `β > 3√3π/16 + 3/4`;
- **(c)** the bound `⟨|ŝ^e(k)|²⟩ ≤ (1/(2β))(1/E + 1/(14−E))` for `π`, and a Bogoliubov lower bound of order `M_+⁴/(βE)`;
- **(d)** Dobrushin uniqueness for `β < 1/7`;
- **(e)** a comparison with block 19.

## Step by step

**Steps 1–2 (pairing, reversibility): hold.** D1:
- the pairing sum is symmetric on `(Z/4)³` for random integer vector fields;
- for the Ising analogue on the ring `Z/4` with `t = e^β ∈ {2, 3}`, rows sum to 1, detailed balance holds on all 256 pairs, and `πP = π`
  exactly.

**Step 3 (Gibbs identity, doubled graph): holds.** This is the single-site integral identity.

**Step 4 (spectrum of `Γ`): holds.** D2 checks every `k` on `(Z/4)³`: layer-symmetric plane waves have eigenvalue `E(k)`, and
antisymmetric ones have `14 − E(k)`.

**Step 5 (block 19 does not apply to `π` directly): holds** as a list of obstructions.

**Step 6 (RP for `θ_Γ`): holds.** D4:
- for every plane, the crossing edges are exactly the pairs `{v, θ_Γ v}`;
- the crossing kernel `e^{β t}` has positive Legendre coefficients.

**Step 7 (Gaussian domination on `Γ`): fails.** The step concludes that a field invariant under the group generated by the `θ_Γ` is
"spatially constant *and* equal on the two layers, hence constant", and so `Z_Γ(φ) ≤ Z_Γ(0)` for every `φ`. That is false:
- **The field `1_A` is invariant and not constant.** Let `A = {(x,0): |x| even} ∪ {(y,1): |y| odd}`. Every `θ_Γ` maps `A` onto itself
  (D3, all bond planes in three directions, `L = 4` and `6`). So `1_A` is invariant under the whole group.
- **The reason is geometric** (D4).
  - A vertical edge `(x,0)–(x,1)` never crosses a reflection plane, and every other edge does.
  - The reflection argument therefore makes a maximizing field constant on the non-vertical edges only, which means two-valued on `A`
    and `B`.
  - The staggered field `1_A − 1_B` is layer-antisymmetric, with `Γ`-Laplacian eigenvalue 2 (the odd sector at `k = (π,π,π)`).
    `Z(t·1_A) ≤ Z(0)` is exactly that mode's infrared bound, and the argument does not supply it.
- **What survives** (D5). Every reflected field `φ^±` of a layer-symmetric field is layer-symmetric (checked exactly, all planes).
  - On that class the vertical edges carry no twist, and the argument closes.
  - So Gaussian domination, and hence `⟨|ŝ_+^e(k)|²⟩ ≤ 1/(βE(k))`, hold for the **even** sector.
  - For the antisymmetric class the staggered field remains.

**Steps 8–10: partly unsupported.**
- **Step 8.** The even bound holds. The odd bound `2/(β(14−E))`, and with it the bound on `π`, rests on the failed part of step 7.
- **Step 9 (LRO).** It removes the odd modes from the sum rule using those odd bounds, so the threshold `3√3π/16 + 3/4` is not proved.
- **Step 10.** The Bogoliubov inequality holds for whatever `M_{+,N}` is. Its use needs step 9, and the upper side of the "sandwich"
  needs the odd bound.

**Step 11 (Dobrushin for `β < 1/7`): holds.** The interpolation bound `TV ≤ (β/2)|ΔS|` gives a one-step product kernel with row sum
`≤ 7β`.

**Step 12 (linear kernel): holds.** D6: the prefactor `7/(2(1−E/14))` runs from `7/2` to `49/2`.

## What the numbers do not show

The check found no violation of the unproved bounds (I1, INFO). In the Ising analogue on the 1D doubled graph (`L = 4, 6`,
`β = 0.1..2`):
- every even and odd infrared ratio is below 1, the largest being `0.7824`;
- the staggered-twist ratio `Z(t·1_A)/Z(0)` stays below 1, the largest being `0.9991`.

The failure is of the proof. The missing lemma is the staggered-twist bound `Z(t·1_A) ≤ Z(0)`, or an odd-sector infrared bound by another
route.

## Verdict

The claim fails at step 7. Steps 1–6, 11, 12 and the even-sector infrared bound hold. The bound on `π`, the LRO threshold and the
two-sided sandwich are not proved.
