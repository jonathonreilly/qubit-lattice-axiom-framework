## 1. Statement attempted

Block 143's assumption (A'), proved in its T6:
- **Where:** at `tan K0 = (5/6, 18/5)` on `Z^2` and `(5/6, 18/5, 1/2)` on `Z^3`.
- **Which functions:** every band function `E(q) = s1 eps(K0/2 + q) + s2 eps(K0/2 - q)`, with `eps = |sin k|` and `s1, s2 = +-1`.
- **Claim:** every stationary point away from the cone points `eps = 0` is nondegenerate.

## 2. Steps

**Step 1. Polynomial variables with no chart gaps. PROVED.**
- The bands depend on `k` only through `sin^2 k = (1 - cos 2k)/2` and `sin k cos k = (sin 2k)/2`.
- So take `C_a = cos 2k1_a` and `S_a = sin 2k1_a`, with `C_a^2 + S_a^2 = 1`. These cover every `k1` mod `pi`, with no point at infinity (unlike `t = tan k`).
- For `k2 = K0 - k1`: `cos 2k2_a = cos(2K0_a) C_a + sin(2K0_a) S_a` and `sin 2k2_a = sin(2K0_a) C_a - cos(2K0_a) S_a`. Both are rational in the data because `tan K0` is rational.

**Step 2. All four band pairs in one system. PROVED; CHECKED V1.**
- Let `e1 = s1 eps(k1)` and `e2 = s2 eps(k2)`, so that `e1^2 = sum sin^2 k1_a` and `e2^2 = sum sin^2 k2_a`. Then `E = e1 + e2`.
- **The gradient.** `dE/dq_a = S_a/(2 e1) - S2k_a/(2 e2)`. Off the cones, stationarity is equivalent to `S_a e2 - S2k_a e1 = 0`.
- **The Hessian.** `d2E/dq dq = H(k1)/e1 + H(k2)/e2`, with each term `f_ab/(2e) - f_a f_b/(4e^3)`, where `f_a = sin 2k_a` and `f_ab = 2 cos 2k_a delta_ab`.
  - This uses `s/eps^3 = 1/(s eps)^3`, valid because `s = +-1`.
  - The two sign flips from `k2 = K0/2 - q` cancel.
- **Check V1.** At a test point, for all four band pairs, these formulas agree with sympy's direct differentiation of `E` to 40 digits. This is a numeric control of the algebra.

**Step 3. The certificate. PROVED; CHECKED G2 and G3.**
- **The ideal.** It is generated by:
  - the circles;
  - `e1^2 - eps(k1)^2` and `e2^2 - eps(k2)^2`;
  - the stationarity equations;
  - `z e1 e2 - 1`, which excludes the cones;
  - the numerator of `det Hess`. Its denominator is a monomial in `e1, e2`, which is nonzero here.
- **The result.** Its reduced Groebner basis is `[1]` in the plane (about 2 s) and in space (about 45 s; the determinant numerator has degree 10).
- **Why this certifies (A').** Suppose some band pair had a stationary point `q` off the cones with `det Hess = 0`. Set `C, S` from `q`, `e_i = s_i eps(k_i)` and `z = 1/(e1 e2)`. Then every generator would vanish there, a common zero of an ideal equal to `(1)`, which is impossible.
- So every stationary point of every band pair off the cones is nondegenerate, even over C.

**Step 4. Controls. CHECKED G2 and C0.**
- Without the determinant, the plane's basis is not `[1]`: stationary points exist.
- At `K0 = 0` the band `E_{+-} = eps(q) - eps(-q)` vanishes identically, so every point is a degenerate stationary point. There the full system's basis is not `[1]`. So the test can fail.

**Step 5. Cones. CHECKED K1.**
- At a cone of record 1, the other record's energy has squared gradient `139761000/606502321` (plane) and `2653455542/8714332815` (space). Both are below 1, and the same holds at record 2's cones.
- So near a cone, `E = tau + s1|y| + t.y + O(|y|^2)` with `|t| < 1`.
- This is a strict conical minimum (for `s1 = +1`) or maximum (for `s1 = -1`), with closed tilted-cone level sets. It is not a smooth stationary point.
