**The wall in CM terms.** Einstein's two pieces (DeWitt kinetic, h·R potential) are invariant only up to a total derivative. On the 3D symmetric-tensor complexes, strictly invariant local densities have fixed orders: after the vector rule (order 1) comes Inc h (order 2), potential O(q⁴); after the scalar rule (order 2) comes C(E) (order 1), kinetic O(q²). Order sums 6 (k³: Gu–Wen L-type, landed 09-14), 4 (k²: Xu 2006, Pretko vector-charge, probes 15/18), 2 (k: comparator, N-type conjecture). - **Family.** Z_{n_G} clock slots; compactified DeWitt and h·R cosines; both rules as penalties, Ũ ~ J̃ ~ g̃. Mechanism: "quantum freeze" of the flat (ω = 0) helicity-0/±1 bands by discreteness; no numerics since 2009.

- **Belief.** A strong-coupling phase: O(1) rule violation in the vacuum, partners gapped non-perturbatively, ±2 left linear; its harmonic limit is the landed nilpotent scalar block.
- **Artifact.** (i) By Route 1, at Ũ ≫ J̃, g̃ the effective H is in the character class to all orders: a linear phase needs Ũ ≲ J̃. ## Applicable no-gos and escapes

- Weinberg–Witten PLB 96, 59 (1980) is silent on a lattice; Jenkins IJMPD 18, 2249 (2009): an emergent graviton is then non-relativistic or has no stress-energy operator.
- Watanabe–Murayama PRL 108, 251602; Hidaka PRL 110, 091601: linear modes on finite spins are type-A Goldstones of a conserved density; a spin-2 order parameter polarizes about its axis, not q̂.

- Moore–Nelson JHEP 09 (2001) 023: partners slower than c with O(1) coupling are excluded by gravitational Cherenkov; S8 weakening is closed.
