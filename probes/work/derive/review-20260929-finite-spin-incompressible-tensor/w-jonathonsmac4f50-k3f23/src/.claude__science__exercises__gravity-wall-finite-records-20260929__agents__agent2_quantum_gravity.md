## Route 1 — Number is the conformal factor: expand about the record-forming state, not a ground state

**Family.** Probe 11's helicity identity `4v₁ = v₂ + 3v₀` on the spin-2 f-sum; invariant: the sign of the compression (transverse-trace) coefficient v₀; obligation: a finite-slot local model, scalar rule exact, in a translation-invariant *non-ground* realized state, with two pure-TT linear modes and nothing else gapless.

**Premise dropped.** S4's "stable quadratic form" = probe 11's P1 (ground state). P1 is a state-selection premise; `realized_state_primitive` says the axioms select no state, so the wall rests on content the registry forbids as a premise. Finite slots do not imply P1. *Lemma (exact):* spin-S slot, `H = ωJ_z`, `O = J₋/√(2S)`: the f-sum in `|S,m⟩` is `−ωm/S`; the sign is the state's, not the dimension's. *Corollary of T2:* pure-TT at O(q²) ⟺ `v₀ = −v₂/3` exactly (Einstein's (½, 0, −⅙)). That negative v₀ is the conformal-factor problem: the gravitational action is unbounded below along conformal rescalings (Gibbons–Hawking–Perry, NPB 138 (1978) 141). GR hides it behind the Hamiltonian constraint; CDT cures it by the measure (Dasgupta–Loll, NPB 606 (2001) 357); causal sets make it Number (Bombelli–Lee–Meyer–Sorkin, PRL 59 (1987) 521); Jacobson fixes the volume for the same reason (PRL 116 (2016) 201101).

**Believe.** Forming a record *lowers* energy in the realized state (formation downhill, count rising irreversibly): the record-density channel is population-inverted.

**First artifact.** (i) Lemma and corollary above. (ii) Rerun probe 18(C)/21 with the transverse-trace channel expanded about the *top* of its ladder (signed effective weight; TT positive; scalar rule exact). PASS iff v₁ = 0; in probe 18's identity `c₁² = c₀²/2 + c₂²/4` that is `c₀² = −c₂²/2`. (iii) Sign test with probe 1's T1 formula `Tr ρ(Φ*(h_x) − h_x)`: any admissible state with it negative? Both computed states (singlet; walker's sea, +2.39 t) are endothermic, so the falsifier is live.

**Residual.** With the momentum rule broken the ±1 modes stay gapless but soft (probe 21(i)'s character argument is sign-independent: exact rules plus unit steps ⇒ slow). Removing them needs the LQG regime, a long ladder in a coherent state far from its ends: spin foams get a graviton only from large-spin coherent boundary states (Rovelli, PRL 97 (2006) 151301; Bianchi–Magliaro–Perini, NPB 822 (2009) 245), and finite link dimension only by q-deformation at the price of Λ (Han, JMP 52 (2011) 072501; Fairbairn–Meusburger, JMP 53 (2012) 022501): `j_max ~ 1/(Λℓ_P²) ≈ 2^400`, about 400 qubits per slot. So the wall ⟺ two facts about the realized state: (P1) ground in the compression channel; (P2) near the ladder's end. Neither is axiom content.

**Cost.** (i) hours; (ii) days (a sign flip in existing runners); (iii) days. **Changes.** S2 stops being load-bearing for the partner half; the wall becomes a testable statement about formation energetics.

## Route 2 — Marolf and Jacobson: gravity without a lattice graviton

**Family.** Entanglement first law `δS = δ⟨K⟩` for small balls at fixed volume (Jacobson 2016); invariant: the area-law coefficient η and its universality; obligation: a boost-like modular Hamiltonian at the ball scale. **Premise dropped.** S1, S8: no local tensor field; the target is the sourced Einstein equation. **Believe.** Boundary record count is the area; η is universal.

**First artifact (falsifier first).** On probe 19's frozen-box ensembles compute `I(ball : outside)` against boundary size and η against record density; non-universal η kills the route at step one. Known obstacles: the Widom log of the free-fermion carrier (04-25); a regular lattice picks a frame, a sprinkling does not (Bombelli–Henson–Sorkin, MPLA 24 (2009) 2579); δA needs a responding geometry (the June posit) unless A is a count. **Cost.** Days. **Changes.** Turns the frozen-box reading into a number.
