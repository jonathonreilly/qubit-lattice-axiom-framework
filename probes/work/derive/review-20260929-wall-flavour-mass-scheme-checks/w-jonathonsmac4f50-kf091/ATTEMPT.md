## Plain-language summary
The lane's family says the three charged-lepton masses are sqrt(m_k) = a(1 + 2 sqrt(r) cos(delta + 2 pi k/3)). With r = 1/2 and delta = 2/9 exactly, the ratio muon/electron comes out 206.7703160. The measured value is 206.7682827(46). That is a miss of 9.8 parts in a million, about 440 to 450 times the measurement error. The miss is already on main as a corrigendum; I reproduce it independently, but that is background, not new.

What I add: (i) a rigorous interval-arithmetic enclosure of the prediction and uniqueness of the fitting phase; (ii) the full covariance treatment (the shared muon mass matters at the 4e-4 correlation level only) and a closed-form split of the chi-square (it is z^2 of one number, m_mu/m_e); (iii) the geometry of the allowed region: the measured ratio defines a thin arc in the (r, delta) plane; r = 1/2 and delta = 2/9 each touch that arc, at points 2e-7 apart; (iv) a scheme analysis that is new relative to main: the one-loop "illustration" on main is mu-independent in its ratio shifts, and a reconstructed second-order map to masses m_i(m_i) (each lepton's MS-bar mass at its own mass) moves m_mu/m_e in the wrong direction (miss grows from 0.98e-5 to 2.0e-5). So neither of the two canonical schemes rescues the pair, and the miss is of the size of (alpha/pi)^2, which is why a scheme must be named. Nothing here derives r or delta, and nothing is a universal exclusion.

Covariance: use independent basis (ln m_e, ln R, ln m_tau) with R = m_mu/m_e from CODATA directly (the MeV muon mass is R*m_e, hence correlated with m_e; treating MeV m_e and m_mu as independent, as main's runner does, gives ratio error 4.5e-6 instead of CODATA's 4.6e-6, which is why main reads ~450 sigma and I read 442). 7. CHECKED (C7b). With the scale fitted the pair reproduces e, mu, tau to -1e-15, +9.8e-6, +3.1e-5 relative: close, not in agreement.
8. CHECKED (D3-D5). Geometry: the measured ratio fixes a curve delta_c(r) in the (r, delta) plane. Along it, the tau mass allows r in [0.49998145, 0.50001194] at 2 sigma. At r = 1/2 the curve sits at delta = 2/9 - 1.75e-7; at delta = 2/9 it sits at r* = 1/2 - 2.13e-7. Both points are in the tau-allowed window. The pair (1/2, 2/9) is therefore off the curve by a transverse step that is tiny in absolute terms (about 1.7e-7 in delta, 2e-7 in r) but 442 sigma of the ratio error. This sharpens "each number fits given the other" into one picture (NEW as a statement of geometry; the numbers are consistent with main's marginals).
9. READING (statistical meaning). The "450 sigma" is (miss)/(measurement error) for a point null on one number.
