## 2. Steps

1. PROVED. Pair rule. Two records at r1, r2 with contents (a,b) -> (u,v) keep P and L iff u + v = a + b and (u - a) is parallel to r1 - r2. Proof: dP = 0 gives v - b = -(u - a); dL = r1 x (u-a) + r2 x (v-b) = (r1 - r2) x (u - a). Since u - a is 0, +-2 e_k or +-e_i +-e_j, nontrivial events exist only for displacements along an axis or a face diagonal (never body diagonals or knight moves). CHECKED: check.py A1, A2 (443232 tuples, 0 mismatches, |d_i| <= 3).
2. CHECKED (REPLICATION, not new). Cluster census numbers of the source (bond 3D 2 of 36; face diagonal 8 of 36; body diagonal 0; 2D plaquette with 4 records: bond-level 120, pair-level 220, whole class 228; 3D plaquette 280/636/758) are reproduced by independent code: check.py B.
3. CHECKED (exhaustive, new ledger). On the excluded state space (5x5 torus N=2, 4800 configurations; 4x4 torus N=3, 35840) every event of O_note, O_class and V* keeps N and P; V* keeps L in every event (0 violations of 119296 events); O_class breaks L in 7168 events, all perpendicular exchanges; O_note in 39424 (exchanges, bond exchanges, re-draws). N=2 closed form: 4V x 2 = 200 perpendicular exchanges, matched. check.py C.
4. PROVED. Kinematic deficit. For every configuration c, P(c) - J(c) = D(c) := sum of contents of the records whose target is occupied. D depends on c only. Exchanges and class events move no occupancy, so no scattering rule can change J - P at any configuration. CHECKED: the set {c : J != P} has 300 elements at N=2 on 5x5 (= 12V: each of 2V adjacent pairs has 6 of 16 content pairs with exactly one record blocked) and is the same for every model (C2d, C3c). Consequence (READING): the kill's "J != P in 300 of 4800" is the same for V*, O and V* without class events because it is a property of streaming, as its own table shows.
5. PROVED (conditional on uniform stationarity) + CHECKED. Canonical sector mean. If the chain restricted to a content-multiset sector (all C(V,N) position sets times all arrangements of the multiset M) is irreducible with the uniform measure stationary, then E[J] = P (V-N)/(V-1). Proof: positions are independent of contents under the uniform measure; the target of a given record is occupied with probability (N-1)/(V-1); sum over records. Uniform stationarity of O: every record of a configuration has exactly one streaming preimage unless its behind-site holds the same content, and the numbers of such deficient records and of blocked-by-same records coincide (landed 2026-09-20 note, T2, line 76). CHECKED on the 4x4 torus, N=2: all 1920 states balance exactly (D1, 0 defects); the four full sectors have E[J]/P = 14/15 exactly (D2); classes confined to lines give 1 (different lines) and (4-2)/(4-1) = 2/3 (same ring) (D3). The grand-canonical (1 - rho) of the landed wind note T3 (line 76) is the large-V limit; step 5 is its finite exact form (small, elementary).
6. CHECKED (exact, new numbers). V* does not keep the uniform measure: 256 of 1920 states have unequal in/out streaming rate (D4). On the eight closed classes of size 60 with P = (+-1,+-1) the exact stationary vector (Fraction elimination, residual zero, D8) gives E[J]/P = 68/77 (gamma = 1), 100/113 (gamma = 2), 52/59 (gamma = 1/2), all strictly below 14/15 (D5, D6); total-variation distance from uniform 0.139 (D7). So at N=2 on this torus the L-repair lowers the energy-current ratio further. The kill's simulation (about 0.82 vs 0.88 at N=3) has the same direction; my exact numbers are for N=2 only.
7. PROVED + CHECKED. Placement lemma. For any stationary pi and bounded function nu of the state, E_pi[G nu] = 0 (G = generator). If an alternative local energy placement changes the energy dipole by a bounded amount nu (energy density = occupancy + a bounded divergence term), the current changes from J to J + G nu and its stationary mean is unchanged. So the shortfall <P - J> = <D> cannot be removed by re-placing energy density with bounded displacements. CHECKED exactly on the V* class for three random bounded nu (E1) and the resulting <J_x> = 68/77 < 1 (E2). READING: this is the placement-independent form of the kill's "(1 - rho)".
8. PROVED (necessity) + CHECKED (sufficiency, per event). Symmetric compact stress. If a change of momentum density has a compact symmetric stress, summation by parts gives dP = 0 and sum (x_k dg_j - x_j dg_k) = 0 = dL. CHECKED with exact rational elimination (replacing the source's floating-point residual): all 374 (P,L)-preserving events on bond, diagonal, L-triple, plaquette, 3-in-a-line and (0,0),(2,0),(1,1) subsets have a symmetric compact stress (F1); the perpendicular exchange of O has none but has a non-symmetric one (F2); random non-conserving plaquette changes have none (F3). Extends the old test to the distance-2 head-on pair used in step 9.
9. CHECKED (new). Synchronous comparator S. For every configuration of: the 2D 3x3 open window with N = 2..9 (1,953,088 configurations, 1,155,999 with a target conflict), the 2D 4x4 window N = 2..4 (503,680), and the 3D 2x2x2 cube N = 2..4 (103,824), the (P,L) class of the occupied sites contains a member with pairwise distinct targets (G1, G3, G4: 0 unresolvable). After collide-then-stream the exact ledger is N, P, L unchanged and total displacement = P, i.e. the energy current per tick equals P identically (G2; this part is an identity once the targets are distinct: L' = sum (r + c') x c' = L(c')). Local continuity of occupancy holds with the bond flux of each move; momentum flux of a move is c c (symmetric). So in S the energy current equals the momentum density site by site, with the compact symmetric stresses of step 8 for the collisions. Conflicts are real: 59% of the 3x3 configurations have one, so collisions are needed (control).
10. CHECKED (new, locality). Pair events at distance^2 <= 2 (bond and face diagonal) do not resolve all conflicts (antiparallel records two sites apart converge on one site: 6 of 576 at N=2, up to 9848 of 344064 at N=6); closures of two-record events with distance^2 <= 4 resolve every configuration of the 3x3 window for N = 2..6 (G5, G6). Closure is existence of a sequence of events, not a single simultaneous local rule.
11. CHECKED (limit of step 9). On periodic tori the result fails even for the weaker P-only class: 3x3 torus N = 3: 72 configurations, N = 4: 432; 4x4 torus N = 4: 224 have no conflict-free reassignment (G8). Example: records on the diagonal coset (0,0),(1,1),(2,2) of the 3x3 torus with contents summing to (2,-1).
12. READING. Combining 4, 7, 9: the T17 kill's final sentence ("exclusion still stops energy flowing as momentum ... the wall stands for both") is true for asynchronous rate-1 streaming with exclusion, with any N,P,L-preserving scattering, and with any bounded energy re-placement; it is not a consequence of one-record-per-site plus N, P, L conservation alone. The schedule (does a blocked record wait, or does the occupant move away in the same tick) carries the shortfall. This changes where the wall sits, not whether the walker wall (blocks 135-143) is touched: it is not.
