## Plain-language summary

The wall entry T21 says a record gas orders into a chessboard below a bond cost of g about 0.412, and calls this an exact threshold. Three things are true and one is not.

- True and exact: the contentless gas is the 3D antiferromagnetic Ising model with g = exp(-4K). So "0.412" is the Ising critical point, 0.4120 = exp(-4 x 0.2216546).
- Not true: that this number is exact. The 3D Ising critical coupling has no closed form and no proof of its value. 0.2216546 is a numerical literature value (reference only; I did not derive it). My small Monte Carlo run only puts the crossing near 0.41 (it is a size-6 against size-14 crossing).
- What can be proved: ordering for every g below 0.36388 (infrared bound, an imported named theorem whose instances I verified exactly), and no ordering for g above 25/49 = 0.5102 (Simon-Lieb, imported). The landed notes only reach 9/62500 = 1.4e-4 with contents absent. The numerical value sits inside the proved bracket; nothing here pins it.
- Scope correction: the "reflection-positivity floor" (the gas is reflection positive only for a binding scale at or above the neutral scale, g = 1 here) is a statement about the gas variables. For the contentless gas on an even torus, a sublattice complement turns the same measure into a ferromagnet, which is reflection positive for every g < 1. So reflection positivity does not by itself exclude g = 0.41 for the contentless gas. It says nothing about the supplied gas with six contents.

Nothing here derives a formation process, a rest mass, or a walker gap.

## Numbered steps

1. **PROVED. The gas is the AFM Ising model.** On a 2d-regular graph with dV edges, with n the occupation: let L be the number of edges with equal occupancy at both ends. Counting edges by endpoint values, Σ_edges (n_x + n_y) = 2dN, so the empty-empty edges number dV − 2dN + B, hence L = dV − 2dN + 2B and B − dN = (L − dV)/2. At ζ = g^-d (d = 3 is the landed ζ = g^-3), weight = g^((L−dV)/2) = g^(-dV/2) (g^(1/2))^L. This is the Ising model with σ = ±1 for empty/occupied, aligned neighbours penalised by e^(−2K) = g^(1/2), i.e. **g = e^(−4K)**, antiferromagnetic. This is T1 of the landed note; recovery, not new. CHECKED exactly (A1-A4): the ratio of gas weight to (1/2)^L equals 2^(dV) on all 65536 configurations of the 4x4 and 4x2x2 tori (g = 1/4) and on 300 random configurations of 4x4x4 (Fractions).
2. **PROVED (algebra). Normalization.** With no contents the "bond scale" g of the gas equals the binding scale c in the vacancy law and the neutral scale is c0 = 6/(p+q+4r) = 1 (equal pair weights), so g < 1 is "below the neutral scale". Same bookkeeping as `...PAIR_WEIGHT_TRANSIT...2026-09-20.md:27` (reflection-positivity floor) and the landed gas note.
3. **READING. What "0.412" is.** exp(−4 K_c) with K_c the 3D simple-cubic Ising critical coupling, 0.2216546 (literature, reference only; the landed pair-weight note also uses it only to calibrate a simulator, `...PAIR_WEIGHT_TRANSIT...2026-09-20.md:174`). It is a numerical value (Monte Carlo, series, bootstrap), not a theorem. No closed form exists and I know of no rigorous determination. T21's "exact Ising point" (kill note) is true of the identity in step 1 and false if read as "exact value".
4. **IMPORTED THEOREM (P3), instances CHECKED. Gaussian domination.** For the ferromagnet with e^(Kss') on an even torus, S(k) = <|σ^(k)|^2>/V ≤ 1/(2K E(k)), E(k) = Σ_i (1 − cos k_i), for k ≠ 0. The AFM gas is the ferromagnet in τ_x = (−1)^x σ_x, so the staggered structure factor obeys the same bound. CHECKED exactly (C1-C3): exact rational S(k) on the 4x4 and 4x2x2 tori at e^(−2K) = 1/2 and 1/3; the worst ratio S·2K·E is 0.72 (4x4, 1/2); the exact sum rule (1/V)Σ_k S(k) = 1; the normalisation of the constant 2K is thereby fixed (I tested it before relying on it).
5. **PROVED, conditional on P3. Staggered order for g below g_IR.** The sum rule gives <m_stag^2> = 1 − (1/V)Σ_{k≠0} S(k) ≥ 1 − G_L/(2K), G_L = (1/V)Σ_{k≠0} 1/E(k). CHECKED (C2): holds with room on the small tori. Then G_L → G_∞ = ∫d^3k/(2π)^3 1/(3 − Σcos k_i) = 0.5054620197 (Riemann sums of an integrable singularity; standard; CHECKED D2-D3: G_L increases with L and stays below G_∞ for even L = 4..128, and a fit reproduces G_∞ to 1e-6). The constant G_∞ is CHECKED two independent ways (D1: Bessel integral ∫e^(−3s) I0(s)^3 ds versus Γ-product, equal to 1e-23). Hence liminf_L <m_stag^2> ≥ 1 − G_∞/(2K) > 0 iff K > G_∞/2 = 0.25273101, i.e. **g < g_IR = exp(−2 G_∞) = 0.363883** (D4, D5). At g = 1/4: <m_stag^2> ≥ 0.2708 (D7). This is order of the periodic Gibbs measure in the usual infrared-bound sense (the averaged staggered correlation stays bounded below), for the contentless gas, grand canonical, ζ = g^-3, even tori. It improves the contentless threshold of the landed note, 9/62500 = 1.44e-4, by a factor 2527 (D6). The landed statement is different (framed boxes, pointwise <σ_x> ≥ 0.82, distinct framed limits); mine is not a pointwise bound and is for the symmetric torus measure. Not interchangeable.
6. **PROVED, conditional on P3. The disordered side.** Simon-Lieb with S = {0} gives χ ≤ 1/(1 − 2d tanh K), so no order for tanh K < 1/6, i.e. **g > ((1 − 1/6)/(1 + 1/6))^2 = 25/49 = 0.5102** (D8). CHECKED instance (E1): the one-site Simon-Lieb inequality holds exactly for every x ≠ 0 on the 4x4 and 4x2x2 tori at tanh K = 1/3.
7. **CHECKED. The bracket.** g_c ∈ [0.36388, 0.5102], where g_c is any transition value if one exists; the literature number 0.412047 lies inside (D9) and the proved width is 0.146 (D10). 8. **PROVED (exact algebra) and CHECKED. Reflection positivity and the "floor".** Across a bond plane the cross-bond form of the gas/AFM weight is [[a,b],[b,a]] with a/b = g^(1/2) (a < b): determinant a² − b² < 0, not positive semidefinite (B1, exact). After the sublattice complement the form is [[b,a],[a,b]], determinant b² − a² > 0 (B2).
