## 3. Why the verdict is nevertheless PARTIAL, not full derivation

The distinctness in 2.3 is necessary but not sufficient. The decisive
question is **point 4 of the attack**: does the derived chiral two-fold
legitimately multiply the spatial-corner count *at the place the magnitude
formula actually uses `16`*? It does not.

### 3.1 The hierarchy `16` is a determinant SPECTRAL count

The exact source of `16` in the hierarchy formula is the staggered Dirac
**determinant power** on the minimal block, NOT a generic "corner count":


(`L_s = 2`, `L_t = 4`, per
[`HIERARCHY_MATSUBARA_DETERMINANT_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-02.md`](HIERARCHY_MATSUBARA_DETERMINANT_NARROW_THEOREM_NOTE_2026-05-02.md),
effective_status **retained**). The exponent decomposes as

```text
16  =  4 (taste degeneracy per Matsubara mode)  x  4 (L_t temporal modes).
```

The per-mode factor `4` is the **4-fold taste degeneracy** `= 2^{d/2}` at
`d = 4`. The honest-status note's reading "`16 = 2^4` BZ corners" and this
note's reading "`16 = 2^{4/2} x L_t`" are the **same `16`** computed two
ways; both are **momentum-space mode counts on `Z^4`**.

### 3.2 The `4`-fold taste needs `Z^3 -> Z^4` (P2), not `8 x 2`

In genuine `d = 3` the staggered taste degeneracy is the **non-integer**
`2^{3/2} ~ 2.83` — there is no integer 4-fold taste; the `2^3 = 8` spatial
tastes collapse onto the 2-dimensional `M_2(C)` carrier
(`STAGGERED_TASTE_IS_THE_QUBIT_...`). The integer `4 = 2^{4/2}` per-mode
factor (and hence `u_0^{16}`) therefore **requires the Wick rotation

the load: using the genuine 3D spatial count `2^3 = 8` instead gives
`v_alt ~ 5.39 x 10^{10}` GeV, wrong by 9 orders of magnitude.

### 3.3 The chiral two-fold does NOT supply the determinant power

This is the crux. `det(D + m)` already enumerates **all** eigenvalues,
both chiral halves; `gamma_5` **labels** those eigenvalues into `S_+ / S_-`
but does not create independent determinant factors. Concretely, the
per-mode factor `4` is the **taste** multiplicity, already summed over
chirality; the temporal factor `4` is `L_t`. There is no separate
"`x2` from chirality" slot in the power. So:

```text
8 (spatial corners)  x  2 (chiral grading)   !=   the determinant power 16.
```

The `8 x 2_chiral` decomposition is a true arithmetic factorization of the
integer `16`, but it is **not the factorization the formula instantiates**.
The formula instantiates `2^{4/2} x L_t`, whose taste factor is the
rejected 4D count. The chiral two-fold lives on the spinor-module grading,
a different slot the determinant power does not read. Hence the native
`8 x 2_chiral` reading **cannot replace** the `Z^3 -> Z^4` content.

### 3.4 The Yukawa `256` exponent is a TENSOR-FACTOR count (bounded)

For the lepton scale, the finite core is `dim_C(M_2(C)^{otimes d}) = 4^d`
with `d = 4` an **explicit bounded parameter** (M2_TENSOR note,
effective_status **retained_bounded**; the note states it does NOT derive

operator algebra, not spacetime dimensions and not corners. Numerically
`256 = 4^4 = 16^2 = (2^3 x 2)^2`, but the M2_TENSOR exponent is a bounded
copy-count, so the `256` is not derived from `d=3+1` by that surface
either. (Its empirical match `a_lepton^2 ~ m_W/256` remains an
**open_gate**.)
