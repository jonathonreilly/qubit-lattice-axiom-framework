## Verdict in plain words
The wall bundles two claims. That the grid tick differs from a real-time step is true and harmless: lattice theory defines real time as the continuous flow of the Hamiltonian read off the transfer matrix, and a real-time step at equal edges is unstable in 2 or 3 space dimensions anyway. That 16 exists only in Euclidean time is false as stated: for free fermions the real-time space rebuilt from the Euclidean block is exactly two copies of the Hamiltonian one, so 16 versus 8 is a regulator choice, not a time choice. That prices the wall at a named premise. It does not derive 16; the interacting case stays open (T02).
