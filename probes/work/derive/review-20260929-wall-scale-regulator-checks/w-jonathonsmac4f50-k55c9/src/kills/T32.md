## Errors found
1. "Flow stops at alpha* = 0.042": T32_scratch/fp_reach.py shows n = 16 needs about 550-610 e-folds (240-265 decades) to cover 90% of the way. Over the 44 e-folds to 1 GeV alpha only falls 0.080 to 0.070. That is weak coupling, not a fixed point.
2. It also needs all 16 tastes massless. The repo says the staircase decouples them and the primary chain uses n_f = 6, 5 (YT_P2_TASTE_STAIRCASE...:Part 6). Missed prior art: same note (b_3(16) = 1/3, Part 7) and GAMMA_FULL_VS_GAMMA_CRIT...:60-70.
3. Pure glue is labelled "hidden"; it is explicit at CONFINEMENT_STRING_TENSION_NOTE:195, with quark screening at :113, :169-170, :184.
4. "Repo does not need it" is unsupported. The chain uses the pure-gauge beta = 6 plaquette (L06-W7: P 0.55-0.62 moves alpha_s(M_Z) 0.1235-0.1153). If 16 tastes are real, that input is wrong too (T29); unsaid.
5. The "cheaper question" is harder: a certified enclosure for 16-taste lattice QCD inherits T30.
6. "Misses 6 by 3.8e5" is an activity ratio (PLAQUETTE_VALUE_DERIVATION...:40-41); in beta it is 1.9e4.
7. "0.205 +- 0.007" should be +-0.016 (my jackknife, errcheck_output.txt), and the value drifts with T (0.244, 0.223, 0.205).
8. Tests: T-A's "volume failure" comes from fitting to R = L/2 on 8^4 (design flaw). T-D uses periodic slabs, not Def 2.1's Dirichlet slabs, and its kill outcome was already excluded by the known transition (beta_c(N_t = 6) about 5.89), so it could not decide R2. Finite-T smeared effective forces are not Bachas bounds.
9. "proved (literature)" for Chatterjee and Bachas breaks the brief's rule.

Verified correct: the overlabelled-note finding (HADRON_LANE1...:149; COSMOLOGICAL_CONSTANT_SPECTRAL_GAP...:246-248); n-scan and T-E arithmetic; T-C values; Polyakov transitions.

## Corrected plain-language verdict
The wall stands: no one can prove an area law and gap for pure-glue SU(3) at beta = 6, and the repo's chain uses exactly that measure. The best route, Chatterjee's reduction, ends at a premise as hard as the wall. The taste content may change the infrared theory, but that is T30's question, and the quoted fixed point is never reached in 19 decades.
