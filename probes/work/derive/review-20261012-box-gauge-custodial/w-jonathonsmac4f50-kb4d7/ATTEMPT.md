## 0. Plain words

R8-5 showed that a gauge-invariant lattice QED with an exactly relativistic "box" still has an order-one split between the electron and photon speeds, because the photon couples to the full charge of every mode, including the zone-edge modes. It left one door open: rotate the charge density into the energy-band frame outside the box so the soft photon sees no pair creation there, and add a gauge-invariant transverse (Pauli-type) coupling to put back whatever Lorentz structure is wanted.

I tested the first half of that door and found where it stops.
- The rotation works as advertised in one narrow sense: outside the box the minimal pair-creation current is exactly zero, with Gauss-law commutativity and charge rigidity kept exactly (S2-S5).
- It does not remove the contamination. The interband current is moved into the shell between box and edge, where a Cauchy-Schwarz bound keeps it above a computed floor, and the electric response goes up, not down (S6, S7).
- "Minimal coupling" of the rotated hopping is not unique. Two lattice dressings that are both exactly gauge covariant give photon cone splits of +3.5 and +26.5 (units e^2/16pi^2) for the same density frame and the same hopping (S8). So the transverse flux-dependent kernel decides the photon cone; it is not a decoration.
- With the straight-path dressing the photon-side split is order one, changes sign with the edge mass, and has a tuned zero (S7). I did not compute the electron side, so the actual cone split of the specified construction is still unknown (S11).

I did not find a Ward-consistent construction that controls the contamination, and I did not refute one. Nothing here is derived from the four axioms.

## 2. Setting (S1, ASSUMED)

Box QED (BQ), R8-5 §3: one 4-component Dirac fermion per site, h(k) = a(k).Gamma with Gamma = (alpha_x, alpha_y, alpha_z, beta), a = (S(k_x), S(k_y), S(k_z), m + sum_j W(k_j)), S = rho k + (1-rho) sin k, W = r(1-rho)(1-cos k), rho a C^infinity plateau (1 on |k|<=b1, 0 for |k|>=b2). E = |a|, n = a/E. With m>0, r>=0 we have n_4 > 0 everywhere. Non-compact U(1) photon, continuous time, one loop, one species. Photon-side responses use the static polarization at O(q^2): P_E = coefficient of the Coulomb (rho-rho) bubble, P_M = coefficient of the current-current bubble with diamagnetic term, both with the normalization fixed in `check.py` (section F) and cross-checked against the source runner; dc := 16pi^2 (P_M - P_E)/2 in units of e^2/16pi^2 (delta c_gamma). All numbers below use m = 0.5 (IR-benign); m -> 0 was not done.

Specified construction (R8-5 §4.2(b)-(c)): density rho(y) = sum_x c(x-y) psi~_x^dag psi~_x, psi~ = U psi, U(k) a smooth unitary band frame (U = 1 on the box, band frame outside), plus an arbitrary gauge-invariant transverse coupling K.

## 3. Steps

**S2. Reduction lemma (PROVED; CHECKED A1-A9).** Let U be a translation-covariant unitary, c a scalar (or pairwise-commuting matrix) function, rho(y) = psi^dag m_y psi with m_y = U^dag (c(. - y) (x) 1) U.
(i) The m_y commute: they are U^dag-conjugates of multiplication operators. (ii) sum_y m_y = (sum c) 1, so the charge symbol is the constant (sum c) times the identity: the charge eigenspace is all of C^n at every k, rank constant (Lemma C satisfied, with nothing tied to an energy band). (iii) Kernel mu(k',k) = hat c(k'-k) W(k') W(k)^dag, W = U. (iv) In the tilde variables the gauge transformation is a pure local phase with smeared parameter, so every gauge-covariant bilinear Hamiltonian with H(0) = H_0 is, up to gauge-invariant flux-dependent terms, the Peierls dressing of h~ = U h U^dag with the potential smeared by c. [Argument: covariance fixes H_{ts}(u) up to a factor gauge-invariant in the links; divide by the holonomy along a chosen reference path; what is left depends on fluxes only and vanishes at zero flux.]
Consequences. (a) A density of this class cannot depend on the pair invariant mass; the vertex is hat c(k'-k) times a unitary factor. Any invariant-mass form factor must sit in the transverse vertex (this answers the open item at report :189). (b) Within the pairwise-commuting matrix-c class the same holds channel by channel. The classification of ALL translation-covariant commuting families is not attempted.
Exact finite-lattice checks (3x3 torus, n=2, Gaussian rationals): A2-A4 projector/commutation/charge identity/translation covariance; A5 smeared density; A6 a localized non-orthogonal density does not commute (control); A7 the rotated-frame dressing is exactly covariant under the gauge group generated by the rotated densities; A9 the unrotated orbital dressing is not (frame and coupling must be rotated together).

**S3. Current in the original variables (PROVED; CHECKED B4, B5, F2, F3).** d(R h R^dag) = R h' R^dag + [R' R^dag, R h R^dag]: the rotated minimal coupling is the original one with a non-Abelian connection R'R^dag (for the slice, phi' beta alpha_x). Its interband weight is Tr[P+ h~' P- h~'] = 2 E^2 (theta~')^2, independent of E'. It is zero wherever the rotated polar angle theta~ is constant, i.e. wherever the frame equals the band frame. So the Ward-fixed soft pair-creation current (report §4.2(a)) is switched off outside by choice of frame; this confirms the report's "suggested" remark at :183-188 for this class.

**S4. A global smooth frame exists for BQ (PROVED; CHECKED B2, B3, F1, F2).** For m>0, W>=0 one has n_4>0, so the rotor R = cos(phi) + sin(phi) beta e.alpha (e = (S_j)/|S|, B = beta e.alpha, B^2 = -1) with phi = (1-rho_3) theta/2, theta = arccos n_4, rho_3 = rho(k_x)rho(k_y)rho(k_z), is C^infinity (composition of the plateau with analytic maps; sin(theta~)/sin(theta) is analytic in |S|^2) and unitary, with no bundle obstruction. R h R^dag = E(cos theta~ beta + sin theta~ e.alpha), theta~ = rho_3 theta (exact identity checked with rational Dirac matrices, B2). It equals h on the box and E beta (fixed band projector (1-beta)/2) outside the shell. Hopping is therefore C^infinity-local. (Gevrey class not claimed.)

**S5. Charge-eigenspace constancy vs fixed energy band (PROVED; CHECKED F4, B3).** Lemma C fixes the multiplicity of the charge eigenspaces (here the full C^4, rank 4 everywhere, in both frames). It says nothing about the band subspace P_-(k). In the original frame P_-(k) varies (deviation 0.167 between sampled outside points); in the rotated frame it is exactly (1-beta)/2 outside (deviation 0). The frame rotation converts charge-space constancy into band-subspace constancy outside the shell without touching charge rigidity.

**S6. Floor on the electric channel (PROVED; CHECKED F5, F8, F9).** Claim: P_E(rotated) >= 2 int_{|k_y|,|k_z|<=b1} d^2k/(2pi)^3 theta_e(k_y,k_z)^2 / int_{b1}^{b2} 2E dk_x, with theta_e the Dirac polar angle at the cube face. Proof: P_E = <|d_x n~|^2/(2E)> (cubic symmetry; pair weight formula: B5 with the factor in C1). On each x-line theta~ goes from theta_e at k_x=b1 to 0 at b2, and |d_x n~| >= |d_x theta~|, so by Cauchy-Schwarz int |d theta~|^2/(2E) >= (int |d theta~|)^2 / int 2E >= theta_e^2 / int 2E. Sum the two slabs. Numbers (x16pi^2, m=0.5, r=1): shape (1,2) floor 2.01 vs computed 10.06; shape (2,3) floor 7.04 vs 22.61. The floor is a lower bound on one channel only; it does not bound dc.

**S8. Minimal coupling of the rotated hopping is ambiguous at O(F) (PROVED A8; CHECKED E1-E3, F12, F13).** Rotating the frame makes the tilde hopping non-separable (diagonal hops). A hop (R_x, R_y) then has no canonical path. Exact finite-lattice check A8: the x-first and y-first link-path dressings are both exactly gauge covariant, agree on pure-gauge links, and differ on generic links by a flux-dependent factor, i.e. by a transverse nonminimal term. For the vacuum response: exact diagonalisation of a 14x14 torus with a 9-hop matrix-valued non-separable Peierls model gives a^2 coefficients 0.2511 (straight path) and 0.4023 (trapezoid, H = (H_xy+H_yx)/2); the one-loop formulas reproduce both to <=1.7e-5 (E1, E2) and the electric coefficient is identical (E2). In the BZ code the same rotated density and hopping give dc = +3.48 vs +26.47 (F12); box QED, whose hops are axis-only, has no ambiguity (F13). So "frame-rotated density + minimal coupling" does not by itself define the photon cone; the O(F) (and O(dF)) dressing contributes at the same order as the Maxwell term because it multiplies the O(1) minimal vertex. **This is the new scoped result (HIT in `check.py`).**

**S9. Physical pole (PROVED given the imported Sigma_xi form; CHECKED D1, D2).** The xi-dependent self-energy has the form c1 a + c2 a X a, a = G^-1(p) (report :250-253; the identity (a-b)b^-1(b-a) = 2a-b-ab^-1a behind it holds, D1). The pole is where a(p) has a null vector u; first-order pole shift is <u|Sigma|u>. Since a u = 0 and a is Hermitian on shell, c1 a + c2 a X a annihilates u from both sides for ANY X, c1, c2 (D2, exact rational Dirac matrices): the O(e^2) pole, hence the speed, is xi-independent for every xi and m, not only as an "(1-xi)-small" off-shell scan. The finite-m B-A coefficient at p=0 is an off-shell diagnostic, as the referee noted (verdict :9); the speed is defined at the pole in the m -> 0 limit where beta-type p^2 terms (proportional to m) drop. Imported, not re-derived: the Ward identity and longitudinal-seagull algebra giving the stated form.

**S10. Transverse kernel K: what can and cannot be said (SUGGESTED).** (a) Any Hermitian gauge-invariant one-body K enters the gapped-vacuum energy at second order with a definite sign (-sum |K_cv|^2/gap <= 0), so a K^2 term can only lower P_M (raise the effective permeability); raising P_M needs the cross term with the minimal vertex, whose sign is not fixed. At the r=1 shapes above P_M > P_E for the straight path, so a K^2 term alone has the right direction on the photon side; this is an argument, not a computation. (b) Locality: K with C^infinity symbol gives super-polynomially decaying hopping. (c) Positivity of the matter Hamiltonian: a quadratic fermion Hamiltonian with bounded one-body part is bounded below per volume for any bounded K; reflection positivity of the Euclidean action with K was not analysed. (d) Counting: one number per speed at one loop, a free function K(k): the photon half can be tuned (S7 shows zeros already without K); whether the electron half can be tuned simultaneously and, more importantly, whether any principle fixes K without tuning is not shown. This is the R7-5 referee's "imposed functional custodial condition" with the gauge constraints now checked (S2-S5).

**S11. FIRST UNRESOLVED STEP (UNRESOLVED).** The electron-side speed delta c_e of the rotated law is not computed. It needs a photon-sector dressing for non-axis hops (BQ's photon lives on axis links only), which by S8 is exactly the transverse kernel; so delta c_e and the full Delta = delta c_e - delta c_gamma of the specified construction are undetermined. Also not done: explicit K, m -> 0, two loops, compact/finite link truncations.

**S12. READING.** The route is neither refuted nor derived. What survives: (i) the Ward/Gauss/charge-rigidity constraints are met by a rotated density, so Theorem B is not an obstruction to switching off the minimal pair-creation current outside the box; (ii) that current reappears in a shell with an electric floor and a photon cone that depends on the dressing; (iii) a Lorentz-invariant answer would then be a statement about the nonminimal kernel, which the axioms do not supply (`MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md:114-130`).
