## 0. Plain words

- The previous work (R8-4, R9-7) showed that the "toy N" two-loop failure came mostly from leaving out 14 of the 24 time orderings of the one connected graph. With all 24, and a vertex function that depends only on the invariant mass of the pair created together, the two-loop dispersion closes exactly for bosons (R9-7's "P2"). That model lets legs escape the lattice momentum box.
- This attempt (1) writes the complete model down, vertex function by vertex function; (2) re-verifies the 24-ordering formula against an exact finite-Fock calculation in rational arithmetic, with complex kernels, mixed statistics and a sign rule derived from Wick contractions; (3) finds exactly which statistics assignments give the bosonic signs; (4) confines the legs with a plateau on the dressed line's momentum and measures what that costs; (5) shows that the all-fermion (finite-per-site) assignment is not closed and that its defect is exactly minus twice a sum over 12 sign-flipped orderings.
- Result in one sentence: closure is available only with bosonic X and Y, and the confinement cost is exponentially small in the cutoff in this 1+1D toy; with the finite-per-site (all CAR) assignment the first failing step is the 12 sign-flipped orderings, and the one-loop factor h does not repair it.

## 2. Setting and every premise

- **S1 (supplied).** Toy N of R8-4: 1+1D, infinite volume, continuum dispersion `E = sqrt(m^2+q^2)` on every leg, species X, Y, A, B (outer quartet) and Y, U, C, D (inner quartet), masses 0.30, 0.33, 0.37, 0.41, 0.29, 0.35, 0.43 (`r84_toyN_cont.py:19-22`), plateau `F(s) = 1 - sigma((sqrt(s)-L1)/(L2-L1))` with `(L1, L2) = (1, 1.4)`, `sigma` the `e^{-1/x}` smooth step. Observable: the `O(g_o^2 g_i^2)` coefficient of `E(X_p) - E(vac)`, normalised as `m^2_mix(p) = 2 E_p mix(p)`. "Closure" means `m^2_mix(p)` is p-independent; the figure of merit is `eps = [m^2(p) - m^2(0)]/|m^2(0)|` at `p = m_X/3` (rapidity fixed under mass scaling).
- **S2 (supplied dynamics).** Perturbation theory in `H = H0 + g_o V_o + g_i V_i`, Rayleigh-Schroedinger fourth order, `E3 = 0`, `E4 = <VRVRVRV> - E2<VR^2V>`. Admissibility supplies no such H (`MINIMAL_AXIOMS_2026-06-29.md:116-121`).
- **S3 (the complete crossing set, defined here).** The only connected graph is the sunset `X -> Y A B` with the `Y` line dressed by the inner sunset `Y -> U C D`. Its four vertices carry these monomials (normal-ordered `A^dag(C) A(D)`; each with its Hermitian conjugate):
  - outer, momentum-conserving, kernel `kappa_t`: `dec` `a+_Y a+_A a+_B a_X`; `vac` `a+_X a+_Y a+_A a+_B`; `22` (2-to-2) `a+_A a+_B a_X a_Y`; `31` (reverse decay) `a+_X a+_A a+_B a_Y`;
  - inner: `idec` `a+_U a+_C a+_D a_Y`; `ivac` `a+_Y a+_U a+_C a+_D`.
  - The A,B pair is always created or absorbed together, as is the U,C,D cluster (checked, A1b), so XA->YB, A->XYB and similar monomials cannot enter this graph.
- **S4 (vertex functions of model M\*, defined here).** All four outer types carry the same function, all with the Y-momentum plateau:
  `kappa_t = c(q_Y) Phi(s_AB) / sqrt(2E_X 2E_Y 2E_A 2E_B)` for t in {dec, vac, 22, 31} (so the 2-to-2 and reverse-decay vertex functions equal the decay ones: pair invariant mass of the created-or-absorbed A,B pair, blind to X and to the energy of Y), with `Phi = F(s_AB)`; inner `kappa = c(q_Y) Psi(U,C,D) / sqrt(2E_Y 2E_U 2E_C 2E_D)`, `Psi = F(s_UC)F(s_UD)F(s_CD)`. `c(q) = plateau(|q|; C1, C2)` with `C2 = 1.3 C1`; `c = 1` is the unconfined case (R9-7's "P2"). Phases: all kernels real positive (invariants `J1 = J2 = 0`, see step 5). `c` is a frame-fixed function of the Y mode momentum, so it is not an invariant-mass function; that is the price of confinement.
- **S5 (statistics).** Each species is CCR boson or CAR fermion; every monomial must contain an even number of fermion operators.
- **S6 (not assumed here).** No lattice dispersion outside the box, no d=3, no microcausality, no cluster separability, no non-perturbative ground state. "Box-confining" below means every leg momentum stays within the printed bound; whether the R7-1 box law admits that bound in this toy is inherited from R8-4's leg plateau `A1 = 2.0` (`r84_toyN_cont.py:19`) and not rechecked.

2. **[CHECKED A2, A3] Exact finite Fock = 24-ordering formula.** `check.py` builds the Hamiltonian on a ring `Z_L` (L = 2, 3), 7 species, random rational energies and random complex rational kernels per momentum-conserving mode tuple, applies explicit CAR/CCR operators in an exact rational Fock basis (unnormalised occupation basis for bosons), and computes the RS4 mixed coefficient `E4(1,1) - E4(1,0) - E4(0,1)` for `X_p` minus vacuum. The closed form is the sum over 24 orderings and all mode labels of `sign * product of four kernels (conjugated on h.c. vertices) / product of three denominators`. The sign is derived, not fitted: `(-1)^(crossing pairs)` of the Wick-contraction arcs of fermion species in the string `a_p [M_latest ... M_earliest] a^dag_p`. Result: exact equality (zero difference) in 10 cases: all bosons; {A,B}, {U,C}, {A,B,U,C}, {X,A}, {X,Y,U}, {A,C,Y}, all fermions; p = 1; L = 3 all fermions. 3. **[PROVED + CHECKED A4, A4b] Complex kernels and rephasing.** Under `a_s -> u_s a_s` (|u| = 1) the six type phases change by `sum_cre chi - sum_ann chi`. The gauge action has rank 4 on the six phases, so there are exactly two invariants, `J1 = phi_dec - phi_vac - phi_22 + phi_31`, `J2 = -phi_dec + phi_22 - phi_idec + phi_ivac` (sympy null space). Every ordering's total kernel phase is `a J1 + b J2` with `(a,b) = (0,0)` in 12 orderings, `(0,+-1)` in 3 each, `+-(1,1)` in 3 each. A species-level gauge transformation leaves the exact Fock coefficient unchanged (A4). So the two-loop dispersion depends on kernel phases only through `(J1, J2)`; real positive kernels are `J1 = J2 = 0`. At `J = pi/2` closure is lost (B5e): `eps = -4.6e-3` for `J2 = pi/2`, `-6.7e-3` for `J1 = pi/2`, `-0.22` for both. This was not in the sources (R9-7 used real kernels).
