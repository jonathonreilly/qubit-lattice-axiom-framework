## 4. Claimed flaw in X3’s earlier tests

Substantially confirmed, with one overstatement corrected.

- The free open-time, periodic-space Wilson kernel has exact boundary-localized zero modes, while antiperiodic space or the torus removes them ([k2t2_geometry_and_sanity.out:1–12](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/chirality_ex/ref_K2/scripts/k2t2_geometry_and_sanity.out:1)). Hence X3 lacked a uniform gap and its locality diagnosis was incomplete.
- X3’s \(2.6\!-\!5.7\times10^{-3}\) quadrature change was indeed hundreds to thousands of times larger than the K2 effect ([X3_COMBO_REPORT.md:177–181](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/chirality_ex/ref_K2/X3_COMBO_REPORT.md:177)). Its “no violation” result was therefore inconclusive.
- But “zero modes independent of the gauge field” and “the overlap was therefore not local” are too strong. Gauge fields generally shift the edge states; K2’s own boundary-Wilson-line experiment leaves the opposite boundary zero mode untouched ([k2t2_geometry_and_sanity.py:33–38](/private/tmp/claude-502/-Users-jonBridger-Projects-Physics-baremetal-probes--claude-worktrees-toe-leverage-analysis-e8a790/af5789b9-888d-43b1-90de-58a07e0f2129/scratchpad/chirality_ex/ref_K2/scripts/k2t2_geometry_and_sanity.py:33)). The free zero modes invalidate a uniform-gap argument, but do not prove that rough bulk plaquettes played no role in X3’s random configurations.

On the \(2\times12\) open-time cylinder with antiperiodic space, \(M=1\), \(m=0.05\), two-dimensional vector-like U(1) overlap fermions, and a positive-type Fejér triangle weight on every crossing plaquette, there exists an eight-configuration gauge-invariant link-reflection kernel whose smallest eigenvalue is \(-4.4603\times10^{-5}\), or \(-7.4055\times10^{-6}\) relative to its largest eigenvalue, while the Wilson control is positive; the sign is numerically secure and all sampled configurations are admissible with a large Wilson-kernel gap.
