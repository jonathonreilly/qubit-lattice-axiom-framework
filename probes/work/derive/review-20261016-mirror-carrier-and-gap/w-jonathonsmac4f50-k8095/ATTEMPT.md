# Carrier count and the interacting mirror gap: what the symmetry group allows

Task `J:derive:review-20261016-mirror-carrier-and-gap:a1`. Worker: Claude Sonnet 5.5, same family as the sources, unrefereed. No repository file edited.

## Plain words first
1. Counting facts (kept short, all re-checked, none new): a real "reduced" staggered field has 8 real components, which is one Dirac fermion (two Majorana-Weyl). Two doublets can be halved, but the half is one ordinary doublet, so nothing is gained. Three right-handed neutrino labels cannot be matched by reduced fields without a surplus fourth. Real (SO(N)) stand-ins are not Standard Model carriers.
2. The eight-Majorana quartic has gap 14W and large W gaps every mode. In the checked dimer of two clusters nothing is left light, so these facts say nothing for or against a chiral phase.
3. New piece: in the supplied nearest-neighbour staggered law, the site-parity symmetry P_stag swaps the two Weyl chirality blocks of every momentum fibre. Time reversal Theta_M and the shifts do not. Together the three symmetries move any one of the four (chirality x taste) blocks to every other. So a state that keeps P_stag cannot have a light left-handed block and a gapped right-handed one. A chiral light sector needs P_stag broken, either in the interaction or spontaneously in the state. That leg is also one of the two symmetries the strain-zero argument relies on.
4. Not shown: that a P_stag-breaking, shift-breaking, Theta_M-keeping interaction with a unique gapped heavy sector exists. That is the first unresolved step.

## Steps
Labels: PROVED (argument given), CHECKED (executed in `check.py`), ASSUMED, READING.

1. ASSUMED. The lattice law above. CHECKED (L1-L7, `check.py`): B is real antisymmetric; `eps B eps = -B`; `S_mu` with `zeta_mu = (-1)^(x_{>mu})` commutes with B (the naive `zeta = eta` does not: control); the `S_mu` anticommute pairwise and square to translation by 2; P_stag commutes with B.
2. CHECKED (F1-F4, all 64 momenta q in {1,i,-1,-i}^3 on the 4^3 torus). On envelopes `xi = q^x u(x mod 2)` the law reduces to `h(k) = -sum_j sin k_j G_j`, with `G_j u(x) = eta_j(x) u(x+e_j)` on 8 classes; `S_mu -> q_mu S0_mu`; P_stag maps fibre q to 1/q with `u -> eps u`; Theta_M maps q to 1/q with `u -> eps conj(u)`. Because both sides are linear in `(q_j - 1/q_j)`, the three unit directions give all q.
3. CHECKED (A1-A3, A10). The G_j, the S0_mu are real symmetric involutions, each triple pairwise anticommuting; every S0_mu commutes with every G_j; eps anticommutes with all of them.
4. PROVED + CHECKED (A4, A5, A6, A9). Define chirality `Sigma = -i G1G2G3`. It is hermitian, `Sigma^2 = 1`, traceless, purely imaginary, commutes with every G_j hence with `h(k)` for all k. Since eps anticommutes with each G_j it anticommutes with the product: `eps Sigma eps = -Sigma`, so P_stag exchanges the blocks. S0_mu commute with G_j, hence with Sigma. Theta_M: `eps conj(Sigma) eps = eps(-Sigma)eps = +Sigma`, so Theta_M preserves them.
5. CHECKED (A7). `S1S2S3 = -i Sigma` exactly: the chirality label is the eigenvalue of the central shift element. (So the chirality projectors are k-dependent on the lattice; the statements below are fibre-wise spectral statements.)
6. CHECKED (A10, A11, B1). Taste generators `T_mu = S0_mu Sigma` are involutions, pairwise anticommuting, commuting with G_j and Sigma. `eps T_mu eps = T_mu` (P_stag trivial on this taste, matching NF-1's `Gamma_eps = beta x 1`), while `eps conj(T_mu) eps = -T_mu` (Theta_M antiunitary, flips all three taste Paulis). The projectors `Pi_{s,t} = (1+s Sigma)/2 (1+t T_3)/2` are four orthogonal rank-2 projectors summing to 1.
7. CHECKED (B2-B4). Action on block labels (s = chirality, t = taste): S1, S2, Theta_M flip t and keep s; S3 keeps both; P_stag flips s and keeps t. The group generated acts transitively on the four blocks. Only P_stag changes s, so every word that fixes a block contains an even number of P_stag factors, and an even number of factors from {S1, S2, Theta_M}.
8. PROVED (short). Spectral identity. Let H commute with P_stag and the state `omega` be P_stag-invariant (a unique ground state, or any invariant state). Let `Psi(q)` be the Fourier-transformed 8-component field, `G_ab(t,q) = omega(Psi_a(q,t)^dagger Psi_b(q))`. Step 2 gives `P Psi_b(q) P^-1 = eps_b Psi_b(1/q)`, so invariance gives `G(t,1/q) = E G(t,q) E`. With `E Pi_L E = Pi_R`: `Pi_R G(1/q) Pi_R = E (Pi_L G(q) Pi_L) E`. The left- and right-chirality spectral functions are equal at q and 1/q, interaction included. CHECKED for the free propagator (W4, exact at four momenta). Corollary: with unbroken P_stag the light sector is left-right symmetric, so it is not chiral. The same argument with S1 gives equal spectral weight in taste t = +1 and -1 under the unbroken-shift hypothesis.
9. READING (consequence). A chiral light block (one of the four) needs a state or Hamiltonian invariant only under the block's stabiliser: S3, S1S2, Theta_M S1, Theta_M S2 and their products. P_stag is not in it, and neither is any product containing P_stag an odd number of times. Alternatives to "unbroken": (a) explicit P_stag breaking in the interaction; (b) spontaneous P_stag breaking: vacua `omega` and `omega o P` with `A_L^{omega o P} = A_R^{omega}`, allowed to be chiral, but the NF-1 strain-zero theorem as stated in TW1 section 3(c) (P_stag forces commutation) then lacks its hypothesis in such a vacuum; (c) for taste: explicit shift breaking or spontaneous taste-polarised vacua. TW3's sentence that Theta and P_stag "do not forbid" selective gapping (TW3 report, 3(d) point 4) is correct for the T-taste split and silent on chirality, which is where P_stag acts.
10. CHECKED (W1-W3). On the `Sigma = +1` block `h(k)^2 = |sin k|^2` (massless Weyl). The space of matrices anticommuting with all three G_j has dimension 8 and each member anticommutes with Sigma, so its diagonal blocks vanish: every gap-opening bilinear mass couples L to R. A single chirality block carries no bilinear mass at all, with or without Theta_M and P_stag. READING: for a complex staggered field with a chiral light block, the bilinear strain-zero question is kinematic and the P_stag leg of the protection is not what protects it; whether strain acts on such a block through the heavy sector is not computed. For the reduced (real) field Sigma is imaginary, so complex conjugation exchanges the two blocks: chirality is not an independent label there (READING, from A4).
11. CHECKED + PROVED (C1-C9). Carrier counts, disjoint from `tw1_alg.py`: Frobenius-Schur indicators from explicit finite subgroups (SU(2) doublet via Q8 = -1; U(1) charge 1 via Z4 = 0; SO(3) triplet via the 24 cube rotations = +1; SU(3) triplet via its centre = 0); real commutant of one doublet is the quaternions (division algebra, so no invariant real 2-plane), of two doublets 16-dimensional with the invariant diagonal 4-plane; 8 real comps = 1 Dirac; 3 ν_R labels need 2 reduced fields; complex staggered fields give `2R + 2Rbar` so n families + m mirror families match iff n = m even (checked n, m <= 8). Not new.
12. CHECKED (Q1-Q6). Eight-Majorana quartic (signs from the supplied 14-term list): characteristic polynomial `(x+14) x^8 (x-2)^7`, so gap 14W; flipping one sign breaks it (control). Fidkowski-Kitaev pattern of `T^2` and `T P T^-1` for 2, 4, 6, 8 Majoranas from exact matrices (for 2 and 6 the antiunitary needs a parity factor to fix all gammas). New elementary remark, PROVED by operator counting (Q5): for 4 Majoranas the only T-symmetric parity-even hermitian operators are `1` and `P`, so no interaction can give a unique symmetric ground state; for 8 there are 72. Known (FK). Not a new result.
13. CHECKED (D1-D4). Two coupled clusters (16 Majoranas, 256 states), `H = W(Omega1+Omega2) + t V`, `V = i sum_a g_a(1)g_a(2)`, `||V|| = 8`. At `t = 0` the spectrum is exactly `-28, -14 (x16), ...`: unique ground state, gap 14. For W = 10 and 3 with t = 1 the numerical gap exceeds the Weyl bound `14W - 2t||V||`. All 16 Majoranas are gapped, both chirality blocks and both tastes alike, so the 14W gap and the large-W limit of this model are a trivially gapped phase and say nothing about a chiral lattice phase. Finite clusters only; no thermodynamic limit, no 3D lattice.
14. UNRESOLVED (first unresolved step). Existence (or exclusion) of a cube-local interaction, breaking the shift group and P_stag but keeping Theta_M and translation by 2, that gaps three of the four blocks of every fibre with a unique gapped heavy sector and a gapless fourth block; and, if it exists, whether strain induces a light-block mass once P_stag is broken.
