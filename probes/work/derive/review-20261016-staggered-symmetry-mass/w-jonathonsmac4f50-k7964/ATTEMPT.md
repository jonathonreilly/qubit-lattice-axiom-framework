## 2. Steps

**Step 1 [PROVED, CHECKED B2]** The epsilon structure is a Z2 grading. For every Hermitian `h` with `eps h = -h eps` (bipartite hops of any odd range, any link lift): `[eps,h] = 2 eps h`, hence `||[eps,h]||_F^2 = 4||h||_F^2`, exactly. The commutator is therefore never zero for `h != 0`, so continuous time with `exp(i theta Q_eps)` is not a symmetry of `H`. This is the exact content of "anticommutation with epsilon is not the literal continuous commutation".

**Step 2 [PROVED, CHECKED F1, F2, G1]** In `S_E`, the axial rotation `chi->e^{i theta eps}chi, chibar->chibar e^{i theta eps}` leaves the hop term invariant for every `theta` (because `{eps,h}=0`) but multiplies `chibar d_tau chi` by `e^{2 i theta eps}`; it survives only if time is also staggered. In regulator R4 (`D4` on `Z^3 x Z_tau`, all four hops bipartite) `{eps,D}=0` and the quarter-turn leaves `chibar D chi` invariant (G1, 256 sites, exact). So the continuous `U(1)_eps` claimed by the C5 panel is a property of R4, not of R. In R the exact remnants are `Theta` (= time reversal composed with the quarter turn of the axial rotation and a vector rotation by -pi/2, F2) and `P_stag`. Which regulator the framework uses is not fixed by the axioms (READING; first-order consequence: the protecting symmetry set differs between R and R4).

**Step 3 [PROVED, CHECKED E1-E3, B6, F3]** `Theta` holds for the whole gauged, interacting class: any Hermitian hop whose entries connect opposite sublattices with abelian link products (`Theta` maps `a_xy W_xy chi_x^dag chi_y` to `conj(a_xy) W_xy^dag chi_y^dag chi_x`, which is the Hermitian partner), the Maxwell terms (`A_j` even, `E` odd), and any even polynomial in the Theta-odd densities `(n_x - 1/2)` and correlated hops. So `Theta` is equivalent to bipartiteness; it is lift-independent. Exact 16x16 check on four sites (E2) and sign bookkeeping (F3): the terms `d_tau`, hop, `A_0` are Theta-even, the mass `m eps` is Theta-odd. Control E3.

**Step 4 [PROVED, CHECKED E4, E5]** Exact nonperturbative lemma (short proof): if `Theta H Theta^{-1} = H` and the ground state is non-degenerate then `G_xy(i0)^* = -eps_x eps_y G_yx(i0)` (Lehmann sum, antiunitarity), while Hermiticity gives `G_xy(0)^* = G_yx(0)`; hence every same-sublattice entry of the zero-frequency propagator vanishes, with arbitrary interactions. Checked at 40 digits on an interacting four-site cluster (same-sublattice entries 1e-40, opposite 0.46; Theta-odd control 0.36). Scope: this removes the epsilon-commuting (untwisted) masses of an interacting theory. It does not touch masses that sit between opposite sublattices (Step 6).
