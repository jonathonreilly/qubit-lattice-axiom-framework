## F1: permitted incomplete outcomes are not fully supported by the analyzer

**Location:** wrapper lines 119–125; analyzer lines 30–39 and 53–63.

The wrapper explicitly emits a `wrapper_exception` row with `case`, `status`, `error` and `traceback`, but no `name` and potentially no per-run receipt. The analyzer reads `row['name']` before testing the status and later requires a receipt for every started row. Consequently one such permitted failure aborts analysis of all otherwise usable histories. A separately assembled 2496-case manifest, with two synthetic completed histories, one row in the exact wrapper-exception schema, and all remaining cases not started, reproduces `KeyError: 'name'` at analyzer line 31. Its complete inputs, stdout, traceback and expected-exit receipt are preserved.

The nonexception version of the same fixture also exposes the zero-completion behavior: the declared grid has twelve cells, but only the one with completed rows appears in `cells`. The plan asks for a completed count for every cell. In addition, `conditional_on_cap_completion = (n != expected)` also labels deadline or verification exclusions as cap censoring. Enumerating only observed groups means the seed index is the observed-cell index rather than the declared-cell index if an earlier cell has no usable histories.

**Narrow correction:** handle the actual `wrapper_exception` schema before requiring a run name/receipt, preserve its failure details in exclusions, and construct cell/status counts from the declared grid, including zero-completion cells with no estimable values. Keep cap censoring, nonstart, process failure and verification failure distinguishable; derive bootstrap seed indices from the declared lexicographic grid if that is the intended declared indexing. This does not change any successful nondegenerate cell's estimator.

## F2: one undefined ratio aborts all otherwise defined summaries

**Location:** analyzer line 17 and lines 56–61.

Ratios divide by the empirical mean S1 or mean W without a denominator status. A subsequent assertion requires every point estimate and every bootstrap entry to be finite. This can fail on allowed full configurations. For example, a full matching consisting of x-axis dimers starting at even x has zero winding and zero first-axis-shell power. Such a matching is reachable by a finite sequence of births with positive probability for every finite beta,kappa>0. In fact

    F_x = sigma/3 + (-1)^(y+z)/2,     F_y = F_z = -sigma/6,

so its Fourier support lies away from the first-axis shell on the declared even tori, and all plane fluxes vanish. The independent full-state fixtures include this configuration.

The preserved all-zero-winding synthetic cell produces an undefined winding fourth-moment ratio and aborts at line 61, although the means, time/site summaries and positive-S1 shell ratios remain defined. A stronger separate control has **positive** empirical mean W: one of two histories has W=0 and the other W>0. A two-row bootstrap resample has zero mean W with probability 1/4. The point estimate is defined, but the required all-finite-bootstrap assertion still aborts the complete analysis.

**Narrow correction:** retain the defined means and diagnostics, and report an explicit undefined point/interval status for each affected ratio. Specify how zero-denominator bootstrap replicates are handled. Silently dropping them and calling the result the original unconditional percentile bootstrap would change the stated procedure. This finding does not say these events occurred in production, whose values remain unread.

## F3: the declared across-history standard errors are absent

**Location:** protocol lines 32–34; analyzer lines 56–63 and 72–84.

The protocol promises means, standard errors across runs, and whole-history bootstrap intervals. The analysis plan supplements definitions and does not explicitly withdraw that requirement. Actual output entries contain only `estimate` and `CI95_pointwise`; neither standard errors nor a stated deviation are emitted. The synthetic successful output confirms this.

**Narrow correction:** add the declared across-history standard errors for primary means, with an explicit undefined status where fewer than two histories are available. If ratio standard errors are also intended by the protocol, use paired covariance or the declared paired bootstrap and identify the convention. Alternatively document the reporting deviation transparently while preserving the original predeclared protocol. Do not silently rewrite the historical plan after seeing production values.

## Generator reconstruction and implementation check

For a matching M, let B(M) count **unoriented** vacant-vacant nearest-neighbor edges. For every occupied middle vertex b and vacant neighbor c, its unique partner a supplies exactly one ordered length-two slide (a,b,c). Thus H(M), the number of slide channels, equals the number of occupied-vacant bonds. The generator at nu=0 is

    L f(M) = beta sum_vacant_edges [f(M+edge)-f(M)]
             + kappa sum_(a,b,c) [f(M-ab+bc)-f(M)].

The slide moves record a to b and record b to c; its inverse is (c,b,a). It preserves both immutable identities and their pairing. The integrated geometric birth intensity is beta, independent of the omitted continuous content mark. The simulation consequently checks the geometric projection, not the operation that recognizes unknown physical quantum states.

C++ birth ids `3*x+i` enumerate each unoriented bond once for N>=4. Slide ids `6*b+d` enumerate each occupied-vacant bond once, with all turns included. The program implements total hazard beta B+kappa H, an exponential waiting time, proportional category choice and uniform choice within the active category. The finite-precision standard-library draws implement the usual Gillespie algorithm; no stronger random-number claim was inferred.

The active catalog stores occupancy-dependent edge indices, **not stale partner triples**. Births change the occupancy of a,b; slides change the occupancy of a,c while b remains occupied. The refresh routine updates all directed/undirected indexed bonds incident to each changed occupancy. Therefore refreshing a,c after a slide is sufficient even though b's partner changes: the executed partner is read from the current state. The independent controls inspect actual active vectors and their inverse positions, rather than trusting the program's `--catalog` predicates alone.

`catalog_harness.cpp` includes the unchanged supplied Model but implements its own coordinate arithmetic, channel enumeration and reference state updates. The fixture generator independently enumerates all 108 matchings of an eight-vertex cube embedded internally and another 108 across the periodic boundary of an N=4 torus. Additional N=4 and N=6 configurations include emptiness, all three columnar directions, nonzero-winding matchings, one removed pair, and a full matching with mixed orientations.

The checks cover 243 configurations, 37,092 individually executed birth channels, 4,248 individually executed slides and their exact marked inverses. Among those slides, 3,168 turn and 1,332 cross a periodic seam. Every transition's complete partner/identity/site-birth vectors and both refreshed active catalogs agree with the reference calculation. These counts describe finite control coverage; the occupancy-dependency argument above is what supports the general catalog update.

Three tiny independently seeded fresh growth runs (N=4,4,6) finish under a 200,000-event cap, and an N=4 one-event run is correctly nonfull. Their state and event budgets are checked; they are not estimates of production observables or a kinetic screen. The final-state NumPy wrapper is exercised on the three completed toys only. Each compressed archive is reopened, compared with the original arrays, and independently reconstructed back into exactly the same original ASCII bytes and SHA-256. Only redundant files newly created inside this review directory are removed by that supplied wrapper function.

## Fourier, winding and conditional moment definitions

With sigma_x=(-1)^(x_1+x_2+x_3), positive-bond matching indicators n_i(x), and F_i(x)=sigma_x[n_i(x)-1/6], the exact charged identity is

    sum_i [F_i(x)-F_i(x-e_i)] = -sigma_x 1_{x vacant}.

For the orthonormal site transform z_i(k)=V^(-1/2) sum_x exp(-ik.x) F_i(x), write d_i=1-exp(-ik_i). Then

    S_L = |sum_i d_i z_i|^2 / sum_i |d_i|^2,
    S_T = (sum_i |z_i|^2-S_L)/2.

This uses the Hermitian longitudinal projector with d as the divergence **row**. Replacing `d @ z` by `vdot(d,z)` is generally incorrect. The independent countercontrol at ell=(1,-1,0), N=6 has correct divergence below 3e-17 and incorrect conjugated-row residual sqrt(3). Equivalently, the component-dependent half-bond phase converts the normal to the real sin(k_i/2) vector; that independently reconstructed expression agrees.

The C++ uses the orthonormal convention directly; NumPy uses `fftn(...,norm='ortho')`. All sixteen saved modes are checked on every fixture, totaling 3,888 comparisons. The largest power discrepancy is about 3.21e-16. Counts by |ell|^2 are 3,6,4,3 for 1,2,3,4. In particular the second **axis** shell is S4, not the diagonal S2. The tabulated S4/S1 ratio is consistent with that distinction.

At full packing, the plane flux Phi_i is independent of the plane, and sum_x F_i=N Phi_i. Hence

    W = sum_i Phi_i^2/(3N) = |sum_x F(x)|^2/(3V).

The code computes the same quantity. Subtracting the 1/6 term contributes zero to each plane sum on these even tori. The partial-state charge is retained, `winding_valid` is false there, and capped cases are excluded from full-state spectra. Exact Gauss or exact plane equality by itself says nothing about the distribution across winding sectors.

Under the additional homogeneous, circular Gaussian and common-variance comparison assumptions in the plan, one mode has four real Gaussian degrees of freedom, the mean of three independent axis modes has twelve, and winding has three. For Q=(sum_{j=1}^r X_j^2)/r, independence and E X_j^4=3 give E Q^2=(3r+r(r-1))/r^2=1+2/r. Thus the three stated ratios are exactly 3/2, 7/6 and 5/3. The separate rational control reconstructs this Wick calculation. These are conditional benchmarks; integer windings, a Gauss constraint and immutability do not imply them.

## Statistical reconstruction and source limits

The normal analyzer path correctly constructs one row per completed history, averages modes within the row, forms cell means, and computes ratios **of means**. The bootstrap samples complete rows with common indices across numerator/denominator columns. It preserves within-history correlations, including between shell and winding statistics. Both first-axis fourth-moment numerators are distinct and correctly implemented.

For two independently assembled synthetic rows, a scalar reference implementation reconstructs every estimator and all 10,000 paired resamples under the declared seed. All point estimates and percentile endpoints agree. A mean-of-within-history-ratios countercalculation differs, as intended. This verifies the implementation of the specified resampling law, not the inferential coverage of a percentile interval or the independence of actual production streams.

The wrapper's declared grid, seed arithmetic, nu=0, kappa=1, cap, two-worker count, source/binary pins, deadline gate, result hashing and array checks were inspected. Distinct integer seeds are verified in its code; statistical independence of the underlying streams is an assumption of the Monte Carlo use, not a proved property here. No production binary, raw trajectory, final archive, receipt set, completion counts or resulting estimate was authenticated in this task. The fresh test binary is compiled from the frozen C++ with its compiler command/version logged. The whole wrapper production entry point was not run; only its isolated final-state verification function was used on our tiny files.
