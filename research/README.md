# Research sections

Each folder here is the research record of one program: mathematics on a
supplied model, in which no axiom of this repository enters. These texts have
no rows in the audit ledger and no audit status. Landing them grants no
standing. The rule is the row "Research sections" in
`docs/ai_methodology/SETTINGS.md`.

| Section | What it is | Verification its README states |
|---|---|---|
| [ym4d/](ym4d/README.md) | Parts and posted notes on 4D lattice Yang–Mills, working on the Sussman–Alpöge account | verification inventory open; obligation listed there |
| [mass-gap/su2-aligned-response/](mass-gap/su2-aligned-response/README.md) | Five notes of the SU(2) gauge-aligned response lane | none yet; listed there as an open obligation |

Each part says who wrote it and who read it. Words such as "frozen" and
"checked line by line" are defined in each section's README. They are project
words, not the standing words of `docs/ai_methodology/RULES.md` section 1.

A new section adds its row here in the PR that adds the section.
