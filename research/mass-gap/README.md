## The recorded target

The program targets a positive Hamiltonian gap for a volume-first continuum limit of four-dimensional Wilson SU(2) Yang–Mills, on the sector generated by tr(F^2); see [gap-skeleton-final.md](library/gap-skeleton-final.md), Section 1. The texts cite Balaban's work and the external account `YM4_1400.tex` by Ethan Sussman and Levent Alpoge, with Claude and Codex credited as authors of that account, at its recorded commit `2bc0658f91d2`. These are historical provenance claims. This snapshot neither verifies that account nor establishes continuum existence; the final skeleton leaves T0 open. Any use of external existence or limit statements remains conditional on their exact hypotheses and applicability.
