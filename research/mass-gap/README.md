# Mass gap for four-dimensional SU(2) Yang–Mills: working record

This section holds the mass-gap program of the YM4D work in this repository, in the open. It is snapshot 1. The working branch is `claude/ym4d-hard-route` (draft PR #10132); the files here are byte-identical copies of the branch at commit `90bf9e6f5` (hashes in [SHA256SUMS.txt](SHA256SUMS.txt)).

**No mass gap is proved.** This is a research record, not framework science. No axiom of this repository enters these texts. They have no rows in the audit ledger and no audit status. A note that "holds" was checked line by line by the model named; that is a research check, not an audit.

## The target

Wilson lattice Yang–Mills with gauge group SU(2) in four dimensions. The target (GAP) is a positive gap of the Hamiltonian of a volume-first continuum limit, on the sector generated by tr(F^2), as stated in [library/gap-skeleton-final.md](library/gap-skeleton-final.md) Section 1. The ultraviolet side, the construction of the limit, is the work of Tadeusz Balaban and of the complete account `YM4_1400.tex` by Ethan Sussman and Levent Alpoge, with Claude and Codex as authors of their text (commit `2bc0658f91d2`). The mass-gap program uses their statements, not their proofs, and only where a file says so.

- **The limit half needs the centre-even sector only** (Theorem EV-L), and the crossing step works for exactly three sector sets, periodic, full and kappa (Proposition CL, Theorem S-w; [gap-flux-exercise.md](library/gap-flux-exercise.md), row 731): checked by Codex gpt-6-astra max (row 732).

## What is open

The crux: for SU(2) at an effective coupling of order one, either small centre-even thermal sums on all cubes, or one of the three seeds at one physical size, uniformly in the cutoff. Proved gap results elsewhere verify their criterion at the scale of the correlation length by a small number there, a second small number after an exact change of variables, exact closure, or a correlation inequality that gives a dichotomy ([gap-form-exercise.md](library/gap-form-exercise.md), row 735, not checked). None of these is known for four-dimensional SU(2). Not checked: rows 733 and 735.
