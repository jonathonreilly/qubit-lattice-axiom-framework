# ENS cavity-calibrated microscopic-shape test

Frozen exploratory design before computing the new fit, 2026-09-27. Prior exposure: the complete public CSV, article discussion of harmonic discrepancies, and earlier KIT residuals have already been read. This is retrospective withheld-observable testing, not a blinded prediction or native TOE test.

Target: test a supplied one-transparency short-junction Andreev potential. Its shape parameter tau is calibrated using a cavity transition with the transmon in level2, rather than using the higher transmon transitions to be evaluated. This is an additional calibration measurement, not a coefficient known a priori. Hypothesis of one shared transparency is a restrictive model-family choice and need not describe the device. A good comparison would not identify the mechanism uniquely (series inductance can also cause harmonics).

Hamiltonian in GHz: H=4EC(n-ng)^2+V_tau(phi)+Omega a†a+G n(a+a†). V_tau(phi) proportional to -sqrt[1-tau*sin²(phi/2)], with the cos(phi) coefficient normalized to -EJ1. All its higher Fourier coefficients follow from tau, without separate fits. tau=0 is the pure-cosine limit. Counterrotating terms retained. Standard canonical quantum mechanics, charge-to-circuit mapping, short-junction ground-state potential, zero-temperature approximation, and physical readout are imported.

Calibration data: ENS f01,f02,fres1,fres2,fres3 from pinned Experiment.csv (Willsch2024 DOI10.26165/JUELICH-DATA/LGRHUH). fres1 indexes transmon state0, fres2 state1, fres3 state2. Fit EC,EJ1,Omega,G,tau using exactly these five values. No higher qubit transitions or fres4..7 enter fit, initial guesses, model selection or optimizer stopping. Declared starts tau .01,.2,.6; EC.18, EJ1~20, Omega7.75,G.1. Bounds EC [.03,1], EJ1[1,100], Omega[7,8.5],G[.001,1],tau[0,.99]. Require residuals below1e-7GHz for an exact calibration solution; otherwise retain best residuals and boundary flags as model tension, not an exact calibration. Baseline cosine model fits only f01,f02,fres1,fres2; its fres3 is additional evaluated information, unlike the extended model.

Primary numerical truncation: n=-16..16, lowest14 transmon states, photon cutoff9; compare n=-20..20,M18,K12 before empirical claims. FFT4096 Fourier samples, compare8192 if needed. State labels from unique dominant bare-state overlaps; print minimum overlaps, preserve branch changes. Spectroscopy convention: mean of ng0 and ng.5 endpoints, explicitly supplied and not established by the CSV as measured parity averaging; preserve both endpoint predictions to expose dispersion.

Evaluation only after freezing calibration parameters: transmon f03..f06 drive frequencies f0j/j and cavity fres4..fres7. Report signed errors in MHz, endpoint spread and cutoff change. No statistical significance claim without source uncertainties. Initial practical comparator is1MHz for drive errors as a declared resolution scale, not a justified ENS Gaussian error; cavity accuracy must be read from original preparation/readout source. Assess conditioning by a scaled finite-difference calibration Jacobian and perturbation of the fifth cavity calibration; weak identifiability may prevent a strong conclusion even with point agreement.

Next: verify ENS preparation/readout and precision in Lescanne2019; implement both model families; freeze all numerical calibration outcomes before evaluation. Keep failures and do not add extra harmonics merely to fit held-out outputs.
